## Chapter 3173 — Such a Big Appetite

In the main hall, Yang Kai watched the old man with a smiling expression, while everyone else looked startled and uncertain.

The old man said, "My lords... who might you be?"

"Still playing dumb!"

"This old man does not know what young brother is talking about."

Yang Kai looked him up and down and grinned. "The corpse aura on you is quite strong. You must be well versed in refining corpses. Which member of Netherworld Sect are you?"

"Netherworld Sect!" Ye Xijun and the others' expressions changed, and they all glared at the old man. None of them could see anything special about him, but since Yang Kai had said it, he definitely was not wrong.

Every cultivator in the Star Domain hated the name Netherworld Sect to the bone. They had thought that after sweeping the world clean three years ago, everything had been settled, but they never imagined there would still be a fish that had escaped the net, and right under their noses at that.

"Young brother must be mistaken. This old man is no Netherworld..."

"What did you come to my Dark Star for? What are you looking for?"

The old man shook his head. "This old man does not understand your meaning."

Yang Kai raised his hand, and a chilling murderous intent enveloped the old man. In the next instant, the old man's hunched figure suddenly straightened, and his aura erupted with a roar.

Ye Xijun and the others stared in shock. Only then did they realize that the old man before them was no Origin Returning Realm cultivator at all. He was clearly a peak Void King Realm master, his aura even stronger than any Void King Realm master they had ever encountered, as if he might break through the shackles of the Void King Realm at any moment.

No one knew what Secret Technique he had used, but he had hidden his cultivation without letting even a drop leak out. If Yang Kai had not seen through him so clearly, no one present would have noticed the slightest flaw.

The killing blow he expected never came. Yang Kai lowered his raised hand again. How could the old man fail to realize he had been toyed with? He stopped pretending and only snorted coldly. "You forced me to do this."

He had indeed been hiding on Dark Star in search of something. He had not wanted to confront Lingxiao Sect so soon, but Heaven had not granted him his wish. Since matters had reached this point, he could only start killing.

As he spoke, he summoned a great banner into his hand. The long banner unfurled, blood Qi surged, and the entire hall turned a deep crimson.

Inside the hall, everyone from Lingxiao Sect instantly felt as if they had fallen into a surging river of blood. The blood Qi around them gave off an extremely powerful corrosive aura, making it hard to breathe and weakening their Saint Yuan.

Everyone's expression changed.

They all knew this Blood Sea Banner. In their clashes with Netherworld Sect cultivators over the years, every one of them had experienced the Blood Sea Banner's might, but none had ever seen one so terrifying!

It seemed that once this Blood Sea Banner was summoned, even a Third Stage Void King Realm cultivator would be powerless to resist.

"There is a road to Heaven, yet you refuse to take it; there is no gate to Hell, yet you insist on barging in. Today, this old man will turn every last one of you into..." The old man got only halfway through his words before he suddenly bit his tongue, because in the sea of blood, a small figure had strolled up before him, raised her head, and was gazing up at him innocently.

Not the slightest fluctuation of power came from that figure, yet she was strangely unaffected by his Blood Sea Banner. That tiny figure suddenly seemed as majestic as a towering mountain, making him feel as if he had to look up at her.

"How is that possible!" The old man paled in horror.

This Secret Treasure of his had long since surpassed the limits of the Star Domain. It was also a treasure bestowed by a certain lord. He had dared to remain fearless after his identity was exposed precisely because he relied on this Secret Treasure's might.

After all, no one in this Star Domain should have been able to escape the imprisonment and corrosion of his Blood Sea Banner. Anyone who fell into it was doomed.

Yet now, this little girl of seven or eight had ignored the power of his Blood Sea Banner.

The little girl before him blinked, lifted one small hand, and grabbed. The old man felt as if lightning had struck him and could not help staggering back several steps. His face paled slightly, and his horror deepened.

His connection to the Blood Sea Banner... had actually been severed!

When he looked up again, the blood color shrouding the hall had vanished without a trace, and the Blood Sea Banner was already in the little girl's hand. She casually stuffed it into her Spatial Ring, then turned and walked back without even glancing at him.

The old man's throat went dry, and he swallowed hard.

His greatest reliance had been stripped away so easily, and even his mind had suffered a backlash. The old man, who had been wild with arrogance a moment ago, instantly withered like a venomous snake with its fangs pulled. None of his earlier contempt remained.

Compared with the wound caused by losing his Secret Treasure, the blow to his spirit was even greater.

The feeling that little girl gave him... was barely weaker than that lord's.

How could such a powerful person exist in this Star Domain? No wonder the Great Wilderness Star Domain had been slaughtered to the last over these years. With this person overseeing things, how could the invasion possibly have succeeded?

"Was this Secret Treasure upgraded for you by that person?" In the main hall, Yang Kai sat with an imposing, unrestrained posture, one hand propping up his cheek as he regarded the old man at his leisure.

The old man trembled, bowed all the way to the floor, and said respectfully, "Whatever My Lord wishes to know, this old man will tell everything he knows without reservation. I only ask My Lord to spare this old man's life." The situation was no longer in his favor, and he instantly became far more obedient.

"You do not seem qualified to bargain with me."

The old man lowered his head. "I beg My Lord to spare this old man's life. This old man has never done anything harmful to this Star Domain."

Yang Kai thought about it seriously for a moment, then nodded. "Good. Then I will not kill you."

"May My Lord's words be taken as truth?"

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Usually, when I say something like that, I am probably bluffing."

The old man's face immediately went pale.

"However..." Yang Kai changed the subject. "This time, if I say I will not kill you, then I will not kill you."

"Many thanks, My Lord." The old man was so grateful that tears streamed down his face. "May I ask what My Lord wishes to know?"

"You are Netherworld Sect's Sect Master, aren't you?" Yang Kai asked.

The old man was stunned, then smiled bitterly. "My Lord's eyes are bright as torches. This old man is unworthy, but I have the honor of serving as Netherworld Sect's three hundred and forty-fifth generation Sect Master."

"What?"

"He is Netherworld Sect's Sect Master?"

The main hall burst into noise, and everyone stared at the old man with incomparable anger. Netherworld Sect had been the chief culprit behind the Great Wilderness Star Domain's invasion. Over the years, they had killed many members of Netherworld Sect, even several Vice Sect Masters and Elders, but they had never found any trace of Netherworld Sect's Sect Master. Those Elders and Vice Sect Masters had not known where he was either. They had only heard that after arriving in Hengluo Star Domain, the Sect Master had left alone. As for where he had gone and what he had gone to do, no one knew.

Among all the information they had gathered, there had been almost no news of this man at all.

Who would have thought this old fellow had hidden on Dark Star at some point, and only today had Yang Kai found him? This was probably a case of being too clever for his own good. He thought the most dangerous place was the safest, but he did not know that after Yang Kai became Master of the Star Domain, his perception of Dark Star had grown sharper and sharper. How could Yang Kai fail to notice an existence so plainly carrying Netherworld Sect's aura?

If he had hidden on some other star, Yang Kai probably would not have bothered looking for him, and he might have stayed hidden for a while longer.

"Was that Secret Treasure of yours upgraded by that person?" Yang Kai asked the earlier question again.

"That person? I do not know who My Lord means..." The old man looked at Yang Kai in alarm and suspicion.

"That fellow from your Great Wilderness Star Domain."

"You have met that lord?" the old man asked in horror.

Yang Kai shook his head. "No." After a pause, he said, "We exchanged blows once in secret."

The old man was dumbfounded. Instinctively, he felt this fellow was boasting wildly. No matter what cultivation the man before him had, how could he survive exchanging blows with that lord? But then he suddenly remembered the abnormalities he had sensed a few days earlier and cried out in alarm, "You are already the Master of this Star Domain?"

"Yes."

"No wonder, no wonder!" the old man muttered. No wonder he had felt something strange in the Star Domain a few days ago, and no wonder Yang Kai could exchange moves with that lord. So he had already become the master of this Star Domain.

Only the Master of a Star Domain was qualified to exchange blows with the Master of another Star Domain.

Yang Kai watched his expression, and a thought suddenly struck him. "You hid for many years and acted alone. Were you searching for my Hengluo Star Domain's Star Domain Origin?"

The old man's expression shifted, but since Yang Kai had already exposed him, he no longer tried to hide it and nodded. "I was only acting under orders."

Yang Kai snorted coldly. "That fellow truly has quite the appetite!"

Crack...

Thunder boomed, lightning crashed down, and wind and clouds gathered in the sky. Dense black clouds suddenly formed overhead, like a heavy quilt. Beneath that blazing Heavenly might, everyone felt unbearably oppressed.

After becoming Master of the Star Domain, his every movement, smile, anger, and curse could affect this world.

Seeing this, the old man had no more doubts and finally confirmed that Yang Kai had indeed become Master of the Star Domain.

Yang Kai continued to sneer. "He wants to swallow my Hengluo Star Domain in one bite." From the looks of it now, it was no longer accurate to say Netherworld Sect was the chief culprit behind the invasion by the Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators. Most likely, Netherworld Sect had also been driven and used by someone, and the shadow behind it all was naturally the Master of the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

By using the war between the cultivators of the two great Star Domains to throw Hengluo Star Domain into chaos and destabilize it, he could force the Star Domain Origin to reveal traces of itself. At that point, as long as this old man found the Star Domain Origin, the person on the other side of the darkness could act to devour and refine it.

Once he successfully refined Hengluo Star Domain's Origin, the two great Star Domains would probably truly merge together, no longer separate from each other.

At that time, that person would become the master of two Star Domains, and his strength would rise along with the tide.

The method was not especially clever, but it was extremely effective.

If Yang Kai had not returned to the Star Domain, that person might already have succeeded by now. When that happened, Hengluo Star Domain would certainly become a vassal of the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

"This old man also does not know that lord's purpose," Netherworld Sect's Sect Master said, his brows lowered and eyes downcast.

"Is that so?" Yang Kai gave him a meaningful smile, making the old man's heart jump.

"Forget it. Come with me." As Yang Kai spoke, he stood, grabbed the old man by the collar with one hand, and took a few steps. In the next moment, they were already in the Starry Sky.

"My Lord, spare me!" the old man shouted, his face full of panic.

Earlier, when he faced that strange little girl, he had felt she was impossible to oppose by force. But only now, facing Yang Kai directly, did he understand that he could not even summon the thought of resisting.

This was the power of the Master of a Star Domain!

He did not think Yang Kai's own strength was much greater than his. The reason Yang Kai could casually seize and control him was entirely because the Star Domain Origin had acknowledged him. If he had such an opportunity as well...

"What are you shouting for?" Yang Kai slapped the back of his head.
