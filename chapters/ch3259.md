## Chapter 3259 — Consider It at Length

Reaching the Southern Territory from the Northern Territory in a single instant was not impossible. A cross-territory Space Array could do it. But such things were exceedingly rare even across the entire Star Realm. It seemed only a handful of Great Emperor Sects possessed them, relics passed down from ancient times, and no one even knew whether they could still be used.

The woman and the older man had not thought of that before, but the whispers around them finally jolted them awake.

Could the array they had just used have been a cross-territory Space Array? The more they thought about it, the more likely it seemed. Only a cross-territory Space Array could leave such severe aftereffects. After all, they had crossed hundreds of millions of li in an instant; ordinary transmissions could not compare at all.

Where had Lingxiao Palace gotten a cross-territory Space Array? The territory Lingxiao Palace occupied had originally belonged to Wenqing Sect. Most of them had visited Wenqing Sect before, and they basically knew what kind of foundation it had. Wenqing Sect could not possibly have owned a cross-territory Space Array.

They had long heard that Yang Kai, the Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace, was proficient in the Laws of Space. Could he even arrange a cross-territory Space Array? If so, that was a truly grand undertaking!

The woman and the older man looked at each other and saw the same shock in one another's eyes. The surrounding whispers grew louder and louder, with the words "Southern Territory" appearing again and again. Some still refused to believe it, while others, like them, stared at the Space Array with burning eyes.

If this truly was a cross-territory Space Array, it was an enormous treasure trove. Since Yang Kai could see the profits to be made from the flow of goods between two territories, they could naturally see them as well. With an array connecting north and south, any Sect could grow rich.

To be cautious, the woman was just about to find someone from Lingxiao Palace and ask around when her gaze swept across the area and her face went blank.

The older man had still not recovered from the shock of reaching the Southern Territory in an instant through a Space Array. Seeing her expression, he was startled and sent a voice transmission. "What is it?"

The woman did not react at all. She only stared fixedly in one direction. Puzzled, the old man followed her line of sight and was instantly dumbstruck.

Over there stood eight or nine stone men several zhang tall, their bodies all made of hard, distinct blocks of stone. Each had an extraordinary aura. The sight alone struck the eye with tremendous force. Bodies seemingly piled together from chunks of rock brimmed with an indescribable sense of power, and no one would dare doubt their formidable strength in battle.

These... these could not be the legendary Stone Puppets, could they? Were they not said to have gone extinct? How had so many appeared here?

If that had been all, it might have been bearable, but opposite the Stone Puppets stood rows of shadowy figures. There were not many of them, only about thirty or so, yet every one of them radiated Yao Qi that soared to the heavens, terrifying beyond measure. Earlier, the woman and the older man had been too shaken by the existence of the cross-territory Space Array to notice this Yao Qi at all.

Now that they probed carefully, both of their expressions changed.

These yao were all Yao King-level existences, and not one of them was someone the two of them could contend with.

The woman and the older man clearly saw the three Yao Kings of Lingxiao Palace mingling with the other yao, laughing and chatting together. When they counted carefully, there were actually thirty-two...

In other words, there were thirty-two Yao Kings here! What kind of force was that? One had to know that most so-called top Sects only had a single Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master holding them down. Li Dragon Palace only had Li Jiao, Mitian Sect only had Mi Qi, Wenqing Sect back then had only one as well, and Ice Heart Valley likewise had only Bing Yun.

By comparison, the power represented by these thirty-two Yao Kings was terrifying to the extreme. To put it bluntly, even if the two hundred Emperor Realm Masters from the Northern Territory gathered here joined forces, they still might not be able to beat them. Only if they truly united as one would they have the strength to fight.

Were they helpers the Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace had brought in too? Where had such a group of Yao Kings come from? Why had no one ever heard of them before?

Gulp...

The sound of people swallowing rang out one after another. Everyone's attention shifted away from the cross-territory Space Array and onto these Yao Kings. The Yao Kings clearly noticed as well. Many of them split open their bloody maws as though playing a prank, and the Yao Qi around them grew even more terrifying. The many Emperor Realm Masters from the Northern Territory watched with their hearts in their throats, afraid these fellows might go mad, charge in, and start a massacre. If that happened, they would likely be doomed.

The woman suddenly recalled a rumor. Her pupils shrank, and after glancing at the two men and one woman standing before those Thirty-Two Yao Kings, she quietly transmitted her voice. "Thirty-two Yao Kings. Does that remind you of anything?"

"Remind me of what?" the older man replied without thinking.

The woman said, "I heard that in the Eastern Territory, there is a Wild Ancient Land that still preserves the aura of the wilderness, unchanged through ten thousand years and countless ages. And within that Wild Ancient Land are the Four Great Saint Venerables, each with eight powerful Yao Kings under their command..."

The older man murmured in a daze, "Four Great Saint Venerables, each with eight Yao Kings?" Was that not exactly thirty-two? In truth, he had heard this rumor as well, but a rumor was only a rumor. Without seeing it with one's own eyes, who would dare believe it?

Yet there could not be such a coincidence in the world. The Wild Ancient Land had its Thirty-Two Yao Kings, and there just happened to be thirty-two here too.

The older man's face went deathly pale. "You mean they came from the Wild Ancient Land? But that is in the Eastern Territory..."

The woman said, "So what if it is the Eastern Territory? We came from the Northern Territory, did we not? So that is it. So the three Yao Kings of Lingxiao Palace came from the Wild Ancient Land!"

The existence of Yingfei, Xie Wuwei, and Xi Lei was no secret. It was just that many people in the Northern Territory had never understood where Yang Kai had subdued three Yao Kings and brought them back to Lingxiao Palace to guard the place. Only now did this question that had puzzled everyone for so long find a suitable answer.

"That does make sense. Those Stone Puppets rumored to have gone extinct long ago probably came out of the Wild Ancient Land as well." The older man nodded lightly, then suddenly sucked in a cold breath and said in a trembling voice, "If these Stone Puppets and Yao Kings are from the Wild Ancient Land, then... then who are the three in front?"

The woman turned her head stiffly to look at him. She had thought of this question long ago, and now spoke each word with care. "Divine... Spirits!"

The older man immediately lowered his head. After weighing it for a while, he transmitted, "This old man feels that what we discussed earlier was somewhat ill-considered. We should think it over at length."

They had originally planned to wait until this matter was over, then link up with the other Emperor Realm Masters of the Northern Territory to resist the pressure from Lingxiao Palace. But now, seeing Yang Kai casually pull out such a huge group of helpers, Stone Puppets, Yao Kings, and even Divine Spirits among them, who would dare make trouble?

Although they might all be in the Eastern Territory, with a cross-territory Space Array, traveling from the Eastern Territory to the Northern Territory would take no more than a breath. Since they had appeared here now, that meant there was also a cross-territory Space Array on the Eastern Territory side. If they truly angered Lingxiao Palace and Yang Kai brought this group to the Northern Territory, then even if every Sect in the Northern Territory joined forces, they still might not be his match, let alone when everyone's hearts would certainly be divided.

The woman nodded in deep agreement. "Actually, it is not bad for the Northern Territory to have a leading Sect. It is still better than being scattered sand."

This was probably self-consolation.

The cultivation of the two hundred-plus Emperor Realm Masters from the Northern Territory was plain to see, and all of them were experienced and well-informed. Since the woman and the older man could see the origins of the Stone Puppets and Yao Kings, there was no reason the others could not.

Now that they saw so many Yao Kings and even Divine Spirits obediently waiting here, who would dare have any complaints? One after another, they lowered their eyes and waited honestly off to the side, the shock in their hearts beyond words.

Mi Qi and Li Jiao had gathered together. Mi Qi's expression kept shifting, and he secretly cried out in relief. Fortunately, he had not completely torn face with Lingxiao Palace back then. Otherwise, how could Mitian Sect still exist now? He feared he would not even know how he died.

Li Jiao handled himself rather well. After all, he had once caused a great disturbance on Dragon Isle together with Yang Kai, and he knew about Yang Kai's close relationship with a Dragon Girl. So although he was somewhat astonished by Yang Kai's methods and connections, he was one of the few who appeared relatively calm. He even felt a trace of inexplicable superiority, thinking that if Yang Kai truly called over the Dragon Clan of Dragon Isle too, then things would become lively indeed.

It was just that he was not clear what attitude Dragon Isle currently held toward Yang Kai. Logically speaking, after causing such trouble with Dragon Isle, Yang Kai was bound to be its mortal enemy. The problem was that Yang Kai had also consummated a husband-and-wife relationship with a Dragon Girl.

Just as everyone was reeling in shock, Yang Kai had already led the final batch of people onto the Space Array.

In an instant, every eye turned toward him.

Yang Kai swept his gaze over the crowd and said in a clear voice, "This place is already the Southern Territory. Wuhua Hall is not far from here. In a moment, I ask all of you to unite your efforts, follow me to save people, and prevent the demonized from fleeing. Your first priority is to protect yourselves and avoid being corroded by Demonic Qi. Your second is to capture those who have been corroded by Demonic Qi. If you can avoid killing, do so."

"We cannot kill them?" The crowd broke into an uproar.

Fan Wu frowned. "Since they have already Entered the Demonic State, why spare their lives?" If they could not kill them, they could only capture them alive. Although everyone present was quite strong, taking people alive would still be troublesome. Moreover, what use was it to capture them? Entering the Demonic State was easy, but shaking off Demonic Intent was extremely difficult. In his view, once someone was corroded by Demonic Qi, there was no need to leave them alive.

"I have my own plans. Of course, if there is no other choice, I would rather kill them than allow a single demonized person to escape this place." Yang Kai's expression turned solemn. "Does everyone understand?"

"Understood." The two hundred-plus Emperor Realm Masters from the Northern Territory shouted in unison. At this point, no one dared deny Yang Kai face. A group of Yao Kings and three people suspected to be Divine Spirits were watching from over there.

"First, surround Wuhua Hall and await my orders!" Yang Kai waved his hand and took the lead, rushing toward Wuhua Hall. "Move out!"

The group followed close behind in a mighty procession.

A moment later, a faint curtain of light appeared in everyone's field of vision. It was Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array. Seeing the state of the great array, everyone's heart tightened. Even a single glimpse was enough to reveal the foundation of a top Sect.

As they approached the great array, Yang Kai swept both hands left and right, and several hundred people instantly split into two groups, flying out in orderly fashion along Wuhua Hall's array. One person stopped at regular intervals, and in less than half an incense stick's worth of time, they had completely surrounded Wuhua Hall.
