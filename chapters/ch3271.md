## Chapter 3271 — Exorcism

It was like scattering a handful of salt into boiling oil. As the halo spread, an inconceivable scene unfolded.

Crimson light burst from the bodies of more than ten thousand demonized people. One after another, they seemed to have been whipped into a frenzy, their momentum soaring in an instant.

Wen Zishan, Ma Qing, and Xiao Yuyang all changed color, shock written across their faces.

Ma Qing cried out, "What Secret Technique is this?"

Such a Secret Technique was simply unheard of, unseen before. As the crimson light flared, the momentum of more than ten thousand demonized people rose sharply, as if their strength had been boosted by a full step out of nowhere. A Secret Technique like this could be called nothing short of a miracle.

Just imagine two evenly matched forces locked in bloody battle. If one side suddenly received the support of such a Secret Technique, the outcome would no longer be in doubt.

And that was not even mentioning the fact that Yang Kai's Secret Technique could cover more than ten thousand people at once.

Ma Qing had never seen it. Xiao Yuyang had never seen it either. Wen Zishan naturally had never seen it as well. All three were dumbfounded.

Fan Wu and the other two, however, who had followed over to see what was happening, exchanged a glance. Cang Gou frowned and said, "Why do I feel like this is..." The last few words were spoken so softly that even though Ma Qing and the others strained their ears, they still could not hear them clearly.

Fan Wu nodded. "It does look somewhat like it."

Luan Feng frowned. "That should not be right. How could he possibly display this kind of Secret Technique?"

Although the three of them had never seen this kind of Secret Technique either, the Divine Spirit Origin inheritances each of them possessed contained similar memories. Still, since they had never seen it with their own eyes, they did not dare be too certain.

The three of them spoke back and forth, leaving Ma Qing and the others burning with curiosity. They could not help feeling almost frantic, thinking that if there was something to say, why not say it openly? What was the point of being so mysterious? But with three Divine Spirits in front of them, they did not dare act too presumptuously.

Just then, the incantation on Yang Kai's lips changed, and he pointed downward again. Pure, flawless white light gleamed at his fingertip, and another halo spread out.

As the milky-white light swept past, more than ten thousand demonized people groaned in pain all at once. Immediately afterward, strands of Demonic Qi seeped out of their bodies and vanished into nothingness.

Ma Qing's eyes widened. "It really works!"

It was not as though they had never tried to expel the Demonic Qi from these demonized people's bodies, but they had never found the right method. That strange Demonic Qi was extremely troublesome. Even if they could drive out some of it, they had no way to purge it completely, much less do so as effortlessly as Yang Kai.

One had to remember that they could only expel it one person at a time, while Yang Kai had enveloped more than ten thousand people with this Secret Technique in a single cast. The gap between them could not be measured in li.

"Ancient Shamanic Art!" Fan Wu's expression stirred as he called out in a low voice.

If it had only been suspicion before, then now it was certain. The Secret Technique Yang Kai was displaying was actually an Ancient Shamanic Art!

"Ancient Shamanic Art?" Ma Qing's eyes went round as he looked at Fan Wu in astonishment and humbly asked, "Brother Fan means this is a Secret Technique passed down from ancient times?"

Fan Wu glanced at him and ignored him.

After all, he was a Divine Spirit, and the many Yao Kings under his command were each comparable to Third-Stage Emperor Realm Masters. Ma Qing himself was only at that level. If he went to the Wild Ancient Land, he would be no more than one of Fan Wu's subordinates, yet he actually dared address Fan Wu as a brother. It would have been strange if Fan Wu had paid him any attention.

Seeing the disdain in Fan Wu's eyes, Ma Qing flushed bright red. Unfortunately, his fist was not as big as the other's, so he did not dare flare up. It was hard to say just how awkward he felt.

Wen Zishan laughed inwardly, but his face remained perfectly calm as he said, "This boy really has no shallow fortune. He even managed to obtain a Secret Technique from ancient times. This has truly helped us greatly."

Fan Wu said faintly, "This has nothing to do with whether he obtained one."

"How so?" Wen Zishan looked at him curiously.

Perhaps because of Yang Kai, Fan Wu’s attitude toward Wen Zishan was still fairly decent. After pondering for a moment, he said, “In the present Star Realm, it is not as if there are no Secret Techniques passed down from the ancient and remote eras, but have you ever seen anyone use them?”

Wen Zishan frowned. "I have not. Perhaps their owners treasure them too much and hide away to cultivate them."

Luan Feng gave a soft laugh. "They cannot be cultivated."

Wen Zishan was stunned. "Why not?"

Luan Feng said, "Every era has its own World Laws, and under the corresponding World Laws, corresponding Secret Techniques are born. Shamanic Arts belong to the ancient era, and only under World Laws like those of the ancient era can they be cultivated. The laws of the Star Realm today simply cannot meet the requirements."

Ma Qing and Wen Zishan looked at each other. "There is such a principle?"

Although the two of them were not weak in cultivation and were hardly short on experience, they truly had never heard of such a thing. Only peculiar existences like Divine Spirits, who could inherit memories through their Origins, could understand a little of it.

Xiao Yuyang nodded slightly from the side and said, "I have heard the Great Emperor mention something similar. The Great Emperor once said that if many of our Star Realm's current Secret Techniques were placed in another era, they would probably be unable to take root and bear fruit."

Fan Wu nodded. "Exactly."

Ma Qing asked in amazement, "But since Elder Yang can display it, that means he successfully cultivated it. Why is that?"

Yes, why was that? Not only did he not understand, the three Divine Spirits did not understand either. They even suspected that Yang Kai might have encountered some other opportunity in the Wild Ancient Land, or that he had successfully cultivated this Ancient Shamanic Art there.

The Wild Ancient Land had earned its name because that region preserved the aura of the ancient wilderness, unchanged through countless years. The World Laws of the Wild Ancient Land were somewhat different from the broader environment of the Star Realm.

Ordinary cultivators who entered the Wild Ancient Land would all be suppressed to some degree. The world believed this was due to the Primordial Aura, but in truth, it was caused by the difference in World Laws.

If there was any place in this world that could allow Yang Kai to cultivate this Ancient Shamanic Art, it could only be the Wild Ancient Land.

But Fan Wu and the others had never heard of any Shamanic Art inheritance in the Ancient Land. On top of that, Yang Kai had not stayed in the Ancient Land for very long, so where had he cultivated it?

"Since the three of you recognize this as an Ancient Shamanic Art, do you know exactly what it is?" Ma Qing asked again.

Fan Wu said, "If I am not mistaken, what that boy displayed first was the Bloodlust Art."

"Bloodlust Art?" Ma Qing raised a brow.

Fan Wu said, "It stimulates the Essence-Blood in the bodies of those affected by the spell, greatly increasing their strength for a certain period of time. I believe all of you know similar Secret Techniques."

Ma Qing froze for a moment. After envying it for so long, it turned out to be this sort of Secret Technique. Naturally, they all knew similar Secret Techniques. Not to mention the few of them, even ordinary cultivators had probably grasped some version of one; the only difference was how good or bad they were.

But while stimulating one's Essence-Blood could increase one's strength for a short time, afterward, one would inevitably become weak and fall into a recuperation period of uncertain length.

"This is one of the fundamental Shamanic Arts the Ancient Barbarian Tribe relied on to survive," Luan Feng said, taking over. "The Ancient Barbarian Tribe did not possess the flourishing Martial Dao we have now. Only a small number of those with outstanding aptitude among them could cultivate Shamanic Arts, while the rest tempered their bodies, muscles, and bones. However, there are also rumors that some could become Saints through the flesh alone, with methods not at all inferior to the Great Emperors of today. It was precisely because the bodies of those ancient Barbarian Tribesmen were so powerful that they could withstand the Bloodlust Art again and again, surviving in harsh environments. With the physical constitutions of today's Star Realm cultivators, I fear they could not endure it many times."

"So there was such a thing." Ma Qing sighed, his face filled with emotion. Now that he knew the truth behind the Bloodlust Art, there was nothing left to envy. After all, it required a strong physique to support it.

"The three of you truly are experienced and knowledgeable." Although he knew they had learned these things through their Origin inheritances, a bit of flattery cost nothing, and courtesy invited courtesy.

Fan Wu and the others did not explain the second Secret Technique further, but everyone understood it well enough. It should be a Secret Technique similar to the Mind-Clearing Art, only far more effective. It could almost be called the natural enemy of that Demonic Qi.

Over there, Yang Kai tirelessly cast the Exorcism Spell again and again. Low roars and pained howls continued to echo through the valley, and strands of Demonic Qi seeped out from the bodies of more than ten thousand demonized people. Like snowflakes beneath the scorching sun, they quickly dissolved into nothingness.

Half a day later, the condition of the more than ten thousand demonized people in the valley had improved greatly. Some had gradually regained their senses and sat down cross-legged, cooperating with Yang Kai's Exorcism Spell while regulating themselves.

One day later, Yang Kai had no choice but to stop and rest for a while.

Casting the Exorcism Spell without pause had nearly drained his Emperor Yuan dry. After taking several Emperor Pills, he took out some Source Crystals and meditated to recover. Only after an hour did he resume.

After a full three days, the Demonic Qi in the bodies of the more than ten thousand demonized people in the valley was finally purged clean. Still, not all of them were saved. At least a thousand people had been completely devoured in body and mind by the Demonic Qi, and even Yang Kai's Exorcism Spell could no longer bring them back. Applying the Exorcism Spell to them would only harm them, without the slightest saving effect.

For a time, the valley was a scene of bleak misery. The cultivators who had recovered were led by their respective Elders to different places to rest, while Xiao Yuyang and the others had long since decided how to handle the remaining thousand or so demonized people.

Since they could not be saved, they could only be given a swift end.

Xiao Yuyang acted personally. With one move, he nearly overturned the entire valley, burying the thousand or so demonized people along with it. The scene became even more tragic. Soft sobs could be heard everywhere. This time, every great Sect that had come to participate in the Southern Territory Martial Gathering had suffered losses, regardless of strength. The most miserable Sects had almost been wiped out, with only two or three survivors left.

Qingyang Shrine had also suffered losses. Even one Emperor Realm Elder had died. Among the disciples who had come to participate in the Martial Gathering, nearly half of the Void King Realm and Origin Returning Realm disciples had perished, though only a few Dao Origin Realm disciples had died. This was only because Wen Zishan had sensed that something was wrong and protected them with all his strength; otherwise, the losses would have been even more severe.

Their foundation had been badly damaged. Truly badly damaged.

"Elder Yang, we still need you to take a look at these people."

While he was resting, Ma Qing, Wen Zishan, and Xiao Yuyang arrived together. Behind them, a group of Emperor Realm Masters escorted two groups of people over. Neither group was large: one had only seven or eight people, while the other had only a dozen or so.

From their outward appearances, there was nothing unusual about these people, except that all of their cultivations had been sealed. The seven or eight people on the left wore gloomy expressions and looked utterly dispirited, while the dozen or so on the right kept crying out that they had been wronged.

Most of these people were Emperor Realm Masters, while the remaining few varied widely in strength.
