## Chapter 3308 — I Can Transform Too

After Yang Kai killed Yin Lesheng, Fu Bo had originally been unwilling to make an enemy of Yang Kai again out of fear of the Divine Spirit Luan Feng. But when he learned that even the foundation of the Great Wilderness Star Domain had been wiped out, he could no longer endure it.

Yang Kai narrowed his eyes. "You actually know about the Great Wilderness Star Domain!"

A flash of insight crossed his mind, and he vaguely understood. No wonder Netherworld Sect had dared to jump out and oppose him again this time. So that was how it was.

Fu Bo realized he had misspoken and immediately shut his mouth. Yet the baleful aura around his Ghost King body surged even higher, and his battered confidence swelled again. Earlier, despite assuming this overwhelmingly powerful form, Yang Kai had suppressed him until he could hardly breathe and left him in a sorry state. By now, however, Fu Bo had seen the truth. Yang Kai's movements were fast and nimble, but he had no real way to deal with him. The Ghost King body was incomparably tough. How could a mere Second Stage Emperor Realm cultivator break through it so easily? With that to rely on, he was as good as invincible.

When he saw Yang Kai thrust his sword at him again, Fu Bo snorted coldly. He neither dodged nor retreated. Instead, he reached for that broad longsword, seized it in one hand, and yanked it hard. A tremendous force pulled through the blade. Astonishment flickered across Yang Kai's face as he stumbled forward, dragged straight toward Fu Bo.

"Boy, die!" Fu Bo shouted with a vicious laugh. His sharp ghost claw swept down like a striking eagle, descending through the air toward Yang Kai's head as if he could already see himself crushing Yang Kai's skull.

But just as his scheme was about to succeed, unease suddenly welled up in his heart. He looked over and saw that Yang Kai, who should have been flustered and panicked, had instead revealed a meaningful smile. With his other hand, Yang Kai brought four fingers together and jabbed toward Fu Bo's palm like lightning.

In that hazy instant, Fu Bo seemed to sense Yang Kai's aura undergo a tremendous change. A mysterious, profound feeling rose from him, and Fu Bo even saw traces of scales suddenly appear across Yang Kai's exposed skin. They resembled snake scales, yet were far more intricate and unfathomable.

While he was still wondering, pain shot through the great hand he had thrust toward Yang Kai.

Fu Bo was shocked. He withdrew his hand like lightning, released the Myriad Sword, and shot backward.

He looked down, and his eyes shrank. Several bloody holes had appeared in the palm of his hard ghost claw, fresh blood trickling from them.

He was injured? Fu Bo could hardly believe his eyes. No one knew better than he did how sturdy this Ghost King body was. How could it possibly be injured? And judging from the wounds, Yang Kai had clearly pierced his palm with his fingers.

How had he done it? Fu Bo raised his head in a daze and looked at Yang Kai, wanting to see exactly what trick Yang Kai had just used. But the moment he looked over, his eyes shrank again, for Yang Kai's body had also changed dramatically.

Two horns rose from his head, and his body stood three zhang tall. A layer of golden scales covered his face, neck, and every bit of exposed skin. His hands had become a pair of claws, their sharp tips flashing with cold light. No matter how one looked at it, his form had a certain similarity to Fu Bo's Ghost King body.

The only difference was that one aura was ghostly, dark, and eerie, while the other was vast and righteous. The two auras clashed in the void, forming a stark contrast.

Fu Bo's heart lurched. He studied Yang Kai's new form carefully, and a thought that made him tremble rose in his mind. Pointing at Yang Kai, he stammered, "You, you, you..."

Yang Kai had already put away the Myriad Sword. He flicked the blood from the dragon claw of his right hand, then looked at Fu Bo at his leisure. "What a coincidence. You can transform, and I can transform too. I just wonder which of us is stronger."

Fu Bo's heart sank. He refused to believe his own guess, but the sight before him proved that guess true.

"That is..." On the other side, Red Ghost was also stunned beyond words, while Tang Sheng, Qian Xiuying, and Lan He were even more dumbstruck. None of them had expected the situation to change like this.

Earlier, although Yang Kai had held the upper hand and suppressed Fu Bo with his speed and nimble movement technique, everyone knew that situation could not last long. So what if he moved quickly? So what if his moves were flexible? If he could not injure his enemy, he would only exhaust himself for nothing. But if Fu Bo found an opening, a single strike would be enough to heavily wound Yang Kai. The attacks of that Ghost King body were not so easily endured.

They had still been somewhat worried, yet in the blink of an eye, the situation had changed like this.

"Dragon Bloodline?" Red Ghost felt a chill race up his spine. That Yang Kai actually bore a Dragon Bloodline? Did that not mean he had a Half-Dragon body, that he was a descendant of the Dragon Clan? This was no ordinary bloodline. Anyone who bore a Dragon Bloodline, even an impure one, would have limitless future achievements. He was also proficient in Space Force and possessed the Mountain and River Bell... Any one of these foundations and methods would probably require an enormous opportunity to obtain, yet at this moment, all of them were gathered in one person.

Even a favored son of heaven like Red Ghost could not help feeling jealous. He could only sigh that the heavens were unfair, granting Yang Kai so many advantages. Then he remembered how recklessly he had wanted to duel Yang Kai earlier, and he felt incomparably glad that he had retreated before it was too late.

Judging from the methods Yang Kai had displayed again and again, if he truly wanted to kill him, he probably would not need a second move...

"Bastard!" Fu Bo cursed, looking as miserable as if he had swallowed a dead fly. He was not cursing Yang Kai, but the person who had provided him with intelligence. That person had never said Yang Kai possessed a Dragon Bloodline body. Had he known this earlier, how could Fu Bo possibly have come to trouble him?

Although he did not know how many generations Yang Kai's Dragon Bloodline had passed through, since it was tied to the Dragon Clan, Netherworld Sect absolutely would not dare provoke him lightly. Who knew whether they might draw out that colossal existence known as Dragon Isle? If Dragon Isle were truly stirred, even ten Netherworld Sects would not be enough for them to slaughter.

Fu Bo understood that the person who had provided the intelligence must have known Yang Kai's abilities, because that person had said he had once fought a great battle with Yang Kai in the Great Wilderness Star Domain. Otherwise, he would never have told Fu Bo to join forces with Brahma Heaven Sacred Land and come out at full strength.

But looking at it now, this had not been a wise move at all. The result of coming out at full strength was that both great forces suffered disastrous losses, and even after Fu Bo brought out his trump card, he could not gain the slightest advantage.

The Ghost King body was indeed extraordinary, with a powerful physique and boundless strength, but that depended on what it was being compared to. Against a Dragon Bloodline...

A three-zhang dragon body was already an extraordinary bloodline awakening for a Half-Dragon with impure blood.

At that moment, Fu Bo actually felt the urge to turn and flee. If he had known Yang Kai's limit was a thirty-zhang dragon body, he probably would not have been able to muster even the courage to oppose him.

While Fu Bo was still dazed, Yang Kai had already struck first. Fu Bo did not even see how he moved. He only felt a strong wind rush against his face, and when he looked up, Yang Kai had already stretched out a dragon claw toward him.

Fu Bo was shocked and instinctively raised his hand to meet it. Their claws crossed, and Fu Bo cried out in pain as several more bloody holes were pierced into his huge ghost claw, blood flowing freely.

It did not end there. Yang Kai had already pressed in close, clawing, scratching, kicking, and punching, using every method available. He completely abandoned the fighting style of an Emperor Realm Master and fought a pure flesh-and-blood battle.

The Ghost King roared, green ghost Qi spraying from his mouth. The flesh all over his body bulged as he resisted with all his strength.

Boom, boom, boom...

Amid one explosive boom after another, the two enormous figures tumbled and clashed across the plain. They moved so quickly that it was almost impossible to see them clearly, like two streams of light tangling and chasing each other, flickering in and out as fresh blood sprayed across the sky.

A moment later, the two figures suddenly separated.

Yang Kai's three-zhang dragon body stood proudly in the void, his expression cold as he looked down. Several dozen zhang away, Fu Bo's Ghost King body was gasping for breath, riddled with countless bloody holes that had nearly turned that five-zhang body into a blood-soaked figure. Paired with his green face and fangs, he looked truly terrifying and savage. Even Fu Bo's right arm now hung limp and powerless, as if boneless. It had clearly been broken.

The Ghost King body, said to be incomparably hard and possessed of boundless strength, had been beaten into this state in close combat. If they had not seen it with their own eyes, probably no one would have dared to believe it.

Yet the one responsible for all of this was a Half-Dragon body bearing Dragon Clan bloodline, so the few people watching from afar found it only natural.

"Elder Chi's eyes are keen indeed. Tang admires you!" Tang Sheng sighed. Earlier, when Red Ghost said he felt Yang Kai could survive, Tang Sheng had not really believed him. After all, the two great forces had brought so many Emperor Realm Masters. Let alone Yang Kai, a rising star of the Second Stage, even an old veteran of the Third Stage would probably have had no chance of survival.

But the course of the battle was not decided by numbers. With his various inconceivable methods and reliances, Yang Kai had wiped out most of the Emperor Realm Masters from the two great forces in an extremely short time, like an autumn wind sweeping away fallen leaves. The surviving remnants scattered one after another, and no one dared to meet his edge head-on.

Xu Changfeng's sneak attack had indeed been exquisitely timed, but a single Divine Spirit, Shi Huo, had been enough to make him return empty-handed. Then Yang Kai had used a Half-Dragon body against Fu Bo's Ghost King body and actually gained an overwhelming advantage.

Fu Bo probably had no hope of victory at all. Whether he could leave alive this time was still unknown. And Xu Changfeng?

Tang Sheng turned his head and glanced toward Xu Changfeng, a trace of bewilderment in his eyes, because Xu Changfeng's situation was no better than Fu Bo's. A Third Stage Emperor was certainly powerful, but how could the Divine Spirit Shi Huo be easy to deal with? This fellow was the terrifying existence that had once annihilated Black Tortoise Sect, and its fierce reputation had resounded for so many years. For Xu Changfeng to face it was simply eight lifetimes of misfortune. From beginning to end, he had not launched a single attack. Holding that pitch-black longsword, he had done nothing but defend. Even so, he was forced into peril again and again, left utterly wretched. How could he still have any of his former scholarly elegance? His hair was disheveled, and he was a miserable sight. Defeat and death were only a matter of time.

A deeply unreal feeling suddenly rose in Tang Sheng's heart. Before the great battle began, he had thought Yang Kai was doomed, but the many changes that followed had truly caught everyone off guard.

Now, instead, he felt that Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng were certain to die.

He suddenly recalled the question Lan He had asked earlier: "If he survives, will you regret it?"

Would he regret it? Of course he would. Tang Sheng's heart was full of bitterness. If he had known earlier that this would be the outcome, no matter what, he would have built a good relationship with Yang Kai and personally taken him to the Eastern Sea to search for traces of Spirit Beast Island.
