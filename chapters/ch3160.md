## Chapter 3160 — Great Things Can Be Expected

"Of course." Yang Kai nodded. Given his current relationship with Li Jiao, there was no real need for thanks, but since Xia Ningshang had spoken, he naturally had to do as she asked.

"Ten years!" Xia Ningshang suddenly said.

"What?"

Xia Ningshang smiled faintly. "Within ten years, Tongxuan can recover."

The cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain had needed only a few years to wreak this poisonous devastation, yet even with Yang Kai spending a vast fortune in Source Crystals and laying down a heaven-shaking Grand Array to help, recovery would still take ten years.

Destruction was always easier.

"And you're satisfied with that?" Yang Kai asked, grinning at her.

Xia Ningshang blinked. "What more could there be?"

Yang Kai said, "Tongxuan Continent isn't like other stars. Its grade was a little low to begin with, so merely recovering won't do. Besides, ten years... is still a bit too long."

Xue Yue's eyes shifted thoughtfully. "Husband has a way to make this continent even better?"

"Where there's a will, there's a way." Yang Kai laughed. He did have a few plans in mind, but he did not know whether they would work, so he dared not make promises in front of them. If he said it and failed to do it, wouldn't that be embarrassing?

Xue Yue, however, seemed to see right through him and curled her lips. "If you can make Tongxuan better, then I'll..."

"Then you'll what?" Yang Kai stared at her, eyes blazing.

Xue Yue’s face flushed slightly, but she thrust out her chest and said, “I will let you do as you please.”

Yang Kai's eyes lit up. "I want all of us sleeping under one quilt!"

"Bah!" Xue Yue spat at him.

Yang Kai flashed between her and Xia Ningshang, wrapping an arm around each of them with a shameless grin. "If you want the horse to run, you have to feed it grass. One quilt, all of us together! Come on, one quilt!"

"Scoundrel!" Xue Yue glared at him fiercely, her myriad charms nearly hooking Yang Kai's soul right out of him. Then she lowered her head shyly. "Convince Ningshang first."

Little Senior Sister's face turned as red as blood. "How did we suddenly start talking about this?"

Yang Kai said solemnly, "The union of Yin and Yang is the Great Dao of Heaven and Earth. Mm, then it's settled."

"Who settled anything with you?" Xia Ningshang panicked. That kind of thing was simply impossible for her to even imagine.

Yang Kai lowered his head and pecked her red lips, taking only the lightest taste before stopping. In an instant, she froze as if struck by lightning.

She had never been so intimate with Yang Kai in front of someone else before.

Xue Yue watched with a smile, only to be caught off guard when Yang Kai sealed her red lips with his.

Again, it was only a brief touch before he pulled away, not going too far. It was not that Yang Kai was unmoved; he simply still remembered Xue Yue's words. Until Xia Ningshang recovered, he could forget about touching her. If he insisted now, he could certainly have his way, but how could that compare to the anticipation in his heart for all of them sharing one quilt?

Hmph, two naive women. Did they think it would only be the two of them? When the time came, Shan Qingluo and Su Yan would not get away either.

Laughing loudly, Yang Kai flew off.

Xue Yue touched her red lips and stamped her foot. "Rotten man!"

Amid the spiritual rain, Yang Kai stood with one hand on his waist, sweeping his arm out with bold command and pointing with fervor. "Everyone, Great Wilderness Star Domain invaded for more than ten years, slaughtered the cultivators of our Star Domain, seized our women and wealth, and left countless living beings homeless and suffering. Now, it is time for us to strike back!"

The downpour roared, but it could not drown out Yang Kai's voice, which reached every ear with perfect clarity.

"Follow me to war! Tooth for tooth, blood for blood!" Yang Kai raised a hand and pointed into the distant Starry Sky.

"Blood for blood!"

Their roars shook the heavens. The resentment they had built up over the past few years had not vanished with Tongxuan Continent's cleansing; instead, their fighting spirit burned even higher.

...

Somewhere in the boundless Star Domain, on a Dead Star, Yang Kai sat atop a barren mountain with one hand propping up his cheek, eyes closed in thought.

His Divine Sense churned through his Sea of Consciousness, weaving through the endless Star Chart above it.

This Star Chart mapped the entire Hengluo Star Domain. He had obtained it by chance when he first entered the Star Domain years ago, but at the beginning, he had not paid it much attention and had merely treated it as a tool for finding his way. With this Star Chart, he had never once lost his bearings in the Star Domain.

Even then, he had noticed that this Star Chart differed from those drawn by ordinary Star Chart Masters. It seemed to be changing at every moment, corresponding to every corner of the entire Star Domain. However, because the Star Chart was so vast and the Star Domain so immense, no one would notice the changes without careful investigation.

Only later did he learn that the Star Chart he had obtained by chance was actually the Star Domain Origin of the entire Star Domain.

It was precisely through this Star Chart that he had been able to sense a faint thread of connection to Hengluo Star Domain from within the Ancestral Domain and return here.

Wu Kuang had once said that whenever he went to a Star Domain in the past, he would seek out that Star Domain's Origin and refine it, thereby becoming the master of that Star Domain and freeing himself from the rejection of its World Laws.

A Star Domain Origin could take countless forms and had no fixed shape. Compared with the Stellar Origin of a single Cultivation Star, a Star Domain Origin was almost a thing of legend, something one could only encounter by chance.

For Yang Kai to obtain one was nothing short of a heaven-sent opportunity.

Unfortunately, he had never been able to make use of this Star Domain Origin, for the simple reason that he had never refined it.

Only by successfully refining this Star Domain Origin could he truly become the master of Hengluo Star Domain. At that point, he would not even need to act personally. With a single thought, he could slaughter every last cultivator from Great Wilderness Star Domain.

Half a year had passed since he left Tongxuan Continent. During that half year, he had been trying to refine this Star Domain Origin, but he had not made the slightest progress.

Even though his cultivation had now reached the Emperor Realm, a Star Domain was incomparably vast and contained countless stars. Trying to refine it was the same as trying to take control of all those stars, a task harder than ascending to the heavens.

Something had to be wrong.

Even Wu Kuang had to seal his own cultivation after entering a Star Domain, only daring to act without restraint after he had refined its Star Domain Origin.

Since Wu Kuang could do it, Yang Kai might not be unable to. He secretly regretted not asking Wu Kuang more about this matter back then. If he went to the Ancestral Domain to look for him now, whether he could find him was one question, and even if he did, that fellow might not tell him anything.

He was also secretly glad that he had not made any bold claims in front of Xue Yue and Xia Ningshang. Otherwise, he would probably be too embarrassed to face them now.

Back then, Yang Kai had only had a vague plan in mind: refine this Star Chart, become the master of this Star Domain, and then control the power of the entire Star Domain to nourish Tongxuan Continent. If he could do that, what would be so difficult about raising it to the level of Dark Star or Shuiyue Star?

Now, it seemed he had thought too simply.

"Still no good?" A clear, lovely voice sounded beside him.

Yang Kai opened his eyes, pulled Liu Yan into his arms, and kept his cheek propped on one hand. "I have an idea. I just don't know if it will work."

Liu Yan looked up at him, reached out to feel the hard stubble on his chin, and said softly, "If you have an idea, try it. Either way, there is nothing to lose."

"That's exactly what I was thinking." Yang Kai nodded, rose, and asked, "How long until they gather here?"

"About ten days until the appointed time."

"Ten days is enough. Stay here for now. If there is any news, notify me. I'll go test this new idea."

"All right." Liu Yan nodded.

Yang Kai stamped both feet, and his figure shot straight into the sky like an arrow loosed from a bow, soon vanishing from sight.

He plunged into the Starry Sky and flew forward without aim, moving at an unhurried pace. As he flew, he urged his Emperor Yuan and left his aura along the way. At the same time, he compared his path against the Star Chart in his Sea of Consciousness and marked out an area that was neither too large nor too small.

With the Star Chart as his reference, he was not worried about choosing the wrong place.

He kept flying around the area as if drawing a circle, leaving traces of his aura throughout the space inside it.

Five days later, he arrived at the very center of that circle, suddenly stopped, and closed his eyes.

His Divine Sense sank into his Sea of Consciousness, silently sensing the Star Chart.

All at once, he trembled, surprise and joy spreading across his face.

He could clearly sense an unusual area within the Star Chart in his Sea of Consciousness, and that area was exactly the place where he had spent the past few days leaving his aura.

There was hope!

Perhaps the direction he had been pursuing for the past half year had been wrong. If this method worked, it would prove that refining a Star Domain Origin and refining a Stellar Origin were two completely different matters.

Previously, he had been trying to use the method for refining a Stellar Origin, acting directly on the Star Chart in his Sea of Consciousness, but it had produced no effect at all. Looking at it now, moving step by step from the surface inward was the true path.

What method should he use to refine it?

Aside from the Star Refining Art, he had no other suitable Secret Art.

Once his mind was made up, he hesitated no longer. He immediately circulated the Star Refining Art and immersed his mind in that special region of the Star Chart.

In an instant, Yang Kai felt something wondrous, as if his thoughts had spread outward to fill every place he had traveled over these five days, tightly linking together the aura he had left behind. Without opening his eyes, he could see every rustle of grass and every stir of wind within that area, every detail laid bare.

The Star Refining Art circulated slowly, and his mind left its brand on a certain section of the Star Chart, gradually changing the color of that region.

There were no grand waves, no dramatic turns.

Everything seemed calm and natural.

At a certain moment, Yang Kai suddenly opened his eyes and burst into laughter.

This sudden idea had truly led him to the right method.

Looking into his Sea of Consciousness, he saw that the small region of the Star Chart had turned a slightly different color from the rest, and in the corresponding stretch of Starry Sky, a special area had appeared as well.

Yang Kai did not use any power. He merely shifted his thoughts, and his figure began flickering nonstop through that area, leaving afterimage after afterimage behind him, as if countless Yang Kais existed there at once.

This was a special stretch of Starry Sky, a stretch that already belonged to Yang Kai! Within this area, he was just as he was on Dark Star, able to rule over everything.

His figure suddenly stopped, and eager anticipation flashed through his eyes.

If even releasing his cultivation caused no trouble, then it would prove that his idea was truly correct. When the time came, as long as he spent a little more time, he would be able to refine that Star Chart and become the master of Hengluo Star Domain.

Once the thought came, he acted!

Yang Kai drew a deep breath and let out a thunderous shout. A strange sound rose from the depths of his soul, and the shackles on his sealed cultivation suddenly snapped open.

Dao Origin Realm First Stage, Second Stage, Third Stage, Emperor Realm!

He remained perfectly safe. Heaven and Earth showed no force of rejection, and no hostility appeared.

Complete success!
