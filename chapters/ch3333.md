## Chapter 3333 — Finding Fault

Zhu Qing said unhurriedly, "We Dragon Clan are born powerful, but improving our bloodlines has always been a headache. Raising a bloodline by even a single tier takes countless years. Now a shortcut like this has suddenly appeared, one with no hidden dangers at all. Do you think they would not be tempted?"

"Uh, let me ask something. How many women are there on your Dragon Isle?" Yang Kai coughed lightly.

Zhu Qing said, "Aside from me, Second Elder, and Third Elder, there are four more."

Yang Kai rubbed his chin. "That few?" He had long known the ratio of men to women among Dragon Clan members on Dragon Isle was terribly uneven, but he had not expected there to be only seven Dragon Clan women even if one counted every last one. Zhu Qing was already his woman, and Second Elder and Third Elder would certainly have no designs on him. As for the remaining four...

Zhu Qing slanted a look at Yang Kai. "I am warning you. Do not get any ideas about them."

Yang Kai put on an utterly solemn face. "How could I possibly get ideas about them? I would be thanking the heavens if they did not get ideas about me. If it really is as you say, my very existence is a great tonic to them." No wonder Fu Ling had kept posing and flirting in front of him before. So that had been her plan. Then again, although Fu Ling's conduct left much to be desired, she did have the looks and figure for it.

Zhu Qing sighed. "They cannot really be blamed. The chance to advance one's bloodline is too tempting to my Dragon Clan, and they do not want to miss it either. In any case, no matter what, while you are on Dragon Isle, do not leave my sight."

"Mm, mm, I know." Yang Kai nodded again and again, patting his chest. "I promise I will stay with you day and night and never leave your side without good reason."

How could Zhu Qing not hear the meaning behind his words? She shot him a reproachful look. "Let us go pay respects to the two Elders first."

Since he had already agreed to Zhu Qing, naturally he could not back out now.

Fortunately, the first person they visited was Great Elder Zhu Yan. Yang Kai did not have much of an opinion about this Great Elder. In his impression, this Dragon Clan Great Elder did not seem to have much of a temper, but his strength was beyond question.

Although Zhu Yan's name contained the character for flame, he was not a Fire Dragon, but an Azure Dragon.

On Green Wood Island, where he lived, towering ancient trees spread shade everywhere. Each tree was enormous beyond compare, old enough that one could tell at a glance it had grown for countless years. At the very center of Green Wood Island stood a tree even larger than the rest, peerless and alone, occupying an area a thousand zhang across. Its spreading crown almost covered half the island.

When Yang Kai and Zhu Qing arrived, they found Zhu Yan standing beneath the roots of that tree in the golden rooster stance, one leg bent, his palms pressed together, utterly motionless. The Great Elder's upper body was bare, and below he wore only a pair of loose cloth trousers. With each deep, heavy breath, he seemed capable of stirring the winds and clouds of heaven and earth. His muscles knotted like the coiling roots of an ancient tree, and every inch of flesh held terrifying power.

When he opened his eyes, immense majesty descended from above, making Yang Kai's skin ache.

In the next instant, however, the sharp light in Zhu Yan's eyes vanished, replaced by calm.

"Great Elder!" Zhu Qing stepped forward and saluted.

Yang Kai also hurried forward a few steps, cupped his fists, and said, "Yang Kai greets Great Elder."

Zhu Yan did not speak. Instead, he closed his eyes again, exhaled softly, and slowly ended his cultivation exercise. He took clothes from his Spatial Ring and, while dressing, looked at Yang Kai with a faint smile. "You have quite some nerve, daring to come to Dragon Isle again."

Yang Kai turned to look at Zhu Qing and grinned. "It has nothing to do with courage. Since Qing'er is here, naturally I would come."

Zhu Yan nodded lightly. "At least you have some heart, coming over at once to see this old fellow."

Yang Kai raised a brow. "Great Elder knew I came to Dragon Isle?" Only after asking did he realize the question was a little foolish. Since Fu Ling had seen him, she had surely passed the news along already. After all, she was responsible for guarding Dragon Isle's entrance. If she failed to report even this, that would be true dereliction of duty.

Zhu Yan said, "To tell the truth, this old man is not quite sure what attitude I should take toward you."

Yang Kai frowned slightly. "What does Great Elder mean?"

Zhu Yan clasped his hands behind his back and said flatly, "You are a Human, yet you abducted a Dragon Girl of my Dragon Clan. That is something my Dragon Clan cannot tolerate. Last time, you trespassed on Dragon Isle and openly snatched away the bride on the day of my Dragon Clan's great celebration, causing my Dragon Clan to lose all face. A clansman even died because of you, and the one who died was an Eighth-Tier Thunder Dragon, causing my Dragon Clan a tremendous loss. Taken together, the hatred between you and my Dragon Clan can be called irreconcilable. Tell me, should this old man treat you as an enemy?"

"Great Elder..." Zhu Qing's expression changed drastically. She had thought Great Elder had a gentle temper, which was why she had brought Yang Kai to see him first. As long as Yang Kai could pass Great Elder's hurdle, the matters afterward would be much easier to handle. She had not expected Zhu Yan to say something like this the moment they arrived. How could Zhu Qing not be alarmed? From Great Elder's tone, he seemed very much inclined to make Yang Kai pay blood for blood. If Great Elder were truly forced to act, Yang Kai would not have an easy time of it today.

But she had only just spoken when Yang Kai cut her off with a faint smile. “Great Elder, if you want to find fault with me, you will never lack excuses.”

Zhu Yan narrowed his eyes. “Find fault with you? Everything this old man said is true, every last word of it. What excuse is there?”

Yang Kai shook his head. "If Great Elder is using these matters to pressure me, this Junior also has something to say."

Zhu Yan said indifferently, "This old man is all ears."

Yang Kai said, "Great Elder says I abducted Zhu Qing, but I disagree. Love between a man and a woman is only natural. How could true affection be restricted by race? Besides, Zhu Qing is still on Dragon Isle, so how can you call that abduction? Mm, if Great Elder says I stole Zhu Qing's heart, then I admit it."

Zhu Yan's expression remained indifferent, betraying neither joy nor anger, and he showed no intention of speaking.

Yang Kai continued, "Since Zhu Qing is already my woman, how could she marry someone else? So snatching the bride was not something I wanted to do, but something I had to do. More than that, I lowered myself that day and took out the Dragon Isle Token, hoping to exchange it for Zhu Qing's freedom. Unfortunately, your Dragon Clan did not seem very willing to acknowledge the Dragon Isle Token. The rumor that anyone holding a Dragon Isle Token could make a request, and that Dragon Isle would never refuse so long as it could fulfill it, turned out to be an outrageous lie. Great Elder has not forgotten that, has he?"

Zhu Yan had no answer. The matter of the Dragon Isle Token had indeed been Dragon Isle's fault.

Yang Kai went on, "As for Fu Chi's death... that was Wu Kuang's doing. What the hell does it have to do with me? If the Dragon Clan wants revenge, just go find Wu Kuang. Or does the Dragon Clan also bully the weak and fear the strong, picking on a Junior like me only because you cannot beat Wu Kuang?"

Zhu Yan pondered for a long while before raising his head. "If you are going to discuss the matter, discuss the matter. Why insult my Dragon Clan?"

Yang Kai said defiantly, "This Junior is merely offering a rebuttal or two to prove his innocence."

Zhu Yan snorted. "Sharp-tongued."

Yang Kai grinned. "That is also an ability."

Zhu Yan laughed despite himself. "Although what you said is very reasonable, it remains a fact that the Dragon Clan suffered tremendous losses because of you. Let us set Fu Chi's death aside for now. Those Dragon Blood Flowers, at least, were stolen by you, were they not?"

Yang Kai said solemnly, "What Dragon Blood Flowers? I have never seen any."

Zhu Yan said angrily, "Stop playing dumb in front of this old man. It would have been bad enough for you to pick those Middle-Level and Upper-Level Dragon Blood Flowers, but why did you pick even the Lower-Level ones? Were those of any use to you? Now Dragon Isle has barely any Dragon Blood Flowers left. For at least a thousand years, my Dragon Clan clansmen can forget about advancing their bloodlines by even an inch. How do you intend to settle this account?"

Yang Kai's expression twitched. He really had not acted honorably in this matter. Openly snatching the bride and injuring Fu Chi could still be said to leave reason on his side, but stealing the Dragon Blood Flowers had been entirely his fault. It was just that he had needed to advance his own Dragon Bloodline that day and, forced by circumstances, had resorted to such a poor measure. Now that he wanted to ease relations with the Dragon Clan, this matter was rather difficult to solve.

But since things had already reached this point, what else could Yang Kai do? He could only brace himself and say, "What does Great Elder want? Please state your terms. For Qing'er, I will accept them."

Zhu Yan snorted. "Do not make yourself sound so righteous. Since you did wrong, you naturally ought to make up for it."

Yang Kai looked at him sidelong. "Let us make this clear first. If Great Elder insists on making things difficult for me, do not blame this Junior for not giving you face."

Zhu Yan was both angry and amused. "Does this old man need a wet-behind-the-ears boy like you to give me face? Face is something one earns for oneself, not something others hand out."

Yang Kai nodded. "Great Elder is right."

Zhu Yan said, "You stole the Dragon Blood Flowers, leaving my clansmen unable to advance their bloodlines for a thousand years. But if you can solve this problem, then the grievances between you and the Dragon Clan... can be set aside for now." He did not say they would be wiped clean. After all, Yang Kai bore at least some responsibility for Fu Chi's death.

"How does Great Elder want me to solve it? Compensate the Dragon Clan for those Dragon Blood Flowers? Although I still have some on hand, they are only Lower-Level ones, Dragon Blood Flowers left over from last time. The Middle-Level and Upper-Level ones were all used up back then."

At those words, the corner of Zhu Yan's eye twitched. "The Middle-Level and Upper-Level ones were all used up? Even if you ate them as food, you could not possibly have eaten that many, could you?" He had been hoping Yang Kai still had some Dragon Blood Flowers left, only to receive this answer instead. Zhu Yan's heart went cold.

Even when the Dragon Clan consumed Dragon Blood Flowers, refining their medicinal effects took time, yet Yang Kai said he had used them all up back then. In other words, he had completely consumed those several hundred Middle-Level and Upper-Level Dragon Blood Flowers in a very short time. Even if he still had some Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers, what use would they be?

Yang Kai sniffed. "Yes!"

"Bastard!" Zhu Yan flew into a towering rage. This time, he was truly furious, and he nearly failed to hold himself back from giving Yang Kai a savage beating, frightening Zhu Qing so badly that she flashed forward and blocked his way.

Fortunately, although Zhu Yan was angry, he had not lost all reason. After fuming for a while, he seemed to think of something, and his mood suddenly calmed. He looked Yang Kai up and down, then said thoughtfully, "I hear you have the ability to make the Dragon Clan's bloodlines advance rapidly?"

As soon as he said this, a cold light flashed through Zhu Qing's eyes and vanished, while Yang Kai was left somewhat dumbfounded.
