## Chapter 3279 — Building Momentum

Less than half a day later, after touring all of Luocha Sect, Yang Kai took Ran Yirou and Yu Keren out again and left through the mountain gate.

Inside the main hall, Luocha Sect's last five Dao Origin Realm cultivators were busily preparing the conferment ceremony and drafting invitations when a female disciple suddenly entered, went to Qin Pei's side, and whispered a brief report about the mountain gate.

Qin Pei waved her away, then laughed despite herself. "This Guest Elder of ours really cannot sit still."

Tao Yingruo asked, "What happened?"

Qin Pei replied, "He actually took two disciples out with him."

Tao Yingruo's eyes brightened. "Where did he go?"

Qin Pei said, “I was told he went out for a casual stroll. He should be headed to Luo City.” Luo City was a city under Luocha Sect’s jurisdiction, with a population of several million, and more than half of Luocha Sect’s income came from this city.

"Luo City..." Tao Yingruo pondered for a moment, her eyes growing brighter still, then nodded. "Good. Very good."

The other Dao Origin Realm cultivators all looked at her strangely. Qin Pei asked, "Senior Sister means..."

Tao Yingruo set down the invitation in her hand, lifted her teacup, took a sip, and said, "Why are we going to such lengths to prepare this conferment ceremony and send invitations far and wide?"

Qin Pei thought for a moment and said, "To build momentum, of course!"

Their only Emperor Realm Sect Master was dead, and Yang Kai had taken the initiative to ask for the position of Guest Elder. How could Luocha Sect not make use of such a fine opportunity? The greater the momentum they built now, the less the surrounding forces would dare look down on Luocha Sect in the future.

Tao Yingruo said, "And that momentum rests on Elder Yang. When we build momentum, we are only borrowing his strength. It is different when he personally goes out and shows himself. Just wait. This trip to Luo City is as good as him announcing his relationship with our Luocha Sect in advance. Let us see who still dares belittle us after this."

The eyes of the other Dao Origin Realm cultivators lit up. Qin Pei said, "Senior Sister is saying Elder Yang did this deliberately?"

Tao Yingruo nodded. "Otherwise, why would he not rest properly after having only just arrived?" Then she smiled faintly. "We truly owe him a great favor."

Realization dawned on everyone, and gratitude filled their faces.

Only two days later did Yang Kai return with Ran Yirou and Yu Keren. From then on, he stayed on Luocha Peak, keeping his door closed all day as he cultivated in retreat.

The effect of his outing soon became clear. Although Luo City was governed by Luocha Sect, the shops in the city did not all belong to Luocha Sect. Most of the surrounding forces had businesses there as well. Yang Kai had taken Ran Yirou and Yu Keren openly through Luo City, so anyone paying attention would have found it hard not to see them, and naturally sent the news back to the forces behind them one after another.

Meanwhile, Luocha Sect's disciples returned from delivering invitations and reported that every force that received an invitation had been exceptionally courteous to them, each declaring that they would certainly attend the conferment ceremony. A few forces had even sent Emperor Realm experts to personally accept the invitations. Every sign showed that Luocha Sect's crisis had already passed. With Yang Kai's protection, no one would dare have designs on Luocha Sect again.

When the news reached Luocha Sect, Tao Yingruo and the others were naturally overjoyed and threw themselves even more earnestly into preparing the conferment ceremony, with more care than they would have given to the succession of a new Sect Master. Then again, Luocha Sect was now leaderless. It had no Emperor Realm expert of its own, and no one was qualified to assume the position of Sect Master, so Tao Yingruo, as the strongest among them, would act as Sect Master for the time being, waiting until the day she advanced to Emperor Realm before truly taking up the Sect Master's authority.

One day, while Tao Yingruo was busy, Yang Kai suddenly arrived unannounced.

Facing the great benefactor who had preserved Luocha Sect, Tao Yingruo naturally did not dare neglect him. She hurriedly invited him in and personally served him fragrant tea.

Only after they were seated did Tao Yingruo ask, "Has Elder Yang grown accustomed to living here? Have the disciples been lacking in their service?"

Yang Kai smiled and waved a hand. "I am used to wandering outside, so it makes little difference where I stay. Yirou and Keren have also served me very attentively."

"That is good." Tao Yingruo smiled, her charm rippling through the gesture. "If you need anything, simply instruct them." There was one thing she truly found curious. According to Ran Yirou and Yu Keren's daily reports, ever since Yang Kai returned from Luo City that day, he had done nothing but cultivate and had not summoned them again at all.

So there really were men in this world who could remain completely unmoved by beauty right in front of them. That alone was enough to make Tao Yingruo admire him greatly. She believed Yang Kai understood why she had arranged for Ran Yirou and Yu Keren to serve him. If Yang Kai truly had any interest, he would only need to go with the flow. Whether it was Ran Yirou, Yu Keren, or those dozen or so other female disciples, none of them could possibly resist.

As time passed, Tao Yingruo grew more and more satisfied with this Guest Elder. He only gave and never asked for anything in return. What else could she say about such a person?

"I have come today because there is a matter I would like to discuss with Acting Sect Master." Yang Kai's expression turned solemn.

Tao Yingruo said, "Elder, please speak freely. Everyone in my Luocha Sect will certainly comply."

Yang Kai smiled. "It is nothing major. I simply want to leave something in Luocha Sect."

"Something?" Tao Yingruo was stunned.

Yang Kai said, "I believe Acting Sect Master has heard that this Lord cultivates the Laws of Space and can arrange cross-territory Space Arrays."

Tao Yingruo nodded. Ever since the incident at Wuhua Hall, this had no longer been any secret. The reason Yang Kai had been able to bring in more than two hundred Emperor Realm cultivators, three Divine Spirits, and over thirty Yao Kings as helpers in the short span of a single day was the cross-territory Space Array he relied on. Otherwise, how could he have traveled back and forth between the Eastern Territory, Northern Territory, and Southern Territory in a single day?

Hearing him mention it now, Tao Yingruo's eyes lit up despite herself, and she said excitedly, "Elder means..."

Yang Kai nodded. "Correct. I want to arrange a Space Array inside Luocha Sect and connect it to my Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory. You also know I cannot remain stationed here year-round. Once the conferment ceremony is over, I will leave. In the future, Luocha Sect will still have to rely on yourselves, but it is best to be prepared for the unexpected. If Luocha Sect truly encounters some danger one day and I am not here, you will likely be powerless to resist. With a Space Array, however, things will be different. You can go to my Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory for help at any time."

Although she had already guessed as much, Tao Yingruo was still wildly excited when Yang Kai truly said it aloud.

To be honest, it was not as if she had never considered this before. Since Yang Kai was willing to come to Luocha Sect and serve as Guest Elder, perhaps she could ask him to connect Luocha Sect and Lingxiao Palace, thereby securing long-term and effective protection.

But for one thing, she did not know what resources Yang Kai would have to consume to arrange a Space Array. In her mind, something as miraculous as a cross-territory Space Array would absolutely require an enormous expenditure. If it went badly, even selling all of Luocha Sect might not be enough to pay for it. For another, she did not want to give Yang Kai the impression that she was greedy for more after already receiving so much.

She never imagined Yang Kai would bring it up on his own.

At once, she said with delight, "Elder truly is a very good person."

Yang Kai laughed despite himself. "It is only a small matter. Still, Acting Sect Master's agreement is required."

"I agree, of course I agree." Tao Yingruo nodded hurriedly, her state of mind, cultivated over so many years, almost shattering. At that moment, she looked like a child who had just received a new toy. Then she suddenly paused and said, "Only, I do not know what Elder needs. Please draw up a list, Elder, and I will order people to gather everything."

"I do not need anything else. If you have Ethereal Crystals and Ethereal Jade, just give me a little." Yang Kai had collected a huge pile of materials for arranging Space Arrays, enough to make another hundred or so with plenty to spare. But since she had brought it up, he might as well accept a small amount as a token gesture.

"Good. I will have people find a way to gather those two things."

"There is one other matter. As for the location of this array, does Acting Sect Master have anywhere in mind?"

Since they were going to arrange a Space Array, they naturally needed to choose a tightly guarded place.

Tao Yingruo asked, "Where does Elder think it would be best to arrange it?"

Yang Kai pondered for a moment. "Looking across all of Luocha Sect, the forbidden land behind Luocha Peak is the most suitable place. However, it is a forbidden land after all..."

Tao Yingruo immediately said, "Wherever Elder thinks it can go, place it there. I have no objections."

Her straightforwardness left Yang Kai momentarily stunned. He nodded and said, "Then fine. It will be in the forbidden land."

Tao Yingruo suddenly smiled faintly. "Elder went to investigate the forbidden land. Were you very disappointed?"

Yang Kai said somewhat awkwardly, "Not disappointed exactly. Just a little curious."

Tao Yingruo pursed her lips and laughed. "Disappointment is normal. Normally, there is nothing especially strange about that forbidden land. If one wishes to glimpse its true secrets, one must wait until the night of the full moon."

Yang Kai raised his brow. "The night of the full moon?"

Tao Yingruo shook her head. "I am not very clear on the specifics either. I only heard the Sect Master mention once while she was alive that only on the night of the full moon would some unimaginable changes appear in the forbidden land. If Elder is truly interested, you might as well wait a few more days. Five days from now will be the night of the full moon."

"Then I really will have to take a good look." Yang Kai's interest was immediately roused. He had investigated that forbidden land once before and come away deeply disappointed, never expecting that it hid another mystery, one that would show no clues until the proper time. This instantly stirred his curiosity, and he resolved to go take a careful look on the night of the full moon five days later.

It was clear that, to repay his kindness, everyone in Luocha Sect, from top to bottom, had completely dropped their guard against him. Putting himself in their place, Yang Kai could understand their feelings. No matter the circumstances, one-sided giving could never be sustained for long.

He asked a few more questions, only to learn that even Tao Yingruo herself had never gone to the forbidden land, so naturally she did not know what would appear there on the night of the full moon. In all of Luocha Sect, only Yu Luocha had been qualified to set foot in that place.

After taking his leave of Tao Yingruo, Yang Kai went straight to the forbidden valley and took out a large pile of materials to arrange the Space Array.

Half a day passed in the blink of an eye, and the Space Array had already taken shape. Yang Kai then casually refined a Teleportation Token, summoned Ran Yirou and Yu Keren, explained the token's use to them, and taught them how to activate the array so they could pass the information on to Tao Yingruo.

With this Space Array, Luocha Sect would have no worries in the future. After Tao Yingruo received the Teleportation Token, it went without saying that she personally came to thank him. Yang Kai simply took her along to experience cross-territory teleportation, sending them straight back to Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory and leaving her marveling endlessly, unable to calm herself for a long time.
