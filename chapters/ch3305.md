## Chapter 3305 — I Bet He Can Live

The long spear Yang Kai had thrown punched straight through the Emperor Realm Master's body and shot on ahead of him. The Brahma Heaven Sacred Land experts in its path all paled and fell back in horror, none daring to meet its edge head-on.

A black scar lingered in the void where the spear passed, refusing to fade.

That strike had torn space itself.

Everyone froze. A chill climbed from the soles of their feet to the crowns of their heads, and for the first time they understood that a man could possess such terrifying brute strength.

"Saint Lord..." The Emperor Realm Master, his body pierced through by the spear and a gaping hole torn through his chest and abdomen, barely made it to within ten zhang of Xu Changfeng. Blood poured from his mouth as he weakly reached toward Xu Changfeng, as if trying to grasp his last thread of life. In the end, it was useless. His body went limp, and he fell from the sky, crashing to the ground in a spray of blood and flesh.

Five breaths, five Emperor Realm Masters. In only five breaths, Yang Kai had slaughtered them all, yet they had not even forced him to reveal his true ability. They had been no different from five ants casually pinched to death.

Heaven and Earth fell silent. The dozens of Emperor Realm Masters from Brahma Heaven Sacred Land and Netherworld Sect all swallowed hard, feeling as if they were trapped in a dream.

Yang Kai's figure flickered, and he landed atop the Mountain and River Bell as it spun endlessly beneath him. Wind brushed past, tossing his black hair, and his bearing was wild and arrogant as he shouted, “This Yang still stands. Who goes to the underworld next?” His fierce gaze swept over them, eagle-like and wolfish, yet not a single person dared meet his eyes. One after another, they looked away.

More than a dozen li away, Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying were both stunned senseless, simply unable to accept what they had just seen.

Was this still the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm? How could this possibly be the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm? Even a Third-Stage Emperor might not have such divine might, right? They had long known that some people in this world could fight above their rank and kill powerful enemies as easily as eating a meal, but it was not as though they had never met such people before.

Brahma Heaven Sacred Land's former Holy Sons, Chang Hao and Chang Xian; Yin Lesheng, that disciple of Netherworld Sect's Sect Master Fu Bo; Yao Si, son of Nether Soul Great Emperor; and Red Ghost as well, all belonged to that type. They could often defeat opponents one Stage above them and even had a chance to kill such enemies. Existences like them were the geniuses known to all.

Given enough time and room to grow, they would surely claim a place for themselves in the world to come.

Only now did Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying realize that even so-called geniuses could be compared, and that among geniuses, greater geniuses still existed. Yang Kai had killed five First-Stage Emperor Realm Masters with his cultivation at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, so it did not count as fighting above his rank. Even so, the scene just now was far more shocking, and far harder to believe, than watching a First-Stage Emperor Realm Master kill a Second-Stage Emperor Realm Master.

No wonder Chang Hao, Chang Xian, and Yin Lesheng had all died at his hands. This was probably what it meant for there to always be a Heaven beyond Heaven, and a man above man.

Strange light rippled through Lan He's beautiful eyes, and excitement surged in her heart. She had broken faith with Yang Kai earlier, which had already left her uneasy and full of guilt. Then she had watched so many people surround him and thought he was certain to die.

If Yang Kai truly died outside Heavenly Wolf Valley, then what happened today would likely become a lifelong knot in her heart. Yet Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying had sealed her cultivation, so she could not even go to help him. She could only stand here and watch helplessly, and that torment of the heart was unbearably painful.

Lan He turned her head and looked at Tang Sheng with complicated eyes. "Master, if he survives, will you regret it?"

Tang Sheng trembled when he heard this. He knew Lan He was still dissatisfied with his earlier choice and still resented it. It was only because he was her Master that she could not say more or demand anything from him. He said lightly, "Once a choice is made, there is no regret. Besides, do you think he can survive?" It could not be denied that the wanton slaughter within those few short breaths had truly been shocking, leaving anyone who saw it stirred and shaken. Nor could it be denied that Yang Kai was absolutely one of the rare geniuses of the Star Realm. He was only at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, yet he already possessed such might. If he advanced to the Third Stage, what then? By that time, he might even be able to contend with Li Wuyi.

If Heavenly Wolf Valley could have befriended such an existence in advance, the benefits would have been immense. It would not have mattered even if he came from the Southern Territory.

And Yang Kai had indeed come to Heavenly Wolf Valley for help before. As long as they agreed to take him to find Spirit Beast Island, Heavenly Wolf Valley could naturally build a good relationship with him. If Heavenly Wolf Valley needed something from him one day, would they still have to worry that he would ignore them? A tiny investment for a rich return; who would not like such a bargain?

That was why Lan He had asked. Heavenly Wolf Valley could have easily befriended a future supreme expert, yet it had let the chance slip away for nothing.

But everything depended on one thing... he had to survive!

Brahma Heaven Sacred Land and Netherworld Sect had come out in full force this time. Even losing five First-Stage Emperor Realm Masters did not affect the overall situation, because they still had two Third-Stage Emperors and seven or eight Second-Stage Emperor Realm Masters!

Yang Kai's performance was dazzling, but the ending was probably already set.

Lan He had no answer. Asking herself honestly, could Yang Kai survive in such a situation? He had already killed five of the other side's people, and all of them were Emperor Realm Masters. This was now a grudge that could only end in death.

In today's battle, either Yang Kai would be slain here, or the two great Sects would be beaten until they fell apart. Anyone with eyes could tell which side held the advantage.

"I bet he can live!" a voice suddenly came from beside them.

Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying, who had been focused on the battlefield, both started in surprise. Neither of them had noticed when someone approached. Granted, most of their attention had been elsewhere, but it still showed just how strong the newcomer was.

Once they recognized the voice, however, the two of them relaxed.

The speaker was Red Ghost, the strongest expert of Ahan Palace's younger generation. His red hair and crimson eyes were his most obvious marks. Warlike by nature and as bloodthirsty as they came, he gave the entire Eastern Territory a headache, because no one wanted to be targeted by this fellow.

If Lan He had not been a woman, he would have charged into Heavenly Wolf Valley long ago to challenge her to a duel.

"Elder Chi thinks that highly of him?" Tang Sheng raised a brow. In terms of seniority, Red Ghost counted as his junior. Tang Sheng and Ahan Palace's Hall Master were figures of the same generation. Even so, Red Ghost was already an Emperor Realm Master and an Elder of Ahan Palace, and his future achievements would not be below Tang Sheng's. Tang Sheng was not qualified to call him some worthy nephew, so he could only address him as Elder Chi.

Red Ghost shrugged, ignoring Tang Sheng's question. Instead, he looked at Lan He and asked, "Junior Sister Lan thinks he'll die?"

Lan He shook her head. "I don't know. I hope he lives."

Red Ghost gave a frivolous whistle and studied the battlefield with great interest, grinning as he said, "Valley Lord Tang, why don't we make a bet?"

Tang Sheng's eyes flickered. "Bet on what?"

Red Ghost grinned. "Naturally, we'll bet..." Halfway through, he suddenly laughed despite himself and shook his head. "Forget it, forget it. There's no point."

Tang Sheng asked in surprise, "Elder Chi truly thinks he can survive?" Although Red Ghost had not finished speaking, how could Tang Sheng not know what he had meant to say? It was nothing more than a bet on whether Yang Kai lived or died.

Red Ghost tapped his own head. "I trust my instincts."

Tang Sheng asked, "Then why not bet?" He did not ask because he loved gambling, only because he found Red Ghost's sudden change in attitude strange.

Red Ghost gave an awkward laugh. "When a giant elephant battles a pride of lions, an ant should simply watch. What right does it have to bet on someone else's life and death? Wouldn't that be overestimating itself?"

As soon as he said this, Tang Sheng, Qian Xiuying, and even Lan He all stared at him in indescribable astonishment.

Red Ghost was the strongest expert of Ahan Palace's younger generation. Even across the entire Eastern Territory, even the whole Star Realm, he was one of the few outstanding youths. Among those of his age in the Eastern Territory, perhaps only Yao Si could firmly suppress him, but Yao Si was the son of Nether Soul Great Emperor, so there was no comparing the two.

Someone so outstanding would surely be proud and unwilling to bow beneath anyone. Yet here and now, he had compared Yang Kai to a giant elephant and called himself an ant.

If they had not heard it with their own ears, no one would have believed it.

If he was an ant, then what were the countless living beings of the Star Realm, dust?

Red Ghost's crimson eyes narrowed slightly, and he said lightly, "Does Valley Lord Tang think I'm giving him too much credit?"

Tang Sheng came back to his senses and smiled faintly. "Elder Chi is too modest."

"Modest..." Red Ghost murmured. Perhaps it was modesty, but that feeling could not be wrong. Earlier, when Yang Kai had asked him how he would choose, he had already sensed it: if he really fought Yang Kai, he would absolutely die! His end probably would not be much better than those five people's.

After seeing Yang Kai act, that feeling had only grown stronger, until he almost no longer doubted it.

Tang Sheng said in shock, "Elder Chi means the two great forces may be in trouble today?"

Red Ghost smiled. "I didn't say that. I only said that fellow will live." He had followed Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng here, and along the way he had vaguely felt that something was not quite right, as though the two great forces had more strength than what they had shown on the surface.

Yet he could not quite figure it out. The two great forces had already come out at full strength to deal with a single Yang Kai, which was giving him enormous face. How could they possibly have hidden strength as well?

With one side prepared and the other caught unaware, even if Yang Kai escaped with his life today, he probably would not come out of it well. Both sides would suffer losses. That was Red Ghost's judgment, though he did not say it aloud.

Tang Sheng said lightly, "Then let us see whether Elder Chi's judgment is correct. This Tang still believes his chances of survival are slim." Although no wager had been made, both men had already stated their views. As for whether Yang Kai lived or died, they could only see it for themselves.

While they spoke, Yang Kai stood proudly atop the Mountain and River Bell. He looked down on all sides and issued his challenge, yet not one person dared step forward to accept death. The sight was truly breathtaking.

Seeing that none of them moved, Yang Kai grinned, showing a mouthful of white fangs. "Since all of you are being so reserved, this Lord will not stand on ceremony."

His gaze shifted toward Netherworld Sect, cold and murderous, as though no one else were present.

The moment that gaze landed on him, Fu Bo felt a chill crawl over him and roared, "Raise the banners!"

More than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters from Netherworld Sect summoned their Secret Treasures in unison, each one a Ten Thousand Souls Banner or a Blood Sea Banner.
