## Chapter 3229 — Good That He's Unwilling

"Murong Xiaoxiao!"

"Present."

"Yang Kai!" Qiu Ran looked his way, and so did everyone else. Yang Kai froze, pointed at himself, and said, "I'm going too?"

Qiu Ran's face darkened. "Did you not hear the discussion just now?"

Yang Kai had not heard a thing. He had been too busy chatting with Murong Xiaoxiao. The Shrine was probably sending Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao along to give them more experience. Though both of them had attended that Martial Gathering before, they had done so as disciples. Now that they had been promoted to Elders, things were different. Going out more would help them gain experience and get to know more people. In the future, they might have to lead teams out themselves, and when that time came, they would not be completely lost.

But why was he going? Counting him, Qiu Ran had just named five people. For a matter involving those below the Emperor Realm, did they really need five Emperor Realm Elders to lead the team? Was that not making a mountain out of a molehill?

Besides, Lingxiao Palace had countless loose ends to handle, and its hundred thousand-plus disciples had not fully settled in yet. Yang Kai was not especially willing to attend the Southern Territory Martial Gathering either. After a moment's thought, he said, "Senior Brother, forgive me, but Junior Brother still has matters to attend to..."

Gao Xueting said, "You are still a Shrine Elder, after all. Never mind that you have not shown your face for so many years; since you can go this time, you should. Otherwise, others may forget the Shrine even has an Elder like you."

Every Emperor Realm Master was a Sect's mainstay, its pillar of stability. The number of Emperor Realm Masters a Sect possessed was directly tied to its status, and the difference between one more and one fewer was enormous.

Gao Xueting clearly wanted Yang Kai to show himself more, so others would know the Shrine had an Emperor like him.

Since she had put it that way, Yang Kai could only nod. "Fine, then Junior Brother will make the trip."

You Kun burst out laughing. "Junior Brother, there is no need to look so reluctant. You should know that every Martial Gathering is a time when Emperor Realm Masters from all sides gather. In private, there will be exchange meetings and auctions both large and small. If you go, you might even find something good. Otherwise, why do you think the Shrine would send so many Elders?"

So that was part of it too. Still, Yang Kai was fairly wealthy now. He could support a hundred thousand-plus people, so what need did he have to hunt for good things? He could only smile, nod, and say, "Senior Brother You is right."

"Then the matter is settled. Everyone, go back and prepare early. We set out in one month."

With the various matters decided, everyone dispersed.

Yang Kai stopped Di Rong and told him he wanted to visit the Cultivation Methods Pavilion to copy some cultivation methods and Secret Techniques. Di Rong naturally agreed at once and personally led Yang Kai there.

After spending most of the day there and paying some Elder Contribution Points, Yang Kai walked out of the Cultivation Methods Pavilion perfectly satisfied. He was not in a hurry to leave, so he invited a few Elders he knew reasonably well and hosted a banquet for them on his Spirit Sword Peak.

He did not dare invite Gao Xueting. It was not that he meant to slight her; the problem was that what had happened the last time Gao Xueting drank had scared him. This woman usually looked dignified and composed, but once she drank, she became a different person. It was truly hard to watch.

After that incident, Qiu Ran had fined all the Elders quite a few contribution points. Only Yang Kai had escaped punishment because it had been his first offense. How could he dare invite her this time?

Unfortunately, she came on her own. The moment she sat down at the table, everyone exchanged looks and, without a word, put away the wine jars. The meal lost all flavor.

Yang Kai stayed in Qingyang Shrine for two days. By the third day, one hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators and three hundred Void King Realm cultivators had been counted and organized, and all of them came to report on Spirit Sword Peak.

When Yang Kai appeared, voices rang out like collapsing mountains of gold and toppling pillars of jade. "Greetings, Elder Yang."

Yang Kai swept his Divine Sense over them, stood with his hands behind his back, and nodded in satisfaction. The four hundred of them were in good spirits, and the Shrine had not been careless with their cultivation either. Men and women alike were all elites. In truth, any cultivator able to enter Qingyang Shrine would not have poor aptitude; if they did, the Shrine would not accept them.

Qiu Ran and Gao Xueting stood nearby. After saluting the two of them, Yang Kai faced the four hundred disciples and said, "You have probably heard about the matter from the Deputy Hall Master. This time, I have invited you to Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory to serve as instructors. I hope all of you will devote yourselves fully, and this Elder will not treat you poorly."

"Yes!" everyone answered in a roar.

Yang Kai turned his head. "Do Senior Brother Qiu and Senior Sister Gao have anything to say?"

Qiu Ran stepped forward. "No matter what you see shortly, keep it to yourselves. You may not casually reveal it even to your fellow disciples. If I discover any leak, this old man will show no mercy."

"As commanded!" The disciples were puzzled, unsure why Qiu Ran would give such a warning.

Yang Kai knew it must be because of the cross-territory Space Array. From the looks of it, the Shrine valued this cross-territory Space Array even more than he had imagined and was determined to keep it secret. Yang Kai himself did not mind. It had already been exposed anyway, and without him personally activating it, or without the token, no one could operate the thing.

"Set out!" Qiu Ran swept his great sleeve and led the way, with everyone following close behind.

Before long, they arrived before the cave in the rear mountain. Yang Kai had the four hundred enter in groups of ten and stand on the array.

There was no helping it. The cave was too cramped, and the array laid out inside was not large. Sending ten people at a time was already the limit.

Qiu Ran and Gao Xueting watched eagerly from the side, clearly wanting a better look.

Yang Kai found it amusing and did not hide anything. Instead, he had Gao Xueting take out the token and proactively taught her how to activate it.

Four hundred people was not that many, and in barely half an hour, all of them had been sent over.

By the time Yang Kai appeared in Lingxiao Palace with the last group, the main hall was already packed. Gao Xueting had come along as well. Not everyone had the chance to travel from the Southern Territory to the Northern Territory in an instant, and she was deeply curious about this Lingxiao Palace of Yang Kai's, so naturally she wanted to come have a look.

Hua Qingsi, having received the news, hurried over. Together with Bian Yuqing, she greeted Gao Xueting. The two sides gathered and talked for a long while, then Hua Qingsi led those several hundred people away. Presumably, they would soon be assigned to the various peaks, and once that happened, the pressure on the instructors from Li Dragon Palace would ease considerably.

Gao Xueting did not leave. Since she had come all this way, she naturally wanted a tour. Yang Kai personally accompanied her and showed her around Lingxiao Palace.

Though Gao Xueting remained composed on the surface, inwardly she was deeply shocked. First was the number of disciples in Lingxiao Palace. It truly was as Yang Kai had said: more than a hundred thousand. On one spirit peak after another, figures could be seen everywhere. It was hard to imagine where so many people had been recruited from.

Second was the number of masters in Lingxiao Palace. Gao Xueting distinctly sensed the auras of many Emperor Realm experts, two or three of whom even seemed to be at the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm. Their auras were unlike those of ordinary humans, however. Their Yao Qi soared to the heavens, clearly marking them as experts at the level of Yao Kings.

She did know Yang Kai had Yao Kings under him. Back then, Yang Kai had brought Yingfei to the Southern Territory, after all, and stirred up some talk. She simply had not expected there to be more than one. Apart from those Yao Kings, the Chief Steward and Second Steward she had just met were both Emperors as well, and there were clearly other hidden Emperors on the other mountain peaks.

Such a foundation was clearly not something a newly established Sect should possess. If these hundred thousand-plus disciples truly grew up, then beneath the whole sky, aside from the Sects of the Great Emperors, who could compare?

There were also the many arrays that seemed perfectly integrated with nature. Although Gao Xueting was not proficient in the Dao of Spirit Arrays, she still had an eye for such things. The arrays covering the spirit peaks were extraordinarily profound and clearly came from a master's hand.

Curious, she asked about them and learned that Yang Kai actually had an Imperial-Grade Formation Master under him! Gao Xueting's mouth fell open in a perfect circle.

If she knew there was also an Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiner and an Imperial-Grade Pill Master here, especially that the Imperial-Grade Pill Master was Sublime Pill Great Emperor's disciple, who knew what she would think?

Gao Xueting remained in Lingxiao Palace for two days before finally taking her leave. It had to be said that what she saw over those two days had shaken her deeply. Before, she had thought Yang Kai founding a Sect was simply nonsense, and had even advised him to just stay at the Shrine. Now it seemed she had worried for nothing.

Lingxiao Palace was in no way inferior to the Shrine. How could being an Elder at the Shrine compare with being the Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace?

Yang Kai escorted her to the Space Array. Gao Xueting turned to him and said, "Junior Brother, I have been thinking. Since your Lingxiao Palace and the Shrine are connected by an array, making travel convenient, could we perhaps exchange materials and make up for what each side lacks? If we can send the Northern Territory's specialties to the Southern Territory, then send the Southern Territory's specialties to the Northern Territory, we will certainly earn a sizable profit in the middle."

Yang Kai sneered inwardly. I have been using this road to riches for ages. Do I really need you to remind me? Still, now that the Space Array's existence had been exposed, there was no harm in cooperating with the Shrine a little.

Although Ziyuan Commerce Guild was not bad, the Shrine was not weak either. With the Shrine's strength, the materials it gathered definitely would not be poor.

Yang Kai nodded. "I was just thinking of discussing this matter with Senior Brother Qiu. After Senior Sister returns, please mention it briefly to him. If the Shrine is interested, Junior Brother will support it with both hands."

"Senior Brother Qiu should agree." For a matter that benefited everyone, Qiu Ran had no reason to refuse. "Once you finish handling matters here, return to the Shrine as soon as possible. Discuss it with Senior Brother in person then."

"I will remember."

Gao Xueting warned, "You absolutely must not be late. Do not forget the Martial Gathering."

"I know, I know."

Gao Xueting glared at him, then activated the Space Array and left.

Only after watching her leave did Yang Kai turn around.

Unexpectedly, another fluctuation came from the Space Array behind him. He turned back and saw another figure standing on the array.

"Brother Li." Yang Kai grinned.

The newcomer was none other than Li Jiao.

He had probably teleported over from the array at Li Dragon Palace. Since the distance was not far, there were no aftereffects. Hearing Yang Kai, he cupped his fists and said, "Brother Yang."

"How did it go?" Yang Kai asked.

Li Jiao sighed. "I have failed your trust. Mi Qi is not very willing."

Yang Kai's eyes brightened. "Good that he is unwilling. Good that he is unwilling."

Li Jiao smiled bitterly again and again. How could he not know what Yang Kai was thinking? He had previously intended to advise Mi Qi as well, lest he give Yang Kai an excuse to establish his authority, but what use would persuasion have been in such a matter? If he said too much, they might truly fall out on the spot. Sighing inwardly, he knew Mi Qi was likely in for misfortune, and could only hope that Yang Kai would not go too far.
