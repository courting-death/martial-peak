## Chapter 3268 - Luring Him into the Trap

Fortunately, Lei Gu seemed determined to wipe him out and had no intention of letting him escape. That saved Yang Kai some effort.

In the middle of his frantic flight, he grabbed a handful of Spirit Pills and stuffed them into his mouth whole, cursing indistinctly, "Are you fucking sick? Keep chasing me and I won't be polite."

Lei Gu sneered without end. "Boy, this day next year will be the anniversary of your death. Are you satisfied with the burial place this Lord chose for you?" As he spoke, the round-headed hammer in his hand turned into layers of light and shadow, hammering at Yang Kai until he could only dodge, with no strength to block at all. Several times it grazed past him by the narrowest margin, only for Yang Kai to evade with his Space Divine Abilities.

Anyone else, wounded so badly, would never have escaped with their life from an enraged Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master. Only Yang Kai could manage it through teleportation, but he looked wretched beyond words, and the sight put Lei Gu in an excellent mood.

Back in Wuhua Hall, this boy had summoned several hundred helpers to surround him, all eyeing him like tigers watching their prey. Now the tables had turned, and at last he would learn how quickly fortune could shift.

Still, there was one thing Lei Gu had to admit. This boy's ability to preserve his life truly was first-rate. Once his Space Divine Abilities were displayed, they were mysterious and unpredictable enough to make anyone envious. If he had not known that the Power of Space could not be cultivated by just anyone, Lei Gu might even have spared him, captured him alive, and carefully questioned him about the exact cultivation method.

Yet that was precisely why Lei Gu became even more determined to kill him. If he did not seize this chance to eliminate Yang Kai today, he likely would not have many opportunities in the future.

After the chase had gone on for quite some time, Yang Kai was somehow still alive and kicking, his tenacious vitality almost unbelievable. But Lei Gu saw clearly that every time Yang Kai used that teleportation Secret Technique, he spat out a mouthful of Golden Blood. Clearly, the burden was too great, and he could barely withstand it.

“Boy, if you stop now, this Lord can still give you a quick death. If this Lord catches you, I will make you unable to live and unable to die!” Lei Gu threatened as he pursued him.

Yang Kai was practically fuming, grief and indignation written all over him. "The world is vast. If you don't take this chance to flee, I'm afraid you won't get another one. Do you really think I came here alone?"

At those words, alarm flashed across Lei Gu's face, and even the motion of controlling his Imperial Treasure paused for an instant. But he quickly recovered and roared, "No matter how silver-tongued you are, don't dream of leaving this place alive today!"

He clearly thought Yang Kai was playing tricks. True, when the two of them had left Wuhua Hall one after the other, someone would certainly have trailed them, but after his efforts over the past few days, Lei Gu believed it was impossible for anyone to still have a hold on his whereabouts. Even if there were pursuers, he would have shaken them off long ago. Yang Kai's alarmist words now were obvious proof that he was at the end of his strength.

"Good, good, good. If you don't believe me, just wait and see!" Yang Kai clutched his chest. Sensing a fierce gust bearing down from behind, he hurriedly urged the Laws of Space, intending to repeat his old trick. But the moment he moved, violent pain tore through his chest, almost stealing his breath. Even the Emperor Yuan inside him suddenly fell into disorder, and his Space Divine Ability failed to activate.

That badly startled him. His Incarnation had already set a trap with Liu Yan and the others and was only waiting for him to lead Lei Gu into it. If Lei Gu killed him here, all that effort would be wasted.

It was not the first time he had suffered such serious injuries, nor the first time he had been chased so miserably, but it was the first time he had ever felt so close to death.

In that split second, Yang Kai abruptly turned around and poured all the strength in his body into the Myriad Sword, slashing down through the air.

All color drained from the world. Between heaven and earth, only the radiance of that sword remained.

The round-headed hammer smashed down, the sword light shattered, and Yang Kai coughed blood as he was thrown backward. The sound of bones breaking burst from both his arms, and his entire body flew away like a tattered sack.

Injuries piled on top of injuries, but at the most critical moment, he had made the right choice and saved his life. Borrowing the force of the round-headed hammer, he flew backward and waved his hand, releasing the two Wood Spirits from the Xuan Realm Pearl.

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu clearly had not yet grasped the situation. When they appeared, both of them looked blank.

"Lend me a hand!" Yang Kai forced out the words. The moment he spoke, blood gushed from his mouth like a fountain.

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu were shocked by what they saw. Then they turned and looked at Lei Gu chasing from behind. How could they fail to understand that Yang Kai was being hunted? The two exchanged a glance and did not hesitate. Both used a wondrous method, their bodies suddenly scattering apart and turning into specks of fluorescent light that gathered behind Yang Kai's shoulder blades.

In an instant, two delicate wings appeared on Yang Kai's back.

When the Wood Spirit Race fought alongside the Stone Puppets, they used this method to assist them. Years ago, Yang Kai had seen the Elder and the Wood Spirit Clan Chief do the same, so he was no stranger to it.

The wings beat, and Yang Kai shot forward like an arrow from the string, moving with astonishing speed.

Lei Gu, pursuing from behind, was startled by the sight, suspicion flashing across his face. What sort of method was this? He had clearly been on the verge of killing Yang Kai, yet Yang Kai had somehow produced a pair of wings. It was simply inconceivable. More than that, after gaining those wings, the boy's flight speed had suddenly increased by a great deal.

Lei Gu flew into a rage. He put away the round-headed hammer, and Demonic Qi rippled around him. No one knew what Secret Technique he used, but he also sped forward, clinging tightly to Yang Kai's trail.

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu did not understand the full situation. They only knew Yang Kai was fleeing pursuit, so both flew with all their strength. But Lei Gu had set his heart on killing Yang Kai. How could they possibly shake him off?

The distance between the two sides closed rapidly, filling the two Wood Spirits with anxiety. The wings they had become beat faster and faster.

Yang Kai weakly pointed them in a direction, then closed his eyes, silently circulated his profound art, and refined the medicinal effects of the Spirit Pills he had just swallowed.

The chase continued for a cup of tea's time. As they passed through a valley surrounded by mountains on all sides, Yang Kai suddenly opened his eyes, brilliant light flashing in them.

Behind him, Lei Gu had just flown into the valley when alarm bells suddenly rang in his heart. An incomparably dangerous feeling swept through his body, making him halt abruptly and look around with a guarded expression.

A hundred zhang away, Yang Kai turned around at his leisure, spat out a mouthful of bloody saliva, and looked at Lei Gu with perfect composure. "Chase me. Why the fuck aren't you chasing anymore?" A cup of tea's time spent healing, together with his own powerful recovery ability, had finally allowed Yang Kai to catch his breath. Although he was still far from recovered, at the very least he was no longer as weak as before.

Hearing this, Lei Gu gave Yang Kai a cold look, then turned and made to leave.

A figure flashed behind him. Fan Wu stood with his hands clasped behind his back, blocking the path, and said faintly, "This way is blocked."

Lei Gu's eyes narrowed. Without changing his expression, he slid to the left, but another figure abruptly appeared there as well, revealing a woman dressed in white palace robes.

Looking to the right, he saw another man appear like a ghost, lean and not very tall.

Lei Gu's heart sank. Of course he knew who these three were. Yang Kai had made a point of introducing them to him a few days ago, and he knew all three were Divine Spirits.

How could this be? They had been lying in ambush here all along, waiting for him to walk right into the trap. Judging from everything Yang Kai had done earlier, Lei Gu had actually been foolish enough to let himself be lured in.

The only thing Lei Gu could not understand was how they had contacted one another in secret. He had never seen Yang Kai take out a Transmission Compass, nor had he found any trace of Yang Kai leaving secret marks behind.

Three Divine Spirits... Lei Gu could not even force a bitter smile. He was no match for a single Divine Spirit, much less three. For a moment, he was sick with regret. If he had known this would happen, he would have fled straight into the Southern Marsh. Why had he chased him at all? Now he had plunged headfirst into someone else's trap, and escaping would be all but impossible.

At this life-and-death juncture, however, he was extremely decisive. Divine Spirits blocked his left, right, and rear, and they were not opponents he could resist. His figure flickered, and he pounced forward. Only the front offered a path to survival. In his current state, Yang Kai was no match for him.

Facing Lei Gu as he charged over with ferocious momentum, Yang Kai stood motionless, a mocking smile tugging at the corner of his mouth.

Lei Gu immediately felt that something was wrong and realized Yang Kai must have some hidden backup. But now that the arrow was on the string, it had to be loosed. Demonic Qi surged around him, transforming into a giant palm that grabbed toward Yang Kai.

As long as he could capture Yang Kai, he could make those three Divine Spirits hesitate for fear of hurting him. Then he could repeat his old trick and leave at his leisure.

The giant Demonic Qi palm tore through the air. Just as it was about to seize Yang Kai, a petite figure suddenly flashed out from behind him. It was a little girl of seven or eight, exquisitely pretty and adorable.

But what the little girl did next nearly scared Lei Gu out of his wits.

She opened her small mouth and blew out a breath. That gust instantly transformed into heaven-toppling flames, as if it could burn all things in the world to ash. The instant the pitch-black giant Demonic Qi palm touched those flames, it was burned to pieces and lost its form. More than that, the flames transformed into a fire blade in midair and slashed down through the air, heading straight for his face.

The aura of death enveloped him, and Lei Gu's expression changed drastically. Although he could not immediately tell what kind of fire this was, he knew he absolutely could not let it touch him.

He raised the round-headed hammer, and it swelled with a boom, becoming as large as a house as it blocked the space above his head.

The fire blade struck the round-headed hammer but failed to break through its defenses. Yet when Lei Gu withdrew his Imperial Treasure, he saw a tiny cluster of flames still burning on the hammer. However hard he urged his strength, he could not put it out, and the round-headed hammer's light flashed wildly as it burned.

"I told you already. If you kept chasing me, I wouldn't be polite, but you just wouldn't listen. Cough, cough, cough..." Yang Kai clutched his chest. He had wanted to mock Lei Gu a little more, but he had the will and not the strength, so he could only give up.

Lei Gu's face turned ashen. He knew there was no escaping today no matter what he did. The hatred in his heart could not be washed clean even with all the waters of the Three Rivers and Five Lakes.

"Do it!" Yang Kai gave a light wave of his hand.

Four figures pounced toward Lei Gu from the front, back, left, and right, and four sharp killing intents locked onto him.

Lei Gu raised the round-headed hammer high and shouted, "The Demon Race is immortal!"

As he spoke, the Demonic Qi around him surged violently, and his entire body rapidly swelled.

Seeing this, Fan Wu and the others all stopped short and drifted back.
