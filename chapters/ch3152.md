## Chapter 3152 — Luring the Snake Out of Its Hole

"Ningshang!" Xue Yue stared, stunned in place. She had never imagined such a turn of events. At first, she thought Xia Ningshang had only been agitated by Yang Kai's return, but now it seemed the matter was nowhere near that simple.

Yang Kai sat sideways on the bed, letting Xia Ningshang rest against him. He slid his arms beneath hers and clasped her small hands from behind, pouring pure power into her body in a steady, ordered stream.

He had sealed his cultivation, so although he could only display the strength of a peak Void King Realm cultivator, the nature of his Emperor Yuan had not changed much. Emperor Yuan stood two full levels above Origin Qi and Saint Yuan, and when it flowed into Xia Ningshang's body, it immediately produced a miraculous effect.

The seven black spots that had been spreading across Xia Ningshang's body slowed visibly, even showing signs of being suppressed. The black Qi roiling over her also calmed again.

Yang Kai worked at it for half a quarter of an hour, careful to the extreme and not daring to relax for even an instant, but even then he could only suppress those seven "ink spots" to the size of fists. After that, he was powerless to do more.

He knew these "ink spots" were only the outward sign showing on Xia Ningshang's body. The true cause lay in whatever Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators had done to Tongxuan Continent. If he had guessed correctly, it was the effect of those sinister arrays. Tongxuan Continent and Xia Ningshang were bound together; harm one, and both suffered. To solve Xia Ningshang's current problem, he first had to deal with the disaster on Tongxuan Continent.

In other words, unless those arrays were torn out by the roots, there was no chance of expelling these "ink spots."

Removing the arrays would not be difficult. Yang Kai only had to make the trip himself. Before that, however, he had to stabilize Xia Ningshang's vitality.

The black Qi and ink spots covering her were devouring both her vitality and Tongxuan Continent's at every moment. She had already been extremely weak, and when they suddenly erupted today, they immediately dealt her a grievous blow.

If he did not stabilize Xia Ningshang's vitality first, she was in danger of dying.

Yang Kai raised a hand and tossed several jade bottles to Xue Yue. "Take out the Pills and feed them to her."

Xue Yue caught them and hastily pulled out the stoppers, pouring out several Spiritual Pills. She pried open Xia Ningshang's small mouth and placed the pills inside, then pressed one hand to her chest and released a faint thread of Saint Yuan, slowly guiding the pills down to help her refine their medicinal efficacy.

The effect was limited. These Spiritual Pills were only Dao Origin-Grade, so there was only so much they could do. Yang Kai did have many Emperor Pills on hand, but with Xia Ningshang's cultivation, she absolutely could not take them. A single one would be enough to make her body burst apart and kill her.

He slapped his forehead and cursed himself for being a fool. At such a critical moment, he had actually forgotten about that thing.

His consciousness sank into the Small Xuan Realm. A moment later, a jade-green leaf suddenly appeared in Yang Kai's hand. Resting on it was a drop of milky-white sap, pure and flawless, yet filled with unimaginable vitality. It seemed more like a living thing than a drop of sap.

The leaf was the same.

The leaf came from the Everlasting Tree, and so did the sap.

Either one was incomparably precious. Even in the Star Realm, treasures like these would be enough to make people fight until blood flowed, yet Yang Kai took them out now without the slightest hesitation. If it could resolve Xia Ningshang's current crisis, he would not begrudge even letting her refine the Everlasting Tree itself. The only problem was that he had still never found a way to refine the Everlasting Tree, and one leaf with one drop of sap was already the limit of what Xia Ningshang could endure.

He pried open her mouth and placed the leaf and sap inside together. Then Yang Kai raised his hand and gently patted her back, sending both down into her stomach.

Emperor Yuan stirred faintly. Yang Kai held her as if she were a piece of delicate porcelain, his movements gentler than could be described. The slightest carelessness could bring disaster down on Xia Ningshang in his arms.

The Everlasting Tree's leaf and sap slowly dissolved, spreading together with the medicinal efficacy of the Spiritual Pills she had taken earlier.

A faint green light suddenly bloomed inside Xia Ningshang's body and melted into the black Qi, diluting it.

At the sight, Xue Yue wept with joy.

Although she did not know exactly what method Yang Kai had used or what he had given Xia Ningshang, the scene before her clearly showed that Xia Ningshang was improving.

A household really could not do without a man after all.

She could not help with anything at the moment, so she could only keep softly calling Xia Ningshang's name, hoping to wake her.

The green light gradually deepened. Her exhausted vitality slowly recovered, and the black Qi covering her body was forced down. The two colors wove ceaselessly over Xia Ningshang, gradually reaching a kind of balance.

The Everlasting Tree's efficacy was astonishingly powerful. If it had been anyone else, even someone gravely injured and on the brink of death might have leapt up full of life by now. Xia Ningshang's situation, however, was different. In the end, the root of the problem still had not been solved.

Xia Ningshang let out a soft murmur. Her eyelashes trembled, and then her eyes slowly opened a sliver.

Xue Yue called softly, "Ningshang, Ningshang!"

"Sister... Yue." Xia Ningshang's eyes were a little unfocused. Only after she made out Xue Yue's face did her thoughts gradually clear, but soon she realized she was lying in a broad, comfortable embrace, her breath filled with that scent she had missed so much.

She tilted her head back onto Yang Kai's shoulder and looked up at his profile, smiling bitterly. "Junior Brother!"

She had not wanted to see him, yet in the end he had still seen her like this. She must look terribly, terribly ugly.

"Call me Husband!" Yang Kai lightly touched his forehead to hers.

Xia Ningshang's pale face flushed faintly, the color vanishing almost as soon as it appeared. Lowering her head, she called out softly.

Yang Kai laughed, “It is fine for Su Yan to call me Junior Brother, but surely not all of you should call me that. I am still the head of this family, after all. How am I supposed to keep any dignity?” Turning his head to look at Xue Yue, he asked, “And you?”

Xue Yue said shyly, "Save Ningshang first."

Xia Ningshang said, "I feel much better." After just a few sentences, her complexion had improved greatly, and even her voice had grown stronger. She no longer felt as miserable as she had before. Wrapped in that green light, her whole body felt warm and comfortable, and she wished she could simply lie in this man's arms until the end of time.

Xue Yue said in annoyance, "Ningshang, how can you do this?"

Xia Ningshang pressed her lips together and smiled.

Xue Yue said, "Husband!" She glared at Yang Kai, her expression clearly asking if he was satisfied now.

A figure flashed in from outside the door. It was Meng Wuya, his face full of anxiety. He and the others had all been outside the main hall earlier and knew nothing of what had happened here. Now, seeing Xia Ningshang lying shyly in Yang Kai's arms while Xue Yue was smiling again, he felt his heart loosen for no clear reason, and the gloom that had weighed on him for so long suddenly seemed ready to scatter.

Yang Kai and Xue Yue met each other's eyes and immediately understood.

Yang Kai laid Xia Ningshang back down and said, "Lie here properly. Wait for this husband of yours to go kill a few people and vent this anger for you."

But as he turned to leave, his clothes were tugged. Xia Ningshang lay on the bed, staring at him in terror, and the look in her eyes was enough to break one's heart. She was afraid, terrified that Yang Kai would leave as he had last time and not return, vanishing without a trace for dozens of years. She was afraid everything just now had only been a beautiful dream. If that was the case, she would rather not have her anger vented. She would rather he stayed by her side forever and never left.

Yang Kai patted her hand. "Don't worry, I'll be back soon. How about this: count to one hundred, and I will be standing in front of you again."

"What if you don't come back?" Xia Ningshang asked.

"I definitely will!"

Xia Ningshang nodded lightly, let go, and closed her eyes.

Yang Kai gave Xue Yue and Meng Wuya a look, and the three of them stepped out.

Liu Yan stood prettily just outside the door, looking up at him.

Yang Kai patted her head. "Stay here and watch over her."

Liu Yan nodded obediently.

Meng Wuya frowned slightly, unable to understand why Yang Kai would ask a little girl to stand guard here. He did not even know what relationship the two of them had. Could she be Yang Kai's daughter?

It had to be said, Liu Yan's appearance made that misunderstanding easy.

But even if she was Yang Kai's daughter, she had no trace of cultivation anywhere on her body. What could she possibly do by guarding this place?

A moment later, the group entered the outer hall. Only then did Meng Wuya hurry to say, "That pack of bastards from Great Wilderness Star Domain seems to be fleeing. Yang Boy, just tell us what to do."

It was precisely because he had sensed signs of Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators fleeing in defeat that he had hurried over to find Yang Kai and discuss countermeasures.

As they spoke, they had already reached the outside of the hall, where a crowd stood gazing up.

Following their gazes, Yang Kai immediately saw streaks of light flying out from every direction and fleeing into the Starry Sky. Among them were quite a few enormous Warship silhouettes.

Sure enough, just as Meng Wuya had said, Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators were fleeing Tongxuan Continent.

A cold smile curved Yang Kai's lips. Flee? Where could they possibly flee? He had already planned to deal with Great Wilderness Star Domain's people after seeing Xia Ningshang and Xue Yue, but he had not expected them to scatter early like frightened birds.

Did they think that would let them escape death? How could Little Senior Sister's suffering not be repaid a hundredfold, a thousandfold?

Everyone looked to him. Although he had only just returned, the moment he stood there, he became the pillar holding them all up.

He pointed toward the sky, his voice low. "Hold them up. There is no need to fight to the death. Just don't let them escape easily."

Everyone's spirits lifted at his words, and they answered as one.

"Wait." Someone suddenly shouted.

Everyone turned and saw that the speaker was Purple Star's current helmsman, Zi Wuji.

Yang Kai glanced at him. He did not recognize the man, and his brow creased slightly.

"Greetings, Brother Yang. I am..."

Yang Kai cut him off. "If you have something to say, say it quickly."

A trace of displeasure flashed through Zi Wuji's eyes. He knew Yang Kai was no ordinary cultivator. Several dozen years ago, Yang Kai had already been a Second Stage Void King Realm master, and he had even gone to some mysterious place, so his cultivation was probably higher now. Even so, Zi Wuji was still the Master of Purple Star. Though he was presently sheltering under someone else's roof, his own strength was not weak either. With a powerful enemy before them, every bit of strength was precious, and in recent years Xue Yue, Xia Ningshang, Meng Wuya, and the others had all treated him with some courtesy.

For Yang Kai to act so forcefully the moment he arrived naturally displeased him.

Suppressing his thoughts, Zi Wuji said, "Great Wilderness Star Domain has both numbers and strength. As far as I know, they have several Third Stage Void King Realm masters, and now more than a dozen Warships escorting them. If the people here charge in alone, I'm afraid it will be like sheep entering a tiger's den."
