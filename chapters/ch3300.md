## Chapter 3300 — Mountains and Rivers Shall Meet Again

In the rear valley of Heavenly Wolf Valley stood a quiet, secluded place with a single thatched hut. Ling Yinqin had built her home there, living in great simplicity.

According to Lan He, after she left the Shattered Star Sea back then, she began asking after Ling Yinqin's whereabouts. As it happened, Ling Yinqin had also traveled all the way to the Eastern Territory. Once the two met and talked, Ling Yinqin understood Lan He's intentions and immediately thanked her profusely before following her to Heavenly Wolf Valley.

At the time, she was only at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm.

After that, with Lan He vouching for her, she naturally joined Heavenly Wolf Valley without trouble and moved into the place where her husband had lived before his death. She spent her days apart from worldly strife, devoted to quiet cultivation. After all these years, she had actually advanced to the Emperor Realm. With that level of strength, Tang Sheng granted her the position of Elder. Still, everyone in Heavenly Wolf Valley knew that Elder Ling did not involve herself in any of Heavenly Wolf Valley's affairs, great or small, nor did she have much contact with anyone else. Day after day, she simply kept watch beside that thatched hut. Those who knew the truth were all moved by her devotion.

When Yang Kai arrived with Lan He, Ling Yinqin was sitting quietly before an earthen grave. Lush green grass covered the mound, and wildflowers had even bloomed across it. This should have been the place where her husband's ashes were buried.

Sensing someone arrive, Ling Yinqin opened her eyes. She first saw Lan He leading the way and gave her a faint smile. Then she saw Yang Kai following behind her, and after a brief stunned pause, pleasant surprise appeared on her face.

"Elder Ling, someone has come to see you." Lan He walked over with a smile.

"Big Sister Ling, long time no see." Yang Kai also smiled and cupped his fists.

"Yang Kai!" Ling Yinqin looked completely surprised, but the smile on her face came from the heart. "What are you doing here?"

"I was passing through the Eastern Territory and happened to run into Lady Lan He. Thanks to her invitation, I came to Heavenly Wolf Valley for a visit, so I stopped by to see you as well."

Ling Yinqin looked him over and said, "After so many years, you seem to have grown much stronger again."

Yang Kai smiled. "I heard Big Sister Ling has also advanced to the Emperor Realm."

Ling Yinqin turned back to glance at the earthen grave, then said softly, "I have little to do on ordinary days, so I only cultivate. Come, let us talk inside."

She warmly welcomed Yang Kai and Lan He into the thatched hut. The furnishings inside were extremely simple, without the slightest decoration: only a table, chairs, a bed, and bedding. Even so, the room had a fresh, natural feel.

Ling Yinqin brewed fragrant tea, and the three sat down to talk. When the conversation turned to how she had come all the way to the Eastern Territory after leaving the Jixu Sea Secret Realm back then, Ling Yinqin could not help sighing with emotion. At the time, she had only been at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm. Traveling from one Territory to another had taken her several years, and more than once along the way she had escaped death by a hair. Fortunately, her luck was good, and each time she turned danger into safety. She finally reached the Eastern Territory, where she soon met Lan He, who had been looking for her.

Ling Yinqin naturally thanked Yang Kai again and again. If he had not mentioned the matter to Lan He in the Shattered Star Sea, it would not have been easy for someone in her circumstances to join Heavenly Wolf Valley. After all, she had not grown up in Heavenly Wolf Valley. For someone of unknown roots and background, what force would dare take her in at random? Heavenly Wolf Valley was also considered a respectable power in the Eastern Territory, and when such a force accepted disciples, it generally preferred quality over quantity. It would rather raise its own from childhood than casually accept people of unknown origin, lest unnecessary trouble arise.

Once she entered Heavenly Wolf Valley, everything settled down. She came here, began a half-reclusive life, and usually no one disturbed her.

As they chatted, she also asked about Bing Yun, Liu Qianyun, and the others. After learning that all was well, she felt relieved.

Another half day passed before Yang Kai rose to take his leave.

Ling Yinqin urged him again and again to stay a few more days, and Lan He also joined in trying to persuade him, but since Yang Kai knew Tang Sheng's attitude, how could he remain and draw resentment? He found an excuse and left.

Ling Yinqin wanted to see him off, but he stopped her and had her stay behind. In the end, she could only stand before the thatched hut and watch his back disappear.

"Brother Yang, can you really not wait a few more days? I can think of another way." Lan He followed beside Yang Kai as they flew toward the outside of Heavenly Wolf Valley, her voice somewhat gloomy.

Yang Kai shook his head with a smile. "No need. I still have other important matters to attend to. For now, I will not go to Spirit Beast Island."

"But..." Lan He opened her mouth, but before she could finish, a young man suddenly flew in from the side and called out urgently, "Senior Sister Lan, Valley Lord summons you. He has something to discuss with you."

Lan He nodded. "I will see Brother Yang out, then go meet Valley Lord."

The young man, however, flashed forward to block their path and said with a serious expression, "Valley Lord said you are to hurry over immediately. It seems to be urgent."

Lan He's fine brows furrowed when she heard this, clearly having realized something. She snorted coldly. "I am only seeing Brother Yang off. I will be there right away. Can it not wait even this short while?"

Embarrassment flickered across the young man's face. He cupped his fists and said, "Senior Sister Lan, please forgive me. It is Valley Lord's order, and I am only obeying instructions."

Seeing that Lan He still seemed about to argue, Yang Kai raised a hand and said, "Lady Lan, go. I know the way out of the valley. There is no need to see me off."

"Brother Yang..."

Yang Kai waved, then his figure flickered and shot toward the valley exit like lightning. His voice drifted back from afar. "Mountains and rivers shall meet again. If fate allows, we will meet another day."

"Brother Yang, be careful in all things!" Lan He hurriedly shouted after him. Only once Yang Kai's figure had vanished from sight did she turn an angry glare on the young man. His scalp prickled under her stare, and he could only smile apologetically as he explained, "Senior Sister Lan, it was Valley Lord who..."

"Hmph!" Lan He was plainly displeased. Turning around, she flew off in another direction. She wanted to ask her Honored Master exactly what he meant. Yang Kai had done her a kindness, yet now that he was in trouble, not only could she not help, she was not even allowed to see him off. What face would she have to meet him in the future?

It had to be said that Heavenly Wolf Valley's attitude toward Yang Kai today had disappointed Lan He greatly, and pent-up anger burned in her chest.

Outside Heavenly Wolf Valley, Yang Kai had only just appeared when someone flew toward him head-on. At first, Yang Kai paid the person no mind, but as they brushed past each other, the other man suddenly exclaimed softly, stopped, turned around, and called out, "Could that be Brother Yang Kai over there?"

Yang Kai stopped and looked back, finding the situation rather amusing. He had come to the Eastern Territory and somehow kept running into acquaintances one after another.

The speaker had already turned back. He stopped a dozen or so zhang in front of Yang Kai, looked him over, and said with delighted surprise, "It really is Brother Yang. I heard you had come to Heavenly Wolf Valley, so this Qi rushed over without stopping. I never imagined I would meet you here."

"Qi... Hai?" Yang Kai frowned. If he had not heard the man call himself this Qi, Yang Kai probably would not have remembered him, but that self-address, together with his somewhat familiar face, immediately brought to mind certain matters from the Shattered Star Sea and the passage to the Ancient Land.

Qi Hai laughed loudly when he heard this. "Brother Yang still remembers this Qi. What good fortune for this Qi."

Yang Kai looked him up and down, then asked curiously, "Are you not from Qi Tian Stronghold? What are you doing here?" If he remembered correctly, Qi Tian Stronghold should have been not far from the Ancient Land. In Desolate City outside the Ancient Land, there were many people from Qi Tian Stronghold.

At that thought, Yang Kai's mind stirred.

He had walked out of the Ancient Land and visited Desolate City. Right after that, many people in the Eastern Territory had learned of his appearance, and even Lan He of Heavenly Wolf Valley had heard the news. There was a root to every matter. Although Yang Kai had made no particular effort to hide himself along the way, there truly were not many people in the Eastern Territory who knew him. So where, exactly, had the news of his appearance in the Eastern Territory come from?

He had been somewhat puzzled before, but he had not taken it to heart. Only now, after seeing Qi Hai, did he feel as though the clouds had parted to reveal the bright moon. So the source had been right in front of him all along.

But why had Qi Hai delivered himself right to Yang Kai's door? Yang Kai narrowed his eyes slightly, his expression thoughtful as he looked at Qi Hai.

Qi Hai, however, was completely unaware and said solemnly, "Naturally, I came for Brother Yang."

Yang Kai said coolly, "May I ask what for?"

Qi Hai gave a bitter smile. "It is still the matter from back then..." As he spoke, he bowed all the way to the ground and said respectfully, "Please, Brother Yang, show mercy and save my wife's life. My wife has little time left. If this drags on any longer, I fear she will not last another month."

Yang Kai was startled. "Your wife is still alive?" If he remembered correctly, Qi Hai's woman had been afflicted with Heavenly Frost Earth Drizzle, one of the Ten Great Poisons. Later, he had paid some attention to this poison and knew that it was extremely fierce. Those afflicted by it were almost beyond saving, but if one could find the True Phoenix Fire, which had the power of nirvana, then there would still be a thread of hope.

But how many years had it been? Qi Hai's woman had been poisoned before the Shattered Star Sea opened, so nearly twenty years should have passed by now.

Poisoned by Heavenly Frost Earth Drizzle for twenty years and still alive. What did that even mean? Could Qi Hai's woman be from the Dragon Clan?

Qi Hai's expression turned sorrowful. "My Qi Tian Stronghold has an unusual treasure that can seal things in ice. My wife has been sealed within it all this time, her vitality and bodily state frozen, which is how she has survived until today. But as time passes, its effect has grown worse and worse. If the poison is not cured soon, I fear my wife will truly..." As he spoke, tears had already filled his eyes. He looked every bit the devoted lover, and Yang Kai felt heartsore just watching him. Men did not shed tears lightly, much less Qi Hai, who was now already in the Emperor Realm. Such deep feeling made it clear how profound the bond between him and his wife was.

If he put himself in Qi Hai's place, and Su Yan or Xia Ningshang were the one to suffer such a thing, Yang Kai guessed he probably would fare no better.

Qi Hai composed himself, then continued, "In earlier years, I once heard that Brother Yang had appeared in the Eastern Territory, but unfortunately, this Qi was unable to meet you at the time..."

Yang Kai immediately felt somewhat awkward. "At the time, I had important matters to attend to, and besides, back then... I had no way to help you."

Qi Hai said, "As long as there is True Phoenix Fire, the poison can be cured and her life extended. Could it be that Brother Yang did not obtain the True Phoenix Fire in the Shattered Star Sea? This... how is that possible?" He was completely certain that the True Phoenix Fire was in Yang Kai's hands. If even that was wrong, would that not be a huge misunderstanding?

Yang Kai shook his head. "The True Phoenix Fire was indeed obtained by me, but at that time, it was not with me. Even if I had wanted to help you, I had no way to do so."
