## Chapter 3190 — I'm Not Dead Yet

Yet his cultivation was hardly confined to a single sword. Even with his Secret Treasure taken from him, he still had a hundred ways to kill the boy in front of him.

Yang Kai stuck a pinky into his ear and dug at it lazily. "I've heard that so often my ears are about to grow calluses. If you've got the skill, then fucking kill me right now!"

"As you wish!" The fury on the feather-crowned man's face vanished into calm. In a few steps, he appeared before Yang Kai like a ghost, with his fist clenched and thumb raised, then pressed it toward Yang Kai's enormous dragon head.

The move was not fast. On the contrary, it was extremely slow, almost like a tortoise crawling.

When experts clashed, every breath and every fraction of an inch could decide victory or defeat, yet he seemed to be doing the opposite. His posture was relaxed and graceful, as if he were about to crush a filthy bug.

Yang Kai felt as if he were facing a mortal enemy. As he watched that thumb press toward his forehead, he had the strange illusion that someone had cast a body-binding spell on him. His whole body went rigid, unable to move, and as the fist closed in inch by inch, Yang Kai clearly saw the void collapse around it.

If that move landed on his forehead, it would probably burst his head open.

Yang Kai suddenly opened his mouth. Dragon roars rolled out in waves, shaking the stars. Violent power burst from his mouth, whipping the feather-crowned man's robes and sending his hair flying. In the man's Sea of Consciousness, it was as if a giant dragon were dancing, churning the waters and dragging at his attention.

"Dragon Breath!" The feather-crowned man's brow twitched, and his disbelief only deepened.

Although the Dragon Clan were few and had not appeared in the world for ages, he had once dealt with a Dragon Clan member in his early years, so he knew a little about them. The Dragon Clan were skilled at spitting Dragon Breath. It was not some Secret Technique, but the Dragon Clan's Innate Divine Ability. Dragon Breath was immensely powerful, in no way inferior to top Secret Techniques, and because it was an Innate Divine Ability, it could be used with great ease and speed. This was the advantage of being born a Divine Spirit, something other living beings could never hope to match.

The instant Yang Kai opened his mouth, the feather-crowned man sensed the power of that Dragon Breath. More than that... this Dragon Breath was astonishingly pure, as if it had truly been spat out by a Dragon Clan member.

The Dragon Clan member he had met years ago had only just reached adulthood and possessed only a Ninth-Tier Dragon Bloodline, yet the power of his Dragon Breath seemed inferior to this boy's.

What was going on? Even if Yang Kai had cultivated some Secret Technique that let him transform into a half-dragon body, he still should not have been able to release Dragon Breath like this. The Star Realm had countless Secret Techniques related to the Dragon Clan, but absolutely none of them could reach such a degree.

The raised thumb stopped three inches from Yang Kai's forehead and could not press down any farther.

Yang Kai's eyes lit up, and he grinned. "So you are suppressed after all!"

He had finally seen through it. Even if this fellow was some Star Envoy of the Star Court who oversaw countless Star Domains, after coming to this Lower Plane Star Domain, he still could not do whatever he pleased. Perhaps it also had something to do with Yang Kai taking that long sword from him.

In short, the feather-crowned man could not bring out his full strength right now!

Yang Kai's confidence soared. Although his connection to Hengluo Star Domain had been severed by that sword strike, leaving him unable to borrow the many advantages of being a Star Domain's Master, he still had one edge over his enemy: in this Star Domain, he was not suppressed at all. Even the World Laws no longer rejected his existence. He could unleash his strength as he pleased, provided he did not care what destruction he caused.

Fighting at his peak against a suppressed enemy, even if that enemy might be a Great Emperor himself, left Yang Kai with nothing to fear.

"Then what the hell are you acting so arrogant in front of me for?" An object suddenly appeared in Yang Kai's right hand. It was a small bell, and the moment it appeared, it spun and expanded, soon growing several zhang tall. Yang Kai grabbed the bell's handle as if he were gripping a brick and smashed it viciously at the feather-crowned man's forehead.

The feather-crowned man's expression changed. He felt an indescribable suppressive force envelop him, giving him the sense that he could not break free. It was probably too late to pull back now. Just as Yang Kai had said, after losing that sword, he was heavily restricted and bound within this Star Domain, unable to display his full strength. Otherwise, he never would have looked so wretched.

Gritting his teeth, he pushed all his strength into his frozen thumb and pressed it fiercely down toward Yang Kai's forehead.

He had hoped Yang Kai would recognize the danger and retreat, avoiding an outcome where they both went down together. To his surprise, Yang Kai had no intention of stopping. The great bell whistled through the air and smashed onto his head with a deafening clang. The feather-crowned man's head spun, stars bursting before his eyes, and he was sent flying again. The precious robe on his body flashed wildly. If not for its protection, the consequences would have been grim.

Yang Kai's dragon head also snapped violently backward. His entire neck seemed to break, bending at a strange ninety-degree angle. His majestic body, as massive as a small mountain, froze in place and did not move for a long while.

Shua...

The feather-crowned man returned in a flash, lingering fear written across his face, his complexion slightly pale. Although the precious robe had absorbed most of the damage, it could not block that pure physical force. His internal organs were still churning, and the taste of blood rose in his throat. He looked at the twenty-zhang dragon body before him with a complicated expression.

If someone had told him earlier that anyone in this Lower Plane Star Domain could injure him, he never would have believed it. Yet now, someone truly had.

His gaze shifted to the great bell in Yang Kai's right hand. Flowers, birds, fish, beasts, mountains, rivers, and great streams were engraved upon it, vivid and lifelike, flowing without pause. An ancient, desolate aura radiated from it, as if it had crossed space and time from the barbaric wilderness.

"Mountain and River Bell!" The feather-crowned man's eyes narrowed, then filled with wild joy.

This was actually the Mountain and River Bell!

This was an extremely famous Primordial Era Treasure. Origin Cauldron Great Emperor had relied on the Mountain and River Bell to achieve his position as a Great Emperor, so its value was obvious. However, after the War of Emperors ended and Origin Cauldron Great Emperor died, the Mountain and River Bell disappeared without a trace.

He had heard that someone had obtained the Mountain and River Bell in the Shattered Star Sea years ago, but he did not know the details.

Only now, seeing it with his own eyes, did he realize that the boy before him was the lucky one who had obtained the Mountain and River Bell.

No wonder even he had struggled to withstand that smash. This was the Mountain and River Bell, not some ordinary Imperial Treasure. Even a Great Emperor would benefit greatly from obtaining it. In other words, the one who had injured him was not Yang Kai, but the might of the Mountain and River Bell.

When the bell rang, it suppressed mountains and rivers; its Imperial Aura reversed the Heavens. With such resounding fame, who under Heaven did not know of it?

He had truly come to the right place this time! Not only could he obtain a Small World, even the Mountain and River Bell had fallen into his hands. With this treasure, why would he need to fear being unable to become one of those ten in the future?

If the Small Xuan Realm had stirred his greed, then the Mountain and River Bell was something he absolutely had to possess. This treasure was far more valuable than a Small World, even though that Small World also seemed extraordinary.

Even with his temperament, his lips felt dry at this moment. He licked them and reached out toward the Mountain and River Bell.

The instant he touched it, a wondrous sensation surged through his entire body, giving him a sense of sublimation. He sensed even more clearly how extraordinary the Mountain and River Bell was, and his desire to possess it became stronger still.

He reached out and tugged, but the Mountain and River Bell did not budge.

Kacha cha...

The sound of bones shifting rang out. The feather-crowned man started slightly and looked up, only to see Yang Kai's supposedly broken head lifting back into place. Golden Blood trickled from a bloody hole in his forehead, staining his cheeks gold.

"You..." The feather-crowned man was stunned. Yang Kai had taken one of his Divine Ability moves head-on, yet he was actually fine?

"I'm not dead yet, bastard!" Yang Kai suddenly exerted his strength. The feather-crowned man was caught off guard, and Yang Kai snatched the Mountain and River Bell back.

Then Yang Kai turned the bell over and brought it down over him, roaring, "Suppress!"

In an instant, the flowers, fish, birds, beasts, mountains, and great rivers on the bell seemed to come alive. A suppressive force even more terrifying than before enveloped the void.

The feather-crowned man seemed unable to react and was directly covered by the Mountain and River Bell.

Instead of rejoicing, Yang Kai was startled. He looked up to the side and saw the feather-crowned man standing there, having appeared at some unknown time, frowning at him. What he had suppressed was only an afterimage!

"Suppress, suppress, suppress!" Yang Kai simply threw out the Mountain and River Bell and formed a hand seal with one hand to control it. The bell's mouth was dark as a fierce beast's maw, intent on swallowing the feather-crowned man whole.

The feather-crowned man's figure drifted unpredictably, advancing and retreating by turns as he flickered back and forth through the void without the slightest pattern. Yet at every critical moment, he somehow avoided the Mountain and River Bell's suppression.

His face no longer held the careless contempt from before. It was as if he had reassessed Yang Kai's strength and finally grown serious.

After making him suffer losses again and again, Yang Kai did indeed have the right to be taken seriously.

His voice drifted over from every direction. "An innocent man invites disaster by carrying treasure, boy. You should understand that principle. Leaving aside your Small World, this Mountain and River Bell alone is enough to bring death upon you. Why not obediently hand it over to this Lord? This Lord can keep you safe."

"Fuck your mother!" Yang Kai answered with relish.

The feather-crowned man continued, "Must you insist on courting death?"

Yang Kai laughed loudly. "You may have nothing else worth my attention, but I think your life will do. Why not obediently hand it over to this Young Master? This Young Master will give you a quick death."

"Sharp tongue. Empty bluster." The feather-crowned man slowly shook his head, looking as if Yang Kai had sorely disappointed him. His figure suddenly came to a halt, and his cold gaze fixed on Yang Kai.

In that instant, a chill ran through Yang Kai's entire body. Without hesitation, he sent layer after layer of halos rippling from the Mountain and River Bell, sealing off the space in every direction and suppressing Heaven and Earth.

His vision blurred, and the feather-crowned man vanished from sight.

An azure palm descended from the sky, carrying a sense of world-destroying force as it bore down on him.

Forget Yang Kai's twenty-zhang dragon body; even a star would probably be shattered by that palm.

Was this his true strength? Yang Kai's heart trembled. Even suppressed, a Great Emperor was still a Great Emperor!

This man's cultivation was likely no weaker than any Great Emperor Yang Kai had ever seen. All his previous carelessness had simply come from not taking the fight seriously.

Could he block such power? The question surfaced in his mind for only an instant before melting away like spring snow.

He had to block it! If he could not, he would die!

Dragon roars poured from his mouth, rolling on without end. Within those roars was a complex, obscure language, like a low chant from the ancient wilderness, stirring the stars across the sky.
