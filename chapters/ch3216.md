## Chapter 3216 — Something Happened

Jade slips were laid out on both sides of every wooden cabinet. Yang Kai took the ones on the left to copy, while Ji Yao took those on the right. The two kept the same pace and rhythm as they moved forward, separated only by a hollowed-out wooden cabinet. Whenever they looked up, they could even see each other clearly.

There was nothing to choose when copying these jade slips. Yang Kai was taking everything in sight, planning to copy every jade slip from the fourth to the eighth floor of Ice Heart Valley's Cultivation Methods Pavilion and bring them all back.

For a time, the Cultivation Methods Pavilion was utterly quiet. Only the jade slips in their hands kept changing.

The fourth floor held several thousand jade slips, but even so, the two of them finished copying them in just half a day.

"Let's go to the next floor," Ji Yao said, taking the lead.

The fifth floor was slightly smaller than the fourth, and there were fewer wooden cabinets as well, only about ten in all. The number of jade slips stored there was also only around half that of the fourth floor.

With their earlier experience, the two of them worked even faster and finished copying everything in just over two hours.

Then came the sixth floor, the seventh floor...

At first, Yang Kai was still a little uneasy, always feeling that nothing good could come of Ji Yao following him here. But seeing her act as usual, and hearing that even the few things she said were only casual small talk, he gradually relaxed.

What he feared was Ji Yao bringing up what had happened in the Ancient Land back then. At the time, Ji Yao's mind had been muddled and unclear, and she had mistaken him for Bing Yun, forcing Yang Kai to impersonate her for a while. Still, he had seen some things he should not have seen, and if word of that spread, it would damage Ji Yao's reputation.

Now that he saw her so composed, he slowly let the matter go. What had happened back then was truly an accident. He understood that, and Ji Yao understood it too, so naturally there was no reason to keep holding onto it.

After they entered the eighth floor, there were only a few wooden cabinets. A rough count showed fewer than a hundred jade slips.

But the cultivation methods and Secret Techniques stored in these jade slips were already among Ice Heart Valley's highest-grade inheritances, apart from those secrets that were never passed on. They were suitable for Emperor Realm cultivators. The fact that Bing Yun was willing to share them with Yang Kai showed just how generous she was toward him. If it had been anyone else, they probably would not even have been allowed through the doors of the Cultivation Methods Pavilion.

Since there were not many jade slips, Ji Yao did not help with the copying anymore. Instead, she walked to the window and gazed into the distance, her side profile carrying a faint trace of loneliness.

Yang Kai continued copying by himself.

"I heard you brought four wives back from the Star Domain?" Ji Yao suddenly said.

Yang Kai looked up at her and saw that she still only showed him her side profile. If he had not been paying attention, he might have thought he had imagined it.

Cold sweat immediately broke out on his forehead. "Who did you hear that from?"

Ji Yao turned to look at him, a teasing smile at the corner of her mouth. "I've already met Junior Sister. When can I meet the other three?"

What do you want to meet them for! Yang Kai grumbled inwardly. What puzzled him even more was how Ji Yao knew about this. Although he had already been back from the Star Domain for over a month, and the fact that he had brought four wives with him was no secret, who would be bored enough to tell Ji Yao about it?

"You planted spies in my Lingxiao Palace?" Yang Kai stared at her in disbelief. If not, there was no way Ji Yao could have known these things.

"You still haven't answered my question," Ji Yao said, neatly avoiding the point.

Yang Kai smiled bitterly. "If Junior Sister Yao wants to meet them, you can come to Lingxiao Palace at any time."

Ji Yao snorted softly and flicked her sleeve. "I would like to, but I'm afraid some people wouldn't welcome me."

"Who would dare!" Yang Kai glared.

Ji Yao looked straight at him without blinking.

Yang Kai pointed at his own nose. "You don't mean me, do you? What kind of joke is that? If Junior Sister Yao wants to visit Lingxiao Palace, you may go whenever you like. Absolutely no one would fail to welcome you."

"Those are your words." Ji Yao pressed her lips into a smile. "Then I'll have to visit Lingxiao Palace more often in the future."

She had in fact gone to Lingxiao Palace quite often before, but nine times out of ten she failed to see Yang Kai. The news she received was always that Yang Kai had gone out and had yet to return. Even if that was true, it inevitably gave her the illusion that Yang Kai was avoiding her, leaving her somewhat disappointed. Now, hearing Yang Kai say this, that little bit of unease instantly vanished like smoke.

"Right, I also heard that Miss Zhu has fallen into your demonic claws as well?"

The corner of Yang Kai's eye twitched. "Who has such a big mouth?" Several possible candidates flashed through his mind. The three great Yao Kings? No, that was unlikely. They had no reason to wag their tongues behind his back. Then was it Hua Qingsi? Or Nanmen Dajun? Hou Yu?

It was probably that woman Hou Yu! Yang Kai's heart turned ruthless. If it really was her, he would lock her in confinement for a hundred years and make her refine Secret Treasures nonstop, absolutely not letting her out before the full hundred years were up.

"Senior Brother truly is impressive, surrounded by beauties and enjoying the blessings of many wives. Others can only envy you."

Yang Kai said awkwardly, "Junior Sister Yao, if you have nothing else to do, how about helping me copy these jade slips? There aren't many left."

Mainly, what kind of thing was this to discuss with Ji Yao, a woman? It felt strange. If he were discussing it with a man, Yang Kai might even have been interested.

Ji Yao looked at him teasingly. "Senior Brother, are you embarrassed? What's there to be embarrassed about? Aren't all men like this, wishing they could claim every beautiful woman under Heaven?"

"That's not right." Yang Kai shook his head firmly. "I won't deny that beautiful women always make men look a few more times, and perhaps have a few idle fantasies, but claiming every beautiful woman under Heaven is far too unrealistic."

"Is that so?" Ji Yao's face went expressionless. "In other words, Senior Brother already has five beauties at his side and is satisfied?"

Yang Kai said solemnly, "How could I dare be anything but satisfied?"

Ji Yao walked slowly toward him and asked sternly, "Does Senior Brother dare guarantee that he won't go chasing flowers and flirting around in the future?"

Yang Kai raised a hand to his forehead. "Junior Sister Yao, we really should change the subject."

"Senior Brother, are you avoiding my question? Or do you still have other thoughts in your heart? If another woman throws herself into your arms, would you refuse her?" She stopped in front of Yang Kai and looked up at him stubbornly, a faint, quiet fragrance brushing his face.

Yang Kai took a step back until his back pressed against the wooden cabinet. The corner of his mouth twitched. "Junior Sister Yao, you're meddling too much!"

Ji Yao said, "If it were before, I could have sat by and ignored it, but since Master has agreed to accept Junior Sister into the Sect, Junior Sister and I are family. Although this is the first time we've met, I can tell that our Junior Sister has a cool, detached temperament. In matters between men and women, she probably won't restrain you at all, so as her Senior Sister, I naturally have to think a little more on her behalf."

"You're being unreasonable." Yang Kai frowned.

Ji Yao sneered. "You say one thing and mean another."

Yang Kai raised his brow. "How do I say one thing and mean another?"

"You just do."

"Don't talk as if you understand men so well." Yang Kai looked her up and down, then gave a cold snort. "If this Senior Brother isn't mistaken, Junior Sister, you're probably still... a virgin, aren't you?"

Ji Yao's fair cheeks flushed, and she snapped in embarrassed anger, "Rogue!"

Yang Kai spread his hands. "You're the one who insisted on discussing this topic with me. I'm merely speaking to the matter at hand. You've never even met a suitable man and have spent all your time cultivating bitterly in Ice Heart Valley, so how could you understand what men think?"

"Who says I haven't met one!" Ji Yao said angrily, her chest rising and falling sharply as her large eyes glared at Yang Kai.

Yang Kai felt inexplicably guilty under her stare and could not help looking away. He shrugged and said, "That's why I said we should change the subject. There's no meaning in continuing this discussion."

"No!" Ji Yao reached out and grabbed Yang Kai by the collar. "I used to think you were a decent person, that even as a man you knew how to keep yourself clean. I never expected you to be all gilded surface and rotten inside. If we don't make this clear today, Junior Sister will probably suffer for it in the future."

Yang Kai flew into a rage. "Who's all gilded surface and rotten inside?"

This was too much. How was he all gilded surface and rotten inside? This was simply a personal attack. How could he tolerate it?

Ji Yao tightened her grip, and Yang Kai was immediately pulled toward her. She said, enunciating every word, "I'm talking about you."

"Ji Yao!" Yang Kai shouted. "Don't go too far!"

"I am going too far, so what can you do to me!" Ji Yao raised her head, her face full of anger and not the slightest intention of backing down. She was half a head shorter than Yang Kai, yet at that moment she somehow seemed to be looking down on him.

Yang Kai glared at her fiercely, hot breaths flaring from his nostrils. Her flower-like, jade face was close at hand, and the two full curves pressed against his chest sent over an astonishing softness and heat. Yang Kai was momentarily dazed, and the menace in his expression changed at once. Just now, he had been focused only on arguing and getting angry with her, and had not realized how close they were. Now that he suddenly noticed, his heart began to pound.

The corner of his eye twitched, and his tone softened. "Let's talk properly. Let go first." He reached out, took her small hand, and tried to pry it open, only to find that Ji Yao had no intention of loosening her grip. Yet the instant their skin touched, that tender, pale little hand trembled slightly, and her cheeks slowly reddened.

Their eyes were still locked, but the tension between them was no longer drawn like swords and bows. Instead, a trace of charm and ambiguity had crept in.

Yang Kai took a deep breath and said with patient earnestness, "Great aunt, I was wrong, all right? Let go first, then we'll talk."

"I won't!" Ji Yao had lost the courage to meet his eyes, but her face remained stubborn.

Yang Kai said in a low voice, "Something will happen."

Ji Yao said nothing, but her silent resistance made her attitude clear.

"I'm warning you one last time. Something really will happen." Yang Kai's face was serious.

Ji Yao remained unmoved.

If he could no longer endure, then there was no need to endure anymore!

Yang Kai breathed out softly, slowly lowered his head, tilted it to the side, and pressed his mouth toward her red lips.

Ji Yao's body suddenly trembled violently, and the small hand that had been gripping Yang Kai's collar clenched even tighter.

A powerful masculine aura rushed over her. The distance between them closed rapidly, and Ji Yao's eyes shut all at once. Only her long eyelashes trembled nonstop, betraying the nervousness in her heart.

A soft, cool touch brushed his lips, seeming to carry a faint sweetness as well, so pleasant one could gladly linger and forget to leave.

Ji Yao's trembling body instantly stiffened, as though someone had cast a body-binding spell on her.

Yang Kai's movements were incomparably gentle, as if afraid of startling her. Each contact ended as soon as it began, like a dragonfly skimming the water. Her stiff, tender body gradually softened, and at some point the small hand that had been gripping Yang Kai's collar had loosened. Yang Kai held it in his palm and turned it behind her back.

Her waist tightened, and her entire body was pulled forward against him.
