## Chapter 3275 — Speculation

The group had clearly learned about Wuhua Hall on their way here, and their expressions were grave. The sound of someone tearing through the air drew near, and everyone turned to look. Wen Zishan said, "Yang Kai is here."

Lei Hong and Xue Zhengmao had met Yang Kai before. When Yang Kai had gone to Star God Palace to temper himself in the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda, they had both been there, so he was no stranger to them. Even so, they could not help marveling now. If Xiao Yuyang had not explained the situation beforehand, they never would have believed that Wuhua Hall's crisis had actually been turned around by Yang Kai alone.

He had managed to bring in so many powerful reinforcements in a single day, and he even seemed capable of arranging a cross-territory Space Array. Such talent was simply dazzling. Back when he had visited Star God Palace to enter the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda, Lei Hong and Xue Zhengmao had paid him little mind, but now they already regarded him as an equal.

"Hall Master, Saint Lord Ma, Gold Envoy Xiao..." Yang Kai landed and cupped his fists to each of them in turn, then exchanged greetings with Lei Hong and Xue Zhengmao.

Lei Hong and Xue Zhengmao glanced at each other, then suddenly bowed all the way to the ground. "Elder Yang turned back the tide and rescued many comrades of the Southern Territory from disaster. On behalf of Star God Palace, we offer our deepest gratitude." Protecting the Southern Territory had always been Star God Palace's responsibility, yet Star God Palace had known nothing of Wuhua Hall's plot, resulting in heavy casualties throughout the Southern Territory and dealing a serious blow to Star God Palace's prestige and reputation. If Wuhua Hall's scheme had truly succeeded, Star God Palace would have lost far more than face. The very foundation of the entire Southern Territory might have been shaken.

Their gratitude at this moment was entirely sincere.

Yang Kai raised a hand. "You two are too courteous. It was only my duty." Then he sighed. "Unfortunately, everything happened so suddenly that I could not fully resolve it. The Southern Territory has still suffered a grievous wound. I am ashamed."

Xue Zhengmao shook his head. "Without Elder Yang this time, the Southern Territory would have faced a true disaster. Preserving so many lives was Elder Yang's achievement alone."

Yang Kai smiled modestly. "I was only fortunate enough to know a few friends. I would not dare claim the credit."

Xue Zhengmao and Lei Hong fell silent. The two of them had once looked down on Yang Kai. Even when Yang Kai visited Star God Palace last time, they had not taken him seriously. The main reason was that another Elder of Star God Palace, Tan Junhao, had died at Yang Kai's hands. Whether or not Tan Junhao had truly done wrong, he had still been an Elder of Star God Palace, and both of them had long had some friendship with him. Yang Kai killing Tan Junhao was the same as slapping Star God Palace in the face. The Great Emperor was magnanimous enough not to care about such things, but Xue Zhengmao and Lei Hong could not simply let it go, and they had always nursed some hidden resentment toward Yang Kai's actions.

Only today did their opinion of him change. He was neither arrogant nor impatient, and his disposition was far from ordinary. No wonder he had gone farther and faster than others of his generation.

“Everyone from Star God Palace came?” Yang Kai glanced at the group not far away. Emperor Realm Masters were everywhere among them, at least over a hundred in number. So many Emperor Realm Masters were clearly not all from Star God Palace; there should have been people from other Sects as well.

Xue Zhengmao said in shame, "After hearing the news, we traveled day and night and flew here at full speed. We also called over people from several other Sects along the way, but we were still a step too late." The main reason was that Star God Palace was rather far from here, and the person sent to deliver the news had been delayed for several days on the road, so they had only just arrived.

When they arrived, the matter had already been resolved, so the trip had been for nothing. Still, they could not simply turn around and leave. Wuhua Hall's plot had indeed been shattered, but the events this time still had to be investigated thoroughly.

"Where is the Great Emperor?" Yang Kai asked, puzzled.

He had not seen the Great Emperor anywhere. Logically speaking, with a matter this serious, there was no way the Great Emperor would fail to appear. When Yang Kai had sent someone to Star God Palace to report the news, he had been counting on the Great Emperor coming here in person. As long as Radiant Moon Great Emperor showed himself, people like Lei Gu would not amount to anything.

At these words, Wen Zishan and Ma Qing also showed puzzled expressions. Ma Qing said, “That’s right. Why don’t we see the Great Emperor?”

Xue Zhengmao replied, "The Great Emperor heard that the matter here had already been resolved, so he returned to Star God Palace."

Everyone was left rather speechless. Since he had already come this far, why not come over and take a look? After all, this was no small matter. He ought to have inspected it personally no matter what. Yet the Great Emperor had simply turned back halfway. Since this involved the Great Emperor, however, no one dared say much.

Yang Kai asked casually, "Princess Lan Xun did not come either?"

Lei Hong said, "Her Highness the Princess is currently in retreat. Elder Yang is kind to be concerned."

Yang Kai answered with a soft oh.

Xue Zhengmao said, "Although the Great Emperor did not come, the Great Elder and I can handle this matter. There are, however, a few things I would like to ask Brother Yang." He had quietly changed his way of addressing Yang Kai to Brother Yang, which sounded much more cordial.

Yang Kai cupped his fists. "Elder Xue, please ask whatever you wish. This one will certainly tell all he knows and hold nothing back."

Xue Zhengmao said, "I have already heard Elder Xiao describe the general course of events, but I know nothing about what happened on your side. If Brother Yang does not mind..."

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "There is nothing to mind. It happened like this. During the banquet on the final day of the Martial Gathering, I suddenly received a transmission from my temple's Elder Luo, Luo Chen, asking me to step outside..."

He briefly recounted how Luo Chen had led him outside, hiding nothing, including the ambush and assassination attempt.

"Actually, there is one matter I would like to ask all of you about." Yang Kai frowned deeply. Even now, remembering what had happened that night left him with lingering fear.

Xue Zhengmao asked thoughtfully, "What is it?"

Yang Kai said, "The person who tried to assassinate me in the dark made no sound at all. I did not even notice when they drew close behind me. After I counterattacked, their movements became even more ghostly; they turned into smoke and scattered, leaving me with no solid target to strike. Someone like that absolutely cannot be nameless. I wonder whether any of you have an impression of them. When did such a figure appear in my Southern Territory?"

If there was any fish that had escaped the net this time, that killer was the only one. Yang Kai had been badly injured by them, yet he had not even seen their face clearly. He did not even know whether the other party was male or female. It was a bitter loss indeed.

One thing was certain: that person absolutely possessed Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivation and was extremely proficient in the Dao of Assassination. Yang Kai even suspected that this person might be one of the existences close to the Great Emperor level that Yang Yan had mentioned before.

According to Yang Yan, there were still some reclusive old monsters in this world. Their cultivation had already reached its absolute limit, and they could not improve even a fraction further. It was simply that the bottle of Heaven and Earth had limited capacity, preventing them from glimpsing the Dao of the Great Emperor and leaving them trapped at a bottleneck. Most of these people lived in seclusion in the mountains and forests, or cultivated silently in retreat, unseen by ordinary people. If such a figure had acted, that would explain things. But Yang Kai had not sensed the slightest trace of Demonic Qi from that person. In other words, the killer had not become a demon, so why would they be colluding with Wuhua Hall?

Everyone frowned when they heard this. Ma Qing said, "Such methods are truly unheard of." He turned to Wen Zishan. "Do you have any impression?"

Wen Zishan slowly shook his head as well.

Xue Zhengmao said, "Perhaps... it was not done by someone from my Southern Territory."

Lei Hong's expression shifted. "You mean..."

Yang Kai looked at them and frowned. "Do you have some speculation?"

Xue Zhengmao pondered for a long while before finally speaking. "Such elusive assassination methods cannot be cultivated overnight. As far as I know, there is indeed one place in this world capable of raising such people."

At this, Ma Qing and Wen Zishan clearly thought of something as well, and both looked startled. "Surely not?"

Yang Kai also came to a sudden realization. "Elder Xue means the West..."

Xue Zhengmao immediately raised his hand. "Without real evidence, I would not dare draw wild conclusions."

Yang Kai knew what he was wary of, so he did not finish the thought. After this reminder, however, he felt it really was possible. That assassination art was extraordinary; an ordinary person could never possess such ability.

Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivation, mastery of assassination—all kinds of signs really did point to that force.

It was just... how had the Southern Territory's affairs become involved with that side? And how had that side gotten mixed up with demonized people?

Lei Hong frowned. "It should not be to that extent. That place commands an entire territory, after all. How could it do something like this?"

"Let us set this matter aside for now. In the future, if you have the mind to, pay more attention and do not relax your guard. Brother Yang was truly lucky to escape this calamity. If that person harbors evil intentions and sets their sights on someone else..." Xue Zhengmao did not finish, and even he shuddered.

Enemies hidden in the dark were the most terrifying. The person who had attacked Yang Kai was skilled in assassination. Anyone truly targeted by such a person would have to sleep with one eye open from then on.

Still, since Yang Kai had escaped from that person's hands, the few Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivators felt they themselves should be all right. But just because they would be fine did not mean their fellow Sect members would be fine. How many Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivators were there?

There were too few clues. Yang Kai had exchanged only a single move with that person, and even he could not tell whether they were male or female, much less expect anyone else to identify them.

Next, Yang Kai briefly described what had happened afterward. When everyone learned that Yang Kai truly could arrange a cross-territory Space Array, they were amazed all over again. Although they had already inspected that array before this, a guess and a confirmation were two different things.

In any case, it was thanks to that Space Array that Yang Kai had been able to gather so many helpers within a single day and resolve Wuhua Hall's crisis.

What he described did not differ much from what Xiao Yuyang and the others already knew. The only difference was that they had not known about Yang Kai's own experiences. When they learned that Yang Kai had followed Lei Gu all the way to the Southern Marsh and then set a trap to kill him, everyone sighed with emotion.

Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivators were few in number anywhere, and they had long had some friendship with one another. Lei Gu had been possessed by Demonic Intent, lost his own mind, and ultimately met a tragic end, so naturally they could not help feeling saddened.

Next came settling the survivors from the great Sects of the Southern Territory. As time passed, news of what had happened here gradually spread throughout the Southern Territory, and the experts left behind by the great Sects rushed to Wuhua Hall one after another, first to investigate the situation and second to confirm the casualties among their own people.

Almost every day, people came and went from Wuhua Hall. In less than ten days, Wuhua Hall grew quiet. Those who ought to leave had basically all left. After this incident, the Southern Territory would likely need a very long period of rest and recovery.
