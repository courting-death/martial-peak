## Chapter 3239 — All Sides Move Out

Hua Qingsi smiled faintly. "Palace Master is worrying too much. Ice Heart Valley would never refuse something this beneficial."

Yang Kai asked curiously, "How so?"

Hua Qingsi said, "Because Senior Bing Yun was missing for so many years, and because Ice Heart Valley was constantly suppressed by Wenqing Sect, they do not have much industry to their name. The cities under their control are few as well, only two or three, it seems. Most of their production and income come from the spirit flowers and rare herbs planted inside the valley. In recent years, Ice Heart Valley has also taken in quite a few disciples, so I expect they are short on resources right now. With benefits being delivered to their door, how could they possibly refuse?"

Yang Kai stroked his chin and thought for a moment. "In that case, we can count them in too."

He did not know Ice Heart Valley as well as Hua Qingsi did. Since Hua Qingsi said so, that was surely how things stood. If they could truly pull Ice Heart Valley onto the same war chariot, then the Northern Territory's four top Sects would be joining forces. Who in the Northern Territory would dare stand in their way?

Before long, he came to a decision. "Then count them in too."

Hua Qingsi smiled. "There is no time to lose. I will invite someone over right away."

Yang Kai nodded, and Hua Qingsi flew out at once.

Less than half an hour later, she brought a woman with an icy bearing into the main hall.

Yang Kai's face darkened at once, and he shot Hua Qingsi a faintly reproachful look. The person she had brought was actually Ji Yao. Ever since those two furtive encounters with her, Yang Kai had been somewhat afraid to see her. On one hand, the secrecy was thrilling; on the other, he felt he had wronged Su Yan and the others, leaving him deeply conflicted. Most importantly, even now, he still could not quite figure out what Ji Yao wanted.

From beginning to end, she had never discussed the matter with him. She seemed to let him do as he pleased, yet would not let him get too far, leaving Yang Kai with the nagging feeling that she had some hold over him.

Now Hua Qingsi had gone and brought Ji Yao over, of all people. His mood could be imagined.

Hua Qingsi looked puzzled. She seemed to have noticed that the Palace Master's expression was off, but she could not worry about that now. Cupping her fists, she said, "Palace Master, Elder Ji has arrived."

Yang Kai forced out a faint smile. "Junior Sister Yao."

In front of everyone, Ji Yao revealed nothing unusual. She gave a cool salute. "Senior Brother Yang."

Yang Kai coughed lightly. "I invited Junior Sister Yao here because there is a matter to discuss. I believe Chief Steward has already spoken with Senior Bing Yun about it. I wonder what Senior thinks." If he wanted to drag Ice Heart Valley into this, Bing Yun's attitude was crucial; without her approval, everything else was meaningless.

Ji Yao said indifferently, "Honoured Master said that Ice Heart Valley survived back then only because Senior Brother Yang resolved its crisis. Now that Senior Brother Yang has encountered a difficult problem, everyone in Ice Heart Valley should naturally lend their full support. If Senior Brother has any instructions, Ice Heart Valley will certainly not refuse."

Yang Kai's eyes flashed, and he smiled faintly. "Excellent. Then I will have to trouble you." Looking around, he said, "Since everyone is here, let us settle on a plan. This matter has to be resolved as quickly as possible."

Since they had decided to do this, there was no need to drag it out. In any case, the four great Sects had already joined forces, and no one in the Northern Territory could contend with them. They did not need schemes or tricks at all; they simply had to push forward.

The plan was drawn up quickly. In essence, the four families would deploy their forces together, take Lingxiao Palace as the center, and spread out across the Northern Territory to announce Lingxiao Palace's status as overlord. In return, the great Sects and cities of the Northern Territory would have to offer Lingxiao Palace a yearly tribute of cultivation materials.

Once the discussion was settled, Ji Yao, Li Jiao, and Mi Qi immediately returned to their respective Sects to mobilize manpower.

The next day, before Lingxiao Palace's Grand Guardian Array, teams of cultivators came speeding in. On the left was a graceful crowd made up entirely of women, most of them strikingly beautiful. Their temperaments each had their own charm, like spring orchids and autumn chrysanthemums, enough to dazzle the eye. Naturally, this group consisted of Ice Heart Valley disciples.

On the right were the forces of Li Dragon Palace, while the disciples of Mitian Sect gathered behind them. Each group numbered over a thousand, and together they made a force of three thousand.

The number itself was not large, but the proportion of experts among them was terrifying. Emperor Realm and Dao Origin Realm cultivators could be seen everywhere, and even the weakest were still Void King Realm.

Lingxiao Palace's forces stood in the middle, led by the three great Yao Kings. Ye Hen and the other Dao Origin Realm cultivators had also come out, but the rest looked rather lacking. They were basically all Void King Realm and could not compare with the other three families at all.

The disciples who had come from the Star Domain truly were not ready for important tasks yet, which was why Lingxiao Palace's strength was so sharply divided. The strong were absurdly strong, while the weak were pitifully weak.

Hua Qingsi summoned Li Jiao, Mi Qi, and Ji Yao, then spread out a map. The map had already been marked with red dot after red dot, each one representing either a city of considerable scale or the territory of a Sect or clan.

After some discussion and arrangements, the several thousand cultivators were divided into eight-person groups and sent out in all directions. Each group had two disciples from each family, and each was led by an expert at the Dao Origin Realm or above.

Their targets were naturally the marked locations on the map. The sole purpose of this trip was to make those cities, Sects, and clans acknowledge Lingxiao Palace's overlord status, then pay tribute on schedule each year.

Yang Kai had already given the order. If they knew what was good for them, all the better. If they did not, then beat them if possible; if they could not be beaten, send word back, and stronger masters would naturally go handle it. The Emperor Realm masters of the four top Sects were no pushovers.

Of course, most of the places assigned to teams led by Dao Origin Realm cultivators were easy targets. The hard bones would naturally be visited in person by teams led by Emperor Realm masters. With the four families joining forces, he believed not many people would dare cause trouble.

Yang Kai also led a team straight toward the headquarters of several first-class forces in the Northern Territory.

Five days later, in the main hall of Myriad Ancestors Sect's headquarters, Sect Master Hua Yu stood before the hall and cupped his fists in farewell from afar. Only after that group of eight vanished from sight did he drop the stiff smile from his face, heave a long sigh, and reveal a look of stifled grievance.

Vice Sect Master Cheng Dahai walked over and asked bitterly, "Sect Master, are we really going to hand thirty percent of our yearly income to Lingxiao Palace?"

"Exactly. That boy says a few words, and we have to hand over thirty percent of the benefits already in our grasp. What will others think of our Myriad Ancestors Sect?" an Elder also shouted through gritted teeth.

Hua Yu said gloomily, "Then what do you suggest?"

"If Sect Master wishes, this subordinate will take people right now to intercept and kill him, then bring that jade slip back. When the time comes, with the dead unable to testify, no one will know our Myriad Ancestors Sect was behind it."

"Exactly. That boy's actions have long since provoked public outrage. Countless people in the Northern Territory want him dead. Who would know it was us?"

"You?" Hua Yu turned and glanced at the Elders and Protectors beside him, then shook his head. "You are no match for him."

The Elder who had spoken said, "How do we know without trying? That boy is only at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. If all of us attack together..."

"There is no need. I have already tried." Hua Yu's expression turned bleak.

"Already tried?" Everyone looked at one another. Only then did they understand why Hua Yu had taken Yang Kai to the secret room earlier. As it turned out, he had wanted to test Yang Kai's ability, or perhaps... see whether there was a chance to take him down. After all, no one was willing to hand benefits over to someone else for no reason.

"What happened?" Everyone looked at Hua Yu expectantly.

"I was no match for him at all." Hua Yu gave a bitter smile.

The Elder said, "Sect Master, why raise another's prestige while lowering your own? You are also at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, so you should not be far behind him, right?"

Hua Yu said, "It is precisely because our cultivations are about the same that I agreed so readily. He let me attack ten times and stood there without moving an inch, yet I could not even touch his clothes."

"What?" Everyone was shocked. Some even wondered whether they had misheard.

They were both Second Stage Emperors, yet the other party had stood there without moving and allowed ten attacks, while the Sect Master could not even touch his clothes? What did that imply? In other words, Yang Kai had blocked the Sect Master's attacks with his protective Emperor Yuan alone?

"Yet he took this old man down with a single casual strike." Hua Yu dropped another thunderclap, leaving everyone dazed. He sighed. "Against such methods, what can our Myriad Ancestors Sect use to resist? If we had not complied with his wishes today, I fear our Myriad Ancestors Sect's ten-thousand-year foundation would have been destroyed in an instant."

"He would dare!" Cheng Dahai shouted.

Hua Yu glanced at him. "He probably really would. The four top Sects have joined forces. If they truly set their hearts on destroying our Myriad Ancestors Sect, what could we use to stop them? His reputation is well deserved. A few days ago, when I heard Mitian Sect had submitted to Lingxiao Palace, I thought someone was spreading false rumors. Now it seems the news was true."

Everyone fell silent, their expressions bleak.

Handing over thirty percent of their annual income meant everyone would receive thirty percent less each year. No one could be happy about that.

The matter progressed more smoothly than expected. Yang Kai led his team across the Northern Territory, carrying the weight of Mitian Sect's submission. Wherever he went, he barely had to lift a finger before obtaining written agreements from those Sects to hand over thirty percent of their profits each year. Not a single family dared resist. At most, they acted like Myriad Ancestors Sect, secretly testing Yang Kai's ability before finding a graceful way to back down.

Yang Kai did not spend all his time hurrying from place to place. Whenever he found a suitable location, he would also arrange a Space Array and connect it to Lingxiao Palace. That way, if any family failed to honor its promise in the future, Lingxiao Palace could arrive at any time and teach them a lesson.

In the Northern Territory, every place controlled by a city, Sect, or clan had to hand over thirty percent of its income. Thirty percent from one or two families might not amount to much, but thirty percent of the entire Northern Territory was rather terrifying.

It meant that even if Lingxiao Palace did nothing in the future, it could still take thirty percent of the Northern Territory's profits. Moreover, through its ties to these cities, Sects, and clans, it could collect large quantities of Northern Territory specialties and expand trade between Lingxiao Palace and the Southern Territory. The materials gathered from the Southern Territory could also be distributed throughout the Northern Territory for sale, and the price difference alone would be a considerable sum.

After more than twenty busy days, Yang Kai returned directly to Lingxiao Palace through a Space Array. He had already dealt with the Northern Territory's most stubborn holdouts. Things had gone so smoothly, first, because news of Mitian Sect's submission had spread far and wide. Even Mitian Sect had submitted, so what choice did the others have? Second, this operation was backed by the four families together, and their combined might left no one brave enough to resist.
