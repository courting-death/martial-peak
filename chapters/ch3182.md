## Chapter 3182 — Forward, Gungun

"What the hell is this thing!" Yang Kai hovered in the void, one hand on his chin as he stared ahead in astonishment.

The enormous black ball formed from the Small Xuan Realm hung before him, but now it had actually split open into a huge mouth!

It really was a huge mouth, and a terrifying one at that.

The mouth stretched a hundred li from end to end, like an enormous spatial crack! More than that, it was filled with sharp fangs. As the teeth opened and closed, they clacked loudly, as if they could gnaw through everything in existence. Even Yang Kai felt his scalp tingle at the sight.

When the Small Xuan Realm finished swallowing all of Yellow Springs Star, the black ball had violently wriggled and twisted before taking on this form.

Yang Kai almost could not accept it. How had his Xuan Realm Pearl turned into this? No, strictly speaking, the Xuan Realm Pearl itself had not changed much. It was still only a bead the size of a dragon eye. What had changed was the Xuan Realm Pearl's devouring Domain.

With the whole of Yellow Springs Star as nourishment, what kind of monster had he raised? Indeed, at first glance, the Xuan Realm Pearl's current devouring Domain looked exactly like a monster: a round, unimaginably huge pitch-black body with no facial features, only a huge mouth packed with fangs, hideous and savage.

"Heaven and Earth Will?" Yang Kai frowned and murmured.

The information coming from the Small Xuan Realm was growing clearer and clearer. He could actually sense longing and hunger from it. Could this be the manifestation of the Small Xuan Realm's Heaven and Earth Will? Or perhaps it should be called an Artifact Spirit.

The Xuan Realm Pearl had originally been nothing more than an Imperial Treasure, one without Spiritual Awareness, yet at this moment, it seemed to possess a life of its own.

This black ball monster, so enormous that even in his Dragon Clan form he would feel incomparably tiny before it, should be the Xuan Realm Pearl's Artifact Spirit. Its consciousness was not distinct, only a budding thing, instinctively craving more to devour. The huge mouth and fangs were the concrete manifestation of the Artifact Spirit's instincts.

"Ha!" Yang Kai burst out laughing and suddenly thought of the little black dog he had left at Lingxiao Palace, the one with the Guixu bloodline.

Everything connected to him seemed to have the ability to devour.

The little black dog was like that, the Xuan Realm Pearl had become like that as well, and there was even less need to mention the Incarnation. He did not know whether it was Heaven's Will at work or simply destiny.

Still, the devouring of the Incarnation and the Xuan Realm Pearl was somewhat different from the little black dog's. The first two relied on the Heaven Devouring Battle Art when they devoured, while the little black dog relied on its innate Divine Ability. In the end, however, all of them devoured in order to strengthen themselves.

A flash of inspiration suddenly crossed Yang Kai's mind. Had Wu Kuang seen Guixu back then? Was that why he had created the Heaven Devouring Battle Art based on Guixu's characteristics? If so, the similarity between them made sense.

Clack, clack...

The sound of teeth opening and closing kept ringing out, as if urging Yang Kai on.

“Good, good, I will take you to the next place!” Yang Kai stood in the void and looked around. The Xuan Realm Pearl developing a vague will was not necessarily a bad thing. Every world had its own will, after all. Heaven’s Will was invisible and intangible, yet it influenced the growth and flourishing of all living things. For the Small Xuan Realm to gain such an opportunity would also be of great benefit to its future growth.

But where should he go now? The Star Domain was vast. If he failed to pick the right direction, he would likely waste a great deal of time on the road.

There! Yang Kai looked up in one direction. With a flicker of his figure, he landed atop the black ball, sat down cross-legged, and swept out a hand. "Set out!"

The black ball seemed able to sense his command. Without needing to be driven at all, it rushed off in that direction on its own, and at a startling speed. As it flew, its teeth clacked as they opened and closed, scattering savage sounds along the way.

He had chosen this direction for a reason: many of the Yellow Springs Star cultivators who had fled earlier had gone this way.

Yang Kai did not know how the Cultivation Stars were distributed throughout Great Wilderness Star Domain, but how could these natives not know? Since they had gone in this direction, it meant there were other Cultivation Stars this way.

One man and one black ball drifted through the Starry Sky, speeding toward an unknown destination. It was a strange scene beyond words.

The darkness that could devour stars seemed to have no effect on Yang Kai at all. Instead, it served as a vessel, bearing his weight.

He reached out and patted the new "mount" beneath him. It felt soft to the touch, like he was patting a cloud, which did not quite match its savage face. In fact, if it stopped clacking its teeth and kept its mouth closed, it would look rather simple and adorable. But it had only just been born, after all, and its devouring instinct made it keep biting at the air, so Yang Kai could not be bothered to stop it.

"I need to give you a name. I can't keep calling you black ball." Yang Kai stroked his chin and muttered to himself. It was just like Liu Yan. Her true body was only a Refining Furnace, but after being given a name, her life could be considered complete.

"Got it!" Yang Kai's eyes lit up, and he snapped his fingers. "From now on, you'll be called Gungun."

It rolled its way across Yellow Springs Star, devouring the whole star clean. Gungun, echoing the rumble of its rolling advance, was indeed a fitting name.

"Clack, clack..." Its teeth opened and closed, striking together with crisp sounds.

"You think it's good too, right? Then it's settled." Yang Kai smiled broadly, feeling that he was truly a genius at naming. He clapped his hands, then looked troubled again. "But I can't favor one over the other. Guixu can be called Little Black."

With a thought, he forced an inescapable new name onto the Guixu strange beast whose fierce reputation resounded throughout the Demon Domain. Who knew what those Demon Kings and Demon Emperors would think if they found out.

"Gungun, forward!" Yang Kai pointed ahead like a child who had just received a new toy, so excited he was practically dancing.

"Clack, clack!"

"Gungun, devour!"

"Clack, clack!"

Along the way, they encountered several enormous meteorites, and Gungun plowed straight through them. Its huge mouth opened and closed, swallowing those meteorites into its belly and tossing them into the Small Xuan Realm.

But Yang Kai could clearly sense that Gungun did not much like the taste of these meteorites. If Yellow Springs Star had been a delicious feast, then these meteorites were bran pickles, almost impossible to swallow.

After this happened several times, Gungun lost all interest in devouring the meteorites drifting through the Starry Sky. Even some Dead Stars could hardly rouse its appetite.

On the other hand, even without Yang Kai's guidance, it changed direction on its own several times, as if it had a keen nose and could smell where the delicious food was.

Yang Kai did not stop it. One month later, his guess was confirmed.

A star not much smaller than Yellow Springs Star appeared in the distance. Even without landing on it, Yang Kai could tell that it was definitely a fairly good Cultivation Star.

Gungun really could sense where the Cultivation Stars were!

...

On Kuanglang Star, the entire population seemed to be facing a great enemy, and countless living beings looked up at the sky.

Several days ago, a small black dot had suddenly appeared in the sky, hanging between Kuanglang Star and the sun. At first, few paid it any attention, but as the days passed, that small black dot grew larger and larger, until today it actually blotted out the entire sun, plunging Kuanglang Star into darkness. It was as if a solar eclipse had arrived, leaving all living things uneasy.

An indescribable sense of crisis grew in every heart.

Even the great sea heaved and rolled restlessly.

Nearly eighty percent of Kuanglang Star's territory was covered by seawater. Islands large and small dotted it like chess pieces scattered across a board, forming the footholds where Kuanglang Star's living beings made their homes.

This was a world dominated by the Sea Race. On this star, humans and yao had extremely little living space.

Gazing up at the darkness blocking the sun, many Sea Race members could not help recalling the rumor that had spread over the past month: wherever the black ball rolled, everything was devoured clean, and all things ceased to exist!

The rumor had begun with some humans who had fled here. According to those people, they came from Yellow Springs Star, where a terrifying demon had descended, controlling a black ball that devoured Heaven and Earth and swallowing the entire Yellow Springs Star.

The Sea Race had always prided themselves on their nobility and looked down on the humans and yao, believing that all these creatures born on land were utterly lowly. Why would they believe the words of a few refugees?

No Sea Race member took these rumors seriously, but they spread very quickly. In less than a month, more than half the Sea Race on Kuanglang Star had heard them.

Only now, as they lifted their heads and stared at that black ball, did the recent rumors echo unbidden in their minds, filling them with fear and unease. Could there really be a darkness in this world capable of devouring Heaven and Earth?

The world stirred. From above an enormous sea island, a figure shot into the sky, flying straight toward that darkness.

Countless Sea Race members saw this, and their uneasy hearts immediately steadied.

"It's Sir Ao Wei!"

"That's right, we have Sir Ao Wei. Why should we fear that black ball?"

"If it dares come, Sir Ao Wei will make it know his might."

"It really is courting death. Does it think our Kuanglang Star is the same as Yellow Springs Star?"

The Sea Race cried out one after another. Ao Wei's name became a rolling tide, almost powerful enough to drown out Heaven and Earth.

Ao Wei, the Lord of Kuanglang Star, was regarded by all the Sea Race as a savior. It seemed that as long as he made a move, the black ball falling toward Kuanglang Star would immediately be reduced to ashes. The will of billions of living beings seemed to take form, adding fortune to Ao Wei's body and letting him uphold the will of Heaven and Earth.

At this moment, he could truly be called invincible.

Only, no one noticed how grave Ao Wei's expression was. Even with the fortune of Heaven and Earth and the will of billions of living beings gathered upon him, he inexplicably felt a chill run through his body, and the urge to turn and flee surged within him.

But others could flee. As this star's Star Lord, where could he go?

He looked up at the black ball, his heart filled with loathing and dread.

These feelings were not truly his own, but Kuanglang Star's Heaven and Earth Will, the sensation fed back to him by the Stellar Origin. It was precisely this sensation that made him so anxious and uneasy.

It was like a mouse meeting a cat, or a rabbit meeting a hawk, as though it had encountered its natural enemy in the unseen order of things.

He stood in place, without even the courage to fly a little higher. All he could do was quietly await the black ball's arrival while his mind raced for a way to resolve the crisis before him.
