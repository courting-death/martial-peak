## Chapter 3348 — Breaking Out of the Shell

With this thought, Yang Kai simply let it do as it pleased.

Sure enough, the consciousness clung tightly to him, and Yang Kai felt it drawing something from his body. Whatever it took caused him no harm, but clearly benefited the consciousness enormously.

Not far away, Qiong Qi stared dumbfounded at the scene. A steady stream of golden aura flowed from Yang Kai into the pitch-black dragon egg. As the egg absorbed it, the vitality within grew stronger and stronger, and the patterns across its shell shone ever more brilliantly.

The radiance was one thing, but the egg's majestic Dragon Pressure was impossible to miss.

This was causing quite a commotion!

Qiong Qi could not help worrying that the Dragon Clan might discover what was happening. Any member of the Dragon Clan who passed within a hundred li would surely notice the disturbance, and if they came to investigate, then the fun would truly begin.

Fortunately, his fears never came to pass. Despite the scale of the commotion, several days went by without anyone being alerted.

Then one day, while Qiong Qi was keeping watch over the surroundings, he suddenly heard a thump.

He turned and saw a visible halo erupt from the dragon egg cradled protectively in Yang Kai's arms, spreading outward with a thunderous boom. As Qiong Qi watched, the egg finally stopped absorbing Yang Kai's aura.

It was about to hatch!

The thought came to Qiong Qi unbidden.

Although he had never witnessed a dragon egg hatch before, the sight before him left no doubt about what was about to happen.

Had Yang Kai truly succeeded in hatching it? Qiong Qi could hardly believe it. As far as he could sense, the dragon egg had been deathly still, without the slightest trace of vitality, yet Yang Kai had somehow brought it back from the dead. He wondered how the Dragon Clan would react if they found out.

Thump... Another beat rang out, resembling both a heartbeat and the pounding of a drum, as a halo rippled away in every direction.

Thump, thump, thump...

The thumps grew faster and more insistent. Visible halos surged wildly outward one after another, as though someone were tossing stone after stone into a lake.

Then the thumping abruptly ceased. In its place came another, very different sound.

Crack...

A hairline crack appeared in the pitch-black dragon egg, running down the middle from top to bottom.

Crack, crack...

The dragon egg, as tall as a man, suddenly split open down the middle. Qiong Qi's eyes widened as he tried to see exactly what kind of member of the Dragon Clan Yang Kai had expended so much energy to hatch.

Yet he could not make out what was inside, for a beam of pure white light shot from the dragon egg and soared straight into the clouds.

At the same time, a resounding dragon roar rose into the air and pierced Heaven and Earth.

Somewhere far away, answering dragon roars rose one after another and echoed throughout Dragon Isle.

Dragon phantoms appeared overhead as strange sounds filled the heavens. Countless illusory Great Dragons seemed to race and weave through the skies of Dragon Isle. Dragon Pressure permeated the air, making Heaven and Earth tremble.

All across Dragon Isle, people looked up in unison, shock and confusion written across their faces.

On Ice Snow Island, Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun stood side by side atop an iceberg. Fu Zhun looked haggard, her face pale. Ever since the day she discovered that Lin'er had been stolen away, she had not been herself. She could neither eat nor sleep in peace and had no heart for cultivation. That a mighty Tenth-Tier Great Dragon could grow thinner by the day was almost inconceivable.

Despite investigating for days, Zhu Yan had not found a single clue. He had secretly searched the residences of their dozen or so clansmen without finding any trace of Lin'er. He had come today to ask Fu Zhun if she had any other leads, only for this phenomenon to appear after they had exchanged a few words.

The Dragon Clan's two Great Elders looked up in unison, both visibly shaken.

"The Dragon Temple is shaking, and the Dragon Souls are manifesting. This... this means..." Zhu Yan's eyes widened, and he cried out, "A new clansman has been born?"

Zhu Yan had seen this before and naturally knew what the phenomenon represented. It appeared whenever a new member of the Dragon Clan was born, though this time it seemed far grander in scale than ever before.

The sight stunned Fu Zhun so thoroughly that she momentarily forgot her grief over her missing child. In a daze, she murmured, "Did any of our clansmen show signs of an impending birth?"

Zhu Yan shook his head. "There were no signs at all. If there had been, how could you and I not know? None of the women in the clan have even conceived a dragon egg, so how could a new clansman possibly be born?"

Before a new member of the Dragon Clan could be born, a dragon egg first had to be conceived, then incubated for many years. Yet there was not a single dragon egg anywhere on Dragon Isle, so a new birth should have been impossible.

"But this phenomenon..." Realization suddenly flashed across Fu Zhun's face, and she exclaimed, "Lin'er! It's Lin'er! Lin'er has been born!"

Zhu Yan's first reaction was that it was impossible. There had long been no hope for Lin'er; he had confirmed that himself. After so many years, how could Lin'er possibly hatch now?

Yet after thinking it over, he realized he could not rule out the possibility.

Aside from the dragon eggs placed in the Dragon Tomb, Lin'er was the only dragon egg left on all of Dragon Isle. If a new clansman truly had been born, it could only be him.

Even the Great Elder, who would not have batted an eye if a mountain collapsed before him, clutched his beard and yanked out several strands. He swallowed hard. "Could it... really be Lin'er?"

By the time Zhu Yan recovered from his shock, Fu Zhun was already a streak of light racing toward the source of the phenomenon at astonishing speed.

Frantic with worry, Zhu Yan hurried after her. Whether it was Lin'er or not, a new clansman had finally been born on Dragon Isle. This was a tremendous cause for celebration for the entire Dragon Clan, and as the Great Elder, he naturally had to investigate for himself.

At the same time, a dozen—perhaps twenty—figures shot into the air from all across Dragon Isle. Apart from Fu Ling, who had been assigned to guard the entrance as punishment, every member of the Dragon Clan was alerted, and all of them flew toward the Spirit Island where Yang Kai was.

On the Spirit Island, Yang Kai slowly opened his eyes, yawned, and stretched lazily before finally sitting upright with a bewildered look on his face. Resting his chin on one hand with his elbow propped against his knee, he fell into deep thought.

He had spent the past few days in a muddled haze, and having only just awoken, he had yet to make sense of the situation. All he knew was that the surrounding Spiritual Qi was unusually active and that waves of dragon roars kept reaching his ears.

"So noisy!" Yang Kai rolled his eyes and looked around. Not far away, Qiong Qi was staring at him in amazement.

As his vision slowly came into focus, Yang Kai grinned. "Old Qiong, why do you look like you've seen a ghost?"

Qiong Qi's lips twitched as he cupped his fists. "Congratulations, Young Master. Your great undertaking is complete."

"What do you mean, complete?" Yang Kai leaned forward slightly, curiosity in his eyes.

Qiong Qi gave him a strange look. "Young Master, have you forgotten what you were doing before?"

Yang Kai blinked. "Before?" He slapped his forehead as realization dawned. "I remember now. I was incubating that dragon egg. Where is it?" He looked down, searched for a moment, then spotted the shattered eggshells scattered across the ground. "Huh? What's going on here?"

Qiong Qi gave a helpless laugh. "Young Master, you already succeeded in hatching that dragon egg!"

Yang Kai was stunned. "I succeeded?"

Qiong Qi raised a hand and pointed toward the sky.

Only then did Yang Kai look up. His gaze sharpened, piercing the void and taking in the scene above. He broke into a broad grin and laughed. "Hahaha! This Young Master really hatched that dragon egg?"

"Absolutely. This old man witnessed it with his own eyes."

Yang Kai clapped his hands in delight. "I knew it! I knew I would succeed." He looked up again and studied the scene in the sky, nodding repeatedly. "Mm, the little fellow is quite lively. To be this large at birth, he has a promising future—very promising indeed."

Qiong Qi smiled wryly. No matter how promising the little fellow's future might be, it had nothing to do with Yang Kai. That was Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun's child; what did any of this have to do with him?

Dragon roars echoed as the myriad dragon phantoms gradually faded from the sky and the strange phenomena throughout Heaven and Earth slowly subsided.

A beam of white light shot straight down from the highest heavens, falling directly toward the Spirit Island.

Only then did Qiong Qi finally see what sort of creature Yang Kai had hatched.

It was a flawless little white dragon, crystalline and smooth, like a piece of polished white jade!

It was roughly two or three zhang long and as thick as an arm. At first glance it resembled a giant python, but its crystal-clear horns revealed its true identity. Such a size was nothing remarkable on Dragon Isle. Judging by its size alone, it probably possessed only a Second- or Third-Tier bloodline, hardly worth mentioning among the other Great Dragons.

But for a newborn, such a size was extraordinary.

What ordinary member of the Dragon Clan was ever this large at birth?

A White Dragon! Could he be a Water Dragon? Qiong Qi shook his head. As far as he knew, Dragon Isle's Third Elder, Fu Xuan, was a Water Dragon, but her true form should be blue.

Perhaps he was an Ice Dragon! His mother, Fu Zhun, was an Ice Dragon, so there was a good chance he had inherited that trait. However, the white of Fu Zhun's true form was not quite the same shade as the little white dragon before him, so Qiong Qi could not yet determine his attribute.

The little white dragon seemed to feel an instinctive affinity for Yang Kai. After descending, he circled Yang Kai several times before stopping in front of him.

The next instant, to Qiong Qi's astonishment, the little white dragon twisted and transformed into a fair-skinned, chubby little boy who looked only four or five years old.

The chubby little boy had a charmingly innocent air about him. His skin was as white as jade and delicate enough to bruise at a touch. He beamed at Yang Kai, revealing a pair of tiny fangs that made him look utterly adorable.

Qiong Qi's eyes almost popped out of their sockets.

He could assume human form the moment he was born? This chubby little boy was exceptionally gifted. Even members of the Dragon Clan, for all their innate advantages, could not transform immediately after birth. Yet he had done it, clear proof of just how extraordinary he was.

Then again, he was the child of two Tenth-Tier Great Dragons and had even been incubated by someone with a Half-Dragon body. It was only natural that he would be extraordinary.
