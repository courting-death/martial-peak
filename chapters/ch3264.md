## Chapter 3264 — What Can It Do to Me?

Feng Ming was not so lucky. Bound by the Laws of Space, he had yet to recover when the Moon Blade arrived before him and pierced straight through his body. A crescent-shaped hole opened in his chest, and through the wound, his organs could be seen clearly.

He lowered his head and stared at the savage opening in his chest and abdomen, his eyes bulging, still unable to quite believe it. Feng Ming was, after all, at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, roughly on par with Yang Kai, yet he had been reduced to this state in a single exchange. Being caught off guard was certainly part of it, but the other party's methods were far too shocking.

If he had truly been only Feng Ming, that one strike would have left him unable to move, with nothing to do but wait for death.

But he had been born through possession by Demonic Intent, so even with a fatal wound, he did not immediately collapse. Instead, dense Demonic Qi poured from the injury in his chest and abdomen, roiling and squirming as it sealed the gap.

On the other side, after dodging the Moon Blade, Lei Gu let out a furious roar and used all his strength to break free from the Laws of Space. Terror on his face, he retreated, trying to slip back into the darkness. As long as he could avoid a few of Yang Kai's moves, he could conceal himself again. Within this boundless Demonic Qi, Yang Kai could forget about finding any trace of him.

How could Yang Kai let him have his way? After sending out the two Moon Blades, he had already raised his hand and tossed out a small beast about one chi long, pitch-black from head to toe. It looked just like an ordinary village dog, but the instant it appeared, a terrifying light burst from its eyes, filled with joy and excitement.

Guixu!

Back then, after returning from the Frozen Earth, Yang Kai had kept Guixu in Lingxiao Palace. He had not brought it along when he went to Dragon Isle, but since the enemies on this trip were demonized people and numerous Demonic Intents, Guixu was their perfect nemesis. How could he not bring it? When he had returned to Lingxiao Palace earlier, he summoned it and quietly tucked it into the Small Xuan Realm.

When Guixu appeared, Lei Gu froze for a moment, still unable to understand why Yang Kai would bring out this small beast now. Deep in his heart, however, a chill rose up, the horror of facing a natural predator.

Myriad Sword swept up and transformed into a net of sword light, enveloping Lei Gu and sealing off his escape routes to either side.

Guixu pounced to Lei Gu's front flank in an instant. Its great mouth split open, and the small canine head suddenly swelled larger than a house. Within its jaws lay a chaotic nothingness as it bit down at Lei Gu from above.

"Guixu!" Lei Gu paled in shock. He had failed to recognize Guixu only because everything had happened so suddenly. Now that he saw this strange sight, how could he not remember? This was Guixu!

The strange beast Guixu was famous throughout the entire Demon Domain. Any Demon Race member who had lived long enough knew how terrifying it was. The Guixu before him was not the legendary horror that could devour an entire world, but at the very least, it possessed a trace of Guixu bloodline. That alone was enough to make every member of the Demon Race turn pale at the mention of it.

An extremely terrifying aura spread from that pitch-black maw. Lei Gu felt his whole body go cold, and with Yang Kai's sword net sealing him in, he had no path of retreat for the moment.

Just as he was about to suffer a miserable end in Guixu's vicious mouth, Lei Gu suddenly stretched out a hand and beckoned.

A figure charged out from the side and threw herself in front of Lei Gu. Her long hair streamed behind her, her dress snapped in the air, and her beautiful face was cold and indifferent, both eyes swallowed entirely by black.

Gao Xueting!

Yang Kai's eyes widened at the sight, and the sword net descending on Lei Gu instantly scattered into nothing. He had already guessed that Gao Xueting had met with misfortune, and now it seemed he had been right. Most likely, she and Luo Chen had both been eroded by Demonic Qi and fallen to the demonic path.

Gao Xueting stood motionless in front of Lei Gu, showing no intention of resisting at all. How could Yang Kai bring himself to strike a killing blow? As he withdrew his move, however, he saw Guixu's great mouth closing, about to swallow Lei Gu and Gao Xueting together.

Without thinking, Yang Kai flashed to Guixu's side and kicked out.

Guixu let out an awu whimper as it was sent flying. When Yang Kai turned back toward Lei Gu, his face twisted. "Despicable!"

Lei Gu had seized that opening to press one hand against Gao Xueting's crown. Demon Yuan pulsed unsteadily from his palm, ready to kill her at any moment.

Lei Gu showed not the slightest joy at surviving. His face twitched nonstop, and his eyes nearly spat fire as he looked at Yang Kai. He had never imagined this boy would actually make a fool out of him. The main reason was that Yang Kai's acting had been too convincing. Facing Feng Ming's fatal strike, he had not dodged or evaded at all, letting Feng Ming drive a claw three cun into his chest.

Could he really have been certain that Feng Ming was only testing him?

Taking Gao Xueting hostage now to preserve his own life was something he had been forced into, more like the last struggle of a cornered beast. It should not have been like this. After so many years of planning, and after paying the price of so many Demon Seeds, things should have ended with the Southern Territory's several hundred Emperor Realm cultivators and tens of thousands of elite disciples captured in one net. Then the entire Southern Territory should have been silently turned into part of the Demon Domain.

Instead, the plan had first gone wrong with Yang Kai, forcing Lei Gu to change strategy. Worse still, Yang Kai had summoned so many powerful helpers in a single day that the plan no longer had any hope of succeeding. In the end, Yang Kai had even turned his own scheme against him and fooled him. Lei Gu's mood was naturally foul to the extreme.

After vanishing for more than a dozen years, why had he appeared at this critical moment? And why did he keep ruining his good affairs at every turn?

"Release her!" Yang Kai flicked Myriad Sword and pointed it at Lei Gu.

Lei Gu stared at Yang Kai with a sinister, venomous expression, unmoved, as if he had already cast his own life and death aside.

Yang Kai met his stare without backing down. Seeing no sign that Lei Gu would compromise, he secretly felt a headache coming on. He had considered this situation before, but he had not known where Gao Xueting was, so there had been no way to rescue her in advance. Sure enough, Lei Gu had taken her as a hostage. What left him most helpless was that Gao Xueting had fallen to the demonic path and had no intention of resisting at all. Instead, she obeyed Lei Gu completely.

Whimpers came from the side. Guixu climbed to its feet and whined at Yang Kai a few times, looking utterly wronged. Seeing that Yang Kai had no intention of paying it any mind, its two little eyes rolled about before fixing on Feng Ming.

Feng Ming had suffered a grievous injury, but he had sealed the wound with Demonic Qi, so his life did not seem to be in danger. Yet the moment Guixu stared at him, his hair stood on end, and he quietly edged toward Lei Gu.

Guixu crouched on the ground, its little eyes tracking his every step. Then, without the slightest warning, it sprang up and pounced at Feng Ming, swift as lightning.

Feng Ming's face changed dramatically, and he flashed away again and again, trying to evade. Yet somehow, Guixu's bloody maw had already descended from above, its foul stench swallowing him whole. Feng Ming roared, "Save me!"

His shout cut off abruptly. Only a gulping sound followed. Visible to the naked eye, a lump bulged in Guixu's throat and rolled down its gullet toward its belly.

Back when Guixu's bloodline had first awakened, Feng Ming had been swallowed once before. At that time, however, Guixu had still been far too weak, and Feng Ming had relied on his powerful cultivation to carve out a path to survival from Guixu's mouth and escape calamity.

But years had passed, and Guixu had long since grown. In the Revolving World, Guixu had devoured a vast amount of Demonic Qi and Demon Race members, turning them into the foundation of its own strength. Feng Ming had been possessed by Demonic Intent and reduced to a demonized person; even if he had met Guixu unharmed, he would have found it extremely troublesome, let alone after being heavily injured by Yang Kai. How could he resist? He was swallowed in a single bite.

Lei Gu's expression shifted again and again as he watched Guixu from the corner of his eye, his face twitching slightly.

After swallowing Feng Ming, Guixu rolled its tongue, looking as if it had tasted something delicious and wanted more. It stared eagerly at Lei Gu, seemingly wanting to swallow him as well.

Ever since returning from the Revolving World, it had not eaten anything to its taste. Today, it had finally encountered demonized people, so naturally it wanted to feast. Unfortunately, Yang Kai would not allow it, and it did not dare act rashly. It could only settle for second best, opening its great mouth as an extremely powerful suction came from within.

The roiling Demonic Qi all around surged into its mouth, thinning at a speed visible to the naked eye.

"You can't escape," Yang Kai said calmly, looking at Lei Gu. "Surrender now, and perhaps I'll spare your life!"

Lei Gu said calmly, "Do you take this Lord for a three-year-old child?"

Yang Kai coldly snorted. "Your life is not worth that much to me."

Lei Gu said fiercely, "But her life is another matter." As he spoke, the large hand pressed against Gao Xueting's crown exerted slightly more force, and pain immediately crossed her face.

Yang Kai gritted his teeth. "What do you want?"

Lei Gu asked, "Why didn't you fall to the demonic path?" This question had troubled him all along, and he was desperate to understand it. Leaving aside everything Yang Kai had done just now, even at this moment, Demonic Qi still coiled around his body. He had clearly been eroded by it, so how could he remain clear-headed and avoid falling to the demonic path? Unless Lei Gu understood this, he would not be able to rest easy.

"Mere Demonic Qi. What can it do to me?" Yang Kai sneered. Within his Dantian was sealed an even purer ancient Demonic Qi. Compared with that, the surrounding Demonic Qi was nothing but a small fry before a true master. Even without his Dragon Clan bloodline, Yang Kai had an extremely strong immunity to this Demonic Qi. As he spoke, his body shook, and blood light blazed all over him. The Demonic Qi that had invaded him was forced out completely, and he returned to his original appearance.

"What about the heart demon?" Lei Gu's cheek twitched.

Yang Kai scoffed. "What heart demon?"

Lei Gu said, "You killed so many people, and many of them were your fellow disciples..."

Yang Kai snorted. "A few illusions think they can shake my state of mind?"

"How could you be certain?" Lei Gu roared. Those who had been killed were indeed illusions, but within this boundless Demonic Qi, they had seemed extremely real. Watching fellow disciples he was close to die tragically beneath his own sword should have left a flaw in his mind even if he had harbored some doubts, unless his heart was firm as stone and his will unshakable, or unless he had some way to distinguish truth from falsehood.

"Xiao Bai!" Yang Kai revealed an inscrutable smile.

"What?" Lei Gu frowned, clearly not understanding.

"If you don't understand, then don't ask. You'll only expose your ignorance." Yang Kai coldly snorted.

When he had first encountered Xiao Baiyi, even Yang Kai had failed to distinguish truth from falsehood. It had to be said that Lei Gu's method really was excellent, but the moment the words Xiao Bai left Xiao Baiyi's mouth, Yang Kai knew something was wrong.

Xiao Baiyi would never admit that he was Xiao Bai. He would only solemnly restate his own name.
