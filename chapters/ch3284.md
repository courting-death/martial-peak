## Chapter 3284 — Repaying a Favor

So when all was said and done, those few owed Wu Kuang their thanks. If Wu Kuang had not gone on a killing spree, they would never have had the chance to attain that supreme status.

"But there will always be a chance, will there not? Great Emperors are not immortal, after all." Sheng Yuzhu smiled faintly. Perhaps in her prime, she had been a beauty with a sweet smile, but her current appearance was hardly suited for it; that smile looked uglier than crying. "Your greatest advantage is your youth."

Yang Kai nodded. "Senior is right." He had only cultivated for about a hundred years so far, and with his aptitude, reaching the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm, or even the point of standing half a step from the Great Emperor position, would not be a problem. His lifespan was long. If that day truly came, he might well have a chance to stand at the peak.

Sheng Yuzhu said slowly, "If such a day truly comes... I mean, if it ever does, I will tell you how one can achieve the position of Great Emperor."

Yang Kai jolted, his eyes brightening as he stared at Sheng Yuzhu. "Senior knows the secret to becoming a Great Emperor?"

This question had always puzzled him. He knew only that one had to grasp the Great Might of Heaven and Earth to become a Great Emperor; beyond that, he knew nothing. Yet how exalted was a Great Emperor? Even mastering the Great Might of Heaven and Earth did not guarantee success. Now, Sheng Yuzhu's words let him glimpse boundless hope.

Sheng Yuzhu said, "If Wu Kuang had not plotted against me back then, I might have had a chance myself. I have experienced something similar once before. What do you think? Do I know or not?"

Yang Kai immediately felt restless, as if an itch had settled deep in his bones. He almost wished he could pry Sheng Yuzhu's mouth open by force and dig out every secret she knew. If he could truly understand the secret of becoming a Great Emperor, then once the opportunity arrived, he would be ahead of everyone else.

In the end, however, he was not someone who could not tell right from wrong. Sheng Yuzhu was repaying his favor; how could he betray his own principles?

It had to be said that Sheng Yuzhu's repayment was enormous. Although it sounded vague and unreliable, it was worth more than any Secret Treasure or rare treasure.

He grinned and said, "Can you tell me now?" Although he knew it was rather unlikely, he still had to try. Perhaps she really would be overcome with generosity and teach him everything she knew.

Sheng Yuzhu slowly shook her head. "The time has not come. Telling you now would be useless. Focus on your cultivation. Opportunities are reserved for those who are prepared."

Yang Kai nodded and stopped pressing. Then his expression suddenly turned solemn again. "When that time comes, this Junior may have to compete with Senior. Is Senior not worried about making a powerful enemy for herself?"

Even if some opportunity really did appear, it would be exceedingly slim. It could not be like the last battle in the Shattered Star Sea, when several Great Emperor positions were vacated all at once. For figures like Great Emperors, the death of even one would shake Heaven and Earth.

So even if an opportunity appeared in the future, it would most likely be a single position. Sheng Yuzhu was essentially creating a powerful enemy for herself, and this enemy would know everything she knew.

Sheng Yuzhu smiled when she heard this. "In the struggle for the Great Dao, one side lives and the other dies. Besides, do you really think that when the time comes, there will only be the two of us? At that moment, everyone under Heaven with the qualifications will sense it. Perhaps you and I can even join forces for a time."

"So that is what Senior had in mind." Yang Kai's doubts vanished. He had to admit she made a great deal of sense. When that time truly came, all the old monsters hidden away in secluded cultivation throughout the world would probably swarm out. What difference would one more or one fewer person make? Instead, Sheng Yuzhu could join hands with him against their enemies, then settle life and death at the final moment.

With that thought, Yang Kai no longer hesitated. He cupped his fists and said, "Many thanks for your generous favor, Senior. This Junior is deeply grateful. Now, let us leave this place."

"I will have to trouble you." Sheng Yuzhu nodded lightly. Though she tried to keep her voice calm, it clearly trembled, showing just how stirred she was.

After all, she had been trapped here for tens of thousands of years. If she still could not get out, she might truly turn into a pile of withered bones.

Yang Kai knew she could not move easily, so he did not stand on ceremony. He walked straight over, apologized, then reached out and picked her up. She was so light in his arms that it felt as if he were holding a bundle of bones, leaving Yang Kai sighing inwardly.

An existence only half a step from becoming a Great Emperor had actually fallen into such a state. How much suffering had she endured over the years? Moreover, she was a woman. He had no idea how she had provoked Wu Kuang, only that she had been plotted against before she even had time to contend for the position of Great Emperor.

The Laws of Space surged, and Yang Kai opened a passage along the path by which he had entered. In the blink of an eye, he was back in the mountain valley.

Rich Spiritual Qi flooded in from every direction. Sheng Yuzhu, cradled in his arms, could not help letting out a moan, and her cloudy eyes suddenly shone with astonishing light. How many years had it been? How many years since she had felt Spiritual Qi? In that Void Crack, there had been only endless emptiness and chaos. If she had not had a divine skill protecting her, she could never have endured for so many years.

Yang Kai could clearly feel Sheng Yuzhu trembling slightly, and he understood her feelings. Deliberately changing the subject, he said, "This is the Southern Territory's Luocha Sect. Senior may not have heard of it, but this Sect has just suffered a great calamity, and countless Masters in the Sect were killed or injured. I have some ties with someone here, so I came to serve as a Guest Elder and offer what protection I can. If Senior does not object, why not recuperate here for the time being and make further plans once you have recovered a little?"

"This body is only lingering on its last breath. Anywhere is fine." Sheng Yuzhu truly did not mind. She only greedily breathed in the air around her, as if she had been reborn.

Yang Kai said, "Since Senior does not mind, I will make the arrangements."

"Wait a moment." Just as Yang Kai was about to leave, Sheng Yuzhu suddenly patted his arm.

Yang Kai did not know what she meant, so he remained where he was.

By now, dawn had arrived and the east was beginning to brighten. After they waited for a good while, warm light slowly spread across the earth. Sheng Yuzhu gazed toward the east, and when she saw the sunrise, her eyes held both sorrow and excitement. "I never thought I would have another chance to see this."

Yang Kai was speechless. He had thought she had some matter to handle, but who would have imagined she had deliberately stayed behind just to watch the sunrise? It could only be said that no matter how many tens of thousands of years she had lived, a woman was still a woman.

Then again, Yang Kai truly admired her. If he had been trapped in a Void Crack for tens of thousands of years, even if he had some means of preserving his life, his temperament would probably have changed beyond recognition, perhaps even driving him mad. Yet Sheng Yuzhu had not broken. Her character and willpower were plain to see. Yang Kai had no doubt that once she recovered to her peak, when the struggle for the Great Dao truly arrived, he might not be her match. Perhaps no one under Heaven would be her match.

But so what? Sheng Yuzhu did not mind sharing the greatest secret of all, so how could Yang Kai, a grown man, be afraid? Those of their generation who pursued the Martial Dao should keep their hearts unclouded and press forward with resolve.

Gazing eastward, Yang Kai felt boundless ambition well up in his chest. His conversation with Sheng Yuzhu had given him a sense of urgency. Opportunities were indeed reserved for those who were prepared. Although he did not know when that elusive chance would arrive, before then, he had to earn the qualifications to contend with the heroes under Heaven. If he lacked even those qualifications, then even if the opportunity came, he could only watch helplessly as it slipped through his fingers.

Strength was the key to everything. He had to raise his cultivation to the level required to enter that contest.

After accompanying Sheng Yuzhu for a while as she watched the sunrise, Yang Kai brought her back to Luocha Peak.

When Ran Yirou and Yu Keren saw Yang Kai return carrying a woman so thin she was nothing but skin and bones, both of them were startled. Sheng Yuzhu's current appearance was simply too frightening, but they did not dare ask questions. Following Yang Kai's instructions, they respectfully settled her in a wing room.

Once the two women had withdrawn, Yang Kai said, "Senior, please recuperate here for now. If you need anything, just tell me."

Sheng Yuzhu did not put on airs. "Can you lend me some Source Crystals and Spirit Pills? I will repay you later."

Yang Kai's immediate priority was to improve his strength as quickly as possible and prepare in advance, while Sheng Yuzhu naturally needed to recover her strength as soon as she could. Still, Yang Kai did not know whether her cultivation had fallen after being trapped for tens of thousands of years. Perhaps it had declined somewhat, but those who survived great disasters were bound to find fortune afterward. With her perseverance and determination, recovering should not be too difficult.

Since she spoke so freely, Yang Kai did not act coy. He took out an empty Spatial Ring, filled it with several million Upper-Level Source Crystals and some Spirit Pills, and left it with her.

Sheng Yuzhu thanked him gratefully.

When Yang Kai stepped out the door, he happened to run into Tao Yingruo hurrying over.

"Elder." Tao Yingruo cupped her fist and glanced into the hall. Clearly, she had heard some news and had come to see what was going on.

Yang Kai said, "Acting Sect Master, you came at just the right time. There is something I need to inform you about."

He was just about to explain Sheng Yuzhu's situation and let Tao Yingruo know that Sheng Yuzhu would be recuperating in Luocha Sect from now on, but before he could speak, Sheng Yuzhu's voice transmission sounded beside his ear. "Ask her to come in."

Yang Kai paused, then smiled. "Acting Sect Master, please go in. That Senior seems to have something to say to you."

"Senior..." Tao Yingruo was greatly alarmed. In her eyes, Yang Kai was already a lofty existence, yet he still addressed someone else as Senior. Could the person he had brought back be a Third-Stage Emperor Master?

But according to Ran Yirou and Yu Keren's report, that person seemed to have trouble moving, her aura was unstable, and she was so thin she was practically skin and bones. What virtue or ability did such a person possess to be Yang Kai's Senior?

Though these thoughts ran through her mind, she did not dare be negligent. After apologizing to Yang Kai, she hurried inside.

Yang Kai waited quietly outside.

Ran Yirou and Yu Keren came over, knelt together at his feet, and said softly, "Elder, please forgive us." Both lowered their brows and eyes, looking pitifully obedient.

Yang Kai looked down at them in astonishment. Then he realized what was going on and could not help laughing. These two women were probably afraid he would blame them for tipping off Tao Yingruo. But then again, even without them, Yang Kai would have gone to find Tao Yingruo himself. This was Luocha Sect, after all, and it really would not be proper for him to bring someone back for no reason.

He helped them up and casually comforted them with a few words, finally setting their minds at ease.
