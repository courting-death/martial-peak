## Chapter 3165 — Lend It Whether You Want To or Not

Silence filled the hall, and killing intent seemed to hang in the scorching air.

The guests looked at one another, half convinced their ears had failed them. How else could they have heard such nonsense?

"What did you say?" Chi Huo's face darkened at once.

What an insolent brat! He actually dared say he wanted to borrow Fire Cloud Star's Stellar Origin? Was a Stellar Origin something that could be borrowed just because one asked? The reason he could stand so high above others, the reason even ordinary Third Stage Void Kings had to bow their heads before him, was ultimately because he was a Star Lord.

If he lent out the Stellar Origin, what advantage would he have left? Besides, he had never heard of a Stellar Origin being borrowed away.

Killing intent surged like a tide. If Yang Kai’s attitude and words just now had only made him somewhat displeased, then this sentence had already stirred Chi Huo’s murderous intent. The Stellar Origin could almost be called his reverse scale, and he immediately decided that this brat had come to ruin the occasion. Secretly making up his mind, he resolved to make sure Yang Kai came today but never returned.

"Brother Chi, calm your anger!" Yang Kai raised a hand. "I came here in sincerity. Brother Chi, you may as well hear me out."

Chi Huo laughed despite his fury. "Good. Keep talking." He wanted to see just how silver-tongued this boy could be.

"If Brother Chi can lend out the Stellar Origin, you can keep this Star Domain at peace. That would count as immeasurable merit. Of course, I also know the Origin is of great importance to Brother Chi, so naturally I would not borrow it for nothing. I will certainly give you proper compensation."

Chi Huo froze for a moment, then burst into loud laughter. "Compensation!"

He already stood at the very peak of the Star Domain. What in this whole world could possibly be worth his notice? This brat's wild boasting was truly irritating. Could one of Chi Huo's enemies have sent him here just to make a fool of him?

"Compensation!" Yang Kai said solemnly. With a turn of his palm, a mass of brilliant, many-colored energy appeared.

"That is..." Chi Huo's expression sharpened, and he suddenly rose from his chair, staring at the mass of light in disbelief. The other guests also lost themselves for an instant, their faces filling with shock and greed.

"This is also a Stellar Origin, but it has no corresponding Cultivation Star," Yang Kai explained. "With this as compensation, though, I believe Brother Chi's cultivation will not be affected too greatly. Mm, once the matter in this Star Domain is settled, I will also return Fire Cloud Star's Origin Power to Brother Chi. This way, Brother Chi will have gained a Stellar Origin for free."

"Which star's Origin is this?" Chi Huo murmured.

Yang Kai spread his hands. "I already said this Origin has no corresponding Cultivation Star."

He still had quite a few Origins like this in his possession. He had obtained them all from the Shattered Star Sea back then. He had given some away, but he still had extras on hand.

Chi Huo's expression shifted, but not because Yang Kai's proposal tempted him. Even if Yang Kai really did compensate him with a new Origin, without a corresponding Cultivation Star, his status as a Star Lord would exist in name only. How could that compare to the carefree pleasure of being Fire Cloud Star's Star Lord?

He had simply thought of killing Yang Kai and seizing the treasure.

Yet the moment the thought appeared, a powerful warning suddenly rose in his heart, as if cautioning him that if he truly attacked the young man before him, eight or nine times out of ten he would come to no good end.

The hot blood surging through him cooled in an instant, and his mind cleared as well.

He sat back down and shook his head firmly. "This Lord will not lend Fire Cloud Star's Origin. Please leave."

Inwardly, he sneered. This boy clearly did not understand the principle that wealth should not be flaunted. Chi Huo could indeed suppress his greed and refrain from attacking him, but the moment the boy left Flame Palace, someone else would make a move. Then he could conveniently see what the boy was made of, and it would not be too late to decide whether to act.

The radiance faded, and Yang Kai put away the Origin. Rubbing his forehead, he said with a slight headache, "Ai, speaking nicely really does not work after all."

He had wanted to try courtesy before force. Who knew he would still have to make a proper fight of it?

Turning around, he seized Cui Mengyuan by the collar and shouted in a low voice, "Boy, what grievance do you have? Speak quickly. This Young Master will stand up for you today!"

"Ah?" Cui Mengyuan started in fright. Ever since he had followed Yang Kai into Flame Palace, his eyes had never left the bride. Only when Yang Kai took out the Stellar Origin had he been dazed for a moment, but he had quickly fixed his attention on the bride again. Now that Yang Kai had suddenly questioned him like this, he naturally had no idea what was going on.

"Ah what? I heard everything you said just now. Do you know the bride?"

Yang Kai was practically spitting in his face. Cui Mengyuan nodded foolishly. That was why he had braved death to charge into Flame Mountain in the first place.

"Are you and the bride in love with each other? Have you pledged yourselves to each other for life?"

Cui Mengyuan nodded again, his eyes filled with humiliation and unwillingness.

"Did this old bastard bully the weak, abuse his power, and snatch your woman without any regard for your feelings?"

"Yes!" Cui Mengyuan roared, fists clenched tight, his teeth nearly biting out blood.

"Good!" Yang Kai shouted. He tossed Cui Mengyuan aside, turned to look at Chi Huo, and said slowly, "I have felt your resolve. Today, this Young Master will help you snatch the bride back!"

Cui Mengyuan was both shocked and delighted. He had never imagined such a dramatic turn of events. He had come alone into Flame Palace today already resolved to die, yet who would have thought someone would actually stand up for him?

Soon, however, he seemed to realize something and said bleakly, "My Lord, many thanks for your kindness, but..."

"Shut your mouth!"

Cui Mengyuan immediately shut his mouth and dared not speak again.

In the main hall, Chi Huo lowered his eyes, his expression gloomy, and said faintly, "Right and wrong arise from loose tongues, and trouble comes from forcing oneself to stand out. Junior, this Lord has given you some face. Do not take yourself too seriously."

This fellow was simply making trouble for no reason. Sure enough, he had not come with good intentions.

Yang Kai laughed loudly, pointed at Chi Huo like a halberd, and shouted, "Old bastard, the Star Domain is engulfed in war and foreign enemies have invaded, yet not only do you refuse to lend a hand, you even sealed off Fire Cloud Star. You are simply unreasonable. Today, this Young Master came here for the sake of all living beings. If you cooperate, then fine. Since you refuse, do not blame this Young Master for showing no mercy." In that moment, he looked exactly like some great villain standing on the moral high ground and preparing to burn, kill, and plunder, immediately drawing curses from the surrounding guests.

Chi Huo's red hair flew up, and his robes snapped in the air. "Outrageous!"

Yang Kai said, "You will lend the Stellar Origin whether you want to or not!"

As the words fell, he hurled himself toward Chi Huo.

"Insolent!" Chi Huo flew into a rage. With a single thought, the might of heaven and earth descended in dreadful majesty. An invisible pressure fell from the sky, making everyone present feel their breath catch, as if a great mountain had been pressed against their chests.

Yang Kai, who bore the brunt of it, stalled at once and abruptly appeared. His body bent slightly, as if he might be crushed flat at any moment.

This was the might of a Star Lord. Upon this star, he could rule over all things. With a single thought, he could kill without leaving a trace.

The guests erupted in cheers, clapping in delight.

Chi Huo's expression, however, turned extremely solemn. Instead of joy, he felt alarm, because Yang Kai's performance had exceeded his expectations.

This brat had actually not been crushed flat! Sure enough, he was no ordinary man.

Chi Huo's thought moved again, and the pressure suddenly increased. Yang Kai gave a muffled groan as the floor beneath his feet cracked with a boom, yet he only sneered. "You have the power of a star. Do I not have one as well?"

In the distant Starry Sky, a star flickered, and a ray of light shot over, crossing hundreds of millions of li in the blink of an eye before falling upon Yang Kai. Light blazed all around him, so dazzling that no one could keep their eyes open.

Although this place was not Dark Star and was not Yang Kai's home ground, it was still within the same Star Domain after all. Naturally, Yang Kai could draw Dark Star's power to himself and use it to resist Fire Cloud Star's World Laws.

In an instant, the pressure eased.

And it was not over yet. Across the boundless Star Domain, stretch after stretch of Starry Sky also gathered mighty power, resonating strangely with Yang Kai and becoming a source of his strength.

These stretches of Starry Sky were precisely the regions he had spent two years refining. Although the power gathered from this vast Starry Sky was not as pure as Dark Star's stellar power, it had quantity on its side, and that naturally could bring about unimaginable changes.

"Break!" Yang Kai shouted.

Crack...

A sound rang out as if something had shattered.

Yang Kai's hunched figure straightened in an instant, tall and rigid as a spear.

Disbelief flashed through Chi Huo's eyes, and he rose from his chair.

Before he could turn another thought over in his mind, a figure had already appeared in front of him, and a large hand like an iron hoop clamped around his neck. The power in that hand was so great that he could not even muster the will to resist.

An invisible imprisoning force enveloped his entire body, severing his connection with Fire Cloud Star in that instant. Not only that, even the Stellar Origin he had refined into his body was suppressed.

His eyes trembled violently. What kind of terrifying power was this? Who was this man?

Clink, clatter...

All around, wine cups fell to the floor. The guests stared with mouths agape, as if trapped in a dream, their eyes filled with nothing but shock.

Star Lord Chi Huo had actually been lifted by one hand by a mere boy, and from beginning to end, he seemed not to have had any time to react.

The clash between them had lasted only an instant, so fast it dazzled the eyes. Many people had not even understood what had happened before victory and defeat were already decided.

Such a scene could only mean one thing: one person's strength far exceeded the other's. Only then could such an instant defeat occur.

But...

Chi Huo was already a Star Lord and already a Third Stage Void King. Who could surpass him by that much? Their minds could barely keep up. This scene would surely shock everyone present for the rest of their lives.

"Ah?" Cui Mengyuan cried out, his gaze blank. The surprise had come so quickly that he had not had time to prepare himself.

The bride also covered her red lips with one small hand, her beautiful eyes lifting in shock to gaze up at Yang Kai. She only felt that this figure towered like a mountain. Though he was clearly close at hand, she could not see his face clearly, as if he stood far beyond the Nine Heavens.

Yang Kai turned his head, grinned at her, and said, "Go quickly. Fly far away with that boy."

As he spoke, he waved his hand. Without caring whether she was willing or not, he directly threw her and Cui Mengyuan beyond the three-thousand-li Flame Mountain.

Mm, he supposed she was willing. After all, when Cui Mengyuan appeared in the main hall, she had shown an extremely worried expression.
