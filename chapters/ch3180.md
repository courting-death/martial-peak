## Chapter 3180 — Black Ball

The Xuan Realm Pearl, now acting as the carrier for the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, glowed pitch-black across its surface, as though the entire pearl had turned into a black hole. When the black hole first appeared, it was only the size of a dragon's eye, but it expanded rapidly as it fell.

By the time it struck the ground, it was already about one li across.

This was the limit Yang Kai could currently achieve, barely enough to count as the Small Xuan Realm's devouring Domain.

Without a sound, an enormous hole melted into the ground. Grass and trees vanished without a trace, a deep pit opened in the earth, and everything was swallowed into the Small Xuan Realm.

Only after it bored a hundred li straight underground did Yang Kai stir his thoughts.

The Xuan Realm Pearl rose unhurriedly, flew out of the ground, and sped into the distance while skimming the surface.

At a glance, the black hole, a full li across, seemed to roam over the land like a living creature. Wherever it passed, everything disappeared.

In the blink of an eye, a long rift appeared beneath the spirit peak. The black hole crashed into the belly of a nearby mountain and instantly pierced straight through it, melting out a great tunnel through which the scenery on the far side could be seen.

The mountain shook violently, and a figure shot out from its belly, covered in dust as he bellowed, "Who is it? Get out here!"

This man had been meditating inside his own cave residence when the disturbance from the devouring suddenly woke him. Thinking a powerful enemy had invaded, he immediately rushed out to investigate. His cultivation was not especially high, only at the Second Stage of the Origin Returning Realm, but on the present Yellow Springs Star, that was already quite respectable. He rarely met anyone he could not handle, so his words carried plenty of confidence.

The next moment, however, his eyes went wide, and he stared foolishly at the black hole roaming over the earth below. A chill swept from his head to the soles of his feet, and he could not help shivering.

What the hell was that thing? How could it have such terrifying power? He watched with his own eyes as everything in the black hole's path turned to nothingness, as if even space itself had been devoured clean.

Figures shot out from the surrounding peaks, clearly having been disturbed from their cultivation just as he had. They soon discovered the Xuan Realm Pearl and watched in stunned silence. Their Divine Senses exchanged back and forth, but none of them could make sense of it.

They had all cultivated for many years and were not lacking in experience, but what they were seeing and hearing now still went far beyond anything they could imagine.

"There's someone over there. Do any of you recognize him?" the first cultivator to appear suddenly asked after spotting Yang Kai and pointing toward him.

Everyone looked over, then shook their heads one after another.

"Is he the one causing trouble here?"

"Who is he? He looks unfamiliar."

"Could he be an Elder from Netherworld Sect?"

"Should we go ask?"

"You go if you want. That fellow does not look easy to provoke."

After some pushing and shoving, the task of speaking with Yang Kai still fell to the first man who had appeared. He flew cautiously toward Yang Kai, cupped his fists from afar, and said, "This one is Wu Zimin. Greetings, Sir. May I ask Sir's honored name?"

Yang Kai raised his eyes and glanced at him. "What is it?"

Wu Zimin's heart was uneasy. Although he was terrified, he still had some courage and said, "May I ask, Sir, have we offended Sir in some way?"

Yang Kai propped his cheek in one hand and replied leisurely, "You have not."

Wu Zimin's face fell. "Since we have not offended you, why is Sir doing this?"

"Is there a problem?"

Wu Zimin said, "My cave residence and those of my friends are right here. With Sir using such methods, how could our cave residences remain safe? If this continues, even this spirit mountain will be lost."

Before he finished speaking, a miserable howl came from nearby. Someone's cave residence had already been devoured, leaving his face as bitter as if he had swallowed gall, while the mountain peak that held the cave residence also began to collapse.

Yang Kai curled his lip. "What does that have to do with me?"

Wu Zimin pleaded, "Sir, please withdraw your Divine Ability at once."

Yang Kai shook his head. "No! Not only am I going to eat this spirit mountain, I am going to eat the whole of Yellow Springs Star."

"Ah!" Wu Zimin's mouth fell open, his face blank.

"If there is nothing else, hurry up and get lost. I have not felt like killing people lately." Yang Kai waved a hand, shooing him away like a fly.

Wu Zimin's face shifted between green and red, and after a moment's hesitation, he asked, "Sir is not from Netherworld Sect?"

"You're the one from Netherworld Sect. Your whole family is from Netherworld Sect."

Wu Zimin said, "Since Sir knows this star is called Yellow Springs Star, do you know how powerful Netherworld Sect is?" He was still hoping to use Netherworld Sect's prestige to intimidate Yang Kai and make him retreat in the face of difficulty.

Yang Kai laughed loudly. "Netherworld Sect has already been destroyed by me."

"Impossible!"

"Go, go, hurry up, and stop making noise!" Yang Kai waved his hand, and Wu Zimin felt a fierce wind blow against his face, knocking him off balance and forcing him back more than a hundred zhang.

Hong long long...

Another mountain peak collapsed. The black hole had devoured its foundation, leaving it without support.

Wu Zimin fixed his eyes on it, and his heart bled. That spirit peak was precisely where his own cave residence stood. Now that the spirit peak had collapsed, his cave residence was surely gone as well. He turned and glared at Yang Kai, furious, yet he dared not speak.

"What do we do?" The several cultivators had never seen anything like this and immediately lost their heads.

Wu Zimin pondered for a moment, then made a swift decision. "This man is powerful, and we are no match for him. Hurry to Netherworld Sect and report this matter." When the sky fell, the tall ones would hold it up, and Netherworld Sect was the tallest existence on Yellow Springs Star.

Everyone found this reasonable and immediately sped off with Wu Zimin toward Netherworld Sect.

After driving away a few flies, Yang Kai continued urging the Xuan Realm Pearl to devour. Wherever the black hole passed, it swept through all obstacles, unstoppable. In only a single day, the entire chain of spirit mountains was devoured clean, leaving only a huge basin behind. Anyone who saw it would likely not dare believe their own eyes.

Once cultivators reached a certain level, they could indeed move mountains and overturn seas, but even then they would have to display an overwhelmingly powerful Divine Ability merely to accomplish it. Who could make a spirit mountain disappear silently within a single day?

After devouring such a spirit mountain, Yang Kai clearly felt that the black hole formed by the Small Xuan Realm had expanded somewhat. In other words, its devouring Domain had grown larger.

A larger devouring Domain meant greater devouring efficiency.

This one-day experiment verified the conjecture in his heart, and from that point on, he hesitated no longer. With a leap, he sped into the distance, while below him, the black hole formed by the Small Xuan Realm rolled over the ground like a black ball, plowing a deep trench through the earth. What was missing from that trench was not only Netherworld Sect's land, but also Laws and Spiritual Qi, and such a trench could never recover.

Above the wilderness, a Warship came toward him head-on. A conspicuous skull symbol marked the Warship as one belonging to Netherworld Sect, though this Warship was not of especially high grade, only Origin-Grade.

In order to invade Hengluo Star Domain, Netherworld Sect had practically come out in full force. From the Sect Master down to the disciples, more than ninety percent of its people had left at once, leaving only ten percent behind on Yellow Springs Star to guard the Sect.

A few days earlier, Wu Zimin and the others had gone to Netherworld Sect and, after great difficulty, managed to meet one of its stewards. They reported what they had seen, only for that steward to dismiss the matter and suppress it without reporting it upward.

Over the last few days, however, more and more people came to Netherworld Sect with reports that a terrifying fellow had appeared on Yellow Springs Star. They said this man drove a black ball that devoured the land wherever it went, and wherever it passed, whether wasteland or high mountain, desert or snowy field, even cities and Sects, all were devoured clean by the black ball, and all things ceased to exist.

Only then did Netherworld Sect realize that something was terribly wrong. The Elder left behind to hold the fort immediately gathered his men and prepared to meet that evildoer.

The Warship had traveled less than a thousand li from Netherworld Sect when a rumbling sound suddenly came from ahead, like muffled thunder rolling over the earth.

"What happened?" Inside the Warship, an aged old man asked. He was the Elder left behind to guard Netherworld Sect.

A disciple immediately projected an image of the outside world inside the ship.

A chilling scene appeared before them.

In the far distance, a young man was speeding toward them, and a thousand zhang below him, a huge black ball moved with him. The black ball rolled across the earth, leaving behind an indelible mark that stretched for who knew how many li. From afar, it looked as though the earth itself had been wounded.

"It's him!" Wu Zimin cried out in horror. It had only been a few days, so how could he have forgotten this man's face? Only now did he understand that when Yang Kai said he would eat the whole of Yellow Springs Star, he had not been making empty threats.

"This is the black ball that devours all things!" The Elder's eyes also widened. Although he had received many reports over the past few days, without seeing it with his own eyes, it was hard to imagine that such a bizarre thing could exist in this world.

The reports had not been wrong. That black ball was truly terrifying; everything enveloped by its darkness was devoured clean.

"Not good, he's coming toward us!" a disciple shouted in terror.

"Dodge!" the Elder barked. Although he had not truly exchanged blows with Yang Kai and had only seen an image, he knew he absolutely could not be Yang Kai's opponent. Leaving aside the difference in their cultivations, that black ball alone gave the Elder an extremely uneasy feeling.

If that black hole touched him, he feared that even he would not escape being devoured.

"He's too fast, we can't dodge!" The disciple's voice already carried a sob.

As they spoke, a figure brushed past the Warship and sped away like wind and lightning, not even glancing at the Warship, as if it were nothing.

The black ball also rolled past beneath the Warship.

Before anyone had time to celebrate, the earth shook violently behind them, as if something had collapsed.

The Elder turned back and cried out in grief, "No!"

They were only a thousand li from Netherworld Sect. Although the Warship had luckily avoided being devoured by the black ball, Netherworld Sect itself, standing on the earth, had not escaped. They watched the black ball roll through Netherworld Sect, splitting the enormous Sect in two at once. A huge chunk seemed to vanish from the middle, and the so-called Grand Guardian Array could not withstand it at all, collapsing in less than a breath.

"Oh, this is Netherworld Sect." Yang Kai glanced back. "Then I really should take good care of it!"

Thus, the people inside the Warship soon witnessed an even more horrifying sight. The black ball actually turned back, then rolled through Netherworld Sect several more times.

A few breaths later, Netherworld Sect... was gone!
