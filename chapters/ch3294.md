## Chapter 3294 — Heavenly Wolf Valley's Lan He

There were two islands in the East Sea: Dragon Isle and Spirit Beast Island. Both were elusive and untraceable. Without the right opportunity, an ordinary person could wander the sea for a lifetime and still never glimpse the slightest clue to their whereabouts.

The world believed these two terrifying forces truly were two islands, but Yang Kai knew Dragon Isle was actually a Small World, its entrance hidden beneath the sea somewhere in those waters. If Li Jiao had not led the way last time, Yang Kai likely would not have found the way in either.

The trip to Dragon Isle had been fraught with danger, and Yang Kai had no idea what sort of treatment awaited him if he entered Dragon Isle again. He might even be treated as an enemy, so he had to make a trip to Spirit Beast Island first.

What left him speechless was that after passing through several towns and asking around again and again, not a single person knew where Spirit Beast Island was.

He had come all the way east to the seaside, yet still had nothing to show for it.

A city stood by the sea, guarding ten thousand li of frontier, towering and magnificent. Cultivators came and went inside the city in an endless stream.

In a restaurant within the city, Yang Kai sat alone beside a third-floor window, drinking by himself. He could not help feeling worried, unsure who he should ask about Spirit Beast Island's location.

Suddenly, a fragrant breeze drifted over, and someone sat down opposite him.

Yang Kai frowned and turned to look, only for a smiling face to enter his view. Her smile was as lovely as a flower, her skin snow-white, her looks outstanding. She was clearly an exceptionally beautiful woman. She wore blue robes that set off her graceful, exquisite figure, her full curves trembling faintly.

Their eyes met, and Yang Kai's expression brightened as he smiled. "You are..."

"Brother Yang, you really are hard to find," the woman across from him said with a soft smile.

"Lady Lan He!" Yang Kai finally remembered the woman's name, and an unexpected joy rose in him, like meeting an old friend in a foreign land. He blurted out, "Why are you here?"

The words had just left his mouth when he slapped his forehead in annoyance. "Right, you were already in the Eastern Territory."

Lan He. If she had not suddenly appeared before him, Yang Kai would almost have forgotten her. He did not know many people in the Eastern Territory. Over the years, he had mainly moved about in the Southern Territory and Northern Territory, and had only dealt with young talents from other Territories in the Shattered Star Sea.

He had naturally met Lan He in the Shattered Star Sea as well. She came from Heavenly Wolf Valley in the Eastern Territory. Back then, in the Shattered Star Sea, she had received some kindness from Yang Kai. The two of them had been stranded together inside a Void Crack, and Yang Kai had been the one to send her out. Otherwise, with her cultivation at the time, she would definitely have been lost inside the Void Crack, never to find her way out. Later, Yang Kai had also entrusted her with asking after another woman, and if possible, looking after her a little.

That woman was Ling Yinqin, who had left the Jixu Sea Secret Realm together with Yang Kai.

The Jixu Sea Secret Realm was that damned place where Bing Yun had been trapped for many years. Ling Yinqin had met a man there, come to know him, fallen in love, and stayed by his side. Unfortunately, her husband had died in an accident. That man was from Heavenly Wolf Valley in the Eastern Territory, the same force as Lan He. After Ling Yinqin escaped the Jixu Sea, she did not leave together with Yang Kai and Bing Yun. Instead, she went to Heavenly Wolf Valley in the Eastern Territory so her husband's ashes could be laid to rest.

Those events were already many years in the past, and many details had blurred for Yang Kai. Still, Big Sister Ling Yinqin had been a warm-hearted and righteous person. When Yang Kai and Liu Qianyun drifted into the Jixu Sea Secret Realm, the first person they encountered was Ling Yinqin. At the time, she had even given him a high-grade Purification Array, an extremely generous gift for Ling Yinqin back then.

After leaving the Jixu Sea, Yang Kai had never seen Ling Yinqin again. They were simply too far apart. Later, when he ran into Lan He of Heavenly Wolf Valley in the Shattered Star Sea, he had casually asked her to help. Now, he did not even know whether Ling Yinqin had ever gone to Heavenly Wolf Valley.

Yang Kai had never expected that, in this vast world, he would actually have the chance to meet Lan He again. He smiled faintly and said, "Truly, one may meet an old acquaintance anywhere under Heaven. Lady Lan, it has been a long time."

To his surprise, Lan He slowly shook her head. "I came specifically to find you."

"Specifically?" Yang Kai raised his brow, then stared in astonishment. "How did you know I was in the Eastern Territory?"

Lan He said, "I happened to hear some news. I heard people mention your name, and I also heard that you had been asking about Spirit Beast Island, so I guessed you would come to the seaside. I waited here, and it seems I guessed right. You really did come."

Yang Kai blinked. "You happened to hear some news, and even my name? That really is interesting. Where did Lady Lan hear this?" Lan He's words carried a piece of information Yang Kai could not ignore. He had come to the Eastern Territory, and someone was already asking about him?

Lan He gave him an annoyed look. "Do you truly not know, or are you only pretending?"

Yang Kai smiled. "If I knew, why would I need to ask you?"

Lan He looked him up and down, then nodded. "Then let me ask you this. Do you know how dangerous your situation is right now?"

Yang Kai asked curiously, "My situation is dangerous? What do you mean?"

Lan He sighed. "I knew you had no idea. Put it this way, you still remember Brahma Heaven Sacred Land, don't you?" When she saw Yang Kai's blank expression, she was at once angry and amused. "You killed two of their Holy Sons in the Shattered Star Sea. Don't tell me you've forgotten. Those two were called Chang Hao and Chang Xian. They were Senior and Junior Brothers, and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land had placed great hopes on both of them."

Yang Kai rubbed his chin. "Mm, now that you mention it, I seem to recall something."

The corner of Lan He's mouth twitched. How could he not even remember the people he had killed? If Chang Hao and Chang Xian knew he had said such a thing, would they not be furious enough to die all over again?

Yang Kai grinned. "I killed too many people in the Shattered Star Sea. Who cares about Chang Hao or Chang Xian?" This was the honest truth. Quite a few young talents had died by his hand in the Shattered Star Sea, each with a formidable background. Who would keep remembering the names of the dead?

After a moment's thought, Yang Kai said, "Lady Lan means Brahma Heaven Sacred Land wants to cause trouble for me?"

Lan He's expression turned solemn. "It is not only Brahma Heaven Sacred Land. Let me ask you this: what grudge do you have with Netherworld Sect? They have moved this time as well."

Netherworld Sect... tsk!

Yang Kai felt a wave of distaste as soon as he heard those three words. His grudges with Netherworld Sect could not be explained in a few sentences. First, many years ago, Yin Lesheng had ambushed him, Chi Yue, and the others in the Starlight Passage. Later, that fellow had made trouble for him at every turn. In the Shattered Star Sea, Yang Kai had made a deal with him to ask about Xiaoxiao's whereabouts, sparing his miserable life in the process. It was from Yin Lesheng that Yang Kai learned about Xiaoxiao, which was why he later went to the Wild Ancient Land. After that, Yin Lesheng chose his moment and brought in a Second Stage Emperor Realm Master from Netherworld Sect to set an ambush outside the Ancient Land passage. Even Yao Lin, Nether Soul Great Emperor's daughter, was there. Yet Yang Kai had broken through it by himself, and Yin Lesheng was killed at that time as well.

Yin Lesheng had been the former Sect Master of Netherworld Sect in the Great Wilderness Star Domain. Through his own ability, he broke through his shackles and entered the Star Realm, where he was recognized as belonging to the same lineage as the Star Realm's Netherworld Sect. Great expectations had been placed on him, and Netherworld Sect's Sect Master, Fu Bo, had even personally accepted him as a disciple. Yang Kai had killed Yin Lesheng and a Second Stage Emperor Realm Master from Netherworld Sect, so the enmity between them was anything but small.

Added to that, he had wiped out Netherworld Sect in the Great Wilderness Star Domain. This hatred could almost be called irreconcilable. Either they died, or he did.

Lan He smiled bitterly. "Brother Yang, you really are something. Of the Eastern Territory's three top forces, you actually managed to provoke two."

If an ordinary person provoked such enemies, how could they still dare show their face in the Eastern Territory? Strictly speaking, Yang Kai was not from the Eastern Territory, so although Brahma Heaven Sacred Land and Netherworld Sect had always wanted to take revenge on him and wash away their hatred, they had suffered for lack of an opportunity. This time, he had appeared in the Eastern Territory of his own accord. How could these two forces restrain themselves? If they missed this chance, they would probably find it difficult to track him down again.

Therefore, as soon as they received the news, the two great forces had almost poured out in full force, determined to kill Yang Kai in the Eastern Territory and preserve their own prestige.

Yang Kai grinned. "If others do not offend me, I do not offend them. Of the Eastern Territory's three top forces, who is the other one? Heavenly Wolf Valley?"

Lan He shook her head. "My Heavenly Wolf Valley only has two Second Stage Emperor Realm Masters presiding over it. How could it be considered a top force? The other one is Ahan Palace." After saying this, she looked at Yang Kai in alarm. "Do not tell me Ahan Palace has a grudge with you too. I heard that fellow Red Ghost is also searching everywhere for traces of you."

Yang Kai looked at her. "Do I look like someone who enjoys stirring up trouble? Besides, what the hell is Red Ghost?"

Lan He burst out laughing. "Red Ghost is not a ghost. He is Ahan Palace's strongest rising star. He advanced to the Emperor Realm in the Shattered Star Sea, so you should have met him before. A fellow with a head full of red hair and a very violent temper."

Yang Kai thought for a moment and vaguely remembered meeting someone like that. A head full of fiery red hair was far too obvious a feature. He shook his head. "I have no grudge with him."

Lan He said, "Then he probably wants to spar with you. That fellow is warlike by nature. In the past, he often sought out Yin Lesheng, Chang Hao, and Chang Xian for fights. You killed all three of them, so he has no opponents left in the Eastern Territory. He likely wants to prove something through you."

Yang Kai snorted. "Then he has chosen the wrong target."

Lan He said, "Enough idle talk. Brother Yang, this place is not safe to linger in. The Eastern Territory's two top Sects have joined hands and have been asking about your whereabouts all along. Fortunately, you moved quickly and did not stay long anywhere, but news that you entered this city will certainly spread as well. If you do not leave now, then once those people arrive, you will not be able to leave even if you want to."

Yang Kai let out a long sigh. "There would be no trouble in the world if fools did not make trouble for themselves."

Lan He said angrily, "How can you still say that at a time like this? Hurry and come with me."

Yang Kai asked curiously, "Come with you? Where?"

Lan He said as if it were obvious, "Heavenly Wolf Valley. Where else? I will take you back there first so you can avoid the storm."

Yang Kai was startled for a moment, then smiled. "Lady Lan, this Yang appreciates your kindness, but your Heavenly Wolf Valley has to make its living in the Eastern Territory. It would be best not to invite trouble on yourselves because of me." Those were, after all, two top forces. Heavenly Wolf Valley definitely could not withstand them.

After he finished speaking, his brow furrowed. He turned to the side and met the eyes of a woman who had just reached the third floor. She hurriedly looked away and walked straight to another table to sit down.
