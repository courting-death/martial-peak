## Chapter 3273 — The Dead Are Gone

At the mountain gate of Wuhua Hall, Yang Kai came walking over and went straight to a small earthen slope. He lowered his head to look at the cold corpse, a flicker of grief passing through his eyes. The body was wrapped in a layer of ice, so although several days had passed, it showed no signs of decay, nor had it been gnawed by wild beasts.

The fatal wound was in her chest, which had been pierced through by a sharp weapon such as a saber or sword. There was only that one wound, so it was clear she had not suffered much before she died. Yet her wide-open eyes were filled with fear and helplessness.

"Palace Master!" Hua Qingsi stepped forward and called softly.

"Why are you here?" Yang Kai asked as he crouched down. He brushed his broad hand over the cold corpse, and the solid ice immediately melted away. Then he gently closed her eyes and smoothed the loose strands of hair from her forehead.

"Elder Ji told me to keep watch here," Hua Qingsi replied. She quietly studied Yang Kai's expression and knew Ji Yao had been right. Palace Master really had known this dead woman. She did not know what their relationship was, but the guilt and grief on Yang Kai's face made it seem that they had not been mere acquaintances. Still, it had not reached the point of affection between a man and a woman.

Yang Kai nodded. Earlier, when Ji Yao had followed him in breaking through Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array and charging inside, he had noticed her use a Secret Technique to seal Yu Zhuo's corpse in ice. But the great battle had been about to begin, and he had simply had no time to linger. Only now, after everything had been settled, did he have time to come and look.

The dead were gone, yet her voice, face, and smile kept rising before his eyes. If she had not died here, then judging by Yu Zhuo's performance at the Martial Gathering, she would have had great hope of advancing to the Emperor Realm in the future. But the path of Martial Dao meant cutting through thorns and brambles, and the slightest carelessness could end with one's soul scattered to nothing.

In truth, he could not claim to have had any deep friendship with her. This time, twenty or thirty thousand people of the Southern Territory had been killed or injured, and even Qingyang Shrine had lost more than a third of its disciples. Yet the only one who filled Yang Kai with regret and heartache was her.

It was a truly inexplicable feeling. Looking at the person before him, Yang Kai could not help recalling the scene from that day after he had refused to see her, when she had suddenly turned back and waved hard at him. No one had expected that brief turn of hers to be their final farewell.

"Palace Master, those people over there are her fellow disciples. They want to lay her body to rest," Hua Qingsi said after waiting for quite a while. Seeing that Yang Kai still had no reaction, she finally spoke to remind him.

Yang Kai looked up and saw a dozen or so people standing fearfully not far away. At their head was a young married woman who looked just over thirty. Behind her stood a group of women, each of them looking bleak and dispirited, while several of the younger ones were quietly sobbing.

Yang Kai nodded. "Please have them come over."

"Yes," Hua Qingsi answered, then walked toward them. She said a few words to the women, and a moment later, brought the dozen or so people over.

"Qin Pei of Luocha Sect greets Elder Yang." The young married woman saluted. Her eyes swept over Yu Zhuo's corpse, and they immediately reddened. In the end, because Yang Kai was right in front of her, she kept herself from crying.

Yang Kai looked over the group and found that Qin Pei had the highest cultivation among them, yet she was only Second Stage Dao Origin Realm. Of the others, only one was First Stage Dao Origin Realm, while the rest were Void King Realm and Origin Returning Realm.

"You are Yu Zhuo's fellow disciples?" Yang Kai asked.

Qin Pei nodded. "Yes. I am Yu Zhuo's Senior Sister."

"Where is your Sect Master?" Yang Kai looked at her. Although Luocha Sect was not considered a particularly powerful Sect in the Southern Territory, he remembered that they had an Emperor Realm Sect Master, and this time, it was precisely their Sect Master who had led the team here to participate in the Martial Gathering.

Qin Pei lowered her head and said in a choked voice, "Sect Master is gone as well."

As soon as she said this, the women behind her who had been sobbing could no longer hold back and all began to weep. Luocha Sect was an all-female Sect, and such Sects always drew attention. Inevitably, some lustful men would set their sights on them. In the past, with an Emperor Realm Sect Master overseeing things, along with marriage ties to the surrounding Sects and families, they had still been fairly stable. Now, however, their Sect Master had died tragically, and the Dao Origin Realm cultivators who had come to the Martial Gathering had been reduced to only two survivors. Their very foundation had been wounded. It would be difficult for Luocha Sect to gain a foothold in the Southern Territory again. Once the news spread, the fate of Luocha Sect's many female disciples would be worrying indeed. Thinking of the future and grieving over the present, how could this group of women hold back their sorrow?

Yang Kai fell silent.

Qin Pei took a cloth bag from her Spatial Ring and offered it to Yang Kai.

"This is..." Yang Kai looked at her in confusion.

Qin Pei wiped away her tears and forced a smile. "Junior Sister Yu Zhuo asked me a few days ago to keep an eye out and collect some seeds. She probably meant to give them to you. I gathered them a little late and had not had time to hand them to her yet."

Yang Kai pursed his lips and reached out to take the cloth bag, yet this small bag felt unbearably heavy in his hand.

Qin Pei went on, "Junior Sister Yu Zhuo admired Elder Yang very much. Some time ago..." As she spoke, she found herself unable to continue. Covering her mouth, she wept softly, and overcome with grief, the dozen or so women behind her also held one another and broke down crying.

Hua Qingsi's heart ached at the sight, and her eyes reddened slightly.

Only after a long while did Qin Pei calm herself. She took a deep breath, smiled, and said, "For Junior Sister Yu Zhuo to have known Elder Yang before her death was truly her good fortune."

Yang Kai’s grip on the cloth bag tightened slightly, and his chest felt unbearably stifled.

Qin Pei said, "If Elder Yang does not mind, I would like to take Junior Sister's body back to the Sect for burial."

Yang Kai nodded lightly, unable to say anything in reply.

Only then did Qin Pei crouch down and gently stroke Yu Zhuo's head. After that, she stored Yu Zhuo's corpse in her Spatial Ring.

"Many thanks, Elder Yang!" Qin Pei gave a curtsy and was about to leave with her dozen or so fellow disciples.

"Wait a moment." Yang Kai raised a hand and called her back.

Qin Pei turned around. "Does Elder Yang have any other instructions?"

"What plans do you have from now on?" Yang Kai asked, looking at her.

A trace of confusion passed through Qin Pei's eyes, and she shook her head. Over the past few days, she had also thought about this question. The Sect Master was dead, so where were Luocha Sect's thousand-plus disciples supposed to go? Now the strongest person left in the Sect was only a Third Stage Dao Origin Realm Elder who had stayed behind. Such strength was nowhere near enough to protect Luocha Sect's foundation. Luocha Sect did have some Sects and families with whom it had long maintained marriage ties, but without the deterrence of an Emperor Realm Master, those marriage partners were likely more eager than anyone to swallow Luocha Sect whole. How could they possibly offer protection?

Luocha Sect's future could already be foreseen unless they could secure a powerful outside ally. But without knowing such an ally inside and out, even bringing one in might amount to inviting a wolf into the house. All these misgivings left Luocha Sect's future bleak.

"Do you want to rebuild your Sect, or join another force?" Yang Kai asked. "If it is the latter, I can make some introductions for you. The Southern Territory or the Northern Territory would both be fine. Your choices in the Southern Territory are limited, but in the Northern Territory, you can pick any Sect you like. Whichever one you want to enter will be fine."

Qin Pei's eyes lit up when she heard this. "Elder Yang is willing to help my Luocha Sect?"

Yang Kai said, "Yu Zhuo and I... could be considered friends. Although she is gone, now that her Sect is in trouble, I cannot simply stand by and watch."

With this confirmation, Qin Pei could hardly contain her joy, while the dozen or so Luocha Sect disciples all stared at Yang Kai in disbelief. In their eyes, Emperor Realm Masters had always stood far above others. Those who had not reached the Emperor Realm were simply not qualified to speak with them as equals. That was why they had remained standing far away and had not dared to approach.

After all, Hua Qingsi, who had been guarding Yu Zhuo's corpse, was an Emperor Realm Master.

Although their Sect Master had also been fairly kind and approachable while alive, the majesty of an Emperor Realm Master was still there, and no one dared act presumptuously in front of her.

If even a First Stage Emperor Realm Master was like that, there was no need to mention Second Stage or Third Stage Masters. This Elder Yang was also an Elder of Qingyang Shrine, giving him far greater weight and status than an ordinary Emperor Realm Master. For that reason, Qin Pei had been rather cautious when speaking with Yang Kai just now, but who could have known he would be so approachable, even saying that he and Yu Zhuo had been friends?

The word friends carried far too much weight. Which Emperor Realm Master would be willing to befriend a Dao Origin Realm cultivator? Even if such people existed, they were exceedingly rare. If Yu Zhuo could hear these words, who knew how happy she would be?

As soon as she thought of Yu Zhuo, sorrow filled Qin Pei's expression again, and she said firmly, "We want to rebuild the Sect!"

Luocha Sect was the place where they had grown up, the home of this group of women. If it could be rebuilt, who would willingly abandon it and live under another's roof?

The women behind her all nodded hard as well.

"I see..." Yang Kai pondered for a moment, then asked, "Has your Luocha Sect ever hired a Guest Elder?"

Qin Pei shook her head. Luocha Sect had only one Emperor Realm Master. If they truly wanted to hire a Guest Elder, they would certainly have to choose an Emperor Realm Master, but ordinary Emperor Realm Masters all had their own Sects. Who would go to Luocha Sect to serve as a Guest Elder? As for hiring a Dao Origin Realm cultivator, there was simply no need.

"Then hire me. From now on, I will be Luocha Sect's Guest Elder." Yang Kai forced out a faint smile.

Qin Pei froze on the spot. From what Yang Kai had said earlier, she had known that he intended to help them rebuild their Sect, but she had never expected him to do it this way. In her mind, Yang Kai would at most announce that Luocha Sect was under his protection, making those with malicious intentions think twice. She had never imagined that Yang Kai would be willing to lower his honored status and go to Luocha Sect as a Guest Elder.

This was a Guest Elder. Even with the words Honored Guest attached, it was still the same as bearing Luocha Sect's mark. If Luocha Sect truly faced trouble in the future, how could Yang Kai refuse to help?

Seeing that Qin Pei did not answer, Yang Kai rubbed his nose and said, "Is it that your Luocha Sect only allows women? If so, I can recommend someone else to you. Her cultivation is no worse than mine." The person he meant was naturally Ji Yao. He believed Ji Yao would be willing to help with this small favor.

"No, no!" Qin Pei hurriedly waved her hands. What kind of joke was that? Even if Luocha Sect really had such a rule, it no longer did at this moment. What kind of person was Yang Kai? The greatest contributor in the battle at Wuhua Hall, someone who could casually command more than two hundred Emperor Realm Masters, over thirty Yao Kings, and three Divine Spirits. Let alone the fact that he himself was an Emperor Realm Master; even if he were not, no one in this world would dare look down on him anymore. With him serving as Luocha Sect's Guest Elder, who would still dare cause trouble?
