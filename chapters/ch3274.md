## Chapter 3274 — If It Is Sneaking Around, So Be It

"Elder Yang's willingness to serve this Sect as an Honored Guest is naturally our good fortune. It is just that this Sect has declined into poverty and has few sources of income, so regarding the monthly stipend... Elder Yang may have to suffer a little grievance." Qin Pei's voice grew softer and softer, and by the end even her face had gone red. She was truly worried they could not afford to hire Yang Kai. If he were an ordinary Emperor Realm Master, that would be one thing, but Yang Kai was no ordinary Emperor Realm Master. If they really invited him over, offering too little would be shameful, while offering too much was more than Luocha Sect could bear.

Yang Kai lifted the seed-filled cloth bag in his hand. "This is enough."

Qin Pei looked at him, deeply moved and grateful. That bag of seeds was not rare and had cost her less than a hundred Source Crystals. She knew that if not for Yu Zhuo's sake, there was no way he would accept such a losing arrangement. He truly had treated Yu Zhuo as a friend. For a time, grief and gratitude mingled in her heart. If Yu Zhuo were still alive, who knew how happy she would have been.

She suddenly said, "Then, Qingyang Shrine..." Yang Kai was an Elder of Qingyang Shrine, yet he was coming to Luocha Sect to serve as an Honored Guest. Would Qingyang Shrine agree to that?

Yang Kai said, "It is fine. Hall Master Wen will not object."

"Many thanks, Elder Yang." Qin Pei bowed deeply.

Yang Kai raised a hand and said, "Take your fellow Sect members and find somewhere to rest. Once matters here are handled, I will make a trip to Luocha Sect with you and settle this properly."

"Yes." Qin Pei respectfully acknowledged his instructions, then led the group of women away.

Yang Kai turned to Hua Qingsi and instructed, "Notify me when the Great Emperor arrives."

He had previously sent an old man to Star God Palace to report the situation and request aid. Judging by the time, they should be close. Xiao Yuyang had already set out to welcome the Great Emperor's honored arrival, so Yang Kai estimated it would take two days at most.

Hua Qingsi nodded and watched Yang Kai leave.

Matters at Wuhua Hall had basically settled. With Guixu's ability to identify them, there was no need to worry that any demonized cultivators were still hiding here. The Emperor Realm Masters from the Northern Territory and the Yao Kings from the Wild Ancient Land could not very well remain any longer, so Yang Kai gathered them together, led them to the Space Array he had arranged earlier, and sent them back.

The group from the Northern Territory was easy enough to handle. After witnessing Yang Kai's ability to rally others this time, every one of them became more obedient than the next. If he told them to stay, they stayed; if he told them to leave, they left, without a word of complaint. The Yao Kings from the Wild Ancient Land, however, all looked at Yang Kai with lingering reluctance, as though he were some irresistible prize. If the three Divine Spirits had not been standing guard nearby, they probably would have rushed over long ago to curry favor.

Even so, when he sent them away, each Yao King looked more aggrieved than the last. The female Yao King Du Mi'er was even full of tender affection as she openly teased him, "When Sir has free time, you must come to the Ancient Land and visit this servant. This servant will miss you."

Luan Feng's face darkened on the spot, while Fan Wu and Cang Gou looked deeply worried, inwardly sighing over and over.

After all that bustle, he finally sent everyone away.

Yang Kai turned around and sighed. "Junior Sister Yao, did you really think I could not see you sneaking around?"

Ji Yao stepped out from behind a large tree not far away and walked over.

Not many people had come from Ice Heart Valley. When Yang Kai sent them away just now, he had not seen Ji Yao at all. A sweep of his Divine Sense told him she was hiding nearby, but he had not exposed her and only spoke up now.

Their eyes met, and Yang Kai asked helplessly, "You are not going back?"

Ji Yao said, "I will stay with you."

As she spoke, she took Yang Kai's hand and, with a light leap, sped off in one direction.

Her jade hand felt bonelessly soft and smooth in his, and a faint fragrance lingered at the tip of his nose, making him relax despite himself. Yang Kai did not know what she intended to do, but he did not resist and simply let her pull him along.

Before long, they arrived atop a mountain peak. By now, Wuhua Hall was almost deserted. The surviving cultivators who had come to attend the Martial Gathering were basically all gathered in the valley, and the disciples of Wuhua Hall itself had been nearly wiped out, so the buildings on this peak were empty as well.

Whether by coincidence or not, the mountain peak Ji Yao had chosen without thinking was none other than the place where Qingyang Shrine's group had stayed before.

She went straight into a wing room and closed the door. Only then did Ji Yao let go of Yang Kai. She drew a deep breath, closed her eyes, and stood there as her heartbeat suddenly quickened.

Yang Kai turned back to look at her and saw her standing there as if leaving herself entirely at his mercy. He could not help laughing. "What are you doing?"

Ji Yao opened her eyes, looked at him, and asked in return, "What do you want to do?" Her expression said he could do whatever he pleased.

Yang Kai was speechless for a moment. You were the one who pulled me here, all right? Now you are asking me what I want to do? Then his thoughts shifted, and he guessed what Ji Yao had in mind. Warmth stirred in his heart, and he said with an amused smile, "I am not that fragile."

Ji Yao stared at him with wide eyes, and it was impossible to tell whether she believed him or not. Without warning, Yang Kai pulled her into his arms, making her whole body stiffen as she cried out in surprise, "Didn't you say..."

Yang Kai grinned. "I am not that fragile, but since someone has delivered herself to my door, there is no need to be polite." With a slight exertion of strength, the two of them rolled onto the bed together.

Ji Yao nearly wept from helplessness. Her whole body trembled as a large hand began roaming over her. His fingertips seemed to carry an electric current, and wherever they passed, they sent waves through her, drawing her already taut nerves even tighter.

Heavy breaths trailed up from her lower abdomen, brushing over her chest and neck before finally falling against her face.

Ji Yao hurriedly closed her eyes, her long lashes trembling constantly. In the next instant, her lips were seized without restraint, and an extremely suppressed sound escaped her throat.

Yang Kai's movements this time were extremely rough, without the slightest hint of tenderness. His large hand kneaded hard enough that even Ji Yao felt pain, and his kiss left her feeling as though she were about to suffocate, as if she might die at any moment.

After a long while, their lips parted. Ji Yao gasped for breath. Although she did not open her eyes, she could clearly feel his sharp gaze sizing her up. Her cheeks burned, and she did not dare open her eyes to meet his.

Just as she thought there would be no escaping him this time, Yang Kai suddenly lay down on his side, rested his head on her lower abdomen, and did not move for a long while.

Ji Yao opened her eyes in puzzlement, slowly sat up, moved Yang Kai's head onto her thigh, and lowered her head to study him. Their eyes met, and Yang Kai smiled faintly. Ji Yao asked, "Do you feel any better?"

As she spoke, she reached out and slowly smoothed the hair Yang Kai had mussed earlier, her movements as gentle as a newly wedded wife's.

Yang Kai reached out, took her soft hand in his, and patted it gently. "After we go back, I will pay Senior Bing Yun a visit."

Ji Yao trembled and asked nervously, "See her... for what?"

Yang Kai looked up at her and said as if it were only natural, "For the matter between you and me, of course. I believe Senior Bing Yun should not stand in our way." He had never wanted to leave feelings scattered everywhere, but after what had happened with Yu Zhuo, his state of mind had subtly changed. Much of his former indecision was gone, replaced by a measure of decisiveness.

The blush on Ji Yao's face immediately faded, leaving her a little pale. "No."

Yang Kai was stunned. "No?"

Ji Yao shook her head. "Do not tell Honored Master."

Yang Kai froze, then smiled again. "Do you think Senior Bing Yun is blind? I am afraid she saw through your feelings long ago. It is just that if things truly become like this, you will have to suffer some grievance. You know as well as I do that I still have Su Yan and the others..."

"I know, but do not tell Honored Master."

Yang Kai was truly puzzled this time. "Why?"

"No reason. In any case, you are not allowed to tell her."

Yang Kai was caught between laughter and exasperation. "You and I are already like this, so why keep hiding it? Would it not be better to lay everything out and be open about it?"

Ji Yao said nothing and only kept shaking her head.

Yang Kai felt his heart sink, and he asked uneasily, "You are unwilling to be with me..."

"That is not it."

Yang Kai said, "Then do you know that the two of us being like this counts as sneaking..." In the end, out of consideration for Ji Yao's face, he was too embarrassed to finish the thought.

Ji Yao lowered her head and said, "If it is sneaking around, then so be it. I like things as they are."

Yang Kai stared at her in astonishment, half wishing he could knock her head open and see what was inside.

Ji Yao suddenly said coldly, "If you dare tell anyone, I will enter death seclusion from this day forth and never see you again."

Yang Kai was greatly shocked and hurriedly soothed her. "All right, all right, all right. I will not say anything."

Only then did Ji Yao's color return to normal. She sat there quietly, looking down at Yang Kai's face with eyes full of tenderness. The wing room was utterly still. Yang Kai lay across Ji Yao's supple thighs, wrapped in her faint fragrance, feeling the fatigue of the past few days swept clean away as he relaxed completely.

After an unknown length of time, Yang Kai suddenly opened his eyes, took a Transmission Compass from his Spatial Ring, poured in his Divine Sense to investigate, then rose and said, "The people from Star God Palace have arrived."

"Your hair!" Ji Yao called out.

Yang Kai's hair was a complete mess. If he really ran out like this, anyone would know what wonderful deed he had been up to.

Yang Kai hurriedly turned around and sat on the edge of the bed. Ji Yao knelt behind him and tidied his hair, then pulled him to his feet and smoothed the wrinkles from his clothes, her expression serious. Yang Kai smiled faintly and reached out to draw her into his arms.

Ji Yao warily placed a hand against his chest and frowned. "Proper business is more important."

Before she could finish, Yang Kai bent down and kissed her, though he stopped after the barest taste.

A moment later, the two walked out of the hall side by side and flew toward the valley.

Somewhere in the valley, Wen Zishan, Ma Qing, and the others were gathered together. Xiao Yuyang, who had gone to welcome the Great Emperor's honored arrival, had also returned. Beside them stood a burly man of late middle age, clearly no longer young but with jet-black hair and an astonishing aura. There was also a grizzled old man whose eyes held a restrained divine light. These two were none other than Star God Palace's Great Elder, Lei Hong, and Second Elder Xue Zhengmao. Both possessed powerful Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivations. Counting Xiao Yuyang as well, Star God Palace had three Third-Stage Emperor Realm Masters, offering a glimpse of the foundation of a Great Emperor Sect.
