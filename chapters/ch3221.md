## Chapter 3221 — Guilty Conscience

"Palace Master wants us to move out?" Ye Hen's heart gave a jolt. After the calamity Thousand Leaves Sect had suffered back then, only two or three hundred disciples remained from the several thousand they once had, and even those survivors varied wildly in strength. If not for Yang Kai's care over the years, they likely would have been unable to hold on at all, much less see the disciples' cultivations soar. Even he, the Sect Master, had already advanced to the Emperor Realm.

"Sect Master Ye still has a choice." Yang Kai raised one finger. "First, Thousand Leaves Sect can fully merge into my Lingxiao Palace. From now on, there will be no Thousand Leaves Sect in this world, only Thousand Leaves Peak, with you as its Peak Master. We discussed this before, but circumstances are different now. If we do it this way, the other Peak Masters will have nothing to object to."

Ye Hen's expression shifted slightly. If that happened, Thousand Leaves Sect would truly be gone. After a moment of hesitation, he asked, "And the second choice?"

Yang Kai said, "I send you back to Thousand Leaves Sect's old grounds. Chief Manager Hua passes that way every time she goes to the Southern Territory. From what I hear, no one has occupied the place for the time being, though it is a bit run-down. As long as Sect Master Ye has the disciples make some simple repairs, you should be able to settle there. As for safety, there is no need to worry too much. Sect Master Ye is now in the Emperor Realm as well and has some ability to protect himself. With those Heavenly Puppets besides, I imagine no one will be blind enough to come looking for trouble. On top of that, I am still a Guest Elder of Qingyang Shrine. When the time comes, I will speak to Qingyang Shrine and have them take greater care of Thousand Leaves Sect. Naturally, that will give you the space and time to grow stronger."

Ye Hen was deeply moved and said gratefully, "Palace Master is too kind."

He also knew Yang Kai had already gone as far as he could. Ye Hen felt no resentment. The fact that Yang Kai was willing to discuss this matter with him so frankly was already the greatest respect. Otherwise, with Yang Kai's current strength, he could have thrown them out outright.

Still, if they truly left now and returned to Thousand Leaves Sect, would they ever have another chance to avenge their blood feud?

The disciples' cultivations had advanced at astonishing speed over the years, and even he had broken through to the Emperor Realm, all thanks to Lingxiao Palace's excellent cultivation environment and its steady stream of resources. Once they returned to the Southern Territory, everything would have to begin again from nothing. Even if Qingyang Shrine could protect them, where would they get cultivation supplies? Thousand Leaves Sect was indeed skilled at refining puppets, but puppet refinement also required vast quantities of materials.

More importantly, would the disciples truly be willing to return to the Southern Territory with him?

They had lived here for twenty or thirty years. The several hundred disciples of Thousand Leaves Sect had long since grown used to this life. To put it bluntly, this place was practically a paradise on earth. It was easy to go from hardship to comfort, but hard to go from comfort back to hardship. Who would willingly return to that barren place where even birds would not shit?

"Sect Master Ye, take some time to think it over. There is no hurry to give me an answer." Seeing the struggle on his face, Yang Kai sighed, patted his shoulder, and rose to his feet.

"There is no need to think it over." Ye Hen suddenly spoke.

Yang Kai turned to look at him.

Ye Hen stood and cupped his fists respectfully. "Ye Hen is willing to lead the disciples of Thousand Leaves Sect in submitting to Lingxiao Palace, but I have one request."

Yang Kai grinned. "Let's hear it."

"When the day comes and the time is right, please allow me, Palace Master, to lead the several hundred disciples of Thousand Leaves Peak to Flowing Shadow Sword Sect and demand justice!"

Flowing Shadow Sword Sect was the very Sect that had destroyed Thousand Leaves Sect back then. Though they had acted under another's orders, they had indeed been the ones to do it. Ye Hen had cultivated painstakingly for so many years for the sake of one day setting foot in Flowing Shadow Sword Sect and avenging his dead disciples.

"If Palace Master can agree to this condition, this subordinate is willing to serve Lingxiao Palace with all he has."

"I agree. How could I not?" Yang Kai chuckled. "You and I discussed this matter back then, and I will say the same thing now: if you want to take revenge yourself, then cultivate hard. Flowing Shadow Sword Sect is not made of mud."

Ye Hen nodded in acknowledgment. With a sweep of his broad sleeve, he cupped his fists and gave a formal salute. "Ye Hen, Peak Master of Thousand Leaves Peak, greets Palace Master!"

Although he had called Yang Kai Palace Master before, the meaning was clearly different now. From this day onward, the people who had once belonged to Thousand Leaves Sect truly belonged to Lingxiao Palace, and their various puppet-refining and puppet-controlling methods would also become part of Lingxiao Palace's foundation.

To be honest, Yang Kai was not all that willing to let Ye Hen leave either. It was only that what he had said was the truth. Thousand Leaves Sect had just two or three hundred disciples, yet they occupied an extremely fine main peak, and the other Peak Masters did indeed have many complaints. If they had been his own people, that would have been one thing, but the key point was that Ye Hen and the others, no matter how one counted them, were still outsiders. They had occupied such a good place only because they had arrived relatively early.

Now, there was no need to worry about that.

He told Ye Hen that in a few days, people would be arranged to move into Thousand Leaves Peak. For such a large main peak, housing fewer than three thousand people would simply be a waste.

Yang Kai returned from Ye Hen's place feeling lighthearted.

Upon entering the main hall of High Heaven Peak, a maid immediately came forward to greet him. “Palace Master, Senior Ji has arrived.”

"Brother Ji? Is something the matter?" Yang Kai asked in surprise.

The maid covered her mouth and smiled. "Not Lord Ji Ying. Lady Ji Yao."

Yang Kai stopped mid-step and asked, rather guiltily, "What is she here for?"

Something had happened last time in Ice Heart Valley's Cultivation Methods Pavilion. Though the memory had left Yang Kai with plenty to savor afterward, he could not quite figure out Ji Yao's attitude. The two of them had already shared such intimacy, yet afterward Ji Yao had acted as if nothing had happened. No matter how he looked at it, it was strange.

Fortunately, she had warned Yang Kai not to let Su Yan find out about it. Otherwise, he would still have to worry whether she might use it to threaten him. Since she had given that warning, he certainly would not leak the secret of his own accord either.

He had been busy since returning and had pushed the matter from that day to the back of his mind. Now that he heard Ji Yao had come, he could not help feeling a little uneasy.

Ji Yao's emotions had seemed somewhat out of control that day. She could not have regretted it now and come running over to settle accounts with him, could she?

If that was really the case, he would lose all face.

The maid said, "I do not know. After Lady Ji Yao arrived, she went inside and told this maidservant to tell you that once you returned, you were to go see her."

Yang Kai scratched his neck, looking deeply uncomfortable, then looked back at the maid. "What expression did she have when she arrived? Was she angry?"

The maid said uncertainly, "I do not think so. Lady Ji Yao always looks cold, so I cannot tell whether she was angry."

"You cannot tell whether she was angry?" Yang Kai glared.

The maid hurriedly lowered her head. "This maidservant truly does not know."

"Forget it, forget it." Yang Kai waved her off. "Go about your work."

The maid accepted the order and fled.

Yang Kai paced back and forth in the main hall, truly unable to guess why Ji Yao had come. By rights, it would be somewhat awkward for the two of them to meet now, and since Yang Kai did not know what she was thinking, he hesitated and could not bring himself to go in.

After pacing several times, he even reached the entrance of the main hall once and decided not to bother with Ji Yao. In any case, she could not stay in Lingxiao Palace forever. If he simply waited her out, he could avoid this awkwardness.

Then he thought about it again. The one who should feel awkward was not him, but her. If she was not afraid, what was he, a man, afraid of? In any case, she was the one who had suffered the loss.

He turned around and strode inside with his chest out and head high, bold as could be, his bearing as swaggering as a dragon and a tiger.

After searching for quite some time, he finally found Ji Yao in a side room.

This was Su Yan's room, built against the cliff. Beyond the balcony lay a bottomless precipice, clouds and mist churning and curling through it.

When Yang Kai pushed the door open and entered, he saw Ji Yao standing by the railing, gazing at the setting sun. The sunset's afterglow fell over her, gilding her entire figure.

A breeze brushed past, lifting her beautiful hair and stirring her dress, as if she might rise into the sky at any moment.

Ji Yao did not even turn her head, as though she had not sensed Yang Kai's arrival at all.

Yang Kai closed the door and walked over, stopping beside her as he took in her profile—the snow-white skin, the slender curve of her neck, the graceful swell of her chest. Standing there as still as a quiet maiden, she possessed a charm all her own.

"Junior Sister Yao is here." Yang Kai chuckled, rubbing his hands together.

Unable to figure out her current attitude, Yang Kai did not dare be too brazen. After something like that happened between a man and a woman, the man would naturally feel as if he had taken advantage, and so he could not help feeling somewhat guilty.

"I came to pick up a few things." Ji Yao turned her head and glanced at him.

"What things?" Yang Kai asked curiously.

"Some changes of clothes for Little Junior Sister," Ji Yao replied.

Yang Kai stared at her blankly. Only after a long moment, when he realized there was nothing more coming, did he say, "That's all?"

Ji Yao nodded lightly.

Yang Kai did not know whether to laugh or cry. "Did you need to come in person for that?"

As cultivators, all of them had Spatial Rings, and everyone was sure to keep plenty of spare clothes in their rings. Yang Kai had some in his Spatial Ring, and he was certain Su Yan did too.

Surely there was no need to make a special trip just to pick up a few changes of clothes.

"Am I not welcome?" Ji Yao arched her dark brows.

"No, no, no. Absolutely not. How could you not be welcome?" Yang Kai denied it at once.

Ji Yao lifted her chin. "Then go find them. It would not be proper for me to rummage through Little Junior Sister's things."

"Then wait here a moment." As he spoke, Yang Kai turned and went into the room, stopping before the wardrobe to search. Su Yan was not especially particular about clothing, but with her own beauty as a foundation, she looked stunning no matter what she wore.

There were not many clothes in the wardrobe. Yang Kai took them all out and stuffed them into an empty ring.

When he turned around, he found that Ji Yao had followed him in. She sat sideways on the bed, quiet to an almost unnatural degree.

Yang Kai handed her the ring and said, "They are all here. When you go back, tell her to cultivate in peace at Ice Heart Valley. If she needs anything, just send word, and I will have someone deliver it."

"Mm." Ji Yao accepted it with her fair hand, slipped it casually onto her finger, then raised her hand in front of her face and examined it, as though the ring were a gift from Yang Kai.

"Does Junior Sister have any other business?" Yang Kai asked.

"No."

If you do not, then why are you still sitting here? Yang Kai was speechless, but he was too embarrassed to ask her to leave. He did not know whether it was just his imagination, but this meeting felt different from every previous one. He kept feeling terribly guilty and wished he could send Ji Yao away as soon as possible so he would not have to remain so uneasy.

"Do you have business to attend to?" Ji Yao raised her head and looked at him, her beautiful eyes bright.

"No." Yang Kai scratched his head, thinking, What business could I possibly have? You are the one with business.

"If you do, go handle it. There is no need to mind me."

"Heh heh..." Yang Kai's mouth twitched. A guest was still a guest. How could he leave one here alone?
