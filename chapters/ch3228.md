## Chapter 3227 - Elder Council

Since the matter was settled, Yang Kai had no wish to drag it out and urged, "There is no time to lose, so I'll have to trouble Senior Brother to choose suitable people as soon as possible."

Qiu Ran raised a hand. "No hurry."

Yang Kai was startled. "Senior Brother, you are not thinking of going back on your word, are you?"

Qiu Ran snorted. "Since I agreed, how could I go back on it? It is just as this old man said earlier: there is no precedent for this. Let me ask you, for each Dao Origin Realm cultivator you borrow, how many Elder Contribution Points should you be charged per month?"

Yang Kai immediately grew wary. "Senior Brother, you had better not ask for an outrageous price."

Qiu Ran said, "This is not something I can decide alone. We need to call the other Elders over, discuss it together, and work out a suitable arrangement. We also need to inform the Hall Master."

That was fair enough. Hearing this, Yang Kai nodded. "That is only proper."

Qiu Ran and Gao Xueting immediately each took out a Transmission Compass and notified the Shrine's other Elders.

Before long, figures flew in from outside one after another. They were all familiar faces, all Elders of the Shrine. Even the two newly promoted Elders, Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao, had come.

In the Shrine, any disciple who advanced to the Emperor Realm became qualified for a seat among the Elders. It might only be an empty title without much real authority, but an Elder was still an Elder. Their status was completely different from a disciple's, and so was the treatment they received.

Yang Kai greeted everyone in turn. Xiao Baiyi remained as cold and aloof as ever and said little, while Murong Xiaoxiao's smile was unchanged. She came to Yang Kai's side and exchanged a few words with him.

He asked about Xia Sheng and learned that Xia Sheng was still guarding Linghu Palace and rarely returned to Qingyang Shrine.

"Senior Brother Xia said to thank you for last time. If there is a chance someday, he will invite you to Linghu Palace for a drink."

"Last time..." Yang Kai was puzzled for a moment, then quickly realized she meant the trip to the Southern Marsh and the incident with the demon cave. Hua Yulu, the Palace Master of Hundred Flowers Palace, was Xia Sheng's friend. Back then, Xia Sheng had entrusted her to Yang Kai's care, and fortunately, Yang Kai had lived up to Xia Sheng's expectations and kept Hua Yulu safe.

He smiled and waved it off. "A small matter."

Just then, Qiu Ran stood up. The hall fell silent, and everyone turned to look at him.

Looking helpless, Qiu Ran said, "The Hall Master says he will not be coming. We are to discuss and resolve this matter ourselves." Strictly speaking, a matter involving more than a hundred Dao Origin Realm disciples was no small thing, and Wen Zishan should certainly have been present. But he had always been lazy, and usually paid no attention to the Shrine's affairs, leaving them all for Qiu Ran to handle. Naturally, he would not show himself today either.

Everyone seemed to have expected this. You Kun's raspy voice boomed, "If the Hall Master is not coming, then he is not coming. It makes no difference whether he is here or not. We can discuss it ourselves all the same."

No other Elders would speak of their own Hall Master behind his back like this. Only Qingyang Shrine would. When the one above set a crooked example, those below naturally followed.

Everyone nodded in agreement. Qiu Ran looked utterly pained and cursed inwardly at the Shrine's rotten customs.

Without further discussion, everyone took their seats.

Yang Kai looked left and right, cupped his fists, and said, "Then Junior Brother will withdraw for now."

Qiu Ran said, "This is your matter to begin with, so there is no need for you to avoid anything. Sit down and listen."

Fine, no need to leave. Yang Kai turned around and sat down. Like Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao, he was also a newly promoted Elder. However, Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao were proper Shrine Elders, while he was only a Guest Elder. So even though his contributions to the Shrine were considerable, his seat was still arranged at the very back, right beside Murong Xiaoxiao.

In this sort of council meeting, Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao were still not qualified to take part in the discussion, mainly because they lacked experience. They had been called over so they could listen and prepare for the future. Sooner or later, they too would hold great authority in the Shrine and make decisions about the futures of the disciples beneath them.

Qiu Ran cleared his throat and first explained that Yang Kai had brought a large number of Emperor Pills and exchanged them for a great sum of Elder Contribution Points. The Elders around the hall all looked him over in astonishment, their faces full of envy. More than one hundred and fifty thousand Elder Contribution Points! Such a number had never appeared before. Even entering the Divine Roaming Mirror world once a year only cost two thousand points. That one hundred and fifty thousand-plus was enough for sixty or seventy years.

Envy was useless, though. Emperor Pills were not something just anyone could refine. Everyone became even more convinced that Imperial-Grade Pill Masters truly earned money easily. Without a sound, one hundred and fifty thousand contribution points had fallen into his hands. How long would it take to spend them all?

Yang Kai sat there calm and composed, looking perfectly unconcerned. In reality, he was secretly chatting with Murong Xiaoxiao by Divine Sense transmission.

After the amazement passed, someone immediately raised the same concern Qiu Ran had earlier and asked how Yang Kai should be allowed to spend this sum of contribution points.

Qiu Ran then told them that Yang Kai wanted to borrow one hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators and four hundred Void King Realm cultivators. That result had already been discussed, and Qiu Ran had made the decision, so naturally no one objected. What needed discussion now was how many Elder Contribution Points these people should be worth each month.

Fortunately, Yang Kai could not be considered an outsider, so everyone discussed the matter fairly and impartially. In less than half an hour, they reached a result.

Yang Kai would pay ten Elder Contribution Points per Dao Origin Realm disciple each month and three Elder Contribution Points per Void King Realm disciple each month. By that calculation, he would pay out two thousand two hundred contribution points every month, or twenty-six thousand four hundred points a year.

More than one hundred and fifty thousand contribution points would be enough to pay for nearly six years.

This result was still within Yang Kai's means. When Qiu Ran asked him, Yang Kai naturally had no objections, and the matter was settled on the spot.

Six years should be enough for the disciples who had come from the Star Domain to become familiar with the Star Realm. It should also be enough for some of the Star Realm's Void King Realm cultivators to advance to the Dao Origin Realm. At that point, the veterans could guide the newcomers and pass on cultivation experience, forming a virtuous cycle.

Besides, if he ran out of contribution points, Yang Kai could simply go find Ji Ying, get some Emperor Pills, and exchange them for more. To him, it was hardly a problem.

"Since it is rare for all of us to gather in one hall, let us also discuss the Southern Territory Martial Gathering one month from now." Qiu Ran picked up his teacup, took a few sips, then set it down. "The Southern Territory Martial Gathering was originally held once every five years. However, everyone knows what happened in recent years. The demon cave appeared, Demonic Intent revived, and people were thrown into panic. The cultivators of our Southern Territory have been hunting down those escaped Demonic Intents all this time, so the Martial Gathering kept being delayed. Now that the dust has finally settled, we cannot be careless with this Martial Gathering after more than ten years."

"Martial Gathering? What Martial Gathering?" Yang Kai turned to Murong Xiaoxiao and asked by Divine Sense transmission.

Murong Xiaoxiao replied, "It is a Southern Territory tradition, held once every five years and jointly hosted by our Qingyang Shrine, Wuhua Hall, and Tianwu Sacred Land. The three families contribute resources together and prepare some valuable prizes. Star God Palace also bestows a few things. Cultivators below the Emperor Realm from across the Southern Territory can participate, competing for rankings at three levels: Dao Origin Realm, Void King Realm, and Origin Returning Realm. There are individual rankings and Sect rankings, and the prizes are awarded according to rank. It can also be considered a way for the various great Sects and families to test the cultivation results of their disciples. In any case, every Sect takes it quite seriously, especially the three top Sects. No one wants to fall behind in the rankings, so every Martial Gathering is very lively, with experts gathered like clouds."

"There is such a thing!" Yang Kai clicked his tongue in amazement.

Murong Xiaoxiao pursed her lips in a smile. "Senior Brother has not spent much time in the Southern Territory, so you have never heard of it. The last Martial Gathering was held at our Shrine. Senior Brother Xia, Senior Brother Xiao, and I all participated."

"How did you do?" Yang Kai asked with interest.

Murong Xiaoxiao said, a little embarrassed, "In the Sect rankings, we were slightly ahead of the other two. In the individual rankings, Senior Brother Xia was a little weaker than Wuchang and had to settle for second place. I only made it into the top ten."

Yang Kai comforted her, "Wins and losses are common in war. Junior Sister need not take it to heart."

"The three families take turns hosting the Martial Gathering. Last time it was us, so this time it should be Wuhua Hall's turn."

"Right, I heard Senior Brother Qiu say just now that the Southern Territory has been hunting Demonic Intents these past few years. How is the situation?" Yang Kai knew about the Demonic Intents. He, Hua Yulu, and the others could be called the chief culprits. During the trip to the Southern Marsh, the demon cave appeared, and Gong Yue, the former Family Head of the Tianhe Valley Gong family, was corroded by Demonic Intent and entered the demonic path. He slipped into Qingyang Shrine, opened the demon cave, and released thousands upon thousands of Demonic Intents.

It was just that afterward, Yang Kai had been in a hurry to search for Zhu Qing, so he had gone to the Frozen Earth and been tied up until only now returning. More than ten years had passed in the blink of an eye, and he did not know how things had turned out since then. Still, with Star God Palace leading the effort to slay demons and eliminate devils, there was no need to worry too much.

Hearing Yang Kai ask about the Demonic Intents, Murong Xiaoxiao went pale, as if she had recalled some extremely terrifying scene. Softly, she said, "Many people died."

Demonic Intents were strange, formless, and nearly impossible to guard against. Yang Kai had personally faced one back then. Gong Yue's cultivation was not weak, yet he had been silently invaded by Demonic Intent and fallen into the demonic path. Most terrifying of all, after Gong Yue fell, he himself felt nothing was wrong. Instead, his nature had been twisted by that Demonic Intent, and he had been willing to die in Qingyang Shrine if it meant releasing thousands upon thousands of Demonic Intents to bring disaster to the world.

If Murong Xiaoxiao said many people had died, then a great many people must truly have died. It was hard to imagine what the Southern Territory had been like over these past ten-plus years.

Just then, Qiu Ran announced in a loud voice, "Since that is the case, the matter is settled. This old man will remain behind to hold the Shrine. This trip will be led by the Hall Master, and the accompanying Elders are as follows. Luo Chen!"

A fair-faced, beardless man stood in response, cupped his fists, and said, "As commanded!"

Yang Kai asked curiously, "The Hall Master is going too? Is that not giving Wuhua Hall far too much face?"

Although this Southern Territory Martial Gathering was not small in scale, it was, after all, a matter for those below the Emperor Realm. He had thought one or two Elders leading a team over would be enough. Who would have expected Wen Zishan to go as well?

Murong Xiaoxiao smiled and transmitted, "Star God Palace sends people over for every Martial Gathering. The Hall Master is only going to make an appearance."

"So that is how it is." Yang Kai nodded slightly. So they were not giving Wuhua Hall face, but Star God Palace. That made sense. Since people from Star God Palace were coming, it would not look right if Wen Zishan did not go. It was only an appearance anyway, nothing important.

Over there, Qiu Ran called out again, "Gao Xueting!"

"As commanded." Gao Xueting rose and cupped her fists.

"Xiao Baiyi!"

"Yes."
