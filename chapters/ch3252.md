## Chapter 3252 — The Heavens Are About to Change

Ever since advancing to the Emperor Realm, he had rarely met anyone who could give him a real fight. He had even killed several Third Stage Emperor Realm Masters, so a subtle shift in his mindset had been hard to avoid. Only today did he understand how vast the world was, and how many hidden talents it held. The price of looking down on others was suffering for it himself.

His escape from death this time was entirely thanks to his Half-Dragon Body. Without it, that black shadow's sneak attack from behind might have taken his life. Without it, the strange poison on the dagger could have left him dead without a grave.

It was the toughness of that Half-Dragon Body that had allowed him to trap the dagger's advance with his own flesh, sparing him a fatal wound and buying him time to slow the poison's spread. He had truly survived by the skin of his teeth.

Looking back on everything that had happened tonight, Yang Kai was full of doubts. He had come here only because Luo Chen had borrowed Gao Xueting's name to pass him a message. Otherwise, why would he have run over to this place for no reason? And that black shadow had clearly been lying in ambush here by design. What exactly did this matter have to do with Luo Chen and Gao Xueting?

He had even contacted Gao Xueting earlier and received some response from her. If not for that, he would never have trusted someone so easily.

Those dozen or so Emperor Realm Masters who had entered the Demonic State made him feel even more that something was terribly wrong.

The Southern Territory had only recently suffered the Demon Calamity. During the dozen or so years he had spent fighting back in the Hengluo Star Domain, the Southern Territory had been hunting down those fleeing Demonic Intents, and supposedly they had all been purged. So where had so many people who had entered the Demonic State come from?

The more Yang Kai thought about it, the more alarmed he became. Those dozen or so people were all Emperor Realm Masters who had come to attend this Martial Gathering, and even during the day he had noticed nothing unusual about them. They had all entered the Demonic State without a sound, so what about the others? The valley had gathered several hundred Emperor Realm Masters and tens of thousands of elite disciples from the Southern Territory. If all of them had been caught in this, it would be a disaster beyond imagining.

Not daring to think any further, Yang Kai flickered out of the Small Xuan Realm.

On the mountain peak, the Mountain and River Bell stood silent. The summit was a wreck, still marked by the great battle. Faint movement came from inside the bell; clearly, the enemies suppressed within were trying to break free. But the suppressive force inside the bell was extremely strong, so how could they possibly escape at will?

Yang Kai slammed a fist into the bell. A deafening clang rang out, and the inside immediately fell quiet, followed only by a few miserable cries.

When Yang Kai put away the Mountain and River Bell, the five Emperor Realm Masters he had suppressed earlier all lay unconscious on the ground. He reached out and grabbed the white-haired old man. Emperor Yuan surged, sweeping through the man's body, and Yang Kai's heart sank.

The situation was just as he had seen. This old fellow had already entered the Demonic State, but judging from his condition, it had not happened long ago, and the Demonic Qi had not eroded him too deeply. He could still be saved.

Yang Kai's expression turned solemn, and his aura changed with startling abruptness. In an instant, it became ancient and desolate, as if he himself had stepped out of a distant, primordial world, carrying a sense that he did not belong in the present age.

The power of a Shaman.

Yang Kai was not only an Emperor Realm Master, but also a Shaman King deeply versed in all kinds of Shamanic Arts. From his previous experience, the power of Shamans was undoubtedly the most effective way to counter Demonic Qi.

He pressed his hand onto the old man's head and began chanting long, strange incantations. A hazy layer of light instantly enveloped the old man, and in the next moment, a faint crimson glow rose from his body.

Bloodlust Art! In that ancient world, Yang Kai had fought bitterly against the Demon Race for many years and had rescued people who had entered the Demonic State more than once. Bloodlust Art was highly effective at resisting the erosion of Demonic Qi, and with his Shamanic Arts assisting it, pulling this old man back from the Demonic State should not be difficult.

However, Bloodlust Art worked by stimulating a cultivator's Essence-Blood. This old man was already advanced in years, so this would likely affect his lifespan somewhat. But how could Yang Kai worry about that now? He only wanted to quickly understand what had happened today before deciding what to do next.

With Bloodlust Art already cast, his incantation changed again, this time into the Art of Exorcism.

The old man instantly trembled like a leaf, his face twisting as if he were enduring terrible pain. Wisps of black smoke seeped from his seven orifices, and the moment they left his body, they vanished into nothingness. This was the Demonic Qi that had invaded him, now being purified by the Art of Exorcism.

Suddenly, the old man opened his eyes. They were pitch-black from end to end. Even with all his strength sealed by Yang Kai, he still bared his teeth like a fierce beast, growling and roaring at Yang Kai as if he would not stop until he had torn the flesh from Yang Kai's bones.

The scene before him reminded Yang Kai of what he had once experienced in that ancient world, where the Demon Race and the Barbarian Tribe slaughtered each other in brutal war, flesh and blood flying everywhere. Without seeing it firsthand, it was hard to imagine just how cruel the struggle between the two races truly was.

Before long, however, the old man's struggling and roaring subsided. Less and less Demonic Qi spilled from his seven orifices, and the blackness in his eyes gradually faded.

After several dozen breaths, the old man looked as if he had been dragged out of a pool. He collapsed limply to the ground, blinked, and asked blankly, "This is... what happened?"

Yang Kai looked down at him. "Do you recognize me?"

The old man's vision gradually focused, and he said in surprise, "You are Elder Yang of Qingyang Shrine?"

"You don't remember what happened to you?" Yang Kai asked in a low voice.

The old man frowned in thought. A moment later, his face changed drastically. "This old man just now... just now... what exactly happened?"

He seemed to retain some impression of what had just happened, and was not completely ignorant of it.

Yang Kai said, "You were eroded by Demonic Qi and entered the Demonic State. When did you enter the Demonic State, and how?"

"Entered the Demonic State!" The old man's face changed, going deathly pale. Then he gave two dry laughs and shook his head. "Impossible. This is impossible!" He still looked unable to believe it. The Southern Territory had been hunting those Demonic Intents for years, so he naturally knew what entering the Demonic State meant, and had even seen others fall into it. A person's temperament would truly change completely; they would recognize neither kin nor friend, and could kill even their own wife and daughter without hesitation. But he had never imagined such a thing would befall him, and fear and unease gripped him for a time.

Yang Kai grabbed him by the collar and said coldly, "There is nothing impossible about it. Look at the four of them!"

The old man turned to look at the four unconscious people nearby. When he saw that black Demonic Qi still coiled around them, his pupils shrank, and he had no choice but to face reality. Otherwise, there was simply no explaining all the strange things that had happened to him.

"Think carefully. Was there anything suspicious?"

The old man hesitated. "This afternoon, Madam Han and I accepted Elder Feng's invitation and went to meet Hall Master Lei. After that... I don't remember much."

"Feng Ming, Lei Gu?" Yang Kai's expression changed. In the entire Southern Territory, the only two people this old man would call Elder Feng and Hall Master Lei were probably those two. If they were the chief culprits...

Yang Kai whipped his head around and looked toward Wuhua Hall, worry written across his face.

"Exactly." The old man slowly shook his head. "Surely Hall Master Lei wouldn't have done anything... Elder Yang, where are you going?" Before he could finish, Yang Kai suddenly shot into the sky and sped off in one direction, vanishing from sight in the blink of an eye.

The old man staggered to his feet, feeling unbearably weak. He looked at the four unconscious people lying on the ground, then at the traces of the great battle all around him. In vexation, he slapped his thigh and said bitterly, "The heavens are about to change."

He faintly sensed that the currents of the world were about to shift.

With four people who had entered the Demonic State beside him, he could not simply leave them be. If they woke up, they would likely go out and wreak havoc again. But he had at least some friendship with all four of them, so he could not kill them either. All he could do was find a way to restrain them for now, then seek out Yang Kai later and have him save them. Since Elder Yang had been able to save him from the Demonic State, saving four more should be no great difficulty.

Wuhua Hall was in utter chaos. Sounds of fighting and killing rang out everywhere, and figures fled in every direction. Bodies fell one after another into pools of blood, their lives and Daos extinguished. In less than an hour, one of the Southern Territory's three great Sects had become a hell on earth.

Emperor Realm Masters wrapped head to toe in black Qi shuttled back and forth, cutting people down as easily as chopping melons and vegetables. Their pitch-black eyes were filled with cold cruelty. More than a hundred Emperor Realm Masters formed an encircling line, forcing all those fleeing back toward the valley, the place where the Southern Territory Martial Gathering and tonight's feast had been held.

Feng Ming held a Transmission Compass and checked the message within. His brow furrowed, and he turned to Lei Gu beside him. "News came from that side. They failed."

Lei Gu's robes snapped around him. Although his entire body was also wreathed in pitch-black Demonic Qi, he clearly had not lost his reason, because he had not merely been eroded by Demonic Qi. He had been possessed and taken over by a Demonic Intent.

The Lei Gu standing here was no longer the Lei Gu of Wuhua Hall.

Feng Ming was clearly the same.

Hearing this, Lei Gu snapped, "How could they fail?"

The arrangement had been foolproof. How could it possibly have failed?

"I don't know the details. That man simply escaped calamity," Feng Ming said, shaking his head.

"Trash!" Lei Gu's face turned vicious. It was precisely because he feared that man's Space Divine Ability that he had gone to such lengths to get rid of him first, lest that man use his Space Divine Ability to disrupt the situation. He had never expected them to fail, and that left him extremely dissatisfied. Still, he knew that since even that man had failed, the result might not have been much better if Lei Gu had gone himself. One could only say that man proposed, but Heaven disposed. It seemed he had still underestimated that man's ability.

"Move faster. Settle this before word leaks out, as quickly as possible," Lei Gu ordered.

Feng Ming nodded. His figure flickered, and he shot into the night sky.

Under the cover of night, Yang Kai's figure flickered and shifted, his face tense. His heart had eased slightly, but he was also deeply worried.

He had originally suspected that this matter had something to do with Luo Chen and Gao Xueting. The facts proved that it did, but what eased him slightly was that this should not have been their own intention. Luo Chen and Gao Xueting had likely been eroded by Demonic Qi as well, then manipulated by someone else.

Yang Kai had also suddenly remembered that during the day, Luo Chen and Gao Xueting seemed to have gone out together. Looking at it now, they had probably been summoned by Lei Gu. Their situation was likely the same as the old man's; they had both carelessly fallen into the trap.

No matter how he thought about it, he could not understand. Was the Southern Territory's Demon Calamity not supposed to have been pacified? How could a scheme on this scale still have been set in motion? Moreover, it had been deliberately timed for the Southern Territory Martial Gathering. This was clearly a vast arrangement, and not the work of a single day. It had probably been planned long ago.
