## Chapter 3299 - Applying Pressure

However, from their perspective, it was only natural for them to have such concerns. Yang Kai had no reason to blame them, though he was still somewhat displeased.

A head poked in through the doorway, and its owner met Yang Kai's eyes.

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Miss Lan."

Lan He stepped inside awkwardly, embarrassment written all over her face. "Brother Yang."

Yang Kai asked curiously, "What is it, Miss Lan? Are you feeling unwell?"

Lan He lowered her head and stared at the tips of her feet. "Brother Yang, I'm sorry." Yang Kai did not know whether Heavenly Wolf Valley had a token from Spirit Beast Island, but she did. She also knew the news was no fabrication. Her Master and Master's Wife were simply afraid that befriending Yang Kai would bring trouble to Heavenly Wolf Valley, which was why they had said what they did.

If she were the one in charge, she naturally would not begrudge him such a small favor. Yet although she was an Elder of Heavenly Wolf Valley, she could not promise Yang Kai anything at a time like this, much less do anything that would betray Heavenly Wolf Valley. On one side was her benefactor; on the other, her Sect. Lan He was torn beyond words, and the more she thought about it, the guiltier she felt facing Yang Kai, until she could hardly bring herself to meet his eyes.

Yang Kai walked up to her and smiled. "Miss Lan, there is no need to take it so seriously. I am already more than grateful that you informed me about Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land. As for that Spirit Beast Island token, since it was fabricated, there is no need to dwell on it."

Lan He fell silent for a while, then suddenly raised her head, her expression resolute. "Brother Yang, wait a few more days. I will think of a way for you."

Seeing her expression, Yang Kai knew she was probably planning to plead with Tang Sheng and ask him to take out the Spirit Beast Island token. But if she really did that, it might well create a rift between master and disciple. Smiling faintly, he said, "There is no need. It is not as though I must go to Spirit Beast Island. To me, if I can go, I will; if I cannot, it does not matter much. Making you worry like this is my fault for saying something I should not have."

Lan He felt awful hearing this. This matter was clearly Heavenly Wolf Valley's fault, clearly because her Master had his misgivings. When he had first heard from her that Yang Kai had arrived, he had been happy enough to want to meet him and had even said he would personally accompany him to search for Spirit Beast Island. Later, when he learned that two top forces were making trouble for Yang Kai, his attitude had changed at once. Yet now she was the one being comforted by him.

"Where is Big Sister Ling? Can you take me to see her?" Yang Kai asked, changing the subject.

"Of course. Brother Yang, come with me." Lan He's attention was indeed diverted, and she agreed readily.

As Lan He led him deeper into Heavenly Wolf Valley, she remained silent the entire way, only answering briefly when Yang Kai asked her something. Her earlier liveliness and cheer were completely gone. Clearly, the guilt still weighed on her.

...

In another great hall, Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying hurried inside. Two people were already waiting in the hall. One was an old man wreathed in eerie Yin Qi, as though he had only just walked out from the gates of hell. The other was a dignified middle-aged man in a plain black robe. He stood there with his hands clasped behind his back, like a sharp sword pinning down the entire hall, making the atmosphere inside unbearably oppressive.

Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying were both startled when they saw them. Stepping forward, they cupped their fists and said, "Sect Master Fu, Saint Lord Xu, your honored selves have graced us with your presence, yet this Tang failed to welcome you from afar. Please forgive me." Inwardly, Tang Sheng thought that these two had come quickly indeed. Yang Kai had barely arrived at Heavenly Wolf Valley before these two followed right on his heels. He secretly rejoiced that he had possessed the foresight not to rashly agree to Yang Kai's request; otherwise, Heavenly Wolf Valley truly would not be able to stay out of this matter.

These two were the helmsmen of the Eastern Territory's two top forces, and both possessed the powerful cultivation of the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm. The fact that they had moved together showed Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land's attitude clearly. For all he knew, the two great forces had already sent out every expert they had.

Tang Sheng sighed inwardly. Yang Kai was probably in grave danger this time. So what if he had Space Divine Abilities to rely on? With so many experts taking action, once they disturbed the Void, even Space Divine Abilities would not let him escape easily. He could only hope Little He would not blame him in the future.

The ghostly Fu Bo sat with his eyelids lowered, unmoving like an old monk in meditation. Even though this was Heavenly Wolf Valley, and even though he was only a guest, he still had no intention of giving Tang Sheng any face.

Xu Changfeng, on the other hand, gave a faint sound of acknowledgement, though his back remained turned to Tang Sheng and his wife as he said lightly, “Tang Sheng, how many years has it been since Heavenly Wolf Valley was founded?”

The question came out of nowhere, but Tang Sheng heard something unusual beneath it, and a chill crept through him. After pondering for a moment, he said, "About twenty to thirty thousand years."

Xu Changfeng nodded lightly, turned around, and said calmly, "Twenty to thirty thousand years. That can be considered a long inheritance. Your Heavenly Wolf Valley has had its days of glory, once standing among the Eastern Territory's top forces, and it has also declined, even nearly to the point of destruction. But since it has endured for twenty to thirty thousand years without falling, it must have something unique to rely on."

Tang Sheng forced out a faint smile and said, "Beasts have an instinct for seeking fortune and avoiding calamity. Since my Heavenly Wolf Valley keeps company with Battle Wolves, naturally we have that instinct as well."

Xu Changfeng stared at him for a while, his gaze heavy with meaning, before he finally nodded. "Very good. I trust you understand why Sect Master Fu and I have come this time."

Cold sweat poured down Tang Sheng's forehead as he said, "Saint Lord Xu, please rest assured. My Heavenly Wolf Valley will not do anything to court its own destruction. My foolish disciple merely acted without understanding and unintentionally brought the man back. Heavenly Wolf Valley has no intention of sheltering him. He will leave very soon."

"Hmph!" Fu Bo suddenly snorted coldly. "You can try sheltering him."

At these words, Qian Xiuying could not help glaring angrily at Fu Bo, feeling rather unwilling to accept this. Yes, he was a Third Stage Emperor Realm Master and also the leader of one of the Eastern Territory’s top forces, but this was Heavenly Wolf Valley. As a guest, what did he mean by acting so arrogantly toward her, the host? Heavenly Wolf Valley had the protection of Spirit Beast Island’s token, and she truly did not believe Netherworld Sect would dare make a move against Heavenly Wolf Valley.

As long as that token remained, as long as Spirit Beast Island's lingering prestige endured, Heavenly Wolf Valley would not face a disaster of annihilation.

As if he sensed her gaze, Fu Bo suddenly lifted his eyelids. His sharp eyes met Qian Xiuying's, and he gave a cold, mocking laugh.

In the next instant, Qian Xiuying's beautiful face went pale. A chorus of wailing ghosts and howling wolves seemed to erupt beside her ears, shaking her Divine Soul and sending a chill through her entire body.

Tang Sheng flashed forward and blocked Qian Xiuying from view, saying in a deep voice, "Sect Master Fu, what is the meaning of this? My Heavenly Wolf Valley has no intention of intervening in others' grudges and disputes, so please conduct yourself with dignity, Sect Master Fu."

Fu Bo said coldly, "This Lord simply saw that Madam Tang seemed unconvinced and helped her recognize reality. What? Are you unconvinced too?"

Tang Sheng was furious, but he did not dare say anything more.

Xu Changfeng said, "Tang Sheng, your noble Sect's Grand Guardian Array can isolate Heaven and Earth, can it not?"

Tang Sheng answered reflexively, "The Grand Guardian Arrays of every Sect all have that function..." As soon as the words left his mouth, he seemed to realize something and said in alarm, "What does Saint Lord Xu mean by asking that?"

Xu Changfeng looked at him calmly. "What do you think?"

Fu Bo let out a strange laugh, rose to his feet, and said, "Saint Lord Xu means for you to activate the Grand Guardian Array and isolate this stretch of Heaven and Earth, lest someone run away. You also know that boy has some ability."

Tang Sheng's expression changed greatly. "Could it be that you two intend to make a move inside my Heavenly Wolf Valley?"

Xu Changfeng looked at him and said, "What is wrong with a ready-made burial ground?"

"No!" Tang Sheng refused flatly.

Fu Bo narrowed his eyes and said, "Tang Sheng, think before you speak. A brain is a good thing. If you do not use it, is it not just an ornament?"

These two called him Tang Sheng again and again, addressing him by name without the slightest intention of taking him seriously. Tang Sheng and his wife heard it all and dared be angry but not speak. Now, faced with such cold ridicule, even Tang Sheng found it difficult to restrain himself. Even a clay figure had a spark of temper, much less the Master of a Sect. He had already made his stance clear: he would not shelter Yang Kai. He had not expected these two to still press him so aggressively, actually dreaming of making their move inside his Heavenly Wolf Valley.

Taking a deep breath, Tang Sheng said solemnly, "This matter is absolutely impossible. To tell you the truth, that man saved my disciple's life. My disciple brought him back this time to ask for our help, and this Tang has already refused him, leaving her uneasy. If I then activate the Grand Guardian Array to isolate Heaven and Earth and let him die in my Heavenly Wolf Valley, my disciple will certainly be haunted by a heart demon in the future. How could she still have any room to progress? If you two truly want to deal with him, Heaven is vast and Earth is wide; you may choose anywhere you like. Only my Heavenly Wolf Valley is out of the question. Heavenly Wolf Valley will not shelter him, but neither will we interfere in your grievances."

"It truly cannot be done?" Xu Changfeng's sharp gaze bore down on Tang Sheng.

Tang Sheng's face twitched. Sensing the threat in those words, he knew that if he refused this time, Heavenly Wolf Valley would likely face endless trouble in the Eastern Territory in the future. Just as he had said to Lan He earlier, with Spirit Beast Island's protection, no one truly dared to attack Heavenly Wolf Valley openly, but they would certainly trip them up in secret. Heavenly Wolf Valley was a large Sect with no small expenses, and it had many holdings outside the valley. Those holdings would certainly become targets.

He looked toward Qian Xiuying and saw her slowly shake her head, her lips moving slightly.

No sound came out, but Tang Sheng knew she had said Lan He's name. His heart settled at once, and he said firmly, "Please, Saint Lord Xu, do not make things difficult for this Tang any further!"

Lan He had returned alive from the Shattered Star Sea and advanced to the Emperor Realm early, so they naturally placed great hopes on her. In the future, perhaps she might even have a chance to advance to the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm. In other words, Heavenly Wolf Valley's future rested on Lan He alone.

If Lan He's Martial Dao heart were damaged because of this matter, that would be Heavenly Wolf Valley's greatest loss. As for whether their outside holdings would suffer any suppression, that was a matter for the future.

Not to mention, Yang Kai also had some connection to Heavenly Wolf Valley's other Elder, Ling Yinqin. If Yang Kai died in Heavenly Wolf Valley, Ling Yinqin would certainly hold it against Heavenly Wolf Valley as well. That would be equivalent to making Heavenly Wolf Valley alienate two Emperor Realm Masters at once. How could Tang Sheng allow such a thing to happen?

Hearing this answer, Fu Bo laughed coldly again. Xu Changfeng stared at Tang Sheng with an indifferent expression for a while, seemingly seeing his resolve, then nodded faintly. "So be it."

Hearing those words, Tang Sheng could not help letting out a breath of relief. If Xu Changfeng had truly insisted on doing that, he really did not know whether he could refuse. The present situation was already the best outcome. Although he had not been able to shelter Yang Kai, neither had he agreed to activate the Grand Guardian Array and cut off Yang Kai's path of survival. This at least allowed him to offer an explanation to both Yang Kai and Lan He.
