## Chapter 3184 — Changes in the Small Xuan Realm

At the border of Great Wilderness Star Domain, Wu Heng's face was ashen.

He had been locked in a standoff with that all-devouring darkness for half a year. Even after exhausting every ounce of strength, he could only slow its advance, not stop it. In that half year, he had lost well over a hundred million li of Starry Sky!

Such an enormous Domain was several times larger than the territory he had swallowed from Hengluo Star Domain over the previous three years.

Who exactly was the person on the other side? And how had he done this?

He had once driven Great Wilderness Star Domain to devour Hengluo Star Domain, so he naturally knew how difficult the process was. It was painstaking work, something that had to be pushed forward bit by bit. It was absolutely not something that could be accomplished overnight.

Back then, Hengluo Star Domain had not yet had a Master of the Star Domain, so his devouring had faced almost no resistance.

But now, even with him personally holding the line here, the corrosion from the other side was still so swift. Why?

If that had been all, he might have endured it, but another matter weighed on Wu Heng's mind.

Over this half year, deep within Great Wilderness Star Domain, he had lost three Cultivation Stars in succession. First Yellow Springs Star, then Kuanglang Star, then Feixie Star, each vanished from the Star Chart one after another as if they had never existed at all!

Those were three Cultivation Stars, not three meat pies! What method had Yang Kai used to steal them away?

Great Wilderness Star Domain had no shortage of Cultivation Stars, so losing three was not a fatal blow. The key was that this was only the beginning. Three Cultivation Stars had been lost in half a year. What about one year? Ten years? A hundred years...

If he truly waited until the entire Star Domain had no Cultivation Stars left, what meaning would this Star Domain's existence still have? He, the Master of this Star Domain, would be one in name only.

The one thing that let Wu Heng breathe a little easier was that after Feixie Star, Yang Kai seemed to make no further moves. He had no idea what Yang Kai was doing there.

As the Master of the Star Domain, it would be easy enough for him to spy on Yang Kai's whereabouts if he truly wished to. However, he did not dare, because the moment he let his attention waver, the other Star Domain's corrosion would surge.

This could not continue. If it did, his end would certainly be ugly. Did he really have to do that?

Wu Heng's face shifted again and again as anger, humiliation, unwillingness, and all sorts of other emotions churned through him. Never had he imagined that in this Lower Plane Star Domain, on his own territory, someone would force him to such a state!

...

Above a Dead Star, Gungun floated in midair, its enormous pitch-black body contracting and expanding over and over again.

Yang Kai stood beside it, watching it closely.

Even he did not know what was happening, though he had a vague sense of it.

Gungun seemed to be full!

Along the way, Gungun had devoured three Cultivation Stars in total. After it finished devouring Feixie Star, it shut its mouth, stopped making that clacking sound, and remained where Feixie Star had once been as this change came over it.

He tried to communicate with the Small Xuan Realm, only to find that his mind could not enter it. All he could do was wait and see.

If his senses were not mistaken, Gungun was digesting.

Three Cultivation Stars were not so easy to devour, after all. Even after being swallowed into the Small Xuan Realm, they still had to be digested. Otherwise, the slight differences in their World Laws would be enough to make the Small Xuan Realm collapse in on itself, and the living beings on those three Cultivation Stars would surely die without a burial place.

Once this change was complete, the entire Small Xuan Realm would probably be reborn. With that expectation in mind, Yang Kai waited quietly.

He very much wanted to investigate the situation on the Incarnation's side, but the distance was simply too great, and he was also inside Great Wilderness Star Domain, so he could not make contact with the Incarnation at all. Still, Wu Heng was surely having a miserable time. Otherwise, Yang Kai's recent actions would never have gone so smoothly. If Wu Heng could spare even a moment, he could have taught Yang Kai a lesson.

At this thought, Yang Kai gave a dark chuckle. Those who provoked him never came to a good end. Even Wu Kuang had suffered a great loss, let alone a mere Wu Heng.

Days passed little by little. There was no sunrise or moonset in the Starry Sky, and Yang Kai did not know exactly how much time had gone by.

Yet each time Gungun wriggled, it seemed to change a little.

Yang Kai could clearly feel its will becoming much clearer. This could only mean one thing: Gungun's digestion had been a success. It had fused the World Laws of the three Cultivation Stars it had devoured until they could no longer be distinguished from one another.

After a while longer, that will grew clearer still. Although it still could not communicate with Yang Kai, it could already respond in some way when Yang Kai reached out to it.

The most obvious change was Gungun's shape.

Originally, it had been nothing more than a huge black ball. Only after devouring Yellow Springs Star had it grown a great mouth. Now, however, a tail had actually sprouted directly opposite that mouth. The tail slowly lengthened, and at first glance, Gungun looked like an enormous tadpole, rather comical to behold.

Finally, one day, after another bout of wriggling, Gungun stopped changing. Its great mouth opened once more, clacking noisily and radiating urgent hunger.

After digesting three Cultivation Stars, it was ready to start devouring again, and its body had grown much larger. When it had descended upon Kuanglang Star, it had been a hundred li in diameter. Now, it was probably more than two hundred li across.

Yang Kai had no intention of stopping it and simply let it do as it pleased. Gungun immediately turned and sped off in one direction. Although there was only darkness there, Yang Kai knew a Cultivation Star definitely lay that way.

It seemed extremely sensitive to Cultivation Stars. Even across a vast stretch of Starry Sky, it could sniff out their delicious scent.

Yang Kai flickered into the Small Xuan Realm.

This was the first time he had entered since Gungun's change. The moment he arrived in this world, Yang Kai froze.

This was no longer the Small Xuan Realm he knew. The entire world had expanded by who knew how many times. He sent out his Divine Sense, yet even after a long while, it still had not reached the end.

The original Small Xuan Realm could only be considered a tiny world. Even its World Laws had been pieced together by Yang Kai from here and there. If the Small Xuan Realm were a garment, the original one had been covered in patches. Its World Laws were complete, true, but they had not been especially harmonious.

Now, however, the entire world felt smooth and harmonious, without the slightest jarring flaw. Its World Laws had also been perfected to an extraordinary degree, far more profound than before.

This could already be considered a true small world, one more advanced than an ordinary Cultivation Star.

The world held all kinds of terrain: jungles, plains, seas, deserts, basins, everything one could ask for, suitable for every race and kind to settle down, live in peace, and recover.

Countless living auras spread throughout the world, and buildings in all manner of styles rose from the ground.

The living beings of the three Cultivation Stars had been swallowed into this place. After discovering that their lives were not in danger, their first thought was naturally to rebuild their Sects and families. No real conflicts had broken out either. After all, no one knew where they were, much less whether disaster might still descend upon them. Even when beings of different races occasionally met, they restrained themselves and tried as much as possible to avoid conflict.

Yang Kai observed carefully for a while and soon found the original Small Xuan Realm. That space now sat at the very center of the new world. An invisible barrier seemed to separate it from the newborn land, and no one could intrude without permission.

Yang Kai could not help breathing a sigh of relief. He really had been afraid someone would barge into his medicine garden and ruin his Spiritual Medicines. The current situation was ideal, and thinking about it, it was probably thanks to Gungun.

Since he was here, he ought to greet them. At the very least, he had to let the people of this world know who their master was!

At a thought from Yang Kai, the figure of a giant suddenly appeared in the sky. Every living being in this world could see it as long as they looked up.

His gaze was majestic, seeming to look into the void and yet at every single person at once.

All living beings were shocked. As they raised their heads, they could not help feeling the urge to prostrate themselves in worship. Countless people had already fallen to the ground, trembling.

"It's him!" On a newly formed sea island, Ao Wei looked up at the face in the sky, and his own face changed.

The memory of being defeated in a single move not long ago was still vivid. How could he not know now that the other party's methods surpassed his own many times over? It was laughable that he had previously thought himself a Star Lord and wanted to exchange blows with him.

Quite a few people recognized Yang Kai. After all, when he had driven Gungun to devour stars some days ago, many had seen him from afar.

Seeing him again now, their awe deepened.

“I am the Master of this world and bear you no ill will. I only hope that all of you can live and work in peace within this world and lead proper lives. If any among you possess outstanding talent, I can guide them to a higher world to experience an even more wondrous path of the Martial Dao.” He extended two fingers and tapped his own eyes, then grinned. “Work hard, you lot. I will be watching you. Whether you become dragons or worms will depend on your own ability.”

The entire world fell silent. Everyone was too shocked to speak. Only after a long while did they realize that the enormous figure in the sky had already vanished.

Yet those few simple sentences left everyone reeling, especially the Void King Realm masters.

A higher world, a more wondrous path of Martial Dao! It was like dangling a carrot before a donkey, leaving them drooling with desire.

Most of the Void King Realm masters in Great Wilderness Star Domain knew of the Star Realm's existence. After all, Great Wilderness Star Domain had always had a path connected to the Star Realm. Several dozen years ago, the previous Sect Master of Netherworld Sect, Yin Lesheng, had entered that place with several Star Domain experts; no one knew what had become of them in the end.

But there was no denying that taking that passage was certain to be fraught with danger. The slightest carelessness could lead to one's body perishing and Dao vanishing.

So even though that passage had always existed, the cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain did not dare pass through it unless they had tremendous confidence in their own strength.

Now, however, another opportunity had appeared before them.

It seemed that as long as they gained the approval of this world's master, they could be led into the Star Realm.
