## Chapter 3321 — Tricked

After washing up at Ling Yinqin's place, changing into clean clothes, and meditating through the night to regulate his breathing, he finally walked out at dawn the next day, refreshed from head to toe.

Ling Yinqin and Lan He had kept watch outside all night, and both women were stunned when they saw him. His astonishing recovery left them deeply shaken. Yesterday, after Yang Kai's great battle with Cang Mo, there had been almost no part of his body left uninjured, yet after only one night, he showed no outward trace of injury at all.

Such a self-healing ability was truly beyond belief.

"Young Master!" The flashy old man was also waiting outside. A strange light flickered in his eyes when he saw Yang Kai appear, and he greeted him respectfully.

Yang Kai glanced at him and chuckled.

A night of rest had also given him time to think through many things. Although he still did not know exactly who this old man was, or which Divine Spirit he might be, Yang Kai was certain of one thing: the only reason the old man had lowered himself to call him Young Master was because he wanted Yang Kai's protection!

Yesterday, the old man himself had said that Li Wuyi wanted to take him back to Spirit Beast Island, yet he had told Li Wuyi to ask whether Yang Kai agreed first.

This sort of thing... was it any of his business? If Li Wuyi really wanted to take him back to Spirit Beast Island, Yang Kai would approve a hundred times over. Just from his appearance alone, this old fellow did not look like anything good. If he kept following Yang Kai around, who knew whether he would commit some outrageous evil and ruin Yang Kai's reputation? And with the strength he had shown before, Yang Kai had no way to restrain him right now.

So although being called Young Master did sound rather pleasant, Yang Kai would never accept such a hot potato.

He did not have the ability to protect him in the first place, and even if he did, there was no need.

Yang Kai smiled meaningfully, but the old man seemed not to notice and asked, "What instructions does Young Master have?"

"I wouldn't dare, I wouldn't dare!" Yang Kai waved his hand, then turned to Lan He. "Where are the two Seniors now?"

Lan He said, "The two Seniors stayed in Heavenly Wolf Valley last night. They should be at the guest hall now."

"Take me there."

Lan He nodded and immediately led Yang Kai to pay his respects to Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng. Ling Yinqin did not follow. She had no acquaintance with Li Wuyi or Jiu Feng, and the gap in their status was great, so naturally she had neither the standing nor the need to go.

The two of them were not in the guest hall, but in a pavilion atop a mountain peak.

Lan He led Yang Kai along a winding route for quite some time before they found Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng leaning against the pavilion railing, gazing into the distance. Lan He stopped far away, while Yang Kai flew straight over. The flashy old man followed close behind, putting on the bearing of a loyal and devoted guard.

Landing in the pavilion, he solemnly cupped his fists. "This Junior Yang Kai greets the two Seniors."

Li Wuyi raised a brow, and Jiu Feng smiled. "How is it that yesterday you kept calling us Elder Sister and Eldest Brother, yet today we have become Seniors again?"

Yang Kai chuckled. "Yesterday was yesterday, and today is today. Yesterday, the two Seniors deliberately let this Junior climb above his station and raised this Junior's status so that my powerful enemy would know to retreat. Yang Kai understood your intentions perfectly and is endlessly grateful. Today, since that powerful enemy has already left, naturally I cannot keep doing that." He paused, then added, "Besides, Xiaoqi and I call each other brother and sister, while she calls you Uncle and Auntie. If I continued treating the two Seniors as peers, wouldn't that throw the generations into chaos? How should I address Xiaoqi the next time I see her?"

"You really do know too much." Jiu Feng gave him a reproachful look.

Li Wuyi said, "It is only a form of address, a trivial matter. There is no need to take it to heart. By the way, didn't you go to the lower Star Domain? When did you return?"

"I only returned recently."

Li Wuyi's expression turned solemn. "Did you find Wu Kuang?"

As soon as the name Wu Kuang was spoken, even the flashy old man, who had been standing quietly to the side, could not help raising a brow, a chill running through him. Although he did not come from the same era as Wu Kuang, that did not stop him from knowing how terrifying Wu Kuang was.

Yang Kai sighed. "I did see him, but I had no real way to deal with him."

He had been in such a hurry to return to the lower Star Domain because he was afraid Wu Kuang would go to Hengluo Star Domain and wreak havoc. Now that Yang Kai had refined the Star Domain Origin, Wu Kuang no longer had that chance. Even he would be suppressed by the World Laws in a lower Star Domain unless he refined the Star Domain Origin first.

He pondered for a moment, then said, "Moreover, Wu Kuang's current situation is somewhat special. Even if I really had the ability to deal with him, I would not know what to do."

Li Wuyi clearly understood his concern and nodded. "It is indeed difficult."

Two souls sharing one body would be one thing if it were an ordinary person, but the problem was that the body belonged to the Mortal Great Emperor. If there really were a chance, they could not very well kill the Mortal Great Emperor along with Wu Kuang, could they? After thinking it over, Yang Kai felt this matter still depended on the Mortal Great Emperor himself. Unless he expelled Wu Kuang's Divine Soul first, anyone under Heaven would have a headache facing the two of them.

And if this matter were truly traced back to its source, Yang Kai was still the chief culprit.

Back then, when the Shattered Star Sea opened, the Mortal Great Emperor crippled his own cultivation to enter it and search for the hiding place of Wu Kuang's Divine Soul. He had originally had a chance to perish together with him, but who could have expected Yang Kai to barge in and stir up trouble as well? Through all sorts of strange coincidences, the two did not end in mutual destruction. Instead, the Divine Souls of the two Great Emperors became entangled, and that entanglement had continued until now.

Li Wuyi suddenly smiled. "When the sky falls, the tall ones will hold it up. Let us not speak of this matter."

Matters involving Great Emperors should be left for the Great Emperors to worry over. It was useless for them to dwell on it.

Yang Kai agreed wholeheartedly and said, "I still have not asked the two of you. After I left that day, did anything happen over on Dragon Isle?"

Jiu Feng smiled. "You want to ask about that Dragon Girl, don't you?"

Yang Kai coughed lightly. "Please enlighten me, Senior Jiu Feng."

Jiu Feng's expression dimmed. "What good ending could there be? The Dragon Clan is proud and rejects outsiders. What that Dragon Girl did was precisely the one thing the Dragon Clan cannot tolerate. She has already been exiled into the Dragon Tomb and left to fend for herself."

"They are courting death!" Yang Kai flew into a rage, and monstrous murderous intent roared out of him like a tsunami. Even Jiu Feng stared at him in surprise, and the flashy old man likewise shot Yang Kai a startled glance. For someone of his cultivation to release such murderous intent was truly rare. And what was this about a Dragon Girl? She seemed to be this boy's reverse scale.

Could this fellow have an affair with some Dragon Girl? Now that was interesting. No wonder he could transform into a thirty-zhang Half-Dragon body. From the looks of it, he had obtained some Dragon Bloodline inheritance from the Dragon Clan. Tsk tsk, that kind of opportunity was truly nothing to scoff at.

Li Wuyi said, his head aching, "Do not listen to this crazy woman's nonsense."

"Hm?" Yang Kai froze. When he looked back at Jiu Feng, he saw her watching him teasingly, her smile full of meaning.

He had been tricked!

Yang Kai could not help rolling his eyes. "Senior Jiu Feng, does Lord Beast Great Emperor know you are this mischievous?"

Jiu Feng also rolled her eyes. "What the hell does it have to do with him?"

Yang Kai simply ignored her and turned to Li Wuyi. "Senior Li, the Dragon Clan did not do anything to Zhu Qing, did they?"

Li Wuyi said, "What could they do? She was merely punished to remain on Dragon Isle in seclusion and reflect on her mistakes. After all, she is also an Elder of the Dragon Clan now." Although he said this, Li Wuyi still felt that Zhu Qing had received such a light punishment only because the Dragon Clan had given Yang Kai face.

Fu Xuan was also an Elder of the Dragon Clan, yet back then, after her matter with the Beast Great Emperor came to light, she had still been exiled into the Dragon Tomb.

But... could Yang Kai have more face than the Beast Great Emperor? What kind of logic was that?

"At least they know what is good for them." Yang Kai snorted. Being confined to reflect on her mistakes could not be considered too severe a punishment. If Zhu Qing really had been exiled into the Dragon Tomb, he might have had to turn Dragon Isle upside down again this time. Suddenly, he asked, "What about the Third Elder?"

The Dragon Clan's Third Elder, Fu Xuan, was Mo Xiaoqi's birth mother and the Beast Great Emperor's wife. Speaking of which, the two of them were also a pair of ill-fated lovers. The Beast Great Emperor commanded the myriad beasts of the Star Realm, and when his orders were given, none dared disobey. Strictly speaking, the Dragon Clan could also be considered a kind of Yao Beast, only of a much higher grade. Since the Beast Great Emperor wanted to command the myriad beasts, naturally he would not fail to set his sights on the Dragon Clan. If he wanted to subdue a Dragon, then with both sides in the Eastern Territory, he naturally had the advantage of proximity.

After much planning and waiting, the Beast Great Emperor had indeed subdued a Dragon, but not through simple conquest. Instead, he had subdued her in another way, to the point that the two of them even had a daughter.

Whenever Yang Kai thought of this, he could not help admiring the Beast Great Emperor.

Logically speaking, the higher one's cultivation, the lower the chance of producing offspring. And when a Human and a Dragon Clan member came together, the chance was lower still. With both factors combined, there was almost no possibility at all.

Even so, Mo Xiaoqi had still been born. It had to be said that she was absolutely an anomaly.

It was just that when Yang Kai had spent time with Mo Xiaoqi in the past, he had never discovered any Dragon Clan bloodline in her body. He did not know whether she had never possessed it in the first place, or whether the Beast Great Emperor had used some supreme Divine Ability to seal it away.

"The Mistress..." Li Wuyi thought of Fu Xuan, remembering the scene from that day, and smiled slightly. "She is keeping Zhu Qing company."

After Yang Kai left that day, the dragons of Dragon Isle were exhausted in both body and mind. After all, they had just gone through an earth-shaking battle, and Wu Kuang had even seized the chance to kill a dragon. Their losses could be called enormous. The Dragon Clan's numbers were already sparse, often with no new members born for several hundred or even a thousand years, so the death of any one of them was a huge loss.

The Beast Great Emperor had originally wanted to seize this chance to take Fu Xuan away from Dragon Isle and return to Spirit Beast Island. As long as they made it back to Spirit Beast Island, the Dragon Clan would have no real way to deal with him.

But the Second Elder, Fu Zhun, resolutely refused. Yang Kai had experienced that woman's temperament himself: foul and stubborn, as if she had been denied happiness for dozens of lifetimes. With her obstructing him from the side, the Beast Great Emperor naturally flew into a towering rage, and at the slightest disagreement, the two sides nearly fought another great battle.

In the end, Fu Xuan herself spoke up and asked to stay behind.

However, as a compromise, Fu Zhun no longer mentioned exiling Fu Xuan into the Dragon Tomb. Now, the Dragon Clan's two Elders were both confined on Dragon Isle to reflect on their mistakes, all because they had committed the same offense.
