## Chapter 3312 — Strike First

Cang Mo nodded slightly. "Sharp-minded. Not bad, not bad."

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Thanks for the praise. I also know your attainments in Space Force can't be very high. They're just... average."

"You can tell even that?" A hint of surprise crossed Cang Mo's face. Just as Yang Kai had said, he had indeed cultivated Space Force, but he had only barely begun. He could just manage to grasp a trace of its mysteries, and he was still far from seeing through the Laws of Space. This was not because his aptitude was lacking. Since he could cultivate to the level of a Quasi-Great Emperor, how could his aptitude possibly be poor? The Laws of Space were simply too profound. They were not something one could cultivate just because one's aptitude was good enough. Sheng Yuzhu had been trapped in the Void Crack, keeping company with void undercurrents day and night, yet after tens of thousands of years of comprehension, she too had only barely begun. Could anyone say her aptitude was poor?

Yang Kai guessed that Cang Mo's attainments in Space Force were probably about the same as Sheng Yuzhu's.

"If you were truly proficient in the Power of Space, you wouldn't have needed to go to such trouble sealing off space and isolating this region, because no matter what I did, I couldn't escape from your grasp. Yet you went out of your way to cut this world off beforehand. That means you aren't confident you can suppress me in Space Force." Even facing such a powerful enemy, Yang Kai remained calm and spoke with easy assurance.

Cang Mo could not help finding the situation absurd. After a brief silence, he said, "In that case, you are a clever man. A clever man ought to make a wise decision. So, what is your choice?"

He had turned Yang Kai's own question to Qi Hai and Red Ghost back on him, and his intent was plain. When Yang Kai had asked those words earlier, Qi Hai and Red Ghost had both made the wise choice and given him a wide berth. Clearly, Cang Mo hoped Yang Kai would do the same.

Yang Kai grinned. “My choice… is naturally…” His expression suddenly turned savage. “To beat the shit out of you!”

With a flick of his hand, the Laws of Space surged, and a Moon Blade suddenly shot out to the side. Its target was not Cang Mo, but Xu Changfeng, who was being beaten back by the Incarnation and had no strength to retaliate.

In the short time the two had been talking, Xu Changfeng had been suppressed by the Incarnation to the point that he could barely breathe. Although his strength was considerable, the Incarnation had inherited Shi Huo's Origin and possessed the body of a Stone Puppet. How could Xu Changfeng possibly be its match? The fact that he had held on until now already proved how strong he was. He did not even have a chance to ask Cang Mo for help, because if he let out the breath he was holding, he would only lose faster.

He had placed all his hopes on receiving a helping hand from Wu Heng, who was watching from the side. Unfortunately, Wu Heng had been struck by Yang Kai's Years Like a Shuttle Seal and was suffering the erosion of the Power of Time. On top of that, Yang Kai's earlier conditions had thrown his mind into chaos, so he had no intention of paying Xu Changfeng any attention.

Xu Changfeng was so frustrated he nearly spat blood.

Just as he was hard-pressed on every side, Yang Kai's Moon Blade came slashing over, instantly becoming the final straw that broke the camel's back.

Xu Changfeng's vision went dark, and he almost cursed aloud. The pitch-black sword in his hand trembled, sword Qi raging as he blocked the Moon Blade's bisecting force by the narrowest of margins. But by the time he recovered, the Incarnation's fist, covered in barbs, was already smashing straight toward his face.

Finished...

The thought flashed through Xu Changfeng's mind. He barely managed to mobilize all his Emperor Yuan to shield his chest, and then the fist struck him squarely.

With a boom, his protective Emperor Yuan shattered instantly. Bones cracked as Xu Changfeng's eyes bulged, blood sprayed from his mouth, and he flew more than a hundred zhang before crashing to the ground, his fate unknown.

The entire sequence left the onlookers' scalps tingling. Tang Sheng and the others had personally witnessed the defeat of two Third-Stage Emperor Realm experts from the Eastern Territory. One had been suppressed by the Mountain and River Bell, and the other had been sent flying by Shi Huo. The former probably was not dead yet, while whether the latter was still breathing was anyone's guess.

At the same time the Incarnation blasted Xu Changfeng away, Yang Kai formed a hand seal. From within the Mountain and River Bell, which had been suppressing Fu Bo all this time, a great clang suddenly rang out. A halo spread outward in every direction with the bell as its center, accompanied by a rumbling sound. That heaven-and-earth-suppressing might made even Heavenly Wolf Valley, more than ten li away, tremble.

The Mountain and River Bell flew up again, revealing Fu Bo beneath it. At this moment, however, Fu Bo lay limp on the ground like mud, blood flowing from all seven orifices, his face pale as paper, his limbs twitching nonstop. He looked as if he might die at any moment.

Simply being suppressed by the Mountain and River Bell would not have left him in such a miserable state.

It was just that, in order to deal with an enemy as powerful as Cang Mo, Yang Kai had no choice but to recall the Mountain and River Bell first. Naturally, Fu Bo, who had been suppressed inside it, had to be dealt with beforehand. Otherwise, a Third-Stage Emperor Realm opponent would also be quite a headache.

After Yang Kai drove the Mountain and River Bell's power with all his might, Fu Bo had ended up in this sorry state. Yang Kai did not care whether it killed him or not. Either way, Fu Bo had temporarily lost all fighting strength.

The Mountain and River Bell, newly airborne, charged straight toward Cang Mo. Yang Kai and the Incarnation followed close behind, their two figures weaving past each other as they pressed in for the kill.

Against such a powerful enemy, sitting still and waiting for death was no wise move. The only proper path was to strike first.

"Impudent!" Cang Mo was clearly stunned. He had never imagined that Yang Kai would actually dare attack him. Did this boy not know the gap between them? Moreover, Yang Kai's earlier curse had also angered Cang Mo. How many years had it been since anyone had cursed him? Yet today, some brat who knew nothing of the immensity of Heaven and Earth had cursed him to his face, and in such a crude, vulgar manner.

With that furious shout, Cang Mo slowly pushed a palm forward.

Heaven and Earth shuddered, buzzing without end. The whole world seemed on the verge of collapse. The might of this palm made Tang Sheng and the others, watching from afar, feel as if doomsday had arrived, as if they would die in the very next moment.

Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying instantly paled. Lan He gave a soft cry, and blood spilled from the corner of her mouth. Only Red Ghost's battle intent suddenly soared. His eyes burned as he stared toward the clash, as though trying to rely on his own vision to see through the mysteries of that palm.

But after only an instant, Red Ghost suddenly spat out a mouthful of blood, his expression instantly turning dispirited.

Tang Sheng's expression changed drastically. How could he not understand now that even standing here was unsafe? The strength of Cang Mo's Divine Ability had already surpassed the limits of his imagination. Even the aftershocks alone might kill them.

He hesitated no longer and shouted to Qian Xiuying, "Retreat!"

The husband and wife acted at once. One wrapped up Lan He, whose cultivation had been sealed, while the other grabbed Red Ghost, and they rapidly withdrew into Heavenly Wolf Valley. At the same time, they activated the Grand Guardian Array, and only then did they feel safer.

When they looked up again, an incomparably huge palm print, seemingly vast enough to blot out the sky, collided with the Mountain and River Bell.

Clang, clang, clang, clang…

Dense bell tolls resounded through Heaven and Earth. It had clearly been only a single impact, yet countless sounds rang out, as if that palm had struck the Mountain and River Bell thousands upon thousands of times in one instant.

Layer after layer of ripples surged from the Mountain and River Bell, sweeping out in every direction.

The Void shattered, and the earth split open. Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng, whose lives had already been uncertain, both trembled from head to toe, and then... went still. Clearly, they had been caught in the Mountain and River Bell's aftershock.

The seven or eight Emperor Realm cultivators who had been lucky enough to survive earlier now dropped toward the ground one after another, utterly unable to keep themselves aloft.

Wu Heng looked even more terrified. He drove his protective Emperor Yuan with all his strength, defending himself, and the deep Emperor Yuan around him rippled under the impact of the bell tolls, layer after visible layer.

It was not only Wu Heng's Emperor Yuan that rippled. Heavenly Wolf Valley's Grand Guardian Array did as well. As those ripples spread outward, Tang Sheng felt a wave of lingering fear and secretly thanked his fortune. Luckily, he had acted decisively and retreated at once. Otherwise, this blow would likely have brought disaster down on them too. He and his wife probably would have been fine, but Red Ghost and Lan He, who were only at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm, would definitely have been injured.

Cang Mo's palm carried the will to reign over Heaven and Earth, making all living beings beneath the heavens tremble.

Even the Mountain and River Bell, said to be able to suppress Heaven and Earth, was struck until it swayed unsteadily and flew off at an angle, revealing the two figures hidden behind it.

It was not that the Mountain and River Bell was weak. It was the foundation by which Origin Cauldron Great Emperor had achieved the position of Great Emperor, and it had even suppressed the True Phoenix Fire for tens of thousands of years. The problem was simply that Yang Kai's strength was limited, so the power he could unleash could not compare to Origin Cauldron Great Emperor's.

If Yang Kai's cultivation equaled Cang Mo's, the Mountain and River Bell's power would naturally rise another level.

Two strange hissing sounds rang out. Immediately after that palm, Cang Mo pointed two fingers from afar at Yang Kai and the Incarnation. Two streams of light shot toward them, his manner relaxed and casual, as if he were merely reaching out to crush insects.

Yang Kai and the Incarnation both felt as if they were facing a great enemy, because they were precisely the two insects about to be crushed.

The Incarnation roared wildly. The strange flames burning around it flared even fiercer, and its body suddenly expanded, transforming in an instant into a giant more than ten zhang tall. Hunching forward, it launched the barbs on its back with sharp sounds. Like a rain of flaming arrows, they met the two streams of light head-on.

Hissing sounds rang out without end. The burning barbs touched the two streams of light and turned to powder one after another, scattering down from the sky. It took more than a hundred barbs to intercept them before those two streams of light finally dissolved into nothing.

The Incarnation's back, which had originally looked like a hedgehog's, was now bare.

Before long, however, new barbs grew out, restoring it completely.

Using the Incarnation's attack as cover, Yang Kai shifted through space and rushed to within thirty zhang of Cang Mo. His right eye suddenly glowed gold, and as his vertical pupil appeared, a lotus bud emerged within it.

Bloom, Lotus!

Cang Mo glanced over coldly. The lotus immediately reflected in his eyes, yet it seemed to take root there, blooming at terrifying speed. The price of its bloom was his Divine Soul power, which flowed away like the tide.

A sharp pain stabbed through his mind, and astonishment filled Cang Mo's face.

With his experience, he could naturally tell that Yang Kai's move was an extremely rare and powerful Divine Soul Secret Technique. In all his years of life, he had never seen such a strange method of attack. Caught off guard for a moment, he had actually fallen for it.

Divine Soul power surged in his Sea of Consciousness as he tried to destroy the blooming lotus, only to find it useless. The more he urged his Divine Soul power, the faster the lotus bloomed. Darkness filled his vision, leaving only the sight of that lotus unfolding. Nothing else could be seen.
