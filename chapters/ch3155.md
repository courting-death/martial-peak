## Chapter 3155 — Kill Him

As the people of Nine Heavens Sacred Land and the cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain fought a life-and-death battle above the clouds, a furtive figure suddenly appeared inside the deserted Nine Heavens Sacred Land.

The figure wore a long purple robe, as if Purple Qi From the East had gathered around him, noble beyond compare.

Zi Wuji!

He had not charged out with Gu Jianxin. He had only made a feint, then slipped back into Nine Heavens Sacred Land. Gu Jianxin and the others had their minds fixed on the enemy, so how could they notice such a small move? And although Xia Ningshang's protection kept outsiders from invading the Sacred Land, he could still be counted as one of their own, so its impregnable defenses might as well not have existed for him.

He returned to the main hall with ease, muttering nonstop under his breath as if cursing something, his face twisted with malice and resentment.

A moment ago, he had received a secret order. It put him in a difficult position, but if he wanted to keep his life, he had no choice but to steel himself and act. If he succeeded, not only would he survive, he might even get a taste of that beauty's favor. It would be killing two birds with one stone.

He crept toward the inner hall, doing his utmost to hide his aura.

He knew that a Star Lord would normally see through his movements, especially when he was this close to her. But that assumed the Star Lord was in good enough condition and had the spare strength to sense her surroundings.

Lady Xia was in terrible shape, worse with every passing day. He had confirmed as much earlier from Gu Jianxin, so despite his unease, he still had some confidence that he could accomplish his goal.

After passing through the inner hall, he soon reached a spot not far from a side room. He glanced around the corner and was instantly overjoyed.

The door to the room was actually open, without the slightest sign of a protective Formation.

The heavens were truly helping him!

Before his delight could rise, a clear, melodious laugh suddenly reached his ears.

Zi Wuji was badly startled and froze on the spot.

He recognized the laugh as Xia Ningshang's voice. Although it was still rather weak, it was clearly nowhere near as bad as he had imagined.

What was going on? By all rights, was she not bedridden and too weak even to speak? How could she still laugh aloud? And who was laughing and chatting with her? In an instant, Zi Wuji began to lose his nerve. The order could not be defied, but if his own life was at risk, he still had to think carefully about whether to carry it out.

A thought flashed through his mind, and he remembered who had been speaking with Xia Ningshang.

That little girl of seven or eight! Of everyone who had entered earlier, she was the only one who had not left. It had to be her.

Zi Wuji let out a soft breath. A false alarm after all. If it was only that little girl, there was no need to worry too much. The only thing he had to consider now was Xia Ningshang's actual condition. If she had the strength to laugh, did she also have the strength to mobilize Star Force? If so, he was probably no match for her.

Just as he wavered, Xia Ningshang's voice suddenly came from inside. "If you have business, come in and speak. Why hide outside... cough cough..."

He had been discovered!

Zi Wuji was startled at first, but quickly calmed himself.

Since Xia Ningshang was not unconscious, it was only natural that she had discovered him. He was so close to her, after all. And judging from her voice, she truly was extremely weak. After a brief struggle, he made up his mind at once. No longer bothering to hide, he hurried toward the side room and called out, "Lady Xia, something terrible has happened."

He barged into the side room, showing just the right amount of panic on his face.

Xia Ningshang's expression changed, and she could not help straightening a little from where she leaned against the bed. "What happened?" she asked urgently.

Zi Wuji looked grief-stricken. "Brother Yang, he... he... ah!" He beat his chest and stamped his foot, deliberately leaving her hanging.

Although Xia Ningshang had her suspicions, she still could not help turning pale. With a flicker of her Divine Sense, she shifted her attention to the battlefield above the clouds.

At that instant, Zi Wuji exploded into action. Purple light flashed from both his palms as he brought them down over Xia Ningshang's head. With feigned reluctance, he said, "Lady Xia, please don't blame me. This Zi was forced into this. Rest assured, I won't take your life, only..."

Before he could finish, his expression froze.

A tiny figure had suddenly blocked his path. Her clear black-and-white eyes gazed at him quietly, without the slightest trace of emotion, as though she were looking at a stone, a clod of earth.

For some reason, that gaze sent a throb of unease through Zi Wuji's heart.

"Scram!" Zi Wuji roared. Since he had already made his move, there was no turning back. Little girl, you don't know the immensity of Heaven and Earth, so I'll start with you.

His palm slapped down toward Liu Yan's head.

The strike landed solidly, and Zi Wuji swept past Liu Yan's side, yet he still turned back to look at her in horror, his eyes trembling.

Not a scratch!

This exquisitely delicate little girl had taken his full-strength blow, yet nothing had happened to her. She still stood there quietly, without even swaying.

A chill crawled through him. Had he... gotten something wrong?

Before he could figure it out, a fiery snake coiled around him and bound him tight.

Thus, when Yang Kai finished dealing with the cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain and returned, he found Zi Wuji tied up in a corner, shivering. The fiery snake flicked its tongue, its appearance savage. Flames roiled across it, yet it released no scorching heat at all; otherwise, Zi Wuji would have been burned to ash long ago.

"So it really was you!" Yang Kai glanced at him, as if he had expected as much.

The Third Elder's earlier words had been vague, but with Yang Kai's shrewdness, how could he fail to infer something from them? One look over the battlefield had been enough for him to guess what the Third Elder meant.

Liu Yan tugged at the hem of his clothes and pointed at herself, making it clear that this was her achievement, then put on an expression that said, Hurry and praise me.

Yang Kai rubbed her little head. "Good work."

Liu Yan narrowed her eyes in pure enjoyment.

"Aiya, Little Senior Sister, why are you sitting up? Hurry and lie down!" Yang Kai walked to the bedside, about to help Xia Ningshang lie back down.

Xia Ningshang gave him a faint smile. "I'm all right."

Nourished by the essence of the Everlasting Tree, and with Yang Kai having destroyed the seven Eight Wastelands Sub-Arrays and checked the poisonous spread of the "ink spots," she felt much better than before. She should have been resting carefully, but she was curious about Yang Kai's experiences outside over the years, so she had sat up and pulled Liu Yan over to talk with her.

Liu Yan was considerate as well, telling her only some amusing stories from Yang Kai's travels and not mentioning the dangers he had faced over the years at all. Naturally, she made Xia Ningshang laugh again and again.

"Have you counted to one hundred?" Yang Kai reached out and tapped her nose.

Xia Ningshang said, "Not yet."

How could she have really been counting? She had spent the whole time chatting with Liu Yan.

Yang Kai laughed. "Looks like I kept my word, then."

As they spoke, Xue Yue walked in from outside. When she saw Zi Wuji, she paused. "What happened here?"

Zi Wuji pleaded with a mournful face, "Lady Yue, save me!"

Xue Yue frowned, looked at him, then at Xia Ningshang. Although she did not know the whole story, with her intelligence she quickly guessed most of it. Her pretty face darkened at once. "You dared?"

There had not been the slightest warning before this. It was not that he had hidden himself so well; rather, with all the external threats and internal troubles over the years, and given his identity, no one had imagined he would actually defect to Great Wilderness Star Domain's side.

Thinking back now, however, he did seem to have behaved suspiciously in a few ways over these past years.

"I didn't mean to! I was forced into it too!" Zi Wuji said bitterly.

"It was only a single restriction!" Yang Kai snorted.

He had not examined Zi Wuji carefully earlier, but now that trouble had occurred and he looked again, Yang Kai immediately discovered an extremely well-hidden restriction on his body. Whoever had planted it had used exceptionally skillful methods. Given the level of cultivators in the Star Domain, no one could have detected any clue without a close and careful inspection.

Moreover, the restriction had been planted at least seven or eight years ago. From the looks of it, it had been placed when Purple Star fell.

Zi Wuji said, "If I had any choice, how could I have done something so disloyal? Lady Xia has sheltered us for many years, and this Zi is deeply grateful. But this restriction was extremely difficult to break. This Zi tried for years and found no way. Worse, whenever the attempt was discovered, I suffered terrible torment. If..."

A ray of light shot over, cutting him off.

Horror filled Zi Wuji's face. He thought Yang Kai was about to kill him without mercy, but once the light entered his body, he immediately felt himself lighten from head to toe, as though some shackle had been broken.

His mind stirred, and he stared at Yang Kai in shock.

The restriction planted by an expert from Great Wilderness Star Domain had actually... vanished!

This should have been cause for joy, but Zi Wuji could not feel happy at all. Thick regret swept over him like a tide. If he had laid out his situation honestly to Yang Kai earlier, he might already have regained his freedom. But now...

His lips moved, yet not the slightest sound came out. It was as if his mouth were full of bitter gall.

"Kill him!" Yang Kai said coldly.

"No! Spare me!" Zi Wuji cried out in terror.

At a thought from Liu Yan, the fiery snake coiled around him immediately came alive, and scorching heat began to spread.

"Wait!" Yang Kai said again, giving Liu Yan a look. "Throw him outside and kill him. Don't dirty this place."

Liu Yan nodded and controlled the fiery snake to carry Zi Wuji out. A moment later, a scream drifted faintly back from afar.

Xue Yue shook her head and sighed. Truly, he had refused the road to Heaven and forced his way through the Gates of Hell instead. With the overall situation already settled, he had still insisted on courting death. Purple Star had suffered blow after blow for several dozen years now, and after this, it would probably never recover.

Although Hengluo Commerce Guild's situation was not good either, its foundation was still intact. As long as it could recover those Cultivation Stars, there would still come a day when it could rise again.

"Little Senior Sister, you should rest!" Yang Kai held Xia Ningshang's soft hand and laid her back down, allowing no argument.

Xia Ningshang looked helpless and could only let him have his way, but her cool little hand held tightly to Yang Kai's, refusing to let go, as if he would disappear the moment she released him.

Her red lips moved, but before she could speak, Yang Kai said, "I'll stay here with you. I won't go anywhere."

Xia Ningshang nodded, only then closing her eyes.

Yang Kai turned to look at Xue Yue, then smiled and opened his other arm.

Xue Yue shot him an annoyed look, then walked over with bright, valiant grace and leaned into his embrace.

Liu Yan pouted and walked out unhappily, though she still closed the door very gently behind her.
