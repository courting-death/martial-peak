## Chapter 3188 — Seizing by Force and Plunder

From the moment the feather-crowned man had appeared, his bearing had been cold to the extreme, his eyes hollow and indifferent, as though no person or matter beneath the Heavens was worth his notice. Only now, when Gungun appeared, did he show any interest.

Yang Kai clearly saw the desire for possession in his eyes.

Gungun was the embodiment of the Small Xuan Realm's World Will, and could also be considered the Artifact Spirit of the Xuan Realm Pearl. After devouring five or six Cultivation Stars, however, its body had grown unimaginably huge, and it had even developed a huge mouth, sharp fangs, and a long tail, making it look like an obese tadpole.

Its birth had been pure coincidence, something Yang Kai had never expected. During these days, while he had been moving freely behind the lines of Great Wilderness Star Domain, he had also considered a certain question: did Gungun's existence, or rather the changes in the Small Xuan Realm, conflict with Hengluo Star Domain's existence for him?

He was the Master of Hengluo Star Domain and also the Master of the Small Xuan Realm, making him the master of two worlds in total. Yet in all things, biting off more than one could chew was never wise, and the Martial Dao placed particular importance on this point. Naturally, owning more worlds was not necessarily better. If he wanted to comprehend World Force, he could do so through Hengluo Star Domain, and from a certain perspective, the Small Xuan Realm could not compare to an entire Star Domain.

To him, then, these two worlds had seemed somewhat redundant, and one of them would sooner or later become an idle burden.

But the instant Yang Kai saw the look in the feather-crowned man's eyes, he understood that he had been wrong.

Hengluo Star Domain and the Small Xuan Realm did not conflict with one another, nor was the Small Xuan Realm inferior to an entire Star Domain. In fact, in certain places he knew nothing about, the value of the Small Xuan Realm was likely far greater than that of a Star Domain.

Otherwise, why would this feather-crowned man reveal such an expression?

Yang Kai flashed forward and blocked the feather-crowned man's way. The man's eyes lowered, as if he had been somewhat angered, but that trace of anger soon vanished. He asked, "This Small World is yours?"

Yang Kai naturally knew he was not asking about Hengluo Star Domain, but about the Small Xuan Realm. With this man's vision, seeing through Gungun's essence at a glance was not strange. It would have been strange if he could not.

"So what if it is? So what if it isn't?"

"If it is not, this Lord will take it. If it is, this Lord will still take it."

"Then why ask?" Yang Kai's brow lowered.

The feather-crowned man shook his head. "Naturally, I must ask first. Although this Lord is stronger than you, I do not take things by force!"

"What, are you planning to trade me something for it?"

"Correct." The feather-crowned man nodded. "This Small World is of great use to me. Name your price. Do not worry that this Lord cannot afford it."

Yang Kai shook his head. "Sorry, but it is of great use to me as well." Although he still did not quite understand why the other party was so eager for Gungun, how could Yang Kai possibly exchange it for some benefit? Forget the possibility that it might be of great use in the future; even if it were useless, Yang Kai would still never trade it to this feather-crowned man.

"Leaving it in your hands will only waste a heavenly treasure."

"How is it a waste!" Yang Kai's face sank. Looking down on someone had its limits. He did not deny that the other party's strength was extraordinary and far above his own, but the difference in their years of cultivation was probably just as enormous. If he had not still had some misgivings, Yang Kai would likely have thrown out a line about not bullying the young and poor right in his face. Then again, after thinking about it, he was no longer all that young and had long since left the ranks of youth behind, so he let it go.

"Boy, you should have some self-awareness." The feather-crowned man snapped impatiently, looking for all the world as if Yang Kai should feel honored that he had taken a fancy to his possession. "Trade it to this Lord, and I certainly will not let you suffer a loss. This Lord can decide to gift you that neighboring Star Domain. You may refine and devour it as you please, and I can even hand that Star Domain Origin over to you."

With just a few casual words, he decided the fate of Great Wilderness Star Domain. Who knew what Wu Heng would think if he heard such a statement.

Yang Kai shook his head. "Not interested!"

The feather-crowned man said, "I will help you advance to the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm within ten years!"

Yang Kai glared at him. “What, since you cannot force me to hand it over, you plan to rob me outright?”

Even if he really was a Great Emperor, Yang Kai had seen Great Emperors before, and had even fought side by side with one. How could he be cowed by this man's presence? If he truly angered this Old Master, Yang Kai would transform into a twenty-zhang dragon body and let him learn what it meant for Dragon Pressure to be as vast as the sea.

"Boy, do not refuse a toast only to drink a forfeit!" The feather-crowned man's face darkened, clearly truly angered. He considered his offer sincere enough. A Star Domain, plus advancement to the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm within ten years, would require even him to pay an enormous price. All he wanted in exchange was a mere Small World, yet this boy had unexpectedly refused to agree at once.

Yang Kai grinned. "I refuse both the toast and the forfeit."

As soon as the words left his mouth, the vast power of the Starry Sky pressed in madly, bearing down on the feather-crowned man's head.

Strike first and seize the advantage!

Although he had not spent much time with this feather-crowned man, Yang Kai could already tell that this fellow had little patience. Since Yang Kai had made up his mind not to trade the Small Xuan Realm to him, they would tear away all pretense sooner or later. Rather than passively wait to be beaten, he might as well attack first. Perhaps he would still have the strength to fight.

In an instant, the space around the feather-crowned man turned into a solid cage. Space Force surged and tangled, while Void Cracks darted like swimming fish, weaving around him from every side.

Although Yang Kai was currently only a First Stage Emperor Realm Master, inside Hengluo Star Domain he was the master of Heaven and Earth. Even if the other party really was a Great Emperor, enduring the power of this entire Star Domain would hardly be pleasant.

Yang Kai fixed his eyes on the scene, and his heart lurched. Dumbstruck, he almost suspected his eyes had deceived him.

The feather-crowned man's body was full of openings, yet the space around him bore no force at all. The Star Domain power Yang Kai had mobilized could not press anywhere near him, and merely lingered off to the side.

The precious robe he wore bloomed with starlight, blocking all that power outside. His expression was also faintly stunned, probably because he had not expected Yang Kai to be so recklessly bold as to dare attack him.

Furious to the extreme, he laughed instead. "Courting death!"

If Yang Kai had not attacked, then at most, the feather-crowned man would have seized and plundered. The Master of a Star Domain was not someone he could simply kill at will. After all, his duties did not permit him to do so; otherwise, if others seized on it as evidence against him, he would face endless trouble later.

But since Yang Kai had taken the initiative to attack and provoke him, then killing him would be killing him. Most likely, no one would say much.

As the feather-crowned man slowly raised one hand, Yang Kai instantly felt as though he were facing a mortal enemy.

Then the feather-crowned man drew the sword at his waist and slashed fiercely forward, shouting, "Sever!"

A formless, intangible, yet profoundly mysterious power burst forth. It did not injure Yang Kai in the slightest, but it still shook him to his core, and he cried out involuntarily, "How is that possible?"

Those words had always been the line his opponents liked to use. Only now did he finally understand how those opponents had felt when they said them.

Unbelievable, beyond imagination, completely incomprehensible!

With that single sword strike, the feather-crowned man had actually severed Yang Kai's connection to Hengluo Star Domain!

One had to understand that Yang Kai had spent tremendous effort refining Hengluo Star Domain's Star Chart, its Star Domain Origin, in order to become the Master of this Star Domain. The connection formed through such refinement went deep into his Divine Soul. Unless someone killed Yang Kai, it should never have been severed.

Yet under the might of the feather-crowned man's casual sword strike, the impossible had become possible.

Without anyone directing it, the surging Star Domain power scattered all at once. Yang Kai tried hard to communicate with the Star Chart, but he was actually unable to make it respond at all. In the unseen workings of Heaven, it was as if a set of shackles had locked away the entire Star Domain Origin, leaving his advantage as Master of the Star Domain completely gone.

"You actually dared attack me!" After slashing out with his sword, the feather-crowned man did not press the advantage and kill him. Instead, he looked at Yang Kai with open mockery. "This Lord is a Star Envoy of the Star Court, governing countless Star Domains, and you actually dared attack me?"

"Star Court!" Yang Kai's expression shifted. This truly was a mysterious and long-absent name.

He knew of the Star Court's existence. It was said that after the war of the Great Emperors all those years ago, the surviving Great Emperors had created it to prevent another incident like Wu Kuang's, with the purpose of sealing and suppressing the passages to the Lower Plane Star Domains, as well as protecting and monitoring the living beings there. The Star Court would not actively interfere in the affairs of the Star Domains, instead allowing them to take their own course and live or die by their own fate. But if another Wu Kuang appeared, the Star Court would certainly act in time.

The Star Court was extremely mysterious, and not many people in the entire Star Realm knew of its existence. Back when Yang Kai wanted to return to Hengluo Star Domain, he had asked around in many places, and someone had told him that through the Star Court, he could go to any Star Domain he wished.

But that person had never told Yang Kai where the Star Court actually was.

Speaking of which, Yang Kai had only managed to return to Hengluo Star Domain thanks to Wu Kuang. If Wu Kuang had not borrowed the power of the Dragon Temple to forcibly tear open a Void Corridor, Yang Kai likely would have had to make a trip to the Star Court to seek a way back.

Within the Star Court, experts were as numerous as clouds, and a Great Emperor was stationed there year-round.

It was said that the one stationed in the Star Court in recent years was Iron Blood Great Emperor.

No wonder. No wonder this feather-crowned man could sever his connection with Hengluo Star Domain with a single sword strike, rendering his greatest reliance useless. Since the other party was a Star Envoy from the Star Court, governing countless Star Domains, it was only natural that he possessed such an ability.

The precious robe he wore and the sword at his waist had probably both been specially refined for Lower Plane Star Domains, allowing him to enter deep into a Star Domain without suffering rejection from its World Laws, or at least reducing that rejection.

Yang Kai sensed that this man was not stronger than the other Great Emperors. It was only because of his status, and because he was in this particular environment, that he had given Yang Kai such an illusion.

"Oh? You do not know of the Star Court's existence!" The feather-crowned man read Yang Kai's expression, examined him closely, then nodded. "So that is how it is. You have only been the Master of a Star Domain for a short time. No wonder you are so ignorant of the immensity of Heaven and Earth!"

Generally speaking, in any Star Domain, whenever someone became its Master, they had to register with the Star Court and have their own dossier recorded there. Wu Heng was like this, and so were the other Masters of Star Domains.

Moreover, every Master of a Star Domain was an expert who had come from the Star Realm. Without reaching the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm, it was absolutely impossible to become the Master of a Star Domain. They all went through the Star Court beforehand to obtain the qualifications to refine a Star Domain. Once the refinement succeeded, they would naturally report to the Star Court at the first opportunity, create a dossier, and leave behind their name. This was a kind of restraint, and also a kind of responsibility.
