## Chapter 3153 — Counterthrust

"Besides, they already look ready to break and flee. Why provoke them further? This is exactly why one should not pursue a cornered foe. Brother Yang, please think this through."

"Finished?" Yang Kai looked at him.

Zi Wuji nodded.

Yang Kai ignored him, raised a hand, pointed at the sky, and said, "Tie them down!"

"You..." Zi Wuji froze. He had never expected this man to reject all advice and insist on having his own way.

To a certain extent, his concerns were well-founded. Nine Heavens Sacred Land had gathered a fair number of experts, and their level was extremely high, but compared with Great Wilderness Star Domain's lineup, they were still stretched thin. If they really chased out like this, not only would they fail to wipe out the enemy, they might even lose their own lives in the attempt.

The best response was to hold steady, let Great Wilderness Star Domain's people leave, and give their own side a chance to catch its breath. A gentleman's revenge could wait ten years; would they really lack opportunities in the future?

All his painstaking persuasion had fallen on deaf ears.

He began grumbling inwardly as well. Since you insist on being so willful, let me see who is willing to follow you into this madness.

He was soon stunned.

The moment Yang Kai gave the order, Li Rong and Han Fei answered in deep voices and turned into two pitch-black streams of light that shot straight into the sky, followed by every clansman of the Ancient Demon Clan.

Meng Wuya and Di Mo followed closely behind them.

The sisters Hu Jiao'er and Hu Mei'er transformed into two rays of light, one purple and one cyan, twining around each other as they spiraled upward.

One figure after another rushed out, without the slightest hesitation or doubt. In the blink of an eye, only the people of Purple Star and Sword Alliance remained before the hall.

Had they gone mad? Zi Wuji looked utterly dazed.

Yang Kai lowered his gaze, his eyes piercing through the space. He bent his knees, leaned forward, launched himself out, and vanished in an instant.

"Brother Zi, we should go lend them a hand as well," Gu Jianxin suddenly said.

Zi Wuji turned his head to look at him. "Little Brother Gu, have even you gone mad?"

Charging up now was the same as throwing their lives away. The others were one thing, but Gu Jianxin had made the same choice, and Zi Wuji simply could not understand it. Because of their similar circumstances, he had felt a strong kinship with Gu Jianxin these past few years, so he could not fathom why Gu Jianxin would act so unwisely.

Gu Jianxin smiled faintly. "Without madness, there is no life!" The simple, ancient longsword in his arms trembled without end, setting his blood aflame. He could hardly wait to fight a great battle and release the sword intent accumulated within him.

Sword cultivators walked foremost on the path of slaughter. After hiding away all these years, he felt that even his Sword Heart had been worn down. The situation was far from promising, yet it stirred boundless fighting intent in him.

Wise or not, what did it matter? At this moment, he needed only a great battle to vent himself.

His sword left its sheath with a clear ring, sword light flowing in dazzling arcs. Gu Jianxin became one with his sword and shot out like lightning, while the Sword Servants at his left and right followed without a word.

The corner of Zi Wuji's eye twitched. No matter how shameless he was, he could hardly stay behind alone, so he had no choice but to call Purple Star's cultivators and charge after them. Inwardly, he made up his mind to take only easy openings and absolutely not risk his life. If the situation turned bad, he would run at once.

In the blink of an eye, no one remained before the hall, and all of Nine Heavens Sacred Land quieted down.

...

"Little Senior Sister, thanks!" Yang Kai laughed, his fists striking like dragons as a storm of fist shadows blasted downward.

He had not gone to intercept Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators because he had something more important to do: deal with the remaining seven Eight Wastelands Sub-Arrays. These seven sub-arrays were like tumors growing on Tongxuan Continent. Every moment they remained, they continued to erode the vitality of Tongxuan Continent and Xia Ningshang. Although he had used the Everlasting Tree's leaves and sap to stabilize Xia Ningshang's condition, these seven sub-arrays still had to be dealt with first.

He had meant to rush to the locations of the seven sub-arrays as quickly as possible, but midway through his flight, his body had suddenly grown light. In the next instant, he appeared here, having shifted several hundred thousand li in the blink of an eye.

This was clearly Xia Ningshang's doing. She was Tongxuan Continent's Star Lord, and on this land, transporting one or two people was no problem for her. Although she was still recuperating in the side room, she had clearly realized what Yang Kai intended and used her power as Star Lord to lend him a hand. She was saving herself as well.

Below him lay one of the seven "ink spots." By rights, its condition should have been no different from the largest ink spot Yang Kai had destroyed earlier, but who knew what Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators had done. The ink spot was now spreading madly in every direction, devouring the surrounding land, while an extremely unsettling aura seeped from within it, as though it connected to the Yellow Springs and led straight to purgatory.

Boundless fist shadows smashed down into the ink spot. In an instant, black Qi churned like water dropped into hot oil, triggering an unpredictable chain reaction.

The enormous black spot's expansion suddenly halted, then it violently exploded. A ring of black light rippled out, shaking everything within a thousand li.

Yang Kai withdrew his fists, took a deep breath, and looked down. The ink spot had already lost its shape. Although it had expanded greatly, its color had dimmed.

Whatever methods Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators had used before leaving to make this Eight Wastelands Sub-Array erupt with power, at its root, it was still a Formation. Once the Grand Array was broken, the ink spot no longer posed a threat. As time passed and the world's Spiritual Qi assimilated and nourished the area, this black Qi would disappear one day.

Before Yang Kai could look any longer, he suddenly felt weightless again, as though an invisible hand had lifted him up. When that hand set him down, he had already arrived elsewhere, and below him was another "ink spot" violently spreading in every direction.

Yang Kai repeated the same method!

"Next!"

"Little Senior Sister, don't tire yourself out. If you feel unwell, don't force it. Your Junior Brother's current cultivation reaches the heavens, so hurrying along a little is no problem."

"Right, I forgot to tell you. Su Yan is fine now. I have already brought her back, and she is probably together with Luo'er now."

"Once this matter is settled, we will return to Dark Star together. When the family is reunited, we can have a proper talk."

Yang Kai struck again and again, talking to himself all the while. Although no answer came, he knew Xia Ningshang was watching him and giving him a little help at crucial moments.

Her help undoubtedly saved him a great deal of travel time. Tongxuan Continent was not especially large, but in his present condition, dealing with seven ink spots would still have taken some time.

With the Star Lord's assistance, it all became simple.

However... it also placed a burden on Xia Ningshang. Her condition had only just stabilized, so she truly should not use too much power.

In no more than the time it took to drink a cup of tea, all seven ink spots were eliminated, and all seven Grand Arrays were destroyed.

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "All right, Little Senior Sister. Rest. Leave the rest to me."

A light breeze brushed past, as though something had left Yang Kai's side. He knew it was the wisp of thought Xia Ningshang had used to keep watch over him.

Yang Kai lifted his head. In an instant, his eyes turned sharp as swords, all the tender gentleness from moments ago gone.

High in the sky above the clouds, fresh blood spilled and corpses fell. Figures tangled with one another in fierce battle, violent power surging without restraint.

The forces gathered by Nine Heavens Sacred Land were not weak, but compared with Great Wilderness Star Domain's lineup, they were still inferior. The moment the two sides clashed, they were at a disadvantage. Great Wilderness Star Domain had four Third Stage of the Void King Realm masters alone. Counting Xin Xuanming, whom Yang Kai had killed earlier, that made five!

Although most of them had only advanced recently, the Third Stage was still the Third Stage. How could they be easy to deal with?

Xue Yue tied down one opponent on her own: the Third Elder of Netherworld Sect. Among all Great Wilderness Star Domain's experts, he was the strongest.

Both were at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm. Xue Yue was in the full bloom of youth, while the Third Elder was old but still vigorous. For the moment, they were evenly matched, the power of Secret Treasures and Secret Techniques blooming around them, and neither could do anything to the other.

The strongest against the strongest. It was a choice made out of necessity, and also the best response. If this Third Elder were allowed to free his hands, no one on Nine Heavens Sacred Land's side could withstand him.

Meng Wuya and Di Mo each tied down a powerful enemy, yet both were being beaten into a sorry state. In this short time, they had already suffered minor injuries. Against the techniques of Third Stage masters, they could only maneuver and stall; they simply could not clash head-on.

The Hu sisters combined their twin swords. Although both were only at the First Stage of the Void King Realm, they fared much better than Meng Wuya and Di Mo. Two sword lights, one cyan and one purple, crisscrossed and wove a giant sword net around the last Third Stage of the Void King Realm master from Great Wilderness Star Domain, trapping him for the moment.

Li Rong, Han Fei, Gu Jianxin and his two Sword Servants, Qian Ning, An Ling'er... everyone fought with all they had, keeping Yang Kai's order firmly in mind: tangle up the enemy and keep them from escaping easily.

"You dared chase us out here? I will make every last one of you die!" The Third Elder forced Xue Yue back with one palm, laughed loudly, and rapidly withdrew as he spoke.

Great Wilderness Star Domain's other experts seemed to receive some command and all pulled away from their opponents. One of them even plunged deep into the Hu sisters' sword net and dragged his companion out.

The Third Elder stood in the Void, his hair flying wildly, a smile of triumph spreading across his face. Behind him, more than a dozen Warships lined up in formation, and the mouths of their Crystal Cannons flashed with pale white light, aimed straight at the people from Nine Heavens Sacred Land.

Their flight had only been a feint! Their true purpose was to lure the snake from its hole.

And now, it seemed that purpose had been achieved. The prepared Warships needed only a single volley to blast this group from Nine Heavens Sacred Land until not even scraps remained.

Victory was in sight, yet a stab of unease flashed through the Third Elder's heart. He kept feeling that something was wrong, and it left him deeply unsettled.

He swept his gaze through the crowd, but saw no unfamiliar face worth noting.

Who exactly had broken the Eight Wastelands Formation Core and even killed the Vice Sect Master?

"Damn it!" Meng Wuya shouted under his breath, his face going deathly pale.

The dozen-plus Warships lined up before them were like ravenous beasts that devoured men whole, radiating immense pressure. As the Crystal Cannons flashed, a terrible sense of crisis settled over everyone's hearts.
