## Chapter 3213 — Ji Ying's Goodwill

Pill Patterns were the finest proof of an Alchemist's skill, yet they were something one could only encounter by chance. If a Spirit Pill developed Pill Patterns during refinement, both its medicinal efficacy and its value would double, because such a Spirit Pill would never lose its potency no matter how long it was stored. The vein-like lines on the surface of the Spirit Pill would seal the pill's medicinal power inside, preventing even the slightest trace from leaking away.

If a Pill Cloud appeared, that was even more extraordinary. A Pill Cloud not only possessed the effects of Pill Patterns, but it could even actively absorb the world's Spiritual Qi to nourish the Spirit Pill. The longer it was left alone, the stronger its medicinal efficacy would become. If it were stored for several thousand or ten thousand years, the medicinal power contained within it could only be called terrifying.

Pill Patterns could be encountered but not sought, and Pill Clouds were even less worth hoping for. They were absolute rarities. When Ji Ying refined Spirit Pills, he would occasionally produce one with Pill Patterns, but a Pill Cloud... he had never encountered one even once.

His Fourth Senior Sister, on the other hand, had once enjoyed an astonishing burst of luck and refined a Spirit Pill with a Pill Cloud, but that had only been a Void King-Grade Spirit Pill. At the time, Fourth Senior Sister had already been an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, so refining a Void King-Grade Spirit Pill was naturally effortless for her. Even so, Fourth Senior Sister had still earned Master's praise, and that special Spirit Pill had been treasured and stored away by her, likely to be treated as a family heirloom and passed down through the generations.

For Xia Ningshang, refining a furnace of Void King-Grade Spirit Pills was already the limit of her ability. After all, she was only a Void King-Grade Alchemist. Producing seven finished pills was an excellent result, yet among them, two had actually developed Pill Patterns.

Ji Ying was truly shocked.

One could be called luck, but could two also be luck? This luck was a little too good.

After carefully confirming that there was no mistake, he found that they were indeed Pill Patterns without a doubt. The medicinal efficacy within those two Spirit Pills was more than twice as powerful as that of the other five. Such a result was even rarer than producing nine pills in one refinement.

Ji Ying could also tell that the reason there were only seven finished pills was that Xia Ningshang's understanding of the final Pill-collecting technique was still incomplete. After all, this was her first time putting what she had learned into practice. Given time, once she had fully grasped that Pill-collecting technique, what would producing nine pills count for?

What was truly rare were the Pill Patterns.

Taking a deep breath, he set down the two Spirit Pills with Pill Patterns, then looked at the remaining five and was amazed all over again.

Although he had already expected this furnace of Spirit Pills to be of no poor quality when Xia Ningshang condensed that perfect medicinal liquid, even setting aside the two Spirit Pills with Pill Patterns, the remaining five were astonishingly already Supreme-Level. They seemed only one step away from all developing Pill Patterns.

This Madam Yang was more than merely talented. She was a genius among Alchemy geniuses! She was likely far more outstanding than even Yang Kai.

Recalling Xia Ningshang's performance during the refinement just now, Ji Ying suddenly raised his head and stared blankly at her as an incredible thought flashed through his mind. "Madam Yang... has The Medicinal Spirit Saint Body?"

Since Ji Ying had seen through it at once, Xia Ningshang nodded lightly.

Yang Kai laughed heartily. "Brother Ji truly has sharp eyes!"

"No wonder, no wonder..." Ji Ying smiled bitterly again and again. No wonder she did not need a pill furnace to refine pills, nor even Pill Fire, yet could still refine so brilliantly in midair. So it was The Medicinal Spirit Saint Body! It was laughable that he had just thought Xia Ningshang was deliberately showing off; only now did he understand the reason. When The Medicinal Spirit Saint Body refined Spirit Pills, what need was there for a pill furnace or Pill Fire? She herself was the finest pill furnace, and her Saint Yuan was superior to any Pill Fire.

Turning to Yang Kai, he complained, "Brother Yang, you really kept me in the dark."

Yang Kai blinked. "Hearing of something can be deceiving, but seeing it is believing. What does Brother Ji think of my humble wife?"

Ji Ying said solemnly, "This Ji is inferior!" This was no flattery, but the plain truth. He had suddenly recalled the records he had once read in ancient books. Under the heavens, there were countless strange physiques, each with its own unique qualities. To Alchemists, however, The Medicinal Spirit Saint Body was something they yearned for even in their dreams, because this physique was born specifically for Alchemy. For someone with The Medicinal Spirit Saint Body, refining pills was as simple and effortless as eating and drinking, an innate talent they were born with.

Ji Ying might currently surpass Xia Ningshang in both Alchemy skill and Alchemist grade, but if Xia Ningshang were allowed to grow, not only would Ji Ying be inferior to her, but none of the four great disciples under Sublime Pill Great Emperor would be able to compare with her either. Given time, she might very well reach Sublime Pill Great Emperor's heights and truly enter the Dao through Alchemy.

Yang Kai was stunned for a moment, not having expected Ji Ying to give such a high evaluation. A look of shared pride immediately appeared on his face, and he laughed heartily. "Brother Ji praises her too much. She still needs to learn much more from Brother Ji." After a pause, he added, "Can I take this to mean Brother Ji is willing to accept my humble wife?"

Since Ji Ying had given her such a high evaluation, having him take Xia Ningshang into his Sect should not be difficult.

Famous masters also wanted elite disciples. Ji Ying himself was one of the most renowned Imperial-Grade Pill Masters in the entire Star Realm. Just as Sublime Pill Great Emperor needed someone to inherit his mantle, Ji Ying surely had similar thoughts. But famous masters were common, while elite disciples were not. Xia Ningshang was undoubtedly an excellent choice.

Yang Kai had expected Ji Ying to agree in delight, but to his surprise, Ji Ying frowned instead, looking extremely troubled.

Yang Kai asked suspiciously, "Is Brother Ji still worried about Medicine Pill Valley's rules?"

"No, no." Ji Ying waved his hand. "With such aptitude, and The Medicinal Spirit Saint Body besides, I believe Eldest Senior Brother would be very happy for Madam Yang to join Medicine Pill Valley. He would never turn her away."

"If that is so, why does Brother Ji look like this?" Yang Kai was baffled. For Xia Ningshang's future, he was even willing to send her to Medicine Pill Valley to study the Alchemy Dao and endure the pain of longing for her, so what was Ji Ying still hesitating over? Shouldn't he be overjoyed to accept her?

Ji Ying did not answer. His expression shifted several times, and after hesitating for a long while, he finally seemed to make up his mind. "Brother Yang, I want to recommend Younger Sister-in-law to my Honoured Master."

"Sublime Pill Great Emperor?" Yang Kai was stunned. "Brother Ji means..."

"I fear only Honoured Master is qualified to teach Younger Sister-in-law." Ji Ying's expression was solemn.

To be honest, he very much wanted to take Xia Ningshang under his own wing and teach her carefully. Having such a disciple would bring him glory in the future. But how rare was The Medicinal Spirit Saint Body? He feared that before he could teach her for long, the student would surpass the master. If the master ended up inferior to the disciple, how awkward would that be for him?

On top of that, with Yang Kai's connection in the mix, if he really took Xia Ningshang as his disciple, their seniority would become rather tangled.

That was why he had said this. If Xia Ningshang could enter Sublime Pill Great Emperor's tutelage, they would become fellow disciples, and meeting Yang Kai in the future would not be awkward either.

Yang Kai's face filled with excitement, and he said with great enthusiasm, "Brother Ji's goodwill truly leaves this Yang..."

To be honest, it was not as if he had never thought of having Xia Ningshang take Sublime Pill Great Emperor as her Master, but what kind of person was Sublime Pill Great Emperor? He was not someone one could meet just because one wished to, much less take as a Master. Since Ji Ying happened to be in Lingxiao Palace, Yang Kai had simply come to find him instead. He had never expected Ji Ying to bring this up on his own. It truly filled him with unexpected joy.

He hurriedly said to Xia Ningshang, "Little Senior Sister, quickly thank Brother Ji."

Xia Ningshang had only just arrived in the Star Realm and did not know what sort of person Sublime Pill Great Emperor was, but seeing Yang Kai's demeanor, she knew this person must be an extraordinary existence. She hurriedly moved to bow in thanks, but Ji Ying raised a hand and said, "No rush, no rush!"

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Brother Ji's great kindness cannot be expressed by a single word of thanks from my wife and me. Brother Ji, please do not decline."

Ji Ying smiled bitterly. "I am not declining, only... I do not have much confidence in this matter. If it fails, I ask Brother Yang not to blame me."

"No confidence?" Yang Kai was astonished. "Why do you say that?"

Ji Ying said, "It is like this. In this life, Honoured Master has taken a total of five disciples, and this Ji is the youngest disciple under him, as well as his last disciple! Back then, when Honoured Master accepted me into the Sect, he once said he would not accept any more disciples in the future."

Hearing this, Yang Kai immediately understood why Ji Ying had looked so troubled. Ji Ying did want to recommend Xia Ningshang to Sublime Pill Great Emperor, but the problem was that the Great Emperor himself had once said he would no longer accept disciples. A Great Emperor's word carried enormous weight; how could he easily go back on it?

Only if Xia Ningshang had the aptitude to make Sublime Pill Great Emperor change his mind would there be any hope, and The Medicinal Spirit Saint Body was undoubtedly her greatest advantage. If not for that, Ji Ying would never have brought this matter up on his own. As a disciple of Sublime Pill Great Emperor, he could not possibly tarnish his own Master's reputation. He simply could not bear to see such a physique buried in obscurity.

"So that's how it is!" Yang Kai understood.

Ji Ying said, "This Ji can only guarantee that I will do my best to try. As for whether it succeeds..."

Yang Kai hurriedly said, "Brother Ji, my wife and I thank you in advance for your goodwill. Whether it succeeds or not, we will remember this kindness."

Ji Ying smiled and said, "Since Brother Yang has no objection, this Ji will set off for Medicine Pill Valley at once."

"There is no rush in this matter." Yang Kai said this somewhat insincerely.

Ji Ying said, "You may not be in a rush, but I am." As he spoke, he stood up and strode outside.

"Does my humble wife need to return with you?" Yang Kai asked.

"No rush for now. I will first go test Honoured Master's attitude. If there is a chance, it will not be too late to come pick up Younger Sister-in-law then." Mainly, he had no confidence either. If Honoured Master would not even meet Xia Ningshang, how awkward would that be for her?

Yang Kai and Xia Ningshang escorted him all the way out of the palace.

"I hope everything goes smoothly on this trip." Ji Ying turned around and said this, then his figure flickered and shot into the air. In the blink of an eye, he vanished from the two people's sight. He was in such a hurry that he was unwilling to delay even for a moment.

Gazing in the direction Ji Ying had left, Yang Kai clenched his fist and lightly struck his palm with it, saying excitedly, "If this matter succeeds, Little Senior Sister, you will have a great backer in the future." If she really could enter Sublime Pill Great Emperor's tutelage, then when Little Senior Sister walked the Star Realm in the future, no one would dare provoke her.

The title of Sublime Pill Great Emperor's sixth disciple was so intimidating; who would dare not give her face?

"Junior Brother, that Sublime Pill Great Emperor... what kind of person is he?" Xia Ningshang asked softly.

Yang Kai smiled slightly. "I have not had time to explain this matter to all of you in detail yet. Mm, let us go back and find Su Yan and Luo'er and the others. This is a good chance to tell you about the Star Realm's current situation."

Reaching out, he wrapped an arm around Xia Ningshang's waist and flew toward the main peak where he lived. With his mood greatly lifted, Yang Kai's hand naturally grew somewhat restless, making Xia Ningshang's cheeks flush crimson. She lightly bit her thin lip, her face full of shyness.
