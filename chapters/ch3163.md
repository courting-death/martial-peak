## Chapter 3163 — Refining the Star Domain

Half a day later, the uproar gradually died down. Abandoned Nest was littered with shattered walls and ruins, its corpses piled like mountains.

This den of evil, which had endured for thousands or even tens of thousands of years, had finally been swept clean. Cheers shook the sky as everyone gave voice to the joy in their hearts.

In the dozen years since Great Wilderness Star Domain's invasion began, this was the first victory on such a scale, and it had been an overwhelming one. True, they had only destroyed Abandoned Nest, and they were still a long way from wiping out every Great Wilderness Star Domain cultivator, but today's battle was like a scattering of sparks illuminating the Star Domain, and even those few sparks could set the prairie ablaze.

"I will leave the rest to you." Yang Kai gave the people beside him a faint smile, then drifted away alone. This time, he did not even take Liu Yan with him.

The Incarnation, Liu Yan, Ruan Biting, and Su Yan had all surpassed the limits of the Star Domain. With the many experts the Star Domain already possessed added to their strength, this lineup was more than enough to resolve the Star Domain's crisis with ease. It would only take time.

Yang Kai did not need to stay. He had more important matters to attend to.

"Where is he going?" Ruan Biting asked, glancing at Liu Yan's unwilling expression.

"To refine the Star Domain."

Everyone was shocked.

A Star Domain could be refined too? They had only ever heard of someone refining a Cultivation Star and becoming its Star Lord. They had never known that even an entire Star Domain could be refined. If he truly succeeded, what an astonishing feat would that be? Would he become the master of the whole Star Domain?

No one dared imagine what changes Yang Kai's journey would bring to the Star Domain, nor could they imagine how powerful he had become.

...

In the boundless Starry Sky, Yang Kai drifted along without any particular target or fixed direction. After leaving Abandoned Nest, he flew wherever his steps carried him, constantly urging his strength along the way and leaving his aura behind.

His earlier experiment had proven that the idea in his heart was feasible. Since that was the case, he might as well throw himself into it.

It was a little troublesome and would take more time, but it was still a path forward. During the refining process, he might even discover a more effective method.

Ten days later, Yang Kai had refined a vast stretch of Starry Sky around Abandoned Nest. At the same time, the corresponding area on the Star Chart in his Sea of Consciousness changed color, appearing a little brighter. Compared to the whole Star Chart, this newly lit region was only a drop in the ocean, but it was a good beginning nonetheless.

After successfully refining this stretch of Starry Sky, Yang Kai immediately set his sights on the next.

Day after day, the cycle repeated. Far from finding it dull or tedious, Yang Kai became absorbed in the work. As stretches of Starry Sky, large and small, were refined, and as section after section of the Star Chart in his Sea of Consciousness lit up, he faintly sensed a new, tenuous connection between himself and the entire Star Domain. With a single thought, he could grasp every region he had refined.

If he had possessed such a connection back in the Ancestral Domain, he believed he could have accurately located Hengluo Star Domain without relying on the Star Chart.

The Star Domain was vast and boundless, nurturing billions of living beings. It held dead and ominous lands as well as places brimming with vigorous life. As he refined it, Yang Kai seemed able to experience all of this, and it brought him many different sensations.

One month, two months, half a year, one year, two years...

Yang Kai wandered through the Star Domain like a single leaf of duckweed adrift on water.

As he refined great swaths of Starry Sky, the illuminated area on the Star Chart grew larger and larger, and his refining speed increased along with it. More than that, he also discovered a quicker method of refining.

In an endless Asteroid Sea, Yang Kai shuttled back and forth, leaping from one speeding asteroid to the next and leaving his aura behind.

Half a day later, he passed through the Asteroid Sea. Looking back, he saw it rolling and winding like a giant dragon as it wandered aimlessly through the Star Domain. Wherever it passed, it would scatter his aura along its path, saving him a great deal of travel time.

This was one of the methods he had discovered: borrowing the power of the Asteroid Sea to refine the Starry Sky it passed through. The results were quite good.

Besides this, there was a second method, one that was much more overbearing and direct.

On a certain star, yellow sand filled the sky, and even the clouds were a murky yellow, blocking out the light of the sun and moon.

The star's environment was extremely harsh, with violent winds rising from time to time.

Yang Kai descended from the sky and swept his gaze around. This star was not truly a Dead Star. Although most of its surface was sand and soil, a few lush green patches could still be seen here and there, and strange birds and beasts lived upon it. Sensing Yang Kai's arrival, they scattered in every direction.

This was no Dead Star. A Dead Star had no life, but this place had vitality. Nor was it an aging star.

It was a star in the prime of its growth, slowly giving off vitality. Although there was not much to see for now, if one came back tens of thousands or even over a hundred thousand years later, this star would surely be teeming with life. Even if it could not compare to Dark Star, it likely would not be much worse than Tongxuan Continent.

Naturally, Yang Kai had no intention of waiting tens of thousands or over a hundred thousand years.

He suddenly leapt into the air, then plunged headfirst toward the ground, his entire body spinning like a top.

The earth rumbled, and a passage leading straight underground immediately appeared.

Ten thousand zhang beneath the surface, Yang Kai appeared at ease.

Before him floated an unstable grayish-white mass of energy. At first glance, it looked like the flame atop a candle, dim and indistinct, yet it nurtured obscure World Laws.

This was the star's Stellar Origin. It could not compare with the Stellar Origin of Yang Kai's Dark Star, nor with the Origin of Xia Ningshang's Tongxuan Continent. This mass of Origin Power was like a placenta, still in the midst of gestation.

The Star Domain was vast beyond imagining, so it was not strange for some reviving or growing stars to nurture Origins like this. Cultivators, however, would not choose to refine such things, because doing so brought them no benefit at all and might even cause harm.

Who could say whether such a star would truly grow? If some sudden change one day caused the star to weaken and its Origin to collapse, the cultivator who had refined it would inevitably be implicated.

Yang Kai, however, was not afraid in the slightest. Or rather, he had come deep underground precisely for this still-gestating mass of Origin Power.

If his grand plan succeeded, there would certainly be no possibility of backlash. If it failed, his current strength was more than enough to withstand this tiny bit of backlash.

He reached toward the mass of Origin. The Origin seemed to possess a trace of consciousness and drifted aside, trying to avoid him, but in the end it was still a dead object, and that was the extent of its reaction.

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Follow me, and endless benefits await you!"

Whether it could understand him or not, he seized the Origin Power and urged the Star Refining Art. In less than a moment, he had refined it.

In his Sea of Consciousness, on the Star Chart, the point of light symbolizing this Cultivation Star suddenly flashed and became dazzlingly bright. Soon, however, it returned to its original state, only slightly brighter than before.

Yang Kai observed the Star Chart, determined the location of his next target, and shot straight upward.

Viewed from high above, Fire Cloud Star was brightly lit. Its great cities were peaceful, its common people lived and worked in contentment, and cultivators flowed through in endless streams.

Great Wilderness Star Domain's invasion seemed to have had little effect on Fire Cloud Star. It still retained its former state, prosperous and lively everywhere.

This was not because Fire Cloud Star's cultivators were especially powerful. Although the star's overall strength was respectable, it was still somewhat inferior to the Hengluo Commerce Guild. There was only one reason it had remained intact: this star had a Star Lord presiding over it!

It was different from Tongxuan Continent. Although Xia Ningshang also presided over that place, Tongxuan Continent's foundation had always been weak and could not withstand too much turmoil. Even for a Star Lord, it was hard to cook without rice, which was why Netherworld Sect's people had been able to break in and set up the Eight Wastelands Spirit-Locking Array to poison and ravage it.

Fire Cloud Star was far stronger than Tongxuan Continent. With a Star Lord's methods reinforcing it, Netherworld Sect would have to pay a price if it wanted to invade. That was why Fire Cloud Star had escaped disaster throughout these dozen-plus years of war.

An existence like Fire Cloud Star was no exception in the Star Domain; there were at least five or six such places. Netherworld Sect and Great Wilderness Star Domain's original plan had been to strengthen their defenses and clear the fields, first dealing with the other Cultivation Stars that were easier to conquer, then freeing their hands to deal with the stars protected by Star Lords.

Unfortunately for them, man's schemes were no match for Heaven's will, and Yang Kai's return ultimately caused all their efforts to fall short.

"Which friend has honored us with a visit? This Chi Huo has failed to welcome you from afar. Honored guest, please come over for a chat."

A majestic voice suddenly rang out between heaven and earth, filled with brilliant Heavenly Might. In that moment, all of Fire Cloud Star seemed to fall silent. Everyone looked up, and many people prostrated themselves in devout worship, crying out wishes that Sir Chi Huo's life would be as long as Heaven's.

To Yang Kai's ears, however, the voice boomed like thunder, as if the Thunder God were striking a thunder pestle right beside him. The World Laws even produced a faint repelling force. An ordinary Third Stage of the Void King Realm cultivator would likely have suffered some internal injury in such a situation, but Yang Kai merely dug at his ear with his little finger, grinned, and looked in a certain direction, his eyes flashing like lightning.

Was this supposed to be a show of force?

He smiled faintly and replied, "Sure."

His figure leapt up, turning into a streak of light that flew swiftly away.

The three-thousand-li Flame Mountain was filled with flowing lava. Craters large and small spewed pitch-black smoke, like giant dragons swaying into the Ninth Heaven. The air was unbearably scorching, and those whose cultivation was even slightly lacking could not approach.

Chi Huo's Flame Palace was built atop one of the largest craters.

The palace was towering and grand, crimson as fire. At first glance, it seemed to have been poured entirely from lava, making its master's fiery temper obvious at a glance.

A short while earlier, within Flame Palace, strings and flutes played softly while songs and dances filled the hall. Fire Cloud Star's Star Lord, Chi Huo, was entertaining all the powerful figures of Fire Cloud Star, simply because today was the joyous day he was taking a concubine. As people who made their living on Fire Cloud Star, they naturally had to come and offer their congratulations.

On Fire Cloud Star, nothing mattered more than the Star Lord's affairs, so whether they were willing or not, whether they could make it or not, they had all rushed to Flame Mountain.
