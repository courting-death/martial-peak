## Chapter 3301 — I Can Wait a Little Longer

"So that is how it was..." Qi Hai staggered despite himself, and in that instant he seemed to age considerably. Inwardly, however, he sneered. The True Phoenix Fire was an incomparably precious treasure. Since Yang Kai had obtained it, how could it not have been on him? This was obviously just an excuse. Since you refused to help back then, why bother using this excuse to explain it now?

Yang Kai frowned. "Besides, if your wife was poisoned, you should be trying every possible way to cure her. Why pin your hopes on me?"

Qi Hai said desolately, "There is no other way. Only the True Phoenix Fire can save her life. So the moment I heard Brother Yang had come to the Eastern Territory, this Qi rushed after you without stopping. Unfortunately, Brother Yang travels too quickly. This Qi chased you the whole way but still failed to catch up. Luckily, I heard Brother Yang had come to Heavenly Wolf Valley, so I came here to try my luck." After a pause, he added, "I hear Brother Yang has been asking around for a way to Spirit Beast Island?"

Yang Kai gave him a smile that was not quite a smile. "Brother Qi is remarkably well informed."

How could Qi Hai fail to hear the mockery in his words? Even so, he pretended not to notice and said solemnly, "If so, this Qi may be able to help a little."

Yang Kai looked him up and down. "Brother Qi, don't tell me you know how to get to Spirit Beast Island."

Qi Hai smiled faintly. "This Qi does not know that, but I do know where to find Spirit Beast Island's Beast-Seeking Envoy! The Beast-Seeking Envoy comes from Spirit Beast Island. Once we find him, would we still need to worry about getting there?"

Yang Kai's expression stirred. "Where?"

Qi Hai smiled proudly. "Spirit Beast Island's Beast-Seeking Envoy mainly searches for all kinds of rare and exotic beasts for Lord Beast Great Emperor. Brother Yang, think about it. In the Eastern Territory, where are rare and exotic beasts most abundant?"

Was there even any need to think? Yang Kai almost immediately thought of one place. "Wild Ancient Land!"

Qi Hai clapped his hands. "Exactly. The Wild Ancient Land has the greatest number of rare and exotic beasts, and many of them possess the bloodlines of Ancient Divine Spirits. How could the Beast-Seeking Envoy pass up such a place? My Qi Tian Stronghold sits on the edge of the Ancient Land, giving us a uniquely favorable location. To be honest with Brother Yang, one Beast-Seeking Envoy has lived in my Qi Tian Stronghold year-round."

"What a coincidence." Yang Kai stared at him, then said coolly, "Is Brother Qi about to tell me to return with you to Qi Tian Stronghold, meet that Beast-Seeking Envoy, and conveniently save your wife's life along the way?"

Qi Hai cupped his fists. "If that could be done, this Qi would be endlessly grateful."

Yang Kai nodded and asked, seemingly offhand, "But before that, there is one thing I would like to ask Brother Qi."

Qi Hai said solemnly, "You need not be so polite. Brother Yang, please ask whatever you wish."

Yang Kai said, "The people of Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land are asking about my whereabouts. Brother Qi should know about that, yes?"

"I was just about to mention that to Brother Yang." A trace of shame appeared on Qi Hai's face. "This Qi chased Brother Yang all the way here and also mobilized quite a few of Qi Tian Stronghold's spies in the Eastern Territory. Perhaps we caused too much commotion, and word of Brother Yang's appearance in the Eastern Territory leaked out. Those two great forces heard of it as well, which led to everything that followed. Brother Yang, please forgive me."

"Is that so?" Yang Kai gave no sign whether he believed him or not. With a faint smile, he said, "To be honest, I came to Heavenly Wolf Valley this time to inquire about a path to Spirit Beast Island, but Valley Lord Tang seems somewhat wary of Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land, so he did not grant my request. If Brother Qi leads me back to Qi Tian Stronghold, aren't you afraid those two great forces will come looking for trouble?"

Qi Hai said resolutely, "As long as I can save my wife's life, this Qi is willing to pay any price."

Yang Kai nodded. "Brother Qi's feelings for his honored wife are truly sincere. In that case, there is something I do not understand."

Qi Hai was startled. "What is it that Brother Yang still does not understand?"

Yang Kai grinned and looked around at the surrounding void. "Since you need something from me, why have you joined hands with the people of Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land? You stopped me here and kept me talking to distract me, giving them the chance to make their arrangements in secret. Aren't you afraid that, after offending me, I might refuse to save her out of anger?"

Qi Hai's face suddenly changed, his expression twitching. "Brother Yang, what do you mean?"

Yang Kai ignored him and shouted, "Since you are already here, why skulk about? Why not show yourselves openly?"

"Hahahahaha!" A man's laughter suddenly rang out. "I told you your schemes and tricks could not be hidden from him, but you refused to believe me. Embarrassing, isn't it?"

As the words fell, the void in that direction trembled slightly, and a red-haired, red-eyed young man with an eerie, bewitching air suddenly appeared. He seemed to have been hiding there all along, though no one knew what method he had used to conceal himself. It had been almost flawless.

Yang Kai glanced over, his brow rising. "Red Ghost!"

Although he was not familiar with this fellow and had only met him once in the Shattered Star Sea, Yang Kai still recognized him at a glance. He was Ahan Palace's Red Ghost, the very man Lan He had mentioned before. Mainly, this fellow's appearance was simply too distinctive. It would have been hard not to recognize him.

Red Ghost grinned, looking so much like a vicious ghost that he sent a chill through the heart. "Southern Territory's Yang Kai!" As he spoke, a strange gleam flickered in his red eyes, and he looked eager to act, like a skilled hunter who had finally found prey to his liking and was already flexing his fingers in anticipation of the hunt.

"You little brat, if you keep running your mouth, this Lord will tear it apart!" As that cold voice rang out, another figure appeared on the other side. It was an older man wreathed in dense ghostly qi.

Fu Bo, Sect Master of Netherworld Sect! His seniority was far above Red Ghost's. Although Red Ghost was also an Emperor, he was only a rising star, so Fu Bo did have the standing to lecture him like this.

Red Ghost curled his lip at that, but he did not dare act too recklessly. His own master was not here, and if he truly angered Fu Bo, the consequences would be more than he could bear.

"Sect Master Fu..." Yang Kai looked at Fu Bo and smiled faintly.

Strictly speaking, this was not his first meeting with Fu Bo. Back then, he had even visited Netherworld Sect once, though at the time he had been borrowing Luan Feng's prestige, with Luan Feng accompanying him. Even though Fu Bo knew he had killed Yin Lesheng, he had not dared act too brazenly and could only swallow his anger. After all, Yang Kai was connected to the Divine Spirits of the Ancient Land, so Fu Bo had not dared make any move against him.

This time, if Fu Bo had not received certain information and instructions, Netherworld Sect would not have dared trouble Yang Kai again. But what he had learned had stripped away all his misgivings. This time, old grudges and new hatred would be settled in one stroke.

"Boy, you have some nerve, daring to come to the Eastern Territory again." Fu Bo coldly snorted, glaring at Yang Kai as if fire might burst from his eyes. Around him, one powerful figure after another gradually appeared, all of them exuding a ghostly air. There were a dozen or twenty of them in all, every one an Emperor Realm Master.

Yang Kai said calmly, "Heaven and earth are vast. I go wherever I wish. It is only the Eastern Territory, so what is there to fear?"

Fu Bo sneered. "I hope your ability is as great as your tone."

Yang Kai smiled and said, “Sect Master Fu, you led Netherworld Sect’s elites out in full force this time just to deal with this Lord. Are you not afraid Madam Feng will come looking for trouble afterward?”

Fu Bo's expression changed. Naturally, he knew the Madam Feng Yang Kai mentioned was Luan Feng of the Ancient Land, but that was all. He coldly snorted. "This is a matter of my Human Race. What right do Divine Spirits and the Ancient Land's yao race have to interfere? If they stay out of it, fine. If they truly dare come out, this Lord would like to see whether they still have the lives to return to the Ancient Land."

Yang Kai stroked his chin. "It seems Sect Master Fu has some backing. Mm, since Netherworld Sect's people have come, Brahma Heaven Sacred Land's people must be here too?"

He turned and looked to the other side. Sure enough, a dozen or so figures had appeared there as well. The man at their head was likewise a Third-Stage Emperor, but unlike Fu Bo with his dense yin aura, this middle-aged man wore an indifferent expression and stood as straight as a sword, giving off an indescribable pressure.

"Good!" Yang Kai laughed. "The experts of two top forces are all gathered here. This Lord truly has a great deal of face. But with so many of you here, I wonder how many will leave alive."

Red Ghost was immediately awestruck by Yang Kai. "I've seen people talk big, but I have truly never seen anyone talk as big as you."

Yang Kai turned to look at him. "Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land have enmity with me, so I can understand why they came looking for trouble. Red Ghost, what are you doing here?"

Red Ghost's fiery red hair fluttered as he grinned, radiating a boundless, wicked charm. "Don't misunderstand. I have no hatred or grudge against you, and I am not with them either. I merely came to spar with you. Those fellows from the Eastern Territory were all killed by you, so I truly cannot find an opponent."

Yang Kai nodded. "At any other time, I would not mind sparring with you, but here, today... this is not the time."

"When the mood strikes, one can fight a great battle anytime, anywhere. How is this not the time?" Red Ghost slowly shook his head.

"You misunderstand." Yang Kai sighed. "I mean, if it were not today, I could spar with you. But if you make a move today... I am afraid you will cut a very sorry figure."

Red Ghost suddenly grew excited. "Are you saying I am no match for you?"

"Try me and you will know." Yang Kai smiled faintly, glanced around, and said, "You have all been making arrangements in secret for no other reason than to seal space and leave me no room to escape. I am curious: are you not planning to use some array to isolate this place? Do you need me to give you a little more time? I can wait a little longer."

He could wait a little longer...

Those words were unbearably grating, and the Emperor Realm Masters of the two great forces immediately flared with anger. What did this little brat mean? Did he really think himself invincible under the heavens, as if so many Emperor Realm Masters still could not do anything to him?

When dealing with Yang Kai, everyone's greatest worry had been that he would use his Space Divine Ability to flee. If that happened, then even with so many people present, they really would have no way to stop him. That was why Qi Hai had come forward to delay him, giving the others time to arrange things in secret.

Who could have imagined he would be so arrogant, volunteering to give up his greatest advantage? Once heaven and earth were sealed off, he would have no path to the heavens and no gate into the earth. If he wanted to leave this place alive, he would have to defeat or kill these dozens of Emperor Realm Masters. Who did he think he was? A Great Emperor?
