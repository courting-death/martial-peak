## Chapter 3258 — Few Aid the One Who Has Lost the Way

Amid the whispers, a flash of light swept across the Space Array, and Yang Kai appeared.

Everyone turned to look. Some faces grew solemn, while others filled with suspicion. After all, not everyone had seen Yang Kai before, nor did everyone know he was the Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace. Those who recognized him naturally straightened at once, while those who did not only felt that this young man had an extraordinary bearing and was probably no soft target.

"Greetings, Palace Master Yang." Scattered voices rang out.

Only then did the Emperor Realm Masters who were seeing Yang Kai for the first time learn his identity, and they hurriedly cupped their fists in salute as well.

"Palace Master." Hua Qingsi stepped forward.

Yang Kai swept a sharp, predatory gaze over the hall. Seeing that quite a few people had come, he was reasonably satisfied, and his expression eased a great deal. He asked, "Is everyone here?"

Hua Qingsi said, "Nearly everyone who could arrive within half a day is here. The people from Dragon Tiger Sect and Xuanyun Pavilion have yet to come."

Only a little over two hundred Emperor Realm Masters had gathered in the hall. The entire Northern Territory definitely had far more than this, but Yang Kai had set the deadline at half a day, so Hua Qingsi had been selective in whom she notified. Those who clearly could not reach Lingxiao Palace within that time had not been informed.

This was also thanks to the many Space Arrays Yang Kai had previously set up throughout the Northern Territory to connect with Lingxiao Palace. Otherwise, those able to make it here would probably not have reached even a quarter of this number. It was precisely because those Space Arrays served as transfer points that two hundred Emperor Realm Masters could be gathered in a mere half day.

"Dragon Tiger Sect, Xuanyun Pavilion..." Yang Kai nodded faintly and waved his hand. "If they did not come, then they need not come at all."

Though his tone was calm and carried not the slightest trace of malice, every Emperor Realm Master present still heard the threat beneath those words. They sighed inwardly. Those two Sects would probably never again be able to establish themselves in the Northern Territory. They might even be uprooted by this fellow. They could not help feeling the sorrow of those who shared the same fate, along with anger at Yang Kai's high-handed conduct.

A Sect that had risen only thirty years ago now controlled the future of all cultivators in the Northern Territory, as well as the rise and fall of its Sects. If such words were spoken aloud, likely no one would believe them, but Lingxiao Palace simply had the stronger fist. Moreover, the precedent of Wenqing Sect’s destruction was right there before them, so even if they felt resentment and anger in their hearts, they did not dare show it.

"Everyone, I imagine you are all wondering why I summoned you here to Lingxiao Palace. I will tell you now. First, to save people. Second, to kill people!"

A middle-aged man cupped his fists and said, "May I ask Palace Master Yang, who are we saving, and who are we killing?" With so many experts gathered in one place, whatever they were about to do would be no small matter. Nearly one fifth of the Northern Territory's top combat strength was here. He truly could not imagine what kind of situation would require so many people to move, to the point that even the four top forces had nearly come out in full strength.

Yang Kai glanced at him. He did not know which Sect this man led, but he was only a First Stage Emperor Realm Master and carried an upright air about him. Even meeting Yang Kai's eyes, he showed no fear. Yang Kai nodded slightly and said, "The people we are saving are Southern Territory cultivators. Like all of you, they are Sect Masters and Sect heads of the Southern Territory's various great Sects."

"Southern Territory cultivators?" The hall erupted at once. No one had expected today's matter to involve the Southern Territory. This was the Northern Territory, and the Southern Territory was unimaginably far away. Distant water could not quench a nearby thirst. If Palace Master Yang wanted to save people in the Southern Territory, why had he gathered people from the Northern Territory?

More shocking than that was what those Sect Masters and Sect heads in the Southern Territory could possibly have encountered. It actually required so many people to rescue them. Although they were not from the same territory, since they were all Sect Masters and Sect heads, their strength certainly would not be weak. At the very least, they should be Emperor Realm Masters. If even they could not solve the problem, would people like them be of any use?

"The ones we are killing are demonized people!" Yang Kai shouted, suppressing the noisy discussion.

The hall fell silent in an instant. Every pair of eyes stared at him, and even Ji Yao, who had always been cold and aloof, showed a change in expression.

Yang Kai ignored whatever they might be thinking and continued, "Several hundred Emperor Realm Masters from the Southern Territory have fallen into a trap set by demonized people. They are trapped inside Wuhua Hall and cannot escape. At any moment, they may be corroded by Demonic Qi and fall into the demonic path. Their only hope now is this Lord, so this Lord needs your help to break through Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array, kill the demonized people, and rescue the people of the Southern Territory."

His words sounded utterly unbelievable, leaving everyone dazed. Their first instinct was to think Yang Kai was joking. For one, the Southern Territory was so far away. Who could know what was happening there? Yet Yang Kai spoke with absolute certainty. For another, demonized people were far too remote a concept. They were said to be people corroded by Demonic Qi who had fallen onto the demonic path, but where had that Demonic Qi come from?

"That is the situation. Whether you believe it or not is none of my concern. Once we arrive, you will naturally know. On this trip to the Southern Territory, remember one thing: protect yourselves well. Do not let Demonic Qi corrode you, and be even more careful of Demonic Intent seizing your bodies. If the former happens, this Lord may still have a chance to save you. If it is the latter... you can only rely on your own fortune. Move out!"

Hua Qingsi froze for a moment, then immediately began leading the Emperor Realm Masters present onto the Space Array. Lingxiao Palace's array was quite large and could transmit twenty or thirty people at once. With Yang Kai here, there was no need to embed Source Crystals; he only had to circulate the Laws of Space to activate it.

Light flashed, and groups of people disappeared one after another. Whatever they were thinking, they at least knew better than to raise objections and obediently followed the arrangements.

At the back of the crowd, an ordinary-looking woman and an older man stood together. Both were Second Stage Emperor Realm Masters, and both were Sect Masters of first-class Sects in the Northern Territory. Compared with the four great forces such as Lingxiao Palace and Ice Heart Valley, they were only one step behind. Aside from lacking a Third Stage Emperor Realm expert to hold the fort and having slightly fewer Emperor Realm Masters, their foundations were not weak.

The two leaned close and quietly exchanged a few words through voice transmission.

The woman said, "Lingxiao Palace is going too far. First they gave us only half a day to rush here, and now, without even asking whether we agree, they want us to risk our lives against demonized people. How can they act like this?"

The older man nodded. "Yes, it is excessive."

Previously, everyone had been forced to submit because of Lingxiao Palace's strength and prestige, so they already carried resentment in their hearts. Now they were suddenly being dragged off to risk their lives against some demonized people and save a group of Southern Territory Emperor Realm Masters they did not even know. Naturally, that resentment deepened.

The woman continued, "Does he really think the Northern Territory belongs to Lingxiao Palace? It has only just begun, and already they are acting like this. What will things be like in the future?"

The older man smiled bitterly. "Hard days are ahead."

The woman shot him a sidelong glance. "Do you want to keep being oppressed like this, powerless to resist?"

The older man was startled. "Resist? How do you plan to resist? I advise you not to act recklessly. Lingxiao Palace's foundation is right there. Did you see those three yao? They are three great Yao Kings, each comparable to a Third Stage Emperor Realm Master. How could we possibly be their opponents?"

The woman snorted coldly. "Those who uphold the Way find many allies; those who lose it stand alone. Lingxiao Palace has stirred up the Northern Territory until Heaven is angered and the people resentful. Which Sect has no complaints? Who is willing to be driven around by them? If the Northern Territory's great Sects can unite, what can Lingxiao Palace do?"

The older man said thoughtfully, "You want to reach out to the others..."

The woman said, "For the sake of the future, this is the only way. If Lingxiao Palace knows what is good for it, they will naturally take a step back. If they do not, can they really contend against the entire Northern Territory? Even with the other three families added in, it would not be enough. The key is whether everyone can stand together."

The older man nodded slightly. "After this matter, this old man estimates that everyone will have sensed a hint of crisis. If there is a chance to resist, they probably will not let it pass. Otherwise, with their lives and freedom in someone else's hands, how could they live at ease?"

"After this matter, you and I should go out more often and sound out the others. If there is an opportunity, it will not be too late to discuss it carefully then."

"That makes sense. Then let us settle on that for now."

"Mm, enough for now. It is our turn. I wonder where this is taking us."

The woman and the older man stepped onto the Space Array with several other Emperor Realm Masters. Light suddenly wrapped around them, and dizziness swept over them. They felt as if they were being sent through the endless void. Even with their strength, stars swam before their eyes, and their Seas of Consciousness fell into disorder.

By the time they finally recovered, discomfort had spread through their bodies, and solid ground lay beneath their feet. The woman's face was a little pale, and even her steps were unsteady. A low shout sounded beside her ear. "Do not block the array. Hurry down. More people are coming behind you."

With no time to see who was speaking, the woman hurriedly moved aside, formed an orchid-hand seal, and silently circulated her profound art to ease the discomfort. Suspicion, however, still filled her face.

It was not as if she had never used a Space Array before. She had come to Lingxiao Palace through one this time, and she had often used them while traveling around the Northern Territory in the past. But no transmission had ever been as uncomfortable as this one. This was the first time she had experienced such a feeling.

By all rights, that should not have happened. She was, after all, a Second Stage Emperor Realm Master. How could she have nearly failed to withstand the pressure of a single transmission?

She opened her eyes and looked around, only to see many nearby faces, familiar and unfamiliar alike, deathly pale and still shaken. Clearly, none of them felt well. The older man was beside her too. Their eyes met, and neither of them understood what had happened.

Just then, a burst of whispers came from the side. The voices were not loud, but everyone present was in the Emperor Realm. Who could fail to hear them?

The woman and the older man were both startled and exclaimed in shock, "Southern Territory?"

Impossible, right? This was already the Southern Territory? A moment ago, they had still been inside Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory. How had they reached the Southern Territory in the blink of an eye? Their first reaction was disbelief, but when they swept the surroundings with their Divine Sense, they found that the terrain and climate here were indeed very different from the Northern Territory, as if they truly were in the Southern Territory.

Thinking back to the feeling of that transmission and the records they had seen in ancient books, inspiration flashed through their minds, and a thought neither of them dared believe surfaced.

Together, they turned to look at the Space Array they had just come from, their eyes burning.

A cross-territory Space Array!
