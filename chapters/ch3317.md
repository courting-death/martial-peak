## Chapter 3317 — He Came

His thoughts racing, the old man kept his face perfectly calm and smiled faintly. "Young Master, please don't be angry. Although I can't kill him... I can give him a beating and help you vent your anger. How would that be?"

Yang Kai waved a hand with grand indifference. "Beat him first and let me see. If this Young Master is satisfied, then fine. If not... hmph, hmph."

He also meant to test the waters. Although he could sense how powerful this flashy old man was, whether the man could actually beat Cang Mo was still unknown. Cang Mo had expended a great deal of strength in the battle with him, and though he was far from his peak, the foundation of a Quasi-Great Emperor was still there. He was not someone any random person could beat up.

The old man bared his teeth in a vicious grin. "I will make certain Young Master is satisfied!"

Yang Kai said lightly, "Then I shall wait and see." After saying so, he shot the Incarnation a look, and the two understood each other at once. If this old fellow really could beat Cang Mo half to death, he and the Incarnation would rush in together. When the time came, they would strike fast and hard, kick the man while he was down, and ideally kill Cang Mo on the spot, ending the trouble once and for all.

Yang Kai did not care whether Cang Mo was from Star Court or whether he served Iron Blood Great Emperor. It was not as if Yang Kai had no backing of his own. Moreover, he himself was a Master of a Star Domain, which made him part of Star Court's system as well. His fight with Cang Mo was only internal strife. Others might be wary of Iron Blood Great Emperor, but Yang Kai had no need to worry about such things.

"Who might Your Excellency be?" Compared with Yang Kai's calm, Cang Mo was clearly feeling stifled. Today's battle had gone beyond his expectations again and again. His long-planned scheme had completely collapsed, and now some old fellow had appeared out of nowhere and declared that he was going to beat him up.

Cang Mo could feel that this old fellow would not be easy to deal with, and he even had a faint sense that... he ought to know him. Yet no matter how he searched his memory, he could not recall where he had seen this person before. The old man's dress was so conspicuous and his appearance so distinctive that if Cang Mo had seen him, there was no reason he would fail to recognize him.

Hearing the question, the flashy old man laughed. "There is no need to ask this old man's name. You heard what was said just now. I do not want to kill you. If you obediently let me beat you up so my Young Master can vent his anger, that will be the end of it. If you dare resist..." His eyes narrowed, monstrous hostility surging from him. "Fists and feet have no eyes. If I really beat you to death, this old man would be quite pained!"

"Your Excellency certainly speaks boldly." Cang Mo was a dignified Quasi-Great Emperor, someone who stood almost at the very peak of this world. Aside from the ten Great Emperors, no one had ever dared speak to him like this. The old man's words naturally enraged him.

The flashy old man slowly shook his head. "The people of the world are mostly ignorant, and Quasi-Great Emperors are no different. If you were at your peak, perhaps you could still withstand a few punches and kicks from this old man, but in your current state, hehe..."

The moment his laughter rang out, his body had already turned into a streak of red light and vanished in a flash.

Cang Mo's expression changed. The Thunder Pestle in his hand trembled, and a streak of white lightning shot out, striking straight ahead.

As the red light flickered, the flashy old man's figure reappeared, right in the path of that white lightning. Just as he was about to be struck head-on, he gave a vicious laugh, opened his huge mouth, and sucked in a breath with a whoosh. An extremely terrifying devouring force burst from that mouth, and in the next instant, the white lightning was swallowed into his belly.

Yang Kai was startled by the sight.

Yang Kai knew perfectly well how lethal the white lightning from Cang Mo's Thunder Pestle was. Even his thirty-zhang dragon body had struggled to withstand it, and countless dragon scales had been blasted off. If the old man swallowed that white lightning in one gulp, how could anything good come of it? The inside of the body was always a weak point.

Yet in truth, after swallowing the lightning, the old man showed not the slightest discomfort. Instead, he let out a strange laugh, as if he had just eaten a delicious meal. Then he opened his mouth and spat, and the white lightning actually flew back the way it had come, blasting toward Cang Mo.

What kind of Divine Ability was this! Yang Kai's eyes nearly bulged from their sockets. At first glance, it looked somewhat similar to Guixu's ability. Guixu could also devour another person's energy attack and return it in full, but a closer look showed a difference. Guixu's return did not alter the attack at all, whereas the old man's way of turning an opponent's own methods against him had added his own energy, because a trace of crimson light was now clearly mixed into the white lightning.

The change came too quickly, and the two were too close. Cang Mo was caught off guard and failed to block it. When the lightning flashed past, his own attack forced him to stagger back several steps before he could steady himself. By then, the flashy old man had already pounced in front of him, monstrous hostility roiling out like a vast ocean and wrapping around him.

"You are..." Cang Mo's eyes trembled. He had finally recognized the old man's identity. No wonder he had felt he had seen him before, yet had no impression of his appearance. So that was how it was.

"Divine Spirit!" Yang Kai’s eyes also narrowed slightly as he shouted in a low voice.

Before the old man used his power, Yang Kai had not been able to detect anything, but the moment the old man drew on his own strength, that ancient aura could no longer be hidden. This was clearly the aura of a Divine Spirit. Judging by the feeling it gave off, he seemed stronger than nearly every Divine Spirit Yang Kai had ever encountered apart from the Dragon and Phoenix. The Saint Venerables of the Wild Ancient Land were completely outclassed before him.

What Divine Spirit had this old fellow transformed from? And if he was a Divine Spirit, why was he calling Yang Kai Young Master?

Could it be because of Zhang Ruoxi again? But even that should not go this far. Just look at the way Luan Feng, Fan Wu, and the others in the Ancient Land treated him. Although Divine Spirits dreaded him, they would never set aside their pride to flatter and fawn on him. Yang Kai was truly confused.

As he was thinking this, the old man had already slapped down with one palm. Cang Mo rose up to resist, but it had no effect at all. Just as the old man had said earlier, if Cang Mo had been at his peak, even if he were no match for the old man, he still could have exchanged blows with him for a while. But the previous battle had badly damaged Cang Mo's vitality, and at this moment he could not bring out even sixty to seventy percent of his strength. How could he block the old man's attack?

His protective Emperor Yuan shattered on impact, and the palm struck him squarely in the chest. Blood sprayed as Cang Mo roared again and again, his body flying backward.

The flashy old man clung to him like a bone-deep parasite, his body turning into a streak of red light as he darted up and down around Cang Mo. Every punch struck flesh, beating Cang Mo until he had no power to fight back. In the blink of an eye, Cang Mo had suffered countless palm strikes and kicks. He looked too miserable to behold. Blood flowed from all seven orifices, his face was bruised and swollen, his clothes were covered in fist prints and footprints, and the fury in his eyes erupted like a tsunami.

With a rumble, lightning flared brightly. The flashy old man let out a soft sound of surprise and withdrew.

Lightning flashed all over Cang Mo's body; clearly, his fury had reached its limit. He glared at the old man and shouted, "Evil Spirit, how dare you humiliate me!"

The flashy old man said lightly, "Young Master's orders must be obeyed." Inwardly, he thought, So what if I humiliate you? You should count yourself lucky I have not killed you.

Just as he was thinking this, he heard Yang Kai shout, "Well done! When we return, this Young Master will reward you heavily."

As he spoke, his hands formed Spirit Arts. The Mountain and River Bell spun round and round, its power to suppress Heaven and Earth rising and falling in waves. The Incarnation, meanwhile, carried the Demonic Warhammer, its expression cold as it charged forward alongside Yang Kai.

This flashy old man was so powerful. Whatever his reason for calling Yang Kai Young Master, the current situation was favorable to Yang Kai. If he did not seize the chance to slaughter Cang Mo, would that not be wasting the opportunity?

The moment Yang Kai moved, both the flashy old man and Cang Mo were startled. They stared as the two enormous figures rapidly closed in, and for a moment both of them were stunned.

The old man had not expected Yang Kai to be truly determined to kill Cang Mo. This was the first time he realized the boy was actually this recklessly bold. This old man had already said that Cang Mo was from Star Court and served Iron Blood Great Emperor, yet you still dare harbor killing intent? Are you not afraid Iron Blood Great Emperor will come looking for trouble afterward?

As for Cang Mo, he had already suffered a great loss of vitality, and now the old man had beaten him viciously as well. It was injury piled upon injury. If Yang Kai joined forces with the Incarnation at this moment, they might really be able to leave him here forever.

Thinking this, Cang Mo decided not to linger any longer. It was better to leave first. Treasures were good, but they could not compare to his life.

Hiss...

A soft sound rang out, and a crack split open in the air.

Yang Kai and the Incarnation both slammed to a halt mid-charge. They stared at the crack with wide eyes, their faces full of astonishment.

What kind of day was today? Experts rarely seen in ordinary times were appearing one after another. One had barely left the stage before another arrived, and whether anyone liked it or not, they simply barged into the battlefield. Was this damned place some kind of market?

This Void Crack had not been created by Yang Kai using Space Divine Ability. An expert had shattered the Void and come here! In the entire Star Realm, aside from himself, Yang Kai could think of only one person capable of such a thing.

With this sudden turn of events, Cang Mo also steadied himself. When he saw the crack, he clearly thought of something as well. He breathed out softly, and his expression calmed.

As for the flashy old man, the corner of his mouth twitched. In a flash, he arrived beside Yang Kai and stood there obediently.

Yang Kai's expression shifted. Lowering his head to look at the old man, he asked, "You have a grudge?"

The corner of the old man's eye twitched. "This one does not know what Young Master means."

"Hehe..." Yang Kai bared his teeth in a vicious grin, asked no more, and looked up again.

The crack in the Void suddenly widened, and two figures walked out from within. They were a man and a woman. The man was heroic and extraordinary, handsome and elegant, while the woman had picturesque features and outstanding bearing.

As soon as they appeared, the two swept their eyes over the surroundings, both showing looks of astonishment. Clearly, they had not expected so many experts to be gathered here.

After seeing the man's face clearly, the same thought welled up in both Yang Kai and Cang Mo's hearts: sure enough, he had come!

"That is..." Inside Heavenly Wolf Valley, Lan He stared in astonishment at the man and woman who had shattered the Void to arrive here, then said in shock, "Could that be Sir Li Wuyi?"

She was not an ignorant person. Shattering the Void was a terrifying method. A Great Emperor using their full strength could certainly shatter space, but it would never be possible for them to do it with such casual ease. So the moment she saw the man walk out from that Void Crack, she thought of Li Wuyi.
