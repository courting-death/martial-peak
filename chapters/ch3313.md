## Chapter 3313 — Going All Out

Cang Mo was worthy of his status as a Quasi-Great Emperor. Even in danger, he did not panic. He drew in his Divine Soul power, cutting off the blooming lotus so it could no longer draw on his Divine Sense.

Yet at that moment, a flash of blade light erupted from the heart of the lotus and chopped straight down at his head.

In that instant, Cang Mo actually felt threatened. He shouted under his breath, "Divine Soul Imperial Treasure!"

A Divine Soul Imperial Treasure by itself would not have shocked him. With his cultivation and his countless years of life, what treasure had he not seen? Apart from a rare few things in this world that could stir his greed, he truly looked down on everything else.

What shocked him was that Yang Kai had hidden the strike of this Divine Soul Imperial Treasure inside a Divine Soul Secret Technique. Too much was involved in such a feat. This strike could almost be called exquisitely perfect.

Even if someone defended against the Divine Soul Secret Technique and stopped the lotus from blooming, the true killing move was hidden in the lotus's stamen.

This boy's Divine Soul cultivation was actually this profound? How had he cultivated? The Divine Soul power of a mere Second-Stage Emperor Realm cultivator was actually stronger than that of an ordinary Third-Stage Emperor.

Hidden within the Blooming Lotus Secret Technique was naturally a strike from the Soul-Severing Blade. Yang Kai had acted on a sudden inspiration and used the Blooming Lotus together with the Soul-Severing Blade, and the result was surprisingly good.

Caught off guard, Cang Mo took the Soul-Severing Blade's strike directly against his Divine Soul. The defenses of his Sea of Consciousness shook. Although his profound cultivation allowed him to block it, his vision still went black and his Sea of Consciousness churned.

Yang Kai inwardly cursed. Not long ago, he had used the Heaven-Shattering Strike Secret Technique in the Star Domain, and the recent period of nurturing had been too short, so this slash from the Soul-Severing Blade could not display much power. Otherwise, it would definitely have been enough to give Cang Mo a minor injury. As things stood, it had only disrupted him for a moment.

This was absolutely the strongest enemy he had ever faced!

But even a moment of disruption was enough.

In the instant Cang Mo was recovering, Yang Kai and the Incarnation had already pounced in front of him together. Yang Kai clapped his hands together and pulled them apart, forming an incomparably huge Moon Blade that slashed through the air toward Cang Mo's chest. The Incarnation raised a fist covered in barbs and smashed it down toward Cang Mo's head. Its cooperation with Yang Kai was almost seamless. After all, the Incarnation had been formed from a wisp of Yang Kai's Divine Soul taking up residence in a Stone Puppet body, so when they fought together, they hardly needed to communicate to know each other's intentions.

No matter how powerful Cang Mo was, if that enormous Moon Blade and the Incarnation's fist truly struck him, he would probably be heavily injured even if he survived.

But just then, alarm bells rang in Yang Kai's heart, and an extremely uneasy feeling spread through his whole body.

His eyes widened. Without the slightest hesitation, Yang Kai shot backward. The Incarnation also tried to retreat, but its body was larger, so it moved a little more slowly.

In the next instant, hissing sounds rang out. A ring of lightning suddenly bloomed outward with Cang Mo at its center, and everything within a hundred zhang instantly became a sea of lightning. Countless lightning serpents roamed through the void, turning the world bright for a moment.

The Moon Blade slashing toward Cang Mo's chest was entangled by lightning serpents one after another and dissolved before it could draw near, failing to injure him in the slightest.

The Incarnation, which had not fully withdrawn, was struck so violently that it roared again and again. Lightning crawled all over the Incarnation's body, which stood more than ten zhang tall, and its huge frame trembled violently.

"Thunder Laws!" Yang Kai's eyes narrowed as he secretly felt the situation grow thorny.

Dao Origin Realm cultivators comprehended Laws, Emperor Realm cultivators controlled Laws, and Great Emperors themselves were the World Laws.

Since Cang Mo was a Quasi-Great Emperor, he naturally had Law Power he excelled in, but Thunder Laws... were troublesome. Thunder governed frenzy and slaughter. Once that sea of lightning appeared, the hundred zhang around Cang Mo became a forbidden zone of death. Ordinary people would struggle even to approach him, so how were they supposed to contend with him?

The Incarnation had also withdrawn, though residual lightning serpents still crawled over its body. Its scarlet eyes stared at Cang Mo, and it had clearly sensed Yang Kai's state of mind.

On the other side, Cang Mo used the cover of the lightning sea and finally avoided Yang Kai and the Incarnation's killing blow. As he slowly recovered and his vision cleared, a trace of vexation appeared on his face. To him, as a Quasi-Great Emperor, using Law Power to deal with Yang Kai was already deeply inappropriate. An expert like him ought to have finished Yang Kai off with a few casual blows.

Yet the truth was that he had been forced into a sorry state and driven to use his Law Power. Worse, even after using it, he still had not succeeded! Cang Mo found this hard to endure. He stared coldly at Yang Kai and said, "Angering this Lord will bring you no benefit whatsoever!"

As his words fell, he reached out and grabbed, and a lightning whip suddenly appeared in his hand. The whip was formed purely from thunder and lightning, crackling and snapping without pause. It was cyan, and at a glance, it looked as though Cang Mo were holding a cyan serpent that was writhing and twisting.

Cang Mo raised his hand, and the cyan lightning whip came winding forward, sweeping straight toward Yang Kai at incredible speed.

Yang Kai gave ground, retreating again and again, yet the cyan lightning whip clung to him like maggots on bone, impossible to shake off.

Seeing it draw closer and closer, Yang Kai let out an angry shout. His Emperor Yuan surged as he opened his mouth and roared, the sound like a dragon's cry shaking the universe, then spat out a breath.

Looking over, one could see that what came from Yang Kai's mouth was actually a golden breath.

The lightning whip lunged before him, only to be blocked by that golden breath. Under Cang Mo's stunned gaze, the whip fell apart. The golden breath seemed able to cut gold and sever jade, slicing the lightning whip into scattered pieces.

"Dragon Breath!" Cang Mo's brow rose.

He naturally knew that Yang Kai possessed Dragon Clan bloodline. Leaving aside the many methods Yang Kai had displayed last time in the Great Wilderness Star Domain, his current three zhang dragon body had remained unchanged all this time.

But... a Half-Dragon body could still spit out such pure Dragon Breath? What the hell was going on? And that earlier roar had been like the roar of a True Dragon, enough to make even him tremble.

For the first time, Cang Mo felt as though his mind could not keep up.

While he was dazed, the Incarnation had already pounced over again. It stretched out a hand, and an enormous pitch-black hammer suddenly appeared in its palm. Black Qi coiled around the hammer, giving off an extremely uncomfortable aura as it came crashing down toward Cang Mo.

"Demonic Qi!" Cang Mo's expression changed drastically. In an instant, his mind was drawn to that enormous pitch-black hammer. He felt monstrous Demonic Qi rushing at him, actually stirring up all the dark emotions hidden at the bottom of his heart.

This was a Demon Weapon! Cang Mo had sharp eyes, so he naturally recognized the hammer's origin at a glance. It carried extremely pure Demonic Qi and was clearly a divine weapon from the Demon Domain. Judging from its aura, it had existed for quite some time, and was very likely a Demon Weapon passed down from ancient times.

Where had this Shi Huo gotten a Demon Weapon? What puzzled Cang Mo even more was how it could use the Demon Weapon without being affected by it.

In his haste, the sea of lightning reappeared and enveloped the Incarnation, while the lightning whip in his hand also withdrew, coiled back, and lashed toward it.

At this moment, however, the Incarnation paid no heed. Ignoring everything else, it smashed the Demonic Warhammer in its hand down toward Cang Mo's head, looking as if it meant to drag him down with it. Moreover, an inexplicable layer of power coiled around the surface of its body, actually showing signs of assimilating and absorbing the power of the lightning sea. It no longer looked the least bit as battered as before.

The Heaven Devouring Battle Art devoured the energy of all things under Heaven and turned it to one's own use, and thunder and lightning were naturally no exception. Once the Heaven Devourer Domain was activated, what could a mere sea of lightning do to it?

The Incarnation's death-defying posture truly startled Cang Mo. The Incarnation could throw its life away, but he could not. He was still counting on one day advancing to the rank of Great Emperor and looking down proudly on the world.

The lightning sea was useless, so the lightning whip abruptly snapped straight, like a long spear, and stabbed toward the Incarnation's chest.

With a clang, sparks flew in all directions, and the Incarnation was stabbed so hard it flipped over a dozen times in succession. It barely managed to steady itself, but before it could stand firm, the lightning whip had already struck, wrapping it up like a zongzi. Cang Mo's face was cold as he formed a hand seal, and the lightning whip immediately blazed with light. The power of thunder and lightning surged fiercely, as if it meant to electrocute the Incarnation to death on the spot.

The Incarnation roared wildly, pushing the Heaven Devouring Battle Art to its limit as it madly devoured that thunder and lightning energy, filling its body and turning it into strength for itself.

At the same time, a low shout rang out, "Dragon Transformation!"

As a high dragon roar sounded, Yang Kai's three-zhang body rose section by section. Under the dumbstruck gazes of Tang Sheng and the others, he transformed in the blink of an eye into a thirty-zhang monster. That giant stood with his head reaching the sky and his feet planted on the earth. Dragon horns grew from his head, and a glittering golden dragon tail stretched behind him. His hands had completely transformed into dragon claws, and even the breath spraying from his nostrils was a white stream visible to the naked eye, as though it could stir the winds and clouds of Heaven and Earth.

A lofty mountain one could only look up to!

Tang Sheng and the others all felt a deep sense of unreality. They raised their heads high to look at the transformed Yang Kai, and an unimaginable pressure weighed on their chests, leaving them almost unable to breathe.

The fighting spirit on Red Ghost's face had already vanished, leaving only deep disappointment and bitterness.

He realized that he truly had not known the height of the Heavens or the depth of the Earth. Before this, he had actually wanted to challenge Yang Kai and test his strength. How was he supposed to test such a colossal being? He feared Yang Kai could crush him to death with a single finger.

Was this his full power? Earlier, after seeing Yang Kai display the Laws of Space, the Laws of Time, and the Mountain and River Bell, Red Ghost had thought Yang Kai had already brought out every method he possessed. Who could have known he could still go even further!

Leaving aside just how strong he was at this moment, that thirty zhang body alone exerted tremendous pressure. Those with weaker wills would probably be unable to summon even the courage to fight him.

Just how pure was the Dragon Clan bloodline Yang Kai carried? He could actually transform into such a huge monster. Earlier, when Red Ghost and the others saw Yang Kai take on a three-zhang body, they had already been endlessly amazed, but three zhang and thirty zhang were separated by a full tenfold difference.

Thirty zhang was also Yang Kai's limit after Dragon Transformation.

He knew that against a Quasi-Great Emperor like Cang Mo, he could not hold back in the slightest. Even if he joined forces with the Incarnation, today's victory or defeat was still uncertain. If either he or the Incarnation faced Cang Mo alone, they would probably have only one path: defeat.

This was also the first time he had gone all out since advancing to the Second-Stage Emperor Realm. He wanted to know where his own limits lay.

Yang Kai strode forward, the earth trembling beneath him, and looked down at the tiny ant-like figure below. In just a few steps, he entered the boundless sea of lightning. Violent thunder and lightning crawled constantly over his body with hissing sounds, yet he seemed not to notice at all.
