## Chapter 3253 - Sudden Change

A moment later, Yang Kai returned to Wuhua Hall, but what he saw made the corner of his eye twitch. Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array had already been activated. A semi-transparent light curtain, like a vast bowl turned upside down, sealed Wuhua Hall so tightly that not even a drop of water could leak through.

Before he even arrived, waves of miserable screams reached him, making it clear that many people had already met with disaster. Looking toward a certain part of Wuhua Hall, he saw black Qi soaring into the sky as Demonic Qi surged everywhere.

Yang Kai's expression changed dramatically. If he remembered correctly, the place where the Demonic Qi was rising should have been where tonight's banquet was held. Before he left, several hundred Emperor Realm Masters and tens of thousands of elite Southern Territory disciples had been gathered there. Could all of them already have suffered disaster?

But on second thought, that was not right. If that were truly the case, Wuhua Hall would not have needed to lure him away. There had been several hundred Emperor Realm Masters there, including no shortage of Third-Stage Emperor Realm Masters. If all of them had really been harmed and eroded by Demonic Qi, he would never be able to hold them off.

The reason for luring him away was obvious: Wuhua Hall had feared losing control of the situation and wanted to strike him first. His mastery of the Space Divine Ability was no secret. Once the first strike failed, Wuhua Hall would have no further chance to move against him.

Judging from that, while the situation was dangerous, it had not yet reached the point of no return. Yang Kai guessed that Wen Zishan and the others would not be so easily eroded by Demonic Qi. Perhaps they were still resisting inside Wuhua Hall.

His figure flickered, and he stopped before the array. One sweep of his eyes was enough to make them blaze with fury. Inside Wuhua Hall, countless cultivators were scrambling for their lives. They had clearly sensed that something was wrong and wanted to escape this whirlpool of disaster, but with the Grand Guardian Array blocking the way, how could they get out? Every one of them was stopped. Many were cut down by the Demonized pursuers behind them, while others, seeing no hope of escape, hurried back to look for another way out.

Screams, pleas for mercy, and grief-stricken sobs rang in his ears. People who had been full of life during the day were turned into heaps of mangled flesh in the blink of an eye, and rage burned through Yang Kai. As his eyes swept the area, they suddenly froze, and he stared blankly at a spot several dozen zhang away.

A corpse lay sprawled on a small mound. Familiar clothes. A familiar face. But a hole had been torn through her chest, and her organs could be seen faintly inside. Blood stained the mound beneath her red. It had not yet dried. Her eyes had not closed. Her head was tilted to one side, and she lay there motionless. Those once-bright, dazzling eyes were now dull and lightless, all their spirit gone.

"Yu Zhuo..." Yang Kai's lips parted slightly as he called her name, but no answer came.

Dead? The girl who had come to see him again and again over the past month, whether she had a reason or not, had died just like this? Yang Kai could hardly believe his eyes. Her clear, pleasant voice still echoed in his ears. The image of her turning back to wave goodbye when she left remained vivid beyond compare. He still remembered her nervousness when she had brought over his freshly washed clothes to thank him, and the faint expectation in her eyes during their many conversations.

Because he had once acted recklessly in drunken folly, he now feared that too much affection might wrong a beauty. Yang Kai did not dare, nor was he willing, to become entangled with her, so he had shut her out, making a clean break of it.

Yet that lively figure now lay on a cold mound. As if she had sensed something in the unseen, her eyes had remained open even after death, looking this way, looking at him, who had returned too late...

The blood throughout his body suddenly turned ice-cold. Yang Kai looked away from those lifeless eyes staring straight at him, somehow afraid to meet them. He lowered his head, the hair over his brow casting a shadow across his eyes. His fists clenched tight, and he threw back his head and roared, "Lei Gu!"

Several dozen li away, Lei Gu, standing in the void, heard the shout and turned to look. He snorted coldly. "He actually came back."

His eyes flashed, and he sped toward Yang Kai.

Yang Kai's furious roar had clearly reached many of the fleeing cultivators as well. At that moment, they gathered toward him from every direction and stopped before him, shouting through the Grand Guardian Array, "Senior, save us!"

Yang Kai raised his eyes, sweeping them across one panicked face after another, before his gaze fixed on a spot behind the crowd.

Lei Gu's burly figure suddenly appeared. He stood in the void with his hands clasped behind his back, his face cold and indifferent. His eyes had turned completely pitch-black, and Demonic Qi coiled around his body.

The instant he appeared, he gave a light wave of his hand.

Yang Kai's eyes narrowed, and he shouted to the cultivators ahead of him, "Run!"

But how could they run? Lei Gu possessed Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivation, and now, eroded by Demonic Intent, he had become bloodthirsty and savage. An invisible slash swept out horizontally, cutting several dozen people cleanly in half at the waist. In an instant, blood flowed like a river inside the Grand Guardian Array, and the living wailed. A cut through the waist would not kill at once; instead, it let the victims taste boundless terror before death. Their screams and howls added an endless chill to the world.

"You still dare to come back!" Lei Gu stared coldly at Yang Kai and sneered.

"So it really was you behind this." Yang Kai ground his teeth.

Lei Gu scoffed. "So what if it was? What can you do to this Lord?"

Yang Kai glanced at the Grand Guardian Array blocking his way. His boundless fury and guilt suddenly settled, and he said calmly, "You think one array can stop me?"

Lei Gu said, "Then try it."

Yang Kai pointed at him. "If I don't destroy your Wuhua Hall, I am not a man!"

Just then, a long cry suddenly came from inside. "Yang Kai, Wuhua Hall has already become a Demon Den! Go seek reinforcements!"

Yang Kai started, recognizing Wen Zishan's voice. He had no time to bother with Lei Gu and shouted, "Hall Master, how is the situation inside?"

"We can still hold for a while. Go to Star God Palace!"

Yang Kai's heart sank at those words. He knew the situation inside was probably dire; otherwise, Wen Zishan would not have told him to go to Star God Palace. But Star God Palace was far away. A round trip would take several days at least, and by the time he returned, everything would likely be long over. He could only reply, "Rest assured, Hall Master. I have my own plans!"

Lei Gu suddenly grinned. "Yang Kai, this Lord knows your cultivation was not easily won and cannot bear to make things difficult for you. Why not submit to this Lord? With your abilities, your future prospects are boundless!"

"Submit to you? In your dreams!" Yang Kai shouted. His body shook, and he growled, "Dragon Transformation!"

A resonant dragon roar rang out. A glittering golden phantom of a giant dragon appeared, then vanished in a flash, plunging into Yang Kai's body. Amid a series of crackling explosions, Yang Kai's body swelled dramatically.

Five zhang, ten zhang, fifteen zhang, twenty zhang...

Lei Gu's gaze slowly climbed upward, the expression on his face shifting. Disbelief flashed through his eyes as he stared blankly at that colossal figure and cried out, "Dragon Bloodline!"

He finally understood why that person had failed. This Yang Kai actually possessed a Dragon Bloodline. Though it did not seem especially authentic, a Dragon Bloodline that could be displayed to such a degree was clearly not weak. The Dragon Clan's bodies were innately tough, and they had powerful resistance to poison. No wonder that person had failed.

He simply had not expected Yang Kai to be able to display such a Divine Ability. Had he known earlier, he would certainly have made that person take extra precautions. Perhaps then the strike truly could have succeeded.

The Space Divine Ability, and a Dragon Bloodline as well. Lei Gu's face darkened. If this boy was not eliminated, he would become a great calamity.

Before Lei Gu could recover from his shock, Yang Kai suddenly raised a hand and released his Incarnation. With a single thought, the Incarnation understood Yang Kai's intent. It let out a low roar, and its man-sized body suddenly began to grow.

Twenty zhang, thirty zhang, fifty zhang...

Lei Gu's eye twitched. Everything he had seen today was simply inconceivable. Yang Kai growing so enormous was already beyond expectation, but where had this stone man come from, and how was it even bigger? It looked like Shi Huo, but could any Shi Huo be this large?

"Break the array!" Yang Kai's form towered into the air, and even his voice rumbled like a dragon's roar. As the words fell, he and the Incarnation raised both hands high, clasped their fists, and smashed down together.

As if two great mountains had come crashing down, two enormous dents instantly appeared in the Grand Guardian Array's light curtain.

Last time, Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array had been broken by the Incarnation's brute force. Mitian Sect's foundation was not much inferior to Wuhua Hall's, and even the strongest Grand Guardian Array had its limits. Yang Kai had no doubt that he and the Incarnation together could break open this array.

When the dents appeared, Yang Kai's face lit up, but before he could react, the sunken light curtain rebounded at astonishing speed. At the same time, an extremely powerful force surged back through his fists.

Caught off guard, Yang Kai was sent flying backward. His twenty-zhang-tall body crashed away like a collapsing mountain, rolling across the ground and knocking down a swath of trees.

A rumble sounded nearby. Yang Kai turned his head and saw that the Incarnation had suffered the same fate. And because its body was even larger, the commotion it caused was fiercer than his own.

Chi...

As he rolled, Yang Kai forced himself to adjust his posture and finally steadied himself. The Incarnation did the same on the other side. Their eyes met, and each saw the surprise in the other's gaze.

Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array was very different from Mitian Sect's.

Lei Gu also let out a breath of relief, secretly rejoicing at his luck. If not for the array's ability to rebound brute force, then with the strength of the two monsters before him, perhaps no array under Heaven could withstand their wild assault.

"Again!" Yang Kai shouted. He strode forward, seeming clumsy, yet in truth moving with astonishing speed, and arrived before the array. This time, he drove out straight punches.

Hong...

The array's light curtain caved in again, but another violent force rebounded, shaking his arm numb and forcing him to stagger back. The Incarnation's situation was the same.

Yang Kai's heart sank as he realized how bad this was. Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array could actually rebound brute force. However much strength he struck it with, it returned the same strength back at him. It was practically the nemesis of both Yang Kai and the Incarnation.

If Mitian Sect's array had been like this too, the Incarnation would never have broken it so easily that day.

"Laughable!" Lei Gu sneered at the sight. "An opportunity was placed before you, but you refused to cherish it, so you can blame no one else. When my Demon Race army tramples this world flat one day, do not regret your choice!"

Demon Race army...

Yang Kai's heart stirred, but he was too lazy to pay Lei Gu any attention. A dragon-language chant rang out, and a glittering golden phantom of a giant dragon punched forward, striking the array's light curtain. This time, layer after layer of ripples spread across the light curtain, but there was no sign of a rebound.

Seeing this, Yang Kai understood. This array could indeed rebound brute force, but it could not dissolve energy attacks. In other words, brute force alone could not break the array. That rendered the greatest strength of both him and the Incarnation useless.
