## Chapter 3201 — Hello, All of You

This was a peculiar race, one that had nearly vanished into the long river of history. Its people were born frail and had little ability to fight, but they possessed an extraordinary talent for cultivating plants.

It was said that in ancient times, those mighty masters and extraordinary figures loved to subdue one or two Wood Spirits and set them to tending the Spirit Herbs and medicinal seedlings in their medicine gardens. In a garden of one hundred thousand mu, so long as a single Wood Spirit lived there, the quantity and quality of the harvested medicinal materials would rise by a third.

They were nature's favored children, existences of extreme rarity.

Time flowed on, vast seas became mulberry fields, and through the endless course of history, every landscape in the Star Realm had changed. The rise and fall of races was nothing unusual.

The Wild Ancient Land, however, had remained unchanged from antiquity to the present, so many beings rarely seen in the outside world still existed here.

The Wood Spirits were one such race, and so were the Stone Puppets. The two races had supported and depended on each other through the long years, preserving their bloodlines to this day. The Stone Puppet Race could offer the Wood Spirits powerful protection, while the Wood Spirit Race could grant the Stone Puppets the ability to fly in battle. They were indeed poor fighters themselves, but they could make the clumsy, enormous Stone Puppets soar. Without the Wood Spirit Race's aid, the Stone Puppets would be forever trapped fighting on the ground, where they often suffered greatly.

A dozen or so delicate, exquisite Wood Spirits flew toward Yang Kai, gathering around him and fluttering gracefully like butterflies.

"Hello, all of you." Yang Kai grinned.

Both the Wood Spirits and the Stone Puppets were deeply xenophobic races. Under normal circumstances, they had almost no dealings with the outside world. Many yao living in the Wild Ancient Land had never seen their true faces and had only heard rumors of them.

Yang Kai was different. More than ten years ago, he had visited the Stone Puppet Race's territory and had even been received as an honored guest by the Stone Puppets and Wood Spirits. With that bond between them, their reunion now naturally carried no sense of estrangement.

A female Wood Spirit landed on Yang Kai's shoulder and murmured beside his ear, "The Elder and the clansmen are waiting for you. Please."

Her voice was very soft, and her cheeks seemed faintly flushed. Wood Spirits were easily embarrassed. Yang Kai vaguely felt she looked familiar, perhaps the same one who had poured wine for him back then, but he could not be certain, so he smiled and said, "Good."

The Wood Spirits' innate Divine Ability turned every plant and tree within several thousand li into their eyes and ears, so the moment Yang Kai arrived, they sensed him. These Wood Spirits had come to welcome him.

The Wood Spirits led the way. Before long, they arrived at the Stone Puppets' dwelling place. The ancient trees here towered even higher than those elsewhere, and each hollow in their trunks formed a natural house.

Nine enormous Stone Puppets stood in a line. The one in the center looked rather old, his body even somewhat hunched, with a beard like an inverted stone pillar hanging down to his chest. He radiated the weight of long years and was gazing at Yang Kai with a beaming smile.

Yang Kai walked up to him, placed a hand over his chest, and saluted. "Elder!" Then he looked at the half-chi-tall female Wood Spirit in a floral crown sitting on the Elder's shoulder. "Patriarch!"

"Honored guest, we meet again." The Stone Puppet Elder chuckled, his deep voice shaking the heavens and earth.

"Not so loud." Wood Spirit Patriarch Mu Na reached up and plugged her ears.

"I do not dare call myself an honored guest. Elder may simply call me by name." Yang Kai looked modest. A wise elder always deserved respect, especially when Yang Kai's ties to the Stone Puppet Race ran so deep. Whether Xiaoxiao or the Incarnation, both had come from the Stone Puppets. "I have come uninvited because there is a matter for which I must trouble Elder."

The Elder laughed heartily, though this time he kept his voice much more restrained, as if afraid Mu Na would complain again. "Whatever it is, we can speak after we have had our fill of wine and food. Your last visit was more than ten years ago. It is rare for our home to be lively, so we cannot miss this chance."

"Then... I will follow Elder's arrangement."

At a single command, a wine banquet was laid out. Just as they had more than ten years ago, everyone sat on the ground around an enormous tree root, using it as a table. The Wood Spirits bustled back and forth, and before long, the table was filled with fruits and fine wine.

Night fell, and moonlight spilled through the leaves. Strange fruits and melons hung from the surrounding trunks, flickering with multicolored light and illuminating this small world until it was as bright as day.

The Stone Puppets were usually dull and wooden, but now they were swept up in the excitement, shouting and calling without end. They held wooden cups taller than a person and drank great mouthfuls of fine wine, enjoying the spirit melons and exotic fruits cultivated by the Wood Spirits, while the Wood Spirits danced gracefully nearby.

Beneath the bright moon, amid a splendid feast and fine wine, it was easy to forget every care.

The Stone Puppets' names were simple, running from Shi Yi to Shi Ba, but Yang Kai could not remember which was which, simply because they all looked more or less the same. There had originally been a Shi Jiu as well, namely Xiaoxiao, but he had entered the Blood Gate with Zhang Ruoxi and there had been no news of him since.

The Stone Puppet Race was blessed with extraordinary gifts and boundless strength, and few humans or yao could match them. But the Way of Heaven took from the surplus to make up for what was lacking. The Stone Puppet Race was born limited to ten; if their number exceeded ten, one would surely die young.

In other words, throughout all Heaven and Earth, there would never be more than ten Stone Puppets.

There were nine Stone Puppets before Yang Kai now. Counting Xiaoxiao, that made exactly ten. Although the Incarnation possessed a Stone Puppet body, he could no longer truly be considered a Stone Puppet, so naturally he was not included.

The eight Stone Puppets took turns toasting him. Their cups were twice as large as an ordinary household wine barrel, and they looked determined not to stop until they had drunk Yang Kai under the table. Unable to refuse, Yang Kai could only match them.

The spirit wine brewed by the Wood Spirit Race was rich in Spiritual Energy and had a powerful kick. Even the Stone Puppets could not drink too much of it.

Even with his half-dragon body, how could Yang Kai withstand a drinking relay from this group of simple-minded big fellows?

By the latter half of the night, he toppled over stiff as a board. Amid the Stone Puppets' victorious cheers, a dozen or so Wood Spirits flew over, held him up, and carried him into a wooden house to rest.

When he woke, it was already daytime. His head felt dizzy and swollen, and only after meditating for a while did he finally recover. Secretly, he resolved that next time, he would absolutely not compete in drinking with this group of big fellows.

The female Wood Spirit who had welcomed him yesterday was peeking in from outside the door. Seeing that he had awakened, she said timidly, "Guest, you are awake. The Elder and Patriarch are waiting for you."

"I will go at once. Thank you for your trouble." Yang Kai gave her a faint smile, and her little face flushed red again.

What a peculiar race... Yang Kai thought to himself.

The Wood Spirit Race did not only have a unique talent for cultivating spirit flowers and exotic grasses. Their own bodies were of tremendous value. Rumor had it that the Wood Spirit Race carried the secret of undying life within them; if someone were gravely injured and on the verge of death, swallowing a Wood Spirit alive would immediately bring them back from the brink, with all wounds and illness swept away.

Fortunately, they lived in the Wild Ancient Land and had the Stone Puppet Race's protection. If they had lived outside, they would likely have been exterminated long ago. Human greed was endless. If these living sacred healing medicines were ever targeted, how could they escape such wicked hands?

Leaving the wooden house, Yang Kai headed straight for a nearby tree hollow, entered it, and climbed the spiraling staircase. Before long, he saw the Elder in another tree house.

After saluting, he took a seat.

Yang Kai said, "Is there still no news of Xiaoxiao?"

The Elder shook his head. "Since the Blood Gate vanished, there has been no movement at all. I do not know how Shi Jiu is faring now."

Yang Kai smiled and said, "With Ruoxi looking after him, Elder need not worry too much."

Inside the Blood Gate was Heavenly Journey Palace, and sealed within Heavenly Journey Palace were countless Divine Spirit Origins, all obtained when Heaven's Punisher hunted Divine Spirits back then. Xiaoxiao had gone in with Ruoxi to inherit the Tai Yue Source. As long as he succeeded, Xiaoxiao could transform into the Divine Spirit Tai Yue, and his future would be limitless.

In the Ancient Era, Divine Spirits emerged in endless numbers, but even among Divine Spirits there were differences in strength. If Dragons and Phoenixes stood at the head, then Black Tortoise, White Tiger, Qilin, Qiong Qi, and other such familiar names were existences second only to Dragons and Phoenixes.

Tai Yue also ranked extremely high among the Divine Spirits, no weaker than Qilin or Qiong Qi.

Thus, Yang Kai was also very much looking forward to seeing what Xiaoxiao would become when he walked out of the Blood Gate.

Hearing this, the Elder hesitated, as if he wanted to say something, but in the end he changed the subject. "You said yesterday that you had come with a matter to discuss. What is it?"

"It is like this. I want to build a cross-territory Space Array here, connecting this place to my Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory."

"Connecting to the Northern Territory!" The Elder was startled.

"Boy, that will not do." Mu Na flew out from who knew where, landed on the Elder's shoulder, looked at Yang Kai, and shook her head. "The ten thousand li around us are the pure land of my Wood Spirit Race and the Stone Puppet Race. Other than you, no human has ever set foot here. If you build a cross-territory Space Array here, our lives will certainly be disturbed in the future. I cannot agree."

Whether Stone Puppets or Wood Spirits, both were extremely easy for others to covet. Their very existence was a secret. If that secret spread, endless trouble would surely follow.

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Patriarch, please rest assured. I guarantee that the Space Array will be used only by me. No one else will come here through it."

The main reason was that the array he had arranged last time in Luan Feng's palace had been destroyed by Luan Feng. If Yang Kai wanted to connect the Northern Territory and Eastern Territory, this place was his only viable choice. The account he wanted to settle with Luan Feng was precisely over that destroyed Space Array.

"How can you guarantee that?" Mu Na clearly did not want to agree.

Yang Kai said, "Since I will be the one arranging the array, I can guarantee it. Moreover, if anyone other than me comes here one day, you two may kill them outright. There is no need to hold back."

Mu Na still wanted to say more, but the Elder cut her off. "If you can guarantee that, then there is no real problem."

"Many thanks, Elder!"

Since the Elder had already nodded in agreement, Mu Na naturally could not say much more.

"Where do you want to set up the array?"

"Xiaoxiao's tree house." Yang Kai had already chosen. The place where he had rested earlier was the tree house Xiaoxiao had lived in back then. It had plenty of space, and since it stood within the Stone Puppet Race's living area, it would also be protected.

"That works. If you need any help, just ask."

"Good. Then I will get to work." Yang Kai rose and took his leave.

"Come find me again after you finish. I have something to say to you," Mu Na suddenly said.

Yang Kai looked at her strangely, then nodded.
