## Chapter 3265 — Hesitating to Strike

Before entering the valley, Yang Kai had already suspected that Lei Gu was luring him into a trap. The clearest sign had been Murong Xiaoxiao's two screams. Each had been more urgent than the last, and the intent behind them could not have been more obvious.

Yang Kai had naturally put on a show of being "furious beyond restraint" and charged into the valley alone to see what Lei Gu was plotting. Everything after that had simply been him following the situation where it led, and the result was exactly what he wanted: he had hooked the big fish, Lei Gu. Now, however, Gao Xueting had become the thing that made him hesitate to strike.

In the few words the two exchanged, the Demonic Qi in the valley had already thinned dramatically, all of it swallowed into Guixu's belly. The darkness gradually dispersed, revealing the valley's original appearance. Figures stood one after another, while quite a few corpses lay scattered across the ground.

There was no trace of the valley's former greenery. As far as the eye could see, it was a scene of devastation, the ground almost dyed red with blood.

At the very center of the valley, several thousand people had gathered and arranged themselves into formation after formation, surrounded by light of varying intensity. These were the Southern Territory cultivators who had come to attend this Martial Gathering, the group who had not yet fallen to the demonic path.

Clearly, they had relied on those formations to hold out until now, but no matter how strong or weak their cultivation was, every one of them was deathly pale and obviously at the end of their strength. Around them, more than ten thousand demonized people had gathered, surrounding them completely.

The dispersal of the boundless Demonic Qi left everyone somewhat astonished.

Shua shua shua...

As sharp sounds of movement through the air rang out, one powerful aura after another appeared, filling the valley in the blink of an eye. They were the helpers Yang Kai had brought. Seeing Yang Kai safe and sound, Fan Wu and the others all quietly sighed in relief, but their attention was quickly drawn to Lei Gu.

Everyone could tell that Yang Kai seemed to be in a standoff with Lei Gu, while Lei Gu had a demonized woman in his hands as a hostage. They simply did not know what relationship she had with Yang Kai.

"Little Xueting!" Wen Zishan's voice rang out, his face graver than it had ever been. His purple robe flared, and in the next instant he was beside Yang Kai, staring deeply at Lei Gu.

He did not need to ask to understand the situation before him.

Lei Gu's face darkened as he slowly looked around. Seeing experts standing in every direction, he understood that the tide had turned. This plan could be said to have failed utterly, and hatred seethed in his heart. Although he still had more than ten thousand demonized people, their strength varied wildly, and they had not been demonized for long, so he had to divide his attention to control them. They simply could not be of much use, at most throwing the situation into a little more chaos.

"Do you think you can escape?" Yang Kai looked at Lei Gu and said flatly, "Do you believe that with one command from me, there won't even be bones left of you?"

Lei Gu laughed loudly. "Naturally I believe it, but before this Lord dies, I can still drag her down with me. If you do not believe me, you may try."

Yang Kai nodded. "I believe that too." After a pause, he added, "It is still the same answer. Your life is not worth that much to me. Let her go, and I will let you leave."

"You must not!" an angry shout rang out. It came from Ma Qing, the Saint Lord of Tianwu Sacred Land. He was covered in blood, though whether it was his own or someone else's was impossible to tell, and he cut a sorry figure. Staring coldly at Lei Gu, he said, "He is the chief culprit behind this matter. We cannot let him leave. This incident was clearly planned long ago. If we capture him, perhaps we can question him for intelligence."

Yang Kai and Wen Zishan both tilted their heads and looked him over.

Ma Qing glared and said, "If he escapes today, the Southern Territory may suffer another calamity in the future. Star Envoy Xiao, what do you say?"

Xiao Yuyang frowned without saying a word. In principle, he naturally agreed with Ma Qing, and he knew Ma Qing was speaking without selfish intent. For more than a dozen years, the Southern Territory had been encircling and suppressing Demonic Intent. They had thought they had killed almost all of it, and that even if a few fish had slipped through the net, they could not possibly stir up any waves. Who could have known that Wuhua Hall as a whole had fallen without anyone realizing?

No one knew who had arranged such a massive scheme, or what their purpose was. If they could capture Lei Gu, they really might be able to extract some intelligence from him, which would allow them to take precautions in the future. At the very least, they needed to know which other experts of the Southern Territory had been possessed by Demonic Intent.

For that reason alone, they could not let Lei Gu escape so easily.

But at a time like this, he could no longer make the decision. He was indeed a Star Envoy of Star God Palace, and ordinarily he could represent Star God Palace; with one order from him, no Southern Territory cultivator would dare disobey. But now... Xiao Yuyang glanced at the unfamiliar expert faces all around and smiled bitterly to himself.

His words probably carried no weight here. Yang Kai and Wen Zishan were clearly set on taking Gao Xueting back. If he truly opposed them, he would only embarrass himself.

Seeing Xiao Yuyang remain silent, Ma Qing naturally understood what he was worrying about and no longer counted on him. He looked at Wen Zishan and said in a deep voice, "Hall Master Wen, this Ma knows how you feel, but I still hope Hall Master Wen will put the greater situation first and place the common people of the Southern Territory above all else. You are the Hall Master of Qingyang Shrine. Your every word and deed serve as examples to the Southern Territory's countless cultivators. Think carefully!"

With such a heavy burden pressed onto him, Wen Zishan's expression filled with pain. He slowly said, "Brother Ma, if Madam Qi were the one in such straits, what would you do?" Madam Qi was Ma Qing's wife, the one he had married in his youth, and she possessed the cultivation of the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. At this moment, she was standing right beside him.

Ma Qing's cheek twitched several times before he gritted his teeth and said, "Only by giving something up can one gain something!" His voice was grief-stricken, as if he truly had already abandoned Madam Qi.

The moment those words came out, the palace-robed woman beside him could not help trembling, and her beautiful eyes lowered slightly.

Wen Zishan gave a bitter smile. "Brother Ma has admirable resolve..."

"Shut him up!" Yang Kai suddenly gave Fan Wu a look.

Fan Wu's figure flickered, and he appeared in front of Ma Qing, raising a hand to point at him.

Ma Qing was greatly alarmed. The longsword in his hand bloomed with flowers of sword light, enveloping Fan Wu. Fan Wu snorted coldly, and his Primordial Aura erupted. In an instant, Ma Qing felt as if he had been cast into an ancient world, and the Emperor Yuan in his body actually grew somewhat disordered. Even the sword moves he unleashed were full of openings.

Fan Wu's outstretched hand moved like a butterfly through flowers, passing between the blossoms of sword light before tapping a vital point on Ma Qing's chest with flawless precision. Power surged at his fingertip, ready to take Ma Qing's life at any moment.

The people from Tianwu Sacred Land went pale with shock. None of them had expected their Saint Lord to be subdued in a single move. Fortunately, the other party had no intention of killing him; otherwise, Ma Qing would already be dead.

True, the Saint Lord had exhausted a great deal of strength earlier and was now at the end of his rope, but the foundation of a Third Stage Emperor Realm cultivator was still there. How could he have been defeated so easily?

"Divine Spirit!" Ma Qing's eyes trembled as he stared blankly at Fan Wu before him, still unable to believe it.

Standing in front of him was actually a Divine Spirit! Although Ma Qing had never seen Fan Wu before, there was no mistaking the special aura on the man. This person was a Divine Spirit in human form. Ma Qing did not know exactly what kind of Divine Spirit he was, but with such strength, Ma Qing would never have been his opponent even at his peak, much less now.

What terrified him even more was that a Divine Spirit was actually obeying Yang Kai's every command. This world... what had happened to it? What virtue or ability did Yang Kai possess that allowed him to command a Divine Spirit?

Fan Wu looked at him coldly. "This Lord is in no fine mood, so do not force me to kill!" He tapped Ma Qing lightly on the chest, sending him stumbling back several steps.

Ma Qing opened his mouth, but Madam Qi, who stood beside him, tugged at his sleeve, and in the end he could only let out a long sigh. He could tell that the Divine Spirit before him was probably not bluffing. He truly intended to kill if pushed.

In the valley, several thousand Southern Territory cultivators and more than ten thousand demonized people faced each other in utter silence. It seemed everyone's attention was fixed on Yang Kai and Lei Gu. Those two were the key to resolving this matter.

"Good. Now we can have a proper talk." Yang Kai looked past Gao Xueting and fixed his gaze on Lei Gu. "If you want to die, you can make your move now. I guarantee you will not leave an intact corpse behind."

Wen Zishan's eyes flashed, and his heart leapt into his throat.

Fortunately, Lei Gu's expression did not change, and he showed no intention of killing.

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Looks like you want to live! Well, of course. It wasn't easy to possess a body and be reborn, and you even took over a Third Stage Emperor Realm body. This should be the time to roam free and enjoy yourself. Who would want to die?" His gaze sharpened, and he waved a hand. "But look around. Do you think you can escape?"

"You are threatening me!" Lei Gu snorted coldly.

Yang Kai said, "I am only helping you recognize reality. I forgot to introduce them. See that one over there? That is Fan Wu, a Divine Spirit from the Eastern Territory. And this one is the Divine Spirit Cang Gou. This madam here, beautiful enough to make flowers bloom, is the Divine Spirit Luan Feng... And these are the Thirty-Two Yao Kings under their command..."

Hearing this, Luan Feng curled her lips hard. The Thirty-Two Yao Kings, however, all showed fierce expressions, urging their Yao Yuan as rolling Yao Qi pressed down on Lei Gu.

The many Southern Territory cultivators were filled with indescribable shock and dread. Earlier, seeing Ma Qing subdued by Fan Wu in a single move had already shaken them enough. Only now did they learn that the Divine Spirits who had come here numbered not one, but three.

And there were also the Thirty-Two Yao Kings!

What kind of force was this? It was simply unheard of, unseen in all their lives. Even Star God Palace could not gather such a force, could it?

What eased their hearts a little was that these people seemed to have a good relationship with Yang Kai, so they did not need to worry about them bringing disaster to the Southern Territory.

"And there are more than two hundred Emperor Realm Masters from my Northern Territory..." Yang Kai pointed to the side, and everyone there lifted their heads and puffed out their chests, unwilling to let their presence be overshadowed by those Yao Kings.

Lei Gu angrily shouted, “This Lord was not raised to be frightened by threats.”

Yang Kai said flatly, "I am not trying to scare you. I only want to tell you that we can have a proper talk."

"How?" Lei Gu said in a deep voice. Even as he spoke, he kept his body hidden behind Gao Xueting, giving no one any opening.

"You want to live, and I want her back. The solution is simple. I let you go, and you let her go."

Lei Gu sneered. "First let this Lord leave. Once this Lord confirms I am safe, I will naturally let her return."

Yang Kai sneered back. "Do you think that is possible?"

Wen Zishan cut in, "I will exchange myself for her. You can take me hostage and let your Demonic Qi pour into me as you please!"
