## Chapter 3232 — Breaking It With a Casual Gesture

Yang Kai's group looked aggressive, clearly arriving with ill intent. The Mitian Sect disciples had no idea what they meant to do, but they had at least some discernment and common sense, so unease naturally gripped them.

Yang Kai glanced over and said coldly, "This Lord and Sect Master Mi are old acquaintances. I came from afar to visit him and discuss important matters, so why have you shut your gates against this Lord? Hurry up and open the array. If you delay this Lord's important business with Sect Master Mi, can you bear the responsibility?"

The disciples naturally did not believe a word of his nonsense. They all knew the relationship between their Sect and Lingxiao Palace. Their income had fallen sharply over the years entirely because of Lingxiao Palace, and they had long since come to hate both Lingxiao Palace and Yang Kai. They might not be enemies in name, but they were even more like enemies than true enemies. Why would a man like this come all the way here just to visit their Sect Master? He clearly had no good intentions.

More importantly, the Sect Master had already given orders that the Grand Array absolutely could not be opened without his permission, so of course Yang Kai could not frighten them with a few words.

The leading Dao Origin Realm cultivator cupped his fists and said, “Palace Master Yang, please calm your anger. Sect Master is already on his way and should arrive shortly. Please wait a moment, Palace Master Yang. Whatever matter there is, you may discuss it personally with Sect Master once he arrives.” His words were neither servile nor overbearing, fully displaying the bearing of a great Sect.

Yang Kai snorted coldly. "They say the King of Hell is easy to meet, while the lesser devils are hard to deal with. Sure enough. What is your name?"

The Dao Origin Realm disciple's face twitched. What did that mean? Was Yang Kai trying to make trouble for him? After hesitating for a while, he still did not dare hide it and gave his name.

Yang Kai nodded, pointed at him, and said, "I'll deal with you later."

The man almost wept on the spot. "Palace Master Yang, this one is only following orders. Why lower yourself to my level?"

Yang Kai said, "If you do not want me to lower myself to your level, then hurry up and open the Grand Array. Stop prattling."

The Dao Origin Realm disciple shrank back. How could he dare open the array? He was like an ant on a hot pan, instantly at a complete loss.

Just then, a voice came from behind them. "I wondered who was displaying such might. So Palace Master Yang has arrived. This Mi has failed to welcome you from afar. Please forgive the discourtesy."

"Sect Master!" The disciples turned to look, joy spreading across their faces. The leading Dao Origin Realm disciple was especially delighted, feeling as though he had been delivered from disaster. Over there, with their Sect Master at the head, not one Elder or Protector was missing. Nearly ten Emperor Realm Masters flew over together.

Since the Sect Master had arrived, the matter naturally had nothing more to do with him. He quickly retreated a few steps and stood behind the group of Emperor Realm Masters.

"Sect Master Mi!" Yang Kai looked at Mi Qi with a smile. "It has been many years. I trust you have been well."

Mi Qi cupped his fists. "Palace Master Yang is as elegant as ever, your might undiminished." Though the words were praise, his tone was so strange and sarcastic that it was impossible to miss.

Yang Kai raised a brow, wondering why this fellow looked so confident. He glanced at the Emperor Realm Masters behind Mi Qi, then turned to examine his own side's lineup. Their battle strength was not even close. Where did Mi Qi get the nerve to speak to him like this? Was he relying on the Protective Mountain Array in front of them?

Mi Qi said, "Palace Master Yang, instead of enjoying yourself in Lingxiao Palace, what brings you to my poor backwater?"

Yang Kai laughed loudly. "I was out handling some business and happened to pass by. Since I have not seen Sect Master Mi for many years, I missed you dearly and came specially to pay a visit."

"I see..." Mi Qi cupped his fists again. "Many thanks for Palace Master Yang's concern. This Mi is perfectly well."

Yang Kai said, "Sect Master Mi, are you planning to speak with me like this? Are you not going to invite me in to sit? Speaking of which, this is my first time at your Mitian Sect. I wonder how it compares to my Lingxiao Palace."

Mi Qi shook his head. "Lingxiao Palace is blessed with outstanding spirits and beautiful scenery. My Mitian Sect naturally falls far short." Inwardly, he cursed, Only if my brain were rotting would I invite you in to sit, then changed the subject. "However, today is rather inconvenient. This Mi is entertaining an honored guest, so it is not very suitable. If Brother Yang is interested, why not come another day? This Mi will certainly accompany you personally."

Yang Kai looked down at him. "A guest from afar is still a guest. Sect Master Mi, your hospitality seems rather lacking."

Mi Qi smiled faintly. "Please forgive me, Palace Master Yang. It truly is... inconvenient. It would not be good to offend that honored guest."

"Which honored guest has graced you with his presence? You may as well let this Lord meet him too."

Mi Qi shook his head. "That Lord does not like strangers. Palace Master Yang, I truly must apologize today. Please return."

Yang Kai said lightly, "And if this Lord insists on going in to take a look?"

Mi Qi narrowed his eyes. "Palace Master Yang, please conduct yourself with dignity. This is my Mitian Sect, not Lingxiao Palace. It is not a place where whatever you say goes. If you want to throw your weight around, then go back to Lingxiao Palace and do it there!"

The Mitian Sect disciples were all greatly invigorated when they heard this. Their Sect Master truly sounded domineering, and listening to him was deeply satisfying. This Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace had brought it on himself. Why keep chattering only to invite humiliation?

Yang Kai looked at him meaningfully. "It seems Sect Master Mi really does have something to rely on. Are you not afraid this Lord might raze your Mitian Sect in a fit of anger?"

Mi Qi said proudly, "Heaven watches what men do. Justice naturally lives in the hearts of the people."

Yang Kai waved his hand. "Justice does not live in the hearts of the people. It lives in my hand!" Too lazy to waste any more words, he tore away all pretense. "Mi Qi, since this Lord has come here personally, I have brought you two choices!"

Mi Qi smiled. "Then this Mi would like to hear what those two choices are. I hope Palace Master Yang speaks seriously and does not make a laughingstock of himself."

Yang Kai said expressionlessly, "Submit to my Lingxiao Palace and hand over seventy percent of your yearly income from now on, or... this Lord will turn your Mitian Sect into a branch of my Lingxiao Palace!"

Mi Qi stared at him expressionlessly, as if waiting for him to continue. After quite a while, he finally said, "That's all?"

Yang Kai clasped his hands behind his back. "Choose for yourself."

Mi Qi chuckled, turned to look around him, and said, "Palace Master Yang has told us a joke. Everyone, give him some face."

The Elders and Protectors burst into laughter, and the disciples played along as well. The whole scene was indescribably strange.

Yang Kai watched coldly, while the three great Yao Kings remained solemn.

Mi Qi raised a hand, and the laughter stopped at once. He looked coldly at Yang Kai and said, "There was no trouble in the world until fools stirred it up themselves. Palace Master Yang, if you withdraw now, you can still preserve your dignity. Otherwise, once you find yourself riding a tiger with no way down, you will truly become a joke." He seemed to be offering kind advice, but in truth he could hardly wait for Yang Kai to attack and humiliate himself. He had made up his mind to settle their grudges today, so he would not have to send Lingxiao Palace a large sum of Source Crystals every year from now on.

"Sect Master Mi certainly speaks with confidence today." Yang Kai nodded slightly. "I hope you will not regret it."

Mi Qi shouted in a low voice, "This Lord has never regretted anything he has done in his life."

"Good!" Yang Kai thundered. "Since Sect Master Mi refuses the toast only to drink the forfeit, do not blame this Palace Master for disregarding old ties and razing your Mitian Sect!"

As soon as the words left his mouth, three figures shot out from either side of him. In the blink of an eye, they pounced before the Grand Guardian Array. The crowd had not even seen what happened clearly before a violent energy fluctuation swept out, followed by a rumbling series of explosions that shook the earth and mountains.

The Grand Guardian Array shuddered fiercely several times, ripples spreading across it in layer after layer.

The three great Yao Kings attacked with terrifying momentum. Yao Qi surged and rushed straight to the Ninth Heaven, startling many people into summoning their Secret Treasures and urging their strength as if facing a great enemy.

Mi Qi, however, remained perfectly calm, merely watching the front with a smile. "My Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array may not be the finest in the world, but it is not so easy to break. Palace Master Yang, you should save your strength."

As he spoke, an array flag suddenly appeared in his hand. With a gentle wave, the shaking Grand Array immediately stabilized, and even the ripples vanished. No matter how the three great Yao Kings attacked, there was not the slightest visible reaction.

It was not truly without reaction; all the force of the attacks had simply been dispersed and transferred elsewhere. This was the might of the Grand Guardian Array. Once the array was activated, even beings as strong as the three great Yao Kings could not break it in a short time. Back then, if the people of Wenqing Sect had hidden inside their Grand Array, Yang Kai would have had no way to destroy them. Their mistake was leaving their nest in full force and running to Ice Heart Valley, which gave Yang Kai his chance.

The three great Yao Kings had held nothing back, but after that fierce assault, their strength was already flagging. They flashed back to Yang Kai's side, each of them grim and ashamed, feeling they had failed Yang Kai's trust.

By contrast, the Mitian Sect cultivators burst into loud laughter, their voices full of ridicule and mockery.

Mi Qi said, "Palace Master Yang, are you still unwilling to give up? If you continue, you will only embarrass yourself further."

Earlier, he had also feared the three great Yao Kings might break through his Grand Guardian Array. Now that he saw the Grand Array standing safe and sound, his heart immediately settled. It seemed Lingxiao Palace could do nothing to him even without Old Tao taking action. If he had known this earlier, why the hell had he sent over all those Source Crystals in the past few years? Wouldn't it have been much better to keep them for himself?

Yang Kai looked at him. "If this is what you rely on, this Lord is very disappointed."

Mi Qi snorted coldly. "Shameless boasting."

"What is a mere array worth? Watch this Lord break it with a casual gesture." Yang Kai shook both arms, and everyone behind him retreated together, falling back a hundred zhang in an instant.

Mi Qi frowned, not knowing what Yang Kai was up to. Although he knew Yang Kai's strength was not weak, Yang Kai had not advanced to the Emperor Realm all that long ago. How could he accomplish what even the three great Yao Kings could not?

While he was still wondering, Yang Kai reached into the Void and grasped. A simple-minded-looking stone man suddenly appeared before them.

Everyone stared, eyes wide. No one had seen how the stone man appeared. They had only seen Yang Kai reach into the Void, as if it had been hiding there all along.

The stone man was about half a person's height, with sharp angles all over his body. He looked slow and foolish, but his eyes were astonishingly lively.

As soon as the stone man appeared, he turned toward Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array. Then, with a beast-like roar, he pounded his chest fiercely with both fists.

The crowd stared blankly again. What was this fellow doing? His entrance had been imposing enough, but what was this self-harm supposed to be? Very soon, however, their astonishment turned to terror. Pairs of eyes slowly moved upward, and one head after another tilted back.

Amid the shaking of earth and mountains, the stone man, who had been only half a person's height, began growing at a speed visible to the naked eye.

One zhang, five zhang, ten zhang, twenty zhang, thirty zhang...

Only when he had grown all the way to forty or fifty zhang did he slowly stop. Like a great mountain, he stood across the front of Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array.
