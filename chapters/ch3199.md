## Chapter 3199 — Transforming Into a Spirit

The male Ice Spirit studied Yang Kai carefully for a moment, then slowly shook his head. "You are not!" He pointed at Su Yan. "She is."

"We came together." Yang Kai smiled faintly. "You saw it too. It was your Ice God who brought us here."

The male Ice Spirit stubbornly shook his head. "You are very dirty. She is very pure, so she is the guest."

The corner of Yang Kai's eye twitched. "Boy, the last guy who dared speak to me like that already has grass growing over his grave." At the very least, he was now the Master of the Star Domain, and everything within it fell under his jurisdiction. Yet here was someone pointing at him and calling him dirty.

The male Ice Spirit frowned at him in open disgust, truly unable to understand why the Ice God would bring such a person here. His very presence defiled this place's purity.

Su Yan patted Yang Kai's hand, looked at the male Ice Spirit, and said, "Please take me to meet your Ice God."

She knew the key to refining Ice Soul Star's Origin on this trip surely lay with that Ice God, so she had no intention of wasting time.

The male Ice Spirit nodded, but did not lead the way. Instead, he took an ice pouch from his waist and handed it to Su Yan. "Drink some!" The ice pouch was shaped like a water flask. There was no telling what it held, but it sloshed softly when shaken.

Su Yan accepted it and asked, "I can only meet the Ice God after drinking?"

"Only after drinking are you a guest!" Clearly, one could not simply walk over and meet the Ice God. There seemed to be a few tests involved.

"Good." Su Yan opened the ice pouch as she spoke, and a sweet fragrance immediately drifted into the air. Yang Kai was slightly startled. He had thought the pouch contained some kind of wine, but now it seemed to be something else, probably a specialty of the Ice Spirit Clan. Though he did not know what it was, he was certain of one thing: whatever was inside this ice pouch was absolutely not something an ordinary person could endure.

Su Yan tilted her head back and poured it down, bold in a way rarely seen from her.

The surrounding Ice Spirits had started out looking as if they were watching a good show, but as time passed, their expressions gradually changed. This was especially true of the male Ice Spirit who had handed Su Yan the pouch. His eyes slowly widened, and with a look of shock and distress, he cried, "Enough, enough!"

Su Yan lowered the ice pouch, lifted her sleeve to wipe the corner of her mouth, and asked, "Does this count as passing?"

The male Ice Spirit took the ice pouch back and shook it lightly, then stepped aside with a pained expression. "Please!"

Su Yan glanced at Yang Kai and gave him a look, telling him to stay calm, then composedly followed the winding Ice Path upward.

She had gone less than a hundred zhang when a female Ice Spirit jumped out to block the way, holding an ice-carved plate in her hands. On it sat several things as white as silkworm cocoons. She tilted her head, smiling brightly at Su Yan. "Try some?"

Su Yan picked one up with two fingers and put it in her mouth. An icy flavor burst across her tongue, along with an indescribable sourness.

A moment later, the plate had been eaten clean. The female Ice Spirit stared blankly at her empty ice plate as Su Yan brushed past her.

Halfway up the mountain, Yang Kai remained where he was and waited. Although he was the Master of the Star Domain, he had brought Su Yan here to refine Ice Soul's Origin, so she had to rely on her own ability. Only then would Ice Soul's Origin acknowledge her and aid her future growth. Otherwise, with his current strength, he could simply subdue Ice Soul's Origin by force.

But that would make the entire endeavor meaningless.

Since these were only tests, there should not be much danger. That Ice God, Ice Soul's Origin itself, probably never expected Su Yan to be a cultivator at the Second Stage of the Dao Origin Realm. The so-called tests were hardly a problem for her.

Bored out of his mind, he fixed his eyes on the male Ice Spirit's ice pouch and said, "Let me have a taste too."

The male Ice Spirit immediately shook his head like a rattle drum. "No!"

He was still pained over how much Su Yan had drunk just now.

"Stingy!" Yang Kai curled his lip.

The male Ice Spirit said, "If you drink it, you will die."

Yang Kai sneered. "Dying isn't that easy. Hurry up and let me taste it."

"You really will die," the male Ice Spirit said seriously. "Although you are not the Ice God's guest, since you came together with that guest, I cannot harm you."

"Enough nonsense. Hand it over." Yang Kai stretched out his hand, and the ice pouch was instantly snatched away. He tilted his head back and took a mouthful. Sweetness filled his mouth, followed by an extremely icy aura that spread out and nearly numbed his tongue. In an instant, frost covered his entire head.

Yang Kai was inwardly shocked. He had no idea how this thing had been brewed, but he supposed it was some kind of fruit wine. He could taste more than a hundred spirit fruits in it, every one of them an Ice Attribute spirit fruit.

If a Third-Stage Void King Realm cultivator drank this kind of fruit wine, they would probably suffer terribly, and too much of it would certainly be fatal. No wonder the male Ice Spirit said he would die. If his cultivation had not already surpassed the limits of this world, his life really might have been in danger.

Su Yan, on the other hand, had drunk so much without the slightest reaction, clearly thanks to her Ice-Crystal Yu Body.

Seeing Yang Kai act so recklessly, the male Ice Spirit paled in fright. Although Yang Kai was not the Ice God's guest, he had come here with that guest after all. If he really died here, things would not be easy to settle.

Just as he was growing anxious, Yang Kai shuddered, and the frost on his face instantly scattered. He laughed loudly. "Refreshing!"

He tipped his head back and began gulping it down again, drinking even faster than Su Yan had.

The surrounding Ice Spirits, every last one of them, were dumbstruck. For a moment, they looked at him as if he were a heavenly being.

Although the ice pouch was not large, the space inside it was anything but small. It held at least several dozen jin of fruit wine, yet Yang Kai drained it in one breath. Only after not a single drop remained did he let go, then suddenly belched, his mouth full of sweetness.

He even felt a little drunk, which was truly strange. This fruit wine clearly had no scent of alcohol, yet it could still intoxicate a person. He waved his hand. "If you have anything good to eat or drink, just bring it over."

The male Ice Spirit stared at him as if he were a monster, shook the emptied ice pouch, and stood dazed for a long while before finally raising his hand and beckoning.

A moment later, an ice table was set up on the platform. Yang Kai sat beside it in a bold, imposing posture, and the table was piled with all kinds of Ice Soul Star specialties. There was fruit wine like what he had drunk before, as well as foods he had never seen. Every dish was exquisite and flawlessly pure. Though there was no meat to be seen, the table of delicacies still made one's appetite stir.

A group of Ice Spirits gathered around, staring blankly as he ate and drank with abandon. Several female Ice Spirits even looked determined to make him die here, continuously pouring him wine.

Yang Kai refused nothing. Whenever wine came, he drained it, showing this group of sheltered Ice Spirits what true boldness looked like.

As time passed, more and more Ice Spirits gathered around. The wine and fruit platters on the table were replaced again and again. Yang Kai's eyes had gone hazy with drink, and even his tongue had grown a little clumsy, but he still refused to fall.

Only after a full three days did Su Yan suddenly appear before Yang Kai.

Yang Kai narrowed his eyes and looked her over. With a surge of Emperor Yuan, he sobered up at once. "How did it go?"

Zheng...

Cold light flashed as Su Yan drew her sword. "It is done."

"Profound Frost Divine Sword?" Yang Kai raised his brow. Su Yan had brought this Profound Frost Divine Sword out from Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star back then. It was the sword left behind by Bing Yun, the Founder of Ice Heart Valley, and all these years, she had used it as her personal sword.

However, after she advanced to the Dao Origin Realm, the sword had already begun to fall behind.

Yang Kai had been considering having Hou Yu refine a sword specially tailored for her once they returned to Lingxiao Palace.

But looking at it now, that seemed unnecessary.

The Profound Frost Divine Sword had undergone an enormous change. The entire blade had become as thin as a cicada's wing, and even though Su Yan had not urged any strength, it still emitted an extremely frigid aura. It actually showed faint signs of becoming a Dao Origin-Grade Secret Treasure.

And on the sword hilt, two tiny words as small as fly heads had appeared.

Ice Soul!

Yang Kai asked in surprise, "You refined it into an Artifact Spirit?"

Su Yan shook her head. "It chose this on its own."

Inside the Ice Palace, Su Yan and Ice Soul had spent a day contesting methods, not in a life-or-death battle, but in control over Ice Attribute power. After Su Yan gained Ice Soul's recognition, it voluntarily entered the Profound Frost Divine Sword and transformed into an Artifact Spirit.

This Artifact Spirit was anything but ordinary, simply because it was the Stellar Origin of the entire Ice Soul Star.

No one had ever used a Stellar Origin as an Artifact Spirit before, so even Yang Kai could not say whether this outcome was good or bad. But one thing was beyond dispute: Su Yan had already become Ice Soul Star's Star Master. She and Ice Soul Star were now closely connected, inseparable from one another.

"What happened to them?" Su Yan asked curiously. When she came over just now, she had seen the Ice Spirits looking grief-stricken, as if someone had cut several jin of flesh from their bodies.

"Uh..." Yang Kai burped. "I ate and drank a bit of their stuff. They're terribly stingy."

Su Yan glanced over the ice table and immediately understood what had happened. Smiling, she said, "You ate at least ten thousand years of their stores."

“Did I? Haha, looking at it that way, they were quite hospitable.” Yang Kai patted his belly. Although he was only in human form now, once he revealed his dragon body, it was twenty-five zhang long. Forget ten thousand years of accumulated stores, even a hundred thousand years’ worth could be eaten and drunk clean.

"Ice God!" The surrounding Ice Spirits fixed their eyes on what had once been the Profound Frost Divine Sword and was now the Ice Soul Sword, their expressions complicated.

Radiance flashed, and a pure, flawless, peerlessly elegant woman drifted out from the Ice Soul Sword, making Yang Kai's eyes widen.

Although he had long known that Ice Soul's Origin had developed sentience, he had never imagined it had actually taken human form. Her current state was somewhat similar to Liu Yan's, except their attributes were different. Liu Yan was like fire, while Ice Soul was like ice. If the two met, who knew whether they would clash.

"Ice God!" The many Ice Spirits knelt in unison and shouted.

Ice Soul swept her beautiful eyes around, and her cool, clear voice resounded through Heaven and Earth. "I am going to leave this place."

The many Ice Spirits grew even more grief-stricken, utterly reluctant to part with her.

Ice Soul turned to Yang Kai and said, "Before I leave, please place a protection over this star to shelter them."

"Easy enough." Yang Kai nodded. Even if Ice Soul had not brought it up, he would have done so anyway. This Cultivation Star was already connected to Su Yan, so naturally he could not afford to be careless. He immediately communicated with the Star Domain Origin, drew upon the power of countless stars, and concealed all traces of Ice Soul Star's existence.
