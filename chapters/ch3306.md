## Chapter 3306 - All Who Came Must Die

In an instant, ghostly wails and wolfish howls filled the wilds. The sky darkened, the earth dimmed, and bloody light bloomed, transforming that vast stretch of space into a boundless sea of blood. Blood churned, its stench soaring to the heavens. In that sea, monsters rose and sank while ghostly souls and phantasmal figures drifted through the air. The soul-stealing sounds wound on and on, leaving anyone who heard them dizzy and muddleheaded.

More than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters used their Ten Thousand Souls Banners and Blood Sea Banners together. The sight was magnificent. The endless sea of blood sealed off an independent space between heaven and earth, and inside it Yang Kai would be restricted at every turn, while the people of Netherworld Sect could move like fish in water. With one side weakened and the other strengthened, even if Yang Kai had three heads and six arms, he would likely find it hard to keep his life.

Lan He's heart leapt into her throat. When she looked over, the sky there was already a sheet of blood-red. Yang Kai had long since vanished, and she could not detect even the slightest trace of his aura.

The moment the sea of blood took shape, Xu Changfeng coldly waved his hand. More than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters from Brahma Heaven Sacred Land silently slipped into the sea of blood as well and disappeared, clearly intending to use this chance to strike Yang Kai from the shadows.

Ordinarily, the people of Brahma Heaven Sacred Land could not enter this sea of blood at will. It was a Divine Ability cast by the people of Netherworld Sect, and outsiders who entered would inevitably be restrained in various ways. But since they were joining forces against a common enemy, there was no need to worry overmuch. The people of Netherworld Sect would naturally make it easy for them to act.

In the blink of an eye, the scales of victory tilted. This time was completely different from the earlier probe. Before, although five people had formed a fatal trap, they had still only been five people. Now there were twenty or thirty Emperor Realm Masters, including several at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm.

Watching from afar, Lan He clenched both fists and pursed her red lips, her worry obvious beyond words.

Red Ghost, however, felt his blood surge, excitement lighting his face. If a man could have a great battle like Yang Kai's in his life, he could die without regret. But he also knew that if the two great forces joined hands against him in this way, he would have absolutely no chance of survival.

An imposing furious shout suddenly rang out from within the Blood Sea, shaking the universe. “A pack of ghosts and monsters dares offend this Lord’s might? Break!”

A bell rang, and an intent that suppressed heaven and earth swept in every direction. Towering waves rose from the boundless sea of blood, rolling without end, yet the sea rapidly melted away like snowflakes beneath a blazing sun. A crimson mist even appeared in the sky, clearly formed from the evaporated blood sea.

At the same time, the countless Yin Souls wreaking havoc within the sea of blood turned to ash amid scream after scream.

Boom, boom, boom…

The Mountain and River Bell rang nine times in succession, each peal grander and more majestic than the last, each one more urgent. With the center of the sea of blood as their origin, the sound waves became a force visible to the naked eye and spread in every direction.

Tang Sheng, Qian Xiuying, and Red Ghost all paled and hurriedly cast spells to protect themselves, yet dizziness still washed over them and their eardrums shook. Even the protective Emperor Yuan covering their bodies rippled layer upon layer.

Horror showed on every face, fear filling their eyes. They had long heard that the Mountain and River Bell was a Primordial Era Treasure, something one could only encounter by chance, and that its might was immense. Only after seeing it with their own eyes today did they know the rumors were true.

No wonder Origin Cauldron Great Emperor had been able to use it to break through his shackles and attain a supreme body. Whatever this treasure's origins were, the power it could display was simply beyond comparison with an ordinary Imperial Treasure.

Facing an enemy who wielded such a treasure, anyone whose mind was affected by the Mountain and River Bell would likely lose their head in the very next instant.

When the bell finally fell silent, its lingering note still spread outward, as if it would echo on for three days without fading.

The sea of blood, which had originally seemed to form a world of its own and stand as firm as an iron fortress, was now torn to pieces. Who knew how much crimson blood had evaporated; even its blood-red hue had dimmed.

Muffled groans sounded one after another. From within the sea of blood, several figures wreathed in eerie ghost Qi staggered into view, pale-faced and vomiting great mouthfuls of blood.

These people were clearly Emperor Realm Masters of Netherworld Sect, as well as the masters of those Blood Sea Banners. The Blood Sea Banners used blood as their foundation, so when Yang Kai broke the sea of blood with one move, all of them naturally suffered a degree of backlash.

Their expressions were quite a sight, their eyes full of disbelief. They had never imagined that the heaven-shaking method they had joined hands to arrange would be broken by Yang Kai so easily, injuring them in the process.

Without the favorable timing and terrain those men had created, the rest could no longer hide themselves. One figure after another appeared within the thinned sea of blood. Some belonged to Netherworld Sect, but most were from Brahma Heaven Sacred Land. Without anyone noticing, they had already closed in around Yang Kai, clearly looking for a chance to strike.

At that moment, however, they were in poor condition. Every one of them swayed unsteadily, their expressions flickering. Although they had suffered no backlash from the breaking of the sea of blood, the nine rings of the Mountain and River Bell were hard to endure.

Even Tang Sheng and the others, more than a dozen li away and not deliberately targeted, had been left shaken, their ears ringing and their vision spinning. How much worse was it for these people at such close range?

And so, at this moment, an utterly absurd scene appeared.

Nearly twenty people surrounded Yang Kai, yet every one of them swayed as if drunk, their faces pale as they struggled to steady themselves. The instant they appeared, Yang Kai's sharp eyes swept over them like blades.

"Not good!" Xu Changfeng cried in alarm, then roared, "Retreat! Quickly!"

Inwardly, he cursed Fu Bo bloody. What kind of dogshit Secret Technique was this from Netherworld Sect, so fragile it could not withstand a single blow? It had turned what should have been a perfect ambush into their side's greatest flaw.

But he also knew that Netherworld Sect's Secret Technique was not truly so weak. So many Emperor Realm Masters had sacrificed the Blood Sea Banners together and created a boundless sea of blood; even if he himself fell into it, he would not have gained any advantage. The blame lay only with the extraordinary treasure in Yang Kai's hands. That grand bell's sound-wave attack seemed to be the sea of blood's nemesis, shattering it in the blink of an eye.

Yang Kai burst into laughter. "You can't retreat! Everyone who came dies!"

As the Laws of Space surged, ink-black Moon Blades shaped like crescent moons shot out in every direction, slicing through the Void, through the shattered sea of blood, and into the bodies of his enemies.

Although this stretch of heaven and earth had been sealed off, preventing him from teleporting away, the Space Force within it remained under his control.

Sharp hissing rang out without end. Blood sprayed as Emperor Realm Masters were struck as if by lightning, muffled groans and miserable screams rising and falling. The Moon Blades were simply unstoppable. First Stage of the Emperor Realm or Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, it made no difference; their protective Emperor Yuan was like paper before the Moon Blades, utterly unable to withstand a single blow. Wherever the Moon Blades passed, every person struck was left with a crescent-shaped wound, and within that wound there was nothing at all. The flesh and blood that should have been there had been devoured by the Void.

In the sky, Emperor Realm Masters dropped like dumplings into a pot, plummeting toward the ground. Corpse after corpse smashed down until they no longer looked human, flesh and blood splattering.

In the blink of an eye, more than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters had fallen, and within a thirty-zhang radius around Yang Kai, no enemies remained standing.

Gulp... The clear sound of someone swallowing rang out. Tang Sheng turned his head with difficulty and discovered that it had actually come from Red Ghost, whose Adam's apple was still bobbing. Red Ghost had always been lawless in the Eastern Territory, arrogant beyond measure, yet now his eyes were wide, his expression saying he wished he could pluck them out and throw them onto the battlefield for a closer look.

Tang Sheng wanted to laugh at the sight, but he could not laugh at all, because... more than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters had just died before his eyes. Those were more than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters, not a dozen pigs! Just like that, they had been cut down as easily as withered straw.

He understood exactly what Red Ghost was feeling, because he felt the same. When he glanced at Qian Xiuying beside him, she looked no different. All of them were filled with shock, astonishment, and trembling fear.

When Tang Sheng had met Yang Kai earlier, he had thought him courteous, refined, handsome, properly measured in his conduct, and altogether quite decent. If not for this matter, he would have been more than willing to make Yang Kai's acquaintance.

Only now did he realize how completely wrong he had been.

When this man slaughtered Emperor Realm Masters, he was like an autumn wind sweeping away fallen leaves, and he did not even blink. In other words, when Tang Sheng had met him and chatted idly with him, had he not taken a stroll past the gates of the underworld? The thought sent a shiver through him.

Who had ever accomplished such a feat as killing more than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters in one stroke? Tang Sheng searched his memories and found that, in the world today, there was only this one example. If the five Yang Kai had killed one by one earlier were counted as well, then in this short span of time, almost twenty Emperor Realm Masters had already died at his hands.

How many people had the two top Sects brought in total? Only a little over thirty. More than half had died in this one exchange, so no matter how this battle ended, the two great forces had already suffered defeat, for their losses were too great, so great they were almost impossible to bear.

Emperor Realm Masters were not cabbages one could find anywhere. Even among top forces, raising an Emperor Realm Master was extremely difficult. Now that so many had died just like that, Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng must be in agony, right?

Tang Sheng deliberately glanced at the two of them and found that they were indeed in pain, as if their hearts were about to bleed. Xu Changfeng, who had always remained calm even if mountains collapsed before him, now looked savage and terrifying. As for Fu Bo, there was even less need to mention him. The way he gnashed his teeth made it seem as if he wanted to drink Yang Kai's blood, eat his flesh, and gnaw his bones.

After this battle, the two top Sects would likely suffer a disastrous fall, and without five hundred years, they could not hope to recover their strength.

Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng had clearly realized this as well. Their hearts were so bitter it was as if they had eaten a whole basket of bitter herbs. They had come for revenge, yet in the end, before the old grudge was settled, new hatred had been added, and so many people had died. Even if they truly avenged this grudge, so what? The dead could not return to life, and the losses of the two great forces could not be made up.

To slaughter Emperor Realm Masters like this with his strength alone, and with only a Second-Stage Emperor Realm cultivation at that, was this wretch even human?
