## Chapter 3156 — Solution

The palace doors were suddenly pushed open, and someone shot to his feet with a shout. "Who goes there!"

They were here discussing important matters. Who would dare be so presumptuous as to barge in without even being announced?

They looked up and saw a young man striding in. At first glance, he looked somewhat familiar, as though they had seen him somewhere before, but for a moment they could not place him.

While they were still puzzled, a gust of wind swept past them, and Li Jiao hurried forward in delighted surprise. "Brother Yang!"

Lu Sanniang, Ruan Biting, He Yunxiang, and the others also rose and saluted one after another.

At this, no one else dared underestimate Yang Kai in the slightest. Over the previous month, most of them had witnessed Li Jiao and the others' astonishing methods. He Yunxiang was one thing; she seemed to be only a Third Stage Void King Realm cultivator, and while she was strong, there was still a limit to that strength. But the other three, whether Li Jiao, Lu Sanniang, or Ruan Biting, were all unfathomably powerful. Void King Realm experts were no different from children before them, people they could crush to death with a casual motion.

How could someone treated so solemnly by these people possibly be ordinary?

After looking more closely, several people let out soft cries of surprise, astonishment spreading across their faces.

They had clearly recognized Yang Kai. Yang Kai had once visited Shuiyue Star years ago and met quite a few people there. Even after several dozen years, that memory had not faded. Xue Yue's husband, Hengluo Commerce Guild's ideal son-in-law, the master of Dark Star—any one of these titles was enough to make them take him seriously. They simply had not expected him to know these towering figures as well.

Moreover, judging from the way those great figures treated him, they seemed... rather respectful, even grateful.

They abruptly remembered that Li Jiao had once mentioned in passing that the only reason his group was on Shuiyue Star, suppressing the strong and helping the weak, was because someone had entrusted them with the task.

Looking at it now, was that person not Yang Kai?

For a time, they could not help growing extremely excited.

"Brother Yang, I did not fail your trust. This Cultivation Star has already been recovered, and those small fry have all been dealt with."

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "You have all worked hard. Mm, I came this time because I need to ask a favor."

Li Jiao smiled and said, "Brother Yang, if you have any instructions, just say them. What need is there to call it a favor?"

Yang Kai rubbed his fingers together. "Lend me some money."

"Ah?" Li Jiao froze.

"Source Crystals. Lend me as many as you have. I'll pay you back once we return to the Star Realm."

Only then did Li Jiao confirm that he had not misheard. He laughed despite himself, quickly removed his Spatial Ring, and after a little work, transferred the Source Crystals inside into another Spatial Ring before handing it to Yang Kai. "This is all I have."

Although he was the Palace Master of Li Dragon Palace, he could not possibly carry too many Source Crystals on him. After all, Li Dragon Palace also needed to develop, and the disciples' cultivation required large Source Crystal expenditures. If he carried off all the Source Crystals, the Sect would be unable to operate. What was more, Li Dragon Palace had previously owed Lingxiao Palace an enormous debt, and more than ninety percent of the Source Crystals in the palace had been taken to repay it.

As a result, his Spatial Ring contained fewer than several hundred thousand Upper-Level Source Crystals. He had slightly more Middle-Level and Lower-Level Source Crystals, but even that amount did not match his status as a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master.

In short, he had spent the last two years living rather frugally.

"I have some here." Lu Sanniang walked over and took out her Source Crystals as well. Yang Kai did not mind the small amount and accepted them all.

As for Ruan Biting and He Yunxiang, they were powerless to help with this matter.

In truth, Yang Kai had fought his way through the Star Realm over the years and accumulated an incalculable number of Source Crystals. But most of them had been left in Lingxiao Palace. Although he still had plenty on hand, for his next plan, Source Crystals were something he could never have too many of. No amount would be enough.

"Brother Li, let me ask you something. Have you dabbled in the Dao of Spirit Arrays?" Yang Kai asked after putting away the Source Crystals.

Li Jiao said, "I cannot claim any proficiency, but I do know a thing or two."

"Excellent. Come with me." Yang Kai reached out, took hold of his arm, and headed outside.

Li Jiao was speechless, not knowing why Yang Kai was in such a hurry, but he did not resist. He only gave Lu Sanniang a look, telling her to wait here.

Through the Space Array, Yang Kai and Li Jiao returned together to Nine Heavens Sacred Land.

"Come!" Yang Kai sprang up and shot into the sky.

Li Jiao followed closely behind him.

Before long, the two flew a thousand li into the air. Yang Kai pointed below and said, "Brother Li, take a look."

Hearing this, Li Jiao focused his gaze below, and his brow furrowed. As far as he could see, the entire Tongxuan Continent seemed wrapped in an uncomfortable aura, and the surface of this Cultivation Star bore many obvious scars of poisoning and devastation.

"What does Brother Yang want me to do?" Li Jiao had a vague idea of Yang Kai's plan.

Yang Kai said, "Is there any Spirit Array that can help this Cultivation Star quickly recover its vitality?"

Li Jiao pondered for a moment before saying, "This Li knows of a Spirit Array called the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array. Perhaps it can be of some help to Brother Yang."

Yang Kai raised his brow. "In that case, I must trouble Brother Li to arrange this All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array."

Li Jiao said, "The All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array is divided into several types: the Eighteen All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array, the Thirty-Six All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array, the Seventy-Two All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array, and, at the highest level, the One Hundred and Eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array. Each one consumes vastly different materials, and the conditions required to arrange them rise sharply with each level, but their effects also improve accordingly. If this were the Star Realm, this Li could easily arrange the One Hundred and Eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array, but here..."

"I can help."

Li Jiao laughed helplessly. "Brother Yang, you also know that this world rejects higher-level power. Even we have to suppress our cultivation to move about here, so how could a Spirit Array be any different? The All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array already surpasses what this world can tolerate. I fear that even if it is arranged, it will be useless."

"What if we have the Star Lord's help?"

"This Star has a Star Lord?" Li Jiao was greatly surprised.

This Cultivation Star was ruined beyond recognition, its World Energy exhausted. Even if it had a Star Lord, she would likely be gravely ill, so he had not considered that possibility at all. Now, hearing Yang Kai say this, he suddenly realized something.

Yang Kai wanted him to arrange a Spirit Array to restore this Cultivation Star's vitality. Perhaps it was not for the Cultivation Star, but for that Star Lord!

With that understanding, Li Jiao said, "If we can have the Star Lord's help, then this matter has a good chance of succeeding."

A Star Lord was the ruler of this place. Even if the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array was rejected by the world, the Star Lord could eliminate that rejection, allowing the Spirit Array to operate.

"Then what type of All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array does Brother Yang want this Li to arrange?"

"If we're going to arrange one, naturally it should be the best."

Li Jiao said in shock, "The materials required for that are no small sum."

The One Hundred and Eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array was something even Li Dragon Palace might not be willing to arrange. For a lower plane's Cultivation Star, it was, in the end, hardly worth the cost.

Yang Kai said firmly, "At any cost."

As he spoke, he tossed the Spatial Ring in his hand to Li Jiao for him to inspect.

Li Jiao sent his Divine Sense inside and immediately felt dizzy.

Inside, Source Crystals were piled up like mountains, simply impossible to count, and all kinds of cultivation materials dazzled the eye. Even as the master of a Sect, the sight made his blood surge.

By comparison, the Source Crystals he had just lent Yang Kai did not even count as spare change.

After the shock came fear.

Yang Kai's Lingxiao Palace had not been established for long and had no profitable businesses, so it could not possibly have earned so many Source Crystals. The only possible source was fighting and plundering in all directions. These piles of Source Crystals and Secret Treasures represented enemy after enemy Yang Kai had slain and Sect after Sect he had flattened.

A drop of cold sweat slid down his forehead. Fortunately, he had not fallen out with Yang Kai back then. Otherwise, Li Dragon Palace's fate would definitely have been ugly.

"Is this enough?" Yang Kai asked. "If not, I still have a mineral lode on hand."

"Enough, enough." Li Jiao nodded repeatedly. If this many materials were not enough, there would be no justifying it.

"Then I'll have to trouble Brother Li. If you need any help, just say so."

"Naturally. Mm, I also need Brother Yang to inform the master of this Star that this Li will need some cooperation from them."

"Good!"

Li Jiao immediately took out several blank jade slips. His Divine Sense surged into them as he carved the many essentials of the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array inside, then handed them to Yang Kai.

Yang Kai returned to Nine Heavens Sacred Land to inform Xia Ningshang of the matter, while Li Jiao needed to fly a circuit around Tongxuan Continent and carefully survey its condition before deciding where to arrange the array.

In the Star Realm, the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array was not an especially mysterious Spirit Array. It simply had many variations, and with Xia Ningshang's comprehension, understanding its mysteries naturally was not difficult.

Yang Kai accompanied her in studying it as well.

After several days, they had become familiar with most of it.

On this day, Xia Ningshang suddenly sensed something and smiled. "That man is here."

As she spoke, she gently waved her hand.

Li Jiao was pacing outside Nine Heavens Sacred Land, considering how to enter, or whether he should call Yang Kai and have him come out to receive him, when the dense fog ahead suddenly parted to either side, revealing a passage for him to pass through.

Li Jiao immediately stepped inside.

Inside the main hall, Yang Kai was waiting. Seeing Li Jiao arrive travel-worn, he quickly asked, "Brother Li, how did your investigation go?"

Li Jiao said, "Although it took some time, there should not be much of a problem."

Yang Kai was overjoyed. "That's good."

"I will first tell Brother Yang where the array needs to be arranged. Brother Yang will need to finish the early preparations in advance. Once the Formation Foundations are refined, we can begin setting it up immediately."

As Li Jiao spoke, he took out a jade slip and tapped it with his hand.

After a flash of light, a spherical projection emerged from the jade slip.

Most of the sphere was yellow and blue, unevenly distributed. Yang Kai recognized it at once as a projection of the entire Tongxuan Continent. The yellow naturally represented land, while the blue marked the areas covered by water.

It seemed Li Jiao had put no small amount of effort into this over the past few days. Not only had he covered the entire Tongxuan Continent, he had carefully drawn this map as well. It could not be called one hundred percent accurate, but it was still close enough.

He examined it for a moment, then pointed to one place and said, "Brother Yang, please look. This is where we are now."

Yang Kai nodded, recognizing it as Nine Heavens Sacred Land's location on Tongxuan Continent.
