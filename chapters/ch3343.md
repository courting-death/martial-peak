## Chapter 3343 — Stumbling Upon a Secret Affair

“If you don't want to give them back, then behave yourself.” Yang Kai pointed a warning finger at her.

Fu Ling nodded rapidly, then asked curiously, “Brother-in-law, why did you come back?” Yang Kai's calm composure made it obvious that he had planned to double back all along, but why? He had even gone so far as to shake off Zhu Qing. Had Fu Ling not been guarding the entrance, she probably would never have witnessed this. The thrill of discovering a great secret stole over her, and she began to wonder what might happen if she used it to threaten Yang Kai...

“I have my own plans. Why ask so many questions?” Yang Kai waved impatiently, then said solemnly, “Remember, you didn't see me today.”

Fu Ling blinked her bright, large eyes. “But when you left, Elder Sister Qing was there too.”

Yang Kai said, “You didn't see me come back! Understand?”

“Understood.” Fu Ling nodded.

Yang Kai grinned. “Behave, and you'll be rewarded. But if you dare breathe a word of this...” He snorted softly and formed a hand seal.

Fu Ling's face paled, and she waved both hands frantically. “Don't worry, Brother-in-law. I absolutely, absolutely won't betray you.”

Yang Kai grunted in acknowledgment, then took to the air and retraced his route toward the Dragon Palace.

He was going to storm the Dragon Temple! He could not let half a year of hard work go to waste. That cheap slut Fu Zhun had gone back on her word, so she could hardly blame him for responding in kind. As long as he made it inside the Dragon Temple, she probably could not do anything to him even if she found out. He could control the many Dragon Souls within; once inside, he would be invincible.

He could not very well let Zhu Qing know about this, as it would put her in a difficult position. That was why Yang Kai had pretended to leave before doubling back.

The mere thought of Fu Zhun made Yang Kai seethe. He sneered inwardly. She would not get away with taking advantage of him so easily.

Yang Kai sped onward, sweeping his surroundings with his Divine Sense while taking constant care to conceal both his aura and his figure. Fortunately, the Dragon Clan was small. Every Dragon had a Spirit Island of their own, where they generally remained in seclusion and cultivated in peace. His journey therefore passed without incident, and he did not encounter a soul.

Yang Kai knew where the Dragon Temple lay, having visited it once before. Though he had to skirt a few Spirit Islands this time, he still knew the way.

Two hours later, Yang Kai suddenly stopped mid-flight, wheeled around, and descended onto a nearby Spirit Island. The instant he landed, he dove into a patch of grass and activated the Nothingness Secret Technique, exiling himself into the Void and suppressing his aura and every trace of vitality.

No sooner had he done so than Yang Kai, concealed in the Void, saw a figure fly over his head, startling him into a cold sweat.

Of all people, the one flying past was Fu Zhun!

Why wasn't that cheap slut on Ice Snow Island? What was she doing out here? Yang Kai cursed inwardly. Fortunately, he had kept watch on his surroundings throughout the journey, or Fu Zhun would have caught him red-handed. How could he possibly answer if she questioned him? The situation would have been unbearably awkward.

Fu Zhun was a Tenth-Tier Great Dragon, an existence comparable to a Great Emperor. Yang Kai had no idea whether his concealment could fool her senses, but it was too late to turn back now. He could only gamble on it working.

What rotten luck. He had slipped back in secret, only for the first person he met to be Fu Zhun! Any other Great Dragon would hardly have worried him, so why did it have to be her?

Worse, if he had sensed her just now, might she already have sensed him?

Yang Kai soon realized that he had worried too much.

Fu Zhun had not discovered him and swept directly over his head.

Before he could rejoice, however, Fu Zhun abruptly stopped, turned around, and descended toward the small island.

Had she discovered him? Yang Kai's mind raced as he wondered how he would explain himself when Fu Zhun came over and demanded to know why he was skulking around in hiding. He had forgotten Mo Xiaoqi and returned to find her! En, that was a decent excuse. But then why had he concealed his aura and vitality like a thief? His efforts to hide only made him look guiltier.

Rustling footsteps sounded, drawing closer to him.

Yang Kai sighed inwardly. Whatever came, he would face it. He disliked her anyway. At worst, they would cast aside all pretenses and fight to see who emerged victorious.

Just then, Yang Kai noticed something strange about Fu Zhun. Her expression had lost little of its usual chill, yet profound sorrow hung over her, as though she were grieving over something.

This cheap slut could feel sad too? Yang Kai nearly thought his eyes were deceiving him. A closer look confirmed that she truly was grief-stricken, and so lost in a daze that she trudged forward like a walking corpse whose Divine Soul had fled its body.

No wonder she had not discovered him! Yang Kai understood at once. Even with his body exiled into the Void, someone of Fu Zhun's cultivation and strength should still have noticed something. But grief had clearly left her distracted and out of sorts, allowing him to escape detection by sheer luck.

The question was, what in this world could leave the Dragon Clan's Second Elder—a figure comparable to a Great Emperor—so dejected and heartsick? Fu Zhun did not show it openly, yet her sorrow was so palpable it seemed almost ready to spill from her.

She had clearly returned just now because she had been so lost in thought that she had flown right past the island before turning back and landing on it.

Could the Great Elder be fooling around with another woman? Yang Kai speculated maliciously, a burst of delight welling up inside him. Cheap slut, so your day has finally come!

Whatever his thoughts, Yang Kai did not dare breathe too loudly. He even closed his eyes.

Fu Zhun was getting closer and closer to his hiding place. If he kept staring at her, he might expose himself.

The rustling footsteps gradually receded. Through his senses, Yang Kai followed Fu Zhun into a cave at the base of a nearby cliff, though he had no idea what she was doing there.

Yang Kai did not dare act rashly either, lest he expose his whereabouts, so he could only wait quietly.

He ended up waiting for three full days. By then, Yang Kai had cursed all eighteen generations of Fu Zhun's ancestors! This was not Ice Snow Island, and he had no idea why she had lingered here for so long. Only after three days did he finally hear footsteps again.

His spirits rose. He cautiously opened his eyes and glanced over, then froze!

Th-this... The sight before him almost made Yang Kai think he was dreaming.

Fu Zhun emerged from the cave with red, swollen eyes. She had clearly been crying, and for a long time too; with her cultivation, nothing less could have left her eyes so puffy. Tear tracks still stained her cheeks. Her former glacial demeanor was nowhere to be seen, and in its place was a touching vulnerability.

Yang Kai was dumbfounded!

A mighty Tenth-Tier Great Dragon had actually cried? And not just any Great Dragon, but the Second Elder, famed throughout the Dragon Clan for her cold and ruthless nature!

This wasn't Fu Zhun. It definitely wasn't Fu Zhun. What kind of monster are you? Yang Kai screamed inwardly. His face went through a wonderful series of expressions as the thrill and smug satisfaction of uncovering Fu Zhun's secret swept over him.

Yang Kai reckoned that if he jumped out now, Fu Zhun would certainly lose all face, but she would probably also fight him to the death.

He would find a chance to mock her mercilessly for this in the future. For now, he would let her off.

While his thoughts were running wild, Fu Zhun turned around and said gently, “I'll come see you again next time.”

Yang Kai was startled. There was someone inside the cave? A thoroughly wicked thought sprang to mind, and once it did, he could not suppress it.

Surely not. It couldn't be... could it? The Dragon Clan's Second Elder surely—probably—couldn't have cuckolded the Great Elder, could she? But if not, what had Fu Zhun meant? A scene took shape in his mind: Fu Zhun was keeping a man in a cave on this island and slipping away now and then to meet him in secret. The more he thought about it, the more certain he became that Zhu Yan had been betrayed.

Inwardly, he observed ten breaths of silence for the Great Elder.

But this cheap slut was far too brazen. Keeping a lover on Dragon Isle was bad enough, yet she had spent three full days with him in secret. She had even left in tears, reluctant to part from him. Could the Great Elder truly know nothing about this?

Yang Kai clicked his tongue in wonder. He had truly seen another side of Fu Zhun today.

Fu Zhun glanced back into the cave several more times before finally steeling herself and flying away!

Yang Kai waited until she had been gone for an hour before letting out a soft breath and releasing the Nothingness Secret Technique. He looked in the direction she had flown, then toward the cave, his expression turning exceedingly strange.

The whole situation left him with a headache. He had only come back to storm the Dragon Temple, yet somehow he had stumbled upon this and nearly been caught red-handed by Fu Zhun.

Had it involved anyone else, Yang Kai would not have cared. People could live however they pleased; whether she was a cheap slut was none of his damn business. But if Fu Zhun was keeping a lover on the side, he might be able to use it against her.

At the very least, he had to find out who was inside that cave! If he could capture the man, Fu Zhun would have no choice but to submit.

Yang Kai acted at once, creeping silently toward the cave.

Anyone who had caught Fu Zhun's eye should possess considerable cultivation—at least the Emperor Realm. Yang Kai could not guess exactly how powerful the man might be, but caution came first. He suppressed his aura as much as possible and cautiously probed the cave with his senses as he advanced.

To his surprise, he could not detect the slightest trace of life inside the cave. He could not help finding this strange.

There were only two possible explanations: either there was nothing alive inside the cave, or the person's cultivation was so profound that Yang Kai could not detect them at all.
