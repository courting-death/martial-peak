## Chapter 3194 — The Bottle of Heaven and Earth

Yang Kai immediately explained everything from the beginning: how he had entered the Star Realm, ended up in Biyu Sect, met Wu Mengchuan, and helped him escape in exchange for being taught the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, then how he had killed Wu Mengchuan in the Shattered Star Sea, allowing the Incarnation's cultivation method to be perfected. He went through every detail, hiding nothing.

Yang Yan listened in silence, her elegant brows sometimes drawing together, sometimes easing again. Only after he finished did she turn to the Incarnation Yang Kai had summoned, looking thoughtful.

The Incarnation had been concealed nearby the entire time. Yang Kai had originally planned to summon it only after unsealing his Dantian and demonifying himself, then join forces with it to fight the feather-crowned man. In the end, Yang Yan had appeared at the last moment and stopped him, so this hidden move had never come into play.

"So this clone of yours is the one who cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art?"

"That's right." Yang Kai looked at her warily. "You aren't planning to cripple its cultivation too, are you?"

Then again, the Incarnation was not made of flesh and blood. It had no Meridians or Dantian to speak of, so even if Yang Yan really wanted to cripple it, she would probably have no easy way to do so unless she smashed it to pieces.

"Cultivating the Heaven Devouring Battle Art with the body of a Stone Puppet." Yang Yan circled the Incarnation several times, clicking her tongue in wonder as though she had found some fascinating new toy. "How did you come up with that?"

"Just chance." Yang Kai gave a rueful laugh. "Back then, when Wu Mengchuan taught me the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, I didn't know what he was after, but I could tell it couldn't be anything good. Still, the art itself was truly profound, and I couldn't bring myself to throw it away, so I let the Incarnation try cultivating it. Who knew a careless experiment would actually bear fruit?"

Yang Yan nodded. "Perhaps it wasn't chance after all. This may be an opportunity. The Stone Puppet Clan is born with earthen bodies and stone physiques, without the seven emotions and six desires of other living beings, and their innate Divine Ability makes it especially easy for them to refine and reject all kinds of impurities. Letting it cultivate the Heaven Devouring Battle Art may have been the best possible choice." She understood Stone Puppets, and she also knew some of the Heaven Devouring Battle Art's drawbacks. Somewhere in the dark, she felt as if the Heaven Devouring Battle Art had been tailor-made for the Stone Puppet Clan, not for bodies of flesh and blood.

Wu Kuang might not have been born so cold-blooded and heartless. It was only after cultivating the Heaven Devouring Battle Art that his nature had been twisted, making him see all living beings as ants. For the sake of his own power, he had not hesitated to destroy countless Star Domains.

But a Stone Puppet did not need to worry about that at all. Its innate Divine Ability was enough to refine away every impurity it absorbed, leaving it completely unaffected.

"No wonder so many people who cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art back then kept running into bottlenecks. So they had to devour another person who cultivated this evil art as a catalyst before they could break through their shackles." Yang Yan's beautiful eyes flashed as many things suddenly became clear to her.

Yang Kai said in surprise, "Many people cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art?"

"That was many years ago."

When she said "many years ago," she did not mean several dozen or even a hundred years, nor did she mean several thousand. She meant tens of thousands of years ago! Wu Kuang had relied on the Heaven Devouring Battle Art to stand firmly as the number one person throughout the ages, so naturally the art drew covetous eyes. For some reason, the cultivation method for the Heaven Devouring Battle Art had spread and fallen into the hands of certain ambitious people. Some Emperor Realm Masters had even crippled their own cultivation and started over just to cultivate it.

Yet without exception, every person who cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art became stuck at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm, with no hope of advancing to the Emperor Realm in their lifetime.

It was not because they cultivated it incorrectly, nor because the method they had obtained was wrong. That was simply the nature of the Heaven Devouring Battle Art. Wu Mengchuan was a descendant of Wu Kuang, yet even he had been stuck at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm for years without making the slightest progress, much less anyone else.

Breaking through was simple enough: devour another person who cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art and take everything they had as one's own. It was like raising gu, forcing them to slaughter one another until only the survivor had any hope of transformation.

When Wu Mengchuan taught Yang Kai the Heaven Devouring Battle Art back then, one reason was to use it as the condition for Yang Kai to save him from the Bone Prison, and the other was to raise a target he could devour in the future. Unfortunately for him, he had run into Yang Kai, a cultivator from a Lower Plane Star Domain who was completely unmoved by the Heaven Devouring Battle Art's fearsome reputation, and that was how Yang Kai had resisted the temptation.

Had it been a cultivator born and raised in the Star Realm, they probably would have cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art at once, and the end result would naturally have been tragic.

After Wu Kuang, although quite a few people cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, no one was ever able to advance to the Emperor Realm again. As a result, many people came to believe the cultivation method was wrong, that Wu Kuang had never passed down the true Heaven Devouring Battle Art at all, and that the real method had been buried with him in the Shattered Star Sea long ago. As the years passed, the Heaven Devouring Battle Art gradually faded into obscurity, and no one cultivated it anymore.

Suddenly, Yang Yan thought of something else. Since the Heaven Devouring Battle Art was so perverse, then who had Wu Kuang devoured back then to let the art break through? There must have been another existence who had also cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art. Perhaps... there had been some unknown tale of grudges, love, and hatred behind it, but too much time had passed, and there was no way to trace it now.

She looked up at Gungun again and hesitated. "This is the Xuan Realm Pearl's... Artifact Spirit?" Naturally, the Xuan Realm Pearl was no stranger to her. She had brought it from the Star Realm to this Star Domain back then, and after Yang Kai obtained it, she had left it with him. She had never imagined that when she saw it again, the Xuan Realm Pearl would have become this, not only giving birth to an Artifact Spirit, but also possessing such a powerful devouring nature.

"Exactly!" Yang Kai laughed, his face full of pride.

The Xuan Realm Pearl had been in his hands for many years and had helped him escape danger again and again. Its internal space had also greatly benefited his cultivation of Space Divine Abilities, so Yang Kai naturally valued it highly. Now that it had given birth to an Artifact Spirit, it felt almost like having a son.

He paused, scratched his head, and asked, "Do you want to take it back?"

"If I really wanted it back, what would you do?" Yang Yan looked at him.

Yang Kai waved a hand. "Then take it." It had been hers to begin with.

Yang Yan was stunned. She had not expected Yang Kai to agree so readily.

"But you have to promise me one thing. You can't damage its spirituality. Although I used it as the carrier to display the Heaven Devouring Battle Art and caused it to give birth to an Artifact Spirit, it has done nothing wrong. If you agree to that, then there's no harm in returning the Xuan Realm Pearl to you."

Through this journey across the Great Wilderness Star Domain, during which he had let it devour freely and expand the space of the Small Xuan Realm, Yang Kai had also deepened his comprehension of the Laws of Space.

He had a feeling that he could now refine a world and condense it into a Xuan Realm Pearl himself. So if Yang Yan really wanted to take the Xuan Realm Pearl back, there was nothing he could not let go of. At worst, he could refine another one himself, move the Everlasting Tree and Firmament Tree from the Small Xuan Realm into it, and leave everything else aside.

Yang Yan laughed. "I was only saying it. Don't take it seriously." This thing contained a world of its own. Without mastery of the Laws of Space, even holding it in one's hand would not bring out much of its power; it would only leave a pearl buried in dust. Otherwise, she would not have left such a treasure with Yang Kai back then. Her expression turned solemn. "Use it carefully!"

"I know."

“And this Star Domain as well. Do not continue devouring it. If this matter truly blows up, it will not look good for anyone.” Yang Yan pointed at the boundless darkness as she spoke.

"Fine."

"And when you return to the Star Realm in the future, do not let the Heaven Devouring Battle Art be exposed before the world. Otherwise, disaster will surely fall on you." Yang Yan's expression was solemn as she warned him. She was willing to believe Yang Kai and the Incarnation, but that did not mean others would. Leaving aside anyone else, if Iron Blood Great Emperor learned that someone in this world was still cultivating the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, he would probably be the first to act. The wrath of a Great Emperor was not something Yang Kai could endure right now.

"I'll remember." Yang Kai nodded and secretly let out a breath. He had finally passed Yang Yan's test. After hesitating for a moment, he said, "Let me ask you something."

"What?"

"What cultivation did that guy just now have, exactly?"

Instead of answering, Yang Yan asked, "What do you think?"

"Great Emperor!" Yang Kai said in a deep voice. It was not an illusion. The feeling the feather-crowned man gave him was exactly like facing a Great Emperor. It was only that the man seemed to have been suppressed somehow, and had underestimated Yang Kai besides, which was why he had suffered some hidden losses. If Yang Kai had faced him in the Star Realm, he was sure he would have had no power to fight back.

On Dragon Isle, he had been able to run rampant and fight Dragon Clan Elders comparable to Great Emperors because his greatest reliance was bloodline suppression. Without that suppression of the Dragon Clan bloodline, he could not possibly be a match for any Dragon Clan Elder.

Yang Yan smiled and shook her head.

"How is that possible?" Yang Kai looked shocked. "He's not a Great Emperor? Could he be at the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm? No, no, no Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master could have that kind of strength."

"Nor is he at the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm."

Yang Kai stared, tongue-tied. "Don't tell me there's another realm between the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm and Great Emperor!"

"There isn't. It's just that person... is a Great Emperor, and yet he is not a Great Emperor!"

Yang Kai was completely lost. "What does that mean?"

"How should I put it..." Yang Yan pondered for a moment. If an ordinary First-Stage Emperor Realm Master had asked this, she definitely would not have answered. Sometimes, when one's strength had not reached the proper level, knowing too much could easily become an obstacle to cultivation. But Yang Kai was clearly no ordinary First-Stage Emperor Realm Master. With the strength he had just displayed, he was qualified to touch on these matters.

"If you compare all of Heaven and Earth to a bottle, then the sand, stones, mud, and water inside that bottle are us cultivators. The vessel that is Heaven and Earth has a limited capacity."

An image formed in Yang Kai's mind, and he nodded slightly for her to continue.

"The countless living beings are the water in the bottle. Each drop is too small to notice, yet together they fill all of Heaven and Earth. The larger the grains of mud and sand, the greater the strength they represent. As for Great Emperors, they are simply the stones in the bottle!"

"Ten stones?" Yang Kai's expression turned strange.

Yang Yan smiled. "Exactly. Although the Bottle of Heaven and Earth is vast, it can hold only ten stones, and not one more. That is why there has always been talk of the ten Great Emperors since ancient times. No matter how the ages pass, that number has never increased or decreased. But that does not mean there are only ten stones in the Bottle of Heaven and Earth. There are other powerful existences who have transcended the Emperor Realm but have not received Heaven and Earth's recognition. These people are Great Emperors, yet also not Great Emperors. In terms of cultivation level, they have already reached the realm of Great Emperor, but because of the limits of the Bottle of Heaven and Earth, they cannot become true Great Emperors."

"Like him?"

Yang Yan nodded.

"Like you?"

Yang Yan smiled without answering.
