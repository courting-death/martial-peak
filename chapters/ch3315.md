## Chapter 3315 — Divine Dragon Tail Swing

Refusing to give Yang Kai any chance to close in, Cang Mo poured his Emperor Yuan into the Thunder Pestle, and its power surged. Yang Kai's forward charge was checked at once, but he seemed to have expected it. He held the Mountain and River Bell before him, blocking most of the lightning's might, while the five fingers of his other hand spread wide and a heaven-covering palm came crashing down toward Cang Mo.

It was as though a great mountain had fallen from the sky, wrapped in a biting wind and filled with a life-stealing aura.

With a boom, a huge five-fingered print was smashed into the ground. The earth split apart, and as dust billowed up, Cang Mo had already vanished, having fled into the sky at some unknown moment.

Before he could steady himself, the Incarnation, wreathed in raging flames and covered in backward-pointing spines flashing with cold light, charged out from the side with the Demonic Warhammer in hand and smashed it toward him. Wherever the warhammer passed, it left a trail of shattered space.

Cang Mo roared and turned the Thunder Pestle toward the Incarnation. A beam of white lightning shot from the pestle and struck the Demonic Warhammer. In an instant, Demonic Qi surged, light flashed wildly across the warhammer, and even the Incarnation's dozen-zhang-tall body staggered from the blow. Its killing strike came to nothing.

With a whoosh, Yang Kai opened his mouth and breathed out. A burst of golden Dragon Breath sliced through the void, ignoring the barrier of space, and struck Cang Mo squarely in the shoulder. The violent force drove Cang Mo back more than a hundred zhang before he managed to steady himself. Blood mist burst from his shoulder, as if a large chunk of flesh had been shaken into powder.

This was the first time Cang Mo had been injured since the battle began. Although it looked like only a minor wound, his earlier ease, as though everything were under his control, was gone. Pain spread through him, twisting his face into something savage and terrifying, and the Thunder Laws around him surged even more violently.

At some point, dark clouds had filled the sky, matching Cang Mo's black expression.

The bright sunlight vanished, and the whole world seemed to sink into night. The heavy clouds pressed down on the sky like a quilt, and on everyone's chest as well. Within them, lightning flickered, tearing bright gashes across the horizon.

Having landed a blow, Yang Kai rushed forward without pause. The Incarnation, blasted away moments earlier, hurriedly steadied itself and attacked from the other side.

Two enormous figures, carrying an unstoppable momentum, launched fearless charges at one tiny existence. The sight was truly shocking.

Cang Mo, however, suddenly closed his eyes. The savagery on his face gradually faded into calm. When he opened them again, sharp light flashed in his pupils. He raised the Thunder Pestle high, white lightning shining from its tip and echoing the lightning in the sky, as if it could draw down the might of the Nine Heavens.

"Fall!" he shouted softly.

Heaven and Earth trembled. Bolts of lightning as thick as water buckets crashed down from the sky, each one terrifyingly powerful and impossible to ignore. Yet these bolts seemed to have eyes, all of them striking toward Yang Kai and the Incarnation.

In an instant, lightning flashed across several dozen li. Countless thunder pillars connected Heaven and Earth, turning the entire area into a pool of thunder light filled with killing intent. Cang Mo stood coldly at the very center of this Thunder Pool, holding the Thunder Pestle high as he looked down at Yang Kai and the Incarnation. Though his body was small, he carried the air of one standing high above all others, as if he were looking at two ants.

Their enormous bodies became a great disadvantage at this moment, making it difficult for them to move out of danger. No matter how agile Yang Kai and the Incarnation were, they could not avoid the interference of those countless thunder pillars.

Even while dodging with all their strength, they were still struck by one thunder pillar after another.

Flesh was blasted away and dragon scales shattered, but Yang Kai did not make a sound. His eyes were wider than usual as he darted through the thunder pool and sea of lightning, rapidly closing in on Cang Mo.

The Incarnation was in slightly better condition than him, but not by much. It had no flesh or blood, yet broken stones kept rustling down from its body, and even its flames had dimmed. Although it maintained the Heaven Devourer Domain around itself, its cultivation was still lacking, so it could not devour too much energy in a short time and could only endure by force. One spine after another shot from its body to meet the falling lightning, only to be blasted to pieces.

Roar...

A soaring dragon roar rang out again, and a giant dragon appeared before Yang Kai, tossing its head and swishing its tail. The dragon was a dozen or so zhang long, so lifelike it seemed alive. The moment it appeared, it pounced at Cang Mo to bite him.

Cold-faced Cang Mo's eyes narrowed. How could he fail to recognize this as a Secret Technique of the Dragon Clan?

If this had happened earlier, he might still have been astonished. How could Yang Kai, with a Half-Dragon body, use a Secret Technique belonging to the Dragon Clan? After all, the Dragon Clan's Secret Techniques were inherited arts, and one could only practice them on Dragon Isle. But after tasting Yang Kai's Dragon Blood and witnessing how strange it was, Cang Mo no longer found this surprising.

Cang Mo merely waved his hand, and a thunder pillar falling from the sky changed direction, transforming into a thunder python that met the giant dragon head-on. The two collided in midair, pouncing, tearing, and biting at each other until the sky darkened, the earth shook, and sand and stones flew everywhere.

Yang Kai had already seized the chance to charge out, gripping the Mountain and River Bell and swinging it viciously at Cang Mo.

How could Cang Mo dare take such a strike head-on? Naturally, he dodged early. Unexpectedly, Yang Kai's attack was only a feint. His feet never stopped, and he charged straight at Cang Mo again.

Cang Mo wanted to dodge once more, but the Incarnation had seized the opportunity to entangle him, leaving him unable to break free for the moment. His eyes nearly split with rage as he saw Yang Kai pounce in savagely, the Mountain and River Bell in his hand smashing down at his head again.

Cang Mo was indeed formidable. Under an attack that should have been almost impossible to avoid, he made an inconceivable move, lowering himself as if his body had no bones and narrowly slipping past that terrifying strike.

But before he could recover, a tremendous force lashed across his waist, striking him so hard that his blood churned, his bones cracked, and his eyes nearly bulged from their sockets.

In that hurried instant, he saw only a golden dragon tail flash past.

A dragon tail! Cang Mo swallowed a mouthful of bloody saliva, rage erupting in his chest like a volcano.

Yang Kai laughed loudly. "Haha, how does this Lord's Divine Dragon Tail Swing taste? Uh..." Before he could finish, several bolts of lightning slammed into him, making him shudder all over. His golden hair stood straight up, leaving him looking utterly ridiculous.

"You refuse a toast only to drink a forfeit. Today, all of you die!" Cang Mo's icy voice rang out. Clearly, he was truly furious now. Although he had wanted to make a deal with Yang Kai before, it now seemed that had been nothing but his own wishful thinking. Yang Kai would never compromise with him.

If he wanted that thing, he would have to seize it by force! As for whether that would offend Yang Yan and bring that woman after him... as long as he silenced everyone here, who would ever know?

The great battle erupted once more. Outside Heavenly Wolf Valley, endless rumbling rang out, leaving all the disciples in the valley anxious and afraid.

By now, even more people had gathered around Tang Sheng and the others, all of them high-level members of Heavenly Wolf Valley. Ling Yinqin had come as well, and when she learned that Yang Kai was the one fighting that powerful enemy, she immediately started forward to help, only for the quick-eyed, quick-handed Qian Xiuying to pull her back and scold, "What are you doing?"

Ling Yinqin said calmly, "Lending what little strength I have."

Qian Xiuying said, "If you go, you will only distract him. You will not be able to help at all. Look at that battle yourself. Are you sure you can lend even that little strength?"

Ling Yinqin watched silently for a while without saying a word.

It was true. Even if she wanted to help, she could not interfere in a battle of this level. If she really rushed in, she would only distract Yang Kai and hold him back. Unable to stop herself, she turned a pleading gaze toward Tang Sheng.

Tang Sheng coughed dryly and said awkwardly, "It would be useless if I went too."

At the very beginning, when Yang Kai had been besieged by the two great forces, Tang Sheng might have been of some use if he had joined in. At the very least, he could have restrained one Second-Stage Emperor Realm Master. But how could he step into the battle before them now? He feared he would be electrocuted to death by a thunder pillar the moment he charged in.

They were both Second-Stage Emperor Realm Masters, yet Yang Kai could exchange blows with Cang Mo while he did not even have the qualifications to participate. The comparison left even Tang Sheng somewhat disheartened.

"Master, can he win?" Lan He asked, her face full of worry.

Tang Sheng had no answer. How could he tell whether Yang Kai would win? Today's battle had already surpassed everything he had seen and heard in his life. Before the dust settled, he did not dare make any judgment, nor was he qualified to.

Judging by the situation alone, although Yang Kai had joined forces with Shi Huo, things did not look optimistic. The might of a Quasi-Great Emperor was truly extraordinary. Still, Yang Kai and Shi Huo were no pushovers either. For a Second-Stage Emperor Realm Master to fight a Quasi-Great Emperor to this point was already something to be proud of.

In fact, there was something else Tang Sheng found strange. Logically speaking, Shi Huo could also be considered a Divine Spirit. Although he did not rank particularly high among the Divine Spirits, he should still be roughly equal to a Quasi-Great Emperor, no? Even if he was not a match, the gap should not be too large. So why did this Shi Huo feel somewhat different from the rumors?

What he did not know was that this Shi Huo was not the one he had heard of. If Shi Huo's true self were really here, then although he might not necessarily be able to defeat Cang Mo, protecting himself would pose no problem, and if he fought with his life on the line, he might even drag Cang Mo down with him.

But the Incarnation was a special existence after all: a Stone Puppet body, Yang Kai's Soul Incarnation, and a being that had devoured Shi Huo's Origin. Although it had inherited some of Shi Huo's legacy and abilities, it had simply had too little time to grow.

The current Incarnation had not yet reached its limit. With the Heaven Devouring Battle Art at its disposal, it could not compare to Shi Huo in the short term, but in the long run, it had far greater room to improve. It even had a chance to surpass Shi Huo's distant ancestor and lead Shi Huo's line to its most glorious moment.

It was precisely because of this that, by joining forces with Yang Kai, it had the qualifications to contend with Cang Mo. But that was all it had...

Mountains shook and the earth trembled. The terrain outside Heavenly Wolf Valley had been completely transformed. Within the thunder pool and sea of lightning, two enormous figures sometimes lunged forward and sometimes retreated, hazy and indistinct. Even when Tang Sheng and the others pushed their eyesight to the limit, they still could not make out the situation clearly. They could only vaguely see traces of Yang Kai and the Incarnation; after all, their bodies were so large that it would be hard not to see them. As for Cang Mo, no one knew where he was.

But judging from the sounds of battle, Cang Mo clearly was not holding back much, and had even gained a slight upper hand. Yang Kai's angry roars and Shi Huo's muffled groans were the best proof.
