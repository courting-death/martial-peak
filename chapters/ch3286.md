## Chapter 3286 — Alone With His Shadow

Seeing Yang Kai accept her advice so readily, Hua Qingsi quietly breathed a sigh of relief. She had been willing to risk embarrassing him by exposing the matter because she feared that gaining power might change him in some unexpected way. Greater strength and swelling authority often awakened darker things in a person, but from the look of it now, Yang Kai had not changed.

So Hua Qingsi said bluntly, “If Lingxiao Palace wants to unify the Northern Territory, it absolutely cannot rely on strength alone. That is only an early-stage method. If we truly want everyone’s support, we still have to win their hearts. Suppressing them with bloody methods will certainly be effective, but it will inevitably breed resentment. The destruction of two Sects was no small matter, and recently many Sects in the Northern Territory have been uneasy, afraid that we will turn our blade on them next. We may have no such intention, but how would they know that?”

Yang Kai narrowed his eyes. "Mi Qi acted recklessly and went too far. He should be punished!"

Hua Qingsi cupped her fists. "Palace Master is wise. May I ask what decision Palace Master has reached?"

Yang Kai asked instead, "What do you think, Sister Hua?"

Hua Qingsi pondered for a moment. "Although Mi Qi distorted Palace Master's meaning, he can still be said to have done the wrong thing with good intentions. If the punishment is too heavy, I fear the world will think Lingxiao Palace is timid and afraid of trouble. If it is too light, it will be meaningless. This subordinate believes we can order him to step down in favor of someone more worthy, then let him live out his years in Mitian Sect."

The corner of Yang Kai's mouth lifted. "Sister Hua has thought this through..."

Hua Qingsi curled her lip. "If Palace Master wants to say I'm discarding him once he has served his purpose, just say it. Your face says it all."

"Nonsense." Yang Kai protested aloud, though in his heart he had indeed been thinking just that. The reason Lingxiao Palace had previously been able to sweep through the Northern Territory was partly because its own strength was formidable enough, and partly because it had joined forces with the other three top Sects to pressure the entire Northern Territory. Throughout the process, Mi Qi had rendered considerable service. After witnessing the strength of Yang Kai and Lingxiao Palace in particular, Mi Qi had personally taken the lead, handled matters himself, and led Mitian Sect's Emperor Realm Masters to suppress and subdue quite a few forces along the way.

Now that the overall situation was settled, they were going to order him to step down in favor of someone more worthy. If that was not discarding him once he had served his purpose, what was?

Hua Qingsi said, "Mi Qi cannot really be called a petty villain, but he is ambitious and difficult to control. We can use this matter to give him a warning. At the same time, we can support a puppet inside Mitian Sect and truly bring Mitian Sect under our control." This was Hua Qingsi's real objective.

Of the Northern Territory's four great Sects, Ice Heart Valley had deep ties to Yang Kai, while Li Dragon Palace followed Yang Kai's lead in everything. The only variable left was Mitian Sect. If they brought Mi Qi down this time and secretly backed a Sect Master they could control, then they would truly hold the Northern Territory in the palm of their hand. Once Lingxiao Palace issued an order and the three other top Sects responded as one, would the other forces of the Northern Territory still dare to sing a different tune?

Yang Kai naturally saw Hua Qingsi's intentions and immediately felt that making Sister Hua the Chief Steward had truly been a wise decision. Her cultivation might not be especially high, but she had grown up in Star God Palace and had absorbed much through what she saw and heard there. When handling matters like this, she could turn clouds and rain with a flip of her hand, moving with effortless skill.

Naturally, he had no objections and simply let Hua Qingsi handle it as she saw fit.

With that matter settled, Mi Qi's fate was also decided. When the time came, Hua Qingsi would bring the three great Yao Kings to Mitian Sect, and Mi Qi likely would not even dare think of resisting.

Hua Qingsi then said, "Palace Master also needs to make a trip to Wuhua Hall."

Yang Kai said, "Because of the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens?"

Every top Sect had its own foundation. In the Southern Territory, Star God Palace had the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda, Qingyang Shrine had the Divine Roaming Mirror, Wuhua Hall had the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens, and Tianwu Sacred Land had Martial Intent Hall. Each one was a rare treasure land seldom seen in the world.

Although Wuhua Hall had been destroyed and every last disciple in the Sect had died, its underlying foundation had not been ruined. Leaving aside the blessed land it originally occupied and the wealth Wuhua Hall had accumulated over many years, the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens alone was enough to make countless people covet it.

However, Wuhua Hall's wealth had already been removed under the direction of several Elders from Star God Palace and distributed to the Sects of the Southern Territory. It could be considered compensation for the heavy losses they had suffered this time.

As for that vast estate, Yang Kai had ultimately requested it for himself.

In the matter of Wuhua Hall, Yang Kai had turned the tide and saved tens of thousands of Southern Territory elites from disaster. That feat alone was an enormous merit. Afterward, whatever request he made, the Southern Territory side had no choice but to consider it carefully.

Yang Kai had made no other request. He had only asked for Wuhua Hall's foundation.

Even if Star God Palace was somewhat reluctant, it had no choice but to agree, while Qingyang Shrine naturally would not hold Yang Kai back. If Yang Kai obtained Wuhua Hall's territory, Qingyang Shrine would benefit as well. Since those two had no objections, it no longer mattered whether Tianwu Sacred Land's Saint Lord Ma Qing objected.

And so, Wuhua Hall's foundation could now be considered Yang Kai's private property.

He had once considered moving Luocha Sect over to Wuhua Hall. After all, the spirit mountains and great rivers Wuhua Hall occupied were extraordinary, in no way inferior to Lingxiao Palace. If Luocha Sect really could move there, it would be enormously helpful to its future development.

But after thinking it over, he let the idea go. He had no deep ties with Luocha Sect. The reason he had gone all out to protect it was simply to ease his conscience. Now, with the matter of Sheng Yuzhu added on top, that idea had vanished completely.

As it happened, Lingxiao Palace had far too many disciples. Its foundation was indeed exceptional, but one hundred thousand disciples in a single Sect was still too frightening a number. No Sect in the world had that many disciples.

Wuhua Hall could easily share part of that burden. More importantly, Wuhua Hall also had a treasure land like the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens, which could help cultivators temper their flesh bodies.

Yang Kai understood better than anyone how important a powerful flesh body was. The last time he had been ambushed, he would likely have died there if not for his sturdy physique, much less turned the tide at the brink of collapse.

In anyone else's hands, even such a treasure land would be difficult to use. One was in the Northern Territory and the other in the Southern Territory. Who could look after both?

Yang Kai was different. A Space Array linking north and south let him ignore the barrier of distance entirely. No matter which side ran into trouble, the other could quickly send support, unless someone managed to sneak in and destroy the Space Array in one strike.

During Yang Kai's stay at Luocha Sect, Lingxiao Palace had already begun preparing to take over Wuhua Hall's foundation and establish a Lingxiao Palace branch in the Southern Territory.

To do a job well, one must first sharpen one's tools. Hua Qingsi had already sent Nanmen Dajun ahead to Wuhua Hall to prepare a Grand Array. Once the array was complete, they could oversee the disciples' relocation. Hua Qingsi planned to move fifty thousand people there, which would greatly ease the pressure on Lingxiao Palace. After all, these cultivators had only recently followed Yang Kai up from the lower Star Domain, and all of them were in a period of rapid advancement after long accumulation. Their demand for Spiritual Qi was extremely high. With one hundred thousand people gathered inside Lingxiao Palace, how could the local Spiritual Qi possibly keep up? Relocating half of them would certainly relieve the burden.

"The Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens is indeed profoundly wondrous. This subordinate experienced it once before, and it has a marvelous effect in tempering the flesh body. Yao King Xi Lei is stationed there now, so there is no need to worry about security. However, Palace Master will need to arrange that Space Array again."

"I understand." Yang Kai nodded.

Hua Qingsi sighed again. "Our Palace still has too few Emperor Realm Masters. With a branch being established in the Southern Territory, my only concern is that we will not have many Masters stationed there, which will inevitably tempt some people to covet it."

Among Lingxiao Palace's Emperor Realm Masters, aside from Yang Kai, there was Liu Yan; below her were the three great Yao Kings, Ji Ying, Hua Qingsi, Bian Yuqing, Nanmen Dajun, Hou Yu, and the newly promoted Ye Hen. Even counting every last one, there were only a dozen or so people. Such a number was already terrifying to other Sects, and anyone else who became Palace Master would likely wake from laughter in their sleep, but strictly speaking, Ji Ying, Nanmen Dajun, and Hou Yu were all specialists. If a battle truly broke out, Yang Kai would never let them take part and would certainly protect them first.

The three great Yao Kings were quite strong, but they came from the Wild Ancient Land in the Eastern Territory and had not been raised by Lingxiao Palace itself. They were also yao by nature. As for the remaining few, all of them were First Stage Emperor Realm Masters.

Leaving aside Emperor Realm Masters, even Dao Origin Realm cultivators were so few they could be counted on one hand.

Thus, although Lingxiao Palace looked deep-rooted and flourishing on the surface, only they themselves knew the truth of its situation. Changing the current state of affairs was not something that could be done in a short time.

"I'll solve this problem." Yang Kai's eyes shifted as an idea came to him.

Hua Qingsi glanced at him, seemed to understand something, and smiled.

After finalizing matters with Hua Qingsi and deciding to visit Wuhua Hall the next day, Yang Kai's figure flickered and vanished from the spot.

The next day, Yang Kai set out with Hua Qingsi, his face dark.

"Did Palace Master not rest well last night?" Hua Qingsi read his expression, a teasing smile on her face.

Yang Kai glanced at her and snorted. "With a beauty in my arms, how could I possibly rest?"

Hua Qingsi blushed and could not help spitting, "Shameless!"

She did not expose him, though. As Lingxiao Palace's Chief Steward, how could any movement in the Palace be hidden from her? Last night, Yang Kai had indeed gone looking for romance with Xue Yue. After all, he had been running around outside for a while and had rarely come home, so how could he leave a beauty alone in an empty room? But as far as Hua Qingsi knew, he had not succeeded at all.

Xia Ningshang was utterly absorbed in learning the Alchemic Dao from Ji Ying and had no time to bother with him. He had gone to Medicine Pill Peak, only to be driven out by Ji Ying. If anyone in all of Lingxiao Palace dared deny Yang Kai face, it was Ji Ying alone. Alchemists hated being disturbed while refining Spirit Pills, and Ji Ying was also Sublime Pill Great Emperor's disciple. He was usually mild and amiable, but once he entered the state of pill refining, he was almost a different person.

Su Yan had been sent to Ice Heart Valley, so he had not even seen her this time.

Shan Qingluo had also gone to the Wild Ancient Land under Yingfei and Xie Wuwei's arrangements. The environment in the Ancient Land was very suitable for this yao woman to cultivate.

The last one, Xue Yue, was also in retreat.

Poor Lord Palace Master. He had four Madams in name, yet had spent the entire night alone with only his shadow for company. The thought was miserably pitiful, but now his mouth was tougher than a duck's beak, so Hua Qingsi still had to preserve his dignity and did not say it to his face.
