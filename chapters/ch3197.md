## Chapter 3197 — Ice Soul

"Trying to leave now? Too late. Freeze!" Yang Kai sneered. His words became law, Space itself locking down. The women instantly froze in place as if struck by a body-binding spell, only their eyes left darting about.

Yang Kai strolled over to Shan Qingluo with his hands clasped behind his back, perfectly at ease. Pinching her smooth chin in one hand, he leaned close and said, "You were the one who said that just now, weren't you?"

“Pah!” Shan Qingluo spat lightly. “So what if it was?”

Yang Kai nodded. "Very good. Looks like you really do want to have one. Today, this Husband of yours will grant your wish."

"I'm afraid you don't have the ability!" Shan Qingluo snorted.

"You'll know whether I have that ability soon enough." As he spoke, he paced over to Xue Yue and trailed a finger gently across her face, all the way down to her neck. Xue Yue's cheeks flushed crimson at once, and her tender body trembled.

"Do you still remember our agreement from back then?"

Xue Yue looked away, refusing to meet his eyes, and gritted out, "What agreement from back then?"

Yang Kai smiled. "You said it yourself. As long as I restored Ningshang to full health, you would..."

"Don't say it, don't say it!" Xue Yue cried in panic. Back then, she had been so worried about Xia Ningshang that she had spoken without thinking. Only later, after she calmed down, had she realized how improper it was. Although they had all long since known the affairs of men and women, they had never gone quite that far. Every time she thought of that agreement, her heart raced with fright, let alone now that she had to face it herself.

Fortunately, Yang Kai had been missing without a trace for the past ten years. Who could have known that the moment he returned after all that time, he would bring this matter up?

"What agreement?" Xia Ningshang asked curiously. From the sound of it, this seemed to involve her as well.

Yang Kai said, "Xue Yue said that as long as I could make you recover, she would agree to let me and all of you together..." He stretched out both hands and grabbed at the air a few times, wearing an exceedingly indecent look.

"Ah!" Xia Ningshang was shocked, her face flushing red as she whispered, "How... how could that be allowed?"

Shan Qingluo glanced sidelong at Xue Yue and clicked her tongue in wonder. "I really couldn't tell, little sister Yue. So you're this bold? Still, it is a good idea!" She actually looked eager to try, seemingly even more interested than Yang Kai.

"You think it's a good idea too, do you?" Yang Kai turned to look at her.

"Naturally." Shan Qingluo's eyes turned soft and sultry, as if she could already picture the indecent scene.

With a snap of his fingers, Yang Kai restored Shan Qingluo's freedom. She rose gracefully and came before Xue Yue, bending down to breathe beside her ear. "Although I do think it's a good idea, I'm still not pleased about being sold out behind my back. Little sister Yue, how should I punish you later?"

Xue Yue's ears burned under Shan Qingluo's breath. Closing her eyes in bitter grief, she said, "Qingluo, how can you help him do evil?"

Shan Qingluo laughed seductively. "If you can say it, why can't I do it?"

"I don't agree!" An icy voice rang out, instantly dropping the temperature all around them.

Shan Qingluo turned to Su Yan and said, "Husband, Eldest Sister says she doesn't agree."

Yang Kai burst out laughing. "Today, it isn't up to any of you. Luo'er, bring Xue Yue over." As he spoke, he wrapped one arm around Xia Ningshang and the other around Su Yan, then rushed toward the bedchamber.

Shan Qingluo placed a hand on Xue Yue's shoulder, sprang into the air, and followed close behind.

In the blink of an eye, three delicate bodies lay across the great bed in the bedchamber. Yang Kai and Shan Qingluo stood shoulder to shoulder, both laughing wickedly, like weasels who had stolen chickens.

Xia Ningshang's eyes were squeezed shut, her long lashes trembling, looking as though she had resigned herself to her fate.

Xue Yue turned her head to one side, lightly biting her red lips, nervous and uneasy.

Only Su Yan stared coldly at Yang Kai, icy chill radiating from her entire body. "Release me at once, or don't blame me for ruining your mood."

"Luo'er, deal with her!" Yang Kai pointed.

"Guaranteed to satisfy you!" Shan Qingluo smiled sweetly and bent down before Su Yan, meeting her eyes. In an instant, Shan Qingluo's beautiful eyes turned into a pool of spring water, rippling with enticing charm. She possessed the bloodline source of the Heavenly Moon Demon Spider and was highly proficient in Charm Techniques herself. If Su Yan had been at her peak, she naturally could have ignored it, but Yang Kai had sealed all her cultivation, leaving even the movement of a single finger extremely difficult. How could she resist?

The moment their gazes met, Su Yan felt herself sinking into a whirlpool. Her ice-cold body gradually heated, and waves of tingling numbness spread through her.

Before long, the solid ice began to melt...

Soon, soft, winding moans drifted from the side room, and spring filled the chamber without end.

Several days later, Yang Kai took Su Yan and the others to visit his parents. Naturally, he could not avoid a scolding from Dong Suzhu, but fortunately, with little Xue'er keeping them company now, his parents had gained a measure of comfort.

Ever since their previous encounter, Yang Xue seemed to hold this Eldest Brother of hers in a rather fearful respect and kept looking at him timidly. When Yang Kai called to her and took her roaming through the Starry Sky for a long time, letting her see its vast and magnificent beauty, his place in Yang Xue's heart rose by leaps and bounds. In one stroke, he surpassed Xia Ningshang and the others, ranking only behind her parents.

After spending several days with his parents and enjoying the warmth of family, Yang Kai gave the order, and the relocation of the entire Lingxiao Sect began in earnest. On Dark Star, the great Sects heard the news and gathered one after another to probe whether it was true. Once Ye Xijun confirmed it, they were overjoyed and all declared that they wanted to hitch a ride as well.

Following Yang Kai's instructions, Ye Xijun neither refused nor fully agreed. She only told them to return and carefully select outstanding disciples to gather at Lingxiao Sect. Lingxiao Sect already had enough people. If they really brought every member of the other Sects along too, the total would likely reach several hundred thousand.

Although Lingxiao Palace now had a decent foundation, it still could not support so many people. Yang Kai had to be selective.

The news spread not only across Dark Star, but throughout the entire Star Domain. Every Cultivation Star learned that Yang Kai, Master of the Star Domain, would soon leave the Star Domain for a higher-level world, and that before he left, he would bring a group of outstanding cultivators with him to form his own foundation.

These cultivators would be chosen through layer upon layer of selection. Each Cultivation Star had only one thousand slots, with cultivation level irrelevant and aptitude the only standard. In the Star Domain, even the highest cultivation was merely the Void King Realm, which would not count for much in the Star Realm. What Yang Kai wanted was talent, elites who could one day become pillars of Lingxiao Palace. Lower cultivation did not matter. Once they reached Lingxiao Palace, as long as they had aptitude, how could they not be trained up?

The Star Domain was shaken. If the invasion of the Great Wilderness Star Domain had brought disaster to Hengluo Star Domain back then, what was happening now brought an enormous opportunity, a chance to ascend to the Heavens in a single step. Any cultivator with the slightest ambition was unwilling to miss it.

So, in a very short time, every Cultivation Star in the entire Star Domain became extraordinarily lively.

Taking advantage of this time, Yang Kai brought Su Yan to a world of ice and snow.

When he had first entered the Star Realm years ago, he had gained enormous benefits through his status as a Star Lord. Now that he was going to bring Su Yan and the others back to the Star Realm, he naturally wanted them to become Star Lords before they left.

Xia Ningshang was already the master of Tongxuan Continent, so there was no need to trouble over her, but Su Yan, Shan Qingluo, and Xue Yue had never refined a Stellar Origin.

Although Yang Kai still had some spare Stellar Origins, they had all come from the Shattered Star Sea, and their corresponding stars had long since been destroyed, leaving them no room for growth. Naturally, they could not compare to refining the Origin Power of a Cultivation Star.

As the Master of the Star Domain, Yang Kai only needed to consult the Star Chart to know the entire Star Domain like the back of his hand. Choosing suitable stars for the three women to refine was effortless.

Shan Qingluo had Lu Sanniang accompanying her, while Xue Yue had Liu Yan. Both had good targets, and with their cultivations and the Star Refining Art, there should not be much danger.

Su Yan, however, needed him to personally oversee matters. Although she was already in the Dao Origin Realm, the star Yang Kai had chosen for her was somewhat unusual.

The entire world was snow-white. Viewed from the void, it looked like a giant ball of ice, magnificent and spectacular.

Ice Soul Star was a Cultivation Star famous throughout the entire Star Domain. Yang Kai had heard of it in his early years, before he left the Star Domain, but he had never had the chance to see it in person. Its fame came in part from the star's extreme cold, which prevented both humans and yao from establishing themselves there. Although it was a Cultivation Star, it had never been touched by the Human Race or the Yao Race and remained an extremely pure land.

Its special World Laws had also given birth to a group of extremely unusual living beings: Ice Spirits!

The Ice Spirit Clan was small in number. They could not be seen in the outside world at all; only on Ice Soul Star could traces of their activity be found. They were born unafraid of bitter cold. Icy aura was the cradle in which they grew, and the colder the environment, the more they liked it.

It was said that in the very beginning, the Ice Spirit Clan was kind and hospitable. If wandering travelers accidentally came to Ice Soul Star, they would receive a warm welcome, but as the years passed, the Ice Spirits gradually became xenophobic.

The reason was simple: the guests they had once entertained turned around and aimed their spears at them.

The men of the Ice Spirit Clan were all extraordinarily handsome, and the women were all beautiful as flowers. Coupled with their special physiques and strange existence, this easily drew many covetous eyes.

They were born of Heaven and Earth, spirits of ice and snow. To those who cultivated Ice Attribute Secret Arts, their very existence was the best shortcut to gaining strength. Capture any one of them and Dual Cultivate with them, and one could swiftly accumulate power without the slightest hidden danger.

Many who cultivated Ice Attribute Secret Arts had set their sights on the Ice Spirits. Even those who did not cultivate Ice Attribute Secret Arts could sell an Ice Spirit for an astronomical price if they managed to capture one. Living Ice Spirits, however, were not easy to catch. Rumor had it that if they were made sad, they would die of a broken heart, so even though some Ice Spirits had been captured and sold in earlier years, even defiled, they rarely survived a single night before dying in a foreign land.

Only a small number of experts had used great Divine Abilities to preserve the lives of Ice Spirits, changing their temperaments and making them obey every command.

Ice Spirits themselves were of enormous value, to say nothing of the fact that Ice Soul Star as a whole was a treasure trove.

Ice Soul Star was incomparably frigid, and its special environment gave rise to special products. Many Ice Attribute spirit flowers and exotic grasses grew on this star, all exceptionally precious, with even some unusual varieties that had long since gone extinct elsewhere. Even setting aside the Ice Spirits, who died so easily of broken hearts, the treasures spread across this whole world were enough to drive many cultivators to desperate risks.
