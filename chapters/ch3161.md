## Chapter 3161 — Satisfy You

Abandoned Nest was one of the two most infamous danger zones in Hengluo Star Domain, a place of treacherous terrain and vicious people. The only place as notorious was the equally famous Spine of the Star River.

Between them, these two places had gathered more than ninety percent of all the Star Pirate gangs in the Star Domain. The exiled cultivators who survived here were all steeped in crime, savage and brutal to the bone.

For thousands of years, the three great forces of the Star Domain had clashed with them again and again, and had even joined hands in an attempt to wipe them out. But both Abandoned Nest and the Spine of the Star River were easy to defend and difficult to attack, and both were protected by natural Grand Arrays. They could not be taken lightly. Several assaults failed to achieve anything and instead caused heavy losses, so the matter was left unresolved.

Abandoned Nest was not truly a nest, but an extremely chaotic region. Distorted force fields, a violent Asteroid Sea, and Starry Sky storms formed its natural barriers. Without knowing the way in and out, anyone who lost their bearings inside would be in danger of dying, even a Third Stage of the Void King Realm cultivator.

At the center of this chaotic region lay many shattered, enormous asteroids. Each one was so vast its size was difficult to measure. The many Star Pirate gangs of Abandoned Nest had sunk their roots into these asteroids, multiplying there for countless years and growing stronger all the while.

Those countless Star Pirate gangs, large and small, were the foundation of Abandoned Nest.

Among these Star Pirate groups, the strongest and largest was known as the Blood Fiend Star Pirates, and its leader, Jiang Xuesong, astonishingly possessed an impressive cultivation at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm. Such strength placed him among the peak existences of the entire Star Domain, with few opponents in the world. Since he had chosen the path of evil, he was naturally a king among villains.

Jiang Xuesong had cultivated for fifteen hundred years. Rumor had it that his origins were connected to Sword Alliance, one of the three great forces of the Star Domain. In his youth, he too had been a young genius of Sword Alliance, highly valued and carefully nurtured by its upper echelon.

Yet no one knew what changed. In the end, he killed his Master, murdered his elder brother, betrayed Sword Alliance, and fled into Abandoned Nest. A few hundred years later, this place had one more bloodthirsty Star Pirate Gang.

Jiang Xuesong's hair was streaked with white, and he had already entered his twilight years. For a Third Stage of the Void King Realm expert, this was rare. Though old, he sat as straight as a sword, as if the training he had received in Sword Alliance during his youth still shaped him. Seated there, he was like a sharp sword that had yet to leave its sheath, all its edge hidden away.

Across the table, an extremely aged old man held a teacup and took a light sip, his entire body permeated with a blood Qi that made one feel deeply uncomfortable. Though he was similarly advanced in years, a careful look revealed blood light surging around him, his vitality even stronger than that of a man in his prime. Moreover, this blood Qi was no ordinary blood Qi, for it carried a sinister chill, making him seem truly strange and eerie.

"Leader, have you found anything?" the old man asked softly, setting down his teacup.

In Abandoned Nest, Jiang Xuesong was the ruler. No one had ever been able to speak to him as an equal, let alone question him like this. Though phrased as a question, the words carried the faint arrogance of a superior addressing an inferior.

The old man's cultivation was also at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm, every bit Jiang Xuesong's equal, and the markings on his robes made his origins clear.

Netherworld Sect of the Great Wilderness Star Domain!

The old man was, astonishingly, a Vice Sect Master of Netherworld Sect. To this day, Jiang Xuesong did not know his real name; he only knew that the man called himself Old Ying.

Swords were made for slaughter, and a Sword Heart was resolute, preferring to break rather than bend. This was even more true for a peerless great swordsman like Jiang Xuesong. If anyone else had dared show him even the slightest disrespect, he would have cut them down with one stroke and made them taste his strength. Against Old Ying, however, he had no confidence in certain victory. More importantly, while Old Ying's subtle condescension displeased him, he did not want to make an enemy of Netherworld Sect. Over the past dozen years or so, he had seen Netherworld Sect's strength for himself. If it truly came to a fight, Abandoned Nest could not withstand Netherworld Sect's assault.

Hearing this, he replied in a deep voice, "Nothing useful. The people from your honored Star Domain were destroyed too quickly. They never managed to send back any useful information."

Old Ying said, "Surely there must be some clue?"

Over the past few months, Great Wilderness Star Domain's side had suffered disastrous casualties. More than half the gains from a dozen-plus years of invasion had been destroyed. Star after star had gone silent: Di Chen, Dark Star, Shuiyue Star, Cuiwei Star...

He did not care whether cultivators from other forces lived or died. What mattered to him was the situation of Netherworld Sect's disciples.

Most horrifying of all, he had even lost contact with Tongxuan Continent.

One had to know that a Vice Sect Master was overseeing that place, a man whose cultivation was on par with his own, and four other Third Stage of the Void King Realm cultivators were assisting him. Against such a lineup, what could Hengluo Star Domain possibly use to resist, much less wipe them out?

Perhaps some minor accident had occurred over there and cut off communication.

But the moment this guess surfaced, even Old Ying could not convince himself of it. Instinct told him something was wrong, yet he could not say what. That was why he had Jiang Xuesong send people out to investigate.

In the end, this was the answer he received, and it left him rather displeased.

"There truly are no clues. If Old Ying does not believe me, you may send your own people out to investigate," Jiang Xuesong replied, neither servile nor overbearing.

Unlike the Spine of the Star River, which had submitted to Great Wilderness Star Domain, the path he had chosen was cooperation. He had cooperated with Great Wilderness Star Domain to destroy Sword Alliance and avenge the blood feud of that year. Judging by the results, the cooperation had gone fairly well.

Sword Alliance had indeed been destroyed, and the Cultivation Stars it controlled had all fallen into the hands of Great Wilderness Star Domain cultivators. Sword Alliance had fallen apart, and only its Young Alliance Master had escaped disaster, fleeing to Tongxuan Continent for refuge.

But Tongxuan Continent was also in grave danger. Once Netherworld Sect's plan there succeeded, Sword Alliance's Young Alliance Master would have nowhere to hide and would undoubtedly die.

His thousand-year hatred had been avenged, yet Jiang Xuesong felt little joy, all because he had badly underestimated Netherworld Sect's strength. Now, although he remained the greatest leader of Abandoned Nest, the entire place was basically already in Netherworld Sect's hands. His title had long since become hollow.

If Netherworld Sect truly decided to deal with him, he feared he would not be able to escape either.

This was what came of bargaining with a tiger.

It was just that there was no benefit in dealing with a great sword cultivator who would rather break than bend. It would only create internal strife, so Netherworld Sect had shown restraint. But this could no longer be called cooperation. Once the hare was dead, the hounds were cooked; once the birds were gone, the bow was put away. Jiang Xuesong had long since lost the leverage to cooperate. Though he had not yet been reduced to someone else's running dog, he imagined that once Hengluo Star Domain completely fell, he would be the first person Netherworld Sect dealt with.

Old Ying smiled faintly. Set against his sinister aura, the smile was enough to make one's hair stand on end. "This old man still trusts the Leader's words. It is only that the events of these past few months are strange at every turn. Leader is a local. Do you know of anyone in this world with such heaven-reaching methods?"

Jiang Xuesong raised his sword-like brow in surprise. "Old Ying believes those who lost contact are likely doomed?"

Old Ying sighed. "This old man is unwilling to think so, but I fear there is no other explanation."

If only one or two places had lost contact, that would have been one thing; accidents could explain it. But over the past few months, too many places had gone silent, so many that even Old Ying felt a chill.

Though unwilling to admit it, he knew that some hidden expert on Hengluo Star Domain's side must have emerged from seclusion and was hunting Great Wilderness Star Domain and Netherworld Sect.

Jiang Xuesong was slightly shocked. If even Old Ying said so, then it was probably true. After pondering for a moment, he said, "Back then, there was an old bastard in the Star Domain known as its number one person. His strength was unfathomable."

"Who?"

"Wu Dao!"

Who in the Star Domain did not know Wu Dao's great name? When Jiang Xuesong was young, Wu Dao had already been at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm. After so many years, his cultivation had likely grown even more refined. If he was the one taking action, that would make sense.

"But it should not be him." Jiang Xuesong slowly shook his head.

"Why do you say that?" Old Ying pressed.

"Because he left this Star Domain several dozen years ago and went to another, even more mysterious world."

"Star Realm!" A sharp light flashed through Old Ying's eyes, revealing a trace of yearning before it quickly faded.

"Star Realm?" Jiang Xuesong stared at Old Ying in astonishment. It was his first time hearing the term, and he found it deeply intriguing.

"Continue," Old Ying said, too lazy to explain. Hengluo Star Domain knew nothing of the Star Realm's existence, but Great Wilderness Star Domain did. That was because Netherworld Sect had an Ancestral Founder who had gone to the Star Realm and even established a Sect there. He had once used great Divine Abilities to send down an edict, along with several treasures.

As a Vice Sect Master of Netherworld Sect, he was naturally familiar with the Star Realm. The previous Sect Master, Yin Lesheng, had gone there.

The Star Realm was a place all cultivators yearned for. Its World Laws were more complete, and its Martial Dao path reached higher. If not for his advanced age, he too would have wanted to see it for himself.

Jiang Xuesong said, "If it cannot be Wu Dao, then I cannot think of anyone else." As he spoke, his brow furrowed, and he hesitated. "It cannot be him either."

"Who?"

"A junior named Yang Kai."

"If he is a junior, why mention him?" Old Ying asked, displeased.

Jiang Xuesong said, "Although I have never met Yang Kai, that boy is no ordinary junior. Wu Dao was able to go to the Star Realm entirely because of him. Back then, it was Yang Kai who took Wu Dao and several others away from the Star Domain. At the time, rumors spread throughout the Star Domain that in less than a hundred years, this boy would become the number one person in the Star Domain and take Wu Dao's place. Who would have thought he would leave so early?" He sighed deeply. "That I never fought him once is one of Jiang's regrets."

"If that is your wish, this Young Master does not mind satisfying it."

A voice drifted in from far away. When the first word reached them, the speaker seemed to be several dozen li away, but by the time the final word fell, a heroic figure had already appeared before the great hall.

The dim light of Abandoned Nest fell across his back, casting a long shadow that seemed ready to swallow the entire hall. Darkness hid his face, making his features impossible to see. Only his eyes shone with brilliant light.

Jiang Xuesong and Old Ying both stopped breathing for an instant. The newcomer released no aura at all, yet every hair on their bodies stood on end, a chill running through them.
