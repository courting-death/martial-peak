## Chapter 3261 — Self-Destruction

With a thunderous boom, the demonized man burst apart, leaving not even bones behind. Yet no blood splattered out. Instead, his entire body turned into a mass of pitch-black Demonic Qi that swept around Yang Kai with a whoosh.

Boom boom boom...

As blast after blast rang out, the demonized people self-destructed one after another, and Yang Kai's heart sank.

In the sky, wherever battles raged across Wuhua Hall, masses of Demonic Qi dense enough to seem solid swelled rapidly with the demonized people's self-destruction, like black mushrooms blooming one after another. In the blink of an eye, they swallowed a vast area and formed a black cloud that covered a radius of more than ten li.

The dozen or so Emperor Realm Masters following close behind Yang Kai were caught off guard and were all swallowed by it. Even Yang Kai was no exception.

The people who had been chatting and laughing a moment ago paled in an instant. The thin Demonic Qi they had never taken seriously was now hundreds, even a thousand times denser, and its power had risen sharply as well.

Sizzling sounds rang out as the Demonic Qi corroded everyone's protective Emperor Yuan. Even as Emperor Realm Masters, they felt the Emperor Yuan around them teeter on the verge of collapse, eaten through with hole after hole in an extremely short time. Quite a few cried out in panic.

Without anyone needing to say another word, the dozen or so of them moved almost without hesitation, each flashing away as they fled madly outward.

Only after everyone escaped the black cloud's range and looked back did lingering fear appear on their faces. No one had expected those demonized people to self-destruct, much less for the aftermath to be so terrifying.

Yang Kai's face turned frighteningly dark. Earlier, more than a thousand demonized people had charged toward them, but he and the others had only knocked out three or four hundred. Yet in that instant just now, at least five or six hundred had self-destructed. In other words, in his direction alone, the Southern Territory's elite disciples had lost as many as five or six hundred people.

The other directions were surely the same. The Southern Territory's elites had suffered disastrous losses!

"Brother Chen, you..." a terrified voice suddenly called out.

Yang Kai turned to look, and his eyes narrowed. A middle-aged man stood nearby in complete bewilderment, the Emperor Yuan protecting him already broken. Wisps of pitch-black Demonic Qi coiled around him like living things, boring into him through the pores and seven orifices all over his body. In an extremely short time, his aura changed drastically, and even his eyes showed signs of being covered by black Qi.

Yang Kai did not know who he was, but he should have been one of the helpers he had recruited from the Northern Territory, though Yang Kai did not know which Sect he led.

Seeing this, the people beside him were greatly alarmed. They hurriedly scattered to either side and watched him warily, afraid he might suddenly lash out and injure someone.

The man himself was clearly dazed as well. He spread his hands before his eyes and stared at them blankly, then raised his head to Yang Kai and pleaded, "Palace Master Yang, save me!"

If the Demonic Qi fully corroded him and stripped away his reason, he would be no different from the demonized people who had self-destructed earlier. Then his life and death would no longer be under his control, and he might not even know when he would self-destruct.

As soon as the words left his mouth, Yang Kai had already flashed in front of him. Yang Kai's aura suddenly changed, and as incantations sounded from his lips, he waved his hand and sent out a ray of light that enveloped the man.

Chi chi chi...

The wisps of black Qi seemed to have met their nemesis. The moment the light enveloped the man, they rushed out through his pores, then dissipated into nothing. His chaotic mind steadied, and the relief of escaping death surged through him. Excited, he said, "Many thanks, Palace Master Yang."

Although he did not know what miraculous method Yang Kai had used, he could clearly feel that the Demonic Qi that had invaded his body had been driven out.

"All of you, be careful." Yang Kai swept a cold glance over them. An Emperor Realm Master had almost been forced to Enter the Demonic State. It was stupidity in the extreme. He could also see that the Demonic Qi itself was not especially hard to guard against. These fellows had simply encountered demonized people for the first time, and cutting through them as easily as chopping melons and slicing vegetables had made them careless. Otherwise, as long as they had maintained the Emperor Yuan protecting their bodies, this never could have happened.

Perhaps the impact from those demonized people self-destructing at close range had played a part as well.

Otherwise, back outside Fenglin City, when he had only been at the First Stage of the Dao Origin Realm, how could he have endured so long beneath that overwhelming Demonic Qi? Even if the Demonic Qi was fiercely corrosive, as long as one protected oneself well and gave it no opening to exploit, there would be no serious problem.

"Shamanic Art!" In the crown of a large tree in the distance, a hidden black shadow watched this scene, his eyes narrowing. Others might not know what method Yang Kai had used to drive out the Demonic Qi, but he knew it clearly. That was unmistakably Shamanic Art. In ancient times, the Demon Race had suffered greatly at the hands of those Great Shamans. Precisely because he knew this, he was deeply shaken.

There was still someone in this era who understood Shamanic Arts? Moreover, judging by the way he used it, he seemed extremely proficient in the Dao of Shamanic Arts. Otherwise, he could not possibly have performed even a mere Exorcism so effortlessly.

But how was that possible? Every era gave rise to its own heroes, and every era had its own laws. Under the current laws of this world, how could anyone still cultivate Shamanic Art?

Before the thought had fully formed, his vision suddenly blurred, and a figure appeared before him like a ghost.

The man was horrified. Without even thinking, he shot backward.

"Congeal!" An icy voice reached his ears, making him feel as if he had fallen into an ice cellar. He looked up and saw Yang Kai, who had clearly been several li away, standing right in front of him. After that single word was spoken, the surrounding space suddenly solidified, turning into an invisible cage that trapped him inside.

He struggled desperately, urging his own strength in an attempt to break free of that imprisoning power, but it felt as if he had sunk into a swamp. The more he struggled, the deeper he sank.

"Demonic Intent!" Yang Kai stared into his pitch-black eyes and snorted coldly.

This person was clearly different from the demonized cultivators who had charged over earlier without fear of death. Although those who had been eroded by Demonic Qi had likewise become demonized, they had lost their own ability to think. The one before him, however, could act of his own accord. Without a doubt, this man had been possessed by a Demonic Intent, just like Lei Gu. Judging from his attire, he should have been an Emperor Realm Elder of Wuhua Hall.

"You deserve death!" the man growled through gritted teeth. His body suddenly swelled, and at the first sign of conflict, he actually tried to self-destruct. Clearly, he knew he was no match for Yang Kai and was even less willing to be captured.

"You want to self-destruct in front of me? Look at me!" Yang Kai roared. His right eye suddenly transformed into a golden vertical pupil, and Divine Sense power as tangible as substance poured out.

The Demonic Intent preparing to self-destruct went blank, and his swollen body shriveled back down against his will. Before he could react, Yang Kai grabbed him by the neck and directly stuffed him into a certain place in the Small Xuan Realm to suppress him. Yang Kai did not kill him, because there were still many things he had yet to figure out. Keeping him alive might let Yang Kai pry some useful information out of him.

In addition, Yang Kai wanted to see whether he could rescue this man from possession by Demonic Intent, just as he had rescued those people corroded by Demonic Qi.

Having succeeded in one move, Yang Kai turned back and frowned.

The enormous black cloud in the sky churned without cease, then manifested over a hundred vortices. Dense black Qi poured into those vortices and soon disappeared.

Moments later, the black cloud vanished, and over a hundred figures surging with Demonic Qi were revealed once more. They were, astonishingly, part of the thousand-plus demonized people from just now.

Of those more than one thousand demonized people, as many as five or six hundred had self-destructed, while three or four hundred had been knocked unconscious by Yang Kai and the others. The remaining hundred-plus had been hiding inside the black cloud all along, only now revealing themselves.

After devouring the Demonic Qi formed by their companions' self-destruction, every one of them had advanced greatly in strength. The aura each emitted was now actually not much weaker than that of an Emperor Realm Master.

"What the hell are these things!" The Mitian Sect Elder stared, horrified. Only now did he realize how laughable his earlier underestimation had been. At first, they had not taken those thousand-plus demonized people seriously at all, but who would have thought they could ultimately gather into a force capable of fighting them?

However, everyone soon noticed something strange. Although those hundred-plus demonized people had advanced greatly in strength, they stood stupidly in place without moving, like wooden stakes.

"Hurry and knock them unconscious," Yang Kai shouted as understanding dawned. It seemed these demonized people corroded by Demonic Qi temporarily had no ability to act on their own. The reason they had charged over earlier was that someone had been controlling them.

And the controller should have been that Wuhua Hall Elder possessed by Demonic Intent.

Once Yang Kai captured the controller, these hundred-plus demonized people immediately became like puppets with their strings cut.

Hearing him, the dozen or so people cautiously rushed toward those hundred. Only after exchanging blows did they discover that these demonized people showed no sign of resisting at all. One after another, they were knocked unconscious and fell downward.

Seeing this, Yang Kai grew even more certain of his conjecture. These demonized people corroded by Demonic Qi truly had no ability to act on their own. He simply did not know whether that was always the case, or whether it was because they had been demonized only a short time ago.

At this moment, there was no time to think it over. Yang Kai shot straight up into the sky and looked out, only to see that Wuhua Hall had already split into more than twenty battlefields, each one locked in a fierce struggle.

The situation on his side could be considered good. That Northern Territory Emperor Realm expert had been corroded by Demonic Qi but rescued by him, so he had not entered the Demonic State. The Wuhua Hall Elder who had controlled the demonized people had been captured by him, and the demonized people had also lost the ability to move.

Other places, however, did not have such a good start. Yang Kai clearly saw that some Emperor Realm cultivators from the Northern Territory had already become demonized and were now turning their blades against their former friends and companions, fighting them in a chaotic melee. This left those people both shocked and furious, forcing them to struggle bitterly in resistance while also unable to bring themselves to deal killing blows, tying down a great deal of their energy and manpower.

Meanwhile, the demonized people who had devoured large amounts of Demonic Qi and advanced greatly in strength were now charging in without fear of death, locked in a bitter fight with the allied forces of the Eastern and Northern Territories.

Yang Kai's eyes flashed. He looked toward a certain place below, his Divine Sense surging as he transmitted a few words to the Elder of the Stone Puppet Clan.

The Elder had originally been advancing steadily with the eight Stone Puppets, beating a group of demonized people into a sorry state. Their bodies were special, and they had no fear of Demonic Qi, so they were not at all worried about the danger of Entering the Demonic State. They also possessed boundless strength and were impervious to blades and spears. Ordinary Emperor Realm Masters were no match for them, let alone those demonized people.

If not for his fear that his own strength was too great, making him wary of attacking casually, he would likely have slaughtered the demonized people until they were in complete disarray long ago.
