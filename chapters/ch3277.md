## Chapter 3277 — A Warm Reception

"I imagine you already know why I have come." Yang Kai went straight to the point.

Tao Yingruo nodded. "Our Sect Master has unfortunately fallen, and Luocha Sect is now without a leader. For Sir to become our Guest Elder at a time like this and protect the foundation of our Sect is truly our good fortune. I and all the disciples of Luocha Sect are endlessly grateful. From now on, we will follow Sir's lead."

With that, she bowed again.

Yang Kai waved a hand. "There is no need for that. From now on, carry on as you always have. I am only lending my name."

Tao Yingruo looked up at him and was about to speak again when Yang Kai cut her off. "I know Luocha Sect's current situation, but I do not know Luocha Sect well, and Sect affairs are not mine to meddle in. That is all there is to it. Great Elder need not say any more."

Only after hearing this did Tao Yingruo understand that Yang Kai truly had no designs on Luocha Sect. He had come all this way simply because he felt guilty over Yu Zhuo's matter and wanted to give Luocha Sect his protection.

Gratitude filled her heart, and all her earlier worries vanished. She did not suspect Yang Kai of playing hard to get. His cultivation was plain to see; if he really wanted to use force, no one in Luocha Sect could resist him now. With his current reputation on top of that, she feared that even if Yang Kai truly did something to Luocha Sect, no one in the Southern Territory would stand up for them. To think such a person really existed in the world. For a time, the way she looked at Yang Kai carried a strange new light. And had her earlier words not also been a veiled test?

Qin Pei reminded her from the side, "Great Elder, we should invite Sir inside to rest. Sir must be tired after such a long journey."

A trace of embarrassment appeared on Tao Yingruo's face, and she said ruefully, "That was my oversight. Sir, please." As she spoke, she stepped aside to open the way.

Yang Kai nodded and walked straight ahead without glancing to either side. Several hundred female disciples lined the road, all watching him with beautiful eyes full of curiosity and appraisal. Tao Yingruo quietly observed him and saw that his steps were steady and imposing, his bearing extraordinary. Rarest of all, he did not spare even a single glance for any of the female disciples.

This was not arrogance. It was the composure of someone unmoved by so many beauties within easy reach. At that, Tao Yingruo was truly able to set her mind at ease.

After inviting Yang Kai into the main hall to talk, they served fragrant tea, and the few remaining Dao Origin Realm cultivators of Luocha Sect remained at the side. Naturally, introductions were unavoidable.

Luocha Sect had originally had one Emperor Realm cultivator and thirteen Dao Origin Realm cultivators, but after the incident at Wuhua Hall, its Emperor Realm Sect Master had fallen, and more than half of its Dao Origin Realm cultivators had died as well. Only five remained.

Of these five, Tao Yingruo had the highest cultivation, at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm. Next came Qin Pei and another Second Stage Dao Origin Realm cultivator, while the remaining two were only at the First Stage of the Dao Origin Realm.

With such strength, they could not even compare to the Biyu Sect of the past, so it was only natural that nearby forces would covet them.

Yang Kai did not know much about Luocha Sect, and what little he knew had all come from Qin Pei on the way here. Luocha Sect did not have a long history. Its founder was the current Sect Master, Yu Luocha, and even now only a few hundred years had passed. Less than a thousand years was indeed rather young for a Sect.

Most of the disciples in the Sect had been born orphans and were brought back by disciples who went out to travel. From top to bottom, Luocha Sect had a fairly good atmosphere. The Senior Sisters and Junior Sisters were quite close, and even if there were small conflicts, they did no real harm. Luocha Sect also governed a nearby city, from which more than half of the Sect's expenses were paid, and it also possessed a Yu Copper mine vein.

Since they wanted Yang Kai to serve as Luocha Sect's Guest Elder, Tao Yingruo naturally wanted him to understand the Sect better, so she reported many matters to him one by one. Normally, a Guest Elder's status could not compare to that of a formal Elder, but Yang Kai's cultivation was far beyond theirs, and Luocha Sect would have to shelter under his wings in the future. Without realizing it, Tao Yingruo and the others lowered their own posture to the extreme.

If Yang Kai had not been a man, they might even have asked him to become Sect Master.

Yang Kai grew drowsy as he listened to all these reports. Tao Yingruo clearly noticed this as well and sighed inwardly, feeling that she truly had been measuring a gentleman's heart by her own petty standards. Judging from Yang Kai's reaction, even if Luocha Sect were handed to him outright, he might still find it troublesome. It was laughable that she had actually worried he might have ulterior motives.

Yet this only made Tao Yingruo feel even more at ease with Yang Kai. With such a desireless, undemanding person protecting Luocha Sect, as long as they were given enough time, why should they fear that Luocha Sect would fail to rise?

She tentatively asked whether Yang Kai would be willing to serve as a formal Elder of Luocha Sect, but Yang Kai politely refused, so she immediately let the matter drop.

"Preparations for the conferment ceremony had already begun. I plan to hold it half a month from now. I wonder whether Elder Yang can wait that long?" Tao Yingruo asked.

Yang Kai thought for a moment, then nodded. "That is fine."

He could roughly guess Tao Yingruo's intention. She wanted to announce it to the world at the conferment ceremony. After all, although she had already sent people to spread the news that Yang Kai would come to Luocha Sect to serve as Guest Elder, words alone were no proof. Why should anyone believe it? A conferment ceremony was different. It was an extremely formal rite, and once it was complete, it would mean Yang Kai had truly taken on Luocha Sect's mark. No one would be able to deny it.

Yang Kai guessed that Tao Yingruo would also send out invitations far and wide, inviting many people to witness it.

It was only a small matter, so he let her do as she wished. After all, she had no malice or selfish motive; she was simply thinking of Luocha Sect.

Seeing that Yang Kai had no objection, Tao Yingruo smiled, rose, and said, "In that case, Elder Yang, please rest for the next few days." As she spoke, she gave Qin Pei a look.

Qin Pei immediately rose. "Elder Yang, please follow me."

Yang Kai nodded, stood, took his leave of the others, and followed Qin Pei out.

Luocha Sect's territory was not vast, but it was not small either. Although it did not have the magnificent sight of Lingxiao Palace's hundred peaks gathered together, it still had twenty or thirty peaks. Yang Kai followed Qin Pei all the way to the highest and steepest peak, the one richest in Spiritual Qi, and drifted down.

Jade pavilions stood on the peak, and more than a dozen female disciples immediately came forward to pay their respects.

Qin Pei waved for them to rise, then pointed at Yang Kai and said, "This is Elder Yang, the Guest Elder of our Luocha Sect. From now on, you must all serve him with care."

"Yes!" the dozen or so female disciples answered respectfully. A few bolder ones secretly lifted their eyes to look at Yang Kai, then hurriedly lowered their gazes again.

Only then did Qin Pei turn to Yang Kai. "This was originally the Sect Master's residence. After Senior Sister Tao heard that Elder Yang was coming, she ordered it cleaned up. Elder Yang, please see if anything is not to your liking. If there is, just tell me, and I will do my best to adjust it."

Yang Kai was slightly startled. "Sect Master Yu's residence? Isn't that inappropriate?"

Since this was the Sect Master's residence, what was a Guest Elder like him doing moving in? Although Yu Luocha was dead, Luocha Sect would certainly establish another Sect Master. Was this not like a dove taking over a magpie's nest?

Qin Pei pursed her lips in a smile. "This is Senior Sister Tao's intention, so Elder Yang need not decline."

Yang Kai pondered for a moment, then nodded. "All right, then. I will trouble Junior Sister Qin."

Qin Pei froze for a moment when she heard him address her that way, but she soon beamed. "Then Senior Brother Yang, please rest for now. If you need anything, just instruct them. They will satisfy you."

Qin Pei saluted and took her leave. Yang Kai, however, could not shake the feeling that the look she gave him before leaving was rather meaningful, and that her last sentence was also quite enough to set one's imagination racing.

A group of female disciples serving him and satisfying any request he made; it was hard not to think too much.

Yang Kai stroked his chin and turned to glance at the dozen or so female disciples, making them immediately lower their heads again.

Yang Kai's eyes brightened at the sight.

Leaving cultivation aside, these dozen or so female disciples were all striking beauties with graceful figures. That was no surprise. Luocha Sect was entirely composed of women, so naturally it was not difficult to find a few with pretty faces and lovely figures.

He had not looked carefully earlier, but now, with a brief appraisal, Yang Kai found that these dozen or so women each had their own charm. Some were dignified, some alluring, some lively, and some innocent. The two at the front stood out in particular. One had limpid eyes that seemed naturally veiled in mist. When Yang Kai looked at her, a blush immediately rose across both cheeks, and she lightly pursed her red lips and lowered her head. Every small movement of hers unconsciously exuded a powerful allure, making one want to draw her into their arms and cherish her properly.

Yang Kai had seen no shortage of women with this kind of innate allure, but among all those he had met, only Shan Qingluo, the Enchantress Queen, surpassed her by a level. Shan Qingluo's allure was not deliberate; it flowed out of her naturally without her noticing, and that was what could most thoroughly undo a man.

Yang Kai clicked his tongue in wonder. He did not know whether Luocha Sect had anything like Charm Techniques, but if it did, having this woman cultivate them would achieve twice the result with half the effort.

As for the other woman, the feeling she gave Yang Kai was one of innocence. Her entire being seemed utterly untainted, gentle and delicate enough to make one pity her and want to protect her, as though even a single glance at her would be a kind of blasphemy. Yet it was precisely this extreme innocence that gave rise to a desire to destroy it.

Needless to say, Luocha Sect had chosen these women as the best among a thousand, and the two at the head were probably the most outstanding pair among the Sect's thousand-plus female disciples.

Yang Kai laughed inwardly. It seemed Tao Yingruo had truly gone to great lengths for his sake, sending these two little enchantresses over. Paired with Qin Pei's words before she left, if Yang Kai had even the slightest inclination, something might really happen between him and these two female disciples.

And once something truly happened, would they still need to fear that Yang Kai would not give Luocha Sect more consideration in the future?

Women really did think too much. But if he put himself in their place, Yang Kai could understand what Tao Yingruo and the other Luocha Sect higher-ups were thinking. In all of Luocha Sect, there was nothing that could make Yang Kai linger or care, except beauty.

This was not a honey trap, only a few small methods, so Yang Kai would not blame them. Besides, having so many beauties around him was better than being alone or facing a group of men. At the very least, they were pleasing to the eye.

"Disciple Ran Yirou greets Elder Yang." The pure and flawless female disciple gathered her robes and bowed, poised and natural.

Immediately afterward, the other woman, so alluring she seemed almost to drip with charm, also hurriedly saluted. "Disciple Yu Keren greets Elder."
