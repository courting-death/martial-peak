## Chapter 3292 — Deterrence

Chi Lian licked her crimson lips, a strange light blooming in her vertical pupils. "Sir truly has an excellent memory. To think you even recognize me."

"Heh heh..." Yang Kai laughed dryly and looked over the three great female Yao Kings one by one.

The three before him were each more charming and alluring than the last, each dressed more revealingly than the last. Large swathes of snow-white skin, proud curves, and long, shapely legs were exposed to the air, yet the way they looked at Yang Kai, without exception, seemed as though they wished they could swallow him whole in one bite.

A sudden chill ran through Yang Kai. He turned to Luan Feng, who was standing off to the side with a smile, plainly enjoying the show. "Madam, a word in private."

Luan Feng rolled her eyes, then stepped aside with him and snapped, "What?"

Yang Kai said bitterly, "Madam, are you all playing me?"

Luan Feng smiled without any warmth. "Whatever do you mean?"

Yang Kai flicked his gaze to the side. "I did ask you for three Yao Kings, yes, but what exactly is the meaning of sending me these three?"

Luan Feng feigned ignorance. "Are you dissatisfied with them? Their strength is hardly weak among the Thirty-Two Yao Kings of my Ancient Land. In a head-on fight, they may not be as powerful as Xi Lei, but believe me, if Xi Lei were really made to face all three of them, the one who suffered would definitely be Xi Lei."

"I believe you, but this is far too strange."

Luan Feng's face cooled. "What is that supposed to mean? I have already called them here for you. Are you saying you do not want them now? If you do not, go tell them yourself and see whether they let you off." Secretly, she was delighted. Serves you right, you bastard, throwing your weight around. Did you really think I had no way to deal with you?

“Please don’t.” Yang Kai cupped his fists and bowed. "Madam, since you have already helped me this far, could you help me to the end and exchange them for three other Yao Kings? Three more ordinary ones. These three are really too... dazzling." Forget anyone else; even he could hardly bear facing these three female Yao Kings and had to keep his mind focused at every moment to resist the temptation. If he really brought them back, who knew what kind of chaos would break out in Lingxiao Palace? How would those male disciples have any heart left for cultivation?

Luan Feng slanted a look at him. "You wanted three Yao Kings, and I found them for you. Now you actually want to trade them for three others. Don't you think you are being a little excessive?"

Yang Kai considered this seriously, then nodded. "A little."

"Take them or leave them. If you don't want them, get lost!" Luan Feng snorted.

Yang Kai opened his mouth, but in the end said nothing more. He glanced back at the three female Yao Kings watching him eagerly from nearby and heaved a heavy sigh. "Fine. Them, then."

Luan Feng snorted. "Listen to you, as if you were taking a great loss." After a pause, she said, "I have met your demand. Hurry up and take them away. Do not come over again unless you have real business. If the descendant of Heaven's Punisher comes out of retreat, I will send someone to notify you."

She looked desperate to chase him off. She truly had grown afraid of Yang Kai; the sooner she sent him away, the sooner she could rest easy.

Yang Kai did not linger. Cupping his fists, he said, "My wife is in the Ancient Land, so I must trouble the several Saint Venerables to look after her." He had not gone to see Shan Qingluo on this trip, because he knew she and the others were in the midst of intensive cultivation, and disturbing her would do her no good.

"I know, I know. I guarantee she will not lose a single hair." Luan Feng waved him off impatiently.

After bidding Luan Feng farewell, Yang Kai led the three female Yao Kings toward the Stone Puppet residence. Along the way, he finally learned just how hard it was to endure a beauty's favor. The three great female Yao Kings burned hotter than the last, their words and movements all carrying a teasing undercurrent. This was especially true of Hu Fei, who possessed the bloodline of the Nine-Tailed Heavenly Fox. Her every gesture, every frown, and every smile held endless allure, enough to make a man nearly lose control.

Du Mi’er and Chi Lian were no easy women to deal with either. One was tender and affectionate, the other sinister, venomous, and cold. The three female Yao Kings’ three different auras twined around him, making Yang Kai feel as though he had fallen into a nest of temptresses, his heart and mind both stirred. If he had not kept a firm hold of his true heart all along, he might have found a place on the spot to enjoy himself first.

When they returned to the Stone Puppet residence, all the Stone Puppets stared wide-eyed at the three enchantresses, then at Yang Kai, and their faces took on a look that every man understood.

Yang Kai felt deeply embarrassed under their stares. This was truly not the situation he had wanted. He had come to the Ancient Land this time only to ask for three Yao Kings to guard Wuhua Hall's foundation. He had never expected to bring these three demonesses back with him.

Thinking about it now, that wretched maid Luan Feng clearly had bad intentions. Maybe she wanted these three demonesses to bring him down, then use that opening to do whatever she pleased.

He returned to Wuhua Hall in a sorry state and, under Hua Qingsi's dumbfounded stare, introduced the three female Yao Kings to her, telling her to arrange things as she saw fit.

Before leaving, Yang Kai's expression turned solemn. He looked across the three demonesses' faces and said in a deep voice, "After I leave, you will obey Chief Steward Hua in everything. The human world is not like the Ancient Land, nor will you be as free here as you were there. Since you have come to my territory, you must follow my rules. If you make any mistake, do not blame this Lord for being ruthless."

Hu Fei smiled sweetly. “My Lord, what are you saying? The three of us are very obedient.” As she spoke, a faintly discernible seductive charm bloomed from her. Had it been any other man, his soul would likely have been stolen at once, and he would not have been able to say a single harsh word.

Yang Kai, however, had already been on guard, and he also meant to establish his authority. A golden vertical pupil appeared in his left eye, and his tide-like Divine Sense surged out as a strangling force, crushing Hu Fei's formless charm to nothing.

Hu Fei's flawless, snow-white face shifted slightly. She gave a muffled groan and grew even paler. Du Mi'er and Chi Lian's eyes flickered rapidly as well, and at last a trace of surprise appeared in their watery eyes.

At the same time, Yang Kai quietly stirred his Golden Sacred Dragon Origin Power. A faint dragon pressure spread out and enveloped the three demonesses as he reached toward Hu Fei's tender cheek, so delicate it seemed a breath could break it.

Hu Fei's eyes flew wide. An utterly unbelievable scene appeared before her. Yang Kai was clearly standing in front of her, yet what she saw was a giant dragon reaching toward her with a claw that could tear Heaven and Earth apart. Before that dragon claw, all her defenses seemed unbearably fragile, as if she might be ripped to pieces at any moment.

Her delicate body trembled uncontrollably, and the last trace of color drained from her face in an instant.

Sensing the change in Yang Kai's aura, Du Mi'er and Chi Lian also stiffened as if facing a great enemy, their expressions filled with fear.

It seemed to last only an instant, and yet it seemed to last a very long time. Then the oppressive aura weighing on the three demonesses faded away. Yang Kai smiled and pinched Hu Fei's cheek, soft and tender beneath his fingers. "All of you behave yourselves. There will be no shortage of benefits for you later."

The three demonesses stood dumb as wooden chickens and nodded obediently.

Yang Kai brought his fingertips beneath his nose and sniffed, then winked at Hu Fei. With a loud laugh, he turned and left.

Only after his figure vanished did Hu Fei and the others let out a long breath. They looked at one another and saw the lingering fear in each other's eyes. On the way here with Yang Kai, they had found him rather ordinary, so they had inevitably been somewhat unrestrained. Only now did they understand that this man was extremely terrifying. If they truly provoked his anger, he probably would not show the slightest pity for the fairer sex. That suffocating pressure just now was something they had never felt even from the Saint Venerables.

Hua Qingsi had watched all of this coldly from the side, and only now did she speak. "I am Hua Qingsi, Chief Steward of Lingxiao Palace. How should I address you ladies?"

Her voice pulled Hu Fei and the others back from their thoughts. Du Mi'er was the first to speak, giving her name. She showed no contempt just because she was stronger than Hua Qingsi; instead, she behaved quite meekly.

Hua Qingsi noted down each of their names, knowing that Yang Kai's earlier show of authority had worked. Otherwise, these three female Yao Kings would have given her a headache. The only ones in Lingxiao Palace now who could stand on equal footing with them were Yingfei and the others, but Yingfei and his group were also Yao Kings from the Ancient Land and were sure to be old acquaintances of these three. Hua Qingsi could not use them to intimidate Hu Fei and her companions.

Now, things were much better. With Yang Kai's lingering might holding them in check, she would have an easy time giving them orders. Secretly, she made up her mind that if they refused to behave, she would have Liu Yan deal with them.

Liu Yan could now be considered the strongest expert in Lingxiao Palace apart from Yang Kai. Even a Yao King could not possibly be her opponent.

With three more Yao Kings joining them, Wuhua Hall finally had enough people to hold it down. Once Nanmen Dajun finished arranging the Grand Guardian Array, they could begin preparing for the disciples' migration.

After leaving Wuhua Hall, Yang Kai returned once more to the Ancient Land.

He passed through the Stone Puppet Clan's residence and first went to the place where the Blood Gate had appeared back then.

He was no stranger to this place. Back then, he had fought Shi Huo here in a desperate battle and had nearly been crippled. In the end, Zhang Ruoxi's bloodline had awakened, and she had taken away Shi Huo's Divine Spirit Origin, resolving the crisis.

Afterward, Zhang Ruoxi had brought Xiaoxiao into the Blood Gate, and the Blood Gate had vanished with them. To this day, there had not been the slightest news.

Yao Commanders were stationed near the Blood Gate year-round to keep watch and investigate. Yang Kai was questioned several times when he arrived, but once they heard his name, they all became obsequious, bowing and scraping as they flattered him.

He searched that void for a long time, but after sensing no trace of the Blood Gate at all, Yang Kai could only give up.

The Blood Gate connected to another world, one Heaven's Punisher had opened back then. Within it stood Heaven's Punisher Palace, where many Divine Spirit Origins from ancient times were stored. Divine Spirits were scarce now, but in ancient times they had been countless. These powerful Divine Spirits were born with tremendous strength, and they acted without restraint, bringing calamity to all living beings.

According to the ancient records, Heaven's Punisher appeared out of nowhere and waged war in every direction. With the Heaven's Punisher Sword in hand, she slaughtered all Divine Spirits beneath the Heavens; not even dragons and phoenixes were spared. This person born in accordance with the fortune of Heaven and Earth seemed to be the nemesis of every Divine Spirit.

In the era when Heaven's Punisher lived, all Divine Spirits beneath the Heavens hid away and dared not emerge, and only then did the Human Race gain the space to develop in peace.

The Divine Spirits slain by Heaven's Punisher all had their Origin Power taken and sealed inside Heaven's Punisher Palace. As a result, the number of Divine Spirits dwindled until, by the present day, they could practically be counted on one's fingers. Although many yao had inherited ancient bloodlines, without an Origin, they simply could not awaken the glory of their distant ancestors.
