## Chapter 3176 — Start Taking Revenge

Back when Hengluo Star Domain had no master, the robber Wu Heng had charged in and plundered it at will, with no one able to stop him. Now that Yang Kai had become the Star Domain's master, how could he allow Wu Heng to run wild on his territory?

Just as Yang Kai had been no match for Wu Heng in Great Wilderness Star Domain, Wu Heng was no match for him here.

Yang Kai linked his mind to the Star Chart in his Sea of Consciousness, faced the vast darkness, took a deep breath, spread his arms, and thrust both hands forward, striking a posture as if he were commanding a boundless sea.

The rolling darkness swept back like an ebbing tide. In an instant, it retreated a thousand li and continued shrinking away. Wherever it passed, the original Starry Sky appeared once more.

As the saying went, “If you ate what was mine, spit it back out.” Yang Kai was not merely saying this for show, but truly intended to put it into action.

The retreating darkness suddenly slowed, as though some force at the far end was holding it back.

"Still trying to resist me!" Yang Kai raised his brow and grinned savagely. Naturally, he knew Wu Heng could not swallow this insult and was secretly contending with him.

He was trying to force back the darkness that had devoured Hengluo Star Domain, while Wu Heng exerted his strength from the other side to obstruct him.

But it was pointless. Though the darkness slowed considerably, under Yang Kai's sustained effort it still kept retreating.

Three years ago, darkness had erupted here and the devouring had begun. Wu Heng had spent three full years devouring a stretch of Starry Sky far greater than hundreds of millions of li. Still, compared to that vast span of time, the pace of devouring could not be called fast.

The current speed of restoration, however, was much faster.

The darkness ebbed, compressing little by little toward the center. The black hole that had covered such an enormous region of Star Domain space gradually vanished, and the Void was reconstructed and restored.

In barely a month, all the darkness had been driven away, leaving only the original Void Passage behind.

Back then, Li Jiao's family of three had been tasked with guarding the front of this Void Passage to stop reinforcements and fugitives from Great Wilderness Star Domain.

Yang Kai and Wu Heng had also been clashing in secret for that month, but the result had never been in doubt. It ended in Yang Kai's overwhelming victory.

Gazing at the entrance of that pitch-black passageway, Yang Kai’s eyes began to dart about.

The passage had appeared mainly because, when he left for the Star Realm, he had taken the Star Domain Origin of Hengluo Star Domain with him. With the Origin no longer in the Star Domain, its boundary wall had weakened. Perhaps by coincidence, or perhaps by Wu Heng's design, that weak point had become a passage connecting the two Star Domains.

Now that Yang Kai had brought the Origin back, successfully refined it, and become the Star Domain's master, he naturally had the ability to repair the passage.

With his current methods, doing so would not cost him much strength, only some time and effort.

But once the passage was fully repaired, the connection between the two Star Domains would be completely severed.

With his great hatred still unavenged, how could Yang Kai choose that? So he let the passage remain.

Not only did he leave it unrepaired, he even began hatching crooked ideas.

Since he wanted revenge, the best method was naturally to repay Wu Heng in kind. Wu Heng wanted to swallow his Hengluo Star Domain, so Yang Kai would gnaw away Wu Heng's Great Wilderness Star Domain and let him taste what it felt like to be devoured.

Before that, however, he needed a good method. With Wu Heng personally holding the fort, this plan of his could not succeed as it was.

The area around the passage was silent. Yang Kai could not see anything, but he knew Wu Heng was surely watching him from the other side.

Yang Kai grinned and raised a hand, tracing lines through the Void.

Light flowed. In the space of a breath, an intricate, profound pattern appeared in the Void before him. It was none other than the pattern of a cross-domain Space Array.

Using the Void as the foundation and Star Strength as the material, he arranged a Space Array with a casual gesture, displaying the immense might of a Star Domain Master.

Far away on Dark Star, inside Lingxiao Sect, the Incarnation sat cross-legged, breathing in the world's Spiritual Qi.

Light suddenly flared behind him, and an identical array appeared.

Sensing it, the Incarnation turned his head to look, rose, and stepped into the array.

In the next moment, he crossed a distance of billions upon billions of li and appeared before Yang Kai.

Their eyes met, and everything passed between them without words. The Incarnation walked silently up to Yang Kai, raised one hand, and pressed it to his head. A sea of information poured madly into Yang Kai's Sea of Consciousness.

In a sense, the Incarnation was an extension of Yang Kai's own body. However, because it had been transformed from a Stone Puppet and formed by housing Yang Kai's Soul Incarnation, it had developed some thoughts of its own over the years.

It could converse with Yang Kai and express different opinions, but when necessary, no words were needed at all. Yang Kai could perceive every thought it had, and he could also impose his own will upon it.

The information the Incarnation was now transmitting to Yang Kai was nothing less than the most powerful and most evil Secret Art in all the world, the Heaven Devouring Battle Art!

The Heaven Devouring Battle Art had been created by Wu Kuang. It was, by nature, a Secret Art that swallowed all things under Heaven. Back then, Wu Kuang had used it to devour countless Star Domains and become the greatest man of all time. Several Great Emperors had fallen to kill him, yet tens of thousands of years later, he still managed to seize another body and be reborn.

If those fallen Great Emperors knew this in the underworld, who knew whether they would be furious enough to come back to life?

Yet powerful as the Heaven Devouring Battle Art was, Yang Kai had not cultivated it after obtaining it, because he had always felt the Secret Art had hidden drawbacks.

The Incarnation's cultivation of it only proved his suspicion further. The Secret Art did have drawbacks. Devouring and refining everything in the world sounded unimaginably powerful, but without a corresponding method to resolve the consequences, it could only end in tragedy.

When one devoured and refined everything in the world, one would inevitably absorb all kinds of energies with different attributes, along with numerous impurities. Because those energies differed in attribute, they would inevitably conflict and restrain one another. Once they accumulated to a certain point, they were bound to cause tremendous harm to the cultivator, and might even make their cultivation regress or drive them into Qi Deviation.

Wu Kuang had his own method of resolving this, which was why he could use the Heaven Devouring Battle Art to reign over the world.

Yang Kai knew he did not have that ability. The Incarnation, however, did.

The Stone Puppet clan had unique physiques. They swallowed metal and ate stone, so differences in attributes meant nothing to them. They could simply refine everything directly inside their bodies and return the myriad elements to one.

Strictly speaking, there was no race in the world better suited to cultivating the Heaven Devouring Battle Art than the Stone Puppets. Even Wu Kuang probably had not expected this.

The Incarnation had obtained the Heaven Devouring Battle Art many years ago and had been cultivating it ever since, yet it had never encountered any problems. It had even devoured Shi Huo's Source.

In terms of understanding and comprehension of the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, apart from Wu Kuang, only the Incarnation stood at such a level.

The enormous flood of information surged into Yang Kai's mind, and he slowly digested it. Only then did he realize the Secret Art was far deeper and more profound than he had imagined. If Wu Kuang had not turned to evil, he would have been a peerless genius through the ages.

Half a day later, the Incarnation withdrew his hand, glanced at the black hole not far away, then stood to the side to serve as Yang Kai's Protector in case anything unexpected happened.

Time flowed by, little by little.

Yang Kai lost himself completely in comprehending the Heaven Devouring Battle Art. The more he understood, the stronger the urge became to cultivate this Secret Art at any cost. It was simply powerful beyond imagination. Wu Mengchuan had cultivated it for so many years and still had not reached the Emperor Realm; the man was unbelievably stupid.

But Yang Kai had wandered the world for many years, and his temperament was no ordinary thing. He was still able to restrain that greed.

Only after a full month did Yang Kai slowly open his eyes, his gaze clear.

He stood, stretched, and loosened his muscles and bones. Cracks and pops rang from all over his body. He shook out both hands and found that they had fully recovered. A month had been enough for his injuries to heal completely.

Staring at the Void Passage, Yang Kai bared his teeth in a savage grin. "Let's start taking revenge!"

He linked his mind to the Star Chart, urged the power of the Star Domain, and sent it surging thunderously into the passage. At the same time, he circulated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art to test its edge.

Having recognized the strengths and drawbacks of the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, he naturally would not cultivate it himself.

At this moment, he was using the entire Star Domain as the foundation to activate this powerful Secret Art. In other words, he was making Hengluo Star Domain circulate the Heaven Devouring Battle Art. The one cultivating was not him, but the entire Star Domain. No matter how much energy of differing attributes it devoured, he did not need to digest or resolve it himself. The Star Domain naturally had that ability.

This was a heaven-reaching method only a Star Domain Master could display.

At the other end of the passage, Wu Heng's face was gloomy. He had guarded the place for a month and was bored out of his mind.

He had wanted to cultivate by swallowing a Star Domain, only for someone to ruin everything at the final juncture and even barge into his home to punch him twice. Instinct told him Yang Kai was not easy to provoke, and he feared the man might cause some new trouble.

Yet the entire month had been calm and uneventful. Nothing had happened at all.

It seemed Yang Kai also knew he could do nothing to him and had likely lowered his banners and stilled his drums.

The Void Passage was right there, but Wu Heng had not repaired it either, because doing so would make it seem as if he feared Yang Kai. How could he bear to lose that much face?

In truth, the best outcome for him would be to let the matter fade away unresolved. Swallowing a Star Domain was no small affair. He had only entertained the idea because he noticed that Star Domain had no master. If Hengluo Star Domain had already had a master, he never would have done it.

Wu Heng was willing to turn a great matter into a small one, then let the small one disappear, but Yang Kai was not. A meal's kindness had to be repaid, and even the hatred of a glare had to be avenged. Moreover, because of Great Wilderness Star Domain's invasion, countless people in Hengluo Star Domain had seen their families torn apart, their states destroyed, and their nations ruined. Even Tongxuan Continent had nearly been ravaged, and Little Senior Sister had almost lost her life.

If he did not vent this resentment, what use was Yang Kai's cultivation?

An unusual movement suddenly came from within the passage. Wu Heng snapped his head up and looked over, his face darkening as he gritted out, "You refuse the face I'm giving you, you bastard!"

He felt that his willingness to turn a great matter into a small one and let the small one vanish was already the greatest concession he could make. Who would have thought Yang Kai truly refused to let things rest and now wanted to swallow his Star Domain in return?

Wu Heng made up his mind to make Yang Kai suffer a great loss. Otherwise, the other party would probably never learn the immensity of Heaven and Earth.

Darkness spread from the passage like a tide, like thick ink, madly expanding in every direction. Wherever it passed, everything turned into nothingness, revealing a chaotic black hole.

Wu Heng ignored it and kept retreating, because it was not yet time to act. If he acted now, he could indeed suppress the darkness, but he would certainly fall into an endless tug-of-war with Yang Kai. Only by waiting quietly for the right opportunity could he settle this once and for all.
