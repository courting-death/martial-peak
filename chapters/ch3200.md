## Chapter 3200 — Re-entering the Wilds

Ancient trees soared into the clouds. Thunder rolled and lightning flashed overhead as strange phenomena filled the sky. The space split apart, as if someone had torn open a rift.

An extremely dangerous aura poured from that rift, as though the end of the world were close at hand.

A Yao Marshal with two horns on his head hid trembling beneath a tree, staring up at the sky.

Crack! A thunderclap rang out, deafening and mind-shaking. The Yao Marshal was so frightened that he dropped onto his backside, his face turning pale.

The storm of wind, clouds, thunder, and lightning suddenly drew back. In an instant, the sky was washed clear, with not a cloud for ten thousand li. When the Yao Marshal looked again, he saw that a figure had appeared in the sky at some point, and his heart immediately filled with shock and suspicion.

This was yao territory. Few humans dared venture this deep, and those who did were basically already dead, so he was naturally curious how this person had gotten here. There had clearly been no one there a moment ago.

Looking more carefully, he vaguely felt that this person seemed familiar, as if he had seen him somewhere before.

Before he could think it through, the man suddenly stirred. He closed his eyes and spread his arms, and the Spiritual Qi of Heaven and Earth surged into motion, gathering toward him all at once.

Hong...

An invisible wave of air burst outward, turning into an impact ring visible to the naked eye as it swept across a hundred li. In an instant, the trees swayed and rustled loudly.

The Yao Marshal's eyes bulged. Sensing from that spreading aura that this person was not someone to provoke, he hurriedly held his breath, steadied his mind, and quietly retreated. He was only a Yao Marshal, merely equivalent to a human Dao Origin Realm cultivator. This human who had suddenly appeared, however, was an Emperor Realm Master, and from the looks of it, he had just broken through again. Whether he was now in the Second or Third Stage of the Emperor Realm, a mere Yao Marshal like him was absolutely no match.

He had to report this to Great King at once and let Great King decide what to do.

He just could not understand why this man had come here of all places to break through. Was he courting death? Although the Emperor Realm was not weak, there were many Great Kings here who were stronger than him. If he happened to run into any one of them, they could pluck out his heart and liver to go with their wine.

High in the sky, Yang Kai felt refreshed and invigorated. Long-lost power coursed through his body, and every raise of his hand and step of his foot seemed capable of moving mountains and overturning seas. Unable to suppress the excitement in his heart, he let out a long howl.

Being back in the Star Domain for more than ten years had truly stifled him. At first, to avoid rejection from the World Laws, he had no choice but to seal his cultivation. Later, even after becoming the Master of the Star Domain, he still did not dare wield his strength freely, for fear of damaging the Star Domain.

Only now had the shackles on his body finally been cast off, allowing him to taste freedom once more.

Moreover, just as he had expected, his accumulation in the First Stage of the Emperor Realm was already incomparably rich. The moment he returned to the Star Realm, his strength naturally broke through to the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. With his realm rising and his power swelling, he immediately felt the carefree delight of a bird released beneath the high heavens and a fish leaping through the open sea.

As his howl gradually faded, Yang Kai swept his Divine Sense out to investigate his surroundings.

Although he could be certain he had returned to the Star Realm, the first thing he needed to figure out was where he was!

After bringing Su Yan back from Ice Soul Star, he had stayed on Dark Star for half a year. Only when everything was properly prepared did he move everyone into the Small Xuan Realm, shatter the Void, and return to the Star Realm.

The process had been fairly smooth. With his abilities as Master of the Star Domain, finding a passage to the Star Realm was not difficult, and that passage had even been left behind. In the future, if anyone in Hengluo Star Domain became strong enough, they could enter the Star Realm through it.

Doing this was equivalent to reconnecting Hengluo Star Domain with the Star Realm. In the future, the experts of Hengluo Star Domain would no longer have to fear reaching sufficient cultivation only to waste away with nowhere further to go.

Everywhere he looked were towering ancient trees, so massive that even a dozen people joining hands could not wrap around them. They seemed faintly familiar, and the surrounding environment was steeped in an ancient, wild aura. Yang Kai could not help raising his brow, thinking this was quite the coincidence. Had he arrived at that place?

When setting out from the Star Domain for the Star Realm, he had been unable to precisely locate his destination. Even the Starlight Passage he had reopened through tremendous means was only connected to an approximate location.

A hundred li away, a Yao Marshal was racing along, hurrying toward a certain place.

Yang Kai's figure flickered, and in the next instant, he blocked his path.

The two-horned Yao Marshal halted in alarm and warily sized Yang Kai up.

"Is this the Wild Ancient Land?" Yang Kai asked, looking at him.

The Yao Marshal could not immediately figure out Yang Kai's background or purpose, and knowing he was no match for him, he proved rather cooperative. "Correct!" Inwardly, he found it very strange. Since this fellow had come here, could he really not know this was the Wild Ancient Land? Humans truly were lacking in brains.

"Good." Yang Kai grinned. "Which Saint Venerable do you serve?"

The Yao Marshal was stunned again. "You know the Saint Venerable Lords?"

"Enough nonsense. Are you asking me, or am I asking you?" Yang Kai raised his hand and rapped him on one horn.

The Yao Marshal's face immediately paled. He had not seen how Yang Kai moved at all. In other words, if the human before him wanted his life, taking it would be as easy as pinching a snail between his fingers.

With one foot already inside the gates of hell and a chill running through his whole body, the Yao Marshal became obedient. "This one serves under Yao King Yingfei, subordinate of the Western Saint Venerable."

"Yingfei!" Hearing this, Yang Kai could not help smiling.

The yao grew even more puzzled. "You know our Great King?" In that brief instant, this human's expression had softened considerably, and since he had called Yingfei directly by name, they surely knew each other.

"Yingfei serves under me. What do you think?"

"Ah?" The Yao Marshal froze, instinctively thinking this fellow was bragging. Great King Yingfei was one of the Eight Great Yao Kings under Western Saint Venerable Sir Cang Gou. Even if a human had the good fortune to know him, how could he dare make such shameless claims? But in the next instant, he suddenly seemed to remember something and stared wide-eyed at Yang Kai. "You... you..."

He finally understood why this human had looked somewhat familiar.

More than ten years ago, several people had intruded into the Wild Ancient Land and become caught up in the Blood Gate's upheaval. After a great battle, the Northern Saint Venerable Shi Huo had even fallen because of it. From then on, the Four Great Saint Venerables of the Wild Ancient Land became three Great Saint Venerables. He had heard that a descendant of Heaven's Punisher had entered the Blood Gate, and that another human man shared an extremely close relationship with that descendant of Heaven's Punisher. Even the three Great Saint Venerables treated him politely. When he left the Wild Ancient Land back then, he had taken three Great Kings with him as well, and Yingfei was one of them.

During that battle, this Yao Marshal had also watched from afar. Although he had not seen very clearly, he had still vaguely remembered Yang Kai's figure and features. Now, with Yang Kai's reminder, his sealed memories instantly opened.

What was that human called again... oh, right...

The two-horned Yao Marshal cupped his fists excitedly. "So it is Sir Yang!"

"You recognize me?" Yang Kai asked with great interest. There were countless Yao Marshals in the Wild Ancient Land; he had not expected one he casually ran into to actually recognize him. Since when had he become so famous in the Wild Ancient Land?

"I do, I do. In earlier years, I had the honor of admiring Sir's bearing from afar." The two-horned Yao Marshal was beyond excited, and his expression grew increasingly flattering.

How could he not recognize him? Enter the Blood Gate, gain new life, return to the ancestral bloodline, and transform into a Divine Spirit!

This was a saying that had been passed down in the Wild Ancient Land since ancient times, one every yao knew by heart. Now that the Blood Gate had closed and the descendant of Heaven's Punisher who controlled the Sources of countless Divine Spirits was nowhere to be found, rumors were spreading throughout the Wild Ancient Land that if one wanted to restore the glory of the ancestors and transform into a Divine Spirit, the human called Yang Kai was the key. If not, why would the three Sirs Xi Lei, Yingfei, and Xie Wuwei willingly allow themselves to be ordered about, even leaving the Wild Ancient Land where they had lived for thousands of years to serve under him? Was it not precisely to be closest to the opportunity?

Among the Thirty-Two Yao Kings of the Ancient Land, only those three had such fortune. The remaining twenty-nine Great Kings were incomparably envious of them. If the three Saint Venerables had not strictly restrained them, those twenty-nine Great Kings would likely have already set out from the Ancient Land to search for Yang Kai.

The two-horned Yao Marshal never would have imagined that the opportunity those twenty-nine Great Kings could not find no matter how they searched would suddenly descend upon his own head. The one standing before him was no ordinary human. He was a shortcut to becoming a Divine Spirit and reaching the peak.

He suddenly looked as if he were about to drool. Yang Kai was rather disgusted and moved a few steps away before saying, "Since you recognize me, this will be easy. I have something I want you to do. I wonder if you have time."

"I do, I do..." the two-horned Yao Marshal answered repeatedly. What a joke. Since he had encountered this Sir, he naturally had to seize the opportunity properly. Even if he had no time, he had to make time. "Please give your command, Sir."

Yang Kai's brow twitched. He kept feeling that the way this fellow looked at him was a little off, and he had no desire to waste words with him. He instructed, "Go to Luan Feng's place and tell her that I, Yang Kai, have returned. The account from back then needs to be settled properly. Tell her to prepare herself."

"Account? What account?" The two-horned Yao Marshal looked blank.

"Why ask so much? Hurry up and go." Yang Kai lifted his foot and kicked him.

"Oh." The two-horned Yao Marshal turned and left, but after only a few steps, he suddenly realized something was wrong. If he left like this, would he not be wasting his chance to get close? He hurriedly turned back, but where was there any sign of Yang Kai? Yang Kai had long since vanished. The Yao Marshal immediately slapped his thigh in vexation and cursed himself for being utterly stupid.

He did not dare obey in public while disobeying in private, and could only hope he would have another chance to see Yang Kai and properly work for him. He immediately set out and sped toward Luan Feng's palace.

Yang Kai's figure flickered among the towering treetops as he followed his memory and sped in a certain direction.

Only after an hour did he drift lightly down.

Fragrance drifted through the air, and all living things flourished. Although this place was still within the Wild Ancient Land, it lacked the killing intent and lurking danger found everywhere else in the Ancient Land. Instead, it was peaceful and natural, with an atmosphere completely different from the rest.

Birds rested in the treetops, and beasts ran across the earth. Scenes rarely seen elsewhere in the Ancient Land were ordinary here.

It was as if an invisible power protected this stretch of land, sparing this region of ten thousand li from disaster.

The wind brushed past, and waves of trees rose and fell, as if whispers were being passed among the towering ancient trees.

Palm-sized little figures suddenly flew out from behind the trees one after another. Each was exceptionally exquisite, and their bodies were filled with pure Wood Attribute aura.

Wood Spirits!
