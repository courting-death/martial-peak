## Chapter 3215 — Cultivation Methods Pavilion

If Yang Yan had not been his direct superior, he never would have dared do such a thing. Once it was discovered, punishment would be unavoidable, and if things went badly, he might even be stripped of his status as Master of the Star Domain.

But with Yang Yan protecting him from above, what was secretly bringing a few people over supposed to matter?

"Then what about everyone?" Bing Yun suddenly realized something. Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star alone had brought over several thousand people, so the others were likely even more numerous. After all, Yang Kai himself came from the Star Domain. There was no way he would not look after his friends and family there.

"A hundred thousand or so..." Yang Kai scratched his cheek.

Bing Yun froze. Ji Yao also stared at him in a daze.

What did a hundred thousand or so mean? The entire Ice Heart Valley now had only several thousand disciples, fewer than ten thousand, and that was after years of constantly accepting new disciples. A hundred thousand or so was the equivalent of more than ten Ice Heart Valleys.

"Why did you bring so many people over?" Bing Yun did not know whether to laugh or cry. "Can you even afford to support them?"

A hundred thousand or so people. Just the cultivation resources each person needed would add up to a terrifying amount. Lingxiao Palace had not been founded long, so where could it possibly have the reserves to squander on that scale? She could not help worrying a little about Yang Kai's future.

Yang Kai laughed awkwardly. "For now, it isn't much of a problem."

Bing Yun was astonished. "It seems I underestimated you. Since you have plenty of manpower over there, arrange for someone to send the people from Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star over as soon as possible. Consider it my way of sharing some of your burden." Other people were one thing, but Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star was also a Sect Bing Yun had founded back then. Naturally, she could not ignore those several thousand disciples and grand-disciples. Receiving them into Ice Heart Valley would also make their future cultivation more convenient.

"Many thanks, Senior."

Bing Yun smiled. "I should be the one thanking you. Still, you brat, you are rich and generous, and my Ice Heart Valley has nothing good with which to repay you. In the future, I can only teach Su Yan with care and return this favor that way."

This was clearly meant to tell Yang Kai not to worry. Su Yan would absolutely not be mistreated while she was here.

Yang Kai seized the opening at once and said with a grin, "Senior, this Junior really does have a request."

Bing Yun said, "What is it? As long as it is not too excessive, let me hear it."

Yang Kai put on a bitter face. "Senior, my Lingxiao Palace was founded only recently, so its reserves cannot compare with the other Sects in the Star Realm. Setting aside cultivation resources for now, we can tighten our belts and scrape by, but disciples cannot cultivate on Source Crystals alone. They also need suitable cultivation methods and Secret Techniques. Otherwise, they will be blindly working things out on their own. Even if their cultivation rises, they still won't be a match for others when they go out to fight. If they ended up being killed by someone above their level, how embarrassing would that be..."

Bing Yun gave him an annoyed look. "If you have something to say, say it. Why beat around the bush?"

Yang Kai chuckled. "This Junior was wondering if I could borrow some cultivation methods, Secret Techniques, and the like from Ice Heart Valley. You also know that among those hundred thousand or so people, there are all kinds, and many of them cultivate ice-attributed methods. These are exactly what they lack right now."

Bing Yun smiled. "You destroyed Wenqing Sect, and you also took Wenqing Sect's belongings. Are the reserves of such a vast Sect, accumulated over tens of thousands of years, still not enough for you?" Speaking of which, Wenqing Sect's reserves were even greater than Ice Heart Valley's. It was, after all, a Sect founded by a Great Emperor. Back then, Great Emperor Wenqing had been famous and powerful. It was only that, as the inheritance passed down, each generation became worse than the last, and its reputation gradually declined until it was finally destroyed at Yang Kai's hands.

Although Bing Yun did not know how much Yang Kai had gained from destroying Wenqing Sect, the fact that he could support a hundred thousand or so people gave her some idea. Setting cultivation resources aside, he absolutely had no small number of cultivation methods and Secret Techniques. She remembered that Wenqing Sect's library was even larger than Ice Heart Valley's.

Yang Kai rubbed his hands together. "Naturally, the more cultivation methods and Secret Techniques, the better. The disciples will have more choices, won't they? Of course, if Senior is willing, we can also exchange. My Lingxiao Palace now has a library as well. If any cultivation methods or Secret Techniques inside catch Senior's eye, feel free to take them."

Bing Yun said, "My Ice Heart Valley disciples all cultivate ice-attributed cultivation methods and Secret Techniques. What would I do with anyone else's?"

"Then Senior means..."

Bing Yun said, "If I refused, wouldn't that make me seem far too petty?"

"Many thanks, Senior!" Yang Kai beamed.

Bing Yun waved her hand. "Yao'er, take him to the Cultivation Methods Pavilion for a look. Aside from the secret inheritances on the top floor, he may do as he likes with the rest." Giving such an instruction in front of Yang Kai naturally meant she did not treat him as an outsider. As for those secret inheritances, they were truly the foundation upon which Ice Heart Valley stood. Even Ice Heart Valley disciples could not cultivate them lightly. Only future pillars of the Sect had the qualifications to study them, so naturally they could not be taken away by Yang Kai.

"Uh... this Junior can go by himself. There is no need to trouble Junior Sister Yao."

Ji Yao chuckled. "Since Honored Master has given the order, it is no longer up to you. Senior Brother Yang, come with me." Ji Yao smiled faintly, walked down, and brushed past him on her way outside. A wisp of subtle fragrance swept over him, almost tangible.

"Go. I will chat with Su Yan." Bing Yun waved her hand, dismissing him.

Yang Kai had no choice but to take his leave. Before he went, he quietly sent Su Yan a transmission. "Cultivate well. I will come see you often."

Su Yan nodded gently.

The doors of the great hall closed behind him. Yang Kai followed Ji Yao toward the Cultivation Methods Pavilion. Along the way, wherever they passed, graceful young women abounded. Many Ice Heart Valley disciples stepped aside from a distance, clearing the path on both sides and saluting respectfully.

Even so, they occasionally stole glances at Yang Kai, sizing him up with open curiosity.

All kinds of feminine fragrances mingled in the air, giving one the illusion of having fallen into a kingdom of women.

Neither of them spoke along the way. Ji Yao simply led the way and showed no intention of chatting with Yang Kai.

Before long, a nine-story tower came into view. A plaque hung above it, bearing the three large words Cultivation Methods Pavilion. Quite a few Ice Heart Valley disciples were going in and out, clearly choosing cultivation methods and Secret Techniques.

As soon as he entered with Ji Yao, a palace-dressed woman with her cloud-like hair coiled high came forward and gave a graceful salute. "Senior Aunt Ji!"

This woman had a Dao Origin Realm cultivation, so her strength was not weak. If Ji Yao counted as a first-generation disciple of Ice Heart Valley, then this woman was definitely a second-generation disciple.

After greeting Ji Yao, she lifted her eyes to Yang Kai and immediately understood. "Senior Uncle Yang!"

She had seen Yang Kai before. Although it had only been a distant glimpse back then, in all the world, aside from that Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace, there was surely no one else who could follow behind Ji Yao and walk straight into the Cultivation Methods Pavilion.

Yang Kai smiled and returned the salute. He knew very well how famous he was in Ice Heart Valley, so he was not surprised.

"Call all the disciples out. Senior Brother Yang and I have some business," Ji Yao instructed blandly.

A trace of astonishment flashed through the woman's eyes. Could the rumors be true? Otherwise, why would they clear the place after entering the Cultivation Methods Pavilion? A lone man and woman staying together in one room would not be good for Senior Aunt Ji's reputation if word spread, yet Senior Aunt Ji clearly did not care.

Though suspicion stirred in her heart, she answered, "Yes!"

She turned and went inside.

A moment later, Ice Heart Valley disciples began walking out one after another. After saluting, they took their leave.

In no more than the time it took to drink a cup of tea, the palace-dressed woman returned to report, "Senior Aunt Ji, the disciples have all left."

"Mm." Ji Yao nodded lightly, looked back at Yang Kai, and said, "Come in."

Yang Kai looked thoroughly uncomfortable. When Ji Yao had called for the place to be cleared, he had wanted to stop her, but he had been afraid others would read too much into it, so he kept silent. Now that the matter was settled, he could only go along with it.

Ji Yao, a woman, was not afraid, so what the hell was he afraid of?

The Cultivation Methods Pavilion was divided into nine floors. The higher the floor, the higher the grade of the cultivation methods and Secret Techniques stored there, and correspondingly, the higher the requirements for disciples to enter. Moreover, the cultivation methods and Secret Techniques stored in Ice Heart Valley's Cultivation Methods Pavilion were not limited to ice-attributed ones. There were other cultivation methods and Secret Techniques as well.

After all, Ice Heart Valley had stood for tens of thousands of years. When its disciples traveled outside, they would always gain something. Any cultivation methods and Secret Techniques they obtained, whether deliberately or by chance, would naturally be turned over to the Cultivation Methods Pavilion in exchange for Sect contribution.

The reserves of a Sect basically came from this. They were not built in a day, but required long years of accumulation.

As Ji Yao led Yang Kai forward, she explained the situation inside the Cultivation Methods Pavilion.

After Yang Kai had a rough understanding, he said, "Let's go straight to the fourth floor."

The cultivation methods and Secret Techniques below the fourth floor were truly of little use. Only above the fourth floor were there things suitable for Saint King Realm cultivators.

Ji Yao nodded and followed the spiraling wooden staircase all the way up to the fourth floor, then said, "Senior Brother Yang, please help yourself and see if there is anything you need."

Yang Kai nodded and looked around. He saw that the vast fourth-floor space held at least twenty wooden cabinets, each as tall as a person and several zhang long. The cabinets were divided into small compartments, and inside each compartment sat a jade slip bearing the name of a cultivation method or Secret Technique. Everything was arranged extremely clearly, making them easy to retrieve and read.

Yang Kai casually picked up a jade slip and poured his Divine Sense into it to examine it. He saw that it contained a Secret Technique that could let cultivators cultivate to the Void King Realm. It could not be considered especially precious, but its merit lay in its steady progression, which made it unlikely to cause Qi Deviation.

He immediately stopped standing on ceremony, took out a blank jade slip, and began copying it.

He had prepared for this trip long ago, even stocking more than ten thousand blank jade slips in his Spatial Ring.

And it was not only Ice Heart Valley. He had even set his sights on Li Dragon Palace, having Li Jiao return there long ago to gather some cultivation methods and Secret Techniques and send them over.

In the past, Li Jiao would definitely have made excuses or haggled with Yang Kai. After all, that was Li Dragon Palace's foundation, so how could he casually let outsiders benefit from it? But after his experience of sharing life and death with Yang Kai on Dragon Isle, after Yang Kai's life-saving grace toward Lu Sanniang, and after the camaraderie they had built fighting all over the Star Domain, Li Jiao did not refuse in the slightest. He agreed at once, then led Lu Sanniang and Lu Yuqin back to Li Dragon Palace.

Counting the days, it was about time for him to send the cultivation methods and Secret Techniques over.

Yang Kai was also preparing to make a trip to the Southern Territory to get some cultivation methods and Secret Techniques from Qingyang Shrine.

When the time came, Lingxiao Palace would gather the strength of four peak forces. Its disciples would have a vast range of choices when selecting cultivation methods and Secret Techniques. That starting point alone would be higher than others, and it would be difficult for them not to become strong in the future.

"Shall I help you?" Ji Yao walked over and asked softly.

Yang Kai nodded, took out a handful of jade slips, and handed them to her.

Although he did not know exactly how many cultivation methods and Secret Techniques were in Ice Heart Valley's Cultivation Methods Pavilion, having one more person copying them would still make the work more efficient.
