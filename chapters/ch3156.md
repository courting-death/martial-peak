## Chapter 3156 — Thought of It

First, he had run into Su Yan by chance in the Ancestral Domain. Then he returned to Dark Star to quell the war, went on to Di Chen to lend Shan Qingluo a hand, and now even matters on Tongxuan Continent had settled.

Everyone close to him was safe and sound. That was the greatest blessing. The rest could be dealt with slowly.

Xia Ningshang had been tormented all these years, and she still had to spare part of her attention to maintain Nine Heavens Sacred Land's protection. Whenever Xue Yue and the others went out now and then to harass the Great Wilderness Star Domain cultivators, she had to split her attention to look after them as well. For years, she had never truly rested.

Now that the threat had been removed and Yang Kai had returned, she nestled beside him and soon fell sound asleep.

With her current cultivation, she no longer needed sleep to restore her spirit, yet she still sank into an ordinary deep slumber. She looked peaceful as she curled up beside Yang Kai, and no one knew what she dreamed of, but a smile unexpectedly appeared on her lips.

Xue Yue lay on Yang Kai's other side. The two quietly stirred their Divine Senses, communicating as they told each other about their lives and experiences over the years.

With her warm softness beside him and her fragrance lingering at the tip of his nose, although Yang Kai had no lecherous thoughts, he still could not help leaning in toward Xue Yue’s red lips.

Xue Yue raised a hand to block his mouth and said softly, "If you don't solve Ningshang's problem, don't even think about touching me!"

Tongxuan Continent had been pacified, and nearly all the Great Wilderness Star Domain cultivators who had come here to cause trouble had been slain, but the harm Xia Ningshang had suffered could not be erased so easily. Even though Yang Kai had used the Everlasting Tree to restore her vitality and broken the Eight Wastelands Spirit-Locking Array, as long as Tongxuan Continent had not recovered, Xia Ningshang could not truly return to normal.

Yang Kai scratched his head. "That could take several dozen years, maybe a hundred. You can wait that long?"

Xue Yue snorted. "I don't mind. I'm just afraid you can't."

"You underestimate me!" Yang Kai curled his lip. He had been in the Star Realm for quite a few years now, and if sparks had not flared so fiercely between him and Zhu Qing in the end, he might still be untouched even now. He was no upright gentleman, and beauty would move him too, but he would not go chasing illicit pleasures without principles.

Xue Yue said, "You're not allowed to touch Big Sister or Luo'er either!"

Yang Kai immediately looked crestfallen. "Must you go that far?"

As expected, nothing was more ruthless than a woman's heart! He had four women here. If he was not allowed to touch any of them, what joy would be left in life? The path of the Martial Dao was not all cultivation and glory.

If Xue Yue joined forces with Su Yan and Shan Qingluo, she might really convince them. No, she would definitely convince them.

Xue Yue burrowed a little deeper into Yang Kai's embrace and said sweetly, "Ningshang is like this now because of me. As long as her problem remains unsolved, I won't be able to rest easy. Husband, please, think of a way to help Ningshang recover quickly."

Yang Kai reached out and pinched her nose, saying fiercely, "If you dare say please again, I'll take you right now."

Xue Yue spat at him.

Yang Kai lifted her chin with his hand. "Ningshang won't blame you, so don't blame yourself. If she had been in your situation, she would have made the same choice you did. In the same way, you certainly wouldn't blame her."

"But..."

"No buts." Yang Kai smiled faintly, then rubbed his head and said, "Still, you really have given me a difficult problem." Solving Xia Ningshang's problem was not simple, but even without Xue Yue saying this, he had already planned to do it next.

Several dozen years, or even a hundred, was too long for both him and Xia Ningshang. He could not wait, and neither could Xia Ningshang.

"Husband, you don't have a way either?" Xue Yue looked dejected.

"I'm thinking..." Yang Kai muttered. "Mm, thought of it."

Xue Yue's eyes lit with surprise and delight as she looked up at him.

"If I do well, do I get a reward?" Yang Kai asked, smiling at her.

Xue Yue flushed slightly. Lowering her head, she murmured, "All you ever do is come up with crooked ideas. Ningshang is your woman, so why are you asking me for a reward? That makes no sense at all." Then she pouted. "What reward do you want?"

Yang Kai reached out and tapped her red lips.

Xue Yue glared at him, but she still straightened on her own and leaned down to press her lips to his.

After a bout of lingering intimacy, both of them were breathing hard. Xue Yue suddenly reached out and stopped Yang Kai's large hand, which had slipped into her clothes and was running wild, then slowly shook her head. Xia Ningshang was still sleeping beside them; how could she be so bold?

Yang Kai did not force the matter. Knowing she was not in the mood for much more, he withdrew his hand, pulled her into his arms, and enjoyed this brief moment of peace.

Xia Ningshang slept for five full days, only slowly waking after the fifth day. When she saw Yang Kai sitting beside her in the same posture he had held before she fell asleep, as if he had not moved at all these past few days, her heart ached, and she felt somewhat guilty as well.

After five days of rest, her spirit had improved somewhat, but the problem had not been resolved. Two auras remained tangled around her, one green and one black, though the green aura clearly held the upper hand. As long as they waited a little longer, that black Qi would disappear completely.

There was no need to worry too much about her own condition. The only thing that needed attention now was Tongxuan Continent.

Over these few days, however, Yang Kai had already considered many things and had long since formed a plan.

The only reason he had not acted immediately was that he wanted to wait for her to wake, lest she open her eyes, fail to see him, and be disappointed again.

After spending a tender while with her and instructing Xue Yue to take good care of her, Yang Kai finally rose and headed outside.

Pushing open the door, he turned and saw Liu Yan crouching to one side. He called out to her, and the two of them headed toward the outer hall together.

The great hall was still packed with people. A few days ago, that great battle had wiped out the invading enemies, and the excitement from it had yet to fade.

When they saw Yang Kai appear, Meng Wuya and Di Mo immediately came forward to meet him.

"How is Ningshang?" Meng Wuya asked at once.

"No need to worry. Little Senior Sister's condition is stable. Given time, she will only get better."

Hearing this, Meng Wuya finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Yang Kai swept his gaze around the room, passing over every familiar and unfamiliar face, then cupped his fists and said, "Many thanks to all of you for standing together and helping one another over the years."

Meng Wuya laughed and scolded, "Stinking brat, why are you still acting like an outsider with us?"

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Although Tongxuan Continent's troubles have been resolved, the situation across the entire Star Domain is still far from optimistic, so there may be places where I need everyone to lend their strength in the future. When the day comes that we sweep every enemy from the Star Domain, this Junior will hold a feast to celebrate with all of you."

At those words, everyone fell into thought. They had been trapped on Tongxuan Continent these past few years and knew little about the outside situation, but it was probably not much better out there.

"So please, everyone, hurry and cultivate. Perhaps... we will soon begin a new campaign." As he spoke, Yang Kai tossed a Spatial Ring to Meng Wuya.

Meng Wuya took it and swept his Divine Sense inside, and shock immediately crossed his face.

The Spatial Ring was actually packed with all kinds of cultivation resources, and a single glance showed that they were exceedingly precious. Many of them were things he had never even heard of or seen before.

He did not examine them in detail, but such an enormous stockpile was enough for more than a thousand people to use for several years. With these resources to assist them, everyone's strength was certain to surge in a short time.

"I'll have to trouble Senior Meng to distribute these things. Next, I will open a channel connecting this place to the outside world. Once that is done, this place will be linked to Shuiyue Star, Di Chen Star, and Dark Star. One month. You have only one month. One month from now, everyone, please follow me to war!"

...

Nine Heavens Sacred Land also had a Space Array that Yang Kai had arranged back then. Together with Liu Yan, Yang Kai quickly modified and upgraded the array, linking it to the outside world.

After ordering Liu Yan to remain behind and keep watch, Yang Kai stood on the array and activated its power. Light flashed, and he vanished.

On Shuiyue Star, Yang Kai appeared in that great hall and saw a young girl sitting cross-legged nearby, a large pile of Source Crystals beside her. She seemed to be cultivating.

The girl clearly sensed something and hurriedly opened her eyes. The moment she looked up, she called out in delighted surprise, "Sir Yang."

"Yuqin, is it just you here?" Yang Kai smiled at her.

This girl was none other than Lu Sanniang's daughter, Lu Yuqin. Her cultivation might not amount to much in the Star Realm, but in this Star Domain, it was quite respectable. With her guarding the Space Array here, there was no need to worry about anyone damaging or seizing it.

Lu Yuqin was clearly much livelier than when they had first met, her little face rosy. Hearing him, she nodded and said, "Mother said this array is very important to Sir Yang and told me to watch it here and not let anyone else come near."

"Where are your parents now?" Yang Kai asked.

Lu Yuqin said, "In the city." As she spoke, she pouted. "Neither of them pays any attention to me."

Yang Kai laughed. "They've reunited after a long separation. It's only natural they're being a little affectionate."

Lu Yuqin snorted. "They're stuck together all day. It's so annoying."

Though she said so, there was clearly little real blame in it. On Dragon Isle, she and Lu Sanniang had depended on each other and suffered all manner of bullying. Now that Li Jiao was by their side and taking good care of both mother and daughter, Lu Yuqin was happy to see it, so how could she blame them?

"All right, cultivate carefully. I'll go find your parents." After saying this, Yang Kai released his Divine Sense slightly and sensed for a moment, quickly locating Li Jiao and the others.

In a certain great hall within the city, Li Jiao, Lu Sanniang, Ruan Biting, and He Yunxiang were gathered together. Besides them, there were many completely unfamiliar faces, though all of them had Void King Realm cultivation. When these people looked at Li Jiao and the other three, especially Li Jiao, their eyes almost overflowed with admiration and worship.

There were quite a few of them, more than a dozen in all.

They seemed to be discussing something. Li Jiao sat listlessly in the seat of honor, bored and distracted.

After all, he was a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master. For Yang Kai to leave him here to recover Shuiyue Star was a gross waste of his talents. Working separately from Lu Sanniang and the others, he had completed the task in less than a month.

Still, after recovering it, he had to manage it with some care. After all, he did not know whether Shuiyue Star was important to Yang Kai.

But the affairs here were utterly meaningless to him. He would much rather go roaming the mountains and rivers with Sanniang.

The Void King Realm cultivators seated here were all supporters he had gathered while subduing Shuiyue Star. They were experts originally from Shuiyue Star or Hengluo Commerce Guild who had spent these years hiding here and there while opposing Netherworld Sect. Cowed by his strength, they had all come forward to pledge allegiance.
