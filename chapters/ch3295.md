## Chapter 3295 — Finding It Without Wearing Out Iron Shoes

Yang Kai sighed. "Lady Lan, how well known are you in the Eastern Territory?"

Lan He asked, "Well known in what way?"

"Do many people know who you are?"

Lan He smiled with modest restraint. "Not too many, but probably not few either." She had always had some reputation in the Eastern Territory. Though she could not compare to that fellow Red Ghost, she was still one of the leading figures among the rising stars. Otherwise, she would not have been qualified to enter the Shattered Star Sea back then.

On top of that, Yin Lesheng of Netherworld Sect and the two Holy Sons of Brahma Heaven Sacred Land had all been killed by Yang Kai, making her stand out even more. With her current cultivation at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm, plenty of people naturally recognized her.

"Then that is troublesome." Yang Kai's mouth twitched.

"What is troublesome?" Lan He asked, puzzled.

Yang Kai shook his head. "Nothing." Netherworld Sect, Brahma Heaven Sacred Land, whatever they were, he did not fear them in the least. If they did not come looking for trouble, fine. If they truly dared to come, he would teach them what it meant to strike a stone with an egg. But Lan He had come here out of goodwill to warn him, and in such a public place, there was no avoiding the eyes of people with ulterior motives.

He could leave directly, of course, but Lan He's actions had already dragged Heavenly Wolf Valley into the fire. If the others could not find him, they would likely go to Heavenly Wolf Valley to ask questions, and when the time came, Lan He would probably not fare well either.

Since she had acted out of kindness, how could Yang Kai ignore it? Yet he could hardly tell her that her kindness had only made trouble, so he could only silently consider how to keep Heavenly Wolf Valley out of the matter and avoid being implicated.

While he was thinking this, Lan He said, "Brother Yang, weren't you asking about Spirit Beast Island? Are you trying to go there?"

Yang Kai's eyes lit up. "Lady Lan knows how to get there?"

Lan He pursed her lips in a smile. "The matter is secret, so I do not know much, but the Valley Lord should know."

"Your Heavenly Wolf Valley has a connection to Spirit Beast Island?"

Lan He said, "My Heavenly Wolf Valley's inheritance is connected to the Divine Spirit Heavenly Wolf. In our early years, we once used a secret method to raise a descendant of the Heavenly Wolf. Unfortunately, its bloodline was impure, so it could not reproduce its ancestor's glory. Somehow, Spirit Beast Island learned of it and sent a Beast-Seeking Envoy over. He took the Heavenly Wolf descendant away, but he also left behind a token and told Heavenly Wolf Valley that if we ever faced an insurmountable crisis, we could take the token to Spirit Beast Island in the Eastern Sea and ask for help."

"A token..." Yang Kai broke into a broad smile as he listened. This was truly finding what one sought without wearing out iron shoes. He had never known how to reach Spirit Beast Island, only to unexpectedly learn the way from Lan He.

Yang Kai guessed that the Beast-Seeking Envoy she mentioned was like Old Tao, whom he had met last time at Mitian Sect, someone ordered to search the outside world for all kinds of rare and exotic beasts and bring them back to Spirit Beast Island to be raised.

A descendant of the Heavenly Wolf, even a flawed one, was still worth collecting for Spirit Beast Island. Heavenly Wolf Valley being unable to raise it properly did not mean Spirit Beast Island could not.

But Spirit Beast Island clearly had no wish to take someone else's possession for nothing, so that Beast-Seeking Envoy had left a token as compensation. To Heavenly Wolf Valley, this token from Spirit Beast Island was probably its greatest foundation. With it in hand, even the three top forces of the Eastern Territory likely would not dare harass them casually. Otherwise, if Spirit Beast Island were drawn into the matter, Brahma Heaven Sacred Land, Netherworld Sect, or whatever else would all find themselves unable to bear the consequences.

Lan He smiled faintly and said, "Now does Brother Yang understand why I invited you to Heavenly Wolf Valley to avoid the storm? I know Brother Yang is worried I will bring trouble down on my Sect, but everyone in the Eastern Territory knows Heavenly Wolf Valley has some connection to Spirit Beast Island. Even if they learn you went to Heavenly Wolf Valley, they will be helpless to do anything."

Yang Kai nodded. "I was overthinking it. In that case, this Yang will thank you in advance."

Lan He gave him a reproachful glance. "Brother Yang, saying that makes us sound like strangers. If not for you back then, I would have died long ago in the Shattered Star Sea. How could I be here today? A single drop of kindness should be repaid with a gushing spring, to say nothing of a life-saving grace."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Lady Lan's kindness is something this Yang will remember."

Truthfully, what happened back then had only been a small effort on his part. Lan He had fallen into the Void Crack because of him to begin with, and sending her out had taken him no real effort. Yet after so many years, she had specially come searching for him, offered him a place to avoid the storm, and even risked drawing trouble to Heavenly Wolf Valley. This favor was indeed no small thing.

But from Lan He's point of view, Yang Kai's small effort back then had indeed saved her life. It was hard to say how such a debt should be weighed.

No longer hesitating, Yang Kai tossed down several Source Crystals and left the restaurant together with Lan He.

After he got up, the woman who had just come upstairs quietly took out a Transmission Compass, poured her Divine Sense into it, and began communicating with someone.

In the city, Yang Kai and Lan He walked side by side toward the city gates.

Lan He suddenly frowned. "Brother Yang, your whereabouts have already been exposed. Someone is following us now."

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "That depends on whether she can keep up."

A moment later, the two left through the city gate. Yang Kai said, "Excuse me," then suddenly reached out and took Lan He by the arm. At the touch, Lan He trembled slightly, and a blush rose on her face.

In the next instant, her vision blurred. Heaven and earth spun around her, and by the time she appeared again, she was already somewhere else.

It was not over yet. The same thing happened several more times, and only after more than a dozen such jumps did Yang Kai release Lan He's arm and smile. "From here on, I will have to trouble Lady Lan to lead the way."

Lan He was still dazed. She looked around, then looked back the way they had come, but where was there any trace of that city? Thinking of Yang Kai's attainments in the Power of Space, a terrifying thought surfaced in her mind.

A Space Divine Ability, Teleportation!

Because of Li Wuyi, the people of the Eastern Territory had some understanding of the Power of Space. They knew Li Wuyi could ignore the barriers of space and move instantly from one place to another. That wondrous Space Divine Ability was Teleportation.

But everyone had only heard of such a thing. They had never seen it with their own eyes, much less experienced it themselves.

Yet at this very moment, Lan He had truly felt what Teleportation was like. In that dazed instant, it seemed as if her whole body had shuttled through the Void. It was wondrous, but it also made her shiver.

With such means, who in this world could keep him from leaving? No wonder he dared roam the Eastern Territory alone as casually as strolling through a garden. Then she was startled again and said in surprise, "Brother Yang, you are already at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm?"

Earlier, Yang Kai had not used any strength, so she had not noticed. But the moment Yang Kai urged his Emperor Yuan, she immediately sensed his current cultivation.

Yang Kai nodded. "I only advanced recently."

Lan He looked a little lost. After a long daze, she finally said, "Brother Yang truly is blessed with heavenly talent."

When they first met in the Shattered Star Sea back then, they had both only been at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm, yet his various feats had already made Lan He regard him as extraordinary. Later, when she heard that Yang Kai had advanced to the Emperor Realm in the Shattered Star Sea, she knew his future was limitless.

Even so, she had never imagined that after only a few years, he would break through again.

Lan He herself was now also at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm, and it was likewise thanks to the trip to the Shattered Star Sea that she had obtained the opportunity to advance. But when it came to breaking through to the Second Stage, she had no confidence at all.

After reaching the Emperor Realm, every advancement was incomparably difficult. Each barrier could halt countless heroes and outstanding talents. If not for that, this world would not have so few top forces.

A force needed only one Third-Stage Emperor presiding over it to be called a top force. Heavenly Wolf Valley's strongest combat power was merely at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm.

Lan He suppressed the shock in her heart, steadied herself, identified the direction, then beckoned to Yang Kai and sped off toward Heavenly Wolf Valley.

...

In the seaside city, the great hall housing the Space Array was bustling today.

Powerful figures teleported in from every direction, appearing one after another in the hall and leaving the cultivators guarding the array dumbstruck. They had no idea what kind of day this was, or why so many experts had come to the city.

Before long, at least thirty Emperor Realm Masters had gathered in the hall. At a glance, they were clearly split into two groups. One group was wreathed in sinister Yin Qi, as if they had returned from the purgatory of the Yellow Springs, and gave off an extremely uncomfortable feeling. The other group seemed a bit more normal, but every one of them radiated surging murderous intent, making the atmosphere in the hall unbearably oppressive for a time.

"They are from Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land." The guards gathered together, and someone sharp-eyed recognized the origins of the two groups.

"Why have they come here? Could some rare treasure be about to emerge in our city?" If not, how could so many experts have been drawn here? These were all Emperor Realm Masters, great figures who were usually as elusive as divine dragons.

"I doubt there is any rare treasure. From the look of it, they are here to kill someone."

"Kill someone? What kind of person requires this many Emperor Realm Masters? And who would dare provoke them?"

"If you ask me, who am I supposed to ask? Has City Lord been informed?"

"He has. City Lord is on his way."

The City Lord of this city was only a First-Stage Emperor Realm Master. With so many formidable people suddenly arriving, and from two top forces at that, he naturally could not avoid coming to ask what was going on.

After the news went out, a short, fat old man soon walked in with cold sweat beading on his forehead. The moment he swept his gaze over the hall, his knees nearly gave out, and he almost fainted.

When he first received the news, he had not quite believed it. After all, there was nothing in his city worth caring about, so why would Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land send a whole group of Emperor Realm Masters charging over in force? Now that he saw it for himself, sure enough, there were at least thirty or forty Emperor Realm Masters present, and the auras of the two men at their head were so powerful that he did not dare look directly at them.

The City Lord did have some guesses as to who those two were. One was certainly Netherworld Sect's Sect Master, Fu Bo. The other should be Brahma Heaven Sacred Land's Saint Lord, Xu Changfeng.

Both of these men were famous throughout the Eastern Territory, yet now they had actually come here together.

Cold sweat poured even more heavily from the short, fat City Lord's forehead. He knew that if he failed to handle this properly, his comfortable days as City Lord would likely be over. Not daring to be negligent, he hurried forward to pay his respects.

Who would have thought that neither Fu Bo nor Xu Changfeng had any intention of acknowledging him? They were only questioning a woman.

A moment later, Fu Bo shouted angrily, "Useless trash!"

With a thud, the woman dropped to her knees and said in terror, "This subordinate is incompetent."
