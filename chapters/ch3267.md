## Chapter 3267 - The Dagger Revealed

In terms of flight speed alone, Yang Kai might not be Lei Gu's match. After all, their cultivations were a full level apart. But Yang Kai possessed Space Divine Abilities and could ignore the barriers of space. Even when Lei Gu struggled desperately to pull ahead, Yang Kai caught up in an instant. How could Lei Gu possibly shake him off?

After trying a few times, Lei Gu stopped wasting his strength.

He was cautious enough, though. As he flew, he constantly changed direction, even deliberately circling certain places several times to guard against anyone tracking him from behind.

Yang Kai let him do as he pleased. He said nothing, merely following close behind.

Lei Gu probably never could have imagined that Yang Kai had a method like the Incarnation. He did not need to leave any mark behind. The connection between Yang Kai and his Incarnation, rooted deep in the soul, was the best guide of all.

The Incarnation could sense Yang Kai, and Yang Kai could sense the Incarnation, but outsiders would never be able to perceive that shadowless, formless connection.

After two full days, Lei Gu finally confirmed that no pursuers were behind him. Only then did he unleash his full speed and race in one direction.

After flying for another full two days, a vast swamp suddenly appeared ahead, stretching beyond sight.

They had actually reached the Southern Marsh. Yang Kai did not know whether Lei Gu had come here by chance or by design, but the Southern Marsh was where the first Demon Cave had appeared. Since Lei Gu had come here, he clearly had a purpose. Either he still had helpers lying in ambush and wanted to borrow the strength of those demonized people to deal with Yang Kai, or he wanted to use the terrain to hide himself completely.

Whichever it was, Yang Kai could not let him go any deeper.

So before Lei Gu could truly enter the Southern Marsh, Yang Kai flickered forward and blocked his path.

Lei Gu halted, his large hand still pressed against the crown of Gao Xueting's head, and snorted. "What do you want?"

Yang Kai sighed softly. "Even when you escort a friend a thousand li, there must come a time to part. I have fulfilled my promise. Now it is your turn."

Lei Gu said flatly, "Not enough!"

Yang Kai curled his lip. "You have been fleeing for several days already, and aside from me, no one has chased after you. Release my Senior Sister Gao, and I will let you go!"

Lei Gu snorted. "I decide when to release her. It is not your place to order me around."

Yang Kai said impatiently, "If you never release her, am I supposed to keep following you forever? Lei Gu, do not push things too far, or I will start doubting your sincerity. Once my patience is worn thin, if you still refuse, I can only fight you to the bitter end."

Lei Gu frowned and looked around. After a moment of thought, he said, "Very well. Now that we are here, I doubt anyone will be chasing after us."

"Then give Senior Sister Gao back to me." Yang Kai held out a hand.

Lei Gu bared his teeth in a sinister grin. "If I give her back, do you dare take her? I do not know where your confidence comes from, but right now it is two against one. Are you not afraid this Lord will join hands with your Senior Sister Gao and kill you?"

Yang Kai said calmly, "You can try!"

Lei Gu threw his head back and laughed. "Boy, you are skilled and bold. This Lord admires you. Why not submit to my Demon Race? With your ability and courage, your future would be smooth and boundless. Even becoming a Great Emperor would not be some fool's dream."

Yang Kai looked disgusted. "Cut the crap and release her already. My patience is limited. Do not force my hand!"

Lei Gu's smile vanished, and he snorted. "Since you insist, then catch her." As he spoke, he reached out and gently pushed Gao Xueting from behind. Gao Xueting immediately flew toward Yang Kai.

In an instant, Gao Xueting was before Yang Kai, but before he could act, the Demonic Qi covering her body suddenly began to writhe and swell madly. An extremely dangerous aura poured from her, and she even opened her arms as if to embrace him.

"Shameless!" Yang Kai roared in fury. He had known Lei Gu would never release her so easily. Just as Lei Gu had said, he might even control Gao Xueting and make her join him against Yang Kai, but Yang Kai had never imagined Lei Gu would directly control Gao Xueting to Self-Destruct.

If Gao Xueting really Self-Destructed, all their efforts would be wasted, and at such close range, Yang Kai absolutely could not escape unscathed. The Self-Destruction of a Second Stage Emperor Realm expert would be terrifying beyond measure. Lei Gu could then seize the chance to strike ruthlessly.

Fortunately, Yang Kai was not unprepared.

In that split second, Yang Kai's left eye transformed into a golden vertical pupil. His Divine Soul strength surged out like a tide, wrapping Gao Xueting completely. At the same time, he opened his hand, the Xuan Realm Pearl clenched tightly in his palm, and urged it to open an entrance to a world. A huge vortex appeared before his hand.

Gao Xueting, wrapped in roiling Demonic Qi and surging with danger, plunged headfirst into the vortex. As if swallowed by the maw of an invisible beast, she vanished in the blink of an eye.

Without the slightest pause, Yang Kai immersed his mind in the Small Xuan Realm and urged its power to suppress her. Only after seeing with his own eyes that the Demonic Qi on her body was beginning to stabilize did he finally relax.

With a boom, an enormous force slammed into his chest and abdomen. Agonizing pain spread through him, jolting him back to his senses. He spat a mouthful of Golden Blood, his aura wilting, but he used the force of the blow to retreat several hundred zhang.

Several hundred zhang away, Lei Gu stared at Yang Kai in astonishment, gripping a round-headed war hammer, his eyes filled with shock and uncertainty.

Gao Xueting had vanished right under his nose! He could not help wondering whether his eyes had deceived him.

Using Gao Xueting's Self-Destruction to injure Yang Kai, then seizing the chance to take his life himself, should have been a foolproof plan, unless Yang Kai ignored Gao Xueting's fate and dodged away.

But under his watch, Yang Kai had shown no sign of dodging at all, while Gao Xueting had abruptly vanished just before her Self-Destruction, causing a small mishap in his plan. His reaction was swift as well. Even though something had gone wrong with Gao Xueting’s part, he immediately rushed forward and, in the instant Yang Kai was distracted, smashed his hammer into Yang Kai’s chest.

That strike used the full force of his cultivation without the slightest reservation. A Second Stage Emperor Realm cultivator should have had no chance of surviving it and should have been smashed to pieces.

Then something even more unexpected happened. Yang Kai did not die. Bones cracked, his chest caved in, and his aura weakened to an extreme, yet he actually survived.

Only now did Lei Gu realize he had underestimated the toughness of Yang Kai's body. He had once seen Yang Kai transform into a half-dragon with his own eyes, so he naturally understood that he had underestimated the depth of that half-dragon body.

But now that things had reached this point, Lei Gu had no intention of retreating. Even if this boy possessed Space Divine Abilities, how long could he last with such heavy injuries? The Southern Territory plan had failed, all because of him. How could Lei Gu let him walk away free? He had to kill him here.

Their eyes met. Lei Gu's murderous intent surged, his gaze cold and severe. Yang Kai's aura wavered and his face was pale, yet he bared his teeth in a vicious grin. The grin tugged at his wounds, and he spat another mouthful of Golden Blood.

Lei Gu raised the round-headed hammer and pointed it at Yang Kai from afar. "Boy, you ruined this Lord's grand affair. Today, you die!" As he spoke, the round-headed hammer turned into a streak of light and appeared above Yang Kai's head in an instant, carrying power enough to destroy heaven and earth as it smashed down.

Before the attack even landed, the space shattered, cracks spreading one after another.

Yang Kai forced himself to hold on and raised a hand. The Mountain and River Bell appeared, spinning rapidly, and flew straight toward the round-headed hammer.

A tremendous clang rang out. A visible shockwave spread in a circle, like a formless blade cutting the whole world in two. Light flashed wildly over the Mountain and River Bell as it was sent flying, but the round-headed hammer was blocked as well.

Yang Kai, however, could not withstand the impact. He plummeted and smashed into the mire, landing in a sorry state. Not daring to remain where he was, he hurriedly shot back into the sky, retrieved the Mountain and River Bell, and turned to flee in the direction he had come from.

A tiger fallen to the plains was bullied by dogs; a dragon stranded in shallow water was mocked by shrimp. Yang Kai was filled with grief and indignation.

If he had been at his peak, Yang Kai would not have feared Lei Gu even as a Third Stage Emperor Realm cultivator. With a bit of luck, he might even have been able to kill him. But just now, to suppress Gao Xueting's Self-Destruction, he had been forced to delay for that single instant, and Lei Gu's Imperial Treasure had blasted him squarely in the chest. His organs had all been knocked out of place. If not for his half-dragon body holding together, that blow alone would have been enough to smash him to pieces.

Even so, his current condition counted as a serious injury. How could the full-strength strike of a Third Stage Emperor Realm cultivator wielding an Imperial Treasure be easy to withstand? The Emperor Yuan in his body circulated sluggishly, making it difficult for him to act. Even though he had used the Mountain and River Bell to block the other party's strike, he was only clinging to life.

If he did not flee now, he would be unable to escape once Lei Gu tangled him up. He could not help feeling annoyed. If he had known it would come to this, he would have kept Liu Yan hidden in the Small Xuan Realm all along. With Liu Yan as his trump card, Lei Gu would have suffered dearly. As long as Yang Kai could delay until reinforcements arrived, Lei Gu's death would be certain. Unfortunately, he had never had a chance to do so before.

Now, he could only rely on himself to deal with Lei Gu.

As he fled, the wind roared behind him, and a terrifying pressure descended from the sky. Yang Kai looked up and immediately had the urge to curse.

Dense hammer shadows were crashing down from overhead. Clearly, Lei Gu had flown into a true rage and had no intention of letting him leave alive.

Yang Kai took a deep breath and forced down the pain wracking his body as the Laws of Space surged around him.

Hammer shadows filled the sky and smashed down, blasting one huge crater after another into the ground. Yang Kai was smashed to pieces as well. But Lei Gu's gaze suddenly shifted to the side.

Not far away, Yang Kai's figure strangely appeared. Clearly, he had used a Space Divine Ability to avoid the fatal attack.

Lei Gu roared in anger. He had long heard that those proficient in Space Divine Abilities were best at escaping with their lives, and only now, seeing it for himself, did he know that reputation was deserved. But from the distance Yang Kai had teleported, he also judged that Yang Kai had indeed reached the end of his strength and could not use such life-saving methods more than a few times. He immediately shot into the sky and pursued Yang Kai relentlessly, like a maggot clinging to bone, shouting, "Boy, where do you think you are running!"

Yang Kai was delighted when he heard that. Just now, he had still been worried that Lei Gu might not chase him. If that happened, Yang Kai would have no choice but to turn back and entangle him, which would be far too dangerous. One careless move, and he might capsize in the gutter. In his current state, he truly could not fight anyone at full strength.
