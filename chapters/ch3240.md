## Chapter 3240 — I Am the Hall Master

The affairs of the Northern Territory had already been settled. There were still a few loose ends to tie up afterward, but he no longer needed to concern himself with them. With Hua Qingsi presiding and the other three families cooperating, everything would be resolved before long.

By his reckoning, the timing was just about right. Yang Kai said goodbye to Hua Qingsi, then took Bian Yuqing with him through the Space Array to Qingyang Shrine.

He did not return to his own Spirit Sword Peak, instead flying straight toward Spirit Jade Peak, where Qiu Ran resided.

This was Bian Yuqing's first visit to Qingyang Shrine, so curiosity was only natural. She looked around as they went, quietly comparing Qingyang Shrine with her own Lingxiao Palace, and found that Qingyang Shrine's environment seemed to fall a step short. She could not help feeling a little proud.

She was from the Southern Territory. The Biyu Sect she had belonged to back then had been nothing more than an unranked Sect, without even a single Emperor Realm master. Qingyang Shrine, by contrast, was one of the Southern Territory's top Sects, far stronger than Biyu Sect, so she had naturally held it in deep reverence. Had she still been with Biyu Sect, she would never have had the qualifications to set foot in Qingyang Shrine. Now, however, as Lingxiao Palace's Second Steward, she did. Looking at Qingyang Shrine from this new position naturally left her with a different feeling.

She watched the tall, heroic figure speeding ahead and secretly rejoiced that she had met a noble benefactor. If not for Yang Kai, how could she ever have advanced to the Emperor Realm? She might well have been killed outside Linghu Palace, much less hold such authority now. In all of Lingxiao Palace, she ranked only below Yang Kai and Hua Qingsi, a Second Steward in name and in truth.

On Spirit Jade Peak, a guest arrived. Qiu Ran woke from meditation and received Yang Kai and Bian Yuqing in the main hall.

After Yang Kai introduced them, Bian Yuqing saluted with perfect composure. "Greetings, Deputy Hall Master Qiu."

She had heard Qiu Ran's renowned name before. As the Deputy Hall Master of Qingyang Shrine, he was truly a man who stood above others. Before coming here, she had felt somewhat anxious; meeting such an important figure, someone she would once never have had a chance to see, naturally left her with many thoughts. Yet once she actually met him, she found he was only so much after all. In that moment, she realized she was no longer the woman she had been back then. After spending years in Lingxiao Palace, where she even saw the three great Yao Kings on a regular basis, a Second Stage Emperor Realm master could no longer put much pressure on her.

"Second Steward." Qiu Ran cupped his fists, which served as his greeting.

Yang Kai said, "Senior Brother Qiu, you can discuss the details of the cooperation with Second Steward Bian. I will not keep you company."

The main reason he had brought Bian Yuqing here was to handle the cooperation with Qingyang Shrine. Gao Xueting had mentioned the matter before, and since she had been the one to raise it, he naturally could not ignore it, lest that woman catch him out again and nag him endlessly. Hua Qingsi was currently overseeing the affairs of the Northern Territory and could spare no attention, so the task of discussing cooperation with Qingyang Shrine naturally fell to Bian Yuqing.

Qiu Ran was startled. "For such an important matter, Junior Brother Yang does not need to make the decisions personally?" The meaning behind his words was clear: Yang Kai had brought over a Second Steward to negotiate with him, but could she truly speak for him? This involved no small amount of profit. If they reached an agreement here only for Yang Kai to refuse to acknowledge it afterward, that would be awkward. After all, Qiu Ran had personally seen Yang Kai haggle with him over every detail.

Yang Kai laughed heartily. "Senior Brother Qiu, please rest assured. As long as the two of you reach an agreement, I will have no objections." How could Qiu Ran know about the great moves Yang Kai had recently made in the Northern Territory? Cooperation and material exchange with Qingyang Shrine could indeed bring considerable profit, but Yang Kai no longer took it very seriously. Besides, he was also cooperating with Ziyuan Commerce Guild, and Ziyuan Commerce Guild was a business to begin with. That side was the real bulk of the Southern Territory trade; Qingyang Shrine was merely an accompaniment. If not for the fact that he was a Guest Elder of the Shrine, why would he have brought these benefits here at all?

He turned and walked out, then looked back and reminded her, "Second Steward, where appropriate, you may yield a point or two. I am, after all, an Elder of the Shrine."

"Yes!" Bian Yuqing answered respectfully.

Qiu Ran looked gratified. For Yang Kai to say such things in front of him naturally improved his impression of Yang Kai greatly. Sure enough, matters were easier to handle with one of their own.

Qiu Ran and Bian Yuqing immediately began discussing the matter in detail.

Yang Kai, meanwhile, went straight to Gao Xueting's Purple Bamboo Peak. After meeting her, he asked about the Southern Territory Martial Gathering and learned there were still three days before they would depart, leaving them plenty of time. He then went to find Murong Xiaoxiao and Xiao Baiyi to catch up, since his earlier visit had been hurried and he had not had time to say much.

Murong Xiaoxiao was fine. She was warm by nature and had a good temperament. They had all known one another since the Dao Origin Realm and had advanced all the way to become Shrine Elders, so their casual conversation was quite pleasant.

Xiao Baiyi, on the other hand, was truly irritating. His face was even colder than Ji Yao's, and he looked as if Yang Kai owed him several million. They had barely exchanged a few words before he was trying to drive his guest away, which made Yang Kai want to beat him up.

Since a guest could hardly stay when the host would not keep him, Yang Kai left in a huff.

He rested and adjusted himself on Spirit Sword Peak, and three days passed in the blink of an eye.

Early in the morning, sunlight spread across the land and all things flourished. On a certain spirit peak, several dozen figures stood together, the leader among them especially striking in a purple robe.

Yang Kai arrived through the air and stopped in midair. Seeing the man in purple, he paused slightly, then cupped his fists. "Greetings, Hall Master."

The man in purple was none other than Wen Zishan, the Shrine's Hall Master. A warm smile hung on his face as he stood with his hands behind his back, carrying himself with enough grace and charm to be described as elegant and unrestrained. Hearing Yang Kai, he looked over and nodded with a smile. "Elder Yang, you have worked hard."

"Sharing the Shrine's burdens is this subordinate's duty," Yang Kai said solemnly.

There was no helping it. In Lingxiao Palace of the Northern Territory, he was the Master of a Palace, but here he was only a Guest Elder. The proper courtesies still had to be observed.

"Wait a little longer. Little Xueting has not arrived yet. Ai, women really are troublesome. Even leaving the house takes them forever."

As soon as he said this, quite a few people glared at him. Even Murong Xiaoxiao, who had arrived early, pouted. Being lumped in like that for no reason naturally soured her mood.

Wen Zishan seemed to realize it as well. He tugged at the stubble on his chin, lifted his head toward the sky, and put on a weathered expression. "The wind is so clamorous today."

"Elder Luo, Elder Xiao, Elder Murong." Yang Kai greeted the other three Emperor Realm masters one by one. Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao needed no introduction, while Luo Chen was a cold-faced middle-aged man.

Yang Kai was not particularly familiar with him. The man had an aloof temperament and rarely interacted with any of the Shrine's Elders. Yang Kai only knew that such a person existed; they had not really dealt with each other.

The three of them returned his greeting in turn.

"These are the disciples participating in the Martial Gathering this time?" Yang Kai raised his head and looked at the several dozen people standing neatly behind them.

Those several dozen disciples cupped their fists in unison. "Greetings, Elder Yang."

Among them were Dao Origin Realm cultivators, Void King Realm cultivators, and even Origin Returning Realm cultivators. After all, the Southern Territory Martial Gathering itself was divided into several tiers: Dao Origin Realm against Dao Origin Realm, Void King Realm against Void King Realm, and the same for Origin Returning Realm. Naturally, the Shrine also had to select the best cultivators from each level to participate.

The dozen or so disciples in the front row were naturally Dao Origin Realm. Behind them came the Void King Realm and Origin Returning Realm cultivators, the ranks clearly separated.

Yang Kai nodded with a smile and noticed quite a few familiar faces among them. It seemed they had participated in the Four Seasons Land trial together back then, though they had not interacted much and were only vaguely acquainted. Back then, they had been in the Dao Origin Realm, and now they were still in the Dao Origin Realm. Advancing to the Emperor Realm was not so simple. Many people would spend their whole lives stuck at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm.

Xia Sheng, Xiao Baiyi, and Murong Xiaoxiao had been able to advance partly because their aptitude and comprehension were outstanding, and partly, perhaps, because of the opportunities they had obtained in the Four Seasons Land and the Shattered Star Sea.

As Yang Kai looked them over, his attention suddenly settled on one young man's face, because he really did know this person and even remembered his name.

Xue Yi!

Back when Yang Kai had first come to the Shrine with Qin Chaoyang, who held the Mortal Token, his purpose had been to obtain a quota to enter the Four Seasons Land. Once the Mortal Token appeared, the Shrine could hardly refuse, so they naturally gave him a spot. But the quotas for entering the Four Seasons Land had already been allocated, and with the Shrine giving one away, someone had naturally lost theirs.

The quota that had been taken away belonged precisely to this fellow called Xue Yi.

When the news spread, Xue Yi refused to accept it and even sought out Yang Kai for a fight. Naturally, he had not been Yang Kai's opponent.

It had happened many years ago, and if Yang Kai had not seen him here, he certainly would not have remembered it. Their eyes met. Xue Yi looked somewhat embarrassed, yet there was also a fierce determination in him, the kind born from shame and the desire to improve. Back then, they had all been in the Dao Origin Realm. Now Yang Kai had already become an Emperor, while Xue Yi was still struggling along in the Dao Origin Realm, so his feelings were inevitably complicated.

Yang Kai, for his part, only smiled calmly. He saw that this fellow had not wasted his years either. Back then, Xue Yi had merely been at the Second Stage of the Dao Origin Realm; now he was already at the Third Stage. Moreover, he stood very close to the front, clearly a candidate on whom the Shrine placed high hopes.

After briefly recalling these old matters, the two men met each other's eyes and looked away. Xue Yi secretly let out a breath. He had truly been afraid Yang Kai would settle old accounts with him. Their statuses were different now, and if Yang Kai really wanted to make things difficult for him, he would have no way to resist.

Just then, a streak of light sped over from the direction of Purple Bamboo Peak and came to a halt before everyone in an instant.

Wen Zishan's eyes lit up. "Little Xueting is dressed so beautifully today. Are you getting ready to marry someone? Truly worthy of celebration, worthy of celebration!"

"Shut your stinking mouth!" Gao Xueting shot Wen Zishan a cold glance, giving him no face at all.

Wen Zishan said, "Aiya, why so fierce? Be careful no one dares to marry you. If you still cannot marry out, you will become an old maiden."

Gao Xueting's gaze grew even colder, and she looked as though she might stab him to death with one sword. She snorted. "None of your damn business." Such a beautiful woman had actually been driven to curse aloud, which showed just how foul her mood was.

The disciples all lowered their brows and looked properly obedient, their expressions wooden as they let the two people's quarrel go in one ear and out the other.

Luo Chen looked down at the ground, Xiao Baiyi looked up at the sky, and Murong Xiaoxiao studied her own fingers, seemingly debating whether to trim her nails. Yang Kai leaned over and praised her casually, "Junior Sister, your fingers are very beautiful."

Murong Xiaoxiao's face instantly flushed bright red, and she did not know where to put her hand.

Everyone turned to look at him, and Yang Kai also realized he had misspoken. His expression twitched.

Fortunately, Gao Xueting spoke up. "Is everyone here?"

Xiao Baiyi nodded. "Everyone is here."

"Since everyone is here, let us set out." With that, she took the lead and sped forward.

The group followed close behind.

Wen Zishan froze for a moment, then stroked his chin and said, "I'm quite sure... I am the Hall Master."
