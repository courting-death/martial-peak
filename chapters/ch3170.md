## Chapter 3170 — He Succeeded

Moon Blade in hand, Yang Kai held it back without striking. At the same time, the Jingkong Beast King had already pounced close.

It had clearly sensed something. The Jingkong Beast King's massive body suddenly came to a halt. It was hard to imagine how it could control such a colossal form so freely, going from full speed to utter stillness in a single instant.

Then the two eyes atop its head slanted downward, fixing on the spot where Yang Kai stood.

"Hmph!" Hot air burst from Yang Kai's nostrils, and the aura around the Moon Blade in his hand grew even more dangerous.

The Jingkong Beast King's gaping mouth snapped shut. It seemed to have only just noticed him, and a humanlike light flickered in its eyes.

Yang Kai jerked his chin at it, making his stance clear: retreat, or fight. His towering arrogance almost condensed into substance.

One enormous figure and one tiny figure faced each other in a strange standoff amid the Starry Sky. Only after more than a dozen breaths did the Jingkong Beast King suddenly leap, passing over Yang Kai's head. Its huge body seemed to plunge into an invisible lake and merge into the Void. Soon, it vanished, leaving only an enormous Void Passage slowly closing behind it.

Yang Kai let out a soft breath of relief and dispersed his Space Divine Ability.

Just as the Jingkong Beast King had realized he was no easy prey, he had also sensed how difficult this fellow would be to handle.

He was at the critical juncture of refining the Star Domain, so why would he invite trouble now? The Jingkong Beast King's retreat was the best possible result. If they had fought, never mind whether he could kill the Jingkong Beast King, his attempt to refine the Star Domain would most likely have gone up in smoke.

"If you have any other tricks, use them all!" Yang Kai laughed loudly, one hand on his waist as he pointed a halberd-like finger at Heaven and Earth.

The hostility that had been shrouding the surroundings slowly faded. At the same time, Yang Kai discovered that the conflict among the countless Stellar Origins inside him had also eased considerably.

The discovery left him unable to hide his delight. It seemed... after everything that had happened, the will of this part of Heaven and Earth was beginning to compromise and acknowledge him.

Nothing could be better. Yang Kai knew when to be sensible, so naturally he would not do anything else to provoke that will of Heaven and Earth. Without further hesitation, he closed his eyes and began refining the Stellar Origins.

Time flowed slowly by. Yang Kai's body shrank day by day, while less and less light shone from his skin. This was clearly a sign that the refining was progressing well.

At last, the final trace of light withdrew, and Yang Kai returned to his original appearance.

His eyes snapped open. A hint of weariness lingered in his expression, but his spirits were soaring.

The refining was not yet complete. Only the final step remained.

He spread both arms and shouted, "Go home! All of you, go home!"

Countless rays of light suddenly flowed out from all over his body, transforming into clusters of Stellar Origin Power. They circled him several times, then vanished like flashes of lightning.

Over the years, he had refined Stellar Origins everywhere in order to turn the entire Star Domain into puzzle pieces, making his final refining easier. Now, he was releasing those Stellar Origins for that same final step.

Suppression alone could not accomplish this. Only true acknowledgment could bring their hearts and intentions into accord.

Countless Origins scattered in every direction and vanished without a trace in the blink of an eye. Centered on Yang Kai, it was as if a meteor shower had swept through the Star Domain.

Only the final Stellar Origin remained, circling him again and again, filled with attachment and dependence.

The Stellar Origin of Dark Star!

It did not immediately leave like the other Origins. Instead, it stayed behind, as though it had thoughts and ideas of its own.

Yang Kai smiled faintly and reached out to hold it up. The Origin shifted and wriggled in his palm, transforming into myriad forms.

"Go on," Yang Kai said softly. Only then did Dark Star's Origin turn into a streak of light and fly toward Dark Star.

"Come!" Yang Kai shouted in his heart, then closed his eyes and immersed his Divine Sense in his Sea of Consciousness.

The Seven-Colored Treasure Isle turned back into the Warm Spirit Lotus and released its suppression of the Star Chart. The Star Chart slowly rose from within his Sea of Consciousness, weaving and shuttling together with Yang Kai's Divine Sense until the two could no longer be distinguished.

With a rumbling boom, Yang Kai felt his entire body shake, as if something had exploded. Looking out, he saw the Star Chart once more hanging high in the sky of his Sea of Consciousness. When he raised his head, the heavens were filled with twinkling stars.

However, things were very different now from back then. Although the Star Chart had been settled in Yang Kai's Sea of Consciousness and could be of some use to him, it had not truly belonged to him.

Now, however, the Star Chart was his. Every inch and every point of it carried his aura.

"So that was it!"

The instant he completely refined the Star Chart, a new layer of understanding suddenly appeared in Yang Kai's mind.

Only now did he understand how the Great Wilderness Star Domain had been able to invade Hengluo Star Domain. The reason was actually connected to him!

When he had gone to the Star Realm years ago, he had taken the Star Chart with him, which meant he had also taken away the Origin Power of this Star Domain. Without its Origin, the Star Domain barrier, once as solid as an impregnable fortress, had developed a slight flaw.

That almost negligible flaw was precisely what formed the passage connecting the Great Wilderness Star Domain and Hengluo Star Domain, allowing the cultivators of the Great Wilderness Star Domain to invade this place.

Yang Kai scratched his head, feeling rather embarrassed.

The invasion by the Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators had killed many people and brought disaster to many Cultivation Stars. Yang Kai never would have imagined that he was partly to blame. But things had already come to this, so all he could do was feel embarrassed.

Gathering his thoughts, Yang Kai's mind surged. He wanted to experience what it felt like to become the Master of the Star Domain.

It felt somewhat similar to becoming the Master of Dark Star. With a single thought, everything came under his control, except that this range had expanded by a thousandfold, ten thousandfold.

His mind radiated outward in every direction, extending farther and farther, and Yang Kai could perceive everything wherever he directed his attention.

Very soon, he saw Tongxuan Continent.

After all these years, Tongxuan Continent had indeed recovered quite a bit. The suffering and devastation from the early years had left no trace. With the one hundred and eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array enveloping it, and Xia Ningshang personally keeping watch, ten years had been enough for Tongxuan Continent to recover its vitality.

However... this was still far from enough.

Tongxuan Continent's foundation had always been weaker than that of other Cultivation Stars, and it could not produce truly powerful cultivators. Yang Kai had been so eager to refine the Star Domain Origin and become the Master of the Star Domain partly because he needed it himself, and partly for Xia Ningshang and Tongxuan Continent.

Only by becoming the Master of the Star Domain could he borrow the power of the entire Star Domain to bring enormous benefits to Tongxuan Continent and help Xia Ningshang rise along with it.

Now it was time to test it.

Yang Kai stood where he was, merely waving both large hands.

On Tongxuan Continent, inside Nine Heavens Sacred Land.

Xia Ningshang was speaking with Xue Yue. Ever since the war ended three years ago, the entire Star Domain had returned to normal, and they no longer had to worry about invasion by powerful enemies. Hengluo Commerce Guild and Sword Alliance were both rebuilding. As for Purple Star... this Star Domain no longer had a Purple Star. Among the three great forces, Lingxiao Sect had replaced it and become the foremost power!

Xia Ningshang's aura had already returned to normal, with none of her former weakness remaining. Her skin was fair and rosy, her aura restrained; clearly, she had already reached the peak of the Third Stage of the Void King Realm. Xue Yue was no worse. Although she was not a Star Lord, she also had a special physique, and with enough resources, her cultivation progress naturally would not fall behind others.

Suddenly, Xia Ningshang raised her head and glanced outside, as if she had sensed something.

"What is it?" Xue Yue asked, puzzled.

"This is..." Xia Ningshang stood and, with a single step, arrived outside the hall. Xue Yue drifted over as well and came to a stop beside her.

Both women looked up at the sky and immediately froze.

Beneath the clear blue sky and bright sun, streaks of light crossed the heavens. Each one contained unimaginably vast energy as it fell like a meteor.

Xue Yue's face could not help paling. Even from such a great distance, she could feel the destructive power in those meteor-like things. If even one of them fell, Tongxuan Continent would suffer a heavy blow. But when she looked out, how could there be only one? The sky was densely packed with flowing light, simply too many to count.

They were not the only ones who saw it. At that moment, every living being on Tongxuan Continent was shaken by the sight.

Xia Ningshang's expression turned solemn. Just as she was about to activate her Stellar Origin to block them, she suddenly saw a huge cloud in the sky twist and shift, then transform into a clear human face.

"Ah!" Xia Ningshang cried softly.

"Husband!" Xue Yue was dumbfounded as well.

The human face formed from the changing cloud was undoubtedly Yang Kai's. Although they had no idea how far away he was from the two of them, it was as if he could see them. He smiled straight at them, then even blinked in an utterly infuriating manner.

That instant of hesitation had already cost them the chance to intercept.

Moreover, the moment she saw Yang Kai's face, Xia Ningshang knew this was his doing, and she no longer intended to stop it.

She was simply curious what Yang Kai intended to do. This momentum, enough to destroy Heaven and Earth, was no illusion.

The meteors struck the ground, yet there was none of the impact or destruction they had imagined. Instead, they silently vanished.

One streak of light fell straight toward Nine Heavens Sacred Land, filling everyone's vision with brilliance until they could see nothing else.

When the light scattered, the Spiritual Qi throughout Nine Heavens Sacred Land had clearly grown denser. Moreover... other things were quietly changing as well.

Xia Ningshang sensed it most keenly. As a Star Lord, she clearly felt Tongxuan Continent's World Laws gradually becoming more complete and more solid.

More complete laws meant stronger cultivators could be born, and greater power could be brought to bear.

The entire star radiated joy, allowing Xia Ningshang to feel a great delight as well.

"Ningshang, this is..." Seeing the smile covering her face, Xue Yue could not help asking.

"He succeeded!" Xia Ningshang replied.

Xue Yue suddenly understood, and an unusual light bloomed in her beautiful eyes. "He finally succeeded?"

He had left ten years ago and had sent no word since. Occasionally, they heard that someone had glimpsed Yang Kai somewhere in the Star Domain, but only in passing.

No one had gone looking for him. Everyone knew what he was busy doing.

They simply had not expected him to succeed in a mere ten years.

Tongxuan Continent's current changes were the best proof. Only by successfully refining the Star Domain and becoming the Master of the Star Domain would he be qualified to draw in the power of stars beyond the heavens and transform Tongxuan Continent in this way.

It could be said that this transformation was being carried out almost without regard for cost. Tongxuan Continent's elevation came at the price of drawing power from other places, but since it concerned Xia Ningshang, how could Yang Kai possibly be stingy?
