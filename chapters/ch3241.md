## Chapter 3241 — Conflict Outside the Mountain Gate

Along the way, Yang Kai finally discovered a side of Wen Zishan no one usually saw.

He was a chatterbox!

The man muttered and prattled on without end. Yang Kai had always thought him somewhat unreliable, but they had never spent much time together, so he had not known what Wen Zishan was truly like. This time, he had seen it for himself.

First, Wen Zishan pestered Gao Xueting for a while and naturally received nothing but cold shoulders for his trouble. In all of Qingyang Shrine, only Gao Xueting dared deny him face like that.

After getting nowhere with Gao Xueting, he slowed down and flew alongside Yang Kai and the others, talking with great animation about everything ancient and modern. Luo Chen and Xiao Baiyi were both rather aloof and rarely spoke in the first place. Out of respect for his position as Hall Master, they answered a few times, then closed their eyes as though conserving their strength. That left Yang Kai and Murong Xiaoxiao to suffer.

Fortunately, Wen Zishan did not pester them for too long. Half a day later, he fell back again and mixed with the Dao Origin Realm, Void King Realm, and Origin Returning Realm disciples, allowing Murong Xiaoxiao to quietly breathe a sigh of relief.

The disciples at the rear were quite willing to give him face. More importantly, Wen Zishan, the lord of an entire hall, was putting on no airs at all, so how could they, mere disciples, fail to cooperate? Wen Zishan even clapped a few of them on the shoulder and praised them as "the future pillars of the Shrine; the Shrine will depend on you from now on." Those disciples practically shone, so excited they could hardly contain themselves. They wished they could bare their hearts to Wen Zishan then and there so he would know their loyalty to the Shrine could be witnessed by Heaven and Earth and sworn before the sun and moon.

Yang Kai glanced back from time to time, the expression on his face wonderfully complicated.

He had always thought that he, as Lingxiao Palace’s Palace Master, was already rather unreliable, but beside Wen Zishan, he was merely a small fry before a true master.

They sped along the whole way, passing several cities and transferring through Space Arrays.

By evening, the group reached the place where Wuhua Hall was located.

Majestic mountains rose and fell in an unbroken chain, like a giant dragon crouching upon the earth. Beautiful scenery filled the range, and its atmosphere changed with endless variety.

Wuhua Hall was, after all, one of the Southern Territory's top Sects, so it naturally occupied a blessed land. The environment here was in no way inferior to Qingyang Shrine's and even possessed an added grandeur. From afar, clouds and mist steamed between the peaks, and the afterglow of the setting sun dyed the world gold.

Before Wuhua Hall's mountain gate, crowds surged and the place was extraordinarily lively. Clearly, with the Martial Gathering drawing near, many people had already arrived. The Martial Gathering was a grand event for the entire Southern Territory. Leaving aside the generous rewards that made people's eyes burn with envy, anyone who distinguished himself at the Martial Gathering would surely receive attention from his elders afterward. Many martial artists of humble birth had also come, hoping to fight for a future there.

There was precedent for this. Although martial artists of humble background could not take part in the group competition, most who performed well in the individual competition could successfully join one of the great Sects.

For the great Sects, rank and face mattered most, while the generous rewards were secondary. But for small Sects and families, those rewards were truly tempting, and with a chance to leap through the dragon gate before them, who would willingly miss it?

For that reason, every Southern Territory Martial Gathering was packed with excitement and filled with countless participants.

The Martial Gathering would begin tomorrow. Qingyang Shrine had timed its arrival carefully. As one of the top Sects, it naturally had to maintain a little bearing. Wen Zishan might not be very reliable, but he still understood what mattered in situations like this.

Today, Wuhua Hall had not activated its Grand Guardian Array, but many people were outside welcoming guests.

The Qingyang Shrine party had barely landed when an older man dressed entirely in black immediately came forward to greet them. Beside him followed a refined, scholarly young man. Both had Emperor Realm cultivation. The older man was at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, while the young man was at the First Stage.

"Hall Master Wen's arrival brings splendor to our humble Sect. Please forgive us for failing to welcome you from afar." The older man approached with a genial smile, cupped his fists, and exchanged courtesies.

Wen Zishan also put on the bearing of a Hall Master, as if the chatterbox on the road had not been him at all. With a faint smile, he said, "Elder Feng is too polite. Is Elder Feng in charge of receiving guests this time?"

The older man was none other than Feng Ming, an Elder of Wuhua Hall. Yang Kai had seen him more than once, so he was not exactly a stranger. Feng Ming had also cooperated with Gao Xueting many times. Back in the Four Seasons Land, during the Demonic Qi invasion outside Fenglin City, and when the Demonic Intent revived in the Southern Marsh, he had appeared each time.

However, Yang Kai and Feng Ming were not of the same generation and had little personal interaction. The young man behind him, though, was someone Yang Kai had dealt with before.

That young man had clearly noticed Yang Kai as well. His eyes brightened, and he cupped his fists. "Brother Yang, you came too."

Yang Kai returned the salute. "Brother Zhuang."

This young man was none other than Wuhua Hall's rising star, Zhuang Bufan. He had appeared in both the Four Seasons Land and within the Shattered Star Sea. He, Yang Kai, Xiao Baiyi, and Murong Xiaoxiao were all of the same generation, so they naturally knew one another. Now that they had gathered in one place, of course they had to chat a little.

As a result, Luo Chen was left with no one paying attention to him. However, he was aloof by nature and did not find it particularly awkward, simply standing there without a word.

As they were talking, a series of sharp hisses suddenly came from the distance and rapidly drew closer. Everyone turned and saw an enormous longsword racing in from the edge of the sky. Its terrifying Sword Intent seemed capable of wounding people from afar, so no one dared underestimate it.

Feng Ming smiled faintly. "Saint Lord Ma has arrived."

Wen Zishan curled his lip in distaste. "He really knows how to time things." He seemed to feel as if his side had lost out by arriving early.

For the Southern Territory Martial Gathering, Star God Palace would send people to observe. Since Wen Zishan had personally led a group here to give them ample face, Tianwu Sacred Land, another top force of the Southern Territory, naturally could not fail to show proper regard. Its Saint Lord, Ma Qing, had also personally led a group here.

They were all old acquaintances, so the moment Wen Zishan saw that sword light, he knew Ma Qing had arrived.

Sure enough, the sword light flashed to a stop nearby. When the radiance dispersed, several dozen people appeared. At their head stood an old man with white hair and a youthful face. His frame was lean and dry, but his eyes were sharp as swords, his posture straight, and his whole person gave off an unstoppable edge.

So this was Ma Qing? Yang Kai could not help studying him curiously. This was his first time seeing the Saint Lord of Tianwu Sacred Land, and he had never expected the man to be an old fellow. By comparison, Wen Zishan was much more pleasing to the eye.

Feng Ming excused himself and went forward to greet them, exchanging courtesies for a while.

Ma Qing and the others cupped their fists in return. The people Tianwu Sacred Land had brought this time were about the same as Qingyang Shrine's: four or five Emperor Realm Elders leading several dozen disciples of various levels from their Sect. Clearly, all of them were elites among elites.

"Brother Wen." Ma Qing kept one hand behind his back and stroked the beard on his chin with the other, nodding faintly to Wen Zishan.

Wen Zishan said with some distaste, "Brother Ma is very punctual." He seemed to be mocking the other man for arriving exactly on time.

Ma Qing's expression remained calm. "The disciples under me are rather troublesome, so we were delayed on the road."

Wen Zishan smiled. "If you knew they were troublesome, you should have set out earlier. It would be rude to keep Brother Lei waiting too long."

"Brother Wen seems to have only just arrived as well, no?"

"Chi..." Wen Zishan curled his lip. "I arrived half a day ago. I have merely been speaking with Elder Feng until now."

The two of them seemed to have some sort of grievance between them. The moment they met, neither found the other pleasing in the slightest.

It was not only the Hall Master and Saint Lord. The disciples under their respective Sects were the same.

Several dozen Qingyang Shrine disciples glared angrily at several dozen Tianwu Sacred Land disciples. Naturally, the other side refused to show weakness and glared right back, looking as if they were fire and water.

Yang Kai watched in wonder. Although he was Qingyang Shrine's Guest Elder, to be honest, he did not know much about Qingyang Shrine's affairs, nor did he know what grudge it had with Tianwu Sacred Land. The moment the disciples met, they had actually begun a silent clash.

The Hall Master and Saint Lord above were troublesome, the disciples below were no pushovers, and the Elders caught in the middle were no different.

Yang Kai turned his head and saw Murong Xiaoxiao and the others locking eyes with Tianwu Sacred Land's four Emperor Realm masters. Lightning seemed to crackle in the air between them.

What the hell was going on? Yang Kai was utterly confused.

From among Tianwu Sacred Land's four Elders, a young man stepped out. He was wild and unruly, his eyes cold and sharp, as if a vicious beast lay dormant inside him and might erupt to wound someone at any moment. His eyes were also extremely strange: one deep blue, the other fiery red, making him look odd to the extreme.

Wuchang! He truly lived up to his name.

Among the Southern Territory's top Sects, every house in every generation had its rising star. Qingyang Shrine's rising star was Xia Sheng, Tianwu Sacred Land's was Wuchang, and Wuhua Hall's was Zhuang Bufan.

Yang Kai had dealt with Wuchang in the Four Seasons Land and knew how difficult this fellow was. He seemed to possess some special constitution, and his individual combat strength was terrifying. In the last Southern Territory Martial Gathering, Xia Sheng had lost to him and had to settle for second place in the individual battles. Although Qingyang Shrine recovered some face in the group battle, when people spoke of the foremost figure among the Southern Territory's younger generation, the first person they thought of was Wuchang, not Xia Sheng.

Of course, Lan Xun of Star God Palace was also exceptional, but Star God Palace did not send disciples to participate in the Martial Gathering, so there was no way to rank her.

Yang Kai privately wondered whether the two Sects' behavior had something to do with the last Southern Territory Martial Gathering. In the individual battles, Xia Sheng had been bested by the other side, leaving Qingyang Shrine rather unwilling to accept the result. In the group battle, Tianwu Sacred Land had then been beaten down by Qingyang Shrine, so Tianwu Sacred Land could not have felt too pleased either.

Wuchang swept his mismatched eyes over the crowd, then looked at Yang Kai. "Xia Sheng did not come?"

He even seemed a little disappointed.

Yang Kai said, "Senior Brother Xia is stationed at Linghu Palace and cannot get away."

"Coward!" Wuchang snorted coldly.

Xiao Baiyi's lowered eyes snapped open, and he fixed Wuchang with a sword-sharp stare. Murong Xiaoxiao also looked at Wuchang in anger. They had only just met, yet Wuchang had already started insulting people, and the person he insulted was Xia Sheng, the leading figure representing Qingyang Shrine. Who on Qingyang Shrine's side could possibly swallow that?

Over there, Feng Ming glanced this way as if by chance, yet he showed no intention of stopping them and continued chatting and laughing with Wen Zishan as before. Wen Zishan also acted as if he had heard nothing. When those below clashed, it was not appropriate for him, the Hall Master, to step forward. Ma Qing was the same.

Although the Southern Territory's three great top Sects often cooperated, their competition was fierce as well. Each wanted to stand above the others. However, with Star God Palace presiding over them, no one dared completely tear away all face, so letting the people below make a little trouble was harmless enough...
