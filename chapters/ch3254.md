## Chapter 3254 — Deploying Troops and Dispatching Generals

Seeing this, Yang Kai and the Incarnation hesitated no longer. Both unleashed their Divine Abilities and bombarded Wuhua Hall's great array.

Amid the ancient, profound cadence of Dragon Language, one Dragon Clan Secret Technique after another transformed into Golden Dragons and surged forward. The flames around the Incarnation also blazed fiercely, turning the ground beneath it into a sea of fire that burned at the great array's light curtain.

For a time, ripples spread layer upon layer across the great array's light curtain, its radiance flashing wildly.

Lei Gu snorted coldly at the sight. An array flag appeared in his hand, and with a wave, he drove it to stabilize the array. Though Demonic Intent had corroded him, warping his temperament so greatly that he had almost become another person, he could still use everything Lei Gu himself had mastered with ease. This array flag was none other than the main flag of Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array. In his hands, the array immediately became as solid as a fortress.

Yang Kai's eyes nearly split with rage as he watched. If the array did not break, how could he enter Wuhua Hall? How could he save Wen Zishan and the others? How could he avenge the woman who had died? Anxiety burned through him. Was going to Star God Palace for help truly his only choice? But if the round trip delayed him by several days, who knew what would happen to Wen Zishan and the others? For all he knew, Demonic Qi might corrode them and reduce them to Demons.

Since Lei Gu had dared to act at this moment, he clearly had great confidence.

Yang Kai turned his head and saw the Incarnation wreathed in roaring flames, still burning at the great array. Yet from the look of it, breaking through would take far more than a short while. Even if he helped, it would not be enough.

Lei Gu spoke coldly again, "Junior, since you ruined my plans, I will make you beg for life and be denied death."

A flaw had already appeared in this plan. Yang Kai had not died, which meant the news here would definitely spread. In other words, the plan had failed. Fortunately, it had not failed completely. So many Emperor Realm Masters and tens of thousands of cultivators had still fallen into the trap, which was better than nothing.

If all those people were turned into Demons, they would become a force that absolutely could not be underestimated, and they could open the way for the great plan to come.

Suddenly, Yang Kai stopped attacking and drew back. He stared coldly at Lei Gu, circulated his power, and shouted, "Hall Master, can you hold on for one day?"

Lei Gu frowned when he heard this, unable to tell what Yang Kai meant by setting a deadline of one day. Judging by the present situation, the boy's best option was to seek help from Star God Palace, but Star God Palace was far away. How could he possibly return in a single day? Even with his mastery of Space Divine Abilities, that was impossible.

Wen Zishan's voice came from within Wuhua Hall, "We will do our utmost!"

Another voice followed, "Boy, why are you still wasting time? Hurry to Star God Palace!" It was Ma Qing, Saint Lord of Tianwu Sacred Land. He sounded flustered and exasperated, as if he had not expected Yang Kai to still be delaying here instead of leaving.

Yang Kai fixed Lei Gu with a deep stare and shouted, "Good. Everyone, hold on. In one day, I will definitely rescue you all from this sea of suffering!"

With that, he turned and left. Out of the corner of his eye, he glanced at the cold corpse on the earthen slope, and an inexplicable pain gripped his heart as if a hand had clenched around it.

A few flickers later, he had vanished from Lei Gu's sight. Once he was gone, Lei Gu also stopped urging the array flag, and the flashing light of the Grand Guardian Array gradually settled. He turned to glance at the Incarnation. "Divine Spirit Shi Huo?"

The Incarnation stood tall and imposing, looking down at him through the great array. Its eyes were like burning flames, flickering without rest. It shared a mental connection with Yang Kai, so it needed no lengthy exchange to understand his plan. At the same time, Yu Zhuo's death had filled it with anger and guilt. It remained here partly to tie down Lei Gu's attention, preventing him from devoting himself fully to dealing with Wen Zishan and the others, and partly to look for a chance to damage the great array. If it could break the array before Yang Kai returned, that would naturally be best. Even if it could not, it could still wear down some of the great array's power and prepare for the plan to come.

Seeing that the Incarnation did not speak, Lei Gu said coldly, "Divine Spirits are so noble. Since when has one fallen so low as to be commanded by a human? You have utterly disgraced the name of Divine Spirits."

If the Incarnation had truly been Shi Huo himself, perhaps those words would have enraged it. But the Incarnation was Yang Kai's extension, after all, so how could it possibly care about such an attempt to sow discord?

Seeing no reaction from it, Lei Gu frowned, gave up on the useless effort, and turned to fly deeper inside.

Before he could leave, however, the Incarnation urged its flames again. They climbed up the great array's light curtain and burned until firelight surged into the sky.

Lei Gu's eye twitched. He waved the array flag in his hand, and a layer of dark light rippled across the light curtain, cutting off the force of the flames.

...

Elsewhere, Yang Kai shuttled through the Void. In only a short while, he returned to the mountain peak where he had been ambushed earlier. The white-haired old man was meditating to regulate his breathing, and the four Demonic Qi-wreathed people beside him remained unconscious.

Sensing movement, the old man started in fright, but when he opened his eyes and saw Yang Kai had returned, he was overjoyed. "Elder Yang."

"Go to Star God Palace for help at once. Report what happened at Wuhua Hall and ask the Great Emperor to act. Otherwise, the Southern Territory is in danger," Yang Kai said urgently.

"Ah..." The white-haired old man froze for a moment, then nodded hard. "Good." He pointed at the four people. "Then they..."

"Go!" Yang Kai turned and glared at him, like an enraged fierce beast ready to devour someone.

Faced with that terrifying gaze, how could the old man dare say anything more? He also realized that time was precious, so he hurriedly summoned his flying Secret Treasure, fixed his direction, and sped toward Star God Palace.

Only after he left did Yang Kai wave his hand and take the four unconscious people who had entered the Demonic State into the Small Xuan Realm, suppressing them somewhere within it. Immediately afterward, he scattered countless materials with another wave and set to work right there on the mountain peak.

Sending word to Star God Palace and asking the Great Emperor to act was certainly one way out, but Yang Kai was not optimistic. It was not that he doubted the Great Emperor's strength; it would simply take too much time. By the time the Great Emperor arrived, he feared everything here would already be settled.

If he wanted to resolve this calamity and avenge the woman who had died, he had to rely on something else.

Yang Kai had his own plan.

The image of that cold corpse lying across the earthen slope kept flashing through his mind. Those dim, lifeless eyes seemed carved into the depths of his soul, forever staring at him, tormenting him with every breath.

Strictly speaking, they had known each other for less than a month, and their connection had not been especially deep. Fighting and killing were common on the path of the Martial Dao. One person's death should not have shaken him so badly, yet his self-reproach was endless. That turn and wave goodbye had unexpectedly become an eternal farewell.

The power of the Space Array surged without rest. The many materials gradually fused atop the mountain peak, forming the rough outline of an array, and as time passed, the array slowly grew complete.

In less than half a day, a brand-new Space Array had already taken shape.

With no time for careful inspection, Yang Kai flashed onto it, looked once toward Wuhua Hall, and urged the array's power. Light flashed, and he vanished.

Northern Territory, Lingxiao Palace.

Yang Kai appeared atop the Space Array, startling the person guarding it.

Bian Yuqing was still here guarding the great array today. When she saw Yang Kai, she immediately rose and cupped her fists. "Palace Master!" Her eyes suddenly fixed on Yang Kai's waist, and she said in shock, "Palace Master, you are injured?"

Although the wound from the black shadow's sneak attack was no longer serious, the bloodstains remained, so naturally they could not escape Bian Yuqing's notice.

"Where is the Chief Steward?" Yang Kai turned his head and asked.

Bian Yuqing froze for a moment. Yang Kai felt different today. His aura was fierce and threatening, like a volcano on the verge of eruption, radiating an extreme danger that made her heart pound. She did not know who had injured him, or who had provoked him.

This was Bian Yuqing's first time seeing Yang Kai in such a state, so she hurriedly replied, "The Chief Steward just returned yesterday and should be resting."

"Call her over."

"Yes." Bian Yuqing hurriedly took out a Transmission Compass and poured her Divine Sense into it. A moment later, she raised her head. "Palace Master, please wait a moment. The Chief Steward will be here shortly."

Barely a dozen breaths passed before Hua Qingsi hurried in from outside. She gave Bian Yuqing a suspicious glance. When Bian Yuqing had transmitted to her just now, she had told her to hurry to the transport area in such an urgent tone that Hua Qingsi had not dared delay and had rushed over without stopping.

Seeing Yang Kai standing there with a dark expression, she was stunned.

Before she could speak, she heard Yang Kai ask, "How many Emperor Realm Masters can we mobilize right now?"

Hua Qingsi blinked. "Palace Master, do you mean the entire Northern Territory, or just my Lingxiao Palace?"

"The entire Northern Territory!"

"Then may I ask what Palace Master needs them for?"

"To kill, take revenge, and exterminate a Sect!" Yang Kai forced the words coldly through clenched teeth.

Hua Qingsi was startled and realized that something major must have happened. Otherwise, Yang Kai would never be so thick with murderous intent. She answered solemnly, "If there is enough time, three or four hundred will be no problem."

During this period, Lingxiao Palace had been leading the other three Sects in integrating the great forces of the Northern Territory. Although not much time had passed, many Sects had already chosen to submit. There was no helping it. With the four top Sects joining hands, who in the Northern Territory could resist? Since they had submitted, whenever Lingxiao Palace issued an order, they had to obey whether they were willing or not.

"Good. I give you half a day. Mobilize every Emperor Realm Master you can and gather them here. Half a day from now, I will come pick you up." Yang Kai turned and stepped onto the Space Array again. After a brief pause, he said darkly, "Tell them that if anyone dares make excuses not to come, or refuses to cooperate, once I have time, I will exterminate his entire family!"

"Yes!" Hua Qingsi's expression changed, and she still wanted to ask more, but Yang Kai had already vanished from the Space Array.

Hua Qingsi and Bian Yuqing looked at each other, both completely at a loss. Hua Qingsi asked, "What happened to Palace Master?"

Bian Yuqing shook her head. "I don't know. He did not say anything."

Hua Qingsi frowned slightly, vaguely feeling that Yang Kai must have run into trouble. But what kind of trouble could require mobilizing so many Emperor Realm Masters to help? She had also seen the bloodstains on Yang Kai's body. Although she did not know how serious his injury was, the fact that someone of Yang Kai's ability had been injured showed just how powerful the enemy was this time.

Not daring to neglect the matter, she and Bian Yuqing briefly discussed things, then each took out a Transmission Compass and sent out messages. One after another, they stepped onto the Space Array and began summoning experts from across the Northern Territory.
