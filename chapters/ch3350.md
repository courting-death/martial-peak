## Chapter 3350 — Perfectly Justified

For a moment, every member of the Dragon Clan turned to Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun, wondering what exactly was going on.

Fu Zhun was so completely absorbed in Yang Xiao that she had eyes for nothing else. How could she possibly notice everyone staring? Her face was pale and weary, yet her eyes brimmed with happiness, all because Yang Xiao had just told Zhu Qing that she was his mother! For the first time, her habitually icy gaze had softened, and her face was filled with yearning. It was almost impossible to believe that this woman was the Dragon Clan's Second Elder.

Zhu Yan sighed softly. “That's right. This truly is Lin'er. Back then, this old man did not place Lin'er's dragon egg in the Dragon Tomb. I hid him elsewhere.”

Fu Zhun had actually been the one to do it, but the Great Elder claimed sole responsibility. After all, if the matter were investigated, they would be found to have broken the clan's rules, and even an Elder would face punishment.

Fu Xuan looked startled. “In that case, this child really is your offspring?”

Zhu Yan gave a slight nod.

The gathered Dragons were stunned, but their surprise soon gave way to delight. This little fatty was actually the child of the Great Elder and Second Elder! It had been so many years since a new member was born to the Dragon Clan that this alone was cause for tremendous celebration. Better still, the newborn was the offspring of two Tenth-Tier Great Dragons.

Fu Xuan asked curiously, “But Yang Kai, how did you manage to hatch him?” She had examined that dragon egg herself back then and concluded that it had no hope of hatching. Who would have thought it would hatch successfully after so many years?

Yang Kai shrugged. “I'm not sure.”

He truly had no idea. He had not even realized Yang Xiao was being born and had only pieced everything together afterward.

“Lin'er, come here and let Mother look at you.” Fu Zhun's voice was impossibly gentle, leaving the entire Dragon Clan struggling to adjust.

Yang Xiao hid behind Zhu Qing, squirming awkwardly as though he had a bad case of hemorrhoids. He looked uncomfortable beyond belief.

Fu Zhun's gentle manner never wavered. She smiled and beckoned again. “Come here.”

Yang Xiao fidgeted anxiously and shot Yang Kai a pleading look.

“You little brat, get over here!” Yang Kai beckoned to him as well.

Yang Xiao shrank back and said timidly, “Before I do, promise you won't hit me when I get there.”

“Hm? You dare set terms with me?” Yang Kai glared at him.

Yang Xiao's shoulders slumped. Resigned to his fate, he obediently walked over to Yang Kai.

Fu Zhun swayed again at the sight, grief written across her face. He had ignored his own mother's call, yet hurried over the moment an outsider summoned him. The contrast made her feel like an utter failure as a mother.

Zhu Yan did not look pleased either. It seemed as if Yang Kai were deliberately humiliating Fu Zhun.

“What did you want, Father?” Yang Xiao hung his little head as if he had committed some unforgivable crime, his voice weak and dispirited.

Yang Kai grinned and said warmly, “Nothing much, but you can't keep running around naked. Put some clothes on first. You may not be embarrassed, but I am.”

As he spoke, he dismissed the Dragon Transformation Art and returned to his normal size, then took a set of clothes from his Spatial Ring and dressed Yang Xiao in them.

The clothes were a little large, but they barely fit.

Yang Xiao flexed his arms and kicked his legs, then immediately complained, “Why are these girl's clothes?”

“Be glad you have anything to wear! Why are you being so picky?” Yang Kai raised a hand to rap him on the head, but stopped halfway when he felt two sword-sharp gazes bearing down on him. He looked up to find the Great Elder and Second Elder glaring at him like bitter enemies. With an awkward smile, he lowered his hand and pretended he had only meant to straighten Yang Xiao's collar.

Yang Xiao asked curiously, “Father, why do you have clothes for a little girl?”

“I prepared them for a little friend,” Yang Kai explained casually. The clothes belonged to Liu Yan. Although she looked seven or eight years old, girls were naturally smaller, so the outfit fit Yang Xiao reasonably well—certainly much better than an adult's clothes would have.

Yang Xiao's eyes lit up. “Do I have an older brother or sister?”

Yang Kai coughed lightly. “Not yet.” He stole a glance at Zhu Qing and grinned. “But you will.”

Yang Xiao clapped his hands and laughed. “Then they'll be my younger brothers and sisters. Don't worry, Father, I'll take good care of them.”

“That's what I like to hear.” Yang Kai smoothed the creases over Yang Xiao's chest, then turned him around to face Fu Zhun and Zhu Yan. Leaning close to his ear, he said, “Those two are your real father and mother. Their blood flows through your veins. Go on. Don't make them sad.”

He gave Yang Xiao a push, sending him stumbling forward. Yang Xiao turned back and stared at him resentfully, clearly reluctant to go.

Yang Kai glared at him.

Yang Xiao wilted at once. With everyone watching, he shuffled over to Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun one slow step at a time, his eyes darting everywhere.

The Dragon Clan's two Elders stared unblinkingly at the little figure before them. Zhu Yan fared better; as a man, he could still contain his excitement. Fu Zhun, however, was trembling slightly from head to toe. She raised one hand as if to touch Yang Xiao, only to hesitate for fear of making him dislike her. She seemed torn between reaching out and holding back, but the tenderness on her face could almost have melted steel. There was no trace of the Dragon Clan's stern Second Elder in her now.

Truthfully, seeing Yang Xiao be so close to Yang Kai had filled her with envy and jealousy. She had even begun to resent Yang Kai. If he had not secretly done something, why would her Lin'er call her a crazy woman the moment they met? Why would he refuse to acknowledge his own parents when they stood before him?

Now, however, she felt somewhat grateful to Yang Kai. Without that push, Lin'er probably would not have come over at all. They had barely interacted, yet Fu Zhun could already sense his instinctive aversion to her. He seemed reluctant to let her near him.

The sight made Zhu Qing, Fu Xuan, and the others sigh inwardly. They all held their breath and waited to see what would happen.

With a light cough, Yang Xiao sniffed, lowered his head, and said softly, “Your son pays his respects to Father and Mother.”

“Good, good, good!” Zhu Yan finally released the breath he had been holding and nodded over and over, his eyes glistening with tears.

Fu Zhun let out a heartrending cry. “Lin'er!”

She bent down, pulled Yang Xiao into her embrace, and held him tightly, as though afraid she would lose him forever if she let go.

Fu Zhun squeezed Yang Xiao so tightly that he could barely breathe, and his eyes nearly popped out. He instinctively felt uncomfortable, even a little repulsed by the woman holding him. Yet when he caught her fragrance, his agitation faded and an indescribable sense of peace settled over him.

These conflicting feelings bewildered him. He stood motionless in Fu Zhun's embrace, staring blankly ahead.

“Second Elder has finally gotten her wish.” Zhu Qing's eyes were slightly red as she leaned against Yang Kai's shoulder and turned toward him, her fragrant breath brushing his cheek. “You've finally done something truly good.”

Yang Kai rolled his eyes. “What's that supposed to mean? Are you saying I've never done anything good before?”

Zhu Qing pursed her lips in a smile. “As far as Dragon Isle is concerned, you really haven't.”

Yang Kai scoffed. He could not deny that Dragon Isle had suffered considerable losses on his account, but had it not gained even more from him? Those Dragon Blood Flowers alone were worth an enormous fortune.

“But how exactly did you hatch him?” Zhu Qing asked, looking at him curiously.

Yang Kai shook his head. “I really can't explain it.”

Zhu Qing said, “That dragon egg was missing part of its Origin back then, so it had no hope of hatching. Yet Yang Xiao's Dragon Aura is perfectly pure, and there is clearly nothing wrong with his Origin. Could you have restored what was missing from his Origin?”

“I can do that?” Yang Kai was astonished. How could anyone restore an Origin? Had a piece of his Golden Sacred Dragon Origin somehow split off? But he had suffered no loss himself.

“By the way, what Attribute does he have?” Zhu Qing asked.

Yang Kai spread his hands. “How would I know? All I know is that he's a little White Dragon.”

“A White Dragon.” Understanding dawned on Zhu Qing. “Then he's probably an Ice Dragon. The Second Elder is one too.”

“Whatever his Attribute, the child of two Tenth-Tier Great Dragons is bound to have a bright future.” Yang Kai grinned, marveling inwardly at how fortunate this brat Yang Xiao was. He had been born with advantages others could only dream of: two parents comparable to Great Emperors and the blood of the Dragon Clan. Few people in this world would dare provoke him.

Zhu Qing beamed at him. “Do you really intend to take him as your son?”

Yang Kai said, “Why not? I hatched him, so it's only right that he calls me Father.” It had felt a little strange at first, but after hearing Yang Xiao call him Father several times, it had begun to seem perfectly natural.

Zhu Qing said, “But what does that do to our seniority...? I may be a Dragon Clan Elder too, but I'm still a generation below the Great Elder and Second Elder. If Yang Xiao calls you Father, doesn't that make you a brother to the Great Elder? What am I supposed to call you, then? Uncle?”

Yang Kai sucked in a sharp breath and gave her a sidelong look. “Careful what you call me, young lady. Certain names can really get a man's blood pumping, you know?”

Zhu Qing spat. “You can never behave yourself.”

As the two spoke, Fu Zhun continued to hold Yang Xiao, tears streaming down her face until they nearly soaked through his clothes.

The little brat seemed to know what to do. He reached out and gently patted Fu Zhun's back, comforting her without a word. At the steady rhythm of those small pats, Fu Zhun felt she could die then and there without regret.

Zhu Yan watched with a heart full of contentment. The last trace of displeasure he had felt over Yang Kai stealing the dragon egg had long since vanished.

Come to think of it, Lin'er might never have been born if Yang Kai had not stolen the dragon egg. He and Fu Zhun owed Yang Kai an enormous debt for that. Zhu Yan had originally objected somewhat to the name Yang Xiao, but on second thought, it was a fine name. The ambition to soar into the heavens was a worthy one, and in the end, it was only a name. What harm could it do?

Only after a long while did Fu Zhun release Yang Xiao, and even then she kept both hands clasped around his arms. She studied him endlessly through tear-reddened eyes, unable to get her fill of the sight. Her eyes were red and swollen, but her face was all smiles.
