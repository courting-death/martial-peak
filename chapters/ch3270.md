## Chapter 3270 - Does It Hurt?

Wuhua Hall had once been one of the Southern Territory's three top forces, but now it lay in ruins. Broken walls and shattered buildings stood everywhere, the traces of the great battle plain to see.

The surviving cultivators had set up separate camps across the mountain peaks. Lingering fear covered every face, and whenever they thought back to what had happened a few days ago, a chill ran through them.

The group from the Northern Territory, on the other hand, tirelessly moved among the Emperor Realm cultivators of the Southern Territory, asking after them with concern. The Thirty-Two Yao Kings of the Ancient Land had claimed a Spirit Peak for themselves, and no one knew where they had found so much wine and food. They drank and made merry, their festive mood a stark contrast to the gloom hanging over the Southern Territory's side. To them, whether humans lived or died was not worth caring about. Had they not followed Yang Kai here, they never would have gotten involved in the Southern Territory's affairs.

The Southern Territory side was just as happy with this arrangement. After all, none of those Yao Kings were easy to provoke, and the yao race was eccentric and violent by nature. If they really mixed in with the humans, who knew what kind of conflict might break out. There was no one here who could restrain them anyway, so if they wanted to drink and amuse themselves, then let them drink and amuse themselves.

Several days passed in a flash. Murong Xiaoxiao hovered quietly in the air, keeping watch over their surroundings, when her expression suddenly sharpened. She peered carefully in one direction, then, after a moment, called down in delight, "They're back!"

Shua...

Wen Zishan abruptly appeared beside her and looked that way, tense with worry. Several figures were flying over at high speed. Yang Kai led the group, followed by the little girl of seven or eight and the three Divine Spirits from the Eastern Territory. After them... there was no one else.

Wen Zishan's face instantly went deathly pale, and his fists clenched tight.

Murong Xiaoxiao had clearly realized something too. Her pretty face changed slightly, and her eyes reddened.

"Hall Master!" Yang Kai flew up to Wen Zishan and cupped his fists, but when he saw that both Wen Zishan and Murong Xiaoxiao looked wrong, his heart lurched. "What happened?"

Wen Zishan drew a deep breath and asked in a low voice, "Where is Lei Gu?"

Yang Kai said, "I have not failed the mission. Lei Gu is dead!"

Wen Zishan nodded. "Good. You worked hard." He opened his mouth, as if he still wanted to ask something, but he did not dare voice it, afraid of hearing the answer he least wanted.

Murong Xiaoxiao asked in his place, her voice choked. "What about Senior Sister Gao?"

Only then did Yang Kai understand why the two of them looked so grave. They were worried about Gao Xueting's safety. His expression turned solemn, and he said in a low voice, "Senior Sister Gao, she..."

Murong Xiaoxiao suddenly covered her mouth. Tears as large as beans rolled down her cheeks, and in an instant she was sobbing uncontrollably.

Yang Kai was speechless. He had not even finished speaking, so why was she crying? He turned to glance at Wen Zishan and found him utterly grief-stricken, as if all hope had gone out of him. He even swayed a few times, as though he might fall at any moment.

"Senior Sister Gao suffered some injuries, but nothing serious." Yang Kai finished his sentence and, with a wave of his hand, released Gao Xueting from the Small Xuan Realm.

When Gao Xueting appeared, she clearly had not yet adjusted to the sudden transition. She hurriedly circulated her Emperor Yuan to steady herself, then looked up and asked in surprise, "Junior Sister, what happened to you?"

Murong Xiaoxiao's tears were still falling, but she stared blankly at Gao Xueting with wide eyes. Then she broke into a smile through her tears and cried sweetly, "Senior Sister Gao!"

Gao Xueting nodded slightly, then looked at Wen Zishan. Her dark brows drew together. "And what happened to you?"

Wen Zishan swallowed hard and walked toward Gao Xueting step by step, as if he could not trust his own eyes. Only when he was standing right in front of her did he stop and reach a hand toward her face.

A blush rose at once on Gao Xueting's icy face. She leaned back slightly, bit her red lip, and said, "What are you doing?"

"Don't move!" Wen Zishan barked. Gao Xueting stiffened, then obediently stayed still, letting his large hand settle against one side of her face. The faint tremble in his fingers unsettled her. Realizing that Wen Zishan seemed to be worried about her safety, she immediately grew shy, and warmth spread through her heart.

How many years had it been since she had seen him this nervous? The last time seemed to have been when she was a child and had secretly run out to play, only to nearly be bitten to death by a Yao Beast. For a moment, time seemed to turn back, and every little moment they had shared over several hundred years surfaced in her heart.

The next instant, Wen Zishan's hand suddenly tightened. He pinched Gao Xueting's cheek, lifted it slightly, widened his eyes, and asked nervously, "Does it hurt?"

Yang Kai, who had been watching the show with great interest, covered his face with one hand, unable to keep looking.

The blush vanished from Gao Xueting's face, and an icy light flashed in her eyes. A leg shot out from beneath her skirt and kicked Wen Zishan in the lower abdomen, sending him flying with a bang.

Gao Xueting's face was frosty as she snorted. "Does it hurt?"

Wen Zishan hunched over with one hand clutching his abdomen, yet he laughed with pure delight. "It hurts, it hurts, it hurts to death."

"What is wrong with you!" Gao Xueting rubbed her cheek, then turned to Murong Xiaoxiao. "I'm fine. I made you worry."

Murong Xiaoxiao immediately nodded again and again. A moment later, however, her expression darkened. "Senior Brother Luo... was killed."

Yang Kai's expression changed slightly. "Senior Brother Luo is dead?"

Murong Xiaoxiao nodded, and her tears started falling again. Although Yang Kai had already suspected as much, he still sighed when he heard the news. He had not dealt much with Luo Chen, but they were both people of Qingyang Shrine, so a fellow disciple's fate still stirred sorrow in him.

"What about Xiao Bai?" Yang Kai pressed. Although he was certain that the Xiao Bai he had killed before had been an illusion, he still did not know exactly what had happened to Xiao Baiyi.

Murong Xiaoxiao said, "Senior Brother Xiao suffered some injuries, but nothing serious."

Wen Zishan had also returned to his usual manner by now. He sighed softly and said, "This time, the Southern Territory really... suffered disastrous losses."

Yang Kai looked down and swept the area with his Divine Sense, quickly grasping the situation. The losses were indeed disastrous. Of several hundred Emperor Realm Masters, about a third had died, and of the tens of thousands of Elite Disciples, more than half were gone. After this battle, the Southern Territory could forget about recovering without at least five hundred years.

"What is going on over there?" Yang Kai suddenly asked, pointing toward the valley where the Martial Gathering had been held. Countless vines now wrapped around the valley. At a glance, the entire valley had almost become a sphere woven from vines, and within that sphere, streams of fearsome jet-black Demonic Qi kept spreading outward, turning even the vines black.

More than a hundred small Wood Spirits were positioned around the area under the Stone Puppets' protection, seemingly casting a spell.

Wen Zishan said, "It is the work of those Wood Spirits you brought. They seem to have a way to suppress the Demonic Qi, so we gathered everyone who fell into the Demonic State there." After saying this, he sighed again. "But this is no real solution. Their method cannot solve the problem at its root. Sooner or later, those people will be completely corroded by the Demonic Qi, and when that happens, there will be no saving them."

"I'll go take a look." With that, Yang Kai sped off in that direction.

Ma Qing and Xiao Yuyang were also standing guard over there. After all, no one knew whether the Wood Spirits' method would work. If those demonized cultivators suddenly rose up and attacked again, someone had to be able to subdue them.

The two of them had seen Yang Kai return long ago, but with their duty before them, they could not run over to ask questions. Now that Yang Kai had come to them, Ma Qing hurriedly asked, "How is it?"

"Lei Gu is dead."

Ma Qing and Xiao Yuyang both brightened at once. Clapping their hands, they said, "Good!"

If Lei Gu had escaped, who knew what kind of trouble he would cause in the future. Since Lei Gu had already been executed, the incident was essentially resolved. All that remained was dealing with these demonized cultivators.

This gave them quite a headache. There were many of these cultivators, more than ten thousand in all. If they had been killed in battle, that would have been one thing, but now these ten thousand or so demonized people had been captured. They could not simply kill them, but they could not release them either. It was a deeply vexing problem.

Among these more than ten thousand people were disciples from almost every Sect and faction. Who could bring themselves to make the decision to slaughter them all? Although the Wood Spirits' wondrous methods were suppressing the Demonic Qi, this still only treated the symptoms, not the root cause.

Xiao Yuyang said thoughtfully, "Elder Yang, do you have a way to restore these demonized people's minds?" He had only formed this guess because he had seen Gao Xueting return safe and sound with him. Gao Xueting had fallen into the Demonic State before as well.

Since she now looked no different from usual, that meant the Demonic Qi that had invaded her body had been expelled. As for who possessed such wondrous means, it was either Yang Kai or those three Divine Spirits. After witnessing Yang Kai's methods this time, Xiao Yuyang placed even more hope in Yang Kai than in the three Divine Spirits.

"I'll try." Yang Kai nodded. Even without Xiao Yuyang asking, he had intended to do this. Turning to Mu Na not far away, he said, "Patriarch, please withdraw the Secret Technique."

Mu Na nodded and worked together with the Wood Spirits to cast their spell. The sphere of vines wrapped around the valley suddenly shrank back, revealing the scene inside.

More than ten thousand demonized people stood there without moving. Yang Kai watched them for a while and thought that, if his guess was right, they had been demonized for too short a time and temporarily lacked the ability to move on their own unless driven by Demonic Intent.

That, however, made it easier for him to act.

With a flicker, he arrived in the air above the center of the valley, then waved his hand and tossed out the several Emperor Realm demonized people he had suppressed inside the Small Xuan Realm. These were the demonized people who had ambushed him on that mountain peak earlier, only to be knocked unconscious and captured. They had been suppressed in the Small Xuan Realm until now. Since he was going to expel the Demonic Qi, he naturally released them as well.

Once everything was ready, Yang Kai drew a soft breath and closed his eyes.

Under everyone's gaze, Yang Kai stood calm and solemn. Then, all at once, his eyes opened, and his entire aura changed in an inconceivable way, becoming ancient and desolate.

Xiao Yuyang and Ma Qing stared blankly, looking at each other in confusion, unable to tell what kind of wondrous method could be so strange.

A complex chant rang out. Yang Kai slowly pointed downward. A speck of light bloomed at his fingertip, and a halo suddenly rippled out, sweeping over the bodies of more than ten thousand cultivators.
