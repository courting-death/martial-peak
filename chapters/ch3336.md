## Chapter 3336 — I Will Do This

Yang Kai spent several wonderfully carefree days with blue sea, clear sky, and a beauty at his side, almost happy enough to forget all thought of leaving. By day, he wandered the mountains and waters with Zhu Qing, taking in Dragon Isle's scenery; by night, naturally, clouds gathered and rain fell, and he never tired of it.

Zhu Qing also knew such days could not last long, so unless something unexpected came up, she was seldom apart from Yang Kai.

But such days were destined not to last long.

After more than ten days of this, someone finally shattered their leisure.

At the edge of Crescent Island, Yang Kai lay on the beach with his head pillowed on his hands, watching the clouds gather and scatter overhead. Beside him, Qiong Qi sprawled with narrowed eyes, contentedly enjoying this unrestrained life. Man and mount seemed fixed in an eternal painting as the tide rose and fell and sea spray splashed along the shore.

"Old Qiong, this Young Master has decided. I am going to break into Dragon Temple," Yang Kai suddenly said.

Qiong Qi had been dozing with his eyes half closed. At those words, his eyes snapped open and he pushed himself upright to stare at Yang Kai, as if checking whether he had misheard.

"I probably cannot take Qing'er away, but I definitely have to make a trip to Dragon Temple. The Dragon Clan's Secret Techniques are tremendously powerful. Since I am already here, how could I let them slip by? What do you think of the idea?"

Qiong Qi shook his head like a rattle. "Bad idea. Bad idea."

"Oh? Why is it bad?" Yang Kai turned his head to look at him.

Qiong Qi said, "This is Dragon Isle, and Dragon Temple is the Dragon Clan's most sacred and secret place. Trespassing there without permission is forbidden. I fear you would not come back alive."

Yang Kai sneered. "It is not as if I have never been to Dragon Temple. Did Wu Kuang not barge in without permission last time too? To this day, he is still roaming free over in the Ancestral Domain."

"Wu Kuang is Wu Kuang, and you are you." Qiong Qi still looked wholly opposed. After a moment's thought, he said, "If you insist on going, that is fine too. Wait until this old man has left Dragon Isle first, then we can talk."

Yang Kai glanced sideways at him. "Old Qiong, you are quite loyal."

Qiong Qi said, "This old man merely wishes to keep himself useful, so that if the chance comes one day, I may avenge you, Young Master."

"Tsk..." Yang Kai gave a mocking laugh. "Not a bad idea. How thoughtful of you."

Qiong Qi pretended not to understand and continued, "In any case, this old man still advises you to give up the idea, Young Master. If possible, it would be best to ask the Dragon Clan to agree to let you enter Dragon Temple for a look."

Yang Kai snapped, "If the Dragon Clan would agree, this Young Master would not need to trespass. That vile wretch Fu Zhun clearly thinks she has me by the throat. She actually used Qing'er, my weak spot, to threaten me. Utterly hateful! Sooner or later, I will make her learn this Young Master's might."

Vile wretch? Qiong Qi could not help swallowing. He actually called Fu Zhun a vile wretch?

Only now did he realize that this newly appointed Young Master of his truly had no ordinary courage.

"Hm? Someone is coming!" Qiong Qi's expression shifted, and he raised his head to look in one direction.

Yang Kai sensed it as well. He glanced that way and immediately grinned. A streak of red light was racing straight toward Zhu Qing's Palace Chamber, but after detecting Yang Kai's Divine Sense aura, it veered in midair and flew toward them.

A moment later, a figure stood in the void, looking down at them from above.

Yang Kai remained where he was and raised a hand in greeting. "Hello, little brother-in-law."

Zhu Lie frowned at Yang Kai's sloppy, idle posture, thinking his elder sister must truly be blind. How had she taken a fancy to a man like this? Coolly, he asked, "What are you lying here for?"

"Sunbathing. What else?" Yang Kai answered as if it were only natural, beckoning as he spoke. "If you have something to say, come down and say it. Why stand so high?"

Zhu Lie's mouth twitched, but he obediently flew down all the same. When he landed beside Yang Kai, he turned to look at Qiong Qi and said in surprise, "You really took Qiong Qi as a mount?"

Yang Kai's stay on Dragon Isle had not been especially long, but it had not been brief either. There were only so many Dragon Clan members on the island, so the slightest disturbance naturally spread quickly. By now, every Dragon Clan member on Dragon Isle knew that he had been driven out of Ice Snow Island by the Second Elder that day, had nearly been killed after angering her, and also had a Qiong Qi mount.

Zhu Lie had been cultivating Secret Techniques these past few days, and after hearing the news, he hurried over to take a look.

He looked at Qiong Qi in astonishment and said, "You are a Divine Spirit as well. Do you not feel ashamed, lowering yourself like this?"

Qiong Qi blinked, but before he could speak, Yang Kai stood up, glared at Zhu Lie, and said, "Little brother-in-law, what do you mean? What grudge do you have against me that you start trying to turn my mount against me the moment you arrive?"

Zhu Lie frowned. "That is not what I meant. I only feel... ah, forget it. Everyone has their own ambitions." He shook his head, looking bitterly disappointed, as if Qiong Qi's willingness to serve as Yang Kai's mount brought shame on all Divine Spirits.

He turned back to Yang Kai. "Why are you not keeping Zhu Qing company? What are you doing here by yourself?"

Yang Kai snorted coldly. "Zhu Qing is entertaining three dragon women right now. How would I dare go over?"

There were only seven dragon women on Dragon Isle. Apart from Zhu Qing, Fu Zhun, and Fu Xuan, four remained, but Fu Ling was still guarding Dragon Isle's entrance and could not leave without permission. That left only three dragon women here: one from the Zhu line of the Dragon Clan, and the other two from the Fu line.

Over the past few days, those three dragon women had been visiting Zhu Qing with great diligence. They came almost as soon as the sky brightened and did not leave until dark. Their intentions needed no explanation.

Yang Kai had only met them once on the first day, then was immediately sent away by Zhu Qing under some excuse.

Speaking of which, those three dragon women had not left Yang Kai with a bad impression. They did not behave with Fu Ling's obvious seductiveness; when they looked at Yang Kai, they even seemed somewhat embarrassed and shy. Toward Zhu Qing, they were filled with guilt and unease.

Yang Kai strongly suspected that the Great Elder had sent the three of them over. Otherwise, even if he truly could be of enormous help to them, the Dragon Clan's pride would never let them go this far.

Fu Ling behaved that way mainly because she had come into contact with Yang Kai last time, so they were not really strangers to each other.

After hearing this explanation, Zhu Lie naturally understood. The corner of his eye twitched, and he looked at Yang Kai with deep bitterness.

Even Yang Kai's own little brother-in-law was like this, to say nothing of the other male Dragon Clan members. At this moment, they probably wanted nothing more than to flay Yang Kai and devour his flesh. If they did not know that Yang Kai's Golden Sacred Dragon Origin Power suppressed them too severely for them to be his match, they would likely have challenged him to a fight to the death long ago.

After a moment of silence, Zhu Lie said, "I heard the Second Elder gave you a condition?"

Yang Kai asked in surprise, "You know that too?"

Zhu Lie said, "Dragon Isle is only so large, and the Dragon Clan has only so many people. If anything happens, word spreads quickly. Did the Second Elder tell you to compensate the Dragon Clan for the losses you caused last time?"

Yang Kai nodded. "Something like that."

Zhu Lie said, "Then why not think of a way? It is rare for the Second Elder to relent. If you can fulfill her demand, naturally you can take Zhu Qing away from Dragon Isle. Do you want Zhu Qing to live apart from you in the future?"

Yang Kai sneered. "Why should I be the one to compensate Dragon Isle's losses? It has nothing to do with me!"

Zhu Lie enunciated three words, one at a time. "Dragon Blood Flowers!"

Yang Kai's expression twitched. "The Great Elder told you to come, right?"

He had only said it casually, but Zhu Lie's expression flickered. "Do not worry about who told me to come. In any case, as I see it, you have both the skill and the ability to satisfy the Second Elder's condition."

"You think I have the ability?" Yang Kai laughed despite himself. "Do you know how many Dragon Blood Flowers I picked at the time?"

"Two Supreme-Level, thirty Upper-Level, two hundred Middle-Level, and countless Lower-Level, almost a thousand in all!"

"You know quite a lot." Yang Kai was somewhat surprised. These numbers were not far off from his own previous calculations. He nodded and said, "Then do you know how many Dragon Blood Flowers I have left?"

Zhu Lie shook his head.

Yang Kai said, "The Supreme-Level, Upper-Level, and Middle-Level ones are all gone. Only Lower-Level ones remain. Even if I really return the remaining Dragon Blood Flowers, where am I supposed to get the rest? I cannot conjure Dragon Blood Flowers out of thin air, can I?"

Zhu Lie said unhurriedly, "Cultivating Dragon Blood Flowers mainly relies on watering them with dragon blood. The purer the dragon blood, the higher the quality of the flowers that grow. The Dragon Blood Flowers on Dragon Isle were basically all raised with dragon descendant blood. Not only was their quality poor, they also took a long time to mature. But if you are truly willing, you can use your own fresh blood to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers. Your bloodline... may have a miraculous effect on them."

Yang Kai sneered after hearing this, instinctively wanting to refute him, but after giving it some thought, it seemed... very reasonable. He stroked his chin and pondered for a long while before saying, "Do you really think... I can cultivate Middle-Level and Upper-Level Dragon Blood Flowers?"

Zhu Lie said, "How would you know without trying? The Second Elder has already made her position clear. If you truly can meet her demand, then whether you take Zhu Qing away or enter Dragon Temple, she will have nothing to say."

As soon as these words left his mouth, a scene immediately surfaced in Yang Kai's mind: himself strutting openly into Dragon Temple while that vile wretch Fu Zhun could only look on helplessly.

Delight surged through him. "Will she keep her word?"

Zhu Lie sighed. "The Second Elder has always treated her word as binding."

Yang Kai was sorely tempted. He paced back and forth on the beach, his expression shifting, hesitant and unable to decide. Only after quite a while did he clap his hands and say, "Good. I will do this. I refuse to believe I cannot cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers!"

Zhu Lie said, "Then allow me to wish you swift success in advance."

Yang Kai grinned. "All right, all right. Go back and tell the Great Elder that I will compensate Dragon Isle for the Dragon Blood Flowers. Tell him to set his mind at ease. What can I say? I am Dragon Isle's son-in-law."

Zhu Lie's mouth twitched. "I merely mentioned it in passing. This has nothing to do with the Great Elder."

Yang Kai glanced sideways at him. “You came all this way to tell me these things. Wasn’t it because Great Elder put you up to it?”

Zhu Lie still wanted to deny it, but in the end, he nodded and said, “Yes.”

Yang Kai had stolen the Dragon Blood Flowers clean. For the next thousand years, the Dragon Clan on Dragon Isle could forget about improving their bloodlines with Dragon Blood Flowers. There were only two ways to solve the problem: first, Yang Kai could cultivate enough Dragon Blood Flowers; second, Yang Kai could help those few dragon women rapidly improve their bloodlines.

In the end, Zhu Lie still hoped Yang Kai would choose the first option, which was why he had accepted the Great Elder's assignment and come over to persuade Yang Kai...
