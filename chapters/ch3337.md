## Chapter 3337 — Why Not

Once Yang Kai decided to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers, he wasted no time. When he later told Zhu Qing about it, she was delighted, but also a little worried.

She was delighted because if Yang Kai could truly meet the Second Elder's demand, Dragon Isle would have no further reason to trouble him. If he wanted to enter Dragon Temple, the Second Elder would have no grounds to stop him.

What worried her was whether Yang Kai's body could withstand it. Cultivating Dragon Blood Flowers naturally meant drawing a great deal of blood, and even though Yang Kai's cultivation was far from weak, the fresh blood needed for Dragon Blood Flowers to grow was no small amount.

She tried to persuade him, but Yang Kai had made up his mind. He was determined to make up for the loss of the Dragon Blood Flowers. When her persuasion failed, Zhu Qing could only let him be. That night, however, she did all she could to please him, completely setting aside the pride of a dragon woman and serving Yang Kai until he was comfortable from head to toe.

The next day, Yang Kai flew alone toward the Spirit Island where the Dragon Blood Flowers were cultivated. Zhu Qing had wanted to go with him, but Yang Kai stopped her.

After all, cultivating the Dragon Blood Flowers meant he would have to keep drawing blood from his own body. If Zhu Qing were watching from the side, who knew how heartbroken she would be? Yang Kai naturally would not bring her along. If the moment came and he lost the nerve to cut himself, that would be bad.

Yang Kai was not unfamiliar with the Spirit Island's location. After all, Fu Ling had led him there once before.

Fu Ling had been punished for her mistake, so the one guarding the Spirit Island now was that Sixth-Tier green dragon, Fu Qi!

He had been the one who went to Half-Dragon City last time and brought several hundred dragon descendants back to Dragon Isle. Back then, Fu Qi had been arrogant and imposing, but today he was waiting early at the edge of the island.

When he saw Yang Kai flying over, he hurried into the air to meet him and forced out a faint smile. "Brother Yang!"

Yang Kai looked him up and down, then said with a faint, ambiguous smile, "Brother Fu Qi, what are you doing here?"

Fu Qi said, "By the Elder's command, I am here to guard Dragon Blood Island and await Brother Yang's arrival." After a pause, he added, "I heard Brother Yang has decided to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers here? To restore Dragon Blood Island's spirit flowers to their former scale?"

Yang Kai said with a pained expression, "Mm, something like that."

He suddenly regretted it a little. He was the one who had stolen and harvested the Dragon Blood Flowers, yet now he had to restore them. Was this not lifting a stone only to drop it on his own foot? Stealing them had felt good in the moment, but why had he insisted on coming here to suffer for it?

But things had already come to this, and he had already made such bold claims. Backing out now would feel like slapping his own face. Whether it worked or not, he had to at least try. If it truly failed, then there was nothing to be done.

Fu Qi's spirits lifted. "Brother Yang is truly righteous. On behalf of the many brothers and sisters of the clan, this Fu thanks Brother Yang." His rank within the Dragon Clan was rather low; after all, he only had a Sixth-Tier bloodline. Dragon Blood Flowers were extremely useful to him. When he had first heard that Yang Kai had practically swept the Dragon Blood Flowers clean, he had hated Yang Kai to death. Without Dragon Blood Flowers, advancing his Sixth-Tier bloodline would be harder than climbing to the heavens. Accumulated time could indeed purify his bloodline, but that easily required thousands or tens of thousands of years, and even for the Dragon Clan, such ages were endlessly long.

Now that he heard Yang Kai had come to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers, he was naturally overjoyed. As long as Yang Kai could truly restore the Dragon Blood Flowers to their former scale, old grudges and past resentments were not worth mentioning.

Yang Kai raised a hand. "I can't guarantee it will work. I'll just do my best."

Fu Qi said, "The Great Elder said that all the dragon descendants in Half-Dragon City are at Brother Yang's disposal. If you need them, I can bring them over right now."

"What would I need them for?" Yang Kai curled his lip. Dragon Isle's Dragon Blood Flowers had always been cultivated with the fresh blood of the dragon descendants from Half-Dragon City. The results were slow and took an extremely long time. Yang Kai knew this perfectly well. If he did that, he could indeed restore the Dragon Blood Flowers to their former scale, but who knew how many years it would take?

His eyes turned, and he asked, "Did the Great Elder say whether the Dragon Clan on Dragon Isle is also at my disposal?"

Fu Qi was startled. "Brother Yang means to..."

Yang Kai said, "Since Dragon Blood Flowers require dragon blood to cultivate, pure dragon blood should certainly be more effective than the bloodline of those half-dragons, right? Mm, Brother Fu Qi, you look vigorous and healthy, with a fine ruddy complexion. Drawing a little blood should not be a problem. Why not start with you?"

Fu Qi's face changed drastically. He backed away several steps and stared at Yang Kai warily. "No, no."

"Why not?"

Fu Qi said urgently, "We of the Dragon Clan consume Dragon Blood Flowers to strengthen our bloodlines and increase their purity. If we were to draw our own blood to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers, our bloodlines would regress instead. The loss would outweigh the gain."

"There is such a thing?" Yang Kai was shocked. If that was true, then if he drew his own blood, would his bloodline regress as well? When he used the Dragon Transformation Art now, he had a thirty zhang dragon body. If his bloodline regressed, would the half-dragon body he manifested not shrink too?

For a moment, Yang Kai thought of backing out. He did want to make up for the Dragon Clan's losses and leave Fu Zhun with nothing to say, but if it truly harmed his own interests and strength, he would never agree.

As if sensing his thoughts, Fu Qi said, "Brother Yang need not worry about yourself. The Great Elder said that if it is you, you will surely be different from the other Dragon Clan members."

Yang Kai shot him a sidelong look. "How can the Great Elder be so certain?"

Fu Qi smiled awkwardly. "That is what the Great Elder said. I am not too clear on it myself."

Yang Kai was instantly beset by worries. Of course the Great Elder would say that to put him at ease. In any case, Yang Kai was not a pure Dragon Clan member, so whether his bloodline regressed had nothing to do with the Dragon Clan.

That old fellow seemed decent enough, but in truth, he was just as full of schemes as that old witch.

Seeing Yang Kai's gloomy face, Fu Qi knew this was bad. If Yang Kai really washed his hands of the matter, it would all be over. He quickly said, "If Brother Yang feels something is amiss, why not test it first? If it truly damages your bloodline, it would not be too late to stop. With your perception, you should be able to clearly sense the state of your own bloodline."

Yang Kai's expression eased slightly, and he nodded. "That makes sense. There is no time to lose. I will try it now."

"After you, Brother Yang!"

The two entered Dragon Blood Island side by side. Yang Kai was fairly familiar with the route. Dragon Blood Island brimmed with Spiritual Qi, and it could almost be called one of the Spirit Islands in the entire Dragon Palace with the densest Spiritual Qi. After all, this was where Dragon Blood Flowers were cultivated. How could the Spiritual Qi here not be rich?

The entire Spirit Island was a strange dark crimson, as if it had been soaked in fresh blood. The air was filled with a faint bloody scent as well, but it was not unpleasant. On the contrary, it carried a peculiar fragrance.

Strange arrays could be seen everywhere. They had been set up to extract the Essence-Blood of those dragon descendants. Back when Li Jiao was brought here, he had been locked inside one of the arrays. Once the array activated, the Essence-Blood in his body had been drawn out beyond his control and used to nourish the Dragon Blood Flowers growing on the island.

Fortunately, Yang Kai had arrived in time back then. Otherwise, Li Jiao would likely have been drained until his cultivation fell. Even so, he had only recovered because he obtained some Dragon Blood Flowers afterward, and his bloodline had even improved somewhat. It could be considered a blessing in disguise.

Fu Qi walked beside him, staring at Yang Kai eagerly. "Right now, even counting every last plant, there are only about a hundred Dragon Blood Flowers left on the entire island. Most of them are ungraded, and only a few scattered among them are Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers. If we want to restore them, Brother Yang will have to put in a great deal of effort."

Inwardly, he was seething. The chief culprit behind this situation was right in front of him, yet the Great Elder had sent word ordering him to cooperate with this man with all his strength.

What exactly was the Great Elder thinking?

Hearing Fu Qi's words, Yang Kai merely nodded lightly, showing not the slightest embarrassment, as if he knew nothing about any of it.

So-called ungraded Dragon Blood Flowers were still growing and had not fully matured. Even if consumed, they would be of little use and would only be a reckless waste of heavenly treasure. Only graded Dragon Blood Flowers had any effect on the Dragon Clan's bloodlines.

There were four grades: Lower-Level, Middle-Level, Upper-Level, and Supreme-Level. Supreme-Level flowers were the rarest. Yang Kai had picked two last time, and according to Fu Ling, they had been specially reserved by the Great Elder and Second Elder, who had planned to wait a few more days before consuming them. Who would have thought they would end up benefiting Yang Kai instead?

Before long, Yang Kai stopped. He rubbed his hands together for a moment, then said quietly, "Let's begin."

He very much wanted to know how effective his own Essence-Blood would be on Dragon Blood Flowers.

His Divine Sense surged. He flipped his palm, and a Dragon Blood Flower appeared in his hand, one he had taken from the medicine garden.

Yang Kai had stolen so many Dragon Blood Flowers last time that he had not had time to consume them all. He had only used up all the Supreme-Level, Upper-Level, and Middle-Level flowers. The Lower-Level ones had been preserved and transplanted into his own medicine garden.

The flower was crimson, as though soaked in fresh blood, and gave off a faint yet pure Dragon Pressure.

Fu Qi's breath hitched, and he cried out softly, "Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flower!"

Yang Kai was stunned. "Middle-Level?"

How was it Middle-Level? He clearly remembered consuming all the Dragon Blood Flowers that were Middle-Level and above last time, and not by refining them into pills either, but by swallowing them whole. Things had been too urgent then. Although the Upper-Level and Supreme-Level flowers had all been used for pill refining, he had not had time to refine the Middle-Level ones, so not a single Middle-Level or higher flower should have remained. The ones left in his hands should definitely have been Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers. There was no mistake.

But on closer inspection, the Dragon Blood Flower in his hand, whether in color or size, was unquestionably Middle-Level.

After a moment's thought, Yang Kai understood.

His medicine garden could be said to have gathered the essence of Heaven and Earth. Within it was a precious treasure like Heavy Earth, as well as many Earth Vein beads. The Everlasting Tree also stood nearby, radiating vitality, and now the entire world continuously supplied Spiritual Qi. For Dragon Blood Flowers, one year of growth there was roughly equal to ten years in the outside world, or even longer.

More than ten years had passed since he last left Dragon Isle.

In other words, these Dragon Blood Flowers had gone through at least a hundred years of growth, perhaps even two hundred.

It was only natural that they had grown from Lower-Level to Middle-Level.

"Making a fuss over nothing." Yang Kai snorted. "Have you never eaten a Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flower?"

Fu Qi smiled sheepishly. "I only have a Sixth-Tier bloodline. Generally, Lower-Level flowers are enough for me. Only occasionally can I obtain one or two Middle-Level ones. Brother Yang must be laughing at me."
