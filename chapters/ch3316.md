## Chapter 3316 — Young Master, Don't Panic, This Old Man Is Here

This battle shook Heaven and Earth; even ghosts and gods would have shied away from it.

The three mighty beings on the battlefield were all fighting for their lives, and not one of them held back in the slightest.

Only after an hour did the scene gradually become clear. The lightning that had whipped up the winds and clouds thinned, finally allowing Tang Sheng and the others watching from afar to make out the situation on the battlefield.

What they saw made their hearts tremble.

Yang Kai was mangled from head to toe, his body flickering with golden light, clearly the traces left behind after his Golden Blood had dried. The Dragon Scales across his body had curled up, and who knew how many had fallen away. He was truly a miserable sight. Shi Huo, however, was even worse. The barbs covering him had vanished without a trace, leaving his whole body bare and smooth, and the strange flames burning around him had also gone out. As for Cang Mo, he did not seem to be faring well either. His face was somewhat pale, and he could no longer even maintain his strongest Divine Ability.

After fighting to this point, both sides had actually come away badly wounded.

Cang Mo's expression was dark as water, but Yang Kai threw his head back and laughed wildly, "Looks like this is all you're capable of."

The corner of Cang Mo's eye twitched despite himself. It had to be said that today's battle had exceeded all expectations. He had thought that once he personally made a move, capturing Yang Kai would naturally be effortless. Who could have known Yang Kai's methods would be so formidable that even after fighting him fiercely to this point, Cang Mo still had not completely defeated him?

A trace of resentment flashed through his eyes as he stared at the Incarnation. If not for this Shi Huo, Yang Kai would absolutely not be his opponent. Even if Yang Kai could transform into a thirty-zhang Dragon Body, Cang Mo would still have been able to take him down today.

But with Shi Huo helping from the side, turning this into two against one, what did it matter if he held a slight advantage? If he could not kill Yang Kai, he could not obtain that thing, and he would have made an enemy of him for nothing. If Yang Yan learned of this matter...

Cang Mo suddenly felt torn. There was simply no point in continuing the fight. The previous battle had already destroyed the means he had used to isolate this world. It had been a restriction arranged in advance to seal off this stretch of space, and it had not been especially solid. Once he fought at full strength, the restriction naturally broke on its own.

In other words, if Yang Kai wanted to flee now, Cang Mo could not keep him here.

Naturally, he was unwilling to waste energy on something meaningless. After pondering for a moment, he was just about to speak when Yang Kai's expression suddenly hardened. Yang Kai formed seals with both hands, then sent a palm strike crashing down toward him.

The sensation that time had stopped in that instant descended from the sky.

Years Like a Shuttle Seal!

Cang Mo's eyes narrowed, and he answered with a palm strike of his own.

With a thunderous boom, the violent palm forces collided in midair and wiped each other out. Cang Mo stood in the void, borne by the wind, and said coldly, "Stop while you're ahead, boy!" He had already been thinking of retreating, yet Yang Kai was actually pushing his luck. Anger immediately surged within him. He was unwilling to truly fight Yang Kai to mutual ruin, but that did not mean he was afraid of Yang Kai.

Yang Kai spat out a mouthful of bloody water and stared at him with unruly arrogance, his voice booming like thunder, "Old dog, are you admitting defeat?" The words "old dog" were clearly his answer to the "boy" Cang Mo had thrown at him.

Cang Mo's expression darkened slightly. "Although I failed to consider things properly today, you have not suffered any real loss either. Let this matter end here."

Yang Kai sneered, "End here? You mean you can come when you want and leave when you want? Since when is anything under the Heavens so cheap?"

Cang Mo snorted, "What do you want?" Killing intent had already surfaced in his narrowed eyes. If Yang Kai truly insisted on pestering him to the end, he could only keep fighting. He believed that the one to die in the end would absolutely not be him, but he would definitely have to pay a tremendous price.

Yang Kai shook his head. "If this Lord did not still have some ability to protect himself, would you be speaking so politely right now? Since you struck first, don't blame me for striking back. Why waste so many words? Fight! Today, either you die or I perish!"

As his words fell, his dispirited aura suddenly surged, and battle intent blazed in his eyes as he strode forward.

The Incarnation moved with him in silence.

Cang Mo's face darkened. In the end, he had still underestimated this boy's resolve, but since matters had already reached this point, there was no room left to maneuver. They could only settle it by force. He gently drew in a breath, his eyes filling with determination.

Yet at that moment, Yang Kai and Cang Mo both turned to look in the same direction, solemn expressions appearing on their faces at the same time.

From that direction, a streak of light shot toward them at astonishing speed, revealing that the newcomer was no ordinary Master.

Before the person arrived, a voice thick with hostility rang out from afar, "Young Master, don't panic, this old man is here!"

When the first word was spoken, that person was still a tiny black speck far off on the horizon, but by the time the last word fell, a short old man had already appeared on the battlefield.

He was an extremely strange-looking fellow. Although he was no longer young, he wore an outrageously flashy bright-red robe that made him seem rather out of place. Though he had a smile on his face, his features were fierce and vicious, and one glance was enough to give anyone the feeling that this old fellow was no good thing. Every line of his coarse, fleshy face radiated savagery, with none of the kindly, benevolent air someone of his age ought to possess.

Cang Mo and Yang Kai were both bewildered by this person's arrival. In the middle of such a great battle, someone had actually rushed in to interfere with no regard for his life?

Yet the next instant, it seemed only natural, because although this short old man looked rather out of place, no one dared show him the slightest contempt. He was absolutely a Master qualified to take part in this battle.

Yang Kai even sensed an unusual tremor from the Incarnation, a fear born of instinct.

Yang Kai could not help finding this strange. The Incarnation's sentience was governed by a wisp of his Soul Incarnation, and even when facing Cang Mo, it had not shown the slightest fear. How could it be afraid of this short old man? More than that, Yang Kai himself was not afraid, so what was the Incarnation afraid of?

Thinking it over, he realized it was not the Incarnation that was afraid, but Shi Huo's Origin.

What kind of background did this old fellow have, to be able to make Shi Huo's Origin afraid? Yang Kai stared with his dragon eyes, studying him with endless curiosity.

The short old man was also staring at him with burning eyes, every fold of his coarse, fleshy face carrying a sense of scrutiny. A moment later, his eyes flashed, and he nodded slightly. "Young Master, don't be afraid. This old man has come."

Yang Kai turned to look behind him, but there was not a single person there. He looked back at the old man and pointed at his own nose. "Are you talking to me?"

The old man smiled faintly. "Naturally."

Yang Kai gave a dry laugh. "Old sir, you've mistaken me for someone else." Inwardly, he wondered what was wrong with this old fellow. The man had actually opened his mouth and called him Young Master, when they did not know each other at all. Could there be something wrong with his head? With all that hostility, that coarse, fleshy face, and such high cultivation, perhaps he had suffered Qi Deviation while cultivating.

Still, that call of Young Master stirred countless thoughts in Yang Kai's mind, and he could not help thinking of Di Mo, who had once gone through life and death with him.

It had been a long time since he had heard anyone address him that way.

Di Mo was currently in retreat, cultivating bitterly in Lingxiao Palace. The gap between their strength was too great, and only by chasing after him with all he had would Di Mo gain another chance to fight shoulder to shoulder with him.

The old man said, "Young Master, don't joke. Although you have now... mm, changed greatly, in this old man's eyes, Young Master is still Young Master." As he spoke, his expression turned solemn. He cupped his fists and bowed. "Young Master above, please accept this old man's bow!"

Yang Kai looked down at him from above, wondering what in the world this fellow was doing. Could this be some scheme of Cang Mo's? Stealing a glance at Cang Mo, Yang Kai immediately felt amused. That fellow was actually staring at the flashy old man, looking tense and uneasy, clearly having also sensed the flashy old man's strength.

From the looks of it, even if this was some conspiracy, this flashy old man had definitely not been invited by Cang Mo.

Could it really be as he had guessed? Had the old fellow suffered Qi Deviation, muddling his head so badly that he had mistaken Yang Kai for some Young Master? But looking at him, where was there any trace of Qi Deviation? Nor did he seem muddleheaded.

Moreover, what kind of force would someone have to come from to be the Young Master of someone like this?

After a quick thought, Yang Kai nodded. "Fine. Since you acknowledge me as Young Master, then this Young Master won't stand on ceremony with you." He stretched out a hand and pointed at Cang Mo. "Kill him for me!"

Cang Mo was already extremely uneasy, and when he heard Yang Kai's words, his expression changed drastically. As if facing a great enemy, he raised the Thunder Pestle in front of himself and assumed a defensive posture.

Hearing this, the flashy old man turned and glanced at Cang Mo, the corner of his mouth twitching. "Young Master, this... may be somewhat difficult."

Yang Kai lifted his chin at him, hot air spraying from his nostrils as he snorted. "What? Am I, this Young Master, just for show? Do my words carry no weight?"

Seeing him truly put on the airs of a Young Master, the flashy old man felt utterly disgusted. If not for the opportunity Yang Kai possessed, and if this old man did not need to borrow his strength to escape a certain trouble, why would he have come running over to let Yang Kai take advantage of him?

But since matters had already reached this point, he could not very well slap himself in the face. What was more, he also knew that behind this young man stood that woman. Thinking of that woman... the old man's heart shivered.

With a light cough, the old man said, "Young Master's words naturally carry weight. It is just that this fellow's cultivation is quite good, so he is not so easy to kill. Moreover, if this old man has not seen wrongly, he seems to be someone from Star Court. Now that Iron Blood Great Emperor oversees Star Court, if this old man kills him and Iron Blood Great Emperor comes looking for trouble one day, that would implicate Young Master too, would it not?"

Huh... his mind was perfectly clear. Yang Kai stared at him wide-eyed. He even knew that Cang Mo was from Star Court, and further knew that Star Court was currently overseen by Iron Blood Great Emperor. How could his mind possibly be muddled?

It was just... since your mind is clear, old fellow, why would you willingly lower yourself and call me Young Master? Yang Kai was completely bewildered.

Thinking this, Yang Kai coldly snorted. "Since he wants to kill me, naturally I also want to kill him. Could it be that people from Star Court cannot be touched? Cut the nonsense. Just tell me whether you can kill him or not. If you can, then make your move. If you can't, then move aside; this Young Master will do it himself."

The old man nearly choked with rage. This brat had truly been given a little leeway and started pushing his luck. He was sick with regret. If he had known this would happen, why would he have run over? He had gained nothing and dragged trouble onto himself for no reason. Stinking brat, you dare speak to this old man like this? Just you wait. Sooner or later, I will show you how formidable I am.
