## Chapter 3230 - You Just Wait and See

"Brother Li, you must be tired from the journey. Return to Li Dragon Palace and rest for now. This Lord will handle the rest."

Li Jiao felt a touch grateful when he heard this. He knew Yang Kai was showing consideration for the faint friendship between him and Mi Qi, which was why Yang Kai did not intend to bring him along to Mitian Sect. Otherwise, if Yang Kai truly made a move against Mi Qi and he stood by doing nothing, it would be hard to justify morally.

But after thinking it over, Li Jiao still cupped his fists and said, "Brother Yang is worrying too much. This Li may be unworthy, but I am willing to lend Brother Yang a hand." His friendship with Mi Qi was no great thing. Their earlier talks had fallen apart, and that small bit of friendship had already vanished. In the future, if Li Dragon Palace wanted a foothold in the Northern Territory, it would still have to live under Lingxiao Palace's shadow, so at a time like this, his stance naturally had to be firm.

Besides, how could Mi Qi compare to the life-and-death friendship he shared with Yang Kai? He could certainly tell which mattered more.

Yang Kai glanced at him and smiled. "Since Brother Li is so kind, this Lord will not refuse. Wait here a moment. I will go gather our people, and Brother Li can accompany me to Mitian Sect shortly."

"Good." Li Jiao nodded.

Yang Kai's figure flickered, and he went off.

Li Jiao stood in place and pondered for a moment, then gave an instruction to Bian Yuqing, who was waiting nearby. Turning around, he stepped into the Space Array again and vanished from sight.

Before long, several figures rushed over and landed inside the hall.

Yang Kai looked around, but Li Jiao was nowhere to be seen. Surprised, he asked, "Where is he?"

Bian Yuqing said, "Palace Master Li said he would return to Li Dragon Palace first and gather some people to cheer Palace Master on."

"How thoughtful of him." Yang Kai smiled faintly, then turned to look at the three figures behind him. They were none other than the three great Yao Kings.

Although Lingxiao Palace had over a hundred thousand people and quite a few Emperor Realm cultivators, the only ones truly suited for battle were the three great Yao Kings. Hua Qingsi, as Chief Steward, was not suited to fighting and killing; if anything happened to her, it would be troublesome. Bian Yuqing had to oversee the Space Array and likewise could not easily leave. As for Ji Ying, Nanmen Dajun, and Hou Yu, though they were in the Emperor Realm, they were not skilled fighters. They merely excelled beyond others in their own Domains, so naturally it was not appropriate to bring them along at a time like this.

Ye Hen had also advanced to the First Stage of the Emperor Realm, but in a matter like this, bringing him would make little difference either way, so Yang Kai simply did not call for him.

The three great Yao Kings were enough.

He was just about to call Yingfei and the others to set out when he heard robes flutter behind him, followed by the sound of someone cutting through the air.

Turning back, he saw Liu Yan standing at the entrance of the hall, her little mouth pouting as she glared at him in a huff.

How had he forgotten her? Yang Kai slapped his forehead, then waved Liu Yan over.

Only then did Liu Yan break into a bright smile. Lifting her skirt, she bounced over and took one of Yang Kai's hands.

The party of five stepped onto the Space Array. Light flashed, and they disappeared.

In Li Dragon Palace, Li Jiao moved quickly. At his command, seven or eight Emperor Realm cultivators and two hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators had already gathered and were waiting outside the array.

Vice Palace Master Chang Sheng's clothes were disheveled and his hair was a mess, as if he had just crawled out of bed. With his Second Stage Emperor Realm cultivation, he naturally had no need to sleep, but the clear lip print on his face made it obvious what he had been doing.

As he straightened his clothes, he asked in confusion, "Palace Master, where are we going?"

The group had gathered before the Space Array, which was directly connected to Lingxiao Palace. With this setup, could they be going to attack Lingxiao Palace? Many of them guessed as much and were startled by the thought.

Lingxiao Palace was not to be provoked lightly. Who in the Northern Territory did not know that Lingxiao Palace had three Yao King-level experts holding down the fort, as well as a Grand Guardian Array laid down by the Imperial-Grade Formation Master Nanmen Dajun? With Li Dragon Palace's limited forces, they were afraid they could not take it down. Wenqing Sect was the lesson before their eyes, and Li Dragon Palace was not much stronger than Wenqing Sect had been.

"Why ask so much? You will naturally know once we arrive." Li Jiao snorted coldly and deliberately refused to explain. The main reason was that Emperor Realm cultivators in the Northern Territory often dealt with one another, and these Emperor Realm Elders under him had some friendships with many people from Mitian Sect. If he revealed the news now, it might very well leak.

After being rebuked, Chang Sheng looked embarrassed and did not dare say another word.

Light flashed, and five figures appeared on the Space Array. Before anyone could see them clearly, an astonishing wave of Yao Qi rushed over first, frightening the Li Dragon Palace group so badly that their faces changed. When they looked more carefully and realized Yang Kai had arrived, their expressions turned solemn and they secretly breathed a sigh of relief. It seemed their Palace Master was not going to oppose Lingxiao Palace. Good, good.

When they looked at the three figures beside Yang Kai, they shivered inwardly. Even though they had never met them before, judging from that Yao Qi, these three were probably the long-renowned Yao Kings.

There was also a little girl of seven or eight, delicate and pretty as carved jade, which left them at a loss. Could this little girl be Yang Kai's daughter? But they had never heard that Yang Kai had a daughter with anyone. Could she be an illegitimate child born elsewhere? Many of them exchanged looks, their expressions strange.

"Brother Yang!" Li Jiao cupped his fists.

Everyone else quickly followed suit and saluted. "Greetings, Palace Master Yang."

Yang Kai swept his eyes over them and immediately laughed. "What a grand lineup." Aside from Li Jiao, there were eight Emperor Realm cultivators here and two hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators. Considering what had happened with Qingyang Shrine, Li Jiao had clearly mobilized every cultivator at or above the Dao Origin Realm in Li Dragon Palace.

Li Jiao smiled and said, "If Brother Yang feels this is not enough, I can call in more, but in terms of strength..."

"No need." Yang Kai had not planned to bring many people in the first place. It was already an unexpected delight that Li Jiao could mobilize so many experts under him to lend support. Any more would only become a burden. If their strength was lacking, what use would they be?

"Then... shall we set out now?" Li Jiao asked.

Yang Kai nodded.

"Go!" Li Jiao shouted softly and flew into the sky first, everyone following close behind.

Emperor Realm and Dao Origin Realm cultivators flew at entirely different speeds; Emperor Realm cultivators were undoubtedly much faster. To accommodate the Dao Origin Realm cultivators, the Emperor Realm cultivators from Li Dragon Palace brought out flying Secret Treasures one after another and took some Dao Origin Realm cultivators aboard with them.

For a time, flying boats and ships, flying swords and shuttles, war chariots and wind carts appeared one after another. All kinds of flying Secret Treasures transformed into streaks of light that shot across the sky like a meteor shower.

Yang Kai also brought out his own flying boat and stayed inside with the three great Yao Kings and Liu Yan, following behind Li Jiao as they rushed toward Mitian Sect.

Mitian Sect was not close to Li Dragon Palace. The group flew without stopping for two full days and still had not reached their destination.

The Emperor Realm cultivators from Li Dragon Palace, however, had noticed some clues. Why did this flight route look like it led to Mitian Sect? Many of them had been to Mitian Sect before, so naturally they recognized the way.

They exchanged voice transmissions for a while, all of them uneasy, then pushed Chang Sheng forward to ask.

Chang Sheng did not refuse. He was Li Dragon Palace's Vice Palace Master, so this sort of matter was naturally his responsibility. He flew down onto Li Jiao's flying ship, cupped his fists, and asked, "Palace Master, are we going to Mitian Sect?"

"Exactly."

Chang Sheng was startled. "May I ask what Palace Master intends to accomplish on this trip?"

Li Jiao said, "To assist Palace Master Yang in subduing Mitian Sect." At this point, there was nothing left to hide. After all, he would still need them to contribute later; if they did not understand the situation, they might lose their composure.

"Ah!" Chang Sheng looked alarmed and said anxiously, "Palace Master, please think twice. This must not be done."

"Why not?" Li Jiao asked, his face expressionless.

Chang Sheng said, "Lingxiao Palace rose extremely quickly. In earlier years, its reputation spread far and wide after it destroyed Wenqing Sect. It has been quiet all these years, so that prestige has fallen somewhat, but if we truly help Lingxiao Palace subdue Mitian Sect, then Lingxiao Palace will unquestionably become the number one Sect in the Northern Territory."

"So what?" Li Jiao thought to himself that even without subduing Mitian Sect, Lingxiao Palace's current foundation was beyond comparison with other Sects.

Chang Sheng's expression turned grave. "May I ask Palace Master, how does my Li Dragon Palace compare to Lingxiao Palace?"

"Inferior!" Li Jiao answered honestly. Having witnessed Yang Kai's abilities with his own eyes, he even suspected Yang Kai alone could sweep through all of Li Dragon Palace, so naturally they were far inferior, not to mention the three great Yao Kings.

"Indeed. My Li Dragon Palace is already inferior to Lingxiao Palace. If Lingxiao Palace is allowed to subdue Mitian Sect as well, where in the Northern Territory will there still be a place for us? When the lips are gone, the teeth grow cold. At that point, I fear my Li Dragon Palace will also be in constant danger."

Li Jiao looked at him. "Then in your opinion, what should we do?"

Chang Sheng said solemnly, "We ought to join forces with Mitian Sect against Lingxiao Palace. If our two top Sects join hands, perhaps we can still contend with Lingxiao Palace somewhat and prevent it from becoming the sole dominant power."

Li Jiao looked at him and said nothing.

Chang Sheng felt baffled under his stare. "Did this subordinate say something wrong?"

Li Jiao patted his shoulder, sighed, and said, "I do not blame you for thinking that way. It is only human nature. It is just that... there are many things you do not understand."

Leaving aside the fact that Yang Kai had saved him several times and that he personally had no wish to become Yang Kai's enemy, even if they truly joined hands with Mitian Sect, could they oppose Lingxiao Palace? It was impossible. The shadow of the Dragon Clan stood behind Yang Kai. Who would dare provoke him? If it were an ordinary relationship, that would be one thing, but Yang Kai had already become husband and wife in truth with a Ninth-Tier giant dragon.

"Palace Master..."

"You never said any of this, and I never heard it. Go back and tell them to set their minds at ease. This trip will not require you to make a move. You only need to shout and cheer to build up our momentum."

Chang Sheng's expression twitched. "Palace Master means that Palace Master Yang and the few people with him can destroy Mitian Sect?"

"You think they cannot?"

Chang Sheng shook his head firmly. "It is not that this subordinate looks down on Palace Master Yang and the others. The cultivation of the three great Yao Kings speaks for itself, and in single combat, Mitian Sect likely has no one who can match them. But storming a Sect is different. Leaving aside the fact that Mitian Sect has quite a few experts, its Grand Guardian Array alone will be difficult to break. Once the Grand Array opens, even the three great Yao Kings would likely be helpless against it for a short time, would they not?"

"Helpless against it..." Li Jiao gave an inscrutable smile. "You just wait and see."

Chang Sheng was dazed, unable to understand why his Palace Master had such confidence in Yang Kai's side. That was Mitian Sect, one of the top Sects in the Northern Territory. How could its Grand Guardian Array be broken so easily?

But since Li Jiao was so insistent, he could say nothing more. In any case, Li Jiao had already said they had only come on this trip to wave the flag and cheer. The fighting had nothing to do with them, and they would not encounter any danger. He cupped his fists and left to convey Li Jiao's meaning, leaving the several Emperor Realm cultivators looking at one another.
