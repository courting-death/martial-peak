## Chapter 3266 — Bottom Line

Lei Gu sneered when he heard this. "Why would this Lord go to such unnecessary trouble?"

Gao Xueting was already the best hostage in his hands. There was no need to trade her for Wen Zishan. If he really did that, too many things could go wrong. Leaving aside the chance of some accident while Demonic Qi was being poured into his body, Wen Zishan's cultivation was one Stage higher than Gao Xueting's, which alone would make him far harder to control.

Otherwise, there would not have been a complete lack of Third-Stage Emperor Realm experts among the cultivators corroded by Demonic Qi and turned into demons this time. It was not that Lei Gu and Feng Ming had not wanted them; carrying it out was simply too difficult. They had been forced to settle for the next best option and target those at the First Stage and Second Stage of the Emperor Realm instead.

"What exactly do you want?" Wen Zishan had lost all of his usual careless ease, his face more serious than it had ever been. "All I ask is that she be safe. Whatever conditions you have, name them!"

Lei Gu snorted. "I said it already. Let me leave. Once I reach a safe place, this Lord will naturally release her."

"Impossible!" Wen Zishan slowly shook his head. "How am I supposed to trust something with no guarantee at all?"

Lei Gu said with unhurried composure, "If you want her safe, you can only gamble. At worst, we all perish together." He urged the Demon Yuan in his hand, and Gao Xueting immediately gave a muffled groan.

"Stop!" Wen Zishan's face changed drastically, and he raised a hand with a furious roar.

Just as Lei Gu was about to say something more, Yang Kai suddenly spoke. "We cannot agree to your proposal, and you will not agree to mine. How about this? We each take a step back."

Lei Gu turned his gaze to him. "Let's hear it."

Yang Kai clasped his hands behind his back. "I let you leave, but I follow you. Once we reach a place you find suitable, hand my Senior Sister Gao over to me. This is my final bottom line. If you cannot accept it, then there is nothing more to discuss."

Lei Gu did not answer. His eyes flickered. Wen Zishan's heart climbed into his throat as he secretly urged his Emperor Yuan, ready to strike the instant anything went wrong. The scene in the valley seemed to freeze. There was no sound, no movement at all. Time had never crawled by so slowly.

Who knew how much time had passed before Yang Kai took a deep breath and said, "Looks like you do not agree." As he spoke, he raised one hand high and shouted, "Yao Kings, hear my command!"

Boom...

As Yao Qi surged into the sky, the Thirty-Two Yao Kings flickered into motion, surrounding Lei Gu and Gao Xueting in layers upon layers. Every escape route above, below, left, and right was sealed. A bloodthirsty light flashed in every Yao King's eyes, each of them looking eager to act.

"I will count to three. If he still has not answered by then, go tear him apart!" Yang Kai stared coldly at Lei Gu. One finger rose on his lifted hand, and he bit out a single number. "One!"

Wen Zishan went taut. His fists clenched and loosened, then loosened and clenched again. His nerves were stretched to the breaking point.

"Two..." The second finger rose.

Thirty-two killing intents locked onto Lei Gu at once. The Yao Yuan inside every Yao King stirred restlessly. They did not care about Lei Gu or Gao Xueting. To them, both were strangers, and at this moment they obeyed only Yang Kai's command. Since he had said to tear Lei Gu apart after three counts, they truly would charge in and tear him apart. As for whether Gao Xueting lived or died, that was beyond their concern.

Under the weight of those thirty-two killing intents, the Demonic Qi around Lei Gu's body churned violently. Even though he now had the cultivation of a Third-Stage Emperor, he had no chance of surviving against the combined force of the Thirty-Two Yao Kings. He would likely be destroyed in a single exchange, without even a corpse left to bury. He stared deep into Yang Kai's eyes, trying to peer into the depths of his heart, hoping to find even the slightest hesitation or hint of a bluff. To his disappointment, Yang Kai's expression never changed. There was only resolve in his eyes.

When the third finger was halfway up, Lei Gu said in a deep voice, "Only you may follow."

"Naturally!" Yang Kai lowered his hand and clasped it behind his back, calm and grave. But the group standing behind him clearly saw that hand trembling slightly. His heart was clearly nowhere near as calm as he appeared.

They could not help wondering: if Lei Gu still had not spoken just now, could Yang Kai really have ordered the Thirty-Two Yao Kings to rush in?

"Make way!" Yang Kai swept his hand out.

The Thirty-Two Yao Kings surrounding Lei Gu immediately parted to either side, opening a passage.

Seeing this, Fan Wu, Cang Gou, and Luan Feng could not help sighing. These were Yao Kings under their command. Since when had a human earned the right to order them around? Yet these useless fellows obeyed him without the slightest hesitation. It was enough to drive one mad.

"After you." Yang Kai gestured with one hand.

Lei Gu said unhurriedly, "You had best keep your word. If this Lord discovers that you left some trace for them to follow, or if this Lord detects their auras, you will bear the consequences!"

"Rest easy." Yang Kai laughed coldly.

Lei Gu snorted, urged his Demon Yuan, wrapped Gao Xueting in it, and rushed out through the gap in the encirclement. Yang Kai followed close behind at an unhurried pace.

In the blink of an eye, the two figures disappeared from everyone's sight.

Wen Zishan stared in the direction the two had gone, torn with indecision. Naturally, he was not at ease letting Yang Kai pursue alone. He had originally planned to trail them in secret, but Lei Gu's earlier warning left him afraid to act rashly. If he carelessly exposed his aura and Lei Gu sensed it, Gao Xueting would be finished. He could also follow from a great distance, but if he stayed too far back, he would have no way to tell which direction to pursue, and Yang Kai likely would not have a chance to leave any markers.

Ma Qing sighed, patted him on the shoulder, and said, "Brother Wen, since that boy made such a proposal, he must have a way to bring Elder Gao back. You... should wait and see." After saying this, he saw that Wen Zishan had no reaction and could only walk back. He looked at his wife and disciples with concern. "None of you are seriously hurt, are you?"

The disciples shook their heads.

Madam Qi, however, stared at him coldly until sweat broke out across Ma Qing's forehead. The corner of his mouth twitched. "Madam, what is wrong?"

Madam Qi flicked her sleeve and walked aside, her icy voice drifting back. "When we return, write me a divorce letter. You, Saint Lord Ma, are so noble and righteous, while I am only a woman of the household. Perhaps I will drag you down one day. We may as well each go our own way, lest I ruin your reputation."

Cold sweat poured even harder from Ma Qing's forehead, and he hurried after her. "Madam, why would you say that?" He knew that what he had said to persuade Wen Zishan had probably hurt her, but he had only been speaking according to the facts. Where was he supposed to go to plead his case?

On the other side, Wen Zishan stood silently for a long time before finally closing his eyes in pain.

After hesitating for so long, he still had not found the courage to chase Yang Kai and Lei Gu. Even if he set out now, it was already too late. He could only place all his hopes on Yang Kai and pray he could bring Gao Xueting back unharmed, but that hope was far too slim. Yang Kai's strength might be impressive, but Lei Gu was no pushover. He had the cultivation of a Third-Stage Emperor, and Gao Xueting had already been demonized and was under his control.

Wen Zishan truly could not imagine what method Yang Kai could use to snatch Gao Xueting back.

As this thought crossed his mind, he felt someone come to his side and tug at his sleeve. He opened his eyes and looked down to see a delicate little girl of seven or eight gazing up at him with clear, bright eyes.

"You are..." Wen Zishan looked at her suspiciously. Although she seemed like an unremarkable little girl, he did not dare show the slightest contempt. He had seen her take action just now; the aura around her was so powerful that even he felt somewhat inferior.

He truly could not understand where Yang Kai had found so many monsters to keep around him. Never mind that he had easily mobilized three Divine Spirits and the Thirty-Two Yao Kings; even a little girl was this powerful, and there was also that Little Black dog that could devour Demonic Qi. Each was stranger than the last.

"Want to chase them?" Liu Yan asked.

Wen Zishan brightened. "You can track where they went?"

Liu Yan shook her head. "I cannot."

Wen Zishan's eyes instantly dimmed, only for Liu Yan to point to the side and say, "It can!"

He followed her gesture and saw a stone man half as tall as a person standing quietly nearby, making faces at him.

"Him?" Wen Zishan was baffled. This stone man did seem a little different from the others, livelier and more spirited. Seeing Wen Zishan look over, the stone man said, "Follow me!"

Then he stretched out a hand and pointed at Fan Wu and the other two Divine Spirits. "You three come as well."

Fan Wu and the others exchanged glances, but they asked no questions and all nodded.

The Incarnation turned and left. Liu Yan quickly followed. Wen Zishan no longer hesitated; his figure flickered, and he followed close behind Liu Yan. The three Divine Spirits came after him.

They sped along like wind and lightning, spirit mountains and great rivers sweeping past beneath their feet. Everyone in the group had cultivation near the pinnacle, so naturally they flew swiftly.

After they had flown for quite a while, Wen Zishan finally said suspiciously, "This is not the direction they went."

"Shh!" Liu Yan turned her head, raised one pale pink finger beside her lips, and hushed him softly.

Wen Zishan's face twitched slightly, and he did not dare say anything more. He could only follow in silence and hope they were not chasing in the wrong direction. The Incarnation led the way, while the others did their best to restrain their auras behind him. They twisted and turned as though following some serious trail, but aside from Liu Yan and the Incarnation, who were unwavering, Wen Zishan and the three Divine Spirits were completely lost. After chasing all this way, they had not sensed the slightest trace of Yang Kai's or Lei Gu's aura and had no idea whether the direction was right.

What made Wen Zishan even more anxious was that the Incarnation sometimes stopped somewhere for quite a while before setting off again. During those pauses, there was no sign that it was investigating anything. It seemed to be acting entirely on whim, flying wherever it pleased.

Far away, ten thousand li distant, Yang Kai walked with his hands clasped behind his back, strolling as if through a courtyard as he followed close behind Lei Gu. At first, Lei Gu had tried to use his speed to throw Yang Kai off, but after several attempts, he knew it was impossible.
