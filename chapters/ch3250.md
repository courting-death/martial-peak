## Chapter 3250 — Night Assault

Yu Zhuo stuck out her tongue, slipped into the room, and pulled out a beast-hide pouch as though offering up a treasure. She handed it to Yang Kai. "I found some more seeds for you. Take a look."

Yang Kai reached out and took it, then said after a moment's thought, "You don't need to look for any more after this. These are enough."

Yu Zhuo froze, then gave a soft "oh." She looked a little disappointed, but soon rallied and asked, "Senior, what are you collecting so many seeds for?"

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "My Madam asked me to. How could I dare be careless?"

"Madam?" Yu Zhuo was clearly startled. "Senior, you have a Madam?" Why had she never heard of this? These days, she had asked around for quite a bit of information about Yang Kai, yet there had been nothing at all about this.

Yang Kai nodded, lifted his teacup, took a sip, and said mildly, "Several, actually."

Several... Yu Zhuo could not help going blank for a moment, and her mood suddenly sank into deep desolation.

Her usual liveliness disappeared. After learning that Yang Kai not only had a Madam, but several of them, she was clearly distracted. She exchanged a few casual words with Yang Kai, then took her leave on her own initiative.

Yang Kai saw her to the door and slowly shook his head as he watched her depart. He had no choice but to reveal his private affairs. He only hoped it would cut through the tangled mess quickly.

The team battles were still in full swing. The further they progressed, the more elite those who remained became, and the fiercer the fighting grew. Through the team battles, one could glimpse the depths of the Southern Territory's many Sects. The longer a group stayed in the competition, the stronger the Sect behind it was. Naturally, none of the three great forces had been eliminated.

As expected, the final three were the three great forces competing against one another. This group from Qingyang Shrine finally lived up to expectations, overpowering the rest in the competition at the Dao Origin Realm level and taking first place. Although they suffered some setbacks in the competitions at the Void King Realm and Origin Returning Realm levels, it was no great loss.

All the rankings had been settled. Several hundred Emperor Realm Masters and tens of thousands of participating disciples gathered once more in the valley. Xiao Yuyang announced in a clear, carrying voice the top ten lists for each level of the competition, along with the rewards they had earned. The cultivators and Sects whose names were called all shared in the honor, while those who had lost secretly steeled themselves, determined to wash away their shame at the next Martial Gathering.

With this, the Southern Territory Martial Gathering came to an end, but the crowd did not disperse. By custom, on this final night, Wuhua Hall, as host, would hold a banquet for all its guests. Whether the Emperor Realm Masters who had come to oversee matters or the tens of thousands of disciples who had participated, all were invited.

The banquet would be held at night, and there was still some time to wait, so Yang Kai returned to his lodgings.

Before long, the maid came again to report that Yu Zhuo requested an audience.

Yang Kai felt a headache coming on. After pondering for a moment, he said, "Just tell her I'm not here."

Although he had not spelled things out last time, his meaning had already been made clear. Yang Kai did not know what Yu Zhuo was thinking, but he truly did not want to stir up any more unclear entanglements. The maid gave him a curious look, unable to understand why Yang Kai, who had seemed rather kind to Yu Zhuo some time ago, would not even meet her today. But this was not something she could meddle in, so she could only withdraw obediently.

Outside the hall, the maid returned and said to Yu Zhuo, "Elder Yang is not here."

Yu Zhuo said in surprise, "But I saw him come back."

The maid smiled faintly. "Perhaps he went out again."

Yu Zhuo clearly did not believe her. She had personally watched Yang Kai return here, and there had been no sign of him leaving. How could he not be here? Just as she was about to ask the maid to check again, she suddenly seemed to realize something. She forced out a faint smile and said, "Sorry for the trouble."

The maid shook her head. "Miss, you are too polite."

Yu Zhuo turned away, her eyes suddenly a little red. As though acting out of pique, she flew toward her own lodgings. Halfway there, she stopped again, turned back, stared at the wing room where Yang Kai lived, and waved hard before finally speeding away.

Yang Kai stood by the window and saw it all. He sighed faintly. Since things had come to this, he could only silently wish her well and hope she escaped the mire as soon as possible.

That night, the valley was extraordinarily lively. Tens of thousands of people gathered there, and table after table filled nearly the entire valley. Maids and attendants wove through the crowd, while Wuhua Hall's disciples hurried about without pause, continuously serving fine wine and delicacies. Wuhua Hall was holding a grand banquet for all sides, a rare and splendid occasion, and as everyone drank and toasted one another, the atmosphere became fervent and lively.

At the gathering place for Qingyang Shrine's people, Yang Kai looked around and asked curiously, "Where did Senior Sister Gao go?" He had not seen Gao Xueting anywhere.

Murong Xiaoxiao replied, "Senior Sister said she wasn't feeling well, so she didn't come."

"It's nothing serious, is it?" Yang Kai asked, concern written across his face.

Murong Xiaoxiao blushed slightly. "It's nothing major. She'll be fine after resting a while."

Seeing her expression, Yang Kai seemed to understand something and did not ask further.

People kept coming over to offer toasts, so Qingyang Shrine's side naturally could not simply sit there. At Wen Zishan's order, the several Emperor Realm Masters had no choice but to carry their cups out and make a round of toasts.

On the way back, Yang Kai's expression suddenly changed. He turned his head to the side and saw a figure flash past, rush into the darkness, and vanish.

"What is he up to?" Yang Kai frowned, tossed aside the wine cup in his hand, and followed.

A moment later, several li away, someone waited ahead. Yang Kai walked over, cupped his fists, and said, "Senior Brother Luo."

Luo Chen turned to look at him and said indifferently, "Come with me."

"Where?" Yang Kai frowned. Just now, it had been Luo Chen who had quietly sent him a voice transmission, asking him to come over. Speaking of it, although he and Luo Chen had come here together this time to lead the team for the Southern Territory Martial Gathering, they truly had not spoken much.

This fellow spoke even less than Xiao Baiyi. Xiao Bai would at least squeeze out a few sentences now and then, but Luo Chen was practically mute. You could beat him with a stick and still not get a fart out of him.

In the entire Qingyang Shrine, he seemed to count as something of an oddity. According to Murong Xiaoxiao, among the many Elders of the Shrine, not one had a particularly good relationship with him. Yang Kai had not interacted with him much, had exchanged only a few words with him, and had no friendship with him whatsoever, so he truly had no idea why Luo Chen had called him over in the middle of the night.

"Senior Sister Gao wants to see you," Luo Chen said, then led the way.

"Senior Sister Gao..." Yang Kai was stunned, and could not help grumbling inwardly. What was with all this mystery? If Gao Xueting wanted to see him, she could have just come over directly. Why ask Luo Chen to pass on the message?

Pressing down the doubts in his heart, he followed Luo Chen and sped outward.

A moment later, Yang Kai frowned and said, "Senior Brother Luo, this isn't the way back, is it?" From the direction they were headed, it seemed they were flying out of Wuhua Hall, which only made him feel stranger.

Yet Luo Chen, who was leading the way, offered no explanation at all. Yang Kai sensed that something was wrong. He abruptly stopped and fixed his gaze on Luo Chen's back.

Luo Chen also stopped and turned back to look at him.

"Senior Brother Luo, what are you playing at?" Yang Kai asked with a frown.

Luo Chen repeated, "Senior Sister Gao wants to see you."

Yang Kai said, "Why does Senior Sister want to see me?"

Luo Chen said, "I'm only responsible for passing on the message. I don't know the details either."

Yang Kai stared at him for a long while before suddenly flipping his hand and taking out a Transmission Compass. He poured his Divine Sense into it and contacted Gao Xueting.

Luo Chen's behavior struck him as suspicious, so at this point, he could no longer worry about whether he would offend him or not. He first had to contact Gao Xueting and ask clearly.

The compass quickly received a response. After checking it, Yang Kai looked stunned. Gao Xueting really did want to see him, but she had not said what it was about, only that he would know once he came.

Putting away the compass, Yang Kai cupped his fists toward Luo Chen somewhat awkwardly. After all, what he had just done plainly showed that he had not trusted Luo Chen, yet the facts proved he had been overthinking things.

Luo Chen remained his usual self, saying nothing as he continued leading the way.

A short while later, the two left Wuhua Hall. Naturally, no one stopped them along the way.

Yang Kai followed Luo Chen as they sped onward. Only after about half an hour did Luo Chen finally raise a hand and point to a tall mountain in the distance. "Senior Sister Gao is waiting for you over there."

Yang Kai tilted his head and looked at him. "You're not going?"

Luo Chen shook his head, turned around, and left.

Yang Kai was speechless. No wonder you have no friends. With that damned personality, who could possibly get along with you? I really don't know how Gao Xueting managed to make you move. He looked in the direction Luo Chen had indicated. The peak ahead was not far, so Yang Kai flashed forward and sped toward it.

Landing on the peak, Yang Kai looked around. There was no one. When he swept the surroundings with his Divine Sense, he found not the slightest trace of any living creature.

"Senior Sister Gao!" Yang Kai called out. There was no reply, and the night's events seemed stranger than ever. First Luo Chen had come to pass him a message on Gao Xueting's behalf, and now, after he had arrived, there was still no sign of Gao Xueting.

Could it be that she had not arrived yet?

Suspicious, Yang Kai took out his Transmission Compass again and poured Divine Sense into it, wanting to ask Gao Xueting what was going on. This time, however, Gao Xueting gave no response at all.

Just as he was focused entirely on fiddling with the Transmission Compass, a faint shadow silently appeared behind him in the darkness. It was like a wisp of black smoke, emerging abruptly without the slightest warning.

Stranger still, even though it was close enough to touch, Yang Kai did not notice it at all.

The black shadow slowly condensed into a blurry human figure, as if a gust of wind could scatter it. The figure raised one hand, and a pitch-black dagger appeared in its grip, stabbing toward Yang Kai's back at an extremely slow pace.

The moon was dark, the wind was high, and Heaven and Earth were still. Only the calls of birds and insects sounded from the surroundings.

Yang Kai was still fiddling with the Transmission Compass, brow tightly furrowed, with no awareness at all of the danger behind him.

All of a sudden, the birdsong and insect cries stopped short, as if frightened by something. Every sound was wiped away in that instant.

In that same instant, the dagger that had been slowly stabbing toward Yang Kai's back erupted with unimaginable speed and thrust fiercely forward.

Pain stabbed through him. Yang Kai's Emperor Yuan erupted with a boom, and the Transmission Compass in his hand exploded into fine powder. His flesh wriggled and tensed, becoming hard as iron. The one-chi dagger sank in five cun and pierced into his internal organs, but his muscles locked it firmly in place.

The black shadow holding the dagger flickered, and a startled light burst from where its eyes should have been. It seemed not to have expected its certain strike to be blocked. Although the dagger had gone in, it had not achieved the expected effect. In a flash of thought, it quickly understood the crux of the matter: this man's body was unusual, nothing an ordinary person's flesh body could compare with.

Yang Kai gave it no time to react. His eyes flared with anger, and he whipped his head around and punched out.
