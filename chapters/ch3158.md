## Chapter 3158 — All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array

"This is the place on the entire continent where Spiritual Qi is richest and the Laws are most complete. It can serve as the Formation Core of the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array." Li Jiao waved his hand, and a crimson point of light appeared at once over Nine Heavens Sacred Land on the projection, as if he had marked the spot.

The people of Great Wilderness Star Domain had rampaged across Tongxuan Continent for several years, and Nine Heavens Sacred Land really was the only place left safe and sound. Xia Ningshang was also stationed here, making it the most suitable place for the Formation Core.

Yang Kai nodded slightly, showing that he had committed it to memory.

Li Jiao waved his hand again, and eight small black dots surfaced on the projection of Tongxuan Continent, matching the locations of the Eight Wastelands Spirit-Locking Array that Yang Kai had destroyed earlier. Li Jiao said, "These eight places suffered the worst damage. If this Li's guess is correct, someone must have arranged Grand Arrays there before. If we want this star to recover its vitality, we absolutely cannot neglect those eight locations. They must be treated as key points."

"Brother Li is right."

Li Jiao raised his hand once more, and red dots appeared one after another across the spherical projection. "This Li spent several days traveling and surveying various places, and I have chosen a total of one hundred and eight locations for the array. Apart from this Formation Core and those eight locations, there are another ninety-nine. Brother Yang will need to personally visit each one and bury enough Source Crystals there. When the Grand Array is activated, the array will transform those Source Crystals into Spiritual Qi to nourish this star."

"Excellent!" Yang Kai clapped his hands in praise. Over the past few days, he and Xia Ningshang had been studying the one-hundred-and-eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array and had gained some understanding of how it operated and how it should be arranged. After hearing Li Jiao's plan, he immediately felt that this matter had great promise.

If Xia Ningshang wanted to recover quickly, she had to rely on Tongxuan Continent's transformation, unless she could do what Yang Kai had once done on Dark Star: perform a Star Lord's reverse nourishment and draw the power of the stars from beyond the sky into Tongxuan Continent. If she could truly do that, there would be no need for any All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array.

But Xia Ningshang's cultivation realm was still lacking, so it was impossible for her to accomplish what Yang Kai had. A Star Lord's reverse nourishment was far beyond her reach. Yang Kai had managed it easily only because his own realm had already far surpassed the limit Dark Star could nurture.

"If Brother Yang sees no problem with this, then this Li will proceed."

"I'll have to trouble Brother Li."

Li Jiao nodded lightly, then paused and said, "There is one more matter. This Li will need Lady Liu Yan's help."

Yang Kai grinned. "Use her as you please."

Li Jiao needed to refine all kinds of items for the array, and Liu Yan could lend a hand, saving her from sitting around bored all day with nothing to do.

The two of them immediately handed over the materials. Yang Kai took the Source Crystals, while the remaining materials and other items were all left for Li Jiao to handle. Those would become the materials he used to refine the Formation Foundations of the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array.

Once the work was divided, Yang Kai did not delay. He immediately found a suitable place inside Nine Heavens Sacred Land, stamped the ground with his foot, then looked up with a smile. "Little Senior Sister, please lend me a hand."

As soon as he finished speaking, the solid earth beneath his feet suddenly turned to quicksand, and he sank downward without the slightest resistance.

Yang Kai remained still and showed no panic at all, silently calculating his position.

A moment later, he called for a stop. By then, he had already descended a thousand zhang underground.

He took out a great number of Source Crystals and scattered them, letting them fill the depths of the earth. This point in Nine Heavens Sacred Land would become the Formation Core of the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array, so it required an especially large number of Source Crystals.

Although Yang Kai did not count them carefully, he left a full million Upper-Level Source Crystals here before stopping.

A million Upper-Level Source Crystals was an enormous fortune even in the Star Realm, let alone in a lower plane's Star Domain. The Star Domain only circulated Crystal Stones, and even the finest Supreme-Level Crystal Stone could not compare to a single Lower-Level Source Crystal.

If the Spiritual Qi contained in these million Source Crystals were drawn out, it could instantly transform Nine Heavens Sacred Land into a paradise no worse than a Sect in the Star Realm.

Once the Formation Core's arrangements were complete, he immediately flew back up and set off for another location.

An incense stick later, he checked the information in the jade slip in his hand and stopped at a certain place. It lay on the outskirts of a city, but the city that should have been bustling was now empty and ruined, clearly long abandoned.

Yang Kai called out to Xia Ningshang again and repeated the process, leaving a large number of Source Crystals there.

Over the following days, he hurried all across Tongxuan Continent, following the directions in the jade slip Li Jiao had given him and making the early preparations for arranging the Spirit Array.

In deserts, in the depths of the sea, in jungles, and across plains, traces of Yang Kai's busy figure were left everywhere.

He did not let Xia Ningshang intervene while he traveled. Although Xia Ningshang could teleport him between any two points on Tongxuan Continent with a single thought, doing so would still consume some of her strength.

Only when he was burying Source Crystals did he let Xia Ningshang help him a little.

The Source Crystals consumed along the way were beyond count, and he used up nearly ninety percent of what he had accumulated. It was not that he lacked wealth; this matter concerned Xia Ningshang's future, so no matter how much he had, he would not begrudge it.

By the time all one hundred and eight locations had been dealt with, several days had already passed.

He scratched his head, exchanged a few thoughts with Xia Ningshang, who had been watching over him all along, then leapt toward the ground.

The earth opened a passage for him, allowing him to pass through unhindered.

Very soon, he arrived at the earth's core.

This was where Tongxuan Continent's Source had been nurtured, and also where Xia Ningshang had refined that Source back then. It could be called the core of the entire Tongxuan Continent.

Scorching magma flowed there, as if it could burn away everything.

Yang Kai raised his hand, and an enormous Source Crystal lode was drawn out of the Small Xuan Realm by him. It crashed down into the earth's core and sank into the magma. This was the only Source Crystal lode he had on hand, one he had obtained outside Fenglin City back then. It had been kept in the Small Xuan Realm all this time for his Incarnation's cultivation, so quite a bit had been consumed, but to the current Tongxuan Continent, it was still an enormous fortune.

It was easy to imagine that, with Yang Kai providing so many Source Crystals, the state of the entire Tongxuan Continent would improve greatly as soon as the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array began operating. Although it would still need time to repair and nourish itself, it would no longer require several dozen or even a hundred years. In other words, Xia Ningshang's recovery period would also shorten. Perhaps in a few years, perhaps in ten, she could safely return to her original state.

And that was not all. As long as the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array continued running, Tongxuan Continent's condition would grow better and better, and its Spiritual Qi would become richer and richer. When the time came, this star would certainly be able to grow to a level comparable to Dark Star and Shuiyue Star. It would no longer be like before, when nurturing Saint Realm cultivators was the highest it could manage.

It would also be able to nurture Saint King Realm, Origin Returning Realm, and Void King Realm cultivators... all the way to the peak this Star Domain could support. The better it became, the greater the benefits Xia Ningshang would receive.

With bright hopes for the future in his heart, Yang Kai returned to Nine Heavens Sacred Land.

Li Jiao and Liu Yan were refining the Formation Foundations. Even with Liu Yan's help, Li Jiao had exhausted himself terribly by now and looked withered and haggard. If not for the large handfuls of Spiritual Pills he had taken, he probably would have collapsed long ago.

Yang Kai helped from the side.

Two hurried months passed in a flash. On this day, Li Jiao handed the final item to Yang Kai, his bleary eyes carrying a trace of dazed emptiness. "I have not failed your trust."

The one hundred and eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Pillars had been refined, so his task was complete. A few mistakes had occurred during the process, wasting quite a few materials, but Yang Kai had prepared thoroughly, so the losses had little effect.

Yang Kai reached out to take it, thanked him, then raised his hand and brought Li Jiao into the Small Xuan Realm so he could rest and recover inside.

Playing with the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Pillar in his hand, which was about one chi long, Yang Kai looked toward the core of Nine Heavens Sacred Land and smiled faintly. "Are you ready?"

A fresh breeze brushed his face, like an invisible little hand stroking his cheek. This was Xia Ningshang's answer.

"Then let's begin!"

Yang Kai's figure flickered, and he immediately arrived where the Source Crystals had been buried earlier. He took a deep breath, stirred his Emperor Yuan slightly, tossed the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Pillar forward, rapidly changed his hand seals, and pointed toward the pillar.

Streaks of flowing light sank into the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Pillar.

As it fell, the Profound Spirit Pillar seemed to be suddenly invigorated, rapidly growing longer and thicker.

In the blink of an eye, the one-chi-long Profound Spirit Pillar transformed into a heaven-reaching pillar a thousand zhang long, so thick that even several dozen people linking arms could not quite encircle it.

Complex patterns flashed across the Profound Spirit Pillar as it dropped straight down, as if an invisible great hand were gripping it and guiding it into the earth.

Within Nine Heavens Sacred Land, countless people looked up, shock filling their eyes.

Although this Profound Spirit Pillar was not some Secret Treasure, only the foundation of the array, it still radiated a terrifying aura. Against such a thing, let alone taking a direct hit, even being grazed by it would likely leave one without bones or corpse.

The earth turned into a vortex, opening a passage that guided the Profound Spirit Pillar deeper below.

Yang Kai shouted in a low voice, "The pillar of the Formation Core is to be buried six hundred and sixty-six zhang, with three hundred and thirty-three zhang exposed. Little Senior Sister, be careful."

Silently, the Profound Spirit Pillar continued sinking. When it reached the proper depth, it suddenly stopped, and the earth condensed again, binding it tightly in place.

Three hundred and thirty-three zhang, not one bit more, not one bit less.

Yang Kai checked it over, and after confirming there were no mistakes, he flashed away toward another location, Liu Yan following close behind.

The All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array was arranged with one hundred and eight Profound Spirit Pillars as its Formation Foundations. The placement and burial depth of every Profound Spirit Pillar were extremely particular; a tiny deviation would lead to an error of a thousand li. Fortunately, Li Jiao was fairly familiar with this array. It seemed Li Dragon Palace had arranged it before, though not the one-hundred-and-eight version, but the seventy-two All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array.

Although the number was different, the fundamentals were the same, so the information he provided should not contain any errors.

The Profound Spirit Pillars had also been refined by him and Liu Yan together. As long as they were all arranged properly, the Grand Array should activate smoothly.

After two months, Yang Kai revisited the old places, returning again and again to the locations he had visited before and burying the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Pillars above the Source Crystals.

Yang Kai spent a full seven days before completing all one hundred and eight Profound Spirit Pillars. Afterward, he and Liu Yan made another circuit to cross-check everything, and only returned to Nine Heavens Sacred Land after confirming that there would be no mistakes.
