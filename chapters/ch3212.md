## Chapter 3212 — Applying What One Has Learned

With his discernment, Ji Ying could naturally see Xia Ningshang's cultivation. She was only at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm, yet the medicinal materials she had taken out were Void King-Grade, which meant she intended to refine a Void King-Grade Spirit Pill. For a cultivator from a Lower Plane Star Domain, that should already be her limit. In other words, this wife of Yang Kai's was a Void King-Grade Alchemist.

Perhaps... there was still something to look forward to. Ji Ying nodded inwardly and quietly began paying closer attention.

Xia Ningshang gently picked up one of the medicinal materials, and in that instant, her entire aura changed dramatically. She became utterly focused, as if the medicinal material before her was the only thing left between Heaven and Earth.

Alchemy demanded absolute concentration. It could not tolerate even the slightest disturbance or distraction, and this was a quality every Alchemist had to possess.

Ji Ying nodded again. Whatever her Alchemy aptitude might be, this performance alone had merit.

But in the next moment, he frowned, because Xia Ningshang actually urged her Saint Yuan to wrap around the medicinal material in her hand. Her Saint Yuan did not remain constant either; it rose and fell, strengthened and weakened, forming a strange fluctuation. Under that fluctuation, wisps of emerald-green aura soon seeped from the herb, clearly its medicinal liquid.

No pill furnace? A strange light flashed through Ji Ying's eyes.

An outstanding Alchemist might be able to refine a Spirit Pill they were especially skilled at in midair without relying on a pill furnace, but that demanded extremely high skill, and the grade of the Spirit Pill could not be too high. Ji Ying himself, for example, was an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, and he could refine Dao Origin-Grade Spirit Pills without a furnace, but if he did, then even if the Pills formed successfully, their quality and quantity would likely suffer.

A pill furnace was an Alchemist's second life. This was not just an empty saying. Good or bad, a pill furnace was an essential aid to any Alchemist.

Xia Ningshang was only in the Void King Realm, and refining a Void King-Grade Spirit Pill was already the limit of her abilities, yet now she wanted to refine in midair without a furnace. Ji Ying could not help feeling displeased. Even if she meant to show off a little before him, she should not be so overconfident. That was no virtue in an Alchemist. From one glimpse, one could judge the whole; with such a temperament, she likely would not go far.

If anyone else had done this in front of him, he would certainly have scolded them, but remembering that Xia Ningshang was Yang Kai's wife, he held himself back. Even so, the faint expectations he had felt earlier had already vanished like smoke.

The next moment, he froze again.

He had realized that Xia Ningshang's Saint Yuan did not carry a fire attribute aura.

What was she doing? Refining in midair without a pill furnace was bad enough, but she did not even have Pill Fire? How was she supposed to perform Alchemy like this? Did she really think Alchemy was some children's game she could casually play with?

He could no longer restrain himself and turned to look at Yang Kai.

Yang Kai only smiled and said meaningfully, "Brother Ji will know if he keeps watching."

Ji Ying frowned and could only let things take their course, though he was already working out how to tactfully refuse Yang Kai's earlier proposal without damaging Yang Kai's face or their friendship. It was a difficult matter. If Yang Kai had brought anyone else, he could simply refuse outright, but the woman before him was Yang Kai's wife. If he handled this poorly, it could very easily leave a rift between them.

Saint Yuan surged, and the Spirit Herb tumbled. Visible to the naked eye, points of green fluorescence seeped out of the Void King-Grade Spirit Pill material, gradually gathering into an emerald liquid bead the size of a pearl.

Ji Ying's eyes suddenly widened, and he blurted out, "How is that possible?"

The first step in refining a Spirit Pill was condensing the medicinal liquid. Ordinary Alchemists naturally had to rely on a pill furnace and Pill Fire, yet this wife of Yang Kai's had used no pill furnace and did not even possess Pill Fire, but had still miraculously condensed the medicinal liquid from that herb.

He turned to Yang Kai in shock, only to find Yang Kai calm, as if he had expected this all along.

What was going on? Without a pill furnace or Pill Fire, how had this Miss Xia condensed the medicinal liquid? Still unwilling to believe it, Ji Ying released a trace of his Divine Sense to examine it, and his eyes widened even further.

It was indeed medicinal liquid, no doubt about it. Moreover... it was actually extraordinarily pure. The pearl-sized cluster of emerald medicinal liquid contained not the slightest impurity. It was flawless, and its medicinal efficacy had been preserved almost perfectly, without the least damage. Ji Ying's shock was simply beyond words. What he was seeing overturned everything he had understood in his life.

With his ability, if he took out an identical Void King-Grade medicinal material, he could naturally achieve the same result as Xia Ningshang, but he would have to rely on a pill furnace and Pill Fire. More importantly, he was an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, while Xia Ningshang was only a Void King-Grade Alchemist. They were worlds apart, so there was naturally no comparing the two.

He had never imagined that a Void King-Grade Alchemist could condense medicinal liquid to such a degree while working at the limits of her own ability. At the very least, when he himself had been a Void King-Grade Alchemist, he had not been able to do this.

The purity of the medicinal liquid was directly tied to the quality of the final Pills. The purer the liquid and the fewer the impurities, the more it would help the subsequent refinement. In this step, Xia Ningshang had undoubtedly achieved perfection.

When he looked again at the Void King-Grade medicinal material in Xia Ningshang's hand, it had completely withered. Every last drop of medicinal efficacy within it had been refined out, and Xia Ningshang casually tossed the husk aside.

This startled Ji Ying all over again.

Condensing such pure medicinal liquid without a pill furnace or Pill Fire was already astonishing enough, yet she had also squeezed the medicinal efficacy of a single herb to its absolute limit.

It could be said that Ji Ying had never seen an Alchemist like her. Even the several disciples he placed great hopes in were probably inferior to her. If the step of condensing medicinal liquid were scored out of ten, Ji Ying would certainly give Xia Ningshang a nine, deducting one point only out of fear that she would grow proud.

He could not understand how she had done it no matter how he thought about it, for it ran contrary to everything he considered common sense. Calming himself, he continued watching. One medicinal material after another was skillfully condensed by Xia Ningshang into clusters of medicinal liquid, and before long, she had finished condensing every ingredient.

Ji Ying watched in wonder and discovered that the first, nearly perfect condensation had not been a stroke of luck. Every later material was just like the first, its medicinal efficacy completely extracted by her without the slightest waste.

Ji Ying put away the dissatisfaction and contempt in his heart. His expression turned intensely focused as he stared at Xia Ningshang's every movement without blinking, afraid of missing even the smallest detail. Although he still did not quite understand how she had done it, what he witnessed was, to a veteran Alchemist, both a shock and a pleasure.

Once the medicinal liquids were fully condensed, it was naturally time to carve Spirit Arrays and fuse the liquids together.

Ji Ying thought, now that things had reached this point, surely she would use a pill furnace. What she had done just now was already enough to prove her ability.

Contrary to his expectations, Xia Ningshang still showed no intention of taking out a pill furnace. She merely raised both hands before her, palms facing each other, and the pearl-sized clusters of medicinal liquid, wrapped in Saint Yuan, floated between them.

As her Saint Yuan surged, the clusters of medicinal liquid quickly began to dance, whirling around a point in the void with Xia Ningshang's palms as their boundary.

As they spun, they fused and mingled with one another, undergoing wondrous changes.

Ji Ying was already rendered completely speechless. He only felt that this Madam Yang's method of refining Spirit Pills was nothing like the Alchemy he knew; it had far exceeded his imagination.

His Divine Sense remained fixed on the changes in the medicinal liquids, and Ji Ying's expression shifted brilliantly as well: surprise, shock, admiration, all manner of emotions appearing one after another.

The time it took an incense stick to burn soon passed. The medicinal liquids that had been spinning between Xia Ningshang's palms suddenly slowed, but through his perception, Ji Ying could clearly sense that they had completely fused together, their proportions balanced to perfection.

Xia Ningshang held the half-finished medicinal mass in one hand, then tilted her head and thought for a moment. Her other hand suddenly began changing hand seals, sending out streams of Saint Yuan.

"This is..." Ji Ying abruptly stood up.

The hand seals Xia Ningshang was using were actually the Pill-collecting technique he had most recently developed!

During his previous five days of discussion with Yang Kai, he had used this technique to collect no fewer than twenty furnaces of Spirit Pills, but that was all. He had never imagined that Xia Ningshang would completely learn it after watching from the side for only five days.

Not only had she learned it, she was applying what she had learned!

Ji Ying suddenly grew excited. Instinct told him that he might have found a treasure. Her excellent and unusual Alchemy skills could still be explained away as the product of long practice and deep immersion in the Alchemy Dao, but learning his Pill-collecting technique within five days and putting it to use was enough to display her comprehension and aptitude.

Of course, in Ji Ying's eyes, the Pill-condensing technique Xia Ningshang displayed was still not perfect. Some of the transitions midway were rather stiff, lacking the smooth, effortless ease he had when he used it.

But a flaw could not eclipse the jade. In just five days, with no instruction from him and only a bit of observation from the side, she had reached this point. If he personally taught her, would she not have even greater room to improve?

Compared with her, those several disciples he had placed such hopes in were simply rotten wood that could not be carved, mud walls that could not be plastered!

When the final hand seal was struck, the medicinal liquid in Xia Ningshang's hand suddenly split into seven, yet remained under her control. The seven clusters of medicinal liquid spun without stopping, gradually solidifying and drying.

After everything settled, seven round, snow-white Spirit Pills had appeared in Xia Ningshang's hand.

"Master, please inspect them." Xia Ningshang spoke softly, then placed the seven Spirit Pills in her hand on the table and withdrew behind Yang Kai.

Ji Ying's eyes immediately widened. He grabbed the seven Spirit Pills and examined them carefully.

Yang Kai had been watching Ji Ying's every reaction all along, so how could he not know how Ji Ying's attitude had changed? He looked up at Xia Ningshang with a smile, then gave her a meaningful glance to indicate that everything was settled.

"Pill Patterns!" Ji Ying cried softly.

Among the seven Spirit Pills, some had actually produced Pill Patterns. And not just one of them, but two!

What an astonishing ratio. Even Ji Ying was stunned for a moment.
