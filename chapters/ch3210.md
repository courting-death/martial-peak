## Chapter 3210 — Busy, Busy

It took Yang Kai a full half month to properly settle this hundred-thousand-strong crowd. During that time, he was rushed off his feet, and it was fortunate Hua Qingsi and Bian Yuqing were there to help him, or the worry alone would have killed him.

Lingxiao Palace had more than a hundred Spirit Peaks of every size. They had once stood empty and deserted, but now they were packed with people. The smaller Spirit Peaks housed several hundred people apiece, while the largest main peaks held as many as two or three thousand.

With more than a hundred thousand people spread among these hundred-plus Spirit Peaks, Lingxiao Palace instantly became extremely lively.

Managing so many people was indeed troublesome, but Hua Qingsi offered a suggestion: each peak would choose a Peak Master, the more than a hundred Peak Masters would answer directly to her, and the newly settled disciples on each Spirit Peak would be managed by their own Peak Master. That made matters much simpler.

Chi Yue, Ai Ou, Gu Cangyun, and Ghost Ancestor were naturally capable of each presiding over a peak, and each was given a main-peak-level Spirit Peak. Although their current cultivation had not reached the Emperor Realm, they were all at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm, far stronger than ordinary cultivators from the Star Domain.

For this, the four of them even gave up Five Saints Peak, where they had long resided, and moved into their respective Spirit Peaks. The disciples under them were easy to divide as well. Gu Cangyun had originally been the Alliance Master of Sword Alliance, and on this trip, Yang Kai had practically rounded up all of Sword Alliance's elites and brought them over, so he could simply hand every Sword Alliance disciple to Gu Cangyun to manage. Presumably, those disciples would be more than willing. If the numbers were a little short, people could be transferred from elsewhere to make up the difference.

Ai Ou, meanwhile, commanded a Spirit Peak made up of Hengluo Commerce Guild cultivators. Ghost Ancestor took charge of the original Lingxiao Sect, while Chi Yue also took on part of Lingxiao Sect's people. This was really because Yang Kai had assigned those three thousand yao to the three great Yao Kings, leaving her with no one of her own to use.

Others such as Ye Xijun, several Elders of the former Star Emperor Sect, Qiu Yimeng, Di Mo, and the other Void King Realm cultivators all became masters of their own peaks.

Once things were divided this way, the situation across Lingxiao Palace immediately became clear, and future management would be far more convenient.

Yang Kai led the two Chief Stewards, Hua Qingsi and Bian Yuqing, back and forth among the peaks on inspection. Only after confirming that everything was running smoothly did he finally have time to rest for a few days.

This so-called rest did not mean he was idle. He simply was not as busy as he had been at the start.

With more than a hundred thousand people moving in, they naturally needed vast amounts of cultivation resources, as well as cultivation methods. Since everyone had now come to the Star Realm, the cultivation methods from the Star Domain definitely could no longer keep pace, so they naturally had to switch to higher-grade methods.

Fortunately, ever since Yang Kai began wandering the Star Realm, he had killed countless people and had even destroyed a top Sect like Wenqing Sect, so he had indeed collected a great many cultivation methods. They came in all varieties, enough for all kinds of cultivators to choose from and practice.

Hua Qingsi had also planned ahead long ago, establishing a library on one of the main peaks. It held countless cultivation methods and Secret Arts, and she now opened it so the Peak Masters could enter and make their own selections.

The matter of cultivation methods was temporarily resolved. What remained was the matter of cultivation resources.

Cultivators in the Star Domain mostly used Crystal Stones for cultivation, while the Star Realm produced Source Crystals in abundance. The two were on entirely different levels. Fortunately, Lingxiao Palace had built up deep reserves over the years, with Source Crystals piled like mountains in its storehouses. Yang Kai had also extorted a large sum from the three Saint Venerables some days ago, and when that was added to what Hua Qingsi had earned through years of trade with the Southern Territory's Ziyuan Commerce Guild, a rough calculation showed that, at the very least, Lingxiao Palace's disciples would not have to worry about Source Crystals for the next fifty years.

Moreover, trade with Ziyuan Commerce Guild took place every year, and the volume of materials involved was enormous, so even after fifty years, more resources would certainly continue to flow in.

Source Crystals needed no further thought. The only issue left to consider was Pills.

No small number of Void King Realm cultivators had been brought over from the Star Domain this time, and as time passed, the number of people advancing to the Void King Realm would probably only grow.

The Void King Realm itself was not the problem. The real drain on Pills came when a Void King Realm cultivator attempted to break through to the Dao Origin Realm, because any cultivator at that stage needed Origin Condensing Pills to help transform their Saint Yuan into Origin Qi.

Yang Kai had gone through this process back then, and Chi Yue, Ghost Ancestor, and the others had done the same. Without Origin Condensing Pills, a Void King Realm cultivator would need at least ten to twenty years to fully transform the Saint Yuan in their body into Origin Qi. That was for the fast ones; slower cultivators might take several decades, or even a hundred years.

So over the next ten years, or even the next hundred years, Lingxiao Palace would need an incalculable number of Origin Condensing Pills. For such an enormous quantity, relying solely on their own Sect to refine them would never meet demand. Even though Lingxiao Palace now had a Spirit Pill Peak, that was still far from enough.

While training their own Alchemists, they also had to purchase them in large quantities.

For this reason, Yang Kai sent Hua Qingsi, who had only recently returned, on another trip to the Southern Territory. She took a large number of Yao Beast Inner Cores with her, intending to trade them to Ziyuan Commerce Guild for a batch of Origin Condensing Pills to cover the immediate need.

Yang Kai had more than a million Yao Beast Inner Cores in his possession, all obtained back when he entered the Wild Ancient Land and then the Ancient Land's Beast Tomb. They were the accumulation of the entire Ancient Land over tens of thousands, or even more than a hundred thousand, years, and Yang Kai had swept them clean. Among them were plenty of Twelfth-Tier Yao Beast Inner Cores. He believed Ziyuan Commerce Guild would be very willing to make such a trade. For this emergency batch of Origin Condensing Pills, Yang Kai directly took out more than ten thousand Inner Cores of every shape and kind; it could be said that he had truly made a costly sacrifice this time.

After being busy for a full month, Yang Kai finally managed to catch his breath.

On this day, he led Xia Ningshang up a Spirit Peak.

This peak was also one of Lingxiao Palace's main peaks. Compared with the other Spirit Peaks, its scenery was even lovelier, its Spiritual Qi richer, with floral fragrance drifting all around and birdsong filling the air.

Unlike the liveliness of the other Spirit Peaks, this peak remained quiet and secluded despite its great size, because Yang Kai had not allowed the cultivators from the Star Domain to disturb it.

This peak was precisely Lingxiao Palace's Spirit Pill Peak!

There were two other similar Spirit Peaks: Spirit Array Peak and Spirit Artifact Peak. Their corresponding Peak Masters were Nanmen Dajun and Hou Yu. Of course, Yang Kai had forced those appointments through without caring whether the two agreed. In any case, from now on, the two of them were the Peak Masters of Spirit Array Peak and Spirit Artifact Peak.

Spirit Pill Peak, however, had no Peak Master. It was the mountain where the Imperial-Grade Pill Master Ji Ying resided.

Strictly speaking, Ji Ying could not be considered a member of Lingxiao Palace. After all, he was Sublime Pill Great Emperor's fifth disciple. He had only stayed in Lingxiao Palace to refine Pills for Yang Kai because he had lost to him in an Alchemy contest back then, so he could be regarded as one of Lingxiao Palace's Honored Guests.

Ji Ying was a decent man. Not only did he possess Emperor Realm cultivation, but he was also an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, and on top of that, a personal disciple of Sublime Pill Great Emperor. Any one of those three identities would be dazzling anywhere; combined, they were enough to make people feel dread. If he had refused to honor the bet, Yang Kai would have had no way to deal with him. He could not very well kill him, could he? That would definitely offend Sublime Pill Great Emperor, and the wrath of a Great Emperor was no joke.

Yet he had truly stayed, fulfilling his wager with Yang Kai and refining a large number of Spirit Pills for Lingxiao Palace. The Spirit Pills he produced were of an extremely high grade, and their quality was consistently excellent as well.

He also had several disciples under him, and had brought them all to Spirit Pill Peak to serve alongside him.

Across the whole of Spirit Pill Peak, only Ji Ying and his few disciples lived there. Apart from them, there were only some boys responsible for miscellaneous chores.

Yang Kai and Xia Ningshang wound their way up the peak and soon arrived at the summit. In front of the main hall, a man who looked to be in his twenties was waiting. When he saw Yang Kai, he saluted and said, "Palace Master Yang, Master is awaiting your honored arrival. Please follow me."

Yang Kai smiled and nodded, knowing that Ji Ying had sensed his arrival.

After they followed that disciple into the hall, Ji Ying quickly came forward to greet them and said warmly, "Brother Yang, it has been a long time." The two of them had not seen each other for more than ten years, so it had indeed been quite a while. His warmth was not merely because he had lost to Yang Kai in Alchemy back then, but also because of Gongsun Mu.

Thanks to Yang Kai, the remains of Sublime Pill Great Emperor's third disciple, Gongsun Mu, had been able to return to Medicine Pill Valley after his death, so Ji Ying was naturally grateful to him.

"Brother Ji!" Yang Kai replied with a smile.

Ji Ying grabbed Yang Kai's arm and said, "Brother Yang, you came at just the right time. Recently I have been testing a new Alchemy technique and have gained a little insight. I was just about to ask Brother Yang for some guidance."

He was an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, and only a handful of people in the world could discuss Alchemy with him. He did have several Senior Brothers and Senior Sisters, but they had all been taught by the same Master, so discussing matters with them would likely bring little novelty and yield no constructive opinions.

Yang Kai was different. Although Ji Ying did not know who had taught him Alchemy, he knew very well that Yang Kai's skill was not inferior to his own, and moreover, it was unique, a style all its own. If he could exchange a few ideas with him, he would definitely gain something.

"Uh..." Yang Kai was speechless, thinking that Alchemists really were all madmen. He had come here on business, but he had not expected Ji Ying to seize him the moment they met.

Since he still had something to ask of him later, Yang Kai thought that showing some goodwill now might make things go more smoothly afterward, so he could only say modestly, "I would not dare call it guidance. Let us simply discuss it together, Brother Ji."

His victory over Ji Ying in their last Alchemy contest had also owed something to luck. Yang Kai did not believe that he was truly stronger than Ji Ying. Every field had its own specialties, and Ji Ying had areas where he surpassed him as well.

Ji Ying laughed loudly. "Brother Yang is too modest. Even Honored Master praises your Alchemy without cease. It is just a pity..."

"A pity how?" Yang Kai was startled, wondering whether Sublime Pill Great Emperor might have some objection to him.

"A pity that you cannot devote yourself wholeheartedly to the Dao of Alchemy."

So that was what he meant... Yang Kai grinned. "Among the Three Thousand Great Daos, taking one is enough to benefit me endlessly."

Ji Ying paused for a moment, then nodded and said, "That is true."

He immediately stopped wasting words, pulled Yang Kai into his Alchemy Room, took out some precious herbs, and began refining.

Xia Ningshang naturally followed them in. Ji Ying did not know her background, but seeing that her relationship with Yang Kai seemed somewhat unusual, he did not stop her. In any case, his Alchemy was not something just anyone could learn by watching.
