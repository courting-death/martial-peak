## Chapter 3288 — Traces of a Supreme Treasure

An hour later, Yang Kai walked out of the pit and strode onward.

Xi Lei stood behind him and watched in silence. Only after Yang Kai's figure vanished from sight did he let out a long sigh.

In this forbidden canyon, the farther in one went, the greater the pressure became. This place was already the limit of what he, Xi Lei, could reach; otherwise, it would not have tempered him at all. If he went any deeper, he would begin to lose the ability to endure it.

Yet Yang Kai was strolling along as though through his own courtyard, leaving Xi Lei so far behind that he disappeared without a trace. Just how heaven-defying was that flesh body... Xi Lei sighed to the heavens in his heart, feeling thoroughly unhappy. This King was a Yao King, after all, yet he had been outdone by a human. Ruthlessness flashed through him. With an angry roar, he rushed out of the pit, revealed his enormous true body, and continued to accept the Baptism and tempering of the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens.

Deep in the canyon, blood kept spraying from Yang Kai's body. There was hardly an intact patch of skin on him, but each wound swiftly closed under his powerful recovery. The cycle repeated again and again, and while it did help him somewhat, the effect was minimal.

As he went deeper, even the pits in the surrounding cliffs disappeared. He guessed that at such a depth, probably no one from Wuhua Hall had ever reached this place since the Sect's founding, so naturally there would be no pits here.

After walking for another incense stick's worth of time, he had clearly reached the end of the canyon. Up ahead, a peculiar fluctuation of Space Force suddenly spread out.

Yang Kai's heart stirred. Looking up, he saw that more than a hundred zhang away, in midair at the end of the canyon, a space crack more than ten zhang long stretched across the sky like a scar between Heaven and Earth, eternally unhealed.

From within that space crack, terrifying gales blew out without cease, surging forward along the canyon.

Yang Kai's expression cleared with understanding. Only now did he realize where the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens in this forbidden canyon came from. It had all been blowing out of this space crack. Heaven and Earth had their own laws and could repair themselves. When Yang Kai tore space, for example, the torn place would recover as good as new in moments under the effect of the World Laws.

Yet no one knew how many years the space crack before him had existed, nor where it led. The Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens poured endlessly from within, leaving even the World Laws unable to repair it in time, which allowed the space crack to remain for so long.

The sight made Yang Kai click his tongue in wonder, and a restless eagerness rose deep in his heart. He very much wanted to know exactly what place that space crack connected to, and why gales had been pouring from it all these years.

Once the thought came, he acted on it. With the Laws of Space surging around him, he flashed directly before the space crack, then plunged headfirst into it without hesitation.

Three breaths later, Yang Kai shot out in a panic, his entire body drenched in blood like a blood gourd, blood scattering through the sky. Only after retreating several hundred zhang did he shake off that sense of crisis. When he looked back at the space crack, lingering fear mingled with an inexplicable excitement.

When he rushed into the space crack just now, he found himself inside a Void Crack, but that crack was filled with vast, raging gales whose power far surpassed anything in the canyon. The instant he touched them, he was injured. If he had not withdrawn decisively, he feared that in no time at all the gales would have shaved him down to a skeleton, and by then, no matter how strong his flesh body was, he would have died on the spot.

Although he had only caught a fleeting glimpse, Yang Kai had sensed an extraordinary aura within that Void Crack, an aura that could destroy all things. It seemed to be the source of the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens.

Wuhua Hall had stood for tens of thousands of years, and this Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens had existed since its founding, blowing ceaselessly through the forbidden canyon. But all things had a source, and the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens was no exception. It could not possibly have continued unchanged for tens of thousands of years without cause.

That source was hidden within the Void Crack.

Unfortunately, Yang Kai had no idea what it was, much less how to take it out. Even so, there was no denying that it was something extraordinary, perhaps a supreme treasure capable of making even a Great Emperor dread it.

Yang Kai was tempted, but after carefully weighing his current strength, he had no choice but to turn back in disappointment. As he was now, he was not strong enough to take that treasure out and claim it for himself. Perhaps he would have to wait a while longer, until his cultivation rose greatly.

Before long, he returned to the place where Xi Lei was cultivating. When the two met face to face, Xi Lei was startled. "Sir, what happened to you?"

Yang Kai looked rather wretched at the moment. The Golden Blood all over his body had dried, coating him as if in a layer of gold, and even his aura felt somewhat unstable. He had clearly been injured.

Yang Kai sniffed and said, "A small accident. It is far too dangerous inside. Yao King should enter with caution." After saying so, he flashed straight into a nearby pit and began adjusting his breathing.

Xi Lei's expression turned solemn, and he did not doubt him. Even Yang Kai had suffered such a great loss inside, to say nothing of him. With this warning, he would not dare to venture too deep in the future.

Half a day later, Yang Kai walked out of the pit, changed into a clean set of clothes, then bid Xi Lei farewell and leisurely left the forbidden canyon.

After meeting back up with Hua Qingsi and Nanmen Dajun, he chose a suitable location inside Wuhua Hall and began arranging a Space Array. Nanmen Dajun helped with extreme enthusiasm, even setting aside his own affairs.

Back then, Nanmen Dajun had stayed in Lingxiao Palace because he had witnessed Yang Kai's wondrous methods for arranging a Space Array and wanted to remain there to learn a thing or two from him. Unfortunately, although he had already grasped the basic method of arranging the array and could produce a respectable-looking Space Array, it had only the form and none of the ability to traverse the Void.

Only after painstakingly studying it for many years did he realize that he had fallen into a trap: only someone proficient in the Laws of Space could give an arranged Space Array its proper spirit and charm, and only then could it take effect.

But that did not stop him from learning other things from the process. Among the Great Daos of the world, once one method was understood, ten thousand methods could be understood. As an Array Grandmaster, Nanmen Dajun could draw inferences from one case and absorb experience and information useful to himself.

With his help, arranging the Space Array became extremely simple. Since this place would become Lingxiao Palace's branch in the Southern Territory in the future, and fifty thousand disciples would be migrating here as well, the Space Array definitely could not be small. Yang Kai spent a large amount of Ethereal Crystal and Ethereal Jade to arrange an array of the largest scale, one capable of teleporting more than a hundred people at once.

By the time they finished, a full day had passed. Yang Kai then made slight adjustments to the Space Array here, letting it echo from afar with the other Space Arrays and link with them. Only then did he bid farewell to Hua Qingsi and Nanmen Dajun and leave.

When Yang Kai appeared again, he was already in the Wild Ancient Land.

After walking out of Xiaoxiao's tree house, he went to pay a visit to the Elder and Mu Na, the Wood Spirit Clan's Patriarch, thanking them for their assistance last time and bringing many gifts for their two clans.

In the battle at Wuhua Hall, the Stone Puppet and Wood Spirit Clans had contributed greatly. It was they who, when the situation was turning unfavorable, first opened a gap in the Demonic Intent and controlled the demonkin possessed by it. It was also the Wood Spirits who, at the final juncture, used their clan's Secret Technique to suppress the Demonic Qi inside the bodies of those ten thousand-plus demonkin, opening up a vast space that allowed Yang Kai to calmly cast the Exorcism Spell and save the many elites of the Southern Territory.

Both were races that kept aloof from worldly affairs. If Yang Kai had not asked them this time, they likely would not have easily left the place where they had lived for generations to involve themselves in so many disputes. Yang Kai would naturally remember this favor.

After chatting idly for a while, Yang Kai turned to the main topic. "Elder, you are experienced and knowledgeable. I would like to ask you about someone."

The Elder smiled faintly. "I have merely lived a little longer. Who is it you want to ask about?"

"Elder, have you heard the name Sheng Yuzhu?" Yang Kai had naturally not come to the Wild Ancient Land specifically to inquire about Sheng Yuzhu's origins; it was only something he was doing along the way. Although he did not think Sheng Yuzhu was some heinous evildoer, there was never any harm in being cautious. After all, Sheng Yuzhu had promised him certain things, and learning more about that woman would make future cooperation easier.

The Stone Puppet Clan had long lifespans, and the Elder in particular could almost be called a know-it-all of the Star Realm. Naturally, Yang Kai had to consult him.

"Sheng Yuzhu?" the Elder murmured, then slowly shook his head. "I have never heard of her."

"Even Elder has not heard of her?" Yang Kai was stunned.

The Elder said, "It is a very distinctive name. If I had heard it, I would naturally remember it. What is so unusual about her that you care this much?"

Yang Kai sighed. "The matter is like this..."

He then briefly recounted what he had seen in Luocha Sect, though he did not mention Sheng Yuzhu's promise to him. Even so, the Elder and Mu Na were left stunned.

The Elder exclaimed, "There is actually such a person in this world!"

Mu Na also raised a hand to cover her small mouth, her face full of amazement. "To survive alone in a Void Crack for tens of thousands of years... what an astonishing thing." After a pause, she added, "She is truly pitiful..."

Yang Kai nodded. "I also found it very shocking, so I came to ask Elder about it."

The Elder shook his head. "I truly have never heard this person's name."

A strange light flashed through Yang Kai's eyes. "Elder means there is something suspicious about her origins?"

The Elder said, "That is not what I mean. Although I have lived a little longer, it is not as if I know everything. In particular, tens of thousands of years ago, my Stone Puppet Clan had already begun living in seclusion in the Ancient Land. I know little about the Great Dao dispute and only heard the occasional rumor. I do not even know exactly who participated back then. But since she named several Great Emperors in succession, I imagine her identity is not false. She may truly be a figure from that period. What matters is whether, in your view, she is good or evil."

Yang Kai shook his head. "I did not sense any malice."

The Elder smiled. "Then there is nothing to worry about. She has been trapped for tens of thousands of years, so how much of her cultivation could remain? Even if she is an evil person, she is still not a Great Emperor. If she truly dares to go out and commit crimes, do you fear no one will deal with her?"

Yang Kai nodded. "That is precisely why I chose to bring her out. Otherwise, I truly would not have dared to act that way. I would have had to make thorough inquiries first."
