## Chapter 3291 - Three Great Female Yao Kings

At that, Luan Feng's expression tightened. "What do you mean?"

Yang Kai said leisurely, "Madam said so herself just now. This Lord has Heaven's Punisher's descendant behind him. I trust I do not need to remind you what Heaven's Punisher did back then. And in Heaven's Punisher Palace, there must be countless Divine Spirit Origins. Otherwise, why would the Yao Kings of the Ancient Land treat me with such respect, even granting every request? Are they not all hoping for a chance to attain the body of a Divine Spirit one day?"

"Living off a woman and still making such a grand show of it!" Luan Feng looked thoroughly contemptuous.

Yang Kai ignored her and continued smoothly, "This Lord is also a man of great fortune. Back then, I happened to take in a young girl, only for her to be none other than the famous descendant of Heaven's Punisher. Her bloodline even awakened by some miracle. I am not boasting to Madam, but my Ruoxi more or less does whatever I say. Once she emerges from retreat, if I ask something of her, I doubt Ruoxi will refuse."

"What does any of this have to do with Tian Long?" Luan Feng's dark brows drew together.

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Madam possesses the body of a Luan Feng. Although you belong to the Phoenix Race, as far as I know, you are still only a side branch, no? Compared to the authentic Phoenix Race, there remains a gap. Heaven's Punisher slew countless Divine Spirits back then, dragons and phoenixes among them. Could there truly be no Phoenix Race Origin in Heaven's Punisher Palace?"

Having said his piece, Yang Kai fell silent. Under the cover of lifting his teacup, he quietly studied Luan Feng's expression. Sure enough, her face shifted back and forth. Yang Kai was secretly delighted. He knew she would never be able to resist such a temptation.

Sure enough, only after a long while did Luan Feng ask, "Are you certain you can get Tian Long into Heaven's Punisher Palace?"

Yang Kai immediately said, “Even if I have to throw away this old face of mine, Ruoxi will definitely agree.”

Luan Feng drew a deep breath, then nodded lightly. "Boy, I do not refuse to lend you Yao Kings purely out of selfishness. You are human, after all. If you have too many Yao Kings under your command, someone may level the charge of colluding with the yao race against you. That would do you no good either."

Yang Kai said with easy confidence, "This Lord appreciates Madam's kindness, but without overwhelming strength, everything else is empty talk."

Luan Feng's eyes flickered. "Fan Wu and Cang Gou may not agree."

Yang Kai grinned. "With Madam present, how could they refuse to give face? I know that among the Saint Venerables of the Ancient Land, Fan Wu has always been the one in charge, but in terms of strength, Madam stands at the head."

"Spare me the flattery!" As she spoke, Luan Feng rose and turned to walk outside.

"Where is Madam going?" Yang Kai asked, watching her graceful back.

"Wait here. Whether it succeeds or fails, I will give you an answer within five days."

With that promise, Yang Kai knew the matter was settled. He cupped his fists and smiled. "Many thanks for your help, Madam." At least all that talking had not been wasted. Yang Kai also knew that since he had opened a breach through Luan Feng this time, the Ancient Land would no longer refuse him outright if he made further requests in the future. Conversely, if he had failed this time, he could forget about getting any benefits from the Ancient Land ever again.

In excellent spirits, he returned to his room to wait.

In Fan Wu's palace, Cang Gou hurried over as soon as he received the news. The three great Saint Venerables gathered, and none of them looked pleased.

"He has gone too far! Last time, we even helped him, and now this boy has already set his sights on us. He actually wants three more Yao Kings. Why doesn't he just die?" Cang Gou was furious, looking as if he might charge over and fight Yang Kai to the death on the spot.

Fan Wu sighed. "We have sought fortune and avoided disaster in the Ancient Land for so many years. Who would have thought we would finally stumble because of one boy? The Heavenly Way is unfair."

Cold light flashed in Cang Gou's eyes. "Why not go finish him off now and save ourselves future trouble?"

Luan Feng glanced at him. "Are you not afraid Heaven's Punisher's descendant will come looking for you after she emerges from retreat?"

Cang Gou's momentum immediately wilted. He muttered a few words, his expression sullen. To a Divine Spirit, the name Heaven's Punisher was like a mouse hearing a cat cry. The fear came from the marrow of his bones.

Fan Wu said gravely, "Besides, the current Yang Kai is likely not someone we can simply kill at will."

When Yang Kai first came to the Ancient Land, he had already displayed terrifying combat strength, and back then he was only at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm. Several dozen years had passed in the blink of an eye; with his aptitude, he was certainly stronger than before. Worse, he was proficient in the Laws of Space. Unless they sealed off the world in advance and stopped him from breaking through and escaping, Fan Wu had no confidence he could take him down.

And once they made an enemy of him, the consequences were not something they could bear.

Cang Gou said, "Then what do we do? Are we just going to let him keep us under his thumb?"

Fan Wu turned to Luan Feng and asked, "How did Madam Feng negotiate with him?"

Luan Feng lowered her eyes. "I refused with all my strength as well, but he shamelessly brought over the Stone Puppet who refined Shi Huo's Origin, saying he would have that Stone Puppet take up residence in the northern lands of the Wild Ancient Land and inherit the position of Northern Saint Venerable. He even threatened to spread the news to the various Yao Kings. I was forced into a corner."

"Despicable," Cang Gou said, seething with anger.

Fan Wu's eyes flickered slightly. He keenly sensed that Luan Feng seemed to be hiding something, but he did not press her. He knew he would get no answer even if he asked. After a moment of silence, he sighed. "It seems we cannot stop him."

Luan Feng hesitated for a moment. "Although we cannot stop him, we may still be able to trip him up."

Fan Wu looked at her. "How so?"

Luan Feng pursed her lips in a smile. "You two know he has a Madam named Shan Qingluo. She inherited a Divine Spirit Origin and became a yao cultivator. She is currently cultivating here in my Ancient Land."

Fan Wu and Cang Gou both nodded lightly. Naturally, they had heard about this. Shan Qingluo had even been personally sent back by Yingfei, and the story had already spread throughout the Ancient Land. She was not in much danger within the Ancient Land; she had simply chosen to cultivate here in seclusion because its environment suited her.

Cang Gou's eyes lit up. "Madam means we should make a move against Shan Qingluo?"

Luan Feng shook her head. "There is no need. I have received that woman before, and she is fairly good. What she inherited seems to be the Origin Power of the Heavenly Moon Demon Spider. Given enough time, she will become a fierce general of my yao race. Since she is one of our own, why deal with her?"

Cang Gou said, "If we are not dealing with her, why bring her up?"

Luan Feng pursed her lips in a smile. "That boy may look proper enough, but he is a born womanizer. Did you know he has four Madams in total, and also has an improper entanglement with a woman from Ice Heart Valley? On top of that, his relationship with a dragon girl is ambiguous. If we count Heaven's Punisher's descendant as well..."

Cang Gou listened with open envy. "Tsk tsk, what boundless romantic luck."

Fan Wu's eyes shifted, however, and he said, "Having too many women is not necessarily a good thing."

Luan Feng nodded. "Indeed. Those four Madams of his all knew him when he was still insignificant, and none of them can be considered too strong. The woman from Ice Heart Valley seems to be only at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. We met her back then as well. She was that madwoman, though I hear she is no longer mad now. As for the remaining two, one is a dragon girl and one is Heaven's Punisher's descendant. Neither one is willing to live beneath another."

Cang Gou said, "If those two were made to meet..." The thought alone made him shudder. A dragon girl and Heaven's Punisher's descendant were natural enemies. That would be quite the show.

The only problem was that no one knew when Heaven's Punisher's descendant would emerge from retreat.

Luan Feng smiled. "Before that, we can add fuel to the fire. If a crowd of women has him running ragged, I doubt he will have the time to bother with the Ancient Land."

Cang Gou asked, "How do we add fuel to the fire?"

Luan Feng pursed her lips in a smile. "Is this not the perfect opportunity? He wants three Yao Kings, so we will give him three Yao Kings. We shall see whether he has the appetite for them."

Cang Gou listened in complete confusion. Fan Wu, on the other hand, understood at once and smiled as he stroked his beard. "Indeed. Madam Feng's words are reasonable."

"What are you two talking about? Can you explain it clearly?" Cang Gou scratched his head.

Fan Wu and Luan Feng exchanged a smile.

...

The three Yao Kings were quickly chosen, with each of the three great Saint Venerables providing one.

In Fengluo Palace, Yang Kai stared dumbfounded at the three great Yao Kings standing before him, at a loss for words.

It was not that these three great Yao Kings were weak. Among the Thirty-Two Yao Kings of the Ancient Land, every one was an extremely powerful existence. What left Yang Kai speechless was the fact that all three were women.

And each was more charming and bewitching than the last.

One of them was not a stranger. She was the Flower Yao Du Mi'er, whom he had met last time in Luan Feng's bedchamber. This woman's skill at attending to others was first-rate, and Yang Kai had enjoyed it thoroughly last time. At this moment, she was gazing at him with tender affection.

Beside Du Mi'er stood a woman of peerless beauty. Her figure was enchanting and her expression seductive, to the point that even Shan Qingluo could not compare. Her beautiful eyes were bright and misty, like two black holes capable of swallowing a person's Divine Soul and leaving them unable to free themselves. Though she stood there quietly, an indescribable charm lingered around her, making it hard for anyone to look away.

"Hu Fei?" Yang Kai asked tentatively.

The female Yao King was startled, then smiled sweetly, her red lips parting. "I did not expect Sir to recognize me." Her voice was intoxicating enough to make Yang Kai shiver. He felt as if every bone in his body had gone several liang lighter. She truly deserved to possess the bloodline of the Nine-Tailed Heavenly Fox; every movement carried a bewitching allure that made restraint almost impossible. Yang Kai clearly felt heat stirring in his lower abdomen.

He did not know whether to laugh or cry. Only a few days ago, he had been discussing the various Yao Kings with Xi Lei, and Xi Lei had specifically mentioned Hu Fei. He had not expected Luan Feng and the others to send this woman to his side.

Yang Kai turned and glanced at Luan Feng. Seeming to understand the deeper meaning in her eyes, he nodded and said, "Yao King's great name is like thunder in my ears."

Hu Fei giggled. "A little ill repute has dirtied Sir's ears."

Yang Kai turned again to the other female Yao King and saw that her temperament was different from both Du Mi'er and Hu Fei. She did not have Du Mi'er's softness or Hu Fei's seductiveness. Instead, she possessed a cold, breathtaking beauty. Her pupils were strange vertical slits, making Yang Kai immediately think of a poisonous snake. Yet that venomous air did not frighten people at all; instead, it stirred an even stronger desire to fly into the flame, as if one would willingly accept being swallowed alive by her.

Yang Kai's heart stirred. "Chi Lian?"
