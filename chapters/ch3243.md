## Chapter 3243 — This Person Cannot Be Left Alive

Deep into the night, the banquet finally dispersed.

The Qingyang Shrine party followed a maidservant toward their lodgings. Before long, they arrived at a mountain peak rich in Spiritual Qi, with graceful scenery all around and soft moonlight spilling over the earth, bathing the four directions.

A brightly lit palace stood atop the peak. This was where Qingyang Shrine's group would be staying, and the disciples under them had already arrived and gone to rest.

After bringing everyone here, the maidservant respectfully withdrew, and someone else led them on to their guest rooms.

Soon, everything had been arranged. Yang Kai had only just tidied up a little when something stirred in his heart. He took out a Transmission Compass to examine it, frowned, then opened the door and walked out.

After turning a corner, he stopped before another room and knocked lightly.

"Come in," Gao Xueting's voice called from inside.

Yang Kai pushed the door open and entered, only to find Wen Zishan, Luo Chen, Xiao Baiyi, and Murong Xiaoxiao all inside. Puzzled, he asked, "What's going on?"

Wen Zishan held up the jade slip in his hand and gave it a shake. "We have work to do."

As he spoke, he flicked the jade slip over.

Yang Kai caught it, closed the door, and swept it with his Divine Sense. He immediately asked in surprise, "What is this?"

The jade slip contained a list of names, each one matched with a number, leaving Yang Kai completely baffled. He saw his own name inside, and more than once.

Gao Xueting explained, "We came here as team leaders not only to urge the disciples to take the matches seriously, but also to serve as judges."

"Judges?" Yang Kai was taken aback.

"That's right," Gao Xueting said. "The Martial Gathering is divided into three levels: Dao Origin Realm, Void King Realm, and Origin Returning Realm. Leaving the Void King Realm and Origin Returning Realm aside for now, the clashes between Dao Origin Realm disciples cannot be taken lightly. Emperor Realm Masters must preside over them and make the rulings. First, to ensure the matches are fair, and second, to make sure no one is killed by accident during the competition."

Hearing this, Yang Kai understood. Although the Martial Gathering was only a competition, once a fight truly began, the situation could change in an instant, and needless casualties were entirely possible. To prevent that, stronger cultivators naturally had to be on hand to oversee and protect the contestants. For battles between Dao Origin Realm cultivators, only Emperor Realm Masters could intervene or rescue someone in time. Otherwise, if a single Martial Gathering ended with a hundred or eighty dead, it would be an embarrassment for everyone.

Once he understood that, Yang Kai studied the jade slip more carefully and said, "So I am responsible for Arena Eight, for five days in total?"

Gao Xueting nodded. "Mm. The Martial Gathering lasts a full month. During the individual battles, Junior Brother Yang, you only need to take charge of Arena Eight. Remember your schedule. The remaining times will be covered by others in rotation."

Yang Kai nodded to show he had committed it to memory.

So many Emperor Realm Masters had come this time that, even though the Martial Gathering would last an entire month, he only had to watch over it for five days. The rest would naturally be handled in rotation. They could hardly make him oversee it alone for all thirty days.

After a moment's thought, Yang Kai said, "Doesn't this give certain people a chance to play favorites and cheat? If disciples from their own Sect enter the arena, it would be hard not to give them a little extra care."

Wen Zishan chuckled. "Everyone here cares about face. Even with the chance, no one would make it too obvious, and they will do their best to keep things fair. Besides, apart from you judges responsible for each arena, others will also be watching the arenas. Playing favorites and cheating probably won't be that easy."

Gao Xueting said, "In any case, remember this: make absolutely sure no one dies. For everything else, use your own judgment."

Yang Kai nodded. "I'll do my best." He did not take it too seriously. Although this was his first time serving as a judge in his life, with his current strength, keeping two Dao Origin Realm cultivators from killing each other in battle would not be a problem.

Besides Yang Kai, Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao also had the same task. More or less, everyone would have to serve as a judge for five days.

Wen Zishan gave a few more instructions before everyone finally dispersed. Gao Xueting, meanwhile, took out her Transmission Compass, summoned the disciples participating in the competition, and gave them another round of reminders.

At the same time, atop another Spirit Peak, inside a secret room, a burly man sat cross-legged on the floor. Black Qi coiled around him and filled the entire chamber, constantly twisting and roaming like spirit snakes. The sight was terrifying in the extreme.

A knock came from outside the door. The burly man suddenly opened his eyes. His eyes were entirely scarlet-black, without the slightest trace of white; anyone timid who saw them would probably be scared half to death.

The instant he opened his eyes, the black Qi roaming through the secret room seemed to answer a summons and withdrew back into his body all at once. His scarlet-black eyes gradually returned to their original color as well. The black Qi vanished, and he looked no different from an ordinary man.

"Come in!" a dignified voice called. The door opened, and an old man stepped inside.

The burly man looked up at him. "Why has that fellow come?"

The old man frowned. "I am surprised as well. There has been no news of him for more than ten years. Supposedly, he left the Southern Territory long ago. I have no idea why he would appear now."

The burly man said, "What is Wen Zishan playing at? Surely he did not call him here specially because of the Martial Gathering."

"Perhaps it is just a coincidence?"

The burly man snorted coldly. "Coincidence or not, this person cannot be left alive. He will hinder our grand plan."

The old man raised a brow. "Your Excellency means..."

"Prevent trouble before it happens. Find a way to get rid of him."

The old man fell silent for a moment, then frowned. "This person will not be easy to get rid of."

"If he were easy to get rid of, why would I call you here? The Martial Gathering lasts one month. Within that month, come up with a reliable method. You must leave no trace."

"Yes!" The old man cupped his fists. Seeing that there were no further orders, he withdrew. Once he was outside the secret room, he let out a long sigh. If it were only an ordinary Emperor Realm Master, that would be one thing; with enough manpower, they could naturally eliminate him. But that man was different. He controlled the Laws of Space, and even if he could not win a fight, his ability to escape was first-rate. If he were allowed to get away, the news would leak, and all their efforts would fail at the last moment.

This would be difficult! After thinking for a long time, the old man finally made up his mind. From the looks of it, he could only ask the people from that side to act. Only they could approach him without alerting anyone and deliver a fatal blow.

Having made his decision, he hurried away.

The sky stretched clear for ten thousand li, a blue dome washed clean.

In a vast basin ringed by mountains, flags snapped in the wind and heads packed the grounds.

This was the site of the Southern Territory Martial Gathering. At the very least, tens of thousands of cultivators from across the Southern Territory had gathered at Wuhua Hall, and apart from this basin, there was nowhere else that could hold so many people competing together.

Several hundred arenas were scattered throughout the basin like chess pieces. Each had been carefully built and protected by arrays, enough to allow several hundred pairs of cultivators to step up and fight at the same time.

On the eastern mountainside, a row of newly built shelters had been arranged along the steps, with tables already set out. From there, one could take in everything in the valley at a glance. Emperor Realm Masters from the great Sects of the Southern Territory arrived in twos and threes, exchanged polite greetings and modest courtesies, then took their seats one after another.

The people from the three top Sects naturally sat in the highest positions.

On the first day of the Martial Gathering, whether they wished to or not, the leaders of the three great forces had all appeared. An even more important figure would be taking the stage, so they could not afford any neglect.

In the valley, the tens of thousands of cultivators about to participate in the Martial Gathering held their breath and fell silent as tension spread through the crowd. Many looked up toward the mountainside with reverence on their faces, secretly imagining how wonderful it would be if they could sit there one day.

This Martial Gathering was absolutely the best platform for a carp to leap over the dragon gate. As long as one performed brilliantly at the Martial Gathering, those from good backgrounds would receive even more resources for cultivation, while those from poor backgrounds might still rise to the heavens in a single step.

The Emperor Realm Masters on the viewing platform could not appreciate such feelings and continued chatting and laughing merrily.

Suddenly, a streak of light came from afar and descended straight toward the viewing platform.

"He's here." Lei Gu smiled faintly and was the first to rise. Everyone else hurried to their feet as well. Some had been secretly wondering who would dare fly so arrogantly through Wuhua Hall, but once they saw Lei Gu's reaction, they could guess a thing or two, so none of them dared be negligent.

The light landed before everyone, revealing several figures. The man in the lead had an imposing bearing, heroic vigor, and a deep, powerful aura. Astonishingly, he was a Third-Stage Emperor. Those who followed behind him, regardless of cultivation, were all dignified in appearance and extraordinary in temperament.

The people from Star God Palace had arrived, and the one who had come was none other than Xiao Yuyang.

"Greetings, Brother Xiao." Lei Gu and the others cupped their fists together.

Xiao Yuyang smiled faintly and returned the salute. "There is no need for courtesy, everyone. This Xiao has arrived late."

Star God Palace sent people to appear at every Southern Territory Martial Gathering. After all, this was an affair concerning the entire Southern Territory. As the Southern Territory's guardian deity and overlord Sect, Star God Palace naturally had to take part. It did not send disciples to compete, however, but participated in the capacity of supervising and observing.

"Not late, not late. Your timing is perfect." Lei Gu chuckled. Although their cultivation was about the same, Xiao Yuyang had come representing Star God Palace, so how could Lei Gu not give him face?

After inviting Xiao Yuyang to sit and chatting idly for a few moments, Xiao Yuyang said, "The time is about right. Let us begin."

Lei Gu nodded and gave Feng Ming a look. Feng Ming stepped out from the ranks, casually shook out a scroll, spread it open, and held it in both hands. Then he read aloud in a clear voice, "Today, tens of thousands of young talents from the Southern Territory gather here for the grand occasion of the Martial Gathering. Wuhua Hall is deeply honored. Led by Star God Palace, several rules have been drafted, and we hope all participants will abide by them. Any violation will be punished severely, without leniency..."

His voice was not loud, but with the support of Emperor Yuan, it spread through the entire valley, drawing the full attention of tens of thousands.

There were not many rules. Too many restrictions would make the competition meaningless, but the necessary limits were still in place. For example, once someone had admitted defeat, their opponent could no longer pursue them to the death. Likewise, no one was allowed to take any Spirit Pills during a match, especially things that could stimulate potential and make one's cultivation surge. If discovered, not only would the offender be punished, the Sect behind them would suffer as well.

Every Martial Gathering had the same rules. Before coming here, the elders of each Sect had reminded their disciples again and again, so the tens of thousands present naturally knew them by heart, but the necessary procedure still had to be observed.

For a time, only Feng Ming's voice echoed through the valley.

Once the rules had been announced, the rewards came next. This was the true main event. Had they not come here to fight and strive precisely for these benefits? In an instant, the tens of thousands in the valley craned their necks, each one waiting eagerly.
