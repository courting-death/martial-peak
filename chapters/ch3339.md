## Chapter 3339 - Half-Year Term

After a moment of silence, Mu Zhu asked, "May I ask what kind of Spirit Medicine Master wants us to cultivate?"

"Dragon Blood Flowers. You should have seen them in the medicine garden." As Yang Kai spoke, he walked toward the area where the Dragon Blood Flowers were, with the two Wood Spirits following close behind him.

Before long, they arrived. Yang Kai took one look, and his eyes immediately shone with delight as he cried out, "Good heavens."

Several hundred Dragon Blood Flowers were arranged in neat clusters throughout the medicine field, growing amid patches of glittering purple grass and blooming with a deep red light. Just as he had expected, the Dragon Blood Flowers here were no longer the Lower-Level flowers he remembered, but Middle-Level ones!

Six or seven hundred Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flowers!

What did that mean? Back then, when Yang Kai had secretly picked the Dragon Blood Flowers, his entire harvest had only been a little over a thousand. Among them were two Supreme-Level flowers, barely thirty Upper-Level flowers, two hundred Middle-Level flowers, and the rest were all Lower-Level.

Because time had been short, he had refined the Supreme-Level and Upper-Level Dragon Blood Flowers into Dragon Blood Pills and taken them, swallowed the Middle-Level ones whole, and had truly had no time to take the Lower-Level ones, so they had remained in the medicine garden ever since.

Now these Lower-Level ones had unexpectedly all become Middle-Level!

And that was not all.

Apart from these six or seven hundred Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flowers, Yang Kai also discovered many ungraded Dragon Blood Flowers in another medicine field nearby. They were packed densely together, at least a thousand of them, and some already showed faint signs of advancing to Lower-Level.

"Th-this, this..." Yang Kai pointed at that patch of Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flowers. "You did all this?"

Mu Zhu pursed her lips and smiled. "The conditions here in Master's medicine garden are good. Otherwise, they would not be growing so well."

"Then what about these over here?" Yang Kai pointed at the other medicine field, looking puzzled. "Where did you get Dragon Blood Flower seeds?"

Mu Na said softly, "No seeds are needed..."

"No seeds are needed..." Yang Kai was stunned.

Mu Zhu explained, "Mu Na and I cut some roots and stems from the Dragon Blood Flowers and transplanted them here. Master seems to have a Dragon Clan bloodline, so these Dragon Blood Flowers should be of great use to you, yes?"

"More than useful, they are simply..." Yang Kai did not know what to say. He wished he could grab the two Wood Spirits and kiss them fiercely, but Wood Spirits were far too tiny. If he really did that, he might very well frighten them.

After calming himself a little, Yang Kai returned to the matter at hand. "Here is the situation. The origins of these Dragon Blood Flowers of mine are a little improper."

The two Wood Spirits both looked at him, quietly waiting for what came next.

Yang Kai said, slightly embarrassed, "They were all picked from Dragon Isle." He secretly glanced at them, and only after seeing no unusual reaction did he continue, "Now Dragon Isle wants me to make up for their losses, so I have to transplant these Dragon Blood Flowers out."

"You have to return them?" Mu Zhu immediately became somewhat unwilling when she heard this.

The Wood Spirit Race had a special fondness for heaven-and-earth treasures. Yang Kai had always left the medicine garden in their care, and in the hearts of the two Wood Spirits, they had long since come to regard everything inside as their own private property. Of course, if Yang Kai, their Master, wanted to take or use something, that was naturally no problem, but if something inside was to be taken out and given to someone else, the two of them were absolutely reluctant to part with it.

Dragon Blood Flowers were extremely rare and had always only been produced on Dragon Isle. They were much more precious than ordinary Imperial Treasures.

Yang Kai's sudden announcement that he wanted to transplant these Dragon Blood Flowers out made the two of them feel as though they were being forced to part with something beloved. Naturally, their hearts ached terribly.

Yang Kai said, "Originally, I was still considering whether I should keep some behind, but since you have this ability, there is no need to think too much about it." Even if all those Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flowers were transplanted out, there were still more than a thousand ungraded ones in his medicine garden, and Mu Zhu and Mu Na could further increase that number through the Wood Spirit Race's Secret Techniques. It could be said that in the future, the supply of Dragon Blood Flowers would be absolutely endless.

"Must they be transplanted out?" Mu Na asked, pouting.

Yang Kai sighed. "This matter is of great importance. I do not want to, but there is nothing I can do."

Hearing this, Mu Zhu nodded and said, "Since that is the case, we will follow Master's instructions in everything."

Once the discussion was settled, Yang Kai immediately released the two Wood Spirits from the Small Xuan Realm. After arriving on Dragon Blood Island, Mu Zhu said, "Master, please wait a moment. Na Na and I will investigate the situation here first, then make plans."

"Yes, yes." Yang Kai nodded repeatedly and waited where he was, observing the changes in the Dragon Blood Flower he had dripped his Golden Blood on, while Mu Zhu and Mu Na flew off in two directions to investigate Dragon Blood Island.

Only after several hours did the two women return one after the other. They leaned close together and muttered for a while, then suddenly shook their heads and sighed in unison.

"What is it?" Seeing this, Yang Kai's heart lurched. The last thing he wanted now was for anything to go wrong.

Mu Zhu said, "It is nothing much. We only discovered that the Dragon Clan has lost many methods for cultivating Dragon Blood Flowers. If they use their current methods, they will be doing twice the work for half the result."

In ancient times, members of the Wood Spirit Race had once helped the Dragon Clan cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers and accumulated a great deal of useful experience, but as time passed, all of it had been lost. Moreover, many special methods could only be used by the Wood Spirit Race. Even if other races knew them, they would be unable to carry them out.

Yang Kai smiled and said, "You are the experts in this regard. I am afraid no one under the heavens can compare with you. The key is that since we have taken over, we have to do this properly and leave the Dragon Clan with nothing to complain about."

Mu Zhu nodded and said, "Before that, however, I must first ask Master what our goal is."

"The goal?" Yang Kai pondered for a moment, then said, "Back then, I collected one thousand Dragon Blood Flowers from here. Among them were two Supreme-Level, thirty Upper-Level, two hundred Middle-Level, and the rest were all Lower-Level. If I say that is our goal, roughly how long will it take?"

Hearing this, Mu Zhu nodded and said, "That is not too much."

Yang Kai's eyes lit up. Those words gave him tremendous confidence.

Mu Zhu added, "Only those two Supreme-Level Dragon Blood Flowers are somewhat troublesome. As for the others... they can probably be cultivated in just half a year."

"Half a year!" Yang Kai was greatly shocked. "It can be done that quickly?" It was not that he doubted the ability of the two Wood Spirits, only that the timeframe seemed far too short. The growth cycle of Dragon Blood Flowers was anything but brief.

Mu Zhu said, "The prerequisite is that every necessary condition is met. Master also knows what Dragon Blood Flowers need most."

"Dragon Blood!" Yang Kai said solemnly.

Mu Zhu nodded and said, "Correct. The purer the Dragon Blood, the greater its effect on the growth of Dragon Blood Flowers. So we need some Dragon Blood."

"My blood seems to be usable too," Yang Kai said, somewhat uncertain.

"Master wants to draw your own blood?" Mu Zhu's delicate brows furrowed. She did not know why Yang Kai cared so much about this matter, to the point that he would not hesitate to bleed himself to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers.

Yang Kai smiled and pointed at the Dragon Blood Flower that had been transplanted out earlier. "Look at this. I dripped a drop of my own blood on it just now, and it seems to have grown a little."

Hearing this, the two Wood Spirits both flew over and stood to either side of the Dragon Blood Flower, closing their eyes to sense it. Specks of green light suddenly spilled from their bodies, drifting down onto the Dragon Blood Flower like fireflies and wrapping the entire flower disk.

A short while later, the two women jolted at the same time and opened their eyes. Mu Zhu turned to Yang Kai in disbelief. "Master, are you a member of the Dragon Clan?"

"Me?" Yang Kai pointed at his own nose and grinned. "I am not a Dragon. I merely obtained a Dragon Clan Origin and cultivated the Dragon Transformation Art."

"But this Dragon Blood..." Mu Zhu's brows furrowed tightly. She hesitated, then finally only shook her head.

"It cannot be used?" Yang Kai's heart sank. "Impossible. It clearly grew a little."

Mu Na smiled and said, "It is not that it cannot be used. The effect is very good. If it can completely absorb all of your blood, then with our Secret Techniques assisting it, it will definitely be able to grow into an Upper-Level Dragon Blood Flower within half a year. But if you want it to grow to Supreme-Level, that is not something that can be accomplished in a short time. Even if we personally cultivate it, it would take at least a dozen or twenty years."

Yang Kai came to a realization and said, "We will not consider the Supreme-Level ones for now. At worst, we can produce more Upper-Level ones to make up the difference. I collected a thousand back then, so when the time comes, I will just return a few extra to them."

Mu Zhu nodded and said, "That is the only way." Now that the supply of the most important material had been secured, there were no further worries about cultivating the Dragon Blood Flowers. Mu Zhu and Mu Na leaned together and muttered for a while again before Mu Zhu finally said, "Master, please bring the Purple Soul Grass out from the medicine garden."

"What Purple Soul Grass?" Yang Kai looked at them blankly.

Mu Na pursed her lips and smiled. "It is the little grasses in the Dragon Blood Flower medicine field."

Hearing this, Yang Kai slapped his forehead. "So it is those..."

When he had entered the medicine garden just now, he had noticed that the two Dragon Blood Flower medicine fields contained not only Dragon Blood Flowers, but also many small, glittering purple grasses. The purple grasses and the red Dragon Blood Flowers set each other off beautifully. He had not paid much attention at the time, and only now, hearing them mention it, did he learn that they were called Purple Soul Grass.

While casting a spell to move the Purple Soul Grass out of the medicine garden, Yang Kai asked, "What use does this Purple Soul Grass have? Why have I never seen it before?" He was an Alchemist, and an Imperial-Grade Pill Master at that. When it came to the strange flowers and exotic grasses under the heavens, there was no reason for him not to know of one.

Mu Zhu replied, "Purple Soul Grass has long since gone extinct. Only my Wood Spirit Race has preserved some seeds, because it must grow alongside Dragon Blood Flowers. Otherwise, it simply cannot survive. Its presence also has a great effect on Dragon Blood Flowers and can promote their growth. Specifically, this flower and this grass basically complement each other, accompanying and sustaining one another. With Purple Soul Grass present, the growth cycle of Dragon Blood Flowers can be shortened greatly."

Yang Kai's eyes lit up when he heard this. "There is actually such a thing?"

Mu Zhu nodded and said, "Dragon Isle should originally have had Purple Soul Grass, but for some unknown reason, it no longer exists either."

Long years had flowed by, and far too many things had gone extinct. Nor was Purple Soul Grass the only one.
