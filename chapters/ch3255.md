## Chapter 3255 — Please Help Me With Something

Some time ago, Yang Kai had set up many Space Arrays across the Northern Territory, linking them together, so travel was quite convenient. Otherwise, in a mere half a day, how could they possibly find anyone to help? Half an hour later, a single order from Lingxiao Palace had thrown more than half the Northern Territory into turmoil. Countless Emperor Realm Masters were anxious and uneasy, unable to guess why Lingxiao Palace had suddenly summoned them.

Were they planning to burn the bridge after crossing it? But what good would that do Lingxiao Palace? Many people naturally wanted to feign illness, avoid going, or find some other excuse, but the message from Lingxiao Palace left them too afraid to refuse.

Yang Kai had even threatened to wipe out entire Sects. Who would dare treat that lightly? Wenqing Sect had been destroyed by his hand, and the place Lingxiao Palace now occupied was none other than Wenqing Sect's ruins. No one wanted to become a second Wenqing Sect. This fellow was not making idle threats. He was fully capable of destroying other Sects.

People kept teleporting through the Space Arrays to gather at Lingxiao Palace, arriving in groups of three, five, ten, then twenty. The great hall gradually grew packed. When later arrivals saw so many Emperor Realm Masters of the Northern Territory gathered there, they immediately relaxed. It seemed Lingxiao Palace was not planning to burn the bridge after crossing it. Otherwise, there would be no reason to gather so many Emperor Realm Masters in one place.

Some people also gathered around Bian Yuqing to ask about the situation, but how could Bian Yuqing know what was going on? Yang Kai had hurried back, said a few words, then hurried away again. She had no idea what had happened and was completely confused herself, so she could only ignore their questions. Yet no one dared act presumptuously here. Seeing her cold, silent manner, they could only retreat awkwardly, even putting on ingratiating smiles.

She could not help feeling a little emotional. The Emperor Realm Masters she had once had to look up to now had to take their cues from her. This was something Biyu Sect could never have given her. She knew better than anyone that these Emperor Realm Masters did not fear her. She was only a First Stage Emperor Realm Master, and countless people present were stronger than she was. Without the name of Lingxiao Palace behind her, how could such a scene ever happen? She could not help sharing in its glory.

While Lingxiao Palace seethed with activity, Yang Kai had already teleported to the Wild Ancient Land.

Yang Kai appeared in Xiaoxiao's tree house and happened to see a Stone Puppet poking his head out to look his way.

He had no idea which numbered Shi this one was, so he asked, "Is Elder here?"

The Stone Puppet raised a hand and pointed toward Elder's tree house. Yang Kai immediately sped that way.

Before long, he found Elder inside the tree house. Elder looked at him in surprise and said, "Young friend, why have you come back again?"

It had not been long since Yang Kai left, two months at most.

Yang Kai cupped his fists solemnly. "There is something I would like to ask the Stone Puppet Clan to help with."

Elder nodded and said, "No problem."

Yang Kai was stunned. "Elder will not ask what it is?" He had not expected Elder to agree so readily.

Elder chuckled. "From the look of you, it seems you have run into some trouble. The Stone Puppet Clan does not have many friends. When a friend is in trouble, how could the Stone Puppet Clan stand by and watch?"

Yang Kai bowed deeply. "Many thanks for Elder's support. However, Elder should know that this matter concerns the Southern Territory!"

"The Southern Territory?" Elder raised a brow. The Wild Ancient Land was in the Eastern Territory, so affairs in the Southern Territory seemed rather beyond their reach. But when he thought of the Space Array Yang Kai had arranged, he suddenly understood.

"This matter also concerns the Demon Race!"

"The Demon Race!" Elder cried out.

Yang Kai looked at him thoughtfully. "Elder knows about the Demon Race?"

Elder's expression turned grave. "In this world, aside from the Divine Spirits, I fear no one understands the Demon Race more deeply than my Stone Puppet Clan." Divine Spirits had Origin inheritances, and those inheritances contained not only strength, but also the memories and experiences of past generations of Divine Spirits. Thus, every kind of Divine Spirit had absorbed at least some ancestral memories. Even though the Demon Race belonged to a distant age, the Divine Spirits were not completely ignorant of them.

Stone Puppets did not have the convenience of such inheritances, but they lived long lives, and Elder himself had passed through several great eras and once fought the Demon Race in person. How could he not know how terrifying and difficult the Demon Race was?

"The Demon Race has appeared in the Southern Territory?" Elder asked with a grave expression.

Yang Kai said, "More than ten years ago, a Demon Den appeared in the Southern Territory. Someone broke its seal and released countless Demonic Intents. Those Demonic Intents can corrode cultivators, seize their bodies, and live anew, making them impossible to guard against. For the past dozen years, the Southern Territory has been hunting them down. They were said to have been completely purged, but only last night did I realize that was only on the surface. There are still more Demonic Intents hidden in the dark, and they have set an enormous conspiracy in motion against the Southern Territory's cultivators. All the major Sects of the Southern Territory, several hundred Emperor Realm Masters, and tens of thousands of elite disciples have been drawn into it. This Junior was fortunate enough to escape disaster, but I must hurry back and rescue them. If I delay, it may be too late!”

"Such a thing actually happened." Elder was shaken. Although he had not seen it with his own eyes, hearing Yang Kai say that several hundred Emperor Realm Masters and tens of thousands of elite disciples from the Southern Territory had been drawn into this still shocked him deeply. He rose abruptly and said, "A matter involving the Demon Race is not the Southern Territory's matter alone, but a matter for the entire Star Realm. Though my Stone Puppet Clan has long lived in peace in a remote corner, how can we stand aside? There is no time to lose. Let us set out now."

"Many thanks, Elder!" Yang Kai's spirits lifted, and he quickly led the way.

After they descended from the tree house, Elder took his cane and lightly tapped the ground a few times. One Stone Puppet after another immediately walked out of the tree houses, and many Wood Spirits flew over as well.

With a few simple words, Elder conveyed Yang Kai's meaning, leaving the gathered Stone Puppets and Wood Spirits horrified. Clearly, they all knew how terrifying the Demon Race was.

Nothing more needed to be said. Under Yang Kai's direction, they entered Xiaoxiao's wooden house one after another and stepped onto the Space Array.

When Yang Kai built this Space Array two months ago, he had already made some preparations. He had deliberately made it very large precisely in case he one day needed the Stone Puppets to act and could use the array to teleport them. Yet he had never imagined it would come in handy so soon.

The Stone Puppets were too large, so only one could be teleported at a time. Yang Kai arranged for them to leave one by one, then said to Elder, "After you arrive, do not act rashly for now. I do not know how many hidden cards the Demon Race has left, or whether they have reinforcements. Elder, please take them and guard the area around the array. Wait until I summon more manpower, then we will crush the enemy all at once."

"Good. Rest assured, young friend." Elder nodded and was the last to walk into the array.

After sending off the Stone Puppet Elder, Yang Kai shot into the sky without a moment's pause and rushed straight in a certain direction. His Emperor Yuan poured out freely, without any regard for the cost, while he constantly urged the Laws of Space, his figure flickering without pause.

In less than half an hour, a towering palace came into view, grand and majestic. It was the palace of the Wild Ancient Land's Southern Saint Venerable, Luan Feng.

Before he had even landed, he heard an angry shout ring out. "Who goes there!"

A seductive woman flew straight toward him, dressed revealingly, her Yao Qi soaring to the sky. Astonishingly, she possessed the strength of a Third Stage Emperor, not in the least weaker than Yingfei and the others.

Yang Kai glanced at her and found her somewhat familiar. He knew she was one of the Thirty-Two Yao Kings, but he could not remember which one. In his current mood, how could he have the patience to tangle with her? With a sweep of his sleeve, he said, "Move aside."

The Yao King was furious. In the Wild Ancient Land, although she ranked beneath the Saint Venerables, no one had ever dared speak to her like this. Not even the other Yao Kings. When a woman lost her temper, it could be terrifying.

Just as she was about to teach Yang Kai a lesson, she suddenly realized this person looked a little familiar. After a closer look, she was both startled and delighted. Was this not that man? Her anger instantly melted into a smile. "So it is..."

Before she could finish speaking, Yang Kai had already brushed past her and charged on, landing directly inside Luan Feng's sleeping palace. He looked around, then under the shocked gazes of many maidservants, roared, "Luan Feng!"

The Yao King had been snubbed, but she did not dare flare up. Everyone knew this human was connected to the Blood Gate and to the Divine Spirit Origins. They could not curry favor with him fast enough, so who would dare offend him lightly?

Yingfei and the other two must have stumbled into some damned luck to be able to keep following at his heels all this time. It was enough to make others sick with envy. She knew her cultivation was no worse than Yingfei's and the others', and her looks and figure were quite good as well. If she could seize this chance to form a connection with him, perhaps she too might someday gain the advantage of being close to him.

Before those thoughts had finished turning through her mind, she heard Yang Kai's furious roar. Her face twitched. What was wrong with this fellow today? He looked as if he had come to call someone to account, and she had no idea what he intended to do.

She no longer dared approach Yang Kai rashly. She simply hovered in midair and watched anxiously, torn beyond measure. If the Saint Venerable and he started fighting, should she help or not... Offending the Saint Venerable would end badly, but if she offended him, she could forget ever stimulating the Origin of her ancestral bloodline and restoring the might of a Divine Spirit.

“What are you shouting about?” A cold voice rang out, and Luan Feng appeared not far in front of Yang Kai with an icy expression. Her face was so gloomy it seemed a layer of frost could be scraped from it, and the way she looked at Yang Kai was full of disgust and annoyance. Secretly, she felt a headache coming on. Why had this guy come again?

Last time, he had only just extorted a fortune from her. Could it be he still was not satisfied? Was his appetite not a little too large? She was secretly annoyed, so naturally her tone was not very pleasant. But when she saw Yang Kai's complexion, her heart skipped. His expression did not seem quite right. She had not provoked him, had she?

"There is something I need your help with!" Yang Kai wasted no words and came straight to the point.

So he wanted her help. Hearing this, Luan Feng immediately steadied herself. As long as he had not come to make trouble for her, that was fine. She raised a hand and smoothed her sleeve, saying indifferently, "Is this the attitude of someone asking for help?"

He had stormed into her palace and shouted, clearly showing her no respect at all, yet he still had the nerve to say he wanted her help. Although she feared the power behind Yang Kai, that did not mean she could abandon the dignity of a Divine Spirit. She secretly decided to give Yang Kai a show of force, lest this fellow continue to look down on everyone in the future.

"Just say whether you will help or not!" Yang Kai paid no attention to her thoughts, his tone forceful to the extreme.

Luan Feng was both angry and amused. "You have not even said what you need help with. How am I supposed to answer you? If you wanted me to go die, would I have to agree to that too?"

"Naturally, it is something within your ability."

Luan Feng curled her lips. "I do not owe you anything, do I?"

"That is why I said I am asking you to help!"
