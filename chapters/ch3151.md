## Chapter 3151 — Dared Not Meet

Di Mo asked, "Madam Yue, is something the matter?"

He saw Xue Yue's solemn expression, the faint excitement beneath it, and the way she raised her eyes toward the outside of the hall, and immediately realized something.

Yet when he followed her gaze, he saw nothing at all. Nine Peaks was now protected by Xia Ningshang's Star Lord power, so the people of Great Wilderness Star Domain could not possibly force their way in.

Xue Yue forced down the excitement in her heart and walked step by step toward the hall entrance. Seeing this, everyone exchanged glances, then followed her outside the great hall. In an instant, the heroes gathered beyond the doors.

Meng Wuya glanced at Di Mo, and both of them slowly shook their heads, unable to make sense of it.

Suddenly, the Nine Peaks Grand Array hummed and trembled.

Meng Wuya's expression changed. "Enemy attack." The others also tensed as if facing a great enemy.

Xue Yue raised a hand. "No need to be nervous."

At those words, everyone steadied themselves. Looking up, they saw the Grand Array rumbling into motion and parting to either side. It had not been broken by an enemy; it had opened on its own.

Xue Yue controlled the method for operating this Grand Array. Aside from her, only Xia Ningshang could command it. Now, Xue Yue had done nothing, yet the Grand Array had opened by itself. Clearly, Xia Ningshang was responsible.

Before they could understand why Xia Ningshang had done this, a figure slowly emerged from the mist. He seemed to make no particular movement, yet in just a few steps he arrived before them, so fast that no one had time to react.

Every eye fixed on him. In an instant, everyone seemed to have been struck by a body-binding spell and froze stupidly in place. Only Xue Yue quietly gazed at Yang Kai, a complicated light of mingled love and hatred flickering in her eyes as they slowly reddened.

Yang Kai smiled at her, and Xue Yue could no longer hold back her tears. They slid down her cheeks. Though this man had left for several dozen years without a word, he had still appeared before her at the most critical moment, giving her someone to lean on in an instant. She was no longer as lost and uneasy as before, and her restless heart calmed considerably.

"Young Master!" Di Mo's lips trembled with excitement.

"Subordinates pay respects to Saint Lord!" An Ling'er, Qian Ning, and the others also came back to their senses, stepped forward together, and dropped to one knee.

"Greetings, Master!" Li Rong and Han Fei of the Ancient Demon Clan also hurried to salute.

“Yang Kai…” the Hu Sisters raised their hands to cover their red lips and cried out softly despite themselves.

Everyone stared at him in disbelief. No one had imagined he would actually appear here. It felt like a dream.

Zi Wuji said in astonishment, "Brother Gu, could this person be..."

"The Master of Dark Star!" Gu Jianxin answered solemnly, his brow furrowing slightly as he hugged the longsword in his arms a little tighter.

In that instant, the simple, ancient longsword in his arms had actually conveyed a sense of fear. When he looked at Yang Kai, he could not even summon the will to fight. For a sword cultivator, this was all but impossible. Sword cultivators were firm and resolute by nature. When they encountered a powerful opponent, their first thought was usually to fight and temper their Sword Dao cultivation. Yet the moment he saw Yang Kai, his own life-bound longsword warned him, as if telling him that no matter what, he could not possibly be Yang Kai's opponent.

Just how high had his cultivation become? He could even make a Spirit Sword feel fear.

Zi Wuji was stunned when he heard this. So it was him! It was actually him!

Back then, Yang Kai had indeed run wild for a time on Purple Star's main star and had even killed several Purple Star experts. Purple Star later confirmed that Zi Long and his son had both died at Yang Kai's hands. The hatred between the two sides could be called irreconcilable.

However, this was Zi Wuji's first time seeing Yang Kai, so naturally he did not recognize him. Besides, those events were old history. Now Zi Wuji himself had to scrape by under Xia Ningshang's protection, so how could he show any hostility toward Yang Kai?

Yang Kai came before Xue Yue, reached out to wipe away her tears, and said softly, "Don't cry. I'm back."

Her tears really did stop. Xue Yue turned her head aside, refusing to let him touch her, and raised her own hand to wipe the corners of her eyes.

Yang Kai was taken aback, but with everyone watching, he could not very well fuss over it. Waving a hand, he said, "No need for all this ceremony."

Everyone was lifted to their feet at once, unable to resist in the slightest.

"Where is Ningshang?" Yang Kai asked, looking at Xue Yue. Xia Ningshang had guided him here, yet she had not shown herself. Something was very wrong. Coupled with what he had seen earlier, a bad feeling immediately rose in his heart.

Xue Yue lowered her eyes and did not answer.

Yang Kai looked up at Meng Wuya.

Meng Wuya sighed and slowly shook his head.

Yang Kai's expression fell. All his hopes shattered at once, and his heart sank as if plunging into an endless abyss.

"Come with me," Xue Yue said, then walked toward the inner hall. Although she had many complaints against him, when all was said and done, how could this matter be blamed on him? She only blamed herself for drawing their pursuers here and bringing disaster to Tongxuan Continent and Xia Ningshang. She was not an unreasonable woman. Her coldness toward Yang Kai did not come from resentment. More than anything, she felt she had no face to meet him.

If Xia Ningshang really met with some accident, then she would be the chief culprit!

Yang Kai stepped after her and reached out to take her soft hand.

Her small hand was ice-cold, as if it held no warmth. She struggled lightly but could not break free, so she let him hold it, though she still did not dare look at him.

The two of them disappeared, but silence still hung outside the great hall. The long-standing oppression and unease had not eased much with Yang Kai's arrival, because everyone understood that even if Yang Kai's cultivation now reached the heavens and he could drive away Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators, Xia Ningshang's wounds could not be easily healed.

Another small figure slowly approached.

Meng Wuya let out a soft sound of surprise and looked toward her. The next moment, astonishment appeared on his face. He discovered that the small figure was actually a little girl of seven or eight, delicately beautiful and extremely adorable, yet she gave off an extraordinarily strange feeling. After thinking it over, he immediately understood why.

No expression! There was not the slightest trace of joy, anger, sorrow, or happiness on the little girl's face. She was like an ancient well, and it made her seem extremely strange.

A colorful butterfly flew in front of the little girl. She walked up the mountain steps slowly from the foot of the mountain. It looked like she was following the butterfly, but also like she was deliberately experiencing every step of the path, as if the walk itself mattered and she did not want to skip any part of it.

...

"She probably doesn't want to see you." Outside a wing room in the inner hall, Xue Yue stopped and spoke with her head lowered. It was not that Xia Ningshang did not wish to see him; she simply did not dare let him see her haggard appearance. As a woman, Xue Yue could understand. Women always wanted to show their best side to the man they liked.

Yang Kai tightened his hold on her hand. "You've all had a hard time."

Tears once again surged out beyond her control, as if all the longing of these several dozen years, all the grievances she had endured, could dissolve like smoke at those words. Xue Yue shook her head and said, "Ningshang is the one who has suffered most. Talk to her, but don't force your way in."

“Mm.” Yang Kai nodded, turned to face the door, raised one hand, and knocked gently before softly calling, “Little Senior Sister, are you there?"

There was no response from inside, but Yang Kai could clearly sense Xia Ningshang's aura within. It was extremely weak, like a thread that might snap at any moment. The discovery made his heart ache, and killing intent surged through him like a tide. Xia Ningshang's current state was undoubtedly inseparable from what Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators had done on Tongxuan Continent.

All of them had to die! Not one could escape!

"If you're there, could you please open the door?" Yang Kai restrained his murderous intent, his voice still gentle.

There was still no response.

Yang Kai continued, "Ningshang, I'm back. Will you see me? This Junior Brother of yours has missed all of you very much. All these years outside, I often thought of you. Now that I have finally returned, do you really have the heart to keep me shut outside your door?”

He rambled on for a while.

Xue Yue grabbed Yang Kai's arm and slowly shook her head at him. Xia Ningshang might usually be extremely gentle and obedient toward Yang Kai, but when it came to how he saw her, she had her own stubbornness.

Yang Kai sighed, shrugged, and said, "Fine then. Ningshang, rest well. This Junior Brother of yours will go kill off those small fry now, avenge you first, and then come back to see you."

He turned around and gave Xue Yue a look.

Xue Yue understood and turned with him to walk outside.

They had only taken a few steps when a sudden retching sound came from the side room behind them.

Xue Yue and Yang Kai exchanged a glance, and both their expressions changed.

With a crash, the door was knocked open, and Yang Kai flashed into the side room. He helped Xia Ningshang up from the bed, but the feeling in his hands was no longer what it had been back then. The current Little Senior Sister had grown far thinner than before. Although she had not reached the point of being skin and bones, she likely weighed only around seventy jin.

For her height, ninety or even over a hundred jin would have been normal. The bones beneath her thin flesh pressed against Yang Kai's hands, stabbing pain into his heart.

Yang Kai, however, had no mind to dwell on that, because Xia Ningshang was vomiting blood. The bedding was stained a deep red, and the faint black aura that had shrouded her body was now churning endlessly like a living thing. Not only that, some patches had turned extremely dark, as if someone had splashed ink across her body.

This was exactly like what Yang Kai had seen earlier when he looked down on Tongxuan Continent from high in the sky.

The only difference was that those "ink spots" had been spread across the continent, while now they were on Little Senior Sister's body.

He counted them. Neither more nor fewer, exactly seven!

Great Wilderness Star Domain, Netherworld Sect, you are courting death!

Although he did not know why such a sudden change had occurred, Great Wilderness Star Domain had clearly made a move. He had only just broken that Grand Array's Formation Core, and immediately afterward, Little Senior Sister had suffered this vicious blow. There was absolutely some connection between the two.

The ink spots were slowly expanding. They did not spread quickly, but each change affected her entire body. Every bit they grew devoured a little more of Xia Ningshang's vitality, making the black Qi around her body even denser. If they were allowed to cover her whole body, Xia Ningshang would likely face peril.
