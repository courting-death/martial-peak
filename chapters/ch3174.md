## Chapter 3174 — Crossing Realms

After Yang Kai slapped him, Netherworld Sect's Sect Master immediately settled down. The scenery blurred past at incredible speed. Enormous stars swept by one after another, only to be flung behind them and shrink into points of light.

In the space of a moment, the two of them had traveled countless tens of thousands of li.

They suddenly stopped. Yang Kai tossed the old man down, and he hurriedly steadied himself. When he looked around, his eyes narrowed. "This is..."

An enormous black hole loomed before him, churning and writhing like the open maw of some giant beast as it gnawed at this world, as if it meant to swallow it whole.

"On the other side is your Great Wilderness Star Domain!" Yang Kai pointed toward the deepest part of the darkness.

The old man's eyes lit up.

"Want to go back?"

The old man nodded again and again.

"Good. I'll let you go back, but you have to deliver a message for me."

The old man asked cautiously, "What message?"

"Tell that guy to wash his neck clean and wait for me to come chop his head off!"

"This..."

"Scram!" Yang Kai grabbed him by the collar again and, without another word, threw him into the darkness. The old man shrieked in terror. He wanted to resist, but he could not even gather his Saint Yuan, and could only plunge headfirst into the darkness, vanishing in the blink of an eye.

Yang Kai shaded his eyes with one hand and peered after him, then let out a soft whistle.

On the other side of the darkness, three figures stood in place. Apart from the waxy-faced man, the other two were acquaintances of Yang Kai.

Li Jiao and Lu Sanniang!

Both of them were unharmed, with no sign that they had been tortured or injured, but neither had any thought of escaping. In this world, neither Li Jiao nor Lu Sanniang could bring out their full strength, while the middle-aged man before them could.

Suppressing them was as easy for him as pinching snails between his fingers. Whether it was Li Jiao or Lu Sanniang, neither had the slightest strength to resist.

"Whoever you are, you will pay for this!" Li Jiao glared darkly at the middle-aged man, his face vicious with anger. Lu Yuqin had been thrown into the darkness right before his eyes, her fate unknown, so of course he was furious. He only hated that he was a tiger fallen to the plains. If they had been in the Star Realm, he would have already fought this man to the death. Even losing would be better than choking down this frustration.

The middle-aged man ignored the threat and merely said, "Where does that confidence come from?"

Li Jiao sneered. "Because you have no idea who you've provoked."

The middle-aged man finally turned to glance at him, sizing him up. "I doubt you have the ability."

Li Jiao said, "If I did, I would have twisted your head off already."

"You're pinning your hopes on this Yang Kai?" The middle-aged man's brow rose, and he found it secretly amusing.

He had never even heard Yang Kai's name. How strong could the man be? Even if Yang Kai had been lucky enough to become Master of a Star Domain, he probably would not hold that position for long. The middle-aged man had only learned of Yang Kai's existence after speaking with Li Jiao for a while. It was not that Li Jiao wanted to betray Yang Kai, but with Lu Yuqin and Lu Sanniang beside him, he had no choice but to answer some questions.

"Just wait. You'll regret it." Li Jiao sneered and said no more.

No matter where this man came from or how high his cultivation was, as long as he was not a Great Emperor, Li Jiao believed Yang Kai could handle him. Yang Kai had even forced his way through a place like Dragon Isle. What in this world could stump him?

"I hope he's truly as capable as you say. Otherwise, this would be far too dull." The middle-aged man smiled faintly, then suddenly sensed something and turned toward the darkness.

Ripples spread through the darkness. A moment later, a figure shot out from within, arms flailing and legs kicking, his face still filled with terror.

The middle-aged man reached out and seized the figure in his hand.

The old man was still crying out. Only when the middle-aged man barked at him did he finally calm down.

"M-My Lord!" Once the old man saw who stood before him, he cried out in surprise, relief after disaster flooding his eyes. When Yang Kai had thrown him into the darkness, he had thought himself dead for certain.

Who could have known Yang Kai would truly keep his promise and spare his life? More than that, he had even sent him back to the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

"How did you come over from that side?" the middle-aged man asked, looking at the old man curiously as he slowly loosened his grip.

The old man hurriedly said, "This little old man endured humiliation and barely preserved his life so he could return and warn My Lord."

"That person let you come back?" The middle-aged man frowned slightly, seeing through the truth at once.

Netherworld Sect's Sect Master looked embarrassed, but could only nod. "Yes. He had me come over to pass a message to My Lord."

"What message?"

"That boy who doesn't know the immensity of Heaven and Earth declared that sooner or later, he would have My Lord experience his brilliant moves." The old man chose his words carefully, naturally not daring to repeat Yang Kai's original message.

"That's all?" The middle-aged man frowned slightly, clearly surprised. He turned and glanced at Li Jiao and Lu Sanniang. Did that man not care at all whether these two lived or died?

"That's all," Netherworld Sect's Sect Master said with his head lowered.

The middle-aged man thought for a moment, then raised his hand and reached for Netherworld Sect's Sect Master's head. The old man was startled and instinctively wanted to resist, but he knew resistance would be useless. In that brief instant of hesitation, the large hand had already covered his forehead. His entire body stiffened, and even his thoughts seemed to stop.

"I'll look for myself." The middle-aged man spoke indifferently. His Divine Sense surged and poured straight into the old man's Sea of Consciousness, prying into his memories with astonishing means.

Such a method could easily cause irreparable damage to the old man, but the middle-aged man clearly did not care much whether Netherworld Sect's Sect Master lived or died.

The old man's Sea of Consciousness suddenly churned violently, and a ray of light burst from the seawater. An aura that did not belong to the old man surged within that light, then transformed into an incomparably sharp attack that slashed down at the middle-aged man's Divine Sense.

Yang Kai had somehow buried a killing move in the old man's Sea of Consciousness, waiting for the middle-aged man to walk straight into the trap.

"Hmph!" The middle-aged man snorted coldly. Though somewhat surprised, he did not panic. With a movement of his Divine Sense, pure Divine Sense power surged forward to meet the attack.

With a thunderous boom, the Sea of Consciousness boiled. The two bursts of Divine Sense collided and instantly dissolved into nothing. The middle-aged man was slightly astonished, for he discovered that the other party's attainments in Divine Soul power were actually no weaker than his own.

But according to what he had learned from Li Jiao, Yang Kai was only a First Stage Emperor Realm cultivator. How could he possess such powerful Divine Sense?

The two Divine Senses had clashed without either side gaining the upper hand, but Netherworld Sect's Sect Master suffered the consequences. The shock waves from the collision spread out, churning his Sea of Consciousness into utter chaos. The world within his Sea of Consciousness rapidly collapsed as well, and his aura was extinguished.

Like fish in the moat suffering when the city gate caught fire, he had been caught in the disaster. Yang Kai had indeed not killed him, and had even released him, but after this exchange, Netherworld Sect's Sect Master still died an unnatural death in an instant.

There was no hope of investigating anything further. Just as the middle-aged man was about to withdraw from the collapsing Sea of Consciousness, an immense sense of danger descended from above.

His eyes snapped open and he looked up. In the darkness ahead, a vortex suddenly appeared, and within it, a young man crossed the Realm Barrier with a fierce grin, smashing a fist down at him.

"What astonishing courage!" The middle-aged man's expression finally changed, and he cried out inwardly.

One had to understand that this side of the darkness was the Great Wilderness Star Domain, his territory! Anyone who entered this place would be suppressed by him.

Even when he wanted to devour Hengluo Star Domain, he had to proceed slowly and carefully, advancing step by step, and had even sent people to search for Hengluo Star Domain's Origin.

Yet this Yang Kai had simply charged straight over? Was he not afraid of being trapped here forever?

Countless thoughts flashed through his mind in an instant, and the middle-aged man was both shocked and delighted.

If Yang Kai hid inside Hengluo Star Domain and fought him from there, he truly would have no good way to deal with him. They were both Masters of Star Domains, each controlling the power of a Star Domain. Whoever stood on his own territory held the advantage, and here and now, this was his home ground.

Facing the fist crashing down with a roar, arrogance flashed through the middle-aged man's eyes. He connected his mind to the Origin and threw out a punch of his own.

"Dragon Transformation!" a thunderous shout rang out.

Yang Kai's body suddenly swelled, and in the blink of an eye, he transformed into a twenty-zhang-tall half-dragon. The fist crashing down instantly became the size of a small mountain.

The middle-aged man was stunned. Before he had any more time to react, their fists had already collided.

At a glance, the middle-aged man's entire body was smaller than one of Yang Kai's fists, giving the strange impression of a mayfly trying to shake a tree.

Yet their auras were not inferior to each other at all. Both were as towering and majestic as giants.

When their fists met, part of Yang Kai's enormous fist caved in, and the sound of shattering bones rang out. The cracking continued upward, traveling along his arm all the way to his shoulder.

His arm instantly drooped, limp as a noodle.

With that one punch, one of his arms was crippled.

The fact that even his Dragon Transformation body had suffered such an injury showed the depth of the middle-aged man's strength.

However, though Yang Kai had suffered, the other party was not much better off.

The middle-aged man had thought that with the advantage of his home ground and the power of the Star Domain to reinforce him, crippling Yang Kai would be as simple as eating and drinking. Only when their fists collided did he realize how terribly wrong he was.

An irresistible force struck him head-on, and his entire body was sent flying like a meteor. He vanished from sight in the blink of an eye, and no one knew where in the Starry Sky he had been blasted.

"What is going on!" The middle-aged man's eyes went wide, his face full of disbelief. Very soon, however, he understood exactly how Yang Kai had done it.

By all rights, in his own Star Domain, even if Yang Kai possessed heaven-defying methods, he should not have been able to hurt him. But that punch had clearly not been an ordinary strike. It had been wrapped in the other party's Star Domain power.

Only that could explain such terrifying strength.

"Brother Yang!" Li Jiao cried out, both shocked and delighted.

"Trouble's coming, run!" Yang Kai reached for Li Jiao and Lu Sanniang with his other intact hand, scooped them into his palm, then plunged headfirst into the darkness behind him without looking back.

A punch like that could only be used once. There was no way to throw a second. Yang Kai had merely caught the other party off guard. If he waited for that fellow to regain his footing, forget taking Li Jiao and Lu Sanniang away; Yang Kai feared he himself would not be able to leave this Star Domain.
