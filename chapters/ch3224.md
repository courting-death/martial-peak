## Chapter 3224 — How Do You Want to Die

Once inside the hall, Yang Kai wandered around and looked things over. The place had indeed been kept quite tidy. He wiped a hand across the tabletop and found not the slightest speck of dust, making it clear that the two young women had taken great care of it.

He turned back, sat down in a chair, patted the chair back, and looked up. The two young women stepped inside and bowed once more.

"Right, wasn't there an old senior living here as well? Where did he go?" Yang Kai asked, looking at them.

He was naturally referring to Tian Yan, who had reshaped his fleshly body after leaving the Divine Roaming Mirror. Back then, Tian Yan had received Yang Kai's help and used the Life Origin Pill to rebuild his body. Yang Kai had thought he would gain a Great Emperor as a backer, but reality proved otherwise. In the Divine Roaming Mirror world, Tian Yan was indeed an expert comparable to a Great Emperor, but only within the Divine Roaming Mirror. Even after rebuilding his body, he still had to start over from the beginning, almost like a newborn infant, for the Heavenly Way could not be deceived, and the world could not simply produce an extra Great Emperor out of nowhere.

Yang Kai had not understood it at the time. Only after speaking with Yang Yan did he realize that the Vessel of Heaven and Earth could not contain an existence like Tian Yan, so Tian Yan had no choice but to cultivate again from the beginning.

Wen Zishan had originally wanted to choose another residence for him, but Tian Yan selected Spirit Sword Peak as his place to stay.

After that, Yang Kai followed Zhu Lie to the Frozen Earth, entered the Revolving World, made a trip through the Western Territory, entered Dragon Isle, and returned to Hengluo Star Domain. In the blink of an eye, more than a dozen years had passed. Yet when he came back to Spirit Sword Peak this time, Tian Yan was nowhere to be seen. On the entire peak, aside from himself, there were only the two young women in front of him, so Yang Kai could not help feeling puzzled.

"Senior has gone out to travel." A clear voice suddenly came from outside.

Yang Kai looked up and saw a tall figure step inside. She wore a pure white dress, her beautiful hair pinned up with a jade hairpin, looking neat and capable, with a valiant, spirited air about her.

Yang Kai rose and cupped his fists with a smile. "Senior Sister Gao, it has been many years, but your bearing is as splendid as ever."

He was not surprised that Gao Xueting had appeared so quickly. Since Gao Xueting had instructed those two young women to remain here, partly to take care of Spirit Sword Peak and probably partly to keep an eye on him, she would certainly receive word as soon as he returned.

A clear sound rang out, and cold light flashed as a long sword left its sheath. Gao Xueting strode over, rested the blade on Yang Kai's shoulder, and sneered. "Speak. How do you want to die?"

The two young women beside them cried out in alarm. They had never imagined that Elder Gao and this Elder Yang would clash so fiercely the moment they met. How had a sword ended up pointed at him just like that? Could there be some deep grudge between them? They hesitated, wondering whether they should send word to the other Elders, but their cultivation was low and their status humble. How could they possibly have the other Elders' contact information?

Yang Kai's smile stiffened. He swallowed, raised two fingers to pinch the sword's edge, gently moved it away from his shoulder, and forced an apologetic smile. "Senior Sister Gao, what is the meaning of this?"

"You still have the nerve to come back!" Gao Xueting snorted coldly, unmoved.

Yang Kai's expression twitched. "Junior Brother does not understand. Senior Sister, please enlighten me."

"You want to die with a clear understanding?" Gao Xueting looked at him expressionlessly. "Fine, then I will let you die with a clear understanding. Tell me, are you still an Elder of Qingyang Shrine?"

"Naturally!" Yang Kai hurriedly nodded, then said in shock, "Don't tell me I got expelled?"

"Since you are a Shrine Elder, why have you not shown your face for more than a dozen years?"

"It has only been a dozen years or so. That is not very long, is it..." Yang Kai rubbed his nose weakly. "Besides, I am only a Guest Elder. Whether Qingyang Shrine has me or not makes no difference."

“A Guest Elder is still an Elder! The Shrine had only just finished conferring the title on you when you ran off, and from then on there was no news of you at all. Does the Shrine still have a place in your mind?”

“No!” Yang Kai shook his head firmly.

Gao Xueting froze, seemingly not having expected him to give such an answer. A trace of disappointment appeared in her eyes as she slowly withdrew her sword.

Yang Kai grinned. “It has a place in my heart.” As he spoke, he even patted his chest.

Gao Xueting immediately glared at him. "Enough of your glib tongue. If you do not give me a reasonable explanation today, I will petition the Hall Master to expel you as Guest Elder. In any case, whether we have you or not makes no difference."

Why turn my own words against me! Yang Kai was speechless, but he could only smile along and say, "Senior Sister Gao, you know I have Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory. I am tied up with all sorts of affairs over there, and traveling back and forth is not exactly convenient. The Northern and Southern Territories are hundreds of millions of li apart. A single round trip takes several years. Coming back once every dozen years or so cannot be considered excessive, right?"

Gao Xueting snorted. "I told you long ago that you might as well abandon that Lingxiao Palace of yours in the Northern Territory and stay peacefully in the Shrine. A force is not something one person can support alone." Her tone, however, had softened considerably.

Yang Kai naturally knew that one person could not support an entire force. In the past, he might have been able to abandon Lingxiao Palace, but now he had also brought over the people from the Star Domain. More than a hundred thousand people were gathered there. How could he abandon them? Since he had brought them over, he had to take responsibility.

He shot a look at the two young women watching from the side and sent them a quiet transmission. Only then did he nod and bow obsequiously. "Senior Sister, please calm down. Let us sit and talk."

Gao Xueting turned and took a seat, putting away the long sword that had been flashing with cold light. Yang Kai could not help breathing a sigh of relief.

Before long, the two young women served steaming cups of fragrant tea. Yang Kai gestured and said, "Senior Sister, please have some tea. This place is rather simple, and I have nothing good with which to entertain you."

Gao Xueting slanted a glance at him. "So you dislike Spirit Sword Peak for not being good enough? This is the place you chose yourself. If you are not satisfied, you might as well stay at my Purple Bamboo Peak. In any case, my place is very spacious."

"That... would not be appropriate." Yang Kai wished he could slap himself and hurriedly changed the subject. "Just now, I heard Senior Sister say that Senior Tian Yan went out traveling. What does that mean?"

"It means exactly what it says."

Yang Kai asked in surprise, "Did the Shrine send anyone to protect him? Senior is wandering outside alone. Won't he be in danger?"

He was genuinely worried. Although Tian Yan had a Great Emperor's foundation, after reshaping his fleshly body, he had to start everything from the beginning. More than a dozen years had passed, but his cultivation certainly could not be too high. The Star Realm was not like the Star Domain; experts were everywhere. If Tian Yan ran into some villain, he might really capsize in the gutter. If a Great Emperor were killed by an Origin Returning Realm cultivator, that would be the greatest joke under heaven.

"It was Senior's own decision. Who could dissuade him? As for danger... you need not worry. When Senior left three years ago, he had already reached the Saint Realm."

"What?" Yang Kai was so shocked he nearly thought he had misheard.

The path of cultivation ran from Open Origin to Qi Movement, Separation and Union, True Yuan, Divine Roaming, Transcendent, and finally Saint Realm, seven great realms in all. Before Divine Roaming, each realm had nine Minor Stages, while Transcendent and Saint each had three. Advancing step by step, every martial artist had to spend a great deal of time accumulating strength. Even Yang Kai had taken thirty to forty years.

But how long had it been since Tian Yan reshaped his fleshly body? Even counting generously, only a dozen years or so. Three years ago, he had already reached the Saint Realm. In other words, he had spent only ten years cultivating from nothing to the Saint Realm. Now that another three years had passed, his cultivation was probably even higher.

Thinking it over, Yang Kai felt it was only natural. Tian Yan was not an ordinary person, after all. With a Great Emperor's foundation, such astonishing cultivation speed made sense. Ordinary martial artists might encounter bottlenecks, but he would not.

"Although Senior's cultivation is not too high, do not forget what he is best at. Even if he is traveling outside, he is unlikely to encounter danger."

Yang Kai nodded. Tian Yan had been born within the Divine Roaming Mirror world and naturally possessed only a Divine Soul Spirit Body. When it came to studying and using Divine Soul power and Divine Soul Secret Techniques, no one under heaven could match him. In this regard, even Nether Soul Great Emperor was probably inferior to him.

His physical cultivation truly was not very high, but with those strange and unpredictable Divine Soul Secret Techniques as support, he could absolutely fight across several Great Realms.

"Senior really is bold," Yang Kai said with a laugh.

Gao Xueting said, "The outside world is completely unfamiliar to him, so naturally he is unwilling to shut himself away and cultivate in isolation. Going out to see the world will do him good." Her eyes slid sideways, and her tone turned cold. "As for you, why are you running around for no reason?"

How did the topic turn back to me again? Yang Kai was speechless and said awkwardly, "Reading ten thousand books is not as good as traveling ten thousand li, you know. Heh heh..."

"Do not laugh!" Gao Xueting snapped.

Yang Kai straightened his back and sat up solemnly.

The two young women beside them burst out laughing, but when they met Yang Kai's resentful gaze, they immediately lowered their eyes and behaved meekly, as though they had seen nothing and heard nothing.

"Hand it over!" Gao Xueting suddenly extended a hand, her five fingers like white jade.

Yang Kai asked blankly, "Hand what over?"

Gao Xueting sneered. "Stop playing dumb. Back then, you took quite a few spirit flowers and rare grasses from the Shrine. Surely you have not forgotten the task you were given?"

"Oh, you mean that." Yang Kai came to a sudden realization and said with full confidence, "How could I forget? Since it was a task Senior Sister Gao gave me, I had to complete it no matter what. Take a look."

As he spoke, he pushed a Spatial Ring over. While Gao Xueting examined it, Yang Kai said, "The main reason I was delayed in the Northern Territory for so long was that I was refining these things. After that, I happened to enter retreat and cultivate for a while."

"So many!" Astonishment flashed through Gao Xueting's eyes. There was nothing else in the Spatial Ring; it was filled entirely with Imperial-Grade Spirit Pills that Yang Kai had obtained from Ji Ying.

Back then, he had indeed taken many spirit flowers and rare grasses from Qingyang Shrine. It was a mission the Shrine had assigned him, asking him to refine some Emperor Pills. Naturally, he had not forgotten to prepare a few things in advance before returning. Judging by Gao Xueting's reaction, he had passed this test.

"You refined so many Spirit Pills and still had time to enter retreat and cultivate?" Gao Xueting looked at him in surprise. She had assumed these Spirit Pills were all personally refined by him, but with one look, she noticed that something was not quite right. After sizing him up, she asked suspiciously, "Your cultivation..."

Yang Kai grinned. "Senior Sister Gao's eyes are sharp indeed. Nothing can be hidden from you."

He gently urged his Emperor Yuan, and the aura of his cultivation was fully revealed.

This time, Gao Xueting was truly shocked.

Second Stage of the Emperor Realm! This guy had actually attained Second-Stage Emperor Realm cultivation!

How was that possible!
