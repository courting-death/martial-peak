## Chapter 3302 - Ten Thousand Souls Banner, Yin Spirit Appears

"How arrogant!" Red Ghost stared at Yang Kai, dumbstruck, and almost admired him despite himself. In Yang Kai's place, he would never have had such leisure. Not scrambling to break out and flee for his life was bad enough, but Yang Kai was still in the mood to mock them. Did he think he had lived too long?

Xu Changfeng said calmly, "Isolating heaven and earth does not necessarily require some heaven-shaking grand array. Did you think you could rest easy just because you possess a Space Divine Ability? Young man, the world is vast. There will always be things beyond your knowledge."

Yang Kai narrowed his eyes. "Saint Lord Xu, that sounds rather interesting. Should I take that to mean you've already sealed off this stretch of heaven and earth?"

Xu Changfeng smiled faintly. "Why not try it and see?"

Yang Kai nodded, and actually did try. As the Laws of Space surged, he abruptly found that the surrounding world had become an impregnable cage. He could still use the Laws of Space, but he could no longer teleport.

He kept his composure, but inwardly he was slightly startled as he looked around.

He had not noticed the slightest trace of an array being set up here, so how had this surrounding world been isolated so completely that his teleportation could not function? Could there be an expert proficient in the Laws of Space among the enemy camp as well? Only someone equally proficient in the Laws of Space could have sealed off this stretch of heaven and earth without anyone noticing. But that should have been impossible. Gaining even a foothold in the Laws of Space was extraordinarily difficult, and cultivating them was harder still. What kind of person was Sheng Yuzhu? She was an existence who had once contended with Wu Kuang for the position of Great Emperor, yet after being trapped in the Void Crack for tens of thousands of years and painstakingly studying Space Force, she had only barely crossed the threshold.

If Netherworld Sect or Brahma Heaven Sacred Land truly had someone proficient in the Laws of Space, that person would never have remained unknown. Their fame would have spread long ago.

Since he could not figure it out, Yang Kai did not bother dwelling on it. He had never planned to rely on teleportation to break out today anyway.

"Death is at hand. Do you have any last words?" Fu Bo stared coldly at Yang Kai, the murderous intent in his eyes almost solid. His hatred was different from the grudge between Brahma Heaven Sacred Land and Yang Kai. Brahma Heaven Sacred Land wanted to deal with Yang Kai only because two Holy Sons had died at his hands in the Shattered Star Sea back then.

Netherworld Sect had also lost people to Yang Kai, but that was not the real reason Fu Bo wanted to deal with him. The greatest reason was what had happened in the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

The Netherworld Sect of the Great Wilderness Star Domain could be considered the source of the Netherworld Sect in the Star Realm. Only after masters from the Great Wilderness Star Domain's Netherworld Sect came to the Star Realm and achieved success in cultivation did they establish a Netherworld Sect in the Star Realm as well. The two were bound together by an inseparable relationship. Yet not long ago, Yang Kai had uprooted the Netherworld Sect of the Great Wilderness Star Domain. Its Sect foundation was destroyed in an instant, and its disciples were almost slaughtered to the last. When Fu Bo heard the news, he nearly lost his breath.

On top of that, Yang Kai had previously killed Yin Lesheng and Elder Hua Feichen. New hatred and old grudges had surged up together, impossible to tally cleanly.

So what if Yang Kai truly had some connection to the Divine Spirits of the Ancient Land? Back then, Fu Bo could give Luan Feng face, swallow his anger, hide that grudge in his heart, and keep silent. But after the affair in the Great Wilderness Star Domain, Fu Bo could no longer endure. He was determined to kill Yang Kai and avenge this hatred.

Just as he had said, this was a matter of the Human Race. He was indeed wary of Luan Feng, but if the Ancient Land's Divine Spirits and the yao race insisted on meddling in the affairs of the Human Race, there were still people who could deal with them.

"It still isn't certain who will live and who will die. Isn't Sect Master Fu being a little too optimistic?" Yang Kai cast him a sidelong glance, then turned to Qi Hai and shook his head in disappointment. "And you? What is your reason? Do you think that by helping them plot my death, you'll be able to seize the True Phoenix Fire and save your wife?"

Qi Hai's face turned savage. "She's already dead. You killed her. If I don't kill you today, I'll never rid myself of this hatred."

Fu Bo threw his head back and laughed. "Good disciple. Well said."

Yang Kai raised a brow, somewhat surprised. "So you've already joined Netherworld Sect. No wonder you could advance to the Emperor Realm. But Qi Hai, you're wrong."

Qi Hai clenched his fists and gritted his teeth. "How am I wrong?"

Yang Kai said softly, "Your wife's death had nothing to do with me. She died because she entrusted herself to the wrong man."

"Bullshit!" Qi Hai flew into a rage, his face twisted with pain and fury. "Back then, you clearly had the ability to save her, yet you ignored her and let her suffer endless torment until she finally died. All of this is your fault."

Yang Kai sneered. "By that logic, everyone in the world capable of curing your wife's poison is your enemy, because they had the ability but didn't help you. Qi Hai, is there something wrong with your head?"

Qi Hai said coldly, "I don't know whether anyone else had that ability. I only know that you did."

Yang Kai shook his head with a sigh. "And for that, you hated me. Your wife truly did entrust herself to the wrong man. If she were still alive, I wouldn't save her either. Better to let her die sooner, so she wouldn't have to follow a spineless man like you and go on suffering in the future."

"Shut up!" Qi Hai's face contorted, as though Yang Kai had struck a sore spot.

Yang Kai continued as if he had not heard him. "As a man, you couldn't protect your own woman and instead pinned all your hopes on someone else. What responsibility have you ever shown?"

Qi Hai flushed with shame and anger. With a furious roar, he shouted, "I'll kill you!"

That woman's death was the greatest pain of his life, a knot in his heart he could never let go. Otherwise, he would not have gone to such lengths to join Netherworld Sect and find himself a backer. He knew he could not deal with Yang Kai on his own, and since Netherworld Sect also had a grudge against Yang Kai, borrowing Netherworld Sect's strength might give him a chance to fulfill his wish.

And today, they truly had trapped Yang Kai here. With so many experts surrounding him, Yang Kai would surely find it hard to escape even if he grew wings. Provoked by Yang Kai's words and driven by his long-held wish, Qi Hai naturally wanted to kill Yang Kai with his own hands.

As his words fell, he flicked his wrist and summoned a Black Banner. In an instant, Yin Qi billowed, and ghostly cries and wolfish howls filled the world. It was none other than one of Netherworld Sect's two great Secret Treasures, the Ten Thousand Souls Banner.

Yang Kai looked at him coldly, as though at a dead man.

Although he had no hatred or grudge with Qi Hai, since Qi Hai had decided on his own to treat him as an enemy and attack him, Yang Kai naturally would not show mercy. A mere First-Stage Emperor Realm cultivator could be killed with a flick of his finger.

But after Qi Hai summoned this Ten Thousand Souls Banner, he did not immediately attack Yang Kai. Instead, he froze, eyes widening as he stared at his own Ten Thousand Souls Banner.

In that instant, a blurred phantom suddenly drifted out from the Ten Thousand Souls Banner.

It was the figure of a woman, seemingly some kind of spirit body, illusory and ethereal, without substance. Her body was wrapped in an eerie ghostly aura, and her face was pale. Even so, anyone could see that she must have been a peerless beauty in life. Her bearing was gentle and refined, and even that ghostly aura could not conceal her unmatched grace.

"Hm?" Off to the side, Fu Bo, who had been quietly watching the situation unfold and had shown no intention of stopping Qi Hai even when he prepared to act, suddenly stirred. He stared blankly at the woman's ghostly figure, then his eyes lit up and he cried out in a low voice, "Yin Spirit!"

Because of the cultivation techniques and Secret Techniques they practiced, Netherworld Sect dealt with Yin Souls and death year-round. The Ten Thousand Souls Banner had to be refined with Yin Souls, and the more Yin Souls were refined into it, the greater its power.

But while Yin Souls were easy to obtain, Yin Spirits were hard to find. Ordinary Yin Souls had no sentience. No matter how powerful they had been in life, once they died and were refined by Netherworld Sect disciples through Secret Techniques, they would become Banner Souls that obeyed commands.

Yin Spirits were different. A Yin Spirit could be considered another kind of living being. It was also a type of Yin Soul and had no flesh or blood, but it possessed its own consciousness and had enormous room for cultivation and growth.

If a Ten Thousand Souls Banner could obtain a Yin Spirit as its main soul, its power would certainly increase greatly. However, the chances of a Yin Spirit appearing were vanishingly small. Across the entire Netherworld Sect, Ten Thousand Souls Banners were countless, but only a sparse few possessed Yin Spirits, and every one of those was regarded as a treasure by those Emperor Realm Masters.

Yet at this very moment, Fu Bo actually saw a Yin Spirit on Qi Hai's Ten Thousand Souls Banner, and this Yin Spirit seemed more outstanding and purer than any Yin Spirit he had ever seen. Such a Yin Spirit was almost unheard of. For a moment, he was so angry that he gnashed his teeth. If he had known earlier that this nominal disciple of his possessed such a Yin Spirit, he would have found some way to take it long ago. How could he have left it in Qi Hai's hands to be wasted?

After the Yin Spirit appeared, Qi Hai did not drive it to pounce at Yang Kai. Instead, she turned around to face him and quietly looked at him. Although she should have lost all sentience, her eyes actually held a human-like light.

Qi Hai stood frozen in place, trembling all over. Slowly, he stretched out a hand toward the Yin Spirit's illusory face and said in a shaking voice, "Xiuxiu? Is that you?"

Yang Kai's face darkened. "You actually refined her into a Banner Soul?"

Although he had never seen this Yin Spirit while she was alive, anyone present with even a little discernment could tell that the Yin Spirit Qi Hai called Xiuxiu was most likely his wife.

No one had expected him to refine his own wife's Divine Soul. If he had not, how could she have emerged from the Ten Thousand Souls Banner?

The act was utterly devoid of conscience, enough to outrage both men and gods. Even the Netherworld Sect cultivators began looking at Qi Hai differently.

Although they also refined living souls to strengthen the Ten Thousand Souls Banner, none of them had ever laid hands on their own kin, much less their beloved.

"So that's how it is." Fu Bo's expression shifted, as if he had understood something. No wonder Qi Hai had gone out of his way to come to Netherworld Sect back then, offering the entire Qi Tian Stronghold's service to Netherworld Sect as the price for joining the Sect, then later requesting to cultivate the Ten Thousand Souls Banner Secret Technique. So he had wanted to keep his wife's Divine Soul by his side all along.

When a person died, it was like a lamp going out; the Divine Soul would also scatter completely. Netherworld Sect's Secret Technique, however, could preserve the Divine Soul, albeit only as a Banner Soul of the Ten Thousand Souls Banner, and its end was destined to be anything but good. From then on, it would spend its entire existence bound to the Ten Thousand Souls Banner. Not only would it lose any chance at reincarnation, it could also easily be devoured by other Banner Souls.
