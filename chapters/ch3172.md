## Chapter 3172 — There Is a Little Bug

The Moon Blade shattered, and the middle-aged man's brow twitched as a bad feeling struck him. He hurriedly backed away.

It was already too late. The Moon Blade burst apart, Space Force raging wildly as crack after crack split open in the Void.

Those cracks were not scattered at random. Before his eyes, they actually formed three large words: Fuck your mother!

"Space Divine Ability!" the middle-aged man cried out in surprise, shock flashing across his face. Yang Kai's insult, on the other hand, did not bother him at all.

Space Divine Abilities were simply too strange and unpredictable, and far too difficult to cultivate. Besides, how could someone who could wield a Space Divine Ability to this degree be some nameless nobody?

"Could it be him?" A figure surfaced in the middle-aged man's mind. If the man on the other side matched the person he was thinking of, this would be rather troublesome. Spirit Beast Island's Beast Great Emperor was not someone whose prestige could be ignored lightly. That man was a great general under Beast Great Emperor Mo Huang, and if he really had to face him, it would be quite thorny.

But... he had never heard of that person having any connection to a Lower Plane Star Domain. Why would he suddenly appear in Hengluo Star Domain?

He suddenly remembered the family of three who had been swept into this world three years ago. He had not paid them any special attention before, but now it seemed time to have a talk with them. Perhaps... he could learn something from them.

Before the darkness, Yang Kai waited in full readiness.

He waited a day and a night without seeing any sign of the other party counterattacking, and soon found the whole thing terribly boring. He did not know whether his Moon Blade had injured the other party, but after thinking it over, it probably had not. Although the barrier between the two Star Domains had weakened enough for him to launch a strike across worlds, its power certainly would not be too great. The other party had no ordinary methods, so how could he be wounded so easily?

Yang Kai only wondered why he had gone quiet.

He was too lazy to dwell on it. Since the other party was not making a move, Yang Kai could restore his own Star Domain in peace.

The darkness continued to retreat, revealing the Starry Sky it had previously swallowed.

At a certain moment, a sharp gleam flashed through Yang Kai's eyes, and he grinned fiercely. "You've got guts!"

He reached forward and grabbed. A figure suddenly tumbled out of the boundless darkness. Yang Kai frowned, and the moment he saw who it was, he immediately stopped his hand. Then, with a pull and a guiding tug, he drew the figure before him.

"Yuqin?" Yang Kai looked down at the little girl in front of him, his brow faintly furrowed.

Lu Yuqin was not injured, but her face was deathly pale. She had clearly suffered some kind of fright. Her pupils were dilated, and she trembled all over.

Yang Kai called her twice, but she gave no response at all.

With no other choice, Yang Kai urged a bit of his strength to soothe her mind.

Only after quite some time did Lu Yuqin recover. Her eyes gradually regained focus, and when she saw Yang Kai's face clearly, she burst into tears before she could even speak.

"How did you come from that side? Where are your parents?" A faint unease rose in Yang Kai's heart.

"Sir Yang, please save my father and mother."

Yang Kai's heart sank, and he asked in a deep voice, "They're over there?"

"Please, save them." Lu Yuqin clutched Yang Kai's arm, weeping piteously.

"Tell me properly. Stop crying." Yang Kai comforted her for a while.

Only after a long while did Lu Yuqin calm down enough to haltingly recount what had happened over the past few years.

On a certain day three years ago, the Void Passage connecting Hengluo Star Domain and the Great Wilderness Star Domain suddenly erupted. Darkness swept out and swallowed their family of three. By the time they came back to their senses, they found themselves in another Starry Sky.

Only later did they learn that this Star Domain was actually the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

However, with Li Jiao and Lu Sanniang, two existences who had surpassed the Star Domain, their family had not encountered any danger. They simply had never been able to find a way back.

Then, just one day ago, a middle-aged man suddenly found them and spoke with Li Jiao for a long time.

Lu Yuqin did not know what they had discussed. In the end, the middle-aged man grabbed her, brought her to the edge of the darkness, and threw her in.

"What's that guy called?" Yang Kai knew there was someone on the other side of the darkness devouring his Star Domain. Now, with Lu Yuqin's confirmation, he was even more certain that his guess had been right.

Lu Yuqin shook her head.

Before yesterday, she had never seen that man. She only knew that even her father and mother were no match for him. When he captured her, both her parents had tried to stop him, yet they could not even touch the hem of his robes. His strength was so profound it was almost inconceivable.

"What did he say?" Yang Kai continued.

Since that man had thrown Lu Yuqin back, he obviously wanted her to deliver a message.

Lu Yuqin said, "He told me to tell you that if you want my father and mother to live, you must hand over the Star Domain Origin."

"Ha!" The corner of Yang Kai's eye twitched. "Is there something wrong with his head?"

Although his relationship with Li Jiao had become fairly decent over the years, it was nowhere near enough for someone to use Li Jiao as a pawn to restrain him. If Su Yan and the others had been captured, Yang Kai might have considered it.

Besides, even if he really handed over the Star Domain Origin, the other party might not keep his promise to release them. And once Yang Kai lost the Star Domain Origin, he would have no capital left to oppose the other party. In the end, Hengluo Star Domain would still be unable to escape being swallowed.

Had that guy sent Lu Yuqin back with this message just to disgust him?

"Sir Yang..." Lu Yuqin looked at Yang Kai with pleading eyes.

"I'll send you back first. Don't worry, I'll save your parents." Yang Kai patted her shoulder, split off part of his focus to continue suppressing the darkness, then flickered and sped toward Dark Star.

The Star Domain was vast, and anyone travelling through it would normally have to spend a great deal of time on the road. But Yang Kai was now the Master of the Star Domain, so he naturally possessed conveniences others could not. With a single thought, he crossed ten million li.

After a few flickers, Dark Star came into view. With another flash, he appeared directly inside Lingxiao Sect.

A stream of light flew out from a certain Spirit Peak. It was Su Yan, who had sensed Yang Kai's return, and under her lead, the experts of the entire Lingxiao Sect rushed over one after another.

In just a short while, all the high-level members had gathered in one hall.

Ye Xijun stepped forward and said, "Sect Master, there is something I need to report to you." As she spoke, she studied Lu Yuqin, who was leaning against Yang Kai, with some surprise. What she wanted to report was precisely the disappearance of Li Jiao's family of three.

"No need. I already know." Yang Kai handed the sleeping Lu Yuqin to Su Yan beside him and instructed, "Look after her!"

Their eyes met, and everything passed between them without words. Su Yan nodded lightly, then carried Lu Yuqin back to her own Spirit Peak.

Inside the main hall, Yang Kai took a seat, and the others spread out to either side.

Yang Kai swept his gaze around and said, "Everyone has worked hard these years. I have seen all your efforts. Although I did not take part, I still thank you all for everything you have done over the years."

Ye Xijun said, "It is only proper. I only wonder what Sect Master knows about that expanding black hole?"

Yang Kai said, "I only discovered it recently as well. Rest assured, I will handle this matter myself."

Hearing this, Ye Xijun relaxed, and several years of worry finally disappeared. Although Yang Kai often came and went like a divine dragon showing its head but not its tail, leaving the whole pile of affairs for her to handle, he was still very reliable once he truly became serious.

"Husband, what exactly is that black hole, and why is it eroding the Starry Sky?" Shan Qingluo asked in confusion.

Yang Kai said, "The power of the Great Wilderness Star Domain."

"The Great Wilderness Star Domain again?" The group immediately looked indignant.

Ever since the war ended three years ago, the whole Star Domain had entered a period of recuperation. They had thought they could enjoy a few peaceful days, yet before even three years had passed, the Great Wilderness Star Domain had actually popped up again to stir up trouble. This time was even worse; it had actually begun eroding the Starry Sky, leaving them completely at a loss for how to deal with it.

Ye Xijun frowned and said, "Sect Master, how can the Great Wilderness Star Domain erode our Star Domain? Could it be that the Great Wilderness Star Domain is a little higher in rank?"

Yang Kai shook his head and said, "That is not the case. There is no great difference between the two Star Domains, nor any distinction of high or low. This situation has appeared only because someone is pushing it from the shadows."

"Someone?"

"Who?"

"Could it be..."

Yang Kai nodded and said solemnly, "The Master of the Great Wilderness Star Domain!"

The fellow who had clashed with him from the shadows was definitely no ordinary person. Since he could push one Star Domain to devour another, there was only one possibility: that person was the Star Domain Master of the Great Wilderness Star Domain!

Just as Yang Kai was now.

A series of sharp gasps rang out. But they soon remembered that Yang Kai was also a Star Domain Master now, so there was no need to fear too much. It was just... if the Masters of these two great Star Domains fought, who knew how great an impact it would have on their respective Star Domains?

After all, if they fought, it certainly would not be a face-to-face duel. Instead, they would clash while supported by the power of their own Star Domains.

That would inevitably raise stormy waves across both great Star Domains.

"May I ask, Sect Master, which of you is stronger?" Ye Xijun asked the question everyone cared about.

"He is no match for me right now," Yang Kai said calmly.

The area wrapped in darkness had originally been his own territory. Even if it had been devoured by the other party, Yang Kai still held a certain advantage. Driving him out would not be difficult.

"Right now?" Ye Xijun keenly caught the emphasis in Yang Kai's words. "Then what about later?"

Yang Kai laughed. "Who can say what will happen later? Do what one can and leave the rest to Heaven, that is all."

Ye Xijun still wanted to ask something, but Yang Kai had already raised a hand to stop her, a meaningful smile on his face. "There is a little bug."

As he spoke, he suddenly reached forward and grabbed, his movement unhurried.

The Void ahead churned into disorder. Yang Kai's large hand reached into the Void, as if grabbing toward some distant place.

When he withdrew his hand, a white-haired, white-bearded old man had suddenly appeared in the main hall.

The old man looked alarmed and uncertain. He was hunched over and trembling, as if he could hardly stand.

His aura was not very strong either, only at the Origin Returning Realm.

Everyone in the main hall looked at one another, not knowing why Yang Kai had suddenly dragged this old man over. They did not even know where Yang Kai had brought him from. After becoming Master of the Star Domain, Yang Kai's methods had grown more and more unfathomable.

The hunched old man looked around and was startled. The faces around him did not recognize him, but he recognized most of the people here. Panic flashed through his eyes, and he forced himself to remain calm as he said, "Where... is this?"
