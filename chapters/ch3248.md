## Chapter 3248 — Thank-You Gift

Yang Kai was taken aback. If he remembered correctly, this was the woman from Arena Eight in the first match on the first day of the Martial Gathering.

It seemed she was not foolish either. Yang Kai smiled faintly. His first instinct was to refuse. That day, as the judge, it had only been natural for him to help when Yu Zhuo was blasted away, and he had never expected her to repay him. But then again, she had already come all this way; turning her away would seem a little cold. The refusal reached his lips, but he changed it to, "Bring her over."

"Yes!" the maidservant answered and withdrew.

Outside the hall, Yu Zhuo stood respectfully, holding a freshly washed garment level in both hands. She looked rather uneasy, unsure whether Elder Yang would agree to see her. After Yang Kai saved her that day, she had fully recovered after a single day of rest and had wanted to thank him at once. The trouble was that she did not know who Yang Kai was or which force he belonged to. She had spent the past few days asking around and had only just managed to uncover a few clues.

The judge who had saved her and secretly fed her a Spirit Pill was actually a Guest Elder of Qingyang Shrine. Yang Kai's name was familiar to many in the Southern Territory. He had refined Supreme Mystic Pills in the Four Seasons Land, been conferred the title of Guest Elder by Qingyang Shrine, and even clashed and fought with a Star God Palace Elder at the conferment ceremony. News of these events had spread long ago.

It was just that he had kept a low profile over the past dozen years, so people had gradually forgotten about him.

Yu Zhuo had never imagined that she would be fortunate enough to have any connection with such a person, and she could not help feeling surprised. Now that she had found out who Yang Kai was, she had rushed over immediately.

Qingyang Shrine disciples came and went, and after seeing her, they all found her presence a little strange and gave her a few extra looks. Some even asked why she had come. Once they learned that she was here to pay a visit to Elder Yang, they left her alone.

While she was anxiously waiting, the maidservant returned. Yu Zhuo straightened at once and looked at her nervously.

Fortunately, the maidservant smiled faintly and said, "Elder Yang invites you in."

"Many thanks." Yu Zhuo nodded politely.

The maidservant said nothing more and turned to lead the way. Yu Zhuo followed with her eyes fixed straight ahead. Before long, they arrived in front of a side room. The maidservant gestured toward the door, and Yu Zhuo softly drew a breath before stepping inside.

There was only one person in the room, standing by the window with his hands clasped behind his back as he gazed at the distant scenery. Hearing her enter, he turned and glanced over. Their eyes met, and Yu Zhuo immediately recognized him as the judge from Arena Eight.

"Luocha Sect disciple Yu Zhuo greets Senior!" Yu Zhuo quickly stepped forward and saluted.

Yang Kai looked her over, then his gaze settled on the clothing in her hands. Understanding dawned, and he smiled. "Is something the matter?"

Yu Zhuo raised her head and met his eyes openly with her own bright gaze. Respectfully, she said, "A few days ago, Senior was kind enough to save me and even bestowed a Spirit Pill to aid me. Yu Zhuo has come specially to offer her thanks."

Yang Kai waved it off. "It was part of my duty. Lady Yu is too serious."

Yu Zhuo smiled faintly. "Saving me may have been part of Senior's duty, but bestowing a Spirit Pill was an act of kindness. Although Yu Zhuo's mind was muddled at the time, I had not completely lost consciousness. Without Senior's Spirit Pill, I fear Yu Zhuo would not have had the strength to continue the matches over the next few days."

This was the truth. Although she did not know exactly what grade the Spirit Pill Yang Kai had secretly given her was, without that pill, she definitely would not have been able to give her all in the following matches. She strongly suspected it had been an Emperor Pill, because only an Emperor Pill could have such powerful healing effects and allow her to recover completely by the second day.

Yang Kai only smiled faintly and gave no answer either way.

Strictly speaking, as a judge, he should not have given any Spirit Pills to a contestant. However, he had done it without anyone noticing, and he was confident no one had seen through him. Yu Zhuo would not go around publicizing it either, and since she had taken it after the match was over, the matter was not worth fussing over.

Yu Zhuo took a step forward and lifted the garment in her hands slightly. "Senior's clothes have been washed clean. I came specially to return them."

Yang Kai nodded. "Put them down." He made no move to take them.

It was only an ordinary set of clothes. At the time, he had casually taken it out to cover the exposed area around Yu Zhuo's wound and had never thought of getting it back. Still, her conduct left Yang Kai with a favorable impression.

Yu Zhuo obediently placed the garment to one side, then turned and curiously took in the room around her. She did not look timid at all; instead, she seemed poised and at ease.

Yang Kai rubbed his nose. "Anything else?"

Although the door had deliberately been left open to avoid suspicion, it still would not look good if people saw a lone man and woman together in one room. Worse, he was currently a judge. What would people think if he privately met with contestants from the Martial Gathering? If word got out, some petty individuals might well fabricate rumors and slander him.

His words already carried a hint of dismissal.

Yu Zhuo was clearly no fool and heard the meaning behind his words. She hurriedly took another gorgeous robe from her Spatial Ring and said with some embarrassment, "Senior's great kindness is engraved in Yu Zhuo's heart. I did not know what I should give Senior in thanks, but I happened to see a rather nice set of clothes at the market, so I bought it on impulse. If Senior does not disdain it... please accept it." Her voice grew softer and softer as she spoke. Clearly, she also felt that it was not quite proper for a woman to give a man clothing, but she truly had not known what else she could give Yang Kai as a thank-you gift.

Yang Kai's cultivation was plain to see, and when he acted, he had casually used an Emperor Pill to heal her injuries. He was obviously wealthy. With her resources, even if she spent everything she owned on a gift, it would likely still hold little value to Yang Kai.

Rather than waste the money, she had simply bought him a new set of clothes. She had dirtied one of Yang Kai's garments, so giving him a new one in return could be considered a repayment in kind. Whether it was precious or cheap, at least it showed her sincerity.

Looking at that extremely gorgeous robe, Yang Kai did not know whether to laugh or sigh. This was the first time anyone had ever given him clothes, and it was someone he had barely interacted with at that, a woman no less.

If anyone else saw this scene, it would be hard for them not to misunderstand.

He studied Yu Zhuo thoughtfully and said in a flat voice, "As the judge of the Martial Gathering, I will naturally be fair and impartial on the arena. Saving you was nothing more than my duty. Giving you a Spirit Pill was also only a small matter, done because the match had already ended. If anyone else had suffered the same thing, I would have done the same."

In other words, he was telling her not to read too much into it. Yang Kai rather suspected that this woman had approached him with some special purpose. If so, she was a little too practical.

Yu Zhuo nodded when she heard this. "Senior is right."

Yang Kai kept staring at her without blinking.

Although Yu Zhuo did not meet his eyes, she had clearly sensed something from the corner of her vision. Suddenly uncomfortable all over, she lowered her head and said, "If Senior does not like it, I will take it back now."

Her mood dimmed. She gave a graceful salute and said, "I have disturbed Senior. This Junior will take her leave." Thinking about it now, it was only a set of clothes. How had she had the nerve to bring it out? The more carefully she considered it, the more embarrassed she felt.

To her surprise, Yang Kai suddenly said, "Since it is a thank-you gift, how can there be any reason to take it back?"

Yu Zhuo looked up at him, astonished.

Before she could recover, Yang Kai took the garment from her hands. He did not try it on. Mainly, the clothing was a little too gorgeous; wearing it would make him look unbearably flashy. How could Yang Kai possibly dare put it on?

Seeing that he had truly accepted the gift, Yu Zhuo immediately smiled. Two small dimples appeared in her cheeks, and her eyes were clear.

It seemed he had been overthinking things. Judging by her words and expression, Yang Kai already had his answer.

"Then Senior, please rest. This Junior will take her leave." Yu Zhuo saluted again, delighted in both body and mind.

"Wait a moment." Yang Kai raised a hand.

"Does Senior have any other instructions?" Yu Zhuo half-turned and looked at him curiously.

"I heard you mention a market just now. Is there one nearby?"

Yu Zhuo nodded. "There is. Wuhua Hall has a very large market inside, and it sells all kinds of things. Senior has not been there?"

Yang Kai shook his head. He really had not known about it. "Where is it?"

"Below a canyon, not far from here. If Senior wants to go, I can take you there." Yu Zhuo volunteered at once.

"Just point me in the right direction. I will go by myself."

Yu Zhuo smiled. "It is no trouble. In any case, my matches for today are already over, so I have nothing else to do." No one knew exactly what she was thinking; she seemed to have no idea what the words "avoid suspicion" meant. Hearing that Yang Kai wanted to stroll around the market, she became extremely enthusiastic, and at the end she added, "I wonder what Senior wants to buy. I am fairly familiar with that market and can guide you."

Since she meant well, Yang Kai could not very well refuse. After a moment's thought, he simply went along with it and nodded. "Very well. Then I will trouble you."

Yu Zhuo hurriedly waved her hands. "Senior is too polite."

Once the matter was settled, the two walked out. Yang Kai circulated his Emperor Yuan, wrapped it around her, and sped toward the market in the direction she indicated.

He casually asked about her matches over the past few days and learned that she had actually won every bout and had not suffered a single defeat, which made Yang Kai look at her in a new light. In truth, Yang Kai had seen it on the first day. Judging from her performance then, no matter what final result she achieved in this Martial Gathering, she had definitely already caught the attention of many people.

As long as Luocha Sect was willing to let her go, Yang Kai estimated that none of the Southern Territory's three top forces would refuse to take her in. The question was, would Luocha Sect be willing? Definitely not. It was not easy to raise such a disciple; how could they give her up lightly? Moreover, whether she herself was willing to leave Luocha Sect remained unknown.

As they chatted along the way, Yang Kai more or less figured out her personality. She was rather lively and cheerful, and she truly had no schemes in mind. Yang Kai did not dislike people like this. Besides, she was quite good-looking, and her voice was clear and pleasant; having her at his side was not bad at all.

"The Martial Gathering has always been a place where carp leap over the dragon gate. If, when the Martial Gathering ends, someone invites you to join a better Sect, what would you choose?" Yang Kai asked casually.

Yu Zhuo's expression immediately turned solemn. "My Sect has shown me kindness as heavy as a mountain, and my Honored Master treats me like her own child. I will not leave Luocha Sect." After a pause, she glanced at Yang Kai and asked, "Senior, do you want me to join Qingyang Shrine?" Her expression grew troubled, as if she did not know how to choose.

Yang Kai waved his hand. "I was only asking casually. I have no such intention."

"Oh." Yu Zhuo nodded, then suddenly pointed downward. "We are here. That place is the market."
