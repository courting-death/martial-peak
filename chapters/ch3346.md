## Chapter 3346 — You Should Know

On the ruined Spirit Island, Qiong Qi spent quite some time earnestly urging Yang Kai to return the dragon egg to wherever he had found it. Yang Kai not only refused to listen, but gave him a harsh scolding for his trouble.

Helpless, Qiong Qi could only resign himself to his fate.

“Old Qiong, I called you here because I need your advice on how to hatch it.” Yang Kai gave Qiong Qi a solemn look.

Qiong Qi thought, If you're asking me, who am I supposed to ask? This old man may be a Divine Spirit and may have lived for countless years, but I'm not a member of the Dragon Clan. How should I know how to hatch a dragon egg? If he said any of that aloud, however, he might get another faceful of spittle. All he could do was suggest, “Perhaps you should hatch it the way other creatures hatch their eggs?”

“Like this?” Yang Kai promptly lunged forward and wrapped both arms around the dragon egg. He glanced back at Qiong Qi, then scowled before the old man could answer. “Don't you think this looks stupid?”

The dragon egg was as tall as a man, and even with both arms Yang Kai could not reach all the way around it. He really did look rather foolish.

Another idea struck him. He leapt onto the dragon egg and plopped himself down on top. After wriggling into position, he looked down at Qiong Qi. “What about this?”

Qiong Qi's mouth twitched uncontrollably.

Yang Kai raised a hand. “Don't say another word. I know.” This one undoubtedly looked every bit as ridiculous.

Yang Kai slid down from the dragon egg. “Hatching it can't be that simple. If it were, Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun would have succeeded long ago instead of abandoning it. Think harder, Old Qiong. All those years you've lived can't just be for show, can they?”

Qiong Qi sighed. “This old man truly is helpless in this matter. It will largely depend on you, Young Master.”

Yang Kai pulled a long face. “If I had that ability, why would I have called you here?”

Qiong Qi replied, “Actually, this old man thinks Young Master should already know how.”

“I should know?” Yang Kai raised a brow. “Why should I know?”

Qiong Qi smiled faintly. “Young Master possesses the Dragon Clan Origin, after all. Anything to do with the Dragon Clan should come instinctively to you, including how to hatch a dragon egg.”

Yang Kai's eyes lit up. Qiong Qi's words made a great deal of sense. Why were Divine Spirits born with such formidable cultivation and strength? Why did they inherit the cultivation experience and memories of their distant ancestors? Because all of it was sealed within their Origins. When the time was right and they grew strong enough, the priceless inheritance contained within those Origins would gradually be unsealed.

Back then, Yang Kai had managed to shield Li Jiao from bloodline suppression with a Dragon Clan Secret Technique. He could only do so because his Dragon Transformation Art had advanced, allowing him to comprehend the technique from his own Dragon Clan Origin.

If he could comprehend a Secret Technique like that, there was no reason he could not learn how to hatch a dragon egg as well.

“Good, good. That makes a great deal of sense.” Yang Kai felt as though the clouds had parted to reveal the bright moon, and he sank into serious thought.

After a long while, he looked up. “Old Qiong, stand guard nearby. If anyone approaches, let me know at once.”

He could have hatched the dragon egg in perfect safety inside the Small Xuan Realm. All he had to do was hide the Xuan Realm Pearl somewhere, and no one could possibly find it. But this was Dragon Isle, where the Dragon Clan had dwelled for generations. Yang Kai instinctively felt that Dragon Isle was the best place to hatch the egg. Something would likely be missing if he took it into the Small Xuan Realm.

Yang Kai trusted that instinct completely.

“Yes!” Qiong Qi immediately flew off to keep watch over the surrounding area.

Yang Kai circled the dragon egg several times, then reached out and patted it. “I've gone to great lengths for your sake, so you'd better not let me down. If you really can hatch, that will be your good fortune and proof you weren't destined to die. If you can't, then it simply wasn't meant to be. Don't blame the Heavens or anyone else.”

With that, he drew a deep breath and sat cross-legged beside the dragon egg. Clearing his mind of all distractions, he immersed himself in his Sea of Consciousness and quietly reached for the Golden Sacred Dragon Origin Power within him.

He needed to find the hatching method hidden within that Origin.

Silence descended. The only sound was Yang Kai's slow, deep breathing.

An unknown amount of time passed before a resounding dragon roar reached Qiong Qi outside the Spirit Island. He spun toward the sound and saw that Yang Kai had transformed into his thirty-zhang Half-Dragon form. A pair of fierce horns crowned his head, dragon scales covered every patch of exposed skin, and dazzling golden light blazed from his entire body. Behind him, a dragon tail swayed lazily back and forth, whipping clouds of dust into the air.

“Huh?” Qiong Qi found Yang Kai's condition rather strange. The Dragon Transformation Art seemed to have activated unconsciously, while Yang Kai himself had entered an ethereal trance.

Huff... Huff... Each breath sent visible gusts surging from Yang Kai's nostrils. He lay down on his side and curled his body around the dragon egg, coiling his tail around it to shelter it in his embrace.

Was he really going to hatch it like this? Qiong Qi stared, dumbfounded. The dragon egg was clearly devoid of life, so what good could any of this do?

Qiong Qi shook his head and sighed, unable to understand why Yang Kai cared so much. After all, even if the egg really hatched, so what? It was Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun's child. What did any of that have to do with Yang Kai?

Yang Kai himself did not know. From the moment he left that cave, a voice had echoed deep within him, compelling him to try to hatch the egg. His supposed plan to use it to threaten Fu Zhun and keep her in check was merely a secondary concern.

It was pure instinct—a call from his Golden Sacred Dragon Origin Power.

Time passed. Qiong Qi remained outside the Spirit Island and never once left his post. Fortunately, the Dragon Clan was small and few people ever passed through this area, so the days went by without incident.

Occasionally, he checked on Yang Kai, who seemed to have fallen into an extraordinarily deep sleep. Qiong Qi wondered whether even shouting in his ear would be enough to wake him.

Yang Kai never moved from that posture, his body curled protectively around the dragon egg. From afar, he could have been a true Great Dragon in slumber. Even Fu Zhun and Zhu Yan could scarcely rival the majesty of his Dragon Pressure.

Two months passed in the blink of an eye. One day, as Qiong Qi gazed at the setting sun, he sensed something unusual behind him. He turned around and started in alarm.

Yang Kai had pushed his upper body upright, and his eyes had opened at some point. Terrifying golden light blazed from them like two miniature suns, too bright to look at directly.

Then, to Qiong Qi's astonishment, he opened his mouth and spewed out a great spray of Golden Blood.

The Golden Blood drenched the pitch-black dragon egg, dyeing its shell gold.

Qiong Qi's eyelids twitched violently. What in the world was Yang Kai doing? After two months of observation, Qiong Qi was certain Yang Kai had found the hatching method within his Dragon Clan Origin, but he had never heard of Dragons hatching their eggs this way. Wasn't this self-mutilation?

What happened next, however, left him astonished.

The dragon egg was actually absorbing Yang Kai's Golden Blood. The blood coating its shell seeped inside at a rate visible to the naked eye. In less time than it took to drink a cup of tea, the egg was glossy black once more.

Yang Kai lowered himself again and sank back into a deep sleep.

Qiong Qi clicked his tongue in wonder. Could the dragon egg truly still be saved? If it were dead, there was no reason for it to absorb Yang Kai's Golden Blood. Yet Qiong Qi had examined it himself and found no trace of life.

What exactly was going on?

Puzzled, Qiong Qi began watching even more closely.

From that day onward, Yang Kai awakened once every three to five days—though “awakened” was not quite the right word. His eyes would open, but he seemed to register only the dragon egg. After spitting out a mouthful of Golden Blood, he would sink back into his deep sleep.

Each mouthful of Golden Blood was completely absorbed by the dragon egg within moments, and then... nothing.

This continued for three whole months. Then, one day, Qiong Qi sensed a faint pulse of life within the dragon egg. It flickered like a candle flame in a storm, liable to go out at any moment, but it was undeniably real.

It really could still be saved! Qiong Qi was stunned. Yang Kai had been telling the truth when he said there was life left in the egg, but why had Qiong Qi failed to sense it at the time? Was it because Yang Kai possessed the Dragon Clan Origin?

Over the following month, the vitality inside the dragon egg grew stronger and more vigorous, until it seemed as though the Dragon within might break out of its shell at any moment.

Nor was that the only change. Strange lines appeared across the pitch-black shell. At first glance, they looked like some kind of design, but they were impossible to make sense of and seemed profoundly mysterious.

The lines glowed and flickered, releasing an immensely powerful aura each time they flared and faded.

Qiong Qi had no doubt that if the little fellow inside the egg were truly born, it would astonish the entire Dragon Clan. And why wouldn't it? This was Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun's child, the offspring of two Tenth-Tier Great Dragons. How could a child of such parents be anything but extraordinary from birth?

But... would it truly be born? The matter had nothing to do with Qiong Qi, yet after watching it come this far, even he could not help feeling nervous and expectant. Every day, he watched Yang Kai and the dragon egg intently, never looking away for even a moment.

Green Wood Island, Zhu Yan's dwelling place.

A streak of white light swept through the air. Fu Zhun descended before Zhu Yan and gazed at him in silence.

Zhu Yan looked just as he had when Yang Kai last saw him. He stood on one leg beneath the enormous tree root, fully absorbed in his cultivation. Sensing Fu Zhun's aura, he opened his eyes and found her standing there with her red lips gently pursed, as though she wanted to speak but could not bring herself to do so.

He could not help chuckling. “You've come?”

Although they were an old married couple, they usually lived apart on their respective Spirit Islands. Fu Zhun had occasionally visited him in the early years, but she had not set foot here since that incident.

“Is something the matter?” Zhu Yan ended his cultivation and walked over to her with his hands clasped behind his back.

Fu Zhun’s gaze wavered as she turned her head aside. “There’s… something I need to discuss.”

Zhu Yan could not help marveling. Even he had never seen Fu Zhun behave this way before. Why did she look so guilty? He chuckled. “Whatever it is, just say it. We're hardly strangers.”
