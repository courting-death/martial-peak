## Chapter 3262 — Trap

Hearing Yang Kai's transmission, the Elder's expression shifted. He immediately murmured a few instructions to the eight Stone Puppets, and the next instant, all nine members of the Stone Puppet Clan scattered, spreading out in every direction.

In the crown of a tree, a Wuhua Hall Elder possessed by Demonic Intent carefully drew in his aura and concealed himself, blending perfectly into his surroundings. Many people sped past above or below him, yet not one noticed a trace of him.

Hidden there, he watched a battlefield several li away, controlling more than a hundred demonized people whose strength had soared as they fought a bloody, desperate battle against a dozen or so Emperor Realm Masters.

Just then, the world before him suddenly darkened. A mountain seemed to loom in front of him, blotting out the light overhead.

The Wuhua Hall Elder possessed by Demonic Intent was startled. He looked up and saw a Stone Puppet several zhang tall staring straight at him.

He had been found!

He had actually been found!

And by a Stone Puppet, no less.

Since when did Stone Puppets have senses that sharp? He was certain his hiding place had been flawless; unless a Third Stage Emperor deliberately searched for him, there was no chance he should have been discovered.

Without the slightest hesitation, he shrank back, about to retreat. But at that moment, something tightened around his foot, and a tugging force came from below, as if a huge hand had seized him. Horrified, he twisted around and looked down, his face filling with disbelief. What had grabbed his ankle was not a hand at all, but a vine.

His Demon Yuan surged, and the vine instantly burst into dust.

But that instant of delay was enough for the Stone Puppet to strike. Its enormous fist smashed down like a mountain dropping from the sky and landed squarely on his chest. In a flash, the Wuhua Hall Elder seemed to be struck by lightning. Bones shattered in his chest, and his entire body plunged toward the ground like a torn sack. Before he even landed, bits of viscera sprayed from his mouth and nose. His neck lolled to the side, and his aura vanished.

Even possessed by Demonic Intent, this man had only been a First Stage Emperor Realm cultivator. Every member of the Stone Puppet Clan possessed monstrous strength. Even a Third Stage Emperor would be gravely wounded if he took one of their punches head-on, much less this man. That single blow killed him on the spot.

The Stone Puppet turned and sped off in another direction, while a murmur-like voice sounded behind him.

Stone Puppets truly did not have particularly sharp perception on their own, but with the Wood Spirits guiding them, it was another matter entirely. The Wood Spirits and the Stone Puppet Clan complemented each other, fighting side by side in every battle, and the Wood Spirit Race's sensitivity to trees was unparalleled. Those Demonic Intents were hiding among the trees, difficult for others to detect perhaps, but the Wood Spirits only needed to commune briefly with the surrounding trees to clearly sense where the ambushes lay and guide the Stone Puppets straight to them.

The nine Stone Puppets moved together, dragging out one Demonic Intent after another from where they were hidden throughout Wuhua Hall. Some were killed, others wounded. Without these Demonic Intents driving them, the demonized people soon lost their ability to fight as well. One by one, they stood foolishly in place and let others knock them unconscious, then dropped from the sky like falling rain.

Yang Kai swept a cold glance around, and his eyes soon fixed on a certain direction.

That was the valley where the Martial Gathering was being held. When he had left one day ago, Demonic Qi had already been churning there, shrouding the place in pitch-black darkness. Now that he had returned and looked again, it was even more violent than yesterday.

Wen Zishan and the others were trapped inside that valley.

The situation here was already settled. Those possessed by Demonic Intent had more or less been wiped out, and without them, the remaining demonized people could no longer cause much trouble. Yang Kai hesitated no longer and flew straight toward the valley.

Halfway there, three streaks of light rushed over from three different directions and landed beside him. They were Fan Wu, Cang Gou, and Luan Feng, clearly having seen him move and come to support him. No matter how much they disliked Yang Kai, even if they wanted nothing more than to slap him to death, that woman stood behind him, and she was the natural enemy of all Divine Spirits. Back then, before that woman entered the Blood Gate, they had all seen how close she was with Yang Kai, and they knew how important he was to her.

If Yang Kai really died right under their noses, then once that woman emerged from retreat, the three of them would have no good days ahead.

For that reason, Fan Wu and the other two had no choice but to stay at Yang Kai's side in case anything happened.

The four figures sped on without the slightest obstruction and soon reached the edge of the valley, landing on a mountain peak.

As they looked out, all three Divine Spirits frowned, wariness showing in their eyes.

The valley was actually filled with dense Demonic Qi. It churned and writhed like a living thing. No one knew how such Demonic Qi had formed, but one thing was certain: anyone who fell into it would suffer for it.

From the center of the valley came the sounds of battle, as if someone were trapped inside.

"Boy, you ruined my great plan. Do you think you can save them? Wishful thinking." Lei Gu's voice came from somewhere in the valley, drifting unpredictably, impossible to pinpoint.

"Hiding your head and showing your tail, skulking around like a rat. If you have any skill, come fight me openly with blade and spear!" Yang Kai snorted coldly.

Lei Gu laughed. "Childish. This Lord will deal with them first, then come take care of you." As his words fell, the Demonic Qi in the valley churned even more fiercely, and from the valley's center came burst after burst of alarmed cries, mixed now and then with calls for help.

Yang Kai's expression changed. "Junior Sister Murong!"

He had clearly heard Murong Xiaoxiao's voice. His figure flickered as he prepared to charge in, but Luan Feng grabbed him and glared at him. "What are you doing? Trying to get yourself killed?"

"Let go!" Yang Kai snapped, shaking off her hand.

Fan Wu flashed in front of him and blocked his path, saying in a low voice, "Yang Kai, don't be rash. That fellow is obviously trying to anger you and lure you into a trap. If you really charge in, you'll have fallen for his scheme."

Cang Gou also urged from the side, "He's right. The Demonic Qi here is too dense, and the enemy is hidden while we are in the open. We need to think this through."

"Think it through my ass!" Yang Kai sprayed spittle across the three Divine Spirits' faces, his expression savage. "My friends are all inside, and I don't know how much longer they can hold out. If I don't go save them, am I supposed to just stand here and watch?"

"But charging in like this is no answer either. At the very least, we need to know what's happening inside." Fan Wu's brow furrowed. "How about this? Call everyone outside over, and we'll join forces to disperse the Demonic Qi in the valley. Once we do, the situation will be plain to see."

Yang Kai's face was grim, but he did not insist further. It seemed he also knew this was the only option for now.

But just then, Murong Xiaoxiao's cry of alarm rang out again.

"Lei Gu, you're courting death!" Yang Kai's expression turned ruthless. The Laws of Space rippled, and he vanished on the spot. His voice came from within the boundless Demonic Qi; he had already charged in, heedless of everything.

Fan Wu and the others were instantly dumbfounded.

Luan Feng was so angry her chest heaved, and she said with bitter disappointment, "Fool!"

Cang Gou shook his head as well, helplessness written all over his face, as if this brat simply was not worth plotting with.

Fan Wu's expression went black. Gritting his teeth, he said, "What are you standing there for? Follow him."

Whether they liked it or not, Yang Kai had already rushed in, so they had to follow. Fortunately, the three of them were Divine Spirits and were naturally highly resistant to Demonic Qi. For a short time, they did not fear its corrosion too much, but over a longer period, no one could guarantee anything. More importantly, the one they worried about most was Yang Kai. Who knew what sinister scheme Lei Gu had prepared for him? He had actually plunged headfirst into such an obvious trap. It was a miracle he had survived this long.

The moment the three Divine Spirits stepped into the Demonic Qi, their hearts sank. In that single instant, they lost all sense of each other's positions. Surging Demonic Qi filled every direction, making east, south, west, and north impossible to distinguish. Even up, down, left, and right blurred together.

Waves of ghostly wails and wolfish howls rang beside their ears, disturbing their minds. Killing intent surged everywhere, while faint black shadows darted back and forth like fish, so fast their traces could not be grasped.

With such favorable terrain and conditions, the demonized people could unleash strength beyond their limits, while the three Divine Spirits had to split their attention between guarding themselves and resisting the corrosion of the Demonic Qi. As one side rose and the other fell, the situation was far from optimistic.

The three of them were still loyal enough. After entering, they kept calling Yang Kai's name, hoping for a response, but between heaven and earth, it seemed as if each of them had been left entirely alone. Where was there any sign of anyone else?

In the darkness, Yang Kai's figure flickered in and out as he flew forward. Murong Xiaoxiao's cries of alarm kept ringing beside his ears, as if she were fighting someone. Yet when he followed the sound, he never saw so much as a shadow.

All of a sudden, Murong Xiaoxiao let out a scream, only for it to cut off abruptly. A vein jumped on Yang Kai's forehead, and he stopped in midair.

The surroundings fell silent. No matter how Yang Kai strained to listen, he could no longer hear the slightest sound. That scream, cut short so suddenly, seemed to declare that Murong Xiaoxiao had already met with disaster.

Yang Kai's face twisted. He raised his hand and summoned the Myriad Sword. As his Emperor Yuan surged, a heaven-shaking sword light slashed down, and he roared, "Lei Gu, get out here!"

Lei Gu gave no response at all. Instead, from the place the sword light swept through, another person's scream rang out.

Yang Kai froze, recognizing Xiao Baiyi's voice, then flashed over at once. There, a man in white leaned against a large tree, drenched in blood. One hand covered the wound on his body, yet no matter what he did, he could not stop the blood from pouring out. A pool of crimson had gathered beneath him.

"Xiao Bai!" Yang Kai stepped up to him, his voice trembling.

Xiao Baiyi looked up, his face utterly bloodless. When he saw Yang Kai, he gave a bleak smile. "Junior Brother Yang..."

"Your wound..." Yang Kai stared blankly at the injury on Xiao Baiyi's body, a cut that ran straight from his shoulder down past his hip. It was clearly a sword wound, and the fluctuations of Yang Kai's own Emperor Yuan still lingered around it.

Yang Kai was stunned.

"Junior Brother, don't blame yourself..." Xiao Baiyi smiled at him, blood spilling from the corner of his mouth. "I'm finished. Hurry and run. Run as far as you can."

Yang Kai remained where he was, his eyes fixed on the sword wound on Xiao Baiyi's body. It was as if someone had cast a body-binding spell on him. His eyes trembled faintly, filled with fear and guilt, as if he had never imagined that one casual sword strike of his would actually hit Xiao Baiyi.
