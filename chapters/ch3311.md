## Chapter 3311 — An Exorbitant Demand

If it were possible, Lan He would have thrown caution to the wind and gone to help Yang Kai. Even if she could offer only a little strength, it would still be better than standing here and watching.

But she also knew that now even a mighty existence like a Quasi-Great Emperor had appeared. Forget a mere Heavenly Wolf Valley; even the Eastern Territory's three top forces would likely not dare offend him lightly. If she truly provoked such a person, the consequences would be more than she could bear. Bitterness welled up inside her, and for the first time, she felt an overwhelming hunger for strength.

If she were strong enough, she would not be standing here as a bystander. If she were strong enough, she could repay Yang Kai for saving her life. Yet she was only a First-Stage Emperor Realm cultivator...

Back when she had been lucky enough to advance, Lan He had thought herself one of the world's rare experts and had been secretly pleased for quite some time. Only today did she finally understand that there were always Heavens beyond the Heavens, and people above people.

Above the First Stage of the Emperor Realm were the Second Stage and Third Stage. Beyond those were Quasi-Great Emperors, and above Quasi-Great Emperors were Great Emperors! Then what lay above Great Emperors? Was there an even broader road? Her thoughts wandered, and Lan He's expression dimmed. This time, she felt, Yang Kai truly had little chance of surviving.

Even a Quasi-Great Emperor had appeared, so what path to survival did Yang Kai have left? She simply could not understand why such a figure would make things difficult for him. Although she had not spent much time with Yang Kai, in her eyes, he was not some unforgivable villain. Why had one trip to the Eastern Territory made everyone call for his death? Every enemy drawn out by this affair was stronger than the last.

In the void, Cang Mo looked at Yang Kai calmly and nodded. "Not bad. You actually knew this Lord was here as well. No wonder Yang Yan thinks so highly of you."

Yang Yan? Who was that? Tang Sheng and the others were completely lost. This was the first time they had seen either Wu Heng or Cang Mo. Before today, they had never known such figures existed under the Heavens, and now Cang Mo had suddenly brought up another name, Yang Yan...

Tang Sheng and the others could not help feeling powerless. The world these people lived in suddenly seemed impossibly distant from their own. So what if one was Heavenly Wolf Valley's Valley Lord? So what if one was the strongest expert of Ahan Palace's younger generation? They could not even understand what these people were talking about. It was as if they lived in two different worlds, and the realization left them dispirited.

Over there, Yang Kai sneered. "There are no traces of a great array, yet the world has been sealed off. Neither Netherworld Sect nor Brahma Heaven Sacred Land could arrange such a method, and Wu Heng does not have the ability either. After thinking it over, you are the only one who could have done something in secret."

This part of the world had already been isolated. Although it did not prevent him from using other Space Divine Abilities, he could no longer teleport away. Such an extraordinary method was beyond ordinary people. Since Wu Heng could appear here, Cang Mo naturally could as well. The two of them had been in league from the start.

Moreover, compared with his grudge against Wu Heng, Cang Mo coveting his Xuan Realm Pearl and Mountain and River Bell was the greater threat. Last time, with Yang Yan protecting him and the two of them in a lower plane Star Domain, Cang Mo had wisely retreated. This time, however, they were in the Star Realm. Since he had the chance, how could he let it pass?

Seeing Yang Kai look around, Cang Mo said calmly, "No need to search. Yang Yan did not come this time. If you are hoping to hide behind her again, I am afraid that will not be possible."

Yang Kai laughed. "You misunderstand. I am not looking for Yang Yan. I am only curious what method you used to seal off this stretch of the world."

"Want to know?" Cang Mo looked at him and asked.

"Will you tell me?"

Cang Mo said, "It seems we can have a proper talk."

"How do we talk?"

"You want something, and so do I. If each of us gets what we need, naturally we can talk."

Yang Kai smiled at that. With a pinch of two fingers, the Xuan Realm Pearl appeared between them. "You want this?"

Cang Mo's gaze sharpened. His eyes, always as still as an ancient well, fixed on the Xuan Realm Pearl and turned faintly heated, a rare change. He said nothing.

Yang Kai flipped his hand and put the Xuan Realm Pearl away again, shaking his head. "I am afraid I will have to disappoint you. To tell you the truth, this thing is only useful in the hands of someone who cultivates Space Divine Abilities. Even if I gave it to you, I doubt you could open it."

That was precisely why Yang Yan had left the Xuan Realm Pearl to Yang Kai back then. Otherwise, since this thing could help one comprehend the Great Might of Heaven and Earth and pave the path of the Grand Dao, how could Yang Yan not have kept it?

If this thing was useful to Cang Mo, it would naturally also be useful to Yang Yan. Within that world, its holder was the master, making it the most suitable place to comprehend the Great Might of Heaven and Earth.

Cang Mo said, “Since I want it, I naturally have my reasons. Rest assured, I will not take your things for nothing. Whatever you ask for, I can give you.” He put on an extremely magnanimous air, but in the end, he still had some misgivings about Yang Yan. He did not know exactly what relationship Yang Kai had with Yang Yan, only that last time, Yang Yan had gone all out to protect Yang Kai, without even hesitating to fall out with him. Moreover, that incident had happened in Hengluo Star Domain, which inevitably led him to make certain guesses.

If he truly used his strength to bully the weak and snatch Yang Kai's things, then unless he silenced everyone present and made sure the news never spread, he would have no end of trouble if Yang Yan learned of it afterward. When that woman flew into a rage, he might not be able to withstand it.

But if Yang Kai was willing to trade with him, that would be different. There would be no coercion, no robbery, only a fair exchange where each side took what it needed. Even if Yang Yan found out, she would have nothing to say.

After he spoke, Yang Kai fell into thought.

Reading his expression, Cang Mo felt a flicker of satisfaction. It seemed Yang Kai understood the situation after all. Since Cang Mo had personally come here, Yang Kai should know that he could not keep that bead no matter what. Otherwise, why would he show such an expression? Cang Mo immediately pressed his advantage. "Speaking of which, I work with Yang Yan. Although our relationship cannot be called especially good, it is still decent enough. For her sake, this Lord will not treat you unfairly. State your conditions. As long as they are within this Lord's power, I will not refuse."

Wu Heng's face changed dramatically, and he cried out in a low voice, "Sir..." He could not help feeling resentful. Was Sir not being far too accommodating? In any negotiation, one side named a high price and the other bargained it down, but with a single sentence, he had exposed his own bottom line. Was he afraid the other side would not know to ask for the moon, or what? What if this brat made some outrageous demand?

Wu Heng was not the only one uneasy. Tang Sheng and the others were also deeply shocked.

That was the promise and guarantee of a Quasi-Great Emperor. As long as Yang Kai relented even slightly, he could cling to an incomparably powerful backer. Although Tang Sheng did not know exactly what that bead was or why it made a Quasi-Great Emperor care so much, if he were in Yang Kai's place, he would definitely agree at once.

With such a great figure shielding him, even if he could not run rampant through the Star Realm, probably no one would dare provoke him again.

Even Lan He faintly hoped that Yang Kai could agree. After all, compared with treasures, one's life was the most important thing.

"Is that so?" Yang Kai looked at Cang Mo with a faint, ambiguous smile. "I could not tell you were so easy to talk to."

Cang Mo said, "A long road tests a horse's strength, and time reveals a person's heart. This is only your second meeting with this Lord, so how could you know this Lord's nature?"

Yang Kai nodded. "There is some truth to that." Suddenly, he raised a hand and pointed at Wu Heng. "What if I said I want Great Wilderness Star Domain?"

Hearing this, Wu Heng flew into a rage. "Bastard, even an exorbitant demand should have a limit. Who do you think you are, to actually dare..."

Before he could finish, Cang Mo said, "Although this matter would be somewhat difficult, if this Lord put all his strength into arranging it, it may not be impossible."

Wu Heng's mouth fell open at once, and he stared blankly at Cang Mo, simply unable to believe his ears.

He agreed? He actually agreed?

Sir could actually agree to such an unreasonable demand? After the shock came panic. Wu Heng realized he had underestimated how badly Sir wanted that bead. Since he could even agree to such a condition, the bead's importance was obvious. Then unease followed. If things truly went that way, Wu Heng would probably have to hand over the Star Domain Origin. Without the title of Star Domain Master, he would be nothing more than an ordinary Third Stage Emperor. Where would he go from there? His face turned somewhat pale, and his eyes filled with venomous hatred as he looked at Yang Kai.

He did not dare hate Cang Mo, nor could he reveal even the slightest hint of such intent, so he could only direct this hatred toward Yang Kai. Regret flooded through him. If he had known this would happen, why would he have gone to such lengths on this trip to deal with Yang Kai? Had he not dropped a stone on his own foot? For a moment, he wanted to cry but had no tears.

Wu Heng was not the only one astonished. Even Yang Kai was uncertain.

Cang Mo could actually agree to such a demand!

His expression chilled, and he said, "I also want him dead!"

Wu Heng, already in turmoil, was horrified when he heard this. Pursing his lips, he looked at Yang Kai, then at Cang Mo. His Emperor Yuan quietly surged, and he was ready to flee at any moment. If Sir even agreed to this, what was he supposed to do if not run and wait for death?

Fortunately, after Cang Mo heard this, he frowned and slowly shook his head. "I am afraid that will not do. No matter what, he is still a member of Star Court. If he were killed while fighting someone outside, that would be one thing, but this Lord cannot personally act against him. If I truly did, the Great Emperor alone would not let it pass."

Star Court...

Those two shocking words made Tang Sheng, Red Ghost, and the others shudder. They finally understood where these two powerful yet unknown experts came from. They were actually from Star Court, known as the strongest and most mysterious place in the entire Star Realm.

"I understand." Yang Kai nodded slightly, a sharp light flashing through his eyes.

"What do you understand?" Cang Mo asked with a frown.

Yang Kai said, "So you also cultivate Space Force."

Cang Mo said calmly, "What makes you say that?"

Yang Kai smiled. "I told you that no one who has not cultivated Space Force can open that bead, yet you were unmoved. And after I raised such harsh demands, you could still agree, looking utterly determined to obtain it. In that case, you must have a way to open it, which means you cultivate Space Force."

Yang Kai sighed inwardly. He had originally thought that people who cultivated Space Force were rare, yet recently he had encountered two of them. One was Sheng Yuzhu, who had spent tens of thousands of years comprehending a little of Space Force's mysteries, and the other was actually Cang Mo.
