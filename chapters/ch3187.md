## Chapter 3187 — How Am I Not Worthy

"Impudent!" the feather-crowned man snapped softly. The word struck Wu Heng like a thunderbolt, making him shudder as his face went pale.

"Forgive me, Sir!" Wu Heng lowered his head even further. "Something urgent has happened. I had no choice but to ask Sir to descend here."

The feather-crowned man said, "You are the Master of this Star Domain. What could possibly happen here that you cannot handle?"

Wu Heng lifted a hand. "Sir, please look over there."

The feather-crowned man followed the direction of his finger and immediately saw the boundless darkness several hundred thousand li away, along with Yang Kai's face condensed within it. His eyelids lowered slightly, and in an instant he understood the truth of the matter.

Their eyes met, and Yang Kai trembled inwardly. He nearly lost hold of his projection. Secretly startled, he realized the newcomer truly was a Great Emperor; otherwise, a single glance could not have put him under such pressure. Even the darkness's advance and devouring were slightly hindered, slowing by quite a bit.

Wu Heng said mournfully, "Sir, this man and I have some grudges between us, so he has set his heart on devouring Great Wilderness Star Domain, with no regard for its billions of lives. I am no match for him, so I ask Sir to uphold justice for me."

The feather-crowned man did not answer, but his gaze on Yang Kai grew sharper, as though it could pierce the barrier between the two Star Domains and look straight into Yang Kai's heart. Yang Kai immediately felt a prickling unease down his back. By instinct, he drew on his Star Domain's strength and pushed the devouring darkness forward to block that probing gaze.

"Presumptuous!" Seeing this, the feather-crowned man gave a soft shout, as though rebuking Yang Kai for not knowing the immensity of Heaven and Earth, and for daring to hide his secrets. As he spoke, he drew the longsword at his waist and gave it a gentle sweep forward.

The sword strike was silent and utterly plain, yet it swept up the strength of the entire Great Wilderness Star Domain and raised an invisible barrier before the boundless darkness.

Yang Kai felt as if he had rammed headfirst into a wall, leaving him dizzy and dazed. No matter what he tried, he could not make the darkness advance even an inch. Shock swept through him. What was this man's background? How could he do something like this with such casual ease?

Instinct told him this was not the feather-crowned man's own strength. Even if he really was a Great Emperor, he should still be rejected by the World Laws in this Lower Plane Star Domain, let alone be able to recklessly mobilize the strength of Great Wilderness Star Domain.

Wu Heng was the Master of Great Wilderness Star Domain, yet this man had easily done what even Wu Heng could not.

From the looks of it, not only had the World Laws of the Lower Plane Star Domain failed to reject him, but he could mobilize the Star Domain's strength even more smoothly than Wu Heng. He seemed more like the Star Domain Master than Wu Heng did.

This was a huge problem! Some fellow had appeared out of nowhere, and from the look of it, he had no goodwill toward Yang Kai; if anything, he seemed hostile. How was this matter supposed to end peacefully now?

Yang Kai did not even know what relationship the feather-crowned man had with Wu Heng, but judging from the fact that Wu Heng had managed to summon him here so easily, the two were probably connected somehow.

After the feather-crowned man stopped Hengluo Star Domain's devouring with one sword strike, a barely perceptible look of delight surfaced on Wu Heng's face. He secretly thought that risking punishment to summon this Sir had indeed been the wise choice. Only this man could stop his Star Domain from continuing to be devoured.

But... it was still not enough.

Wu Heng watched his expression and said carefully, "Sir, this boy is arrogant, ignorant, rebellious, and rude. He has swallowed up the boundless Starry Sky of my Great Wilderness Star Domain, and even destroyed many Cultivation Stars within it, plunging countless living beings into misery. I ask Sir to punish him severely..."

The feather-crowned man gave him a cold, gloomy glance, his eyes utterly devoid of emotion. Wu Heng's expression stiffened at once, and not another word of his prepared speech would come out. He had hoped to take advantage of this man's authority and force Yang Kai to return the Starry Sky he had swallowed, but only now did he realize that such authority was not so easily borrowed. Even though he had summoned this Sir down, the man would stop Yang Kai's continued encroachment out of duty, but he would never stand up for Wu Heng without reason. If Wu Heng kept talking, he feared he might be punished as well.

After all, as the Master of a Star Domain, he had failed to defend his territory and lost a vast expanse of it while contending with another. If anyone was to blame for such a thing, it could only be his own incompetence.

"Withdraw!" the feather-crowned man shouted softly toward the distant darkness. His voice was calm, but his tone allowed no defiance.

Yang Kai's mind went hazy, as if something had affected him, and the thought of turning and fleeing actually rose within him. The darkness, already unable to advance, churned wildly as his state of mind wavered.

A cool sensation spread through his Sea of Consciousness, jolting him awake. The endlessly churning darkness stabilized again; the Warm Spirit Lotus had taken effect at the critical moment. Yang Kai was horrified. For some reason, he felt this man was stronger than every Great Emperor he had ever seen.

Such an expert should not have been unknown, so why did he not match any Great Emperor Yang Kai knew? Suddenly, Yang Kai recalled something he had once heard: there were more than ten Great Emperors beneath the Heavens!

Enlightenment struck him.

Calming himself, Yang Kai called out, "May I ask Sir's honored name?"

No matter what this person's background was, he was not someone Yang Kai could contend with right now, probably not even by relying on the strength of a Star Domain Master. Perhaps letting the matter end here would not be a bad outcome. In any case, he had already devoured a large portion of Great Wilderness Star Domain's Starry Sky. Although he had not managed to swallow the entire Great Wilderness Star Domain clean, he had long since vented his anger.

Yang Kai had already prepared himself to stop while he was ahead.

He had asked only out of curiosity. He wanted to know the name of such an expert. Perhaps they would have dealings in the future, and forming a good connection would not hurt.

"You are asking me?" The feather-crowned man stared coldly at Yang Kai, his eyes utterly empty, as though nothing beneath the Heavens was worthy of his notice.

"Exactly." Yang Kai frowned. "I hope Sir will enlighten me."

"You are not worthy to know." The feather-crowned man answered faintly, then raised his hand without the slightest courtesy. "Scram back. If you dare act like this again, you will be killed without pardon!"

A vast, irresistible force suddenly rushed at him from the front. The force was clearly formless and invisible, yet Yang Kai seemed to see heaven-shaking waves crashing toward him. If he did not retreat, they would smash him to pieces.

If Wu Heng had possessed such power, Yang Kai would have lost their confrontation long ago. How could he have run so freely through Great Wilderness Star Domain?

The wisest course now was naturally to take the opportunity to withdraw. Although that power was fierce, as long as Yang Kai retreated to his own Hengluo Star Domain, he should be safe. But the feather-crowned man's overbearing attitude and contemptuous tone made Yang Kai's anger flare.

Yang Kai knew his tone had been fairly polite and his attitude decent. He had already intended to let the matter rest, but who would have thought that giving ground would only make the other party more arrogant! A surge of heroic spirit and unyielding will suddenly welled up in him. He connected his mind to Hengluo Star Domain and urged the Star Domain's strength, condensing the boundless darkness into an impregnable defense.

Hong...

A tremendous sound rang out, and layer upon layer of ripples rolled through the darkness, spreading wildly in every direction.

"Hm?" The feather-crowned man raised a brow, surprise flashing across his face.

Although he had only waved his hand casually, his status spoke for itself. That gesture had condensed the strength of the entire Star Domain. He had thought it would be enough to force Yang Kai back, but who could have known it would instead rouse Yang Kai's courage to resist?

Seeing this, Wu Heng was first shocked, then delighted. He was shocked that Yang Kai actually dared challenge this Sir. Did he truly not know this Sir's background? He was delighted because Yang Kai had recklessly offended him and was likely about to suffer for it.

The darkness shook and churned, and the giant projection Yang Kai had condensed swayed unsteadily as if it might collapse at any moment. In the end, however, it slowly stabilized. His face was solemn and imposing. "How am I not worthy?"

Hearing these words, Wu Heng nearly laughed out loud.

What a boy who truly did not know the immensity of Heaven and Earth. He had actually challenged this Sir. Had Wu Heng known this would happen, why would he have needed to fan the flames? If he simply waited for events to unfold, this Sir would naturally deal with Yang Kai.

He could already imagine the glory of holding two great Star Domains and becoming the Master of both.

"This aura..." The feather-crowned man suddenly frowned, his gaze searching through the boundless darkness. "What Secret Art are you cultivating?"

Yang Kai's heart gave a jolt. This was bad.

Although he was using the strength of the entire Star Domain to drive the Heaven Devouring Battle Art and had not cultivated it himself, this Secret Art had, after all, been created by Wu Kuang and was one of the foremost evil arts under the Heavens. If someone with ill intentions noticed it, nothing good would come of it.

Whether or not he had ever used this Secret Art for evil, using the Heaven Devouring Battle Art at all was an enormous crime.

This feather-crowned man had actually sensed a trace of the Heaven Devouring Battle Art within the darkness. His perception was indeed sharp as lightning.

Yang Kai naturally would not answer that question. Instead, he changed the subject and roared, "How am I not worthy?"

The darkness seemed to grow more violent with the surge of his emotions, showing signs of breaking through its restraint and continuing to devour.

"A little interesting." The feather-crowned man nodded slightly, then suddenly flicked his longsword and thrust straight forward. His entire body transformed into a streak of light. Man and sword became one, crossing several hundred thousand li in an instant before the blade stabbed into the darkness.

Yang Kai shuddered, and the giant projection could no longer hold together. It collapsed with a crash.

At that moment, his mind was linked with Hengluo Star Domain, and he had become the boundless darkness devouring the Starry Sky. Although the feather-crowned man's sword had stabbed into the darkness, it was no different from stabbing Yang Kai himself.

His mind reeled, and his consciousness snapped back to where his true body was. A splitting headache seized him, as though his skull were about to burst. Stars swam wildly before his eyes, and his vision refused to focus.

In his Sea of Consciousness, Sword Qi crisscrossed and ran rampant, stirring up heaven-reaching waves.

The Warm Spirit Lotus spun rapidly, and seven-coloured radiance enveloped his Sea of Consciousness, repairing his damaged Divine Sense.

A cool sensation washed over him, and only then did Yang Kai feel a little better. He looked up, and a chill shot straight to the crown of his head.

At some point, the feather-crowned man had appeared not far in front of him. Yet he was not even looking at Yang Kai; instead, his eyes burned as he stared at Gungun beside him.
