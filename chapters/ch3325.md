## Chapter 3325 - Why Are You Laughing?

Yang Kai had long since become thoroughly familiar with arranging Space Arrays, and he did not bother moving elsewhere. He simply found an open patch beside the pavilion atop the mountain and got to work.

Li Wuyi stood quietly to the side with his hands clasped behind his back. As he watched, his expression shifted again and again: surprise, doubt, shock, then sudden understanding. It seemed that simply observing had already yielded him considerable gains.

Half a day later, the Space Array had taken shape. Yang Kai inspected it, and only after confirming that there were no problems did he look at Li Wuyi and say, "Senior, please appraise it."

Li Wuyi gave him a meaningful look, then strode into the Space Array. The Laws of Space rippled around him as he closed his eyes and quietly sensed its workings.

A moment later, he said, "What sort of restriction did you add to this Space Array? It seems this array can only be activated through a special method."

Yang Kai grinned. "Senior is correct. Any Space Array I arrange requires one of the Space Tokens I specially refine before it can be used. Otherwise, even if someone seizes the array, it will be completely useless to them."

Li Wuyi nodded. "A rather ingenious restriction. It also prevents the array from being turned against you. Mm, it seems to be connected to quite a few places. Only..."

"Only what?" Yang Kai asked, smiling at him.

Li Wuyi frowned, half speaking to himself and half asking aloud, "Only, why do those places feel so far apart?" His eyes suddenly snapped open, divine light bursting from them as he cried out, "This is a Cross-Territory Space Array?"

"A Cross-Territory Space Array?" Jiu Feng was startled as well, her small mouth rounding into an alluring shape as she looked at Li Wuyi. "Are you sure?"

Li Wuyi slowly shook his head. "Although I do not know exactly where this array connects to, those places lie in completely different directions and are separated by a tremendous distance. An ordinary Space Array could never link them together. It far exceeds that limit. Yang Kai, is this truly a Cross-Territory Space Array?"

Yang Kai nodded. "Senior's vision is keen. This is indeed a Cross-Territory Space Array."

Jiu Feng turned to stare at him in disbelief. "You little brat can actually arrange Cross-Territory Space Arrays?"

Yang Kai said awkwardly, "Compared to Senior Li's Space Beacon method, it is somewhat cumbersome and not nearly as nimble."

Jiu Feng could not help rolling her eyes. “If you compare them, his method is the crude one!”

Li Wuyi ignored her and remained on the array, sensing it carefully, clicking his tongue in wonder. He had seen Cross-Territory Space Arrays before, but there were only a few in the entire Star Realm, and all of them had been passed down from ancient times. Some had survived to the present, but could no longer be used. He had once spent years studying them, yet had failed to unravel their mysteries. His Space Beacon had been born from the insights he gained during that research.

He had never imagined that Yang Kai could actually arrange a Cross-Territory Space Array.

"Where does this array connect to?" Li Wuyi asked curiously.

"It can reach the Southern Territory and the Northern Territory. It can also reach the Wild Ancient Land in the Eastern Territory."

Li Wuyi said in astonishment, "It seems you have been to quite a few places."

Yang Kai had to have personally visited those places, left Cross-Territory Space Arrays there, and then arranged this one before they could be linked and used for transmission. How could Li Wuyi not understand that? Yet Yang Kai was still young, and he had already roamed through several territories of the Star Realm. He had even visited a place like the Wild Ancient Land and left a Space Array there. In experience and breadth of knowledge, he had already left his peers far behind.

After pondering for a moment, Li Wuyi said, "I am rather curious. Where did you get that array diagram? Did you comprehend it on your own?"

The foundation of a Space Array lay in that array diagram. Without it, the array was nothing more than a dead object, utterly useless. Li Wuyi had also spent many years trying to comprehend it back then, yet still had not been able to see through it. Now that he had seen a complete array diagram in Yang Kai's hands, he was naturally brimming with curiosity.

Yang Kai thought for a moment and said, "I wonder if Senior knows a person named Yang Yan?"

"Yang Yan?" Li Wuyi raised his brow. "The Yang Yan from the Star Court?"

Yang Kai smiled. "It seems Senior does know her."

Jiu Feng asked in surprise, "What, you know her?"

Yang Kai said, "She and I can be considered old friends."

Old friends... Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng exchanged a look, both somewhat speechless. Yang Yan was an existence on the same level as them. Although Yang Kai's cultivation was respectable, he was still only a Second-Stage Emperor, and in terms of age he was at most a hundred years old. Yet he actually said he and Yang Yan were old friends.

If anyone else had said such a thing, the two of them would definitely have thought he was bragging. But since it was Yang Kai, it did not feel quite so abrupt.

"Both of you know that I came up from a lower Star Domain. Back then, Yang Yan was also in that Star Domain, but her true body had been injured and fallen into slumber, while a manifested clone of hers was trapped in humble circumstances. I became acquainted with that clone at that time, and the array diagram for the Space Array came from her." As Yang Kai spoke, he could not help recalling the past and sighing with emotion. "She took very good care of me back then."

"So it was her!" Li Wuyi was momentarily dazed.

Jiu Feng said, "I have heard that woman is unparalleled in both arrays and artifact refining. I do not know whether that is true, but if it was her, then this makes sense."

Yang Yan might not be proficient in the Laws of Space, but her attainments in the Dao of Spirit Arrays were extremely high. Someone like Nanmen Dajun probably could not compare to her at all. A Space Array was also a type of Spirit Array, so it was no surprise that Yang Yan knew the array diagram. It was only because she did not possess the Power of Space herself that she could not arrange a Space Array.

Li Wuyi nodded. "Speaking of that woman, I have just remembered a rumor."

"What rumor?" Jiu Feng asked, looking at him curiously.

Li Wuyi said, "There was once a person with extremely profound attainments in the Laws of Space, so profound that he even had the qualifications to advance to Great Emperor through the Power of Space. Even I, as I am now, am somewhat inferior to him. This person could be called a peerless genius, one rarely seen in countless ages. Later, however, he vanished, and no one knew where he had gone. According to rumor, this person knew Yang Yan, and the two had quite a good relationship."

After saying this, he shook his head again. "But it is only hearsay. I do not know whether it is true."

Li Wuyi himself could not be certain, but Yang Kai's expression stirred as he listened. Back when Yang Yan had handed him the Xuan Realm Pearl, she had said that it belonged to a friend of hers. As for who that friend was, or whether that person was alive or dead, Yang Yan had not mentioned a thing. She had only given it to Yang Kai because he was proficient in the Power of Space and could make use of the Xuan Realm Pearl's capabilities.

Taken together with what Li Wuyi was saying now, Yang Kai felt that this rumor was very likely true.

The Xuan Realm Pearl contained a world of its own, but it was not born naturally from heaven and earth. Someone had definitely refined it. Any existence capable of refining such a thing would have to possess extraordinarily high attainments in the Laws of Space. Yang Kai judged that his current self was still no match.

Jiu Feng smiled. "Since it is hearsay, it cannot be taken as fact."

Li Wuyi laughed and said, "Fair enough. Still, this array has given me many insights, so allow me to study it a while longer." With that, he closed his eyes and stood there quietly.

Yang Kai and Jiu Feng returned to the pavilion to chat while they waited.

A full hour passed before Li Wuyi finally returned, his face bright with delight. Looking at Yang Kai, he said, "Once I return to Spirit Beast Island, come over and arrange an array on the island as well. That will make it much more convenient for everyone to come and go."

Yang Kai laughed. "I could not ask for more."

Li Wuyi had the Ethereal Jade bead Yang Kai had just refined on him. As soon as a message came from that side, Yang Kai could immediately set out for Spirit Beast Island. Arranging another array would not be too difficult either.

His expression then turned serious. "There is one more matter on which I would like to ask Senior's guidance."

Li Wuyi said, "Go on."

Yang Kai said, "During the earlier battle with the two Sects, this stretch of heaven and earth was sealed off. But after carefully sensing it, I did not find any trace of a grand array, which left me deeply puzzled. Without a Spirit Array, how exactly did Cang Mo seal the world? Although he admitted that he cultivated the Power of Space, from what I observed, his attainments in the Power of Space were nothing remarkable. I fear he has only just crossed the threshold. If so, why did he have such an ability?"

He had to understand this matter. Otherwise, if he encountered another powerful enemy in the future and they sealed off the world around him, he would not even have a chance to flee.

Seeing Yang Kai look at him with such thirst for knowledge, Li Wuyi could not help showing a trace of embarrassment. Jiu Feng, however, began giggling at the side, her body trembling with laughter.

Yang Kai asked in surprise, "Senior, why are you laughing?"

Was there something funny about this question?

Jiu Feng said through her laughter, "If you want to blame someone for this, blame this bastard Li Wuyi. The one who sealed off the world was not Cang Mo, but him."

Yang Kai was shocked. "The two of you had already arrived by then?"

Jiu Feng waved her hand. "That is not what I mean. I mean..."

Li Wuyi sighed. "Forget it, I will explain." He coughed lightly and said, "It is like this, Yang Kai. You also know that there are not many people in this world who cultivate the Power of Space, and it is rare to meet a fellow practitioner with whom one can exchange a thought or two. To put it bluntly, if I had not met you today, then in the entire Star Realm there would be almost no one who could discuss this Dao with me. As the saying goes, it is lonely at the top. If someone asks me for guidance on the Power of Space, I will not be stingy. Naturally, I will teach them everything I can."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Senior is benevolent and righteous. This Junior admires you."

The embarrassment on Li Wuyi's face deepened. Scratching his chin, he said, "A few months ago, Cang Mo suddenly came to Spirit Beast Island, saying he had some questions about the Power of Space and wished to ask me for guidance. Mm, so I chatted with him for a while."

He looked at Yang Kai sheepishly and said, "Leaving the rest aside, one of those questions happened to be how to seal off a stretch of heaven and earth by the most effective means."

Yang Kai's mouth fell open. He stood stunned for quite some time before coming back to his senses. "In other words, Cang Mo was able to seal off this stretch of heaven and earth because Senior taught him how?"

Li Wuyi raised a fist to his mouth and coughed lightly. "I have known Cang Mo for many years. I would not say we are close friends, but our relationship is not bad. He does have a little attainment in the Power of Space, and this... mm, I truly did not expect that his purpose was to deal with you."
