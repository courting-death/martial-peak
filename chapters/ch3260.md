## Chapter 3260 — Breaking the Array

As Yang Kai and the others made their move, Lei Gu, wrapped head to toe in Demonic Qi inside Wuhua Hall, had clearly received the news as well. He turned to a demonized man beside him and glared. "Are you sure you saw that right?"

The demonized man cupped his fists. "Reporting to Sir, I am absolutely certain. There are more than two hundred of them, all in the Emperor Realm." He wore Wuhua Hall robes, making it obvious at a glance that he belonged to Wuhua Hall. His cultivation was also at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm, so he was probably originally one of Wuhua Hall's elders. At this moment, however, he had clearly been possessed by Demonic Intent.

Lei Gu's eyes flickered, and he frowned. "How is that possible?"

The Southern Territory's arrangements had been precise and meticulous. They had taken advantage of the Martial Gathering to plot against many Emperor Realm Masters and tens of thousands of elite disciples, striking with preparation against the unprepared. It should have been foolproof, yet unexpectedly, at the final moment, a flaw had appeared with Yang Kai.

He had not expected Yang Kai, who had disappeared for more than ten years, to take part in the Southern Territory Martial Gathering either. That was why he had someone lure him away and assassinate him in secret. Who would have thought even that person would fail, leaving Lei Gu deeply frustrated? Only then did he realize that someone with Space Divine Abilities truly knew how to preserve his life. But less than a day had passed. Where had that Yang brat found so many helpers? And every one of them was in the Emperor Realm.

But the demonized man under him had spoken with such certainty that Lei Gu had no choice but to believe it. To be safe, he decided to go see for himself. After giving the demonized man a few instructions, his figure flickered, and in several bounds he arrived at the mountain gate. The moment he looked out, his expression turned solemn.

Yang Kai, who had been gone for a day, had indeed returned, and he had indeed brought quite a few helpers. Besides the Shi Huo who had been staying here to monitor the situation, there were now eight or nine more Stone Men beside him, as well as a dozen or so Emperor Realm Masters gathered nearby.

Looking left and right, Lei Gu saw that outside Wuhua Hall's entire Grand Guardian Array, someone stood in midair at regular intervals. When they saw him appear, all of them looked over.

They really were all in the Emperor Realm, and there were quite a lot of them. Although Lei Gu could not be certain exactly how many people Yang Kai had brought, from the look of it, the demonized man's report had not been far off.

Where had he found so many Emperor Realm Masters? It had only been a single short day. Even if he had rushed without stopping to the great Sects of the Southern Territory to spread the news, he could not possibly have found so many reinforcements in so little time.

Lei Gu's expression grew grave. He faintly sensed that matters had taken a bad turn. If this plan failed, Yang Kai would absolutely be the chief culprit. Hatred surged in his heart. He had spent so many years plotting, yet his grand plan was actually about to fail at one man's hands. How could he not be furious?

"He really has entered the Demonic State." Ji Yao stood beside Yang Kai, and after seeing Lei Gu, she could not help clicking her tongue in wonder as she looked him up and down. This was also her first time seeing Demonic Qi and a cultivator who had entered the Demonic State. Even so, the black Qi around Lei Gu made her feel intensely uncomfortable, stirring a rejection that seemed to come from the depths of her soul.

So this was Demonic Qi? It was filthy indeed.

"Lei Gu, this place will be your grave today!" Yang Kai roared through gritted teeth, his eyes burning the moment he saw his enemy.

Lei Gu said nothing, his face cold. Suddenly, he turned and left, without the slightest intention of saying even one word to Yang Kai. He had come here mainly to confirm whether the intelligence was wrong. Now that he had confirmed it, there was naturally no need to stay any longer.

Yang Kai was slightly startled, knowing this fellow was likely about to stake everything on one desperate move. He immediately circulated his Emperor Yuan and shouted, “Hall Master, are you there?”

His voice, driven by Emperor Yuan, rolled like thunder into the depths of Wuhua Hall.

A hearty laugh immediately came from inside. "Still alive."

"Hall Master, hold on a little longer. I will rescue all of you at once," Yang Kai shouted again.

Wen Zishan's voice came back. "Good!" Although he did not know what was happening outside, since Yang Kai had left and returned, he must have found helpers. Now that everyone's hope of escape rested on Yang Kai, Wen Zishan could only trust him unconditionally.

"Boy, hurry up and act. If you keep dawdling, your Hall Master is going to be demonized," Ma Qing's exasperated voice also rang out.

Wen Zishan laughed loudly. "Bullshit. You are the one who's going to be demonized."

Hearing this, Yang Kai grinned. Good. If they still had the mood to bicker, the situation had not yet reached its most critical point.

He raised one hand high, his voice ringing out in every direction. "Everyone, hear my order!"

The ring of metal sounded without end. Sabers and swords came out together, Imperial Treasures were summoned one after another, Yao Yuan and Emperor Yuan surged, and everyone gathered their strength, ready to strike.

Yang Kai's raised hand swept down, and he shouted, "Attack!"

The moment the word left his mouth, a pitch-black Moon Blade slashed forward and struck the light curtain of Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array. A muffled hiss came from the point of impact, and the light curtain sank slightly inward, sending layer after layer of ripples spreading out from the depression.

In the next instant, several hundred attacks of all kinds bombarded it from every direction, battering the light curtain until it swayed unsteadily. For a time, Heaven and Earth darkened, Spiritual Qi fell into chaos, and all kinds of Laws interwove like a net.

Before the great array's light curtain could stabilize, it was beaten until it rocked violently, looking as if it might collapse at any moment. Yet to everyone's surprise, the light curtain withstood the first fierce barrage and did not scatter immediately.

Wuhua Hall's Grand Guardian Array was naturally extraordinary. If someone had been presiding over the array, it might even have repaired itself relatively quickly. At this moment, however, the great array was merely defending on its own, without even a person controlling it. Even if it had some ability to repair itself, the effect was negligible.

It was not that Lei Gu was unwilling to stay here and control the array; he simply knew the situation was already lost. With so many experts attacking together, the array would not hold for long even with him presiding over it. Rather than waste time here, it was better to think of another plan. Perhaps there was still a chance to make a final struggle.

The first wave of attacks had only just stopped when the second wave arrived, fiercer than the first. Heaven and Earth changed color, the universe seemed to overturn, and an indescribable rush of hot blood rose in the heart of every Emperor Realm Master.

Never before had so many experts attacked together. That momentum alone was enough to shock the world, and as part of the force creating such a commotion, the helpers Yang Kai had brought naturally felt honored.

With a rumbling crash, the great array collapsed. The light curtain that had covered Wuhua Hall all this time burst apart like a pierced soap bubble.

"Kill!" Yang Kai roared, charging in first.

Ji Yao, who was beside him, followed close behind, her figure graceful. But as she rushed into Wuhua Hall, she turned her head and glanced to the side. On a small earthen slope over there lay the corpse of a woman with a fine figure and beautiful features, but her eyes were wide open, refusing to close in death.

Ji Yao did not know exactly what relationship this woman had with Yang Kai, but just now Yang Kai had clearly glanced that way, and after he did, a trace of pain had flashed through his eyes. Her gaze flickered. She bent a finger and pointed downward, sending out a snowflake that drifted lightly onto the woman's corpse. The snowflake did not melt. Instead, it bloomed with an extremely cold profound intent, and in the blink of an eye, the woman's corpse was sealed inside a block of ice.

A group of demonized people wrapped in black Qi rushed toward them head-on. Their expressions were wooden, as if they had lost all independent thought, like walking corpses. Their clothing differed, but they were clearly all disciples from the great Sects of the Southern Territory. Waving the weapons in their hands, they charged at the group of Emperor Realm Masters without regard for their lives.

Yang Kai took the lead. His figure flickered unpredictably as he swept past one demonized person after another, reaching out to tap the backs of their heads. A group of demonized people immediately dropped from the sky like dumplings falling into a pot, landing in the forest below.

Yang Kai did not use lethal force. To others, perhaps there was no solution once someone was corroded by Demonic Qi and was demonized, but he could expel Demonic Qi and restore these people to sanity. Otherwise, if they truly began a slaughter, the Southern Territory would certainly suffer a grievous loss.

At this time, however, there was no chance to look after them one by one. He could only knock them unconscious as quickly as possible and leave them be. They were not flying very high, so even falling from that height would not kill them, especially with the forest below to break their fall.

The people following close behind Yang Kai copied him. While urging their Emperor Yuan to protect themselves and keep the Demonic Qi from corroding them easily, they knocked the enemies coming at them unconscious.

All the demonized people rushing over were below the Emperor Realm, so how could they be a match? Even if they were fearless of death after being demonized, they were not qualified to fight Emperor Realm Masters. Demonized people fell unconscious one after another, making for a spectacular sight.

But there were simply too many of them. A rough count put their number at no fewer than a thousand, and that was only in the direction of the mountain gate. If the demonized people in every other direction of Wuhua Hall were included, Yang Kai estimated that in this single short day, the number of Southern Territory disciples who had been demonized had already reached as many as ten thousand.

With Yang Kai at their head, the dozen or so of them drove into the ranks of the demonized like a sharp blade, and wherever they passed, none could withstand even a single exchange.

"So this is all they amount to, hahahaha." An elder of the Mitian Sect at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm laughed loudly. Earlier, Yang Kai had gathered so many experts with such grand fanfare that it had inevitably made people nervous, and they had thought these so-called demonized people were something extraordinary. Now that they had exchanged blows, he discovered they were only so-so. It seemed that whether one was a demonized person or anything else, strength was still the root that decided everything.

He was not the only one with this thought. Most people who encountered demonized people for the first time would think the same.

The Demonic Qi on those demonized people was indeed somewhat strange. It was highly corrosive and filled with a sense of evil and filth. But everyone had Emperor Yuan protecting their bodies, and many were also wearing artifact armor, so they had no fear of that thin Demonic Qi.

They cut through the enemy as easily as chopping melons and slicing vegetables. Although the dozen or so of them were surrounded by more than a thousand people, they still chatted and laughed as before, moving freely through the battlefield. Though there were many demonized people, with the methods of these dozen or so, they probably would not need even the time it took to drink a cup of tea to knock them all unconscious. Once the four sides converged, they could charge straight into the depths of Wuhua Hall's hinterland, and Lei Gu would surely have nowhere to run.

But just then, the faces of many demonized people suddenly flushed red, and their bodies swelled as though inflated. Their bellies bulged high, making them look like women ten months pregnant; strained, painful sounds came from their throats, and the Demonic Qi around them heaved violently.

The sudden change stunned many people, and their movements inevitably slowed. Even so, every one of them felt a faint, almost imperceptible sense of crisis.

"Careful." Yang Kai's expression changed, and he shouted. He grabbed the neck of one of the demonized people undergoing the change and squeezed hard, intending to knock him unconscious. But the demonized man only stared at him with wide eyes, and his swelling body showed no sign of stopping.
