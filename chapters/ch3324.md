## Chapter 3324 — Beacon

"Ah, Old Qiong it is, then." Qiong Qi resigned himself to the name.

Yang Kai nodded. "Withdraw for now. I have some matters to discuss with Senior Li."

"Yes." Qiong Qi answered respectfully, turned, and flew far into the distance before landing on the tip of a tree. Standing there with his feet on the treetop and his hands clasped behind his back, he looked as if he were riding the wind through the void. If not for the red robe spoiling the image, he would have had something of an otherworldly expert's bearing.

In the pavilion, Yang Kai said, "As for Qiong Qi, please set your minds at ease, both of you and the Great Emperor. I will discipline him strictly."

Jiu Feng said, "Remember, going too far is as bad as falling short. He is an Ancient Divine Spirit, after all, and has lived even longer than I have. He may sincerely wish to acknowledge you as his master, and I cannot see the slightest reluctance in him, but forcing him too hard would be unwise. You will have to judge that balance yourself."

"I understand." Yang Kai nodded. Having such a wicked spirit at his side was somewhat dangerous, but Yang Kai was no soft persimmon to be casually squeezed, much less some greenhorn fresh to the world. As for Qiong Qi's true nature, he would watch it carefully in the future.

With Qiong Qi's matter settled, Yang Kai immediately began eagerly asking Li Wuyi for guidance on the Laws of Space. Since he had begun cultivating the Power of Space, this was the first time he had ever discussed it with anyone, so Yang Kai could not help feeling excited.

"Senior Li, just now, how did you send that jade slip back to Spirit Beast Island? Could you demonstrate it for me again?"

Li Wuyi smiled faintly. "That is not difficult." As he spoke, he raised one finger, and the Laws of Space rippled at his fingertip as he began sketching in the air above the stone table before him.

Yang Kai focused his eyes and watched, discovering that Li Wuyi was drawing an extremely abstruse and complicated array diagram. As the diagram took shape, Yang Kai could not help giving a soft sound of surprise, because it looked somewhat similar to the array diagram of the cross-territory Space Array he arranged, though it was not entirely the same.

A moment later, Li Wuyi withdrew his hand. The stone table before them had not changed in the slightest, but Yang Kai could clearly sense an additional layer of the Laws of Space atop it. The invisible array diagram had been branded onto the stone table by the Power of Space.

Li Wuyi tossed Yang Kai a blank jade slip. "Put anything you like into it."

Yang Kai nodded. Holding the jade slip, he moved his Divine Sense and engraved his own Soul Brand inside it.

"Give it to me," Jiu Feng said, extending her hand.

Though he did not know what she intended to do, Yang Kai still cooperatively handed the jade slip over.

Jiu Feng took the jade slip, swayed lightly, and shot away, vanishing in the blink of an eye.

Yang Kai stared thoughtfully in the direction Jiu Feng had gone.

After roughly the time it took to drink half a cup of tea, white light suddenly flashed on the stone table before him. When Yang Kai turned to look, a jade slip had appeared there out of nowhere.

Yang Kai raised his brow. "This is..."

Li Wuyi smiled. "Take a look and you will know."

Yang Kai reached out and picked up the jade slip. After a brief examination, he discovered that it was none other than the one he had just marked with his Soul Brand, the very same jade slip Jiu Feng had taken away. Now it had appeared out of thin air before his eyes.

Li Wuyi reached out and tapped the stone table. "I can use the Laws of Space to sketch out a Space Beacon. Wherever I leave a Space Beacon, either this Li himself or an object that has passed through my hands can be transmitted there instantly."

Yang Kai's eyes lit up. "This is exactly like a Space Array."

Li Wuyi nodded. "Precisely. When you normally use a Space Array, you transmit people away, while I transmit objects, which is somewhat easier. But the underlying principle is the same."

Yang Kai clicked his tongue in wonder. "This stone table is just ordinary stone, yet it can still hold a Space Brand?"

Li Wuyi said, "It can, but only for a very short time. If you want one to last, you need to use special materials."

Yang Kai raised his brow. "Ethereal Crystal or Ethereal Jade?"

Li Wuyi smiled. "Exactly."

"I understand." Yang Kai could hardly contain his joy, as if he had obtained a precious treasure. "If this method is used to transmit messages, it would be unbeatable. Who knows how much more useful it is than those Transmission Compasses?" Transmission Compasses were limited by distance; if the distance was too great, they simply could not send a message. This method was different. No matter how far away the target was, the message could arrive in an instant.

Li Wuyi must have left a Space Beacon in advance with the Beast Great Emperor, which was why the jade slip could be transmitted back just now. By the same logic, the Beast Great Emperor's reply could return at once as well.

Yang Kai's heart burned with eagerness, and he immediately began sketching a Space Beacon on the stone table.

Li Wuyi watched from the side, correcting him here and there, and Yang Kai actually mastered the method in a very short time. Before long, a new Space Beacon had taken shape.

Yang Kai stood and laughed. "I will go and be right back."

He flashed away and flew out, just as Jiu Feng was returning. As they brushed past each other, Jiu Feng gave him a suspicious look, then turned to Li Wuyi. "He learned it?"

Li Wuyi nodded. "His attainments in the Laws of Space are not much worse than mine. All he lacks is the accumulation of time. Naturally, he would learn such a small method as soon as he was taught."

Jiu Feng said, "You look very happy. What is there to be happy about when someone has stolen your skills?"

Li Wuyi smiled faintly. “Liu Yan appeared in the world. Are you happy or not?”

Jiu Feng's expression shifted, and she nodded. "So that is how it is."

As they spoke, light flashed on the stone table before them, and an Upper-Level Source Crystal appeared out of thin air. Li Wuyi picked it up, weighed it in his hand a few times, and said, "He can already draw inferences from one example..." Before he could finish, another object appeared in front of him. It was a large stone weighing about a hundred jin. Who knew where Yang Kai had found it, but he had thrown it straight over through the Space Beacon.

It did not end there. Light flashed again on the stone table, and a giant wolf three zhang tall suddenly appeared before Li Wuyi. It was the very kind of Yao Beast seen everywhere in Heavenly Wolf Valley. This Yao Beast's grade was not low, fully at the Dao Origin Realm level. The moment it appeared, it looked utterly panicked, then howled, opened its mouth, and spat a Wind Blade visible to the naked eye at Li Wuyi.

Li Wuyi flicked his finger and shattered the Wind Blade, then laughed. “This has nothing to do with me! You’ve got the wrong person.”

Then he swept his hand out, sending forth a gentle wind that wrapped around the giant wolf and carried it a hundred zhang away before setting it down. The sudden change had frightened the giant wolf out of its wits. The instant it was free, it tucked its tail and fled in disgrace, vanishing from sight in the blink of an eye.

A figure flashed, and Yang Kai returned to the pavilion. Looking thoughtful, he said, "This technique has limits on the size and weight of the object being transmitted. If those limits are exceeded, it seems to become useless."

Li Wuyi said, "That is only natural. If there were no limits, would that not mean you could move a whole mountain over if you wished? But for transmitting messages, it is more than enough."

"Senior is right." Yang Kai nodded. "But being able to transmit living things is already extremely remarkable. Right, Senior, this method should have no problem transmitting a person, should it?"

Li Wuyi said, "Naturally, there is no problem."

Yang Kai rubbed his chin. "If I leave Space Beacons throughout the Star Realm, does that not mean that in the future, with a single thought, I can go wherever I want?" This was far more convenient than shattering the void, and more convenient than arranging Space Arrays as well. But convenient though it was, it could not transmit on a large scale. In that respect, it was inferior to Space Arrays.

Li Wuyi said, "In principle, yes."

"Then I wonder, would it work the same way if a Space Beacon were sketched on Ethereal Jade?"

Li Wuyi smiled slightly. "You can try."

Yang Kai did not hesitate. He immediately took out a palm-sized piece of Ethereal Jade and began sketching. Although the Ethereal Jade's surface was somewhat small, he already had experience, so he worked with ease, condensing the Space Beacon's array diagram onto the jade.

Once he finished sketching, he checked it carefully and confirmed there were no problems.

A thought occurred to Yang Kai, and he looked at Li Wuyi. "Senior, why not leave me a way to contact you? It will make it easier for us to keep in touch in the future."

"That was exactly my intention." Li Wuyi smiled. "I was wondering how long it would take you to think of this, but I did not expect it to be so soon."

Yang Kai chuckled. "A sudden inspiration."

Li Wuyi reached out and handed over a smooth, round bead. It had clearly been carefully polished from Ethereal Jade, and he had even strung it with a strand of Heavenly Silkworm Silk so it could be worn on the wrist.

"Although this method makes contact convenient, the Space Beacon cannot be isolated. If it is placed inside a Spatial Ring, it will have no effect. Wear this on your wrist, and in the future, if I want to contact you, I can do so at any time."

"Senior is thoughtful." Yang Kai glanced at the string of beads on Li Wuyi's wrist and guessed that Li Wuyi had probably left this sort of contact method with many people. He looked at Li Wuyi's Ethereal Jade bead, then at his own. By comparison, his was almost too embarrassing to show anyone. He immediately said, "I will make Senior one just like it as well. Otherwise, it will not be convenient to carry around."

Li Wuyi nodded and said, "That would be fine."

Yang Kai immediately began polishing carefully, and before long, a new Ethereal Jade bead was freshly made. Only then could the two of them be considered to have exchanged contact beacons.

Li Wuyi then said solemnly, "You and I are both proficient in the Power of Space, and each of us has the other's beacon, so transmitting messages is easy. We need only move our thoughts to send a jade slip out. But if others wish to do the same, they must use the paired Ethereal Jade bead. They have no Space Force and cannot locate the target precisely, so they can only rely on the paired bead to find your beacon or mine."

"I understand." Yang Kai nodded. Even if Li Wuyi had not explained this part, he had already thought of it. Earlier, when Jiu Feng sent that jade slip back, she had not used any Ethereal Jade bead, but Li Wuyi had left his own Space Force on the jade slip in advance. Jiu Feng only needed to activate it slightly, and the jade slip would return on its own.

He then continued, "Courtesy demands reciprocity. Since this Junior has learned the Space Beacon method from Senior, I would also ask Senior to appraise the Space Array this Junior can arrange."

Li Wuyi asked with great interest, "You can arrange Space Arrays?"

Yang Kai smiled faintly, wearing an unfathomable look. "Please wait and see, Senior. However, this thing is rather troublesome to arrange, and it cannot compare with the exquisite subtlety of Senior's method, so please be patient."
