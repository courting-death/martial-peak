## Chapter 3171 — Spit Out Everything You've Taken From Me

"Hm? What's going on?" Yang Kai suddenly sensed that something was wrong.

Above his Sea of Consciousness, in one corner of the Star Chart, there was a fist-sized "ink spot."

The "ink spot" looked extremely familiar, just like the sight Yang Kai had seen from high above when he first traveled to Tongxuan Continent years ago. Now that his mind was linked with the Star Domain Origin, he immediately felt a deep malice coming from it.

Moreover, if he was not mistaken, the "ink spot" lay exactly where the Great Wilderness Star Domain and Hengluo Star Domain were connected.

That place was extremely important. Yang Kai had sent Li Jiao and his family to guard it as early as ten years ago, and he had thought it absolutely secure. Only today, after fully refining the Star Chart, did he discover something was wrong.

With a thought, his vision seemed to sweep across hundreds of millions of li and arrive near the "ink spot" in an instant.

The sight that met him shocked Yang Kai greatly.

The Void for hundreds of millions of li around it had become a black hole of unimaginable size. Everything that had originally existed in that part of the Starry Sky had vanished without a trace, even many Dead Stars.

Worse still, the edge of the black hole was steadily expanding outward. Wherever the darkness passed, everything turned into nothingness.

The scene looked as if the darkness were devouring this Star Domain.

Yang Kai flew into a rage!

He had refined the Star Domain Origin and become the Master of the Star Domain. In other words, this entire Star Domain was his backyard. Whatever the hell this darkness was, it was eating away at his territory. As the Master of the Star Domain, how could he tolerate that?

Where was that old bastard Li Jiao? Yang Kai had sent him here to stand guard back then, yet such a huge incident had happened and he had not even sent word. Even if Yang Kai had been rushing all over the place these past years with no fixed residence, if Li Jiao truly wanted to find him, could he really have had no way?

A bit of resentment inevitably rose in his heart. His figure flickered, and in moments he arrived at the edge of that enormous black hole.

From up close, the horror of this black hole became even clearer. Everything before him was darkness. There was nothing at all, no better than a Void Crack. The tide-like darkness slowly spread outward, assimilating everything it passed.

Yang Kai felt his territory slipping away bit by bit.

His expression turned stern, and he connected his mind with the Star Chart. The Star Chart hummed and trembled, as though encouraged, then released a dazzling light.

In that instant, the darkness that had been steadily pressing outward suddenly slowed a great deal.

Seeing this, Yang Kai's brow rose.

He had never encountered anything like this before and had no idea how to deal with it. A moment ago, he had only wanted to try stopping the spread of the darkness, yet after communicating with the Star Chart, it had actually worked.

He immediately understood. Since he was the Master of the Star Domain, his will was the Star Domain's will. Every thought and movement of his could make the Star Domain resonate with him.

With that answer in hand, Yang Kai no longer hesitated. He focused even more deeply on communicating with the Star Chart, borrowing the strength of the entire Star Domain to stop the darkness from spreading.

The darkness's outward spread visibly slowed, then gradually came to a halt.

Yang Kai was not satisfied. Instead, he shouted, "Scram!"

He thrust both arms forward. Invisible World Force squeezed in from every direction, and the boundary of the darkness instantly shrank back by several dozen li, still retreating without pause.

Where the darkness receded, everything it had swallowed reappeared. Yang Kai clearly saw a huge meteorite slowly emerge from the darkness until it was fully revealed.

The discovery delighted him. He had just thought that everything swallowed by the darkness had ceased to exist, but now it seemed that was not the case. Although those places had been devoured, everything was still there, safe and sound. No one could see them, that was all. Only after the darkness was driven away could their original appearance be revealed.

What exactly was this darkness?

Yang Kai drove it back while focusing his senses to investigate.

A moment later, he trembled, shock filling his eyes.

He had actually glimpsed a will of Heaven and Earth within that darkness, a will that did not belong to Hengluo Star Domain! Yet that will was so similar to the one on Hengluo Star Domain's side, as if the darkness itself were another world.

Inconceivable. Truly inconceivable.

Could there really be another world eroding Hengluo Star Domain? Was that world the Great Wilderness Star Domain?

After all, this place was the connection point between the two Star Domains. If the Great Wilderness Star Domain was devouring Hengluo Star Domain, that would make sense.

A surge of relief suddenly rose in his heart. If he had not refined the Star Chart and become the Master of the Star Domain, he feared he would have been helpless against the situation before him. Devouring between worlds was no longer something that could be solved by cultivation alone. He had to borrow the power of the world he inhabited to resist this devouring.

A glimmer of understanding came to him. The reason he had been able to refine the Star Chart so smoothly was probably related to the current situation.

Hengluo Star Domain clearly wanted to borrow his strength to resist this devouring as well.

"Spit out everything you took from me!" Yang Kai had only just become the Master of the Star Domain, and his spirits were soaring. How could he fear this small obstacle before him?

The power of the Star Domain was drawn out and pressed madly toward the darkness.

The darkness had no strength to resist and retreated step by step. The Starry Sky it had swallowed also returned, and as Yang Kai exerted himself, the darkness receded faster and faster.

In less than half the time it took to drink a cup of tea, the darkness had already retreated over ten thousand li.

Compared to the territory it had swallowed, ten thousand li was nothing, but it was still a good beginning. Yang Kai believed that as time passed, he would recover his lost territory faster and faster.

Reality proved him right. The entire Hengluo Star Domain seemed to sense Yang Kai's intent, and the Star Domain Origin cooperated with him without reservation, granting him power vast enough to order Heaven and Earth.

One hundred thousand li, two hundred thousand li, one million li...

The darkness had clearly shrunk by a full circle. At this rate, it would take less than a month for Yang Kai to take back everything he had lost to the darkness.

Yet at that moment, the hairs all over Yang Kai's body suddenly stood on end, and a tremendous sense of crisis enveloped him.

His eyes widened as he stared solemnly into the darkness ahead. It was a stretch of chaotic nothingness, with no vitality and not even any dead matter, yet he was certain the danger had come from within it.

A point of light suddenly bloomed before his eyes and reached him in the blink of an eye.

Yang Kai tilted his head back, opened his mouth, and bit down on the light.

The light kept trembling, as if it were alive.

Yang Kai clenched his teeth. His sharp fangs became all-destroying blades, and with a crack, something shattered. The struggling in his mouth instantly stopped.

"Peh!" Yang Kai spat the thing out, caught it in his hand, and narrowed his eyes.

It was an exquisitely crafted flying dagger about one chi long, its entire body smooth and flawless. At this moment, however, a row of teeth marks had been imprinted on it, and dense cracks covered its surface.

Yang Kai's bite had already destroyed the flying dagger's spirituality.

"This is..." Yang Kai's eyes nearly popped out. "An Imperial Treasure?"

The thing that had attacked him from within the darkness was actually an Imperial Treasure! Its spirituality was damaged and it was covered in cracks, but Yang Kai was not blind. How could he fail to recognize an Imperial Treasure?

The aura of Emperor Yuan even lingered on the flying dagger.

He lifted his head and looked into the darkness again, his eyes burning like flames.

Good, very good. He had thought the devouring between the two worlds was nothing more than a natural disaster, but in the end, it was actually man-made!

This flying dagger Imperial Treasure could not have attacked him for no reason. Judging from the lingering Emperor Yuan aura, someone was clearly trying to kill him from another world, and the person who had acted was definitely an Emperor Realm cultivator.

Even the current Yang Kai could not launch a strike across worlds. The barrier between worlds was too solid; perhaps only a Great Emperor could do such a thing.

But Hengluo Star Domain and the Great Wilderness Star Domain were no longer completely isolated from each other. The darkness was where the two worlds connected, so it was not too difficult for a hidden enemy to ambush him across a world.

As for the reason...

It was simply because he had obstructed the Great Wilderness Star Domain's devouring.

A bold thought sprang into Yang Kai's mind. He could not be certain, but he was probably close to the truth.

"Now this is finally getting interesting!" He could not help grinning. His smile was savage, and he fixed his eyes on the darkness as though he could pierce the barrier between worlds and stare straight at the powerful enemy hidden on the other side.

Ever since returning to the Star Domain, even with Heaven and Earth's rejection, every enemy he had encountered had been nothing but small fry, too weak to withstand more than a few of his blows. Now that such a powerful enemy had suddenly appeared, his long-absent fighting spirit swiftly rose.

"It would be discourteous not to return the favor. Take a move from me as well!" He clapped his hands together, and an enormous Moon Blade slowly took shape.

He did not cut it out immediately. Instead, he poured power into it without pause.

The Moon Blade hummed and flickered, as if it might collapse at any moment, yet under Yang Kai's control, it was steadier than ever.

Only after a long while did Yang Kai look up. First, he threw the flying dagger whose spirituality had been ruined into the darkness before him, then sent the Moon Blade slashing out with a thunderous strike.

Without a sound, the flying dagger and Moon Blade both vanished.

In the endless Starry Sky of the Great Wilderness Star Domain, the scene was exactly the same as on Yang Kai's side, with vast darkness surging.

At the edge of the darkness, a sallow-faced middle-aged man stared ahead with his brow tightly furrowed.

Suddenly, a flying dagger appeared from the darkness.

He casually raised a hand and caught the flying dagger between his fingers. Before he could examine it, a pitch-black Moon Blade slash came right after it.

He was no careless fool, yet he only noticed it when the Moon Blade was already right in front of him.

The main reason was that this Moon Blade was too strange. Silent and traceless, it looked exactly like the darkness, making it extremely difficult to detect.

Sensing the terrifying power within the Moon Blade, the middle-aged man's brow rose and surprise flashed across his face, but he did not panic. Another flying dagger suddenly appeared in his hand, identical to the one Yang Kai had intercepted earlier. He raised it high, light blazing from the flying dagger as it transformed into a heaven-shaking giant blade and came crashing down.
