## Chapter 3237 — Deliberately Humiliating?

His tightly clenched fists slowly loosened, and Mi Qi answered, "Yes!"

"There is no time to lose. Set out now." Yang Kai jerked his chin to the side, allowing no delay at all.

Helpless, Mi Qi waved a hand and sent the orders flying to the elders. "You all heard Palace Master Yang, didn't you? Go..." He waved them off, looking as if he had lost the will to live.

The injured elders, still not fully recovered, looked at one another, grief and indignation plain in their eyes. But even if they resented it, what could they do? They were no match for the other side, and even their own Sect Master had bowed his head. They could only obey.

They immediately split up in different directions, turning into streaks of light as they shot away. Their injuries had not healed, but they were not serious enough to matter, and they could recover slowly on the road. Only now did they understand why those two Yao Kings had wounded them earlier without killing them. This had been waiting for them all along; their lives had been spared so they could run errands.

This had been planned from the start.

If all of Mitian Sect's Emperor Realm cultivators had been killed, who would announce Mi Qi's order? Sending someone else would not carry much weight. Only if Mitian Sect's own elders and Protectors announced it could the matter be made certain.

It was not hard to imagine that news of Mitian Sect's submission to Lingxiao Palace would spread very soon. Mi Qi sighed deeply to himself, and his entire bearing seemed to wither, as if he had aged many years all at once.

"All of you, get up. Stop kneeling. From now on, we're one family." Yang Kai glanced around at the Mitian Sect disciples still kneeling on the ground and lifted his hand.

The group looked to Mi Qi and did not dare move.

Mi Qi shouted in a low voice, "Are you all deaf? Didn't you hear Palace Master Yang? If you want to kneel, then kneel there until you die. None of you ever need to get up again!"

Useless things. How had Mitian Sect raised such a flock of cowards who clung to life and feared death? Their cultivation was inferior, yes, but surely they should not have been scared to their knees by a few words.

After a flurry of movement, the group no longer dared remain kneeling and scrambled to their feet, their expressions awkward.

Mi Qi snorted coldly again. "From this day forward, you may choose not to listen to this Sect Master, but you must listen to Palace Master Yang. Understood?"

"Yes!" Sparse replies came back, all of them weak and dispirited. With their own Sect Master speaking this way, they also felt ashamed.

Mi Qi forced out a faint smile. "Does Palace Master Yang have anything to say?"

Yang Kai waved a hand. What was there to say to them? Dealing with Mi Qi was enough. He turned to Old Tao and said, "Old Sir, if you do not mind, how about coming inside for a talk?"

Behind him, Mi Qi had the urge to roll his eyes. This was Mitian Sect, not Lingxiao Palace, yet Yang Kai had clearly already treated it as his own territory. Still, Mi Qi did not dare say anything. Since he had chosen to submit, Mitian Sect really was Yang Kai's territory now.

Old Tao pondered for a moment, then nodded. "Your Excellency is thoughtful. In that case, this old man will impose."

He also wanted to understand the relationship between Liu Yan and Jiu Feng.

"Please!" Yang Kai gestured. Old Tao returned the courtesy, and the two simply walked inside side by side.

The three Yao Kings followed close behind, while Mi Qi deliberately fell back and walked with Li Jiao.

The two could be considered old acquaintances. Li Jiao had even come over last time to advise Mi Qi, but unfortunately, Mi Qi had not listened. He had thought that inviting Old Tao over would let him rest easy, only to find that Old Tao could not help him avoid disaster either. Seeing Mi Qi end up like this, Li Jiao could not help sighing. He patted Mi Qi on the shoulder, but did not know what to say.

Mi Qi transmitted, "Brother Li, you and I have known each other for many years, and our friendship is no ordinary one. Tell me the truth. Why must Palace Master Yang humiliate my Mitian Sect like this? This Mi believes he has never offended him, and I have sent the yearly debt payments on time, so why would he do this? He has no regard at all for my Mitian Sect's face." Worse, Yang Kai had not given him any time to breathe, sending those Emperor Realm elders out at once.

Mi Qi had originally planned to delay for a while, then think of some other way to see whether he could resolve this calamity. But now, what chance did he have?

Li Jiao looked ahead and thought, What use is it to bring up friendship with me now? When I went over last time, weren't you the one who drove me out? Still, he had no heart to mock him now. He only smiled bitterly and said, "Brother Mi thinks Palace Master Yang is humiliating you?"

"Isn't he?" Mi Qi glared. If not to humiliate him, why make him write the order and then have his own Emperor Realm cultivators go everywhere announcing it?

"That is not the case." Li Jiao shook his head.

Mi Qi asked, "Then why?"

"To establish authority!" Li Jiao spat out the words.

Mi Qi froze. "Establish authority? What authority?" Although Lingxiao Palace had risen only recently, it could still be considered one of the Northern Territory's top Sects. The entire Northern Territory acknowledged as much. Since it was already a top Sect, its prestige was already there. What else did it need to establish? And for whom?

Li Jiao pondered for a moment, then said, "Palace Master Yang wants to unify the Northern Territory."

The main point was that this matter could not stay hidden. Li Jiao believed Yang Kai would make his move soon, so there was no harm in saying it now, and he was not afraid of Mi Qi finding out early.

Mi Qi froze, his mouth opening wider than an egg. Only after a long while did he come back to himself and exclaim, "Unify the Northern Territory?" Such ambition. The Northern Territory was different from the other three territories, where Great Emperor Sects held command. Although Medicine Pill Valley had Sublime Pill Great Emperor, Medicine Pill Valley did not interfere in the Northern Territory's affairs, so the entire Northern Territory could be considered a land without a leader, unlike the Southern Territory, led by Star God Palace; the Western Territory, which honored Shadow Slaughter Palace; and the Eastern Territory, which had Spirit Beast Island.

Mi Qi never could have imagined that Yang Kai's ambition was so great, or that he truly dared to act on it. Dazed, he said, "Isn't he afraid of provoking public outrage and having all the heroes of the Northern Territory rise up to attack him?"

Li Jiao's mouth twitched. "That is exactly why he needs to establish authority."

Mi Qi was not stupid. Though he was somewhat afraid of death, he had cultivated to the Third-Stage Emperor Realm, so his mind was naturally not poor. He had not understood what establishing authority meant before, but now that Li Jiao had explained it, how could he fail to see it?

Mitian Sect was also a top Sect of the Northern Territory, but so what? Lingxiao Palace had beaten it until it had no strength to fight back, forcing it to submit for self-preservation. If even Mitian Sect ended up like this, what choice would the other Sects and families have? Once the news spread, when Lingxiao Palace wanted to extend its reach elsewhere, probably not many people would dare resist.

For a moment, Mi Qi did not know whether to laugh or cry. "Just because of this, my Mitian Sect suffered an undeserved calamity?"

Li Jiao patted his shoulder again and sighed. "Try to look on the bright side. At least you're still alive." In truth, Mitian Sect really was unlucky. Of the four top Sects in the Northern Territory, Ice Heart Valley had deep ties with Yang Kai, and Li Jiao himself had spent many years traveling with Yang Kai, building a decent friendship with him. If Yang Kai wanted to establish authority, Mitian Sect was his only choice. One could only say that Mi Qi deserved his bad luck.

Mitian Sect, as a top Sect of the Northern Territory, carried more than enough weight. If Yang Kai had chosen some smaller Sect instead, the effect definitely would not have been as good.

Mi Qi sighed again and again, but with matters already at this point, nothing he said would help. After learning of Yang Kai's plan to unify the Northern Territory, however, he actually felt somewhat better. Yang Kai was not deliberately humiliating him and Mitian Sect; they were simply one part of the plan.

Before long, the group entered the great hall from earlier.

As the master of this place, Mi Qi naturally began making arrangements at once. He ordered the old banquet removed and a new one brought in, commanded the musicians to play and the dancing girls to dance, and the great hall immediately returned to a scene of music, dancing, and peaceful revelry.

It was just that Yang Kai now sat in the main seat, and aside from Mi Qi, the Sect Master of Mitian Sect, not a single other Emperor Realm cultivator of Mitian Sect could be seen in the hall. In their place were the three Yao Kings and the people of Li Dragon Palace.

Yang Kai raised his cup and invited everyone to drink, and all present agreed.

After three rounds of wine, Yang Kai finally began chatting with Old Tao. They had exchanged a few words on the way, so he knew the man's name. The main reason Yang Kai had kept him for a banquet was to ask him about Dragon Isle.

After causing a great disturbance on Dragon Isle back then, Yang Kai had left from Dragon Temple and entered the Lower Plane Star Domain. He did not know what had happened afterward, whether Beast Great Emperor had taken Fu Xuan away, or how Dragon Isle had punished Zhu Qing, if at all.

Unfortunately, Old Tao did not know either. He had been stationed in the Northern Territory year-round, searching for exotic beasts for Spirit Beast Island, and had not gone back in more than ten years. He did not even know that Beast Great Emperor had led people to force their way into Dragon Isle.

Since Old Tao did not know, Yang Kai did not say much more. His mysterious manner, however, only left Old Tao suspicious and uneasy.

Still, regardless of whether Beast Great Emperor had taken Fu Xuan away, there was no need to worry too much about Zhu Qing. Zhu Qing was already a Ninth-Tier giant dragon, and with the purity of her bloodline, she was fully qualified to serve as an elder of Dragon Isle. Given Yang Kai's previous disturbance there as well, Dragon Isle presumably would not punish her.

Her situation was somewhat similar to Fu Xuan's. Both had joined with humans. If Dragon Isle truly wanted to punish Zhu Qing, Fu Xuan definitely could not escape either, and Beast Great Emperor naturally would not stand by and do nothing. If Dragon Isle wanted to punish Zhu Qing, it would certainly have to punish Fu Xuan along with her. Otherwise, its conduct would be unfair.

Old Tao, for his part, was quite curious about Liu Yan. He probed indirectly into her relationship with Jiu Feng, but after Yang Kai brushed him off, he stopped asking.

Neither side obtained the answer it wanted, but it did not matter much.

By evening, the banquet ended and Old Tao took his leave. Yang Kai had Liu Yan see him off, while he himself, accompanied by Mi Qi, chose a suitable place inside Mitian Sect and began arranging a Space Array.

At first, Mi Qi did not know what Yang Kai was doing. Only after exchanging a series of transmissions with Li Jiao did he learn the truth, and he groaned inwardly. With this Space Array linking their two Sects, it seemed Mitian Sect could forget about escaping Lingxiao Palace's control.

The next day, Yang Kai led the group back to Lingxiao Palace, with Mi Qi accompanying them.

The first thing he did after returning was summon people for a meeting.

Everyone attending had cultivation at or above the Dao Origin Realm, so there were not many of them. Li Jiao and Mi Qi were also present, which made many people who did not know the truth look at one another. Li Jiao being here was one thing; everyone knew he had a good relationship with Yang Kai. But what was going on with Mi Qi?

Even Hua Qingsi was suspicious and confused. She kept glancing at Mi Qi, only to see him give her an ingratiating smile, leaving her completely baffled.

Once everyone had arrived, Yang Kai went straight to the point. "Sect Master Mi has decided to submit his entire Sect to my Lingxiao Palace. From now on, Mitian Sect is a subordinate Sect of my Lingxiao Palace."

With that one statement, everyone was shocked. They almost wondered whether they had heard wrong. Only the three Yao Kings remained calm.
