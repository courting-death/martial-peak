## Chapter 3204 — Asking to Be Fleeced

Since Fan Wu had lowered his stance and clearly meant to befriend him, Yang Kai naturally gave him face. The banquet passed in easy laughter and conversation, host and guests all in high spirits. After three rounds of wine, Fan Wu asked, half casually and half deliberately, "I wonder what important business brings Brother Yang to the Ancient Land this time?"

"Just passing through," Yang Kai answered casually.

"Passing through, is it? Hehe..." Fan Wu chuckled. Naturally, he did not believe such nonsense. From what he had heard, Yang Kai had established a Lingxiao Sect in the Northern Territory. This place, however, was in the Eastern Territory, and more than that, it was the remote, uncanny Wild Ancient Land. No matter how one was passing through, one could not possibly pass through here.

He did not know that Yang Kai was telling the truth. Coming over from Hengluo Star Domain, Yang Kai really had only happened to arrive here. Since he was already here, he might as well set up another Space Array in the Ancient Land while he was at it. To him, it was no more than a convenient errand.

But to Fan Wu, that sounded evasive. The more he listened, the more he felt this boy was hiding sinister intentions, and was probably planning to make a move on the Yao Kings of the Ancient Land. His guard rose sharply.

If Yang Kai were truly allowed to roam the Ancient Land and raise his arm with a single call, Fan Wu feared all twenty-nine Yao Kings would leave with him. How could Fan Wu tolerate such a thing happening right under his nose?

"Does Brother Fan have some advice for me?" Yang Kai glanced sidelong at him, feeling more and more that something about today was strange. Frowning, he said, "If there is something you want to say, we may as well speak plainly. Beating around the bush is rather pointless."

The pointless one is you! the three Saint Venerables cursed inwardly. Luan Feng could not help rolling her eyes. If anyone else had said such a thing, the three of them would already have turned hostile. Whether the other party truly had such intentions or not, so long as there was the slightest suspicion, they could not let it pass. They would have killed him on the spot at once to protect their own interests.

But Fan Wu was certain that Yang Kai's future was limitless. Sooner or later, his own strength would probably equal theirs, and with Heaven's Punisher's descendant backing him from behind, how could Fan Wu dare offend him lightly?

With a dry laugh, Fan Wu said, "I heard Brother Yang founded a Sect in the Northern Territory. The three of us have lived in the Ancient Land for so long without leaving that we were unable to visit and offer our congratulations. We truly feel somewhat ashamed."

Yang Kai waved his hand and said, “Brother Fan overstates it. I merely made a small fuss, nothing more. How could that compare to the three of you living freely and at ease here in the Ancient Land, where heroes are born from blessed lands?”

Those words were the honest truth. Although the Wild Ancient Land was somewhat dangerous, and filled everywhere with the power of the Primordial Era, something ordinary people could not endure at all, this place was also a vast natural Treasure Vault. Spirit flowers and exotic grasses grew in abundance, and there were many Earth Vein Source Crystal mines as well. The three great Saint Venerables occupied an excellent blessed paradise under heaven, with dozens of Yao Kings under their command besides. Yang Kai truly envied them.

If Lingxiao Palace could be founded here, then within a hundred years it would surely become one of the foremost Sects in the entire Star Realm.

But when Fan Wu and the other two heard those words, their hearts sank with a thump. They kept feeling that this fellow had hidden meaning in his words. What now? Not only had he taken a fancy to the Yao Kings under them, he had even taken a fancy to their territory? Did this scoundrel want to seize their land too? This was simply going too far.

With that preconception lodged in their minds, the three Saint Venerables' faces turned rather unsightly. They could no longer be bothered to circle around the matter with Yang Kai. Fan Wu reached out, took several Spatial Rings, set them on the wooden table, and gently pushed them in front of Yang Kai, the smile on his face unchanged.

Yang Kai looked at the Spatial Rings, then at the three Saint Venerables, blinked, and said, "What is this supposed to mean?"

Fan Wu smiled faintly. "Brother Yang's Lingxiao Palace has only recently been established, so it must be a time when large sums are needed. The three of us cannot offer much help, but the Ancient Land does have some output. These modest gifts are hardly worthy of mention, but please do not refuse them, Brother Yang. Consider them a little support from the three of us for your noble Sect."

What was going on? Even Yang Kai was a little dazed.

There was no denying that when he had sent that twin-horned Yao Marshal to pass a message to Luan Feng yesterday, he really had made up his mind to extort a sum from her. He had already thought through his wording: the cross-territory Space Array had cost him a great deal of wealth and effort, and since Luan Feng had smashed it, she naturally had to pay some compensation. If Luan Feng refused, he would shamelessly stay put and refuse to leave. He would definitely be able to squeeze out some benefits.

Two of the Space Arrays he had arranged had already been destroyed. One was Luan Feng's doing, and the other was probably the Iron Blood Great Emperor's. He did not dare seek trouble with the Iron Blood Great Emperor. If they met, he would hide as fast as he could. That left only Luan Feng to suffer the misfortune.

But that matter was aimed solely at Luan Feng. It had nothing to do with Fan Wu or Cang Gou. Besides, he and Luan Feng were not enemies. Back then, he had even benefited from her influence, so naturally he would not take things too far. As long as Luan Feng gave him a little compensation, the matter would end there. It was not as if he was some unreasonable pest.

Unexpectedly, before he even had time to go squeeze them for compensation, they had delivered themselves to his door, and not just one of them, but all three together.

Truly bizarre. Were there really people in this world who took the initiative to come over and be fleeced?

Nine Spatial Rings lay before him. Without thinking, Yang Kai knew they must have been divided evenly among Fan Wu and the others, three from each of them.

Could there be a trick here? Yang Kai found every part of today's events strange. Fan Wu and the others were far too enthusiastic, so enthusiastic that he could hardly stand it.

But even if there was a trick, he was not timid enough to fear inspecting the space inside. He reached out, pinched up one Spatial Ring, and swept it with his Divine Sense. His brow rose, and his eyes widened a little.

Good heavens, so many Source Crystals!

He had no way to count them carefully, but the ring was filled entirely with Middle-Level or higher Source Crystals, with quite a few Upper-Level Source Crystals mixed in. By rough estimate, their value was no less than a hundred million Upper-Level Source Crystals. And how enormous a sum was a hundred million Upper-Level Source Crystals? Converted into Middle-Level Source Crystals, that was ten billion; into Lower-Level Source Crystals, one trillion!

Yang Kai coughed lightly, kept his expression calm, and picked up another Spatial Ring to inspect. Countless spirit flowers and exotic grasses immediately entered his sight, every single stalk rich with medicinal age.

The third Spatial Ring held rare minerals.

The Ancient Land produced only a few kinds of things: Source Crystals, herbs, minerals, and in addition, the Inner Cores of Yao Beasts.

The first three were basically of little use to the yao. Source Crystals and minerals went without saying. The yao did not rely on Source Crystals for cultivation, but on breathing in the essence of the sun and moon and accumulating strength over long years.

Herbs had some use, but no one in the Wild Ancient Land knew Alchemy, so when the yao obtained them, they simply swallowed them whole and could not bring out much of their medicinal effect.

Yao Beast Inner Cores, however, were hugely useful to them. By swallowing other Yao Beasts' Inner Cores, they could rapidly increase their strength.

However, the last time Yang Kai had come to the Wild Ancient Land, he had carried off quite a few Yao Beast Inner Cores. This time, Fan Wu and the others could not gather too many, so they had substituted rare minerals instead.

Three Spatial Rings, each separately filled with one category of goods. All nine Spatial Rings from the three of them followed this pattern.

The wealth Yang Kai had taken from the Ancient Land last time had been considerable, more than three times what these nine Spatial Rings contained, but that time had been different. Then, both sides had torn away all pretenses and fought a great battle. Later, Heaven's Punisher's descendant's bloodline awakened, leaving the three great Saint Venerables anxious and afraid, so they had naturally paid a heavy price to calm the anger in Yang Kai's heart.

This time, they only wanted to send Yang Kai away as quickly as possible. These things already counted as extremely valuable.

Truly, whatever he needed had come straight to him!

There were still more than a hundred thousand people in the Small Xuan Realm waiting to be settled. Once they moved into Lingxiao Palace, what awaited Lingxiao Palace would be an enormous demand for resources, especially Source Crystals. What cultivator did not need them? And there were Pills as well.

On Tongxuan Continent, for Xia Ningshang's sake, he had squandered all the Source Crystals in his Spatial Ring, using almost every last one to arrange the one hundred and eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array. This was exactly when he urgently needed a large quantity of Source Crystals.

Although Hua Qingsi still had Source Crystals on her side, and the trade route between Lingxiao Palace and the Southern Territory should already have been established, in the long run, who would ever complain about having too many Source Crystals?

With this wealth, at the very least, he would not have to worry too much about the early cultivation needs of Lingxiao Palace's disciples.

"Is this for me?" Yang Kai quietly swallowed. It was not that he had never seen wealth before. It was just that this timely help from the three great Saint Venerables was truly baffling.

Fan Wu laughed loudly. "Since we have already brought them out, Brother Yang cannot make us take them back, can you? That would hardly be proper."

"That's right, Brother... Yang. Surely you will give us this much face?" Cang Gou chimed in as well, though calling him Brother Yang felt unbearably awkward, filling his heart with distaste.

"Brother Yang, you should not refuse," Luan Feng said with a smile.

"Since that is the case..." Yang Kai beamed, yet deliberately put on a lofty air. As he swiftly put away the nine Spatial Rings, he said, "Then this Junior will accept respectfully rather than decline. Hahahaha!"

Whatever they were planning, he would take the benefits first and ask questions later. This was far more profitable than the extortion he had expected. Still, since the Ancient Land’s resources were so abundant, should he not make full use of it?

If he could treat the Ancient Land as Lingxiao Palace's rear garden, then what would the disciples ever have to worry about in their cultivation? If they lacked Source Crystals, they could come mine them directly. If they lacked Pills, they could come gather herbs and take them back to refine. They could be completely self-sufficient. If things truly became like that, it would be difficult for Lingxiao Palace not to rise.

His heart stirred, and he blurted out, “Since the three of you think so highly of me, this Junior Brother is willing to swear brotherhood with you, Brothers and Sister. We do not ask to be born in the same year, month, and day, only to share blessings together and face hardships together. What do the three of you think?”

Sharing blessings was one thing, but facing hardships together could be forgotten. If there was something the three Divine Spirits could not handle, he probably would not be of much help either.

"Ah?" The topic changed too quickly, and his thinking leapt too far. Luan Feng could not help letting out a soft cry, lifting one small hand to cover her red lips.

Fan Wu and Cang Gou's brows twitched fiercely as well.

"Pfft..." Mu Na, who had been sitting on the Elder's shoulder all this time, could not help laughing. She watched Yang Kai with keen interest, wondering whether this boy was truly foolish or only pretending. How could he even say such a thing aloud?

"This..." Even with Fan Wu's sharp mind, he did not know how to answer at that moment, and a sudden sense of powerlessness rose in him.

Was this boy not far too shameless? He actually had the nerve to brazenly demand sworn brotherhood with three Divine Spirits. Fan Wu did not deny that Yang Kai had great potential, and perhaps in the future he would stand even higher than Fan Wu himself, but that was only the future. Who knew whether he would live until then? A genius who died young had no value as a friend.
