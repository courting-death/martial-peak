## Chapter 3195 — Ambition

Yang Kai came to a sudden realization, and the confusion that had plagued him for so long finally cleared. No wonder there had always been only ten Great Emperors throughout the countless ages. So Heaven and Earth had limits to what it could contain. It was the same as Tongxuan Continent only being able to give birth to Saint Realm cultivators in the past, and Star Domains only being able to produce Void King Realm cultivators. That was the limit of Heaven and Earth, beyond the reach of human effort.

The Star Realm's limit was ten Great Emperors! Even if many people were no weaker than a Great Emperor in terms of realm, without Heaven and Earth's acknowledgement, they could not become true Great Emperors.

The feather-crowned man was like this. Yang Yan was like this. Even many others like them were the same.

And then there were the Divine Spirits...

Yang Kai had always found it strange. Those Divine Spirits were clearly stronger than the Emperors, yet still seemed inferior to Great Emperors. Now that doubt had finally been resolved.

The ten Great Emperors were existences acknowledged by Heaven and Earth, and were themselves part of the World Laws. Even if their realms were the same, in a true fight, ordinary Divine Spirits absolutely could not compare.

"Are there many people like you?"

"Many, in one sense. Not many, in another." Yang Yan smiled. "But most of them don't show themselves before others, so you don't need to worry too much." Although existences like her were rare, the ages were long, and quite a few people had reached this step, only to stall because they could not obtain Heaven and Earth's acknowledgement. Once one's cultivation reached this level, one's lifespan became extremely long as well. Every one of them was an old monster who had lived for ages, so naturally, their numbers were not small either.

Yang Kai suddenly thought of something else and frowned. "If, according to you, this Bottle of Heaven and Earth has a limit, and there can never be an eleventh Great Emperor, then what am I supposed to do in the future?"

Yang Yan was taken aback. "You really are thinking far ahead."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "That day will come sooner or later." He said it as if it were only natural, leaving even Yang Yan momentarily stunned.

She smiled. "You can wait until a certain Great Emperor reaches the end of his lifespan, then go fight for the Great Emperor position that opens up."

Yang Kai shook his head. "Who knows how many ages that would take." How long was a Great Emperor's lifespan? If nothing unexpected happened, tens of thousands of years, even over a hundred thousand years, would not be the end. He did not dare say they could live as long as Heaven and Earth and shine alongside the sun and moon, but they were probably not far from it.

"Perhaps there is another way." Seeing how serious he was, Yang Yan could not help wanting to tease him.

"What way?" Yang Kai's eyes lit up.

Yang Yan reached out and clenched her hand. "Crush one of the stones in the bottle, and a space will naturally open up."

Yang Kai was startled, then nodded in agreement. "Good idea. Looks like I'll have to find a Great Emperor one day and slaughter him."

Yang Yan looked at him strangely. "Are you serious?"

Yang Kai burst out laughing. "I'm joking. Don't be nervous. Since a Great Emperor has Heaven and Earth's acknowledgement, how could one be so easy to slaughter? Do I really look that ignorant of the immensity of Heaven and Earth?" If it were that easy, the ten Great Emperors would not change hands so slowly.

But there was no denying that this was indeed a method. If things truly reached that point, if it came down to a struggle over the Grand Dao, who could still afford so many scruples? Was a Great Emperor impossible to kill? During the War of the Emperors, not counting Wu Kuang, four other Great Emperors had fallen. From a certain perspective, the four Great Emperors who later rose to their positions all owed Wu Kuang their thanks. If Wu Kuang had not killed those four, they probably would not have had the chance to become Great Emperors even now.

Seeing Yang Kai's expression shift, how could Yang Yan not know that he had truly begun considering the idea? It was not the time to pour cold water on him, so she could only hope he would come to understand the difficulty for himself in the future. Tucking back a lock of hair at her temple, she said, "All right, I've said what needed saying. I should head back."

"Why the hurry!" Yang Kai grabbed her arm.

Yang Yan gave him a disgusted look. "If you have something to say, say it. Take your filthy paws off me!”

Yang Kai said, "Go take a look at Lingxiao Sect. Elder Ye misses you very much." Ye Xijun had quite a deep connection with Yang Yan. Otherwise, she would not have emerged from seclusion back then to help Lingxiao Sect. She had never shown Yang Kai, her Sect Master, much respect, but she respected and loved Yang Yan dearly.

"I won't go. You're the Master of the Star Domain now. Are you afraid there won't be chances to meet again?"

"Speaking of that, I just remembered something. I want to move Lingxiao Sect to the Star Realm. There shouldn't be any problem with that, right?"

"Move the whole thing there?" Yang Yan was slightly startled.

"Is that not allowed?"

Yang Yan frowned. "Aren't there a bit too many people?"

"Not that many, right? Only a few tens of thousands."

"'Only'?" Yang Yan glared at him in exasperation. "Among the cultivators of the various Star Domains, only those whose cultivation reaches the peak are qualified to break open a Void Passage and enter the Star Realm through the Starlight Corridor. In each Star Domain, no more than five cultivators enter the Star Realm every hundred years. Yet now you want to bring tens of thousands of people into the Star Realm. Do you think that's excessive or not? Is that fair to the cultivators of the other Star Domains?"

"Not excessive. Very fair!"

"Stop lying through your teeth!"

"Who told them not to get themselves a good superior?"

"As a Star Envoy, I naturally have to maintain the balance between the various Star Domains. If you're going to cheat and play tricks like this, I cannot agree."

"How can you have no regard for old friendship? Lingxiao Sect was founded by the two of us, after all. They say when one person attains the Dao, even his chickens and dogs ascend to Heaven. Now that I'm the Master of the Star Domain and you're a Star Envoy, I request benefits for Hengluo Star Domain." Yang Kai said this indignantly. In his plans, he wanted more than just to move all of Lingxiao Sect. The talent and resources of the entire Hengluo Star Domain would become his backing.

"Heh heh. That's your business."

Yang Kai was about to argue a little more when his brow suddenly rose, and he followed her gaze off to the side.

She had been staring at Gungun the entire time she spoke. That was a little too strange.

He grinned. "There shouldn't be any problem with me entering the Star Realm alone, right?"

"Of course not." Yang Yan slowly withdrew her gaze and said in a low voice, "Boy, rein yourself in a little. If Iron Blood really notices what's going on over here, you're finished."

"Understood, understood!" Yang Kai replied just as quietly, looking thoroughly furtive.

"All right, that's settled then. I'll head back first. I still have to create a dossier for you. Otherwise, your position as Master of the Star Domain won't be legitimate. Only after you receive the Star Court's acknowledgement can you sit securely in that position." Yang Yan lowered her head and looked at the arm he had been holding all this time, then snorted coldly. "Boy, get one thing clear. From now on, I'm your superior. Don't be so disrespectful."

Yang Kai hurriedly let go. With a flattering expression, he smoothed the wrinkles from her sleeve, cupped his fists, and said, "Respectfully sending off my lord!"

Yang Yan showed the smug expression of someone pleased with a teachable child. Radiance flashed in her hand, and a sharp sword suddenly appeared. At a glance, it was astonishingly identical to the feather-crowned man's sword.

The Star-Cutting Sword. As expected, it was standard equipment for a Star Envoy. As sword light flowed, Yang Yan became one with her sword, shattered space, and transformed into a speck of light that vanished from Yang Kai's sight.

Yang Kai hurriedly called after her, "Right, I founded Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory. Come visit when you have time!"

The light vanished, and he had no idea whether she had heard him. Looking in the direction she had gone, Yang Kai looked as if he had lost something. Only after a good while did he slap his thigh in annoyance. "I forgot to ask her where the Star Court is."

But that was a minor matter. Just as she had said, would he really lack chances to see her again in the future?

Thinking back over his life, he had risen from humble beginnings, yet now he was already the Master of a Star Domain and had established a foundation in the Star Realm as well. All of that had taken him a mere hundred years. After another hundred years, what sort of scene would await him?

No matter what, though, the position of Great Emperor was something he had to contend for. When the time truly came that he had no choice but to fight, he would not hesitate even if it meant shattering one of the stones in the bottle. It was just... who should he choose as his target? Yang Kai suddenly fell into deep distress.

This was like an ant plotting how to kill an elephant. If Yang Yan saw him, she would probably laugh at him again.

He rubbed his head hard, reined in his thoughts, connected with the Star Domain Origin, and let his Divine Sense sink down.

In the boundless darkness, a colossal figure of Yang Kai, vast enough to span Heaven and Earth, manifested. He shouted toward the other side of the darkness, "Dog thief Wu Heng, I'll spare your dog life for now! If you dare set your sights on my Hengluo Star Domain again, don't blame this Young Master for showing no mercy!"

In the Great Wilderness Star Domain, within the Starry Sky, Wu Heng stared at the churning darkness with an ashen face. His eyes were filled with venomous hatred and dread, and his complexion shifted between blue and red. Suddenly, his Qi and blood surged against his heart, and he opened his mouth and spat blood.

This time, he had truly failed to steal the chicken and lost the rice used to lure it. He had originally thought he could devour Hengluo Star Domain and aid his own cultivation, but who would have thought everything would go against him in the end? Not only had he gained no advantage, the other side had swallowed hundreds of millions of li of his Starry Sky, and he had lost several Cultivation Stars.

The Starry Sky had been swallowed, and the Cultivation Stars had vanished without warning, leaving the Great Wilderness Star Domain's Origin Power diminished. As if punishing him, the Origin Power he had refined long ago was actually showing signs of slipping from his control.

After Yang Kai provoked him like this, how could he endure it?

He wanted nothing more than to charge to the other side right now and fight Yang Kai to the death, but when he remembered Lord Star Envoy's instructions before leaving, Wu Heng grew dispirited again.

Even Lord Star Envoy could do nothing to him, so what could Wu Heng do? But if he did not avenge this grudge, how could he possibly swallow this anger?

He was already a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master. Above that was the Great Emperor Realm. He, too, was a man with ambition, so how could he be willing to sink into obscurity here?

Still, a gentleman could wait ten years to take revenge. This matter had to be planned slowly.

In the face of Yang Kai's provocation, he did not dare respond, nor could he respond. Only after a long while did the churning darkness stabilize again, seeming to become the boundary between the two great Star Domains and also to resemble a scar left in Wu Heng's heart.

By then, Yang Kai was already riding Gungun back toward Dark Star.

The matters in the Star Domain were more or less settled, though they had taken more than ten years. Before coming here, he had never imagined it would consume so much effort, but everything had been worth it. With the Star Domain pacified, he had not only become the Master of the Star Domain, but had even gained Yang Yan as his immediate superior. If anything happened, there would be someone to cover for him. His mood was extremely cheerful.

A moment later, he arrived at Dark Star. Yang Kai left Gungun hovering in the void to digest its "accumulated food," while he returned to Lingxiao Sect in a flash.

Inside Lingxiao Sect, atop the main peak, stood a cool pavilion. Su Yan, Xia Ningshang, Shan Qingluo, and Xue Yue were gathered there, all four women together. No one knew what they were talking about, but their sweet voices and laughter rang out without end.

Yang Kai appeared with an air of leisure, coughed lightly, struck a pose, and said with tender affection, "Ladies, I have returned. Sorry to have kept you waiting."
