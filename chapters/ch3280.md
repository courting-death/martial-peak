## Chapter 3280 - Night of the Full Moon

Five days passed in the blink of an eye.

In the forbidden valley, the trees were lush and green, birdsong mingling with the fragrance of flowers. Yang Kai sat cross-legged and looked up at the sky.

The weather was clear, the moon bright, the stars sparse, and the night sky deep. Nothing seemed out of the ordinary. As time slipped by, the full moon slowly climbed high overhead.

Just then, a strange ripple of power suddenly spread out. It was so faint that Yang Kai might not have noticed it at all if he had not been deliberately focusing his attention.

"Has it come?" he murmured. His expression sharpened, and he focused his senses even more carefully. This forbidden land of Luocha Sect only showed unusual changes on the night of the full moon, and that had truly stirred his curiosity.

As he probed with his senses, the surroundings sank back into calm, as if the moment before had only been an illusion.

Before long, however, that strange ripple of power surfaced again. This time it was much clearer than before, flashing once and vanishing the instant it appeared.

"Where is it?" Yang Kai looked around with keen interest, his eyes shining.

The third ripple appeared soon after. Yang Kai, already prepared, snapped his gaze in a certain direction and was immediately stunned.

That direction was the smooth cliff he had noticed before. When he had come here to investigate five days earlier, nothing in the entire forbidden valley had left a deeper impression on him than that cliff. It was polished like a mirror, clear enough to reflect one's image, without the slightest trace of human craft, entirely a marvel shaped by nature. And now, the abnormality had appeared on that very flat, flawless wall of stone.

Yang Kai was somewhat surprised. His Divine Sense swept over the cliff inch by inch as he wondered whether some profound restriction had been laid there, one that had escaped his previous investigation.

But after searching it carefully, he still found nothing.

Fortunately, the strange power rippled more and more often, faster and faster, until at last a wondrous scene suddenly appeared upon the smooth cliff. It was as if the cliff had turned into a mirror. Strange lights and indistinct shadows shifted across its surface, dazzling and blurred, impossible to make out clearly.

As the cliff changed, an enormous amount of Spiritual Qi welled up out of nowhere throughout the valley, filling it to the brim.

Yang Kai could not help being moved. After sensing it briefly, he found that the Spiritual Qi in the valley was every bit as rich as that of the Heaven-Grade secluded areas of Linghu Palace outside Fenglin City. If an ordinary cultivator could cultivate here, one day of effort would match ten days elsewhere.

The valley was nothing like this during the day. Its Spiritual Qi was not much stronger than the surrounding area, yet on the night of the full moon, such an astonishing transformation took place. It truly left one in awe. Still, Yang Kai faintly felt that this change was only the surface. The true key was likely still on that smooth cliff.

He poured strength into his eyes and fixed his gaze on the cliff, trying to discern some clue within it.

The moment he looked closely, Yang Kai let out a startled sound.

In the refracted radiance of the cliff, he had actually sensed something inexplicable. As his attention focused, the folding, reversing rays of light unexpectedly began to show signs of turning into individual characters. The more he concentrated, the clearer they became.

Yang Kai did not dare be careless and memorized them intently.

Only after a while did he realize, to his shock, that this was an extremely remarkable cultivation technique. Now he was truly amazed. On the night of the full moon in Luocha Sect's forbidden valley, a cultivation technique had actually formed on its own upon the cliff. Had he not seen it with his own eyes, Yang Kai would never have believed it.

The technique he saw was incomplete, but after carefully examining it, he found that it seemed suited for women to cultivate and bore some similarity to Luocha Sect's own lineage. With that in mind, he guessed that Luocha Sect's cultivation technique had likely originated from this cliff.

Most likely, Yu Luocha had discovered the abnormality here back then and obtained this opportunity, allowing her to cultivate all the way to the Emperor Realm. It was precisely because of this that she had established a Sect here and founded Luocha Sect.

As Yang Kai's thoughts churned, the scene on the cliff changed again. The blurry, distorted characters disappeared, replaced by a warped human figure. The figure seemed to dance gracefully beneath the moon upon the cliff, yet every gesture also displayed mysterious Divine Abilities, drawing the viewer in until they could hardly look away. Anyone able to immerse themselves in it and comprehend it for a time would surely reap great gains.

Yang Kai did not know how this strange scene had formed. It might have been the work of man, or it might have been born from nature itself. He could only sigh that the vast world truly contained wonders beyond counting.

He immersed his mind in it, carefully comprehending each movement and comparing it with what he himself had learned, and he actually gained quite a bit from it.

This left Yang Kai sighing with regret. Yu Luocha's fortune had truly been poor. Given enough time, with this opportunity alone, she would absolutely have had no trouble becoming a Third-Stage Emperor in the future. Unfortunately, she had died in Wuhua Hall, cut down before she had even had time to grow.

With that, though, he also understood why Yu Luocha had turned this place into a forbidden land and had not even allowed Tao Yingruo and the others to approach.

Something like this was naturally best enjoyed alone. If word spread, it would likely draw covetous eyes from many people. Yu Luocha's selfishness had certainly played a part, but that too was only human nature. Then again, judging by the fact that Yu Luocha had only reached the First Stage of the Emperor Realm, her comprehension must still have been lacking. Otherwise, with this opportunity before her, she never would have stopped at that level.

At a certain moment, the light on the cliff quietly withdrew and suddenly vanished. Yang Kai, still immersed in his many insights, sensed something and turned to look in one direction, his eyes seeming to pierce the barriers of space and fix on a certain place.

He frowned in thought for a moment, then rose and vanished from where he stood. When he reappeared, he was already at the edge of the valley.

He stared silently into the empty space ahead. The Laws of Space suddenly stirred, and he slowly stretched both hands forward. A moment later, they disappeared, as if thrust into the void itself.

"Open!" With a sharp shout, space tore apart, and a Space Crack slowly took form. Yang Kai dove straight in.

All around him were emptiness and chaos, along with void turbulence surging in every direction, enough to make anyone deeply wary. Anyone else who came here would feel that even taking a step was difficult, and one careless move could see them swallowed by the void turbulence and lost in the crack between the void.

Yang Kai, however, remained fearless. This was hardly his first time in such a place. The Laws around him surged, and the oncoming void turbulence was drawn aside by an invisible force, sliding along his body and away. He then wrapped his Divine Sense in the Laws of Space, spread it in all directions, and investigated carefully.

Only after quite some time did disappointment appear on his face. He slowly shook his head and returned the way he had come.

When Yang Kai reappeared in the valley, he finally realized that the whole night had passed without his noticing, and dawn was nearly upon him. Standing there, he looked around, raised his head to the sky, then looked again at the smooth cliff in the distance. A thoughtful look flashed across his face, and an extremely bold guess rose in his mind.

If he was not mistaken, he had some idea of what was behind the changes in this forbidden valley. Unfortunately, he had failed to grasp any traces of it throughout the entire night, only gaining some insight when the abnormality was about to end.

If he wanted to understand it completely, he would probably have to wait another month and take a proper look on the next full moon night.

He could not help feeling somewhat vexed, but when he considered that he had not come away empty-handed, he felt relieved.

On his way back to Luocha Peak, Ran Yirou and Yu Keren were both waiting respectfully outside the hall. The moment they saw Yang Kai, they bowed.

Yang Kai walked past them and headed straight into his room, his voice drifting back. "Notify me when it is time for the ceremony."

"Yes," Ran Yirou and Yu Keren answered respectfully.

Over the next several days, Yang Kai remained in closed-door cultivation and did not step outside.

Only on the day of the ceremony did Yang Kai, attended by Ran Yirou and Yu Keren, dress in formal robes.

This was not his first investiture ceremony. The last one had been held at Qingyang Shrine. Thinking about it, it was rather funny. Both ceremonies had been for his appointment as a Guest Elder, yet each one was grander than the last.

When other Sects invited a Guest Elder, they would not usually hold any ceremony. At most, they would send a notice to the nearby Sects on good terms with them, informing everyone that this person was now their Sect's Guest Elder and asking others to show him some face if they met him outside in the future.

But both Qingyang Shrine and Luocha Sect placed great importance on Yang Kai. Qingyang Shrine aside, the safety of all of Luocha Sect was now tied to Yang Kai alone, so how could they not make the ceremony grand?

Guests came from all around to offer congratulations. Not only did the nearby forces that had received invitations send representatives, but even many people Luocha Sect had never dealt with before hurried over. Facing one unexpected Emperor Realm Master after another, the disciples in charge of welcoming guests at the mountain gate were under tremendous pressure. Tao Yingruo, Qin Pei, and the others did not know whether to laugh or cry; even they had not expected so many people to attend their investiture ceremony.

Luocha Sect had only five Dao Origin Realm cultivators in total. Left with no choice, they sent three of them to the mountain gate to welcome guests, while the remaining two busied themselves receiving people inside. They were so rushed that their feet barely touched the ground, with no attention to spare.

But they all knew that these people had come uninvited not to give Luocha Sect face, but to give Yang Kai face.

After so much time to spread, the matter of Wuhua Hall had already become known throughout the Southern Territory. Moreover, nearly every Sect in the Southern Territory with even the slightest reputation had been involved at the time, and basically everyone who had survived owed Yang Kai a life-saving favor.

Since they had come to offer congratulations, congratulatory gifts were naturally unavoidable.

The disciples in charge of recording the gifts were nearly numbed by shock as one lavish gift after another arrived. Countless heaven-and-earth treasures left a group of Luocha Sect female disciples, who had seen little of the world, staring wide-eyed. Even the hands holding the brushes trembled as they made their entries.

With so many people arriving, the seats prepared at first were far from enough. Tao Yingruo urgently mobilized the Sect's disciples and added nearly five times as many seats, only then barely filling the gap.

On this day, almost all of Luocha Sect's thousand-plus disciples, regardless of cultivation level, were kept running without pause, with not the slightest time to rest.

Afterward, when the numbers were tallied, more than a hundred Emperor Realm Masters had come to Luocha Sect to offer congratulations, and cultivators of other levels were too numerous to count. Nearly fifteen hundred guests had come to the main hall alone.

That number was greater than the total number of disciples in Luocha Sect, truly showing Tao Yingruo and the others what one person's influence could mean.

The ceremony was held as scheduled. Amid countless voices of congratulations, Yang Kai became Luocha Sect's Guest Elder. With his status settled, Tao Yingruo and the others finally put their hearts at ease.
