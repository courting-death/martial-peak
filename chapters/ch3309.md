## Chapter 3309 — Awaiting You for Some Time

But he also knew that some things in this world, once missed, were simply missed, and regret would do no good. He had already told Yang Kai that the Spirit Beast Island token was something he had made up. If he came out with a different story now, would that not be slapping his own face?

Tang Sheng was not that thick-skinned.

Still, he truly admired Red Ghost's judgment. In terms of cultivation, Red Ghost was at the First Stage while he was at the Second Stage, so he held the edge. In age and experience, Red Ghost, a rising star, was even less his match.

Yet before the battle began, Red Ghost had already judged that Yang Kai would survive. What did that prove? It proved that Red Ghost's intuition was far sharper than his own.

Perhaps... this was the greatest gap between him and these rising stars. With such foresight, they were destined to walk ahead of him one day.

Red Ghost said indifferently, "It is too early to say that." There was no satisfaction on his face from having his prediction come true. Instead, he looked even more solemn, his eyes sweeping restlessly through the void as if searching for something.

Tang Sheng frowned at those words, unsure what Red Ghost meant. With the battle in its current state, anyone who was not blind could probably see that Yang Kai's victory was assured. Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng would be fortunate enough just to escape alive, so what else could possibly change? Tang Sheng glanced at the seven or eight surviving Emperor Realm Masters from the two great forces and slowly shook his head. They had watched their own Sect Master and Saint Lord fall into a bitter struggle, yet not one of them had the courage to offer even the slightest help. Their faces were all filled with terror, clearly having been frightened witless by Yang Kai’s earlier slaughter. They could not possibly be the unexpected turn.

The moment Yang Kai dealt with Fu Bo, or Shi Huo dealt with Xu Changfeng, these people would probably scatter like frightened birds and beasts.

That fellow Red Ghost... what exactly was he trying to say? Tang Sheng was thoroughly confused.

In the space of those few words, the battlefield changed again. Killing intent surged from Yang Kai's entire body. He pointed, and the Mountain and River Bell, which had been spinning in midair all this time, suddenly turned into a streak of light and shadow and dropped over Fu Bo's head.

The intent to suppress Heaven and Earth spread out with a thunderous crash.

Yang Kai looked at him coldly. "Fu Bo, since you chose to stick your head out, this Lord will grant your wish today. Die."

Fu Bo paled in fright. His Ghost King Body shifted and flickered as he tried to avoid the Mountain and River Bell's suppression, but no matter how he changed his form, even when he cast spells to block it, he could not delay the Mountain and River Bell for so much as a breath. This Primordial Era Treasure clung to him like a maggot to bone. The aura that suppressed all things in Heaven and Earth locked firmly onto him, as though he could flee to the ends of the world and still never escape it.

He would die! He would absolutely die.

If he had been at his peak, Fu Bo might have been able to find a chance to survive, but after his great battle with Yang Kai, wounds covered his body. How could he still have the strength to withstand the Mountain and River Bell's suppression? As that irresistible force pressed down on him, he could even hear his own bones crack.

"Brother Wu, save me!" Fu Bo could endure no longer and shouted.

The shout came out of nowhere. Aside from Yang Kai, who had been prepared for it all along, Tang Sheng and the others all looked startled.

But the moment the words left his mouth, an unfamiliar figure suddenly charged out of the void and threw a punch at Yang Kai from afar. In an instant, Heaven and Earth trembled, the Laws surged, and an incomparably huge fist shadow wrapped in a world-destroying aura blasted toward Yang Kai.

Tang Sheng and the others were shocked by this sudden turn.

"There really was an ambush!" Red Ghost growled through clenched teeth. Earlier, when he had moved together with the people from the two great forces, he had sensed that they were not showing all their strength, that the two great forces seemed to be hiding something. His cultivation had simply been too low to detect it clearly, leaving him only with a vague feeling.

Seeing this now, he understood at once that his feeling had been right. The people the two great forces had shown openly did not represent the whole of their strength. An expert had been hiding in secret all along.

Judging from the traces of this person's attack, he was shockingly also a Third Stage Emperor. One could even say his strength surpassed Fu Bo's and Xu Changfeng's; otherwise, he would not have been kept as a hidden trump card.

The punch came far too suddenly, and precisely while Yang Kai was wholly focused on driving the Mountain and River Bell against Fu Bo. It could be said that he was completely unguarded. The outcome could almost be foreseen.

All color drained from Lan He's face in an instant. Even Red Ghost could hardly bear to keep watching.

A Half-Dragon body was indeed powerful, even sturdier than Fu Bo's Ghost King Body, but this was a Third Stage Emperor who had hidden in the dark and observed for so long before unleashing a fully prepared strike. Naturally, he was confident it would land.

Red Ghost looked contemptuous. Although he loved battle and was bloodthirsty by nature, he still had a bottom line. Everyone he challenged had a higher cultivation than his own, and he used them to temper his Martial Dao heart.

To begin with, the two great forces sending so many people to deal with Yang Kai, a mere Second-Stage Emperor Realm Master, already amounted to using numbers to bully the few and strength to bully the weak. Now there was even a Third Stage Emperor lying in ambush in the dark. Despicable was no longer enough to describe it.

Under countless gazes, that enormous fist shadow blasted directly into Yang Kai. Sure enough, Yang Kai, focused on dealing with Fu Bo, had no time to react.

As the shockwave exploded, the void around Yang Kai trembled. Yang Kai himself seemed to dissolve into nothingness, as if that punch had blown him to pieces.

At the sight, Lan He trembled and nearly collapsed to the ground.

Dead? How could he be dead? She had not yet repaid him for saving her life, so how could he die just like this?

Fu Bo, who had been in an utterly miserable state, burst into laughter. "No matter how crafty you are, you still had to drink this Lord's foot-washing water. Boy, this is what comes of making an enemy of this Lord."

After saying this, he had not even had time to gloat before his brow suddenly furrowed again. He looked up at the Mountain and River Bell, for its remaining momentum had not diminished at all. It was still falling straight toward the top of his head.

The power that suppressed Heaven and Earth crashed down over him. Fu Bo's five-zhang Ghost King Body suddenly found even taking a step impossibly difficult, and he cried out in alarm, "What is going on?"

Yang Kai had already been killed, so why was this Primordial Era Treasure still suppressing him? Fu Bo was terrified and full of regret. Just now, when he saw Yang Kai blasted into nothingness by the newcomer's punch, he had grown careless and stopped trying to break free of the Mountain and River Bell. That single instant of hesitation had now made escape impossible.

He barely managed to move, but before he could leave his original position, the Mountain and River Bell came crashing down and suppressed him on the spot.

Only then did the newcomer shout, "Careful!"

But how could the warning possibly have come in time? He could only watch helplessly as the Mountain and River Bell swallowed Fu Bo's figure. Fu Bo's furious roars and howls, like ghosts crying and wolves baying, immediately rang out from inside the bell. The Mountain and River Bell was struck until it clanged again and again, but soon all went quiet.

The sound of the Mountain and River Bell already had the power to suppress and disturb the Divine Soul. Now that Fu Bo had been suppressed inside it, the harder he struggled, the more he would suffer. After thrashing about a few times, he no longer dared to move and could only hope with all his heart that the newcomer would save him.

"The years wither and flourish, like a shuttle, like a dream!" A murmuring voice rang out, drawing everyone's attention.

There, Yang Kai, who should have been blasted into nothingness, had reappeared completely unharmed in his original position. His two dragon claws formed an unfathomably profound seal, and his face held a cold indifference that seemed to look down upon all living beings as he pushed a palm forward.

Years Like a Shuttle Seal!

Time seemed to stop for an instant. By the time the newcomer realized something was wrong, a palm print had already reached him. He paled in fright, instinctively sensing that this move was extraordinary. He hurriedly used every means at his disposal, and a cloud of blood mist burst from his body to meet the palm print while he transformed into a streak of blood light and rapidly retreated.

The Years Like a Shuttle Seal passed through the blood mist without the slightest obstruction and struck the newcomer head-on.

He jolted from the impact and spat out a mouthful of blood. Under the corrosion of the Power of Time, he instantly aged by several dozen years, and even a few silver strands appeared among his black hair.

"Years Like a Shuttle Seal, the peerless Divine Ability of the Flowing Time Great Emperor!" Red Ghost's mouth fell open so wide an egg could almost fit inside.

Cold sweat also dripped from Tang Sheng's forehead. "Elder Chi, are you certain this is the Flowing Time Great Emperor's Divine Ability?"

Red Ghost turned to look at him in shock. "Valley Lord Tang is older than I am. Have you not heard the great name of this Divine Ability?"

Tang Sheng said awkwardly, "I have heard of it, but the Flowing Time Great Emperor fell long ago. How did Yang Kai learn that Divine Ability?"

Red Ghost said, "I am only a junior. How would I know such a thing?"

Hearing this, Tang Sheng thought that was true. If even he did not know, how would Red Ghost? He had truly been badly shaken, and his mind was somewhat muddled. Years Like a Shuttle was an immense Divine Ability involving the Laws of Time. Yang Kai actually knew it too?

If it were only a frightening name, that would be one thing, but looking at the state of the person struck by the move, he had clearly aged a great deal in an instant. That could only be the corrosion of genuine Power of Time.

In other words, the Years Like a Shuttle Seal was real!

The Laws of Time and the Laws of Space, two great abstruse Laws, were gathered in one person. This was simply heaven-defying.

Entering the threshold of the Laws of Space was extremely difficult, and cultivating them was even harder, but from ancient times to the present, there had still been a few capable people and extraordinary talents proficient in this Dao. Li Wuyi of Spirit Beast Island, for example, was proficient in the Laws of Space.

But the Laws of Time were even harder to enter than the Laws of Space. Looking across the long river of a hundred thousand years of history, the only one who had become famous throughout the world through the Laws of Time was the Flowing Time Great Emperor.

Some had even speculated that if the Flowing Time Great Emperor had not fallen so early, he would absolutely have been qualified to contend with the Heaven Devourer Great Emperor Wu Kuang.

What puzzled Tang Sheng now, however, was the identity of this expert who had suddenly appeared. After sensing carefully, he confirmed that the other party had Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivation, yet his appearance was completely unfamiliar, matching none of the Emperor Realm Masters Tang Sheng knew.

This fellow seemed to have appeared out of nowhere, leaving no trace to follow.

Yang Kai, on the other hand, had turned the enemy's scheme against him and caught the other party off guard, as if he had expected this all along.

"Wu Heng, this Lord has been waiting for you for quite some time." Yang Kai had succeeded with one move, but he did not press his advantage. Instead, he looked at the newcomer with a mocking expression.
