## Chapter 3278 — Luocha Sect's Forbidden Land

The two women's voices matched their temperaments: one sounded like a ghostly spirit in an empty valley, while the other was sweet, soft, and pleasing to the ear. Yu Keren in particular made Yang Kai feel, the moment she opened her mouth, as if his bones had grown three taels lighter and his whole body were floating toward immortality.

The two women were not especially strong, but their cultivation was still respectable. Both were at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm, and one more step would bring them into the Dao Origin Realm. Given Luocha Sect's current situation, Dao Origin Realm cultivators were true pillars. Tao Yingruo's willingness to send the two of them was enough to show how much importance she placed on Yang Kai. She had no choice but to value him. The safety of the entire Sect now rested on Yang Kai. If Yang Kai truly had certain thoughts, then never mind these two female disciples; even offering themselves to his bed would not be too great a price.

Yang Kai nodded. "You must already know something of this Lord's background. No need to be so restrained. Let us get along peacefully."

"These disciples would not dare." Both women kept their heads lowered.

Yang Kai smiled faintly and left them to it, turning to walk inside.

Ran Yirou and Yu Keren quickly followed him in, while the other female disciples remained outside.

After entering the hall, the two women followed closely behind him, answering only a sentence or two when Yang Kai asked them something and remaining silent at all other times, fully adopting the posture of disciples. Although Ran Yirou appeared poised and composed, Yang Kai could still sense the nervousness and unease in both of them.

The two women had come here under orders to serve Yang Kai, and before this, Tao Yingruo had repeatedly instructed them in person. She had told them plainly that they were not to refuse any request Elder Yang made.

Neither of them was a child, so of course they understood what Tao Yingruo's instructions meant. They had been raised in Luocha Sect since childhood. Though they had gone out to travel several times and had dealt with some men, those men had left them with a very poor impression. Their eyes had been full of covetousness and possessiveness, and not one of them suited the two women's tastes. Because of that, they had developed an instinctive aversion to other men as well. But Luocha Sect was the place that had raised them. Now that the Sect was in trouble, they naturally could not stand aside and watch.

When they accepted this task, the two women had already resigned themselves to their fate. Facing an Elder who could take whatever he wanted from them at any time, how could they possibly treat him as they normally would? Naturally, they became even more cautious.

This place had originally been the residence of Luocha Sect's Sect Master, Yu Luocha, so the furnishings inside naturally carried a feminine style. Under Tao Yingruo's arrangements, however, most of those traces had already been removed. The various objects in the hall seemed to have been newly purchased, and while a faint fragrance still lingered here and there, it was harmless enough.

Before long, Yang Kai had toured the entire residence. He then leisurely walked back out and took the two women with him for a stroll through Luocha Sect. Though the journey here had been tiring, it hardly counted as hardship for him, and he was in no rush to rest. Since he was going to shelter Luocha Sect, he naturally needed to understand the place. Besides, taking in different scenery and local customs was sometimes a form of tempering one's state of mind.

Luocha Sect had twenty or thirty Spirit Peaks of various sizes, each with its own scenery. They began with Luocha Peak, where he was staying.

They took in the sights at a casual pace. Ran Yirou and Yu Keren attended him closely, still answering when asked and otherwise keeping silent.

Only when they reached a certain place did Ran Yirou speak. "The forbidden land lies ahead. It would not be convenient for these disciples to follow. Please forgive us, Elder." As she spoke, she and Yu Keren both stopped.

"Forbidden land?" Yang Kai stopped as well and looked that way a few more times, but he saw nothing special. Nodding, he said, "Since it is forbidden land, let us look elsewhere."

Every Sect had its own forbidden land, and Luocha Sect was naturally no exception. Ordinary disciples were generally not qualified to set foot in such places; only certain powerful experts within the Sect could do so.

Yu Keren said, "If Elder wishes to go, you may go on your own."

Yang Kai pondered for a moment. "Would that be appropriate?" Since they had already said it was forbidden land, what would it mean if he still went in?

Yu Keren smiled sweetly, two small dimples appearing at the corners of her mouth. "It is all right. Senior Aunt Tao instructed us that within Luocha Sect, Elder may go wherever he wishes."

Since she had put it that way, Yang Kai no longer stood on ceremony. In any case, even if Luocha Sect really did have some secrets, he would not take them too seriously. He might as well see what this forbidden land was like. He instructed the two women, "Then wait here for me a moment. I will be back shortly."

"Yes!" the two women replied respectfully. When they raised their heads, Yang Kai had already vanished. They looked at each other and secretly clicked their tongues in astonishment. Although they had not seen many Emperor Realm Masters, they had still seen three or five, yet none of them had ever given the two women the same unfathomable feeling Yang Kai did. Compared with him, their own Sect Master seemed to stand on an entirely different level.

Thinking this, Yu Keren quietly transmitted her voice. "Senior Sister, Elder Yang seems very easy to get along with."

The two of them had spent the past several days on tenterhooks. After all, Tao Yingruo had suddenly assigned them to Luocha Peak to serve the Guest Elder who was about to arrive, then instructed them on all manner of things, so they had inevitably worried that the newcomer would be a hulking brute with a violent temper. Only after meeting Yang Kai did they realize he was nothing like they had imagined. Not only was he not ugly, he even had something of a handsome, distinguished bearing, and his temperament seemed extremely mild.

"Elder may be easy to get along with, but we must not overstep," Ran Yirou said solemnly. "You should speak less. If you really have to speak, change your voice a little, otherwise Elder might think you are trying to seduce him."

Yu Keren flushed scarlet at the scolding and looked as if she might burst into tears. "But this is just how I talk. What am I supposed to do?"

Ran Yirou felt a headache coming on as she looked at her. "There you go again..." Never mind a man; even Ran Yirou herself felt her heart stir when she heard Yu Keren speak like that. That sweet, soft voice was like an invisible little hand, constantly scratching at one's heart.

Yu Keren lowered her head, aggrieved. "All right. From now on, I will not speak in front of Elder."

Ran Yirou sighed. "The Sect now has to shelter beneath Elder's wings. You and I serve him at close quarters, and we represent the Sect's face. We have been Senior Sister and Junior Sister for many years, so of course I know your nature, but Elder Yang has only just arrived. If he really misunderstands something, he will not only look down on you and me, but on the Sect as well."

"Mm, Senior Sister is right." Yu Keren lifted her eyes and nodded firmly, then reached up and pinched her own mouth, saying indistinctly, "Then from now on, I will be mute!"

Ran Yirou shook her head. "There is no need to go that far. If Elder really asks you something, you cannot refuse to answer."

"I will remember." Yu Keren nodded like a chick pecking at rice. Suddenly her eyes darted, and she bit lightly at her red lip. "Senior Sister, let me ask you something."

"What?" Seeing her strange expression, Ran Yirou immediately had a bad feeling.

Yu Keren fidgeted shyly, blushing before she even spoke. "When night comes, if Elder really... really..." She unexpectedly could not go on.

Ran Yirou's cheeks also turned bright red. She reached out and poked Yu Keren on the forehead, gritting her teeth. "You wretched girl, what are you thinking?"

Yu Keren held her head in both hands. "But Senior Aunt Tao said so, and besides, you know it too, don't you? Those Emperor Realm Masters all have countless wives and concubines. I heard that every night..."

"Every night what?" Ran Yirou's voice trembled slightly. As she asked, she felt her cheeks grow unbearably hot, as though they had been burned by fire.

"They cannot be happy without women..." Yu Keren lowered her head and forced out the words.

Ran Yirou fell silent. Yu Keren lifted her eyes, tilted her head to look at her, waved a hand in front of her face, and called, "Senior Sister..."

Ran Yirou smiled freely. “The union of Yin and Yang is part of the Great Dao of Heaven and Earth. If it truly came to that, with his status and standing, how could he possibly be unworthy of us? If anything, we would be the ones reaching above ourselves.”

Stars immediately lit in Yu Keren's eyes, and she looked at Ran Yirou in admiration. "Wow, Senior Sister, you're so open-minded!"

Ran Yirou reached out and pinched her cheek, lightly gritting her pearly teeth. "Is that not because you kept asking and asking? No more questions, and no more talking about this."

Yu Keren's eyes turned watery, and the pain nearly brought tears to them. She begged for mercy, "Senior Sister, spare me! I was wrong, all right?"

...

The so-called forbidden land was nothing more than a valley behind Luocha Peak. Yang Kai made a circuit of it and found nothing worth noting at all. The Spiritual Qi here was only slightly denser than elsewhere, and there was nothing in the valley that could aid cultivation either.

This puzzled him greatly. Since it was a forbidden land, surely it should be hiding some secrets, yet no matter how he looked at it, the valley seemed entirely ordinary.

Yang Kai turned his attention to a cliff face. If there was anything in this valley worth noting, it could only be this cliff. It was smooth as a mirror and remarkably even; it was hard to imagine how such a flat cliff face had formed.

Yang Kai went forward and knocked on it in several places, only to discover that it was not man-made, but a natural wonder. For a time, he clicked his tongue in amazement.

Still, the cliff face was just an ordinary cliff face. Yang Kai found no hidden passages, mechanisms, barriers, or anything of the sort.

It was not that Yang Kai insisted on prying into someone else's secrets; curiosity had simply gotten the better of him. In any case, Tao Yingruo had already said there was nowhere in Luocha Sect he could not go. Since they were being so generous, he had no reason to hold himself back.

Only after investigating for most of the day did Yang Kai return the way he had come, his expression dark.

He could now confirm that the valley was an ordinary valley, and the cliff face an ordinary cliff face. There was simply no secret there at all.

Why would such a place be called forbidden land? Yang Kai could not figure it out no matter how he thought about it. Of course, if he went and asked Tao Yingruo, he would probably learn something, but after thinking it over, Yang Kai decided against it. If he discovered it himself, that was his own ability. If he failed to discover anything and then deliberately asked them about it, he would only make it look as though he coveted someone else's secrets.

When he returned, Ran Yirou and Yu Keren were still standing in the same spot. As he walked up to them, he could not help taking a few extra looks, because both women's cheeks were faintly flushed now and fragrance drifted from their bodies, making it difficult to look away.

They had clearly noticed as well, and both seemed somewhat embarrassed.

Under the two women's continued guidance, Yang Kai went on touring Luocha Sect. They occasionally encountered patrolling disciples along the way. Most of them had already seen Yang Kai, and those who had not could still tell from Ran Yirou and Yu Keren's attitudes who he was, so they naturally let him pass without the slightest obstruction.
