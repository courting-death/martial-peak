## Chapter 3189 — Are You Done Calling Me That?

In other words, even the Master of a Star Domain was under the Star Court's jurisdiction and restraint. They could not simply do whatever they wanted inside their own Star Domain, because if a Star Domain's Master wished it, they had the power to destroy the entire Star Domain.

And this feather-crowned man before him was, in a sense, the direct superior of Star Domain Masters like Yang Kai. Otherwise, Wu Heng was still a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master; there was no reason for him to grovel so humbly before this man.

Yang Kai knew nothing about any of this. He had come into it halfway, bypassing the Star Court entirely to refine Hengluo Star Domain, so naturally he felt no reverence for it.

The feather-crowned man raised his sword above his head, and in that instant his figure seemed to tower like a mountain. It was not that his body had grown larger; his aura had simply surged upward, piercing the heavens and stirring the stars.

"Boy, considering how difficult your cultivation has been, offer up your Small World at once, and this Lord may spare your life."

Yang Kai growled without hesitation, "In your dreams!"

"Boy, you're courting death!" A cold gleam flashed in the feather-crowned man's eyes. His long sword chopped down, radiance flowing across the blade, its might unfathomable. In that instant, between Heaven and Earth, only the brilliance of this one sword remained.

It was not some profound technique, merely a casual swing, yet it seemed to seal off all Yang Kai's paths of retreat and gave him the feeling that there was no way to dodge.

Shua...

Radiance flashed, and the long sword froze in place. Yang Kai brought his palms together and raised them over his head, clamping the sword above his skull.

Several strands of black hair snapped and were pulverized by the invisible Sword Qi.

"Hm?" The feather-crowned man was faintly surprised, clearly not having expected Yang Kai to catch his sword. When this sword fell, even Wu Heng would have to bare his neck and accept death, yet this boy's aura did not seem as powerful as Wu Heng's. What ability did he have to come away unharmed?

Still, he did not care much. Applying strength through his hand, he pressed the long sword down.

Yang Kai instantly felt as if a mountain were pressing down on his head, its weight still increasing, as though it would not stop until he had been flattened. When he looked up, he saw the feather-crowned man staring at him in mockery, as if enjoying the struggles of an ant.

Fury surged through Yang Kai, and vicious resolve rose with it. His left eye instantly transformed into a majestic vertical pupil, and from that pupil a long blade tore through the air, chopping down at the feather-crowned man's head.

"A Divine Soul Imperial Treasure!" The feather-crowned man's eyes lit up. The World-Ending Demon Eye barely affected him, and he instantly saw through the Soul-Severing Blade's true nature.

Imperial Treasures were nothing rare to him, but Divine Soul Imperial Treasures were much scarcer. Of course, although the Soul-Severing Blade was impressive, it was not enough to tempt his greed. The only thing he truly cared about was the Small Xuan Realm; that was what would greatly benefit his cultivation.

He was simply curious how the young man before him possessed so many good things. An ordinary Emperor Realm cultivator should not have such reserves.

With a flicker of Divine Sense, he hastily laid a layer of defense before his Sea of Consciousness.

Hong...

A tremor rang out from the depths of his mind. The hurried defense failed to have the slightest effect. A pure attack took the shape of a long blade and carved a deep ravine through his Sea of Consciousness, as if trying to split it in two. In an instant, waves surged and great billows churned inside his Sea of Consciousness.

The feather-crowned man let out a muffled groan as stabbing pain shot through his head.

The Soul-Severing Blade alone might not have harmed him. With his cultivation, it was already difficult for external things to injure him. But with the Heaven-Shattering Strike added to the Soul-Severing Blade, its power was far more than a simple sum of the two.

Many years had passed since Yang Kai last used the Soul-Severing Blade. All this time, he had been nourishing it with his Divine Sense, storing up an extremely terrifying power. Now, unleashed with the Heaven-Shattering Strike Secret Technique taught to him by Tian Yan, it had indeed proved effective in a single blow.

"Boy..." The feather-crowned man's expression twisted savagely, his shame flaring into rage. He had never imagined he would suffer a loss at Yang Kai's hands. He had thought Yang Kai was merely an ant he could crush at will, only to be bitten by that ant through a moment of carelessness.

Hong...

An enormous fist suddenly filled his vision, and a force like collapsing mountains and overturning seas rushed at him head-on. The feather-crowned man shot away like a meteor, vanishing from sight in the blink of an eye.

Yang Kai stood with his fist withdrawn. At some point, his figure had transformed into a twenty-zhang dragon body, horns growing from his head, a tail trailing behind him, and scales covering his exposed skin. His bearing was awe-inspiring, proud, and utterly domineering.

"You old dog, are you done calling me 'boy' yet?"

Before his voice had even faded, a flash of cold light burst out. The feather-crowned man became one with his sword and crossed tens of thousands of li in an instant, arriving before Yang Kai and slashing at his dragon head.

Yang Kai reached out with a dragon claw and seized the long sword.

With a clang, metal rang against metal. Sparks flew in all directions as his dragon scales cracked.

Yang Kai clenched the sword tightly, paying no heed to the wounds its razor edge cut into him and letting the matchless Sword Qi surge into his body to wreak havoc. With his other dragon claw, he formed a seal one-handed, and wondrous Laws immediately began to surge.

"This is..." The feather-crowned man's eyelids sank. In that instant, he actually felt time stop flowing, and even his five senses fell prey to illusion.

"The Laws of Time!"

"The years wither and flourish, like a shuttle, like a dream!" Yang Kai struck toward him with one palm, his expression solemn.

"Years Like a Shuttle Seal!" For the first time, the feather-crowned man's expression changed slightly, and he actually called out the name of this Divine Ability in one breath. Clearly, he recognized this Divine Ability of the Flowing Time Great Emperor. A trace of hesitation flashed through his eyes, but it quickly turned into resolve, and he pulled back.

But it was already too late. Yang Kai's palm seal fell, and the power of Time completely enveloped him.

Yang Kai, however, frowned. Looking up, he saw the feather-crowned man several dozen li away, light flickering around his body as it resisted the erosion of the Power of Time. In just a short moment, that power was completely dispersed.

From several dozen li away, the feather-crowned man examined Yang Kai seriously for the first time.

A half-dragon body, and he had even cultivated the Years Like a Shuttle Seal. What was this boy's background? The Years Like a Shuttle Seal he displayed was only a superficial imitation compared to one personally used by the Flowing Time Great Emperor, but it still involved the profoundly mysterious Laws of Time. If the feather-crowned man had not withdrawn decisively just now, he might truly have suffered a minor injury.

That small injury was not unbearable, but once the Power of Time eroded him, dispelling it would cost him something. A noble son does not sit beneath a crumbling eave; he had no wish to pay even a bearable price.

"Boy, what is your name?" The feather-crowned man stared at Yang Kai, a little more caution in his gaze.

If it were only the Years Like a Shuttle Seal, that would be one thing. The Flowing Time Great Emperor had fallen no one knew how many years ago. Even if Yang Kai had obtained his inheritance by fortunate coincidence, he was only a lucky man, not someone the feather-crowned man needed to dread.

But that twenty-zhang body gave him an extraordinary feeling. It was clearly only a half-dragon body, yet it made one feel as if they were facing a true Great Dragon!

Moreover, he had never heard of anyone cultivating a half-dragon body to such a degree.

“Now you’re asking me?” Yang Kai grinned at him, his left hand tightening around the long sword as fresh blood streamed down. When the feather-crowned man had pulled back just now, he had tried to take this sword with him, but with Yang Kai's brute strength, how could Yang Kai let him succeed? He had kept the sword behind.

Hearing this, the feather-crowned man immediately felt a bad premonition rise in him and hurriedly said, "Forget it, this Lord does not want to..."

"You are not worthy to know this Young Master's name!" Yang Kai threw his head back and laughed, looking as arrogant as if he had finally avenged himself.

The feather-crowned man's face darkened. Of course he knew Yang Kai was deliberately throwing his own words back at him. His expression sank at once, and he formed a seal with one hand, shouting in a low voice, "Sword, come!"

Although Yang Kai had kept hold of his long sword, it was still the feather-crowned man's Secret Treasure, connected to his mind and spirit. Yang Kai could not truly seize it. With a single thought, he could recall the sword, which was why he had withdrawn without hesitation just now.

At his command, the long sword clenched tightly in Yang Kai's palm immediately rang and leapt, struggling with all its might to break free of Yang Kai's control.

Wild Sword Qi surged through it, drilling into Yang Kai's arm and destroying his flesh and Meridians.

Peng peng peng...

One bloody hole after another burst open along Yang Kai's arm. From those wounds, Sword Qi ran wild and fresh blood flowed.

Yet he clenched his teeth and held on with a death grip, refusing to let go. Pain twisted his face into something as hideous as a demon.

Watching this, the feather-crowned man sneered, "A mayfly trying to shake a tree. You overestimate yourself."

"There are cows flying in the sky!" Yang Kai looked up at him, his eyes shining brightly beneath his thick black brows.

The feather-crowned man was startled, thinking Yang Kai had used some sinister trick again, and hurriedly glanced upward, only to find nothing there.

Yang Kai laughed. “You blew them away with all your hot air.”

The feather-crowned man froze for a moment before coming back to his senses. The corners of his eyes twitched, and his hand seal changed again as he shouted in a low voice, "Sword, come!"

In an instant, Sword Qi ran wild between Yang Kai's five fingers and sword light blazed. What he held no longer seemed to be a sword, but a star on the verge of exploding. Even his Dragon Transformation body could not withstand those sharp strands of Sword Qi. Piece after piece of flesh was sliced away, exposing ghastly white bone.

"I still won't give it to you!" Yang Kai roared, the sound like a dragon's cry, so deafening it nearly burst the ears. He glanced around, his eyes suddenly brightening, then flashed in front of Gungun. With a fierce throw, he flung the long sword straight into Gungun's mouth.

Gungun swallowed it with a gulp, then clamped its mouth shut. Its enormous body suddenly began to expand and contract violently.

"You..." The feather-crowned man flew into a rage. He had never imagined that in a contest like this, his own Secret Treasure would actually be taken from him. Thick humiliation surged through him, and he wished he could grind Yang Kai's bones to dust right then and there to vent his hatred.

"Excellent!" Yang Kai's left hand drooped. His arm was already a mangled ruin, so damaged he could barely lift it, and the flesh of his palm was completely gone, leaving only ghastly white bone. Yet there was not the slightest trace of pain on his face, only wild delight.

Trading one arm for the other party's long sword was not a losing deal.

"You. Are. Dead!" The feather-crowned man stopped paying attention to his long sword. Now that Gungun had swallowed it, even if he had abilities that reached the heavens, he could forget about retrieving it immediately. It was equivalent to being banished into another space, making the connection between him and the sword extremely faint.
