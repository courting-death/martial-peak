## Chapter 3249 — Dark Horse

The market was enormous. A canyon more than a dozen li long was almost entirely given over to it. Shops lined the ground on both sides and filled the caves in the rock walls, while countless martial artists streamed back and forth amid the endless calls of vendors hawking their wares.

This was Wuhua Hall's market. Every shop belonged to Wuhua Hall, though some were businesses its disciples had established here. One could find practically anything for sale. It was usually lively, but never to this degree.

The main reason was the recent opening of the Martial Gathering. Tens of thousands of martial artists from the Southern Territory had gathered here, so naturally some of them came to browse. Before Yang Kai even landed, he saw martial artists of every manner of dress moving below, while disciples from the various Sects, wearing matching robes and traveling in groups of three or five, wove continuously through the crowd.

In truth, every great Sect had a market like this. Qingyang Shrine had one as well, though Yang Kai had never gone to see it. Presumably, it was not much inferior to this place.

They landed before the market without being stopped, and Yang Kai walked straight in with Yu Zhuo. Women were naturally drawn to such bustling places, and Yu Zhuo was already cheerful and lively by temperament, so once she arrived here, her face lit up. With both hands clasped behind her back, she walked backward and looked at Yang Kai as she asked, "Senior, what did you come here to buy?"

Though she was walking backward, it was as if she had eyes on the back of her head. Now and then, she merely shifted her shoulders and slipped past the crowd.

"Some seeds," Yang Kai said, looking left and right.

He had come here on a whim precisely to buy seeds. Mu Zhu and Mu Lu had needed too many of them lately. Although he had traded for some at the previous exchange meeting, it had been nowhere near enough for their use. When he delivered those seeds, the two Wood Spirits had urged him again and again to get more as quickly as possible.

Before, since he had not thought there was a market here, there had been nothing to do about it. Now that he knew, he had simply come by while he had the chance.

"Seeds?" Yu Zhuo was rather surprised, as if she had not expected Yang Kai to have come for that. After thinking for a moment, she said, "I know where to find some. Senior, come with me."

As she spoke, she turned and darted into the crowd, nimble as a fish swimming through water. Yang Kai followed at an unhurried pace, yet he did not fall very far behind.

Before long, the two arrived before a shop that sold spirit herbs. Under normal circumstances, this sort of shop would also sell some seeds on the side, though certainly not in any great quantity.

That proved to be the case. At Yu Zhuo's inquiry, the Shopkeeper brought out some seeds. Yang Kai gave them a brief inspection and found them acceptable, so he was about to buy them and leave.

To his surprise, Yu Zhuo actually began haggling with the Shopkeeper. Sharp-tongued and quick-witted, she managed to force the price down by about one-fifth, which Yang Kai found rather amusing to watch.

When it came time to pay, Yu Zhuo even tried to pay before him. How could Yang Kai let her do that? Naturally, he refused.

After leaving that shop, Yu Zhuo led Yang Kai on to the next one. She seemed to know the place like the back of her hand; who knew how many times she had been here before.

By the time nearly half a day had passed, they had already reached the end of the market that stretched for more than a dozen li, and Yang Kai had gained quite a harvest. Although the seeds he bought were not especially precious, the two Wood Spirits had not asked for much, so he would let them plant these and try them out.

"Senior, is there anything else you want to buy?" Yu Zhuo asked, blinking up at him.

"Not for now." Yang Kai smiled faintly, looked at her, and said, "You still have to compete tomorrow, so go back early and rest. I troubled you today."

Yu Zhuo smiled, her eyes curving into crescent moons. "It was no trouble, no trouble. If Senior has any instructions in the future, just say the word." After a pause, she added, "Then I will go first. Senior should also return early and rest."

Yang Kai nodded. Only then did Yu Zhuo soar into the sky. After flying some distance, she looked back at Yang Kai again and waved hard.

Watching her leave, Yang Kai weighed the beast-skin pouch of seeds in his hand, then casually tossed it into the Small Xuan Realm and set it before the medicine garden. He believed Mu Zhu and Mu Lu should be able to find it. Then he turned and flew back toward the spirit peak where Qingyang Shrine had settled.

News that Yu Zhuo of Luocha Sect had come looking for Yang Kai spread through Qingyang Shrine's people as if it had grown legs. Murong Xiaoxiao even came over specially to ask about it, as though there were some unspeakable relationship between Yang Kai and that Yu Zhuo, leaving Yang Kai at a loss for words.

Even Gao Xueting looked at him a little strangely. As for Xiao Baiyi, that went without saying; his face was full of contempt, which left Yang Kai rather annoyed.

The following days remained uneventful. Every few days, Yang Kai would come out of seclusion for a day and go to the valley to serve as a judge. As time passed, more and more martial artists were eliminated, and those who could still stand on the arenas all had at least some ability.

During this period, Yu Zhuo once again stepped onto Arena Eight. Her opponent was still a Third Stage Dao Origin Realm cultivator. This battle was even harder than her match on the first day, but at the critical moment, Yu Zhuo stubbornly turned things around with her ability to adapt on the spot, then used all her strength to blast her opponent off the arena.

Even so, Yang Kai could tell that while Yu Zhuo was quite capable, it would be difficult for her to enter the top ten. Her cultivation was her greatest weakness. If she had also reached the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm, she absolutely would have a place in the top five of this Martial Gathering.

Yang Kai did not attend the exchange meetings between Emperor Realm cultivators again. In any case, there was nothing good there. Gao Xueting, however, came back and passed him a message, saying quite a few Emperor Realm cultivators still wanted to trade with him for Emperor Pills, and he could name his price.

Yang Kai ignored them all. Taking out Emperor Pills to exchange for seeds that day had only been a casual act. How could he possibly throw out even more Emperor Pills and invite others' covetous eyes?

On the twentieth day of the Martial Gathering, the results of the individual battle rankings came out.

The one who took first place was not a disciple of Qingyang Shrine, nor even a disciple from one of the three great forces, but someone from Eight Directions Sect.

Yang Kai even knew this person. It was, astonishingly, Su Zi, the precious daughter of the previous Sect Leader of Eight Directions Sect. This was truly a tremendous upset, and it left all three great forces looking somewhat embarrassed.

Yang Kai had not had much to do with Su Zi. He only knew that she was deeply devoted to Luo Yuan. Back then, in the Four Seasons Land, Yang Kai had refined the Supreme Mystic Pill, and Su Zi had sought him out to exchange for one, intending to give it to Luo Yuan so he could take it and ascend to the Emperor Realm sooner. Luo Yuan, however, had not appreciated the gesture. Instead, he had shoved the Supreme Mystic Pill straight into her mouth and forced her to swallow it. Her affection had met with indifference; that incident made it plain enough.

After so many years, Su Zi's cultivation had also reached the peak of the Third Stage Dao Origin Realm. With the Supreme Mystic Pill from back then as her foundation, advancing to the Emperor Realm would not be difficult. This time, she had even overwhelmed tens of thousands of young talents from the Southern Territory, bringing glory to Eight Directions Sect.

Because of this, Eight Directions Sect, a Sect that had once been obscure and unknown, again entered the sights of many experts.

In fact, ever since Luo Yuan advanced to the Emperor Realm, Eight Directions Sect had already ceased to be the small Sect it had been back then. Its power had expanded fiercely, and Luo Yuan had also taken over as Sect Master of Eight Directions Sect.

This time, Su Zi had also taken first place in the individual battles of the Martial Gathering, so one could imagine what great honour this had brought to Eight Directions Sect. The current Eight Directions Sect merely lacked a bit of foundation, with only Luo Yuan, an Emperor Realm Master, presiding over it, but given time, once he and Su Zi both fully matured, they would surely be able to lead Eight Directions Sect toward prosperity.

One had to know that the reward for first place in the Martial Gathering was the chance to cultivate in the secret grounds of the three great Sects. With the Supreme Mystic Pill assisting her as well, Su Zi would likely advance to the Emperor Realm after emerging from retreat this time. Countless people were truly red-eyed with envy.

Second place went to Xue Yi of Qingyang Shrine. He could be considered to have lived up to expectations, suppressing the participating disciples from Wuhua Hall and Tianwu Sacred Land.

Third place went to someone from Wuhua Hall. Tianwu Sacred Land had missed the top three this time, and the Elder leading their group wore a worried, bitter expression, probably unsure how he would answer for it after returning. Farther down the rankings, most disciples from the great Sects placed toward the front, displaying the foundations of the various great Sects.

With that, the individual battles came to an end. The remaining ten days would be the team battles.

The competition venue was still this valley. Several hundred Emperor Realm cultivators spent a day dismantling the hundreds of arenas and dividing the entire valley into more than a dozen regions. Each region was large enough for over a hundred people to charge and maneuver within.

In the individual battles, one judge was enough, but that would not do for the team battles. Each region required five judges, to ensure as far as possible that no one died.

Yang Kai was naturally recruited as well. As before, everyone took turns serving as judges, rotating once every three days.

In order to compete for the team battle rankings, every family's disciples gave it their all. On the competition grounds, they fought with thunderous intensity. All kinds of methods emerged one after another, and schemes and tricks unfolded in turn, leaving the onlookers amazed. It was truly difficult to imagine that these fellows would go to such lengths for a ranking.

But that was no wonder. The rankings concerned the face of each Sect. Who would dare be careless?

Qingyang Shrine had sent only elites. Regardless of how the individual battle rankings had turned out, they swept through the team battles without resistance. For the first few days, they could not find a real opponent at all, and only when they encountered disciples from the other two top Sects did the fighting become truly fierce.

On this day, Yang Kai was once again meditating and resting when a maid came to report that Yu Zhuo requested an audience.

Ever since Yu Zhuo had come that day, she had visited every few days. She did have a proper reason: she mainly came to deliver seeds to Yang Kai.

After seeing how interested Yang Kai had been in seeds at the market, she had gone back and asked her fellow disciples to help collect some, then brought them to Yang Kai.

She had already delivered seeds three times. Although they were not precious, Yang Kai vaguely felt that something was not quite right.

The way this woman looked at him had gradually grown a little too attached. Yang Kai was no fledgling, so how could he fail to understand what she was thinking? Perhaps the way he had saved her on the first day had planted something in her heart, and through their contact over these days, it had gradually begun to take root and sprout.

Although she concealed it very carefully, Yang Kai was not blind. How could he fail to see it?

Yang Kai felt it was necessary to have a proper talk with her. Right now, he truly did not want to entangle himself with any more women. The matter with Ji Yao still had not been clarified. Thus, he had the maid bring her in.

Before long, a head peeked out from beside the doorframe, and Yu Zhuo said with a grin, "Senior, I did not disturb you, did I?" After several interactions, she had discovered that Yang Kai did not put on airs, so she had become much more at ease around him.

"You are already here. What does it matter if you disturbed me?" Yang Kai looked at her in annoyance. After several interactions, the two of them had indeed become much more familiar with each other.
