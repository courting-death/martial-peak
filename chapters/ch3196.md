## Chapter 3196 — Whose Child

The laughter and chatter vanished in an instant, and several pairs of beautiful eyes swept toward Yang Kai.

Xia Ningshang shot to her feet in excitement. "Junior Brother!"

"Hm?" Shan Qingluo gave a soft snort, caught Xia Ningshang by the arm, and pushed her back into her seat. Xia Ningshang immediately looked at Yang Kai apologetically, stuck out her tongue, and gave him a helpless look.

Su Yan and Xue Yue also looked away, leaning close to whisper about something. One looked indifferent, while the other wore a faint, unreadable smile.

Aside from Xia Ningshang, the other three women seemed not to have seen him at all.

Yang Kai's eyes widened. What was going on?

Liu Yan was sitting beside Su Yan. Yang Kai looked to her, his lips moving silently a few times. Liu Yan raised her little hand, lightly drew it across her neck, then stuck out her tongue and rolled her eyes back, looking as if she had died with grievances unavenged.

Yang Kai laughed despite himself and immediately understood. These women were probably blaming him for disappearing for years again without a word, and had joined forces to put him in his place.

Yang Kai naturally was not afraid. Women only needed coaxing. If coaxing did not work, then he could settle it in bed.

He was just about to speak again when a tiny figure crawled out from beneath the stone table, ran up to him, planted both hands on her waist, and raised her head to stare up at him.

Yang Kai lowered his head and studied her in surprise. The tiny figure was actually a little girl who looked only four or five years old, sturdy and adorable, with a charmingly naive air. Her hair was tied into two little braids, she wore neat little clothes, and around her neck hung a golden lock. Several silver bracelets circled her wrists, all flashing with spiritual light; every one of them was an extraordinary Secret Treasure.

Their eyes met, and Yang Kai was utterly astonished. Whose child was this?

The little girl pointed at Yang Kai and demanded in a milky voice, "Who are you? How come I've never seen you before?"

Yang Kai was instantly amused. He crouched down and said, "What do you think?"

The little girl tilted her head and sized him up, examined him seriously for a moment, then shook her head with great confidence. "You don't look like a good person. Eat my fist first!"

Her soft little hand clenched into a fist and actually came swinging straight at Yang Kai's face, only for Yang Kai to block it with the light touch of one finger. The little girl was startled and pushed with all her might, but she could not move him in the slightest. Her face flushed from the effort, and she kept making little grunting noises.

Yang Kai let go, and the little girl lost her balance, tumbling straight into his arms. Yang Kai reached out with both hands, pinched her cheeks, and pulled them to either side, immediately stretching her little face out of shape. This clearly made her furious, and with her back against Yang Kai, she punched and kicked him wildly.

Yang Kai looked up and asked, "Whose child is this? Why is she here?"

Shan Qingluo turned around and crossed one leg over the other, the skin of her inner thigh faintly visible, stirring the imagination. She smiled at Yang Kai and said, "If she's here, whose child could she be? She's ours, of course."

Yang Kai said in shock, "Ours?"

He hurriedly turned the little girl in his arms around, studied her carefully, and stared. "Eh, she really does look a little like me!" He had not looked closely when he first saw her, but with Shan Qingluo's reminder, he immediately noticed that the girl's eyes and brows did seem somewhat similar to his.

"Our family has a child?" Yang Kai held the little girl by the body, lifted her high into the air, and burst into laughter.

Then, as he laughed, the sound died in his throat. The corner of his eye twitched, and he looked at the little girl. "How old are you this year?"

The little girl ignored him, merely waving her clenched fists. “Hurry up and put this Grandaunt down, or do not blame me for being impolite!” Her voice was soft and childish, yet she put on an air of great seniority. She punched and kicked with all four limbs, but how could she possibly touch even a single hair on Yang Kai?

Yang Kai turned to the others again. "How old is she this year?" Could she be more than four or five? Over ten? If she was his family's child, there was no reason for her to be this young.

Shan Qingluo said faintly, "She just turned five, I suppose."

"Five... years old!" Yang Kai clenched his teeth, veins jumping wildly on his forehead.

He had been gone for ten years, so where had a five-year-old child come from? Yin and yang had to join according to the Great Dao of Heaven and Earth; children could not simply leap out from between the rocks.

Then he suddenly saw Shan Qingluo wearing a smug expression as though she were watching a good show, while even Xue Yue’s delicate shoulders trembled slightly, as if she were holding back laughter. Su Yan had her back to him, so he could not see her expression, while Xia Ningshang looked utterly sympathetic.

He immediately understood something and asked with a smile, "Who gave birth to her?"

Shan Qingluo crossed her legs the other way, changing her posture, then lifted her chest and looked at him with a smile tugging at her lips. "If I said I did, would you believe me?"

Yang Kai shook his head. "I wouldn't."

There was clearly no point asking them anymore. These women had obviously discussed this beforehand and come together to laugh at him, even finding some little girl from who knew where. Little Senior Sister wanted to warn him, but clearly did not dare provoke everyone’s wrath, leaving her unable to speak despite having words in her heart.

Needless to say, Shan Qingluo, that enchantress, had definitely started this. Only she would dare behave so boldly.

"Do you think I won't know just because you won't tell me?" Yang Kai snorted, brought four fingers together, and tapped them with his thumb a few times. "Let this Young Master calculate with his fingers... got it. Ha, you're my younger sister?"

He had once been the Master of Dark Star. Although he had given up that identity in order to refine the entire Star Domain, eavesdropping on a little information was naturally still easy for him.

In a single instant, tens of thousands of pieces of information from Lingxiao Sect passed through his mind, and he arrived at a startling conclusion: this little girl was actually his younger sister!

He actually had a younger sister? He suddenly remembered that when he had returned and seen his parents years ago, his mother seemed to have mentioned something like this. He had not expected that by the time he came back again, the matter would have already bloomed and borne fruit.

His old father truly had grown stronger with age! Yang Kai could not help admiring him.

After learning the little girl’s identity, Yang Kai felt an even greater closeness to her. In the unseen workings of fate, he sensed a faint and ethereal yet unbreakable connection linking him to the child before him. That connection was called blood kinship.

No wonder he had thought her eyes and brows resembled his a little. Since they were brother and sister, it was only natural.

He gently put the little girl down, only for her to kick at him and snap angrily, "I'm your Grandaunt!"

"You're not big, but you certainly have a temper!" Yang Kai was amused. He did not move, yet the little girl kicked empty air. She could not swallow her anger and was preparing to kick again when Yang Kai crooked a finger and lightly flicked her forehead.

With a thud, the little girl staggered back several steps, clutching her forehead with both hands. She stared at Yang Kai in shock, as if she had never imagined he would actually dare hit her.

Since the day she was born, everyone in Lingxiao Sect had obeyed her every wish. An ordinary child naturally would not have received such treatment, but she was different. She was the daughter of Fourth Master Yang and Dong Suzhu, Yang Kai's younger sister. Even the Sect Masters of Dark Star's top Sects had to call her Second Young Lady when they saw her.

Her sisters-in-law doted on her even more. Whatever she wanted, she got; they were just short of plucking the stars and moon down for her.

In Lingxiao Sect, the little girl was practically a retired emperor. Forget being hit, she had never even lost a hair. Now that she had suddenly been attacked today, she was instantly stunned.

"I am your Eldest Brother!" Yang Kai smiled at her. "Seniority cannot be confused."

The little girl blinked, her mouth twitching as if she were about to cry.

"No crying!" Yang Kai crooked his middle finger, making as if to flick her again.

The corners of the little girl's mouth had already drooped all the way down, but she really did force back her tears. She only turned to look at her sisters-in-law, as if asking for help.

Su Yan remained perfectly still, Xue Yue pretended not to see, Shan Qingluo watched with great interest, and only Xia Ningshang still looked unbearably sympathetic.

Although this Second Young Lady of the Yang Family had been spoiled from birth and had a few small faults, her nature was not bad. Xia Ningshang had spent the most time looking after her and understood her best. Once, while the girl was playing on the rear mountain, a sudden downpour fell from the sky, yet she braved the heavy rain and busied herself beneath a tree. Xia Ningshang watched curiously and discovered an ant nest under the tree. The girl was using leaves to build the ants a shelter from the rain. That year, she was only three.

From one small detail, one could see the whole. After that, Xia Ningshang doted on her even more. Being spoiled from birth and showered with affection was nothing remarkable; what was rare was the kindness that came from her very nature.

"At least you know how to read the situation." Yang Kai smiled faintly, raised his hand, and rubbed her head a few times. "This is the first thing Eldest Brother will teach you: endure. Those who are too rigid are easily broken. Remember that?"

The little girl nodded foolishly, clearly not understanding much of it.

Yang Kai smiled. "Looks like you're still not convinced. How about this: if I can call out your name, you call me Eldest Brother. What do you say?"

The little girl said angrily, "Say it!" Inwardly, she was even a little pleased with herself. This was her first time meeting the man in front of her, so how could he know her name? Yet she did not stop to wonder how, on that same first meeting, Yang Kai had known she was his younger sister.

Yang Kai calculated on his fingers again and smiled. "Yang Xue?"

"You... how do you know?" Yang Xue widened her eyes, all but regarding Yang Kai as a heavenly being. Her clear eyes were filled with worship.

"Just tell me whether I was right," Yang Kai said.

Yang Xue lowered both hands and said dejectedly, "Right!"

Yang Kai said, "Then what should you do?"

Yang Xue pouted, turned her head aside, and said unwillingly, "Eldest Brother!"

Yang Kai smiled and said, “That is the second thing. See, without trust, a person cannot stand. Since you promised someone something, you cannot go back on your word!”

"Oh." Yang Xue looked thoughtful.

"Good, that's enough for today. Go back and tell Father and Mother that I'll visit them in a few days." As he spoke, he gave Liu Yan a meaningful look.

Liu Yan stood up from beside Su Yan, walked to Yang Xue, and held out her little hand.

Yang Xue took the initiative to place her hand in Liu Yan's. Liu Yan urged her strength, and the two little figures immediately flew down toward the foot of the peak. In a daze, Yang Xue looked back several times.

"They really do look like sisters." Yang Kai watched them leave, smiling faintly as warmth filled his heart.

Yang Xue was only five, and Liu Yan now looked only seven or eight. Standing together, the two of them naturally looked like sisters.

Turning around, Yang Kai lowered his eyes and grinned fiercely. “Who was it just now who said Little Xue’er was born to her?”

"I have something to do, so I'll be leaving first!" Su Yan stood and made to take her leave.

"I just remembered, I have something too." Xue Yue looked panicked.

Xia Ningshang’s cheeks flushed crimson as she said softly and timidly, “My Pills…”

Only Shan Qingluo remained unmoving as a mountain, looking at Yang Kai with a provocative expression.
