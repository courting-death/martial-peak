## Chapter 3234 — What the Hell?

Old Tao might not look like much, but as he drifted over now, his broad sleeves billowed in the wind, lending him a faint air of an immortal sage that made people see him in a new light. The aura radiating from him was also clearly that of a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master.

Yingfei glanced over from the corner of his eye and gave a vicious laugh. Striking like lightning, he subdued the last Mitian Sect Elder, slapped a sealing restriction onto him to seal his cultivation, then swayed left and right as he went to meet Old Tao.

Among the Thirty-Two Yao Kings of the Wild Ancient Land, Yingfei might not rank in the top three in terms of strength, but when it came to speed, no one could match him. On this point, even those three Divine Spirits would probably have to admit defeat.

In the blink of an eye, Yingfei was only a hundred zhang from Old Tao, and he was still closing in fast.

Old Tao, flying straight toward him, did not so much as change expression. He reached out and patted a cloth sack at his waist, then flung it at Yingfei.

The cloth sack opened into the wind, its mouth aimed at Yingfei. A surging suction force poured out from within, howling as it drew up wild winds on all sides.

Yingfei did not dare take it lightly. He did not know what sort of Secret Treasure this cloth sack was, but anything brought out by a Third-Stage Emperor Realm expert could not be weak. His figure cut sharply into a zigzag, about to slip away from the direction of the sack's mouth.

Who could have expected the cloth sack to swell with sudden speed, becoming larger than a house in an instant? From within, a cyan great handprint shot out, grabbing straight for Yingfei.

Yingfei started and instinctively tried to dodge, only to discover in horror that the Yao Yuan in his body had begun to churn chaotically, as though something were interfering with it. Caught off guard, he was seized squarely by that great handprint, pain twisting across his face.

The moment the cyan great handprint seized Yingfei, it immediately pulled back and shrank into the cloth sack. Yingfei vanished along with it. Old Tao raised a hand and recalled the cloth sack.

"Yingfei!" Xi Lei's face changed drastically as he cried out in alarm, staring at the cloth sack with deep dread. As a fellow Yao King, he naturally knew how troublesome Yingfei was. Yet this Yao King, famous for his speed, had been taken away by a cloth sack without the slightest power to fight back. If Xi Lei had not seen it with his own eyes, how could he have believed it?

He knew this was not the white-haired old man's own skill, but the power of that cloth sack Secret Treasure. What the hell was that cloth sack Secret Treasure, to have such an astonishing effect?

Off to the side, the poisonous mist Xie Wuwei had summoned also roiled violently, enough to show how greatly his mood had been shaken.

Mi Qi, who had been forced back step by step by Xi Lei, burst into laughter instead. "Many thanks, Old Tao!" His painstaking preparations had indeed come into use. At this moment, he admired his own foresight beyond measure.

If it had been any other Third-Stage Emperor, Old Tao might not have handled things so easily. Unfortunately for them, these were Yao Kings. Dealing with the yao was Old Tao's specialty.

While Xi Lei was distracted, Mi Qi shot backward and instantly broke free of his entanglement. He landed beside Old Tao, cupped his fists, and said, "Old Tao, this band of thieves has gone too far in bullying others. Please lend me your strength once more."

Old Tao remained expressionless and nodded lightly. "Very well."

Things had already come to this. Since he had made a move, he might as well see it through to the end. Besides, the sudden appearance of three Yao Kings here had also surprised him. If he captured these three Yao Kings, he could complete his own task. Even without Mi Qi's request, he would have continued.

"Old dog, die!" Thunder arcs coiled around Xi Lei as the phantom of a vicious beast flashed behind him and disappeared. Clearly, he was truly enraged. He had thought coming with Yang Kai to Mitian Sect would result in an easy victory, only for Yingfei to be captured in a single exchange. Yingfei's capture was a loss of face for the Wild Ancient Land, so how could Xi Lei let the matter rest?

Facing Xi Lei's blazing fury, Old Tao's expression did not change. His response was perfectly direct: he tossed out the cloth sack in his hand once more.

Xi Lei's charging figure suddenly froze. Looking up, he saw the cloth sack open above his head, releasing a powerful suction force from within. That much was one thing; with his strength, he could withstand it. The problem was that the cyan great handprint shot out from the cloth sack again. The moment the handprint appeared, Xi Lei felt his Yao Yuan grow somewhat chaotic.

His soul nearly fled his body in fright, and he immediately understood how Yingfei had fallen into this trap.

Before he could resist, the cyan handprint grabbed him, clenched tight, and dragged him straight back into the cloth sack.

This time, Old Tao did not recall the cloth sack. Instead, he formed a Spirit Art with his hand, and the sack's mouth turned toward the mass of poisonous mist in the air.

The poisonous mist had been created by Xie Wuwei. Once the battle began, he had dragged several Mitian Sect Emperor Realm Masters inside. No one knew how the fighting within was going, but from the cries of the Mitian Sect Elders, it was clear Xie Wuwei held a great advantage.

The cyan great handprint emerged again, though it was dimmer than before. It seemed to be a consumable thing, one that would vanish completely after only a few more uses.

With Yingfei and Xi Lei's examples before him, however, how could Xie Wuwei dare let down his guard? The poisonous mist churned, and Xie Wuwei suddenly rushed out, fleeing at full speed toward Yang Kai.

Yet the cyan handprint clung to him like a maggot on bone, impossible to shake off, and it was terrifyingly fast. Before Xie Wuwei could get far, it seized him in one grab and swiftly withdrew.

Only after Xie Wuwei had been taken into the cloth sack did Old Tao form another Spirit Art. The sack's mouth tightened, then it drifted back to his hand, and he hung it at his waist.

This series of changes happened in a flash, so quickly that people could barely follow it. The three great Yao Kings had charged in with ferocious momentum, brimming with battle intent, while the people of Mitian Sect had been utterly unable to resist. Yet a white-haired old man and a strange cloth sack had overturned the entire situation, leaving everyone stunned.

How could Li Jiao and the Li Dragon Palace Emperor Realm Masters still dare charge forward? They had only just assumed an attacking posture before they drew back in fright.

If even the three great Yao Kings were no match for that white-haired old man, what could they possibly amount to?

Mi Qi clearly had not expected things to go so smoothly either. After a moment of stunned silence, he threw back his head and laughed, arrogant to the extreme. Standing beside Old Tao, he pointed at Yang Kai, whose face was twitching. "Little thief, you dared offend my Mitian Sect. Today is the day you die!"

Old Tao was lifting a wine gourd to his mouth. Hearing this, he glanced sideways at Mi Qi, knowing full well that Mi Qi was borrowing his might to throw his weight around, but he said nothing. In any case, he had gained quite a bit from this trip, and letting Mi Qi borrow a little prestige would cost him nothing.

Yang Kai acted as if he had not heard a thing. He simply stared hard at that unimpressive white-haired old man. He had come here to make an example of Mitian Sect and establish his authority, yet before he could establish anything, the other side had captured three Yao Kings. What the hell?

It was not that he had refused to save them just now. The main issue was that everything had happened too quickly. Yingfei and Xi Lei had been captured before he could react, and when Xie Wuwei fled toward him and he wanted to help, Liu Yan had held him back, leaving him to watch helplessly as Xie Wuwei was taken away.

Otherwise, with his Space Divine Abilities, saving one Xie Wuwei should not have been a problem.

What was this old fellow's background? And what sort of Secret Treasure was that cloth sack?

Just as Yang Kai was full of doubts, Liu Yan suddenly walked past him step by step.

Yang Kai lowered his head and glanced at her, warning, "Be careful." Liu Yan was certainly not weak, but that cloth sack Secret Treasure was strange beyond belief, and Yang Kai did not know whether she could handle it.

Liu Yan remained calm, her pace unchanged.

Old Tao was still gripping his wine gourd and pouring wine into his mouth when he fixed his eyes on her. Suddenly, his eyes went wide, and wine spilled from the corners of his mouth, soaking his robes.

He hurriedly patted himself dry, then studied Liu Yan more carefully. The corner of his eye twitched despite himself.

Why did she... look so much like that person?

His thoughts drifted, and he recalled events from more than a decade ago. Back then, he had returned to the island to report to a certain Lord. During a pause in their conversation, he had glimpsed a figure seated upright at the mouth of a volcano.

Although he had been far away, he had still seen that the figure was a little girl, only seven or eight years old, dressed just like this. Out of curiosity, he had asked that Sir about the little girl's identity, but that Sir had only answered with a profound, unreadable smile, and Old Tao had not dared ask more.

More than a decade had passed, and he had not seen her clearly at the time, but that seven- or eight-year-old girl's figure had left an incredibly deep impression on him. Seeing someone like her now, he could not help thinking along those lines.

Looking closer, she did resemble her. She really did!

"Little thief, is there no one left under you? What is the meaning of sending a child over?" Mi Qi was the very image of a petty man drunk on success, clinging close to Old Tao while shouting wildly. After witnessing Old Tao's methods, he had grown even more fearless, though he still did not dare stray too far from Old Tao, lest Yang Kai seize an opening to ambush him.

"Shut up!" Old Tao snapped.

Mi Qi immediately fell silent. He glanced at Old Tao in astonishment, only to find him staring solemnly at the approaching little girl, cold sweat gradually beading on his forehead. The sight shocked Mi Qi. Could this little girl be someone they could not afford to provoke? Otherwise, why would Old Tao have such an expression?

Suspicious, he looked more closely and saw that the little girl walking toward them had a tranquil expression, as calm as an ancient well without a ripple. Her eyes were clear black and white, pure and spotless. One look was enough to tell she would grow into a beauty. If she already had such bearing as a child, what would she be like when she grew up?

As the two sides drew closer and the little girl still showed no intention of stopping, Mi Qi also grew a little nervous. He secretly urged his Emperor Yuan, ready to strike at any moment.

Liu Yan stopped ten zhang away.

Seeing her up close, Old Tao became even more certain his guess was right. This was probably the very person he had seen on the island back then. For a moment, he did not know whether to laugh or cry. He had never imagined he would meet her in a place like this. This was truly a case of family failing to recognize family.

Although he was almost certain, he still had to ask the necessary question. After hesitating for a moment, he secretly sent her a voice transmission.

Liu Yan's Divine Sense surged, and she sent a reply back.

The scene suddenly turned strange. The Mitian Sect and Li Dragon Palace groups stood ready for battle, swords drawn and bows bent, while a little girl and a white-haired old man exchanged voice transmissions in the middle of the battlefield.

Before long, Old Tao nodded slightly. Liu Yan turned and left, returning to Yang Kai's side in a few leaps.

"You know that old fellow?" Yang Kai asked, looking at Liu Yan suspiciously.

Liu Yan said, "I don't know him. He seems to have seen me before."

"What do you mean?"

"He's from Spirit Beast Island."

"The Beast Great Emperor's Spirit Beast Island?" Yang Kai was startled.
