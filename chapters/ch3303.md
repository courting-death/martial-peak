## Chapter 3303 - Kill

In the end, Qi Hai's hand never reached Xiuxiu's face. A Yin Spirit did not even have a physical body, so how could it be touched? His large hand passed straight through Xiuxiu's cheek, stirring a halo of light through her Yin Spirit form.

Qi Hai's face was filled with pain and self-reproach as he murmured, "Xiuxiu, is it really you?"

It was clearly his first time seeing Xiuxiu appear in such a state. Just as Fu Bo had guessed, Qi Hai had gone to such lengths to join Netherworld Sect for two reasons: first, to borrow Netherworld Sect's strength for his revenge, and second, to obtain Netherworld Sect's Secret Technique. Yet although he had successfully kept Xiuxiu's Yin Soul inside his Ten Thousand Souls Banner, he had never known that she still retained the intelligence she had possessed in life, not until this moment, when Xiuxiu appeared of her own accord.

Xiuxiu smiled faintly at him and parted her red lips. "Do not... keep making the same mistake."

Qi Hai froze. I was wrong? Wrong how? He had never imagined that after Xiuxiu appeared, the first thing she would say to him would be this.

A moment later, the woman turned to Yang Kai, bowed gracefully, and pleaded, "Please spare him this once. He was not always like this."

Perhaps it was the powerful, keen intuition of a Yin Spirit, or perhaps it was her final concern for her beloved, but in that unseen realm of fate, she sensed that if Qi Hai attacked Yang Kai here, he would lose his life to the Yellow Springs in the very next moment. That was why she had come out of the Ten Thousand Souls Banner on her own, hoping to preserve Qi Hai's life.

Yang Kai gazed at her and felt her emotions. He sighed inwardly, his killing intent slowly fading, then nodded. "If he does not court death, I will not act against him."

"Thank you." Xiuxiu nodded gratefully.

Yang Kai was silent for a moment before saying, "Back then... I could not help you." The True Phoenix Fire had been devoured by Liu Yan and turned into a phoenix egg, and Liu Yan had then been taken away by Jiu Feng. He truly had been unable to help this woman.

Xiuxiu smiled faintly. "Life and death are decreed by fate. There is nothing to mind."

"Xiuxiu!" Qi Hai called again, his voice trembling.

Xiuxiu turned around and stretched out her translucent little hand, gently brushing toward his face just as Qi Hai had done a moment ago. Softly, she said, "Go back. Return to Qi Tian Stronghold, then live well. Otherwise, I will not forgive you."

"But..." As Qi Hai spoke, he glanced at Yang Kai. He had wanted with all his heart to avenge his woman and wipe away this hatred, but Xiuxiu had no intention of asking him to take revenge. Instead, she was urging him to leave this troubled place. For a moment, Qi Hai was left completely at a loss, unsure what he should do.

"Go back!" Xiuxiu said again, her eyes filled with attachment and reluctance to part. Then, from her translucent body, a dazzling light suddenly bloomed.

Qi Hai was horrified and cried out, "Xiuxiu, what is happening to you?"

Xiuxiu answered him with the familiar smile Qi Hai knew so well. Amid that blazing radiance, she suddenly broke apart, turning into specks of fluorescent light that filled the world.

Seeing this, Fu Bo could not help showing bitter grief, his heart aching so badly it nearly bled. This Yin Spirit had actually self-destructed! This was a Yin Spirit, a treasure Netherworld Sect could only encounter by luck and never seek by force. Just now, he had still been thinking that he would definitely find a chance to take her from Qi Hai someday, but the thought had barely finished crossing his mind before he discovered he would never have that chance.

He had truly never encountered a Yin Spirit that could self-destruct. That alone showed how outstanding this Yin Spirit was. Perhaps even the few Yin Spirits within Netherworld Sect could not compare with her.

Fu Bo's expression twitched, and for no reason at all, he felt as if a precious treasure had slipped through his fingers.

Panicked, Qi Hai reached toward those specks of fluorescent light, as if trying to hold them back, but it was like drawing water with a bamboo basket. In the end, he caught nothing. A moment later, the light scattered. Qi Hai sank to his knees, his face twisted with pain and streaked with tears, his fists clenched tight as he threw back his head and roared. That roar was heart-rending, filled with unwillingness and helplessness. Clearly, he too understood Xiuxiu's fate.

A brief calm fell over the field, like the eye of a storm.

Only after a while did Yang Kai sigh, look at Qi Hai, and ask, "What is your choice?"

The dazed, dispirited Qi Hai jolted back to his senses. He raised his head and looked at Yang Kai with lifeless eyes as Xiuxiu's final words echoed beside his ears. Slowly, he shook his head, then rose and retraced his steps, his back desolate.

"Worthless thing!" Fu Bo snorted coldly. Although he could be considered Qi Hai's nominal Master, he had no intention of forcing him to stay now. Qi Hai had lost all will to fight, and even if he remained, he would not be of much use, so Fu Bo simply let him leave.

Before long, Qi Hai's figure vanished from sight.

More than a dozen li away, three figures stood quietly on a small hillside, watching the situation unfold from afar.

They were none other than Tang Sheng, Qian Xiuying, and Lan He of Heavenly Wolf Valley.

With so many experts suddenly appearing outside the Sect, the people of Heavenly Wolf Valley naturally noticed. They were not blind, after all. After the news was reported back, Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying immediately came out to investigate. Lan He also ran over, but the two of them kept her by their side and would not let her go forward.

Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying both looked gloomy, deeply resentful of the way Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land were acting. Xu Changfeng had previously suggested that Tang Sheng activate the Grand Guardian Array to seal off the world and use Heavenly Wolf Valley as the battlefield against Yang Kai, but after Tang Sheng refused, Xu Changfeng had not pressed him.

But what was the meaning of blocking the gate of someone else's Sect to deal with Yang Kai? If they truly wanted to act, could they not wait until he had gone a bit farther? If those who did not know the truth heard of this situation, they might think Heavenly Wolf Valley had joined in as well. Helplessly, though anger burned in their hearts, their fists were not as big as the others', and there was no reasoning with them. All they could do was watch from afar and make sure Heavenly Wolf Valley was not dragged into the fallout.

Seeing what had happened with Qi Hai and Xiuxiu, Qian Xiuying could not help being moved. "That man is truly one of deep feeling and sincere nature. It is only a pity he took the wrong path."

Tang Sheng gazed at Qi Hai's departing back and said, "He seems to be from Qi Tian Stronghold."

Lan He's expression was rather complicated as well. Matters of love between men and women were, after all, easier to stir a woman's heart. Just now, when she saw Xiuxiu self-destruct her Spirit Body, she had almost burst into tears. Although she did not know the other woman, she could still feel Xiuxiu's feelings for Qi Hai.

By choosing to self-destruct her Spirit Body, Xiuxiu had undoubtedly used the most decisive method possible to end this grudge. Otherwise, Qi Hai would certainly have attacked Yang Kai, and when that happened, only Heaven knew what fate would have awaited him.

A sudden impulse rose in Lan He's heart, making her wish she could rush over right now and fight shoulder to shoulder with Yang Kai, slaughtering every shameless person in this world. But Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying had long since taken precautions. The instant she arrived, they sealed her cultivation, making it clear that they would only let her watch and would not allow her to interfere.

More than a dozen li away, Yang Kai faced the encirclement of several dozen Emperor Realm Masters without the slightest change in expression, as if he did not take these people seriously at all. Such bearing left even Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying, who were watching from afar, with no choice but to admire him.

Yang Kai turned to look at Red Ghost, with his red hair and crimson eyes, and said calmly, "Qi Hai has left. What is your choice?"

Red Ghost gazed quietly at Yang Kai, only to be shocked as alarm bells rang wildly in his heart. Instinct told him that if he failed to recognize when to advance or retreat and attacked now, his end might be miserable. He might even... die!

Red Ghost was startled the moment this thought appeared. The other party was a figure of the same generation as himself, and both of them could be considered rising stars of their respective territories. Red Ghost also enjoyed great fame in the Eastern Territory. Even against Nether Soul Great Emperor's son, Yao Si, he would not be in danger of losing his life. Yet against this man, why did he feel such an intense sense of crisis before even making a move?

He knew very well that in Yang Kai's current situation, if Red Ghost attacked, Yang Kai could not afford to show the slightest mercy. Once he held back, his momentum would falter, and Yang Kai still had to face several dozen Emperor Realm Masters after this. In terms of momentum, he could not compromise in the least.

Therefore, Yang Kai would definitely attack with all his strength.

If Yang Kai attacked with all his strength, would Red Ghost die? Red Ghost's cheek could not help twitching. The words of challenge had already reached his lips, but what came out instead was, "All this romance has ruined my mood. Forget it for today. Survive this first, and then we can talk."

As soon as he said this, the warning signs in his heart vanished completely, and for no reason at all, he actually felt as if he had let out a long breath.

Yang Kai nodded. If Red Ghost really wanted to oppose him at a time like this, Yang Kai absolutely would not hold back. No matter how powerful Red Ghost was, he was still only a First-Stage Emperor Realm Master. If Yang Kai truly beat him to death here, then he could count himself as having offended all three of the Eastern Territory's top forces.

Yang Kai did not want to make himself the target of Heaven's wrath and men's resentment either. Red Ghost's choice to retreat was undoubtedly exactly what he had hoped to see.

Yang Kai glanced left and right, then sneered. "This Lord is right here. Since you all want my life, if you do not act now, what are you waiting for?"

"A yellow-mouthed brat dares call himself This Lord before this old man? Once I capture you, I will surely draw out your soul and refine your spirit." Fu Bo snorted coldly and waved his large hand forward. "Kill him!"

On the other side, Xu Changfeng spat out a single concise word. "Kill!"

Emperor Yuan surged. The wind and clouds changed abruptly, and Laws sprang up everywhere. Several dozen figures suddenly scattered in all directions, surrounding the place where Yang Kai stood so tightly that he truly had no path to Heaven and no gate into the Earth.

And in that very instant, five figures were already pressing in, taking turns to strike.

At the front, one man shook the long spear Imperial Treasure in his hand, piercing the air with sharp sounds. Spear shadows filled the sky and crashed down toward Yang Kai's head. Behind him, the other four crossed and wove like butterflies flitting through flowers, their movements dazzling, making it impossible to tell from which direction they would attack next.

Although these five came from different great Sects, they were all Emperor Realm experts. Even though this was their first time joining forces against an enemy, they moved as if they had rehearsed beforehand, their coordination flawless.

Watching from afar, Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying sighed.

They actually did not quite understand why the two great forces had sent so many people to deal with Yang Kai, even dispatching Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng personally. In their view, with the killing formation created by these five alone, Yang Kai already had no way to survive.

After all, if either of them were in his place, they would not be able to withstand such an offensive. Even if they barely managed to block it, they would certainly be heavily injured, and in an environment like this, heavy injuries were the same as death!

Since that was the case, was sending out so many Emperor Realm Masters not making too much of a minor matter?

Lan He could not help crying out in alarm. Her face turned deathly pale, and she closed her eyes, unable to keep watching.
