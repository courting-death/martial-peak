## Chapter 3226 — Iron Rooster Qiu Ran

"Let's go." Gao Xueting put away the token, turned, and headed outside. With her back to Yang Kai, the corner of her mouth lifted in an almost imperceptible smile.

Yang Kai stood there sighing for a moment before following after her.

He had admitted so readily that the Spirit Array was his work for two reasons. First, the other side had discovered it long ago, so there was no point pretending ignorance. Second, he had come this time to borrow some manpower for Lingxiao Palace. Once he brought people through this place, the array would certainly be exposed anyway. Rather than scramble to cover it up then, he might as well reveal it now.

As for whether Gao Xueting might go to the Northern Territory in the future to summon him for alchemy, that was no longer worth worrying about. Ji Ying was there anyway. If pills really needed refining, he could just throw the job to Ji Ying.

Spirit Jade Peak was where Qiu Ran, Deputy Hall Master of Qingyang Shrine, cultivated in seclusion.

When Yang Kai and Gao Xueting arrived, Qiu Ran was meditating. After receiving the report, he came out of his room and met the two of them in the hall.

Yang Kai rose and saluted. "Greetings, Senior Brother Qiu."

Gao Xueting, however, remained seated. They were all family here and saw each other often, so there was no need for such courtesy.

"A rare guest. When did Junior Brother Yang return?" Qiu Ran gave him an expressionless look, gestured for him to sit, and took the main seat himself.

Yang Kai chuckled. "I only just got back and came straight here to pay my respects to Senior Brother Qiu. After so many years, Senior Brother's bearing remains as fine as ever. Truly something to celebrate." As he spoke, he sat down as well.

Qiu Ran raised his eyes. "I dare not accept such praise. Junior Brother Yang is an extraordinary man, a Divine Dragon who shows his head but never his tail. If you had stayed away any longer, this old man was afraid he might forget our Shrine even had someone like you."

That was exactly what Gao Xueting had said. Yang Kai badly wanted to roll his eyes, but he kept an apologetic smile on his face. "Senior Brother takes this too seriously. The Shrine's kindness to me is no less than giving me a new life. I have always kept the Shrine and all my Senior Brothers and Senior Sisters in my thoughts."

Qiu Ran neither agreed nor disagreed. He merely glanced at Gao Xueting, who gave a slight, composed nod, as if she had quietly transmitted something to him. Qiu Ran's expression eased, and he nodded. "It is good that Junior Brother feels that way. When you are away from home, remember to take care of yourself. In any case, you are still an Elder of the Shrine and carry its reputation. No matter the circumstances, you must never damage the Shrine's prestige."

"I will remember it." Yang Kai nodded solemnly.

Qiu Ran said, "Good. If there is nothing else, go about your business. This old man still has matters to handle."

What was that supposed to mean? He was already being shown the door? I have not even brought up my real business yet. Yang Kai looked stunned and hurriedly glanced at Gao Xueting, signaling her desperately, only to find her sitting there with her sleeves folded, eyes lowered in perfect composure, utterly unmoved.

Ruthless! She actually dared to kick away the ladder after climbing up. If he had known, he would never have given her that token.

Since Gao Xueting would not speak up for him, Yang Kai could only do it himself. Cupping his fists, he said, "Senior Brother, Junior Brother still has something to report."

"What is it?" Qiu Ran raised his eyes and glanced at him.

Yang Kai said, "When I left back then, I took many spirit flowers and rare herbs from the Shrine. Senior Sister Gao asked me to refine them into Spirit Pills for the Shrine's use. Now Junior Brother has lived up to expectations and returned with the task complete."

"Oh?" Qiu Ran raised his brow. "In that case, you have already refined the Spirit Pills?"

"Exactly!"

"Bring them out for a look."

Only then did Gao Xueting say, "They are all with me." She slipped the Spatial Ring from her jade-like finger and handed it to Qiu Ran.

Qiu Ran accepted it and swept it with his Divine Sense, astonishment flashing across his face. So many? And they were all Emperor Pills, with quality that seemed quite good. He knew Yang Kai was an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, and he had agreed when Yang Kai took those spirit flowers and rare herbs back then, but he had never imagined that after a dozen years, Yang Kai would actually bring back such a large batch of Emperor Pills. Having an Imperial-Grade Pill Master among their own people was indeed wonderful. Qiu Ran was already considering whether he should give Yang Kai even more Spirit Medicines. As long as they could be refined into Emperor Pills, what did it matter if they waited another dozen years?

"Mm, not bad," Qiu Ran said indifferently. He could not praise him too much, or it would likely be harder to ask him to refine pills next time.

Yang Kai said, "Serving the Shrine is part of Junior Brother's duty." After a pause, he rubbed his nose. "Senior Brother, I wonder how many Elder Contribution Points these can be exchanged for?"

Qiu Ran said, "That will require a careful calculation."

"Then please calculate it quickly, Senior Brother."

Qiu Ran blinked at him. "Are you in a hurry?"

"Not especially." Yang Kai raised a fist to his mouth and coughed dryly. "Of course, if Senior Brother can settle the account now, that would be ideal."

Qiu Ran frowned at him, unsure what trick he was trying to play. Still, since they would be counting on him to refine pills in the future, they had to give him some motivation. He nodded. "Very well. This old man will calculate it for you."

"Many thanks, Senior Brother." Yang Kai beamed.

Qiu Ran said no more. He poured all the Emperor Pills from the Spatial Ring onto the table before him in one go.

Emperor Pills naturally could not be stored bare. Each one was kept in a box of a different attribute, enough to preserve its medicinal efficacy in the short term. There were jade boxes, gold boxes, silver boxes, wooden boxes, and all manner of other varieties.

In an instant, the table was covered with boxes the size of osmanthus cakes, stacked several layers deep. There were at least two or three hundred of them.

Gao Xueting also leaned over, mainly because she had never seen so many Emperor Pills gathered in one place in her life. It was truly a rare sight. In the past, whenever the Shrine wanted Emperor Pills, they had to ask someone else to refine them. Not only did they need to gather the materials themselves, they also had to offer a hefty payment, and if the Alchemist happened to be busy, they even had to wait in line.

To put it bluntly, the Emperor Pills Yang Kai had taken out were nearly equal to the total number of Emperor Pills the Shrine had obtained over the last five hundred years. Qiu Ran and Gao Xueting were both delighted and deeply shocked, but neither allowed the slightest trace of it to show.

"There has been no precedent for this, but Emperor Pills are difficult to obtain, so today this old man will convert them into Elder Contributions for you." As Qiu Ran spoke, he casually opened a wooden box, carefully examined the Spirit Pill inside, and nodded. "This pill can be considered Middle-Level. After deducting the cost of materials, this old man will count it as eight hundred Elder Contributions. Do you agree?"

Yang Kai quickly did the math in his head.

He had previously heard Gao Xueting, You Kun, and the others mention that the Shrine's Elder Contributions were rather difficult to earn. At most, each person could accumulate five or six thousand Elder Contributions a year, and in leaner years, only two or three thousand, while a single trip into the Divine Roaming Mirror World cost two thousand contribution points.

Elder Contributions could be bought with Source Crystals, but only up to two thousand points a year, just enough for one tempering session in the Divine Roaming Mirror. The exchange rate was one to one thousand, meaning one thousand Upper-Level Source Crystals could be traded for a single Elder Contribution Point. Anyone who wanted more Elder Contributions could only do work for the Shrine. For example, Gao Xueting had earned some Elder Contribution Points by leading a team to the Four Seasons Land back then.

If eight hundred contribution points were converted into Upper-Level Source Crystals, that would be eight hundred thousand. This was after deducting the material costs, since the materials for Alchemy had been taken from the Shrine. Calculated this way, it was somewhat cheaper than buying them outside, but Yang Kai at least bore the title of Shrine Elder, so a slightly lower price was only natural.

Inwardly, Yang Kai grudgingly accepted it, but he still said shamelessly, "Can it not be a little more?"

Qiu Ran said, "As the Shrine's Deputy Hall Master, this old man oversees all its affairs, great and small. All I seek is fairness. If you think it is too little, you may ask Junior Sister Gao."

There was no need to ask. Yang Kai knew the answer himself and said, "How about one thousand points? Round it up to a whole number."

"Eight hundred!" Qiu Ran ignored Yang Kai completely. The moment he finished speaking, he stuffed the jade box into his own Spatial Ring.

"Serves you right for not being able to find a dual cultivation partner," Yang Kai muttered, thoroughly dissatisfied.

"What did you say?" Qiu Ran looked up at him.

"Nothing, nothing. Senior Brother Qiu, please continue."

Qiu Ran snorted, then picked up another jade box. He opened it, took a look, and nodded with a smile. "Upper-Level Emperor Pill, one thousand contribution points." He stuffed it into his Spatial Ring and picked up another.

He gave each jade box a brief appraisal, then quickly calculated its contribution value. Upper-Level pills counted for one thousand points, Middle-Level for eight hundred, and Low-Rank for only five hundred. As the total kept climbing, Yang Kai was delighted. From time to time, he chimed in from the side, explaining from the perspective of a professional Alchemist how excellent these various Spirit Pills were, how difficult they were to refine, and how much time they had taken, all in the hope that Qiu Ran would award him a few more contributions.

But Qiu Ran wore the look of an utterly impartial judge and ignored him completely, leaving Yang Kai gnashing his teeth in frustration.

"Senior Brother, wait a moment. Although this Spirit Pill is Middle-Level, its pill fragrance is rich and its patterns are clear. One look is enough to tell that it is nearly Upper-Level. Is giving only eight hundred points not a little too little?" Yang Kai suddenly raised his hand.

Qiu Ran said, "Then how much do you think should be given?"

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Since it falls between Middle-Level and Upper-Level, let us split the difference. Nine hundred points will do. That way, neither side suffers, right?"

Qiu Ran chuckled and pushed the jade box in front of him toward Yang Kai. "Junior Brother makes a fair point. Why not appraise them for me? This old man can relax a little."

I would like to, but would my appraisal count for anything? Would it not still need your nod in the end? He smiled awkwardly. "What is Senior Brother saying? I was merely discussing the matter on its merits. I had absolutely no intention of overstepping."

"Hmph!" Qiu Ran's face went cold, and he put away the jade box. "Seven hundred points."

"Wait!" Yang Kai was shocked. "How did it become seven hundred points? It was eight hundred just now!"

"Six hundred!"

Yang Kai immediately fell silent. If he kept talking, the price would probably drop again. He turned to Gao Xueting for help, hoping she would say something fair, only to find that this woman had actually raised her head to stare at the roof beams, as if a flower had bloomed up there.

Yang Kai's expression twitched. Gritting his teeth inwardly, he thought, Fine, you are ruthless. Who was the one talking about fairness just now? This is nothing but going back on your word and acting however you please.

After a round of counting, half an hour later, Qiu Ran said, "The total is one hundred fifty-six thousand three hundred contribution points. Has this old man made any mistake?"

"No!" Yang Kai lowered his head, dispirited. The number matched his own calculation.

Gao Xueting said expressionlessly, "Congratulations, Junior Brother."

Yang Kai snorted. After being squeezed and held down by that iron rooster Qiu Ran, what was there to congratulate? Still, if these one hundred fifty thousand-plus contributions were converted into Upper-Level Source Crystals, they amounted to more than one and a half billion, an astonishing sum by any measure.

Among the Shrine's many Elders, no one had ever accumulated more than ten thousand contribution points. Compared to the others, Yang Kai could be said to have become rich overnight.
