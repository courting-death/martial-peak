## Chapter 3342 — Sneaking Around

Thinking about it carefully, though, it really was possible. Yang Kai had been born a human, but even he was no longer sure whether his current body counted as Dragon Clan or Human Race, while Zhu Qing was naturally a pure-blooded member of the Dragon Clan.

If the two of them truly had children, there was an extremely high chance they would be Dragons.

The fact that Mo Xiaoqi did not have the Dragon Clan's bloodline was probably due to Beast Great Emperor Mo Huang. Mo Xiaoqi likely inherited more from her father.

Once he understood what Dragon Isle was planning, Yang Kai's face darkened. "Is Great Elder joking with me?"

Zhu Yan raised a fist to his mouth and coughed lightly. "Ever since ancient times, my Dragon Clan has had few clansmen. Losing even one is an enormous blow, so this old man would not joke with you about such a matter."

This old thing was serious? Yang Kai stared at him, dumbstruck, then frowned and said, "If that is the case, I am afraid I must disappoint Great Elder. Even if Qing'er and I had such intentions, who knows how long it would take us to have children? And even if we truly did, I would not hand them over to the Dragon Clan. My children would naturally stay by my side, and I would personally raise and teach them!"

He already had the cultivation of the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, and if Zhu Qing's cultivation were counted properly, hers was even a little higher than his. For the two of them, having children really would not be easy. The higher one's strength, the lower the chance of producing offspring.

If he really agreed to this demand, he feared there might be no hope for ten years, or even a hundred. Was he supposed to stay on Dragon Isle all that time?

"As long as you work hard, there is always hope," Zhu Yan urged earnestly.

Yang Kai flicked his sleeve. "Great Elder need not say more. Since the Dragon Clan refuses to honor its word, I have nothing to say. If you truly want to add members to the Dragon Clan, Great Elder should put in more effort himself. Why come begging me?"

The moment he said this, Fu Zhun snapped, "Impudent!"

If Great Elder was going to put in effort, it could only be with her. For a junior like Yang Kai to say such a thing naturally left her unable to save face.

Yang Kai gave her a cold look and said nothing. Then he turned back to Zhu Yan. "Consider the Dragon Blood Flowers on the island my gift to the Dragon Clan. From this day onward, I will have no further ties with the Dragon Clan. Farewell!"

With that, he turned and sped away.

Zhu Qing sighed. In a flicker, she hurried after Yang Kai. She had thought that after Yang Kai cultivated so many Dragon Blood Flowers for Dragon Isle, relations between the two sides would surely improve, and perhaps Dragon Isle might even accept him. Who could have known things would turn out like this? She could not help feeling some resentment toward Fu Zhun.

"That boy has quite a temper," Zhu Yan said, watching Yang Kai's retreating figure as he slowly shook his head. Then he gave a laugh. "He bears the Ancestral Dragon Origin, yet he actually says he has no further ties with my Dragon Clan... Is that possible?"

Zhu Kong said, "I am afraid he himself still does not quite understand that he is already a member of the Dragon Clan. It is just that the Ancestral Dragon Origin is of great importance, so we cannot state it plainly to him. Sigh."

Zhu Yan said, "Precisely. That is why this old man hoped he and Qing'er could have children. If they did, then in the future he would at least have some bond with my Dragon Clan and would not act so recklessly. Unfortunately, it seems to have rather backfired."

Fu Xuan's fine brows drew together. "Can we not simply tell him? Must we handle it this way?"

Zhu Yan smiled bitterly and shook his head. "How noble is the Ancestral Dragon Origin? If we truly explained the matter to him, then in the future all of Dragon Isle would have to answer to him. With his cultivation still short of that level, thrusting him into such a lofty position would be no good for him or for my Dragon Clan."

Fu Xuan opened her mouth, but in the end, she only sighed.

High in the sky, Yang Kai and Zhu Qing flew side by side.

After stewing for a while, Yang Kai calmed down considerably. Although Fu Zhun had played him for a fool, the trip had not been entirely fruitless. At the very least, he had confirmed that Zhu Qing had not been punished by the Dragon Clan, and with the Dragon Blood Flowers he had spent half a year cultivating, she would presumably receive quite a few as well.

With that thought, Yang Kai took another Spatial Ring from his robes and handed it to her.

Zhu Qing accepted it and swept it with her Divine Sense, then said in surprise, "Why are there so many Dragon Blood Pills?"

There were three hundred Dragon Blood Pills inside the Spatial Ring, and their quality was extremely high. Quite a few had even formed Pill Patterns.

Zhu Qing had not stayed by his side at every moment during the past half year, so Yang Kai had naturally refined quite a few Dragon Blood Pills in his spare time. In any case, cultivating Dragon Blood Flowers had not taken much effort for the two Wood Spirits, and these Dragon Blood Pills had all been refined from Upper-Level Dragon Blood Flowers, so their quality was naturally excellent.

Yang Kai smiled. "I kept them for you. When I am not by your side, you can take them to improve your bloodline. When I come see you next time, I will bring you more."

"You are leaving now?" Zhu Qing's small hand tightened around the Spatial Ring as she looked at him with reluctance. The past half year had been very happy. Although she knew Yang Kai could not remain on Dragon Isle for long, she had not expected their parting to come so suddenly.

Yang Kai reached out and wrapped an arm around her waist, tilting his head to look at her. "Want to come with me?"

Zhu Qing placed her head on his shoulder and pursed her lips. "I'm afraid!"

Yang Kai was stunned. "What are you afraid of?"

No sooner had he spoken than Zhu Qing pinched him at the waist. "Those four wives of yours!"

Yang Kai grimaced, torn between laughter and tears. "What are you afraid of them for?" You are a Dragon. If anyone should be afraid, it should be them.

Zhu Qing shifted awkwardly. "I just am."

No Dragon Woman had ever shared a husband with others. After all, Dragon Women held noble status, and winning the favor of one was an extraordinary stroke of ancestral fortune. Yet Zhu Qing felt guilty, as if she had stolen another woman's treasure. Besides, Second Elder's attitude toward Yang Kai was very poor. If she truly ignored everything and left with him, she did not know how Dragon Isle would react.

If it brought trouble down on Yang Kai, that would be terrible.

After she told Yang Kai her worries, he no longer pressed her and simply nodded. "I will come see you when I have time. I arranged a Space Array on a small island outside the entrance, so traveling back and forth will be very convenient in the future. If you have a chance to leave Dragon Isle, you can also use that Space Array to go to Lingxiao Palace."

Zhu Qing nodded. "I will remember." After a moment of silence, she suddenly recalled something and said, "If you leave now, what about Xiaoqi? You brought her here. Do you not need to take her back?"

Hearing her say this, Yang Kai suddenly remembered Mo Xiaoqi. Before he came here, Li Wuyi had specifically told him to send Mo Xiaoqi back to Spirit Beast Island afterward.

After pondering for a moment, he said, "Xiaoqi is here with her mother, so there is nothing to worry about. I will not tell her." He had other plans in mind as well, so for now it was better not to alert Mo Xiaoqi. If she wanted to leave with him, that would be troublesome.

Zhu Qing nodded. "That is fine too. When I go back, I will ask Third Elder and see what she says."

Yang Kai murmured in assent. For a time, neither of them spoke as they held each other and flew onward.

Before Dragon Isle's entrance, Fu Ling was idling on a dirt slope. Sensing something, she turned her head and saw Yang Kai and Zhu Qing arriving together. She immediately went up to meet them, all smiles. "Big Sister Qing, Brother-in-law!"

Zhu Qing nodded to her, then turned to Yang Kai and said, "Be careful on the road."

"You should go back," Yang Kai said, waving to her before glancing at Fu Ling.

Fu Ling blinked. "Brother-in-law, are you leaving?"

Yang Kai said coolly, "If I do not leave, am I supposed to stay here for the rest of my life?" As he spoke, he patted Zhu Qing's soft hand, looked at her one last time, then vanished into the Void Passage in a flash.

Zhu Qing stood there watching for a long time before she sighed. Then she looked at Fu Ling, flipped her hand, and summoned a jade bottle into her palm before casually tossing it over.

Fu Ling caught it in one swift grab and asked curiously, "What did Big Sister Qing give me?"

Zhu Qing smiled. "Look for yourself and you will know."

Fu Ling said, "So mysterious. Could it be something good?" As she spoke, she hurriedly opened the jade bottle, spread her palm, and poured out a few Spirit Pills. Their fragrance reached her nose, and Fu Ling's small mouth fell open in surprise. She looked up at Zhu Qing. "This is..."

Zhu Qing said, "Dragon Blood Pills refined by your brother-in-law. He wronged you in what happened last time. If not for him, you would not have been punished to stay here for a hundred years. These are his compensation to you."

Fu Ling's eyes lit up. "Brother-in-law said this is compensation for me?"

Zhu Qing frowned. "Do not read too much into it. He only feels bad about what happened."

"I'm not, I'm not," Fu Ling said, hurriedly waving her hands. Beaming with joy, she narrowed her eyes and said, "Brother-in-law really is a good person, and he can even refine Dragon Blood Pills. I told the Elders long ago that we should go outside and capture an Imperial-Grade Pill Master to refine Dragon Blood Pills for us, but they just would not listen. Wow, this bottle actually has ten Dragon Blood Pills. These are... Pill Patterns?"

After obtaining a bottle of Dragon Blood Pills, she was so excited she nearly danced for joy, crying out again and again.

Her bloodline was not very high either. Like Fu Qi, she had a Sixth-Tier Dragon Bloodline, so this bottle of Dragon Blood Pills was of enormous use to her. Although it still would not raise her bloodline to the Seventh-Tier, it was equivalent to decades, or even a hundred years, of accumulation.

Just like that, all her resentment toward Yang Kai vanished. With compensation like this, what did it matter if she had been implicated a little?

She turned the Dragon Blood Pills over excitedly in her hands, and by the time she came back to her senses, Zhu Qing had already left.

A man's voice suddenly sounded beside her ear. "You look happy. What good thing did you get?"

Fu Ling started, turned her head, and glared. "Brother-in-law, why did you come back again?" The speaker was actually Yang Kai, who had only just left.

Yang Kai did not answer. Instead, he glanced furtively around and asked in a low voice, "Zhu Qing is gone, right?"

Hearing this, Fu Ling immediately gave him a knowing smile. "Brother-in-law, you left and came back, and now you are acting so sneaky..." She retreated two steps and pursed her red lips, her eyes growing misty. "Brother-in-law, what are you trying to do?"

Yang Kai looked her up and down, then sneered. "Relax. I am not going to do anything to you."

Fu Ling said angrily, "Am I so ugly?"

Yang Kai said, "This has nothing to do with whether you are ugly or not. Cut the nonsense, or give me back that bottle of Dragon Blood Pills."

Fu Ling hurriedly hid both hands behind her back and shook her head again and again. "No!"

There was no way Yang Kai was getting them back. Dragon Blood Pills were far too precious to give up. No matter what, she was keeping them.
