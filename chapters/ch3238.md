## Chapter 3238 — Aboard a Thieves' Ship

Inside the hall, every pair of eyes turned toward Mi Qi. Mi Qi knew how to read the room and immediately stepped forward, cupping his fists. "Palace Master Yang's divine abilities are unrivaled, and your heart embraces the world. This Mi is sincerely convinced. All of Mitian Sect is willing to submit to Lingxiao Palace. From this day forward, we will obey your commands. Through mountains of blades or seas of fire, we will harbor no second thoughts."

Whether he meant it or not, the words were well chosen, and they earned him a bit of goodwill from the people of Lingxiao Palace.

Li Jiao's mouth twitched. He had not expected Mi Qi to be so decisive. The two of them had dealt with each other in the past, but he had never seen this side of Mi Qi before. When Yang Kai had demanded his submission earlier, Mi Qi had resisted and maneuvered more than once, yet now he had put on the manner of a man resigned to his fate and had arranged both his position and attitude perfectly. Li Jiao could not help seeing him in a new light. Even so, he still felt a touch of sorrow, like the fox grieving after the rabbit's death.

Li Dragon Palace and Mitian Sect were similar in strength. Li Jiao estimated that if he had not gone through life-and-death ordeals with Yang Kai several times, Mitian Sect's fate would have been a warning of what awaited Li Dragon Palace. It was precisely because he had some friendship with Yang Kai that Yang Kai had not moved against Li Dragon Palace. Then again, Li Dragon Palace was not much better off than Mitian Sect now. It was not a subordinate Sect in name, yet seemed even more like one in practice.

"You've all heard Sect Master Mi. Get acquainted with one another," Yang Kai said, waving a hand.

Hua Qingsi smiled and took a graceful step forward, offering a gentle salute. "I am Hua Qingsi, Chief Steward of Lingxiao Palace. Greetings, Sect Master Mi."

"Chief Steward, you are too courteous." Mi Qi returned the salute, though he was rather curious. What kind of damned post was Chief Steward? Why had he never heard of it before? But from where she stood, she was clearly ahead of everyone else. Even the three Yao Kings ranked below her. It seemed her position in Yang Kai's eyes was not low, so he could not slight or offend her in the future. He would have to build a good relationship with her too. Otherwise, who knew when she might make things difficult for him behind the scenes?

He even felt a flicker of suspicion. Could Yang Kai and this woman have some sordid relationship? Otherwise, how could a mere First Stage Emperor Realm cultivator like her hold such a high position?

Hua Qingsi had no idea what was going through his mind. If she had known, she would certainly have fought him to the death. Still smiling, she gestured to the side and introduced, "This is Yao King Xi Lei. Sect Master Mi should already have met him."

Mi Qi smiled and cupped his fists. How could he not have met him? Back at Mitian Sect, Xi Lei had been the one to entangle him in battle, and Mi Qi had clearly felt that he was somewhat outmatched.

Xi Lei grinned at him, baring a mouthful of fangs and making Mi Qi feel a little uneasy.

Hua Qingsi continued, "This is Yao King Yingfei."

Yingfei gave a faint nod.

"And this is..."

The introductions continued one by one, and Mi Qi kept a smile plastered on his face. No matter how high or low the other party's cultivation was, he always felt a step beneath them. There was no help for it. Mitian Sect had already submitted to Lingxiao Palace, so in front of these pillars of Lingxiao Palace, he naturally did not dare put on airs.

Xie Wuwei, Ye Hen, Nanmen Dajun, Hou Yu, and many other Emperor Realm cultivators; Qin Chaoyang, Chi Yue, Ai Ou, Ghost Ancestor, Gu Cangyun, Ruan Biting, and a group of Dao Origin Realm cultivators...

By the time he had met them all, he found there really were quite a few.

Mi Qi knew Nanmen Dajun, but this was his first time learning that the renowned Imperial-Grade Formation Master was actually serving in Lingxiao Palace, and he could not help being shocked. Nanmen Dajun enjoyed a great reputation in the Northern Territory. Although he could not compare to Sublime Pill Great Emperor's disciples, an Imperial-Grade Formation Master was still an Imperial-Grade Formation Master, immensely sought after wherever he went. Mi Qi had once thought of recruiting him too, but Nanmen Dajun had simply ignored him.

If it had been an ordinary First Stage Emperor Realm cultivator, Mi Qi would definitely have taught him a lesson, but Nanmen Dajun's status as an Imperial-Grade Formation Master made him wary, so he had not dared act recklessly. If he touched Nanmen Dajun, the other experts of the Northern Territory would likely not let him off. Mitian Sect could not afford to provoke the anger of so many powerful masters either.

When he then heard that Hou Yu was an Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiner, Mi Qi nearly stared his eyes out.

What was going on with Lingxiao Palace? Nanmen Dajun being here was one thing, but they actually had an Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiner too? An Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiner! How many Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiners were there in the entire Star Realm? And this Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiner was even a woman.

He truly had no idea where Yang Kai had recruited this band of strange talents. Three great Yao Kings, an Imperial-Grade Formation Master, and an Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiner. A foundation like this would be enough to astonish anyone no matter where it appeared, and now that it was gathered in one place, it was even harder to believe.

Mi Qi suddenly felt that submitting might not be a bad thing after all. Lingxiao Palace's foundation was indeed above Mitian Sect's, so submission was not much of a loss. In the future, there might even be times when he would need to rely on Nanmen Dajun and this Hou Yu. It was just a little hard to swallow in terms of face.

Hua Qingsi's next words stunned him on the spot. "This Palace also has an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, though he is currently refining Spirit Pills and cannot conveniently come out. I ask Sect Master Mi's forgiveness."

"Imperial... Imperial-Grade Pill Master!" Mi Qi almost bit his own tongue, dumbstruck.

Hua Qingsi smiled faintly. "Speaking of which, Sect Master Mi should know this Imperial-Grade Pill Master."

"I know him?" Mi Qi was shocked. A thought flashed through his mind, but instinctively, he did not dare believe it.

Hua Qingsi said, "Indeed. Great Master Ji Ying. Sect Master Mi should be no stranger to him, yes?"

Mi Qi could not help swallowing. His first reaction was to look toward Li Jiao, his face full of silent inquiry, as if he wanted to know whether this was true.

Ji Ying! That was the fifth disciple under Sublime Pill Great Emperor's seat, and he was actually in Lingxiao Palace too? He suddenly remembered the time he and Li Jiao had come here together and watched Ji Ying compete with Yang Kai in Alchemy. Back then, not only had he and Li Jiao made a bet with Yang Kai, Great Master Ji seemed to have made one with Yang Kai as well.

Later, he had thought it over. Although Ji Ying had lost, as a disciple of Sublime Pill Great Emperor, Yang Kai probably would not dare do anything to him, and Ji Ying certainly could not have stayed in Lingxiao Palace.

Yet from the looks of it now, Ji Ying really had stayed.

Did Medicine Pill Valley have no objection? Did Sublime Pill Great Emperor have no objection? Mi Qi felt dizzy as he looked from person to person in the hall. One Sect had actually gathered three top masters: an Imperial-Grade Formation Master, an Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiner, and an Imperial-Grade Pill Master. It also had the three great Yao Kings standing guard. What kind of Sect was this? Even Great Emperor Sects could not match it in some respects, could they?

All at once, he felt his defeat was not undeserved.

Still dazed, he heard Yang Kai speaking from the platform. "My Lingxiao Palace has a considerable number of disciples. Although the storehouses have some reserves now, we cannot simply sit on what we have and exhaust them. We need a steady income to sustain ourselves. Chief Steward has already taken over the cities and territories once governed by Wenqing Sect, so we can collect taxes from those cities every year and even conduct some business of our own, but that is still only a drop in the bucket. Lingxiao Palace has ten or twenty times more disciples than Wenqing Sect did, so we need ten or twenty times more territory if we are to endure. I called you here today to announce one matter: this Palace Master has resolved to unify the Northern Territory and make the various cities and Sects of the Northern Territory pay yearly tribute to Lingxiao Palace. Only then can we ensure that Lingxiao Palace's foundation remains standing. What do you think?"

He looked around. The three great Yao Kings all wore indifferent expressions. In any case, they were nothing more than Yang Kai's hired muscle. They would strike wherever Yang Kai pointed, and they were not afraid of making a mess of things. At worst, they could return to the Wild Ancient Land and hide.

Hua Qingsi and the others, however, were startled. Even Hua Qingsi had never heard Yang Kai mention this before. Hearing it for the first time today, she could not help being astonished. She truly had no idea when Yang Kai had come up with such an idea.

Lingxiao Palace was currently in a period of vigorous growth. In truth, it was not the time to make enemies. If they simply developed in peace for a hundred years, with their enormous number of disciples, they would surely rise like the sun at high noon and dominate the Northern Territory.

But one look at Yang Kai told Hua Qingsi that he had already made up his mind. Trying to persuade him now would be useless. She could not help feeling puzzled. If they truly did this, they would definitely provoke resistance from countless cultivators throughout the Northern Territory. After all, who would willingly give up benefits already in their mouth?

Besides, where would Lingxiao Palace find enough people to carry this out? The newly arrived disciples were still settling in, and their cultivations were not high. Sending them out would accomplish almost nothing.

Yang Kai, however, ignored all of that. Looking as if he intended to cut straight through the mess, he swept his gaze over everyone and said, "Since none of you have any objections, it seems you are all very supportive. Excellent."

Everyone's mouths twitched. Would objecting be useful? Lingxiao Palace was entirely yours to decide, all right? Usually you did not manage anything, but once you did, one word from you settled the matter.

Yang Kai said, "I know what you are worried about. It is nothing more than the manpower problem. There is no need to worry about that. Brother Li and Sect Master Mi will lend Lingxiao Palace a hand. When the time comes, with our three families joining forces, who in the Northern Territory can stand against us?"

Li Jiao and Mi Qi cupped their fists together. "We are willing to obey Palace Master Yang's commands."

Li Jiao had long since prepared himself, so he felt nothing in particular, but Mi Qi was wailing inwardly. He knew Mitian Sect had now boarded a thieves' ship. Yang Kai wanted to unify the Northern Territory and make those cities and Sects offer up cultivation resources every year, but Lingxiao Palace could not spare any useful manpower. When the time came, the ones taking the lead would be the forces of Mitian Sect and Li Dragon Palace.

If people held a grudge, they would definitely hold it against these two families, while Lingxiao Palace could hide safely behind them, sheltered from the wind and rain, and enjoy endless benefits. How vicious.

In other words, Lingxiao Palace itself basically did not need to expend any effort. It only had to send a few people to supervise things.

Yang Kai had seen enough from Mi Qi's expression and manner to know exactly what he was thinking, but he was too lazy to bother with him. Nor was he planning to keep all the benefits for himself. Since Li Dragon Palace and Mitian Sect were participating, once the benefits came in, he certainly would not let them work for nothing. He would share some with them, more or less. As for how much, that would depend on his mood.

Hua Qingsi thought for a moment, then said, "Palace Master, should we inform Ice Heart Valley about this?"

Of the Northern Territory's four top Sects, three were joining forces while Ice Heart Valley alone remained completely unaware. That was not exactly proper. If Yang Kai had no friendship with Ice Heart Valley, that would be one thing, but the key was that their relationship was excellent. If there were benefits to be had and he did not bring Ice Heart Valley in, it would seem rather ungenerous.

Yang Kai's expression twitched when he heard this. "I have thought about Ice Heart Valley too. I just do not know what they would think."

The women of Ice Heart Valley cultivated Ice Attribute Secret Arts and kept aloof from worldly affairs. Yang Kai truly found it difficult to bring up something that would offend the masses. If Bing Yun scolded him for it, that would be rather unpleasant.

In his plan, once they obtained benefits in the future, he would simply share some with Ice Heart Valley. After all, Su Yan was cultivating there, so he could treat it as a gesture of goodwill toward Ice Heart Valley.
