## Chapter 3154 — I Didn't Hear Clearly

Warships had always been ideal weapons for storming cities and seizing territory, and the might of Crystal Cannons was even more overwhelming.

More than a dozen Warships and several hundred Crystal Cannons. Even Netherworld Sect's Third Elder could not easily predict how much destruction they could unleash.

The only thing certain was that the people of Nine Heavens Sacred Land could not stop them!

Xue Yue and the others truly could not stop them, but they had no choice but to try. Behind them lay Nine Heavens Sacred Land, and behind it, Tongxuan Continent. If they dodged, the attack would fall on Nine Heavens Sacred Land, on Xia Ningshang.

Xia Ningshang's condition had only just stabilized. If she truly endured a full volley from several hundred Crystal Cannons, her injuries would only worsen.

Xue Yue flickered forward, her long hair flying as she stood proudly in the Void.

Figure after figure gathered around her, forming a defense as solid as a wall of bronze and iron.

The Third Elder froze for a moment, then burst into delight. "Since you won't dodge, then all of you can die!"

He swept his hand out, ready to order the cannons to fire.

The Crystal Cannons shone with blinding light, their gathered energy already pushed to the limit.

A tremendous rumble erupted, and the world itself seemed to shake.

The imagined Crystal Cannon volley, that Heaven-destroying, Earth-shattering scene, never came. Instead, a terrifying aura rushed in from behind, so powerful it made hearts clench.

The Third Elder whipped his head around, and his eyes shook with shock.

There, a black-haired young man had charged out from the side at some unknown point, his hair flying wildly. Alone, he hurled himself toward the dozen-plus Warships of Great Wilderness Star Domain. Beside those enormous vessels, his figure was like a firefly before the bright moon, carrying an air of tragic resolve.

Yet when he struck the Warships, their built-in defenses were no sturdier than paper before him.

The Third Elder saw him swing his fist almost casually, and a huge hole opened in the side of one Warship. With a violent crash, fire burst everywhere, screams rang out from within, and one life aura after another vanished.

In the blink of an eye, the young man burst out from the Warship's far side and shot straight toward the second.

Like a meteorite falling from the heavens, he carried unstoppable force as he pierced through one Void King-Grade Warship after another.

Hong, hong, hong...

Explosions boomed from every Warship. By the time he burst out of the last one, not a single vessel remained intact. The Crystal Cannons that had been flashing with dazzling light now turned wildly unstable, their glow flickering bright and dim.

Hong long...

One Warship suddenly exploded and vanished into flame. Its enormous hull listed, creaking loudly as it slowly fell.

Another Warship was swallowed by fire...

A third...

A fourth...

In just a few breaths, not one of the dozen-plus Warships survived.

Every cultivator from Great Wilderness Star Domain felt his soul nearly fly from his body. A chill swept from their heads to the soles of their feet, leaving them cold all over, their hands and feet icy. Everyone who looked at the young man did so with horror and fear.

Who exactly was this person? He had destroyed more than a dozen Warships so easily. Was this still something human strength could achieve?

A flash of insight crossed the Third Elder's mind, and he cried out involuntarily, "It's you!"

He had been wondering all along who had broken the Eight Wastelands Formation Core and killed Vice Sect Master Xin Xuanming. Only now, after witnessing this Heaven-shaking method, did realization strike him.

It was him! It was definitely him!

With such methods, there could be no one else who had destroyed the Eight Wastelands Formation Core and killed the Vice Sect Master.

Who exactly was he? How could he be this powerful?

Xue Yue let out a soft breath. She had already steeled herself to die here rather than let those Crystal Cannons bombard Tongxuan Continent. She had not expected him to arrive at the critical moment and give her someone to lean on in an instant. After such a plunge and sudden turn, her emotions churned, yet she looked proud. This was the man she had chosen!

Gu Jianxin stared blankly, a bitter taste rising in his mouth. No wonder his longsword had warned him. Had this guy already grown this powerful? Back when they first met, Yang Kai's cultivation had still been below his own. Gu Jianxin had known his future was limitless, but how could he have imagined Yang Kai would pull so far ahead in just a few dozen years?

Yang Kai had left him so far behind that he could no longer even see his shadow. What was his cultivation now? Could he already compare to the legendary Starry Sky Emperor?

No wonder Yang Kai had only told them to tie down the cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain. That was truly all they needed to do. With the strength he had just displayed, even if everyone from Great Wilderness Star Domain rushed him at once, they probably still would not be his match.

In an instant, the situation reversed completely, all because one person had arrived.

The many cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain went ashen. Facing that young man, they could not even muster the courage to fight.

"Kill!" Yang Kai spat out a single cold word. When he opened his mouth, he was still standing where he was, unmoving. By the time his voice fell, he had already appeared on the other side. The Third Elder saw only a faint, almost illusory shadow pass through his side. Before anyone could react, Yang Kai had already left at leisure.

The Third Elder was badly startled, instinctively feeling as if he had just walked past the gates of hell.

Chi...

A strange sound rang out. The many cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain looked toward it and saw a sight that nearly shattered their courage.

A headless corpse stood in midair, blood spraying from its neck like a fountain. The corpse still held a defensive posture, but its head had vanished.

They stiffly turned their heads, and sure enough, they saw it. The head was now in the hand of that young man who had appeared so suddenly, its expression blank, as if even in death it had not understood what had happened.

That had been a Third Stage of the Void King Realm master!

The expert who had just fought Meng Wuya and beaten him into a sorry state had now been beheaded outright by Yang Kai.

No one had seen how he did it.

"Kill!" Xue Yue cried. The anger and resentment she had accumulated over the past several years, along with her guilt toward Xia Ningshang, erupted all at once, turning into chilling murderous intent as she charged first toward the cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain.

How could the others hesitate? After witnessing Yang Kai's methods with their own eyes, they knew at once that today's battle could not be lost. One after another, they used their movement skills and leapt forward.

The great battle resumed, but the situation had completely reversed. Great Wilderness Star Domain's side had been cowed by Yang Kai's divine might and had no will to fight, while Nine Heavens Sacred Land advanced with unmatched fury.

The outcome of the battle had been decided from the moment it began.

Yang Kai was not idle either. The torment Xia Ningshang had suffered still stuck in his throat like a fishbone, so how could he not collect some interest?

Like a fierce tiger descending the mountain, he rushed straight at the remaining Third Stage Void King Realm masters. A palm to the left, a fist to the right, and these normally lofty, overbearing masters burst one after another into blood mist, utterly powerless to resist. In the blink of an eye, only the Third Elder remained alive.

The Third Elder looked around in horror. How could he not know that the tide had turned beyond saving?

He had never seen strength like this in his life. No one in the Star Domain could be this man's opponent. His vision blurred, and Yang Kai was already before him, only half a chi away.

Before he could pull back, his neck was seized and he was lifted high into the air. He could not summon the Saint Yuan in his body at all, and even his bones seemed on the verge of being crushed.

Only now, facing him in person, did the Third Elder fully understand how terrifying this man was.

"You had quite a lively time while I was away from home, didn't you?" Yang Kai looked at him coldly. With the slightest squeeze, he could end the man's life.

"Wait!" the Third Elder shouted, his face flushed.

Yang Kai gave a savage grin. "If you have any last words, say them quickly. Wait any longer and you won't get the chance."

The Third Elder's expression turned vicious. "Let me go, or you will regret it for the rest of your life."

Yang Kai looked at him in surprise. "What did you say? I didn't hear that clearly. Say it again."

The Third Elder said coldly, "Your Star Lord! Shouldn't you go back and check on her? If nothing unexpected has happened, she should have been captured by now."

Activating the seven Eight Wastelands Sub-Arrays to lure the snake from its hole had only been one preparation. He had made a second as well, and it targeted Xia Ningshang! Nine Heavens Sacred Land's experts had all come out, leaving its defenses practically nonexistent. This move could not possibly fail, and it was his greatest support. Unfortunately, before he could even reveal this trump card, the Third Stage Void King Realm masters on his side had been slaughtered until only he remained.

Inwardly, he bitterly hated how decisive and ruthless Yang Kai had been. He had only wanted to lure the snake from its hole, but who could have known he would draw out a man-eating tiger?

"Really?" Yang Kai tilted his head.

"You can go confirm it yourself! My Netherworld Sect has fallen today, and this Old Man asks for nothing else. Spare my life, and I will spare your Star Lord."

"Idiot!" Yang Kai tightened his grip, and the Third Elder's head instantly parted from his neck. Fresh blood splashed out, and disbelief filled the flying head, as if even in death he could not understand what was wrong with Yang Kai. He had clearly told him the truth, so did Yang Kai not need to confirm it?

His thoughts broke off, sinking into eternal darkness.

Yang Kai stood in midair, looked over the crowd, and curled his lip. "Sure enough, you can guard by day and guard by night, but the thief inside your own house is hardest to guard against."

He suddenly remembered the warning Long Tianshang had given before losing his reason. Perhaps that warning was meant for today. From the look of things, Long Tianshang must have learned some secrets around the time he was refined into a Heavenly Corpse.

He glanced down at Nine Heavens Sacred Land below, and his brow rose. "Forget it. I'll go back later."

He was not worried about Xia Ningshang's safety at all. The Third Elder had exhausted every scheme, but he probably never imagined Liu Yan was still guarding Xia Ningshang. With little Liu Yan at her side, who could possibly hurt her?

He attacked again, his figure shuttling through the Void as one expert after another from Great Wilderness Star Domain fell.

Many saw the situation turning against them and tried to flee, but how could Xue Yue and the others let them escape so easily? They all used their methods to intercept them.

On top of that, Great Wilderness Star Domain's masters dwindled the longer the battle went on. As one side weakened and the other gained strength, the fight soon became a slaughter.

After just half an hour, the battle ended. Not a single cultivator from Great Wilderness Star Domain survived; all had been killed to the last. As for Nine Heavens Sacred Land's side, aside from the casualties suffered at the very start while intercepting them, not even one person was injured after Yang Kai arrived to support them.

Most of them were drenched in blood from head to toe, some of it their own and some of it their enemies', and they panted heavily.

Pair after pair of eyes fixed on Yang Kai, filled with reverence and wonder. Everyone was deeply curious. Just how powerful was he now? In today's battle, he had seemed to act only in passing; it was nowhere near his full strength...
