## Chapter 3328 - Big Talk

The blazing sun hung high in a cloudless blue sky.

A streak of red light split the horizon and vanished in an instant. Even if someone happened to notice it, they would never have been able to make out what it really was.

Qiong Qi's speed could be summed up in one sentence.

Fast, very fast, unbelievably fast!

He was worthy of being a long-established Divine Spirit. His rank among Divine Spirits was extremely high. Though he could not compare with the honored Dragons and Phoenixes, the name Qiong Qi was still a resounding one among Divine Spirits.

Qiong Qi's back was extremely broad. Yang Kai and Mo Xiaoqi sat facing each other without feeling cramped in the slightest. Mo Xiaoqi had spread a thick beast-hide blanket over Qiong Qi's back, and sitting there chatting with Yang Kai about everything under the sun left her almost beside herself with happiness.

The last time Yang Kai had seen Mo Xiaoqi was in the Shattered Star Sea. Back then, she had been enraged by others, the seal on the butterfly mark on her face had broken, and she had nearly run wild. In the end, Zhang Ruoxi had stepped forward and suppressed her.

Many years had passed since then.

But though years passed easily, friendship endured. From birth until now, Mo Xiaoqi had spent most of her life on Spirit Beast Island. The first time she secretly ran away from home, she met Yang Kai in the Southern Territory, so naturally she treasured this only friend of hers deeply.

Thinking about it now, Yang Kai found it rather strange. How had Mo Xiaoqi made it from the Eastern Territory to the Southern Territory back then? Her strength had not been very high at the time, and the two Territories were separated by hundreds of millions of li. With no one escorting her, how had she safely crossed such a vast distance?

Looking back, Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng had probably been protecting her in secret the whole time. Only this little girl had thought she had successfully run away from home and had been so pleased with herself.

Mo Xiaoqi had little experience of the world, while Yang Kai had roamed far and wide and seen a great deal. Once they started talking, the topics naturally kept coming. Most of the time, however, it was Yang Kai who spoke while Mo Xiaoqi cupped her cheeks in both hands and listened quietly, utterly absorbed.

When they spoke of the old swindler they had met back in Fenglin City, the two of them burst into laughter again in perfect understanding.

Back then, they truly had eyes but failed to see. The dignified Mortal Great Emperor had taken countless incarnations and played among the mortal world, yet they had mistaken him for a swindler and had even held quite a grudge against him.

As time passed, however, Yang Kai clearly noticed a change in Mo Xiaoqi's mood. She grew nervous, expectant, and uneasy, and the closer they came to Dragon Isle, the more obvious it became.

Clearly, she was extremely anxious. This trip to Dragon Isle was to meet her mother, and she did not know how she should face the woman who had given birth to her yet whom she had never met.

In the end, the little girl became terribly quiet. Even when Yang Kai deliberately brought up interesting topics to draw her out, all he received in return was a very forced smile.

Yang Kai had no good way to help with this. He could only let her settle her own feelings.

Qiong Qi naturally knew the way to Dragon Isle, so on this trip Yang Kai did not need to use the Dragon Isle Token again to confirm their direction.

After several days passed in a blur, Qiong Qi descended onto a small island above the blue, rippling sea.

"This is the island closest to Dragon Isle?" Yang Kai looked around and asked casually.

Qiong Qi replied in his buzzing voice, "Reporting to Young Master, there is no island closer than this within a thousand li."

He did not know why Yang Kai wanted to find such a small island, but since Yang Kai had given the order, he would simply obey.

"Good. Then here it is." Yang Kai nodded, jumped down from Qiong Qi's back with Mo Xiaoqi, and swept out his Divine Sense, quickly finding a suitable spot.

"Eldest Brother Yang, what are you doing here?" Mo Xiaoqi looked around curiously. Countless little islands like this dotted the sea, and there was nothing special about this one, nor would it produce any heaven-and-earth treasure. Yet Yang Kai had asked Qiong Qi to find a place like this first.

"Arranging a Space Array," Yang Kai explained briefly.

Dragon Isle was the same as the Xuan Realm Pearl. It formed a world of its own and had its own World Laws. Therefore, even if Yang Kai arranged a Space Array inside Dragon Isle, he would not be able to make it resonate with other Space Arrays from afar, because Dragon Isle's world would cut off the connection between the arrays.

Yang Kai estimated that he would inevitably have dealings with Dragon Isle in the future. The distance between them was too great, making travel inconvenient. If he left a Space Array here, coming and going later would be much simpler.

Although he would still have to travel a little, it was better than flying all the way over from the Wild Ancient Land.

Mo Xiaoqi gave a soft oh. Although she did not understand Yang Kai's intention, she asked no further and merely followed obediently behind him.

Before long, the two of them arrived inside a cave. It should have formed naturally, located somewhere on the side of the island near the sea and extending all the way into the island's interior.

The inside was dry, a natural shelter from the wind.

Yang Kai immediately got to work. Mo Xiaoqi helped from the side as well, though all she did was make more trouble. Yang Kai did not scold her. Instead, he encouraged her quite a bit, and her mood finally recovered somewhat.

More than half a day later, a brand-new Space Array had been completed. Yang Kai inspected it and confirmed there were no problems before leading Mo Xiaoqi out of the cave.

Once they were seated on Qiong Qi's back again, the old thing unexpectedly gave his wings a shake, turned his head, and asked, "Young Master, are we really going to Dragon Isle?"

Yang Kai smiled. "What, are you afraid?"

Qiong Qi bared his teeth. "Afraid? Not exactly. It is just that damned place, Dragon Isle... is far too hostile to outsiders. Young Master should be careful once you go there."

What he was truly curious about was Yang Kai's relationship with the Dragon Clan. Earlier, when he saw Yang Kai transform into a thirty-zhang dragon body, he knew Yang Kai carried the Dragon Clan bloodline. But the Dragon Clan loathed those with impure bloodlines, and that loathing was even more intense than their rejection of other races.

If Yang Kai truly had a Half-Dragon bloodline, it would be best for him to avoid the Dragon Clan forever. Why would he still want to barge into Dragon Isle? Was that not walking straight into a trap?

Qiong Qi very much wanted to warn Yang Kai that Dragon Isle had a Half-Dragon City, where many beings with Half-Dragon bloodlines lived. They appeared powerful, but in truth all of them were under house arrest on Dragon Isle. Whenever the Dragon Clan had need of them, they would be taken away to have their blood drawn and used to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers.

If Yang Kai went now, perhaps he too would be trapped in Half-Dragon City, then taken away to have his blood drawn.

With a Half-Dragon bloodline and a thirty-zhang dragon body, there would be quite a lot of blood to draw...

Although he disliked Yang Kai using him as a mount, if Yang Kai truly fell into Dragon Isle and lost his freedom, Spirit Beast Island would definitely come after Qiong Qi again. For his own sake, he had to give Yang Kai a reminder.

Yang Kai grinned. "No matter how hostile to outsiders they are, they still cannot drive this Young Master away. If they anger this Young Master, I will beat every last one of them."

Qiong Qi could not help rolling his eyes when he heard this, thinking that this newly acknowledged Young Master really did know how to boast. Your strength is indeed impressive, and you can even exchange moves with someone like Cang Mo, but what kind of place is Dragon Isle?

That was the gathering place of the Dragon Clan, the dwelling of a dozen or two giant dragons. Even if this old man went there, he would have to keep his tail tucked in a little. Otherwise, he would definitely take a savage beating and be thrown out of Dragon Isle.

Especially those two old dragons, Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun. If they truly went all out, even a Great Emperor would be unable to do anything to them for a while. This old man was definitely no match for them, so someone like you wants to beat them? What kind of joke is that? This old man had lived so many years, and this was the first time he had heard such refreshingly outrageous bragging.

"What? You don't believe me?" Yang Kai seemed to have sensed Qiong Qi's thoughts and bent down to ask.

Qiong Qi said, "I believe you. How could I not? Young Master's Divine Abilities are peerless. What does a mere Dragon Clan amount to?"

"Looks like you don't believe me." Yang Kai gave a mocking laugh. Then his eyes shifted and he said, "If I can make those Dragon Clan members behave obediently, what will you do?"

Qiong Qi's eyes widened. "If Young Master can truly do that, then this old man will sincerely acknowledge you as his Young Master. From now on, whenever Young Master gives an order, this old man will not dare disobey."

"Oh..." Yang Kai drawled strangely. "From the sound of that, you are not sincerely acknowledging me as your Young Master right now?"

Qiong Qi shook his head with an air of indifference. "I was forced by circumstances and had no choice. Young Master is an understanding man, so why expose it?"

Yang Kai nodded. "At least you are honest. If you really obeyed me in everything, I would have to wonder whether you had some other purpose."

"Heh heh, Young Master, please do not blame me. This old man has no other way either. But if you can truly make all those Dragon Clan members behave obediently, you will naturally be qualified to be this old man's Master. Following you would not disgrace this old man." Qiong Qi paused, then continued, "But if Young Master cannot do it, this old man has only one request."

"Speak!"

Qiong Qi smiled awkwardly. "In the future, please do not use this old man as a mount. Although this old man once served Flowing Time Great Emperor as a mount, I am already quite old now. I must at least keep some face."

"Fine!" Yang Kai nodded.

Qiong Qi's spirits lifted, and he was overjoyed. This brat boasted bigger than the sky; this time, he was surely going to lose. Although Qiong Qi was not against following Yang Kai, he was still somewhat unwilling to be used as a mount by him.

Flowing Time Great Emperor was Flowing Time Great Emperor. You may have cultivated the Years Like a Shuttle Seal and grasped a little of the Laws of Time, barely counting as his successor, but how could you be compared with him, much less do the same thing Flowing Time Great Emperor had done?

"Young Master, Dragon Isle is just below. You two, sit steady." Qiong Qi suddenly gave a low shout. His enormous body dove straight toward the sea below.

Before he even arrived, the seawater parted on its own, revealing a round passage leading directly to the seabed.

They sped through without a single drop of water touching them.

In the next instant, an enormous Void Passage appeared in the depths of the seabed, leading to some unknown place.

Yang Kai had experienced such a scene once before, so he naturally knew that the Void Passage led to Dragon Isle. Last time, if he had not relied on the Dragon Isle Token to locate it, he probably never would have imagined that Dragon Isle's entrance was actually beneath the sea.

Qiong Qi had clearly been to Dragon Isle before as well. Without the slightest hesitation, he plunged headfirst into the Void Passage. In an instant, the seawater around them vanished, replaced by a stretch of chaotic nothingness.

Even the surrounding space and time seemed to have frozen solid.

"Hm?" Qiong Qi suddenly let out a startled sound, because this Void Passage unexpectedly gave him a sense of killing intent lurking on every side. It had not been like this the last time he entered. Worse, when he looked around, he could not find the exit.

His expression tensed in an instant. If he got lost in the Void, it would all be over.
