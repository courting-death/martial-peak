## Chapter 3179 — Omen of Great Misfortune

Yang Kai rubbed his chin in thought for a moment. "Since you're so sincere about seeking peace, this Young Master will give you a chance."

Wu Heng hated him so much he could barely contain it, but he could only grit his teeth and say, "I am willing to hear the details."

Yang Kai spread his arms. "I want half of this Star Domain!" He said it as casually as if he were asking for half a cake.

Blue veins stood out on Wu Heng's forehead as he forced out each word. "Wishful thinking!"

With Wu Heng's pride and self-respect, there was no way he could agree to such a harsh condition. More importantly, if he really handed Yang Kai half the Star Domain, he would completely lose his control over Great Wilderness Star Domain. At that point, would he not be at Yang Kai's mercy, to be kneaded flat or round however Yang Kai pleased? How could he possibly do something so self-destructive?

Yang Kai lowered his eyes and said in a deep voice, "The chance is right in front of you. If you don't grasp it, it will be gone."

Wu Heng suppressed his anger and shook his head. "That is absolutely impossible. Friend, please make another proposal."

Yang Kai spread his hands. "Then there's nothing to talk about." He turned as if to leave again.

Wu Heng hurriedly said, "To show this Lord's sincerity, this Lord can give you a Cultivation Star as compensation for Your Excellency's losses."

Naturally, he could not cut away half a Star Domain, but a single Cultivation Star would not shake his foundation. Great Wilderness Star Domain had many Cultivation Stars, and losing one would not matter much.

Yang Kai stopped and looked back at him. "Truly?"

Wu Heng was slightly taken aback, then nodded. "Indeed."

Yang Kai said, "Then I want the Cultivation Star where Netherworld Sect is located."

Wu Heng answered without thinking, "Good!" After a pause, he said, "From today on, Yellow Springs Star belongs to you." Netherworld Sect had basically been wiped out to the last man. Even if a few people remained, they were only unpresentable cultivators. As the Master of Great Wilderness Star Domain, Wu Heng could make this decision without anyone objecting.

Besides, even if he gave it away, could Yang Kai actually take it? This was a Cultivation Star, not a lump of dirt. Wu Heng had only made the offer offhand; he had not expected the other party to actually agree.

Yang Kai smiled. "Then I'll have to trouble Brother Wu to point the way. I should go take a look at my own territory."

As he spoke, he quietly communicated with his Incarnation and had it slow its devouring.

Wu Heng clearly noticed as well. He gave Yang Kai a meaningful look. Though he did not know exactly how Yang Kai had done it, it was obvious that even while Yang Kai stood inside Great Wilderness Star Domain, he could still control the Star Domain Origin of Hengluo Star Domain.

It was truly bizarre. They were both Masters of Star Domains, yet Wu Heng had no such ability. If he entered Hengluo Star Domain, never mind controlling Great Wilderness Star Domain's Origin, even communicating with it would be difficult.

The pressure from that side suddenly eased, and the thought of taking this chance to kill Yang Kai immediately surged through Wu Heng's mind. But the thought only flickered once before he stopped himself, because he had a better plan.

"Please restrain your strength a little more. Otherwise, I won't be able to spare the attention to send you over," Wu Heng said, looking at Yang Kai.

Yang Kai nodded and once again communicated with his Incarnation.

Sure enough, the pressure from Hengluo Star Domain weakened a great deal once more.

Yang Kai suddenly looked wary. "You won't take the chance to attack me, will you?"

Wu Heng remained calm. "If Your Excellency cannot trust me, then this Lord has nothing to say."

Yang Kai waved a hand carelessly. "Forget it, forget it. Send me over first."

"As you wish." Wu Heng nodded faintly and pointed. A Void Passage immediately appeared behind Yang Kai, leading toward the depths of the Star Domain in that direction.

Yang Kai poked his head into the passage for a look, then kept shaking his head. "An omen of misfortune. An omen of great misfortune!"

Wu Heng's eye twitched. "Your Excellency still does not trust this Lord!" Although he truly had no good intentions, he obviously could not show it.

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Wrong, wrong."

"What is wrong?" Wu Heng asked in a deep voice.

"I'm saying you have an omen of misfortune, Brother Wu. If you don't believe me, look behind you."

There was already no need to look back. The moment Yang Kai said this, Wu Heng clearly felt the pressure from Hengluo Star Domain surge. Startled, he hurriedly resisted with all his strength, but he still could not stop the darkness from spreading toward him.

A strange movement came from behind him. When he turned to look, Yang Kai had already stepped into the Void Passage and was looking back with a warm smile, calling out, "Many thanks for Brother Wu's kind intentions. We'll meet again another day!"

"Bastard!" Wu Heng cursed. With a thought, he collapsed the Void Passage and tried to shred Yang Kai inside it, but it was clearly too late.

After waiting a moment, his expression changed again. "How is that possible!"

Yang Kai had clearly passed through the Void Passage to Yellow Springs Star, hundreds of millions of li away, so why was there still no sign that the pressure from Hengluo Star Domain was weakening? Earlier, Wu Heng had thought Yang Kai could control Hengluo Star Domain's Origin because he was too close. That was precisely why he had decided to send Yang Kai farther away. As long as Yang Kai entered the depths of Great Wilderness Star Domain, his connection with Hengluo Star Domain's Origin would surely weaken, or even be cut off.

When that happened, Wu Heng could free his hands to deal with Yang Kai. A single casual thought would be enough to doom him beyond redemption.

Only now did he understand why Yang Kai had been so fearless.

No matter how great the distance, Hengluo Star Domain's devouring showed no sign of stopping. It had even grown more violent than before, forcing Wu Heng to focus all his attention on resisting it. How could he have any strength left to deal with Yang Kai?

Could it be that he really was not the Master of Hengluo Star Domain?

Unease stirred in Wu Heng's heart. He did not know whether sending Yang Kai to Yellow Springs Star had been right or wrong, and he could not shake the feeling that he had planted a hidden disaster for himself, one that would sooner or later bring calamity down on his head.

...

Yellow Springs Star was one of the highest-grade Cultivation Stars in Great Wilderness Star Domain. After all, it was Netherworld Sect's main star, and compared to Shuiyue Star of Hengluo Star Domain, it was not inferior in the slightest. Its Spiritual Qi was extremely rich, and spiritual mountains, great rivers, grotto-heavens, and blessed lands could be found everywhere. Netherworld Sect was the master of this Cultivation Star.

However, the last Star Domain invasion had caused Netherworld Sect to suffer disastrous losses. Now, those left behind on Yellow Springs Star were nothing more than a sorry collection of dregs, with not even many Void King Realm cultivators among them.

Yang Kai stepped out of the Void Passage and arrived directly above Yellow Springs Star. He looked around, examined it for a while, and was extremely satisfied.

He was in no hurry to put his plan into action. First, Yang Kai flew around Yellow Springs Star for a while, then leisurely descended toward a great mountain.

The great mountain towered high, lush with Spiritual Qi, clearly extraordinary at a glance. Countless birds and beasts roamed the mountain, and several cultivators' cave residences were protected by great arrays. Someone was clearly cultivating here in secluded retreat, though whether they belonged to Netherworld Sect was unknown.

Yang Kai was too lazy to bother with any of this. He went straight to the highest peak, flipped his hand, and a bead the size of a dragon's eye appeared in his palm.

Xuan Realm Pearl!

This was a trophy Yang Kai had obtained from Star Emperor Sect back then. Traced back several tens of thousands of years, it was a treasure Yang Yan had brought over from the Star Realm. According to Yang Yan, it had been formed after one of her friends refined an entire world. Since Yang Kai, like that person, cultivated Space Divine Abilities, Yang Yan had left it to him.

Over the years, the Xuan Realm Pearl had left many indelible marks on Yang Kai's growth and had helped him greatly many times.

He and the Xuan Realm Pearl had also long since become united in mind and spirit. It was one of his most important trump cards.

His plan this time was simple: use the Heaven Devouring Battle Art to devour a Cultivation Star and repair the damaged Small Xuan Realm.

This was also a sudden idea. Yang Kai did not even know whether it could be done. But with Hengluo Star Domain's devouring as a precedent, he felt the plan had a very high chance of success.

Hengluo Star Domain could be considered a world of its own. Since it could serve as the carrier for the Heaven Devouring Battle Art and devour another world, the Xuan Realm Pearl should naturally be able to do the same.

The Xuan Realm Pearl was also a world of its own. Fundamentally, there was not much difference between it and Hengluo Star Domain. The gap was merely that one was larger and the other smaller.

If the Xuan Realm Pearl was a small world, then Hengluo Star Domain was a medium world, and the Star Realm was a great world. Worlds could devour one another.

The Cultivation Stars within a Star Domain were likewise small worlds of their own.

As one of these countless small worlds, Yellow Springs Star was the target Yang Kai planned to have the Xuan Realm Pearl devour. If it succeeded, the Small Xuan Realm would very likely be fully repaired, and might even rise to another level.

When that time came, outside, his Incarnation would direct Hengluo Star Domain, while inside, he would use the Small Xuan Realm to stir up chaos. Great Wilderness Star Domain would surely be beset by trouble from within and without, and Wu Heng would be exhausted trying to cope. Once enough time passed, killing him would be no problem.

Yang Kai calmed his mind and deduced the feasibility of this matter. After a long while, his expression suddenly turned solemn. With a flick of his finger, he sent the Small Xuan Realm flying out.

At the same time, he sat down cross-legged, connected his mind to the Xuan Realm Pearl, and transformed into the will of Heaven and Earth of the Small Xuan Realm.

All of a sudden, Yang Kai's field of vision changed, as if he himself had become the Small Xuan Realm. This feeling was not unfamiliar. When he communicated with Hengluo Star Domain's Origin, he would experience a similar sensation, as though his entire being had transformed into that world and become the voice of its Heaven and Earth.

Using the Small Xuan Realm as the carrier, he began circulating the Heaven Devouring Battle Art at an unhurried pace.

He had been through something similar, so the whole process came easily to him, without the slightest hesitation or delay.

As the Xuan Realm Pearl fell downward, it immediately spread a peculiar aura, an aura that could devour all things in the world!

Wherever the Xuan Realm Pearl passed, the void collapsed and dissolved, leaving behind a pitch-black spatial crack, as if someone had sliced a long strip from a piece of delicious food.

It really was possible. Seeing this, Yang Kai was delighted and immediately knew that his earlier conjecture had been right.

The space that had originally occupied that crack had clearly been devoured by the Small Xuan Realm, because Yang Kai could distinctly feel that something new had appeared inside the Small Xuan Realm.

That friend of Yang Yan's, whose name and origins were unknown, had methods that reached the heavens. He had been able to refine a world and create the Xuan Realm Pearl. What Yang Kai was doing now was definitely no less impressive than that person's feat, perhaps even greater.

All this time, the Xuan Realm Pearl's uses for Yang Kai had been storing people, planting spirit flowers and exotic grasses, or hiding from pursuit. Its greatest function had merely been dragging enemies into the Small Xuan Realm to fight them to the death.

But since Yang Kai could pull enemies into the Small Xuan Realm and use its World Force to crush them, he could probably defeat them with his own ability anyway, which made that function somewhat redundant.

But starting today, the Xuan Realm Pearl might be able to display an even greater use! Yang Kai could hardly wait to see the final result.
