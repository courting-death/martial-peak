## Chapter 3304 — Cutting Melons and Chopping Vegetables

Amid the shriek of tearing air, a short, piercing scream rang out. Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying's eyes flew wide, and they stared in horror at the battlefield not far away as if they had seen a ghost.

Lan He also froze for a moment, then quickly opened her eyes.

Just now, she had thought Yang Kai was certain to die, so she had not dared to look. But the scream that reached her ears had not been Yang Kai's voice at all. Now, when she looked out, she saw a scene she would never forget.

Yang Kai was no longer where he had been. Instead, he had appeared above the spear-wielding cultivator, his expression cold and killing intent surging around him like a tide. One large hand was pressed against the man's skull. Emperor Yuan billowed from him, making his robes snap and his black hair fly.

Lan He clearly saw a large section of the spear-wielding cultivator's skull cave in. Both his eyes burst from their sockets, white brain matter sprayed in all directions, and every trace of vitality in him vanished in the blink of an eye. He swayed, then dropped downward.

How could this be? The question flashed through Lan He's mind. Her eyes had been shut, so she had no idea how Yang Kai had escaped the killing formation of those five, much less how he had taken an enemy's life in the snap of a finger. But she did know that no ordinary person could have done such a thing. Even her Master and Master's Wife did not have that ability. Faced with an encirclement so dense and flawless, any Second-Stage Emperor Realm Master would have no choice but to surrender and bare his neck for the blade.

Yet Yang Kai had turned the tables from a hopeless position. Not only was he unharmed, he had killed one of them instead. From his bearing, there was not the slightest hint of distress or nerves. He looked calm and effortless, as if he were butchering chickens and dogs.

That was a First-Stage Emperor! And he had been killed just like that? Lan He could not shake the surreal feeling rising in her heart. She was at the same cultivation herself, so she naturally knew what kind of foundation an Emperor Realm Master should possess. Yet an expert revered by countless cultivators across the world had died at Yang Kai's hands in a single exchange.

She could not help shuddering, a chill seeping from every pore, while a strange light flashed in her beautiful eyes. Vaguely, she felt that Yang Kai might not be so easy to kill this time.

The spear-wielding cultivator had been killed in one blow, and the other four were badly startled as well. Fear and dread filled their eyes, but after only the briefest hesitation, they charged at Yang Kai again.

Yang Kai laughed. "Since you are so eager to die, this Lord will grant your wish!" With a beckoning motion of his hand, the long spear Imperial Treasure flew into his grip. There was no technique to speak of. He simply seized it with both hands, drew it back, and thrust it forward, driving the spear straight ahead with rainbow-like momentum.

That spot had been empty. At first glance, it looked as if Yang Kai had missed, but just as the spear tip, wrapped in Emperor Yuan, pierced the void, a figure appeared there with uncanny timing.

The man was an Elder of Netherworld Sect. He had intended to attack from the side, but he had only just reached his position and had not even regained his bearings when an aura of death stabbed toward him head-on. Shocked, he shook the red banner in his hand, making it burst into a cloud of blood mist. This was his life-bound Secret Treasure, the Blood Sea Banner, and under the cover of that blood mist, he pulled back.

Yet he had only retreated half a step when pain suddenly flared in his chest. He jolted violently, and all the strength in him rushed toward that spot before spilling wildly outward.

A terrifying thought surfaced in his mind, and he hurriedly lowered his head. At some unknown moment, a fist-sized hole had opened in his chest, punched straight through from front to back. Through that hole, he could even see his mangled heart rapidly contracting as fresh blood gushed out like a fountain.

"No!" he howled at the sky, as if he could not believe what had happened to him. What ability did that Yang Kai possess, to break through the cover and defense of his Blood Sea Banner with one spear and come straight for his heart?

Before the thought could fully form, he swayed and plunged headfirst from midair. His life was already gone before he hit the ground.

On the other side, after succeeding with one spear strike, Yang Kai did not pause for even a moment. As he withdrew his spear, he turned the shaft and thrust it diagonally out from beneath his ribs, the entire sequence flowing as smoothly as running water, dazzling the eye and leaving one unable to follow.

With a soft sound, the spear struck the third man in the head as he crept up behind him. Berserk power erupted, directly blasting his head apart.

In his hands, this long spear Imperial Treasure seemed reborn, as if it had gained a life of its own. Every thrust hunted a soul; every thrust claimed a life. And this... was only with him using the Secret Treasure as an ordinary weapon, without having refined it.

It was not over. Gripping the butt of the spear, Yang Kai suddenly swept it fiercely forward.

Directly ahead, the fourth man had already unleashed a killing move and was pouncing toward him with fierce momentum that could not be underestimated.

But the five had moved at the same time, and in the blink of an eye, Yang Kai had already killed three of them. Now that this spear was sweeping toward him, the fourth man's courage faltered, and his attack weakened by a third before it even landed. Without thinking, he pushed his protective Emperor Yuan to its limit and spread his right hand. A round shield-shaped Secret Treasure appeared and blocked the side of his body, as if he hoped to find a little security in this defensive artifact.

With a boom, the long spear slammed into the round shield Secret Treasure, and the impact rang out like colliding planets.

Everyone's eardrums ached and their eyelids twitched. Under that blow, the round shield Secret Treasure flashed wildly, then lost its spirituality all at once. A moment later, it cracked apart and burst into fragments.

A chorus of sharp gasps rang out. At the sight, Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng nearly bit their tongues, unable to believe their eyes. Of course they knew Yang Kai had not refined the long spear at all, and that every attack came purely from brute strength. Yet with a single blow, he had smashed an Imperial Treasure-level defensive Secret Treasure to pieces. How terrifying was that brute strength?

"Retreat!" Xu Changfeng roared almost by instinct. If it had been possible, he would have warned the previous three when Yang Kai struck, but Yang Kai had moved far, far too quickly. Like cutting melons and chopping vegetables, three Emperor Realm Masters had died in a haze of confusion, leaving him no time to react at all.

As he shouted, a pitch-black sword box suddenly appeared behind Xu Changfeng. It was square, about one chi long, and utterly unremarkable, but it radiated an aura capable of destroying the Heavens and wiping out the Earth.

He formed a seal with two fingers, and a pitch-black sword light suddenly shot out from the sword case behind him. In a flash, it arrived behind Yang Kai and slashed toward his back, clearly intending to force him to defend himself by attacking a vital point.

Even for one of the Eastern Territory's three top forces, Emperor Realm Masters were rare talents. Three had died at Yang Kai's hands in a single exchange, and although two of them belonged to Netherworld Sect, Xu Changfeng still felt as if his heart were bleeding. By attacking a vital point, Xu Changfeng meant to force Yang Kai to abandon his plan to kill the fourth man.

His idea was sound, but he never expected Yang Kai to ignore common sense entirely. Yang Kai paid no attention to the pitch-black sword light attacking from behind, and the long spear that had already destroyed the defensive Secret Treasure swept on through the fourth man's body.

With a tearing sound, blood and chunks of flesh scattered down from the sky. The power of that sweep had directly blasted the fourth Emperor Realm Master into a mist of blood in midair, leaving not even his bones behind.

At the same time, a clang rang out, rolling through Heaven and Earth like the peal of a great bell. Everyone's eardrums trembled, and their Divine Souls shook.

Xu Changfeng stared, nearly spitting blood from rage. At some unknown moment, a great bell half as tall as a man had appeared behind Yang Kai. Images of mountains, rivers, birds, and beasts flowed slowly across its surface as if alive, releasing a profound and mysterious aura.

His pitch-black sword light had failed to strike Yang Kai at all. Instead, it had hit the great bell and triggered unpredictable consequences.

"Mountain and River Bell!" A sharp light flashed in Fu Bo's eyes, and naked greed appeared on his face. A moment ago, he had been heartbroken over the death of an Emperor Realm Master from his own Sect; now, all that regret had vanished without a trace.

This was the Mountain and River Bell. This was the foundation on which the Origin Cauldron Great Emperor had achieved the position of Great Emperor back then, the Primordial Era Treasure, the Mountain and River Bell!

Although he had long heard that Yang Kai had obtained both the Mountain and River Bell and the True Phoenix Fire in the Shattered Star Sea, rumors were one thing and seeing it for himself was another. Only now did Fu Bo truly believe what he had heard.

When the bell rings, mountains and rivers are suppressed; when Imperial Aura turns, the Heavens are reversed! Rumor said the Mountain and River Bell could seal and suppress Heaven and Earth, and judging from the feeling of that sound just now, the rumor did not seem false. A single passive ring already had such miraculous power. What would it be like if its might were actively unleashed?

For a moment, Fu Bo's eyes turned red. If he could obtain the Mountain and River Bell today, then in the future, he might well have a chance to claim the dignity of a Great Emperor and become a second Origin Cauldron Great Emperor.

A figure retreated at top speed, panic written across his face. It was the last of the five who had come to attack Yang Kai. Xu Changfeng's warning had come too late. The fourth man had already been swept through the waist by Yang Kai and turned into a rain of blood, leaving this fifth man the only one still alive. Now he fled toward Brahma Heaven Sacred Land's camp, terrified as a stray dog and not daring to look back.

Earlier, the five Emperor Realm Masters from the two great Sects had joined hands to attack, each of them looking as if Yang Kai were already in their grasp. But how many breaths had passed? It seemed less than three, yet Yang Kai had already cut down four of the five as cleanly as a sharp blade through tangled hemp. Their so-called perfect joint attack was as laughable as a child's game. If he did not run, was he supposed to wait for death?

Before he could return to Brahma Heaven Sacred Land's camp, he heard Yang Kai roar behind him. Immediately afterward, something tore through the air and came rushing toward him.

His face changed drastically. When he turned to look, he saw Yang Kai standing in place in a throwing posture. The long spear Imperial Treasure that should have been in his hand was gone, while a streak of light was closing in fast behind the fleeing man.

Madman! the man cursed inwardly. How could he not know what that pursuing streak of light was? It was clearly the long spear Secret Treasure Yang Kai had thrown at him.

The streak of light had not yet arrived, but the sound of it tearing through the air already rang beside his ears like a summons from the underworld. He could not dodge; it was too fast. He could not block; the attack was too fierce. All color drained from his face, and he shouted, "Saint Lord, save me!"

The words had only just left his mouth when a tremendous force struck him squarely in the back. Pain followed, and the impact drove him staggering forward several steps. Then he saw a long spear burst through his chest and continue straight toward Brahma Heaven Sacred Land's camp.
