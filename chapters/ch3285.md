## Chapter 3285 — The Might of a Single Word

After a long while, Tao Yingruo finally came out.

Yang Kai turned to look and could not help starting.

Tao Yingruo's face was flushed, her whole bearing bright with the cheer of someone who had just received wonderful news.

Yang Kai asked curiously, "What has made Acting Sect Master so happy?" Inwardly, he was also wondering what Sheng Yuzhu had said to her that could leave her so openly delighted.

Tao Yingruo did not answer. Instead, she dipped into a graceful bow. "Many thanks, Elder. Elder Yang truly is a blessing to my Luocha Sect."

Yang Kai rubbed his nose, baffled. "What do you mean?"

Tao Yingruo smiled. "The moment Elder arrived, you brought back the Ancestral Founder of my Luocha Sect. If that is not a blessing, what is?"

"An... Ancestral Founder?" Yang Kai was startled. Beside him, Ran Yirou and Yu Keren both formed small O's with their mouths in shock, making them look charming for one brief instant. Neither of them understood how their Sect had suddenly produced an Ancestral Founder. Wasn't Luocha Sect's founder the previous Sect Master, Yu Luocha?

They did not understand, but Yang Kai quickly caught on. If his earlier guess was right, then every cultivation technique practiced in Luocha Sect had been passed down by Sheng Yuzhu. Although she had only done it to draw attention and get herself rescued, the inheritance itself was an undeniable fact. Yu Luocha could be considered her disciple, which naturally made everyone in Luocha Sect her disciples and grand-disciples.

It was just that Yu Luocha herself had never known.

Sheng Yuzhu had likely revealed this to Tao Yingruo now because she intended to use this place to slowly recover her strength. Luocha Sect might not be powerful, but it was still a Sect. With an entire Sect behind her, Sheng Yuzhu would recover somewhat faster.

"I have already confirmed it. The Ancestral Founder is indeed the source of my Luocha Sect's many cultivation techniques and secret arts. She was trapped in the forbidden area only because a vile person harmed her. Although the previous Sect Master received everything she had to teach, she failed to discover where the Ancestral Founder was imprisoned, causing the Ancestral Founder to suffer for so many more years. This time, it was only thanks to Elder's sharp insight that the Ancestral Founder was rescued. All of Luocha Sect is endlessly grateful."

Within a Sect, no bond was more reliable than the formal tie between Master and Disciple. Tao Yingruo had already confirmed that the previous Sect Master, Yu Luocha, had reached the Emperor Realm because Sheng Yuzhu had transmitted her arts, so there was no longer any doubt that Sheng Yuzhu was Luocha Sect's Ancestral Founder. Thus, even though this was their first meeting, and even though Sheng Yuzhu's appearance was shocking, Tao Yingruo immediately acknowledged her identity and treated her with a disciple's courtesy.

"It was just a fortunate coincidence. I should congratulate Acting Sect Master," Yang Kai said with a faint smile.

"Congratulations to us both." Tao Yingruo winked at Yang Kai. "Elder is part of my Luocha Sect too, after all."

"True enough." Yang Kai laughed. After the conferment ceremony, he already bore Luocha Sect's mark, and no one could deny that.

Tao Yingruo's expression turned solemn. "However, I must ask Elder to keep this matter secret for us. The Ancestral Founder's present cultivation is not even one ten-thousandth of what it was, and she will need a very long time to recover."

"Naturally." Yang Kai nodded, inwardly relieved as well. He had been worried that Tao Yingruo and the others might object to him settling Sheng Yuzhu in Luocha Sect, and had already made up his mind that if they showed the slightest improper attitude, he would send Sheng Yuzhu to Lingxiao Palace to cultivate in peace. He simply had not expected Sheng Yuzhu to make a move like this, directly establishing her status as their Ancestral Founder. From the look of things, she was willing to stay here.

And with that, how could Luocha Sect not welcome her? Tao Yingruo was probably well aware that if this Ancestral Founder, even trapped and in dire straits, could transmit her arts across space and produce an Emperor Realm Master, then her own strength had likely long since reached the peak. If they could win even a little of her favor, would Luocha Sect still need to fear that it could not rise? Given time, Luocha Sect would likely become even more glorious than before.

"You two, what you saw and heard today must not be leaked either. If I discover otherwise, you will be executed without pardon." Tao Yingruo turned to Ran Yirou and Yu Keren, her face stern and her tone sharp.

"Yes." How could the two women dare refuse? They hurriedly bowed, anxious and uneasy, both realizing that they had probably stumbled upon a Sect secret.

After exchanging a few more casual words with Yang Kai, Tao Yingruo hurried away, likely eager to share this good news with the other Dao Origin Realm cultivators. Although the matter was top secret, Luocha Sect now had only five Dao Origin Realm cultivators. Some things could be kept hidden, but others had no need to be.

With Luocha Sect's affairs settled, Yang Kai had no desire to linger. Although no one knew how long it would take Sheng Yuzhu to fully recover, Luocha Sect had his name protecting it, so likely no one would dare come looking for trouble.

So the next day, he took his leave of Sheng Yuzhu, Tao Yingruo, and the others.

To Yang Kai's astonishment, after just one day apart, Sheng Yuzhu's condition had already improved greatly. Though she still looked like skin stretched over bones, her vitality thin and faint, she was undoubtedly much better than she had been the day before. The relationship between Sheng Yuzhu and Luocha Sect also allowed him to leave with an easy mind.

Since Sheng Yuzhu had declared herself to be Luocha Sect's Ancestral Founder, she would not harbor any ill will toward Luocha Sect.

Yang Kai leisurely returned to Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory.

On Lingxiao Peak, Yang Kai summoned Hua Qingsi and asked about the Sect's affairs and the Northern Territory's recent situation. He learned that everything in the Sect was as usual, and that its hundred thousand disciples were all cultivating diligently. Although he had led people to the Southern Territory as reinforcements last time, Lingxiao Palace had not actually sent that many people, and many still did not know the incident had ever happened.

As for the Northern Territory, after witnessing Yang Kai's astonishing ability to rally forces, no one dared act out anymore.

Previously, Yang Kai had joined forces with the other three top Sects to sweep across the Northern Territory, suppressing all opposition with iron-blooded methods along the way. Even so, quite a few people had remained unconvinced. After the events in the Southern Territory, however, even those who were still unconvinced no longer dared show it. Across the entire Northern Territory, everyone appeared utterly obedient. It could almost be said that in the Northern Territory, Lingxiao Palace covered the sky with one hand.

Two other Sects had also been destroyed.

Dragon Tiger Sect and Xuanyun Pavilion!

These two Sects had been about as strong as Luocha Sect used to be. Each had an Emperor Realm expert holding the fort and roughly one or two thousand disciples, making them second- or third-rate Sects. Yet on a certain night more than a month ago, both were uprooted at the same time. From their Emperor Realm Masters down to their ordinary disciples, not one was left. All were slaughtered clean.

"Did our people do it?" Yang Kai asked, looking at Hua Qingsi.

Hua Qingsi smiled bitterly and shook her head. "It has nothing to do with Lingxiao Palace, though it does have some connection to Palace Master."

Yang Kai blinked. "Me? How come I did not know this had anything to do with me?" He had been in the Southern Territory this entire time and had never returned to the Northern Territory at all. Even if someone had died, what the hell did that have to do with him? If someone else had said this, he would definitely have argued, but the person speaking was Hua Qingsi, and she would never say such a thing without reason.

Hua Qingsi said, "Has Palace Master forgotten? That day, when the many Emperor Realm Masters of the Northern Territory gathered, the people from these two Sects were absent. You once said that since they had not come, there was no need for them to come anymore."

Yang Kai stroked his chin and said, "Did I say that?"

At the time, he had been desperate to save people, and Yu Zhuo's death had left him filled with anger, so it would not have been strange for him to say anything. But he truly did not remember it clearly. Besides, even if he had said it, it should not have been a big deal, right? Both Sects could have reached Lingxiao Palace within the time limit, yet they had not appeared. Clearly, they had not taken Lingxiao Palace's command seriously and were the sort to obey in public while defying orders in secret.

Yang Kai did not deny that he had meant to settle accounts with them afterward, but that had only been a private thought, never something he had said aloud. By now, he had long since stopped taking it seriously, only to learn that these two Sects had already been annihilated.

Hua Qingsi said, "More than a month ago, Mi Qi joined forces with over a dozen Sects of various sizes and uprooted these two forces with thunderous momentum. The heads of those two Emperor Realm Masters were sent over as well, along with the wealth the two Sects had accumulated over the years."

"Bastard!" Yang Kai flew into a rage. "Who told him to do that?"

Hua Qingsi lowered her eyes. "Perhaps Palace Master spoke without thinking at the time, but inevitably, some people listen with intent."

Yang Kai fell silent. If that was the case, then these two forces truly had been destroyed because of him.

Hua Qingsi said, "So Palace Master originally had no intention of finding trouble with them. Mi Qi's attempt to curry favor struck the wrong note. Does Palace Master wish to issue orders for his punishment?"

Yang Kai's expression twitched. "How should I punish him?"

Hua Qingsi said, "That depends on Palace Master's wishes. If you feel Mi Qi went too far, you can cripple his cultivation and have him apologize, giving the dead some justice. Failing that, Lingxiao Palace can issue an official notice stating that this matter had nothing to do with us and was entirely Mi Qi's personal action."

Yang Kai said flatly, "If apologies were useful, why would anyone bother cultivating? Besides, Mi Qi has already done it. If I cripple his cultivation now, wouldn't that only look like a clumsy attempt to cover things up?" After a pause, he said, "Sister Hua, you seem a little resentful."

Hua Qingsi lowered her head. "I would not dare. It is just that those were several thousand lives, after all."

Yang Kai frowned. "Sister Hua, if you have something to say, just say it. Acting like this makes us seem distant."

Hua Qingsi sighed, raised her head, and looked at Yang Kai. "Palace Master became famous at a young age, and your cultivation is anything but ordinary. Now you are even the leader of the Northern Territory. I worry that everything before you may cloud your judgment and make it difficult for you to keep your own state of mind in its proper place. Take this matter, for example. Perhaps you still feel those people's deaths have little to do with you."

Yang Kai listened quietly and did not interrupt her.

Hua Qingsi said, "But in truth, they did die because of you, and all because of one careless word. Palace Master, has this matter shown you the power you hold?"

As soon as she said this, a chill suddenly ran through Yang Kai.

Just as Hua Qingsi had said, he had not felt that the destruction of those two Sects had much to do with him. He had only thought Mi Qi was hateful for twisting his meaning. But after listening to Hua Qingsi lay it out piece by piece, he finally woke with a start.

Certainly, with his current strength, he could destroy those two Sects if he truly wanted to.

But for two or three thousand people to die merely because of a single sentence was truly difficult for Yang Kai to accept. In a daze, he seemed to see his own hands dripping with blood.

Hua Qingsi said, "When one occupies such a position, one must be cautious in speech and mindful of one's duties. Palace Master, please take this as a warning."

Yang Kai's expression shifted slightly. "I will remember."
