## Chapter 3242 — Emperors Gather

"Why insult people?" Yang Kai looked at him calmly.

Wuchang sneered. "He does not even dare come to see me. If that is not cowardice, what is?"

Murong Xiaoxiao said angrily, "Senior Brother Xia is too busy to get away. It is not that he does not dare come see you."

Wuchang stood with his hands behind his back. "Anyone can make excuses. Is he afraid that if he came, he would lose to this Lord again and embarrass himself?"

"That is not it at all!" Murong Xiaoxiao still wanted to argue, but Yang Kai raised a hand to stop her and said in a tone that was neither warm nor cold, "I have heard a little about the last Martial Gathering as well. A momentary win or loss means nothing. Before I came, Senior Brother Xia said Brother Wuchang was quite enthusiastic and told me, his Junior Brother, to be sure to keep you company for a round or two."

He had not even seen Xia Sheng recently, so naturally Xia Sheng could not have given him any such instructions.

But now that he represented Qingyang Shrine, how could he let someone slap them in the face? When trouble came, he naturally had to hit back and hold their ground before worrying about anything else.

"You?" Wuchang looked Yang Kai up and down, then nodded slightly. "Barely passable. You do have some qualifications. I hope you will not disappoint me too much."

"Maybe you will be pleasantly surprised!" Yang Kai raised a brow. This rotten bastard had better not fall into his hands, or Yang Kai would make him pay.

"Shameless boasting!" Wuchang kept sneering.

Chen Wenhao turned his head and shouted, "Wuchang, shut your mouth. This is Wuhua Hall. Do not act recklessly."

Gao Xueting also said, "Enough. Do not say any more."

Wuchang looked resentful, but he could hardly contradict his own Elder in front of outsiders. With a flick of his sleeve, he turned away and looked elsewhere. Yang Kai and the others naturally had no interest in dragging it out, so the disturbance faded away. After all, everyone knew no one could possibly make a move in a situation like this, and further arguing would only make them a laughingstock.

Wen Zishan chuckled. "Young people really are full of fire. Not bad, not bad."

Feng Ming neither agreed nor disagreed. Cupping his fists, he said, "The fellow cultivators of the Southern Territory have more or less all arrived, and the Hall Master is keeping them company. This way, you two." Turning his head, he instructed Zhuang Bufan, "Xiaofan, lead the two Seniors to the guest hall."

"Yes." Zhuang Bufan answered, extending a hand. "Everyone, please."

Feng Ming said, "Hall Master Wen, Saint Lord Ma, please forgive me. This Feng has duties to attend to and cannot accompany you any farther."

"Elder Feng is too polite. We can speak again later." Wen Zishan smiled faintly, clasped his hands behind his back, and followed Zhuang Bufan toward the mountain summit.

Ma Qing also nodded to him, then led the group from Tianwu Sacred Land close behind. This time, no one fought over who went first.

As the group gradually disappeared into the distance, Feng Ming suddenly turned and glanced back. His eyes settled on Yang Kai, and his brow furrowed slightly. Once no one was around, he took out a Transmission Compass and poured his Divine Sense into it, though no one knew whom he was messaging.

Halfway up, the group came upon a Y-shaped fork in the road. Wuhua Hall disciples waited on both sides, and when they saw the guests arrive, they saluted and gestured in invitation.

Zhuang Bufan looked back and said, "Seniors, please arrange for the disciples participating in the Martial Gathering to go left and right."

Their group was going to the mountain summit to rest, and only those in the Emperor Realm had that privilege. The disciples behind them naturally could not follow, a point everyone understood perfectly well.

Wen Zishan turned back and gestured. Gao Xueting immediately arranged for the Qingyang Shrine disciples to follow an attendant down the road on the right. Tianwu Sacred Land naturally chose the left. The disciples of the two houses did not get along very well. If they were really made to stay together, they would probably start fighting before tomorrow's Martial Gathering even began. After all, there would be no Emperor Realm Elders there to keep them in check.

The remaining group followed Zhuang Bufan upward again, climbing the steps. The people from the two great forces kept to opposite sides, as clearly divided as two rivers.

Before long, they reached the mountain summit. A magnificent palace came into view, imposing and extraordinary, with attendants already waiting outside.

Zhuang Bufan led the group toward the palace. As soon as they entered the hall, a burst of noise washed over them, making Yang Kai, Murong Xiaoxiao, and the others exchange glances. They had not expected it to be so lively inside.

One look around left them somewhat startled. No fewer than five or six hundred people had gathered in the great hall, and most of them were actually Emperor Realm experts. Attendants wove among them, and tables filled the hall, laden with fruits, delicacies, fine wine, and jade-like liquor.

The hall was packed with guests and extraordinarily lively. People moved to and fro, raising cups and inviting others to drink.

In the center of the great hall, more than forty young women in the prime of youth danced gracefully. Their clothing was not revealing; instead, each of them seemed bright with youthful vitality, and all were quite pretty. Their waists were lithe and slender, their arms curved as they danced, drawing smiles from some of the onlookers.

Although Wuhua Hall had arranged these dancers to add to the merriment, Wuhua Hall's status was what it was, and it would not go too far with such things. Clothing that was too revealing would inevitably invite contempt. The arrangement before them better displayed Wuhua Hall's bearing.

Most people's attention, however, was not on the dancers. Here, Southern Territory Emperor Realm cultivators had gathered in force, and the Sect Masters of great Sects and Family Heads of clans were assembled in one hall. This was the perfect time to build friendships and connections. Who had the leisure to pay attention to the dancers?

It was not easy for Emperor Realm cultivators to gather in one place, and the Southern Territory Martial Gathering provided an excellent occasion for it.

Wen Zishan and Ma Qing had just led their group inside when someone set down his wine cup, stood, and saluted. "Hall Master Wen, Saint Lord Ma."

Once that voice rang out, the noisy great hall quieted down considerably. Everyone turned to look, and greetings sounded from every side. Wen Zishan smiled and cupped his fists in all directions as Zhuang Bufan led him all the way to the front. Ma Qing, on the other hand, kept his eyes straight ahead, looking cold and aloof. Those who knew him well understood that this was simply his nature, not that he meant to slight anyone.

In the seat of honor, a sturdy man dressed in fitted black martial attire looked over with a smile. Turning to a refined-looking middle-aged man seated below him on the right, he said, "I told you, did I not? The two of them would certainly arrive within the time it takes to burn one stick of incense. See? Here they are."

The refined man smiled. "Brother Lei truly has divine foresight."

Other than Wuhua Hall's Hall Master, Lei Gu, no one here was qualified to sit in the seat of honor.

This refined man was clearly also the Sect Master of a Southern Territory Sect. His cultivation had impressively reached the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, and his seat was arranged extremely close to the front.

"Brother Lei!"

Wen Zishan and Ma Qing stepped forward and cupped their fists at the same time.

Lei Gu rose and said, "You two have had a tiring journey. Please, take your seats."

Although there were many tables in the great hall and the seats were packed, the four tables at the very front, two on either side, remained empty, and no one dared occupy them. These four tables had clearly been reserved for Qingyang Shrine and Tianwu Sacred Land.

Wen Zishan and Ma Qing split to the left and right, each leading his own Elders to their seats.

Wen Zishan and Ma Qing naturally each occupied a table of their own, while the several Elders they had brought crowded together at another. Qingyang Shrine had one more person than Tianwu Sacred Land, so their table was filled exactly to capacity.

Maidservants immediately came forward to pour wine for everyone.

Lei Gu slowly rose, raising his cup high as he said in a clear voice, "The Southern Territory Martial Gathering is a grand event of the world. Since the fellow cultivators of the Southern Territory have shown us such favor by allowing my Wuhua Hall to host it, this Lei and all of Wuhua Hall will spare no effort to create a fair arena for everyone from the Southern Territory. There will be absolutely no favoritism. Here, I wish in advance that the disciples of your various Sects win from their very first battles, enjoy flourishing martial fortune, and carry on the flame for my Southern Territory. This humble wine is hardly enough to show my respect. Everyone, please drink your fill!"

"Many thanks, Hall Master Lei!" In the great hall, everyone answered in unison, raising their cups and drinking together. The scene was truly lively.

This first toast was Lei Gu's courtesy as the host. After that, everyone was free to do as they pleased. Tomorrow was the day the Martial Gathering would begin, and by then things would no longer be so harmonious. Injuries, deaths, and defeats among the disciples would be unavoidable.

Quite a few people came over to toast Qingyang Shrine's table, but most of them did so for Gao Xueting's sake.

Luo Chen did not socialize much and did not enjoy that honor. Yang Kai, Xiao Baiyi, and Murong Xiaoxiao were here purely to broaden their horizons, and they did not know many Emperor Realm cultivators, so naturally no one paid them any attention. They were happy enough to enjoy the peace.

Yang Kai found a chance to ask Murong Xiaoxiao about it and finally understood why their relationship with Tianwu Sacred Land was somewhat strained. Things were indeed as he had expected; it really was because of the previous Martial Gathering. In the individual battle and the group battle, the two sides had traded first and second place, and neither was convinced by the other, so naturally there was some rivalry.

Half an hour later, Lei Gu, Wen Zishan, and Ma Qing left early. The three of them needed to gather and discuss tomorrow's Martial Gathering, including the roster arrangements and the layout of the venues. Although Wuhua Hall had handled everything on its own, and would not go so far as to play tricks with such matters, Wen Zishan and Ma Qing still had to inspect everything for themselves.

After the three of them left, the atmosphere in the great hall immediately grew even livelier. Clearly, their presence had still put some pressure on the others.

Yang Kai drank wine and ate while looking around, appearing somewhat bored. To be honest, he had been somewhat interested in the Martial Gathering at first, but after arriving he discovered it was nothing special. If this matter had not landed on him, he would already be tempted to turn around and go home.

Although nothing major should happen over at Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory, the dust had not settled after all. Without personally overseeing things, he felt somewhat uneasy.

The main reason was that his cultivation was too high now, Second Stage Emperor Realm. It was truly hard for him to take interest in competition between cultivators in the Dao Origin Realm, Void King Realm, and Origin Returning Realm. He had already killed a great many Emperor Realm cultivators, so how could he still be in the mood to watch these petty scuffles?

But Qingyang Shrine had called on him for this, so he could only go through the motions with them.

Suddenly remembering something, he turned to Gao Xueting and asked, "Senior Sister Gao, what did that fellow Wuchang mean earlier when he said Senior Brother Xia was afraid of losing to him again? Does this Martial Gathering also include contests between Emperor Realm cultivators?"

Gao Xueting said, "The Martial Gathering itself does not. There will be some after the Martial Gathering, but participation is voluntary, and there are no rewards. It is purely for exchange."

Yang Kai murmured in understanding and secretly made up his mind. If Wuchang provoked him again when the time came, he would definitely teach him a lesson. Whatever special constitution he had, surely it could not be stronger than a Third Stage Emperor, right?

Yang Kai reckoned that with his current strength, facing a Third Stage Emperor should not be much of a problem. If he brought out all his trump cards, even a Third Stage Emperor would probably have to step aside. He just did not know whether Wuchang would give him the opportunity.

Raising his cup to his lips, he glanced sideways and happened to see Wuchang looking over. His blue and red eyes were exceedingly strange and sinister. Yang Kai let out a sneer, his face filling with disdain, almost driving Wuchang mad with anger.
