## Chapter 3290 — Give Me Three More Yao Kings

"Don't you have anything else to do?"

A full month passed before Luan Feng finally met Yang Kai in the guest hall. Her face was cold as she said, "I hear you have Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory. As the Master of a Palace, why are you running to my Wild Ancient Land instead of holding down your own territory?"

Yang Kai grinned. "Everything there is fine. It makes no difference whether I'm there or not."

Luan Feng said, "And the Southern Territory? The conspiracy there last time clearly ran deep. Even demons showed up. Perhaps some scheme is still brewing in the dark. Don't you want to understand what was behind it?"

Yang Kai slowly shook his head. "My foundation is in the Northern Territory. I only happened to run into the matter in the Southern Territory. Whatever schemes are at work over there, if the sky falls, there will naturally be tall people to hold it up. I'm in no hurry."

Luan Feng glared at him in irritation. "Speak. What exactly do you want?"

"Tea, tea!" Yang Kai raised his cup in invitation.

Luan Feng snorted. "Speak! I don't have that much free time to waste here with you!"

Yang Kai took a sip of tea, set the cup down, and looked troubled. "Since Madam Feng insists, this Yang won't keep hiding it."

Luan Feng sneered again and again, badly wanting to curse him for being a whore while still wanting a chastity arch, but in the end, mindful of her status, she held the words back. She had truly never seen anyone so shameless, and even suspected that Yang Kai’s skin was thick enough to withstand her Innate Divine Ability.

Yang Kai's expression turned solemn, and he said in a deep voice, "I have come this time to ask only one thing, and I ask Madam Feng to grant it."

"If you have something to say, say it. If you have a fart, let it out!" Luan Feng closed her eyes, waiting for him to make some outrageous demand. She was truly afraid she might lose control and spray a mouthful of World-Ending Black Flame at him.

"Borrow people!" Yang Kai spat out two words.

"Borrow what people? How many?" Luan Feng narrowed her beautiful eyes.

Yang Kai said blithely, "If I'm borrowing people, naturally I mean Yao Kings. I don't want many. Just give me three more. The three Saint Venerables can each spare one from among your subordinates. That isn't too much, is it?"

"Hehe..." Luan Feng laughed.

Yang Kai said seriously, "Why is Madam laughing?"

Luan Feng said, "If I don't laugh, I'm afraid I won't be able to stop myself from killing you." Her face was all smiles, but her tone was cold enough to chill the bones.

Yang Kai gave an "oh."

Bang... Luan Feng smashed the table beside her to pieces with one palm, sending tea splashing everywhere. Through clenched teeth, she said, "Yang Kai, don't push people too far! Do you really think that just because a descendant of Heaven's Punisher is backing you, you can do whatever you please in my Ancient Land for the rest of your life?"

Yang Kai rubbed his nose. "I'm not..."

"You still dare say you're not?" Luan Feng was furious. "If not for that, how would you dare disregard me, disregard my Ancient Land! The Ancient Land has always been forbidden ground for humans, but look at you. You come whenever you want and leave whenever you please, and you even left a Space Array here. I let that pass, but you also barge into my Fengluo Palace whenever you feel like it. Do you have any regard for me at all?"

Yang Kai lowered his brows and eyes, letting her vent for a while. Only after her anger had settled did he grin and say, "Madam is taking this too seriously. I really don't mean that. I have always respected Madam a great deal."

Luan Feng sneered. "Do you think I would believe that?"

Yang Kai said solemnly, "I'm telling the plain truth." As he spoke, he patted his chest, looking as if he were speaking with a hand over his conscience.

Luan Feng's chest heaved violently. She practically wanted to slap Yang Kai to death, but if she truly could, she would not have been so angry. Earlier, when she said Yang Kai relied on the descendant of Heaven's Punisher to back him, it had only been said in anger. In truth, she knew that even without the descendant of Heaven's Punisher as a factor, she could do nothing to him.

After meeting Yingfei and the others last time, Luan Feng had heard a piece of news that greatly shocked her. Yang Kai had actually become entangled with a pure Dragon Girl, and he also seemed to have made a trip to Dragon Isle, then returned safe and sound.

She did not know what had happened on Dragon Isle, but that alone made it impossible to look down on him. With a snort, she flicked her sleeve and said, "No matter how silver your tongue is, this time, don't even think of getting your way with me."

Yang Kai pulled a long face. "Madam, there's no need to be so absolute. I'm only borrowing three Yao Kings. The Ancient Land still has twenty-nine of them. Give me three more, and there will still be twenty-six left. To you, that's only a small matter. It won't shake the foundation of your rule."

Luan Feng was so angry she laughed. "You even have the nerve to say three more? Last time, we already lent you three. What do you take Yao Kings for? My Ancient Land is vast, but in total we only have the Thirty-Two Yao Kings. You ask for three, then another three. How much family wealth does the Ancient Land have for you to dig out? I won't lend them!"

Yang Kai cupped his fists and said, “The last time, the last time. This will absolutely be the last time. If I ever come scheming after the Yao Kings again, I’ll be a little dog.”

Luan Feng looked at him from the corner of her eye, her face full of disbelief, then suddenly said, "I'm rather curious. What do you want three more Yao Kings for? Are Xi Lei and the others not enough to hold down your Lingxiao Palace?"

Yang Kai sighed. "For Lingxiao Palace alone, of course they're enough, but Madam also knows about what happened at Wuhua Hall last time. This Yang, though untalented, has already taken Wuhua Hall from the Southern Territory's side and plans to turn it into a branch of my Lingxiao Palace. Mm, so I still need other experts to hold the place down and maintain stability. If not for that, I wouldn't have come to trouble Madam."

Luan Feng said in surprise, "You got Wuhua Hall into your hands too? Star God Palace actually agreed?"

Yang Kai smiled. "It's only a piece of ownerless land. What would Star God Palace be reluctant to part with?"

Luan Feng nodded. "You brat, your ambitions aren't small. The Northern Territory was developing perfectly well, yet you actually want to stretch your hand into the Southern Territory."

Yang Kai pulled a bitter face. "I had no choice." That was the truth. Lingxiao Palace had too many disciples. Otherwise, he would not have set his sights on Wuhua Hall's foundation.

Seeing signs that Luan Feng's tone had softened, however, he hurriedly struck while the iron was hot. "That's how things stand, Madam. Please grant this to me. Just three Yao Kings will do."

Luan Feng said indifferently, “This Queen already said that you can forget about succeeding this time. Once may be allowed, twice may be tolerated, but there will be no third time. The Ancient Land has already yielded to you again and again. Do not keep pushing your luck.”

Sensing her resolve, Yang Kai frowned. "You truly won't lend them?"

Luan Feng said coldly, "There are some words I don't wish to say twice."

Yang Kai nodded. "Since Madam puts it that way, I won't force the matter."

Luan Feng was stunned, as if she had not expected Yang Kai to back down so easily. Before she had time to feel pleased, she heard Yang Kai ask, "Right, Madam, may I ask which Yao Kings submitted to the Northern Saint Venerable Shi Huo back then?"

"Why are you asking..." Halfway through, Luan Feng suddenly realized something. Her beautiful eyes narrowed sharply, and she clenched her teeth.

Yang Kai grinned, stretched out his hand, and released the Incarnation. The Incarnation cooperated by striking a domineering pose, looking truly imposing. Yang Kai pointed at it and smiled at Luan Feng. "Although Shi Huo died back then, his successor is now right here. He intends to enter and rule the northern lands of the Wild Ancient Land. I ask Madam to inform Brother Fan Wu and Brother Cang Gou, and also have those Yao Kings and Yao Marshals who originally belonged to the northern lands hurry back and report for duty."

At just the right moment, the Incarnation grinned at Luan Feng. That smile made Luan Feng's face darken on the spot. Gritting her teeth, she said, "So this was your plan all along."

Yang Kai sighed deeply. "I was forced by circumstances. I ask Madam's forgiveness."

Luan Feng lifted her smooth chin. "Even so, what of it? Do you think that just because it refined Shi Huo's Origin, the Ancient Land has a place for it? Yang Kai, don't be so naive." If she yielded this time, then in the future, the Ancient Land might truly no longer have the confidence or strength to oppose Yang Kai, because the Ancient Land's resolve and defenses had already been breached and shattered by Yang Kai time and again.

Yang Kai said calmly, "Regarding the previous matter at Wuhua Hall, this Lord is endlessly grateful to the Ancient Land and the three Saint Venerables, and I am willing to maintain a good working relationship with you. But if Madam truly insists on blocking me, then don't blame this Lord for disregarding old ties. People strive upward; who wants to go lower? I would like to know whether those twenty-nine Yao Kings will still serve you wholeheartedly once they learn that the three Saint Venerables are obstructing their future prospects."

Luan Feng was furious, her beautiful face losing color. "Do you have any conscience? You're actually threatening me?"

Yang Kai said, "Madam, I only want three Yao Kings to sit in command of the Southern Territory. That doesn't count as an excessive request, does it?"

Luan Feng fell silent. Her gaze kept moving between Yang Kai and the Incarnation, and her heart was filled with bitterness. She knew that this time, no matter what, there was no way to stop him. By first bringing out Shi Huo's successor, Yang Kai had a righteous claim to occupy the northern lands of the Wild Ancient Land. When the time came, if he called for the eight Yao Kings from back then to return, Luan Feng and the others would be powerless to stop him. What was more, to the Yao Kings, being able to follow Yang Kai and serve him was absolutely a good thing, a bright path for their future. If the Saint Venerables insisted on blocking them, they would certainly stir up dissatisfaction, which would be greatly disadvantageous to the Ancient Land.

Seeing her expression change, Yang Kai knew that the purpose of this trip had basically been achieved. He smiled and said, "Madam, there is something I do not know whether I should say."

Luan Feng said angrily, “You have always liked going against me. Is there anything you would not dare say to my face?”

Yang Kai gave a light cough. "It seems Madam has a deep misunderstanding of this Lord. So be it. Time reveals a person's heart, and distance tests a horse's strength. Let us set aside for now what kind of person I am and speak only of little Tian Long."

Luan Feng's face abruptly turned cold, and dense killing intent spread from her. "If you dare set your mind on Tian Long, this Palace will fight you to the death!"

Luan Feng could yield on anything, but her daughter was her reverse scale. For Yang Kai to mention little Luan Feng at this moment undoubtedly touched her most sensitive nerve.

Yang Kai raised a hand. "Madam has probably misunderstood. I don't mean to do anything to Tian Long. No matter how despicable and shameless I may be, I would never lay a hand on a child. I only feel that although she was born to Madam, whether she can inherit everything from Madam in the future is still uncertain. They say dragons give birth to dragons and phoenixes give birth to phoenixes, but there are always exceptions, aren't there?"

Luan Feng said proudly, "How could my daughter be lacking?"

Yang Kai nodded. "Good. Even if little Tian Long can inherit everything from Madam and carry forward Luan Feng's mighty name, so what? Does Madam not want little Tian Long to go a step further than you?"
