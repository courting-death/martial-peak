## Chapter 3314 — You Want to Beat Me to Death?

The Dragon Clan were naturally highly resistant to all kinds of negative powers. Cang Mo's Thunder Laws were impressive, but a mere sea of lightning was not enough to stop Yang Kai.

His enormous body charged into the lightning sea, stirring up waves in an instant. Yang Kai reached down and struck with his palm, but Cang Mo did not dare meet the blow head-on. He retreated far away, his figure turning into a streak of lightning.

Yang Kai's great hand, however, chased after him like a maggot clinging to bone. It opened and closed, grabbing again and again, as if he were trying to catch a flying insect. Tang Sheng and the others were left unsure what to feel at the sight. A dignified Quasi-Great Emperor was being beaten into a panicked retreat by a Second-Stage Emperor Realm cultivator. Everyone felt as if they had fallen into a dream.

Yang Kai's body was huge, but he moved quickly, and Cang Mo reacted even faster. One grabbed, the other fled, and the chase left streaks of afterimages hanging in the Void for a long time.

The Incarnation arrived in perfect coordination, the Demonic Warhammer in its hands radiating a domineering, awe-inspiring might. It and Yang Kai worked together seamlessly, leaving Cang Mo able only to defend, without the slightest strength to strike back.

Yet Yang Kai showed no trace of joy, because although Cang Mo looked wretched, he was not flustered at all. Every response he made was calm and unhurried.

"Bind!" Yang Kai suddenly roared. The Laws of Space surged, imprisoning the four directions and sealing the Void. Cang Mo, who had been shifting back and forth without pause, abruptly froze. He felt as if he had fallen into a swamp, the space around him turning unbearably viscous. The harder he struggled, the deeper he sank.

While he was caught off guard, the Incarnation's hammer had already come crashing down.

He could not dodge. Although he had cultivated a little Space Force himself, it was just as Yang Kai had guessed: he had only touched the threshold and had never comprehended it to the level of Laws. His cultivation was far higher than Yang Kai's, but with the space around him imprisoned, breaking free would take at least a breath of time, and that single breath of delay was enough to decide life and death.

Cang Mo's eyes chilled. He urged his Emperor Yuan, lightning flared around him, and an oval thunder shield suddenly appeared over his body.

With a boom, the Demonic Warhammer smashed into Cang Mo. The oval thunder shield instantly caved in, then shattered, but at the same time, a tremendous force rebounded the Incarnation backward. Lightning coiled around him with a crackling barrage of explosions, and he looked far from comfortable.

Before Cang Mo could react again, Yang Kai's great hand descended from above, blotting out Heaven and Earth. He seized Cang Mo in his palm, clenched tight, and growled coldly, "Extinguish!"

Space Force surged within that great hand, transforming into a strangling power intent on wiping Cang Mo out completely.

The next moment, however, Yang Kai's expression changed drastically. Countless dazzling bolts of lightning burst from the gaps between his huge dragon claws. The lightning suddenly gathered into a single beam, drilled through the back of his hand, which had seemed as solid as a fortress, and shot out the other side.

Yang Kai looked up and saw Cang Mo standing in the Void with an icy expression, holding a Secret Treasure shaped like a pestle. It was only as long as an arm, but it was not to be underestimated. The lightning flashing across the Secret Treasure was not azure, but white, a white as bright as daylight. Golden blood still clung to the pestle, filled with boundless vitality and tremendous energy.

Yang Kai opened his hand and looked down. A huge hole had appeared in the center of his palm. Golden Blood flowed inside it, yet not a single drop fell. The ruined flesh writhed incessantly, rapidly repairing the wound.

"You really are hard to kill!" Yang Kai shook his hand and looked up at Cang Mo.

Even after joining forces with the Incarnation and unleashing their full strength, he still could not take Cang Mo down. The dignity of a Quasi-Great Emperor truly lived up to its name. If a Quasi-Great Emperor was already this powerful, then what about a true Great Emperor? For a moment, Yang Kai felt an endless yearning rise in him.

"There is something strange about your Dragon Bloodline!" Cang Mo narrowed his eyes and studied Yang Kai. He even reached out, dabbed a little Golden Blood from his Secret Treasure, put it into his mouth, and tasted it. A look of extreme shock immediately crossed his face.

He had known before that Yang Kai possessed a Dragon Bloodline body, but he had instinctively assumed Yang Kai's bloodline was impure. Yet for a Half-Dragon with impure blood to possess a thirty-zhang Dragon Transformation body was simply inconceivable. As far as he knew, the purer a Dragon Clan bloodline was, the larger the true body it could manifest.

He had once dealt with an Eighth-Tier Great Dragon, and even when that Eighth-Tier Great Dragon revealed its true body, it had only been twenty zhang long. An Eighth-Tier Great Dragon was already a rather remarkable existence on Dragon Isle. It made no sense for such a dragon to lose out to a Half-Dragon.

Thirty zhang was, at the very least, a level only a Ninth-Tier Great Dragon could reach.

Now, with the power of his Secret Treasure, he had broken free and wounded Yang Kai at the same time. After taking some of the dragon blood and tasting it, he discovered to his shock that although the blood was somewhat mixed and truly could not be considered pure, the energy contained within it far surpassed that of an ordinary Great Dragon.

What was going on? Had this Half-Dragon bloodline reverted to its ancestral origin?

Before that thought had even finished forming, he heard Yang Kai ask in a sinister voice, "How does my blood taste?"

The moment Yang Kai spoke, Cang Mo sensed something wrong. That tiny bit of dragon blood he had licked was giving off a sharp aura inside his mouth. Without hesitation, he opened his mouth and spat it out, only to find that what he had spat out was no longer fresh blood at all, but a small golden sword.

Cold sweat drenched him at once. He could not understand how Yang Kai had done it. Blood that had already left his body could still be controlled freely. Such a method was simply inconceivable.

Not daring to be careless, he sent a flash of white lightning through the Secret Treasure in his hand, reducing all the Golden Blood to nothingness.

Cang Mo sighed faintly and said, "Yang Kai, I have no desire to bully the weak. Your strength is indeed impressive, but you are still a little lacking if you wish to oppose me. You have already proven your ability. My proposal remains the same. Hand over that thing, and I will turn around and leave at once. In the future, if you ask something of me, I will not refuse."

Yang Kai gave an unruly laugh, his voice booming like thunder. "You want it? Then come and take it. If you can seize it, it is yours. If you cannot... then do not blame me if I beat you to death!"

"You want to beat me to death?" Cang Mo's expression turned strange, as if he had heard something utterly absurd.

"That depends on whether you are truly that hard to kill." Yang Kai took a deep breath, then suddenly reached out and beckoned. The Mountain and River Bell spun through the air toward him, and he grabbed it in one hand. Clutching one of its legs, he brought it smashing straight down toward Cang Mo's head without the slightest flourish.

Even with all Cang Mo had seen, the sight made the corner of his mouth twitch.

A perfectly good Primordial Era Treasure, something even Great Emperors would covet, was actually being used like this. Cang Mo could not help feeling regret on the Mountain and River Bell's behalf, as if a priceless pearl had fallen into unworthy hands.

Still, there was no denying how formidable the move was. Even with Cang Mo's heaven-reaching cultivation, he could not ignore it. If that blow landed, even if he survived, he would be severely injured.

At the critical moment, Cang Mo remained perfectly calm. The pestle in his hand pointed from afar, and a white lightning dragon shot from its prongs, blasting toward Yang Kai's chest.

The lightning dragon had yet to arrive, but the aura of death was already rushing at him head-on. Cang Mo was a Quasi-Great Emperor, so how could any Secret Treasure he took out against an enemy be ordinary? This pestle was actually a Thunder Pestle, and moreover an Imperial Treasure Cang Mo had cultivated as his life-bound artifact. He had tempered it for many years with Thunder Laws, and the Heaven-Rending Divine Thunder it unleashed was something even a Great Emperor would have to handle carefully, or else risk injury.

Yang Kai did not know the details of the Thunder Pestle or the Heaven-Rending Divine Thunder, but that did not stop him from trusting his instincts. He knew that although Cang Mo looked calm on the surface, fury was burning in his heart, and this strike held nothing back.

This was probably Cang Mo's strongest attack. If Yang Kai could block it, he would have the qualifications to contend with him! If he could not, then everything would end here today!

In that instant, Yang Kai's expression turned cold and fierce, a trace of resolve appearing on his face. He flipped the Mountain and River Bell in his hand, using the mouth of the bell to meet the oncoming lightning dragon.

White light blazed as the lightning dragon slammed into the Mountain and River Bell. The bell rang again and again, the Void burst apart, and a feeling of Heaven collapsing and Earth splitting spread through everyone's heart, leaving them terrified and uneasy.

In the next instant, every gaze fixed on the spot in the air.

They saw a beam of white light shooting from the Thunder Pestle, connecting Cang Mo and Yang Kai. One figure was tiny; the other towered between Heaven and Earth, forming an overwhelming visual contrast. Yet at this moment, it was that tiny figure who held the upper hand.

Lightning poured out, forcing Yang Kai's enormous body backward step by step and leaving huge footprints in the earth. His body was hunched over, his muscles bulged, and veins stood out across his face as he strained with all his might. The Mountain and River Bell, along with Yang Kai himself, had already vanished from sight, completely swallowed by lightning. Amid the crackling explosions came Yang Kai's furious roars.

Cang Mo continued releasing thunder energy without the slightest intention of giving Yang Kai a chance to breathe, as if he meant to settle everything once and for all with his strongest method. Even now, he still had enough leisure to speak. "Yang Kai, do not fail to appreciate my kindness. I do not want to kill you, but do not think that means I cannot! I will give you one final chance. Think carefully before you answer me!"

"Good..." Yang Kai's roar had turned hoarse, yet it carried out from the lightning with perfect clarity. Cang Mo was delighted when he heard it, thinking Yang Kai had finally yielded under the pressure. Just as he was about to withdraw his Divine Ability and discuss matters properly with him, Yang Kai's voice came from the lightning again. "...It feels good!"

Cang Mo's face darkened.

As soon as he spoke, the lightning filling the sky trembled slightly and actually dispersed by quite a bit. Yang Kai's thirty-zhang body finally emerged again, leaning slightly forward as he endured the tremendous pressure and advanced step by step.

As he walked, his huge dragon claw struck the Mountain and River Bell fiercely in rhythm with his steps.

Clang, clang, clang...

Each time the bell rang, a circular shockwave burst from its mouth. The white lightning pressing forward was shattered by the impact, broken apart and thrown into chaos.

Yang Kai advanced faster and faster, going from a slow walk to a trot, then breaking into a full sprint. The lightning coiling around him could not stop his advance. His dragon scales were tattered and ruined, and there seemed to be no intact flesh anywhere on his body. Every inch of his skin gave off a scorched smell.

Cang Mo's expression finally changed.
