## Chapter 3330 — There Really Is Something Between Them

The thought alone filled Qiong Qi with glee.

Although they were both Divine Spirits, the Dragon Clan's arrogance was hated by every race, and Qiong Qi was naturally no exception. Such a proud member of the Dragon Clan had actually formed some unimaginable relationship with a human?

Now he rather wanted to see what that dragon girl was like. This old man would like to see, after a scandal like this, whether those stinking dragons would still dare act so high and mighty in front of him.

They raced onward like wind and lightning. On Qiong Qi's back, Fu Ling chattered without end, deliberately or otherwise trying to cozy up to Yang Kai. If Mo Xiaoqi had not been standing guard at the side, she probably would have pressed herself right against him.

Her enthusiasm was so intense that even Yang Kai could hardly bear it. He could not help wondering whether something was wrong with this female dragon. Logically, after he had tricked her so miserably, shouldn't she hate him to the bone? Why was she fawning over him at every turn and doing everything she could to curry favor?

If every member of the Dragon Clan was like her, what dignity would they have left?

The blue sea stretched to the sky, waves rising and falling.

One empty Spirit Island after another flashed beneath them, each growing no small number of heaven-and-earth treasures. Dragon Isle's stores could only be called astonishingly rich, yet the treasures that cultivators outside would covet until their eyes went red were nothing in the eyes of the Dragon Clan. Every dragon was immensely powerful in their own right, so these things were of no use to them. The only useful ones, the Dragon Blood Flowers, had nearly been wiped out by Yang Kai's ruthless scheme.

They flew on without pause, and only after a full two days did a small crescent-shaped island appear ahead. The island covered no more than a hundred li in radius. It truly was not very large, but neither could it be called small, and it was more than enough for a single member of the Dragon Clan to live on.

"That is Elder Sister Qing's Spirit Island." Fu Ling pointed at Crescent Island, her tone a little downcast. Though she had traveled with Yang Kai for the past two days, she had failed to improve their relationship. Instead, her excessive enthusiasm had only made him wary of her, leaving Fu Ling rather stifled.

Then there was that little girl, guarding against her as if she were a thief. If not for Yang Kai, Fu Ling would have slapped her to death long ago. Yet this little girl kept watching her every moment. The slightest unusual move from Fu Ling was enough to set her on alert.

"This is where Qing'er lives?" Yang Kai looked astonished. He had been to this island before. Last time, in order to search for traces of Zhu Qing, he had gone to many places and set foot on many Spirit Islands. When he came here then, the place had been completely deserted. He had not expected it to be Zhu Qing's Spirit Island.

Fu Ling smiled. "That's right. Elder Sister Qing should be on the island. She and the Third Elder were punished to stay on Dragon Isle and reflect on their mistakes behind closed doors. Barring any accident, they won't be able to leave Dragon Isle for at least a hundred years. Brother-in-law, if you miss her, just come visit her more often."

Yang Kai snorted. "A hundred years? Is this the Second Elder's order?"

Fu Ling stuck out her tongue. "Who else but the Second Elder could give such an order? The one everyone fears most is the Second Elder."

"Enough. Since we've arrived, I don't need you to guide me the rest of the way. Go do whatever you should be doing." Yang Kai waved her off.

Fu Ling pouted. "Brother-in-law is truly heartless and ungrateful. Once you're done using me, you just toss me aside. I don't want to go. It's rare for me to come back, so I don't want to return so soon. I want to go see Elder Sister Qing too."

Yang Kai gave her a sidelong look. "Do you believe I'll teach you a lesson?" As he spoke, he formed a hand seal.

Fu Ling's face changed at once, and in a flash she leapt down from Qiong Qi's back. With a dry laugh, she said, "Brother-in-law, what are you doing? Fine, fine, I won't disturb you and Elder Sister Qing in your private business, all right?"

Inwardly, she grumbled that he truly had no sense of romance. A beauty had delivered herself to his door, yet he remained unmoved. She really wondered whether he was still a man.

Yang Kai flicked a hand at her, and only then did Fu Ling say reluctantly, "Brother-in-law, you must come see me when you have time. I'm all alone over there. It's terribly dull."

Yang Kai could not be bothered with her. He urged Qiong Qi onward, and they turned into a streak of red light that descended toward Crescent Island.

At one tip of Crescent Island stood a simple, ancient palace. As Qiong Qi's enormous body descended from the sky, a graceful figure flashed out from within.

She looked over, her beautiful eyes meeting Yang Kai's.

Their eyes met, and Yang Kai grinned.

Zhu Qing cried out in surprise and delight, "You're back?"

Last time, Yang Kai had left the Dragon Temple to track Wu Kuang down, and Zhu Qing had naturally been worried. Although Wu Kuang was no longer at his peak, he was still the Heaven Devourer Great Emperor. No one knew whether Yang Kai could match him.

Now, seeing Yang Kai return safe and sound, Zhu Qing could finally let go of the worry that had been weighing on her heart.

As the words left her mouth, she had already flown toward him.

Yang Kai opened his arms and drew her into his embrace, breathing in the faint fragrance of her hair.

Qiong Qi felt wretched all over. Being ordered around as Yang Kai's mount had already left him unwilling and resentful, and now this shameless pair were actually cuddling in broad daylight on his back as if no one else existed. Worse, he could not even lose his temper. He could only stiffen and stand motionless, afraid of disturbing them.

At this moment, however, he was shaken beyond words.

There really was something between them! His Young Master really did have something going on with a dragon girl! This was news that could shake the heavens. In all the tens of thousands of years he had lived, he had truly never encountered anything like it.

Although Zhu Qing had not revealed her true form, he could sense how pure this dragon girl's bloodline was. If they truly fought one-on-one, he most likely would not be her match.

Ninth-Tier? Tenth-Tier? With a bloodline so pure, her status on Dragon Isle definitely could not be low. At the very least, she was an Elder-level figure. How could such an existence have feelings for Young Master? Could Young Master's Half-Dragon bloodline also have something to do with this dragon girl?

As his imagination ran wild, Yang Kai and Zhu Qing were lost in their intimacy. Though neither of them said a word, with their skin pressed close they could feel how much they had missed each other.

Suddenly, Zhu Qing sensed someone watching her. She opened her eyes and found herself looking into a pair of bright, wide eyes.

Mo Xiaoqi stood behind Yang Kai, eyes wide as she stared blankly at the scene before her, seemingly unable to accept it. The sight struck her with enormous force. For reasons she could not explain, a sour ache welled up in her heart, and she nearly burst into tears.

Under that stare, Zhu Qing flushed slightly. She gently slipped out of Yang Kai's embrace and asked with a smile, "And this young lady is..."

Yang Kai did not look embarrassed in the slightest. Calm and at ease, he quietly transmitted a sentence into Zhu Qing's ear.

Zhu Qing's beautiful eyes lit up when she heard it. She stepped forward, warmly took Mo Xiaoqi's hands, and said, "So you're Little Sister Xiaoqi."

Although she and Fu Xuan were both Dragon Clan Elders, strictly speaking, Fu Xuan was her senior, so there was nothing wrong with calling Xiaoqi little sister. Moreover, she had long heard about this young girl and had only finally seen her in person today.

Mo Xiaoqi was clearly startled by her warmth. Her thoughts had already been in a muddle, and now she was even more at a loss. She opened her mouth as if to say something, but not a single word came out. She only turned to look at Yang Kai.

Yang Kai smiled. "This is Zhu Qing. If you don't mind, you can call her Elder Sister Qing."

"Elder Sister Qing..." Mo Xiaoqi called softly, unexpectedly with a sob in her voice. Then she threw herself into Zhu Qing's arms and began to cry, her tears falling like rain on pear blossoms. In moments, she had soaked the clothes over Zhu Qing's chest.

Zhu Qing was instantly dumbfounded. She looked up at Yang Kai, only to find him just as bewildered.

"Did you bully her?" Zhu Qing mouthed silently.

Yang Kai spread his hands, his expression saying this had nothing to do with him.

He truly did not know why Mo Xiaoqi had suddenly burst into tears.

Mo Xiaoqi herself did not know either. She had simply wanted to cry, and in that moment had thrown herself into Zhu Qing's arms.

Helpless, Zhu Qing could only hold Mo Xiaoqi, gently patting her back and comforting her in a soft voice.

Yang Kai wiped the cold sweat from his forehead, secretly glad that the Beast Great Emperor had not seen this scene. Otherwise, the Great Emperor might really skin him alive. Thinking about it carefully, though, he did somewhat understand Mo Xiaoqi's feelings. Now that they had arrived at Dragon Isle, she was about to see her mother, so her emotions were bound to be unsettled. In that state, even the slightest stimulus would surely make them spill over.

The crying continued, but Zhu Qing showed not the slightest impatience, continuing to soothe her with gentle words.

Only after a long while did Mo Xiaoqi stop sobbing. Head lowered, she stepped out of Zhu Qing's embrace and wiped her eyes with the back of her hand, her face so red it seemed about to drip blood.

How mortifying! The first time she met Elder Sister Qing, she had actually cried bitterly in her arms. Who knew what Elder Sister Qing would think of her now? She secretly lifted her eyes, only to see Zhu Qing looking at her with pure concern and not the slightest hint of mockery. Only then did she relax a little.

When she looked at Yang Kai, however, she saw him smiling as though he were watching a fine show. She immediately stamped her foot. "Eldest Brother Yang is a bad person!"

With that, she turned and rushed into Zhu Qing's palace, disappearing in the blink of an eye.

Yang Kai was dumbstruck. Pointing at himself, he asked Zhu Qing, "What does this have to do with me?"

Zhu Qing shot him a reproachful glance, too lazy to waste words on him, then chased after Mo Xiaoqi, leaving Yang Kai and Qiong Qi shivering in the sea wind.

After a long silence, Yang Kai finally said, "Old Qiong, tell me, aren't women strange?"

Qiong Qi nodded with deep conviction. "If they weren't strange, they wouldn't be women."

Yang Kai said, "Old Qiong, you've had this sort of experience too? Hiss... speaking of which, does your Qiong Qi clan have females?"

Qiong Qi rolled his eyes. "Do you think this old man popped out of a stone?"

Yang Kai chuckled. "Just curious, just curious." After a pause, he said, "Stay here for the time being. Don't wander around if there's nothing urgent. Once I finish handling things here, I'll take you away."

"Rest assured, Young Master. This old man understands." This was Dragon Isle. Now that he had come here, how could he dare misbehave? After hesitating for a moment, he reminded him, "Young Master must be careful in all things as well. The Dragon Clan... is not so easy to deal with."

Especially since you actually had an ambiguous relationship with a dragon girl. How could Great Elder Zhu Yan and Second Elder Fu Zhun possibly let you off easily?
