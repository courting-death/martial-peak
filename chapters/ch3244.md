## Chapter 3244 — Yu Zhuo

The rewards for every Martial Gathering were extraordinarily generous. Poor rewards would never convince anyone to risk their life. Under normal circumstances, the three top Sects pooled the rewards together, with Star God Palace also contributing half a share. They covered everything from Spirit Pills and miraculous medicines to Secret Techniques and more, enough to make anyone's mouth water.

What excited the tens of thousands of participating cultivators even more was that the first-place winners of both the individual and group battles would not only receive rich material rewards, but could also enter the hidden cultivation grounds of the three top Sects. Each Sect allowed one month.

The Southern Territory's three top Sects each had a foundation they stood on: Qingyang Shrine's Divine Roaming Mirror world, Tianwu Sacred Land's Martial Intent Hall, and Wuhua Hall's Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens. Each had its own wonders. The Divine Roaming Mirror world needed no explanation; it was a place to cultivate the Divine Soul. The Martial Intent Hall was a hidden ground for comprehending all kinds of profound intents, while Wuhua Hall's Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens was an excellent place to temper the flesh body.

The hidden grounds of the three great forces covered a cultivator's essence, qi, and spirit. If one could pass through all three places, one's future prospects would surely be immeasurable. Faced with rewards like these, who would not be moved? Materials and the like were only external aids; one's own strength was the true foundation.

First place would not only receive all these rewards, but could even freely choose one of the Sects to join and be directly cultivated as a Core Disciple.

Once this reward was announced, every one of the tens of thousands of cultivators in the valley began breathing hard, so excited they could hardly contain themselves.

That said, in every Martial Gathering over the years, first place had always gone to someone from the three great forces, and all of them had been Core Disciples as well. So although this reward was enormously attractive to ninety-nine percent of those present, to the disciples of the three great forces themselves, it was only icing on the cake and not worth caring too much about.

The reason such a reward was offered was precisely for the cultivators outside the three great forces to see, to entice them into bringing out everything they had and striving for the best possible ranking. Over the many years of the Martial Gathering, people really had done it, and more than once. Once-obscure figures revealed their brilliance at the Martial Gathering, were taken in by the three great forces, and ultimately achieved remarkable things.

The reward for second place was slightly inferior. They could only freely choose one force and join as a Core Disciple, while the reward of the three hidden grounds was naturally reduced to two.

Third place was a step lower again. They could choose one force to join, and the hidden-ground reward was reduced to one.

Below that, there were only material rewards, with no chance to freely choose a Sect to join or enter a hidden ground to cultivate.

With rewards like these announced, the tens of thousands of cultivators in the valley naturally all began itching to try. Each secretly resolved to perform well this time. Perhaps they really could rise above their station in a single leap.

After Feng Ming finished reading out the rules and rewards, he turned and glanced behind him. Xiao Yuyang gave him a faint smile, indicating that he had nothing to add. Only then did Feng Ming raise his hand and shout, "Let the competition begin!"

Gong...

The sound of a gong rang out from who knew where, echoing through the entire valley with a deafening crash. Halfway up the mountain, figure after figure shot forth, each trailing streams of light in different colors. Like a meteor shower falling from the sky, they descended one after another toward the chess-piece-like platforms scattered through the valley.

Yang Kai was among them. As a judge, he naturally had to appear first.

It was funny, really. This was the first time in his life he had ever served as a judge, but with his experience and breadth of vision, he remained perfectly at ease.

Standing on Arena Eight, he pinched a jade slip between his fingers and sent his Divine Sense surging into it to investigate. Inside the jade slip was a string of numbered designations. Each number represented a cultivator, and the order of appearance and each person's opponent had already been arranged. All he needed to do was call out the numbers of both sides, observe the match, and judge fairly.

He lifted his eyes, looked around, and called out, "First match, number three zero one eight against number fifteen thousand sixty-one."

As soon as he finished speaking, a petite figure shot up from below the platform, landed steadily on the arena, bowed to Yang Kai, and said in a crisp voice, "Greetings, Senior."

Just like that, he had become someone else's Senior. Yang Kai sniffed. He strongly suspected he might not even be older than her, but his strength was what it was. In this Star Realm, there were already very few people who could stand on equal footing with him, let alone call themselves Seniors in front of him.

He nodded and smiled to acknowledge her, then looked her over carefully. The person who had come onto the platform was a petite woman, her beautiful black hair tied behind her head in two delicate ponytails and fluttering in the wind. A well-fitted dress set off her dainty yet full figure. Judging by her face, she truly was not very old and still carried a touch of youthful freshness, but her cultivation was unexpectedly good, impressively at the Second Stage of the Dao Origin Realm. He wondered which Sect she came from.

He was still sizing her up when another person sprang onto the platform from the other side. It was a tall, imposing man in black martial clothes that snapped in the wind. His aura was aggressive, and his eyes were filled with sinister ruthlessness. He looked extremely hard to deal with.

Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm!

Yang Kai glanced at him and secretly felt that number three zero one eight was probably in trouble. Not everyone was like him, able to fight across Stages and slaughter stronger opponents as easily as butchering chickens and dogs. A single small Stage of cultivation often meant an enormous disparity in strength. Otherwise, what would everyone be cultivating so desperately for? Of course, matters like this could not be judged absolutely. In a life-and-death battle, one's state of mind, grasp of the situation, and ability to adapt were all keys to victory or defeat, and there were no few examples of the weak overcoming the strong.

Seeing her opponent take the platform, the woman numbered three zero one eight cupped her fists and said politely, "Luocha Sect, Yu Zhuo!"

She was announcing her Sect and name. No matter how they fought in a moment, proper etiquette still had to be observed. The judge was watching from the side.

The black-clothed man plainly did not see it that way. He merely raised a hand, pointed at Yu Zhuo of Luocha Sect, and said expressionlessly, "Get down. You are no match for me." His tone was not arrogant. He spoke as if it were perfectly natural, as though he were only stating a fact. And he did indeed have the right to say so; after all, his cultivation was one Stage higher than hers.

When Yu Zhuo heard this, anger flashed across her face. She lightly bit her red lip and said, "We have not fought yet. How do you know I am no match for you?"

The black-clothed man said, “Do not refuse a toast only to be forced to drink a forfeit.”

Yu Zhuo was furious, but then she suddenly took a deep breath and calmed the anger in her heart. Turning to Yang Kai, who looked perfectly calm and unruffled, she asked, "Senior, may we begin?" Since she had come here, how could she possibly be frightened off by a few words from someone else? Still, the other party's contemptuous, eyes-above-everyone attitude had truly lit a fire in her. If not for her decent upbringing, she likely would have attacked already. Why would she bother asking Yang Kai?

Yang Kai lowered his eyes, clasped his hands before his abdomen, and looked perfectly calm and unruffled. He nodded. "You may."

In truth, the moment both of them stepped onto the arena, they were free to begin. Still, Yu Zhuo's refined and courteous manner had won her a bit of goodwill from him.

The instant his words fell, the black-clothed man suddenly charged toward Yu Zhuo like a whirlwind. A great saber as tall as a person appeared out of thin air in his hand, lightning crackling over its blade. In a flash, he was in front of Yu Zhuo, chopping straight down at her head with not the slightest intention of showing mercy to a beauty. From the look of it, he would not be satisfied until he split Yu Zhuo in half with that one saber strike.

Yu Zhuo was startled, clearly not expecting him to attack the moment the match began, and so abruptly at that, without the slightest warning.

In her haste, she flicked out a soft whip. It turned into layer upon layer of whip shadows, protecting the vital points all around her body.

Ding ding dang dang. A chain of ringing impacts sounded, sparks flying in every direction as the two figures on the arena raced one after the other, nearly face to face. The black-clothed man pressed her step by step, his great saber imposing and extraordinary, his attacks broad and sweeping. His was a reckless style that only attacked and never defended. From the very start, he suppressed Yu Zhuo so fiercely she could barely breathe.

His cultivation was already one Stage higher than Yu Zhuo's, and he had seized the initiative, so naturally he held the upper hand.

However, Yu Zhuo clearly had some skill as well. The soft whip in her hand moved as though it were an extension of her own arm, and even after losing the initiative, she managed to block more than a hundred of her opponent's fierce attacks in an instant. Although she was under enough pressure to struggle for breath and signs of defeat had appeared, she was by no means so fragile that she would collapse in a single blow.

The sight made Yang Kai raise his brow slightly, and he began watching in earnest. He had no choice but to take it seriously. He was the judge of Arena Eight, and he had to do his best to ensure that no one died. The moment he sensed something wrong, he would have to intervene.

This was the first day of the Martial Gathering and the very first match. He did not want anything to go wrong.

Honestly, from a personal standpoint, he was inclined toward Yu Zhuo. This had nothing to do with men or women; the main thing was that Yu Zhuo had been very polite to him. The black-clothed man's arrogant manner, on the other hand, was unpleasant to watch. This Old Master was the judge of Arena Eight, at least. Fine, you did not call me Senior, but what was the meaning of coming up here without even glancing at me? Still, a judge could not favor anyone. He could only watch calmly. At the same time, he sighed faintly in his heart, feeling that Yu Zhuo was likely in grave danger. Her cultivation was already inferior, and she had lost the initiative as well. Barring any accident, victory and defeat would soon be decided.

On the arena, the two raced one after the other and reached the edge in the blink of an eye.

Yu Zhuo was being forced backward, so she had no way to pay attention to what was beneath her feet. By the time her foot found empty air and she realized something was wrong, it was already too late. Once she fell outside the bounds of the arena, she would lose whether she had been defeated or not.

The sudden change threw Yu Zhuo into panic. She cried out in alarm, her body abruptly leaning backward as her balance slipped.

The black-clothed man pursuing her had clearly expected this. After all, their viewing angles were different. What Yu Zhuo could not see, he saw perfectly clearly. Seeing his chance, he laughed viciously. "Get lost!"

As he spoke, he chopped his saber down at Yu Zhuo.

Yang Kai almost could not stop himself from intervening.

Yet in that flash of time, Yu Zhuo's body bent to an unbelievable angle. One foot hooked onto the edge of the arena, and her folded-back body just happened to avoid the black-clothed man's cleaving strike through the air. Immediately afterward, her soft whip snapped out and coiled around the great saber. Borrowing the force, she spun and drifted behind the black-clothed man.

The two of them instantly traded places.

Many cultivators watching below the platform froze. Then waves of cheers and applause rose and fell, clearly from Yu Zhuo's fellow disciples shouting encouragement.

Yang Kai was somewhat stunned as well. He had not expected Yu Zhuo to find a way out of such a desperate situation. He had seen it clearly: this was not something Yu Zhuo had planned in advance. Her reaction after stepping into empty air and the panic on her face had not been feigned.

Her escape from the danger of falling off the arena relied entirely on her ability to adapt in the moment.
