## Chapter 3246 — Capsizing in a Gutter

Perhaps Yu Zhuo's match with the black-clothed man had set a good tone, because the cultivators who took the stage afterward all threw themselves into showing what they could do. All manner of wondrous methods appeared one after another, and the fighting was fierce.

Some of them were truly impressive, drawing repeated nods from Yang Kai as he watched. Others fought dull, lackluster battles with nothing worth mentioning and no real highlights at all. If not for his duties as a judge, Yang Kai feared he might have dozed off.

The day passed, and when evening came, the sounds of battle from the arenas throughout the valley died down. Tens of thousands of cultivators rested where they were, waiting to fight again the next day.

Yang Kai also returned to the Spirit Peak where Qingyang Shrine had temporarily settled. The day had left him badly worn out. In truth, all he had done was stand on the arena and watch, and he had not needed to act many times, but keeping his attention focused at every moment truly drained his energy.

He had only rested for an hour when Gao Xueting sent word telling him to come over.

Helpless, Yang Kai could only push open the door and go out. Outside the main hall, several Emperor Realm Elders from Qingyang Shrine were waiting. When Wen Zishan saw Yang Kai appear, he grinned. "Let's go."

"Where to?" Yang Kai asked suspiciously.

"You'll know when we get there." Wen Zishan wore a mysterious expression as he led everyone flying off in one direction.

Only after asking Gao Xueting halfway there did he learn that they were going to attend an exchange meeting.

The Southern Territory Martial Gathering was such a grand event. Tens of thousands of elite cultivators from the Southern Territory had gathered in one place, along with several hundred Emperor Realm Masters, a sight that was extremely rare. During the day, the Emperor Realm Masters had to serve as judges and maintain order throughout the Martial Gathering, leaving them with no time to spare. At night, however, it was time for them to gather and exchange with one another.

This was also the usual custom at every Martial Gathering.

With so many Emperor Realm Masters present, everyone naturally had some good things on hand. Selling those things outright would not be worthwhile, but they could be traded to others for treasures they needed. Of course, as the host, Wuhua Hall would also specially collect many treasures and bring them out at the exchange meeting to start things off.

Nor would this be the only exchange meeting. There would be one on the first night, and after that, one would be held every five days, for a total of six sessions. That would surely ensure that every Emperor Realm Master present came away with some gains.

Hearing that it was an exchange meeting, Yang Kai's interest was piqued.

When they arrived, he indeed saw several hundred Emperor Realm Masters gathered in a large hall. Despite the crowd, the hall was quiet. Only the constant surging of Divine Soul power showed that they were all communicating secretly with one another.

After the people from Wuhua Hall arrived and gave a brief opening speech, the exchange meeting officially began.

As the host, Wuhua Hall indeed took the lead by bringing out several fine treasures, stirring everyone's enthusiasm. After that, people continually went onstage to offer things they no longer wanted in exchange for items in others' possession.

During all this, the exchange meeting was merely a platform. As for how to trade in a way that ensured neither side suffered a loss, that required the two parties to discuss the details carefully in private.

For the time being, Qingyang Shrine's side held back. Yang Kai did not know what the others were thinking, but he himself had seen nothing that interested him. His knowledge and experience were no longer what they had been back then, and ordinary treasures could hardly move his heart. He had mainly come along just to watch the excitement.

Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao, on the other hand, seemed quite tempted by many of the items. Unfortunately, the two had only recently advanced to the Emperor Realm and were not particularly wealthy. With nothing presentable to trade, they could only look on and sigh.

In the latter half of the night, the exchange meeting dispersed, and everyone returned to their own quarters.

When the second day arrived, the Martial Gathering began again, and the valley that had been quiet all night became lively once more.

Yang Kai did not go to join the bustle this time. Instead, he meditated and rested in his guest room. He did not need to take a turn as a judge today, and since he had nothing else to do, he simply entered the Small Xuan Realm to inspect it.

He had been constantly busy lately and had not paid much attention to the Small Xuan Realm's situation. Now that he finally had some free time, he naturally wanted to take a look.

As soon as he entered the Small Xuan Realm, he sensed an invisible will somewhere in this world turn its attention to him for a moment, as if a sleeping dragon had opened its eyes to glance at him before closing them again.

Yang Kai knew that was Gungun's will, so he did not take it to heart.

Back in the Star Domain, he had used the Small Xuan Realm as the vessel for the Heaven Devouring Battle Art to devour Cultivation Stars, unintentionally causing a trace of will to be born within the Small Xuan Realm itself and even transforming into Gungun. This could be considered an enormous gain. However, Gungun did not possess much self-awareness. Its existence was exceedingly strange, and even now Yang Kai did not know exactly how it had been born. Even if he were given another chance, he could not guarantee he could create a second Gungun.

The current Small Xuan Realm was vast and full of life.

Gungun had devoured several Cultivation Stars in the Great Wilderness Star Domain, not only merging their territories into this place, but also bringing in all the living beings who had once lived on those Cultivation Stars. They now all lived within the Small Xuan Realm.

The existence of these living beings, and the birth, aging, sickness, death, arising, abiding, decay, and emptiness that came with them, had given this world the first outlines of a cycle of reincarnation.

It was difficult to put into words, but Yang Kai felt that the current Small Xuan Realm was subtly different from before, as though only now had it become a true world. With a single thought, an invisible, majestic power settled upon him, giving Yang Kai the sudden feeling that he himself was this Heaven and Earth.

The Great Might of Heaven and Earth! Only those who comprehended the Great Might of Heaven and Earth were qualified to achieve the position of Great Emperor.

Clearly sensing this power, Yang Kai was pleasantly surprised. Although he had been able to control the Small Xuan Realm before, he had never sensed the Great Might of Heaven and Earth from this world so easily. It seemed that after Gungun devoured those Cultivation Stars and countless living beings, this world had indeed changed.

Although he was only a Second Stage Emperor Realm Master now, he would absolutely not stop at the Emperor Realm. Even if the path ahead was covered in thorns, he was destined to press forward.

Taking a deep breath, Yang Kai closed his eyes, spread out his Divine Sense, and carefully immersed himself in the sensation.

It was a wondrous feeling. His entire body felt warm, comfortable beyond words. He seemed to have merged with this world, yet also seemed to be lying in the womb, untouched by any disturbance from outside, letting body and mind drift on without end.

There was no need to deliberately sense anything. The world had become a warm bed, making him willing to sleep here and never wake, to remain forever in this wondrous state. Formless, invisible power gathered from all directions, wrapping him layer upon layer like silk spun by spring silkworms.

Heaven and Earth were utterly still. Yang Kai kept his eyes closed, holding on to this wondrous state, when a chill suddenly rose in his mind. All the warmth and comfort vanished in an instant, and an indescribable sense of crisis descended from above. Yang Kai's eyes snapped open. Dizziness washed over him, his vision blurred, and he realized that the Divine Sense he had spread out was showing signs of merging with this world. His expression changed drastically in alarm, and he hurriedly urged his Divine Soul power, forcing himself awake.

The Divine Sense that had spread out like a tide suddenly rushed back. A rumbling explosion went off, deafening him as his vision blackened and he spat a mouthful of blood. Then his whole body toppled stiffly to the ground.

An unknown amount of time passed before his vacant eyes gradually regained focus. Indescribable pain surged through his entire body, as if he had been cut to pieces by a thousand blades. The pain was especially unbearable deep in his head, where it felt as though something were tearing him apart. His fingers twitched, and sensation slowly returned. Only with great difficulty did he sit cross-legged and begin meditating to regulate his breathing.

At that moment, Yang Kai was soaked through with sweat, as if he had just been pulled from the water, and lingering fear showed plainly on his face.

He did not know exactly what had happened. He had merely wanted to use the Small Xuan Realm to comprehend a little of what the Great Might of Heaven and Earth was, yet it had actually shown signs of scattering his Divine Soul. If the Warm Spirit Lotus had not acted at the critical moment, he feared things would have ended badly this time.

What kind of nonsense was this? He had nearly capsized in a gutter over one small attempt at comprehension. Yang Kai did not know whether to laugh or cry.

However, he faintly sensed that the so-called Great Might of Heaven and Earth was beyond what he could touch right now. It was precisely because he had touched upon its existence that he had found it difficult to withstand.

The Emperor Yuan inside his body was in some disorder. Yang Kai wanted to inspect it, but found that he could not properly gather his Divine Soul power, so he could only give up.

Only after resting for quite some time did he slowly recover a little strength. With a thought, he appeared directly in the medicine garden.

The entire Small Xuan Realm had undergone enormous changes, yet the medicine garden remained completely unchanged. This was the core region of the Small Xuan Realm, and also the forbidden area of the entire Small Xuan Realm.

Yang Kai had long ago used certain methods to conceal the medicine garden's existence. From the outside, this vast area appeared hazy and indistinct, making it impossible to see what lay within. Without his permission, even an Emperor Realm Master who came here could forget about forcing their way in.

After entering the medicine garden, Yang Kai discovered that quite a few people had actually gathered around it. They were all Void King Realm cultivators, clearly cultivators from the Cultivation Stars Gungun had devoured. They had probably discovered the strangeness of the medicine garden as well and wanted to peek inside, but their strength was lacking and they could find no way through. At that moment, they were gathered in small groups, discussing ways to enter the medicine garden.

Yang Kai found the sight amusing. He had not expected his medicine garden to be so attractive to these people.

He had settled those hundreds of millions of living beings in the Small Xuan Realm for two reasons. First, when Gungun devoured those Cultivation Stars, it had not been picky and had swallowed those living beings along with them. Although Yang Kai had the means to expel them, doing so would indeed take time, so he had simply let them remain. Second, he had also wanted to see what changes would occur in the Small Xuan Realm after those hundreds of millions of living beings moved in.

The facts proved that the Small Xuan Realm had indeed changed. If the former Small Xuan Realm could be called an empty shell, then the current Small Xuan Realm had become substantial, like a walking corpse gaining its own essence, vitality, and spirit. It had truly become a world of its own. The changes in the Great Might of Heaven and Earth proved as much.

Even so, Yang Kai would not allow them to harbor any improper thoughts toward his medicine garden.

He gently raised his hand, and an invisible force spread outward across the flat ground.

In the next instant, the many cultivators gathered near the medicine garden all showed looks of horror, for each of them was retreating backward beyond their control, as if dragged by an invisible hand.

Whether they were Third Stage or Second Stage Void King Realm cultivators, none of them could resist this force. Before such power, every one of them felt how small they were.
