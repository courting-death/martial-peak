## Chapter 3251 — Appearing and Vanishing Like a Ghost

The violent force erupted, but it passed straight through the black shadow and struck empty air, tearing cracks through the very void.

Since its presence had been exposed, the black shadow no longer bothered hiding. The dagger in its hand thrust forward savagely, determined to take Yang Kai's life.

Chi...

Yang Kai himself did not move, yet he was driven back again and again. The ground beneath his feet looked as if it had been plowed open, two long trenches gouged into it. He reached out and grabbed the dagger buried in his lower back, ignoring the blood streaming from his sliced fingers as he glared at the figure in front of him, trying to see it clearly.

That instant had been a true brush with death, and it left him both shocked and furious. He was shocked because the assassin's attack had left no trace at all; even he had not sensed a thing. This person's assassination art had simply reached a terrifying peak. With such methods, who in the world could defend against him? He was furious because he had actually been ambushed.

He had escaped calamity not because he had detected any murderous intent, but because instinct had told him something was wrong and made him shift slightly aside. Only then had he avoided a vital point. Otherwise, his situation now would likely be truly dire.

Hong hong hong...

Wave after wave of violent power surged through the dagger and exploded inside him, blasting the flesh around Yang Kai's lower back into a bloody mess. He had no choice but to drive his Emperor Yuan with all his strength to resist that explosive force. Golden blood filled his mouth as he shouted, "Who are you!"

Luo Chen had brought him here, and before coming, he had even contacted Gao Xueting, so he had not thought too much of it at all. Who could have expected that he would be ambushed here?

This was clearly a premeditated assassination. He could not understand whom he had offended so badly that they would use such methods to kill him. In this whole affair, what roles had Luo Chen and Gao Xueting played?

He did not want to think too deeply about it, but he had no choice. The urgent task was to deal with the crisis in front of him first, then find out exactly who this assassin was. The matter of Luo Chen and Gao Xueting could wait.

The black shadow did not answer and continued driving Yang Kai forward at high speed.

Yang Kai swept his Divine Sense over it, yet still could not make out the black shadow's true appearance. He could not even detect any vitality from it. It really seemed to be nothing more than a ghostly shadow, not a solid body.

Enraged, Yang Kai threw another punch. The black shadow ignored it completely, allowing the fist to land on its body without suffering the slightest impact.

A tingling numbness spread from his lower back. Yang Kai's expression changed drastically. The dagger was actually coated in a deadly poison. He had no idea what kind of poison it was, only that it was taking effect with frightening speed.

There was no time left to keep probing. The Laws of Space surged out, and Yang Kai spread his five fingers like claws, grabbing down at the black shadow.

The black shadow gave a muffled groan, and its drifting, insubstantial body suddenly became much more solid.

Yang Kai was overjoyed at the sight. His brute strength had no effect on the black shadow, and even his Emperor Yuan could not injure it. If the Laws of Space had also failed, then this time he truly would have been in grave danger.

Fortunately, the Laws of Space did not disappoint him. Under the compression of space, the black shadow clearly found it difficult to endure and showed signs of revealing its true body.

"Sever!" Yang Kai shouted. A Moon Blade appeared out of thin air, turning into a graceful arc as it sliced through the black shadow's body.

Warm blood sprayed out. The black shadow was cut in two, only to strangely rejoin itself into one piece. Still, clear pain showed on that indistinct face. Although the Moon Blade had failed to kill it, it had wounded it.

Before Yang Kai could launch a second move, the black shadow suddenly released the dagger and drifted backward.

How could Yang Kai let it come and go as it pleased? His spread fingers clenched sharply toward it, and the space around the black shadow abruptly solidified.

This move caught the black shadow completely off guard. Only now did it understand just how troublesome the person before it was. No wonder the higher-ups had wanted it to strike first and kill him. Otherwise, he really might ruin their great undertaking.

"Exile!" Yang Kai shouted. The void imprisoning the black shadow instantly began to collapse, and a black hole abruptly appeared behind it. An extremely terrifying devouring force poured out from within, trying to swallow the black shadow whole.

This black shadow was too dangerous. Even a Moon Blade could not kill it, and Yang Kai had no patience left to investigate its identity. He would kill it first and worry about the rest afterward.

The Secret Technique of Exile was a Divine Ability that could banish its target into the Void Crack. No matter how immune to force the black shadow was, once it was exiled into the Void Crack, it would likely have no path of escape and would eventually die of old age within it.

Sensing the aura of nothingness and chaos from that black hole, the black shadow indeed grew flustered. A sharp whistle rang from its mouth, and more than a dozen figures abruptly appeared from every direction. Each held a powerful artifact and, without asking any questions, launched a frenzied barrage at Yang Kai.

Yang Kai's expression changed. He had not expected this fellow to have helpers, and every one of those helpers was in the Emperor Realm.

He swept his gaze over them and was shocked to find that the figures were all too familiar. Every face belonged to someone he had dealt with recently; he had even seen some of them during the day.

These people were actually the Emperor Realm Masters who had led the various great Sects and families of the Southern Territory here to participate in the Martial Gathering. Only, at this moment, the auras coming from them sent a chill through Yang Kai. Looking closer, he saw that every Emperor Realm Master was wrapped in a faint layer of black Qi.

"Demonic Qi!"

The scene before him was far too familiar. How could he not know that these Emperor Realm Masters had all been corroded by Demonic Qi and that both their bodies and minds had Entered the Demonic State? But how could there be Demonic Qi here?

In that brief instant of hesitation, violent energy had already engulfed him.

A small bell suddenly appeared. As it spun, it grew to the height of a person and enveloped Yang Kai within. On the bell, the patterns of mountains and rivers, birds and fish, and beasts seemed to come alive, swiftly roaming across its surface.

The Mountain and River Bell!

Even Yang Kai did not dare casually take a full-force strike from more than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters head-on, especially not now, when he was poisoned and still had a dagger lodged in his lower back.

With a series of booming impacts, light flashed wildly across the Mountain and River Bell, blocking every attack.

The Mountain and River Bell suddenly shrank and returned to Yang Kai's hand. He turned his head and saw that the black shadow, which had been only a hair's breadth from being swallowed by the Exile Secret Technique, had broken free while he was distracted and unable to spare any attention. In the darkness, a pair of indifferent eyes stared deeply at Yang Kai for a moment. Then the figure burst apart, becoming countless streams of black smoke that scattered in every direction and vanished in the blink of an eye.

Strike once, and if it failed, retreat a thousand li.

Yang Kai secretly gnashed his teeth, grief and fury surging beyond measure. Since beginning his journey, he had never suffered such a great loss. He had nearly died at someone's hands, yet he had not even seen the assassin's true face, nor did he know their origins. It was simply a disgrace.

He had no time to investigate. Those dozen or so Emperor Realm Masters had already urged their respective Imperial Treasures again and were attacking him madly, clearly buying time for the black shadow to escape.

Yang Kai yanked the dagger from his lower back, cast a spell to seal the wound, and with a flicker arrived before an old man with a grizzled beard. He threw a punch, smashing it into the man's face.

The grizzled old man did not make a sound. He flew backward face-up and fainted while still in midair.

This old fellow was only a First Stage Emperor Realm Master. How could he possibly be Yang Kai's opponent?

The remaining Emperor Realm Masters, however, showed no fear and had no intention of fleeing. Four people charged out from the left and right, while the others raised their Imperial Treasures and unleashed another wild barrage, caring nothing for friend or foe.

Yang Kai swept a cold glance over them and shifted aside to avoid one focused attack. The two people charging at him, however, were caught off guard and blasted into dust by their own allies, leaving no bones behind. Who knew which Sect's Sect Masters they were, yet they had actually died here.

The Mountain and River Bell struck again, dropping straight toward the three people on the right. The might to suppress Heaven and Earth descended from above. Those three sensed something was wrong and tried to scatter, but it was already too late.

When the bell rang out, the strength in their bodies slipped from their control, their Emperor Yuan fell into chaos, and they swayed as they plummeted downward.

Before they could hit the ground, the Mountain and River Bell had already descended over their heads, trapping all three inside.

Without pausing, Yang Kai flashed beside a beautiful woman and seized the back of her neck with one hand. Urging his Emperor Yuan, he first sealed her Emperor Yuan, then struck the back of her head with his palm, knocking her unconscious before throwing her into the Mountain and River Bell.

In the blink of an eye, half of the dozen or so Emperor Realm Masters lying in ambush here had already fallen. Seeing that the situation was bad, the remaining half did not remain entangled in battle with him. As if by tacit agreement, they scattered in every direction and fled into the distance.

Yang Kai wanted to pursue them, but half his body began to go numb, so he could only grit his teeth and stop, watching helplessly as they escaped.

His Divine Sense swept in every direction. Only after confirming there was no further danger did he reach out and draw over the two people he had knocked unconscious, throwing them into the Mountain and River Bell as well to suppress them. Then his figure flickered, and he hid inside the Small Xuan Realm.

Yang Kai appeared at the medicine garden with a slightly pale face and went straight to the Everlasting Tree, where he sat down cross-legged.

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu came at the sound. The moment they saw Yang Kai in such a state, both were shocked and hurriedly moved to either side of him, casting spells to heal his wounds.

An emerald-green curtain of light enveloped Yang Kai, and with the vitality of the Everlasting Tree nourishing him as well, he immediately felt much better. In truth, the injuries he had suffered this time were secondary. Although the dagger had stabbed into his body and damaged his internal organs, with Yang Kai's current physical constitution and powerful recovery ability, that was not a serious problem.

The real trouble was the strange poison on the dagger. He did not know what kind of toxin it was, but even with Yang Kai's current physical constitution and his cultivation as a Second Stage Emperor Realm Master, he had still fallen victim to it.

Emperor Yuan surged through him, flushing the numb half of his body. With the help of the two Wood Spirits, feeling gradually returned to that side.

It was fortunate that the victim was Yang Kai. If any other Third Stage Emperor Realm Master had been struck by this strange poison, there would likely have been no saving them.

Yang Kai now had a Half-Dragon Body. The Dragon Clan was naturally highly resistant to all sorts of negative conditions, and toxins were included among them. Although the toxin on this dagger was strong, it was still not enough to poison a member of the Dragon Clan to death. Even if an ordinary Giant Dragon were struck by it, it would not take the poison seriously. Yang Kai only possessed a Half-Dragon Body, so he did need to spend some effort expelling it.

The two Wood Spirits were not only skilled at healing injuries; expelling poison was also one of their specialties.

With both sides working together, barely half a cup of tea's time passed before Yang Kai opened his mouth and spat out a mouthful of blood. The blood was no longer its normal golden color, but instead glowed a sickly green. It was pungent and foul-smelling, and when it hit the ground, it hissed and sizzled.

Yang Kai slowly opened his eyes and moved his body slightly. Feeling that there was nothing seriously wrong, he recalled the scene of the ambush just now and still felt a lingering fear.
