## Chapter 3202 — Guarding Against a Thief

The palace was towering and magnificent, its grandeur almost flamboyant. Inside, Luan Feng sat motionless in the seat of honor, wearing a phoenix coronet and full ceremonial dress. Below her, a man paced back and forth, his face tight with anxious unease as he glanced outside from time to time, as though waiting for someone. He had a compact, well-honed build and wore a black robe, his dark hair loosely gathered into a bun behind his head with a jade hairpin. He was none other than Cang Gou, one of the three Saint Venerables of the Wild Ancient Land, the Western Saint Venerable.

The Western Saint Venerable had always been the sort of person whose expression would not change even if a mountain collapsed before him, yet now, for some reason, he looked deeply troubled and conflicted.

After watching him pace around like a headless fly for a while, Luan Feng finally lost patience. Rubbing her forehead, she said, "Sit down. You are making me dizzy."

Cang Gou stopped and looked at Luan Feng with grief and indignation. "Why has he come again? Tell me, why has he run over here again?"

Luan Feng said weakly, "If you are asking me, who am I supposed to ask?"

"Did we not hear that he went to the Northern Territory and established some... some Palace..."

"Lingxiao Palace!"

"Right, Lingxiao Palace!" Cang Gou slapped his palm. "I heard he was doing quite well in the Northern Territory. Instead of lording it over people there, what is he doing running to our Eastern Territory again?" He had never seen anyone like him, crossing territories as casually as eating and drinking, even treating the infamous Wild Ancient Land like a backyard he could stroll in and out of at will.

Luan Feng said mildly, "He says he came to settle accounts with me."

"Settle accounts? What accounts?" Cang Gou stared at her.

Luan Feng lowered her eyes. "Who knows? Perhaps he is just talking nonsense."

Cang Gou eyed her suspiciously. "Are you hiding something from us?"

Luan Feng said, "What could I possibly be hiding from you? But speaking of this, you did not leak the news, did you?"

Cang Gou scoffed. "I am not an idiot. How could I leak it?"

"Good. If those Yao Kings learn he is here..." She trailed off halfway, unable to continue. The mere thought of that scene made her shudder.

Cang Gou had clearly realized the same thing, his face filled with grief and indignation. They were the mighty Saint Venerables of the Wild Ancient Land, yet now they actually had to guard against a human at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm as if he were a thief, frightened and anxious every step of the way. If word got out, no one would believe it.

Since when had a human been allowed to act so outrageously in the Wild Ancient Land?

But that was the truth. Ever since that man had visited the Wild Ancient Land more than ten years ago and thrown the Ancient Land into turmoil, his name had spread throughout the entire place.

If the three Saint Venerables had not joined forces to suppress the matter, the Yao Kings under them would probably have set out to find him years ago.

Naturally, they did not want to find him for revenge. That man was the only person who could speak to Heaven's Punisher's descendant, so all of them wanted to serve him. They hoped that when the Blood Gate opened again, they could win Heaven's Punisher's descendant's favor and gain a chance to restore their ancestors' glory by transforming into Divine Spirits.

Even with the three Saint Venerables suppressing them all these years, the remaining twenty-nine Yao Kings had still kept making petitions, wanting to replace Yingfei, Xi Lei, and Xie Wuwei and serve at Yang Kai's side. Some had even proposed the absurd idea of taking shifts.

What kind of state of affairs was that? A group of lofty Yao Kings were actually scrambling to flatter and fawn over a human for that one faint, unreachable chance, even casting aside their dignity and the glory of being Yao Kings. The three Saint Venerables all found it deeply embarrassing.

Yet after thinking about it, it made sense. The Yao Kings' cultivation had already reached its limit. If they wanted to rise another step from there, they could only begin with their ancestral bloodlines. Most of the yao of the Wild Ancient Land carried the bloodlines of Ancient Divine Spirits, differing only in strength. If they could truly receive Heaven's Punisher's descendant's help, their chances of becoming Divine Spirits were still very high.

So although the three Saint Venerables were furious at their lack of backbone, they could not punish them. Fortunately, after more than a decade, the Yao Kings had gradually calmed down and had finally stopped stirring up trouble.

Luan Feng was now immensely grateful for the choice she had made back then. Destroying that cross-territory Space Array had indeed been far-sighted. Otherwise, if Yang Kai truly kept running to the Ancient Land every few days, the three Saint Venerables would soon become commanders without troops. With no subordinates left to command and all the Yao Kings defecting to the Northern Territory's Lingxiao Palace, that would be the real disgrace.

"What about the Yao Marshal who brought the message?" Cang Gou seemed to remember something, and alarm flashed through him. The news that Yang Kai had appeared in the Ancient Land again absolutely could not spread, or this quiet Ancient Land would likely fall into unrest once more.

"Locked up," Luan Feng replied. How could she fail to think of what Cang Gou had thought of? As soon as the twin-horned Yao Marshal came to report the news, she had placed him under house arrest to prevent the message from leaking.

"Good." Cang Gou let out a breath of relief.

"Have you prepared what I asked you to prepare?" Luan Feng lifted her eyes to him.

Cang Gou flipped his hand, and three Spatial Rings immediately appeared in his palm. "They are all here. Are we really going to spend money to avert disaster?"

"What else do you want to do?" Luan Feng knew perfectly well that since she had destroyed the Space Array Yang Kai had worked so hard to arrange back then, she would have to give him an explanation this time. The simplest and most effective method was naturally to spend money to avert disaster, but she could not be the only one paying. This concerned the entire Ancient Land, so the other two Saint Venerables would not get away either.

Cang Gou said, pained, "Our Ancient Land may be wealthy, but it cannot withstand him coming to plunder us every few days. Last time, he already carried off plenty of good things."

"They were only some stones and herbs. They were not of much use to the yao of my Ancient Land anyway, so if we gave them to him, then so be it. Or..." Luan Feng's beautiful eyes flashed with a cold light. "We can find a chance to get rid of him."

Cang Gou jumped in fright. "Absolutely not!"

What kind of insane idea was that? If they truly killed that man, how could Heaven's Punisher's descendant possibly let the matter rest? Shi Huo's death back then was still vivid in their minds, as if it had happened yesterday. Heaven's Punisher's descendant had taken Shi Huo's Source as easily as plucking something from a bag. She had slain a Divine Spirit as effortlessly as cutting vegetables, her might awe-inspiring. Who would dare provoke her?

She was the natural enemy of all Divine Spirits, like a cat to a mouse.

He had not even considered whether they could kill Yang Kai. If the three Saint Venerables truly set their minds to it, how could they fail to kill a mere cultivator at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm? The problem was simply this: how would they dare kill him?

"Fan Wu is here." Luan Feng suddenly raised her head and looked outside.

A tall, slender figure entered from outside and landed before them. He wore a feather crown and a well-fitted green robe, but his face was as black as the bottom of a pot...

He looked at Cang Gou, then at Luan Feng. "He came again?"

Luan Feng nodded slightly.

Fan Wu let out a long sigh. "A calamity!"

He had never imagined that a mere human could throw the entire Wild Ancient Land into turmoil. If that man's strength were truly overwhelming, then so be it. If their skills were inferior, they would accept defeat. Divine Spirits were formidable, but they were not without opponents. Yet that man's strength was not especially impressive, and he did not even intend to make an enemy of the Ancient Land. Even so, his very existence had already endangered the peace of the entire Ancient Land.

When had the Saint Venerables of the Wild Ancient Land ever suffered such frustration? They could not fight him, and they dared not kill him. Fan Wu finally understood the saying that good people die young while scourges live for a thousand years.

It was talking about people like Yang Kai.

"Where is he now?"

Luan Feng shook her head. "I am not sure. I only know that he suddenly appeared in the Ancient Land yesterday, ran into a Yao Marshal, and ordered him to deliver a message, saying he would come find me."

"No other yao saw him?" Fan Wu's heart leapt into his throat.

"Probably not." Luan Feng frowned. "Otherwise, the Yao Kings below would not be so quiet. You also know that fellow is proficient in the Laws of Space and appears and disappears unpredictably. If he really wanted to hide his movements, those Yao Kings probably would not notice him."

If the news truly spread, those Yao Kings who had long been restless would probably pour out in full force.

"Good, good." Fan Wu finally relaxed.

"What should we do now? Wait here for him to come to us?" Cang Gou looked from Luan Feng to Fan Wu.

"The key now is to find out where he is. Ideally, we should find him before he comes here and stop him from wandering around the Ancient Land."

Luan Feng nodded. "That makes sense." She had been so furious with Yang Kai that she had not thought that far for a moment. Once Fan Wu said it, her thoughts cleared. Light flickered in her beautiful eyes as she said, "Perhaps I know where he is."

She looked up, and she and Fan Wu spoke at the same time. "Stone Puppets!"

Yang Kai's destination was not hard to guess. After all, within the Ancient Land, the only people he had a good relationship with were the Stone Puppet Clan. Since he had appeared in the Ancient Land and had not gone to seek out any of the three Saint Venerables, there was only one place he could have gone.

Although they could not be certain, they were still fairly confident.

Since they had reached a decision, the three Saint Venerables hesitated no longer and immediately set out together, speeding toward the Stone Puppet Clan's territory.

Since they could not wait for him to come to them, they could only take the initiative.

In the entire Wild Ancient Land, the Stone Puppet Clan held an extremely special position because they were not under the Saint Venerables' jurisdiction and could be considered a free clan.

It was not that Luan Feng and the others had never thought of subduing the Stone Puppet Clan. These big fellows, however, were not weak, and their tempers were terribly stubborn. In several conflicts many years ago, though Luan Feng and the others gained some advantage, they still failed to do anything to the Stone Puppet Clan, much less subdue them.

Later, after discovering that the Stone Puppet Clan only moved within a range of ten thousand li and had never harmed their interests, they stopped paying attention to them. Still, there remained some friction between the two sides.

If not for Yang Kai this time, they would not have been willing to go to the Stone Puppet Clan's territory lightly.

Inside the tree house, Yang Kai looked with satisfaction at the Space Array before him.

Arranging this Space Array had gone more smoothly than any previous attempt. Most obviously, the time required had been cut by more than half.

This was not because his strength had improved. The greater reason was that his attainments in the Laws of Space had advanced considerably.

Becoming the Master of a Star Domain, using the entire Star Domain as a carrier to activate the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, and devouring the Great Wilderness Star Domain had brought him benefits not limited to an immeasurable Starry Sky. He had also gained a deeper comprehension of space.

The changes in the Xuan Realm Pearl and the expansion of the Small Xuan Realm likewise involved all manner of spatial mysteries. He had not deliberately cultivated Space Divine Abilities, yet he had gained tremendously without realizing it. His comprehension of the Laws of Space had climbed to another level, and he instinctively felt as if he had touched some profound secret. His entire view had become somewhat different. Though the surrounding space was nothingness, in his eyes its patterns were distinct, with traces he could follow. He stood there, his mind immersed in an extraordinarily mysterious insight, unable to pull himself free for a long time.
