## Chapter 3323 — For You to Decide

"Boy Yang, the old fellow really does seem intent on following you. What do you think?" Jiu Feng looked at Yang Kai with a bright smile, waggling her brows as she said, "Having a Divine Spirit acknowledge you as master counts as a stroke of fortune too."

If anyone else encountered such a thing, who would still care whether Qiong Qi's reputation was good or evil? Most likely, they would be beside themselves with joy and agree without a second thought. Yang Kai merely snorted, utterly unimpressed. "It is not as if I do not already have one."

What do you have? Qiong Qi's eyes widened.

Jiu Feng, however, suddenly thought of Liu Yan and realized he had a point. To others, this was a chance that might not come once in ten thousand years, but Yang Kai already had Liu Yan. The Phoenix Race ranked above Qiong Qi, and while Liu Yan's current strength might not match Qiong Qi's, her potential for growth was far greater. To Yang Kai, having Qiong Qi acknowledge him as master might truly hold little appeal. Besides, anyone could see that Qiong Qi was only trying to borrow Yang Kai's influence. Whether his wish to acknowledge a master was sincere or not was impossible to say.

If they protected Qiong Qi now, only for him to turn on his master and devour him later, would that not be bringing trouble on themselves?

Jiu Feng turned to Qiong Qi. "You heard him. Be good and come back to Spirit Beast Island with us."

Qiong Qi's expression turned grave. "This old man does not wish to fight you."

Jiu Feng said mildly, "As if I want to."

Although Li Wuyi said nothing, the resolve in his eyes made it clear that if they truly came to blows, he would show no mercy.

Qiong Qi suddenly turned his head to look at Yang Kai, and his Divine Sense surged.

Yang Kai had just turned aside to admire the scenery and keep himself out of the matter when his expression suddenly shifted. He looked back at Qiong Qi, and his Divine Sense began to surge as well.

The two of them actually began communicating with Divine Sense right in front of Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng. Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng looked from one to the other, then exchanged a glance, unsure whether to laugh or cry.

Although they did not know exactly what the two were discussing, they could guess that Qiong Qi was certainly negotiating with Yang Kai about recognizing him as master, and that he must have offered some condition tempting enough to move Yang Kai.

From the corner of their eyes, the two could clearly see Yang Kai's expression changing again and again, first surprised, then shocked, then delighted, and finally restless and agitated for quite some time.

In the end, Yang Kai's expression settled, and he fell silent. Qiong Qi stared at him without looking away for even an instant.

After another moment, Yang Kai let out a long sigh, looked at Qiong Qi, and said, "Old Qiong, you win."

Qiong Qi grinned. "Many thanks, Young Master."

"No need to thank me yet." This time, Yang Kai no longer objected to the way Qiong Qi addressed him. Instead, he turned to Li Wuyi and said, "Senior, if I stand guarantor for him, may I keep this old bastard by my side so he need not go to Spirit Beast Island? Senior and Lord Beast Great Emperor may rest assured. I will keep strict watch over him and will absolutely not let him commit any wicked deeds."

Li Wuyi frowned. "You truly want to vouch for him?"

Yang Kai chuckled. "He told me something that interests me greatly. I find it rather hard to refuse."

Li Wuyi had guessed as much. He simply could not understand what Qiong Qi had said to Yang Kai just now to change Yang Kai's attitude so drastically. A moment ago, Yang Kai had looked utterly disgusted and resistant, yet now he was taking the initiative to vouch for him.

Jiu Feng said solemnly, "Qiong Qi is not like Liu Yan. Can you keep him under control?" Liu Yan had followed Yang Kai from the time she was weak and insignificant. It could even be said that Yang Kai had shaped Liu Yan with his own hands, helping her grow and awakening her spiritual wisdom. Liu Yan's loyalty to Yang Kai was beyond question, but Qiong Qi was different. His evil reputation was notorious, and his strength was formidable. She feared Yang Kai would fail to cow him and end up suffering for it instead.

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Since I say so, I naturally have a way to keep him in check." Turning to Qiong Qi, he said, "I will not hide it from you either. That little girl you saw back then has already awakened her bloodline and begun inheriting the power of her distant ancestor. So if you have any other ideas, you had best dismiss them now, lest you get slaughtered by accident."

Qiong Qi was badly startled. "It has already awakened?" Although Yang Kai had not said who he meant, how could Qiong Qi fail to know which little girl he was talking about? Back then, it was precisely because he had sensed Heaven's Punisher's aura that he had been so ingratiating toward Zhang Ruoxi. He had not expected her to awaken so quickly. According to what he had observed then, that girl should have needed at least several hundred years to awaken. For it to happen so quickly could only mean that some unexpected change had occurred in the meantime.

He cupped his fists solemnly. "Young Master, rest assured. Since you are that person's successor, this old man will naturally follow close behind you and serve you like a loyal hound and steed. I would never dare harbor second thoughts."

Yang Kai looked at Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng with a smile. "What do the two Seniors think?" After a pause, he continued, "Of course, if the two of you insist on taking him back to Spirit Beast Island, then so be it."

Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng looked at each other, both considering the same question.

What exactly was the situation with the little girl Yang Kai had mentioned? Why did Qiong Qi seem frightened when he heard news of her? In this world, besides a Great Emperor, who else could frighten him? And Yang Kai had mentioned a bloodline awakening...

Vaguely, both of them seemed to realize something. A sharp light flashed through Li Wuyi's eyes, while Jiu Feng shivered. She desperately wanted to ask Yang Kai about that little girl's exact situation, yet she was afraid to open her mouth.

Only after a long while did Li Wuyi say, "Earlier, I said you were half a member of Spirit Beast Island. Now that you have come forward as guarantor and wish to keep him, if that is truly the case, it can be counted as him remaining under Spirit Beast Island's supervision, so it does not conflict with the purpose of our trip. However, this matter still has to be put to my Lord."

Yang Kai nodded. "Of course. In that case, let us make a trip to Spirit Beast Island first."

With Li Wuyi here, he originally had no need to go to Spirit Beast Island anymore, but since they had to ask Beast Great Emperor, he could only make the extra trip.

Li Wuyi smiled faintly and said, "There is no need to go to so much trouble."

As he spoke, he reached out and took out a jade slip. His Divine Sense surged as he poured a message into it, then he flicked his finger and gave a low shout, "Go."

The tiny jade slip shot away with a whoosh, plunged into the void, and vanished in the blink of an eye.

Yang Kai's eyes went round, and he asked with keen interest, "Senior, can that move of yours send a message back to Spirit Beast Island?" Just now, Li Wuyi had clearly used Space Force to send the inscribed jade slip back to Spirit Beast Island, but how far away was this place from there? Even with a Space Divine Ability, how could it be delivered? And more importantly, how had he locked onto the destination?

For a moment, curiosity gnawed at him, and he wanted to properly seek Li Wuyi's guidance.

Li Wuyi smiled. "It is only a subtle application of the Laws of Space. I will explain it to you in detail later. With your present attainments, learning it should not be difficult."

Yang Kai grinned. "Many thanks, Senior."

Li Wuyi raised a hand. "In this whole world, quite a few people may cultivate the Laws of Space, but very few are proficient to the degree that you and I are. Being able to discuss it with you will benefit me as well."

"Senior is too modest. It should be this Junior learning from you," Yang Kai said solemnly.

Jiu Feng curled her lip. "Is it fun, standing there flattering each other?"

Then Qiong Qi spoke proudly from the side. "The Laws of Space are certainly profound, but compared to the Laws of Time, they are still slightly inferior."

Yang Kai immediately looked askance at him. "You understand the Laws of Time?"

Qiong Qi's face twitched, and he said awkwardly, "I do not." He was merely the mount of the Flowing Time Great Emperor. Although he had followed the Flowing Time Great Emperor for many years and had witnessed the majesty of the Flowing Time Great Emperor displaying the Laws of Time, he had no attainments at all in this Dao.

Yang Kai said, "Law Power depends on the person. Each has its merits. How could there be any clear distinction of strong and weak?"

Li Wuyi's eyes lit up. "Well said. Even the mightiest Law still depends on who wields it."

As they spoke, Li Wuyi's expression suddenly shifted. He reached out and beckoned, and a jade slip broke through the air, landing between his fingers. Clearly, a reply had come from Spirit Beast Island.

So fast!

Only a dozen breaths or so had passed from start to finish. Yang Kai guessed that Li Wuyi's jade slip must have been sent directly into Beast Great Emperor's hands, which was the only reason a reply could arrive so quickly.

Qiong Qi immediately tensed. He stared fixedly at Li Wuyi without looking away for an instant, secretly deciding that the moment Li Wuyi showed the slightest sign that something was wrong, he would bolt.

No matter what, he refused to be confined on Spirit Beast Island.

But no matter how long he watched, Li Wuyi's expression remained as indifferent as ever, without the slightest hint of anything unusual. Qiong Qi's anxiety rose and fell, and he had no idea what Beast Great Emperor's attitude was.

Only after quite a while did Li Wuyi finish reading it. He glanced at Qiong Qi, then looked at Yang Kai and said, "My Lord says... this matter is for you to decide."

As soon as those words came out, Qiong Qi breathed a sigh of relief, then immediately fixed a nervous gaze on Yang Kai. Since the decision now rested in Yang Kai's hands, everything depended on whether his proposal had worked.

It should have had an effect... Qiong Qi secretly prayed.

"For me to decide." Yang Kai rubbed his chin and chuckled. "The Great Emperor is truly giving me face."

Li Wuyi smiled. "My Lord says that since you have some connection with Old Qiong, you are the most suitable person to watch over him. If he truly goes back on his word, it will not be too late for Spirit Beast Island to step in then."

"Good." Yang Kai nodded. "Since the Great Emperor shows this Junior such favor, then I will respectfully accept."

He turned to Qiong Qi. "Old Qiong, from now on, you will stay by my side. You heard the Great Emperor's words as well. From now on, do more good deeds and clear your evil name as soon as possible. If you dare commit evil, then even without the Great Emperor acting, this Young Master will not spare you."

"Yes!" Qiong Qi answered respectfully, then suddenly put on a bitter expression. "Young Master, about this name, Old Qiong... could it perhaps be changed?"

"Changed to what? Old Qi?"

Qiong Qi said, "Not quite, but it is not far off. Back then, Old Master called this old man Xiaoqi. Young Master, look, the Qi in my name and the qi in Xiaoqi sound the same, so if Young Master does not mind, you may call this old man Xiaoqi... Hm? Why are all of you making that face?"

Across from him, Yang Kai, Li Wuyi, and Jiu Feng all looked at him strangely.

Yang Kai's face twitched, and he waved a hand in disgust. "Old Qiong is a perfectly good name. There is no need to change it."

Of all the names he could choose, he actually wanted to be called Xiaoqi...

Mo Huang's daughter, Spirit Beast Island's little princess, was also called Xiaoqi. The same name belonged to an innocent, lively little girl on one hand, and to this coarse-faced old bastard on the other. How could Yang Kai possibly bring himself to call him that? Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng clearly had the same thought.
