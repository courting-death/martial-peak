## Chapter 3263 — How Can You Catch a Tiger Cub Without Entering the Tiger's Den?

"Run!" Xiao Baiyi suddenly forced out the last of his strength and roared. The violent movement tore at his wounds, and the Emperor Yuan protecting his body immediately scattered. Boundless Demonic Qi rushed in through his mouth, nose, and injuries, pushing him to the brink of demonification in the span of a breath.

A howl of agony tore from Xiao Baiyi's throat. He fought to hold on to a single thread of clarity, his eyes trembling violently as pitch black slowly overtook them. Just before that darkness swallowed his pupils completely, a bitter smile crossed his face. Then he drove his strength with no regard for his life.

His body swelled, then exploded with a boom. Shredded flesh and blood sprayed in every direction, as if a flower watered in blood had bloomed beneath the great tree.

Yang Kai threw back his head and howled, the sound brimming with grief and rage. Emperor Yuan surged violently through him, and his eyes turned blood-red.

The howl cut off abruptly. He swept a cold gaze around him, his voice dark and chilling. "Lei Gu, if I don't tear you into ten thousand pieces, I swear I am no man!"

The moment the words fell, Lei Gu's loud laughter rang beside his ears.

Yang Kai was furious. Light flared from the Myriad Sword, and one ribbon-like sword light after another slashed out in all directions, cutting into the darkness and vanishing. As the sword lights poured out, his emotions grew more and more unstable, and his eyes grew redder and redder, until he was like a volcano on the verge of eruption, terrifying to behold.

"Senior Brother Yang..." A soft, uncertain call came from behind him.

Yang Kai did not even look. He turned and slashed out, and a zhang-long sword light tore through the newcomer's protective Emperor Yuan with crushing ease, cutting straight through.

He glared over and saw Murong Xiaoxiao, drenched in blood and in a wretched state, staring at him in a daze. Her beautiful eyes were filled with disbelief. A line of blood appeared from between her brows down to her chin, and with a boom, she split into two halves. Blood and organs spilled across the ground, and her life was extinguished in an instant.

Yang Kai's breathing was heavy. His blood-red eyes froze for a moment, but only for a moment.

Black Qi churned around him and slipped silently into his body, yet he seemed completely unaware. Before long, his entire body radiated an eerie Demonic Qi.

Lei Gu's voice sounded again. "How does it feel to slaughter your fellow disciples? How does it feel to fall into the Demon Dao?"

Yang Kai ignored him, mechanically swinging the Myriad Sword in his hand.

One figure after another, some familiar and some not, walked out from the surroundings, only to be mercilessly cleaved in two by Yang Kai. Within ten steps, every person was an enemy. Soon, the ground was piled with layer upon layer of corpses.

No one knew how much time had passed before Yang Kai finally stood there panting, propping himself up with the Myriad Sword. Black Qi coiled around his entire body, and his eyes had turned pitch black. Even with his cultivation at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, he could not sustain such unrestrained expenditure of power for long.

In the darkness, two figures stood silently, watching Yang Kai's position through the dense Demonic Qi. They were none other than Lei Gu and Feng Ming.

"That should be enough, shouldn't it?" Feng Ming asked.

Lei Gu frowned, still not quite certain. From the look of things, there should not be any serious problem, but out of caution, he slowly shook his head. "He carries the Dragon Clan bloodline and has a natural resistance to Demonic Qi. It is better to be careful. Wait a little longer."

Feng Ming said, "It is not as though no member of the Dragon Clan has ever been demonized. Besides, his Dragon Clan bloodline is hardly pure. If there had been no flaw in his state of mind, he might have been able to resist for a time, but unintentionally killing his fellow disciples caused his Heart Demon to surge. Now he is surrounded by this boundless Demonic Qi as well. With pressure from within and without, lasting this long is already remarkable. In my view, he has completely fallen."

Lei Gu nodded. "That is true..." Even so, he was still hesitant.

Feng Ming volunteered, "I will go take a look. This man is rare. If we can make use of him, it will be a great merit in the future."

"Be careful!" Lei Gu warned.

Feng Ming nodded, then moved toward Yang Kai with a flicker of his figure. He did not let down his guard. Although he was almost certain that Yang Kai had already been demonized, he remained wary, his Demon Yuan quietly surging as he prepared to retreat at any moment.

As he walked, the boundless Demonic Qi parted of its own accord, opening a passage and revealing the scene shrouded in darkness.

They were only ten zhang apart. Feng Ming carefully examined Yang Kai's condition while slowly advancing, and before long he stood three chi in front of him. Without warning, one hand suddenly curled into a claw and seized toward Yang Kai's chest. Fierce energy overflowed from his fingers, faintly whistling through the air, clearly carrying formidable power.

With a sharp hiss, his claw tips sank three cun into Yang Kai's chest, and fresh blood sprayed out.

Yang Kai remained completely motionless, as if he felt no pain at all.

Seeing this, Feng Ming could not help chuckling. He was certain now: Yang Kai really had been demonized. Of course he had. After slaughtering so many people without hesitation, including several of his "fellow disciples," how could he resist the corrosion of Demonic Qi once his Heart Demon surged? From the outside, the boy was indeed wrapped in Demonic Qi. That move just now had only been the final test.

If he had merely been pretending to be demonized, he would certainly have reacted to that fatal strike. His complete lack of resistance meant the matter was settled.

On the other side, Lei Gu relaxed when he saw this. At last, he had something to report. The affair in the Southern Territory had fallen short at the final step, and he still had not known how he would explain it to his superiors. But as long as he could capture Yang Kai now, it would be enough to offset this failure completely.

This man was proficient in the Power of Space and possessed some Dragon Clan bloodline. He would be of great use in the future.

Feng Ming had already withdrawn his hand. Blood immediately poured from Yang Kai's chest, yet Yang Kai's expression stayed wooden, as if he noticed nothing. Feng Ming circled him once and examined him carefully, then nodded and said, "Good, good, good."

Standing in front of Yang Kai again, he suddenly took a pitch-black box from his robes. No one knew what material it was made from; it was neither metal nor jade. When he opened it, an object resembling a seed appeared inside. The seed was also pitch black and only the size of a mung bean, yet it pulsed with a clear Divine Soul fluctuation, as if it were not a seed but a person.

Feng Ming reached out, pinched the seed between his fingers, and slowly pressed it toward the spot between Yang Kai's brows.

The instant the two touched, the pitch-black seed stuck to Yang Kai's forehead. As if alive, it burrowed hard into his head and vanished in the blink of an eye.

At the same time, inside Yang Kai's Sea of Consciousness, a figure appeared. It was wrapped in Black Qi, twisting constantly, its features impossible to make out.

"So this is how you seize other people's bodies?" a voice suddenly said.

The black shadow was startled. It turned toward the voice and saw Yang Kai's Spirit Body hovering quietly in the air above the Sea of Consciousness, holding a long saber in a reverse grip as he smiled. That smile was full of ridicule and mockery.

"You have not fallen!" a hoarse voice rasped, impossible to distinguish as male or female, yet filled with disbelief.

Seeing Yang Kai's Spirit Body, how could it not realize that it and the others had been deceived? If Yang Kai had truly been demonized, his Spirit Body would have been demonized as well. It would only have needed to devour Yang Kai's Spirit Body to occupy his physical body.

How was this possible? The state of Yang Kai’s flesh body clearly showed that he had completely become demonized. The fact that he had been eroded by Demonic Qi was not false, so how could he still remain clear-headed? He had even beaten them at their own game and waited here for him.

More than that, he had not resisted Feng Ming's strike at all just now. How could he be sure Feng Ming would not take the opportunity to kill him? He was truly audacious beyond measure!

"How can you catch a tiger cub without entering the tiger's den?" Yang Kai grinned at it, baring a mouthful of savage fangs.

The black shadow swayed, transformed into a cloud of black mist, and tried to flee from Yang Kai's Sea of Consciousness.

But in this world, Yang Kai was the ruler. Entering was easy; leaving required Yang Kai's consent, unless one had enough strength to tear through the Sea of Consciousness's defenses by force. The black shadow was not weak, but it was only a Demonic Intent. Without the support of a physical body, it could not bring out its full power, so how could it break through the Sea of Consciousness's defenses? For a time, it was like a fierce beast trapped in a cage, charging left and right without finding an exit, unable even to send a message to the outside world.

Yang Kai pointed the Soul-Severing Blade at it from afar, his expression cold.

Locked onto by that aura, the black shadow clearly felt disaster looming over it, and although it had no physical body, a chill ran through its entire being. It was decisive as well. Knowing it could not escape inside another person's Sea of Consciousness, it simply turned and charged at Yang Kai, taking on the posture of one determined to perish together with him.

"Sever!" As the low shout rang out, the Soul-Severing Blade trembled lightly and turned into a blade light, slashing head-on into the black shadow's body.

A shrill scream rang out. The charging black shadow was split in two, then burst apart and vanished without a trace.

After dealing with the Demonic Intent that had invaded his Sea of Consciousness, Yang Kai's expression turned solemn, and his Spirit Body withdrew from the Sea of Consciousness.

In the darkness, Yang Kai opened his eyes and moved his pitch-black pupils. A faint trace of expression appeared on his face, which had long been stiff.

Feng Ming stood before him with his hands clasped behind his back. Seeing this, he nodded and said, "Let's go."

He showed not the slightest suspicion and simply turned to leave. Yang Kai stared at his back, itching to stab him to death with a single sword strike, but he held himself back.

Passing through the dense Demonic Qi, the two soon arrived before Lei Gu. Feng Ming smiled and said, "It is done."

Lei Gu was all smiles as well, looking Yang Kai over and nodding again and again.

Yang Kai cupped his fists. "My lord."

The smile on Lei Gu's face instantly froze, and Feng Ming's expression also turned stunned as he looked back.

Seeing this, Yang Kai's heart skipped. He keenly sensed that he seemed to have given himself away. What could be wrong with simply saying "my lord"? Before that thought had finished forming, Lei Gu and Feng Ming suddenly split left and right, shooting backward.

Yang Kai made his decision at once. As he activated the Laws of Space, the surrounding space instantly solidified, and at the same time, two Moon Blades slashed toward Lei Gu and Feng Ming. Everything happened too suddenly, leaving him no time to prepare a more powerful killing move.

Crack, crack. Lei Gu was worthy of being a Third Stage Emperor Realm expert. Relying solely on his own great strength, he shattered the solidified space. His movement was only slightly hindered, and by a hair's breadth, he avoided the Moon Blade strike.
