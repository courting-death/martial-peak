## Chapter 3345 — I Want to Hatch It

“Never mind how I know. Answer my question first.” Yang Kai looked down at her.

Fu Ling thought for a moment. “It was many years ago, but the Second Elder did indeed bear a child.”

“What happened?”

Fu Ling replied sadly, “Apparently, its Origin was insufficient. It never hatched and died in the egg.”

“Its Origin was insufficient?” Yang Kai frowned. What did that mean?

Fu Ling explained, “Although the Dragon Clan is blessed by the Heavens, with formidable cultivation and strength from birth, we are still Divine Spirits and depend upon our Origins to exist. Every Dragon has a Dragon Clan Origin of their own, including every newborn. Without a strong enough Origin, a child cannot be born. The whole thing is rather strange, now that I think about it. The Great Elder and Second Elder are both Tenth-Tier Great Dragons, so there is no reason their child's Origin should have been insufficient, yet somehow, it was.”

“So that's what happened...” Yang Kai fell into thought, then suddenly sneered. “That wench must have committed so many evil deeds that the Heavens decided to punish her. The Heavenly Way turns in cycles, and in the end, no one escapes Heaven's judgment!”

“W-wench?” Fu Ling stared at him in shock. “Brother-in-law, you couldn't mean... the Second Elder, could you?”

“Who else could it be but that wench?” Yang Kai scowled.

Fu Ling slowly sank into the water until only half her head remained above the surface, staring timidly at Yang Kai. Only now did she realize just how much nerve he had. If the Second Elder ever heard what he had just said, there would be hell to pay.

What she did not know was that Yang Kai had cursed Fu Zhun to her face before. A few curses behind her back were nothing by comparison.

After a moment's hesitation, she said softly, “Actually, the Second Elder may have been somewhat cold before this happened, but her temper wasn't nearly as bad. She changed a great deal afterward.”

“Hm?” Yang Kai raised a brow. “So this incident dealt her a heavy blow?”

“A devastating one!” Fu Ling nodded emphatically. “After all the difficulty she and the Great Elder went through to have a child, it died in the egg because its Origin was insufficient. You can imagine what that did to her. Ever since then, she seems to have been desperate for a new member of the Dragon Clan to be born. That's why she took it upon herself to betroth Elder Sister Qing to Fu Chi back then.”

“She couldn't do it herself, so she pinned her hopes on someone else? How twisted!” Yang Kai curled his lip.

“I also heard that the Second Elder would have let the matter go if the child born to the Beast Great Emperor and the Third Elder had been a pure-blooded member of the Dragon Clan. But Mo Xiaoqi doesn't have so much as a trace of Dragon Clan blood. That enraged the Second Elder, so she banished the Third Elder to the Dragon Tomb.”

Yang Kai had no interest in any of that. “What happened afterward?”

“What do you mean, afterward?” Fu Ling gave him a puzzled look.

“The Second Elder laid a bad egg. What happened after that?”

Fu Ling smiled bitterly. “There was no afterward. They tried for years, but the dragon egg never showed any sign of hatching, so naturally they gave up hope. The Great Elder also felt deeply guilty toward the Second Elder over the matter, so he has always indulged her over the years... Brother-in-law, why are you asking about all this?”

“I'm just asking casually. It's nothing.” Yang Kai waved his hand. “Carry on. Pretend I was never here.”

With that, his figure flickered and he flew off, vanishing in the blink of an eye.

Fu Ling glared resentfully after him, then slapped the water, sending up a shower of spray. Gritting her teeth, she muttered, “That damn man!” Then she turned and resumed her leisurely swim around the lake.

That thing really was Fu Zhun and Zhu Yan's dragon egg!

Yang Kai raced onward, his mind in turmoil. This might... be the opening he needed!

The island lay in ruins, scarred everywhere by the great battle that had reduced its magnificent palace to crumbling walls and rubble.

This was the Spirit Island of the deceased Eighth-Tier Thunder Dragon, Fu Chi. Yang Kai had been here before. Countless cultivators had once been brought here under guard to build Fu Chi's palace, but the great battle had left the island all but destroyed and its magnificent residence a thing of the past.

Yang Kai landed and found a palace that was still relatively intact. He stepped inside, cleared the area, then waved his hand and summoned the pitch-black bad egg before him.

Stroking his chin, Yang Kai circled the bad egg several times, his eyes gleaming with determination.

Just then, a figure streaked in from the distance and landed beside Yang Kai. Cupping his fists, the newcomer greeted him. “Young Master.”

“Mm.” Yang Kai merely nodded, unsurprised. On the way here, he had sent Qiong Qi a message telling him to come. Qiong Qi had been wandering around Dragon Isle, and Yang Kai had not bothered notifying him when he pretended to leave, since he had always intended to return.

He had only called Qiong Qi over now because two heads were better than one. The old fellow had lived for a great many years and could often offer useful advice.

“Young Master, why did you summon this old man—” Qiong Qi broke off with a sharp hiss. His eyes went wide as he stared at the pitch-black bad egg. “Th-This...”

“This what? Stop stammering and spit it out.” Yang Kai shot him a sidelong glance.

Qiong Qi swallowed. “Young Master, is this... a dragon egg?” He whispered the last two words as if afraid someone might overhear him.

“Sharp eyes, Old Qiong. You recognized it as a dragon egg at a glance.” Yang Kai gave him a thumbs-up. He himself had only confirmed it after a careful examination, yet Qiong Qi had known the instant he saw it. Age and experience really did count for something.

Qiong Qi's expression became quite a sight. “Young Master, this old man has something to say, though I do not know whether I should say it.”

“If you have something to say, say it. If you have a fart, let it out. You look like a vicious fiend, so why put on this refined act? Isn't it exhausting?”

Qiong Qi's face darkened, but he answered in all seriousness, “This old man may not know where Young Master stole this dragon egg from, but you had better return it at once. If the Dragon Clan finds out, we're finished.”

"How can you be sure I stole it?" Yang Kai looked at him askance.

If you didn't steal it, did you lay it yourself? Qiong Qi grumbled inwardly. I may be old, but I'm not senile.

“Then dare I ask where this dragon egg came from, Young Master?”

“I found it!” Yang Kai declared with a perfectly straight face.

As if I'd believe that! They were on Dragon Isle, a dragon egg was sitting right in front of him, and Yang Kai actually claimed he had simply found it...

“Enough nonsense. I didn't call you here to discuss that.” Yang Kai raised a hand to cut him off. “This Young Master has a matter on which I need your advice. I trust you'll tell me everything you know and hold nothing back.”

Qiong Qi looked deeply troubled. “Does it concern this dragon egg?”

“That's right!”

Qiong Qi let out a long sigh, feeling as though Yang Kai had dragged him straight into a pit of fire. He regretted his choice more than ever. Had he known this would happen, he would have returned to Spirit Beast Island with Jiu Feng and Li Wuyi. His freedom might have been somewhat restricted there, but at least he would never have found himself mixed up in something like this.

But no, he had acknowledged this fellow as his Young Master, and there had not been a single peaceful day since. It was bad enough that Yang Kai had come to Dragon Isle to throw his weight around; now he had even stolen a dragon egg!

If the Dragon Clan learned of this, master and servant alike would probably be chopped into ten thousand pieces.

“To tell you the truth, this dragon egg is the offspring of the Dragon Clan's Great Elder and Second Elder.”

The moment Yang Kai said this, Qiong Qi's features twitched.

Any dragon egg was already an incredibly serious matter, but this one actually belonged to the Dragon Clan's Great Elder and Second Elder! What are you trying to do? Even if you want to die, you don't have to go about it this way! And... if this was the two Elders' dragon egg, how had Yang Kai managed to steal it? Qiong Qi could not make any sense of it.

“They say this dragon egg is dead, but I don't believe them.” Yang Kai shook his head.

“This is a dead egg?” Qiong Qi asked, startled.

Yang Kai shrugged. “That's what they all say.”

“Who?” Qiong Qi pressed.

“Don't ask so many questions. In any case, they were wrong. The vitality in this dragon egg may be weak, but there is still a glimmer of hope.” As he spoke, Yang Kai pressed a hand against the dragon egg and jerked his head at Qiong Qi. “Come and feel it.”

His curiosity aroused, Qiong Qi came over as instructed, placed a hand on the dragon egg, and carefully probed it with his senses.

The Dragon Clan had already declared it a dead egg, so what made Yang Kai think it still possessed any vitality?

“Well? Did you feel it? There's a faint pulse of life every now and then.” Yang Kai looked eagerly at Qiong Qi.

Qiong Qi gave him a strange look, then slowly shook his head. He had felt nothing at all.

Yang Kai frowned in exasperation. “Probe it more carefully.”

Helpless, Qiong Qi concentrated and tried again.

“There! Again!” Yang Kai suddenly exclaimed.

Qiong Qi's eyelid twitched. He badly wanted to ask whether Yang Kai was toying with him. What pulse of life? When he probed the dragon egg, he found only deathly stillness within. It could not possibly have been any deader.

Yet Yang Kai sounded utterly certain, and he had no reason to lie. Qiong Qi could hardly admit that he had sensed nothing. That would mean he was inferior to Yang Kai! He hurriedly nodded. “Indeed, indeed. There really is the faintest pulse.”

Yang Kai withdrew his hand and burst out laughing. “You felt it too, didn't you? But if it still has a glimmer of life, why does the Dragon Clan insist it's a dead egg?”

Qiong Qi stroked his chin and thought for a moment. “Perhaps its vitality is simply too weak?”

Yang Kai nodded. “That would make sense.” A Dragon's vitality should have been incomparably powerful. A spark this faint was practically no vitality at all.

“What does Young Master plan to do?” Qiong Qi asked. Yang Kai's fascination with a dead egg boded ill, and Qiong Qi could not help worrying.

Yang Kai took a deep breath and said earnestly, “I want to hatch it!”

“What?” Qiong Qi stared at him, dumbfounded.

“I want to hatch it!” Yang Kai repeated.

The corners of Qiong Qi's mouth twitched. He wanted to dissuade Yang Kai, but after thinking better of it, he asked instead, “Then may I ask what Young Master hopes to achieve?”

“Naturally, I'll use it to threaten that wench Fu Zhun and keep her in check.” Yang Kai chuckled coldly, a scheming gleam lighting his face. “This is her child with Zhu Yan. If I manage to hatch it, won't she have to obey my every word from then on? If I tell her to kneel, she'll kneel. If I tell her to stand, she'll stand... Hahahaha!” Yang Kai threw back his head and laughed wildly, while Qiong Qi's heart sank lower and lower.
