## Chapter 3322 — Qiong Qi? Qiong Qi!

At this point, even the Beast Great Emperor was out of options. If Fu Xuan had been willing, he could have forcibly taken her away, but Fu Xuan herself did not want his relationship with Dragon Isle to become too strained. What else could he do? In the end, he could only leave in low spirits.

Before he left, Mo Huang declared that if Fu Xuan suffered the slightest grievance, he would wash Dragon Isle in blood.

After Li Wuyi finished speaking, he suddenly realized something and asked, "You intend to go to Dragon Isle again?"

Yang Kai said, "Naturally. I came to the Eastern Territory this time for two reasons. First, to ask Senior for guidance on some questions about the Laws of Space, and second, to make a trip to Dragon Isle and bring Zhu Qing out."

At those words, Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng both stared at him as if he were a monster.

"Is... there a problem?" Yang Kai looked between them.

Jiu Feng chuckled. "No problem, no problem. I wish you swift success." That was what she said, but she truly had no confidence in Yang Kai's trip. Even her own Lord had been unable to bring the Mistress out, so how could Yang Kai possibly do it?

Yang Kai nodded lightly and said, "Before that, though, this Junior has one more matter to ask the two Seniors about."

Li Wuyi said, "Speak freely. Although yesterday's words were meant to raise your standing, one part of them was not false. As you are now, you can be considered half a member of Spirit Beast Island. Whether you admit it or not, your ties to my Spirit Beast Island run deep. Do you understand that?"

If something happened on Spirit Beast Island's side, Liu Yan definitely would not stand by and do nothing. Since Liu Yan could not ignore it, Yang Kai naturally could not ignore it either. Put another way, if something happened on Yang Kai's side, Spirit Beast Island would not ignore it either. The two sides had long since formed bonds that could not be easily separated.

Yang Kai nodded and smiled faintly. "This Junior understands. It is precisely because I understand that I have not been overly polite with the two Seniors."

"Good."

Yang Kai pointed to the side and asked, "What the hell is with this old fellow?"

The flashy old man's face twitched. "Young Master, I am not a ghost!"

Yang Kai ignored him. "The two Seniors said you came for him and want to bring him back to Spirit Beast Island. I can also tell that he is a Divine Spirit, but why does he call me Young Master and seek my protection? And Senior Li even says I ought to know him, and that his calling me Young Master is not without reason. This Junior is truly confused, so please enlighten me."

Li Wuyi smiled. "You truly do not know?"

What would I know? Yang Kai wondered. He did have some guesses that it might be related to Zhang Ruoxi, but after thinking it through, that seemed unlikely. Even if a Divine Spirit knew about his relationship with Zhang Ruoxi, it would only fear and dread him. It would not flatter him, because flattery meant nothing to Divine Spirits.

He solemnly shook his head and humbly waited for instruction.

"You were the one who let him out," Li Wuyi reminded him.

"I let him out?" Yang Kai froze. "From where?"

Jiu Feng's red lips parted, and a name slipped from her rosy mouth: "Four Seasons Land."

"Four Seasons Land?" Yang Kai was startled. Then he suddenly remembered something, turned his head, glared at the flashy old man, and said, "You are..."

The old man bowed. "Qiong Qi greets Young Master!"

Yang Kai sucked in a cold breath and nearly leapt up in shock.

Qiong Qi! This old thing was actually that Qiong Qi?

Once Four Seasons Land was mentioned, then linked with a Divine Spirit, Yang Kai had already guessed the old man's identity. After all, there had indeed been a Qiong Qi in Four Seasons Land back then, but hearing him admit it in person still shocked him.

It really was Qiong Qi! The mount of the Flowing Time Great Emperor... Yang Kai looked him up and down. How had he ended up like this after taking human form? He was not the least bit handsome or domineering. Instead, his face was all bulging flesh, and he looked so fierce and vicious that the only thing missing was the word "villain" carved into his forehead.

From ancient times to the present, who knew how many Great Emperors there had been, but the Flowing Time Great Emperor was the only one able to make a Divine Spirit lower itself to serve as a mount. Although the Beast Great Emperor of today was known for commanding the myriad beasts, and Spirit Beast Island even had Jiu Feng of the Phoenix Race, their relationship was more like family than master and mount.

Divine Spirits might not be as formidable as Great Emperors, but they still had their own dignity. Who would willingly become someone else's mount? Qiong Qi could be called the sole exception, and no one knew how the Flowing Time Great Emperor had subdued him back then. Even after the Flowing Time Great Emperor fell, Qiong Qi had continued to sleep within the Time Temple, guarding it for countless years.

He had only walked out after Four Seasons Land opened the most recent time.

It was not that he lacked the ability to leave. With his strength, if he had truly wanted to leave the Time Temple, he could have gone at any time, but for certain reasons, he had remained there all along. Back when Yang Kai had explored the Time Temple with his meager cultivation, he had sensed Qiong Qi's aura on the Stairway of Time. Zhang Ruoxi, who had been with him, had even been seized by Qiong Qi using a great Divine Ability.

At the time, Yang Kai thought Zhang Ruoxi had met with disaster. Unexpectedly, she was sent back not long after. Not only was she completely unharmed, she even told him she had met a Yao Beast who called himself Qiong Qi. That Qiong Qi's attitude toward her had been unbelievably good, kindly and benevolent like a grandfather from next door. He had even given her a defensive Imperial Treasure, the Phoenix Cloud Dress, which she still wore to this day.

Back then, Yang Kai had wondered if that Qiong Qi had something wrong with his head. He had seized a young girl for no reason, treated her with extreme respect, and even presented her with a precious treasure.

Looking at it now, Qiong Qi had not been out of his mind. He had simply seen farther than anyone else. He must have sensed the hidden bloodline power in Zhang Ruoxi's body at the time; otherwise, why would he have favored her so much?

A descendant of Heaven's Punisher was not someone he could afford to offend.

Later, after Four Seasons Land closed, Yang Kai also learned from Gao Xueting that Qiong Qi had escaped his confinement. When Qiong Qi appeared that day, he gave their whole group quite a fright. Fortunately, he did not do anything to them and simply left with carefree ease.

Now several dozen years had passed without any word of him, until they met again today.

Yang Kai rubbed his chin and circled Qiong Qi several times with great interest, clicking his tongue in wonder. Only after a while did he stop. "You really are Qiong Qi?"

Qiong Qi lowered his brows and eyes. “Genuine beyond doubt.”

Yang Kai nodded. "Divine Spirits can be good or evil, and Qiong Qi seems to be evil among evils. There is no benefit in pretending to be one either. Fine, it looks like you really are Qiong Qi. No wonder that old dog Cang Mo called you an evil spirit!"

Qiong Qi coughed lightly. "Hearsay cannot be trusted. This old man has long since abandoned evil and turned to good, correcting his past mistakes."

"Is that so?" Yang Kai drew the words out, clearly not believing him.

Qiong Qi sighed. "My evil reputation has ruined me." He looked so miserable and bereft of hope that he seemed ready to die on the spot just to prove his innocence.

Yang Kai turned to Li Wuyi and said, "Senior, please use your Divine Ability to take this evil spirit away, lest he bring disaster to the world outside."

Qiong Qi stared, wanting to cry but shedding no tears. "Young Master!"

"Stop!" Yang Kai raised a hand. "You call me Young Master for no other reason than that I obtained the inheritance of the Flowing Time Great Emperor and cultivated Years Like a Shuttle, right?" Then he suddenly thought of something. "Were you able to find me because I used Years Like a Shuttle earlier? You can sense the aura of time passing?"

"Yes!" Qiong Qi nodded. "This old man guarded the Temple precisely to wait for a fated person to inherit Sir's mantle. After countless years, I finally waited for one, and you, Young Master, are Sir's successor."

Yang Kai chuckled. "It is true that I received the Flowing Time Great Emperor's favor, but calling it an inheritance is a bit much. Besides, the Flowing Time Great Emperor is the Flowing Time Great Emperor, and I am me. The two cannot be confused. You were the Flowing Time Great Emperor's mount, and I greatly admire your loyalty in fulfilling your duty and guarding the Time Temple for so long, but I have also long heard Qiong Qi's famous name. I am not qualified to be your Young Master, so do not call me that at random." If it had been some other Divine Spirit, that would have been one thing, but Qiong Qi's evil reputation was simply too fierce, and Yang Kai did not want to get tangled up with him. After a pause, he said, "Actually, Old Qiong..."

Old Qiong? He actually called me... Old Qiong? Qiong Qi could not help glaring.

"Spirit Beast Island is not bad, either. The land is good, the water is good, and it is blessed with outstanding people and spiritual energy. At your age, there is no need to run all over the place. Wouldn't it be nice to obediently go to Spirit Beast Island and enjoy your retirement? I believe Lord Beast Martial, Senior Li, Senior Jiu Feng, and the others will not make things too difficult for you, as long as you behave yourself over there."

Li Wuyi said, "Exactly. Although you, Old Qiong, have not done anything bad since your most recent emergence, my Lord is worried precisely that you may fall back into your old ways. That is why he asked Feng'er and me to bring you back to Spirit Beast Island. But rest assured, you absolutely will not suffer any harsh treatment there. As long as you stay on Spirit Beast Island, you may do whatever you like."

You are calling me Old Qiong too? Believe it or not, I will swallow you whole!

Qiong Qi snapped, "You want to put this old man under house arrest? Just because of a few worries?"

Li Wuyi frowned. "Please forgive us!"

Jiu Feng said, "During this entire pursuit, have the two of us ever made things difficult for you? We have tried persuasion and warning alike, yet you kept running. What is wrong with Spirit Beast Island? We are not truly trying to imprison you, either. If you want to leave in the future, it may not be impossible."

Qiong Qi shook his head. "In this old man's life, I recognized only Time as my master. Time is dead, but he has a successor, and this old man is willing to follow that successor. If that successor is unwilling, this old man will go off and wander freely on his own. As for Spirit Beast Island, I need not go!"

"Must you be so resolute?" Li Wuyi's brow furrowed deeply. If possible, he did not want to fight Qiong Qi either. Although this fellow was old, he ranked extremely high among Divine Spirits and would be very difficult to deal with. Even if he and Jiu Feng joined forces, their chances of taking him down were less than fifty percent.

Qiong Qi said, "Please, you two, do not make things difficult for this old man any further."

Li Wuyi let out a long sigh. Qiong Qi's gaze was firm, and in that instant, even the air seemed to grow dangerous.

"You truly are willing to follow this boy? To recognize him as your master?" Jiu Feng looked at Qiong Qi with a strange expression.

Qiong Qi said, "This old man is unwilling to become enemies with Spirit Beast Island. Other than this, there is no other way." If he did not follow Yang Kai, he would inevitably have to stand against Spirit Beast Island, unless he could shake off Li Wuyi's tracking.

But Li Wuyi had already planted a Space Secret Seal on him. With a single thought, Li Wuyi could sense his position, so how could Qiong Qi shake him off? After thinking it over, Yang Kai was the only one whose influence he could borrow. Moreover, running into Yang Kai in the Eastern Territory this time was an enormous coincidence. Qiong Qi even felt as if the Flowing Time Great Emperor were protecting him from the unseen. Otherwise, how could it be so coincidental that, just when he had been chased into a dead end, he sensed the aura of Years Like a Shuttle Secret Technique?
