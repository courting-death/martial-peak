## Chapter 3281 — The Woman in the Gap

The ceremony itself lasted only a day, but Luocha Sect did not truly quiet down until three days later, after it had seen off guests from every direction. Over those three days, Yang Kai had been busy moving among the many Emperor Realm Masters. There was no helping it. Since he had become Luocha Sect's Guest Elder, he had to fulfill the duties of the post. By using this ceremony as an opportunity and himself as a bridge, he helped Luocha Sect establish friendly ties with quite a few Sects.

This result left Tao Yingruo and the others overjoyed. It could be said that their goal in inviting Yang Kai to serve as Guest Elder had already been more than fulfilled. From now on, looking across the entire Southern Territory, probably no one would dare provoke Luocha Sect lightly. With Yang Kai as their golden signboard, Luocha Sect only needed to develop steadily, and sooner or later it would have a chance to rise again.

That was no longer Yang Kai's concern. He had Lingxiao Palace of his own, and he had only agreed to protect Luocha Sect because of Yu Zhuo. It was impossible for him to remain stationed here for long.

He had originally planned to leave once the ceremony ended. After all, he had left behind a cross-territory Space Array, so if Luocha Sect truly ran into trouble, Tao Yingruo could go to Lingxiao Palace for aid. But because of the matter of the forbidden valley, Yang Kai had no choice but to delay his departure a while longer. He intended to leave only after investigating it on the full-moon night of the following month.

On Luocha Peak, Tao Yingruo arrived with all of her Dao Origin Realm Junior Sisters. After having Ran Yirou report their arrival, Yang Kai received them in the guest hall.

The five Dao Origin Realm cultivators lined up below him and bowed deeply together. Then Tao Yingruo said, "Elder Yang's great kindness is something Luocha Sect will never forget. In the future, if Elder Yang has any command, Luocha Sect will obey without hesitation."

Yang Kai raised a hand to help them up and waved it dismissively. "Acting Sect Master is too serious. I can be considered part of Luocha Sect now, so this was only my duty."

Tao Yingruo said gratefully, "For Luocha Sect to have Elder Yang is truly fortune bestowed by Heaven." As she spoke, she gave Qin Pei a look.

Qin Pei stepped forward, holding a Spatial Ring in both hands, and presented it with great respect.

"This is..." Yang Kai accepted it and glanced at her in doubt.

Qin Pei said, "These are the congratulatory gifts brought by the various guests during the ceremony. The disciples have recorded everything and made a jade tablet. Elder Yang, please see whether anything is missing."

Luocha Sect could originally have kept such congratulatory gifts, but Tao Yingruo and the others knew their own situation. Those people had not sent gifts for Luocha Sect's sake, but for Yang Kai's. Naturally, they could not keep them, so they handed everything over without retaining a single item.

"There really are quite a few good things." Yang Kai swept a casual glance over them, smiled faintly, and did not refuse, putting the Spatial Ring away. To all of Luocha Sect, this was unimaginable wealth. To him, though, it was nothing much.

Lingxiao Palace now oversaw the entire Northern Territory, and its yearly income was beyond imagination. What did these things count for?

Yang Kai had no intention of sharing any of it with Luocha Sect either, because he knew that even if he offered, Tao Yingruo and the others would not agree to accept it. These women were not that thick-skinned. More importantly, giving a man fish was not as good as teaching him how to fish. If Luocha Sect wanted to develop and obtain cultivation resources in the future, it needed a lasting way to support itself.

He had already made plans for this. "Acting Sect Master, if you have time, you can make a trip to Lingxiao Palace and find my Chief Steward. Perhaps you can discuss a way for Luocha Sect to prosper. In the future, your disciples' cultivation will require all kinds of resources."

Tao Yingruo's eyes lit up when she heard this. "Many thanks, Elder Yang. In a few days, I will set out."

Yang Kai nodded. "When you go, tell her that I will be staying here for another month and will return after that."

"Good."

As far as Tao Yingruo and the others were concerned, Yang Kai could stay in Luocha Sect for as long as he liked. After all, they had even opened the forbidden valley to him, so what other request of his could they not satisfy? It would be best if something were to happen between him and Ran Yirou or Yu Keren.

Unfortunately, that wish never came true.

For the next twenty-some days, Yang Kai remained in his room as usual, keeping his door shut and never going out. Not to mention Tao Yingruo and the others, even Ran Yirou and Yu Keren did not see him again.

Time passed in a flash, and a month later, it was another full-moon night.

Yang Kai arrived early at the forbidden valley and could not help frowning.

The last time he had come here, the moon had been bright and the stars sparse. This time, however, the heavens were not cooperating. Dark clouds stretched endlessly overhead, leaving the entire world dim and gloomy, without the slightest glimmer of light.

He could not help feeling a little worried, unsure whether the change in weather would affect the forbidden ground. Still, if his earlier guess was correct, the weather should not matter much.

He waited quietly. At a certain moment, that familiar sensation appeared once more.

Yang Kai's expression sharpened, and he became even more convinced that his earlier guess had been right.

This time, however, he no longer paid attention to the smooth cliff. Without moonlight, there was not the slightest abnormality on its surface. Instead, he flashed straight to the place he had noticed at the final moment last time.

After a short wait, another faint energy fluctuation rippled out.

This time, Yang Kai was prepared. His eyes widened as he stared into the empty space ahead, excitement rising in his heart. Sure enough, just as he had guessed, when that power fluctuation spread out, an almost imperceptible crack appeared in the void. But the crack vanished in an instant, so the power fluctuation also cut off abruptly and was not obvious.

Yang Kai had sensed this anomaly last time, but his attention had been fixed on the smooth cliff before that, and he had only noticed it at the final moment. As a result, he had been unable to trace the source of the power.

There was definitely something inside the Void Crack.

Every full-moon night, that unknown existence would break open the void, causing a Void Crack to appear here in the valley. Then, through the refraction of the moonlight, some of the scene inside the crack would be reflected onto the smooth cliff.

In other words, the root of the matter was not the cliff, but this Void Crack.

Yang Kai could hardly hold himself back, impatient to learn what mystery this Void Crack was hiding. Even if someone else noticed this, they would probably be helpless. He was different. Proficient in the Laws of Space, he only needed to trace the origin of that power, and he could directly tear space open and enter that Space Crack to investigate.

Last time, although he had also torn space open, Void Cracks were ever-changing and unpredictable. Without following their traces, even using the method in the same place would not allow him to find the source.

After several faint fluctuations of power, the fleeting Space Crack finally appeared in full, as fine as a strand of hair. If Yang Kai had not been the one examining it, even a Third Stage Emperor Realm Master might not have noticed anything unusual.

He hesitated no longer. As the Laws of Space rippled, Yang Kai inserted both hands along that sliver of a crack, tore fiercely to either side, and flashed inside.

In the chaotic, empty Space Gap, Yang Kai froze for an instant, staring blankly a short distance ahead, almost unable to believe his eyes.

Everything before this had led him to suspect that something might be hidden inside the Space Gap. In fact, his guess had been right. This time, by following its trace into the Space Gap, he really had found something.

But what was hidden here was not some exotic treasure or divine weapon as he had imagined. It was a person, a woman!

It should be a woman... A man would hardly wear a long dress with that kind of pattern.

The woman sat cross-legged in the void, so thin she was nothing but skin and bones. Her eye sockets were deeply sunken, her hair was entirely silver-white, and the eyes in those sockets were dim and lifeless, like dead fish eyes. Her vitality was so faint that she seemed ready to die at any moment.

Yang Kai was shocked. Deeply shocked!

How could there be a woman here? He knew the Void Gap was incomparably dangerous. Forget those who were not proficient in the Laws of Space; even someone like him could die if he truly became lost inside it. But he had never imagined that one day he would actually find a woman in the Void Gap!

Judging by her appearance, she was probably one of those unlucky souls from the rumors who had become lost in the Void Gap. He simply had no idea how long she must have been lost to be reduced to skin and bones like this. One had to know that anyone capable of entering the Void Gap could not be weak. And since such a person would not be weak, they would naturally carry recovery Spirit Pills, Source Crystals, and the like.

But looking at this woman's condition, Yang Kai estimated that even if she had not been trapped here for ten thousand years, it had still been several thousand. Otherwise, her physical body would never have withered so badly. The moment he saw her, a scene almost formed in Yang Kai's mind.

Trapped in this boundless Void Crack, she had endured year after year of consumption until everything in her Spatial Ring was exhausted. With no other choice, she could only rely on the Qi and blood of her own fleshly body to maintain her vitality. Who knew how many more years had passed after that, until almost all the flesh and blood in her body had been drained away, leaving her in this skeletal state.

Who was she, and how had she become trapped here?

What shocked Yang Kai even more was that she still was not dead! Her vitality was indeed faint, like a candle flame in wind and rain, ready to be snuffed out at any moment, but she was unquestionably still alive.

Just as Yang Kai saw her, those dim, lifeless eyes suddenly moved. The movement was very stiff, as if they had not shifted for a very long time, but they immediately fixed on Yang Kai.

Their eyes met, and joy at finding hope in a desperate situation, along with disbelief, bloomed in her gaze. A clucking sound came from her throat.

In this place, at this moment, the sight made Yang Kai's scalp prickle.

The woman opposite him opened and closed her mouth several times, that mouth as dry and withered as tree bark, but she could not make any sound. Yet with that bit of movement, her already faint vitality began to drain away rapidly, and the light in her eyes swiftly dimmed.

Cold sweat broke out across Yang Kai's forehead. He was truly afraid she would die just like that.

After a moment of hesitation, he still took a Spirit Pill from his Spatial Ring, pinched it between his fingers, and walked over.

The woman's gaze was immediately drawn to the Spirit Pill in Yang Kai's hand. She looked like a starving ghost who had seen some delicacy, and even her weak breathing grew somewhat hurried.

Yang Kai sighed inwardly. Although he could not sense any powerful energy fluctuations from this woman right now, anyone who could last in the Void Gap for several thousand to ten thousand years had to possess terrifying strength. Yet at this moment, a mere Void King-Grade Spirit Pill was enough to make her react like this. She had none of an expert's bearing left, which left Yang Kai filled with emotion.
