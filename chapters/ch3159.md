## Chapter 3159 — All Things Revive

In Nine Heavens Sacred Land, Yang Kai stood in the air, black hair flying and robes snapping in the wind.

His expression was solemn, and he held a bronze Formation Plate in his hand.

This was the Formation Plate used to control the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array, something he, Li Jiao, and Liu Yan had spent no small amount of effort refining.

Now everything was ready. Only the final step remained.

He closed his eyes and focused for a moment, then brushed his fingers lightly across the Formation Plate. Emperor Yuan pulsed faintly from him, stirring flashes of light across its surface.

At a certain moment, his eyes snapped open, brilliant light shooting from them. Holding the Formation Plate in one hand, he shifted seals with the other, sending streak after streak of light into it as he shouted, "Rise!"

The Formation Plate erupted with radiance!

A low hum drifted up from deep underground. The whole of Nine Heavens Sacred Land shook, startling countless cultivators awake from meditation. They hurried out of their retreat chambers to investigate.

At a glance, they saw the figure standing in the air, and only then understood that he was behind the disturbance, not some powerful enemy invading again. At last, they relaxed.

Within Nine Heavens Sacred Land, the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Pillar that served as the Formation Core suddenly underwent a strange change. Complex patterns and designs flashed across the surface of the thousand-zhang pillar.

An invisible vibration wave suddenly rippled out, spreading thunderously in every direction from the Profound Spirit Pillar at its center. It swept over Nine Heavens Sacred Land, rolled across thousands upon tens of thousands of li, and covered all of Tongxuan Continent.

One Profound Spirit Pillar after another lit up, resonating under the influence of the vibration wave as the patterns on their surfaces flashed with brilliance. If one looked down from the Starry Sky, one would see that the entire Tongxuan Continent seemed to hold one hundred and eight lantern flames, each lighting in turn. Wherever the vibration wave passed, the radiance was dazzling.

The seals in Yang Kai's hands shifted faster and faster, his expression grave. Although his cultivation was profound, he had sealed himself, and now that he had to preside over this Heaven-reaching Grand Array, he did not dare be the least bit careless. Today's matter concerned Xia Ningshang, so he had no choice but to treat it with the utmost caution. Sweat slowly beaded on the tip of his nose.

After the time it took to drink a cup of tea, the Profound Spirit Pillar in Nine Heavens Sacred Land once more released an invisible vibration wave, repeating the earlier movement.

The pillar was like a heart beating in steady rhythm. With every beat, the remaining one hundred and seven Profound Spirit Pillars produced a faint, subtle resonance with it, and as the beats increased, that thread of resonance would connect from end to end until there was no longer any separation between them. At that time, all one hundred and eight would become a single whole, enveloping the entire Tongxuan Continent.

Invisible vibration waves spread again and again, and the one hundred and eight Profound Spirit lantern flames brightened and went dark, repeating in an endless cycle.

As time passed, however, the rhythm slowly quickened.

One day later, the interval of time it took to drink a cup of tea had been cut in half.

Another day later, it was cut in half again.

Three more days passed, and the Profound Spirit Pillar at the Formation Core flashed every few breaths. The remaining one hundred and seven Profound Spirit Pillars did the same. Moreover, with careful observation, one could discover that their flashes no longer lit up one after another, but were beginning to move in unison.

Yang Kai poured his focus into urging the Formation Plate and presiding over the Grand Array, unaware of the passage of time.

Finally, at a certain moment, the flashing of the Profound Spirit Pillar before him seemed to reach some limit. An invisible pressure spread out, as if a ferocious beast from the ancient past had suddenly awakened.

Yang Kai looked exhausted, but his eyes shone with frightening intensity.

This moment had finally come!

Without another instant of hesitation, he changed seals, tossed the Formation Plate high into the air, and sent a stream of light into it. A shout rang out, "Open!"

The Formation Plate hummed.

The one hundred and eight lantern flames that had been flashing without pause all went out in that instant, leaving not the slightest trace of light.

But in the next moment, the extinguished lantern flames rekindled all at once, with no before or after, no distinction between one and another, completely becoming a single whole.

One hundred and eight vibration waves rippled out together. From every Profound Spirit Pillar, one hundred and seven beams of light shot forth, rushing in every direction, slicing across the sky, and weaving a vast net over the entire Tongxuan Continent.

At that moment, every living creature on Tongxuan Continent raised its head and gazed upward. The crisscrossing beams of light seemed like meteors falling from the heavens, gathering into a meteor shower that filled the entire world.

The scene shook the heart, yet no one felt afraid or lost. Instead, they felt an urge to bow down in worship.

Hong hong hong...

Humming rang out without cease, and every Profound Spirit Pillar connected tightly to the other one hundred and seven.

Kacha...

It was as if a key had slipped into a lock; the lock that was the one hundred and eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array opened in an instant and began to reveal its heaven-shaking power.

Majestic heavenly might descended. Clouds gathered, Law Power stirred, and the world began rejecting this Grand Array that surpassed the limits of its existence.

Suddenly, a flawless white jade hand flashed through the air. Wherever that jade hand brushed past, the heavenly might scattered, and everything returned to calm.

Without Xia Ningshang using her Star Lord power to protect it and counter the world's rejection, this one hundred and eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array could never have operated normally. Li Jiao had been right. Even Emperor Realm Masters like them could not exist safely in this world without sealing their cultivations, so how could the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array from the Star Realm do so?

If it had been a slightly lower-grade Spirit Array, that would have been one thing, but the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array was a genuine Imperial-Grade Spirit Array and had long since exceeded the limit this world could endure.

Yet with a single brush of Xia Ningshang's power, the Grand Array was granted permission to exist.

Hong long long. The instant the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array began to operate, not only Nine Heavens Sacred Land but the entire world began to tremble.

The web of light covering the world gradually vanished, but the one hundred and eight Profound Spirit Pillars had linked into one whole, and the Grand Array began to run.

The Source Crystals Yang Kai had buried beneath the Profound Spirit Pillars finally reached the moment to play their part. Guided by the Profound Spirit Pillars, countless Source Crystals transformed into pure Spiritual Qi, which the pillars drew out and released into the world.

"Such rich Spiritual Qi!"

Cries of surprise rang out throughout Nine Heavens Sacred Land.

In that instant, the already impressive Spiritual Qi within the Sacred Land seemed to surge to new heights, and it still had not reached its limit. It kept climbing, and in the blink of an eye, a layer of spiritual mist wrapped around Nine Heavens Sacred Land, so thick that no one could see their own hand before their face. It was as if they had been plunged into an ocean of Spiritual Qi, leaving their minds and spirits refreshed.

No one had ever felt such dense World Energy before. As a blessed cultivation paradise on Tongxuan Continent, Nine Heavens Sacred Land was naturally first-rate, but everything feared comparison. Because Tongxuan Continent’s Spiritual Qi had dried up in earlier years, it had been unable to produce overly powerful cultivators. Thus, even Nine Heavens Sacred Land at its peak could not compare with an ordinary spirit mountain or great river on Dark Star or Shuiyue Star. The gap between them was far from small.

But now, Nine Heavens Sacred Land's cultivation environment was not much worse than ordinary places on Dark Star or Shuiyue Star, and it was still improving. Given enough time, Tongxuan Continent might very well become a Cultivation Star like Dark Star and Shuiyue Star.

This same scene was taking place across Tongxuan Continent. Every cultivator who had survived the war cheered with joy and rushed to spread the news.

The blazing sun shone high in the sky, yet raindrops began to fall.

The cheering cultivators froze where they stood. They stared blankly upward, reached out to catch the raindrops, touched them to their tongues, and were stunned on the spot.

Spiritual rain!

This rain was no ordinary rain. Every drop contained abundant Spiritual Qi and could almost be said to have condensed from Spiritual Qi itself.

When the spiritual rain first appeared, it was only a sparse drizzle, but very soon, a torrential downpour fell, covering every part of this world without missing a single corner.

Across the withered wilderness, green buds broke through. On the parched earth, little grasses pushed up sharp tips. A sick plough ox gave a moo at that moment and stood again. A dying old man suddenly opened his eyes, and the gaze that should have been cloudy and dull shone bright.

The descendants gathered to mourn him all stared in dumbfounded silence.

As the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array operated, what it brought to the world was not merely an increase in Spiritual Qi, but an unfathomable vitality as well.

Yang Kai still stood in the air, holding the Formation Plate of the one hundred and eight All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array. His Divine Sense spread outward like a tide, inspecting every corner of the world.

Seeing all this, he could not help showing a satisfied expression.

Resolving Tongxuan Continent's hidden danger this time was truly thanks to Li Jiao. Yang Kai himself had only dabbled in the Dao of Spirit Arrays and had never even heard of the All-Heaven Profound Spirit Array. If Li Jiao had not spent these past two months working so painstakingly, this array could never have been arranged.

The results were even better than Yang Kai had expected, making him feel grateful to Li Jiao.

Mm... he might as well write off the debts Li Dragon Palace owed him. In any case, he and Li Jiao could be considered to have gone through life and death together, and Li Jiao had been running himself ragged for him these days. Yang Kai would feel too embarrassed to press him for repayment now.

In any case, those debts did not amount to much for Lingxiao Palace, so why force others to tighten their belts and scrape by?

Having quietly made up his mind, Yang Kai swept his gaze to the side and immediately flew off in a certain direction.

Before the main hall, two figures stood quietly, one beautiful enough to topple kingdoms, the other captivating enough to overturn all living beings.

"Little Senior Sister, why did you come out?" Yang Kai flashed to the two women's side and supported Xia Ningshang by the arm, unbearably nervous. "Why aren't you lying down? Why did you run out here? Slow down, slow down. Don't walk anymore. It's raining outside, so just stay here."

Xue Yue glanced sideways at him and said, "Anyone who didn't know better would think Ningshang was pregnant."

Xia Ningshang blushed at once.

Yang Kai stroked his chin and considered it seriously for a moment. "That can be arranged."

Xia Ningshang hurriedly changed the subject. "I only came out to take a look. I suddenly felt much better just now."

More than much better. Earlier, although Yang Kai had helped Xia Ningshang stabilize her vitality so it would not be eroded, she had still been weak, and her complexion had remained pale. Now, however, color had returned to her cheeks. That was not only because Xue Yue had teased her into shyness, but even more because of the Grand Array's effects.

Although she was a Star Lord and could grasp the state of the entire star with a single thought, seeing it with her own eyes was both habitual and more direct.

"This Spirit Array really is excellent. Junior Brother, thank that Sir Li properly for me." Xia Ningshang looked at the pouring spiritual rain. If Yang Kai had not been standing there, she might have rushed into the rain and danced before she could fully vent the joy in her heart.
