## Chapter 3310 - There Is Someone Else

One hundred thousand li from Heavenly Wolf Valley, a streak of light cut across the sky, swift as a meteor. Anyone with keen enough eyes would have been able to make out its true form: a hale, ruddy-faced old man of small stature.

At that moment, the little old man seemed to be fleeing from something, racing forward without the slightest pause while muttering curses under his breath, his face full of resentment.

All of a sudden, he seemed to sense something. Turning to look in one direction, he revealed an uncertain, startled expression. "That aura... could it be...? No, surely not..."

Just then, not far ahead of him, space heaved and ripples flashed. The perfectly intact space seemed about to be torn open by some tremendous force, and a tiny crack suddenly appeared.

The little old man's eyes sharpened. Gritting his teeth, he cursed, "That stinking brat got here fast. Chasing Grandpa this closely... he'll be the death of me."

Knowing what the other party was capable of, he feared that even with all his own methods, he would not be able to shake him off. His eyes suddenly darted about as a brilliant idea came to him, and with a twist of his body, he unexpectedly sped off in the direction he had just looked.

That direction was precisely where Heavenly Wolf Valley lay.

Three breaths later, the tiny Void Crack suddenly widened, and two figures strolled out from within, one man and one woman. The man was heroic and extraordinary, while the woman was as lovely as a painting; standing together, they truly seemed like immortals.

After a brief scan, the man sighed. "He got away again."

The woman clenched her silver teeth. "That old thing runs quickly enough. It is not as if we want his life, so why is he panicking?"

The man laughed despite himself. "We may not want his life, but we do intend to restrict his freedom. Naturally, he would be unwilling."

The woman shot him a sidelong glance and snorted. "Now you are speaking up for him? What, do you want to let him off?"

The man shook his head. "This is Sir's order. Even if I wanted to let him go, I could not. A vicious creature like that is better kept under our eyes, lest his savage nature flare up again one day and he bring calamity to the living." After a pause, he smiled and said, "If he had stayed in the Southern Territory, that would have been that, but he just had to court death by running to the Eastern Territory. Since he has entered Sir's sight, he can stay on Spirit Beast Island."

"Enough nonsense. Hurry up and chase him. If we delay any longer, he will be long gone," the woman urged impatiently.

The man nodded slightly, reached out, and swept his hand before him. As the Laws of Space surged, a crack split open in the Void, and the two of them walked into it side by side, vanishing in the blink of an eye.

A moment later, the crack slowly faded away without a trace.

...

Outside Heavenly Wolf Valley, Yang Kai had used the Nothingness Secret Technique at the most dangerous moment to exile himself into the Void, avoiding the attacker's killing blow. He then seized the opportunity to send out a Years Like a Shuttle Seal, striking the newcomer until he spat blood. Only then did he look over at him at leisure and sneer, "Wu Heng, this Lord has been waiting for you for quite some time."

The one who had hidden in the shadows and launched a sneak attack on him was none other than Wu Heng, the man he had dealt with not long ago in the Great Wilderness Star Domain. This man was also the Star Domain Master of the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

Wu Heng's expression was grim as he urged his Emperor Yuan to suppress the Power of Time inside his body, blood still dripping from the corner of his mouth. Resentfully, he demanded, "How did you know this Lord was here?"

Although the hatred between him and Yang Kai was not born of a murdered father or a stolen wife, it was still irreconcilable. After all, the Great Wilderness Star Domain under his control had lost billions of li of territory to Yang Kai's devouring. Though that devouring had not damaged the Great Wilderness Star Domain's foundation, it had an enormous impact on his future.

Whether a Star Domain was whole or not also had a decisive effect on his Martial Dao path. The Star Domain's defects had even left the Star Domain Origin within him somewhat unstable, making it impossible for him to settle down and cultivate in seclusion. If things continued this way, his strength might not decline, but advancing any further would be impossible.

Last time, the matter had been settled through the intervention of the Star Court's higher-ups, forcing him to swallow the loss in silence. As for the territories Yang Kai had devoured, he could only grit his teeth and accept the changes.

But while the Star Court could intervene in matters between Star Domains, surely that woman could not interfere if he took revenge in the Star Realm, right? As long as he could kill Yang Kai, he could regain control of the Star Domain territory that had been devoured. He could even go on to devour Hengluo Star Domain and complete the grand plan he had made before.

As the Star Domain Master of the Great Wilderness Star Domain, he naturally had some ties to Netherworld Sect. After that incident, he made a few arrangements and returned to the Star Realm, where he found Fu Bo and had him keep an eye on Yang Kai's whereabouts.

He had not expected the Heavens to favor him so much. Yang Kai, who should have been in the Southern Territory, had actually come to the Eastern Territory so soon. Qi Tian Stronghold sent word to Netherworld Sect, and Netherworld Sect immediately informed Wu Heng, leading to this move.

What Wu Heng could not understand was how Yang Kai seemed to have anticipated his arrival long ago.

Yang Kai chuckled. "Since Fu Bo knew about the Great Wilderness Star Domain, naturally you were the one who told him. Since you two have such a connection, if this Lord still did not know you were lying in ambush, would this Lord not be an idiot? One could even say that you were the one secretly pushing this whole affair along." Otherwise, why would Fu Bo, who had swallowed his anger and kept quiet all those years ago, stir up trouble with him again?

So that was it! Wu Heng turned hatefully toward the Mountain and River Bell, his stare seeming to pierce through it and land on Fu Bo himself. His face was filled with resentment. He had never expected this boy to guess a few clues simply because of one careless remark from Fu Bo.

Seen that way, he had suffered such a huge loss entirely thanks to Fu Bo.

That bastard! Wu Heng cursed inwardly.

Yang Kai's face turned solemn, and he said in a deep voice, "The one surnamed Fu came to no good end after going against this Lord, and the one surnamed Wu will be no different. Wu Heng, you managed to escape with your life last time, so you should have treasured it. Yet this time you still dared to come looking for trouble with this Lord. Do you really think I cannot kill you?"

With that angry shout, murderous intent enveloped Wu Heng as if it had taken solid form. Wu Heng's face went livid, and he snorted coldly. "You? You are not qualified."

Yang Kai raised his brow. "You sound quite confident. It seems you are not the only one who came, Wu Heng. That person came too, did he not?"

The moment he said this, Wu Heng's expression shifted slightly, but he quickly recovered. "What nonsense are you spouting?"

Yang Kai sneered. "Nonsense?" His gaze swept through the Void before settling on a certain spot. "Cang Mo, since you have come, why not show yourself openly? Or do you think this Lord cannot see you?"

There was someone else hidden in the shadows?

Tang Sheng, Qian Xiuying, Red Ghost, and the others were almost thrown into chaos.

They had already been startled enough when Wu Heng suddenly charged onto the scene, because not one of them had ever heard his name, nor did they know where this man had come from.

Yet that made no sense. After all, a Third Stage Emperor could not appear out of nowhere. Anyone with such cultivation should be a figure of resounding fame, but the name Wu Heng was simply too unfamiliar, so unfamiliar that none of those present had ever heard it before.

That alone would have been one thing, but after Wu Heng jumped out, Yang Kai had actually called out another name, Cang Mo, and fixed his gaze on the Void as if certain an expert was hiding there.

As for this fellow called Cang Mo, although they did not know whether he truly existed and he had yet to appear, Tang Sheng and the others all had a strange feeling that he was even stronger than Wu Heng.

The one who appeared last was always the strongest.

Wu Heng, Fu Bo, and Xu Changfeng were already Third Stage Emperors, so what cultivation did this Cang Mo possess? A terrifying thought suddenly surfaced in their minds. Tang Sheng and Red Ghost exchanged a glance, each seeing the other's shock and fear.

"There really is someone else!" Lan He suddenly lifted a hand to cover her red lips, her beautiful eyes trembling as she looked toward the spot in the air. Over there, as space rippled, a figure appeared like a ghost. He wore a red-gold crown and a deep purple cloak, his hands clasped behind his back, simply looking down from above with cool indifference, like a lofty deity gazing over all living beings. Those eyes made others deeply uncomfortable. Under his gaze, she felt like nothing more than an ant.

He did not reveal his cultivation, and no one could even sense what his cultivation was, yet anyone who saw him could tell that he was stronger than everyone present!

"A Quasi-Great Emperor!" Tang Sheng cried out despite himself, cold sweat pouring down his forehead. Red Ghost also swallowed and nodded subconsciously.

Lan He turned to look at her Master. "Quasi-Great Emperor? What does that mean?"

This was the first time she had heard such a title. Although she was also in the Emperor Realm, she was only at the First Stage, and she had not come into contact with many people or matters, so the secrets she knew were limited.

Tang Sheng and Red Ghost had all their attention fixed on Cang Mo and did not hear her question at all, so naturally they paid it no mind.

Instead, Qian Xiuying sighed and transmitted her voice. "Little He, in this world, there are people who stand above Third Stage Emperors but below Great Emperors. Their methods can reach the Heavens, their strength is formidable, and they are only half a step from becoming Great Emperors. Such people are Quasi-Great Emperors, pseudo Great Emperors. Against them, unless a Great Emperor appears, they can almost be called invincible."

Lan He asked in surprise, "There are really people like that?" This was also the first time she had heard of such a thing.

Qian Xiuying nodded. "There are, and quite a few. For example, Sir Li Wuyi, whom you have heard of, is one of the Quasi-Great Emperors, and the strongest among them."

Lan He looked as though she had been enlightened, but soon asked curiously, "Since there are quite a few of them, why have I never heard any of their names before?"

Qian Xiuying said, "Once their strength reaches this level, they can no longer grow any further unless they contend for the position of Great Emperor. Most of them devote themselves to bitter secluded cultivation, preparing for the day that may come. Why would they casually show themselves in public? Still, there are always exceptions. Is Sir Li Wuyi not one?"

Lan He nodded to show that she understood, then suddenly said indignantly, "But this is going too far."

To deal with Yang Kai, a mere Second-Stage Emperor Realm cultivator, it was bad enough that the Eastern Territory's two top forces had come out at full strength. Being slaughtered by Yang Kai until they were in utter disarray was purely their own fault. After that, Wu Heng had appeared and launched a sneak attack from the shadows, shameless to the extreme. Now, there was actually a Quasi-Great Emperor hiding nearby as well. If Yang Kai had not exposed him with a single sentence, that person would probably have continued hiding. Heaven knew when he might have struck from the shadows?

A Quasi-Great Emperor had appeared, and with no Great Emperor present, who could contend with him? Was this not blatant bullying? Standing in Yang Kai's position, Lan He felt unbearably stifled. Her beautiful eyes swept across Wu Heng and Cang Mo's faces, and she found one more hateful than the other, both shameless to the extreme.
