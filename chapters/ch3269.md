## Chapter 3269 - Success

With a boom, dust returned to dust, earth to earth. Lei Gu burst into a cloud of blood mist that scattered through the air.

Fan Wu and the other two grew solemn, all of them moved by his resolve. Even knowing death was certain, there were probably few people in the world who would choose Self-Destruction. In his place, the three of them would have fought like cornered beasts. The final outcome might have been inevitable, but who could know without struggling to the end?

Were all members of the Demon Race this ruthless? The three of them felt a chill.

As they were wondering, they saw Yang Kai fly weakly down and begin searching through the shattered rocks.

Luan Feng frowned, drifted lightly down, and snapped, "What are you looking for instead of healing?"

Yang Kai coughed lightly and said, "If the demon heart remains, the Demon Race does not die!"

In the world of the Thousand Illusion Dreamscape, he had dealt with the Demon Race for years and knew just how tenacious the vitality of powerful demons could be. Even if a demon's flesh body was destroyed, so long as a demon heart survived, they could miraculously revive.

To kill a demon like that, one had to destroy his demon heart completely.

Although Lei Gu had chosen Self-Destruction with such resolve, Yang Kai still had to guard against him slipping away by shedding his shell. Fortunately, after searching for a while, he found nothing resembling a demon heart. It seemed there were some differences between those possessed by Demonic Intent and true members of the Demon Race.

He then had Liu Yan cast a spell to burn the entire area, and only then did Yang Kai let out a breath. Even if Lei Gu had truly planned to shed his shell, under Liu Yan's True Phoenix Fire, he likely had no chance of being reborn.

The Incarnation slipped over from the side. It had originally been hiding below to guard against Lei Gu escaping, but who would have thought it would never get a chance to act? Even so, it was thanks to the connection between it and Yang Kai that they had been able to set the trap so securely and catch Lei Gu off guard.

Otherwise, how could Yang Kai have agreed so easily to follow Lei Gu alone? The Incarnation was his greatest reliance.

The group gathered together but said little. Yang Kai was badly wounded and urgently needed to heal, while the mess at Wuhua Hall still had to be cleaned up. The Thirty-Two Yao Kings were still over there, and without the three Divine Spirits holding them in check, who knew what trouble they might cause? They might behave as meekly as grandsons in front of Yang Kai, but in truth, every one of them was wild and untamable.

None of them had any desire to linger, so they immediately returned the way they had come.

Yang Kai handed the Xuan Realm Pearl to Liu Yan for safekeeping, then hid inside the Small Xuan Realm to heal. The Xuan Realm Pearl had originally been his greatest secret and trump card, but as his strength grew, there was no longer any need to be so deliberate about keeping it hidden.

Fan Wu and the other two certainly would not spread word of it, so exposing it before them was no great matter.

There was naturally no need to dwell on Liu Yan and the others' return. Yang Kai went to the medicine garden and sat cross-legged beneath the Everlasting Tree, while Mu Zhu and Mu Lu used a Secret Technique to draw strands of essence from the tree to heal his wounds.

Yang Kai had used the Everlasting Tree to heal many times before, but mostly by plucking and consuming its leaves directly. The Everlasting Tree had never had many leaves to begin with, and after Yang Kai had picked from it a few times, it was nearly bare. He did not dare touch the few remaining leaves rashly, lest he damage the tree's foundation.

The wondrous methods of the Wood Spirit Race, however, could draw out the vitality within the Everlasting Tree and pour it into Yang Kai's body without harming the tree itself.

Together with Yang Kai's powerful physique and recovery ability, this allowed him to more or less recover in only three days. The speed delighted him. Without the two Wood Spirits' help, the injuries he had suffered this time would have taken at least a month to heal.

In his happiness, he naturally showered Mu Zhu and Mu Lu's wondrous methods with praise, leaving the two Wood Spirits rosy-cheeked and endlessly shy.

Only after he recovered did Yang Kai have time to deal with Gao Xueting.

Gao Xueting had been corroded by Demonic Qi, her body and mind falling into a Demonic State. Earlier, Lei Gu had even controlled her and nearly forced her into Self-Destruction. Fortunately, Yang Kai had pulled her into the Small Xuan Realm and suppressed her in time; otherwise, not even her bones would be left now.

With a flicker, Yang Kai arrived at the cluster of pavilions. Gao Xueting was being suppressed inside one of them.

He pushed open the door and walked in, only to be slightly startled. The moment Gao Xueting sensed someone approaching, she suddenly turned her pitch-black eyes toward him. A strange sound then came from her throat, her expression savage, as if she wanted to devour Yang Kai's flesh and drink his blood.

This change made Yang Kai frown. According to his previous observations, the demonized people in Wuhua Hall fell into two kinds. The first were like Lei Gu and Feng Ming, possessed by Demonic Intent. These were true demonized people, with their own thoughts and will, wholeheartedly serving the Demon Race.

The second kind were like Gao Xueting and the many Southern Territory cultivators who had fallen into a Demonic State after being corroded by Demonic Qi. These demonized people had no thoughts of their own and had to be controlled by the first kind.

But now, Gao Xueting clearly had some consciousness of her own, yet it was not Gao Xueting's true consciousness. Judging from that savage expression, it was a reaction born after falling into a Demonic State.

Yang Kai came to Gao Xueting and looked at her with a complicated expression. He called to her softly a few times, but she did not respond at all. She only kept growling and struggling. If not for the power suppressing her, she would likely have pounced on him and fought him to the death.

Yang Kai could not help feeling suspicious. Could it be that, as time passed, all those demonized people without their own consciousness would become like this and begin acting on instinct?

If so, there would probably be trouble back at Wuhua Hall.

No longer hesitating, Yang Kai's aura abruptly changed, becoming ancient and desolate. He seemed to have stepped out of a painting scroll from the ancient world. His hand formed a seal, and incantations rang from his mouth.

A ray of light shot forth and entered Gao Xueting’s body.

It was none other than the Bloodlust Art. Although this was one of the most common Shamanic Arts and not particularly advanced, it had proven quite effective at resisting the corrosion of Demonic Qi.

In an instant, red light blazed. Gao Xueting's Essence-Blood was stirred, and her whole body seemed to be covered by a crimson curtain of light. A trace of pain involuntarily crossed her face.

At the same time the red light flared, boundless black Qi spread from Gao Xueting's skin, tangling with the red light as the two wore each other away. Gao Xueting's complexion also shifted unpredictably.

The incantation changed, and Yang Kai cast the Exorcism Spell.

Cool light descended from the sky, cleansing Gao Xueting's body and mind. Hissing sounds rang out as wisps of black smoke spilled from her body. As if they had encountered their natural bane, they fled desperately, then dissolved into nothing.

Yang Kai stood before Gao Xueting with a solemn expression, patiently casting the Exorcism Spell again and again.

As time passed, the Demonic Qi spilling from Gao Xueting's body grew thinner and thinner, while the flickering black light gradually showed signs of being absorbed into the red.

Finally, at a certain moment, the black in Gao Xueting's eyes receded. She blinked in confusion, her gaze focused, and she immediately saw Yang Kai standing before her with a smile, his aura ancient and strange.

"Junior Brother..." Gao Xueting called softly, looking extremely weak.

"Senior Sister Gao, you're awake." Yang Kai breathed a sigh of relief. After so long a delay, he had truly feared he would not be able to save Gao Xueting. Fortunately, ancient Shamanic Arts were indeed the bane of Demonic Qi, and his efforts had not been wasted.

"How did you..." Gao Xueting still did not quite understand what was happening. She only felt weak and powerless all over, with some extremely uncomfortable power still lingering in her meridians and body.

"Senior Sister, do you remember what happened before?" Yang Kai asked, looking at her.

"What happened before..." Gao Xueting frowned and tried to think through the haze.

"Southern Territory Martial Gathering, Wuhua Hall!" Yang Kai reminded her.

Gao Xueting froze when she heard this. Immediately afterward, her pupils contracted and she said urgently, "There is a conspiracy at Wuhua Hall! Junior Brother, hurry and warn the Hall Master!"

Yang Kai said, "Senior Sister Gao, stay calm. Wuhua Hall's conspiracy has already been shattered, and Lei Gu and Feng Ming have already been brought to justice."

"Lei Gu and Feng Ming are dead?" Gao Xueting's expression changed, and she quickly asked, "What exactly were they plotting?"

Yang Kai asked in return, "How much do you know, Senior Sister?"

Gao Xueting slowly shook her head. "Not much. That night, Junior Brother Luo and I accepted Sect Elder Feng... Feng Ming's invitation and went to his residence as guests to discuss cultivation insights. More than twenty others came with us as well. But after drinking something, we fell asleep and never woke."

Yang Kai nodded slightly, understanding that Gao Xueting and the others had likely fallen into a Demonic State because of what they drank. He had wondered before how a group of Emperor Realm Masters could have been demonized so easily. As long as they had sensed even the slightest thing wrong, they would only have needed to circulate their Emperor Yuan to resist, and they should have been able to hold on for a while. At the very least, they could have sent out a warning.

Now it seemed Feng Ming had used something akin to poison, allowing Demonic Qi to corrode Gao Xueting and the others from within. Naturally, there had been no way to defend against that.

They likely had not expected Feng Ming to do such a thing either. After all, they all knew one another well, and their friendships went back more than just a year or two. With no guard up at all, they had naturally fallen into his trap with ease.

"What about Junior Brother Luo?" Gao Xueting asked.

"I don't know." Yang Kai shook his head. He truly had not seen any trace of Luo Chen before, and now it seemed Luo Chen's chances were grim.

"Then what is this place?"

Yang Kai smiled and said, "A small world. Rest and recover, Senior Sister Gao. We will return to Wuhua Hall very soon." After saying so, he took out a bottle of healing Emperor Pills and placed it beside her. She had been corroded by Demonic Qi, badly damaging her vitality, and Lei Gu had controlled her earlier and nearly forced her into Self-Destruction. Although Yang Kai had forcibly suppressed her, it had still done her some harm.

Gao Xueting did not stand on ceremony. She picked up the jade bottle, took out a Spirit Pill, and placed it in her mouth. As she refined its medicinal effects, she asked, "What exactly happened?"

"It is a long story." Yang Kai sighed, sat cross-legged across from Gao Xueting, and briefly recounted the events of the past several days. Gao Xueting's expression shifted again and again. She had never imagined that the Southern Territory Martial Gathering would involve such a heaven-shaking conspiracy.

Although Yang Kai spoke calmly, how could she fail to understand that if he had not come along on this trip and used wondrous means to bring in a group of powerful reinforcements from the Northern Territory and Eastern Territory, the Southern Territory might truly have been finished? Had Wuhua Hall's conspiracy succeeded, several hundred demonized Emperor Realm Masters and tens of thousands of demonized elite cultivators would have scattered across the entire Southern Territory. Given enough time, the whole Southern Territory would have become a demon den.
