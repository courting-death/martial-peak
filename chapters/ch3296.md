## Chapter 3296 — Heavenly Wolf Valley

No one paid him any attention, but the short, fat City Lord did not dare show the slightest resentment. He stood there trembling, listening as the group muttered among themselves for a while. They seemed to mention several names, and Heavenly Wolf Valley came up as well.

A moment later, the entire group swept out in a rush. From beginning to end, not one of them so much as glanced at him. Soon after, dozens of figures shot into the air and flew away.

Only then did the short, fat City Lord let out a long breath. His whole back was soaked, and he felt the relief of someone who had narrowly escaped death.

By now, how could he not understand? This group of experts had not come because of him, nor because of his city. They had only been passing through. He really did not know which rotten bastard had provoked these two great forces and made so many experts move out together, frightening him half to death in the process. After cursing fiercely in his heart a few times, he finally felt a little better.

Heavenly Wolf Valley was an enormous valley, surrounded on all sides by tall, imposing mountains. There was only one passage leading in and out of the valley.

As Heavenly Wolf Valley's most outstanding rising star, Lan He held an extremely lofty position in the valley. The moment she led Yang Kai into Heavenly Wolf Valley, the disciples guarding the entrance came forward to greet her. "Elder Lan!"

As they spoke, they looked Yang Kai over curiously, guessing at his relationship with Lan He. This was the first time they had ever seen Lan He bring a man back to the valley.

"Are these Heavenly Wolves?" Yang Kai looked up at the two enormous stone sculptures flanking the valley entrance. The sculptures were clearly quite old, each more than a dozen zhang tall, both posed as if howling at the moon. Their surfaces were mottled and weathered, marked by the passage of time.

Before this, Yang Kai might not have known what sort of creature the stone sculptures depicted, but after hearing Lan He's explanation, he could guess that they were Heavenly Wolves.

Lan He smiled and nodded. "That's right. Heavenly Wolf Valley was founded upon the legacy of the ancient Divine Spirit Heavenly Wolf. These were left behind by our first Ancestral Founder, and twenty or thirty thousand years have passed since then."

"Impressive and extraordinary." Yang Kai nodded, unstinting in his praise.

Divine Spirits were not to be underestimated. Never mind how fiercely he had challenged Luan Feng in the Ancient Land, acting as if he had her completely in hand. If he truly angered Luan Feng enough to make her fight him, he guessed he still might not be her opponent.

Heavenly Wolf was also a Divine Spirit, so it could not be weak.

"Brother Yang, please." Lan He smiled faintly and gestured ahead.

Yang Kai nodded and followed her into the valley.

As Lan He led the way, she said, "I'll take you to meet the Valley Lord first. If you want to go to Spirit Beast Island, we'll need his help."

Yang Kai hesitated. "This matter won't put your Valley Lord in a difficult position, will it?"

Lan He pursed her lips in a smile. "Don't worry, the Valley Lord will agree." She blinked and added, "I forgot to tell Brother Yang, but the Valley Lord is my Master. What his disciple agrees to is as good as him agreeing too."

Yang Kai could not help laughing.

Heavenly Wolf Valley had quite a few disciples, roughly three or four thousand in all, scattered throughout the valley. There were also several Spirit Peaks within the valley, though they were somewhat smaller than the peaks surrounding it. The Spiritual Qi inside the valley was rich, and the fragrance of flowers was pleasant. It could truly be called a land blessed with natural beauty and spiritual grace.

Yang Kai also saw many wolf monsters throughout the valley. Some moved in groups of three or five, some roamed alone, and still others ran freely in packs. The disciples of Heavenly Wolf Valley were not the least bit afraid of them. Some even kept wolf monsters as companions. The Sect's character was strikingly obvious, making Yang Kai sigh inwardly. As expected of Heavenly Wolf Valley; they really were carrying the principle of keeping company with wolves through to the end.

As they were walking, a fierce aura rushed toward them head-on. A gray giant wolf the size of a small calf suddenly blocked the road ahead. Yang Kai clicked his tongue in wonder, for this wolf monster was actually a Twelfth-Tier Yao Beast. In other words, it was equal to an Emperor Realm expert among humans.

However, its aura did not seem very strong. It should only be at the early Twelfth-Tier.

What surprised Yang Kai even more was that he sensed a trace of Lan He's aura from this wolf monster. Recalling everything he had seen just now, he formed a vague guess.

Sure enough, when Lan He saw the giant wolf, she smiled, rushed over, and patted its head. "Little Five, were you waiting for me?"

The giant wolf nuzzled its huge head into Lan He's arms, looking extremely affectionate.

Lan He laughed and played with it for a while before turning back. "Little Five, let me introduce you. This Senior Brother is Senior Brother Yang Kai, the one who saved my life back then." Then she said to Yang Kai, "This is my battle wolf, Little Five."

Yang Kai and the wolf looked at each other. Its eyes were sharp, but it gave a slight nod, expressing its thanks to Yang Kai in an unexpectedly human way. Yang Kai also knew that a Twelfth-Tier Yao Beast had already awakened its sentience and was absolutely not comparable to an ordinary Yao Beast, so he cupped his fist and said, "Greetings, Brother Five."

The giant wolf howled, looking rather displeased.

Yang Kai was stunned.

Lan He laughed until she doubled over, her body trembling like flowering branches, utterly unable to contain herself. "Brother Yang, Little Five is female."

The words had barely left her mouth when a burst of crackling came from the giant wolf's body. Its huge form twisted and transformed, suddenly turning into a burly woman. She stood a full head taller than Lan He, with a broad frame. Her five fingers were splayed and powerful, her nails sharp as blades. She wore a short shirt on top and a short skirt below, leaving her round, thick thighs bare. Her bulging muscles clearly held explosive strength. She waved a fist fiercely at Yang Kai and roared, "Are you blind?"

Yang Kai was terribly embarrassed, but this really could not be blamed on him. A moment ago, how was he supposed to know whether she was male or female?

Lan He jumped up and smacked her on the head. "Don't be rude!"

Only then did Little Five curl her lips. A moment later, her expression turned solemn, and she cupped her fist. "I've heard Little He mention you. Thank you for what happened back then."

Yang Kai smiled. "It was a small matter. No need to take it to heart."

Little Five looked Yang Kai up and down, nudged Lan He with her broad, sturdy shoulder, and said carelessly, "This boy isn't bad. Why don't you take him? You're not young anymore. It's time you found a man."

As soon as these words came out, Lan He was deeply embarrassed.

Yang Kai was dumbstruck as well, thinking, I'm not deaf. If you're going to whisper, at least act like you're whispering. Why say it so loudly?

Lan He flushed and scolded, "Stop talking nonsense. Brother Yang ran into some trouble this time, so I brought him here to find Master."

"What trouble?" Little Five asked curiously.

"It would be useless even if I told you. Be good and wait here. I'll come find you later." As she spoke, she hurriedly called Yang Kai along, afraid Little Five would blurt out some other shocking remark.

Watching the two of them disappear, Little Five snorted. Her figure twisted and transformed once more, turning back into a giant wolf, and she galloped freely across the ground.

"Sorry you had to see that, Brother Yang. Little Five has always spoken without a filter. Please don't take it to heart," Lan He said, somewhat embarrassed.

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Little Five is quite interesting."

Lan He said, "Little Five and I grew up together. If a disciple of Heavenly Wolf Valley has the aptitude, they can subdue a battle wolf. To others, these wolves may be nothing more than helpers, merely Yao Beasts, but to the disciples of Heavenly Wolf Valley, they are family, brothers and sisters."

Yang Kai said, "Back in the Shattered Star Sea, I didn't see you bring Little Five with you."

Lan He smiled bitterly and shook her head. "The quotas to enter the Shattered Star Sea were too precious. Heavenly Wolf Valley only obtained a few, so Little Five couldn't go in."

Yang Kai nodded. He had entered the Shattered Star Sea before as well, so he knew that when the Shattered Star Sea opened, only those who bore a Star Seal would receive the summons and gain the qualifications to enter.

As they talked, the two of them arrived in a great hall. Lan He arranged for Yang Kai to sit and rest, then ordered someone to serve fragrant tea. Only then did she say to Yang Kai, "Brother Yang, please wait a moment. I'll go find Master."

Yang Kai had no objection.

Lan He turned and left again.

On a certain Spirit Peak in Heavenly Wolf Valley, Lan He barged in without ceremony and called loudly, "Master, Master..."

A moment later, a sigh came from within a thatched cottage. "I am here."

Lan He followed the sound over and bowed to an elegant-looking middle-aged man. "Master."

This middle-aged man was none other than Tang Sheng, the Valley Lord of Heavenly Wolf Valley. He was also Heavenly Wolf Valley's strongest expert, with a Second Stage Emperor Realm cultivation.

Although Heavenly Wolf Valley also had another Vice Valley Lord, Qian Xiuying, who was likewise at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, the two of them were husband and wife to begin with. Moreover, Tang Sheng had advanced earlier, so if they truly fought, Tang Sheng should be slightly superior.

Tang Sheng opened his eyes slightly and looked at the disciple before him, quite satisfied. Heavenly Wolf Valley was not a top Sect, and raising an Emperor Realm cultivator was no easy task. Lan He had not failed his expectations and had advanced to the Emperor Realm early. As her Master, how could he be anything but satisfied? Smiling, he asked, "Did you gain anything from this trip?"

Lan He said, "I broadened my horizons a little and heard a few stories, but I didn't gain anything especially great."

Tang Sheng smiled faintly. "On the path of growth, everything you see and hear is experience. Perhaps one day, some sentence you once heard or some matter you once witnessed will become nourishment for your growth, the key to your breakthrough, or even your lifeline."

"Master is right." Lan He looked at him with a bright smile, showing none of the restraint an ordinary disciple would have before their Master. Instead, she was like a daughter facing her father. "This time, I saw that lifeline."

Tang Sheng asked curiously, "What lifeline?"

Lan He said, "Master, do you still remember Elder Ling?" As she spoke, she walked over and poured Tang Sheng a cup of tea.

Tang Sheng picked up the teacup, took a sip, and said, "Elder Ling is an Elder of my Heavenly Wolf Valley, and I see her often. What is there to remember?"

Lan He walked behind him, set both hands on his shoulders, and kneaded them with a playful smile. "What your disciple is asking, Master, is whether you remember why Elder Ling came to Heavenly Wolf Valley."

Tang Sheng sighed. "Naturally, she came for Wu An. Her love for Wu An was unshakable. If not for that, why would I have made an exception and accepted her into Heavenly Wolf Valley, and even granted her the position of Elder?"

Ling Yinqin was not from Heavenly Wolf Valley. It was only because she had married Wu An, one of Heavenly Wolf Valley's disciples from its early years. Unfortunately, Wu An died young in a certain Secret Realm, so she traveled a thousand li to escort his ashes back to Heavenly Wolf Valley, allowing her husband to rest in peace.
