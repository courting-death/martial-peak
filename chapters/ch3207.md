## Chapter 3207 — Two Wood Spirits

Not only were there no conditions, but she would even send two clansmen to help him tend his medicine garden. Such a bargain actually existed in this world...

Yang Kai finally understood just how much the Wood Spirit Race yearned for and pursued rare, precious plants.

Just as the Elder had said, it was simply their nature. Letting them care for the plants was the best thanks Yang Kai could offer. As for Yang Kai, this was a wonderful deal from every angle. He did not have to pay a thing, and he gained two stewards for free. Where under the heavens could he find anything better?

The sudden good fortune left Yang Kai almost dizzy. He paced back and forth inside the tree hollow several times, so excited he could hardly contain himself.

Mu Na frowned at him and said with a strange edge to her voice, "If you will not even agree to this, then I have nothing more to say. In the future, please do not come here again. My Wood Spirit Race does not welcome you! Mm, and neither does the Stone Puppet Clan!"

"I agree. Why would I not agree!" Yang Kai stopped and grinned at Mu Na. “Since Patriarch has shown such goodwill, if this Junior still pushed back and refused, he would truly fail to appreciate your kindness.”

Mu Na lightly snorted and gave him a look that said, at least you know what's good for you.

Yang Kai's expression turned solemn again. "However, there is one matter I must make Patriarch aware of."

"What?"

“If those two clansmen of yours fall into my hands, I’m afraid they will not easily see the light of day again.” If he really brought two Wood Spirits over, he would definitely throw them into the medicine garden in the Small Xuan Realm. Before he had the strength to protect the Everlasting Tree, he absolutely would not let them leave easily.

Mu Na frowned. "What, are you going to restrict their freedom?"

"I have no choice. I ask Patriarch to forgive me."

Mu Na said thoughtfully, "It seems that thing is indeed extraordinary."

Yang Kai said seriously, "If Patriarch does not mind this, then this Junior would be more than willing. Of course, I will not mistreat them either. Patriarch may rest assured on that point."

"I am not worried about that." To Wood Spirits, the greatest joy in life was keeping company with precious plants. Yang Kai had the Firmament Tree in his possession, and even something more precious than the Firmament Tree. Any Wood Spirit would probably be willing to remain by his side and serve him. Besides, since Mu Na had made such a proposal, she had clearly anticipated this already. She immediately nodded. "No problem."

Yang Kai grinned. "Many thanks for Patriarch's kindness!" The trace of displeasure he had felt toward Mu Na earlier had long since vanished. Suddenly, he grew bashful and hesitant. "Patriarch, are two clansmen not a little too few? If possible, could you send seven or eight over?"

The Elder's eyes widened from where he stood to the side, while Mu Na snapped, "Scram!"

"I was only asking. If you do not agree, then forget it. Why curse at people?" Yang Kai tilted his head and muttered.

"Stop pretending to be well behaved after taking advantage!" Mu Na said angrily.

Yang Kai raised his head as if nothing had happened. "Patriarch, please summon two clansmen over quickly. I still have important matters to attend to and cannot remain here for long." He did not want to waste even a moment, afraid Mu Na might go back on her word. No matter what, he had to coax two Wood Spirits away first.

Mu Na was annoyed, but she still raised her hand and clapped.

After two sharp claps, two Wood Spirits soon flew in from outside the house. After saluting Mu Na, both looked toward Yang Kai, clearly prepared for this already.

Yang Kai took a closer look and immediately brightened. One of the two Wood Spirits was an acquaintance, the very one who had gone to welcome him yesterday. She seemed extremely shy; when their eyes met, her face flushed red in an instant, and she looked around uneasily.

The other was also a female Wood Spirit, but she was poised and at ease. When Yang Kai looked over, she pursed her lips in a smile, immediately adding three parts of brilliance to heaven and earth.

"Mu Zhu, Mu Lu, from now on, you will follow this boy and properly help him care for that... thing." Mu Na instructed.

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu answered together, "Yes!"

Mu Na turned her head again to look at Yang Kai and said, "I am handing them over to you. If you dare let them suffer any grievance, or if they are hurt anywhere, I will hold you responsible!"

"Patriarch, rest assured, I absolutely will not let them lose a single hair!" Yang Kai patted his chest as a guarantee. His eyes shone as he looked toward Mu Zhu and Mu Lu. Tsk tsk, from now on these two Wood Spirits belonged to him. Truly wonderful.

"Which of you is Mu Zhu, and which is Mu Lu?" Yang Kai asked with a bright, harmless smile.

The poised Wood Spirit gave a graceful salute. "I am Mu Zhu." She reached out and pointed at her companion, whose face was still red. "She is Mu Lu."

"Mm, mm!" Yang Kai nodded repeatedly and comforted them gently. "I will send you to a place for now. Do not be afraid!"

As he spoke, he reached out toward them.

His five fingers clenched, and the two Wood Spirits immediately vanished.

Mu Na flew into a rage. She raised both hands, and vines as thick as a child's arm instantly appeared inside the tree hollow. They shot over like arrows loosed from the bow and wrapped Yang Kai into a rice dumpling in the blink of an eye. The Wood Spirit Race merely disliked fighting; that did not mean they lacked the power to fight. Their small, exquisite bodies contained strength nearly comparable to that of other races. Mu Na's ability surpassed that of an ordinary Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master, and although she might still be below a Divine Spirit, she was by no means weak.

"What did you do to them? Spit them out right now!"

"Calm down! Calm down!" Yang Kai broke out in a cold sweat. The way she said that made him sound like some vicious beast that ate people without spitting out the bones.

"Where are they?" Mu Na had watched two clansmen vanish before her eyes, without even the slightest trace of aura remaining, so how could she calm down? She had only just handed them to him, and now no one knew whether they were dead or alive. What would happen in the future at this rate?

"In a safe place!" As Yang Kai spoke, the flesh all over his body began to shift, and the Laws of Space surged. He gave a slight shake, and the vines binding him suddenly seemed to be sliced apart by thousands upon thousands of knives, turning into countless flecks of powder that drifted down in a flurry.

Mu Na's eyes narrowed as she watched. This power...

The Elder also stared at Yang Kai in shock. He and Mu Na had lived together day and night for several thousand, perhaps even tens of thousands of years, so he knew better than anyone how strong those vines' binding power was. He himself might be able to rely on brute force to break free, but he absolutely could not do it as effortlessly as Yang Kai had.

Before Fan Wu and the other two arrived, Yang Kai had been immersed in comprehending the Laws of Space and had faintly gained some insight, though it was not yet complete. Now, after a small test, he discovered that his control over Space Force had indeed reached another level. If he were given a little more time to perfect it, he might well develop another new Divine Ability.

However, now was not the time to consider such things. If he did not explain what had happened to Mu Zhu and Mu Lu, Mu Na definitely would not let the matter rest.

"Patriarch, you know I am proficient in the Laws of Space, so there is nothing strange about me having a Space Secret Treasure." As Yang Kai spoke, he took out the Xuan Realm Pearl.

In earlier years, he had hidden the Xuan Realm Pearl carefully because his strength had been insufficient, and there were times when he needed to rely on it. But now that he had the ability to refine a world himself, there was no need to treat the Xuan Realm Pearl as too important.

Light flashed over the Xuan Realm Pearl, and the bewildered Mu Zhu and Mu Lu appeared again.

"A world unto itself!" the Elder cried out involuntarily.

A strange light also appeared in Mu Na's eyes, and realization dawned. "The Firmament Tree and that thing are both inside?"

"Exactly." Yang Kai suddenly grinned. "Does Patriarch want to go in and have a look?"

Mu Na immediately looked wary. "If I go in, I am afraid I will not be able to come out, right?"

"How could that be?" Yang Kai laughed dryly. He really did have that plan. As Patriarch, Mu Na's skill in cultivating spirit flowers and rare grasses was far beyond that of ordinary Wood Spirit clansmen. If he could get her into the Small Xuan Realm... But he also knew it was impossible. The Stone Puppet Clan and the Wood Spirits lived in mutual dependence, and often borrowed each other's strength. If he abducted Mu Na, the Stone Puppet Elder would likely be the first to object.

With this, however, Mu Na understood why Yang Kai had previously said they "will not easily see the light of day again." This Space Secret Treasure formed a world of its own. Once someone entered, they would naturally need Yang Kai's consent to come out.

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu were still completely confused. In that instant just now, the world had spun around them and they had suddenly appeared in another world. Before they had time to carefully examine that world's surroundings, they had returned to the tree hollow again.

"You boy really do have plenty of good things on you." Mu Na clicked her tongue in wonder. Space Secret Treasures that could form a world of their own were rare. They had appeared only a few times in the long river of history. Moreover, only in the hands of someone proficient in Space Force could such a thing exert its greatest effect; others could only envy it from afar.

"Does Patriarch have any other concerns?"

"No. Treat them well."

"Rest assured, I absolutely will not let them suffer the slightest grievance." As Yang Kai spoke, he once again took Mu Zhu and Mu Lu into the Small Xuan Realm, cupped his fists, and said, "This Junior thanks Patriarch for your kindness. One day, when I ride the wind and break the waves across the vast seas, I will certainly not forget Patriarch's care today. Mm, it is getting late, so this Junior will take his leave first. We shall meet again."

The Elder said, "Be careful in all things. In the future, if you ever have need of my Stone Puppet Clan, just come over and let us know."

Yang Kai nodded, strode out of the tree house, and returned to where the Space Array was.

The Space Array surged, light flashed over the array, and Yang Kai's figure vanished.

In the Northern Territory, at Lingxiao Palace, the Space Array had already been covered by a palace hall. The array stood right at the center of the hall, while several disciples on duty sat cross-legged nearby in meditation.

This place was Lingxiao Palace's passage to the outside world, so it was naturally extremely important. An Emperor Realm Master guarded it year-round, and inside the palace hall, layer upon layer of Grand Arrays laid down by Nanmen Dajun protected it. If any outsider dared invade, one only needed to activate the Formation Plate to turn the palace hall into a killing ground. Even a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master would find it hard to escape death.

The Emperor Realm Master on duty here today was none other than Bian Yuqing.

After receiving Yang Kai's help at Linghu Palace and breaking through the shackles of the Dao Origin Realm, she had joined Lingxiao Palace. Because she had experience managing Biyu Sect, she had always assisted Lingxiao Palace's Chief Steward Hua Qingsi in managing the Sect. By now, she could be considered Lingxiao Palace's true Second Steward.

Speaking of which, Lingxiao Palace now had many Emperor Realm Masters. The strongest were the three great Yao Kings, along with Imperial-Grade Pill Master Ji Ying, Imperial-Grade Formation Master Nanmen Dajun, and Imperial-Grade Artifact Refiner Hou Yu. After them came Hua Qingsi and Bian Yuqing, while Ye Hen, the former Sect Master of Thousand Leaves Sect, had also emerged from retreat three years ago and advanced to the Emperor Realm. It could be said that although Lingxiao Palace did not have many people, its proportion of Emperor Realm Masters was terrifyingly high. In this world, there was probably no other Sect that could match it.
