## Chapter 3209 — Over a Hundred Thousand

At the sound of the call, Shan Qingluo froze, then turned to look. Their eyes met, and she cried excitedly, "Mother!"

Back then, she and Biluo had left the Zhongdu World through an extremely strange altar and been teleported straight to Di Chen Star. If Chi Yue had not taken such care of them, even accepting her as an adoptive daughter, their strength at the time would likely have brought them disaster long ago.

To Shan Qingluo, Chi Yue was not only her savior, but also like a second parent. Before coming here, she had known this trip would let her see Chi Yue, and her heart had been full of anticipation.

On the other side, Ai Ou stared at Xue Yue in a daze. Her eyes reddened as she called softly, "Father!"

Ai Ou forced down his excitement, nodded, and said, "Good, good, good. As long as you're safe." He glanced at Yang Kai, all his previous resentment already vanished like smoke. The man had returned to the Star Domain and brought his daughter over. What else was there to say?

Although Su Yan and Xia Ningshang had no relatives to reunite with, they had also not seen Ghost Ancestor for many years, so they naturally stepped forward to salute him.

Gu Cangyun watched eagerly, then asked in a hesitant voice, "Palace Master, you returned to the Star Domain?" Chi Yue and Ai Ou could call Yang Kai by name, or even call him boy, but Gu Cangyun did not have that kind of courage, so he addressed him properly as Palace Master.

"Yes. I just returned from there." Yang Kai smiled faintly.

"Then, I wonder, my Sword Alliance..." Gu Cangyun looked at Yang Kai with anticipation written all over his face.

"Senior Gu, rest assured. I brought Brother Gu over on this trip as well, along with quite a few people from your Sword Alliance."

"Where are they?" Gu Cangyun's eyes lit up.

"No rush. You will see them shortly." More than a hundred thousand people were crowded into a certain area of the Small Xuan Realm, and Yang Kai could not find Gu Jianxin or the people of Sword Alliance for the moment. The reason he had been able to summon Su Yan and the others out so easily was naturally because they had not been staying in the same place as everyone else.

"Dajun, bring me the Mountain Wave Diagram," Yang Kai said, raising a hand.

Nanmen Dajun did not know what he intended, but he did not hesitate. He quickly took out the Mountain Wave Diagram and opened it. In an instant, light rose from the diagram, and a Lingxiao Palace shrunk countless times over appeared before their eyes. Mountain ranges rolled across it in layered waves, and even the smallest stream and hill were shown with perfect clarity.

Since this thing had been made by Nanmen Dajun and Hou Yu, it was naturally marvelous to the extreme.

Yang Kai pointed at several peaks and asked, "People are already living in all these places, right?"

Bian Yuqing leaned over for a look and said, "That is right. Thousand Leaves Peak is where Old Master Ye Hen settled with the people of Thousand Leaves Sect, and Five Saints Peak is where Chi Yue and the others live..." She had been in Lingxiao Palace for more than ten years now, so she naturally knew the distribution of people inside the palace like the back of her hand.

Yang Kai nodded. "Many people will come out later. I need you to settle them in."

"How many?" Bian Yuqing asked.

"More than a hundred thousand!"

Everyone sucked in a breath. Lingxiao Palace had never had many people, and Yang Kai had never planned to widely accept disciples. Ever since Lingxiao Palace had been established, it seemed to have kept its mountain gates closed. None of them had known Yang Kai's intentions until this moment, when they finally understood that he had been preparing for today.

"If there are that many people, I will need help," Bian Yuqing said.

"Mm, call the people from Thousand Leaves Sect over." Yang Kai nodded. "I will get them out first."

Bian Yuqing immediately took out her Transmission Compass and poured her Divine Sense into it, clearly summoning manpower.

Yang Kai, meanwhile, summoned the Xuan Realm Pearl. Wrapped in black Qi, the enormous, tadpole-like Gungun leisurely appeared and opened its dark mouth, stretching across heaven and earth.

The terrifying aura spreading from Gungun made even the three great Yao Kings' expressions shift slightly. They did not even know what this thing was. It was no creature of flesh and blood, yet it gave off an extremely dangerous feeling.

A figure was the first to rush out of that great mouth. He looked around, shock covering his face, then stood rooted to the spot in a daze. It was none other than Meng Wuya.

The rich World Energy around him almost made him think he had stepped into a dream. In the Star Domain, the area where Dark Star's Lingxiao Sect stood was already among the best for Spiritual Qi, but compared to this place, it was simply the difference between earth and heaven.

He did not even need to circulate his Secret Art; the Spiritual Qi burrowed into his body through his pores, leaving both body and mind refreshed.

Another figure walked out of Gungun's mouth, then a third, a fourth...

Every one of them reacted the same way.

Yang Kai called out, "Everyone, don't stop there. Keep moving forward. There are still people behind you who need to come out."

With his reminder, the first group to emerge finally came back to their senses. They looked at one another and saw the excitement in each other's eyes. They had long since heard that this trip was to follow Yang Kai to a higher-level world called the Star Realm. From the looks of it now, they had clearly arrived safely.

They had simply never imagined that this so-called Star Realm would have such a superior cultivation environment.

Ordinary places in the Star Realm naturally were not like this. Even if they were better than the Star Domain, the difference would not be so vast. But this was Lingxiao Palace, the former site of Wenqing Sect, one of the top blessed lands in the Northern Territory. How could its Spiritual Qi not be rich?

People streamed endlessly out of Gungun's mouth. Before long, the square and the sky above it were already packed with people.

At that moment, the people of Thousand Leaves Sect, led by Ye Hen, also hurried over together. There were not many of them, only a few hundred, and the moment they saw this scene, they too were greatly shocked.

After asking around, they learned that these people had actually all come from a Lower Plane Star Domain, and they immediately looked at Yang Kai as though he were a heavenly being.

To cultivators of the Star Realm, anyone who came from a Lower Plane Star Domain was an elite among elites. As long as they did not die halfway, they had a great chance of reaching the Emperor Realm. After all, the cultivation environment of Lower Plane Star Domains was far inferior to the Star Realm's, and such harsh conditions forged their potential.

It was not as though the Star Realm had no cultivators from Lower Plane Star Domains, but they had always appeared only in scattered handfuls. When had there ever been such a grand sight?

Tens of thousands of cultivators appeared in the square, and still more continued walking out. Although their current strength could not be considered high, given enough time, they would surely become Lingxiao Palace's foundation.

Yang Kai no longer had any time to chat. He stood together with Bian Yuqing, pointing here and there at Lingxiao Palace's Mountain Wave Diagram as they discussed matters nonstop, dividing territories and mountain peaks for these people to move into.

One Thousand Leaves Sect disciple after another received orders and left, each leading one or two thousand people toward a certain mountain peak.

This bustle lasted several days. During that time, Hua Qingsi indeed returned from the Southern Territory, and with her help, everything proceeded more and more smoothly. Hua Qingsi looked both happy and troubled, and every so often, the look she gave Yang Kai was full of resentment.

In the past, although Yang Kai had personally appointed her as Lingxiao Palace's Chief Steward, strictly speaking, there had not been much for her to handle. Lingxiao Palace had never had many people, and most of them were Thousand Leaves Sect disciples. With Ye Hen presiding over Thousand Leaves Peak, they could manage themselves without issue.

Over the years, Hua Qingsi had spent most of her effort finding ways to earn cultivation materials for Lingxiao Palace. Her cooperation with Ziyuan Commerce Guild in the Southern Territory had indeed filled her coffers, but so what if she earned more? Without disciples to use them, cultivation materials were only numbers on a ledger.

In other words, her position as Chief Steward had been somewhat undeserved.

Now things were different. Lingxiao Palace had suddenly taken in more than a hundred thousand disciples, and every one of them needed her to coordinate matters, arrange the Secret Arts they would cultivate, and allocate the materials they would need. Hua Qingsi could almost foresee that, for at least the next half a year, she likely would not have a single moment to rest.

The title of Chief Steward was now deserved, but this was also... far too exhausting, wasn't it? Fortunately, Bian Yuqing was there to help share her burdens. Otherwise, she really would have thrown the job aside. Even so, the two of them absolutely could not manage the daily lives of more than a hundred thousand people by themselves; they still needed to come up with a concrete plan.

The last batch to walk out of the Small Xuan Realm was a group of yao, precisely the elites from Di Chen Star. There were quite a few of them, roughly three thousand in all, led by Yu Xiong, and every one of them had Yao Qi soaring to the sky.

Yang Kai beckoned and said, "Yao Kings, I will leave them to you."

The yao cultivated differently from humans, so having the three great Yao Kings teach them was naturally the best choice.

Yingfei, Xi Lei, and Xie Wuwei smiled amiably, their gazes sweeping again and again over those three thousand yao, immediately sending chills through every one of them.

They could be considered outstanding figures on Di Chen Star, elites who had survived the war with Myriad Beasts Mountain Manor, but before the auras the three great Yao Kings revealed, they felt no different from infants. No one knew exactly what realm the three great Yao Kings had reached, but none of them doubted that any one of these three yao could casually blow out a breath and reduce them to dregs.

"At last, something to do." Xi Lei chuckled, his laughter rumbling like thunder.

Like Hua Qingsi, the three of them had been idle ever since coming to Lingxiao Palace. Their greatest amusement had been playing hide-and-seek with Hou Yu, but after they captured her and dragged her back countless times, Hou Yu had also given up on escaping. Now she devoted herself entirely to refining Secret Treasures for Lingxiao Palace.

In her own words, she was going to squander Lingxiao Palace's materials with all her might, just to make Yang Kai's heart ache. Even so, she would not disgrace her reputation as an Emperor Artifact Master. The Secret Treasures refined by her hands were naturally all the finest among Supreme-Level goods.

By now, the finished Secret Treasures piled up in Lingxiao Palace's Treasure Vault were simply countless, and of every grade. If she knew that Yang Kai had brought more than a hundred thousand disciples here today, giving those Secret Treasures a place to be used, she probably would not have the heart to keep squandering materials.

And without Hou Yu, the three great Yao Kings were even more bored.

Yang Kai handing three thousand yao over to them suited them perfectly. At least they could do something for Yang Kai and would not just be staying here eating for free.

"Little ones, follow me." Xi Lei waved and flew first toward a great mountain deep inside Lingxiao Palace.

The three thousand yao looked at one another, some of them still unable to make sense of the situation.

Chi Yue shouted sharply, "What are you standing there for? Get over there, all of you."

Only then did the many yao notice Chi Yue's presence, and they were both surprised and delighted. Back then, before Chi Yue left the Star Domain, she had been the only Yao King on Di Chen Star. It was just that the weight carried by her title of Yao King could not compare with Xi Lei and the others.

But at her command, the three thousand yao still obediently followed Xi Lei away in a mighty procession, making for quite a spectacular sight.
