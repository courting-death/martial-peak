## Chapter 3297 — Keeping Out of It

So if one really traced the relationship, the late Wu An and Tang Sheng were fellow disciples, which meant Ling Yinqin belonged to the same generation as Tang Sheng and stood one generation above Lan He.

When Ling Yinqin first arrived at Heavenly Wolf Valley, she had only been at the Third Stage of the Dao Origin Realm. Over the years, however, Heavenly Wolf Valley had not treated her poorly, and with her own long accumulation finally bearing fruit, she had advanced to the Emperor Realm a few years ago and been promoted by Tang Sheng to Elder.

Lan He said, "Back in the Shattered Star Sea, this disciple met someone. He was the one who told this disciple about Elder Ling. After leaving the Shattered Star Sea, this disciple sent people to inquire after Elder Ling's whereabouts. By coincidence, it did not take long to learn where she was, and we brought her into the valley."

Tang Sheng nodded. "There was such a matter. What, are you asking your Master for credit?" Having another Emperor Realm cultivator was a great help to Heavenly Wolf Valley, so he assumed Lan He was trying to ask for some reward.

Lan He stuck out her tongue. "Nothing like that. I wanted to say that the person who told me about this back then was named Yang Kai."

Tang Sheng said, "He seemed to have saved you once as well, sending you out of the void crack. You told me about that afterward..." As he spoke, he suddenly seemed to remember something. He turned to look at her. "You said just now that you saw a lifeline. Could it be..."

Lan He smiled with her lips pressed together. "Master's insight is keen. This time, I ran into that Brother Yang."

"He is in the Eastern Territory? Did you not say he was in the Southern Territory?"

"Yes, he is from the Southern Territory, but he came to the Eastern Territory this time on some business."

"Then did you bring him back? He is your life-saving benefactor, after all. Since you ran into him, you ought to thank him properly."

Lan He said, "Of course I brought him back. He is in the guest hall right now."

Hearing this, Tang Sheng immediately stood up and glared at her in annoyance. "You girl, since you invited him here, why did you not say so earlier? Why go around in circles? Since he is your benefactor, as your Master, I should go meet him and offer my thanks as well."

Lan He smiled and said, "I already thanked him."

Tang Sheng said solemnly, "A debt of life-saving grace can never be repaid too much."

Lan He seized the opening. "Actually, he came this time because he has something he wants to ask Master to help with. I was worried Master might not agree, but now that you have said that, I can rest easy."

Tang Sheng's steps faltered, and a faint sense of foreboding rose in him. He turned to Lan He. "Why do I feel like you, girl, are up to no good? Hm, pouring tea, massaging my shoulders and back... unasked-for attentiveness always hides either treachery or theft!"

"How could that be? Is this disciple that sort of person in Master's eyes?" Lan He pouted.

Tang Sheng snorted. "Would I not know what sort of person you are?" After a pause, he added, "It seems what he wants this time is no small matter. Speak. What does he want me to help with?"

Lan He hemmed and hawed. "Master..."

Tang Sheng said, "Hurry up and speak, or beware the Sect rules." He lightly tapped Lan He on the head, making her clutch it with both hands.

After hesitating for a moment, Lan He finally said, "He came to the Eastern Territory this time because he wants to make a trip to Spirit Beast Island. Unfortunately, he cannot find a way there."

"Spirit Beast Island..." Tang Sheng's expression shifted slightly, clearly realizing something. He nodded. "I understand."

Spirit Beast Island was elusive and impossible to trace. Ordinary people truly could not find it without knowing the way. Let alone Yang Kai, an outsider, even people from the Eastern Territory did not know exactly where Spirit Beast Island was. Others might not know, but Heavenly Wolf Valley had a way to find it, because years ago a Beast-Seeking Envoy from Spirit Beast Island had left behind a token. With that token in hand, one could travel to the East Sea and naturally locate the general area of Spirit Beast Island.

Smiling faintly, he said, "It is a small matter. Since he has done you a kindness, your Master will personally accompany him and make certain he finds Spirit Beast Island." Although that token was Heavenly Wolf Valley's greatest reliance, merely using it to search for Spirit Beast Island's location did not count as calling in Spirit Beast Island's favor, so Heavenly Wolf Valley would lose nothing. If Heavenly Wolf Valley truly encountered danger in the future, the token could still play its role. It would not be bad to repay Yang Kai's favor this way.

Moreover, he could use the chance to befriend this person a little. Tang Sheng had heard Lan He say before that the young man was powerful, gifted, and, rarer still, proficient in the Laws of Space. His future achievements were bound to be limitless.

Just look at Li Wuyi. It was precisely because he was proficient in the Laws of Space that he had claimed the title of the number one person beneath the Great Emperors. The future Yang Kai might not necessarily be inferior to Li Wuyi. If Tang Sheng could use this chance to build a good relationship with him, it would also benefit Heavenly Wolf Valley in the future.

As these thoughts flashed through his mind, Tang Sheng had already reached a decision. This was not because he was especially calculating, only that such considerations were normal. Moreover, as the Valley Lord of Heavenly Wolf Valley, his heart was tied to the entire Sect, so naturally he had more to think about.

Hearing this, however, Lan He showed no joy. Instead, she looked at him cautiously and said haltingly, "If it were only that, things would be fine. But right now, Brother Yang's situation is not very good."

"Hm?" Tang Sheng frowned in doubt. "What do you mean?"

Lan He said, "Honored Master, have you forgotten? He killed quite a few people in the Shattered Star Sea, including the two Holy Sons of Brahma Heaven Sacred Land, Chang Hao and Chang Xian..."

Tang Sheng's expression shifted. "Brahma Heaven Sacred Land is making trouble for him?"

"And Netherworld Sect..." Lan He's voice grew even quieter.

"What?" This time, Tang Sheng's expression changed dramatically.

One top force making trouble for Yang Kai would have been bad enough, but now two top forces were after him together? How could he possibly survive that? Others would avoid such a hot potato at all costs, so who would be foolish enough to bring him into their own home?

Tang Sheng truly did not know what to say to Lan He. In his anger, he could only raise his hand and point at her again and again.

Lan He said pitifully, "Master, did you not say that even a drop of kindness should be repaid with a gushing spring? What more when he saved this disciple's life? Back then, if he had not sent me out of that Void Crack, this disciple would have died long ago."

Tang Sheng said bitterly, "Those words are not wrong, but timing matters too. With the current situation... you are drawing fire onto my Heavenly Wolf Valley."

Lan He pouted. "Does our Heavenly Wolf Valley not have Spirit Beast Island's token? Those two great forces may be powerful, but surely they would not dare act against us, right?"

Tang Sheng said in exasperation, "They truly would not dare act against us, but do not forget that Heavenly Wolf Valley is in the Eastern Territory, and we are constrained by all sides in everything we do. We cannot grow blindly arrogant just because we have Spirit Beast Island's token. If we anger those two great forces, will my Heavenly Wolf Valley have any peaceful days in the future? What safety would there be for disciples who go out to temper themselves? You girl, you really infuriate me."

Lan He looked lost. "Then what should we do?"

Tang Sheng paced back and forth. After a moment of thought, a decisive glint flashed through his eyes. "Now we can only deny the matter of the token, distance my Heavenly Wolf Valley from this affair, and have him leave as soon as possible."

"But I already promised him." Lan He stared at Tang Sheng in disbelief, as if she had never expected her Honored Master to make such a decision. For a moment, she felt as though she hardly recognized him.

Tang Sheng shook his head. "Your promise does not decide the matter. This Lord is the one who governs Heavenly Wolf Valley."

Lan He was shocked. "Master, are you asking your disciple to become faithless and unrighteous?"

"Impudent!" Tang Sheng turned his head and snapped, his face stern with authority. Then his tone suddenly softened. "Little He, you must understand your Master. You must think more about the future of Heavenly Wolf Valley's several thousand disciples. If there were any other way, your Master would not want to do this either, but helping and sheltering him would be the same as making enemies of those two top forces. My Heavenly Wolf Valley is rooted in the Eastern Territory. How would we survive in the future? You are not someone who fails to understand the greater picture. You should understand this."

Lan He opened her mouth, but in the end, not a single word came out. Bitterness filled her face.

Now that matters had reached this point, she no longer knew how she should face Yang Kai. After all, she had already given her word, yet now she had no way to fulfill her promise. Who knew what Yang Kai would think of her?

But she also knew that once Master had made his decision, nothing she said could change it. After thinking for a moment, she said, "Master, can you let me keep that token for a while? I will take him away from Heavenly Wolf Valley right now and accompany him to find Spirit Beast Island. Once we find Spirit Beast Island, I will come back."

Tang Sheng shook his head. "If those two great forces were not involved, this might not be impossible, but since they are looking for trouble with Yang Kai, you absolutely cannot do this. They will not spare you on account of my face. What if something happens to you?"

"But..."

"There is no need to say more about this." Tang Sheng raised his hand to stop her. "Come with your Master now to meet that Yang Kai."

Lan He followed behind him in a daze.

Halfway there, Tang Sheng took out a Transmission Compass and communicated with someone. A moment later, a woman in palace dress flew over. She was Qian Xiuying, the Vice Valley Lord of Heavenly Wolf Valley, as well as Tang Sheng's wife.

Qian Xiuying glanced at Lan He and smiled. "What is wrong with Little He? You look so listless. Did your Master scold you again? Tell me what happened, and your Master's Wife will stand up for you."

Lan He lowered her head and said nothing.

Tang Sheng sighed and briefly explained what had happened, then told her his decision.

Only then did Qian Xiuying understand why Lan He was so gloomy. After pondering for a moment, she said, "This matter is indeed somewhat troublesome."

In her heart, she also felt somewhat resentful toward Yang Kai. Fine, you killed the two Holy Sons of someone else's force and even killed a personal disciple of Netherworld Sect's Sect Master, but could you not just stay quietly in the Southern Territory? Why insist on running to the Eastern Territory? And since you came, could you not at least hide your tracks a little? Yet the moment you showed your face, two top forces set their sights on you, and now Heavenly Wolf Valley had been dragged into the mess as well. Who had Heavenly Wolf Valley offended?

Although she thought this, none of it showed on her face. Qian Xiuying looked at Lan He and asked, "Did he say why he wants to go to Spirit Beast Island? Does he have any old acquaintances there?"

Lan He slowly shook her head. "This disciple does not know."

Tang Sheng said, "Since that Yang Kai is proficient in Space Force, he likely wants to go to Spirit Beast Island for no other reason than to seek guidance from Sir Li Wuyi."

Qian Xiuying nodded. "That makes sense. In that case, he has no special relationship with Spirit Beast Island either."
