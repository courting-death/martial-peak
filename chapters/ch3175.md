## Chapter 3175 — Another One Surnamed Wu

"Trying to run?" A furious voice rang out from far behind him. The surrounding Void seemed to freeze solid in that instant, and Yang Kai, already leaping into the darkness, jerked to a halt. He was pinned in place only a few chi from the darkness, so close he had nearly escaped.

He turned his head and saw the middle-aged man he had blasted away flickering through the air, closing in on him at terrifying speed.

He had always been the one using Space Divine Abilities to imprison others. He had never imagined the day would come when someone else imprisoned him.

The middle-aged man had not mastered Space Divine Abilities, but as the Master of this Star Domain, he could naturally mobilize its countless Laws and powers. Fighting him in this Starry Sky was every bit as disadvantageous as expected.

After suffering that hidden loss, the middle-aged man's face looked extremely grim, yet his eyes blazed with excitement. He clearly meant to settle this once and for all by leaving Yang Kai here. This truly was a Heaven-sent opportunity. If he missed it, he might never have another chance to swallow Hengluo Star Domain.

"Go!" Yang Kai hurled Li Jiao and Lu Sanniang, whom he had been holding in his hands, into the darkness while pushing his brute strength and the Laws of Space to their limits.

Kacha kacha...

Tiny cracks appeared throughout the surrounding space, darting about like fish, and his imprisoned body finally won the slightest bit of freedom.

He clenched his one intact fist. Something was already hidden in his palm, and all he could do was pray his guess was right. Otherwise, disaster was likely upon him this time.

"Your Excellency truly is righteous and heroic, your spirit soaring to the heavens, but this course of action is simply idiotic." The middle-aged man could not restrain the joy in his heart. In just a few steps, he pounced right up to Yang Kai. Although Yang Kai's previous punch had been powerful, it had caused him no real harm.

As the Master of this Star Domain, standing within this Star Domain, he had countless ways to dissolve the force of that punch. At most, his Qi and blood had churned slightly. Yang Kai, on the other hand, had lost the use of one arm and now delivered the other to him.

So he raised his hand, formed a fist, and prepared to cripple this arm as well.

Their fists collided again. The smile on the middle-aged man's face froze, and his whole body once more became a meteor, whooshing away as he was blasted back. His eyes went blank, filled with incomprehension, and only one question churned through his mind.

How? How was this possible!

The first punch, the one he had failed to withstand, could still be explained by his own carelessness and underestimation. On top of that, Yang Kai had just crossed over from his own Star Domain and could still borrow a trace of that Star Domain's power.

But there was no possible way the second punch should have had the same effect. He had already sealed the space around Yang Kai and cut off his connection to that Star Domain! No matter what, Yang Kai should not have been able to use the power of a Star Domain again.

Yet from Yang Kai's fist, the middle-aged man had clearly felt the power of an entire world crashing toward him. It was somewhat different from the first punch, but also extremely similar.

"Hahahahaha!" Yang Kai laughed wildly, and the sound nearly made the middle-aged man's lungs burst with anger.

It felt like an adult fighting a child, only to be thrown flat on his back again and again. The frustration was unbearable. Of course, Yang Kai was no child, but inside this Star Domain, he was no different from one.

Then an even more infuriating voice rang straight through his mind. "Just you wait. Sooner or later, I'll beat you so badly your own mother won't recognize you."

After throwing down that threat, Yang Kai turned and stepped into the darkness.

By the time the middle-aged man lunged over a second time, there was no trace of Yang Kai. He clenched his fists in hatred, gritted his teeth, and growled, "Yang Kai!"

There had never been any grudge between him and Yang Kai. Their conflict had only arisen from the matter of devouring Star Domains, and when the middle-aged man devised his plan to devour a Star Domain, Hengluo Star Domain had no Master at all. He had merely been trying to devour an unclaimed Star Domain.

In the end, Yang Kai had ruined everything. In other words, Yang Kai had destroyed his plan.

Now, after being toyed with again and again and left in such a sorry state, his anger naturally surged. In his heart, he had already marked Yang Kai as an enemy. Given the chance, he would certainly settle life and death with him.

In Hengluo Star Domain, Yang Kai flashed out, already back in human form.

Li Jiao and Lu Sanniang were standing in the Void, anxiously staring into the darkness.

When they saw him emerge, both were overjoyed and hurried forward to meet him, looking him over with concern.

Then they saw Yang Kai's arms hanging limply at his sides like noodles, and immediately knew he had suffered badly.

"Brother Yang..." Li Jiao's eyes grew damp.

After being captured by that middle-aged man, he had never expected Yang Kai to come save him. He knew that even if he held some place in Yang Kai's heart, he could never compare to an entire Star Domain.

This Star Domain held Yang Kai's friends, family, and Sect. How could he possibly abandon it for Li Jiao?

Li Jiao had already prepared for the worst. Who could have known Yang Kai would willingly brave such enormous danger and charge into a Star Domain controlled by the enemy to rescue them, and even succeed? Such great kindness could not be overstated even by saying he should repay it in his next life.

Yang Kai looked at him in disgust and said, "My hands hurt enough already. Seeing you like this is making my head hurt too."

Li Jiao's old face flushed red.

Yang Kai added, "I was only giving it a try. If I hadn't managed to rescue you this time, that would have been your fate. Of course, I would have avenged you."

Li Jiao said, "This Li understands. In any case, no matter what, I cannot thank you enough."

Lu Sanniang hesitated, wanting to speak. Yang Kai said, "Yuqin is fine. She is in Lingxiao Sect now. You should go there too."

"What about you?" Li Jiao asked with concern.

Yang Kai said, "I'll stay here and see how things develop. Besides, I still need to heal." After a pause, he added, "Right, do you know who that man is?"

Li Jiao replied, "He calls himself Wu Heng!"

"Another one surnamed Wu?" Yang Kai frowned, his expression turning strange. No one surnamed Wu was any good. The greatest villain in the history of the Star Realm had also been surnamed Wu.

Li Jiao clearly knew who he meant and shook his head. "This man should have no connection to that one. They merely share the same surname by coincidence."

"Best if there is no connection." Yang Kai nodded. If this Wu Heng really was connected to Wu Kuang, that would be a serious problem. The Star Domain could not afford another Heaven Devourer Great Emperor, especially when the original Heaven Devourer Great Emperor was not even dead yet.

Li Jiao did not know Wu Heng's exact origins, so after a few more words Yang Kai sent the two of them back to Dark Star.

Sitting cross-legged in the Void, Yang Kai urged his own strength to restore his arms.

Both arms had been shattered. Although he had just left Wu Heng in a sorry state, he had not escaped without paying a price. All kinds of Laws coursed through his arms, wantonly destroying his flesh and meridians. With such terrifying injuries, anyone else's arms would likely have been crippled for good.

Fortunately, Yang Kai's physique was extraordinary and his recovery ability was extremely strong. Now that he had returned to his own territory, he could borrow the power of this world and draw those many Laws out of his body with ease, letting them disperse into the Void. Not only had he avoided losing much, he had even added a little energy to his own Star Domain, though the amount was insignificant.

Only after a long while did his arms rise slightly.

The hidden danger had been removed. For the rest, he only had to recover slowly, and it should not take long before he was back to normal.

He took out some healing Pills and swallowed them, shortening the recovery time even further.

His right hand had been clenched tight and had not loosened even earlier. Only now did he open his palm. A bead the size of a dragon eye lay quietly there.

The Xuan Realm Pearl!

His first punch had blasted Wu Heng away because he had caught him off guard. On top of that, he had just crossed over from his own territory, and while standing at the boundary between the two Star Domains, he could still mobilize a little of his Star Domain's power.

But the second punch had not relied on Star Domain power. He had succeeded by using the Xuan Realm Pearl!

If a Star Domain was a world, then the Xuan Realm Pearl was also a world. They simply differed in size. Compared with an entire Star Domain, the world inside the Xuan Realm Pearl was like a grandson before his grandfather.

Still, they shared one similarity. Both could provide the power of an entire world.

As the Master of the Xuan Realm Pearl, Yang Kai could naturally mobilize its power. He had once used the World Force of the Xuan Realm Pearl against his enemies, leaving the Small Xuan Realm in ruins and its Laws collapsed. Later, he had gone to the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda in Star God Palace and used the Xuan Realm Pearl to devour a great deal of Origin Power before finally repairing the Xuan Realm Pearl and raising it to another level.

Repeating that old trick this time had also been a last resort. Fortunately, the result did not disappoint him.

Without the Xuan Realm Pearl, even if his second punch could topple mountains and overturn seas, he could forget about forcing Wu Heng back even half a step.

"Heaven bless me, Heaven bless me!" Yang Kai held the Xuan Realm Pearl in both hands and muttered like a lunatic for a while. Only then did determination fill his face, and he immersed his Divine Sense into the Xuan Realm Pearl to investigate.

The corner of his eye twitched at once, and his expression shifted again and again.

After a long while, a miserable howl resounded through the Starry Sky.

As he had expected, the Small Xuan Realm was a complete mess again. The world that had been fully repaired inside the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda had collapsed once more. Its Laws were chaotic, mountains and rivers were overturned, river water flowed backward, and spatial cracks stretched across the Small Xuan Realm like scars upon the world.

At a glance, the whole world looked utterly ruined, as if it might collapse at any moment.

Yang Kai’s heart bled. This trip to save Li Jiao had truly cost him dearly, but fortunately, the man had at least been rescued.

Moreover, the greatest fortune amid misfortune was that the area around the medicine garden had not suffered much damage. The medicine garden was the most important part of the Small Xuan Realm.

This was partly because he had deliberately protected it, and partly because the Everlasting Tree and the Firmament Tree were planted there.

Both the Everlasting Tree and the Firmament Tree were unique in all the world. Their very existence could not be destroyed, so the place where they grew was naturally incomparably sturdy.

A moment later, Yang Kai withdrew his mind from the Small Xuan Realm and returned to normal.

Although the Small Xuan Realm had been badly damaged, it had not been destroyed, so there was still a chance to repair it. In the future, he would see if there was another opportunity to enter the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda. But when he remembered the circumstances of his departure from Star God Palace back then, he dismissed the idea.

The last time he had entered the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda, he had caused Star God Palace a great deal of trouble. Radiant Moon Great Emperor would probably never graciously permit him to enter again.

Putting away the Xuan Realm Pearl, Yang Kai looked toward the darkness ahead. For now, the most urgent matter was still to resolve the trouble before him.
