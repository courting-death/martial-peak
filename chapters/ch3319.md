## Chapter 3319 - Cherish It While You Can

As his thoughts turned, Tang Sheng relaxed.

Off to the side, Cang Mo gave a bitter smile. "Since he is someone from Spirit Beast Island, this matter was indeed my fault. I hope Brother Li and Lady Jiu Feng can forgive me."

He might be a Quasi-Great Emperor, but he was not a stubborn fool. Given the present situation, he was no longer at his peak, Yang Kai still had that evil spirit helping him, and even Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng had arrived. No matter what, there was no way he could do anything to Yang Kai this time, so he admitted his mistake readily, lowered his stance, and gave Spirit Beast Island all due face.

He had to give them face. The Beast Great Emperor was not someone he could afford to offend. Although he belonged to Star Court, the ten Great Emperors took turns overseeing Star Court. Perhaps one day, he would have to serve under the Beast Great Emperor.

Li Wuyi nodded. "Fortunately, nothing serious happened." Although Cang Mo and Yang Kai both looked wretched and neither one's injuries were light, neither was in mortal danger. That alone was a blessing amid misfortune.

Still, he could not understand why Cang Mo had gone looking for trouble with Yang Kai. His earlier remark about killing for treasure clearly meant Yang Kai had something Cang Mo coveted. Li Wuyi glanced at the Mountain and River Bell in Yang Kai's hand, and something clicked. He vaguely understood, suspecting that this Mountain and River Bell might have been what brought Yang Kai this trouble.

But this fellow was extraordinary too. He had actually traded blows with Cang Mo and survived. The last time Li Wuyi met him on Dragon Isle, he had sensed that this boy was recklessly bold, daring to charge into a dragon's pool and tiger's den with his meager cultivation. Now that they met again, he was still the same.

This boy could cause even more trouble than I could when I was young... I only wonder whether this matter can still end peacefully.

Just as he was thinking this, he suddenly heard Yang Kai ask, "Eldest Brother Li, you know this fellow?"

Li Wuyi nodded. "We've known each other for many years."

Yang Kai nodded lightly. "Then Eldest Brother Li should keep your eyes open when making friends in the future. Some people may show one face while hiding another heart. They're used to playing despicable, shameless tricks, and who knows, you might get plotted against one day."

Cang Mo's face chilled. Being mocked to his face by Yang Kai naturally left him displeased, but he had no intention of explaining himself. Li Wuyi also looked rather embarrassed by those words. He realized he truly should not have come here today. Caught in the middle like this, he could hardly please either side. Irritated, he could not help shooting a glare at that flamboyant old man. If not for him, why would he have tracked him all the way here?

Yang Kai's expression turned solemn, and he asked in a low voice, "If I want to kill him, Eldest Brother Li, will you help me or help him?"

Li Wuyi pondered for a moment before saying, "If you insist, then this Li can only stand aside and watch."

Yang Kai could be considered half a member of Spirit Beast Island, while Li Wuyi himself also had some friendship with Cang Mo. If the two of them truly fought to the death, helping either side would be inappropriate, so he could only stay out of it. Of course, the best outcome would be to turn a major matter into a minor one, and a minor one into nothing at all. But seeing Yang Kai's murderous look, he knew persuasion would probably be useless at a time like this, so he simply stated his position outright.

"Good!" Yang Kai barked, then bared his teeth in a ferocious grin. "Those words from Eldest Brother Li are enough."

Li Wuyi was taken aback. Did this boy think I would help Cang Mo, so he blocked me with words first? But with his current strength, how could he possibly kill Cang Mo?

Yang Kai's expression was cold and severe. He turned and glanced at the flamboyant old man standing beside him. The other party seemed oblivious, lowering his gaze as if absorbed in meditation. Yang Kai's mouth twitched. This old fellow was probably not someone he could count on.

Although he did not know why the old man had sought his protection, the man had clearly said just now that killing Cang Mo would be difficult, and that he was unwilling to provoke the Iron Blood Great Emperor. They had no ties between them, so asking him to help kill a Quasi-Great Emperor would almost certainly be refused.

With only himself and his Incarnation joining hands, there was no way they could finish off Cang Mo.

Yang Kai sighed inwardly. Letting such a fine opportunity pass left him somewhat unwilling, but since matters had reached this point, he did not want to keep making a scene. Changing tack, he said, "Since Eldest Brother Li has a decent friendship with this person, I'll spare his life today on Eldest Brother Li's account. Cherish it while you can, old dog!"

Cang Mo's face darkened, and he laughed from sheer anger. "Spare this Lord's life? Boy, your tone is higher than the heavens. Aren't you afraid of spraining your tongue?"

Yang Kai's expression turned fierce. "If you're looking to die, I can oblige you."

Cang Mo still wanted to say something, but Li Wuyi suddenly said, "Brother Cang, no matter who was right or wrong today, let it end here."

Since Yang Kai already intended to turn a major matter into a minor one and a minor one into nothing, how could Li Wuyi stand by and watch the two sides flare up again? Both sides stopping here was the outcome he most wanted to see. Yang Kai was young and hot-blooded, so persuading him would not be easy. Cang Mo was different. He could not possibly fail to understand that enduring for a moment could avert greater trouble. So Li Wuyi did not try to persuade Yang Kai, and instead directed his words at Cang Mo.

Cang Mo did give him face, or perhaps simply took the step offered to him. Hearing this, he nodded and said, "Since Brother Li has said so, I won't lower myself to argue with him." After a pause, he cupped his fists. "Today is inconvenient. Another day, I will visit Spirit Beast Island to see Brother Li and Lady Jiu Feng. I will take my leave first."

As he spoke, his figure flickered and shot into the distance, unwilling to remain even a moment longer.

Not far away, the pale-faced Wu Heng saw him leaving and hurried after him. The moment this fellow appeared, he had been struck by Yang Kai's long-prepared Years Like a Shuttle, and the Power of Time had been eating away at him ever since. He had been circulating his strength to resist it the whole time. After that, while Yang Kai and his Incarnation joined hands in a great battle against Cang Mo, they had no attention to spare for him, and he was even less capable of interfering, so he had only watched from nearby. Only now did he finally move.

But wanting to leave did not mean he would get his wish. Something flashed, and a massive figure suddenly blocked his path, cutting off the light overhead. A huge shadow swallowed him whole.

Looking up, he saw Yang Kai's thirty-zhang-tall dragon body standing there, head lowered as he looked down at him. A mocking smile hung at the corner of Yang Kai's mouth, enough to send a chill through anyone.

"You... what do you want?" Wu Heng started and backed away in panic.

A fierce aura was closing in behind him. Without looking, Wu Heng knew it was the Divine Spirit Shi Huo. Hemmed in from front and back, he had no chance of resisting. He had witnessed Yang Kai and his Incarnation battling Cang Mo from beginning to end, and he knew very well that he was no match for these two monsters.

In his panic, even his will to resist grew pitifully weak, and he cried out, "Sir, save me!"

"Not even the Heavenly King himself could save you!" Yang Kai snorted coldly. Today, he had been forced to let Cang Mo go. One could say the circumstances had left him no choice, or that his own strength was lacking. Since Cang Mo had left, there was nothing he could do, and revenge would have to wait for another day. But if Wu Heng escaped too, Yang Kai would never accept it.

Tracing everything back to its source, this man was the true culprit.

If not for him trying to devour Hengluo Star Domain back then, how could that Star Domain have suffered under the cultivators of Great Wilderness Star Domain, leaving countless Cultivation Stars plunged into misery? Likewise, without him, Yang Kai would never have formed a grudge with Cang Mo.

Back in the Star Domain, Yang Kai had already wanted to kill him, but he had no chance then. Now that the opportunity was right in front of him, how could he miss it? As he spoke, his dragon claw reached out and grabbed toward Wu Heng.

Panicked, Wu Heng raised his hand and struck out with a palm. A giant palm print met the dragon claw, and although it made the claw pause slightly, it still could not save him from being seized.

The Laws of Space surged, black light flashed, and Wu Heng vanished.

"Stop!" Only then did Cang Mo's furious shout arrive, but it was clearly already too late. Wu Heng had already been taken into the Xuan Realm Pearl by Yang Kai and suppressed there. What meaning could that shout have now?

Cang Mo, who had been about to flee, had already turned around and was staring at Yang Kai coldly. "What did you do to him?" He suspected Wu Heng might not be dead, because he knew Yang Kai possessed a Secret Treasure that formed a world of its own, and that object was the greatest target of his trip. Judging from everything he had just seen, Wu Heng had very likely been taken into that Secret Treasure.

"What the hell does that have to do with you?" Yang Kai gave an unruly smile. "Are you leaving or not? If not, you can stay here forever."

Cang Mo stared at him coldly, knowing there was no chance of getting Wu Heng back from Yang Kai unless he could defeat him, and that was clearly impossible. He nodded gloomily. "Mountains and rivers will meet again. We will see each other soon."

Yang Kai chuckled. "I'll be looking forward to that day."

A ferocious smile curled Cang Mo's lips, and a meaningful look flashed through his eyes. He turned and flew into the distance, vanishing without a trace in the blink of an eye.

Yang Kai frowned, vaguely feeling that letting Cang Mo leave today had been a grave mistake, though he could not understand why he felt that way. Then again, with his current strength, he truly could not keep Cang Mo here.

After a great battle filled with one upheaval after another, the curtain finally fell.

Inside Heavenly Wolf Valley, the watching crowd only now felt exhaustion wash over them. Although they had not taken part in the great battle, the many dizzying turns had kept their nerves taut the entire time and consumed a great deal of Divine Sense. Even now, everyone still had the same feeling.

That in the next moment, some other expert might tear through space and arrive...

In the end, no such thing happened.

Yang Kai and the Incarnation both returned to their original forms.

Jiu Feng clicked her tongue in wonder as she looked at the Demonic Warhammer in the Incarnation's hand. "Boy Yang, where did you find that thing? Why does it look like a Demonic Weapon?"

Yang Kai laughed. "Elder Sister Jiu Feng's eyes are sharp. It is indeed a Demonic Weapon."

"It really is a Demonic Weapon?" Jiu Feng's expression changed.

Yang Kai said, "No need to worry. This thing can't hurt anyone." As he spoke, he took both the Incarnation and the Demonic Warhammer into the Xuan Realm Pearl, clearly unwilling to say more on the matter.

Jiu Feng's beautiful eyes flashed, and she was just about to offer a few more reminders when Yang Kai said, "Please wait a moment. Allow this Junior Brother to deal with the spoils of battle."

In this battle, quite a few Emperor Realm cultivators had died or been injured. The two top Sects of the Eastern Territory had been almost completely wiped out. Even those still alive were badly wounded. More than thirty Emperor Realm Spatial Rings remained, including the Spatial Rings of Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng, two Sect Master-level figures, so their stores were naturally abundant. Although Yang Kai now commanded a vast household and great enterprise, he naturally would not refuse ready-made gains.
