## Chapter 3338 — Need Your Help

The Dragon Blood Flowers gathered on Dragon Isle were all distributed according to fixed quotas. There were clear rules for what quality of Dragon Blood Flower each bloodline could receive; they were not handed out at random. A Sixth-Tier Giant Dragon like Fu Qi was generally only qualified to take Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers.

Only at the Seventh-Tier would one take Middle-Level flowers, and only at the Eighth-Tier would one take Upper-Level flowers.

As for Tenth-Tier bloodlines like the Great Elder's and Second Elder's, they could only take Supreme-Level flowers. Dragon Blood Flowers below Supreme-Level had no noticeable effect on them, so taking them anyway would only be a waste. It was better to give those flowers to other Dragon Clan members to improve their bloodlines.

Fu Qi had lived for many years, but even counting them all, he had taken fewer than ten Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flowers. Now, on all of Dragon Isle, only a few unranked and Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers remained, so this single Middle-Level stalk stood out all the more, drawing the eye at once.

Yang Kai knew a little about all this, so after hearing Fu Qi's explanation, he said nothing more. Instead, he carefully transplanted the Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flower in his hand into an empty patch of ground before him. A pit still remained there, clearly left from the last time Yang Kai had stolen Dragon Blood Flowers.

Afterward, Yang Kai bent a finger and swiped it across his fingertip, cutting open a tiny wound. Silently circulating his profound art, he forced out a drop of fresh golden blood and let it fall along the roots of the Dragon Blood Flower.

Beside him, Fu Qi's eyes widened, and even his breathing quickened. He stared fixedly at that drop of Golden Blood without looking away for an instant.

Yang Kai actually heard him swallow. He could not help turning to look at him. "Brother Fu Qi, are you hungry?"

"No, no, no..." Fu Qi hastily waved his hands, only for his throat to bob again despite himself.

At first, Yang Kai thought Fu Qi was drooling over the Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flower, but on closer inspection, Fu Qi was staring straight at the wound on his finger. A chill ran through Yang Kai, and without letting it show, he tucked his hand behind his back. What the hell was going on? Could his Essence-Blood be useful even to male members of the Dragon Clan? But after thinking it over, it would not be strange if it was. Zhu Qing had advanced from an Eighth-Tier Dragon Bloodline to the Ninth-Tier because of her intimate relationship with him. In other words, something in his body could rapidly raise a Dragon Clan member's bloodline.

His Golden Blood might truly work on every member of the Dragon Clan.

That was not good... Yang Kai felt a shiver of dread. If that was true, would the Dragon Clan capture him and bleed him dry so everyone could get a share?

Using his own blood to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers and compensate the Dragon Clan for their losses was one thing. But if the Dragon Clan really wanted to drink his blood, Yang Kai still could not quite get past that.

He waved Fu Qi off. "All right, you can go. I can handle things here myself. You do not need to stay and keep me company."

After Fu Qi's reaction just now, how could Yang Kai dare let him stay?

Fu Qi froze for a moment. "But my duty is to guard this place..."

Yang Kai shot him a sidelong look. "What is there to guard on this broken island? A hundred or so Dragon Blood Flowers that barely amount to anything? You think I would pick them and take them away?"

You say that as if you have never picked them before... Fu Qi grumbled inwardly. Aloud, he said, "The Great Elder ordered me to..."

"Are you leaving or not? If you are not leaving, I am."

"You're leaving? Where would you go?" Fu Qi was startled.

"Heaven and Earth are vast. Wherever I please."

Fu Qi cried in alarm, "But Brother Yang, are you not supposed to cultivate the Dragon Blood Flowers? How can you just walk away?"

Yang Kai sighed heavily. "That was my original plan, but someone keeps ruining my mood. Now I have no desire to continue."

Fu Qi's face flushed red. He knew his behavior just now had probably upset Yang Kai. Thinking about it, no wonder. He had actually looked ready to drool over Yang Kai's blood. It had been pure instinct; he knew with absolute clarity that the drop of Golden Blood would be enormously useful to him.

However, with Yang Kai putting it that way, Fu Qi could only say, "I still have to ask the Great Elder for instructions on this matter."

"Then hurry up and ask!" Yang Kai urged, impatience written all over his face.

Fu Qi immediately used a Secret Technique to contact the Great Elder. A moment later, he said dejectedly, "The Great Elder says this place no longer needs to be guarded..."

Yang Kai gave a satisfied grunt. "The Great Elder is reasonable."

Fu Qi said helplessly, "In that case, this Fu will take his leave."

"Go, go." Yang Kai waved his hand.

Fu Qi cast a reluctant glance at the newly transplanted Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flower before finally turning and flying away. He still felt a pang of regret. If only that drop of Golden Blood just now had been given to him. Although he did not know exactly what it would do for him, it would definitely be more effective than consuming a Dragon Blood Flower.

Only after watching Fu Qi leave did Yang Kai finally relax. He stood in place, rubbing his chin as he quietly observed the changes in the Dragon Blood Flower.

He did not know whether it was an illusion, but in just this short while, the Dragon Blood Flower seemed to have changed ever so slightly. A faint golden halo flowed over its crimson blossoms, making it look especially alluring and graceful.

After watching for a while longer, it seemed to have changed again, yet also seemed no different at all.

Yang Kai could not quite make sense of it. Still, since Dragon Blood Flowers needed dragon blood to grow, his Golden Blood should have a powerful effect. After all, his bloodline was enough to suppress most of the Dragon Clan on Dragon Isle.

Yang Kai was in no hurry to transplant the other Dragon Blood Flowers. He first had to see what effect his blood had on this one stalk before making further plans. If it proved useless, then there was nothing more to think about. He would simply charge straight into the Dragon Temple and spare himself the suffering here.

But standing there staring at it would not do either, so Yang Kai simply started wandering around the island.

He had wandered around Dragon Blood Island once before, but back then all his attention had been on searching for Dragon Blood Flowers, and he had had no leisure to take anything in. This time, he had a proper look.

Aside from Dragon Blood Flowers, Dragon Blood Island did have a few other plants, but none were precious medicinal materials. They were all ordinary vegetation. Perhaps because the island had been shrouded in dragon blood mist year-round, everything here had taken on a crimson hue and looked exceedingly strange.

Yang Kai also saw quite a few of the hundred or so surviving Dragon Blood Flowers. Most were indeed unranked. In both size and color, none could compare with the one he had transplanted.

After idling away half a day, Yang Kai returned. He looked closely, and his brows immediately lifted in delight.

The Dragon Blood Flower really had changed greatly. When he had kept staring at it earlier, he had not been able to see much, but now that he looked again, the flower head was clearly larger, and its color had grown even richer. The faint golden light covering the petals had not vanished. Instead, it gave off a brighter glow than before. This clearly showed that his drop of Golden Blood was still taking effect, and continuously at that. He just did not know how long it would last.

This discovery lifted Yang Kai's spirits at once.

Since it was effective, that meant he could satisfy the Second Elder's demand. When the time came, if she still dared fart out a word against him entering the Dragon Temple, he would beat the shit out of her!

Yang Kai suddenly felt boundless motivation and immediately set about transplanting even more Dragon Blood Flowers.

In any case, his medicine garden still had about six or seven hundred Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers left. At worst, he would give up six or seven hundred drops of Golden Blood. He believed that as long as his Golden Blood fully took effect, then even if he could not restore the original quantity, the quality would definitely be no problem.

With a flicker of his Divine Sense, another Dragon Blood Flower appeared in his hand.

Yang Kai stared blankly, because this Dragon Blood Flower was Middle-Level too.

He brought out another stalk. Still Middle-Level.

The third stalk was the same, also Middle-Level...

Yang Kai's expression suddenly turned strange, as if he had thought of something. His face changed dramatically, and in a flash, he entered the Xuan Realm Pearl.

He suddenly realized he had been a fool! All this time, he had actually forgotten about those two.

Mu Zhu and Mu Na!

When it came to cultivating Dragon Blood Flowers, these two were grandmaster-level figures. To the Wood Spirit Race, nothing was more delightful than cultivating Spirit Medicines. Moreover, ever since Yang Kai had handed the medicine garden over to the two of them, he had paid very little attention to it. By now, they had expanded the medicine garden to more than ten times its former size and planted all kinds of strange and unusual things there.

Only now, after every Dragon Blood Flower he took out turned out to be Middle-Level, did Yang Kai suddenly realize something. Could all those Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers in his memory have improved in quality?

Leaving aside the special nature of his medicine garden, having the two Wood Spirits, Mu Zhu and Mu Na, tending it was enough to turn the impossible into the possible.

Yang Kai appeared leisurely in the medicine garden, then stood there staring in a daze.

Looking out, he could not see the end of the medicine garden at a glance. Spirit flowers and rare grasses bloomed in dazzling profusion. Rows of trees had appeared as well, neatly arranged in a pleasing order. Some were in flower, filling the entire garden with a peculiar fragrance, while others had already borne fruit. The bright red and golden-yellow fruits made one's appetite stir at a glance.

The Everlasting Tree and Firmament Tree stood at the very center, and not far away was that rare Life Origin Fruit tree.

Countless specks of fluorescent light drifted lazily through the entire medicine garden like fireflies. The medicine garden was simply a fairyland.

Yang Kai stared in utter amazement. Was this still his medicine garden? He had never seen a better place.

Once he someday reached the peak of the Martial Dao, bringing his women here to live in carefree seclusion would not be a bad choice at all.

“Mu Zhu, Mu Na?” Yang Kai called softly, lowering his voice like a little thief sneaking in to steal herbs.

Two tiny figures suddenly shot over from the left and right, then hovered together before him. After exchanging a glance, Mu Zhu asked, "Does Master need something?"

"I've run into a bit of trouble!" Yang Kai still kept his voice low. "I'm afraid I need your help."

His furtive manner made Mu Zhu nervous too, and she could not help lowering her voice. "If Master needs something, you only need to give the order. There is no need to be so polite."

Although Mu Na did not speak, she nodded her little head fiercely at the side.

"Good. But this should not be difficult for you. It mainly involves cultivating a kind of Spirit Medicine."

Mu Zhu smiled. "That is our strong suit. Only..." She turned her little head, looking left and right. "Master, why are you speaking like this? Is someone here eavesdropping?"

Yang Kai blinked, raised a fist, and coughed lightly, finally returning to his normal voice. "No, no. Mainly, I was afraid of disturbing the peace and serenity of this place."

The two Wood Spirits stared at him, both somewhat unsure whether to laugh or cry.
