## Chapter 3203 — Excessive Enthusiasm

In a daze, he slipped into an extremely peculiar state. He lifted one hand slightly and sent a gentle palm strike forward. It was only an unconscious movement, and when the strike went out, there was not the slightest reaction around him.

He kept striking, sometimes turning palm into fist, sometimes spreading his five fingers like spears, sometimes changing fist back into palm. He moved entirely as he pleased, with no pattern to speak of, yet his motions carried a peculiar rhythm.

A hundred li away, the three Saint Venerables were speeding over when they suddenly stopped above a stretch of treetops.

Luan Feng frowned and said, "Ahead is the territory of the Stone Puppet Race. I wonder whether that boy is there."

"I hope he is," Fan Wu said, looking utterly worn out. The three great Saint Venerables had all set out together just to bring that boy a few things and send him on his way. If word got out, people would laugh themselves to death.

Cang Gou suddenly sniffed, then asked with a strange expression, "Do either of you feel anything?"

"What?" Luan Feng turned her head to look at him.

Cang Gou frowned. "I cannot quite say. It is just... watch out!" His expression suddenly changed, and he pointed behind Luan Feng with a shout.

Luan Feng sensed it too. Danger struck from behind her, bringing with it a tremendous pulling force, as if something meant to swallow her whole. In that urgent moment, a pair of magnificent wings spread from her back, and with a whoosh, she beat them and shot into the air.

Looking back, she saw that a huge black hole had appeared behind the place where she had been standing, from who knew where. When her Divine Sense swept over it, the black hole was filled with emptiness and chaos. The space around it was melting and shattering, while an extremely strange vibration spread from within.

She actually sensed a fatal threat from that black hole. If she had truly been swallowed just now, her end would likely have been anything but pleasant.

"Laws of Space!" Fan Wu's face darkened. Before his voice had faded, ripples spread through the void before him. That strange vibration surged out again, using the void itself as its medium as it crashed toward him.

Fan Wu's expression changed drastically. He threw himself backward and lay flat. A visible layer of ripples swept over him, severing a lock of his hair before stretching straight out for a hundred li. Wherever it passed, enormous tree crowns were shaken into dust.

Black holes emerged one after another, and layer upon layer of vibrating force came from every direction, weaving an absolute killing ground through that vast stretch of space and trapping the three great Saint Venerables within it.

All three were shocked and hurriedly used their own methods to dodge and maneuver, avoiding those invisible killing moves.

Fortunately, they were no ordinary people and were powerful in their own right. Otherwise, the first wave of attacks alone would have been enough to take their lives.

From high above, the space within a radius of several hundred li was in complete chaos. Caught inside, the three great Saint Venerables could barely tell one direction from another. Even when they clearly fled in opposite directions, they somehow ran straight into each other.

The entire world seemed to have become a cage that imprisoned all three of them. No matter how hard they tried, they could not break through that invisible blockade. Their faces showed horror mixed with a trace of astonishment.

It was not as if no one in this world was stronger than them, but they were Divine Spirits after all. Yet now, someone had toyed with the three of them in the palm of his hand without even showing his face. It was simply too inconceivable.

"Did he do this?" Cang Gou asked with wide eyes as he dodged one invisible killing impact after another.

The Laws of Space were not something just anyone could master. Yang Kai had only appeared in the Wild Ancient Land yesterday, and today they were being attacked with Space Divine Abilities. Cang Gou could not think of anyone else who would use such methods.

He suddenly realized that the boy was far stronger than he had been a dozen years ago. If Yang Kai had possessed such methods back then, Shi Huo probably would not have beaten him so miserably.

"Where is he!" Luan Feng was furious. Two feathers had fallen from the wings on her back, torn away because she had not dodged quickly enough and had been caught in that invisible Power of Space.

The situation before them was dangerous, but for them, it was not yet a matter of life and death. Luan Feng simply could not understand why Yang Kai would attack them without a word. Had that boy gone mad? Even if his strength had improved over the past dozen years, surely he would not go so far as to make enemies of the three great Saint Venerables.

Moreover, the three of them had not provoked him this time. If this was over the Space Array, then he was making far too much of a small matter.

Fan Wu said nothing, his expression cold and stern. Suddenly, he raised two fingers and drew them together across his eyes, then shouted in a low voice, "Open!"

He looked out, observing from another angle, and his expression changed. "Not good. This place is about to collapse."

He possessed a Heavenly Eye Divine Ability and could see the essence of things others could not. Under his Heavenly Eye, this vast stretch of void was trembling endlessly. The space looked unharmed, but in truth it was already filled with spatial cracks that even Divine Sense could not detect. If this space were a great flatbread, then it had already split apart into countless tiny fragments. It simply could not be seen from the outside.

Cang Gou and Luan Feng's expressions changed when they heard this, and they realized how serious the problem was. Without further hesitation, both revealed their true forms.

With a great rustle, an enormous, magnificent bird like a phoenix soared into the sky, spitting black flames to open a path as it strained to break free of the space's restraint and race outward. Cang Gou also transformed into a monster several zhang tall and followed close behind Luan Feng. Relying on his Heavenly Eye Divine Ability, Fan Wu slipped through the countless cracks.

A short while later, the three finally escaped that dangerous area.

Luan Feng and Cang Gou transformed back into human form and looked back solemnly, lingering fear still in their hearts. There was no denying that this kind of power already seemed capable of threatening them. If it really was Yang Kai's doing, then they would have to properly reassess their relationship.

The vibrating space suddenly calmed. One black hole after another slowly shrank and vanished without a trace. The layers of vibrating force also settled, and the space that should soon have shattered actually became perfectly intact again.

"What is he playing at?" Cang Gou frowned, displeased. "A show of force?"

Although he dreaded the descendant of Heaven's Punisher behind Yang Kai, that was all. In his view, Yang Kai was nothing more than a man living off a woman's support. Without Heaven's Punisher's descendant backing him, what did a mere cultivator at the First Stage of the Emperor Realm count for before him? Cang Gou had already been holding in a bellyful of frustration on this trip with Luan Feng and Fan Wu. Now he had suffered this treatment before even seeing Yang Kai, so naturally he was in no pleasant mood.

Luan Feng's face had darkened as well. She felt Yang Kai was truly going too far. No matter what, the three of them were Divine Spirits. Even if she had smashed that Space Array without warning back then, Yang Kai should not be so rude.

Fan Wu shook his head slightly. "It does not seem to have been intentional."

He had seen clearly just now that none of it had been aimed at them. If it really had been a show of force, the attacks would not have been so loose and scattered.

"Could it really have been accidental?" Cang Gou said indignantly.

"Perhaps he is cultivating some Divine Ability..." Fan Wu had a faint guess, and as soon as the words left his mouth, even he was startled. This Divine Ability clearly had no fixed form yet. In other words, it had not been perfected. Yet an unperfected Divine Ability already had such might. If he truly managed to cultivate it successfully, what then?

He hardly dared think further. No wonder Heaven's Punisher's descendant regarded him so differently. From the looks of things, even without her backing him, he was not someone who could be casually offended.

Humans might not live as long as Divine Spirits, their starting point might not be as high, and they might even be inferior to ordinary yao at first, but their room to grow was truly terrifying.

As his thoughts churned, a voice suddenly rang beside their ears. "The three of you have come to visit, yet I failed to welcome you from afar. Please forgive me!"

The three great Saint Venerables' expressions shifted. They naturally recognized Yang Kai's voice. After exchanging glances, Fan Wu said, "Brother Yang is too polite. We are the ones who came uninvited."

A trace of surprise flashed through both Cang Gou and Luan Feng's eyes.

Fan Wu's way of addressing Yang Kai was quite thought-provoking... Divine Spirits had their own pride, and Emperor Realm cultivators were not qualified to call them brothers.

"Since you have come, then come in and talk."

Fan Wu smiled slightly. "Then we will trouble you!" He gave Luan Feng and Cang Gou a look, then stepped forward and sped ahead.

Influenced by him, Cang Gou and Luan Feng also put away their displeasure. Among the three great Divine Spirits, Luan Feng was the strongest, but Fan Wu was famed for his wisdom, so when they gathered, he was usually the one they followed.

A moment later, the three great Saint Venerables arrived at the Stone Puppet Race's dwelling place. In an open area surrounded by countless thick trees, Yang Kai stood there smiling as he watched the three descend from the sky.

Eight Stone Puppets stood spread out to either side, glaring at the three with hostile eyes and obvious ill intent. The Stone Puppet Race's relationship with them was not very good.

The Elder stood beside Yang Kai, with the Patriarch of the Wood Spirit Race sitting on his shoulder. Up in the treetops, many Wood Spirits poked their heads out to look this way, but none dared show themselves casually.

Fan Wu stepped forward and greeted the Stone Puppet Elder. "It has been many years since we last met, yet Elder's bearing is as fine as ever."

The Elder froze for a moment, somewhat surprised, but still returned the greeting and said, "Saint Venerable is too polite." Since the other party spoke so courteously, he naturally could not respond coldly. Moreover, the Elder himself was not fond of fighting or contention.

"We have come uninvited this time only because we heard Brother Yang had appeared in the Ancient Land. We thought he must have come here, and now we see we were right."

Yang Kai answered with a smile, though he was somewhat puzzled. He kept feeling that Fan Wu was being a little too enthusiastic. Also, had he not told that twin-horned Yao Marshal to send word to Luan Feng that he would go find her? Why had they come looking for him instead?

Naturally, he did not know how much influence he now had in the Ancient Land. How could the three great Saint Venerables dare let him wander around the Ancient Land as he pleased? If not for that, why would they lower their noble status?

Yang Kai did not wish to trade veiled words and probing remarks with him, so he asked directly, "May I ask what important business has brought you here?"

"Nothing much. We only came over to have a look," Fan Wu replied with a smile.

As if I can believe that. Yang Kai dismissed the claim inwardly and quietly grew vigilant. Aloud, he said, "Since that is the case, let us sit and talk."

He then sat with Fan Wu and the others at the wooden table used for last night's grand gathering. Mu Na lightly clapped her hands, and the Wood Spirits flew out from among the treetops one after another, bringing melons, fruits, water, and wine and setting them down.

Fan Wu smiled and said, "I have long heard that the Wood Spirit Race excels at cultivating spirit melons and exotic fruits, but I never had the chance to see them for myself. It seems I have such good fortune today. This Lord will use their hospitality to offer a toast to Brother Yang."
