## Chapter 3227 — Borrowing People

Qiu Ran put the Emperor Pills away again. Stroking the beard on his chin, he pondered for a moment before saying, "I wonder how Junior Brother plans to spend such a large sum of contribution points?"

Too many contribution points were not necessarily a good thing. In principle, the Elders could use their points to exchange for anything the Shrine offered, whether various cultivation resources or profound cultivation methods and Secret Techniques. But Yang Kai's points amounted to far too great a sum, so great that even Qiu Ran felt his heart tremble.

If Yang Kai really exchanged them for resources, the Treasure Vault the Shrine had built up over so many years would likely shrink by a huge margin.

Naturally, this was not something the Shrine wished to see. If those Elder Contribution Points merely remained on the books, the Shrine would lose nothing, but if Yang Kai really exchanged them for materials and carried them away, the disciples would have to tighten their belts when cultivating in the future.

Yang Kai had come prepared. Hearing this, he said, "Senior Brother, you also know I have a Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory. I have taken in some disciples now, but Lingxiao Palace's foundation is still shallow and its heritage lacking, so I would like to ask the Shrine to let me copy some cultivation methods, Secret Techniques, and the like to bring back. Would that be convenient? Of course, Junior Brother can spend contribution points for them."

Qiu Ran said, "You are an Elder of the Shrine. Copying some cultivation methods and Secret Techniques is naturally no problem. As long as they are not especially important, you will not need contribution points."

There was actually such a good thing? Yang Kai immediately perked up. Earlier, Qiu Ran had looked like a miserly iron rooster, so Yang Kai had thought the man was deliberately making things hard for him. Now it seemed he had misunderstood. He asked tentatively, "Naturally, the more cultivation methods and Secret Techniques the better. I would like to get some of the precious ones too."

Qiu Ran said, "Go discuss that with Junior Brother Di yourself. The Cultivation Methods Pavilion is under his charge, and no one knows better than he does which things require contribution points and which do not."

The Junior Brother Di he mentioned naturally meant Di Rong. Although Yang Kai was not as familiar with him as he was with Gao Xueting, they had drunk together and been punished together, so they could hardly be called strangers.

"That works!" Yang Kai nodded.

"Anything else?" Qiu Ran looked up at him.

Yang Kai said, "I would also like to borrow some people from the Shrine."

"Borrow people?" Qiu Ran raised an eyebrow. "What do you mean?"

Yang Kai said, "The disciples who recently came to my Lingxiao Palace do not have high cultivations, and they need people to teach and guide them, so I would like to borrow some people from the Shrine to serve as instructors. Of course, I will not borrow them for nothing. I can use Elder Contribution Points to cover their compensation." This was the main reason he had come. The one hundred instructors from Li Dragon Palace were already crying bitterly to the heavens, so he had to bring in more help as soon as possible.

"There is no precedent, but it can be done. Junior Sister Gao, what do you think?" Qiu Ran turned to Gao Xueting.

Gao Xueting said, "It is not impossible. In any case, he is not an outsider. The only question is how many people Junior Brother wants to borrow, and what cultivation they need to have."

"Two hundred," Yang Kai replied.

"Mm."

"Dao Origin Realm?"

"Pfft..." Qiu Ran had just taken a sip of tea and spat it out at once. He quickly set down the cup, patted the water from his chest, and stared at Yang Kai in shock. "Junior Brother, you want to borrow two hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators?"

"Senior Brother heard correctly. Two hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators."

Gao Xueting said in astonishment, "What do you need so many Dao Origin Realm cultivators for?" These people were going to Lingxiao Palace to serve as instructors, and an instructor would naturally teach a group of people. Even if each instructor taught two hundred people, two hundred instructors could teach forty thousand.

Did Lingxiao Palace have four thousand people?

"Did I not just say? I am inviting them over as instructors."

Gao Xueting frowned. "How many disciples has your Lingxiao Palace recruited?"

Yang Kai sniffed. "Over a hundred thousand, I suppose..."

Qiu Ran and Gao Xueting both froze, almost thinking they had misheard. They looked at each other, and Qiu Ran sent a transmission, "How many did he just say?"

Gao Xueting replied, "I heard over a hundred thousand. I wonder how many Senior Brother heard?"

It was over a hundred thousand, all right!

The corner of Qiu Ran's eye twitched. "Why did you recruit so many disciples?" The key point was that, from what Yang Kai had said, these disciples' cultivations were not high. A Sect's strength did not depend on how many people it had, but on whether they were elites.

Qingyang Shrine only had three or four thousand disciples, yet it was one of the top Sects in the Southern Territory. Lingxiao Palace, however, actually had over a hundred thousand disciples. That was over a hundred thousand people, not over a hundred thousand pigs. Could he afford to support so many people? More importantly, what was the point of supporting over a hundred thousand people with low cultivations?

No Sect had ever had so many disciples. It was simply unheard of.

"I had no choice." Yang Kai smiled bitterly. This matter was truly inconvenient to explain to outsiders. It was enough that his own people knew. If others learned that he had brought those hundred thousand-plus people over from a Lower Plane Star Domain, he feared even Qingyang Shrine would try to snatch them.

Over a hundred thousand disciples and two hundred instructors. That really was not much.

Qiu Ran straightened his expression and said, "Does Junior Brother know how many Dao Origin Realm cultivators my Shrine has?"

"I do not." Yang Kai shook his head. Although he was qualified to know such things, no one had ever told him, so how could he know?

"Three hundred fifty-two," Qiu Ran said, giving an extremely precise number. "Excluding the Dao Origin Realm disciples stationed at our various properties, only two hundred eighty-nine remain in the Shrine." The implication was clear: if you take two hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators in one go, what is the Shrine supposed to do?

Yang Kai nodded. "Two hundred eighty-nine. The Shrine's heritage is extraordinary indeed. That is more than enough to meet Junior Brother's needs. Please grant this request, Senior Brother."

"..." Qiu Ran nearly cursed aloud. He even began to suspect Yang Kai was doing this on purpose. Those two hundred eighty-nine people each had their own duties in the Shrine; they were not idle. Every one of them had a post. Transferring a few people out would not be a major problem, but two hundred was far too many. He believed Yang Kai could not possibly be unaware of this, yet the boy was plainly pretending not to understand.

Taking a deep breath, Qiu Ran said, "Two hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators is impossible. At most, I can lend you thirty."

Yang Kai widened his eyes. "Thirty? What am I supposed to do with thirty people?" Thirty people were not even enough to fill the gaps between his teeth.

"Take it or leave it." Qiu Ran turned and picked up his teacup. Just as he was about to drink, he remembered he had sprayed tea into it earlier and set it down again in annoyance.

"Come now, Senior Brother. We are all one family; there is always room to discuss things. Is Senior Brother unable to decide this matter? If you cannot decide, I will go discuss it with the Hall Master."

Qiu Ran said, "It will be useless even if you ask the Hall Master."

"Then a little fewer. One hundred eighty will do." Yang Kai took a step back.

Qiu Ran's face remained expressionless. "Forty."

"One hundred seventy."

"Fifty!"

"One hundred sixty, then. That should do, right? Senior Brother, do not go too far. I am inviting them to be instructors, and they will be compensated. I absolutely will not make them work for nothing."

"Sixty!" Qiu Ran glanced sideways at him. "It seems Junior Brother's Lingxiao Palace is quite wealthy. Not only can you support over a hundred thousand disciples, you can even pay compensation. Junior Brother is truly capable."

"I am barely scraping by myself. Senior Brother also manages a household, so you should understand my difficulties. One hundred fifty. I cannot go any lower."

"It is precisely because I understand your difficulties that I must think on your behalf. Seventy. No more."

The two of them went back and forth, sounding exactly like buyers and sellers haggling over vegetables in a marketplace. Gao Xueting watched from the side, speechless. The two of you are discussing dozens, even hundreds, of Dao Origin Realm disciples. Could you please take this a little more seriously?

After a bout of matching wits, Qiu Ran dug in at eighty and refused to give even one more.

Yang Kai slapped the table in anger. "Senior Brother, you had best not force me."

Qiu Ran leisurely adjusted his sleeve. "Junior Brother, let us talk properly. Giving you eighty Dao Origin Realm instructors is already the Shrine's limit. How am I forcing you?"

"There really cannot be any more?" A fierce light shone in Yang Kai's eyes, as if he bore Qiu Ran some deep blood feud.

Qiu Ran slowly shook his head.

"Good." Yang Kai pointed at him. "You said it. I do not want the instructors anymore. Not a single one."

Qiu Ran was a little surprised, and a faint sense of unease suddenly rose in him.

Yang Kai spread his hands. "Then I will have to trouble Senior Brother to exchange all of my more than one hundred fifty thousand contribution points for Upper-Level Source Crystals. Junior Brother is now supporting over a hundred thousand people, and the shortage of cultivation resources is too great. I urgently need Source Crystals to fill the gap, so please lend me some support, Senior Brother."

Qiu Ran's face darkened, and he nearly cursed out loud.

What one feared truly came to pass. Earlier, when he calculated those contribution points, he had felt the sum was far too enormous, which was why he wanted Yang Kai to spend some of them. Who would have thought that after going around in circles, they would end up right back where they started?

More than a hundred thousand contribution points, exchanged for Upper-Level Source Crystals, amounted to over a billion. It was not that the Shrine could not produce them; the problem was what happened afterward. On top of that, they would still need to trouble him to keep refining Emperor Pills in the future. If Qiu Ran stubbornly insisted on his own way, he might very well anger him.

“Cough, cough…” Qiu Ran clenched his fist and coughed lightly. “Junior Brother, do not get agitated. Let us talk this over properly. It is not as if we have finished negotiating yet.”

Yang Kai snorted twice, slumped back in his chair, crossed his legs, and said with a smug little bounce in his voice, "One hundred forty Dao Origin Realm instructors. Senior Brother, handle it as you see fit."

Qiu Ran said darkly, "That is truly too many."

"Too many? I think it is fine." Yang Kai thought to himself that Li Jiao had been extremely straightforward. He had asked him to send over some Dao Origin Realm cultivators, and Li Jiao had arranged it at once. By contrast, the Shrine kept dawdling and delaying, as if Yang Kai were trying to lure people away.

"A little fewer."

Yang Kai said decisively, "Then please exchange them for Source Crystals for me, Senior Brother."

Qiu Ran's face darkened again.

Gao Xueting suddenly said, "How about this, Junior Brother Yang? One hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators, plus three hundred Void King Realm cultivators. Since the disciples on your side do not have high cultivations, Void King Realm cultivators can teach some of them as well. Not everyone needs a Dao Origin Realm instructor."

Yang Kai glanced at her and knew she was right. Besides, one hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators was more or less what he had expected. The only reason he had asked for two hundred at the start was to leave Qiu Ran room to bargain.

Three hundred Void King Realm cultivators was no small number. Divided among the various peaks, each peak would still receive two or three. Although some Void King Realm cultivators had also come from the Star Domain, this was precisely the time for them to cultivate hard, and they had no time to teach anyone.

With these four hundred people going over to help, Lingxiao Palace's current situation should improve.

After pretending to ponder for a long while, he said, "Since Senior Sister Gao has spoken, Junior Brother will give you face. However, the number of Void King Realm cultivators is a bit low. Make it four hundred."

After saying so, he looked at Qiu Ran.

Qiu Ran gritted his teeth. "Fine!" Giving Gao Xueting face but not him was simply outrageous.

Yang Kai immediately beamed, cupped his fists, and said, "Many thanks, Senior Brother Qiu."
