## Chapter 3218 — I Desire to Unify the Northern Territory

An alliance? Watching over and supporting each other? This was a good thing. Lingxiao Palace had a solid foundation now, and its disciples alone numbered over a hundred thousand, but in the Northern Territory it was still a new force. If it could ally with Li Dragon Palace, that would certainly benefit its future development.

Besides, Li Jiao was no outsider anymore, so Yang Kai naturally had no reason to refuse and agreed on the spot.

Li Jiao chuckled, cupped his fists, and said, "Then from now on, my Li Dragon Palace will be relying on Brother Yang. We will follow Lingxiao Palace's lead in all things."

He understood Yang Kai's abilities better than anyone, so he knew this alliance still had a clear senior and junior partner. Li Dragon Palace could not compare to Lingxiao Palace. He said it with complete candor and felt no embarrassment over it.

An alliance between two great top forces was a major matter, yet the two of them settled it in a few brief exchanges, without even signing a written agreement, because both of them knew there was no need for one.

"Brother Yang, since we have formed an alliance, that makes us one family now." Li Jiao chuckled. "As for those debts... could you perhaps waive them for us?"

Back then, Li Jiao had followed Ji Ying to Lingxiao Palace to stir up trouble. When Yang Kai and Ji Ying were competing in Alchemy, Li Jiao had seized the chance to make a wager, only to lose miserably and end up owing Lingxiao Palace decades' worth of debt. Every year, he had to repay over ten million Upper-Level Source Crystals, forcing Li Dragon Palace to tighten its belt so much these past years that life had become unbearably hard. The Elders and Deacons under him complained constantly, and sometimes they could not even gather enough Source Crystals for the disciples' cultivation.

Only a dozen years had passed, and Li Dragon Palace was already struggling to hold on. Whenever Li Jiao thought of the twenty or thirty years still left in the repayment period, his head ached.

"You're trying to trap me!" Yang Kai glared at Li Jiao. Those debts were no small sum.

Li Jiao chuckled and shamelessly said, "In any case, we are not outsiders, not outsiders."

Yang Kai snorted. "Waiving them is not impossible."

Li Jiao immediately seized the opening and cupped his fists. "Many thanks, Brother Yang."

Yang Kai raised a hand. "But I have one condition."

Li Jiao said, "Please speak, Brother Yang."

Yang Kai said, "You know what state my Lingxiao Palace is in right now. More than a hundred thousand disciples have only just arrived. We need not worry about cultivation resources, Secret Arts, or Secret Techniques for the time being, but cultivation experience is indispensable, and they need people to guide them. My Lingxiao Palace has too large a gap in its ranks and cannot spare enough people to teach them, so I would like to ask Brother Li to lend some support."

On the road of cultivation, having guidance and fumbling along alone were two entirely different things. Yang Kai had learned that deeply over his own journey. He had already been planning this before, and now that Li Jiao had delivered himself to his door, how could Yang Kai let him slip away?

If Ice Heart Valley's disciples did not all cultivate Ice Attribute Secret Arts and Secret Techniques, making them unsuited for the general masses, he would have asked Bing Yun to transfer some people over to help when he visited Ice Heart Valley earlier. Unfortunately, the attributes of the Secret Arts and Secret Techniques cultivated by Ice Heart Valley's disciples were too narrow, and might not suit other cultivators.

"What sort of support?" Li Jiao asked, puzzled.

"I will have to trouble you to transfer a group of experienced instructors from Li Dragon Palace to teach the disciples of my palace how to cultivate."

So that was all. He had thought it was some great matter. Li Jiao immediately thumped his chest and guaranteed, "No problem." After a pause, he added, "But Lingxiao Palace has too many disciples. Even with my Li Dragon Palace's support, I fear it still may not be enough."

With more than a hundred thousand disciples, even if one instructor taught five hundred people, they would still need two or three hundred instructors. How could Li Dragon Palace send out that many people? After all, anyone serving as an instructor had to be at least in the Dao Origin Realm. Anyone below the Dao Origin Realm would be useless. The cultivators who came from the Star Domain could already cultivate to the Void King Realm on their own. What they lacked was cultivation experience above the Void King Realm. Even if Li Dragon Palace had that many Dao Origin Realm cultivators, it could not possibly dispatch them all.

"Just help with the immediate need first. I will think of another way for the rest." Yang Kai sighed.

With so many people came all kinds of trivial matters. He could not handle every detail. All he could do was lay a foundation for Lingxiao Palace as a whole. As for how it developed afterward, that would depend on his subordinates' management and the disciples' own drive to improve.

"How many people does Brother Yang need?" Li Jiao asked.

"One hundred!"

Li Jiao pondered for a moment, then nodded. "That can be done!"

One hundred people was not a small number, but Li Dragon Palace could still spare them. Any more would be impossible. Since they were to be instructors, they had to know how to teach others to cultivate. Not everyone could take on such a post. Cultivating oneself and teaching others to cultivate were two different matters. For a Sect as large as Li Dragon Palace, gathering a hundred instructors should still be possible, even if it took some effort.

"Then those debts can count as the monthly salaries for these one hundred instructors. Brother Li has no objections, I trust?"

"No objections, no objections!" Li Jiao answered with a cheerful smile. Strictly speaking, those debts amounted to an enormous fortune. Sending out a hundred instructors to offset them was a tremendous bargain, so how could he object? Moreover, these hundred instructors would certainly return in the end, so no matter how he calculated it, he would not lose out.

He rose and said, "There is no time to lose. This Li will hurry back now and send over the people Brother Yang needs as soon as possible."

"I will go with you!" Yang Kai said.

Li Jiao froze for a moment, then understood. "Brother Yang wants to connect our two palaces?"

"It will make travel between us more convenient."

"That works too." Li Jiao did not refuse, and immediately set off with Yang Kai and Lu Sanniang. The three of them sped toward Li Dragon Palace.

All three were Emperor Realm Masters, and with a flying Secret Treasure assisting them, they naturally moved extremely quickly. In just half a day, they reached Li Dragon Palace.

Li Jiao led the way, bringing Yang Kai to a quiet, remote corner of Li Dragon Palace.

Yang Kai immediately began arranging the Space Array. By nightfall, with stars scattered across the sky, the array was complete. With this array connecting Lingxiao Palace and Li Dragon Palace, even a distance of ten million li could be crossed in an instant, truly allowing them to watch over and support each other.

Yang Kai had intended to hurry back at once. After all, Lingxiao Palace had many trivial matters at the moment, and there were still many things he needed to handle personally, but Li Jiao insisted on keeping him behind, saying he wanted to hold a banquet in his honor.

Yang Kai could not very well refuse him, so he could only agree.

In the main hall, lamplight flickered, illuminating the vast chamber down to the finest detail.

Everyone of status in Li Dragon Palace came to keep them company. From Palace Master Li Jiao at the top to the Protectors and Elders below, they filled an entire table.

Perhaps they had already received the news from Li Jiao and knew that Li Dragon Palace would no longer have to hand over its Source Crystal income to Lingxiao Palace every year. With everyone's mood greatly lifted, their attitude toward Yang Kai naturally changed as well. One after another, they bent over backward to flatter him, while Li Jiao loudly boasted of the life-and-death friendship between himself and Yang Kai. Once the wine was flowing freely, he even slung an arm around Yang Kai's shoulders, utterly forgetting how much he had hated and dreaded Yang Kai back then.

The atmosphere was lively, with dancing girls below adding to the cheer. Yang Kai sat upright beside Li Jiao and did not put on any lofty airs. In the end, he was currently only at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, and after witnessing even greater power, what airs did he have left to put on? This made the higher-ups of Li Dragon Palace feel all the more that Yang Kai was quite a decent person.

There was wine, hearty drinking, and drunken eyes gone hazy.

The banquet lasted until the latter half of the night before everyone finally dispersed. Li Jiao ordered young and beautiful maids to attend Yang Kai while he rested, but Yang Kai politely declined and returned to Lingxiao Palace that very night. Li Jiao personally saw him off.

Before the Space Array, Yang Kai suddenly turned to Li Jiao. "Brother Li, I desire to unify the Northern Territory. What do you think?"

The night wind brushed Li Jiao's face, and his drunkenness vanished in an instant. Shocked, he said, "Unify the Northern Territory?"

"Exactly!" Yang Kai had only thought of it on a sudden impulse. More than a hundred thousand disciples in Lingxiao Palace were waiting to be fed. Although there was no need to worry about cultivation resources in the short term, and although he had the trade route with the Southern Territory's Ziyuan Commerce Guild and a strong chance of opening trade with the Eastern Territory's Wild Ancient Land as well, in the long run, simply living off stored wealth was no solution. If Lingxiao Palace wanted to keep developing for generations, it needed its own sources of income and industries.

Back then, Wenqing Sect had indeed possessed many industries of its own. There were more than twenty large and small cities nearby, and every year it received a great deal of tribute from those cities. In return, Wenqing Sect protected their safety.

However, after Yang Kai destroyed Wenqing Sect, he had only plundered its wealth and paid no attention to the industries it left behind. In other words, those twenty-odd large and small cities were not under Lingxiao Palace's control at all.

Taking them back would be simple, though. With Lingxiao Palace's current strength, sending out any Yao King to intimidate them would be enough. There was no fear that the other side would refuse to compromise.

Yet such income clearly could not satisfy the cultivation needs of over a hundred thousand disciples.

If he could unify the Northern Territory, then there would be nothing to worry about. The resources of the entire Northern Territory would be his to take as he pleased. Forget a hundred thousand people; he could support even a million.

But he also knew there would likely be resistance. Even if Lingxiao Palace had the bigger fist, the Northern Territory's billions of cultivators were not easy prey. The Yao Kings were indeed powerful, but there were only three of them. Moreover, their identities were too sensitive, making it unsuitable for them to show themselves outside for long periods. Otherwise, if they happened to provoke some great expert into coming out to slay demons and eliminate devils, the loss would outweigh the gain.

He did not believe the Northern Territory had no experts. The experts he could see were only the ones on the surface. There would always be capable people and unusual talents hidden beneath the water. Yang Kai did not know of them now only because he had not touched their interests. Once he truly provoked them, those people would definitely not sit still and wait for death.

Among the Northern Territory's several top forces, Lingxiao Palace, Ice Heart Valley, and Li Dragon Palace could now be considered aligned in advance and retreat. If Yang Kai truly wanted to unify the Northern Territory, these two families would definitely have no objections, and Yang Kai would not touch their interests either. The other forces, however, would have to step aside.

Li Jiao understood Yang Kai's temperament. He knew this fellow was bold because of his skill, someone who dared to charge in and out even of Dragon Isle. In this world, there was probably nothing he would not dare to do. If someone else had asked this question, Li Jiao would likely have laughed it off, because it was not very realistic. But since Yang Kai had asked it, he was probably truly going to do it, and he also had the confidence to pull it off.

Li Jiao looked up and saw that Yang Kai's eyes were clear, without the slightest hint of drunkenness. Knowing Yang Kai was not speaking under the influence, he pondered for a moment and said, "If Brother Yang truly has this intention, this Li will certainly assist with all his strength..." Having only just reached an agreement with Yang Kai to watch over and support each other, Li Jiao naturally would not go back on his word. "It is just... has Brother Yang prepared himself to be denounced by thousands and cursed by the Northern Territory's billions of cultivators?"

Yang Kai laughed loudly. "What do other people's lives and deaths have to do with me? I only care about my Lingxiao Palace."

Li Jiao nodded. Every man looked out for himself. Since one had set foot on the Martial Dao, one had to be prepared to fall at any time. Killing and being killed were both commonplace.
