## Chapter 3289 — Roll as Far Away as You Can

After bidding farewell to the Stone Puppet Elder and Mu Na, Yang Kai went straight to Luan Feng's palace.

Outside Fengluo Palace, the two Yao Commanders guarding the gate stood with imposing authority, baleful auras surging. All at once, a figure flashed, and someone landed before them.

One of the Yao Commanders, broad-mouthed and flat-nosed, saw this and barked, "What..." Halfway through, he swallowed the last word, for he had clearly seen Yang Kai's face.

Yang Kai's great name had spread far and wide through the Ancient Land. The various Yao Kings knew him, of course, but even the Yao Commanders, Yao Generals, and common yao soldiers under those Yao Kings had heard his name like thunder in their ears.

More than that, Yang Kai had forced his way into Fengluo Palace several times, and Luan Feng, of all people, had been unable to do anything about him. From top to bottom, who in Fengluo Palace did not know this baleful star?

After Saint Venerable returned last time, she had issued a strict order: if they saw that brat Yang Kai again, they were to kill him without mercy. Whatever happened afterward, she would bear it.

That was her command, but how could the Yao Commanders under her dare carry it out? Never mind whether they were Yang Kai's match; even if they really could beat him, they still would not dare make a move.

So the moment they saw Yang Kai walking straight over, the two Yao Commanders guarding the gate automatically ignored the order to "kill without mercy" and instead put on smiling faces, cupped their fists, and welcomed him. "So it is Sir Yang."

Who knew whether Luan Feng would be angered to death if she saw this scene.

Yang Kai gave a soft mm, clasped his hands behind his back, and passed calmly between the two Yao Commanders. If anyone unaware of the situation saw him, they would probably think Yang Kai was returning to his own home.

The Yao Commander who had spoken first saw that this was going badly and hurried after him, bowing and scraping. "My lord, what brings you here?" As he spoke, he shot urgent looks at the other Yao Commander, meaning for him to hurry and notify Saint Venerable.

The main problem was that every time Yang Kai came to the Ancient Land, he came straight to Fengluo Palace, and whenever he came to Fengluo Palace, nothing good ever happened. Saint Venerable's anger over the last matter had not yet subsided, yet after such a short interval, Yang Kai had actually come again. Who knew what trouble he would cause.

Yang Kai was not afraid of Saint Venerable, but that did not mean they were not afraid. When Saint Venerable lost her temper, the ones to suffer would still be small fry like them.

"Is that any of your business?" Yang Kai glanced sidelong at him, reached out, and pushed him aside, then strode forward with dragon steps and tiger gait. The Yao Commander staggered at once, but he did not dare show the slightest temper. He could only trail close beside Yang Kai, step for step, trying to stop him.

But how could he stop him? When he annoyed Yang Kai, Yang Kai simply grabbed him with one hand and threw him away.

A group of maidservants came toward him. They stared wide-eyed for a moment, then scattered with a crash, fleeing in utter panic.

Yang Kai rubbed his nose, his face darkening. "What is that supposed to mean?" He turned his head and looked around, but there was not a single person in sight. With a cold laugh, he drew in a breath and called out, "Madam Feng, this Yang has come to see you."

His voice rang loud and clear, spreading through all of Fengluo Palace. Anyone who was not deaf could certainly hear him.

But Luan Feng clearly did not want to see him, so there was no response at all.

Yang Kai gave a heh, his eyes shifted, and he released his Divine Sense to sweep the area. Then his figure flickered, and when he appeared again, he was already inside a courtyard.

In that courtyard, a delicate little girl was doing who knew what. When she suddenly saw Yang Kai smiling at her, she could not help being startled.

After looking more carefully, the little girl said, "Ah, I know you."

Yang Kai smiled and nodded. "I know you too, little girl. We meet again."

The little girl before him was none other than Luan Feng's daughter. Back when Yang Kai had first entered the Star Realm, after escaping Biyu Sect and while heading for Fenglin City, he had happened to encounter little Luan Feng being surrounded and blocked. It was then that he had truly witnessed the might of a Divine Spirit, and it was also then that he first saw Luan Feng's true body, leaving him with an indelible impression.

But he had never imagined that he would have such deep ties with Luan Feng, nor that they would still be dealing with one another even now. Back then, she had been an existence high above him, yet now he could stand on equal footing with her.

Several dozen years had passed, but little Luan Feng had not grown in the slightest. This was not because she was underdeveloped, but because she possessed the authentic body of a Divine Spirit, and her growth period was naturally extremely long.

In truth, Yang Kai had always been curious about who little Luan Feng's father was. He had never heard of Luan Feng having a lover. Which raised the question: where had little Luan Feng come from? She could not have leapt out of a stone, could she?

"Your name is Tian Long, right?" Yang Kai asked gently, his smile perfectly harmless.

Little Luan Feng nodded, pointed at Yang Kai, and said, "You're called Yang Kai. You're the worst person under Heaven."

Yang Kai's smile instantly stiffened, his expression twitching. "Who told you that? That's complete nonsense!"

"Mother said it," little Luan Feng replied, her face full of innocent candor.

Yang Kai said indignantly, “Nonsense. How am I the worst person under Heaven?”

Little Luan Feng tilted her head and looked at him, then giggled. "Mother told me to beat you up when I grow up. Are you very strong?"

Yang Kai took a deep breath. Children spoke without malice, children spoke without malice. Still, Luan Feng, just you wait! Forcing out a smile, he said, "When you grow up, you'll know whether I'm strong or not." Then he changed the subject. "Are you playing here by yourself?"

Little Luan Feng's eyes turned, and she beamed. "Are you trying to scheme against me? Then you had better be careful. I can burn you with fire."

Yang Kai said in alarm, "That would be terrible. I might be burned to death by you."

“Hee hee hee…” Little Luan Feng laughed until she clutched her belly. “You’re really funny when you act scared.”

The corner of Yang Kai's mouth twitched. He really was stupid, he thought. Little Luan Feng only looked like a little girl; in truth, she had lived at least several dozen years. How could he judge her by ordinary standards? Trying to handle her with the same tricks he would use on an ordinary child was simply inviting humiliation.

He nodded lightly and said, "Tian Long, your birth is noble, and your future is limitless. But in the end, you are still a child, and children should have playmates. How about I find you one? She can play with you, and then you won't be so lonely."

Little Luan Feng lifted her chin and said arrogantly, "That depends on whether she's qualified."

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Would a Fire Phoenix do?"

Little Luan Feng looked startled, then smiled. "Bragging. Where could you possibly find a Fire Phoenix to keep me company?"

Yang Kai shook his head grandly. "I'm really not bragging. There is a Fire Phoenix in my Lingxiao Palace. Mm, she's about your age and also a little girl. I believe the two of you should have many interests in common. After all, if you trace things back to the origin, you can be considered of the same race."

"Really?" Little Luan Feng looked visibly tempted.

"As real as real can be."

Little Luan Feng thought seriously for a moment, pursed her lips, and said, "I know what wicked idea you have in mind, but if what you said is true, then I won't be angry with you."

"Whether it's true or false, you'll know once you come with me to Lingxiao Palace," Yang Kai coaxed patiently. "My Lingxiao Palace has many fun things."

Little Luan Feng hmphed. "I'm not that fond of playing, but..." Her face showed clear hesitation. Clearly, the Fire Phoenix Yang Kai mentioned was very tempting to her.

"Enough!" A cold, clear voice rang out. Hearing it, little Luan Feng lifted her head, then giggled and pounced over, hugging the leg of the beautiful elegantly dressed woman who had appeared there. Looking up, she called sweetly, "Mother!"

Luan Feng’s face was so cold it seemed a layer of frost could be scraped from it. She stroked Little Luan Feng’s head, then looked at Yang Kai with disdain. “Making a move on a child? Do you have no shame?”

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Madam Feng, what are you saying? I haven't done anything to Tian Long. I merely felt her childhood was lonely and bitter, so I wanted to find her a playmate." He looked again at little Luan Feng. "You can ask your mother whether what I just said is true."

Tian Long did indeed ask, "Mother, he says he has a Fire Phoenix there, about my age. Is it true?"

Luan Feng was furious. "This man lies with every word. How can you believe him?"

Yang Kai's face darkened. "Madam, I can't pretend I didn't hear that. You know the truth perfectly well. It's not as if you didn't see her last time. Not only did you see her, but many Yao Kings of the Ancient Land, including Brother Fan and Brother Cang Gou, saw her as well. Teach your child if you want, but why smear my name?"

Luan Feng gnashed her teeth. "You brought it on yourself!"

Yang Kai said, "If not for that, how could I have seen Madam? I fear Madam would not have agreed to see me this time no matter what, right?"

Luan Feng was so angry her chest heaved. “Do you think a mere woman like me is easy to bully? Is that why you come to my Fengluo Palace every time?”

Yang Kai looked astonished. "What does Madam mean by that? I simply feel that Madam and I hit it off from the start and can talk rather well."

Luan Feng snapped, "Who the hell hit it off with you? Go hit it off with Fan Wu and Cang Gou instead." She truly was beside herself with anger. Yang Kai had come to the Ancient Land several times, and every time, he made straight for Fengluo Palace. She had never once seen him go to Fan Wu's or Cang Gou's territory. This was beyond endurance.

Yang Kai sighed leisurely. "I offered my heart to the bright moon, alas, the bright moon shines upon the ditch. Ai!"

Luan Feng’s response consisted of only two words. “Heh heh…”

Yang Kai's expression turned serious. "Madam, don't be angry. If anger damages your health, it would spoil your beauty."

Luan Feng said angrily, "None of your damn business!" A dignified Divine Spirit, and a woman besides, had actually been provoked into cursing. From that alone, one could see how foul her mood was.

Yang Kai rubbed his nose. "I'll go have some tea for now. Once Madam's emotions have settled, we can discuss other matters."

"Roll as far away as you can. Fengluo Palace doesn't welcome you." Luan Feng left those words behind, took Tian Long, and walked off.

Yang Kai looked embarrassed, and in the end he could not bring himself to state his purpose. Luan Feng was on the verge of exploding. If he provoked her further, the loss might outweigh the gain. Women really were somewhat unreasonable when they got angry, so Yang Kai decided to wait until her mood had settled before speaking.

With that in mind, Yang Kai stayed in Fengluo Palace.

Shamelessly and brazenly, he stayed.

No one attended to him. The maidservants and guards in Fengluo Palace all avoided him from afar, treating him as if he were invisible, yet he acted utterly unaware. On his own, he found a room, meditated, and waited.

Several leisurely days passed, and Luan Feng still did not appear. Yang Kai was in no hurry. Occasionally, he came out for a walk, looking as if he truly regarded this place as his own home.

Naturally, Luan Feng could not truly ignore him. Every day, she sent people to watch him in secret, and when she learned what he was doing, she was so angry her lungs nearly exploded.
