## Chapter 3341 — Going Back on One's Word

Zhu Kong kept shaking his head, stunned. "Unbelievable, truly unbelievable. How can there be so many Dragon Blood Flowers on this island? And... all of them are so high in quality."

When Yang Kai had boldly declared half a year ago that he would make up for the Dragon Blood Flowers he had stolen and harvested back then, almost no one in the Dragon Clan had believed he could do it. Their expectations had been extremely low: if he simply returned the Dragon Blood Flowers still in his possession, that would have been good enough.

After all, how could he spit back out what he had already swallowed?

Before coming here, the Dragon Clan Elders had all prepared themselves somewhat and made a rough guess at Dragon Blood Island's current state. At best, they expected a few hundred Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers.

Yet what lay before them was far more than a few hundred Lower-Level Dragon Blood Flowers. The Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flowers alone exceeded that number.

Yang Kai had not only returned all the Dragon Blood Flowers he had stolen back then, he had returned several times that amount—more than ten times as much, even!

Where had he found so many Dragon Blood Flowers?

Fu Xuan looked at Yang Kai and asked, "Exactly how many Dragon Blood Flowers are on the island?"

Yang Kai smiled faintly and said, "Two hundred Upper-Level, five hundred Middle-Level, one thousand Lower-Level, and as for the unranked ones... roughly two thousand!"

Although everyone had formed a vague estimate while surveying Dragon Blood Island just now, hearing Yang Kai report this string of numbers still startled them.

Two hundred Upper-Level?

Five hundred Middle-Level?

Had they heard him correctly? If Yang Kai had said this when they first arrived on Dragon Blood Island, the Dragon Clan Elders would never have believed him. Back then, the entire island had contained fewer than thirty Upper-Level Dragon Blood Flowers, a far cry from two hundred, while there had been only around two hundred Middle-Level ones. Now there were more than five hundred.

And there were one thousand Lower-Level ones, plus two thousand unranked ones...

Those terrifying numbers alone were more than four times what they had been back then.

But after seeing the island with their own eyes, everyone felt those numbers... should be more or less accurate. Yang Kai would not exaggerate about something like this. After all, the Dragon Clan could carefully count them afterward. If the actual numbers differed from what he had reported, he would only embarrass himself.

In other words, this enormous number of high-quality Dragon Blood Flowers was real.

If everything before his eyes had not seemed perfectly normal, Zhu Yan might even have suspected that Yang Kai had spent the past six months setting up some heaven-defying Illusion Array here, causing him to hallucinate.

But... half a year. It had only been half a year. How had such a thing happened?

Four pairs of eyes stared at Yang Kai as if they had seen a ghost in broad daylight. Only Zhu Qing remained calm. She had known the result long ago; over the past half year, she had watched the island change day by day.

Under everyone's gaze, Yang Kai sighed again. "Unfortunately, I couldn't cultivate any Supreme-Level Dragon Blood Flowers. That is something of a regret."

Mu Zhu and Mu Na were capable of cultivating them, but doing so would take ten or twenty years. How could Yang Kai spare that much time? Even those Upper-Level flowers had been forced into existence one by one by the two Wood Spirits, using the existing Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flowers without regard for cost or effort.

For this, Yang Kai had released at least a large vat's worth of Golden Blood. Fortunately, his recovery ability was good, and the process had been spread across half a year. Otherwise, his bloodline really might have regressed.

"Supreme-Level Dragon Blood Flowers are far too rare. It is normal that you couldn't cultivate any." Zhu Kong wiped the cold sweat from his forehead. If Yang Kai had truly cultivated Supreme-Level Dragon Blood Flowers, that would have been the truly inconceivable part.

That was exactly the line Yang Kai had been waiting for! Delighted inwardly, he ignored everyone else and fixed his eyes on Fu Zhun. "What does Second Elder think? Can everything I have done make up for Dragon Isle's losses from back then?"

He looked unruly and pleased with himself, his gaze fierce, as if he would start a fight if she dared say a single word against her conscience.

Fu Zhun said nothing, mainly because she truly could not bring herself to say anything against her conscience.

"It is enough to make up for the losses from the Dragon Blood Flowers back then!" Zhu Yan nodded lightly. Although two Supreme-Level Dragon Blood Flowers were missing, the Upper-Level, Middle-Level, Lower-Level, and even unranked Dragon Blood Flowers currently on Dragon Blood Island far surpassed what had existed back then. If this still did not count as compensation, that would be impossible to justify. It could even be said that not only had Yang Kai made up for the loss, he had made Dragon Isle a tremendous profit.

"Hahahaha!" Yang Kai threw back his head and laughed. Half a year of hard work had finally paid off. Once the Great Elder said those words, the matter was practically settled; Fu Zhun's silence no longer mattered. In his excitement, he even pulled Zhu Qing into his arms in front of everyone and kissed her on the cheek, making her blush all the way down to her neck.

Zhu Yan was dumbstruck as well. He coughed dryly and said, "Boy, how exactly did you do this? Did you already have so many Dragon Blood Flowers on hand?"

Yang Kai looked at him warily. "Great Elder does not need to concern himself with how I did it. This may sound disrespectful to my elders, but I advise Great Elder to abandon whatever thoughts are forming in your mind. I paid a tremendous price to cultivate these Dragon Blood Flowers. Results like these cannot be repeated. In the future, handle the Dragon Blood Flowers yourselves. I will not cultivate such things for Dragon Isle again."

"I was only asking, only asking." Zhu Yan looked embarrassed. He had indeed wanted to understand a little more. After all, if Dragon Isle could master such a miraculous method, the Dragon Clan would no longer need to worry about Dragon Blood Flowers, and their clansmen could take large quantities of them to improve their bloodlines. Who would have thought Yang Kai would see through him at once and shut him down in advance?

Yang Kai said lightly, "Now that Dragon Blood Island has Purple Soul Grass, the Dragon Blood Flowers will only grow better in the future. The Dragon Clan has cultivated Dragon Blood Flowers for many years, so you do not need me to tell you what to do. Good, this matter ends here." With that, he lifted his chin slightly toward Fu Zhun and said each word distinctly, "I. Want. To. Enter. The. Dragon. Temple!"

Fu Zhun gave a mocking laugh. "In your dreams!"

Yang Kai flew into a rage. "You are going back on your word?"

Fu Zhun said lightly, "I have always kept my word. When have I ever gone back on it?"

Yang Kai jabbed a finger at her and shouted, "Half a year ago, you said that as long as I could make up for the losses I caused Dragon Isle back then, you would agree to any request of mine. Now I have done it, yet you are reneging. Do you dare claim you are not going back on your word?"

"Did I say that?" Fu Zhun turned her head and looked aside.

Yang Kai froze in astonishment. For a moment, he was speechless, but he soon sneered. "Qing'er was standing right there at the time. Is there any point in denying it?"

Zhu Qing nodded. "Second Elder, you did say that." Her voice was low, and she was clearly somewhat angry as well. She had stayed by Yang Kai's side throughout the past half year and personally watched him work himself ragged on Dragon Blood Island. He had given away a large vat's worth of Golden Blood for this. Now that all was said and done, Second Elder had actually spoken such shamelessly false words. Zhu Qing truly found it hard to accept.

She never would have imagined Fu Zhun was this kind of person.

"Correct, I did say that." Fu Zhun turned back to look at him. "So what?"

Yang Kai turned to Zhu Yan, his voice ice-cold. "Great Elder, can the Dragon Clan go back on its word too?"

Zhu Yan sighed. "Of course the Dragon Clan keeps its word."

"What a joke." The corner of Yang Kai's mouth curled up, but there was not the slightest trace of amusement on his face. Fu Zhun's attitude had enraged him to the extreme.

Zhu Yan continued, "But did you truly fulfill Second Elder's requirements?"

Yang Kai pointed to the side. "Do these not count?"

Zhu Yan nodded. "These naturally count. The losses from the Dragon Blood Flowers you stole and harvested back then can be considered repaid. But what about the other losses? Think carefully. What exactly did Second Elder say at the time?"

Yang Kai naturally remembered Fu Zhun's exact words, and he immediately sneered. "Is there any point in playing word games like this?"

Zhu Yan slowly shook his head. "This is not a word game. Back then, a giant dragon of the Fu Lineage did indeed die because of you. By all rights, with such a grudge between us, my Dragon Clan should fight you to the death. However, you also have certain ties to my Dragon Clan, so it would be unwise to tear apart all cordiality. Even so, no matter what, you must give my Dragon Clan an explanation."

Yang Kai sneered repeatedly. "Fu Chi died at Wu Kuang's hands. If you want revenge, go find him. What does it have to do with me?"

Zhu Yan said, "But considering Wu Kuang's condition at the time, if you had not struck first and heavily injured Fu Chi, Wu Kuang could never have succeeded. You bear at least half the responsibility for Fu Chi's death!"

"He should be grateful he died at Wu Kuang's hands. At least he got a swift death! If I had been the one to act, he would not have died so easily." Yang Kai grinned savagely.

Zhu Qing clenched her fists and looked at Zhu Yan. "Great Elder, Fu Chi is already dead. What meaning is there in bringing this up now? Are you saying you want Husband to pay with his life?"

Yang Kai narrowed his eyes, secretly hating himself for having been blind. If he had known this would happen, why would he have helped the Dragon Clan cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers at all? He should have charged straight into the Dragon Temple, borrowed the power of the Dragon Souls, and beaten Zhu Yan, Fu Zhun, and the rest flat.

Zhu Yan said, "There is no need to go that far. Fu Chi was not killed by him, after all, so how could we ask him to pay with his life?"

"Then please speak plainly, Great Elder!"

Zhu Yan coughed lightly, glanced at Zhu Qing, then looked at Yang Kai and said, "Because of you, the Dragon Clan lost one clansman. As long as you can add another clansman to my Dragon Clan, Dragon Isle will allow you to come and go as you please from now on, and no one will obstruct you in anything."

Yang Kai could not help laughing in derision. "Add another clansman to your Dragon Clan? How interesting. Where am I supposed to find one of your Dragon Clan's clansmen?"

Zhu Yan said, "There is no need to find one. You already have that ability."

Yang Kai frowned deeply. "What do you mean?" This old thing spoke in riddles, leaving people utterly confused.

Zhu Qing, however, seemed to realize something, and her pretty face could not help turning red.

Fu Xuan also understood at that moment and looked at Yang Kai in sudden realization.

Zhu Kong smiled faintly and quietly transmitted a sentence to Yang Kai.

Yang Kai's expression shifted at once. He turned to look at Zhu Qing, then pointed at his own nose. "You think that if Qing'er and I have children, they will definitely belong to the Dragon Clan?"

After all that winding around, this old thing had been aiming for this all along! Yang Kai even suspected they had planned it from the very beginning, setting trap after trap for him to jump into. The matter of the Dragon Blood Flowers had merely been a pretext. They really were crafty old foxes.
