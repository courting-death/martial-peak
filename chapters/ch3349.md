## Chapter 3349 — It's Not What You Think

Yang Kai's eyes widened as he stared at the chubby little boy in astonishment. The whole thing seemed rather unbelievable—had he really hatched this little fellow? Still, Yang Kai could sense something of his own aura about him. It was only natural, considering how much Dragon Blood and other things the boy had absorbed from him over the past several months.

Yang Kai glanced down and immediately grinned. It was a boy. Although he had successfully assumed human form, he was stark naked, so the proof between his legs was plain to see.

As Yang Kai studied him with great interest, the little boy raised his small, pale hands and cupped his fists. “This child greets Father,” he declared in a clear, childish voice. “Father, please bestow a name upon me!”

Yang Kai's mouth fell open wide enough to swallow a house.

Qiong Qi was just as dumbstruck, wondering if his ears were playing tricks on him.

What had the little boy just said? Surely he had misheard him?

The boy's clear thoughts and remarkable eloquence immediately after hatching were astonishing enough, but Qiong Qi was even more shocked by what he had called Yang Kai. How could he casually address just anyone as “Father”?

Yang Kai was clearly baffled as well. Leaning forward slightly, he pointed at his own nose and asked with an odd expression, “What did you call me?”

The little boy smiled. “I had no hope of ever hatching, but Father gave me life and allowed me to experience this world. You granted me a new life, so it is only right that I call you Father.” He spoke with perfect clarity, then cupped his fists again. “Father, please bestow a name upon me.”

Yang Kai scratched his head and frowned. “Do you know where you came from?”

The little boy nodded. “Of course this child knows. Father imparted many things to me while hatching me.”

“Did I?” Yang Kai looked utterly lost. He had spent the entire hatching process in a daze, acting purely on instinct. How was he supposed to know what he had imparted to the boy?

Qiong Qi's expression shifted. He recalled the golden aura that had constantly flowed out of Yang Kai and poured into the dragon egg. It now appeared that the golden aura had contained more than just Dragon Aura. Something else had been mixed into it, granting the little boy remarkable intelligence from the moment he was born.

Qiong Qi even felt that the boy strongly resembled Yang Kai in his manner and bearing, though they looked nothing alike.

Rubbing his chin, Yang Kai feigned reluctance. “This really isn't my place, but since you insist, this Lord shall bestow a name upon you.” After giving it some serious thought, he clapped his hands. “How about Xiao, meaning ‘sky’? You soared straight into the highest heavens as soon as you were born, so it seems quite fitting.”

“Yang Xiao,” the little boy murmured. His round eyes lit up, and he smiled, revealing two tiny fangs. “Many thanks to Father for bestowing this name. From this day forward, this child shall be called Yang Xiao.”

With a whoosh, Fu Zhun arrived. Even before reaching this place, she had sensed an aura connected to her by blood. The connection shook her so deeply that she nearly began to tremble. She needed no further proof; she was certain her Lin'er had been born.

Brimming with anticipation, she arrived just in time to witness the scene before her. Her vision went black, and she nearly fainted.

That fair-skinned, delicate little boy was undoubtedly her Lin'er, yet he was calling Yang Kai Father and declaring that his name would be Yang Xiao from now on?

How could Fu Zhun possibly accept this?!

She had no idea what Yang Kai had done to her Lin'er, but he was practically stealing her son from her. How could Fu Zhun tolerate that? All her pent-up rage and guilt erupted into boundless fury as an icy aura swirled around her. She raised her hand and struck at Yang Kai with her palm. “Little thief, die!”

At that moment, she no longer cared whether Yang Kai and Zhu Qing had consummated their relationship, nor whether he possessed the power of the Ancestral Dragon Origin. Only one thought echoed through her mind: Kill Yang Kai, set matters right, and make Lin'er understand the truth.

Heaven and Earth changed colour beneath the full-powered strike of a Tenth-Tier Great Dragon. Ice Principles flooded the void, with Yang Kai bearing the brunt of their force. The space around him seemed to freeze solid, and before the attack even reached him, frost had already blanketed his thirty-zhang Dragon body. His expression changed at once.

Before he could react, something flashed before his eyes. Yang Xiao had darted in front of him, spreading his arms wide to shield him. The fair-skinned little boy glared at Fu Zhun, his face set in anger. “Where did this madwoman come from, daring to run wild here?!”

Despite his angry shout, his childish voice carried no menace whatsoever. If anything, it only made people want to laugh.

Yet that shout drained all colour from Fu Zhun's face. She pulled back her palm strike with all her might, and the Principles around her collapsed with a boom. She might have wanted to kill Yang Kai, but how could she ever hurt her own Lin'er?

Fu Zhun cried out and spat a mouthful of Dragon Blood, her aura rapidly weakening. Whether it was from the backlash or the shock of Yang Xiao's words was impossible to tell. Her slender body swayed, nearly plummeting from the sky, while she stared blankly ahead.

Fortunately, Zhu Yan arrived in time. He caught her and pulled her into his arms. “Are you all right?” he asked anxiously.

Fu Zhun did not answer. She merely pursed her pale lips and stared fixedly at Yang Xiao, her eyes filled with utter despair. Yang Xiao's words had wounded her far more deeply than the backlash from her own palm strike. Her son had taken a thief for his father!

A thump sounded, and Yang Xiao stumbled. He turned around, clutching his head with both hands. “Father, why did you hit me?” he asked plaintively. Tears welled in his eyes, and he nearly began to cry.

No matter how mature and worldly he had seemed moments ago, he was still a newborn child. Of course he felt wronged after being hit, especially since Yang Kai had been the one to do it. After all, he had just put his life on the line to shield Yang Kai.

“Madwoman?” Yang Kai rebuked him sternly. “That's your mother!”

“My mother?” Yang Xiao shrank back, then turned to look timidly at Fu Zhun. When their eyes met, a spark of hope lit Fu Zhun's eyes, but Yang Xiao merely looked embarrassed and glanced away.

No wonder her voice had sounded familiar. She was the one who had often come to speak to him while his mind was still hazy.

Although he knew his origins, this was still the first time he had seen Fu Zhun in person. How could he have known she was his mother? He had only wanted to show off a little in front of Yang Kai, yet he had ended up making a spectacular blunder. Mortified, he could only scratch awkwardly at his ears and cheeks.

“Lin'er?” Zhu Yan's lips trembled as he stared at Yang Xiao in a daze. “Are you Lin'er?”

Yang Xiao took a deep breath and cupped his fists. “For your information, my name is Yang Xiao!”

“Yang... Yang Xiao?” Zhu Yan's expression turned strange. His gaze swept across the scene before settling on Yang Kai. Lin'er had appeared here with Yang Kai and was now somehow calling himself Yang Xiao. Yang Kai had to be involved.

The Great Elder fixed Yang Kai with a piercing, resentful stare. Yang Kai turned his head aside and whistled.

It wasn't his fault. The little fellow had insisted on being given a name the moment he was born, so Yang Kai had casually come up with one, and the boy had immediately claimed it as his own. What else was he supposed to do?

Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh...

Beams of light sped in from every direction as members of the Dragon Clan who had sensed the commotion arrived one after another. They were all startled by the scene before them.

Zhu Qing stared at Yang Kai in astonishment. “Didn't you leave?”

Yang Kai raised a fist to his mouth and coughed dryly. “I came back.”

He had originally planned to force his way into the Dragon Temple. Once he caused a commotion, Zhu Qing would discover his intentions and surely understand. Yet before he had even made it inside, he had somehow produced a Dragon Son. How was he supposed to explain this?

Zhu Qing glared at him, knowing he must have been plotting something and had even deceived her. Now was not the time to press him about it, though. She looked curiously at Yang Xiao. “Who is this?”

Yang Kai laughed and was about to explain when Yang Xiao cupped his fists. “I am Father's son. My name is Yang Xiao.”

“Your son? Yang Xiao?” Zhu Qing's face immediately darkened.

Yang Kai promptly rapped him on the head and shouted, “Who told you to speak out of turn? Who told you to speak out of turn?!”

“Father, why are you hitting me again?” Yang Xiao cried, covering his head as he scampered away, aggrieved beyond measure.

Zhu Qing said with biting sarcasm, “A grown man beating up a child. How very manly of you.”

Yang Kai wiped his mouth and put on a solemn expression. “Qing'er, let me explain...”

Zhu Qing turned her head away. “There's nothing to explain. This child is brimming with Dragon Aura, so he must be the clansman newly born to our clan. Congratulations. Your efforts certainly bore fruit quickly. By the way, where is the child's mother? Which of my sisters is she?” As she spoke, she studied several Dragon women, searching for some clue.

“It's not what you think...” Yang Kai said mournfully.

Yang Xiao ran over to Zhu Qing and pointed at Fu Zhun. “My mother is over there.”

Zhu Qing turned to look, stunned. She looked from Fu Zhun to Yang Kai, her expression growing stranger by the moment until she was staring at them in utter disbelief. Watching from nearby, Zhu Lie's face also began to twitch.

Yang Kai and the Second Elder... Surely that was impossible?

“If you can't say anything sensible, then keep your mouth shut!” One look at Zhu Qing's face told Yang Kai exactly what she was thinking. He wanted nothing more than to beat Yang Xiao's backside raw, but the boy was a cunning little imp. He seemed to realize that Yang Kai was wary of Zhu Qing and kept hiding behind her, denying Yang Kai any chance to lay a hand on him.

“But you're the one who said she was my mother,” Yang Xiao complained. He looked up at Zhu Qing. “I didn't lie to you.”

Zhu Qing nodded repeatedly. “I know.”

Yang Kai sighed. “He is the child of the Great Elder and the Second Elder. I hatched him, so he decided I was his father.”

Every member of the Dragon Clan was left staring, speechless.

A Dragon Son born to the Great Elder and the Second Elder? When had the two Elders ever had a child?

The Second Elder had indeed laid a dragon egg many years ago, but hadn't it lost all hope of hatching and been placed in the Dragon Tomb? How had a Dragon Son suddenly appeared out of nowhere?
