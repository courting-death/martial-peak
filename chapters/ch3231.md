## Chapter 3231 — Old Tao

Inside Mitian Sect's great hall, music and dancing filled the air. Strings and flutes lingered over the room, while below, a group of scantily dressed dancers twirled gracefully.

In the hall, a group of Emperor Realm cultivators sat at banquet tables arranged in two rows to either side. Before each person stood a table laden with exquisite fruits and fine wine. At the head of the hall, Mitian Sect's Sect Master, Mi Qi, sat shoulder to shoulder with an old man whose hair and beard were completely white, entertaining him with great warmth.

The old man was advanced in years. His clothes showed no sign of wealth or status, and he would not have stood out if tossed into a crowd. Several pouches hung from his waist, bulging full with who knew what.

Yet this was the very old man who made someone of Mi Qi's standing personally keep him company. His seat was even level with Mi Qi's own, which showed just how much Mi Qi valued him.

Below, Mitian Sect's Elders and Protectors also kept raising their cups in invitation.

The old man refused no toast. Fine wine that others rarely had the chance to taste slid down his snow-white beard, making the watching Elders ache in their hearts.

They had spent no small amount of effort and wealth brewing these spirit wines. On ordinary days, they could not even bear to drink them themselves. Now they had brought them out to entertain a guest, only for the guest to waste them so carelessly. Unfortunately, the old man's status was not something they could afford to offend. No matter how they grumbled inwardly, they could only keep smiling on the surface.

The old man drank while leering at the slender, charming dancers below. In one hand, he clutched a handful of grape-like berries and stuffed them into his mouth one by one, eating until his mouth watered and he forgot all thought of leaving.

The berries were strikingly beautiful, glowing with a blue sheen, each one crystalline and translucent like a jewel.

Those familiar with the fruit, however, knew that however pretty it looked, it absolutely did not taste as good as it appeared.

Blue Jade Fruit counted as a kind of spirit fruit and could be used to refine Spirit Pills, but it had few uses. If eaten raw, it was extremely sour and astringent, difficult to swallow. On top of that, it was hard to cultivate, so even across the entire Star Realm, it was very rare.

It was not very useful, did not taste good, and was difficult to cultivate, so naturally no one was willing to spend much effort on it.

Mitian Sect, however, had poured a great deal into Blue Jade Fruit, cultivating large quantities of it every year for no other reason than that this old man called Old Tao loved the stuff. Everyone had their own tastes. The Blue Jade Fruit that ordinary people could barely swallow was, in Old Tao's eyes, the most delicious thing in the world. No one knew why his palate was so strange.

Old Tao had an extraordinary background, and Mi Qi had met him by chance. When Mi Qi invited him to Mitian Sect as a guest years ago, Old Tao happened to see the Blue Jade Fruit and was instantly taken with it. Mi Qi naturally catered to his preference, paying a great price to open a Blue Jade Fruit orchard in the medicine garden and tend it carefully every year. When harvest time came, he would invite Old Tao over to taste the fruit, and when Old Tao left, he would even pack up all the Blue Jade Fruit and send them with him.

After so many years, Old Tao had formed a habit. Every year, he would come to Mitian Sect for a short stay to satisfy his craving. With this regular back and forth, their relationship naturally became familiar.

The day Li Jiao delivered Yang Kai's message and left, Mi Qi immediately had someone send word to Old Tao, telling him that the Blue Jade Fruit were about to ripen and inviting him over. Old Tao naturally agreed at once and rushed over without stopping, whereupon Mitian Sect immediately served him fine wine and good food.

Mi Qi knew Mitian Sect was no match for Lingxiao Palace; those three great Yao Kings were not there for show. Ever since Yang Kai had cheated him and Li Jiao back then, he had brooded over it. If he could now borrow Old Tao's hand to free himself from Lingxiao Palace's grip, he could bear any price. The three great Yao Kings were indeed formidable, but before Old Tao, they were likely still not enough.

He toasted Old Tao warmly while sneering inwardly, wishing Yang Kai would hurry up and deliver himself to the door so he could learn that Mitian Sect was not a place he could bully at will.

Just then, someone suddenly walked in from outside and quietly transmitted a few words to Mi Qi.

Mi Qi's expression changed at once. He hurriedly set down the wine cup in his hand and said, "Old Tao, please sit for a moment. This Mi will go and return at once." His face was filled with panic.

Old Tao looked at him in surprise. "What is it?"

Mi Qi forced out a faint smile. "A small matter. There is no need to trouble Old Tao. I will handle it myself."

Old Tao tossed a Blue Jade Fruit into his mouth and chuckled as he chewed, revealing a mouthful of blue teeth. "Brother Mi, you are treating this old man like an outsider."

"How could I dare?" Mi Qi hurriedly waved his hand. "Old Tao's visit to my Mitian Sect truly brings splendor to our humble Sect. How could I regard Old Tao as an outsider?"

Old Tao said, "If this concerns your Mitian Sect's secrets, then Brother Mi need not tell me. But if you have run into some trouble, Brother Mi, do not keep it hidden. Although this old man has little strength, I still have a bit of face. Perhaps I can help Brother Mi resolve the matter somewhat."

He could see that Mi Qi's expression was not right. Clearly, he had run into some trouble.

Mi Qi hesitated for a while, looking as though he were struggling with himself. Only after quite some time did he sigh and say, "Since Old Tao does not treat this Mi as an outsider, this Mi will not mind airing his family's shame." As he spoke, he lifted his wine cup and drained it in one gulp, as though drowning his sorrows.

"Family shame? What happened?" Old Tao's curiosity was piqued.

Mi Qi sighed. "It is still that Lingxiao Palace."

Old Tao started, then gave him a strange look. "That Lingxiao Palace that rose not long ago? This old man remembers that Lingxiao Palace's Palace Master swindled you out of quite a few Source Crystals."

Mi Qi had told him about this many years ago. At the time, he had seemed to want Old Tao to go to Lingxiao Palace and mediate so Yang Kai would waive those gambling debts. But how could Old Tao casually get involved in someone else's disputes? Although he came to Mitian Sect every year to taste some Blue Jade Fruit, and Mi Qi treated him well, these were ultimately small favors. They were not worth offending a top Sect over. His background was indeed considerable, but that did not mean he could invite every kind of trouble onto himself. Besides, those were gambling debts, not something Yang Kai had snatched from Mi Qi's hands.

"Shameful, truly shameful!" Mi Qi looked as though the past was unbearable to recall.

"What are Lingxiao Palace's people doing here?" Old Tao's face twitched. "Have they come to demand the Source Crystals you owe? If so, this old man advises you to spend the money and avert disaster."

Mi Qi cursed inwardly. All the good things he had given Old Tao over the years might as well have been fed to a dog. The moment Old Tao heard it was Lingxiao Palace, he actually told him to spend money to avert disaster. Sure enough, the man could not be relied on at a critical moment. But since Old Tao had come this time, Mi Qi had to borrow his prestige no matter what.

Otherwise, why would he have invited Old Tao over so warmly? He had been waiting precisely for this moment, for Yang Kai to come knocking. So far, everything was going according to plan.

With a grief-stricken expression, he said, "Old Tao is right. Mi Qi is not one to go back on his word. If one dares to gamble, one must accept defeat. Over the years, my Mitian Sect has tightened its belt and sent Lingxiao Palace large sums of Source Crystals every year. But that Yang thief still seems unsatisfied. A few days ago, he even sent someone to inform this Mi that this Mi must lead Mitian Sect in surrendering allegiance to his Lingxiao Palace, or else he would flatten my Mitian Sect."

Li Jiao had indeed spoken of surrendering allegiance, but the part about flattening the Sect was Mi Qi spouting nonsense.

Old Tao's face darkened when he heard this. "This Lingxiao Palace is far too arrogant."

"Exactly. Although this Mi is untalented, I am still the master of a Sect, with several thousand disciples under me. How could I trade humiliation for glory and live under another's roof? So this Mi refused at the time and begged Lingxiao Palace to show mercy and spare my Mitian Sect this once."

"And then?" Old Tao pressed.

Mi Qi said with a grim face, "A disciple just reported that Yang Kai, Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace, has brought more than a dozen Emperor Realm cultivators to my Mitian Sect, along with over two hundred Dao Origin Realm cultivators. From the look of it, they truly mean to flatten my Mitian Sect."

The corner of Old Tao's eye twitched. "Surely it has not gone that far?" He no longer felt like tossing the Blue Jade Fruit in his hand into his mouth. He faintly sensed that he had fallen into a trap. Though old, he was no fool. Mi Qi had specifically invited him over, and it just happened to coincide with a large force from Lingxiao Palace coming to attack. This was clearly Mi Qi's plan. Damn it, the man wanted to borrow his influence.

Resentment rose in his heart. He thought, Did I not just eat some of your fruit every year? Did you have to trap me like this? On the surface, however, he said, "Sect Master Mi, perhaps you are worrying too much. This may be a misunderstanding."

Mi Qi said sternly, "That Yang thief has already arrayed his forces. How could it be a misunderstanding?"

Old Tao dropped the Blue Jade Fruit in his hand as if it had become scorching hot, then gave a dry cough. "Brother Mi has not even met him yet. How can you be certain it is not a misunderstanding? Perhaps once you meet and exchange a few words, the matter will be settled."

Settled, my ass! How could Mi Qi not know that Old Tao was trying to shirk responsibility? But he could not force the matter either. He could hardly say, You have eaten plenty of my Blue Jade Fruit every year, so now that I am in trouble, you have to help me, could he?

Old Tao added, "Moreover, Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array is quite extraordinary. Even if Palace Master Yang has come to attack, Brother Mi need not worry too much. Whether they can break the Grand Array remains to be seen."

Mi Qi squeezed out a faint smile, nodded, and said, "Old Tao's words are reasonable. In that case, this Mi will go investigate. I hope this truly is only a misunderstanding."

Old Tao immediately said, "Brother Mi, go quickly and return quickly."

The corner of Mi Qi's mouth twitched. Seeing that Old Tao truly had no intention of going out with him, he finally understood that the so-called friendship between the two of them amounted to little. Grief and anger welled in his heart, but he could not fall out with him, so he could only say, "Very well. Old Tao, please wait a moment. I will go out and take a look."

With that, he flicked his broad sleeve and walked outside, giving the seated Elders and Protectors a look as he went. The group immediately left their seats and followed behind him.

In an instant, Old Tao was the only one left in the great hall. He looked at the young beauties still dancing gracefully below, then at the Blue Jade Fruit before him, and suddenly found everything rather dull.

Outside Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array, Yang Kai hovered calmly in the air with his hands clasped behind his back. Li Jiao stood beside him. Behind them, the three great Yao Kings stood with their Yao Qi soaring to the heavens, each with a fierce light in his eyes. Farther back were Li Dragon Palace's Emperor Realm and Dao Origin Realm cultivators, all wearing somewhat complicated expressions. They had not expected to actually come to Mitian Sect to cause trouble this time.

What exactly did this Palace Master Yang want? Their own Palace Master was truly strange as well, actually cooperating with him like this.

Inside the Grand Array, a group of Mitian Sect disciples with fairly low cultivations trembled as they huddled together for warmth, as if that would make them feel safer. The two sides stared at each other across the Grand Array.

Meeting the terrifying gazes of the three great Yao Kings, they immediately felt their hearts tremble with fear, so frightened that sweat broke out all over their bodies.
