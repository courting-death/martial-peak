## Chapter 3181 — Stealing a Star

Inside the Small Xuan Realm, vast swathes of land appeared out of thin air. Invisible Laws and Spiritual Qi poured into this small world along with the devouring, blending into the Small Xuan Realm and making its own Laws and world sturdier and more complete.

Along with that land came all manner of living beings. Most were human, though there were also some Monster Beasts swallowed by the black ball. Having suddenly arrived in another world, all of them were badly shaken.

These living beings had all been swallowed because they were too slow to avoid the black ball. They had thought themselves doomed, only to come back to their senses and find themselves safe and sound, which naturally left them stunned. They ran about spreading the news, preparing to explore the mysteries of this world and figure out their situation, only to be confined to a certain area by an invisible force, unable to go very far at all.

All of Yellow Springs Star had fallen into chaos.

As time passed, everyone learned that a demon-like existence was driving a huge black ball capable of devouring all things, rampaging across Yellow Springs Star. His Divine Abilities were supreme, and no one could stand against him. Even the famous Netherworld Sect had been flattened.

As a result, cultivators with the ability to flee Yellow Springs Star set off one after another, no longer daring to linger on this star for even a moment. Those without the ability to escape could only pray that the Heavens cherished life, but what awaited them was a terrifying devouring.

Two months later, Yellow Springs Star had been transformed beyond recognition. Viewed from the air, this star, which Netherworld Sect had used as its main star and which had once been rich in Spiritual Qi, blessed with beautiful mountains and clear waters, and home to countless living beings, was now utterly dead and silent. Cities large and small had vanished, Sects and families were gone, and even the many spiritual mountains and great rivers had disappeared without a trace...

Yellow Springs Star now looked like an enormous asteroid. Deep, endless ravines still scarred its surface, and it had shrunk by a large margin compared to before.

If cultivators from the other stars of Great Wilderness Star Domain came to inspect it, they would never recognize it as Yellow Springs Star. Most likely, they would only think it was a silent Dead Star.

"Troublesome!" Yang Kai hovered above Yellow Springs Star, swept his Divine Sense through the Small Xuan Realm, and muttered to himself.

He had not expected so many residents to suddenly appear inside the Small Xuan Realm, but there was nothing he could do about it. Yellow Springs Star was not a Dead Star, but a thriving Cultivation Star with a flourishing population. When he urged the Xuan Realm Pearl to devour the star, the cultivators above the Saint King Realm on Yellow Springs Star could flee once they saw something was wrong, but how could those below the Saint King Realm escape? There were also countless ordinary mortals. He could hardly expect them to leave Yellow Springs Star and flee into the Star Domain.

That would only be courting death.

So Yang Kai had simply followed through to the end and swallowed these living beings into the Small Xuan Realm along with the earth itself.

What to do with these living beings now was indeed a troublesome matter.

Take them to another Cultivation Star and release them there? But these living beings numbered far more than a hundred million. A full eighty percent of Yellow Springs Star's population had been taken into the Small Xuan Realm by him. Even if he wanted to release them, he did not have the time, and what Cultivation Star could accommodate so many living beings all at once?

Thinking it over, why should he release them at all? In any case, he had no intention of killing them, so he could just let them live in the Small Xuan Realm and fend for themselves.

A world could only produce endless vitality when all living things flourished and competed. Perhaps, in the future, these living beings would bring unexpected changes to the Small Xuan Realm and make its World Laws more complete and advanced. Yang Kai could clearly feel that the Small Xuan Realm did not reject the arrival of these hundreds of millions of living beings. On the contrary, it seemed rather pleased to accept them.

There was another point as well. He had founded Lingxiao Palace in the Star Realm, and such a vast Sect needed a constant stream of fresh blood. That fresh blood would naturally be newly recruited disciples.

Where would he recruit them from? The Northern Territory certainly had no shortage of talent, but who could know every newly recruited disciple inside and out?

The living beings inside the Small Xuan Realm were different. As the master of the Small Xuan Realm, Yang Kai had accepted them as inhabitants, and with a single thought, he could see through people's hearts. He could easily choose disciples with decent temperaments and good aptitudes from among these hundreds of millions of living beings and nurture them from childhood. In that case, there would be no need to worry about their loyalty to Lingxiao Palace.

The more he thought about it, the more he felt it was a good idea, as if an unintended act had unexpectedly borne fruit.

When trouble turned into good fortune, his mood naturally improved.

Examining the Small Xuan Realm more carefully, he found that it had indeed changed greatly. The outer layer of the entire Yellow Springs Star had been swallowed, and everything devoured had replenished the Small Xuan Realm. The damaged places inside the Small Xuan Realm were already almost completely repaired.

The Small Xuan Realm's total area had also expanded severalfold.

The original Small Xuan Realm, though not small, could hardly compare to an entire Yellow Springs Star. Although both were small worlds, the difference between them was still enormous.

If Yellow Springs Star could be called a small world in the strictest sense, then the world inside the Xuan Realm Pearl could only be considered a tiny one. Now that the Small Xuan Realm had swallowed an elephant like a snake, it had grown in every respect.

Suddenly, a flash of inspiration crossed Yang Kai's mind, and he vaguely grasped something.

The Small Xuan Realm had also been refined by human hands, created from the refinement of a small world. Since someone else could refine the Small Xuan Realm, why could he not upgrade and transform it? Yang Kai did not think himself much worse than others.

Moreover, the gains and insights from upgrading and transforming it, from constructing a world, were probably not something he could obtain through secluded cultivation. They would be of tremendous help in his comprehension of World Force, and World Force was the key to becoming a Great Emperor!

At this thought, Yang Kai grew so excited that he could hardly contain himself, and the idea in his heart became even firmer.

After gnawing away Yellow Springs Star's surface layer, the Small Xuan Realm's devouring Domain had also expanded many times over. Originally, the black ball had only been about one li in diameter, but now it was fully ten li across.

An expanded devouring Domain meant greater devouring efficiency, and it also meant Yang Kai could transform the Small Xuan Realm more quickly.

Yellow Springs Star was already ruined. Just as Yang Kai was preparing to set off toward a nearby star, he suddenly sensed something and looked at the huge black ball with a strange expression.

He had actually sensed longing from within the black ball.

His heart stirred, and he said in disbelief, "No way..."

He had obtained the Xuan Realm Pearl many years ago, but it had only ever been a dead object with useful functions. Even though it formed a small world unto itself, it was still a dead object.

Yet at this moment, Yang Kai clearly sensed something different. Thinking back to the Small Xuan Realm's pleased acceptance of those hundreds of millions of living beings, he suddenly realized that he might have struck it rich this time.

"As you wish!" Yang Kai laughed loudly, drove the huge black ball forward, and plunged toward the greater remaining portion of Yellow Springs Star.

Since even the Xuan Realm Pearl did not disdain this bare, meteorite-like star, what reason did he have to disdain it? In any case, it would only take some time.

The large meteorite became a medium meteorite, and the medium meteorite became a small meteorite...

Yang Kai and the black ball swept around Yellow Springs Star again and again. Each time they circled it, Yellow Springs Star shrank by a layer. The scene looked strange in the extreme, as if a divine being's mouth had taken Yellow Springs Star as food and was biting off mouthful after mouthful, swallowing it into its belly.

Until finally, all of Yellow Springs Star disappeared.

The instant Yellow Springs Star was devoured clean, the Small Xuan Realm suddenly changed in a way that caught Yang Kai somewhat off guard.

...

At the same time, Wu Heng, who was still at the edge of Great Wilderness Star Domain resisting that boundless darkness, paled. He hurriedly split off part of his attention and sank it into his Sea of Consciousness to observe the Star Chart.

A moment ago, he had seemed to feel that something was missing, but after looking carefully, he found nothing.

Just as he was about to withdraw his attention, his eyes suddenly widened. He stared at a certain point on the Star Chart in disbelief and cried out in horror, "This is impossible!"

He had been the Master of Great Wilderness Star Domain for many years and knew the entire Star Domain like the back of his hand. He knew exactly which star stood in which position. Yet at this moment, he clearly discovered that a star had vanished from his Star Chart.

That star was called Yellow Springs Star!

Three months ago, he had sent Yang Kai to Yellow Springs Star, intending to use the opportunity to kill him. Unexpectedly, he had instead trapped himself in a dilemma. Not only had he failed to kill Yang Kai, Yang Kai had even used a scheme to pin him here, leaving him unable to move or spare any attention.

During these three months, he had been constantly resisting the encroaching darkness and had simply had no strength to spare to investigate Yang Kai's movements. In his view, Yang Kai had merely gone to Yellow Springs Star to vent his anger and kill some people from Netherworld Sect. Apart from that, what trouble could he possibly cause?

To him, this did not affect the overall situation and was not worth too much attention. Netherworld Sect was already in its twilight years. Even without Yang Kai wreaking havoc there, it would not have lasted much longer. Besides, the rise and fall of power, birth, aging, sickness, and death were the natural order of all things. The most important matter at present was how to withstand the encroachment of the opposing Star Domain.

Never could he have imagined that in just three months, Yang Kai would give him such an enormous surprise!

Using some unknown method, Yang Kai had actually made the entire Yellow Springs Star disappear.

It was truly gone, completely and utterly vanished!

How had he done it? How could such a thing even be possible? A Cultivation Star had been stolen just like that? Wu Heng himself would not believe it if he heard it aloud. This was not something that could be placed inside a Spatial Ring. This was a real, genuine star!

If it had not been stolen, then where had Yellow Springs Star gone?

He was the Master of the Star Domain and controlled the Star Domain Origin. As long as he wished, he could pay attention to anything happening in any corner of Great Wilderness Star Domain. Yellow Springs Star's disappearance was absolutely not an illusion.

Wu Heng's mood sank to rock bottom. Because he had split his attention, the power resisting the darkness weakened greatly, causing the other Star Domain's encroachment to speed up once more. Panicked, he hurriedly gathered his thoughts and no longer dared to be distracted by anything else, but the unease in his heart grew stronger and stronger, almost becoming tangible as it filled his chest.

He knew he had suffered a terrible defeat this time, one so severe he had never imagined possible. He had never expected his boat to capsize in the gutter of this Lower Plane Star Domain.
