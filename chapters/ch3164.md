## Chapter 3164 — Lend Me One Thing

As the Star Lord of Fire Cloud Star, Chi Huo also possessed cultivation at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm. He stood almost at the very peak of the Star Domain, and cultivating any further held little meaning, so five hundred years ago he had begun to give himself over to pleasure, tasting fine wines and enjoying beautiful women, living as happily as anyone could wish.

The concubine he was taking today was only a First Stage Origin Returning Realm cultivator, but she was a peerless beauty.

Most cultivators cared little for ceremony. In the palace hall, Chi Huo and the bride sat side by side, both dressed in bright red. Guests from every side celebrated to their hearts' content, and whenever they came forward to toast him, they presented lavish gifts as well. Their flattery and fawning drew peals of thunderous laughter from Chi Huo.

The bride sat still, a faint smile on her face, dignified and graceful in every respect. Many women pointed at her in secret, their whispers full of envy and jealousy, convinced that she had latched onto a powerful patron and that from this day on, a sparrow had become a phoenix.

When a Second Stage Void King Realm expert came forward to offer his toast, Chi Huo had just lifted his wine cup when he suddenly raised his head and looked into the distance. His gaze seemed to pierce the void, and his expression turned extremely grave.

The great hall instantly fell silent. Every eye turned to him.

Chi Huo set down his wine cup and shouted in a clear voice, "Which friend has honored us with a visit? This Chi Huo has failed to welcome you from afar. Honored guest, please come over for a chat."

One sentence shocked the entire hall.

They had never seen Sir Chi Huo act so politely before. His cultivation had already reached the peak, and he was Fire Cloud Star's Star Lord besides. It could almost be said that he held the lives of the star's billions of cultivators in his hands. Even another Third Stage Void King Realm expert would have to humble himself before him.

What kind of person could make Sir Chi Huo treat him so solemnly? Who could possibly be qualified for such treatment?

While they were still puzzled, an utterly unfamiliar voice reached their ears. "Sure."

A strange feeling rose in them. Although they had yet to see the speaker, that reply was far too presumptuous, almost as if he had no idea how high the Heavens were or how deep the Earth was. Facing Fire Cloud Star's Star Lord, he had not shown even the most basic respect.

The lively mood in the hall chilled at once, and everyone's expression turned strange.

Yang Kai streaked like lightning across the three-thousand-li Flame Mountain, heading straight for the towering Flame Palace.

A shout suddenly came from below. "My Lord, please take me with you!"

Yang Kai looked down and saw someone standing on a slab of black stone. Lava churned around him, the heat was blistering, and the surroundings were harsh and perilous.

This person was only a Second Stage Origin Returning Realm cultivator. He was not old, about twenty-seven or twenty-eight, with a square face. His clothes hung in rags, and he must have suffered badly to reach this place. Much of his exposed skin bore burns from the lava, and the protective Saint Yuan around him had gone so dim it looked ready to collapse at any moment.

As Chi Huo's place of residence, how could the three-thousand-li Flame Mountain be somewhere ordinary people could trespass? Without guidance, a Second Stage Origin Returning Realm cultivator would only end up buried here.

Yet he had not retreated. Though he was in an utterly miserable state, his resolve was unwavering, as though even if Yang Kai refused him, he would still force his way to Flame Palace, and even death here would leave him without complaint.

"You called me?" Yang Kai stopped and glanced at him.

"Please take me with you, my Lord. Cui Mengyuan will be endlessly grateful!" The young man bowed deeply. Although his face was covered in grime, his etiquette was flawless, making it obvious at a glance that he had been raised with exceptional care.

"Why should I take you with me?" Yang Kai grinned. "What is in it for me?"

Cui Mengyuan shook his head. "Aside from my life, this Cui has nothing."

Yang Kai curled his lip. "Your life is not worth much to me."

Cui Mengyuan's eyes dimmed. He sighed softly and stopped pressing the matter. Just as he leapt up to fly away, the rolling lava around him seemed to come alive, condensing into fire pythons that snapped at him.

Cui Mengyuan's heart sank. Knowing he was certain to die, he simply gave up resisting and cast one last, deeply reluctant look toward Flame Palace.

The scorching heat suddenly vanished, and the pain he had expected never came. Cui Mengyuan froze, then realized someone had saved him. The fire pythons lunging in from all sides turned back into lava and fell away.

He looked up at Yang Kai in gratitude. "Many thanks, my Lord."

"Forget it. Since fate brought us together today, I will take the loss and bring you along!" Yang Kai grinned, grabbed Cui Mengyuan by the collar, and carried him like a chick. In an instant, they sped away like wind and lightning, the surrounding scenery whipping past.

In the blink of an eye, they arrived at the top of Flame Mountain.

After Yang Kai tossed him down, Cui Mengyuan still had not quite recovered his wits. He was about to thank him again when he saw Yang Kai stride toward Flame Palace, so he gritted his teeth and hurried after him.

The palace gates stood wide open, revealing a scene of gilded splendor and extravagant luxury.

As Yang Kai stepped inside, gazes converged on him from every direction, filled with scrutiny and faint hostility.

At the head of the hall sat a large man with red hair and a red beard, as imposing as an iron tower. Dressed in a bright red robe, he looked like a mass of flame capable of burning all under Heaven to ash.

His red pupils were like two dazzling lanterns, flickering with a frightening light.

Cui Mengyuan slipped out from behind Yang Kai and looked at the bride beside Chi Huo, his expression complicated. His lips moved, and he murmured a single word.

Quiet as it was, it did not escape Yang Kai's ears.

The bride also trembled, but she soon lowered her eyes and returned to normal.

Chi Huo and Yang Kai met each other's eyes, one overbearing and aggressive, the other calm and deep. Invisible forces seemed to clash inside the hall, and in an instant the atmosphere became oppressive to the extreme.

All of a sudden, Chi Huo laughed and reached out to take the bride's jade-like hand. "Today is this Lord's day of great joy, and now an honored guest has come to my door. Truly a cause for celebration. May I ask the honored guest's noble name?"

Although he asked, he remained seated above him, looking down from on high. He did not even glance at Cui Mengyuan, treating him as if he did not exist.

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "A name is only a label, so there is no need to mention it. I came uninvited today because I have a matter to trouble Brother Chi with."

Chi Huo lowered his eyes and said lightly, "Whatever it is, let us speak of it after today. Today is the joyous day this Chi takes a concubine. If Young Brother does not mind, you may stay and drink a cup of wine."

He had deigned to ask a question, yet received no proper answer, and displeasure immediately stirred in him. This youth was far too arrogant, leaving him with no desire to say much more. And the boy had actually called him Brother Chi? Was that not a bit too self-important? Did he really think that because Chi Huo had given him a little face, he could speak to him as an equal? The only reason Chi Huo did not drive him away outright was that he vaguely sensed Yang Kai's strength, and because today was a day of great joy, he did not wish to invite trouble.

On any ordinary day, with his temper, how could he have endured this?

His form of address for Yang Kai had quietly changed to Young Brother, naturally carrying a lofty air.

He thought Yang Kai would obey, only for Yang Kai to shake his head. "This matter is of great importance. Although I am not in a hurry, the sooner it is dealt with, the better. Brother Chi, please forgive me."

Chi Huo's face darkened.

He and Yang Kai had never met, so there could be no grudge between them. After only a few exchanged words, he merely disliked Yang Kai's arrogance, which was not enough to warrant taking action. But if this fellow pushed his luck, Chi Huo did not mind adding another splash of blood to Flame Palace.

He said in a deep voice, "Is that so? Then say it. Let me hear exactly how important this matter is."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "It concerns the peace of this Star Domain!"

Chi Huo froze for a moment, then laughed like thunder. The many guests laughed as well, all of them openly mocking him.

A cultivator had suddenly stepped forward and claimed he had business concerning the peace of this Star Domain. It was truly impossible not to laugh, as if a frog had run up to tell them the sky was about to collapse.

The guests here were all perceptive people and had long since noticed the change in Chi Huo's attitude. Seeing Yang Kai act as if he did not know when to back down, how could they hold back? Immediately, an old man with a goatee shook his head and said, "Boy, this is Flame Palace, not a place for you to spout nonsense. Go back to wherever you came from. Sir Chi Huo is in a good mood today and likely will not fuss over you too much."

"That's right, boy. Hurry up and get lost. Do not delay our toasts."

"Sir Chi Huo treated him so politely, and he turns out to be a madman. Truly laughable."

"This Star Domain has been at peace for ages. How dare you spread heresy and mislead the crowd!"

...

The various jeers passed Yang Kai by like a breeze. Unmoved, he simply frowned at Chi Huo and said, "Brother Chi, have you sealed off all news from outside? Why?"

These guests seemed to know nothing about what had happened in the Star Domain over the past few years. They even claimed it had long been peaceful. Did they not communicate with the outside world? Thinking back to the barrier he had encountered when he first arrived at Fire Cloud Star, Yang Kai immediately understood. It was not that they did not communicate with the outside world, but that this star's Star Lord had sealed everything off.

They simply could not obtain any news from outside.

He turned to the goateed old man. "Old Sir, do you know of Great Wilderness Star Domain? Do you know of Netherworld Sect?"

The goateed old man frowned and said, "What nonsense are you talking about?"

"Where did this mad boy come from? His wild ravings are spoiling everyone's mood. Sir Chi Huo, please permit me to throw him out." Someone spoke with righteous indignation. This had been a perfectly good wedding feast, but the boy had stirred everything up the moment he arrived, robbing them of their chance to flatter Chi Huo. Naturally, they were unhappy.

"Brother Chi, why did you do this?" Yang Kai looked at Chi Huo, somewhat puzzled.

To save himself? If so, that was far too laughable. If Hengluo Star Domain fell, how could a single Fire Cloud Star resist?

Chi Huo said lightly, "It has nothing to do with you."

Yang Kai thought for a moment, then nodded. "True enough." Why meddle so much in someone else's affairs? Even so, he still gave a reminder. "Brother Chi, there is no need for this. Over the past two years, Great Wilderness Star Domain and Netherworld Sect have already lost their momentum. The cultivators of my Star Domain have won victory after victory and killed countless enemies, but the war is not over yet. If Brother Chi is willing, you can go lend our side a hand. I believe that with everyone from Fire Cloud Star assisting, the war will end a little sooner."

Chi Huo grinned. "Why do you not go lend them a hand?"

Yang Kai said, "I have a more important matter to handle, which is precisely why I came here today."

"Then this Lord would like to know exactly what this more important matter is."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "I ask Brother Chi to lend me one thing!"

"Lend what?" Chi Huo asked in a deep voice.

Truly laughable. Every guest who came here had brought gifts. It was one thing for him to arrive empty-handed, but he actually wanted to borrow something? Killing intent had already risen in Chi Huo's heart. If Yang Kai dared say anything that displeased him, Chi Huo would certainly strike with the force of thunder.

"This star's Origin!" Yang Kai pointed at the ground beneath his feet.
