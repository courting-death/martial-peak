## Chapter 3183 — Rebirth Amid Destruction

"What the hell is that thing!"

Ao Wei's expression changed drastically as the darkness descended from the sky.

It was a colossal black sphere, nearly a hundred li across, with a fanged maw split open at its center. The clacking that reached his ears made his hair stand on end and sent a chill through his entire body.

Even as a Star Lord, he had no courage to face such a monster head-on. Its overwhelming pressure bore down from above, leaving his chest tight and his breathing strained.

"Who dares block my path? Are you tired of living?" a voice suddenly called out. Ao Wei was startled, and only then did he notice someone standing above the black sphere. It was a young man, standing calmly on the back of that black sphere monster and looking down on him from above as if he were staring at an ant.

Ao Wei swallowed lightly, cupped his fists, and called out, "Ao Wei, Star Lord of Kuanglang Star, greets Sir."

"Oh?" Yang Kai raised a brow. "You're this star's Star Lord?"

"Indeed!"

"Perfect. This Young Master wants to devour your Kuanglang Star, so cooperate with me a little. Once it's done, there'll be plenty of benefits!" If he could get the Star Lord to cooperate, the devouring would definitely be much easier.

Ao Wei's eye twitched, and he nearly wondered if he had misheard. This man had clearly come with ill intent, yet after brazenly declaring that he wanted to devour Kuanglang Star, he actually wanted Ao Wei to cooperate? Anger flared in him at once, but he did not dare let it show. Suppressing his temper, he said, "May I ask how my Kuanglang Star has offended Your Excellency?"

Yang Kai said impatiently, "There is no offending or not offending. I just happened to come here, that's all."

Happened to... Ao Wei had never imagined that this world-shaking catastrophe was merely the result of chance. His body straightened, and he said in a clear voice, "The Heavens cherish life. Billions of living beings have lived and multiplied on Kuanglang Star for generations. If Your Excellency devours this star, what will become of them? Those of our generation contend for the Great Dao, and life and death are decided by fate, but what does that have to do with the innocent? If Your Excellency truly destroys Kuanglang Star, karmic sin will surely cling to you and harm your future cultivation. I ask Your Excellency to think thrice."

His advice was sincere, and his earnestness showed in every word.

Yang Kai braced one hand on his thigh, leaned forward, and grinned. "What are you trying to do?"

No aura came from him, yet Ao Wei's expression still changed. For a moment, he felt as if a giant beast from the Primordial Era had opened its jaws toward him, ready to swallow him whole. Fortunately, he was Kuanglang Star's Star Lord, and the unseen fortune of Heaven and Earth was gathered around him at this moment. Otherwise, he might truly have turned and fled. He cupped his fists again and shouted in a low voice, "Please think thrice!"

Yang Kai rubbed his chin and considered it with a serious expression for a moment, then nodded. "Done."

"Then Sir means..." Ao Wei looked at him eagerly.

"My mind is made up. Hurry up and get out of the way!" If devouring a Cultivation Star truly meant the deaths of billions of living beings, Yang Kai would certainly be unable to bear it. Heaven and Earth were heartless, treating all things as straw dogs, but even with his impressive cultivation, he had not reached the point of seeing all living beings as ants he could casually slaughter.

The Small Xuan Realm's devouring would indeed destroy a star, but it would not harm a single living being on it. It would merely give them a different place to live. Moreover, Yang Kai was confident he could make the Small Xuan Realm better than any Cultivation Star. In other words, what he was doing now would not harm those billions of living beings at all, and in the distant future, it would even bring them endless benefits.

So he had no need to think any further.

Ao Wei's expression shifted again and again, but he refused to yield. As a Star Lord, he had no room to retreat. His body shook, and the aura of a peak Void King Realm master spread out. At the same time, scales appeared across his face, lending him a somewhat Yao Beast-like appearance. From his appearance, he clearly was not a human. Even though he knew he was no match and that striking out would end badly, Ao Wei still stood in his path and shouted coldly, "If Sir insists on this, then step over this Ao's corpse first."

His voice was not loud, yet under the influence of the will of Heaven and Earth, it spread across the entire Cultivation Star. The billions of living beings on Kuanglang Star were instantly moved, feeling that having such a responsible Star Lord was truly a blessing for all of them. Invisibly, Ao Wei's momentum climbed to a new height, as if those few words had brought him tremendous benefit, yet even that failed to give him the slightest sense of safety.

"Oh? You're a Jiao?" Yang Kai studied the scales on his face and the beast-like traces on his body, catching a somewhat familiar scent in the air.

"Indeed!" Ao Wei nodded. There was no need to hide this, since it was no secret.

Yang Kai nodded. "I know a Jiao, but... you're too weak."

Ao Wei had been courteous and humble all along, keeping his posture extremely low, but these words still left him deeply unconvinced. He already stood at the peak of the Void King Realm and was Kuanglang Star's Star Lord besides. In all of Great Wilderness Star Domain, almost no one was his match, yet this level of cultivation was being called too weak?

Anger surged up and washed away some of the fear in his heart. Gritting his teeth, he said, "Is that so? Then this Ao would like to experience Sir's methods."

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Don't refuse to believe me when I say this. That fellow could probably exchange a few moves with me. As for you..."

Ao Wei shouted furiously, "Then this Ao would like to see how many moves Sir needs to defeat me!" As he spoke, he hurled himself at Yang Kai.

A faint dragon roar rang out, though it was far from pure. Jiao had Dragon Clan bloodlines, but those bloodlines were thin, so how could they compare with a True Dragon? Still, with the power of the entire Kuanglang Star supporting him, his momentum was imposing enough.

In an instant, Heaven and Earth changed color, and wind and clouds surged. A Star Lord's attack was naturally no ordinary matter.

Yang Kai seemed to have no intention of defending himself, nor did he adopt any stance to attack. He simply watched the charging Ao Wei without expression.

“This man is far too arrogant. He simply does not take me seriously. I must teach him a lesson.” Yang Kai’s attitude undoubtedly enraged Ao Wei completely, and he attacked without holding anything back, unleashing nearly every bit of his strength.

An invisible power suddenly spread from Yang Kai.

Ao Wei froze in midair, his eyes flying wide in extreme shock.

In that instant, he seemed to see a giant dragon coiling before him, its enormous head looming overhead. All of his strength rapidly collapsed and scattered, unable to gather at all. He could not even draw a breath.

Then the dragon head lunged, its great mouth opening wide before swallowing him whole.

Ao Wei screamed miserably, his clothes soaked through with sweat.

Having cultivated to this degree, he was rarely frightened by anything. Even when Yang Kai had arrived with Gungun just now, he had still been able to confront him, question him, and even attack, but before a giant dragon, he did not even have the courage to look it in the eye.

This was the suppression of bloodline.

By the time he came back to his senses, there was no giant dragon before him at all, only an utterly detestable young man smiling at him.

An illusion? If it was an illusion, it had been far too real.

Before he could make sense of it, Yang Kai raised a hand and said, "Get in here."

Gungun opened its great mouth and swallowed him in one bite.

My life is over! The thought flashed through Ao Wei's mind. He closed his eyes and stretched out his neck to await death, but after waiting for quite a while, no pain came. When he opened his eyes, he was stunned.

He had actually appeared in another world. This world looked rather chaotic. All around him were millions upon millions of qing of land and stone, piled in a disorderly sprawl. As far as he could see, the same sight stretched everywhere.

After sensing his surroundings for a moment, he was startled again, because the Spiritual Qi of Heaven and Earth here was extremely rich, several grades better than Kuanglang Star's. Only the World Laws seemed somewhat incomplete, making the power in his body circulate a little sluggishly.

What the hell was this place? Hadn't he been swallowed by that pitch-black giant beast? How had he ended up here?

Something glittering nearby caught his attention, pinned beneath a boulder. He walked over, lifted the boulder away, and a plaque immediately came into view.

"Netherworld Sect!" Ao Wei's eyes narrowed, and he could hardly believe what he was seeing.

Wasn't this... wasn't this Netherworld Sect's plaque? Although he was Kuanglang Star's Star Lord, he had visited Yellow Springs Star in his early years and entered Netherworld Sect. This was precisely the plaque that had stood before Netherworld Sect's mountain gate.

He was certain he had not mistaken it, and that weathered, timeworn air could not be faked. This was definitely Netherworld Sect's mountain gate plaque, so how could it be here?

As he recalled the rumors that had been spreading on Kuanglang Star during this period, an exceedingly bold guess surfaced in his mind.

His ears twitched as some sounds reached him.

Ao Wei flew up and looked into the distance, just in time to see a group of people approaching together. He immediately flew over, exchanged a few words with them, and became even more shocked.

These people... were actually all residents of Netherworld Sect!

"Sir Ao Wei... lost!"

"Sir Ao Wei was actually swallowed in one bite!"

"The end has come! The end has come!"

On Kuanglang Star, countless pairs of eyes had seen Ao Wei swallowed by that pitch-black giant beast in a single bite, without even the strength to resist. The sight left everyone feeling as if they had plunged into an ice cellar.

They had never imagined that Ao Wei, their Star Lord, would be defeated so easily. They had still been counting on him to save them from disaster, but now that hope instantly turned into deep despair.

When Yang Kai drove Gungun down toward Kuanglang Star, no one dared stand in his way again.

Gungun's great mouth opened and closed, devouring island after island, large and small, and even the boundless seawater was swallowed into its belly.

People cried and wailed, fleeing in every direction, while countless others cursed and reviled.

Yang Kai paid them no mind, sitting cross-legged on Gungun's back as it gnawed through the whole of Kuanglang Star in a single direction.

What a bunch of ignorant fools. This Young Master is taking you to a world of bliss, and you still don't appreciate it. Those who escape can only count themselves unlucky.

His thoughts suddenly stirred. What difference was there between what he was doing now and what Heaven Devourer Great Emperor Wu Kuang had done back then? Wu Kuang had brought disaster to the Star Domain and destroyed countless stars as well, ultimately provoking the wrath of Heaven and the resentment of men until the Great Emperors surrounded and killed him.

No. Wu Kuang was Wu Kuang, and he was himself. Their methods were completely different.

On the stars Wu Kuang destroyed, all living beings were wiped out. Yang Kai, however, was merely sending those living beings to another world and had not harmed them at all. On the contrary, many years from now, they would probably have to thank him.

Order was reestablished amid chaos. Only through destruction could there be rebirth.
