## Chapter 3169 — Jingkong Beast King

Before Yang Kai could rejoice, another change occurred.

The countless Stellar Origins gathered within him suddenly erupted, and immense surges of energy tore through Yang Kai's body, rampaging without restraint.

Yang Kai had sensed this as early as when he refined Fire Cloud Star's Stellar Origin. Too many Stellar Origins in one body would inevitably give rise to irreconcilable conflicts. Back then, he had relied on his powerful cultivation to forcefully suppress the Origins inside him.

But after all these years of traveling and refining, the Origins inside him were no longer the meager handful he had possessed back then.

There were thousands, tens of thousands!

In an instant, his body seemed to turn into a battlefield where thousands or even tens of thousands of children were shouting, wrestling, and fighting without end. If this had happened to an ordinary cultivator, even one in the Emperor Realm, their meridians would likely have been ruined and their flesh body destroyed in short order.

But Yang Kai's body was extraordinarily tough. After so many years of cultivation, he had always paid close attention to body tempering, and the strength of his flesh body was far beyond the ordinary. With his half-dragon body on top of that, this commotion was not much to him. He only felt faint stabs of pain pulsing through him.

Yang Kai's Spirit Body withdrew from his Sea of Consciousness and immediately discovered what was happening to his body.

"After the Sea of Consciousness comes the flesh body? Refining the Star Domain Origin really isn't easy!" He had expected something like this, so he did not panic. Instead, a fierce fighting spirit rose in him, eager for the challenge.

Multicolored light burst from every pore across his body. In an instant, Yang Kai seemed bathed in a sheet of rosy clouds, brighter and more magnificent than any ordinary sunset glow. Even his mouth, nose, and eyes shone with dazzling light.

Stellar Origins differed greatly from one another, and each had its own color. When they manifested on Yang Kai's body, this was the scene they produced.

If anyone had been watching, they would certainly have feared that he might explode and die at any moment.

Countless Law Powers, broadly alike yet subtly different, clashed violently within him. These were the Laws inherent to the Stellar Origins themselves. Their sheer number soon reached a turning point, and intense pain swept through him, as if his body might collapse at any moment.

Only now did he realize he had underestimated just how destructive these bratty children could be when they threw a tantrum.

Not daring to be careless, he let out a thunderous shout. "Dragon Transformation!"

A resonant dragon roar rang out. The phantom of a golden giant dragon flashed behind Yang Kai, then plunged into his body. Crack, crack, crack. The sound of bones shifting echoed out in a dense, continuous chain.

Yang Kai's still rather heroic figure swelled rapidly, as if someone had blown air into him.

His long hair flew wildly, and two small bumps rose on his forehead. Short dragon horns pushed out from those fleshy mounds, sturdy dragon scales covered his exposed skin, his hands became sharp dragon claws, and a dragon tail abruptly appeared behind him.

Three zhang, five zhang, ten zhang, fifteen zhang, eighteen zhang...

Twenty zhang!

In a flash, Yang Kai had transformed into a half-dragon, half-human monster, standing alone in the Starry Sky.

He could actually transform into a twenty-zhang dragon body. That was an unexpected joy. Yang Kai remembered that when he used the Dragon Transformation Art on Dragon Isle, he had only reached eighteen zhang. He had not expected to grow another two zhang after all these years.

Although two zhang was not much, it reflected the purity of his Dragon Clan bloodline. Back then, he had taken many Dragon Blood Flowers and Dragon Blood Pills, but he had not been able to absorb all their medicinal efficacy at once. Much of it had remained hidden in his flesh and blood. The extra two zhang of length was undoubtedly the result of those hidden medicinal effects slowly shaping him over the years.

He gently exhaled, and two streams of white smoke billowed from his nostrils.

Nearly all his cultivation erupted in that moment, no longer held back.

The void shuddered and collapsed. Such strength had already surpassed the limit this world could endure. Even if he fought no one and merely stood there, he could make Heaven and Earth tremble.

Around him, the void was like an unsettled lake, rippling layer after layer as it wavered constantly between collapse and restoration.

Immense hostility enveloped him. It was the will of this Star Domain.

In the empty silence of the Starry Sky, strands of lightning appeared from nowhere and flickered. In a single instant, the thousand-li area around him became a Thunder Pool. Lightning arcs leapt and merged into one continuous expanse, quickly turning into thick bolts that blasted toward him from every direction.

Yang Kai threw back his head and laughed, wild as a madman. He made no deliberate attempt to resist, allowing the lightning to strike his body while focusing all his attention on suppressing the power of the myriad Stellar Origins inside him.

Hong long long...

The Starry Sky flickered between light and darkness. Under the thunder's bombardment, his dragon scales soon split apart and his flesh charred black.

The will and repulsion of this Star Domain were indeed difficult to endure. Even with his heaven-reaching strength, he could not simply ignore them.

However, his recovery ability was already extremely formidable, and now that he also possessed this half-dragon body, any damage he suffered healed quickly.

Thunder rumbled in wave after wave, bombarding him for a full half hour. Yang Kai's body only grew straighter and more imposing. Across his twenty-zhang-tall half-dragon body, the myriad Stellar Origins still clashed without end, light bursting from every pore as different Law Powers tangled and collided.

Suddenly, the thunder's power weakened considerably, and the lightning arcs filling the area grew fewer.

Yang Kai's eyes flashed, and he coldly snorted.

If he had no Stellar Origins, if he had not spent the past ten years laying the groundwork, he absolutely would not have dared to release all his strength like this. The only result would have been this world rejecting him outright or blasting him to dregs.

But because he had suppressed the Stellar Origins, and because of his many years of effort, his aura had long since spread throughout the entire Star Domain. Even now, with this world's will noticing his actions and rejecting him, he had gained the means to resist.

All Heaven and Earth could do was make him suffer a little more and try to force him to retreat.

How could Yang Kai retreat? He had endured years of hardship for the sole purpose of refining this Star Domain. Now that success was within reach, how could he possibly let go?

When two sides contended, one was bound to yield and fall. Yang Kai believed with absolute certainty that it would not be him. The weakening thunder before him was undoubtedly the best sign, and it even left him somewhat dissatisfied. He had not expected this world to show weakness so quickly.

"Hm?" Yang Kai suddenly raised his head and looked in a certain direction.

Faintly, he sensed something approaching, but when he investigated carefully, he found nothing at all.

While suppressing the myriad Origins inside him and trying to settle them down, he focused his mind and carefully observed his surroundings.

Sure enough, something was approaching, and at tremendous speed. In the void, layer after layer of ripples swept past, visible to the naked eye.

At a certain moment, the void a thousand li ahead suddenly split open into an enormous black hole, as if that stretch of Starry Sky had been melted away. A colossal creature leapt out from within it.

As the colossal creature appeared, a strange sound rang out. It was neither sharp nor powerful, yet it carried a dreamlike quality. At first, it sounded somewhat like the mournful cry of a dolphin, brimming with despair over life and lingering attachment to it, yet it was absolutely not a sound any dolphin could make.

The colossal creature's shape also resembled a dolphin, except it was a giant dolphin magnified tens of millions of times!

The Starry Sky was the ocean through which it roamed, and the void was its home!

"Jingkong Beast!" Yang Kai's eyes widened as he recognized the colossal creature.

It was actually a Jingkong Beast! Such a creature was truly rare. In his early years, Yang Kai had once seen a Jingkong Beast and had even killed one, obtaining its Inner Core. Jingkong Beasts could be considered ancient relic species. They were born proficient in the Power of Space, an innate ability rooted deep in their bones. It was rumored that they could freely shuttle between different planes without encountering any obstruction.

The void power that made others turn pale at the mere mention of it was the very resource they used to grow, and the place they most liked to dwell was the Void Crack. They could devour the chaotic void currents within as nourishment.

This was precisely why Jingkong Beasts were so rarely seen. Most of the time, they swam through the Void Cracks, making it very difficult for ordinary worlds to catch even a glimpse of them.

Yang Kai never would have expected a Jingkong Beast to appear at a time like this.

"No, it's a Jingkong Beast King!" Yang Kai compared it to his memories from years ago and soon discovered that this was no ordinary Jingkong Beast, but a Jingkong Beast King. That mountain-like body was absolutely not something an ordinary Jingkong Beast could possess.

The dreamlike cry rang out again and poured straight into Yang Kai's ears whether he wanted to hear it or not, making his head swim once more.

The Jingkong Beast King's tail fin swept lightly, and ripples spread through the void. It crossed a thousand li in a single leap and arrived not far in front of Yang Kai, so fast he almost had no time to react.

Before this Jingkong Beast King, his enormous twenty-zhang dragon body was like a speck of dust before a mountain.

Yang Kai truly felt as if a great mountain were pressing down on him, making it hard to breathe.

Yet the Jingkong Beast King seemed not to have noticed him at all. Its huge mouth opened, and that giant maw was like a Void Passage connected to a space of chaos and nothingness, ready to swallow Yang Kai into its belly in the next instant.

The Jingkong Beast King could not have appeared here for no reason, and it was even more impossible that it had failed to notice him. Though powerful, it was not invincible, and the aura Yang Kai was radiating was far from weak. If it possessed even the slightest reason, it would never have charged straight at him like this, much less opened its mouth at just the right moment to swallow him.

In that instant, Yang Kai felt the full malice of this world.

The Jingkong Beast had appeared here, and looked as though it were charging straight at him, definitely because this world had guided it. The Thunder Pool had weakened only to prepare an even stronger killing move.

As the saying went, Heaven and Earth were not benevolent and treated all things as straw dogs. How vicious. Truly vicious.

Under ordinary circumstances, Yang Kai would not have minded a great battle with this Jingkong Beast King. Perhaps he could even kill it and take its Inner Core. The Inner Core of a Jingkong Beast King would definitely be of great benefit to his cultivation of the Power of Space. But today, he had something more important to do, so he could only spare it for now.

He clapped his hands together, then pulled them apart, and an enormous Moon Blade suddenly took shape. With his current twenty-zhang body, how could the Moon Blade he condensed possibly be small? If he truly slashed it out, even the Jingkong Beast King before him would not be able to ignore it.
