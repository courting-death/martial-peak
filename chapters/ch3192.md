## Chapter 3192 — Seeing Yang Yan Again

What kind of joke was that? This guy had inexplicably run over to stand up for Wu Heng, fought him, and now he actually wanted Yang Kai to return his Star-Cutting Sword? He could go fuck himself! In truth, it was not only the feather-crowned man who felt annoyed; Yang Kai was equally annoyed. His reinforcements had arrived at a rather poor time—a little too early.

If the feather-crowned man knew what he was thinking, he would probably mock him again for overestimating himself.

But no matter what, the Star-Cutting Sword was his spoils of war. How could he hand it back so easily? Unless the feather-crowned man knelt down and called him Grandfather a few times, then maybe he could consider it.

"There was no trouble under Heaven until fools made trouble for themselves. Do you think this Lord cannot take back the Star-Cutting Sword?" The feather-crowned man looked coldly at Yang Kai, then glanced at Gungun beside him.

During this fight, Gungun had been unable to lend any strength. It was not that Yang Kai had deliberately hidden his hand. After Gungun swallowed the Star-Cutting Sword, it had been rampaging unchecked through the Small Xuan Realm, and Gungun had to mobilize the power of the entire Small Xuan Realm to suppress it. Even then, the suppression showed signs of instability. The slightest carelessness could cause tremendous damage to the Small Xuan Realm.

Yang Kai was just about to speak when the woman said, "Leave this to me."

The feather-crowned man glanced at her and nodded lightly. "Good!"

Since she had spoken, he naturally could not object.

The woman turned around and gazed at Yang Kai. Their eyes met, and Yang Kai thought he saw her wink at him, though it might have been an illusion.

Her pleasant voice suddenly rang in his mind. "The Star-Cutting Sword has to be returned."

Yang Kai frowned. "We can't keep it?"

That sword was extremely peculiar. It could actually sever his connection to Hengluo Star Domain, and the reason he had been able to clash with the feather-crowned man this time was because, from the very start, he had risked injury to exile the Star-Cutting Sword into Gungun's body.

Yang Kai had a feeling that if he had not done so, he would have lost long ago.

"It cannot be kept. The Star-Cutting Sword does not belong to him. It belongs to the Star Court. I have one as well; it is the assigned sword of a Star Envoy. If you insist on keeping it, you will only make an enemy of the Star Court."

"It actually belongs to the Star Court!" Yang Kai was astonished, then perked up. "In that case, if I refuse to return it, won't he be in serious trouble?"

The woman was visibly stunned, as if she had not expected him to think that way. Generally speaking, once someone learned that the Star-Cutting Sword belonged to the Star Court, they would certainly feel they had no choice but to return it. After all, an arm could not twist a thigh. Yet his thoughts had gone straight to a different outcome.

She could not help laughing. "Correct. He would be severely punished, and might even be stripped of his Star Envoy status." Then her tone shifted. "But you would not have an easy time either."

"Understood, understood!" Yang Kai nodded again and again.

As a Star Envoy, losing the assigned sword bestowed by the Star Court was a serious dereliction of duty, while Yang Kai, who had taken the Star-Cutting Sword, would likewise be punished for showing contempt toward the Star Court.

"If you trust me, leave this matter to me. I will make sure you do not suffer a loss."

"I trust you!" How could he not? If she truly was that person.

"Good. I will go negotiate with him."

"Bleed him dry!" Yang Kai transmitted.

The woman pursed her lips in a smile, then turned and began communicating with the feather-crowned man. Their Divine Senses surged, and Yang Kai had no idea what they were saying. He only saw the feather-crowned man's expression shift again and again, sometimes grave, sometimes furious, while he kept shaking his head.

Clearly, the woman had set some condition that was difficult for him to accept, and Yang Kai felt thoroughly delighted as he watched.

This fight had originally been a clash between the Masters of two Star Domains, but in the end, it had dragged in two great Star Envoys.

So I have a backer now too? Yang Kai looked at that familiar yet unfamiliar face, his thoughts churning. Thinking about it, it made sense. Back then, she had once come to this Star Domain. Since she was a Star Envoy, it was only natural that she governed this Star Domain.

Silently, he withdrew the Dragon Transformation Secret Technique, and his body returned to its original size. Exhaustion swept over him, and his left hand throbbed with unbearable pain.

Although today's battle had not lasted long, it had been extremely dangerous. As the Master of a Star Domain, he had gained no advantage from fighting on his own ground. He had relied entirely on his own strength. Fortunately, the Dragon Transformation Art had made some progress, so the fight had not been entirely without gain.

He circulated his strength to heal the injuries on his left hand and quietly waited for the result.

A moment later, the woman held out her hand toward Yang Kai. "Hand it over."

Yang Kai naturally knew what she meant. His mind connected with the Small Xuan Realm, and Gungun immediately opened its great mouth. A streak of sword light flashed like lightning and shot out from within, cold Sword Qi cutting straight toward Yang Kai.

Yang Kai's expression did not change, and mockery appeared on his face.

The Star-Cutting Sword suddenly changed direction one chi from him, circled him once, and only then fell into the feather-crowned man's hand.

The feather-crowned man's face was ashen. He sheathed the sword, cupped his fist to the woman, and said, "I will take my leave first." Turning around, he stepped into the darkness behind him and vanished in the blink of an eye. From beginning to end, he had not learned Yang Kai's name, and Yang Kai had not learned his, yet they had formed a grudge without even meaning to.

Only after the feather-crowned man left did the woman turn around and look at Yang Kai with a beaming smile.

Yang Kai grinned as well. With a shift of his feet, he rushed up to her and looked her over from head to toe, as if trying to recognize her all over again.

The woman's smile vanished. Anger touched her pretty face, and she cried delicately, "Bold madman, you actually dare cultivate the Heaven Devouring Battle Art! Speak, how do you wish to die? I can give you a quick end!"

Yang Kai froze. For a moment, the woman before him seemed utterly unfamiliar, and he could not help wondering whether she was truly the person he knew.

"Stop messing around!" Without another word, he opened his arms and pulled her into his embrace.

Her long lashes fluttered several times, and her bright eyes filled with astonishment, as if she had not expected things to turn out this way. Caught off guard, she actually let him hug her.

Yet Yang Kai felt not the slightest desire.

This was not intimacy between a man and a woman, only the embrace of old friends reunited after a long parting.

A slender jade hand rose and pressed against Yang Kai's chest, pushing him away. That hand looked tender as a young scallion, yet it carried a vast force he could not resist. One chi apart, Yang Kai smiled freely and complained, "Yang Yan, you made me search so bitterly."

Yang Yan tilted her head. "Yang Yan? Who is that? I don't know her!"

Yang Kai nodded repeatedly. "I almost didn't recognize you either."

Back then, she had been extremely reserved. She wore a black robe all year round and even kept the hood pulled over her head, as if she were terrified of letting anyone see her true face. Meeting her again several dozen years later, who could have imagined she would have such an elegant, noble side? That lofty, otherworldly bearing had nearly made him afraid to recognize her.

He could not help recalling the time she had been frightened and thrown herself into his arms, and he could not resist glancing once more at her chest.

"What filthy thoughts are you having?" Yang Yan seemed able to see through his mind and reached out to knock him on the head.

There was a dong. Yang Kai rubbed his head and said, "Why hit me? Careful, or I might fall out with you."

Yang Yan glared at him. "Try it."

Yang Kai gave a cheeky grin. "Well, I was only saying it. Don't take it seriously. Mm, right, you are also a Star Envoy of the Star Court. Does that mean that after you left back then, you returned to the Star Court?"

"Correct."

"No wonder." Yang Kai nodded. No wonder Yang Yan had entrusted Lin Yun'er to Iron Blood Great Emperor. She was a Star Envoy of the Star Court, while Iron Blood Great Emperor was the Great Emperor currently guarding the Star Court. The two of them could be considered colleagues, so communicating would be convenient. From the look of things, though, Iron Blood Great Emperor was also somewhat neglecting his proper duties and probably did not stay in the Star Court much. Otherwise, Lin Yun'er would not have gone so many years without seeing Yang Yan again. Suddenly, he clenched his fist and said, "Since you were in the Star Realm, why didn't you come find me!"

Yang Yan asked curiously, "Are you my son? Why should I go find you?"

Yang Kai staggered and looked at her with a grief-stricken expression. "Heartless and faithless. As expected, nothing is more poisonous than a woman's heart."

Yang Yan smoothed the hair beside her ear and raised her eyes. "And what affection is there between us?"

Yang Kai waved a hand. "Enough, let us not bring up the past. What's past is past. We should talk about the present. That grandson just now said Star Envoys oversee countless Star Domains. Doesn't that mean that as the Master of a Star Domain, I have to fall under your jurisdiction too?"

Having a backer was certainly nice, but being under someone's jurisdiction was unpleasant. Still, one came with the other, just like light and darkness.

Yang Yan patted her chest and said, “That’s right. From now on, I am your direct superior. Boy, seeing as you have some promise, serve me well in the future and I will support you.”

Yang Kai's eyes widened. He reached out, grabbed her by the neck, and shook her hard. "What did you do to my Yang Yan? Hurry up and give my Yang Yan back!" The old Yang Yan had been so good. How had she become a different person?

In an instant, her hair was disheveled and her hairpins askew. Yang Yan glared at him. "Get your dog paws off me."

Yang Kai instantly withdrew his hands, stroked his chin, and acted as if nothing had happened. "Fine, fine. This works too. If any blind fools show up in the future, I will use your name." Although he would be subject to jurisdiction and restraint, at the very least he would not be bullied again like just now, when any random Star Envoy could come along and oppress him. Then his expression stirred. "Can I have one of those Star-Cutting Swords too?"

Yang Yan rubbed her neck, tidied her hair, and said with contempt, "Wishful thinking. That is a Star Envoy's assigned sword. You, a lower-class Star Domain Master, dare dream of owning one? Go piss and take a look at yourself, hahaha!"

Yang Kai's mouth twitched fiercely, and he regretted it endlessly. He really should have strangled her to death just now. At least then he could have preserved that bit of beauty in his heart.

He held his forehead, dispirited. "Then if I accept the Star Court's jurisdiction, what benefits do I get?"

Yang Yan spread both hands. "Nothing at all."

"I refuse!" Yang Kai said with firm resolve.

"Sorry, but I am afraid you cannot refuse. Star Domain Masters who are not bound and governed by the Star Court are generally stripped of their qualifications."

Yang Kai clenched his fist. "This is a big shop bullying its customers. On what basis do you give me no benefits at all, yet still expect me to obey you?"

Yang Yan slanted him a look. "Do you think you are visiting a brothel? Big shop bullying its customers? If you want to be free of restraint, that is also fine."

"How?" Yang Kai's eyes lit up.

"Just become a Great Emperor."

Yang Kai cursed under his breath several times.

"What did you say?" Yang Yan glared at him.

"Nothing." Yang Kai sniffed and said with righteous dignity, "Forget it. For the sake of this Star Domain, jurisdiction it is. As the saying goes, sacrifice the small self to fulfill the greater self. Heh, my awareness is truly high."
