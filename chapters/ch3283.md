## Chapter 3283 — Old Monster

That was obviously impossible. If Sheng Yuzhu really were a Great Emperor, she would probably have shattered the Void and left this place long ago.

Great Emperors bore the destiny of Heaven and Earth and were acknowledged by the Heavenly Way. A mere Void Gap could not trap such an existence.

"So he really did succeed," Sheng Yuzhu murmured, a strange look in her eyes. After a pause, she said, "No wonder you misunderstood. Only, when he schemed to trap me here, he was not yet a Great Emperor."

Yang Kai's mouth fell open wider than an egg. Those words... contained a rather enormous amount of information.

Wu Kuang had been famous for far too long. The name Heaven Devourer Great Emperor was known to everyone throughout the Star Realm, but who the hell knew exactly when he had become a Great Emperor? Yet Sheng Yuzhu was actually telling Yang Kai that Wu Kuang had not yet reached the position of Great Emperor when she was trapped here.

Then came the question: just how long had she been trapped? One had to know that the War of the Emperors only happened long after Wu Kuang became the Heaven Devourer Great Emperor, and the War of the Emperors itself could be traced back thirty thousand years.

In other words, Sheng Yuzhu had been trapped here for at least thirty to forty thousand years. And in all that time, she had still been unable to escape. After painstaking study, she had only just gained an initial grasp of the Power of Space, barely managing to open a Space Crack and send a message outside.

Yang Kai almost suspected she was lying. How could anyone survive this long in a place like this?

Sheng Yuzhu ignored him and continued speaking to herself, "Back then, Wu Kuang and I were evenly matched, and both of us had the qualifications to contend for the position of Great Emperor. That was exactly why I fell victim to his plot."

Yang Kai's eyes shone. "You competed with Wu Kuang for the position of Great Emperor?" What a bold, magnificent thing that was, and a woman had actually done it. It was almost impossible to imagine. Such a figure must have been dazzling in those days, rising swiftly to fame.

Sheng Yuzhu smiled bitterly. "I never even got the chance. He plotted against me first, and I have drifted through the Void ever since."

Yang Kai looked at her sympathetically. "That is quite the grudge..." In a struggle for the Grand Dao, all means were fair. It could not really be said that Wu Kuang had acted despicably, though he was certainly no good person to begin with. Sheng Yuzhu had simply been one move short.

Still, to have one's Grand Dao stolen was an irreconcilable hatred. Yet Sheng Yuzhu spoke of it so calmly now, without much resentment or bitterness, that Yang Kai could not help thinking more highly of her and feeling that she probably could not be too wicked.

With that, he could confirm Sheng Yuzhu's former cultivation. She was probably the kind of existence Yang Yan had mentioned, someone only half a step from becoming a Great Emperor, unable to achieve a Great Emperor's body only because she had yet to receive the acknowledgment of Heaven and Earth.

Such a figure was absolutely beyond comparison with an ordinary Third Stage Emperor. If the conditions were right, people like that could challenge the Great Emperor position at any time and replace one of the current ten Great Emperors.

Sheng Yuzhu looked Yang Kai up and down again and said, "You do not seem very old, yet you already have the cultivation of the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. Given time, you may well be able to contend with the heroes of the world."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Where the heart is set, the body follows."

Sheng Yuzhu nodded. "Very good. With such ambition, there will surely be a place for you between Heaven and Earth in the future." After a pause, she added, "You still have not told me whether Wu Kuang is alive."

Yang Kai sighed. "Dead!" This was naturally not the truth. Although Wu Kuang's fleshly body had been destroyed, his Divine Soul was still entangled with Duan Hongchen's, and the two shared one body. Strictly speaking, he was half dead and half alive.

Yang Kai answered this way simply because he wanted to see how Sheng Yuzhu would react. If she showed anything abnormal, he would have to consider whether he should bring her out at all.

Hearing this, Sheng Yuzhu clearly froze for a moment, then said blankly, "Dead?"

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Heaven Devourer Great Emperor threw the Star Domain into chaos. Wherever he passed, all life was plunged into misery and the stars were destroyed. The various Great Emperors could not bear it, so they joined forces and slew him in the Shattered Star Sea."

Sheng Yuzhu looked at him. "When did that happen?"

"About thirty thousand years ago."

“So long ago…” Sheng Yuzhu’s expression was complicated as she sighed faintly. “He succeeded through Heaven Devouring, and he was ruined by Heaven Devouring. Cause and effect revolve in a cycle, and retribution never fails.”

Yang Kai said, "Senior should be happy."

Sheng Yuzhu nodded. "I should be. In the early years, I already felt he would not come to a good end. I never thought he would end up being attacked by everyone at once. Forget it. Since he is dead, all those grudges can be considered settled. Let us not mention him. Duan Hongchen, Yao Jun, Zhan Wuhen, and Chu Tianji, how many of them do you know?"

Cold sweat slid down Yang Kai's forehead, and his expression twitched. "Senior Hongchen is a model for my generation, and I have had the fortune to deal with him a few times. Nether Soul Great Emperor, Yao Jun, could also count as someone I have met once. But who are Zhan Wuhen and Chu Tianji?"

Sheng Yuzhu had casually tossed out four names, and two of them belonged to Great Emperors. Although Yang Kai did not know who Zhan Wuhen and Chu Tianji were, he vaguely had a few guesses.

Sheng Yuzhu said, "Zhan Wuhen cultivated the Iron Blood Record and loved battle more than anyone. Perhaps he has another title now, and his true name is no longer known. As for that Chu Tianji... he is a complete charlatan, though he does have some ability to glimpse the secrets of Heaven."

The cold sweat on Yang Kai's forehead flowed even more heavily, and he said weakly, "The people Senior is referring to may be Iron Blood Great Emperor and Heavenly Nexus Great Emperor."

Yang Kai wailed inwardly. As expected of an old monster who had lived for tens of thousands of years, everyone she knew was a Great Emperor! But that was only natural. Since he had asked her to name a few people who might still be alive, she would naturally choose the strongest figures she knew. And if top figures from that era had survived until now, they would never remain unknown.

With such connections, leaving aside what her strength was now, if she really got out, who would dare provoke her? Even if the various Great Emperors encountered her, they would probably have to give her some face.

"They have all become Great Emperors..." A look of reminiscence appeared in Sheng Yuzhu's eyes, as though she were recalling events from long ago. Soon, she came back to her senses and smiled. "They should still be alive, yes? You need only ask them who I am, and they will naturally tell you."

"They are all still alive and well." Yang Kai wiped the sweat from his forehead and said with a bitter expression, "But the various Great Emperors are all elusive figures. This Junior cannot find them either."

Of the four Great Emperors, Duan Hongchen was probably still entangled with Wu Kuang in the Ancestral Domain. Although Iron Blood Great Emperor governed the Star Court, he was just as elusive. Last time, he had appeared in the Western Territory, but unfortunately, he had not met Yang Kai at all. Instead, he had destroyed Yang Kai's cross-territory Space Array and kept Lin Yun'er behind.

Yang Kai guessed that if he ran into Iron Blood Great Emperor again, he definitely would not be greeted kindly. After all, back then, he had almost abducted little Yun'er.

As for Nether Soul Great Emperor, Yao Jun, that went without saying. Last time, in the Wild Ancient Land of the Eastern Territory, Yang Kai had clashed a little with his daughter, Yao Lin, and had nearly killed her. In the end, it was Nether Soul Great Emperor's Soul Descent that came and resolved the situation. That grudge was neither big nor small. It was already magnanimous of Nether Soul Great Emperor not to pursue the matter, so how could Yang Kai possibly deliver himself to the man's door?

As for Heavenly Nexus Great Emperor, Yang Kai had heard that he lived in the Valley of Destiny, but which Territory the Valley of Destiny was in, and which way its gates faced, were not things Yang Kai could know.

In the end, Sheng Yuzhu's level was simply too high. Never mind that she was now so thin she was nothing but skin and bones, and that even speaking left her weak and breathless; a starving camel was still bigger than a horse. With friendships spanning tens of thousands of years, everyone she knew had a resounding name.

"Then what should we do?" Sheng Yuzhu also felt somewhat helpless. Too much time had truly passed. If it had only been several thousand or ten thousand years, that would have been easier. Emperor Realm Masters had long lifespans, so she could have named a few more people for Yang Kai to verify. But after tens of thousands of years, how many could have survived until now? Those who had were almost all Great Emperor-level beings.

Yang Kai pondered for a moment, then said, "There is no need to go to all that trouble. I will bring Senior out now."

Sheng Yuzhu asked in surprise, "You are not afraid I am a bad person anymore?"

Yang Kai smiled freely. "Even if Senior is a bad person, after being trapped here for tens of thousands of years, how much of your cultivation could possibly remain? Even if you wanted to do evil, I fear you would be powerless. What's more, if Senior truly is heinous beyond redemption, Wu Kuang's fate is a lesson right before your eyes."

Since Sheng Yuzhu knew so many Great Emperors, those Great Emperors clearly knew what kind of person she was as well. If such a day ever came, the various Great Emperors certainly would not sit by and ignore it, so there was no need to fear that she could stir up any real trouble.

Sheng Yuzhu said gratefully, "Then many thanks." After a pause, she added, "However, I am not someone accustomed to accepting favors. If you save my life, perhaps I can offer you something in repayment."

Yang Kai said seriously, "It is only a small effort. Senior need not take it to heart." This was not him playing hard to get; he truly thought so. In Yang Kai's view, Sheng Yuzhu probably did not have many good things on her. Even if she did have some precious treasure, was he lacking such things? The Mountain and River Bell in his hand alone was enough to interest even Great Emperors. Sheng Yuzhu could not possibly take out a Secret Treasure more powerful than the Mountain and River Bell.

Sheng Yuzhu said, "Listen to me finish first. It will not be too late to refuse afterward." She looked as though she had already decided Yang Kai would be unable to resist the temptation.

Yang Kai also became interested and assumed an attentive posture.

Sheng Yuzhu said, "I said earlier that you are young, yet already have such cultivation. Given time, you may have a chance to contend with the heroes of the world. But have you ever wondered why, when this world is so vast, there are only ten Great Emperors?"

Yang Kai said, "This Junior once heard someone say that this Heaven and Earth is like a bottle. The bottle's capacity is limited and cannot hold more than ten Great Emperor existences. I do not know whether it is true or not."

Sheng Yuzhu said, somewhat surprised, "You actually know this..."

"I only heard it somewhere." Yang Kai smiled faintly.

Sheng Yuzhu nodded. "Correct. This bottle of Heaven and Earth can indeed only hold ten Great Emperors. If one wants to become a Great Emperor, one can only wait until a position opens, then contend with the heroes of the world!"

Yang Kai sighed. "Who knows how long that will take?"

A Great Emperor's lifespan was incredibly long. Unless some accident occurred and one died, how could a position possibly open? After the War of the Emperors, several new Great Emperors had indeed been born, but that was all thanks to Wu Kuang. In the battle of the Shattered Star Sea, several Great Emperors had died at Wu Kuang's hands. Sublime Pill Great Emperor, Flower Shadow Great Emperor, Night Shadow Great Emperor, and even Radiant Moon Great Emperor had all advanced later.
