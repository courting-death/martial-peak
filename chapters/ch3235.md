## Chapter 3235 — Forget It

Liu Yan had lived on Spirit Beast Island for a time. Since she said so, there should be no mistake.

Yang Kai had not expected this white-haired old man to have such a background. He had dealt with several people from Spirit Beast Island before, and had even met the Beast Great Emperor and stirred up Dragon Isle together with him, so he naturally knew how deep Spirit Beast Island's foundations ran. Although there were not many people on the island, none of them could be underestimated. Whether Li Wuyi or Jiu Feng, which of them was not famous throughout the world?

In the past, Yang Kai had only thought of them as ordinary Third-Stage Emperors, but ever since his conversation with Yang Yan, he had begun to suspect that Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng belonged to that group caught between the Emperor Realm and the Great Emperors. Only because the world's bottle had limited capacity were they unable to attain the position of Great Emperor. Otherwise, they might well be figures capable of standing shoulder to shoulder with the Great Emperors.

Of course, all this was only Yang Kai's conjecture. He could not say for certain whether it was true.

Still, there was no doubt that the white-haired old man opposite him came from Spirit Beast Island.

After asking a few more questions, Yang Kai finally understood the situation. The white-haired old man seemed to have once seen Liu Yan from afar, but at the time, Liu Yan had been comprehending the mysteries of the Fire Phoenix Origin and had not noticed him. That was why Liu Yan said the other party knew her, while she did not know him.

What had let Liu Yan see through his origins was the cloth-bag Secret Treasure the white-haired old man used.

She had seen this kind of Secret Treasure in Jiu Feng's hands before. The people of Spirit Beast Island called it a Spirit-Beast Pouch. It was a very peculiar Secret Treasure, one that had an especially obvious suppressive effect on the yao, but conversely had little effect on other races.

Sealed inside the Spirit-Beast Pouch was a Beast Controlling Seal, and that seal originated from the Beast Great Emperor. Once the Spirit-Beast Pouch appeared, any existence related to the yao would generally be unable to escape its bindings.

This was the fundamental reason the three great Yao Kings had been captured so easily. If they truly fought, the white-haired old man might not necessarily be a match for any one of them, but with the Spirit-Beast Pouch, he could easily take away all three Yao Kings.

"Spirit Beast Island is in the Eastern Territory. What is this old fellow doing in the Northern Territory?" Yang Kai looked puzzled. He had lost a great deal of face today, which left him rather gloomy. If the white-haired old man had not come from Spirit Beast Island, he definitely would not have let the matter rest.

But since the matter had already been explained, Yang Kai figured they probably would not end up fighting after all, though he was still curious about why the white-haired old man had come here.

Liu Yan said, “Spirit Beast Island has gathered strange beasts from all under Heaven, and those strange beasts were naturally captured. There are people on the island specifically responsible for such matters, and he is one of them. He likely came to the Northern Territory to search for strange beasts as well.”

"So that's how it is." Yang Kai nodded lightly.

While the two spoke, Mi Qi's expression shifted back and forth. Although he could not quite grasp how the situation was developing, he had seen Liu Yan and Old Tao communicating secretly just now.

A bad feeling suddenly welled up in his heart. Not daring to hesitate, he cupped his fists and said, "Old Tao, if you can lend this Mi another hand, I will certainly be endlessly grateful, and I will present another generous gift afterward."

At present, he could only count on Old Tao to act. Otherwise, his Mitian Sect absolutely could not match Yang Kai's side.

Old Tao sighed, raised his hand, patted him on the shoulder, and said, "Forget it. Whatever they want, just give it to them."

Mi Qi's eyes went wide. No matter what, he had never expected Old Tao to say such a thing.

What he did not know was that Old Tao was utterly helpless as well. Although he was from Spirit Beast Island and had come to the Northern Territory under orders to search for rare beasts, his status on Spirit Beast Island was not especially high in the end. On Spirit Beast Island, Lord Beast Great Emperor naturally stood supreme, and below him were Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng.

That little girl seemed to have a close relationship with Lady Jiu Feng. How could he dare offend her?

While Mi Qi was still dazed, Old Tao took the cloth bag from his waist again, cast a spell, and opened the Spirit-Beast Pouch. Three figures immediately shot out from inside. They were none other than Yingfei and the others.

The three great Yao Kings all looked shaken. The moment they appeared, they swept their eyes around, then turned and saw Old Tao and Mi Qi. After exchanging a glance, they pounced toward Old Tao to kill him in a triangular formation, almost without the slightest hesitation.

Although all three of them had suffered a great loss to the Spirit-Beast Pouch just now, their eyesight was viciously sharp, and they had immediately seen the pouch's flaw. This Secret Treasure was indeed quite powerful, but it seemed able to target only one person at a time.

If the three of them attacked together, they could kill Old Tao before the Spirit-Beast Pouch had a chance to show its might.

In an instant, Yao Qi surged to the sky, and three distinct murderous intents locked onto Old Tao, startling him so badly that every hair on his body stood on end. Mi Qi was even more frightened, staggering backward and nearly falling to the ground.

"Stop!" Yang Kai shouted.

Xi Lei and Xie Wuwei halted in midair, but Yingfei's figure suddenly dissolved. It was only an afterimage. His true body had already pounced in front of Old Tao, one eagle claw stopping just an inch from Old Tao's face. The force bursting from that claw actually made Old Tao's skin sting.

Having taken an unwarranted trip through the gates of death, Old Tao was drenched in cold sweat. He looked up at Yingfei, who was close enough to touch, and secretly rejoiced that he had dealt with this Yao King first. Otherwise, with Yingfei’s speed, he might truly not have been his match.

"Yao Kings, do not be rude!" Yang Kai's voice came from behind again. Since Old Tao was from Spirit Beast Island, he was not an enemy. What was more, Old Tao had voluntarily released the three Yao Kings, so how could Yang Kai sit by and leave him to die?

Yingfei was quite obedient. He withdrew his eagle claw, held his body perfectly still, then drifted backward, as though some force behind him were pulling him away. In an instant, he stood shoulder to shoulder with Xi Lei and Xie Wuwei. All three kept their eyes fixed on Old Tao, their faces full of vigilance.

They had no choice but to be vigilant. Just now, they had suffered a great loss at this old fellow's hands and lost all face. If it happened again, they would have no face left to remain at Yang Kai's side.

Once Yingfei retreated, Mi Qi finally came back to his senses as well. Although he did not know why Old Tao had suddenly changed his mind, and had even released the three great Yao Kings he had already taken in, Mi Qi also knew the matter could not be forced. Almost without hesitation, he turned and rushed into Mitian Sect.

"Old sir, please wait a moment. This Lord will go and return shortly, then chat with you in detail!" Yang Kai smiled slightly at Old Tao. His figure suddenly flickered, and he vanished in an instant.

"Space Divine Ability!" Old Tao's eyes narrowed.

As someone from Spirit Beast Island, he naturally knew of Space Divine Abilities. Li Wuyi was proficient in this Dao, and Old Tao had seen him use Space Divine Abilities to come and go in a flash many times before. Yet he had never imagined he would see such a miraculous method from someone else.

Judging from this level of mastery, it did not seem much worse than Li Wuyi's. A chill ran through Old Tao's heart. He knew that the reason this man had not attacked just now was probably thanks to that little girl. Otherwise, Old Tao feared he might not even have known how he died.

People who possessed Space Divine Abilities were not easy to provoke. Although Li Wuyi was also a Third-Stage Emperor, he enjoyed the title of the first person beneath the Great Emperors, and what he relied on was none other than Space Divine Abilities.

On the other side, Mi Qi was fleeing at top speed when his vision blurred, and Yang Kai appeared ahead of him, blocking his path with an icy expression. Yang Kai shouted, "Where do you think you're going?"

Mi Qi gritted his teeth and took on the air of someone ready to risk his life. A long spear appeared leisurely in his hand. A point of cold light flashed, then the spear shot out like a dragon, its ringing cry continuing without end as it enveloped a vast area.

He was also a Third-Stage Emperor, so naturally he had some life-preserving trump cards. The moment he attacked, he displayed a powerful foundation.

The spear shadows that filled the sky swept up a murderous aura, their might truly extraordinary. The Emperor Realm cultivators from Li Dragon Palace watched with twitching eyelids, privately judging that no matter what they did, they could not possibly survive such an offensive.

Yet Yang Kai simply stood there bold as brass, showing no intention of dodging at all. They turned to glance at Li Jiao, only to see their Palace Master calm and composed, without the slightest worry.

Of everyone present, Li Jiao understood Yang Kai's abilities best. Yang Kai even dared to clash head-on with the Dragon Clan, so how could he fear a mere Mi Qi? There was simply nothing to worry about.

Facing that frenzied offensive, however, Yang Kai's expression turned strange.

This was his first time fighting someone since reaching the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, and his opponent was a Third-Stage Emperor at that. Although Mi Qi's attack was not weak, in Yang Kai's eyes, it did not seem all that frightening.

He casually reached out, thrust his hand into the spear shadows that filled the sky, and suddenly grabbed through the void.

The spear shadows collapsed and scattered. Mi Qi's movements abruptly froze, his eyes bulging wider than eggs, his face looking as if he had seen a ghost. With that casual grab, Yang Kai had seized the spearhead with incomparable precision from among countless feints.

With the long spear caught, how could the killing move continue?

Mi Qi's expression changed drastically, but his reaction was extremely quick. The long spear suddenly shook, and a rotating force traveled along the shaft. At the same time, he shouted in a low voice, "Let go!"

He was counting on this move to shake Yang Kai off. He was not without confidence in doing so; what he relied on was his own cultivation, one Stage higher than Yang Kai's.

That rotating force reached Yang Kai's palm in the blink of an eye. As expected, it twisted Yang Kai's wrist aside. Mi Qi was just about to press the advantage when a fierce light flashed across Yang Kai's face. The wrist that had been twisted away suddenly turned back again, carrying a momentum that seemed to set chaos back to order.

A rotating force completely opposite to the one from just now rebounded along the spear shaft. Before Mi Qi could understand what had happened, that enormous force sent his entire body spinning several times.

He forcibly urged his Emperor Yuan to steady himself, but Yang Kai still snatched the long spear from his hands out of thin air.

For a moment, he was dizzy and disoriented, his face full of horror.

What tremendous strength! He could clearly feel that Yang Kai only had the aura of the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, so how could he possess such terrifying strength? This was true brute force.

He simply did not know the current state of Yang Kai's body. How could he possibly resist a Half-Dragon Body? If it were Li Jiao, he absolutely would not compete with Yang Kai in strength. That would be pure self-inflicted suffering.

Before he could steady himself, wind whistled down over his head. He looked up and was scared out of his wits. Yang Kai was still gripping the spearhead and was actually using Mi Qi's Secret Treasure long spear like a staff, smashing it down viciously.

Mi Qi crossed his arms above his head, and two armguard-like objects appeared out of thin air on his forearms.

Boom...

The spear tail smashed into the armguards, making their light flash wildly before it quickly dimmed. This Imperial Treasure had actually been beaten by that tyrannical strike until all its spirituality was lost, while Mi Qi, crushed beneath an immense and irresistible force, fell like a meteor and smashed straight into a mountain peak below, leaving a cracked pit in the summit.
