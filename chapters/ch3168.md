## Chapter 3168 — Suppress

On this day, Li Jiao had just finished disposing of another group of cultivators who had reached this place while trying to flee back to the Great Wilderness Star Domain. He returned to the meteorite with effortless ease.

Lu Sanniang came forward to greet him with a smile.

After so many years together, the distance that had once stood between them was gone. Li Jiao also treated Lu Yuqin as if she were his own daughter, which moved Lu Sanniang deeply. She had even begun to wish she could spend the rest of her life here with him, growing old together in this place.

But she knew this was only a vain hope. Li Jiao and Yang Kai would have to return to the Star Realm eventually, and when that time came, she would take her daughter and follow Li Jiao to Li Dragon Palace.

For that very reason, she cherished the happy days before her all the more.

"Tired?" Lu Sanniang asked softly.

Li Jiao said proudly, "Just a bunch of clowns jumping around." Although he had sealed his own cultivation, he still had the foundation of a Third Stage Emperor. Killing a group of ants below the Void King Realm was using a great talent for a trivial task.

He guessed that Yang Kai's main reason for sending him to guard this place was to give him time and opportunities to be with Lu Sanniang.

Reaching out, he slipped an arm around Lu Sanniang's soft waist and gently kissed her forehead.

Lu Sanniang blushed deeply and chided him, "Yuqin is here."

Before the words had even left her mouth, Lu Yuqin suddenly darted out of the stone house with both hands over her eyes. She turned into a streak of light and flew into the distance, her voice drifting back, "I'm going out for a walk. It's so stuffy staying here."

"That girl!" How could Lu Sanniang not know what her daughter was thinking?

Li Jiao said, "Now she isn't." As he spoke, he bent down, swept Lu Sanniang up by the waist, and strode toward the stone house. Lu Sanniang's heart gave a little jump, and as she leaned into that broad chest, her whole body softened.

His long stride suddenly stopped. The smile froze on Li Jiao's face, and he abruptly raised his head to look toward the Void Passage a thousand li away.

In that instant just now, he had actually felt danger. Instinctively, he found it almost absurd. After all, with his cultivation, what in this Lower Plane Star Domain could possibly threaten him? To put it bluntly, in this Star Domain, aside from Yang Kai, no one could possibly be his opponent.

Mm, Liu Yan had to be counted as an exception as well.

The Void Passage seemed unchanged, yet somehow deeper than before. Darkness suddenly expanded across Li Jiao's vision, spreading outward at a terrifying speed. Vast swaths of the Starry Sky were swallowed by that darkness and vanished without a trace, while the Void Passage expanded in that very instant.

"Not good!" Li Jiao's expression changed drastically, and he turned toward the direction Lu Yuqin had gone.

Not far away, Lu Yuqin seemed frozen with fright. She stood rooted in place, staring blankly at the darkness spreading toward her.

There was no time to think. Holding Lu Sanniang with one arm, Li Jiao flickered forward and rushed toward Lu Yuqin. In the blink of an eye, he reached her, seized her hand, and barked, "Go!"

He fled with the speed of thunder and lightning. Though he had no idea what had happened here, this was already beyond his ability to handle. He had to tell Yang Kai as soon as possible.

All of a sudden, every hair on Li Jiao's body stood on end. Somewhere in the unseen depths, he felt as if someone were spying on him, as if a pair of invisible eyes were watching his every move.

A soft sound of surprise came from beside his ear, faint and indistinct, as if it were only an illusion.

But in the next moment, Li Jiao felt an enormous binding force wrap around him, abruptly halting his flight.

"Whatever happens, don't leave my side!" At this life-or-death moment, Li Jiao shouted to Lu Sanniang and her daughter, urged his Emperor Yuan, and wrapped both women within it.

Darkness swallowed them, and the light before their eyes vanished!

...

Day after day, year after year.

Yang Kai shuttled through the Star Domain, forgetting the passage of time as he immersed himself completely in refining.

Inside his Sea of Consciousness, ninety percent of the Star Chart had already lit up, with only the final sliver remaining. Over the years, his footsteps had nearly covered the entire Star Domain, and he had not missed even the farthest, most remote places.

Guided by the Star Chart, he never lost his way and knew clearly where he had been and where he had not. Everything proceeded in perfect order.

Gathering countless Stellar Origins into one body and spreading his aura throughout the entire Star Domain was an immense and magnificent undertaking, yet Yang Kai did not find it particularly tedious.

As he continued refining, more and more insights gradually came to him.

After everything he had experienced on this return trip to the Star Domain, he had already been extremely confident about advancing to the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. Now, he was almost one hundred percent certain that as soon as he returned to the Star Realm, he would be able to advance and break through at once.

It was still not enough! One tiny bit remained unrefined.

In truth, what he wanted to know even more was exactly how Wu Kuang had refined the Star Domain Origin. He certainly had not relied on slow, grinding effort the way Yang Kai was doing. Perhaps Wu Kuang could accomplish the same thing in the shortest time and with the greatest efficiency.

But then, the man had lived for so many years. It was only natural that he possessed some astonishing Divine Abilities and Secret Techniques.

Time passed, and who knew how much longer went by.

When the last section of the Star Chart in his Sea of Consciousness lit up, Yang Kai heard a great rumbling boom, as if muffled thunder had exploded in his mind. The blast left him dizzy, with golden stars dancing wildly before his eyes.

Once he recovered, however, joy filled his face.

It was finally done!

How long had it taken? Eight years? Nine years? Or ten? He could hardly remember anymore.

But it was not over yet. All he had done over the years was divide the entire Star Domain into puzzle pieces of various sizes. Though he had refined them to a small degree, puzzle pieces were still puzzle pieces. Even if they were fitted together to form a complete picture, many flaws and gaps still remained. If those flaws were not removed, he would never become the Master of the Star Domain.

Only the final step remained, yet it was also the hardest step to take.

With a slight movement of his thoughts, Yang Kai flickered and shifted among the puzzle pieces of the Star Domain. Before long, he arrived at the very center of the entire Star Domain.

He took a deep breath and slowly adjusted his condition.

At a certain moment, he suddenly opened his eyes and spread his arms, as if to embrace the entire Star Domain.

His Space Divine Ability and Divine Sense power surged at the same time. His Divine Sense shuttled through the Void, leaping forward as it radiated throughout the entire Star Domain.

In an instant, Yang Kai felt as if his entire self had vanished, as if he had become nothingness and filled the whole Star Domain. The Divine Sense in his Sea of Consciousness poured away like floodwaters released from a sluice.

The Seven-Colored Warm Spirit Lotus seemed to sense this and suddenly bloomed with seven-colored light. A steady stream of power flowed out, replenishing Yang Kai's consumption.

Above the Sea of Consciousness, countless stars twinkled, as if each star were blinking, casting uncertain shifts of light and shadow across the Sea of Consciousness.

In that instant, a wondrous change came over the vast Star Domain. Every living being in the entire Star Domain, no matter where they were or what their cultivation was, felt a boundless will descend from the sky.

This will was far grander than the will of a Star Lord. It was the will of Heaven and Earth, the will of this Star Domain.

When Yang Kai divided this Star Domain into countless puzzle pieces and refined them, he had not felt any obstruction. But when he tried to refine and merge the entire Star Domain, Heaven and Earth naturally refused to allow it.

Even the Star Chart, which had always stayed peacefully in Yang Kai's Sea of Consciousness, now showed signs of wanting to leap out and abandon him.

Sensing this, Yang Kai was startled.

To refine a Star Domain, the first requirement was to possess the Star Domain Origin.

If the Star Chart in his Sea of Consciousness ran away, then what the hell was he supposed to refine?

Light flashed wildly within the Star Chart, transforming into a surging mighty force that churned Yang Kai's Sea of Consciousness upside down. It seemed to be warning him to retreat before the difficulty and let it leave.

Heart-rending pain tore through his mind. Yang Kai gritted his teeth without making a sound and sealed off his entire Sea of Consciousness, refusing to let it leave no matter what.

His Divine Sense was damaged, and for a moment Yang Kai felt as if the sky and earth had gone dark.

Fortunately, the Seven-Colored Warm Spirit Lotus kept releasing pure power, nourishing his Divine Soul. His damaged Divine Sense immediately recovered, leaving the situation throughout his Sea of Consciousness locked in a stalemate.

But with things as they were, Yang Kai had no strength to spare for anything else.

A fierce gleam flashed in his eyes. Inside his Sea of Consciousness, he manifested his Spirit Body, reached out, and with a beckoning motion grasped a great saber in his hand. Pointing it at the Star Chart overhead, he shouted, "Cause trouble again, and I'll cut you down with one slash!"

The saber was the Soul-Severing Blade, which Yang Kai had also nourished for many years with the Secret Technique Heaven Breaking Strike. If this slash fell, even the Star Domain Origin would likely suffer for it.

Killing intent raged. Yang Kai clearly was not joking.

Yet the Star Chart remained unmoved. Its light continued to flicker, and with each flash it sent out an invisible force that interfered with Yang Kai's Sea of Consciousness.

It seemed to possess some spirituality and had seen through Yang Kai, realizing he was only fierce on the outside.

"Refusing the toast only to drink the forfeit!" Yang Kai was furious. He could even cut down a Third Stage Emperor, yet a mere Star Chart dared act so wantonly. Unfortunately, he truly could not attack. Without this Star Chart, how could he refine the Star Domain?

He tossed aside the Soul-Severing Blade and formed seals with both hands. Within his Sea of Consciousness, the Seven-Colored Warm Spirit Lotus transformed into the Seven-Colored Treasure Isle and crashed upward.

"Suppress!"

The Seven-Colored Treasure Isle flew high into the air, rose above the Star Chart, and suddenly released an immense suppressive force that slowly pressed down.

The Star Chart's light flickered even more violently.

Yet as the Seven-Colored Treasure Isle descended, the Star Chart was forced down bit by bit.

Seeing this, Yang Kai was delighted and knew his method was correct.

The Star Chart was a Star Domain Origin. Although rare and precious, it was not unique in the world. Every Star Domain had its own.

But the Warm Spirit Lotus, like the Everlasting Tree, was the only one of its kind under the heavens. There was absolutely no second one. Judging from this point alone, both the Warm Spirit Lotus and the Everlasting Tree were far more precious and of a higher grade than a Star Domain Origin.

The facts proved that the Warm Spirit Lotus did indeed have the ability to suppress the Star Chart.

As the suppressive force enveloped it, the Star Chart was pressed down bit by bit. Its flickering slowed considerably, and its interference became less frequent. Yang Kai finally caught his breath and focused completely on urging the Warm Spirit Lotus, drawing out its unimaginable effects.

With a rumbling boom...

The Seven-Colored Treasure Isle fell, suppressing the entire Star Chart beneath it as it sank into the Sea of Consciousness.

The seawater in the Sea of Consciousness was entirely formed from Yang Kai's Divine Sense. Wrapped in that seawater, the Star Chart settled down even further. At the same time, Yang Kai felt the connection between himself and the Star Chart grow much closer.
