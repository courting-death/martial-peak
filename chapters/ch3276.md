## Chapter 3276 - Luocha Sect

If anyone benefited most from the Wuhua Hall incident, it was undoubtedly Yang Kai.

Nearly every Sect in the Southern Territory, great or small, now owed Yang Kai a favor, and that single matter pushed his prestige and reputation to an almost unbelievable height. The Sect Masters and Family Heads of those forces all made a special trip to thank him, and of course they could hardly avoid saying that if he had any instructions in the future, they would not dare disobey. Sincere or not, with such declarations in hand, Yang Kai would certainly face no obstruction when acting in the Southern Territory from now on.

After more than half a month of constant bustle, Wuhua Hall finally quieted down. Everyone who ought to leave had left, and the vast Sect now stood empty and desolate. It was hard to believe this place had, not long ago, been one of the Southern Territory's three top forces, filled with noise and people.

By now, Yang Kai had already set out with Qin Pei and the other women, heading for Luocha Sect.

Since he had agreed to become their Guest Elder, Yang Kai naturally would not put the matter off. Since he was already in the Southern Territory, he might as well settle it first. He was already a Guest Elder of Qingyang Shrine, and now he was going to serve as Guest Elder for another Sect as well. Strictly speaking, that was somewhat inappropriate, and no Emperor Realm cultivator had ever done such a thing before.

Wen Zishan, however, did not mind. Perhaps he had heard something, for instead he told Yang Kai to speak up if he needed any help. Yang Kai was naturally deeply grateful.

Compared to a top Sect like Qingyang Shrine, Luocha Sect could only be considered a second- or third-rate force in the Southern Territory as a whole. It was stronger than Biyu Sect and the Thousand Leaves Sect of years past, since it had once had an Emperor Realm cultivator presiding over it, but that was all. Now even Luocha Sect's Sect Master had fallen in Wuhua Hall, and sixty to seventy percent of the Sect's Dao Origin Realm elites had been lost as well, causing its strength to decline drastically. It could be said that if Yang Kai had not stepped in, a Sect like this would not last half a year before being swallowed up by nearby forces.

The women's cultivations varied, and so did their flying speeds. Yang Kai did not want to waste too much time, so he simply summoned his Flowing Cloud Shuttle and carried them toward Luocha Sect.

The Flowing Cloud Shuttle was a flying Secret Treasure Hou Yu had custom-made for Yang Kai, so its speed naturally needed no explanation.

Seven or eight days passed in a flash, and a Spirit Mountain appeared in the distance.

Qin Pei pointed ahead. "We're here."

Yang Kai looked over and found that the place occupied by Luocha Sect was actually quite good. Of course, compared with Wuhua Hall or his own Lingxiao Palace, it fell short by far more than a little, but for Luocha Sect's own level of strength, it was already more than enough.

He put away the Flowing Cloud Shuttle and released the dozen or so women. Seeing that familiar scenery, smelling the scent of home, and then thinking of the fellow disciples who had died and their own recent escape from death, all of them felt as if they had been given a second life. Their hearts ached, and they nearly burst into tears again.

Yang Kai felt a headache coming on. What he feared most was these women breaking into tears at every turn, so he quickly changed the subject. "Looks like the news of your Sect Master's death has already spread."

Qin Pei was startled, but after looking around, she immediately understood what Yang Kai meant.

At this moment, Luocha Sect's Grand Guardian Array was actually active, and in the woods outside the array, many ghostly figures were hidden. Qin Pei clearly sensed the auras of several Dao Origin Realm cultivators among them.

Her heart tightened, and she knew Yang Kai was right. The news of the Sect Master's fall had indeed spread, and the surrounding forces that had once been friendly with Luocha Sect were already impatient to make a move on it. Otherwise, why would there be so many scouts in these mountains?

It was easy to imagine what would have happened if she had not returned with Yang Kai this time and had instead led the remaining disciples back herself. Most likely, they would have been ambushed before they even entered the mountain gate.

She secretly hated how shameless these people were in their greed. After all, among these friendly forces were many who had taken Luocha Sect disciples as brides, and while the Sect Master was alive, they had often kept in contact and helped one another. Yet the moment the Sect Master died, those hateful faces had shown themselves so quickly. The fickleness of human relations saddened her as well. Only the person drinking the water knew whether it was warm or cold, and the feeling sat bitterly in her heart.

As her thoughts wandered, she suddenly sensed an extremely terrifying aura surge out from Yang Kai. Formless and invisible, it radiated in every direction and covered a vast area in an instant. That aura was powerful and heavy, yet it carried a chilling killing intent that made the blood run cold. The dozen or so female disciples all shuddered together and looked at Yang Kai in alarm.

After spending these days with him, everyone had felt that Yang Kai, this Emperor Realm cultivator, was different from the rumors. He was approachable and very easy to talk to. Earlier, a few female disciples had even asked him questions about cultivation, and he had patiently answered them all, giving the Luocha Sect disciples considerable benefit.

If Qin Pei had not feared disturbing Yang Kai's rest and issued strict orders forbidding the disciples from bothering him, Yang Kai probably would not have had a moment of peace on the way back.

Yet in this instant, that amiable man seemed to have transformed into an Asura from the Blood Prison. Just a trace of his aura leaking out made it difficult for them to breathe, leaving their bodies cold and their hands and feet icy.

Only then did they understand that no matter how approachable an Emperor Realm cultivator seemed, he was still an Emperor Realm cultivator, a height far beyond them.

Flap, flap...

The birds in the jungle were startled and beat their wings to fly, yet no matter what they did, they could not rise, as if some invisible force had stripped away their ability to fly. One after another, they dropped toward the ground.

At the same time, the figures hidden in the jungle acted as if they were facing a terrible enemy and retreated one after another. No longer caring about concealment, each used their movement technique and fled swiftly.

The news that Luocha Sect's Sect Master had fallen in Wuhua Hall had already spread through this region. After all, so much time had passed, and with interested parties pushing the matter along, it could not be hidden even if one wanted to hide it. They had also heard that an Emperor Realm cultivator had agreed to come to Luocha Sect as a Guest Elder, and that this Emperor Realm cultivator seemed to be a Second Stage Emperor Realm, none other than Yang Kai, whose name had spread far and wide because of the Wuhua Hall incident.

However, no one knew whether the news was true or false. It had come from inside Luocha Sect, so it could not be trusted completely. Perhaps it was only a smokescreen Luocha Sect had released to protect itself, so they naturally wanted to investigate. If the news was true, it would not be too late to adjust their plans. If it was false, then there was nothing to worry about, and everyone could take a bite out of Luocha Sect's fat meat. Luocha Sect had not stood for an especially long time, but it still had several hundred to a thousand years of history behind it, so there were sure to be some reserves inside the Sect. Leaving that aside, the thousand or so female disciples alone were a tremendous fortune.

But the moment Yang Kai's Emperor Realm aura appeared, their delusions shattered. Only then did they understand that the news was true. Yang Kai, who had made such a brilliant showing in Wuhua Hall, had really come to Luocha Sect. Regardless of whether he intended to serve as some Guest Elder, with him here, no one would dare set their sights on Luocha Sect.

They could not help feeling a little jealous. Luocha Sect was an entire Sect of women, and Yang Kai, a single man, had entered their midst. The romantic fortune involved was enough to send the imagination running wild.

Watching the figures below flee like stray dogs, Qin Pei's expression turned extremely complicated. She even saw several familiar figures and knew their names and which forces they came from.

When the Sect Master was still alive, she had dealt with those people, and they could be considered to have at least some friendship. Yet these very people were the ones so impatient to move against Luocha Sect.

Qin Pei clenched her fists, understanding at last that only the strong had the right to stand in this world.

"Let's go." Yang Kai's voice pulled her back. When she turned to look, the terrifying aura around him had vanished at some point, and he had returned to his earlier approachable appearance.

A few of the lower-cultivation female disciples behind him lightly patted their chests, exchanged glances, and stuck out their tongues. When they looked at Yang Kai's back again, there was a little more fear in their eyes.

"Sir, please wait a moment. I will have Great Elder open the array at once," Qin Pei said. She took out a Transmission Compass and contacted someone.

A moment later, Luocha Sect's Grand Guardian Array slowly closed. Qin Pei led the way in front, and the group descended toward the platform at the summit.

On the platform stood a woman who looked around thirty, graceful and full of mature charm. Her figure was alluring, yet her bearing was quite dignified, like a young lady from a noble house. Her pale cyan dress was exceedingly simple, but it set off her curves all the more, and her aura had also reached the level of a Third Stage Dao Origin Realm cultivator.

This was Luocha Sect's Great Elder, Tao Yingruo, and she was also the only Third Stage Dao Origin Realm cultivator in all of Luocha Sect. For this Southern Territory Martial Gathering, Luocha Sect's Sect Master had personally led the team and left her behind to guard the Sect, so she could be considered to have escaped calamity. Had she truly gone with them, no one knew whether she would have returned alive.

Behind Tao Yingruo, several hundred women in identical pink dresses stood in rows on either side. Each one was quite beautiful, yet they were utterly silent. At this moment, they all stared up into the sky with wide, lovely eyes full of expectation and curiosity.

Yang Kai descended with Qin Pei, swept his gaze around, and smiled faintly. Luocha Sect really did value him, he thought. The Sect had only a little over a thousand disciples, yet it had mobilized seventy percent of them just to welcome him. It seemed that aside from those in retreat and those with duties to attend to, everyone else had come.

Back when Yang Kai was still staying at Wuhua Hall to handle various trivial matters, a Dao Origin Realm cultivator from Luocha Sect had hurriedly returned and reported that Yang Kai would soon come to serve as Guest Elder, so Luocha Sect had long since made preparations.

Qin Pei stepped forward and said a few things to Tao Yingruo. Tao Yingruo immediately came forward and bowed gracefully. "Tao Yingruo of Luocha Sect greets Sir. Sir has had a tiring journey."

Yang Kai lifted a hand lightly and smiled. "Great Elder, there is no need to be so polite. From now on, we are all family."

Hearing this, Tao Yingruo finally relaxed. Although she had long since heard that Yang Kai was coming to serve as Guest Elder, she had still found it somewhat unbelievable. A Second Stage Emperor Realm cultivator, and one of the most influential figures in the entire Southern Territory besides, had no need to come to Luocha Sect and suffer this hardship, even for Yu Zhuo's sake. Before seeing Yang Kai, she had still been anxious and uncertain, unsure whether he had some other scheme. After all, many people were now setting their sights on Luocha Sect. If Yang Kai also harbored ill intentions, the whole of Luocha Sect would have no strength to resist.

Now that she saw he was gentle, courteous, and approachable, she had a very good impression of him and felt much more at ease.
