## Chapter 3287 - Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens

In the Southern Territory, outside Wuhua Hall, Yang Kai and Hua Qingsi arrived through the Space Array. The moment they stepped out, Yang Kai turned back and destroyed the thing he had gone to such effort to arrange.

In any case, he would have to arrange another one inside Wuhua Hall, so there was no need to keep this one.

Before long, the two of them entered Wuhua Hall together. Yang Kai swept out his Divine Sense and immediately detected quite a few people moving about inside, at least a thousand of them. Hua Qingsi had sent them over in advance to help Nanmen Dajun survey Wuhua Hall's terrain and assist him in setting up the arrays.

Nanmen Dajun himself was floating in midair, constantly looking this way and that, gesturing with his hands, and muttering something under his breath in a strange, rambling fashion, utterly absorbed.

Sensing someone approach, he turned his head. His expression immediately turned solemn. "Palace Master, Chief Steward."

Yang Kai nodded, stopped beside him, looked down over the scene below, and asked, "How are things?"

Nanmen Dajun asked, "Which aspect does Palace Master mean?"

Yang Kai said, "Roughly how long will it take to set up the array?"

Nanmen Dajun pondered for a moment before replying, "Wuhua Hall's own arrays are already quite extraordinary, so there is no need to change everything from the ground up. Give me another month, and I should be able to raise the Grand Array. After that, with a few careful adjustments, it should be perfected within half a year."

"Good. This is your specialty, so I will have to trouble you to put more effort into it. If you need anything, just tell the Chief Steward."

Nanmen Dajun grinned. "Without the Chief Steward's support, this subordinate would never dare make such a guarantee." He was now Lingxiao Palace's Chief Formation Master, and whenever he set up arrays, Hua Qingsi gave him whatever he asked for, whether people or materials. Otherwise, how could the Grand Array of a top Sect be arranged so easily?

Back when he roamed the Northern Territory, his reputation had been great, but he had been alone after all, and there were many things he had the will for but not the means. Now, however, with the whole of Lingxiao Palace at his back, there were only arrays in this world he could not think of; there were none he could not arrange.

All of a sudden, he felt that this way of life was not bad at all. He could give free rein to all his ambitions; nothing made him happier than that. After spending all these years in Lingxiao Palace, his heart had settled down somewhat.

Now, even if Yang Kai wanted to drive him away, he would probably have to think it over carefully. After all, without Lingxiao Palace's support, even a Formation Master like him would be like a clever cook without rice. Joining another Sect was certainly an option, but what Sect could support him as strongly as Lingxiao Palace did?

It was not only him; Hou Yu was the same.

At first, when Yang Kai had captured Hou Yu and brought her back, she had tried to escape again and again.

But as time passed, had she not gradually settled down too? In Lingxiao Palace, everyone treated them politely, no matter who they were. They were given ample respect and authority, and they were fully supported in achieving something in their respective Domains. Where else could they find such a good patron?

Both he and Hou Yu had selected a group of disciples in Lingxiao Palace and were preparing to train them properly, to see who might be qualified to inherit their mantle. With that bond in place, their ties to Lingxiao Palace only grew closer.

In the current Lingxiao Palace, the three special Spirit Peaks, Pill Peak, Artifact Peak, and Array Peak, were producing talents in droves. Add to that Thousand Leaves Peak and its group of fellows studying the Dao of Puppets, and Lingxiao Palace's disciples would have far too many choices in the future. Did they dislike cultivating? No matter. Alchemy, artifact refining, arrays, puppets; there would always be something to their liking.

After offering Nanmen Dajun a few more words of encouragement, Yang Kai left on his own.

Wuhua Hall had many forbidden areas, but the most heavily guarded was still the place where the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens was located. It was protected by layer upon layer of Grand Arrays; anyone who trespassed without permission would either die or be gravely injured.

However, as soon as Nanmen Dajun arrived, he had broken most of the Grand Arrays here, so the forbidden area was now completely unobstructed. As Yang Kai made his way in, he could not help clicking his tongue in wonder.

Although he had long heard the name of the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens, this was his first time seeing it.

The forbidden area was a long canyon. Looking out, he saw a gray, hazy world whose end seemed impossible to glimpse. Wind howled all around the canyon, and merely hearing it was enough to know how terrifying it was. The surrounding cliffs were covered in marks, as though hacked by blades and swords, but no blade or sword had made them. They were the work of the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens.

Yang Kai narrowed his eyes and studied it for a while, then probed in with his Divine Sense. To his surprise, he felt as if blades and swords were pressing against him, as though the omnipresent Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens could slice even his Divine Sense to powder.

Fortunately, his Divine Sense was powerful; otherwise, he might really have been injured.

Without hesitation, he stepped into the canyon.

With that single step, it was as if he had entered another world. The air around him rushed violently, whipping up gust after gust. The formless, intangible breeze seemed to turn into sharp weapons, cutting at Yang Kai again and again.

His clothes offered not the slightest protection. Even Yang Kai's protective Emperor Yuan was ignored; the omnipresent gale passed straight through it and struck directly at Yang Kai's skin and flesh.

The discovery made him click his tongue in wonder. No wonder this damned place could temper the flesh body. Even for someone as strong as he was, protective Emperor Yuan had no effect here, much less for others. Anyone who wanted to endure it could only rely on the strength of their flesh body.

He had no idea where the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens in this canyon came from, or how it could flow on endlessly without ever running dry.

Yang Kai's flesh body was powerful, so naturally he did not fear such faint cuts. Bracing himself against the gale, he walked deeper in, step by step.

The farther he went, the stronger the gale became. One hollow after another appeared in the cliffs around the canyon, some large and some small. The large ones could hold seven or eight people, while the small ones could hold only one.

Yang Kai guessed that these hollows had been left behind by generations of Wuhua Hall disciples. Cultivating here would always carry danger; once they realized they could no longer hold on, they could duck into a nearby hollow to hide and heal.

The farther inward he went, the stronger the gale became, yet Yang Kai remained completely unbothered. Instead, his eyes shone. A good place, truly a good place. With this foundation, it would be simple for Lingxiao Palace's disciples to temper their flesh bodies in the future. They would only need to cultivate here for a while, and the strength of their bodies would rise. With a strong flesh body as a vessel, everything became easier, and it could even affect how smoothly they cultivated in the future.

The canyon was extremely long. Yang Kai walked for a full half an hour without seeing the end. By then, the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens had already begun to sting his skin, and it would occasionally cut tiny wounds across his body. Those wounds, however, vanished almost as soon as they appeared, erased in an instant by his powerful recovery.

From up ahead came waves of low, suppressed roars. Yang Kai listened for a moment, smiled faintly, then tapped his feet several times and picked up speed.

Before long, an enormous figure appeared ahead, seven or eight zhang long and five or six zhang tall. Lightning flashed all over its body, and it radiated an extremely fierce, terrifying aura. At first glance, the colossal creature looked like a rhinoceros, with a sharp single horn growing from its head. It kept tossing its head and tail. As the surrounding gale swirled around it, blood continually sprayed from its huge body, yet it stood firm without retreating, roaring wildly again and again, its voice shaking the four directions.

Huff, huff. As if sensing someone approach, the huge creature turned its head, its two wok-sized eyes completely blood-red.

Their eyes met. Yang Kai nodded lightly and, in a flash, slipped into a hollow beside him.

Strangely enough, the moment he entered, the omnipresent gale vanished.

A moment later, a burly figure flashed in. It was Xi Lei, looking embarrassed as he said, "Sir, why have you come?" Having Yang Kai accidentally see his original form was indeed embarrassing. Although he came from the yao race, he had rarely revealed his true body since taking human form, except when he was locked in a stalemate against an enemy and had no choice but to fight with his life on the line.

Just now, although he had not been fighting anyone, he had been doing everything he could to withstand the ravages of the Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens, and he had looked rather wretched. Naturally, Xi Lei did not want anyone to see him like that.

"I came to see what is so special about this Starry Gale Beyond the Heavens." Yang Kai smiled faintly and gestured with his hand. Xi Lei immediately sat cross-legged in front of him, shook his head, and said, "This place is not bad. Sir's decision to take this foundation was indeed extremely wise."

Yang Kai raised a brow. "Yao King thinks so too?"

Xi Lei said, "Of course. Give me some time, and my flesh body can rise another level."

Yang Kai nodded. "If it is useful even to a Yao King, then it seems this place really is quite good."

Xi Lei chuckled. "But this place is still quite dangerous. Anyone who lacks the strength and rashly goes too deep is only courting death." As he spoke, he suddenly started, abruptly remembering that Yang Kai had been standing not far from him just now, looking calm and unruffled. He had been nothing like Xi Lei's own sorry self.

Only then did he understand that Yang Kai's physique was stronger than his by who knew how much. Xi Lei could not help feeling somewhat discouraged. He was an Ancient Land Yao King, after all. In terms of strength, he ranked among the top three of the Thirty-Two Yao Kings, yet when it came to the flesh body, he was actually inferior to a human?

The reason he, Yingfei, and Xie Wuwei all followed Yang Kai's lead was naturally because of that descendant of Heaven's Punisher. They were counting on Yang Kai's relationship with that descendant of Heaven's Punisher, hoping that when the Blood Gate opened again, they would be the first to benefit.

But as they spent more time with him, the three great Yao Kings discovered that Yang Kai was no ordinary First Stage Emperor Realm Master, nor even an ordinary Second Stage one. If they truly fought him one on one, they might not be able to beat him.

Not to mention, he had even subdued a dragon woman. Whenever the three great Yao Kings thought of that, they could only admire him from the bottom of their hearts.

Yang Kai nodded and said, "Since this place is useful to Yao King, then Yao King may cultivate here at ease. While you are at it, keep watch over this place as well."

Xi Lei smiled. "That is exactly what I had in mind."

Yang Kai changed the subject. "Speaking of the Ancient Land's Thirty-Two Yao Kings, I am only familiar with you three. What are the other Yao Kings like?"

Xi Lei stroked his chin. "Those fellows... none of them are easy to deal with, anyway. Take Xiong Kaishan, for example. He looks dull and simple, but his belly is full of rotten schemes, and he is sinister to the bone." He looked as if he had suffered badly at Xiong Kaishan's hands, his resentment running deep. Then he suddenly gave a lewd smile. "But those female Yao Kings do have their charms, especially Hu Fei. It is said she has the bloodline of the Nine-Tailed Heavenly Fox. Tsk, tsk..." He shivered, then straightened his expression. "Sir, if you ever run into that yao woman, you must be careful. Whatever you do, do not fall for her tricks. Her charm techniques are unmatched under the heavens."
