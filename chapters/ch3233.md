## Chapter 3233 - In a Sorry State

An unimaginable pressure rushed toward them. The monster, huge as a small mountain, seemed less like it stood before them than like it was pressing down on their chests, making it hard to breathe.

Even more terrifying, at some point sharp stone pillars had sprouted all over the stone man's body like barbs, while evil flames billowed from head to toe.

"Shi Huo!" Mi Qi's eyes narrowed sharply. He had some discernment after all, and he recognized traces of that being in the aura radiating from the Incarnation.

But why was Shi Huo here? Years ago, he had heard that a Divine Spirit named Shi Huo lived in the Wild Ancient Land of the Eastern Territory and had even destroyed a Black Tortoise Sect. He did not know whether this was that same Shi Huo, but what puzzled him even more was this: when had Shi Huo become capable of growing so enormous?

Information about Divine Spirits was public. Anyone who cared to look could gather at least some of it, so Mi Qi could not be called ignorant of Shi Huo; he simply had never seen him with his own eyes. As far as he knew, even if Shi Huo had some Secret Techniques to enlarge or shrink his body, he absolutely could not become this huge. What the hell was this?

What he did not know was that the true body of this Shi Huo before him was a Stone Puppet, and that it had once devoured a Floating Continent. Although the Heaven Devouring Battle Art had refined away all its impurities, the Incarnation could grow even larger if he wished. What did a mere forty or fifty zhang amount to?

Even Yang Kai's twenty-zhang dragon body could only carry his shoes in comparison.

When the roar faded, the Incarnation stared down with wide eyes, fierce and savage, like a wrathful deity glaring from above.

A fist larger than a palace clenched tight. Its arm drew back, then smashed down with savage force. Before the blow even landed, a piercing sound tore through the air.

Mi Qi's face changed drastically. All his earlier calm and poise vanished.

Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array was extraordinary, no doubt. With him presiding over it personally, it could withstand the attacks of the three great Yao Kings without taking the slightest damage, but against the onslaught of such a colossal monster, he had no confidence at all.

Most arrays under the Heavens could be broken by brute force. If an array held firm, breaking it simply required more power.

Mi Qi could not imagine how powerful this absurdly enormous Shi Huo was, nor did he dare try. In his haste, he waved the array flag in his hand again and again, sending streams of mysterious light shooting out to strike the array's light curtain. The curtain flared, growing much more dazzling.

Hong...

The Incarnation's fist slammed down, and the whole of Mitian Sect shook. Countless houses collapsed in that instant. Amid the rising dust, figures shot into the sky one after another and looked around in confusion. When they saw the enormous figure in the distance, they all froze where they were.

Mitian Sect was protected by its great array, and as long as the array remained intact, no outsider could force their way in. Yet the Incarnation's punch was pure strength. Even the Grand Guardian Array could not neutralize it completely; instead, as the array dispersed the force, it spread throughout Mitian Sect.

Near and far, Mitian Sect lay in shambles.

The place where Mi Qi and the others stood was even more terrifying. When that gigantic fist fell, everyone there instinctively felt as though the sky itself had collapsed.

The force transmitted through the air turned into a shockwave, sending sand and stones flying across a vast area and blotting out the light of the sun and moon.

Pu pu pu... A string of muffled bursts rang out. Blood and flesh splattered as some Mitian Sect disciples with low cultivations were shaken to powder by the invisible impact, exploding into blood mist on the spot. Only cultivators at or above the Dao Origin Realm survived, but their faces were pale and their eyes overflowed with horror.

Mi Qi was badly shaken. At this moment, he could spare no attention for whether those weaker disciples lived or died. His eyes were fixed on a single spot on the array's light curtain, the place where the Incarnation's fist had struck. That light curtain, which had seemed as solid as a fortress, had split open with countless fine cracks that spread madly in every direction. In the blink of an eye, they stretched for several hundred zhang.

Mi Qi was frightened. He had known the monster's punch could not be underestimated, but he had never imagined it would be this violent.

Was this really the kind of power Shi Huo could unleash? Shi Huo was not supposed to excel in strength, was he?

Seeing the monster raise its fist again, Mi Qi did not dare hesitate. He waved the array flag in his hand frantically, Emperor Yuan surging as streams of light shot away.

The trembling light curtain gradually steadied, and under his efforts, the cracks that had appeared slowly vanished. Before he could even catch his breath, the second punch came crashing down.

This punch seemed to strike not Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array, but Mi Qi's own heart. A chill ran through him, and for no reason at all, he felt the aura of death rushing toward him.

The same scene played out again. All of Mitian Sect shook violently once more, and even more houses collapsed. Fortunately, when the Emperor Realm Masters behind Mi Qi saw that things had gone wrong, they all cast spells together to resist, blocking the shockwave that came through the air and preventing the weaker disciples from suffering further casualties.

Kacha cha...

Brittle cracking sounds rang out. The array light curtain that had just been repaired split open again, this time with more cracks, packed more densely together and spreading even farther. Mi Qi was scared out of his wits and once more waved the array flag to stabilize the great array.

The Incarnation raising its fist yet again filled him with despair. The Protective Mountain Array he had always been so proud of seemed unable to shelter Mitian Sect from this storm. It could be said that ninety percent of the arrays in the world could not withstand such a savage bombardment.

He roared, "Elders, assist me!"

Only when his voice rang out did the Emperor Realm Masters behind him wake as if from a dream. One by one, they hurriedly took out array flags, all of them used to control the Grand Guardian Array. The flag in Mi Qi's hand, however, was the main array flag, while theirs were merely branch flags.

Everyone's expression turned solemn as never before. As their array flags waved, dim light rippled across the great array's curtain, and the cracks slowly repaired themselves.

The Incarnation shook its head. Failing to break the array with two punches seemed to have angered it as well. Its palms suddenly came together, rose high overhead, then smashed down as clasped fists.

They could not block it. They absolutely could not block it!

Although the attack had yet to land, that same thought surfaced in the hearts of Mi Qi and all those Emperor Realm Elders and Protectors. It was an instinctive feeling.

And in truth, they were right. When the Incarnation's third strike fell, the wavering light curtain suddenly stiffened. Then, with the point where its two fists landed as the center, dense cracks spread in every direction.

Under the terrified stares of Mitian Sect's people, the light curtain shattered with a crash.

The Grand Guardian Array had been broken!

A hundred zhang away, the three great Yao Kings shot forward like lightning. In the blink of an eye, they charged into the midst of Mitian Sect's Emperor Realm Masters, giving them no time to react. Yingfei was the fastest and took the lead, with Xi Lei and Xie Wuwei close behind.

Claw shadows filled the sky. The Mitian Sect Emperor Realm Masters, still unable to accept that the great array had been broken, were beaten by Yingfei until they could only defend themselves, with no strength to strike back.

The gap in cultivation was plain to see. Mitian Sect had only one Third-Stage Emperor, Mi Qi, and in single combat even he might not be Yingfei's opponent. What chance did the others have?

Cyan lightning flashed around Xi Lei as he charged straight at Mi Qi without a care, grinning savagely. "You refused a toast only to drink a forfeit. Hand over your life!" His broad palm, like a palm-leaf fan, clawed down at Mi Qi's head.

Mi Qi threw away the array flag in his hand and swiftly summoned a steel-ring-like Secret Treasure, joining battle with Xi Lei. The grief, rage, and terror in his heart were beyond words.

Xie Wuwei flashed past close behind Xi Lei. The phantom of a huge scorpion appeared behind him, shaking its head and swaying its tail, while the stinger at the end of that tail glowed an oily green, clearly carrying a deadly poison. His whole body shuddered, and a cloud of jade-green mist exploded outward, engulfing four or five Emperor Realm Masters at once. Their figures vanished in the blink of an eye. Only waves of alarmed cries came from within, and no one knew how the fight inside was going.

The moment the three great Yao Kings appeared, they took on all of Mitian Sect's higher-ups.

Li Jiao raised his arm and shouted, "Kill!"

Although he had come this time to cheer Yang Kai on, matters had already reached this point, so naturally he could not stand by and watch. Since things had gone this far, he might as well see them through. Helping Yang Kai take down Mitian Sect was the best choice.

The Elders from Li Dragon Palace were still in a daze, mainly because the scene before them was too difficult to accept.

Mitian Sect and Li Dragon Palace were both top Sects of the Northern Territory, and both had stood there for tens of thousands of years. Yet now, Yang Kai had broken through Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array with such ease? Before this, they had always thought Yang Kai had destroyed Wenqing Sect only through luck. After all, the people of Wenqing Sect had run to Ice Heart Valley to seek their own deaths.

But looking at it now, that had only been wishful thinking on their part.

Mitian Sect's great array had been broken just like that. If Li Dragon Palace became enemies with Lingxiao Palace, their own end probably would not be much better.

Everyone had originally felt that Li Jiao following Yang Kai's lead in everything damaged their own prestige, but now they saw that their Palace Master was not timid or incompetent; he had simply possessed foresight from the start.

Hearing Li Jiao's call, they all came back to their senses and hurriedly charged after him.

They might not be very willing to contend with Mitian Sect head-on, but they were certainly willing to beat a drowning dog.

The people of Mitian Sect instantly suffered bitterly. The three great Yao Kings had already pinned all of them down, and now Li Jiao had led a group of Emperor Realm Masters whose numbers were in no way inferior to theirs into the battle. How could they possibly fight?

In an instant, they were suppressed on all fronts. One Emperor Realm Master after another was beaten until they spat blood and fell to the ground, unable to rise.

Mi Qi roared in grief and rage, "Li Jiao, I will never share the same sky with you!"

Yang Kai coming to deal with him was one thing. Yang Kai had wolfish ambition and deserved to die for it. But Li Jiao had actually interfered as well, and that was truly hard for him to accept.

Li Jiao sent a Mitian Sect Elder tumbling with a palm strike, then snorted coldly. "Mi Qi, a wise man submits to circumstances. It is not too late to lower your head to Palace Master Yang. Otherwise, I fear this will end in regret."

"In your dreams!" Mi Qi growled through gritted teeth. He did have a bit of backbone.

Mainly, he still had a trump card he had not used. Seeing Xi Lei send a thunder arc slashing toward him, he used its momentum to flip away and shouted, "Old Tao, save me!"

On a nearby mountain peak, the white-haired, white-bearded Old Tao looked utterly speechless. He had only come over to eat some fruit and drink a little wine, yet he had somehow run into this. With matters having reached this point, if he still could not guess Mi Qi's intentions, then he would have lived all these years in vain.

But as the saying went, it was hard to refuse someone whose food one had eaten and whose gifts one had accepted. Over the years, Mi Qi had treated him with courtesy, inviting him over every time the Blue Jade Fruit ripened. If Old Tao had not encountered this matter, that would be one thing, but since he had, standing aside with folded arms would be hard to justify.

Besides, Mi Qi had already shouted the plea aloud. If Old Tao kept pretending not to know, that would be going too far.

With a long sigh, Old Tao tapped his foot and drifted over.
