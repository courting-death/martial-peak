## Chapter 3332 — The Sisters Are Very Interested in You

After Yang Kai teased her like that, Zhu Qing's face flushed red, but she still clenched her teeth and said, "Whether I'm hungry is none of your business. In any case, I know you've certainly eaten your fill."

Yang Kai's eyes widened. "Nonsense. Nothing of the sort. Your husband is starving right now."

Zhu Qing gave him a meaningful look. "I remember you once told me that in that lower plane Star Domain, you also had... mm, four wives. What? You went back to the Star Domain this time and didn't see them?"

Yang Kai scratched his cheek. "Well... I saw them. I saw them."

Zhu Qing said, “Speaking of which, you had been apart from them for quite a few years. After such a long separation, with longing pent up on both sides, did nothing really happen?”

Yang Kai gave two dry laughs. "The weather on Dragon Isle is quite nice."

Zhu Qing snorted and jabbed a finger into his chest. "And you still dare tell me you're hungry?"

Yang Kai caught her little hand, fawning over her as he played with it and gently kneaded it. With an apologetic smile, he said, "Change the subject, change the subject." This topic left him rather awkward. Su Yan, Xia Ningshang, Shan Qingluo, and Xue Yue were one thing; those women had always stayed together and were used to it. What had always given Yang Kai a headache was how to arrange for Zhu Qing to meet them.

His four wives were certainly top-class existences in the Star Domain, but compared with Zhu Qing, they were still far inferior.

After all, Zhu Qing was a Dragon Woman. With such a status, who could compare with her? He was truly afraid that Zhu Qing would feel unhappy about it.

Zhu Qing shot him a reproachful glance, then suddenly pulled her hand free and carefully straightened his clothes. Affection moved through her eyes as she said, "Now isn't the time. Wait until... Third Elder and the others have left."

Yang Kai nodded again and again. "Good, good." Then he suddenly gazed at Zhu Qing with deep affection and said softly, "Qing'er, you're so good to me."

Those few simple words surpassed any sweet talk. Zhu Qing heard them and rejoiced inwardly. Though she showed nothing on her face, the corners of her mouth still lifted slightly, and she said, "Stop trying to unsettle me. I'll take you to pay respects to Great Elder and Second Elder. When you see them, you'd better restrain yourself."

"See Great Elder and Second Elder?" Yang Kai's eyes bulged, and he shook his head. "Seeing Great Elder is one thing, but why should I see Second Elder? Not going. Not going!"

From the bottom of his heart, he rejected that woman Fu Zhun. She was the one who had exiled Fu Xuan into the Dragon Tomb, and she was also the one who had wanted to marry Zhu Qing to Fu Chi back then. Yang Kai had not the slightest bit of goodwill toward her.

Zhu Qing glared at him. "You have to go whether you want to or not."

Yang Kai chuckled. "Can you force a cow to drink by pushing its head down? Aren't you afraid I'll get into a fight with that old witch the moment I see her?"

Zhu Qing frowned. "What old witch? Don't talk nonsense. If Second Elder heard you say that, wouldn't she be angered to death?"

Yang Kai stuck out his neck and said, “If she is not an old witch, then who is? Qing’er, let me tell you, just because she tried to marry you off to that Fu Chi back then, I will never be able to stand her in this lifetime. Luckily, that brat was slaughtered by Wu Kuang. Otherwise, I would have killed him myself!”

Zhu Qing sighed. "I know you hold resentment in your heart, but she is still the Dragon Clan's Second Elder, and I am also a member of the Dragon Clan. You should know how difficult it is for my Dragon Clan to reproduce. Every Dragon Clan member has a responsibility to continue the bloodline. Although I hated what she did back then as well, from the Dragon Clan's standpoint, there was nothing wrong with her actions."

"Nothing wrong?" Yang Kai's voice abruptly rose as he glared at Zhu Qing. "She wanted to marry you to someone else, and you're actually telling me there was nothing wrong with what she did?"

Zhu Qing said, "I already said, from the Dragon Clan's standpoint."

Yang Kai gritted his teeth. "I don't care what you were before. What you need to remember now is that you are first my woman, Yang Kai's woman, and only then a member of the Dragon Clan!"

Zhu Qing blinked her large eyes and looked at him blankly. "Are you getting angry at me?"

Yang Kai fumed for a while, then waved a hand dismissively. "Why would I be angry at you? I'm angry at that old witch."

Zhu Qing burst out laughing and stepped forward to hold his arm. "All right, I'll remember. From now on, I'll consider you first in everything, and only then the Dragon Clan."

Yang Kai glanced down at her chest, which his arm had squeezed slightly out of shape. Heat stirred in him, and he swallowed before saying, "Why are you clinging to me like this? If you have something to say, say it properly. In broad daylight... where is your propriety?" After saying this, he glanced left and right, looking furtive.

Zhu Qing ignored him and instead pressed herself even closer, her eyes soft and seductive. "Then promise to come with me to pay respects to the two Elders first."

Yang Kai looked askance at her. "You think tempting me a little will be enough? How childish. I said I'm not going, so I'm not going. Even if you beat me to death, I won't go. Mm, paying respects to Great Elder alone is fine. As for that old witch, the farther away she dies, the better!"

Zhu Qing said softly, "Actually, after what happened last time, Second Elder's attitude changed a great deal. Otherwise, why do you think she didn't exile me into the Dragon Tomb? Back then, even Third Elder was exiled into the Dragon Tomb by her, and Third Elder was as close to her as a sister."

Yang Kai laughed coldly. "What fine sisterly affection! As the elder sister, she actually exiled her younger sister into the Dragon Tomb and left her to fend for herself. If Lord Beast Great Emperor hadn't arrived in time, how could Third Elder still be alive? She should be glad she made a wise decision, otherwise she probably wouldn't have had a good end." After a pause, he added, "Mm, she must have felt the pressure. She already provoked Beast Great Emperor; if she provoked me too, how could she bear it?"

Seeing that neither coaxing nor threats worked on him, Zhu Qing could not help stamping her foot. "What will it take for you to come with me to pay respects to Second Elder?"

Yang Kai turned his head away. "Nothing. In any case, I'm not going."

Zhu Qing said in annoyance, "Then do as you like. In any case, if you miss me in the future, you can come to Dragon Isle to see me. We'll just keep carrying on like this without proper name or status. Anyway, you have four wives, so you'll never be lonely, and you're keeping a Dragon Clan lover on Dragon Isle besides. How impressive." After saying this, she turned her head away and ignored Yang Kai.

Hearing her mention a lover, Yang Kai immediately thought of Ji Yao and inexplicably felt somewhat guilty. His eyes shifted, and he secretly glanced at Zhu Qing, only to see her eyes were slightly red and she looked on the verge of tears. He could not bear it. Walking over, he turned her to face him and said, "Angry?"

Zhu Qing kept her head turned aside and ignored him completely.

Yang Kai could not help scratching his ears and cheeks, secretly wondering whether he had spoken a little too harshly just now. With some difficulty, he said, "Qing'er, you know I truly dislike that woman's attitude and methods. I still haven't settled the account with her from last time. That's only because I don't have the ability yet. Once I do, I guarantee I won't let her off."

Zhu Qing said softly, “Then do you understand? I am of the Dragon Clan, and she is the Dragon Clan’s Second Elder, but I am also your woman. If you insist on staying at odds with her, I will be the one caught in the middle.”

Hearing her say this, Yang Kai was a little dumbfounded. Thinking it over carefully, it was indeed true. He could make as much trouble as he wanted and refuse to give Fu Zhun any face, but that only seemed to make things even harder for Zhu Qing. At this thought, Yang Kai nodded and said, "Fine. I'll go with you to pay respects to... the two Elders!"

Zhu Qing lifted her head in delight. "Really?"

Yang Kai reached out and pinched her cheek, pulling her face slightly out of shape. "For Qing'er, what harm is there in climbing a mountain of blades or descending into a sea of fire?"

Zhu Qing immediately looked at him warily. "Sweet talk comes out the moment you open your mouth. Be honest, exactly how many women have you seduced outside?"

Yang Kai did not know whether to laugh or cry. "How did the topic get dragged over to that?"

Zhu Qing snorted. "You look proper on the surface, but you're full of crooked thoughts. I knew your true nature the first time we met."

Yang Kai let out a long sigh. "Qing'er, you've misjudged me. I've been in the Star Realm for several dozen years, and before I met you, I always kept myself pure as jade. I remained unmoved before all manner of beauties. Only you made me unable to restrain myself."

Zhu Qing's eyes immediately grew a little misty. Facts proved that whether they belonged to the Human Race or the Dragon Clan, as long as they were women, they had little resistance to sweet talk.

Yang Kai then changed the subject. "But that time couldn't really be blamed on me either. I didn't know such an accident would happen."

Zhu Qing said in shy annoyance, "Could it be blamed on me, then?"

"Blame me, blame me!" Yang Kai immediately took full responsibility and chuckled. "Actually, speaking of which, if not for that accident, you and I might not have reached this point today."

Zhu Qing's face flushed slightly, clearly recalling the scene of their first meeting as well. But as she thought about it, her expression suddenly changed. "Right, did you see Fu Ling?"

"I did." Yang Kai nodded, not knowing why she had suddenly brought this up. "She said she had been punished to guard Dragon Isle's entrance over there for a hundred years. I came in from that side, so of course I ran into her. She was also the one who brought me here. Otherwise, how would I know which Spirit Island you lived on?"

"She brought you here?" Zhu Qing paled in shock and looked at Yang Kai nervously. "She didn't do anything to you, did she?"

Yang Kai laughed despite himself. "What could she do to me?"

Zhu Qing thought for a moment and said, "True. You were with Xiaoqi, so she really couldn't do anything to you."

Yang Kai looked at her in confusion. "What is it? Why do you seem so wary of her?"

Zhu Qing gave him a somewhat helpless look. After hesitating for a while, she still decided to tell the truth. "You know that I originally only had an Eighth-Tier Dragon Bloodline, and an Eighth-Tier Dragon Bloodline is already considered very high on Dragon Isle. Before me, only Third Elder and Fourth Elder had Ninth-Tier Dragon Bloodlines, but after being together with you, I... I... unexpectedly broke through to Ninth-Tier."

Yang Kai laughed loudly. "Qing'er, no need to thank me. It was what I should do."

Zhu Qing bashfully punched him, then returned to the subject. "Now the clansmen on Dragon Isle all know why my bloodline suddenly advanced, so some sisters seem to be... very interested in you."

"What do you mean?" Yang Kai was stunned.

Zhu Qing glared at him. "Do you really not understand?"

Yang Kai's eyes shifted. He very much wanted to pretend to be innocent, but he could not manage it no matter what. Pointing at himself, he grinned and said, "Could it be that all the Dragon Girls on your Dragon Isle want to make a move on me?"
