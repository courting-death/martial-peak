## Chapter 3220 — Words Fail to Agree

"The Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace!" Li Jiao replied.

Mi Qi's eye twitched. He bared his teeth. "Why him?" His face filled with disgust; clearly, he had not the slightest goodwill toward Yang Kai. That was no surprise. Although he had only met Yang Kai once, that one meeting had cost Mitian Sect an enormous amount of Source Crystals. Even now, every time he thought back on it, his heart ached like it was being carved apart, and he regretted ever going along to watch the excitement.

He glanced sidelong at Li Jiao. "Has Brother Li not suffered enough under his oppression? Why are you in such a hurry to curry favor with him now?" His tone carried a hint of mockery, and resentment stirred in his heart. He and Li Jiao could at least be considered friends. Their friendship was not especially deep, but it was still better than any relationship he had with Yang Kai. Now Li Jiao had actually come to deliver a message on Yang Kai's behalf, so of course Mi Qi was displeased. He felt Li Jiao was far too spineless. For someone who carried a half-dragon bloodline, it was truly a bit disgraceful.

Li Jiao was somewhat embarrassed by his words. A trace of awkwardness appeared on his face, but it was not convenient to explain that his relationship with Yang Kai had greatly eased, or that Yang Kai had even written off all the debts Li Dragon Palace had owed for years. He could only say bitterly, "There is nothing I can do. Who told him to be the creditor? When you live under another's roof, you have no choice but to bow your head."

"Hmph!" Mi Qi slapped the table, a fierce light flashing in his eyes. "That despicable villain plotted against us with underhanded schemes back then. If not for Grandmaster Ji's sake, why would this Lord have paid him any attention all these years?"

This was the truth. Mitian Sect was, after all, one of the Northern Territory's top Sects, and Mi Qi himself was a Third-Stage Emperor Realm expert. Even though he had lost a bet to Yang Kai back then, he could have simply refused to honor it afterward. With such a huge sum of Source Crystals involved, what did a little face matter? Unfortunately, the incident had happened right under Ji Ying's nose, and later Mi Qi heard that even Ji Ying had ended up in Lingxiao Palace and become its Chief Alchemist. Only then had Mi Qi been forced to compromise.

If he really refused to pay, he might provoke Ji Ying's anger. He was not afraid of Yang Kai, but he was still quite wary of Ji Ying. Either Ji Ying's identity as Sublime Pill Great Emperor's disciple or his identity as an Imperial-Grade Pill Master was enough to make him impossible to take lightly.

Li Jiao thought to himself that there had been no underhanded scheme at all; Yang Kai had won fair and square in Alchemy. If Li Jiao had not gone through life and death with Yang Kai several times, his thinking would probably not have differed much from Mi Qi's. After those journeys together, however, Li Jiao's opinion of Yang Kai had changed considerably. He felt Yang Kai was actually quite decent, provided one did not provoke him.

Mi Qi picked up his wine cup and drained it in one gulp, as if venting the anger in his heart. In a low voice, he asked, "Brother Li, what did that wretch ask you to convey?"

Li Jiao said calmly, "Palace Master Yang's meaning is that Mitian Sect should submit to Lingxiao Palace."

"What?" Mi Qi's eyes widened. "I think I misheard. Brother Li, what was that you just said?"

Li Jiao sighed inwardly. How could he not know that Mi Qi was asking despite already understanding? There were only the two of them here, without any background noise. A cultivator at the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm like Mi Qi could not possibly have failed to hear a few words clearly. Helpless, Li Jiao could only repeat what he had said.

"Heh heh!" Mi Qi laughed from sheer anger. A cold light flashed through his eyes as he toyed with the wine cup in his hand. "Then I wonder, how exactly is this submission supposed to work?"

His tone was chilling. Clearly, he also bore some resentment toward Li Jiao. Since Li Jiao dared to speak such an unreasonable condition aloud, it was hard for him, as the messenger, to remain likable.

Before Li Jiao came, he had already known that the small friendship between him and Mi Qi would likely end here. Seeing Mi Qi's expression now, he thought this was indeed the case. Feeling a little regretful, he said, "Brother Mi, rest assured. Palace Master Yang seems to have no intention of seizing Mitian Sect's territory. It is only that Lingxiao Palace currently needs a large amount of cultivation resources. Palace Master Yang's meaning is for Mitian Sect to hand over a portion of its annual income to Lingxiao Palace from now on."

Mi Qi said with heavy sarcasm, "And how much is this portion, I wonder?"

"Half?" Li Jiao asked tentatively. Yang Kai had not mentioned any figure, but if it were too little, it certainly would not satisfy Yang Kai's appetite.

"Half..." Mi Qi smiled broadly and lifted his wine cup. "Brother Li, drink."

Li Jiao was puzzled, but he still raised his cup and clinked it against Mi Qi's.

As the wine entered his stomach, there was a sharp crash as Mi Qi smashed his cup to the floor, slapped the table, and rose to his feet, shouting in a low voice, “This is going too far! Do you really think my Mitian Sect has no one left?” He had not cultivated all the way to this day by being easily frightened. If he handed over half of Mitian Sect’s income just because anyone came demanding it, he would never have lived until now.

This was not the same as those debts. Although the debts were not small, there would come a day when they were paid off. In about another thirty years, they would be cleared. But if Mitian Sect truly submitted to Lingxiao Palace as Li Jiao said, then it would have to hand over half its income every year from then on, unless Mitian Sect were wiped out.

How could he agree to such bullying? If he truly agreed, how would the world look at him? How would the disciples of Mitian Sect look at him?

"Brother Mi, calm down..."

“Shut up!” Mi Qi glared at Li Jiao, his hair bristling with rage, and sneered disdainfully. “Brother Li truly has fine integrity. Back then, the two of us were humiliated together, and after being exploited and oppressed by Lingxiao Palace for so many years, it would be one thing if you had no thought of revenge, but now you have actually joined hands with that vile beast. Palace Master Yang, hehe… I truly had no idea that Brother Li latched onto his thigh and became so eager to cling to his leg hair.”

Li Jiao looked awkward under the rebuke, but the words were indeed hard to hear. His face hardened. "It is difficult to explain in a few words."

He could hardly tell Mi Qi everything he had experienced with Yang Kai over the years. Setting aside the fact that their friendship had not reached that point, the secrets involving Dragon Isle alone could not be leaked lightly.

"What fine words!" Mi Qi's eyes were full of naked mockery. "Brother Li knows how to read the times and is willing to shelter under another's wing. This Mi does not blame you. Everyone has their own aspirations. But this Mi will not sink as low as you."

The implication was that Li Jiao was greedy for life and afraid of death, a craven coward.

Li Jiao's face turned grim. "I am only responsible for passing on the message. Brother Mi, why make your words so ugly? Whether this matter succeeds or fails has nothing to do with me."

"Those who follow different Daos cannot plot together. Palace Master Li, please."

Brother Li had become Palace Master Li. Clearly, Mi Qi intended to draw a clean line between himself and Li Jiao.

Li Jiao shook his head and rose. Out of consideration for that slight friendship, he still reminded him, "Sect Master Mi, please think twice. Before Lingxiao Palace had even risen, it was already able to destroy Wenqing Sect. Now that several dozen years have passed, it is likely even stronger. How do you think Mitian Sect compares to Wenqing Sect? Why invite disaster upon yourself?"

Mi Qi sneered. "Palace Master Li should mind his own affairs. My Mitian Sect's business needs no concern from you. If Yang Kai wants my Mitian Sect to submit, he may as well come personally and speak with this Mi. Then we can see whether everyone in Mitian Sect will agree!"

"I have said all there is to say!" Li Jiao nodded faintly, cupped his fists, turned, and left.

He had already delivered the message. As for what choice Mi Qi would make, that was Mi Qi's affair. Since Yang Kai wanted to use Mitian Sect to establish his might before the entire Northern Territory, he had likely expected that Mitian Sect would not agree to such a demand easily. Perhaps this situation was exactly what Yang Kai wanted to see. Otherwise, what would he use to establish his might? Of the Northern Territory's four top Sects, two had good relations with Lingxiao Palace, leaving only Mitian Sect.

In the great hall, Mi Qi stared coldly in the direction Li Jiao had gone, a disdainful sneer on his face. He felt Li Jiao was being rather alarmist. He also had no idea what method Yang Kai had used to make Li Dragon Palace follow his lead so completely that even Li Jiao would come running to deliver a message for him.

He had heard plenty about Wenqing Sect's destruction back then. In the Northern Territory, that had been a major event; it would have been hard not to know of it.

Wenqing Sect's strength had indeed been no worse than Mitian Sect's. Such a vast Sect had been destroyed just like that, but it did not mean Mitian Sect would be so helpless as well.

The reason Wenqing Sect had been destroyed was that all its experts had poured out in full force and gone to Ice Heart Valley. In the end, Yang Kai led a group of experts and joined forces with Ice Heart Valley to sweep them clean.

Mi Qi had no intention of attacking Lingxiao Palace. If Yang Kai truly came to provoke him, then with Mitian Sect's Grand Guardian Array and the experts within the Sect, he could absolutely contend with him for a while. If he could lure Yang Kai into the great array, he might even be able to kill him.

Taking the initiative to attack and standing firm to defend one's Sect were clearly two different things.

There was one point, however, that he had to guard against. Yang Kai had three great Yao Kings under him, each comparable to a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master. They would not be easy to deal with.

Mi Qi's eyes shifted, and he clapped his hands.

A black-robed man immediately entered from outside the door.

Mi Qi looked at him. "Are the Blue Jade Fruits in the medicine garden about to ripen?"

The black-robed man did not know why he had suddenly asked this, but he cupped his fists and said, "They will ripen in one month."

Mi Qi nodded. "Perfect. Send word to Old Tao and invite him over to taste one or two."

The black-robed man cupped his fists in acknowledgment, accepted the order, and left.

Turning around, Mi Qi looked toward Lingxiao Palace and sneered several times. Yang Kai, oh Yang Kai, you had best behave yourself. If you truly do not take my Mitian Sect seriously, then this time, I will make sure you never return.

...

"Sect Master Ye, that is the situation. You know what things are like in my Lingxiao Palace right now. There are simply too many disciples. All one hundred peaks are packed full. Some Spirit Peaks house two or three thousand people; it is so crowded that stepping outside feels like going to market. Your Thousand Leaves Sect does not have many disciples, yet you occupy such a large Spirit Peak. Quite a few Peak Masters have already begun to object. After thinking it over, I could only thicken my skin and come discuss it with you." Yang Kai looked helpless. Opposite him sat Ye Hen, Sect Master of Thousand Leaves Sect, while the table of wine and dishes between them remained completely untouched.

"Palace Master's meaning is..." Ye Hen looked at Yang Kai inquiringly.

"Back then, I brought you all to Lingxiao Palace for two reasons. First, the cultivation environment here was good and free from disturbance, suitable for you to rest and recover your strength. Second, the calamity Thousand Leaves Sect suffered began because of me, and this Palace Master felt guilty over it."

"Palace Master speaks too seriously. My Thousand Leaves Sect was simply less skilled than others. Speaking of it, the fact that Thousand Leaves Sect was able to preserve its inheritance is also thanks to Palace Master's care."

"There is no need for thanks." Yang Kai raised a hand. "Today, however, I must play the villain."

"I am willing to hear the details."

Yang Kai smiled bitterly. "The old saying goes that one mountain cannot hold two tigers. Although applying that to my Lingxiao Palace is not quite fitting, that is roughly the idea. It is not that this Palace Master is burning the bridge after crossing it; it is only that having another Sect inside my Lingxiao Palace truly is not proper."
