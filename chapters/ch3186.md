## Chapter 3186 — Inviting the Star Envoy

Yang Kai could not sense any of this. Even if he had, there would have been nothing he could do to stop it. Wu Heng was the Master of a Star Domain, and inside Great Wilderness Star Domain, he could wield power akin to that of a god. Yang Kai himself had no chance of defeating him here. The one capable of contending with him now was not Yang Kai, but Gungun.

Gungun did not disappoint him either. No matter how much Star Domain power Wu Heng condensed and hurled at it, Gungun swallowed it all down.

One large figure and one small figure wove through the Star Domain, tangling and chasing each other in an almost absurdly lively pursuit.

This would not do. This could not go on. Wu Heng was frantic with anxiety.

Every breath he wasted here meant another vast stretch of Starry Sky in Great Wilderness Star Domain being devoured. As the Starry Sky shrank, the power he could mobilize would shrink with it. If this continued, his defeat was inevitable.

He might actually lose! Even as a mere possibility, the thought was inconceivable to Wu Heng. Before coming to find Yang Kai, he had never imagined things could turn out like this.

Yet his current situation truly left him trapped between advance and retreat.

Yang Kai also sensed that something was wrong.

He found a moment to inspect Gungun's condition and finally discovered the abnormal changes inside the Small Xuan Realm.

Just as Gungun had needed time to rest and digest after devouring three Cultivation Stars, Wu Heng's attacks, condensed from star power, were not so easy to swallow. They placed even greater pressure on Gungun than directly devouring Cultivation Stars.

If this went on for too long, the Small Xuan Realm definitely would not be able to bear it. When that happened, things would turn ugly. He had to let Gungun rest for a while and find a chance to digest all that star power.

Two enemies, each burdened with different concerns, made the same choice at this moment and sped toward the edge of Great Wilderness Star Domain.

Wu Heng was anxious to return and restrain Hengluo Star Domain's devouring while driving away Yang Kai, this robber. Yang Kai, meanwhile, was anxious to take Gungun somewhere safe to rest and recover.

Both sides moved extremely quickly. Wu Heng was the Master of the Star Domain, so there was no need to explain his speed. With a single thought, he could cross hundreds of millions of li. Though Gungun's body was huge and bloated, every leap carried it across a terrifying distance, and with Wu Heng guiding it along, whether intentionally or not, its movements were almost like teleportation.

In less than half an hour, the endless darkness came into view.

Before long, both sides reached the border between the two great Star Domains. Yang Kai's spirits lifted. He immediately established contact with his Incarnation, and in the unseen, he felt Hengluo Star Domain's power supporting him.

Although he was still inside Great Wilderness Star Domain, this place already counted as the intersection between the two Star Domains, so the suppression on him was no longer so obvious.

He could not help bursting into laughter. "You refuse the road to Heaven, yet insist on charging into the Gates of Hell. Wu Heng, I really must thank you."

As the words left his mouth, he drove Gungun past Wu Heng and rushed back into Hengluo Star Domain.

After sending away this powerful enemy, Wu Heng felt no joy. Instead, alarm shot through him. His heart sank as he realized he might have made a mistake.

In the next instant, an extremely terrifying force surged from the boundless darkness, so powerful that he felt he could hardly resist it.

Within that darkness, Yang Kai's smiling face emerged, clearly appearing before his eyes.

"You really are the Master of a Star Domain!" Wu Heng cried out, thick regret flooding his heart. If he had known this earlier, he never would have sent Yang Kai back. He had thought Yang Kai was not the Master of a Star Domain and could not be allowed to run wild behind his lines, which was why he had wanted to drive him away. That way, he could focus entirely on the pressure from Hengluo Star Domain without any worries at his back.

But now, it was clear this had been a disastrous move!

Yang Kai truly was the Master of Hengluo Star Domain!

No matter how hard Wu Heng thought, he could not understand it. If Yang Kai was the Master of Hengluo Star Domain, then who had been presiding over the devouring between the Star Domains when he charged into Great Wilderness Star Domain? Could Hengluo Star Domain have two Masters? That was impossible. There was only one Star Domain Origin, so how could two people refine it?

An extraordinarily bold conjecture surfaced in his mind. Wu Heng's face turned livid, and he ground out through clenched teeth, "A clone!"

Only that could explain all his doubts. Yang Kai must have a clone he could control as freely as his own arm, allowing him to maintain the devouring between the Star Domains even while he charged into Great Wilderness Star Domain.

Clone techniques were no secret, nor were they especially difficult Secret Techniques to cultivate. Many experts had practiced them before, but ordinary clones could not do much. At most, they could be sent out to scout dangerous areas, which made them rather useless. On top of that, cultivating such a Secret Technique required extremely precious materials and a great deal of time.

Under normal circumstances, no one would spend much effort on clone techniques, and they were useless in life-or-death battles.

But now, the clone Yang Kai possessed was so powerful that it was almost unbelievable. Otherwise, it could never have directed the power of the entire Hengluo Star Domain.

The darkness rolled forward like a tide, advancing in wave after wave. Vast stretches of Starry Sky vanished from sight. Wu Heng retreated again and again, collapsing like a routed army!

Earlier, when the Incarnation had urged the Star Domain Origin to devour, Wu Heng had already struggled to withstand it and could only desperately suppress its advance. Now that Yang Kai had returned to the Star Domain and his true body was urging the Star Domain Origin, its might naturally rose another level.

On top of that, Great Wilderness Star Domain had lost vast stretches of Starry Sky over these past days while Hengluo Star Domain's territory had expanded. With one side rising and the other falling, Wu Heng was even less of a match.

He could not stop it. He absolutely could not stop it! Wu Heng's face turned ashen. Never had he imagined that a passing impulse to swallow a masterless Star Domain would bring down such a calamity. Yang Kai had only become the Master of a Star Domain recently, so how could he wield such strength?

They were both Masters of Star Domains. By all rights, he should have held the upper hand in this struggle, but reality was the exact opposite.

Wu Heng's figure flickered, and he shot back a million li.

Within the darkness, the face Yang Kai had condensed quietly stared at him and mocked, "Want to run? Once this Young Master eats your Great Wilderness Star Domain clean, I would like to see where you can run!"

Yang Kai felt indescribably pleased. No wonder Wu Heng had conceived the idea of devouring Hengluo Star Domain. Becoming the Master of a Star Domain and devouring other Star Domains actually brought a tremendous sense of pleasure, allowing one to clearly perceive the transformation of World Force during the devouring.

He made up his mind to devour the entire Great Wilderness Star Domain completely and merge the two great Star Domains into one. When that happened, he would be the Master of both great Star Domains.

Facing his mockery, Wu Heng's expression was calm as still water, as if he had already accepted his fate, but a trace of resolve flickered in his eyes.

He suddenly took a jade tablet from his Space Ring. Specks of starlight dotted its surface, as if countless stars had been branded into it, and an extremely mysterious aura spread from it.

From a million li away, Yang Kai saw this and could not help frowning.

Although the distance was great, he could still sense that the jade tablet was not some Secret Treasure. Why was Wu Heng taking such a thing out at a time like this? Then again, he had no idea what this jade tablet was. In any case, it did not look ordinary.

Wu Heng's expression turned solemn. His hands formed several mysterious seals, then he pointed at the jade tablet, raised it high, and shouted, "Inviting the Star Envoy!"

Specks of fluorescent light suddenly burst from the jade tablet, like hundreds of millions of fireflies streaming out. They gathered into a dazzling pillar of light that shot straight into the sky.

Above Wu Heng's head, at the top of the pillar of light, white radiance blazed forth and gathered into a circle several li across. The circle was filled with complex patterns that rotated without any discernible order, dazzling the eyes of anyone who looked at them.

Yang Kai, however, froze, because those patterns looked somewhat familiar.

When he looked more closely, he was immediately dumbstruck.

Wasn't that the array pattern needed to arrange a Cross-Domain Space Array? What the hell was that jade tablet, and how could it construct such a pattern in an instant?

Space Force surged and rose.

Unease suddenly welled up in Yang Kai's heart.

Combining Wu Heng's earlier shout with the scene before him, Yang Kai keenly sensed that someone seemed to be about to tear through space and arrive from within that pattern.

Sure enough, within the dazzling light, a large foot suddenly stepped out from the circular Grand Array. Like an exiled immortal descending from the heavens, a figure slowly appeared.

Yang Kai stared with wide eyes and was astonished to discover a handsome man wearing a feathered crown and holding a feather fan, refined and elegant in bearing. The man was graceful as jade and so handsome that Yang Kai felt sick just looking at him. A long sword hung at his waist, giving him the air of a roving scholar-swordsman. As he descended from the circular pattern, both his eyes were closed.

Wu Heng, however, stood there solemnly, gazing up at him.

Suddenly, the man opened his eyes. They were like thunder and lightning, seeming to see through all things. Wu Heng hurriedly lowered his head, as if one extra glance would be disrespectful to this person.

Yang Kai, meanwhile, felt every hair on his body stand on end as a tremendous pressure descended on his heart.

He was not entirely sure of Wu Heng's true strength, but estimated he was a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master. Yang Kai had seen plenty of such cultivators before, and facing them placed no pressure on him at all. Yet this feather-crowned man who had descended from the sky made his breath catch, even though the other party was not deliberately targeting him.

Only one kind of person could make him feel this way!

This person was not in the Emperor Realm. He was a Great Emperor!

Which Great Emperor was he? The names of one Great Emperor after another flashed through Yang Kai's mind, but he found that none matched this person. Stranger still, Wu Heng had actually been able to summon him.

What exactly was this "Inviting the Star Envoy" he had just shouted? Star God Palace also had the title of Star Envoy. Xiao Yuyang, for instance, was a Golden Star Envoy, but that was not a formal position in Star God Palace. Xiao Yuyang's position in Star God Palace was Elder.

The so-called Star Envoy was a title used throughout the Southern Territory. Star God Palace's Star Envoys were mainly responsible for communicating with and mediating conflicts among the great Sects of the Southern Territory. Within Star God Palace, the Star Envoys also held other positions. The two identities were separate but did not conflict.

This Star Envoy was clearly not from Star God Palace. Even Radiant Moon Great Emperor was only roughly this powerful. No, this man seemed slightly inferior to Radiant Moon Great Emperor, but Yang Kai's experience was limited, and he could not tell exactly where the difference lay.

"Sir!" Wu Heng cupped his fists and bowed.

The feather-crowned man lowered his head and looked down at him. "Wu Heng?" he asked faintly.

"Yes."
