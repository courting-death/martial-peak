## Chapter 3327 - Carry Out Your Duty

"Tsk, giving up already?" Jiu Feng glanced over. It was rare for two people proficient in the Laws of Space to clash, and she had expected their battle to shake Heaven and Earth and make ghosts and gods weep. Who would have thought Yang Kai would concede after only a few moves? It left her quite unsatisfied. She looked him up and down and asked, "Did he hit you?"

Yang Kai nodded lightly and offered no explanation.

Strong as Jiu Feng was, she had not been able to see Li Wuyi's attack clearly. That fist had shifted directions several times in the Void, following Yang Kai like a maggot clinging to bone. No matter how he dodged, twisted, or moved, he could not avoid it. In the end, it had lightly landed on his body, touching and withdrawing in the same instant, moving in and out with perfect ease. In a contest of Space Divine Abilities, Yang Kai had undoubtedly lost. His methods were useless against Li Wuyi, while Li Wuyi could strike him. That alone was enough to decide who was superior.

"To receive Senior's guidance today, this Junior is deeply grateful." Yang Kai cupped his fists solemnly. "I will redouble my efforts and strive to defeat Senior one day."

Li Wuyi laughed heartily. "Good. I will be waiting for that day."

Yang Kai smiled. "Now that matters here are settled, this Junior will take his leave. I will visit the two of you on Spirit Beast Island another day."

Li Wuyi raised a hand. "No hurry. Don't leave yet. There is still something I need your help with."

Yang Kai looked puzzled. "What is it?"

"Wait a moment." After saying this, Li Wuyi flickered and vanished without a trace.

Even with his Divine Sense, Yang Kai could not detect the slightest trace of Li Wuyi's aura. He understood that Li Wuyi must have used that Space Beacon and teleported to some unknown place.

He turned to Jiu Feng with a questioning look, but Jiu Feng said, "Don't look at me. I don't know what this is about either."

Yang Kai nodded. Since Jiu Feng said she did not know, then she definitely did not know. Asking would be pointless, so he simply waited patiently.

Fortunately, less than half a cup of tea later, obvious fluctuations of Power of Space suddenly rippled beside Jiu Feng. A moment later, Li Wuyi reappeared, and someone else appeared with him.

It was a young girl with a butterfly-shaped mark on her face. Her features were stunningly beautiful, enough to topple cities, and her temperament was bright and innocent, but the huge butterfly mark covering half her face spoiled the overall beauty. She wore a fitted green dress that showed off every line of her exquisite, graceful figure.

As soon as she appeared, the girl looked around blankly and happened to meet Yang Kai's eyes.

"Xiaoqi?" Yang Kai looked surprised.

"Eldest Brother Yang?" Mo Xiaoqi's beautiful eyes lit up, joy written plainly across her face. She rushed out from beside Li Wuyi and ran toward Yang Kai, only to seem to realize something and hurriedly stop three zhang in front of him. Her cheeks flushed as she asked, "Is it really you, Eldest Brother Yang?"

"Who else would it be?" Yang Kai smiled faintly and even raised both hands to brush the hair on his forehead back, as if to let Mo Xiaoqi get a clearer look at him.

"Why are you in the Eastern Territory?" Mo Xiaoqi asked, surprised and delighted, her red lips lightly pursed.

"I came here to handle a few things and happened to run into Senior Li and Senior Jiu Feng."

Mo Xiaoqi looked him up and down, then pouted. "You came to the Eastern Territory and didn't even think to visit me on Spirit Beast Island. Eldest Brother Yang, did you forget all about me long ago?"

Yang Kai smiled. "Of course not. I was planning to make a trip to Spirit Beast Island, but I didn't know how to get there, so I was delayed."

"So that's how it is..." Mo Xiaoqi nodded, clasped her hands behind her back, and said with a mix of delight and petulance, "Fine, then. In the future, whenever you come to the Eastern Territory, you have to remember to visit Spirit Beast Island first."

"I will, I will." Yang Kai nodded again and again.

Not far away, Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng watched in silence, both unable to hold back a sigh.

Jiu Feng sent a Divine Sense transmission. "This doesn't look good. Why did you bring this little girl here?"

Li Wuyi replied, "A few days ago, when I sent word to the Great Emperor, I mentioned that this brat Yang Kai was going to Dragon Isle. The Great Emperor had me go back and bring Xiaoqi here, saying he wanted her to accompany Yang Kai to Dragon Isle to meet Mistress."

Hearing this, Jiu Feng finally understood and sighed again. "It is time they met. It has been twenty or thirty years, and she has not seen her own mother once since the day she was born. Poor child. When she was little, she kept asking me whether the Great Emperor had picked her up somewhere outside."

Li Wuyi said, "The last operation was too dangerous, so we didn't bring Xiaoqi along. She made a fuss over it for a long time."

Those words reminded Jiu Feng of what had happened more than ten years ago, and she could not help shivering. She nodded. "This is fine too. There is no harm in letting her go meet her, but isn't the Great Emperor worried Dragon Isle might do something to her... No, with Mistress there to look after her, Dragon Isle won't do anything to Xiaoqi. Besides, Boy Yang is going too. Dragon Isle will likely have its hands full with him this time, so how could they have the spare attention to bother with Xiaoqi? Gigigigi, the Great Emperor has made a clever move. He wants Boy Yang to go draw fire so Xiaoqi and Mistress can pour out their hearts to each other."

The two of them quietly exchanged Divine Sense transmissions on this side, while Yang Kai and Mo Xiaoqi whispered together on the other. After they chatted for a while, who knew what they said, but Mo Xiaoqi covered her mouth and laughed sweetly, while Yang Kai laughed despite himself and shook his head.

Li Wuyi strolled forward and said, "Yang Kai, since you are going to Dragon Isle this time, take Xiaoqi with you. Over there... someone wants to see her."

Hearing this, Yang Kai looked at Mo Xiaoqi and understood. Fu Xuan had to be missing her daughter. He secretly transmitted to Li Wuyi, "Does Xiaoqi know?"

Li Wuyi nodded faintly and transmitted back, "She does. The Great Emperor did not hide this from her, but when the time comes, I will have to trouble you to look after her."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Senior, rest assured. This boy will make certain nothing happens to Xiaoqi."

Li Wuyi smiled. "If the Great Emperor weren't assured, he would not have let Xiaoqi come. Mm, once the matter is settled, remember to send Xiaoqi back to Spirit Beast Island."

"I will." After saying this, he cupped his fists. "In that case, this Junior will take his leave."

"Be careful on the road." Li Wuyi nodded.

Yang Kai turned his head and glanced into the distance. Lan He was watching from over there, so he smiled and waved to her. Lan He froze for a moment, clearly understanding, then waved back.

Ling Yinqin, however, was nowhere to be seen, and Yang Kai did not disturb her peace. He simply called to Qiong Qi, "Let's go."

Qiong Qi stepped forward and sized Mo Xiaoqi up, thinking to himself that no wonder Young Master had disliked that form of address so much before. So there was also a little girl called Xiaoqi...

His fierce, fleshy face made Mo Xiaoqi rather uneasy, and a thought immediately popped into her mind: this old thing was no good.

Qiong Qi squeezed out a faint smile at her. Mo Xiaoqi stuck out her tongue, looking frightened.

Qiong Qi immediately looked depressed. Turning back, he saw Yang Kai staring straight at him, so he quickly composed himself and asked, "Does Young Master have any other instructions?"

Yang Kai asked curiously, "What instructions could I have? Since you have acknowledged me as your master, you should naturally carry out your duty."

Qiong Qi was baffled by this, but a moment later, understanding dawned. His expression twitched. "Young Master, you can't be thinking..."

"Hm?" Yang Kai glanced at him from the corner of his eye.

Qiong Qi's old face turned sheepish. He shuffled about, his eyes darting here and there.

Yang Kai immediately cupped his fists to Li Wuyi. "Senior, I have changed my mind. Please, Senior, use your Divine Ability and take this old thing away."

Before he even finished speaking, Qiong Qi twisted and transformed, suddenly becoming an enormous creature five zhang long and three zhang tall. It was shaped like a yak, with two horns on its head, fiery-red skin over its whole body, and a pair of fleshy wings spread wide enough to seem capable of covering the sky and blotting out the earth. It was truly majestic and imposing, its aura extraordinary.

Scalding heat puffed from its nostrils with each heavy breath, visible to the naked eye.

Yang Kai was immediately amused, thinking this old thing at least knew how to read the situation. He circled it a few times, clicking his tongue in wonder, then flickered and stood on Qiong Qi's back. Reaching out, he called to Mo Xiaoqi, "Come up."

Mo Xiaoqi was a little stunned. Although Spirit Beast Island had plenty of Yao Beasts, and even the Divine Spirit Jiu Feng, she had truly never ridden such an existence as a mount before, especially after seeing the old man's human form. The moment she thought of that fierce, fleshy face, Mo Xiaoqi felt an instinctive resistance.

But since Yang Kai called to her, she still happily leapt up.

"Until we meet again!" Yang Kai cupped his fists to Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng, then lightly tapped his foot.

Qiong Qi beat his wings and shot straight into the sky. In an instant, he became a streak of red light and flashed away like lightning, his speed almost unbelievable. Then again, if he had not been this fast, Li Wuyi would probably have caught him long ago. How else could he have escaped with his life while Li Wuyi was hunting him?

Gazing after Yang Kai and the others, Li Wuyi said, "We should go back too."

Jiu Feng nodded. Their main reason for coming out this time had been Qiong Qi. Now that Yang Kai had taken Qiong Qi in, whether he did good or evil in the future had nothing to do with them. Yang Kai would discipline him himself; if that truly failed, it would not be too late for them to intervene.

The next moment, Li Wuyi urged the Laws of Space, wrapped Jiu Feng up, and vanished from where they stood.

Only after a long while did Tang Sheng, Qian Xiuying, and the others, who had been watching from afar, slowly come forward to investigate. Once they confirmed that none of the visitors remained, they finally let out a long breath. Although the arrival of such honored guests filled everyone in Heavenly Wolf Valley with pride, the gap in strength and status between the two sides was too great, and receiving them had been extremely stressful.

Now that they had safely sent them off, they naturally relaxed.

Although the people had left, traces of the Laws of Space still seemed to linger around the pavilion, and even an array had been left behind. Tang Sheng and the others studied it for a long time and determined that it was a Space Array. They did not know whether Li Wuyi or Yang Kai had arranged it, but after trying many times, they still could not activate the array, and they did not dare destroy it rashly. They could only designate the pavilion as a forbidden area and bar ordinary disciples from entering. But this was, after all, the place where two experts proficient in the Laws of Space had sat and discussed the Dao. Would it not be a pity to let it go to waste? Later, Heavenly Wolf Valley selected several disciples with excellent aptitude to come here and meditate, hoping to see whether they could comprehend anything from this place.

If Heavenly Wolf Valley gained even one cultivator of the Power of Space because of this, they would have profited enormously.
