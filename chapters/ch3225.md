## Chapter 3225 - Can't Hide It Any Longer

Seeing Yang Kai again after more than a dozen years, Gao Xueting found that he had actually reached the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm, equal to her own cultivation!

Gao Xueting could hardly believe it, but she had no choice but to accept it. That Second Stage Emperor Realm aura could not be faked. Still, was his cultivation speed not absurdly fast? When she had first met him, the Four Seasons Land had been about to open, and Yang Kai had used a Mortal Token to exchange for one of the Shrine's entry quotas. Back then, he had only been at the First Stage of the Dao Origin Realm.

A few fleeting years later, he had returned from the Shattered Star Sea already an Emperor.

How many years had passed since then? And now he had broken through again.

Gao Xueting herself was a Second Stage Emperor Realm cultivator, so she naturally understood how difficult each advancement was at the Emperor Realm. Qingyang Shrine had no small number of Emperor Realm masters, but most of them were only First Stage. There were only three at the Second Stage: besides her, there was Deputy Hall Master Qiu Ran and Granny You, who guarded the Divine Roaming Mirror.

Yet Yang Kai, a junior who had risen later, had actually caught up to them. At this rate, sooner or later he would surpass them.

As for disciples of Yang Kai's generation, such as Xia Sheng, Xiao Baiyi, and Murong Xiaoxiao, they had only just managed to stabilize their First Stage Emperor Realm cultivation. Who knew how long it would take them to advance to the Second Stage? By comparison, these so-called genius disciples were nothing but a crowd of mediocrities.

A saying rose unbidden in Gao Xueting's mind: each generation brings forth new talents, and the new replaces the old! The young man sitting in front of her with that apologetic smile was like a newly risen star. If nothing unexpected happened, sooner or later he would shine with dazzling brilliance, illuminating the entire Star Realm.

"Not bad," Gao Xueting said with a faint note of praise, though in her heart she knew this was far more than not bad. It was practically a miracle. She truly had no idea how he cultivated, or what kind of cultivation art he practiced.

"Senior Sister Gao, how many Elder Contribution Points do you think I can exchange these for?" Yang Kai asked.

Gao Xueting put away the ring and said lightly, "Emperor Pills are hard to come by. If this many Emperor Pills are converted into Elder Contributions, the total will certainly be significant. As for the exact amount, I am not sure. Senior Brother Qiu oversees such matters. If you want a precise calculation, we will have to ask him."

"Then what are we waiting for? Let's go find Senior Brother Qiu right now." Yang Kai rose to his feet.

"You seem very eager," Gao Xueting said, giving him a suspicious look.

Yang Kai said, "Not really. It's just that this is my first time making a contribution to the Shrine, after all, so of course I want to know what it is worth. Once a man has results, he has motivation, right?"

"Fine. I will go with you to see Senior Brother Qiu and have him calculate a number." Gao Xueting also stood.

"After you, Senior Sister!" Yang Kai said, gesturing with one hand.

When they reached the entrance of the main hall, Gao Xueting suddenly stopped, turned back to Yang Kai, and looked at him with puzzlement.

"Why are you looking at me like that? Is there a flower on my face?" Yang Kai asked, confused.

"No." Gao Xueting slowly shook her head. "Tell me honestly. How did you get back?"

"What do you mean, how did I get back? I just came back." Yang Kai looked perfectly calm as he lied through his teeth with a straight face.

"If you returned, why did the disciples guarding the mountain make no report? If you came in from outside, you would have had to pass by my Purple Bamboo Peak, so why did I not notice anything at all?"

Yang Kai said, "This Junior Brother is proficient in the Laws of Space. A few quick swishes, and I was back. I suppose Senior Sister simply did not notice."

"Is that so?" Gao Xueting gave him a noncommittal glance, but she did not press him further. She turned and led the way, flying off through the air.

Watching her back, Yang Kai rubbed his nose and wondered where he had slipped up. Unable to figure it out, he could only follow Gao Xueting into the air.

To his surprise, before he could catch up, Gao Xueting suddenly turned and sped off in another direction.

"Senior Sister Gao, aren't we going the wrong way?" Yang Kai called from behind. Although he did not spend much time in Qingyang Shrine, he still knew that Spirit Jade Peak, where Qiu Ran lived, was not in that direction. Gao Xueting ignored him and flew on, soon descending into a valley.

Yang Kai's heart gave a jolt, and he abruptly sped up.

In the valley, inside a certain cave, Gao Xueting stood silently before a Space Array.

Yang Kai walked in with his nerves stretched taut. He called softly to her, but received no answer. Bracing himself, he stepped forward and cried out in surprise, "Eh, what is this? Why is there an array here?"

Gao Xueting turned to look at him. "Keep pretending!"

Yang Kai put on an innocent look. "Senior Sister Gao, what are you talking about? How am I pretending?"

Gao Xueting sneered, “This is the back mountain of your Spirit Sword Peak. Are you telling me you do not know what lies here?”

Yang Kai complained bitterly, "I don't know." Looking at Gao Xueting, he said, "You don't believe me? I really don't know. You know I hardly live here. How would I know what is going on in this place?"

"You really don't know?" Gao Xueting asked.

"I really don't!" Yang Kai said solemnly.

Gao Xueting nodded and looked ahead. "From the look of it, this seems to be a Space Array, and it was set up only a few years ago. I do not know whose handiwork it is, nor where it leads. Junior Brother, stand guard for me. I will investigate it."

"Uh..." Yang Kai had no idea how to talk her out of it, so he could only nod. "All right. Senior Sister, be careful."

Gao Xueting immediately stepped onto the array, took out some Source Crystals, and placed them into the grooves around it. She then urged her Emperor Yuan, trying to activate the array, but there was no response at all.

Yang Kai stood with his eyes lowered and his expression composed, thinking to himself: Who could activate an array I set up without the specially made token? For that reason, he was not worried that Gao Xueting would suddenly be teleported to the Northern Territory.

Gao Xueting tried for a long time, but she still failed to activate the array and could only step back down.

Yang Kai said, "It looks like this array has fallen into disrepair over the years and probably lost its function. Since it no longer works, it is useless. We don't need to bother with it."

Gao Xueting shook her head. "Perhaps there is another activation method that we do not know. We also do not know where this array leads, and this place lies in the heart of the Shrine. We absolutely cannot leave such a hidden danger here. Otherwise, if someone used this array to infiltrate the Shrine one day, we would not even notice."

With a clear, ringing zheng, cold light flashed. Gao Xueting had drawn her sword, Emperor Yuan surging around her.

Yang Kai's eyelid twitched. "Senior Sister, what are you doing?"

"Whatever it is, I will destroy it first so we can stop worrying." As she spoke, she raised her sword and prepared to strike the array.

"Wait!" Yang Kai shouted.

Gao Xueting turned to him. "What is it?"

Yang Kai scratched his cheek and said with a perfectly serious expression, "Senior Sister, please do not act rashly. This is a Space Array, after all. Although we do not know where it leads, what if it connects to some blessed land unknown to the world? If Senior Sister destroys the array like this, would that not cut off our connection to that blessed land?"

"Then what do you suggest?"

Yang Kai cupped his fists. "This Junior Brother is untalented, but I happen to be proficient in the Laws of Space. Leave this array to me to study for a while. Perhaps I can discover something."

"And how long does Junior Brother intend to study it?"

"One or two months?"

"Mm."

"Or maybe thirty to fifty years?"

"Mm?"

Yang Kai said, "I can't say for sure. This Junior Brother can only promise to do his best."

Gao Xueting waved a hand. "There is no need for all that trouble. We will simply destroy it. Junior Brother, move aside. This array looks rather sturdy, and I will not be holding back."

"Senior Sister Gao..." Yang Kai raised a hand to his forehead, nearly at his wit's end.

"Do you have anything else to say?" Gao Xueting looked at him as if telling him to hurry up and leave his last words, as though the sword in her hand was about to cut down Yang Kai, not the array.

Yang Kai lowered his head and said, “Senior Sister Gao, do you perhaps know something?”

Gao Xueting snorted. "That depends on what you want me to know."

"Ai!" Yang Kai sighed, knowing he could not hide it any longer. "How did you discover it?" He had thought this array was hidden well enough, and Spirit Sword Peak was covered by the great array activated through his Elder Jade Token. Unless someone searched inch by inch, it should have been impossible to find.

Gao Xueting said, "The last few times, you came and went like a ghost. It would have been difficult not to arouse suspicion. Our Hall Master is also a man with far too much time on his hands. During the dozen or so years you were away, he naturally studied Spirit Sword Peak carefully. Did you think one great array could block his investigation? He has a Formation Plate that controls the array at an even higher level. Speak. Where does this array lead?"

Yang Kai lowered his gaze, looking obedient and stiff. "Northern Territory."

"Lingxiao Palace?" Gao Xueting raised a brow.

Yang Kai nodded.

"As expected, it is a cross-territory Space Array!" Gao Xueting snorted coldly. "You made it?"

Yang Kai smiled awkwardly. "Yes."

"Why were you being so secretive?"

"I was not being secretive. I only wanted to tell you all when the timing was right," Yang Kai argued.

Gao Xueting naturally did not believe him. If she had not confronted him in person today, there would probably never have been a right time. Still, she could understand Yang Kai to some extent. The existence of this array was far too astonishing, and likely could not be revealed lightly.

"Why can't I activate it?" This was not Gao Xueting's first attempt. When Wen Zishan had discovered the array earlier, she and Wen Zishan had already tried, but no matter what they did, they could not use it.

"It requires a token I specially refined." Since it had already been discovered, there was nothing left to hide. To be fair, Qingyang Shrine had treated him quite well. The main reason he had kept the matter so secret was that he feared Gao Xueting would use him as free labor.

Everyone in Qingyang Shrine knew he was an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, and they had long been itching for him to refine Emperor Pills for the Shrine full-time. But how could Yang Kai have the time to refine pills? Hiding away in Lingxiao Palace naturally gave him peace and quiet. Now that the array was exposed, Gao Xueting would probably be able to come looking for him anytime she liked.

"What token? Let me see it."

"Can we skip that?" Yang Kai asked weakly, looking at Gao Xueting.

Gao Xueting held out her hand, making it clear she would not let the matter drop until he handed it over.

Yang Kai could only take out a token. Gao Xueting immediately snatched it from him, held it in her hand, and examined it. She found nothing special about it, except that Yang Kai had sealed Power of Space within the token.

She vaguely understood that this sealed Power of Space was the key to activating the array.

With a casual flip of her hand, she put the token into her Spatial Ring.

The corner of Yang Kai's mouth twitched, but he did not dare say anything.
