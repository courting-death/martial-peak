## Chapter 3318 — Half a Member of Spirit Beast Island

Tang Sheng nodded lightly, looking deeply reverent. "That's right. That is Sir Li."

How resounding was the reputation of the number one person beneath the Great Emperors? In the Star Realm, once one's cultivation reached a certain level, very few people had not heard of Li Wuyi.

Yet not many had actually seen him. After all, Spirit Beast Island was elusive and hard to find, and Li Wuyi himself was proficient in the Laws of Space, appearing and vanishing without a trace. He was not someone just anyone could meet. In his early years, Tang Sheng had once had the fortune to see him. Back then, Tang Sheng had not yet reached the Emperor Realm; he had only been a youth.

Now, in the blink of an eye, several hundred years, nearly a thousand, had passed. Tang Sheng had already reached middle age, but time seemed not to have left the slightest mark on Li Wuyi. He looked just as he had when Tang Sheng saw him nearly a thousand years ago, still so high-spirited, still so dazzling that one could scarcely look straight at him.

Tang Sheng sighed deeply. In this life, he feared he would never have any hope of reaching such a level. Even reaching the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm would be a stretch. Turning to look at Lan He, whose eyes shone with an unusual light, and Red Ghost, whose gaze gleamed brilliantly, he wondered whether they would have that chance.

"If that person is Li Wuyi, then the woman beside him... could she be the legendary Jiu Feng?" Qian Xiuying's expression shifted as she looked at the beautiful young matron dressed all in red.

Spirit Beast Island's two great experts, Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng, both had incomparably resounding names. The former was famed for his mastery of the Laws of Space, while the latter was renowned for her status as a Divine Spirit. She was of the Phoenix Race, whose numbers were even smaller than the Dragon Clan's.

Tang Sheng nodded. "It should be. But why are they here? Could the commotion from the great battle have drawn them over?"

Although Spirit Beast Island was also in the Eastern Territory, it was far too distant from this place. Even if a great battle had broken out here earlier, Spirit Beast Island could not possibly have sensed it. The only possibility was that they had already been nearby, which was why they could tear through space and arrive so quickly.

"How lively." In the sky, the Void Crack slowly faded away. Jiu Feng swept her beautiful eyes over the surroundings and laughed softly. Then her gaze settled on Yang Kai. Her phoenix eyes narrowed slightly, then suddenly lit up. "It's you, boy?"

Although Yang Kai's physique had changed enormously, she recognized him at a glance. After all, this was not the first time she had dealt with Yang Kai. Back on Dragon Isle, she had seen Yang Kai's Dragon Transformation body, so naturally it was not unfamiliar to her now.

"Greetings, Senior Jiu Feng, greetings, Senior Li!" Yang Kai cupped his fists to the two of them and grinned. This was rather interesting. He had been planning to find time to visit Spirit Beast Island and seek out Li Wuyi to discuss the Laws of Space, but he had not expected the man to come looking for him instead. That saved him some trouble.

"Senior this, Senior that. Am I that old?" Jiu Feng's face darkened.

Yang Kai grinned. "Big Sister Jiu Feng is in the very bloom of youth and beauty. How could you be old? This Yang Kai spoke carelessly and said the wrong thing. Big Sister Jiu Feng, please don't take offense."

Jiu Feng beamed and nodded again and again. "A teachable child!" She looked thoroughly delighted.

At the side, Li Wuyi raised a hand to his forehead. "Could you maybe show a little shame?"

Jiu Feng glanced sideways at him. "Are you saying I have no shame? You dare say I have no shame?"

Li Wuyi pointed at Yang Kai. "I'm talking about this boy. His ability to change with the wind is truly remarkable. If the Dragon Clan knew you were like this, I wonder whether they would clean house."

Yang Kai laughed loudly. "The Dragon Clan has no say over me. More importantly, Eldest Brother Li, Big Sister Jiu Feng, why have you come here?"

Li Wuyi was stunned senseless by that "Eldest Brother Li." Whenever they had met before, Yang Kai had called him "Senior" at every turn, always with proper respect. In the blink of an eye, he had somehow become Eldest Brother.

But on second thought, since Jiu Feng had already become Big Sister, he could only go along with being Eldest Brother for now. Otherwise, wouldn't he be taking advantage of Jiu Feng? When this great aunt flew into a rage, even he found her a little hard to handle.

"That is a long story. What happened on your side?" Li Wuyi did not explain in detail, but his gaze drifted, intentionally or otherwise, over the flamboyant old man standing beside Yang Kai as if seeking protection.

It really did have something to do with this old fellow. Yang Kai understood at once. Earlier, when that Void Crack appeared, the old man had immediately run to his side, and Yang Kai had already formed a guess. Now, one look at Li Wuyi's expression told him he had guessed correctly.

Only... had Spirit Beast Island's two great experts both taken action just to deal with him? What Divine Spirit incarnation was this old man, to have actually provoked Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng into chasing him down together?

Besides, if you were being hunted, why not hurry up and flee for your life? Why run over here and latch onto me as your Young Master? Now you were even standing beside me as if seeking protection. Did you think this old master could protect you?

A hundred thoughts raced through Yang Kai's mind. He smiled faintly and said, "It's nothing much. Someone merely coveted a few good things in my possession, so he racked his brains trying to kill me and seize my treasures." As he spoke, he cast a cold, sinister glance toward Cang Mo and chuckled.

"Kill and seize treasures!" Jiu Feng's dark brows furrowed, displeasure written across her face.

Yang Kai appeared to have nothing to do with Spirit Beast Island, but in reality, his ties to it ran very deep.

First, Spirit Beast Island's little princess, Mo Xiaoqi, was very close to Yang Kai. Over the years, that girl Xiaoqi had mentioned Yang Kai countless times on Spirit Beast Island, and she had long wanted to leave the island to visit him. Unfortunately, the Beast Great Emperor had grounded her, leaving the little girl in great misery. How could Jiu Feng fail to see that she seemed to harbor a hazy affection for Yang Kai? If something happened to Yang Kai, wouldn't the little girl turn the heavens upside down?

Second, the mistress of Spirit Beast Island was Dragon Isle's Third Elder, while Yang Kai and Dragon Isle's Fifth Elder had already become husband and wife in truth. Last time on Dragon Isle, they had even fought shoulder to shoulder and caused a great disturbance. Even the Great Emperor had praised Yang Kai highly. With this indirect connection between them as well, they could almost be called connected by marriage and kinship.

Third, Liu Yan was utterly loyal to Yang Kai, her whole heart bound to him. Liu Yan had inherited the True Phoenix Fire and could just barely be considered a member of the Phoenix Race, making her one of Jiu Feng's few family members.

Taken together, Yang Kai seemed on the surface to have no relationship with Spirit Beast Island at all, but when counted carefully, their ties were extremely tangled and complicated.

That was why Jiu Feng's attitude toward Yang Kai was so friendly this time, to the point that she deliberately made him change how he addressed her in order to draw their relationship closer.

Now that she had suddenly heard someone wanted to kill Yang Kai and seize his treasures, Jiu Feng was naturally angry.

She turned to look, and her brow furrowed despite herself. How could it be him?

Li Wuyi also looked over and said in astonishment, "Brother Cang?"

"Brother Li, Lady Jiu Feng." Cang Mo coughed a few times and gave a slight cupped-fist salute, his aura somewhat weak.

Li Wuyi did not know whether to laugh or cry. "The person Little Yang said wanted to kill him and seize his treasures wouldn't be Brother Cang, would it?" Although he asked, he knew that was probably the truth. Looking at Cang Mo and Yang Kai's current states, it was obvious they had just fought a great battle, and one in which both had gone all out. The result had been mutual injury; neither of them had come out well.

Cang Mo did not answer, instead asking, "Brother Li is familiar with this boy?"

Li Wuyi said, "How should I put it... mm, Little Yang can just barely be considered half a member of Spirit Beast Island."

Leaving aside Mo Xiaoqi's hazy affection for Yang Kai, just the Dragon Clan's Third Elder and Fifth Elder, plus the relationship between Liu Yan and Jiu Feng, meant there was no getting around calling him half a member of Spirit Beast Island.

Cang Mo was visibly shaken. "This boy is actually one of Spirit Beast Island's people?" Although Li Wuyi had said half a member of Spirit Beast Island, what kind of existence was Spirit Beast Island? Since Li Wuyi acknowledged him to this extent, it meant Yang Kai was deeply involved with Spirit Beast Island.

Otherwise, Li Wuyi could simply have said there was no relationship at all. Cang Mo was truly shocked.

He could only laugh bitterly to himself. If he had known this earlier, how could he have done such a thankless thing? Not only had he failed to snatch what he wanted, he had also needlessly provoked such an enemy. Just now, he had even been beaten by that old man until he lost all face.

This time, he had truly lost on both fronts.

Inside Heavenly Wolf Valley, Tang Sheng and the others all stared blankly.

Although they were not close to the battlefield, Tang Sheng and the others were reasonably strong cultivators. By focusing their hearing, they could still make out what Li Wuyi was saying.

Half a member of Spirit Beast Island? Tang Sheng felt stifled with resentment. Since you are half a member of Spirit Beast Island, how could you not know where Spirit Beast Island is? You even ran to Heavenly Wolf Valley to seek help. Wasn't that just cheating people?

Previously, he had thought Yang Kai had no significant relationship with Spirit Beast Island. To avoid offending two great forces, he had refused to help. He had even specifically ordered someone to call Lan He back when she was sending Yang Kai away, lest they become too deeply involved and draw fire upon themselves.

Only now did he learn that Yang Kai's relationship with Spirit Beast Island was excellent. If he had known this earlier, how could he have made such a foolish decision? Heavenly Wolf Valley held a token bestowed by a Beast-Seeking Envoy of Spirit Beast Island, and that alone could be treated as its greatest protective talisman, enough to make every force in the Eastern Territory dread them and dare not move rashly against Heavenly Wolf Valley. If they had been able to forge a connection with Yang Kai this time, then Heavenly Wolf Valley rising in the future to become one of the top forces would not necessarily have been a fool's dream.

Tang Sheng looked deeply vexed, as if he had watched an enormous opportunity slip through his fingers.

Lan He gave him a quiet glance and sighed inwardly.

Earlier, she had asked Tang Sheng whether he would regret it. Now, the answer was clear. The vexation and regret on Tang Sheng's face were plain to see.

Qian Xiuying had been married to him for many years, so how could she fail to sense what he was thinking? She reached out, held his hand, and said gently, "Such is timing, such is fate. In your position, you naturally had to consider many things. If you had another chance, what choice would you make? Would you change it?"

Another chance? Tang Sheng thought for a moment, then said, "No."

Even if he had another chance, he would still make the same choice, because judging by the situation at the time, he had no other choice.

Qian Xiuying smiled faintly. "Then there is no need to be vexed. This opportunity was not meant for Heavenly Wolf Valley, and it cannot be forced."

Hearing her say this, Tang Sheng suddenly felt enlightened. He nodded and said, "Madam is right. I was overthinking it." Fortunately, although Heavenly Wolf Valley had not helped Yang Kai much this time, it had not kicked him while he was down either. They had even rejected Xu Changfeng's earlier proposal to activate the Grand Guardian Array and cut off the world, so in the end, they had not offended Yang Kai.

Given the relationship between Lan He and Ling Yinqin, Yang Kai presumably would not come looking for trouble with Heavenly Wolf Valley.
