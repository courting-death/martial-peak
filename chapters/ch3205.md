## Chapter 3205 — What Exactly Is That?

If they really agreed on the spot, the only ones who would lose out would be the three Saint Venerables. Once they were all sworn brothers and sisters, could they refuse to help with Lingxiao Palace's affairs? They would surely have to send a dozen or so Yao Kings over to hold the fort for this Fourth Brother.

This was exactly what they feared most. They could not avoid it quickly enough, so why would they walk into it themselves?

The atmosphere, which had originally been fairly harmonious, suddenly stiffened.

Yang Kai read their expressions at once, so how could he fail to see what they were thinking? He snorted coldly to himself, thinking that in this world, strength really was what gave a person the right to speak. These three fellows probably looked down on him a little. Otherwise, why would they look so troubled?

He had only said it offhand and had not truly meant it. All he wanted was the Ancient Land's abundant resources. Since they were unwilling, why would he force the issue? A melon pulled from the vine by force would not be sweet. Besides, Yang Kai did not think he was any worse than these three fellows. He had even killed his way in and out of Dragon Isle. Why would he take three Divine Spirits, all far inferior to the Dragon Clan, seriously?

He laughed and said, "Only joking, only joking. Please don't take it seriously."

Fan Wu wiped the cold sweat from his forehead and forced out a smile. "Brother Yang is truly an amusing person. Come, come, this Fan will toast you another cup." As he spoke, he shot Luan Feng and Cang Gou a meaningful look.

After a few cups of wine went down, the awkward atmosphere eased considerably.

By evening, brilliant sunset clouds filled the sky, and Fan Wu and the other two stood to take their leave.

Yang Kai said warmly, "I'll see you off."

"No need, no need," Fan Wu hurriedly said. "We can see ourselves out. Brother Yang should rest." They had not wanted him to move around the Ancient Land too much to begin with, so how could they let him send them off?

"Then... very well!" Yang Kai nodded, cupped his fists, and said, "Then forgive me for failing to see the three of you off properly."

"Not at all. Brother Yang, please stay!" Fan Wu smiled with restraint, then bid farewell to the Elder and Mu Na before flying high into the air with Luan Feng and Cang Gou.

Yang Kai waved enthusiastically. "This Junior Brother will remember the kindness the three of you have shown today. Another day, I will visit your residences to express my thanks. Safe travels, all three of you!"

Fan Wu and the other two had just turned to leave when they staggered, nearly plunging headfirst from the sky. Yang Kai could clearly see Fan Wu draw several deep breaths, while Luan Feng even turned back and glared at him.

The three figures became streaks of light and vanished. Yang Kai stood where he was, brows tightly furrowed, unable to make sense of it no matter how he thought it over. Scratching his head, he said, "Strange. Truly strange!"

"Pfft!" Mu Na burst out laughing again.

The Elder was also all smiles, unable to hold back his amusement.

Yang Kai turned back to look at them. "Elder, Patriarch, what do you think those three fellows meant today? They came all this way just to give me gifts? I don't seem to have much friendship with them." He was only somewhat familiar with Luan Feng. As for Cang Gou and Fan Wu, he had merely dealt with them last time.

Even with Zhang Ruoxi behind him, they should not have gone this far.

"Do you really not know, or are you pretending?" Mu Na was speechless. To her, Fan Wu and the others' intentions today were plain as day, yet Yang Kai remained utterly confused and had even brought up sworn brotherhood.

Wasn't that practically forcing them into a dead end?

Yang Kai's expression turned solemn, and he cupped his fists. "Patriarch, please enlighten me."

"How have you managed to live this long?" Mu Na stared at him in amazement.

The Elder chuckled and said, "It is not fair to blame him for being slow if he cannot figure this out. I suspect even he himself does not know the inside story."

Hearing this, Mu Na thought for a moment and said, "True enough. Very well, boy, I will tell you what was going on with them today..."

After listening to Mu Na's explanation, Yang Kai finally understood. Pointing at himself with wide eyes, he said, "They're afraid I'll lure those Yao Kings away?"

Mu Na said, "The Yao Kings' cultivation has more or less reached its peak. If they want to advance further, their only option is the Blood Gate. And you are the only one who can put in a word with that descendant of Heaven's Punisher. Shi Jiu was able to enter the Blood Gate thanks to you. With that precedent, you should understand what kind of place you hold in those Yao Kings' hearts, no?"

Yang Kai was still a little dazed. "I knew about this long ago, but I never imagined the influence would be this great."

Yingfei, Xi Lei, and Xie Wuwei were the three Yao Kings serving under him. Back then, when he took them away from the Ancient Land, he had merely been borrowing their strength. Later, once the matter was settled and Yang Kai wanted to send them back, the three Yao Kings had been extremely unwilling. And after learning that the Space Array had been destroyed, allowing them to remain at Lingxiao Palace, the three of them had been overjoyed.

It was not that they had forgotten their roots. People sought higher places, just as water flowed downward, and Yao Kings were no different.

Yang Kai said thoughtfully, "In that case, if I raised my arm and called out in the Ancient Land, wouldn't I be able to take every Yao King away?"

"Probably."

Yang Kai said, "If I told them to join me in wiping out Fan Wu and the others..."

"Wishful thinking!" Mu Na despised the idea without the slightest courtesy. "The three Saint Venerables have ruled the Ancient Land for countless years. Under the weight of their long-established authority, which Yao King would dare oppose them head-on? For the sake of entering the Blood Gate, they might be willing to leave with you and obey your commands, but if you dared tell them to deal with Fan Wu and Luan Feng, I fear you would be disappointed."

Yang Kai chuckled. "I was only saying it casually." Then his expression brightened. "From the looks of it, I really should come walk around the Ancient Land more often in the future."

As long as he came, he would not even need to do anything. He would only have to let the news spread, and Fan Wu, Luan Feng, and Cang Gou would probably send over more benefits just to send him away.

Of course, this sort of thing could work once or twice, but not a third time. Divine Spirits were not easy to provoke either. If he truly angered them and both sides gave up all pretense of civility, things would turn ugly.

In any case, now that Yang Kai understood the cause, his mood improved greatly. Although he had not managed to turn the entire Ancient Land into Lingxiao Palace's rear garden, perhaps he could establish a cooperative relationship with the Ancient Land's three Saint Venerables in the future. Only a relationship that benefited both sides could remain stable for long.

The Ancient Land produced no small amount of materials, and Lingxiao Palace could easily trade them Pills in exchange for those materials. The yao merely lacked people capable of refining Pills; that did not mean they could not take Spirit Pills. On the contrary, human-refined Pills were rare goods among the yao. Even if Lingxiao Palace sold them at ten or twenty times the price, demand would probably still outstrip supply.

At that point, Lingxiao Palace would have its cooperation with Ziyuan Commerce Guild in the Southern Territory and its cooperation with the Wild Ancient Land in the Eastern Territory. By exchanging needed goods between them and earning a modest margin, Lingxiao Palace would never again have to worry about cultivation resources for its disciples.

Moreover, this was an exclusive business. Even if others were envious, they could not copy it. Who told them not to have a cross-territory Space Array?

A vague idea had already taken shape in his mind. Once he returned to Lingxiao Palace, perhaps he could have Hua Qingsi draw up a concrete, workable plan.

"Right, Patriarch, you said earlier that you had something to tell me. What was it?" Yang Kai suddenly remembered and asked.

"Come with me." Mu Na's expression turned solemn, and she flew toward the tree hollow.

Yang Kai did not know what she wanted to do, so he could only follow closely behind.

Inside the tree hollow, Mu Na turned to face him. Only after Yang Kai walked in did she raise her hand, and the hollow suddenly sealed itself, cutting them off from the outside.

What did she need to say that required such secrecy? Yang Kai was puzzled, but he also knew Mu Na would not harm him. Everyone knew the Wood Spirit Race disliked conflict.

Just as he was about to speak, Mu Na suddenly flew toward him, drew close to his face, and said, "Close your eyes."

Yang Kai obediently did as he was told. A natural fragrance soon lingered at the tip of his nose, and he could not help breathing it in a few more times. Speaking of which, Mu Na was also a stunning beauty; it was only the limitations of the Wood Spirits that left her palm-sized. If she were enlarged to the size of an ordinary human, she would absolutely be a beauty capable of bewitching all living beings.

Her scent was very pleasant, and Yang Kai's mind suddenly grew calm as an ancient well, as if he had entered a serene communion with nature.

A short while later, Yang Kai heard a strange movement, and his expression immediately turned odd, because he sensed Mu Na circling around him and constantly sniffing at something.

"Patriarch, what are you doing?" Yang Kai secretly cracked one eye open and asked.

If an ordinary woman acted like this, Yang Kai would definitely think she was trying to seduce him, but Mu Na clearly was not. Did he have some unusual aura on him? Otherwise, what was she sniffing?

There was no response. Mu Na continued downward, then suddenly stopped at Yang Kai's Dantian. After sniffing a few times, shock appeared on her face, and she raised a hand to cover her red lips.

The tree hollow was quiet. Yang Kai waited for a while and said, "Can I open my eyes?"

"Mm."

Yang Kai opened his eyes, only to see Mu Na frowning, looking troubled.

Today's events really were strange in every way. First Fan Wu and the others had taken the initiative to come bearing gifts, and now the Wood Spirit Patriarch had dragged him into a tree hollow and sniffed him all over...

"Do you know what our Wood Spirit Race excels at most?" Mu Na suddenly asked.

"Growing spirit melons and exotic fruits, and brewing fine wine! Right, you're also quite good at song and dance." Yang Kai answered without even thinking.

Mu Na raised a hand to her forehead. "So that is what our Wood Spirit Race amounts to in your eyes."

Yang Kai said in surprise, "Isn't it?"

Mu Na smiled bitterly. "Of course not. We are skilled at growing spirit melons and exotic fruits because we are born with the ability to communicate with plants. Presumably, you have also heard the rumors that in ancient times, our Wood Spirit Race served as the stewards who managed medicine gardens for those powerful experts."

Yang Kai nodded firmly.

"This is our Wood Spirit Race's innate Divine Ability. In our hands, any plant in this world can be used to its greatest effect, because we know their natures intimately. No other race can compare to us in this regard."

Yang Kai put on a look of sudden understanding, though he had absolutely no idea what Mu Na actually wanted to say.

"It is precisely because we have such an innate Divine Ability that our Wood Spirit Race can sense the presence of certain very unusual plants." Mu Na stared at Yang Kai with burning eyes. "Do you have some especially precious plant on you, such as a flower, a grass... or a tree?"

Yang Kai's eyelids twitched, but he said solemnly, "Why do you say that?"

He really did have an especially precious plant on him. He even suspected Mu Na might already know something.

Mu Na said, "Don't be nervous. I only know because I smelled a certain scent." After a pause, she added, "Actually, when you came here last time, I already sensed something, but I did not dare be too certain."

"And now you can be certain?"

"Mm." Mu Na nodded. "Can you tell me what exactly that is?"
