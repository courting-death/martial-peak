## Chapter 3222 — Still No Reaction?

With no other choice, he could only make idle conversation with Ji Yao for a while, just to keep the atmosphere from growing too awkward.

Fortunately, now that Su Yan had come up, they at least had something to talk about. Yang Kai asked a few questions about her, and Ji Yao answered them one by one. Su Yan had been doing quite well in Ice Heart Valley lately, and her Senior Sisters were all extremely kind to her. Some of that was no doubt out of consideration for Yang Kai, but more of it came from the bonds between fellow disciples. In any case, Bing Yun had already spoken; Su Yan was their Junior Sister now, and from here on, she was family.

When Bing Yun later learned that Su Yan possessed the special Ice-Crystal Yu Body, she was even more pleasantly surprised. Yang Kai had not mentioned it when he first brought Su Yan to Ice Heart Valley to acknowledge her roots and return to the Sect. He had meant to, but Bing Yun had accepted Su Yan so readily that he had hardly needed to say a word.

One sat while the other stood. From time to time, Yang Kai turned his head to look outside.

His meaning could not have been clearer. It was nearly dark, so it was about time for her to leave. Otherwise, once the night deepened and all was quiet, a lone man and woman sharing a room would not sound good if word got out.

Ji Yao, however, paid no attention to this at all. She simply sat there in silence, as if nothing about it bothered her.

Night fell. The rolling clouds and mist outside the window had grown difficult to make out, their hazy blur carrying a beauty of its own.

Yang Kai was rather speechless. He truly could not figure out what Ji Yao was trying to do. Had she come to settle accounts with him over what had happened last time? But she did not quite look like it. If she really wanted to settle accounts, she could just say so. Why keep lingering here?

Standing there indefinitely wasn’t going to accomplish anything. It made him look as though he were meekly standing at attention while she lectured him. Yang Kai thought to himself that, as the dignified Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace, he was at least someone of status and standing. This was his territory, after all. Why should she be sitting while he remained standing?

He turned around, smoothed his robes with both hands, and sat down on the edge of the bed, neither too close to Ji Yao nor too far from her.

"Right, how has Junior Sister Liu been?" Yang Kai asked, fishing for something to say. By Junior Sister Liu, he naturally meant Liu Qianyun, who had entered the Star Realm with him through the Starlight Passage back then and ended up stranded in Biyu Sect. Later, through a twist of fate, she had entered a Small World together with Yang Kai, and it was there that they had met Bing Yun. After leaving that Small World, Liu Qianyun had joined Ice Heart Valley.

Strictly speaking, Liu Qianyun had not started out cultivating Ice Attribute Secret Arts, so by Ice Heart Valley's standards for taking disciples, she did not qualify. But she had nowhere to settle, and Yang Kai could hardly ignore her, so he had asked Bing Yun to accept her.

"Junior Sister Liu has been in retreat lately, attempting to break through to the Emperor Realm. I have not seen her in several years, but I imagine she is fine," Ji Yao replied faintly.

This was the advantage cultivators from Lower Plane Star Domains had: their aptitude and comprehension were both outstanding, giving them an excellent chance of reaching the Emperor Realm. Liu Qianyun had been one of the foremost experts of the Great Wilderness Star Domain, and after entering Ice Heart Valley, with abundant cultivation resources and a superior environment at her disposal, her long accumulation had burst forth all at once, giving her the momentum to rise rapidly.

Yang Kai nodded lightly, happy for Liu Qianyun.

Ji Yao's next words made Yang Kai's heart skip: "Senior Brother is interested in Junior Sister Liu as well?"

Yang Kai stared at her and said solemnly, "Do not talk nonsense. You will ruin her reputation."

Ji Yao toyed unhurriedly with her sleeve. "Has Senior Brother ruined so few reputations?"

What was that supposed to mean! Had he not merely lost control for a moment that day? Besides, that incident could not be blamed entirely on him, could it? Was she really going to keep holding it over his head forever? If she felt she’d gotten the short end of the stick, then fine—he’d let her take advantage of him this time.

Turning to stare hard at her, Yang Kai said sternly, "Junior Sister Yao, I think you may have misunderstood me."

"Hehe..." The corner of Ji Yao's mouth lifted slightly, neither confirming nor denying it.

Yang Kai immediately grew angry from embarrassment. He leaned toward her, veins throbbing on his forehead, and said, "Can you not laugh like that? It is very disrespectful."

He had spoken in anger, but once the words left his mouth, Yang Kai suddenly froze.

A trace of panic had flashed across Ji Yao's face, and her long lashes trembled several times in quick succession.

Yang Kai came back to his senses with a twitch of his expression and only then realized he had nearly pressed his mouth to her face.

Now this was awkward again... He had meant to explain things properly, but now it looked as if he was taking advantage of her instead. This really was mud in the crotch; even if it was not shit, it looked like shit. If he pulled back now, it would only make him seem guilty. Yang Kai's eyes darted about as he tried to think of a way to brush the subject aside without making it obvious.

Then he saw a faint blush rise on Ji Yao's fair little face, making her look especially lovely. A faint, elegant fragrance also drifted around the tip of his nose and slipped into his nostrils, like an invisible little hand scratching at his heart.

What was going on? Yang Kai was a little stunned.

Why had she not dodged? He had accidentally leaned in so close that, logically, Ji Yao should have moved away, yet she clearly had no intention of avoiding him. Her eyes only flickered with a hint of panic as one small hand unconsciously twisted her snow-white sleeve, her head lowered in shy obedience.

Yang Kai's gaze slid over her fair cheeks, delicate earlobes, and slender neck. From this angle, he could even faintly glimpse a little scenery at her chest. Unfortunately, her clothes were fastened too tightly, so he could not really see anything clearly, though the darkness between the folds of cloth left endless room for imagination.

He swallowed, turned his head aside, and whistled as if nothing had happened, while his backside shifted over a bit, bringing him much closer to Ji Yao.

Hm? No reaction?

He shifted a little farther, then a little farther still...

Warmth came from beside him. Clearly, their bodies were already touching.

Even now, Ji Yao still did not react, nor did she show any intention of moving away. But the moment Yang Kai pressed against her, he distinctly felt her body tremble.

No way, right? Yang Kai cried out inwardly as a thought churned in his mind, impossible to suppress.

One hand braced on the edge of the bed, he silently reached the other around behind Ji Yao, slowly drew it in, and pulled her into his arms.

The whistling stopped. Yang Kai sat upright and proper, eyes fixed straight ahead, though he kept watching Ji Yao from the corner of his eye.

Ji Yao turned to look at him, her sharp gaze slicing across his face like a knife. Even with Yang Kai's thick skin, he felt his face burn.

But matters had already reached this point, so what else was there to say? His eyes steadied. With a face full of resolve, as if he would press forward even against thousands upon thousands, he turned to meet Ji Yao's gaze.

Their eyes met. Yang Kai's gaze was fiercely aggressive, like that of a hungry beast, while Ji Yao was the one who looked away.

So you are not that tough after all! Yang Kai laughed wildly in his heart, then lowered his head and leaned toward her.

Ji Yao's eyelids swiftly fell shut. Her body trembled nonstop in Yang Kai's arms, and when their lips met, she suddenly went rigid from head to toe. Yang Kai secretly found it amusing. This was not even the first time, so why was her reaction still so intense? She had seemed the same way a few days ago in Ice Heart Valley's Cultivation Methods Pavilion, and now she was still like this. She really had not made the slightest progress.

Savoring her sweet taste, Yang Kai soon parted her lips, seeking out her tongue and boldly teasing it with unrestrained abandon.

Ji Yao was terribly clumsy and did not cooperate in the slightest, simply letting Yang Kai do as he pleased. If not for the tremors running through her, Yang Kai might have thought he was holding a block of wood.

The hand around Ji Yao's waist slowly moved upward and settled on her delicate shoulder. Then, with a slight exertion of strength, he laid her flat on the bed. Yang Kai turned over on top of her, one hand holding her back while the other slipped in along her waist, utterly unrestrained.

Like thunder stirring earth and dry wood catching flame, the scorching atmosphere nearly set the room ablaze.

One had to strike while the iron was hot. Since things had already gone this far, Yang Kai naturally meant to see them through. He grabbed Ji Yao's belt with one hand and was just about to tug it free when, unexpectedly, Ji Yao reached back and caught his wrist. Her eyes, which had been tightly shut all this time, suddenly opened, filled with pleading.

This again?

Yang Kai was rather speechless. It had been the same last time in the Cultivation Methods Pavilion. Kissing seemed to be allowed, but once things went any further, Ji Yao was not quite willing.

Yet Yang Kai could clearly feel that she had already been moved by passion. Her cheeks were flushed so red they seemed about to drip blood, her heart pounded violently beneath her high chest, and her breathing was equally fierce.

Yang Kai tentatively tugged at the belt again, only to find Ji Yao gripping his wrist tightly. Helpless, he could only settle for the next best thing, his large hand sliding upward along the belt.

There was still resistance, but it was not nearly as firm as before.

How could Yang Kai care about that now? She had stirred up the fire but would not let him eat; fine, if she would not let him eat, she at least had to let him enjoy himself. He drew out his other hand, caught both her wrists, bent them up over her head and pinned them there, while his other hand directly covered the fullness before her chest.

A soft moan escaped Ji Yao's lips. Yang Kai leaned down again and sealed those cherry-red lips.

No one knew how much time had passed before the two on the bed had changed positions. Yang Kai lay below while Ji Yao sat astride him, her clothes disheveled, her hair mussed and hairpins askew. Where was there still the slightest trace of her pure and aloof image?

The two mounds before her chest kept changing shape under the ravaging of Yang Kai's large hands, yet she kissed Yang Kai's lips in total abandon. Unfortunately, she clearly had no technique to speak of, only touching him like a dragonfly skimming the water, leaving him both amused and irritated.

"Junior Sister Yao, I have a request!" Yang Kai suddenly said.

Ji Yao's movements abruptly stopped. Then she lightly pushed against his chest with both palms, and her whole body drifted backward, landing beside the bed.

Yang Kai said anxiously, "What is that supposed to mean?" Everything had been going perfectly well, so why had she run off the moment he spoke?

Ji Yao bit her red lip and shot him a faintly annoyed glare. "Scoundrel!" Then she turned, rushed out of the room, and shot straight into the sky beyond the railing, vanishing in the blink of an eye.

"How am I a scoundrel? I had not even said what the request was." Yang Kai chased to the railing, but where could he still see her?

Well, this was just great. Last time, when he went to Ice Heart Valley, he had taken advantage of her in the Cultivation Methods Pavilion; this time, she had come to Lingxiao Palace and taken advantage of him. Did she think that evened the score?

Who knew whether there would be another chance in the future? Yang Kai could not help feeling vexed that he had not hardened his heart today and dealt with her on the spot. The main reason was that his heart had softened the moment he saw that pleading look in her eyes.

With a long sigh, Yang Kai stepped out of the room, searched through the palace for a while, then went straight to a side room and pushed the door open.

Inside, Xue Yue was sitting in meditation. When she saw Yang Kai arrive, she could not help asking, "Husband, why are you here?"

Yang Kai did not answer. He walked up to her and swept her into his arms by the waist.

"Where are we going?" Xue Yue asked, wrapping her arms around his neck.

"To find Luo'er." Yang Kai laughed wickedly.
