## Chapter 3326 - Please Grant Me Guidance

If Li Wuyi had known Cang Mo had ulterior motives, how could he have passed on his experience and let him use it to harm Yang Kai?

Then again, he had no idea what kind of grudge Yang Kai and Cang Mo could possibly have. Their status and strength were worlds apart. One was an esteemed Quasi-Great Emperor from Star Court, while the other was a rising star at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. Barring an accident, two people like them might not cross paths for hundreds of years.

That was why Li Wuyi had held nothing back when Cang Mo came to ask for guidance back then. Now that Yang Kai brought it up, Li Wuyi felt rather embarrassed. He had not meant any harm, but in the end, he had still caused Yang Kai some trouble.

Fortunately, Yang Kai's foundation was far from ordinary. Had it been any other Second-Stage Emperor Realm cultivator, he would likely have fallen into Cang Mo's hands long ago. At that point, whether he lived or died would have depended on a single thought from Cang Mo.

"So that is how it was!" Yang Kai nodded lightly and smiled. "Senior need not blame yourself. Some people simply have sinister hearts. This matter has nothing to do with Senior."

Li Wuyi said sheepishly, "This Li does bear some responsibility, more or less. Fortunately, nothing happened..."

Yang Kai changed the subject. "Then what exactly was the method Senior taught him? With his attainment in the Power of Space, he was actually able to seal off this stretch of heaven and earth without anyone noticing. This Junior is truly curious about that!"

Li Wuyi said, "Space forms a complete whole, intact and flawless. Even if an external force damages it, the World Laws will repair and restore it. I think you should understand that."

Yang Kai nodded. "Naturally. This Junior has torn space apart countless times."

Li Wuyi smiled faintly. “Then when tearing through space, did you ever sense that the Space Barrier differed in strength from place to place?”

Yang Kai nodded repeatedly. "Exactly. Space is extremely firm in some places, while in others it is comparatively weak. Tearing space apart in those firm places takes twice the effort for half the result, but in the weaker places, it can be torn open with ease."

Li Wuyi said, "Correct. Space has both weak and firm places, and those weak points are the so-called Space Nodes."

"Space Nodes?" Yang Kai's brow rose.

Li Wuyi waved his hand, and the Laws of Space surged, enveloping an area of a hundred zhang around them. "From what you can sense, how many Space Nodes are there in this stretch of space?"

Yang Kai closed his eyes to investigate, then opened them a moment later. "Three. One thirty zhang from Senior's left hand, one fifteen zhang behind you, and one seventy zhang to your right."

"Exactly three. If you want to teleport away, you must use these three Space Nodes to shuttle through the Void. But what if I do this..." As Li Wuyi spoke, the Laws of Space shifted again, bright and imposing, as though something tangible were pouring into those three Space Nodes.

Yang Kai's expression changed. "Heaven and earth have been sealed!"

Li Wuyi smiled at him, withdrew his Divine Ability, and said, "Do you understand now?"

Yang Kai pondered for a while, then raised his head. "Senior means that as long as these Space Nodes are sealed by some method, heaven and earth can be cut off without a trace, preventing me from teleporting away?"

"Correct." Li Wuyi nodded. "If heaven and earth are a bottle, then these Space Nodes are the holes in that bottle. When you and I teleport, we must pass through these holes to leave it. But once the holes are plugged, we can only be trapped inside."

Yang Kai had heard the saying that heaven and earth were a bottle more than once, but Li Wuyi's explanation was plain and easy to understand.

In truth, Yang Kai had long since realized this point and had been using it all along. Only after Li Wuyi pointed it out, however, did he suddenly feel as if everything had become clear.

Still puzzled, he asked, "Senior has such attainment in the Laws of Space, so sealing Space Nodes is naturally no problem for you. But how was Cang Mo able to do it?"

With his attainment in the Power of Space, even if he could detect those Space Nodes, he should not have been able to seal them without anyone noticing, right?

Li Wuyi said, "Presumably, he borrowed the power of some artifact. Back then, I gave him that suggestion as well. From the look of it now, he must have found someone to refine a few things for him. As for what they were, there is no way to find them now. Your great battle with him already destroyed his seal. In truth, although this method can seal off heaven and earth, the seal can also be broken if the battle becomes too intense."

Yang Kai drew a soft breath. "But if he had possessed the strength to completely suppress me, he could have captured me at his leisure."

Cang Mo had calculated everything very well. The only thing he had failed to predict was Yang Kai's true strength. Had it been any other Second-Stage Emperor Realm cultivator, even one proficient in the Laws of Space, there was no way he could have escaped under those circumstances.

Unfortunately for Cang Mo, he had run into Yang Kai, who assumed a thirty-zhang Dragon Transformation body and joined forces with his Incarnation to fight him until heaven and earth shook.

Now that he understood what had happened, however, it would not be so easy for others to target him this way in the future. At the very least, he could take precautions in advance. Even if the Space Nodes were sealed again, he could think of a way to break through them before escaping.

In the pavilion, the sun set and the moon rose. Yang Kai and Li Wuyi sat facing one another and talked at length, while Jiu Feng kept them company off to the side, looking rather bored.

Their discussion lasted a full three days.

During those three days, Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying of Heavenly Wolf Valley naturally came over several times to have a look. After all, Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng had come as guests, and as hosts, they had to show proper hospitality. Who would have thought they would not even be able to see them?

Before they could even approach the pavilion, they felt space trembling in wave after wave, as if it might collapse at any moment. Looking over from afar, they saw Yang Kai and Li Wuyi smiling from time to time, or stretching out their hands to gesture at something.

Tang Sheng sighed inwardly. Yang Kai's future was indeed boundless. With only a Second-Stage Emperor Realm cultivation, he could actually sit and discuss the Dao with someone like Li Wuyi. Once he matured in the future, who would dare look down on him?

Heavenly Wolf Valley had truly missed a Heaven-sent opportunity. Tang Sheng sighed ruefully. After living for so many years, his judgment was still inferior to his disciple's.

Three days later, Yang Kai was glowing with vigor, and Li Wuyi's eyes shone with sharp light as well. Although they had been sitting here discussing the Laws of Space all this time, neither of them looked tired. On the contrary, both were in excellent spirits, and both had gained a great deal.

Yang Kai suddenly said, "Senior, this Junior has an unreasonable request."

Li Wuyi looked at him with a broad smile. "You want to fight me?"

Yang Kai was stunned. "Was it that obvious?"

Li Wuyi smiled faintly. "Your eyes are full of eagerness, and your fighting spirit is surging. How could I not sense it?"

Yang Kai nodded, then said solemnly, "I have never fought someone proficient in the Laws of Space before. Today, this boy will be bold and presumptuous. Senior, please grant me your guidance!"

Li Wuyi said, "What a coincidence. I was thinking the same thing."

The two exchanged a glance, then their figures flickered at the same time. In the next instant, they both left the pavilion and stood in midair a hundred zhang apart, as if they had agreed on it beforehand.

Yang Kai's face was solemn. "Senior's cultivation reaches the heavens. When this Junior strikes, I will not hold back. Please be careful, Senior."

Li Wuyi said, "Although I will not go all out, I will not suppress myself too much either. Do not lose too badly."

Yang Kai grinned and shouted, "Senior, please receive this move!"

As he spoke, he clapped his hands together, then pulled them apart, and an incomparably huge Moon Blade took shape. Yang Kai gave a fierce smile. "This is a Space Divine Ability this boy comprehended on his own, Moon Blade. Please appraise it, Senior!"

As his words fell, the pitch-black Moon Blade whistled toward Li Wuyi.

Li Wuyi's expression turned solemn. "Condensing the power of the Void and gathering it into the slash of a Moon Blade. Not bad, not bad." As he spoke, his expression was serious, but his bearing remained relaxed and carefree. Facing the Moon Blade, he showed no intention of dodging. Only when that enormous Moon Blade was about to reach him did he suddenly raise a hand and brush it aside.

As the Laws of Space surged, the pitch-black Moon Blade suddenly vanished into nothingness. Li Wuyi had forcibly smoothed away this Space Divine Ability with the Laws of Space. This was the first time a Moon Blade had ever been erased by someone in such a manner.

"Excellent methods, Senior!" Yang Kai praised, but his hands did not hesitate in the slightest. As his fingers flicked again and again, Moon Blades appeared in dense clusters, blotting out the sky and covering the earth as they attacked Li Wuyi, as if he meant to cut him down on the spot.

Li Wuyi laughed despite himself. "The same move has no effect on me." His palm slapped down from the sky, and the Moon Blades vanished without a trace before they could even get close. Suddenly, Li Wuyi's expression sharpened, and he raised his brow. "So there was a killing move hidden within."

Yang Kai chuckled, reached out, and clenched his hand toward him. "Exile!"

Where Li Wuyi stood, an enormous black hole immediately appeared, like an invisible fierce beast opening its great maw and swallowing him in one bite.

The black hole flickered once and disappeared. Li Wuyi vanished from this world along with it.

Jiu Feng said excitedly, "You killed him?"

Qiong Qi, who was watching from afar, glanced sideways at her, his cheek twitching nonstop. What was wrong with this woman? Was she happy that Li Wuyi had been killed? Were they not on the same side?

Yang Kai did not answer. Instead, his expression turned grave.

Li Wuyi's voice suddenly rang out between heaven and earth. "Boy, take a punch from me too!"

The moment he finished speaking, a fist appeared in front of Yang Kai out of thin air, without the slightest warning. Moreover, only a fist had appeared, with no arm and no body, making for an incredibly strange sight. But judging from its color and size, it was undoubtedly Li Wuyi's fist.

Yang Kai had guarded against every possibility, yet he still had not expected Li Wuyi to launch such a bizarre attack.

Caught off guard, he was already a little too late to dodge.

He could only urge the Laws of Space and shout, "Nothingness!"

In that instant, his figure became almost transparent as he exiled himself into the Void, trying to avoid the blow.

Li Wuyi's soft exclamation rang out, carrying a trace of surprise, as though he had not expected Yang Kai to avoid his fist by such a method at this critical moment.

However... that was all.

Ripples suddenly spread from the fist, and then it vanished without a trace.

In the next instant, Yang Kai reappeared not far away, his expression tense as if facing a great enemy. The moment he showed himself, he flashed away again and merged into the Void.

Only after this happened several times did Yang Kai finally steady himself. He patted his chest with a bitter smile and shook his head. "Senior's methods are astonishing. This Junior admits defeat."
