## Chapter 3217 — Blame Me?

On the lowest floor of the Cultivation Methods Pavilion, the elegantly dressed woman who had earlier addressed Ji Yao as Senior Aunt looked up anxiously. A moment ago, she seemed to have faintly heard raised voices.

The Cultivation Methods Pavilion had already been cleared out. Aside from her standing guard at the entrance, only Ji Yao and Yang Kai remained inside, so the argument had to have come from the two of them. She simply could not understand what could make them quarrel so loudly that even she had heard it.

Nothing had happened, had it? Palace Master Yang had done Ice Heart Valley a great kindness, and she had also heard that Senior Aunt Ji and Palace Master Yang were on quite good terms in private. Senior Aunt Ji had been missing for so many years, and it was Palace Master Yang who had brought her safely back. Since that was the case, how could they end up arguing?

She wanted to go and investigate, but she was afraid of stumbling onto something she should not see, so she hesitated and did not move.

Fortunately, the quarrel came and went quickly. In a flash, everything went quiet again, which eased her mind somewhat. She listened closely, but could not make out anything unusual, and in the end could only shake her head with a sigh, keep guarding the door, and silently pray that nothing would go wrong.

She did not know that something really had happened on the eighth floor of the Cultivation Methods Pavilion.

Ji Yao was an innocent, inexperienced woman. How could she be a match for an old hand at romance like Yang Kai?

He had hardly done anything yet. After he merely wrapped one arm around her waist and kissed her red lips for a while, Ji Yao went limp as mud, every trace of strength leaving her. If Yang Kai had not been holding her waist, she would likely have collapsed on the spot.

Her breathing came fast, her chest rose and fell sharply, and her cheeks burned like fire.

Yang Kai was no longer satisfied with light, fleeting tastes. He pried Ji Yao's teeth apart, caught her soft, fragrant little tongue, and savored it without restraint.

Ji Yao let out a soft cry and startled herself, never knowing she could make such a strange sound. That sound also jolted her awake from her daze. Her eyes flew wide, and strength surged from somewhere within her. She shoved Yang Kai away, twisted out of his embrace, and leaned against the wall, gulping for air like a fish that had leapt onto shore.

Yang Kai smacked his lips, lingering over the taste from moments ago, then grinned at her. "I told you something would happen!"

Ji Yao pressed a hand to her chest as though her heart might leap out if she did not hold it in place. The flush had yet to fade from her face, and her beautiful eyes still held a watery haze, but she gave a cold laugh. "Now what do you have to say for yourself?"

"Say what?" Yang Kai looked baffled.

"Fine gold and jade on the outside, rotten padding within. The moment a woman throws herself into your arms, you lose all self-control!" Ji Yao said bitterly.

"You blame me?" Yang Kai was speechless. Wasn't this your fault? If you hadn't acted like that, how would I have acted like this? I am still a man, after all.

Ji Yao said coldly, "The facts are right before us, and you still want to argue!"

Yang Kai lowered his eyes. A few strands of black hair fell over his forehead, hiding his expression, and he said faintly, "Junior Sister Yao, you offered yourself to the tiger just to prove you were right?"

Ji Yao said, "Yes!"

Yang Kai lifted his head and broke into a sinister grin. "Junior Sister Yao, you certainly have guts. Aren't you afraid of being eaten until not even your bones are left?" As he spoke, he pressed toward her step by step. She was already leaning weakly against the wall, and with Yang Kai closing in, she had nowhere to run. Her long eyelashes began trembling violently again, and she cried in alarm, "What are you trying to do!"

Yang Kai braced one hand against the wall and used the other to lift her white, jade-like chin, like a demon whose wickedness had been completely unleashed. He looked at her with a faint, dangerous smile. "What do you think I am trying to do?"

Before she could react, he bent down and kissed her again.

This time, he showed no gentle tenderness, instead breaking straight through layer after layer of resistance and taking what he wanted without restraint.

Ji Yao immediately found herself breathless, almost suffocating. Only then did she understand that men were not to be provoked lightly.

Since this was her second time experiencing such a thing, she at least fared a little better than before and could sense some of what was happening. Yet because of that, it became even harder for her to bear. Something seemed to be stirring restlessly inside her. Her blood rushed through her body many times faster than usual, and astonishing heat poured from her, as if it would burn her clothes to ash.

When a large hand slid up from her waist toward her chest, Ji Yao finally grabbed Yang Kai's wrist, opened her eyes, and shook her head in silent pleading.

Yang Kai had been watching her reaction the entire time. Seeing this, he smiled faintly and did not force the matter further.

After a long while, their lips parted. A glistening thread still connected them. Ji Yao stared at it in a daze, and her already crimson cheeks flushed even deeper with shame.

She ducked down, slipped out from beneath Yang Kai's arm, and staggered to the stairway. There, she stopped, turned back, and glared fiercely at him. "Absolutely! Absolutely do not tell Junior Sister!"

Then she hurried downstairs as if fleeing.

"Still acting so fierce!" Yang Kai watched her disappear and snorted with laughter. A lingering fragrance remained on his hand. He raised it to his nose and sniffed, his whole face dazed with infatuation.

Then again, he had only come here today to get some cultivation methods and Secret Techniques from Ice Heart Valley. How had things with Ji Yao developed to this point? Thinking back on what had just happened, it actually felt a little dreamlike.

But what was done was done. Thinking about it now would not help. He only wondered whether things would be awkward the next time he met Ji Yao.

What bothered Yang Kai was that she had told him not to tell Su Yan. He had originally thought that since Ji Yao had offered herself to the tiger, she would surely believe she had gained some leverage over him. He had not expected her to be even more afraid than he was of the matter being exposed. Yang Kai stroked his chin and pondered for a while, then shook his head. He truly had no idea what Ji Yao had been playing at today.

On the first floor of the Cultivation Methods Pavilion, the elegantly dressed woman hurried over when she heard movement. "Senior Aunt Ji."

Ji Yao stared straight ahead as if she had not heard. Her mind was still caught in the sudden turn of events from moments ago. What had happened had shaken her beyond imagination, and she was nowhere near as calm as she appeared.

The woman said, "This disciple heard some noise from above just now. Did Senior Aunt quarrel with Palace Master Yang?"

Ji Yao glanced at her and frowned. "You heard it?"

The woman said in alarm, "This disciple did not mean to. It was just... somewhat loud."

Ji Yao's eyes flickered. "What did you hear?"

The woman lowered her head. "I did not hear clearly."

Ji Yao did not know whether she was telling the truth, so she could only warn her, "Do not spread this around. Senior Brother and I merely had a small disagreement just now."

"Oh." The woman nodded, then asked curiously, "Senior Aunt, why is your face so red?"

Ji Yao said with an unnatural expression, "He angered me."

The woman had no reason to doubt her. Still, people who cultivated Ice Attribute methods were generally quite steady of mind, so she was truly curious what Senior Aunt Ji and Palace Master Yang had been arguing about to leave Senior Aunt so angry. That Palace Master Yang really was something. If even someone as good as Senior Aunt Ji had been angered, he had to be in the wrong.

On the eighth floor, there were not many jade slips. Yang Kai finished copying and sorting them after only a short while, then looked up toward the ninth floor. That place should contain Ice Heart Valley's secret inheritances, the ones not passed to outsiders.

The ninth floor had no defenses against him at all. If he wanted to enter, he could naturally do so with ease. But since Bing Yun had not given him permission, he of course would not trespass. In the end, he had come here to collect cultivation methods and Secret Techniques only to give the disciples more choices. He did not need anything too profound.

The harvest was quite good. He just did not know how things were going on Li Jiao's side.

Yang Kai went downstairs at a leisurely pace, and the woman immediately stepped forward to salute.

"You haven't left yet?" Yang Kai looked in astonishment at Ji Yao, whose back was turned to him. He had thought that after what had happened just now, she would avoid him, but to his surprise, she was actually waiting here.

Ji Yao turned back and said, "Why should I leave? I haven't done anything shameful."

You can actually say that? Yang Kai's eyes widened. Sneaking around behind her own Junior Sister's back with her Junior Sister's man, and that still was not shameful? But since he was involved too, Yang Kai naturally could not say anything. He only nodded and said, "The matter here is finished, so I should head back."

"I'll see you off."

Yang Kai did not refuse. He did not know whether it was his imagination, but as they walked, he kept feeling that she was deliberately keeping a little distance from him. He made a few attempts at conversation, but received no real response, which left him rather bored.

He very much wanted to ask Ji Yao: What exactly was that just now? After what happened between us, why are you still wearing that icy face, as if we have some deep hatred between us?

Could she be angry? The thought made Yang Kai's heart sink. It really was possible. He had indeed gotten a little carried away just now and gone too far. It would be only natural for Ji Yao to be angry.

But one hand alone could not clap. If you were unwilling, couldn't you have refused from the start? Why was her resistance so weak that Yang Kai thought she had tacitly consented, leading him to push his luck?

This was awkward. He truly had no idea how they were supposed to face each other in the future.

Very soon, they reached the forbidden ground on Ice Island. Yang Kai stepped onto the Space Array and glanced at Ji Yao, only to find her standing there quietly, her expression perfectly normal.

Yang Kai sighed inwardly, cupped his fists, and said, "I will have to trouble Junior Sister Yao to look after Su Yan."

Ji Yao replied faintly, "Rest assured. She will not suffer the slightest grievance here."

Yang Kai nodded and activated the Space Array. Light flashed, and he vanished.

After returning to Lingxiao Palace, Yang Kai summoned Hua Qingsi, handed her everything he had obtained on this trip, and had her place it in the Cultivation Methods Pavilion so the disciples could choose from it for their cultivation.

After Hua Qingsi accepted it, she reported, "Palace Master Li came by earlier and delivered many cultivation methods as well. I have already thanked him, but he seems to have some matter to discuss with you."

"Where is he?"

"In the Discussion Hall."

"I'll go take a look."

As he spoke, Yang Kai flew off. In the Discussion Hall, Li Jiao sat upright, with Lu Sanniang beside him, keeping him company. The two were whispering together, laughing and smiling from time to time.

They suddenly sensed someone pass the entrance. Looking up, both of them brightened and rose to salute.

"Brother Li, you were looking for me?" They were no longer outsiders. After they had shared life and death, honor and disgrace several times, Yang Kai had gradually changed his opinion of Li Jiao, so there was no need for formalities.

Li Jiao smiled and said, "This Li would like my Li Dragon Palace and Brother Yang's Lingxiao Palace to form an alliance, so that from now on we may watch over and support each other. What does Brother Yang think?"
