## Chapter 3208 — Take a Look at These People

Such a lineup surpassed even the foundations of the Star Realm's top Sects. After all, powers like Li Dragon Palace, Ice Heart Valley, and Qingyang Shrine only had a single Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master presiding over them.

Light flickered atop the Space Array, startling Bian Yuqing, who was meditating.

She looked up, thinking Hua Qingsi had returned from the Southern Territory, and quickly rose to greet her.

Before she could get close, Yang Kai had already appeared. Bian Yuqing's expression turned solemn, and she quickly cupped her fists and said, "Palace Master has returned."

The few disciples standing guard nearby also hurried over to pay their respects, all of them deferential. Speaking of which, more than ten years had passed since Yang Kai last left the Northern Territory for Dragon Isle, and in all that time, there had not been the slightest word from him. If Zhu Qing had not made a special trip over to tell everyone what had happened, they would probably have thought he had met with some accident.

However, Zhu Qing had not said that he had gone to a Lower Plane Star Domain. She had only said that he was cultivating in retreat and might take some years to emerge.

Now, as soon as Bian Yuqing sensed the fluctuations coming from Yang Kai, she realized they came from someone at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm and was shaken to the core. In barely a dozen years, he had actually broken through to the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. What kind of freakish cultivation speed was that? Thinking back, when she first met him, he had only been at the Second Stage of the Void King Realm. Compared to him, she could not help feeling ashamed of her own inadequacy.

"Steward Bian." Yang Kai grinned. Meeting an old acquaintance again felt especially warm. Although Hengluo Star Domain was his homeland, Lingxiao Palace was the foundation he now stood upon. After ten years of twists and turns, he had finally returned, so he was in quite a good mood. "Are you on duty today?"

"Yes!" Bian Yuqing answered honestly.

"Mm, nothing has happened in the palace these past few years, right?"

"Everything is well. Palace Master, please rest assured."

Lingxiao Palace's foundation spoke for itself, and the news that the three great Yao Kings were stationed here was no secret either. In the entire Northern Territory, apart from Sublime Pill Great Emperor's Medicine Pill Valley, who could compare? Naturally, no blind fool dared come looking for trouble, so the past dozen years had been calm and uneventful.

"Only, the people from Li Dragon Palace have come several times to ask about Palace Master Li Jiao's whereabouts."

"Mm, no need to bother with them." Yang Kai waved his hand. "Have Big Sister Hua come see me."

Bian Yuqing said, "Manager Hua is not in the palace right now."

"Where did Big Sister Hua go?" Yang Kai was stunned.

"It is the end of the year now. Steward Hua went to the Southern Territory half a month ago, likely to conduct some transactions with Ziyuan Commerce Guild. Judging by the timing, she should return in the next couple of days."

Years ago, Yang Kai had reached an agreement with Ziyuan Commerce Guild. The two great territories were far apart and produced different goods, making them perfect trading partners. Over the years, Hua Qingsi had maintained that relationship, bringing Lingxiao Palace a considerable income.

"I see. Then have Nanmen Dajun and Chi Yue's group come over."

"Yes."

Yang Kai took a step, his figure melting into the void. When he reappeared, he was already above Lingxiao Palace. Looking down, he saw mountain ranges layered beneath him, shaded by lush trees and grass, the scenery beautiful and full of varied majesty. Peaks rose and fell at different heights, and clusters of buildings sat among the mountains, though most of them were empty. These buildings were basically the property left behind by Wenqing Sect back then, but Lingxiao Palace had always had few people, and with no one to live in them, they had been left idle.

Yang Kai flew a circuit around all of Lingxiao Palace, quietly observing for a long time, and soon had a plan in mind.

Since he had returned, the first thing he needed to do was naturally release the people in the Small Xuan Realm. The sooner they entered this world, the sooner they could adapt to the Star Realm's World Laws.

After today, Lingxiao Palace's population should be able to break past one hundred thousand.

An eagle's cry rang out, and a figure sped toward him. Once it drew close, it transformed into a sturdy, hook-nosed man who cupped his fists and said, "Young Master Yang."

"Yingfei. It has been many years. I trust you have been well." Yang Kai smiled faintly.

Yingfei said, "Many thanks for Young Master Yang's concern. All is well."

As they spoke, Xi Lei and Xie Wuwei also hurried over. Clearly, all three had sensed Yang Kai's aura, but Yingfei was a little faster and had arrived first.

After they all paid their respects, Xi Lei asked curiously, "Young Master Yang, what are you looking at?"

"Looking to see how many people my Lingxiao Palace can accommodate."

Xi Lei laughed, "We have few people and plenty of land here. There are over a hundred Spirit Peaks alone, so housing a hundred or two hundred thousand people shouldn't be a problem. What, is Young Master Yang preparing to throw open the palace gates and accept disciples on a grand scale?"

"There is no need for that." Yang Kai shook his head, then said casually, "Right, I just returned from the Ancient Land."

The three great Yao Kings all turned solemn and exchanged glances. Yingfei asked cautiously, "Did the Saint Venerables... say anything?"

"I met them briefly, then they drove me out. They weren't very welcoming." Yang Kai grinned, and when he recalled Fan Wu and the others' expressions, he still found it somewhat amusing.

"Surely not..." Yingfei was speechless. Although he could guess why the three Saint Venerables did not welcome Yang Kai, he could hardly say it outright.

Xi Lei hesitated. "Did the Saint Venerables mention us?"

As soon as he said this, Yingfei and Xie Wuwei both looked toward Yang Kai, their nerves stretched taut. No matter what, they were still Yao Kings of the Ancient Land and took orders from the three great Saint Venerables. If the Saint Venerables ordered them back to the Ancient Land, they would have no way to refuse. Otherwise, it would be betrayal.

But they were definitely unwilling to just go back like that. They had stayed by Yang Kai's side for so many years and still had not found the opportunity they wanted. Leaving now would be far too great a loss.

"Not at all." Yang Kai shook his head. "It looks like from now on, you are my people."

The three exchanged a glance, cupped their fists together, and said in solemn voices, "We are willing to render the service of dogs and horses!" Their eyes shone with excitement. It was naturally best that the Saint Venerables did not summon them back, and they were happy to remain by Yang Kai's side and serve him. Although life in Lingxiao Palace was somewhat quiet and there was not much to do, this was the best way to build a connection with the descendant of Heaven's Punisher.

"Work hard, and I won't treat you unfairly in the future." Yang Kai said it casually, but both speaker and listeners understood the meaning behind his words, making the three great Yao Kings even more excited.

"Let's head back." Yang Kai turned and flew downward. Before long, he landed in front of a huge square. Nanmen Dajun and Bian Yuqing were waiting there, and when Nanmen Dajun saw Yang Kai arrive, he hurried forward to greet him. "Greetings, Palace Master!"

"Boy, you finally came back." Chi Yue, Ghost Ancestor, Gu Cangyun, and Ai Ou also came forward to greet him.

Yang Kai cupped his fists in salute. No matter how high his cultivation was now, these few could all be considered his seniors, especially Chi Yue and Ai Ou. One was his mother-in-law and the other his father-in-law, so how could he not show respect?

"Boy, where did you run off to enjoy yourself this time? I heard you even subdued a member of the Dragon Clan?" Ai Ou looked at Yang Kai in obvious displeasure, his tone dripping with sarcasm.

This brat was simply outrageous. Back in the Star Domain, he had already had four beauties, and after coming to the Star Realm, he still went around flirting and stirring up romantic trouble. He had even provoked a member of the Dragon Clan and formed an extremely intimate relationship with her. Naturally, Ai Ou had some complaints. He did not dare act too presumptuously toward the Dragon Clan, but Yang Kai was different. Who cares how high your cultivation is? If you wrong my daughter, I will not let you off lightly.

And it was not just the Dragon Clan member. Ai Ou had also heard that there was a woman in Ice Heart Valley who seemed to have some entanglement with Yang Kai as well. It was almost beyond what he could endure. If not for his lack of strength, he would have given Yang Kai a harsh lesson long ago.

"It was an accident, an accident." Sweat broke out on Yang Kai's forehead. He had only mentioned the matter with Zhu Qing to Su Yan, and he did not know whether Su Yan had told Luo'er, Xue Yue, and the others. In any case, Yang Kai had never heard them ask about it.

"An accident?" Ai Ou huffed angrily. "How can you face those girls? They are still in the Star Domain, and we do not even know how they are doing. Boy, if you have the leisure to go flirting around, you would be better off finding a way back to see them and bring them over too. With your current ability, you should be able to do that, right?"

"I was just about to speak with you about that matter." Yang Kai smoothly followed his lead.

"What matter?" Chi Yue looked at him suspiciously.

“Everyone, take a look and see who these are.” Yang Kai smiled faintly, raised his hand, and swept it forward. Communicating with the Xuan Realm Pearl through his mind, he caused one figure after another to appear where his large hand passed.

Su Yan, Xia Ningshang, Shan Qingluo, and Xue Yue appeared together. The four women had clearly been summoned without warning and were still somewhat dazed, not knowing what had happened. Yet standing there side by side, they were like spring orchids and autumn chrysanthemums, each beautiful in her own way, all of them peerlessly magnificent.

Nanmen Dajun and Bian Yuqing clicked their tongues in wonder. They looked over the four women, and both of them brightened at the sight.

Although Su Yan and the others’ cultivations were not worth much in their eyes, each of these four women was a peerless beauty. If an ordinary man could obtain even one of them, it would be a blessing cultivated over several lifetimes. Yet Yang Kai had taken all four for himself and enjoyed the blessings of many beauties at once, leaving the two with only one thought: as expected of Yang Kai.

The three great Yao Kings, however, were not focused on Su Yan and the others' looks. Although there was no denying that these four women were stunningly beautiful even to the yao race, the three of them sensed an extraordinary aura from two of them.

Especially the woman in front, the one with the cold expression. The aura around her actually made even them feel the urge to bow in worship. It was somewhat similar to Luan Feng's, yet even more splendid and noble. She stood there, yet seemed to rise above the clouds, soaring through the Nine Heavens and looking down upon the world.

The other charming woman was slightly inferior, but her aura was likewise not something an ordinary person could possess.

Divine Spirits! The three great Yao Kings exchanged a glance, their eyes full of shock. They were yao and were extremely sensitive to the aura of Divine Spirits, so they saw at a glance that Su Yan and Shan Qingluo both possessed Divine Spirit Origins. It was just that their strength was still rather low. If they were allowed to grow, the three of them feared they would be no match for them.

"Luo'er?" Chi Yue was stunned. She stared at Shan Qingluo in a daze, almost unable to believe her own eyes.

A moment ago, Ai Ou had still been telling Yang Kai to find a way to make a trip to the Star Domain and bring Shan Qingluo and the others over. Who would have thought that in the blink of an eye, Shan Qingluo would truly appear right before her?
