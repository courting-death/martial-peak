## Chapter 3340 — Don't Be Too Shocked

A whetted blade made for better work. Purple Soul Grass greatly benefited the growth of Dragon Blood Flowers, so the two Wood Spirits planned to cultivate the Purple Soul Grass first and use it as fertile ground for the Dragon Blood Flowers they would later transplant.

Cultivating Purple Soul Grass was simple enough. Although there were no seeds left, the medicine garden already had living Purple Soul Grass. Once Mu Zhu and Mu Na used their Secret Techniques to divide and cultivate it, all of Dragon Blood Island would soon be covered in Purple Soul Grass.

The time Mu Zhu gave was one month.

The two Wood Spirits kept busy, while Yang Kai found himself with nothing to do. Fortunately, Zhu Qing came over and kept him company for a few days, so he was not quite so bored.

Zhu Qing was rather surprised after meeting the Wood Spirits as well. She had never expected such a peculiar race to be following Yang Kai. Knowing the Wood Spirits' special abilities, she was now filled with confidence that Yang Kai could satisfy the Second Elder's demand.

Zhu Qing was not the only one to visit during this time. Fu Qi and Zhu Lie also came by several times, clearly wanting to check on the progress, most likely on the Great Elder's instructions.

Without exception, Yang Kai stopped them outside the island. The two Wood Spirits were busy, and he did not want to expose what was happening inside Dragon Blood Island. If the Dragon Clan grew greedy for the two Wood Spirits and demanded that he leave Mu Zhu and Mu Na behind, everything would be finished.

Fu Qi and Zhu Lie could only feel helpless. Though they ran over every few days, they had no idea what was happening inside Dragon Blood Island.

After more than half a month of this, Yang Kai grew annoyed and found an opportunity to give Fu Qi and Zhu Lie a beating.

After that, Fu Qi and Zhu Lie no longer showed themselves. Helpless, the Great Elder could only make indirect inquiries through Zhu Qing. On the whole of Dragon Isle, Zhu Qing was now the only one who could get Yang Kai to tell the truth.

When the Great Elder asked, Zhu Qing could not very well refuse to answer. Fortunately, Yang Kai had already instructed her, so she only told the Great Elder that everything would be clear after half a year and that he would certainly not disappoint the Dragon Clan.

Half a year... That was brief indeed, especially for the long-lived Dragon Clan. To them, it was no more than the snap of a finger, so they could afford to wait.

Dragon Blood Island changed day by day. Sure enough, just as Mu Zhu had said, the Purple Soul Grass was ready after one month.

More than half of Dragon Blood Island was covered in tiny, glittering purple grasses, a beautiful sight from afar. Wherever these little grasses grew was where the Dragon Blood Flowers would be transplanted.

Once the preliminary work was complete, Yang Kai followed the two Wood Spirits' instructions and brought the Dragon Blood Flowers out of the medicine garden.

Each time he took one out, Mu Zhu and Mu Na carefully transplanted it onto Dragon Blood Island. As they worked, the two Wood Spirits were meticulous, their expressions as devout as pilgrims.

Two days later, all the Middle-Level Dragon Blood Flowers had been transplanted. A few days after that, some unranked Dragon Blood Flowers were transplanted as well.

Yang Kai had also prepared his own Golden Blood in advance. Following Mu Zhu's instructions, he drew a large jar of it and handed it over for them to use.

His bloodline showed no signs of regressing, much to Yang Kai's relief.

All that remained was to wait. With the two Wood Spirits tending to everything, Yang Kai had no need to interfere much. He found a spot, built himself a wooden hut, and spent his days inside meditating and cultivating.

Only occasionally would he go out for a stroll and check on the Dragon Blood Flowers' growth. From time to time, either Mu Zhu or Mu Na would also come over to ask Yang Kai for more Golden Blood.

The days passed peacefully. Zhu Qing, however, spent a great deal of time here, coming over to accompany him whenever she had nothing else to do.

Half a year later, by the edge of Dragon Blood Island, Yang Kai stood with his hands clasped behind his back, treading the void and riding the wind. He gazed bleakly toward the horizon, striking the pose of a man peerless beneath the Heavens, lonely as falling snow.

At the edge of the sky, streaks of light suddenly appeared, red, white, green, azure, and more besides, racing swiftly toward him.

Vast, ocean-like Dragon Pressure spread outward in wave after wave, stirring the blue waters into a thousand surging breakers against the shore.

As the light flashed, five figures appeared in front of Yang Kai.

Along with Zhu Qing came the Great Elder Zhu Yan, Second Elder Fu Zhun, Third Elder Fu Xuan, and Fourth Elder Zhu Kong. All four Elders of the Dragon Clan had arrived together with Zhu Qing.

As soon as he appeared, Zhu Yan hurriedly asked, “Boy Yang, Qing’er said the matter on your side is already finished. Is it true?”

When Zhu Qing had gone to inform him earlier, he had hardly dared believe it. Although Yang Kai had long since relayed the half-year deadline through Zhu Qing, now that the moment had arrived, Zhu Yan still could not help feeling anxious, afraid that Yang Kai had been making a fool of him.

After all, Yang Kai had stolen a great many Dragon Blood Flowers back then, and by his own admission, he had eaten every Dragon Blood Flower at Middle-Level and above. How could he possibly make up for that in such a short time?

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Whether it is true or not, Great Elder need only wait and see." As he spoke, Yang Kai gave Fu Xuan and Zhu Kong a slight nod in greeting. As for Fu Zhun... Yang Kai could not be bothered with her!

Fu Zhun's expression was icy, but she showed little reaction. It would have been strange if Yang Kai had shown her any goodwill. This attitude was only normal.

Zhu Yan said solemnly, "Boy, do not make empty boasts. You know how important Dragon Blood Flowers are to my Dragon Isle. If you dare run your mouth about this matter, this old man will absolutely not spare you."

Yang Kai sneered. "Aren't they just a few trifling Dragon Blood Flowers? You make them sound like priceless treasures."

A few trifling Dragon Blood Flowers... Zhu Yan's face darkened at that. They were one of the Dragon Clan's foundations. How were they trifling?

Zhu Qing said, "Great Elder, words are no proof. Seeing is believing. You should look for yourself."

Zhu Yan nodded repeatedly. "Good, good."

Fu Zhun had already flickered away, shooting toward the interior of Dragon Blood Island. Her icy voice drifted back from afar. "I would like to see what tricks you are playing!"

Yang Kai laughed mockingly. "Slow down. No one is going to snatch it from you, so what's the hurry?"

Fu Zhun paused, turned back to glare fiercely at Yang Kai, then continued flying onward.

Zhu Yan could not be bothered to waste any more words on Yang Kai either. Unable to wait, he followed after her to see what things were like inside. Fu Xuan and Zhu Kong likewise excused themselves and followed close behind.

Zhu Qing watched their backs and said, "I hope they won't be too shocked."

Over the past half year, Zhu Qing had watched Dragon Blood Island change day by day and witnessed the Wood Spirit Race's miraculous methods with her own eyes. Otherwise, she feared she would not have known how to react upon seeing Dragon Blood Island as it was now.

Shock and awe? Joy and excitement? She feared that any Dragon Clan member who saw Dragon Blood Island at this moment would be unable to suppress their emotions.

Yang Kai held out his hand to her. "Let's go and see what kind of faces they're making."

Zhu Qing smiled faintly and placed her hand in his, and the two flew off side by side.

A moment later, they arrived at the edge of Dragon Blood Island. Looking up, they saw the Dragon Clan's four Elders hovering in midair, lined up side by side, and then... nothing more.

The four of them looked as if someone had cast a body-binding spell on them. They stood motionless, gazing into the distance. Fu Xuan had even raised a hand to cover her red lips, an unusual light flashing in her beautiful eyes.

Zhu Yan also looked utterly shocked; Fu Zhun wavered between alarm and disbelief, and Zhu Kong blinked. "Am I dreaming?"

If this were not a dream, how could such a scene be before their eyes?

Amid the purple grasses covering the mountains and plains, countless crimson Dragon Blood Flowers bloomed in brilliant competition, each brimming with vitality and arranged in neat rows.

Unranked, Lower-Level, Middle-Level, and even Upper-Level, every grade was here!

With a single glance, Zhu Kong saw no fewer than five Upper-Level Dragon Blood Flowers alone. There were thirty or forty Middle-Level ones as well, while the Lower-Level and unranked ones were impossible to count.

And this was only one area, a single corner of Dragon Blood Island. Judging the whole from this one spot, just how many Dragon Blood Flowers were on Dragon Blood Island now?

Fu Zhun clearly did not dare believe this was real either. After a moment's hesitation, she flickered forward, obviously wanting to see the state of the entire Dragon Blood Island. Perhaps... only this one area had Dragon Blood Flowers, while the other places had none at all.

She was not the only one with that thought. Zhu Yan, Zhu Kong, and Fu Xuan had clearly considered the same possibility, and they all scattered in different directions to carefully inspect the entire Dragon Blood Island.

Yang Kai sneered and turned to Zhu Qing. "They still don't believe me."

Zhu Qing said, "It isn't that they don't believe you. This is simply too hard to believe. Letting them see it with their own eyes is better than a thousand words from you."

Yang Kai nodded. "True enough."

The two waited where they were.

A short while later, Fu Zhun was the first to return. For the first time, she looked at Yang Kai in an extremely strange way, her eyes searching, as if she wanted to see into the depths of his heart and understand exactly how he had accomplished such a thing within half a year. She clearly wanted to ask something, but in the end she was too embarrassed to speak.

Yang Kai held his head high and his chest out, standing as straight as a sharp sword.

Light flashed, and the others returned one after another, all staring at Yang Kai in disbelief.

Yang Kai stood even straighter.

"Boy Yang..." Zhu Yan was the first to speak. Pointing below, he asked, "Those are Purple Soul Grass?"

Yang Kai turned to him. "Great Elder is truly experienced and knowledgeable. You actually recognize Purple Soul Grass. Impressive, impressive."

Zhu Yan could not help rolling his eyes. It was clearly praise, so why did it sound so awkward? But he had no time to argue and pressed on. "Where did you find Purple Soul Grass?"

Purple Soul Grass had gone extinct. Otherwise, the Dragon Clan would definitely have gathered it to cultivate Dragon Blood Flowers. After all, with this kind of Spirit Herb present, Dragon Blood Flowers would grow faster.

Who could have expected something long thought extinct to reappear in the world, and on such an enormous scale that it covered nearly all of Dragon Blood Island?

Leaving aside everything else, these Purple Soul Grasses alone were an enormous fortune for the Dragon Clan, a treasure that could not be traded for anything.

"Great Elder need not concern himself with where I found them. Since they've been planted here, they're my gift to the Dragon Clan."

Zhu Yan thought to himself, As if you have a choice. What, do you still think you can take them back? This old man would fight you to the death before letting that happen.

This was an unexpected joy. Zhu Yan said cheerfully, "Good, good, good. It is rare for you to show such sincerity. Then my Dragon Clan will gratefully accept them."

Yang Kai said in a strange, sarcastic tone, "What can I say? I am the Dragon Clan's son-in-law. If I have anything good, of course I won't hide it away." As he spoke, he kept his eyes fixed on Fu Zhun.

Fu Zhun looked away, acting as if she had not seen him.

Wretched bitch! Yang Kai cursed in his heart.
