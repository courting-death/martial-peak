## Chapter 3166 — All of You, Behave Yourselves

"You... who exactly are you!" Chi Huo forced the words out through clenched teeth.

He was burly, a full head taller than Yang Kai, yet now Yang Kai held him in one hand like a helpless chick. The sight made for a jarring contrast.

"Would my name mean anything to you?" Yang Kai sneered. Ignoring him, he silently urged the Star Refining Art.

Chi Huo suddenly understood. Earlier, when he had asked for Yang Kai's name and received no answer, he had assumed the boy was arrogant and unruly. Now it seemed Yang Kai had simply considered him unworthy of an answer.

Suddenly, unbearable pain swept through his body. Chi Huo shuddered violently, his eyes bulging wide. Clearly sensing something, he cried out in horror, "Don't!"

"Come out!" Yang Kai suddenly raised a hand and pressed it against Chi Huo's Dantian. His five fingers hooked into a claw, then he seized and drew inward.

A ball of scarlet light was immediately drawn out. Yang Kai flung Chi Huo aside with a backhand motion, then lowered his head to study the scarlet glow in his palm and nodded in satisfaction.

Off to the side, Chi Huo staggered to his feet in a sorry state, his face pale and his vitality badly damaged.

Yang Kai had not injured him directly, but after having his Stellar Origin so forcefully stolen, how could Chi Huo possibly be unharmed? He looked up, his eyes filled with grief.

His own Stellar Origin had been seized just like that? That was the Origin Power he had risked his life and spent so many years refining. It was only by relying on it that he had become a Star Lord and unified all of Fire Cloud Star.

Without that Origin, he was nothing more than an ordinary Third Stage Void King Realm cultivator, perhaps even a little weaker than most Third Stage cultivators. After all, severing his connection to the Origin had left him with hidden injuries.

Seeing this, the many guests could not help feeling a chill of dread. Cold washed over them in waves, and they froze in place, not daring to make the slightest reckless move.

Even a world-shaking figure like Fire Cloud Star's Star Lord could not withstand a single move from that youth. He had not only been subdued in an instant, but even his Origin had been forcibly taken away. How could any of them dare oppose Yang Kai now? Their eyes brimmed with awe and terror.

Under countless staring eyes, Yang Kai opened his mouth, pushed the scarlet light inside, and swallowed it whole.

In an instant, his skin turned blood red, as if it had been scorched by flames.

Yang Kai gave a muffled grunt, sank his Qi to his Dantian, and urged the Star Refining Art to suppress and refine it.

But he soon discovered something was wrong.

The Dark Star Origin Power inside him was actually clashing with Fire Cloud Star's Origin. The two seemed innately hostile, and Dark Star's Origin Power produced an unimaginable rejection toward Fire Cloud Star's Origin.

This was somewhat unexpected.

Before coming here, Yang Kai had also refined the Origins of several stars, but those Origins had all been very weak, so he had not felt anything. Only now did they react.

The World Laws of every Cultivation Star were different. Since differences existed, coexistence was naturally difficult.

Yang Kai quickly understood this point. Unflustered, he continued urging the Star Refining Art.

His next plan required far more than refining a single Stellar Origin. He intended to refine every Stellar Origin in the entire Star Domain. If he could not even overcome this small difficulty before him, how could that plan ever be carried out?

Splitting his focus in two, he soothed Dark Star's Origin Power while harmonizing and refining Fire Cloud Star's Origin. On the surface, the left and right sides of his body bloomed with different lights that attacked each other.

Star power ran rampant. In an instant, the Flame Palace collapsed, the towering hall reduced to ruins as the guests fled in every direction. Those who had come to offer congratulations were all powerful cultivators, so this small accident was not enough to claim their lives, but it left them even more shaken by the strength of Yang Kai's methods.

Even the aftershocks from the clash between those two Stellar Origins left them feeling almost unable to withstand it. It was hard to imagine how Yang Kai, standing at the very center of the conflict, could remain unharmed.

Yang Kai was not unharmed at all. He felt as if two unruly children were fighting inside him, battling for control of his body. He soothed one side and harmonized the other, but it had little effect. Instead, his Qi and blood churned, his face flushed, his veins bulged, and he felt wretched beyond words. Aside from those two children, several infants were also joining the commotion. Those infants were undoubtedly the Stellar Origins he had refined in recent days. Compared with the Origin Power of Dark Star and Fire Cloud Star, these Origins were incomplete and not particularly strong. They had been behaving themselves at first, but once stirred up by Fire Cloud Star's Origin, they too became restless.

Soothe, reconcile, soothe, reconcile...

Soothe and reconcile your grandmother's leg!

"All. Of. You. Behave. Yourselves!" Yang Kai roared, each word bitten off sharply, and fiercely urged the Star Refining Art, madly suppressing those Stellar Origins.

The whole world fell quiet at once.

Yang Kai's aura gradually steadied, and the Origin Power that had been clashing endlessly inside him slowly calmed as well.

Misbehaving children really did need discipline!

Still, he knew this was only a temporary measure. There was no problem for the moment, but as he refined more and more Stellar Origins in the future, this kind of conflict would only intensify. If he could not think of a foolproof solution by then, it might plant a hidden danger.

Future problems could wait for the future.

In his Sea of Consciousness, within the Star Chart, the point of light representing Fire Cloud Star flickered. At the same time, Yang Kai formed an inseverable connection with Fire Cloud Star itself.

With a single thought, all of Fire Cloud Star came under his control. Heaven and Earth trembled, clouds churned, as if announcing to the world that the Star Lord had changed.

The guests who had come to congratulate Chi Huo hurried forward and saluted respectfully. "Greetings, Sir!"

Even putting aside the terrifying strength Yang Kai had displayed earlier, his current status as Fire Cloud Star's Star Lord alone forced them to lower their heads. From now on, their lives would depend on his favor. How could they dare be careless?

"Greetings... Sir!" Chi Huo also lowered his head. In that instant, he seemed to have aged several hundred years, his expression bleak.

He, too, knew how to read the situation. Earlier, he had dared confront Yang Kai because he believed he had something to rely on. Reality had proved that belief an illusion. Now that he was at another man's mercy, how could he not bow his head? Even if hatred filled his heart, he absolutely did not dare think of revenge.

At his peak, he had not been able to withstand even one move, much less now.

Yang Kai gave Chi Huo a meaningful look, and Chi Huo trembled all over, afraid Yang Kai would wipe him out completely.

Fortunately, Yang Kai had no such intention. Looking around, he said, "There is something I need your help with."

The goat-bearded old man who had previously scorned Yang Kai hurriedly said, "Whatever instructions Sir has, please speak freely. This old man will see them done properly."

"Indeed, indeed. Please command us, Sir."

Yang Kai said, "Chi Huo sealed off this star. I expect you still don't know what has happened in the Star Domain over the past few years, do you?"

The goat-bearded old man hesitated. "May I ask what Sir is referring to..."

"The Great Wilderness Star Domain invaded more than ten years ago, seizing my Star Domain's Cultivation Stars and slaughtering my Star Domain's cultivators. Their wolfish ambition is unforgivable!"

"Ah?" The group paled in shock. Although they had heard Yang Kai mention this matter before, no one had taken it seriously. Now that they heard it again, they had no choice but to do so.

There was actually another Star Domain? And it had invaded? Such a thing was simply inconceivable.

"Fortunately, the situation is no longer so dire. The cultivators of my Star Domain are counterattacking. This Young Master wants you to go support them and slaughter the Great Wilderness Star Domain cultivators to the last!"

The goat-bearded old man said, "May I ask... where should we go to offer support?" They had not even heard of the Great Wilderness Star Domain, so naturally they did not know where to go.

"Find out yourselves!" Yang Kai had been traveling alone for two years and did not know where the flames of war had spread by now, but if they truly tried, how could they fail to find news? Although Chi Huo had sealed off Fire Cloud Star, that was precisely why its strength had been preserved relatively intact. If these people went to assist, they would make a decent supporting force.

"Yes, yes, yes." The goat-beard answered at once, inwardly sighing that such an expert was indeed unpredictable.

"Stop dawdling. All of you, set out at once." Yang Kai waved his hand, and a gale swept out as if swatting away flies. The group of Void King Realm cultivators lost their footing and were all blown away.

In an instant, he alone remained atop the ruins of the Flame Palace.

He found a place and sat down cross-legged.

He sank his Divine Sense into his Sea of Consciousness and observed the Star Chart.

In one area, several points of light flickered. They corresponded to the Cultivation Stars he had refined recently, and the brightest among them was Fire Cloud Star.

These points of light were not arranged at random. If lines were drawn between them, they would form an irregular polygon across the vast Star Chart.

Yang Kai's mind stirred slightly. From tens of millions of li away, he urged the Origin Power of those stars. All of a sudden, on the Star Chart, that invisible irregular polygon began glowing from its edges, layer upon layer of soft light spreading toward the center.

Wherever the light passed, the Starry Sky took on the auras of those stars and was slowly refined by Yang Kai.

Yang Kai clearly felt that stretch of Starry Sky take on his aura.

Delighted, he knew his earlier guess had been correct and immediately pressed on.

In only three days, Yang Kai finished refining it. When he looked again, the irregular polygon outlined by those points of light on the Star Chart was already connected to his mind.

Such an irregular polygon might be insignificant on the Star Chart, but the corresponding stretch of Starry Sky was extremely vast. If Yang Kai had refined it using his previous method, it would have taken at least half a year.

But refining it this way had taken Yang Kai only a month from start to finish.

He only needed to refine the Origins of several Cultivation Stars first, then use those Cultivation Stars as a foundation to spread outward in every direction. With half the effort, he could achieve twice the result, saving a great deal of time and energy.

The Star Domain was too vast. He had spent two years refining it, yet the territory he controlled still amounted to less than one twentieth of the whole. In other words, at his previous pace, thoroughly refining the entire Star Domain would take at least forty or fifty years.

Forty or fifty years was far too long! How could he possibly spend that much time lingering in the Star Domain?

Now that he had this method, his efficiency would certainly increase a great deal. With the auras he had left in those many Asteroid Seas as well, it could rise a little further.

Ten years. Within ten years, he would refine the entire Star Domain and become its Master!
