## Chapter 3282 — Sheng Yuzhu

To be honest, after finding such a woman in the Void Gap, Yang Kai had no idea what to do with her, since he did not know whether she was good or evil.

If she was some monstrous villain, saving her life would be no different from helping evil flourish. But if she had only stumbled into this place by accident and become trapped, standing by and doing nothing would be far too cruel.

He was almost certainly the last chance the woman before him would ever have. If he simply walked away, she would die beyond all doubt.

So, whatever the case, Yang Kai decided to let her recover a bit of vitality first. Once he had found out more about her origins and character, it would not be too late to decide what to do.

He stepped forward and offered her the Spirit Pill in his hand. The woman, little more than skin stretched over bones, stared at it hungrily and struggled to take it, but the pitiful thing seemed to lack even the strength to open her mouth. All she could do was raise her eyes and look pleadingly at Yang Kai.

Yang Kai did not know whether to laugh or sigh. He suddenly felt he had been a little too cautious. Even if this woman really had been a heinous villain in the past, in her current state, she would likely need several decades, perhaps even a century, before she could recover.

Left with no choice, he reached out, pinched her mouth open, placed the Spirit Pill inside, and circulated his Emperor Yuan to help her swallow it.

Gratitude flickered in the woman's eyes, and she closed them to regulate her breathing.

Yang Kai did not stay idle. He pressed one hand to her shoulder and, without asking for permission, carefully poured his Emperor Yuan into her body to help her refine the pill's medicinal efficacy. This gave him a clear sense of her internal condition, and what he perceived left him inwardly alarmed.

He had no idea what cultivation this woman had possessed at her peak, but right now, her flesh and blood had dried up, her Meridians had nearly withered away, and blockages filled her body. Yang Kai had to focus completely and guide his Emperor Yuan with extreme care. The slightest mistake would be enough to make her body burst apart and kill her.

A single Void King-Grade Spirit Pill was naturally nowhere near enough to restore her fully. Even with Yang Kai helping her refine all its medicinal efficacy, it only steadied her wavering vitality. Her skeletal appearance did not change in the slightest, though at least a little color finally returned to her dead-fish eyes.

Yang Kai sighed inwardly again and again. It was said that even ants struggled to live, let alone people, but if someone truly had to be trapped in a place like this for thousands upon thousands of years, Yang Kai felt death would be better. At least death would end everything once and for all. Being trapped here was simply living torment.

Yet this woman had survived all the same. What kind of will and resilience did that require? Her cultivation of heart and mind had to be unimaginable for her to endure under such conditions.

Once the Void King-Grade Spirit Pill had been refined, the woman stared straight at Yang Kai again, her eyes full of longing. Clearly, she wanted him to give her more Spirit Pills.

Yang Kai pretended not to understand and stood there scratching his cheek.

When staring at him got her nowhere, the woman did not press the matter. She closed her eyes again, and after recovering for a while, she opened her mouth. A ragged syllable forced its way out, as if her voice were being torn loose. Clearly, it had been far too long since she last spoke. Even with a Spirit Pill sustaining her vitality, she still could not form a full sentence.

After trying for a long time with broken, wordless sounds, she finally managed to speak: "Many... thanks!"

They were only two simple words, yet they seemed to exhaust all her strength. After saying them, she fell silent again.

Yang Kai nodded and smiled. "It was no great effort. May I ask how Your Excellency should be addressed?"

The woman froze, and confusion surfaced in her eyes.

Watching her expression, Yang Kai thought, She can't have been trapped here so long that she has even forgotten her own name, can she?

But very soon, the woman answered with great effort, one word at a time: "Sheng... Yu... Zhu!"

A name was the mark of a person's entire life. Even if one forgot it for a moment, a little thought would always bring it back.

Yang Kai was startled. What a strange name. Thinking carefully, he could not recall anyone he had dealt with who bore that surname, nor had he ever heard the surname before.

"This junior is Yang Kai." Yang Kai cupped his fists. "I am currently a Guest Elder of Luocha Sect. I happened to discover some abnormalities in the forbidden area and followed the traces here. I never expected to meet Your Excellency. Were the abnormalities inside my Luocha Sect's forbidden area your doing?"

Sheng Yuzhu nodded, though Yang Kai could not tell whether she was sparing with words or simply found speech too difficult.

"As expected..." Yang Kai laughed despite himself. The strange phenomena he had seen in the forbidden area had already made him suspect that their source lay inside this space crack. Now that he had found Sheng Yuzhu here, he immediately concluded that the abnormalities had to be man-made.

Sheng Yuzhu was trapped here, so she had used some profound Divine Ability to leave clues through that hair-thin space crack. If anyone discovered those clues, they would naturally become curious about the Void Crack and eventually find her.

But with such inexplicable clues, under all Heaven, apart from Yang Kai, probably only Li Wuyi of Spirit Beast Island could have discovered them. Only the two of them were proficient in the Laws of Space and capable of sensing that crack's existence.

Otherwise, Luocha Sect's Sect Master, Yu Luocha, would not have remained unaware all this time, believing only that the phenomenon in the forbidden area was a marvel born of heaven and earth, a blessing bestowed upon Luocha Sect by the Heavens.

In other words, everything Yu Luocha had comprehended from that smooth cliff face had been deliberately left behind by Sheng Yuzhu. Looked at that way, Yu Luocha could be considered an unofficial disciple of Sheng Yuzhu, and everyone in Luocha Sect could be called Sheng Yuzhu's disciples and grand-disciples.

Judging from Sheng Yuzhu's current state, however, she clearly no longer had the ability to do such things. Yang Kai guessed that the method had been laid down more than a thousand years ago and had somehow maintained itself in an inconceivable state ever since, resulting in the changes that appeared every full-moon night.

"How long has Your Excellency been trapped here?" Yang Kai asked again. He was truly curious about this question.

Sheng Yuzhu slowly shook her head. "I do not... quite remember."

What a sorrowful answer. Hearing it, Yang Kai could not help wanting to shed a handful of bitter tears for her. She could not even remember how long she had been trapped, which meant it had obviously been a very, very long time, so long that she had lost all means of measuring it.

"Does Your Excellency understand the Power of Space?" Yang Kai suddenly thought of something else.

That thin space crack in the valley's forbidden area could not have appeared for no reason. It could only have been made by someone.

Sheng Yuzhu said, "Trapped... too long. I could only... think of a way myself." After a long pause, she continued with difficulty, "Only... a slight understanding."

Yang Kai nodded in deep agreement. That space crack was far too small, no thicker than a strand of hair. Yu Luocha had cultivated in the forbidden area for more than a thousand years without sensing it. If Yang Kai himself had not been proficient in the Laws of Space, he would never have discovered it either.

So it made sense for Sheng Yuzhu to say she had only a slight understanding. If she knew nothing about it, she could not have created that space crack at all. If she were truly proficient, she would likely have widened it long ago and escaped at her leisure.

Yang Kai estimated that her attainments in the Power of Space had only just crossed the threshold and were still far from touching the level of Laws.

"Young Brother..." Sheng Yuzhu suddenly called.

"What instructions does Your Excellency have?" Yang Kai looked at her.

Sheng Yuzhu said, "Since you... can come here, that means you can tear space. Can you take me... away?" After speaking for a while, she gradually became more fluent, and her hoarse voice slowly turned smooth and pleasant, though it still lacked strength. As she spoke, her eyes were full of longing. After waiting for so many years, someone had finally come before her, and he was clearly proficient in the Laws of Space. If she wanted to escape, she would have to place her hopes on Yang Kai. How could she let such a good chance slip by?

Hearing this, Yang Kai stroked his chin and said with a troubled expression, "But I still do not know whether Your Excellency is a good person or a bad person."

Sheng Yuzhu blinked. "If I say I am a good person, will you believe me?"

Yang Kai shook his head and said seriously, "Of course not."

Sheng Yuzhu said, "If I were a bad person, I would not have been schemed against back then, stranded here, and left without seeing the light of day."

Yang Kai shook his head. "Words alone prove nothing. If Your Excellency truly is an evildoer who stops at nothing, I absolutely cannot let you leave."

Sheng Yuzhu sighed. "How do you want me to prove whether I am good or evil? I have been trapped here for who knows how many years. Even if I had people I knew back then, I fear most of them are already dead. As for the ones still alive, you may not know them. Are you really going to go out and investigate?"

"That is not impossible." Yang Kai raised his brow. "Then who do you know? Mm, if you think they could not have lived until now, there is no need to mention them. Just pick a few long-lived ones and tell me."

Sheng Yuzhu laughed despite herself. "You really are cautious."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "I have no choice. Please forgive me."

Sheng Yuzhu thought for a moment, and the first name that came out of her mouth gave Yang Kai a fright. "Is Wu Kuang still alive?"

Yang Kai's eyes went wide, and his expression changed several times. "Wu Kuang? Which Wu Kuang?"

Sheng Yuzhu looked at him thoughtfully. "From your reaction, it seems you have heard the name. In that case, did he succeed?"

Cold sweat streamed down Yang Kai's forehead. "Which Wu Kuang is Your Excellency talking about?"

Sheng Yuzhu gave him a meaningful look. "No one else. The one you are thinking of. The one who cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art."

Yang Kai's face changed dramatically, and he demanded in a low shout, "Your Excellency knows Wu Kuang?"

Sheng Yuzhu chuckled. "Naturally. The fact that I ended up trapped here is entirely thanks to him."

"What?" Yang Kai felt as if his head could not keep up. This woman before him, little more than skin stretched over bones, had actually been trapped in this Void Gap because of Wu Kuang? Did that mean she was from the same era as Wu Kuang? For a moment, Yang Kai could not help breaking out in cold sweat.

Earlier, he had guessed that Sheng Yuzhu must have been trapped here for no short time, at least several thousand to ten thousand years. But if she was from the same era as Wu Kuang, what did several thousand to ten thousand years amount to? That was a full several tens of thousands of years.

Just thinking about it made one shudder. A person had been trapped in this sunless place for tens of thousands of years, yet not only had she not gone mad, she had kept trying to send information out and lure someone over to rescue her.

This Sheng Yuzhu was definitely an old monster of the highest order!

Suddenly, Yang Kai realized something was wrong and sneered. "Does Your Excellency think I am young and easy to fool?"

Sheng Yuzhu was astonished. "What do you mean?" She did not understand why Yang Kai suddenly seemed ready to turn hostile.

Yang Kai snorted. "That Wu Kuang was known as Heaven Devourer Great Emperor, the number one person throughout the ages, with cultivation that reached the Heavens. If he had truly acted against you, how could you still be alive? Is Your Excellency going to tell me that you are also a Great Emperor?"
