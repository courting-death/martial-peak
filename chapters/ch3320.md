## Chapter 3320 — I Want Half

With that, he dragged his injured body across the battlefield. Most of the Emperor Realm Masters were already dead, and the few who were not were barely clinging to life. Yang Kai did not care which was which. He smashed them all apart with a single palm strike, and Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng were no exception.

Fu Bo had first been suppressed by Yang Kai with the Mountain and River Bell, and then Yang Kai had activated the bell's power and struck him viciously, knocking him unconscious and leaving him gravely injured. If that had been all, it would not have been too serious.

The problem was that the heaven-shaking battle between Yang Kai's Incarnation and Cang Mo had swept across too wide an area. The unconscious Fu Bo had lain almost at the center of the battlefield, so naturally he could not escape. In particular, no one knew how many times he had been struck by the Nine Heavens Lightning Cang Mo had summoned. Unable to defend himself while unconscious, he had been blasted until he was half dead.

In a sense, Yang Kai was granting him release.

Xu Changfeng's condition was no better than Fu Bo's, and in fact was even worse. When he had fought the Incarnation one-on-one earlier, the Incarnation had beaten him until he could not fight back at all, then Yang Kai had ambushed him with a Moon Blade and immediately wounded him severely. After that, he had been caught up in the great battle. By the time Yang Kai found him, his breath was as faint as a thread. If not for the foundation of a Third-Stage Emperor, he would probably have died long ago.

Yang Kai naturally gave him a clean death as well.

Watching him busy himself over there, Li Wuyi sighed. "After today, the balance of power in the Eastern Territory will likely change."

More than thirty Emperor Realm Masters, including Fu Bo and Xu Changfeng, had died. The two top forces had been all but crippled. Over the years, Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land had enjoyed illustrious reputations and used their status as top forces to throw their weight around, but they had also made no small number of enemies. Their Sects' upper ranks had been wiped out in a single battle. Once the news spread, the various Sects and families previously suppressed by those two great forces would naturally rise up and take the chance to strike back.

If nothing else, Ahan Palace alone could not possibly remain unmoved. Two of the three great forces had fallen at once. In the entire Eastern Territory, aside from the overlord Soul Palace, Ahan Palace was now the foremost power.

Jiu Feng said, "This boy really is ruthless."

He had slaughtered so many Emperor Realm Masters all at once without even frowning. Clearly, his killing intent ran deep. Her little princess had developed feelings for such a man; who knew whether that would prove to be a blessing or a curse?

Li Wuyi smiled faintly. "On the road of the Martial Dao, who has not killed and fought their way forward? Either you kill, or you are killed. No one is exempt."

Jiu Feng slanted a glance at him. "You sound like that struck a nerve."

Li Wuyi fell silent, clearly remembering things from his youth. He might look gentle and refined now, but when he was young, his murderous nature had been just as overwhelming. Fortunately, Tang Sheng, Qian Xiuying, Red Ghost, and the others flew over at that moment.

The renowned Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng had come together, and as the host, Tang Sheng naturally could not pretend he had not seen them. No matter what, he had to come over and pay his respects.

They flew up and greeted the pair respectfully. Even someone as unruly as Red Ghost was perfectly deferential before Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng.

The two did not put on airs either, casually exchanging a few words with Tang Sheng and the others, leaving everyone feeling somewhat flattered.

A short while later, Yang Kai finally walked over, covered in blood and grime.

Lan He and Ling Yinqin immediately went to meet him. Two pairs of beautiful eyes turned his way, and Lan He asked nervously, "Are your injuries serious? Are you all right?"

Yang Kai grinned. "I'm fine. I won't die."

Ling Yinqin said, "How could you be so reckless?"

Yang Kai said, "When a blade is at your neck, all you can do is fight back."

Lan He's expression dimmed. "Brother Yang, earlier I..."

Yang Kai raised a hand to stop her. "Young Lady Lan, there is no need to mind it. I understand your position, and besides, you could not have helped in the earlier battle." It was not that Yang Kai looked down on her; it was simply that in a battle of that level, a First-Stage Emperor Realm Master truly could not help at all.

"Come back to my place and rest first. You should wash all this blood off properly, or you won't be fit to meet anyone," Ling Yinqin said, smoothing things over.

Just then, Tang Sheng also invited Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng to visit Heavenly Wolf Valley. After all, they were right on his doorstep; it would be improper not to invite them in.

Hearing this, Yang Kai looked at Li Wuyi and said, "I came to the Eastern Territory intending to visit Spirit Beast Island and seek Eldest Brother Li's advice on some cultivation matters. Since we happened to meet here, if Eldest Brother Li is not in a hurry, why not come with us?"

He truly did need to rest and recover, but he also did not want to miss this chance to speak with Li Wuyi, so he took the initiative to ask.

Hearing this, Lan He and Tang Sheng both secretly breathed a sigh of relief. Since Yang Kai could say such a thing, it meant he really had not taken Heavenly Wolf Valley's earlier attitude toward him too much to heart. Otherwise, he would not have been willing to return to Heavenly Wolf Valley to rest.

Li Wuyi pondered briefly, then said, "This Li came mainly because of him." As he spoke, he looked toward the gaudy old man. The old fellow had been standing there without moving, his eyes fixed on Yang Kai the whole time as though flowers had sprouted from Yang Kai's body.

Hearing Li Wuyi say this, the old man chuckled. "Li Wuyi, if you want this old man to return to Spirit Beast Island with you, you will first have to ask whether my Young Master agrees."

Yang Kai tilted his head and looked at him. "Is there something wrong with your head?" Even now, he was still calling him Young Master. What was this supposed to be?

The old man's expression turned solemn. "Young Master, this old man's mind is perfectly clear. How could there be something wrong with it?"

Yang Kai was so angry he laughed. "You want to acknowledge me as your Young Master? Fine. Go chase down Cang Mo right now, then bring his head back to me."

The old man's expression twitched, and he gave an apologetic smile. "Young Master, please do not make things difficult for this old man. That matter is truly..."

Yang Kai spread his hands toward Li Wuyi. "I don't know what is wrong with this old fellow. Eldest Brother Li, do whatever you want with him."

The gaudy old man immediately looked unbearably aggrieved. "Young Master, how can you be so heartless!"

Yang Kai said angrily, “You and I are complete strangers. Get the situation straight, will you?”

Li Wuyi smiled faintly. "Strictly speaking, Yang Kai, you should know him, and... perhaps there is a reason he calls you Young Master."

Yang Kai looked stunned. "What do you mean?" If only the gaudy old man had said so, that would have been one thing, but Li Wuyi was saying it too, which made it rather intriguing. Yang Kai was truly at a loss.

“If you want to know what it means, ask him yourself.” Li Wuyi waved his hand. “As for you, before this matter is confirmed, don’t even think about running away. You know full well that once I have my eye on you, even if you flee to the ends of the world, I can still find you.”

His tone was wildly confident. Since the gaudy old man could beat Cang Mo, his own strength was naturally extraordinary; at the very least, he was on Cang Mo's level, and he was even a venerable Divine Spirit. Yet faced with Li Wuyi's words, he had no rebuttal.

Because he knew that if Li Wuyi had targeted him, he truly could not escape.

He shook his head and said, "Since I have encountered Young Master, this old man naturally must follow at his side and offer what humble service I can. How could I possibly run? Li Wuyi, do not judge a noble heart by your own petty suspicions!"

"Best if that is so." Li Wuyi nodded lightly, noncommittal. "In that case, we will impose on Valley Lord Tang."

Tang Sheng was overjoyed. "Not at all, not at all. For several Lords to grace Heavenly Wolf Valley with your presence truly brings honor to our humble home. Please, come inside."

As he spoke, he extended a hand in invitation.

Li Wuyi and Jiu Feng took the lead as a matter of course.

Yang Kai glanced sideways at the gaudy old man, who immediately smiled. "Young Master, please."

Yang Kai acknowledged him with a sound and strode forward with his head held high.

As he passed Red Ghost, he suddenly reached out and patted Red Ghost on the shoulder. "I want half of everything in Netherworld Sect's and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land's Sect Treasure Vaults. I will go to your Ahan Palace to collect it later. Don't forget to inform your Hall Master."

Red Ghost stared at him wide-eyed. The words seemed to come out of nowhere, but he understood Yang Kai's meaning at once.

The two great Sects had already been crippled, their upper ranks wiped out. Even if they still had Emperor Realm Masters guarding their Sects, there definitely would not be many. In this state, the two great forces' fortunes had run out, and they were destined to decline.

Faced with such enormous benefits, Ahan Palace could not possibly stay out of it. In other words, the wealth Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land had accumulated over so many years would ultimately fall into Ahan Palace's hands.

Red Ghost had already been considering rushing back to Ahan Palace at full speed to report what had happened here to the Sect's upper ranks, then have Ahan Palace send experts to act.

Yang Kai's sudden remark made it clear that he wanted half the benefits from these two great Sects. These two top Sects had each stood for tens of thousands of years. How terrifying must their accumulations be? Half of both together was practically the entire wealth of one Sect.

Red Ghost could not even imagine how vast a sum that would be.

So the instant he reacted, he instinctively gritted his teeth and asked, "On what basis?"

Even if Ahan Palace acted, why should it share what it obtained with someone else, and half at that?

Yang Kai grinned and pointed at the ruined battlefield. "I killed those people. Without my battle today, your Ahan Palace would never have the chance to sit back and reap the fisherman's profit. It isn't too much for me to ask for a little benefit, is it?"

"Uh..." Red Ghost was left speechless for a moment. To be honest, this demand was very excessive, but today he had witnessed Yang Kai's abilities for himself. The two great Sects had both been beaten into cripples by him, so in Yang Kai's eyes, Ahan Palace alone probably did not amount to much either. If Ahan Palace really made an enemy of this man, then the consequences... Red Ghost shuddered just thinking about it.

"Just go back and tell your Hall Master. Whether he gives it or not is his decision." Yang Kai smiled faintly.

Red Ghost pondered for a moment, then nodded. "Good. I will deliver your words. Farewell!"

With that, he wasted no time, transforming into a streak of red light and flying away at top speed.

Right now, time was wealth. He had to return to his Sect before the news spread, then have the Sect's experts move with all haste. Only then could they seize the wealth of those two families before the other forces in the Eastern Territory reacted.

Lan He and Ling Yinqin watched this happen before their eyes, both somewhat speechless. At a time like this, that fellow was still thinking about such things. Still, it had to be admitted that no one could remain unmoved before such enormous wealth.

"You've struck it rich," Jiu Feng said with a smile after Yang Kai caught up.

"Just so-so," Yang Kai said with a laugh. Privately, he thought that he did not have it easy either. With a hundred thousand disciples crying out to be fed, he, as Palace Master, had to find ways to obtain more cultivation resources.
