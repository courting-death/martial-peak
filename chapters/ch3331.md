## Chapter 3331 — Mother and Daughter Meet

Inside the palace, Zhu Qing was holding Mo Xiaoqi's hands and whispering to her when Yang Kai walked in. Whatever they were talking about brought a smile to Mo Xiaoqi's face, but after a long while, she suddenly shook her head again, looking troubled and nervous. Zhu Qing patiently comforted her, and only after quite some time did Mo Xiaoqi give a small nod.

After that, Zhu Qing came over and gave Yang Kai a look.

The two of them left together.

"What did you say to her?" Yang Kai asked, holding Zhu Qing close while one restless hand wandered over her body.

Zhu Qing slapped away his wandering hand. "I asked her when she planned to see Third Elder. She seems a little afraid."

Yang Kai said, "That's only natural. All along the way, she has been caught between anticipation and nerves, and now that she's here, it is even more obvious. What? She doesn't want to see her own mother?"

Zhu Qing said, "It isn't that she doesn't want to. She is just very anxious. Since she's like this, we should invite Third Elder over instead."

"That works too." Yang Kai nodded.

The two set out at once, speeding toward the Spirit Island where Third Elder lived. It was not far from Zhu Qing's Crescent Island, and with their speed, they arrived in only about the time it took to burn a stick of incense. The whole Spirit Island was beautiful, with mountain ranges rising one behind another. Most striking of all was an enormous waterfall plunging from a high peak, its curtain of water joining heaven and earth. Before they even arrived, they could hear its rumbling roar.

Beneath the waterfall was a valley, and several pavilions stood within it. Third Elder Fu Xuan lived there.

Zhu Qing led Yang Kai there with practiced ease. They arrived just in time to see a woman in water-blue palace robes tending to flowers and plants, her expression tranquil and her bearing gentle.

As though sensing something, Fu Xuan looked up, smiled faintly at Zhu Qing, then fixed her gaze on Yang Kai.

"Third Elder!" Zhu Qing stepped forward to salute. Yang Kai also cupped his fists. "Greetings, Third Elder."

Fu Xuan smiled and nodded. "You've come?"

She had met Yang Kai last time and knew about his relationship with Zhu Qing. The two of them were in almost exactly the same position as she and Mo Huang had been, both having violated one of Dragon Isle's greatest taboos. The only difference was that she and Mo Huang had a child, leaving no room to turn back, while Yang Kai and Zhu Qing had not yet reached that step. Because of this, Fu Xuan felt a kinship with them, as people who shared the same fate. Although she was not especially familiar with Yang Kai, there was not the slightest sense of estrangement between them now.

This was also the first time Yang Kai had truly taken a careful look at Fu Xuan. The last time he saw her, she had just escaped after being exiled in the Dragon Tomb for more than a dozen years. She had been at her weakest then, pale and pitiable. Now, she had clearly recovered. Her beauty was stunning, her gaze serene, and she had none of the arrogance one would expect from the Dragon Clan.

Just now, he had heard Zhu Qing say that Third Elder was a Water Dragon, and that among all the Dragon Clan on Dragon Isle, her nature was the gentlest. If one knew her well enough, one would discover that she seemed less like one of the Dragon Clan and more like a recluse.

Hearsay could be false, but seeing was believing. Fu Xuan's every movement truly made one feel as if bathed in a spring breeze. Compared with Second Elder Fu Zhun, the difference was like heaven and earth. Just thinking of Fu Zhun made Yang Kai feel fed up; he really did not know how that old fellow Zhu Yan could stand her.

“Last time, you went after Wu Kuang and only returned after so many years. Did you run into any trouble?”

Yang Kai shook his head and said, “No trouble. I just made Qing’er worry.”

Fu Xuan nodded. "Qing'er was indeed worried. During the years she kept me company, she..."

"Third Elder..." Zhu Qing hurriedly interrupted her and went straight to the point. "Yang Kai came today because he has wonderful news to tell you."

Fu Xuan pursed her lips in a smile. "What good news could there be for me? You little girl, always talking nonsense."

Zhu Qing smiled. "Xiaoqi has come to see you. Does that count as good news?"

"Xiaoqi?" Fu Xuan's expression changed at once. Her beautiful eyes, always warm and gentle as water, suddenly burst with astonishing light. She turned to Yang Kai, her voice excited. "Xiaoqi is here?"

Yang Kai nodded. "Lord Beast Great Emperor knew I was making a trip to Dragon Isle, so he entrusted Little Sister Xiaoqi to me and asked me to bring her here to meet you."

Once she received a definite answer, Fu Xuan could not keep from trembling. She looked behind Yang Kai, but there was no one there. Her voice was hoarse as she asked, "Where is she? Is she here?"

With the purity of her Ninth-Tier Dragon Bloodline and her cultivation as a supreme Divine Spirit, if Mo Xiaoqi had truly followed them here, Fu Xuan would have sensed her at once. Yet now she asked such a question, clearly because her thoughts were already in disarray and she had lost the ability to judge for herself. One could imagine how deeply her daughter's visit had shaken her.

Yang Kai shook his head. "She is waiting for you on Qing'er's Crescent Island."

Zhu Qing said, "Little Sister Xiaoqi seems a little afraid, so she did not dare come over..."

How could Fu Xuan possibly take in her explanation now? Only one voice echoed through her mind: Xiaoqi had come. Xiaoqi had come to Dragon Isle. Xiaoqi had come to see me! She is not far from me. I will be able to see her soon.

After so many years, ever since she had parted from her daughter while the girl was still in swaddling clothes, this was the first time she had been so close to her, the first time she had hope of seeing her again.

Before Zhu Qing had even finished speaking, Fu Xuan's figure flickered and transformed into a streak of light, shooting toward Crescent Island.

Yang Kai and Zhu Qing exchanged a glance, each seeing the helplessness in the other's eyes. Still, they understood how this mother and daughter felt in this moment.

"Let's follow and take a look." As Yang Kai spoke, he took Zhu Qing's hand and chased after her. Since Beast Great Emperor had entrusted Mo Xiaoqi to him, he naturally had to see things through. Although he felt that with Mo Xiaoqi's temperament, she would not do anything rebellious during her first meeting with Fu Xuan, he would still feel more at ease watching in person.

Another stick of incense later, the two returned to Crescent Island.

Fu Xuan, who had gone on ahead, had already arrived, but she had not gone inside the palace. Instead, she stood outside, her face tense with anxiety. Through her Divine Sense, she could indeed perceive an unfamiliar yet familiar life aura within. Even if the seas dried and the stones rotted away, even if heaven collapsed and earth split asunder, she would never forget that special aura. It was an aura connected to her by blood, an aura born from the same source as herself!

Yang Kai and Zhu Qing landed behind her and glanced at each other, neither knowing what to say. Both could sense the turmoil roiling through Fu Xuan's heart, her emotions already beyond her control.

Suddenly, Fu Xuan turned to Zhu Qing and asked nervously, "Qing'er, there is nothing wrong with how I look right now, is there?"

Her expression made it seem as if she feared the slightest flaw might make her daughter dislike her.

Zhu Qing shook her head and said, "No."

"What about my clothes? Are there any wrinkles in my clothes?"

"There aren't."

"My hair? Is my hair messy?"

Zhu Qing: "..."

Yang Kai said, "Third Elder, there is no need to be so anxious. You are Xiaoqi's mother. In my early years, this Junior heard a rather crude saying: even a dog does not despise a poor home, and a child does not despise an ugly mother. No matter what, you are Xiaoqi's mother. There is absolutely no reason she would refuse to acknowledge you. Besides, I doubt there is a more outstanding mother in this world than Third Elder."

"Is that so..." Fu Xuan let out a long breath.

Yang Kai smiled. "Although Xiaoqi did not say much to me, I could feel it throughout the journey. She very much wants to see you, very much wants to know what the person who gave birth to her is like. It's just... she is a little afraid."

Fu Xuan turned to him, her voice trembling. "What is she afraid of?"

Yang Kai said, "She does not know why you left her behind back then and never looked after her. She is afraid that even if she comes to see you this time, you still will not acknowledge her."

Fu Xuan froze at those words. Her eyes quickly reddened, and tears slid down her cheeks. Before they even reached the ground, they transformed into round, crystal-clear beads that fell with a soft clatter.

If someone with ulterior motives saw this scene, they would likely go mad. When the Dragon Clan shed tears, those tears became pearls, and they were extremely rare Alchemy materials.

Fu Xuan murmured, "How could I not acknowledge her? I am the one who has wronged her."

"Third Elder..." Zhu Qing's heart ached. Seeing Fu Xuan cry made her feel terrible as well.

Fu Xuan suddenly wiped her eyes, took a deep breath, and gave a small laugh. "I've made a fool of myself."

Zhu Qing slowly shook her head. In such a moment, how could she possibly laugh at Fu Xuan?

After she steadied herself for a moment, not a trace of distress could be seen. Only then did Fu Xuan step forward and walk into the palace, disappearing inside before long.

Yang Kai and Zhu Qing remained outside and did not follow in to disturb them, but both quietly paid attention to what was happening within.

Through their Divine Sense, they could feel Fu Xuan and Mo Xiaoqi's emotions surging inside the palace, especially Mo Xiaoqi's. Her fluctuations were so violent that one could not help fearing something might happen to her. Then, at a certain moment, those fluctuations reached their peak, and Mo Xiaoqi's tearful cry rang out from inside the palace: "Mother!"

"It's done." Yang Kai breathed a sigh of relief. Although he had never thought this meeting between mother and daughter would lead to any real trouble, the whole process had still been painfully tense. Now that this cry had sounded, Beast Great Emperor's request had been fulfilled, and Yang Kai could finally set down the burden in his heart.

Zhu Qing gently leaned into Yang Kai's arms and said in a low voice, "Will our child be like this in the future too?"

Seeing them, Zhu Qing could not help thinking of herself and worrying.

Yang Kai grinned. “No. If we truly have a child in the future, I absolutely will not let them be separated from you. If Dragon Isle dares stand in the way, we will turn Dragon Isle upside down.”

Zhu Qing smiled sweetly. "Then we'll wait until you have the ability to overturn Dragon Isle before having a child."

Yang Kai glanced left and right, and his large hand once again began to wander restlessly over Zhu Qing's body. In a low voice, he said, "I can give you one right now."

Zhu Qing caught his hand and pinched him hard at the waist.

Yang Kai grimaced. "We haven't seen each other for so many years. Aren't you hungry?" Quietly, he stirred his Golden Sacred Dragon Origin Power, teasing Zhu Qing's heartstrings.

This was the one thing Zhu Qing could least withstand. When they had first met, Yang Kai had used this method, intentionally or not, to take every advantage of her. That Golden Sacred Dragon Origin Power held an indescribable attraction for her, leaving her restless and stirred.
