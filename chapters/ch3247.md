## Chapter 3247 — Changes in the Medicine Garden

"Come any closer and you die without mercy!" A dignified voice rang out between Heaven and Earth, booming like rolling thunder, deafening and clear. The faces of those who had just managed to steady themselves changed dramatically. Recognizing the speaker as that so-called "World Master," how could they dare linger? They fled at once.

The two delicate little figures noticed the disturbance and flew over from the medicine garden like fluttering butterflies. When they saw Yang Kai, they bowed together. "Sir."

They were Mu Zhu and Mu Lu, the two Wood Spirits Yang Kai had brought out of the Wild Ancient Land.

Mu Zhu looked Yang Kai up and down, then lifted a hand to cover her red lips. "Sir, you're injured?" Yang Kai looked so wretched that it was impossible to miss.

Yang Kai gave them a weak wave and continued regulating his breathing.

The two Wood Spirits glanced at each other and nodded in unison. Their figures suddenly shifted, one taking position in the air before Yang Kai while the other flew behind him. At the same time, they formed hand seals, their gestures changing without pause. A moment later, an emerald light curtain appeared and enveloped Yang Kai.

Specks of firefly-like radiance drifted out from within the emerald curtain, filling the space around Yang Kai. With each breath he drew and released, he swallowed them into his belly.

Yang Kai knew what they were doing, and he had no intention of stopping them. He had long heard that the Wood Spirit Race excelled at healing, so how could he fail to understand their purpose now? As the emerald light flowed in and out with his breathing, the pain throughout his body gradually faded, replaced by a faint soreness and itch. Strength slowly returned to his body, and even his Divine Soul, which he had been unable to gather, quickly began to recover.

Looking inward, Yang Kai clicked his tongue in wonder. His body was filled with a green, shimmering light, brimming with vitality as it repaired his damaged flesh, blood, and Meridians.

That green, shimmering light was clearly the work of the two Wood Spirits.

He narrowed his eyes for a look. The little figure hovering before him wore a solemn expression, and sweat slowly beaded on the tip of her nose. It seemed this technique placed some strain on them as well. To be honest, Yang Kai had some trouble telling the two Wood Spirits apart, but judging by the expression of the one before him, she should be Mu Zhu. Mu Lu was the one who got embarrassed more easily.

As if she had noticed Yang Kai peeking, Mu Zhu gave him a small, pursed-lip smile.

Yang Kai closed his eyes without a change in expression. With the two Wood Spirits helping him heal, he relaxed completely.

Barely half an hour passed before Yang Kai opened his eyes again, fully restored.

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu clearly sensed this as well and stopped together. Only then did the emerald curtain that had been enveloping Yang Kai suddenly disperse, and the green, shimmering radiance vanished with it.

"Thank you for your trouble," Yang Kai said.

Mu Zhu pursed her lips. "Sir is too polite."

Yang Kai stood, his Divine Sense sweeping over the medicine garden, and his brow rose despite himself. Although he had planted many Spirit Flowers and exotic grasses here, he had never tended the place carefully. The Everlasting Tree was here, after all. Its vitality was boundless, and with the Heavy Earth in the garden and the many Earth Vein Beads, anything casually planted here would not wither.

But when he looked now, the vast medicine garden had been arranged in perfect order. Each Spirit Herb and precious medicine stood at different intervals, yet the layout was neat and even. Some of the shorter plants had already borne fruit in shades of blue-green, green, and red, making for an extremely pleasing sight.

Yang Kai was certain this was the work of the two Wood Spirits. Whenever he planted herbs in the past, he had done it casually. How could he ever have planned things so carefully?

He even saw many plants he had never seen before, though they all seemed to have only just sprouted, still tender beyond compare.

The man-high Everlasting Tree and the Firmament Tree were especially conspicuous, standing on opposite sides of the medicine garden. Yang Kai looked over and saw one leaf of the Everlasting Tree curled up like a little bed. When he checked the Firmament Tree, he found a similar spot there as well.

Yang Kai chuckled. "Do you usually sleep on those leaves?"

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu exchanged a look, then Mu Zhu said, "Is that improper? If it is, then we..."

Yang Kai waved his hand. "You are the experts when it comes to this. Arrange things however you like."

"Thank you, Sir."

Yang Kai smiled. "Why thank me? I should be thanking you."

In ancient times, the Wood Spirit Race had been dedicated caretakers of medicine gardens. With even one Wood Spirit in a medicine garden, its overall yield could increase by at least thirty percent. Yang Kai had not expected Mu Na to force two clansmen on him, either. From this alone, it was clear how powerfully those rare plants attracted the Wood Spirit Race.

Mu Zhu and Mu Lu had originally been reluctant to part with their clansmen, but after arriving in this medicine garden, they finally understood why the Matriarch had insisted.

The legendary, peerless Everlasting Tree—and the wondrous Firmament Tree as well—were both existences they had only ever seen recorded in ancient texts, yet they had unexpectedly gathered here in this small medicine garden.

The day the two Wood Spirits entered the medicine garden, they were stunned. Only then did they understand the Matriarch's intention.

These two supreme treasures could only be properly tended by the Wood Spirit Race. By caring for them, Mu Zhu and Mu Lu would also receive tremendous benefits. This involved the Wood Spirit Race's special cultivation method, and was not something that could be explained to outsiders.

Mu Zhu bit her red lip lightly and said, a little uneasy, "Sir, we took it upon ourselves to plant a few things here."

"I saw." Yang Kai had long since noticed the many tender shoots just breaking through the soil. Although he did not know what they were, anything the two of them planted would certainly be a rare Spirit Medicine. If they were willing to plant them, Yang Kai naturally would not stop them. Once the plants matured, he would be the one who benefited anyway.

Seeing that Yang Kai had no objection, Mu Zhu finally relaxed. Every Wood Spirit collected many unusual seeds, and the seeds planted in this medicine garden were all treasures she and Mu Lu had gathered themselves. If Yang Kai objected, they would have to dig them up no matter how reluctant they were. Fortunately, he did not seem opposed.

"Plant whatever you want. You don't need to report it to me. Since I've entrusted the medicine garden to you, you can make the decisions here."

"Thank you, Sir." Mu Zhu smiled brightly, though she still seemed to be holding something back.

"If you have something to say, just say it. No need to hesitate."

Mu Zhu glanced at Mu Lu and gave her a push. Mu Lu blushed and said shyly, "Sir, could you expand the medicine garden a little? The space we have now is a bit... small."

The medicine garden was not small. Many of the herbs Yang Kai had collected over the years were planted here, so how could it possibly be small? To the Wood Spirits, however, it still was. Whether Mu Lu or Mu Zhu, either one could tend such a garden alone, so they rather felt they had no room to show their skills.

"Of course it can be expanded. How large do you want it?" Yang Kai asked with a smile.

Mu Lu thought for a moment. "Twice as large?"

"Anything else?"

Mu Lu lowered her head. "If Sir could find more seeds for us outside, that would be even better."

"No problem."

Lingxiao Palace was now strong and influential, so collecting some seeds would naturally be no trouble.

Those were the only requests the two Wood Spirits had. Once they had made them, they looked thoroughly satisfied, then warmly invited Yang Kai to look around the medicine garden. They were not trying to claim credit; they simply wanted to show him what the two of them had accomplished.

Yang Kai did not refuse. He immediately began strolling through the medicine garden with them, the two Wood Spirits accompanying him on either side and explaining things from time to time. It broadened Yang Kai's horizons somewhat. It had to be said that no one could surpass the Wood Spirit Race when it came to understanding these flowers and grasses. They knew the properties, growing environments, maturation cycles, and harvesting methods of every Spirit Medicine like the back of their hands.

After most of the day had passed, Yang Kai left. Before he did, he naturally followed their wishes and doubled the medicine garden's area. There was nothing at all in the newly added section, so Yang Kai simply left it for them to handle.

The next few days passed peacefully. Yang Kai spent most of his time meditating in seclusion in his room, cultivating while recalling the insights he had gained in the Small Xuan Realm. Although that experience had left him in quite a sorry state, it had not been without rewards. At the very least, his understanding of the Great Might of Heaven and Earth had deepened a little.

Several days later, Yang Kai served as a judge for another day. That night, he called Xiao Baiyi and Murong Xiaoxiao, and the three of them attended the exchange meeting together.

Last time, Yang Kai had only gone to join the excitement. This time, he came prepared, with a clear goal in mind.

With so many Emperor Realm Masters rarely gathered in one place, Yang Kai could exchange with them for unusual seeds and satisfy the two Wood Spirits' needs.

As for what he had to trade, he had plenty. He had Yao Beast Inner Cores of every grade, and if he took those out, they would certainly be snatched up. He also had all kinds of Secret Treasures, Spirit Pills, and precious medicines. In the end, however, Yang Kai chose Emperor Pills. After he brought out two bottles, they indeed drew considerable interest, and he got his wish, exchanging them for some plant seeds. Once he returned to his room, he tossed the seeds to Mu Zhu and Mu Lu.

Seeing Yang Kai obtain seeds so quickly, the two Wood Spirits were naturally delighted. Although they had collections of their own, the medicine garden had now expanded, and the seeds they had were nowhere near enough. The seeds Yang Kai brought back solved their immediate shortage perfectly, so they kept urging him to get more.

Yang Kai naturally agreed without hesitation.

However, he probably would not be able to obtain much more at the exchange meeting. Seeds were the sort of thing people might casually collect if they had them, but they would never treasure them the way the Wood Spirits did, so there naturally would not be many.

Yang Kai could only wait until this Martial Gathering ended, then think of a way after returning to the Northern Territory. He believed that with the strength of the entire Northern Territory behind him, collecting some seeds would be no trouble.

One day, Yang Kai was meditating in seclusion inside his room when he suddenly heard a knock at the door.

He opened his eyes and raised a hand, and the door opened. A woman dressed as a maidservant stood outside and bowed. "Elder Yang, someone outside requests an audience."

The maidservant had been assigned by Wuhua Hall to serve them. In truth, Emperor Realm Masters did not need attendants; she was only there to run errands and handle small tasks.

"Who?" Yang Kai found this rather strange. Since this person was requesting an audience, their status had to be lower than his. He could not think of anyone who would seek him out at a time like this.

"A woman. She says she came to thank Elder Yang for saving her life. I believe her name is... Yu Zhuo."
