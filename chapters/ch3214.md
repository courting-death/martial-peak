## Chapter 3214 — Recognizing Ancestors and Returning to the Sect

Ji Ying was highly efficient. Yang Kai had waited in Lingxiao Palace for less than five days when Ji Ying came hurrying back, travel-worn, and went straight to Lingxiao Peak, the main peak.

"Brother Ji, how did it go?" Yang Kai could not read anything from his face and asked with some unease.

Ji Ying said, "Honoured Master agreed to meet Younger Sister-in-law."

The answer left Yang Kai somewhat disappointed, but he also knew Sublime Pill Great Emperor could not possibly take a disciple so casually. Since he had agreed to meet her, there was a chance. If Sublime Pill Great Emperor had refused even to see her, then Yang Kai would have had to give up the idea entirely. At least now, there was still a sliver of hope.

"If Younger Sister-in-law has nothing else to do, she should set out with me now. We cannot keep Honoured Master waiting." Ji Ying had no wish to delay and was ready to return to Medicine Pill Valley at once.

"Then I will trouble Brother Ji." Yang Kai cupped his fists. Xia Ningshang stepped out from behind him, and the two exchanged a few reluctant parting words.

Although they had only just reunited and now had to part again, and although it would not be easy to meet in the future if she truly entered Medicine Pill Valley, in the long run, Medicine Pill Valley was absolutely the best place for Xia Ningshang. Once she returned after mastering what she had gone there to learn, they would naturally have time to stay together for a lifetime. Why cling to every morning and evening now?

Ji Ying released his Multi-deck Ship Secret Treasure, carried Xia Ningshang aboard, and shot into the sky as a streak of light.

Yang Kai stood before the palace and watched. Only after a long while did he look away.

Su Yan, Shan Qingluo, and Xue Yue stood behind him. Yang Kai collected himself, turned, and asked, "Luo'er, are you really not going to the Wild Ancient Land?"

Over the past few days, Yang Kai had already given the four women a rough account of the Star Realm's situation. Xia Ningshang could be arranged to enter Medicine Pill Valley. As for Shan Qingluo, the best place for her was naturally the Wild Ancient Land. Though she had been born human, she possessed a yao bloodline and carried the Origin Power of the Heavenly Moon Demon Spider. In the entire Star Realm, the place best suited to her growth was undoubtedly the Wild Ancient Land.

Yang Kai believed that if he sent Shan Qingluo to the Ancient Land, Luan Feng and the others definitely would not object much. Of course, even if they did object, it would not matter.

It was just that Shan Qingluo did not seem inclined to go. Instead, she preferred to remain in Lingxiao Palace and cultivate.

As for why, Yang Kai could guess a little. She was probably worried Xue Yue would be lonely and had decided to stay and keep her company.

"I am not going." Shan Qingluo pursed her lips in a smile. "If one has the will, where can one not cultivate?"

Yang Kai nodded lightly. "True enough."

Lingxiao Palace was not short on cultivation resources now. Even if Shan Qingluo did not go to the Wild Ancient Land, her cultivation would not be delayed. Since she did not want to go, Yang Kai naturally would not force her. He turned to Su Yan. "Let's go to Ice Heart Valley."

As soon as he finished speaking, his figure flickered and he sped toward the Space Array.

Su Yan followed close behind.

A moment later, in the forbidden area on Ice Island in Ice Heart Valley, the Space Array lit up. The Ice Heart Valley disciple guarding the place noticed the disturbance and immediately came over to investigate. Once she understood the situation, she reported it up the chain.

Yang Kai had not visited Ice Heart Valley many times, but nearly every senior member of Ice Heart Valley recognized him. After all, when Ice Heart Valley had suffered its great calamity back then, Yang Kai had turned the tide and saved Ice Heart Valley from disaster.

In the minds of Ice Heart Valley's disciples, Yang Kai held a rather special status. Otherwise, how could a man freely come and go from this place where women gathered? Any other man who dared show his face would be beaten to death.

After an incense stick's worth of time, Yang Kai met Bing Yun in a great hall.

No one else was present. Only Ji Yao stood attending at the side.

Yang Kai's head ached the moment he saw her. He could not help remembering the embarrassing things that had happened between them in the Wild Ancient Land back then.

Ji Yao wore a strange expression and looked at Yang Kai with a half-smile, which made him feel even more uncomfortable. He kept sensing that there was something hidden in her eyes.

He walked forward without looking aside, cupped his fists, and saluted. "Yang Kai greets Senior."

Bing Yun smiled faintly, her voice gentle. "Why so distant? You brat, it has been more than ten years since you last came, hasn't it?"

Before Yang Kai could answer, Ji Yao said in a strange, sarcastic tone, "Palace Master Yang is an important man with many affairs, and everyone he deals with is rich and powerful. How could he still remember this cold and lonely Ice Heart Valley?"

Yang Kai glanced at her and thought, I have not provoked you, so why are your words so thorny? Even Bing Yun glanced at this third disciple of hers.

Yang Kai forced the corner of his mouth up. "Junior Sister Yao, I trust you have been well."

Ji Yao raised a hand to smooth the hair by her ear and said indifferently, "You used to call me Yao'er, but now it has become Junior Sister."

Yang Kai's eye twitched. Even Bing Yun's eyes flashed with helplessness as she raised a hand and said, "We are not outsiders here. Sit first, then we can talk."

Ji Yao said, "There is no tea to serve you. Palace Master Yang must not take offense."

"Heh heh..." Yang Kai could only laugh awkwardly, at a loss for how to respond.

Bing Yun finally could not bear it anymore and shot Ji Yao a glare. Only then did Ji Yao lower her eyes and put on an obedient look.

"And this is..." Bing Yun glanced curiously at Su Yan behind Yang Kai. She secretly thought Ji Yao's cold words were most likely because this boy had brought a woman with him, so she naturally asked.

Su Yan stepped forward, cupped her fists, and said, "Su Yan greets Ancestor Master!"

"Ancestor Master?" Bing Yun was stunned, and Ji Yao also looked up at Su Yan.

"This..." Bing Yun's eyes suddenly lit with a strange gleam as she stared at the sword hanging from Su Yan's waist. She beckoned, and the sword flew into her hand. Bing Yun gripped the hilt and drew it from its sheath with a clear ring. In an instant, cold light filled the great hall, and an icy aura spread out.

"Profound Frost Divine Sword!" Bing Yun cried softly.

This sword had originally been her personal sword, an item she possessed back when she served as Valley Lord of Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star in the Star Domain. Later, when she left the Star Domain for the Star Realm, she left this sword behind in Ice Heart Valley.

Though tens of thousands of years had passed, and though the Profound Frost Divine Sword had undergone tremendous changes, she recognized it at a glance. This was her personal sword from back then.

“Reporting to Ancestor, it is indeed the Profound Frost Divine Sword!” Su Yan replied respectfully.

Bing Yun looked up at her. "You came from the Star Domain?"

Since the Profound Frost Divine Sword was here, Su Yan's origins were not hard to guess.

Yang Kai smiled and said, "My humble wife once studied for a time at Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star. The Profound Frost Divine Sword is something she obtained from Ice Heart Valley back then."

"Humble wife!" Bing Yun keenly caught that intimate phrase, and a strange light also flashed through Ji Yao's eyes.

Bing Yun gently stroked the sword edge, recalling the past with deep emotion. Only after a long while did she say, "The Profound Frost Divine Sword was once only a Void King-Grade Secret Treasure, but I see it is now Dao Origin-Grade, and it seems to possess considerable spirituality as well. It appears this sword has not been disgraced in your hands."

She sheathed the sword, then raised her hand and returned the Profound Frost Divine Sword to Su Yan. Though it had once belonged to her, the Profound Frost Divine Sword was no longer of any use to Bing Yun. Since Su Yan had obtained it and brought it here, Bing Yun naturally would not snatch it away.

She turned back to Yang Kai and said, "Yang Kai, what is your intention in bringing her to see me?"

Yang Kai grinned. “Su Yan can also be considered a disciple of Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star, so naturally, she is your disciple as well, Senior. Has she not come here to acknowledge her roots and return to her Sect?”

Bing Yun nodded slightly, having guessed Yang Kai's intention. "Very well. I will accept her into the Sect and will not allow her talents to be buried."

Bing Yun was refreshingly direct! Although Yang Kai had known this trip would probably not have any mishaps, he had not expected Bing Yun to agree so readily. Compared with Sublime Pill Great Emperor, Bing Yun was simply far more approachable.

However, although Bing Yun was a Third-Stage Emperor, she was still far below Sublime Pill Great Emperor. It was only right for a Great Emperor to be extremely cautious when accepting a disciple, especially since Sublime Pill Great Emperor had previously said he would accept no more disciples.

Yang Kai gave Su Yan a look, and Su Yan immediately said, "Disciple greets Honoured Master!"

Ji Yao said, "Honoured Master, you spend most of your time in retreat. Why not leave Little Junior Sister's instruction to me?"

Yang Kai was badly startled and looked up at Ji Yao, just in time to see her give him a profoundly meaningful smile. A bad feeling immediately rose in his heart. Before Yang Kai could say anything more, Bing Yun actually nodded and said, "Very well. If I am not free, Su Yan will be entrusted to you. Be sure to teach her with care."

"Yes. I will certainly not fail Honoured Master's trust." Ji Yao answered obediently, then turned to Su Yan and said, "Little Junior Sister, this is our first meeting. In the future, you may call me Third Senior Sister, or you may call me by my name. I am Ji Yao."

"Greetings, Third Senior Sister!"

Ji Yao pursed her lips in a smile. "Let us speak of that later. Honoured Master has not formally accepted you yet. Once the disciple acceptance ceremony is over, we will be one family, and there will be no need to be so distant."

"Yes."

Cold sweat poured down Yang Kai's face. He had the distinct feeling that he had delivered Su Yan into a tiger's mouth. But at this point, it would be awkward to back out. Bing Yun had already agreed to take Su Yan as a disciple, and Su Yan was indeed very suited to remain in Ice Heart Valley and cultivate. What would it look like if he changed his mind now?

Ji Yao would not make things difficult for Su Yan, would she? From what he knew of Ji Yao, it should not come to that.

With that thought, his heart settled slightly, and he said, "Senior, some days ago I made a trip back to the Star Domain and brought over some disciples from Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star. If Senior is willing to accept them, this Junior will arrange for people to send them over after I return."

When Yang Kai left the Star Domain, the people he brought were not limited to those from Dark Star; they came from all the major Cultivation Stars. Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star had naturally been one of his priorities, and he had practically emptied out the entire Ice Heart Valley.

"You returned to the Star Domain?" Bing Yun was utterly shocked.

"Yes. It was also a lucky coincidence."

"How many people did you bring?"

"Is Senior asking about Ice Heart Valley on Chi Lan Star, or everyone?"

"Ice Heart Valley, naturally."

"Several thousand."

Bing Yun froze. She had thought that even if he had the ability to return to the Star Domain and bring some people back, he would bring at most a few, or perhaps a dozen or so. Several thousand truly startled her. Suddenly, her eyes lit up. "You became the Star Domain Master?"

Yang Kai smiled. "Senior's insight is keen."

Only then did Bing Yun understand. No wonder he could bring several thousand people over; he had become the Star Domain Master, the guardian of the Star Domain. Nothing else could explain how he had brought so many people here. She frowned and said, "The Star Domain has its rules, and the Star Realm has its rules. Only those at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm can pass through the Starlight Corridor and fight their way to the Star Realm. Though I do not know what method you used to bring them here, doing this may bring you trouble."

Yang Kai said, "Senior need not worry. Since I dared to do this, naturally there will be no problem."
