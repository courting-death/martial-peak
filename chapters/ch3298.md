## Chapter 3298 - Purely Fabricated

If Yang Kai really had some special connection to Spirit Beast Island, he would have been able to find a way in without coming to Heavenly Wolf Valley for help.

With that thought in mind, Qian Xiuying looked at Lan He again and smiled faintly. "Do not worry, Little He. A man like him should have the Heavens watching over him. Even those two top forces may not be able to do anything to him."

Lan He looked up at her. "Master's Wife means..."

Qian Xiuying said, "Those proficient in the Laws of Space are best at escaping with their lives. If he is no match for the experts of those two great forces, can he not run if he cannot win? Have you ever heard the rumor? Back then, Sir Li Wuyi somehow offended the Flower Shadow Great Emperor and was pursued by her without rest, yet in the end he still relied on his Space Divine Ability to escape disaster. You must understand what kind of figure a Great Emperor is. Although Li Wuyi is known as the first person below the Great Emperors, the gap between him and a Great Emperor is still obvious. Since he could escape with his life from the Flower Shadow Great Emperor, that alone shows how profound Space Divine Abilities are. Even if Yang Kai's attainments in this Dao cannot compare with Sir Li Wuyi's, he should still have ways to protect his life."

Hearing this, Lan He could not help thinking back to the teleportation method Yang Kai had used earlier. Master's Wife's words did make sense. That method was simply miraculous, crossing a thousand li in an instant. The people from Netherworld Sect and Brahma Heaven Sacred Land really might not be able to do anything to him.

The worry in her heart finally eased a little, but her guilt did not lessen in the slightest.

When they reached the guest hall, Lan He knew she had no face to meet Yang Kai, so naturally she did not follow them inside. She only stood outside and waited, looking deeply unhappy.

Seeing her like this, Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying did not force her and simply let her be.

Inside the main hall, a man and a woman arrived side by side. Yang Kai, who had been quietly waiting there, sensed them and immediately rose to look over. With a single glance, he confirmed who they were.

On the way here, Lan He had told him a little about Heavenly Wolf Valley, so he knew that only two Second Stage Emperor Realm Masters presided over the valley. These two were presumably the Valley Lord Tang Sheng and his wife Qian Xiuying whom she had mentioned.

The man was refined and elegant, the woman graceful and beautiful, like immortals descended to the mortal world. Yang Kai had come as a guest, so naturally he could not neglect his manners. He quickly cupped his fists and said, "Yang Kai greets Valley Lord Tang and Vice Valley Lord Qian. I came uninvited, so I hope the two of you will forgive the disturbance."

Tang Sheng laughed heartily, showing a forthrightness somewhat at odds with his refined appearance as he came forward. "Young Master Yang is too polite. You are Little He's benefactor. Your visit brings honor to my humble Heavenly Wolf Valley, so how could there be any disturbance?"

Qian Xiuying also cupped her fists with a smile. "Young Master Yang."

Yang Kai was slightly startled. Although the couple seemed extremely hospitable, there was an almost imperceptible distance in their words. Then again, he and Lan He were not especially close, and this was his first time meeting Tang Sheng and his wife, so he soon understood. Still, when he glanced outside the hall and did not see Lan He, he could not help feeling puzzled.

"Young Master Yang, please sit." Tang Sheng gestured with his hand, then he and Qian Xiuying walked to the two seats above and sat down. He called out, "Someone, serve tea!"

A maidservant immediately came in and arranged everything.

Tang Sheng looked at Yang Kai with a warm smile and bright eyes. "I hear Young Master Yang comes from the Southern Territory?"

Yang Kai nodded. "More or less."

Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying exchanged a glance before he continued, "May I ask where Young Master Yang currently serves? From what this Tang can see, Young Master Yang seems to have already reached the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. At your age, with such cultivation, the place you have joined must be extraordinary."

Yang Kai replied with a smile, "For now, I serve as a Guest Elder at Qingyang Shrine." He did not mention that he was the Palace Master of Lingxiao Palace in the Northern Territory. After all, they had already assumed he was from the Southern Territory. If he then mentioned that he was the master of a palace in the Northern Territory, it would sound a little too much like boasting.

"Qingyang Shrine!" Tang Sheng's expression changed slightly. "The Qingyang Shrine that is one of the Southern Territory's three top forces?"

Yang Kai nodded. "There is only one Qingyang Shrine in the Southern Territory."

Tang Sheng sighed. "This Tang has long heard Qingyang Shrine's great name. The Southern Territory's three great forces, Qingyang Shrine, Wuhua Hall, and Tianwu Sacred Land, are each extraordinary and on par with the three great forces of our Eastern Territory. All of them are top forces of the present age. My Heavenly Wolf Valley can only admit its inferiority."

Yang Kai said, "Valley Lord need not be so modest. In my view, Heavenly Wolf Valley is also quite impressive. Both Valley Lords have profound cultivation, and in time, it may not necessarily fall short of those so-called top forces."

Tang Sheng laughed loudly. "Then I will gladly accept Young Master Yang's auspicious words." After a pause, he continued, "Speaking of which, when this Tang was young, I once held the grand ambition of wandering all four territories of the Star Realm. Unfortunately, in the end, I was tied down by too many matters and never fulfilled that long-cherished wish. I have not even left the Eastern Territory. Truly shameful. Since Young Master Yang comes from the Southern Territory, would you tell me how the Southern Territory differs from our Eastern Territory?"

Qian Xiuying also watched Yang Kai with curiosity, seeming quite interested in the subject.

Yang Kai fell silent for a moment, but since he would soon have something to ask of them, he decided to satisfy their curiosity. Speaking of which, he had traveled through all four territories of the Star Realm, east, south, west, and north, and had experienced the customs of each place. In age, he could not compare with Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying, but in knowledge and experience, he could leave them far behind. He immediately began speaking at length, his words flowing freely. Tang Sheng and Qian Xiuying listened in rapt fascination, as if they were seeing it all for themselves, and kept asking questions.

Yang Kai talked until his mouth went dry, yet the husband and wife across from him still seemed unsatisfied and did not mention the Spirit Beast Island token at all, leaving him thoroughly depressed. He felt Lan He should already have told them why he had come. They kept asking about the Southern Territory, yet stubbornly avoided the token. What was that supposed to mean?

After finally catching an opening, Yang Kai could only take the initiative. "Two Valley Lords, please forgive me. This Yang has come to Heavenly Wolf Valley to ask for a favor."

Hearing this, Tang Sheng sighed and said, “I roughly know what Young Master Yang has come to ask for. Little He also mentioned it to me earlier, but I fear I must disappoint Young Master Yang in this matter.”

Yang Kai was stunned. "Valley Lord means..."

Tang Sheng smiled bitterly. "Back then, my Heavenly Wolf Valley did indeed obtain a descendant of the Heavenly Wolf. Strictly speaking, it did not truly count as one; it had only awakened a trace of its distant ancestor's bloodline, and there was nothing particularly special about it. Later, Spirit Beast Island somehow heard of this, and a Beast-Seeking Envoy came to my Heavenly Wolf Valley to request that Yao Beast. They gave us some compensation. All of that is true. As for the Spirit Beast Island token, however, that was something this Tang fabricated."

"Fabricated?" Yang Kai was shocked on the spot. This kind of thing could be fabricated too?

Tang Sheng said, “Indeed. I deliberately fabricated this news and then spread it, merely to add an invisible layer of protection to my Heavenly Wolf Valley and make other forces somewhat wary of us. Even if Spirit Beast Island heard about this, it was true that they took away Heavenly Wolf Valley’s Yao Beast, and Tang’s actions did not damage Spirit Beast Island’s prestige. With Spirit Beast Island’s ability, they naturally would not stoop to making trouble for my Heavenly Wolf Valley. I simply did not expect Young Master Yang to make a wasted trip today. I am truly ashamed.”

After listening to his explanation, Yang Kai could only smile bitterly. He had thought his luck had finally turned, only to discover that the so-called token had been fabricated. Since things had come to this, what else could he say? A thought struck him, and he asked, "Is Miss Lan afraid to see me because of this?"

Qian Xiuying sighed. "Little He feels she broke faith with you, so she has no face to meet you."

Yang Kai laughed despite himself. "There is no need for that. Miss Lan is overthinking it."

Qian Xiuying said, "That child has always been like this. She takes everything too seriously. She has never forgotten the grace you showed by saving her back then. This time, after meeting you in the Eastern Territory, she naturally wanted to repay that old kindness. She simply did not know whether the rumor was true or false and thought my Heavenly Wolf Valley really had a token from Spirit Beast Island, so she brought you here."

Tang Sheng said, "This matter is this Tang's fault. Back then, I only wanted to use that opportunity to give Heavenly Wolf Valley another layer of protection, but I never expected to implicate Little He. You must not blame her."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Absolutely not." If not for Lan He's reminder, he truly would not have known that Brahma Heaven Sacred Land and Netherworld Sect had set their sights on him.

Qian Xiuying said, "If you truly wish to find Spirit Beast Island, you might as well spend more time looking around the cities by the sea. Perhaps you will find some clues."

Yang Kai nodded, though in his heart he was thinking that if he truly could not find a way there, then he could simply forget about Spirit Beast Island. His main purpose in coming to the Eastern Territory this time was to go to Dragon Isle. Spirit Beast Island was only a side matter.

Speaking of which, Liu Yan had lived on Spirit Beast Island for a long time, but she might not know its location either. When Jiu Feng took her away back then, she had still been a phoenix egg. Afterward, she had gone directly to Dragon Isle, then followed Yang Kai to the Star Domain.

Just then, Tang Sheng suddenly frowned, took a Transmission Compass from his Spatial Ring, and sensed it for a moment. His expression changed at once, and he turned to exchange a glance with Qian Xiuying.

Yang Kai read their expressions and knew Tang Sheng had likely received some important message. With a slight smile, he said, "If the two Valley Lords have matters to handle, please go ahead. This Yang will leave shortly. Another day, if I have time, I will come visit the two of you again."

Tang Sheng stood and said, "Ashamed, ashamed. There are indeed some urgent matters in the valley. Since that is the case, this Tang will not stand on ceremony with Young Master Yang. Please make yourself comfortable."

After saying so, he left together with Qian Xiuying.

Yang Kai watched their backs disappear, reached up to rub his chin, and a strange, meaningful light flashed through his eyes.

He was not a fool. Although Tang Sheng's explanation sounded perfectly reasonable, his earlier behavior had felt somewhat off. Whether the token existed or not, Yang Kai could not say for certain, but one thing was absolutely true: Heavenly Wolf Valley did not particularly welcome him. Otherwise, why would the husband and wife leave together? If there really was some urgent matter, one person could go handle it. Why would both of them need to go?

They had not even shown the slightest intention of asking him to stay as a guest for a few days, and after he said he would leave shortly, Yang Kai had clearly sensed their secret sighs of relief.

They were afraid of inviting disaster onto themselves.
