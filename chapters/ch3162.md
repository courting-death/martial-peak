## Chapter 3162 — Do Not Run

"Who are you!" Jiang Xuesong thundered, rising to his full height.

A figure strode in, crossing the distance to Jiang Xuesong in only a few steps. For one dazed instant, Jiang Xuesong felt as if a giant dragon were bearing down on him. He retreated two steps despite himself and dropped back into his chair. The Sword Heart he had cultivated for fifteen hundred years gave a faint crack, and a tiny fissure appeared in it.

He had been afraid? Only then did Jiang Xuesong snap back to himself and realize what had happened to him in that brief moment. Deep shock spread across his face.

His life had been full of hardship, and the powerful enemies he had faced were beyond counting. Yet no matter when it was, no matter what enemy stood before him, he had never once shrunk back in fear. However many methods you possessed, however heaven-defying your cultivation, he would break you with one sword, even if it meant being smashed to pieces.

It was precisely this trace of resolve that had tempered his flawless Sword Heart.

But in that moment just now, he had felt fear for the first time in his life, and a flaw had appeared in his perfect Sword Heart.

Yang Kai looked down at him, his face almost pressed against Jiang Xuesong's, and grinned. "Chief, what a poor memory you have. You were talking about this Young Master just now, yet you forgot me in the blink of an eye?"

"You are..." Jiang Xuesong's eyes trembled as an utterly absurd thought surfaced in his mind. He said flatly, "Impossible!"

"Anything is possible!" Yang Kai turned and sat on the desk, then looked over at Old Ying. "Are you from Netherworld Sect?"

His casual manner made it seem as if he had not barged into a dragon's pool and tiger's den, but had merely stopped by a familiar neighbor's house. Even with Old Ying's vast experience, the scene struck him as absurd.

"Let me ask you something!" Yang Kai fixed his eyes on Old Ying. "Are you Netherworld Sect's Sect Master?"

"No!" Old Ying shook his head.

Yang Kai rubbed his chin, looking regretful. "Wrong place, then."

A surge of Sword Intent suddenly rose, and a dazzling sword light flashed out, illuminating the great hall. In that instant, all things beneath the heavens seemed to lose their color, leaving only the radiance of that one sword.

Jiang Xuesong attacked without warning, fierce and decisive.

As a swordsman, he should never have ambushed someone from behind. In all his past battles, he had never done so. Every foe he defeated had fallen to him openly and honorably.

But Yang Kai's approach just now had actually made him feel fear, staining his Sword Heart. Shamed into fury, he could no longer care about anything else.

Before the Sword Qi arrived, Old Ying raised his hand, and a great blood-red banner appeared in his palm, its surface roiling. It was none other than one of Netherworld Sect's two most common Secret Treasures, the Blood Sea Banner.

However, the Blood Sea Banner in Old Ying's hand was clearly stronger than any Yang Kai had encountered before. The aura it gave off had already reached the peak of the Void King-Grade. If this world did not forbid it, a little more sacrificial refinement would be enough to turn it into a Dao Origin-Grade Secret Treasure.

He waved the Blood Sea Banner, and a rolling blood sea surged from within it, spreading out to engulf Yang Kai from above.

At the same time, that matchless Sword Intent struck Yang Kai as well. Jiang Xuesong felt a moment of melancholy. Although he did not know this young man's cultivation, after being struck within three chi by the sword skill of a Sword Cultivator like him, how could there be any chance of survival? All the more so with Old Ying helping at this moment.

Although the two had always been cordial on the surface while at odds in their hearts, in this instant they acted as one, coordinating with perfect tacit understanding.

The sword light fell, and Jiang Xuesong's face changed drastically. Old Ying had clearly sensed something too, and his body jolted.

The two exchanged a glance, both seeing the shock in the other's eyes. Without the slightest hesitation, they transformed into two streaks of light and rushed out of the hall, vanishing in the blink of an eye.

Amid the rolling blood sea, a fist suddenly blasted out. The blood sea collapsed, and the sword light vanished without a trace.

Yang Kai took a single step and appeared outside the hall. He did not pursue them. Instead, he leaned against a hall pillar and watched coldly with great interest.

Jiang Xuesong and Old Ying rushed out of the great hall. One look around, and they froze where they stood.

The Abandoned Nest they had considered impregnable had, at some unknown point, been surrounded by more than a dozen pitch-black Warships. An immense formation wrapped the entire Abandoned Nest in layer after layer. From those Warships, figures flew down one after another, charging onto asteroids large and small as they began hunting down Abandoned Nest's cultivators.

When had this happened!

Abandoned Nest was easy to defend and hard to attack. It was protected by a natural Grand Array and by those terrifying Starry Sky storms. Only those familiar with their patterns could find a safe route in and out. Although Netherworld Sect was quite powerful, Old Ying knew that if he were to lead men here to attack Abandoned Nest, he would still have to pay a heavy price. The only reason he had been able to enter safely was that Jiang Xuesong had contacted him on his own initiative more than a dozen years ago.

Yet now, this seemingly safe place had been breached without a sound. It was simply a nightmare.

Powerful auras surged in from every direction, telling Old Ying and Jiang Xuesong one cruel fact with perfect clarity: today, their chances looked grim.

A small figure suddenly flew toward them. Jiang Xuesong's eyes narrowed, and he saw that it was a little girl of seven or eight.

Old Ying had clearly noticed her as well. A cold look crossed his face, and with a sweep of the Blood Sea Banner in his hand, he swept the little girl into the banner.

Jiang Xuesong abruptly shot backward, swiftly putting distance between himself and Old Ying.

"What are you doing!" Old Ying glared at him in anger. "Join hands with me at once and leave this place!"

Was this man sick in the head? At a time like this, he was still trying to keep away from him. Did he really think he could break through the encirclement with one broken sword?

Jiang Xuesong looked at him seriously and shook his head. "Each to his own fate!"

Although he had not seen anything unusual about the little girl, the moment she approached, the Sword Heart within him had sounded an intense warning. A voice shouted in his heart: Farther away. Farther. Farther still!

"Have you gone mad?" Old Ying roared. "The only thing we can do now is..." Halfway through, he suddenly lowered his head to look at his Blood Sea Banner. A crimson flame had appeared on its surface and was spreading rapidly in every direction, burning wildly.

The scorching aura chilled Old Ying to the bone. He desperately urged his Saint Yuan and poured it into the banner, trying to extinguish the flame.

But no matter how hard he tried, the flame remained unmoved. Instead, it spread even faster.

"Pu..." Old Ying spat out a mouthful of blood. His Secret Treasure had been damaged, and he had suffered backlash along with it. A petite figure stepped out from the Blood Sea Banner, then lightly tapped Old Ying on the head with a finger as fair as white mutton-fat jade.

Old Ying immediately collapsed like a deflated ball.

Liu Yan grabbed Old Ying in one hand and lifted him up, then looked ahead, only to see that Jiang Xuesong had already become one with his sword, transforming into a streak of sword light as he shot away.

Damn it! What kind of monster was that little girl? After Old Ying had taken her into the Blood Sea Banner, not only had she come out unharmed, she had broken free, destroyed the Blood Sea Banner, and heavily injured Old Ying with a single finger. Such strength had already surpassed his imagination.

No matter how powerful his Sword Heart was, it could not give him the courage to fight. Only one thought churned in his mind.

Run. Run as far as possible!

"Do not run." A clear voice, like spring water tinkling, suddenly sounded beside his ear.

Jiang Xuesong turned his head and saw the little girl keeping pace beside him, looking perfectly unhurried, while still carrying the dying Old Ying in her hand.

The corner of his eye twitched fiercely, and he nearly had the urge to curse aloud. He bit through the tip of his tongue, sprayed out Essence-Blood, and the sword light grew even faster.

"I already said..." Liu Yan stretched out her small hand and caught the long sword. The sword light instantly went from extreme motion to perfect stillness. She said indignantly, "Do not run!"

That Void King-Grade Secret Treasure sword, incomparably sharp and capable of severing mountains and splitting seas, was caught by her just like that, unable to harm her in the slightest.

"Wa..." Jiang Xuesong spat out a mouthful of blood. This time, it was because the force of the sudden stop had slammed through him. He turned to look at her, almost suspecting he was dreaming.

A moment later, Liu Yan returned to the front of the great hall with Old Ying in one hand and Jiang Xuesong in the other, then threw them both onto the ground.

Old Ying's aura was weak, his injuries far from light. Jiang Xuesong was in somewhat better shape, but he too had lost all will to fight. His eyes were dim, as if covered by shadow.

Yang Kai leaned sideways against the hall pillar with his arms folded. Seeing this, he smiled faintly. "You worked hard!"

Liu Yan pouted, as if blaming Yang Kai for being so polite with her. As though venting her displeasure, she kicked Jiang Xuesong. Jiang Xuesong said nothing, daring to be angry but not daring to speak.

Yang Kai crouched and stared hard at Old Ying. "Where is your Netherworld Sect's Sect Master now?"

Old Ying raised his head to look at him and said shakily, "I do not know."

"How could you not know?" Yang Kai frowned and said viciously, "Old thing, do not try to fool me. With your decent cultivation, you should at least be an Elder. How could you not know where your Sect Master is?"

Old Ying said with a miserable expression, "I truly do not know. He may be in the Spine of the Star River."

"May be?" Yang Kai's brows drew down fiercely, his face savage. "Is he there or not?"

Old Ying trembled. "This old man... does not dare be certain."

"Trash!" Yang Kai cursed, grabbed him by the clothes, and threw him out. Old Ying let out a miserable scream and flew a hundred zhang before suddenly exploding in midair.

Great Wilderness Star Domain had invaded, and Netherworld Sect was the chief culprit. Netherworld Sect's Sect Master was the chief culprit among culprits, so Yang Kai naturally wanted to settle accounts with him. Unfortunately, that man was nowhere to be found here.

Still, it did not matter. This was only Hengluo Star Domain's first counterattack. As long as Netherworld Sect's Sect Master remained in Hengluo Star Domain, they would find him sooner or later.

At this time, the Spine of the Star River should also have begun moving. Su Yan was leading that side, so presumably the Spine of the Star River would not be able to stir up any waves either.

At the same time, Yang Kai had dispatched Li Jiao's family to guard the passage between the two great Star Domains. Great Wilderness Star Domain could forget about receiving reinforcements from there, much less escaping Hengluo Star Domain.

The main reason Yang Kai had waited here for ten days was to catch all of Abandoned Nest in one net and not let a single enemy slip away. He and Liu Yan could certainly rampage through this place, but Abandoned Nest was vast. Without enough helpers, some fish would inevitably escape the net.

Now, things were different. Of the two strongest people in Abandoned Nest, one was dead and the other wounded, and the entire Abandoned Nest had been surrounded by his people. If Great Wilderness Star Domain's cultivators and those Star Pirates thought they could escape from here, they were dreaming.

The sounds of battle and slaughter continued to ring out. Yang Kai lowered his head and looked down at Jiang Xuesong, a trace of disgust flashing through his eyes. "Tell me any information useful to me, and I can grant you a quick death."
