## Chapter 3256 — Yao Kings Gather

This boy was getting more and more detestable, Luan Feng thought, secretly grinding her teeth. "And if I help? If I don't?"

"Help me this once, and I'll remember the favor. Refuse, and I'll leave right now."

Luan Feng sneered. "What use would I have for a favor from you?"

Yang Kai gave her a cold look, nodded, and shot into the sky.

Luan Feng started. He was really leaving? Why did he act the instant he spoke, without giving anyone the slightest time to think? Was he insane? She hated Yang Kai's overbearing attitude, but if he truly bore a grudge against her, she would have no peaceful days in the future. She could only raise a hand and shout angrily, "Is my territory a place you can enter and leave whenever you please?"

"What do you want?" Yang Kai stopped and glanced back at her, the chill in his eyes almost solid enough to touch.

Their eyes met, and Luan Feng's heart gave a jolt. She suddenly realized something was wrong with Yang Kai this time. She had always found him annoying, but at least he had been somewhat reasonable before. Now, he seemed like a different person altogether.

It seemed he really had run into trouble, and trouble that had enraged him.

"Where are you going?" Luan Feng asked in a low voice.

"None of your damned business!"

He had none of the attitude of a man asking for help. Luan Feng was so furious she wanted to slap him dead, but she knew his strength was considerable. If she forced him to reveal all his trump cards, she might not be able to kill him. And once they truly tore off all pretense, the consequences would not be hers to bear. Grinding her teeth, she snapped, "This is the last time."

She had agreed.

Yang Kai wasted no words. "Call Fan Wu and Cang Gou over as well. Also summon the other twenty-nine Yao Kings in the Ancient Land." Then he turned and landed back inside the palace, standing not far across from Luan Feng.

Luan Feng was shocked. "What exactly are you trying to do?"

She had thought he was only asking her alone for help, but who would have known he would make such an outrageous demand? Not only had he included Fan Wu and Cang Gou, he had not even spared the twenty-nine Yao Kings. This was the strongest force in the entire Wild Ancient Land. Could this bastard be planning to wage war against some Great Emperor Sect?

The thought left Luan Feng cold all over. She was a Divine Spirit, but she still knew her own limits. There were only so many Great Emperors in the world, and each one was an existence acknowledged by Heaven and Earth. If they truly faced a Great Emperor, few on the Wild Ancient Land's side would likely survive, to say nothing of the deep foundations a Great Emperor's Sect possessed in its own right.

She looked at Yang Kai and felt a shiver run through her. She had long known this fellow was daring beyond measure, but wasn't this a little too daring?

It had to be said, she was overthinking things.

"What are you standing there for? I want to see them within one hour. Hurry up and contact them!" Yang Kai grew irritated when he saw Luan Feng standing there in a daze. He had given Lingxiao Palace half a day, and this side had to finish assembling within half a day as well. There was no time to waste.

Luan Feng forced down her panic and was in no mood to fuss over Yang Kai's rudeness. Her fine brows drew together. "What exactly do you want us to do? You should at least tell me that much."

"Kill people. Save people."

"Who is the enemy?" Luan Feng's heart rose into her throat the moment she asked. She was truly afraid Yang Kai would spit out the name of some Great Emperor.

Instead, Yang Kai lowered his eyes and uttered two words. "Demon Race!"

"Demon Race!" Luan Feng's expression changed slightly. "Where did the Demon Race come from?" Although she had never seen the Demon Race with her own eyes, the inheritance from her Origin had given her some understanding of them. There had been no trace of the Demon Race on this land for over a hundred thousand years. How had they suddenly appeared?

Yang Kai raised his eyes to her. "Are you planning to stand here talking nonsense with me forever? Within one hour, I want to see those two and the twenty-nine Yao Kings. If they help me this time, I guarantee every one of them will become a Divine Spirit in the future!"

Luan Feng froze, biting her silver teeth in secret, her face bitter. If Yang Kai had merely said these words here, that would be one thing, but once they spread, the twenty-nine Yao Kings would probably all throw themselves under his command. In a matter like this, even if the three Saint Venerables joined hands and stepped forward, they would not be able to suppress it. And if she refused Yang Kai's demand today, she would probably earn the resentment of all twenty-nine Yao Kings. By then, she would have offended both sides.

"You win!" Luan Feng snorted coldly. "One hour isn't enough. Some of the Yao Kings' territories are too far from here. No matter what, they can't make it in one hour."

"How long?"

"Two hours!"

"Then I'll wait here for two hours!" With that, Yang Kai closed his eyes and stood there like a post, motionless.

Luan Feng glared at him, then flashed away into the depths of her palace. No one knew what she went to busy herself with, and Yang Kai did not keep watch over her. In any case, he had said all there was to say. If Luan Feng knew what was good for her, she would naturally lend her full effort; if she did not, it would not be too late to settle accounts later. The Northern Territory was still assembling as well and needed time, so nothing would be delayed.

Luan Feng was clearly angry with Yang Kai. Before she left, she dismissed all the surrounding maidservants too, leaving him planted there like a javelin. No one came to bring tea, pour water, or anything of the sort. Her dislike could not have been more obvious.

A short while later, a fragrant breeze drifted over, and someone landed beside Yang Kai. Sensing the movement, Yang Kai opened his eyes and saw a seductive woman smiling warmly at him. It was the same Yao King he had encountered earlier.

Their eyes met. The seductive woman pursed her lips in a smile and bowed gracefully. "Du Mi'er greets Sir."

Her clothing had been revealing to begin with, and now, whether intentionally or not, she displayed her charms even more, bending herself to flatter him. Her smile was brighter than flowers.

"What is it?" Yang Kai asked coldly.

Du Mi'er waved a hand. "Nothing, nothing. I am a Yao King under the Southern Saint Venerable. I have long admired Sir's heroic bearing, and seeing you today, I find your reputation truly deserved." As she spoke, she waved again and brought a reclining chair out from her Spatial Ring, saying attentively, "Sir, please sit."

Yang Kai nodded lightly, the coldness on his face easing a little, then flicked his robe aside and sat down.

Du Mi'er suddenly leaned close and murmured, "The Saint Venerable has something of a temper and slighted Sir. Please do not take offense." As she spoke, her breath was as fragrant as orchids, and a faint perfume lingered around Yang Kai's nose. It actually dispelled much of Yang Kai's killing intent and anger, gradually calming his turbulent emotions.

He turned his head and looked at her calmly. "If you speak ill of her in private and she hears about it, what then?"

Du Mi'er's face stiffened, and she said awkwardly, "I was only saying it to Sir. I trust Sir will not spread it around."

Yang Kai looked her up and down, somewhat curious. "What kind of yao are you transformed from?"

Du Mi'er smiled with pursed lips. "My true body is a Flower Yao." As she spoke, she lifted a hand and pinched at the empty air. A small golden flower immediately appeared between her fingers, richly fragrant and intoxicating.

Yang Kai nodded. "For plants and trees to become spirits is exceptionally rare." Compared with bodies of flesh and blood, it was countless times harder for plants and trees to gain sentience. Yang Kai had not expected the Yao King before him to be a Flower Yao, much less one who had cultivated to the level of a Yao King. That was extremely rare.

Du Mi'er said wistfully, "Sir flatters me. I have suffered a great deal over many years to achieve what I have today." As she spoke, she quietly watched Yang Kai's reaction. Seeing that he had no intention of taking up the topic, she bit her lip and said, "Sir, I heard Yingfei, Xi Lei, and Xie Wuwei are serving under you?"

"You already know the answer."

Du Mi'er curled her lips. "What can three grown men accomplish? I fear they cannot even serve Sir properly. I truly do not know what the Saint Venerables were thinking. They should have sent a woman to attend Sir well. At the very least, someone should take care of Sir's daily needs."

"You're very good at serving people?" Yang Kai gave her a sidelong glance.

Du Mi'er seemed eager to try. "Would Sir like to find out? I can see Sir is troubled and weighed down with worries. That is no good. With a great battle imminent, it would be best to clear both body and mind, so that you can exert your full strength. If Sir does not disdain me, I can show a little of what I can do." After saying this, she looked at Yang Kai earnestly, waiting quietly for his answer.

When Yang Kai showed no reaction for a long while, she gritted her teeth, hardened her heart, and stepped behind him. Placing both hands on his shoulders, she bent down beside his ear. "Sir only needs to relax. Leave the rest to me. I will make certain you are satisfied."

The soft, fragrant breeze brushed past Yang Kai's ear like an invisible little hand, rippling through the heart and smoothing away the anger and negative emotions he had been suppressing all this time.

Two small hands, supple as if boneless, kneaded his shoulders and head with shifting pressure. Faint waves of Yao Yuan and Divine Sense spread out, and soon Yang Kai felt as if he had been placed in a sea of flowers, with vast Heaven and Earth around him, endless blossoms, and a clear, empty body and mind.

Seeing Yang Kai's tense expression gradually ease and his aura slowly steady, Du Mi'er smiled and became even more attentive.

As Luan Feng's message spread, figures burst forth from every direction of the Wild Ancient Land, each with Yao Qi soaring to the heavens and Yao Yuan billowing around them. Many Yao Generals and Yao Commanders raised their heads to look on, baffled, not knowing what matter had drawn out their own Great Kings.

One hour later, more than a dozen Yao Kings had already gathered inside Luan Feng's palace. The rest were still on the road, but it was believed they would all gather here before long.

The gathered Yao Kings all came to the courtyard where Yang Kai was. Some sat, some stood, all staring straight at Yang Kai and Du Mi'er. Under so many eyes, Du Mi'er felt no shame at all. Instead, she kneaded Yang Kai even more diligently, her slender neck held high like a proud rooster.

"Slut. Whore. Shameless bitch!" A beautiful female Yao King saw her acting this way and could not help cursing under her breath, her face full of disdain.

Beside her, a fur-covered male Yao King grinned and said, "You can go too."

The female Yao King coldly snorted. “I am not that shameless.”

The male Yao King looked her up and down and sneered. "Why pretend to be pure? Which Yao King in our Ancient Land hasn't had a taste of you?"

The female Yao King flew into a rage. "I'll tear your mouth apart."

The male Yao King reached out to pull her into his arms. "With what?"

"Get away from me!"

"Enough, stop fooling around. Does anyone know what the Saint Venerable summoned us for this time?" Another Yao King stepped forward and asked, looking puzzled.
