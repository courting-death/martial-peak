## Chapter 3293 — Old Grudges

By the time he emerged from the Ancient Land, more than ten days had passed.

On the outskirts of the Ancient Land stood a worn-down Desolate City where many cultivators lingered. The Wild Ancient Land had a fearsome reputation, but it also nurtured countless precious treasures, and its special environment still drew many cultivators to brave its dangers. Over the years, a place for traveling cultivators to stop, rest, and resupply gradually developed outside the Ancient Land. That was how Desolate City came to be.

Yang Kai had been here before. Back then, he had found a guide here, Old Ban, and under Old Ban's lead had safely entered the Ancient Land. Because of Old Ban, he had also come into conflict with Yao Lin, the daughter of Nether Soul Great Emperor. Yao Lin might not have been evil, but she had been spoiled since childhood, and her temper was arrogant and overbearing. Relying on the experts protecting her, she insisted on barging into the Ancient Land and forced Old Ban to guide her, only to be taught a lesson by Yang Kai. Unwilling to accept it, she later, for reasons unknown, joined forces with Yin Lesheng of Netherworld Sect to ambush him before the exit of the Ancient Land passage, only for Yang Kai to beat them into a complete rout. At the critical moment, Nether Soul Great Emperor descended through Soul Descent, forcing Yang Kai to withdraw in alarm.

Coming to this dilapidated Desolate City brought Old Ban to Yang Kai's mind.

Back then, Yang Kai had been grateful for Old Ban's guidance. Seeing the old man living in hardship with only his granddaughter beside him, he had brought them to the Northern Territory, arranged for Old Ban's granddaughter to enter Ice Heart Valley, and found Old Ban himself a livelihood in one of Ice Heart Valley's subordinate cities.

He had not seen them once in all these years, but by now, Old Ban's granddaughter should have grown up. Cultivating in Ice Heart Valley, her prospects should not be too poor.

Yang Kai passed through Desolate City without stopping. He only indulged in a brief moment of recollection before leaving at once.

His destination on this trip was Dragon Isle!

Sheng Yuzhu's words had filled him with urgency. Although the ten Great Emperors were all still alive, no one could say when the chance to compete for a Great Emperor's position might appear. If that moment truly came and his cultivation was not enough, he would have no one to blame but himself.

That was why Yang Kai wanted to raise his strength as quickly as possible. It was always better to prepare for trouble before it came than to clutch at help in a panic.

He had only recently advanced to the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm. Even if he entered secluded retreat and cultivated bitterly, he would not be able to reach the Third Stage in a short time. But advancing his cultivation was not the only way to raise his strength. For him, a breakthrough in the Dragon Transformation Art and the tempering of his Dragon Bloodline body might prove even more effective than a simple rise in realm.

And for that, Dragon Isle was undoubtedly the best choice.

Besides, Zhu Qing was still on Dragon Isle. Last time, he had used the passage Wu Kuang forcibly opened inside the Dragon Temple to travel to the lower Star Domain. He had no idea what had happened to Zhu Qing afterward, much less whether Dragon Isle had continued making things difficult for her. If he did not go and see for himself, he would not be able to rest easy.

If possible, it would be best to bring Zhu Qing out. Failing that, he at least had to set up a Space Array on Dragon Isle and connect it to Lingxiao Palace. Otherwise, going back and forth would be far too troublesome.

Before going to Dragon Isle, however, he also wanted to visit Spirit Beast Island.

He had long wanted to discuss the Laws of Space with Li Wuyi. In this world, it was rare to find someone else so proficient in that mysterious power, and Li Wuyi was known as the number one person beneath the Great Emperors. His attainments in the Laws of Space were likely even higher than Yang Kai's. In the past, Yang Kai had simply not been qualified to seek him out.

When it came to cultivating the Laws of Space, Yang Kai had always had to feel his own way forward. If he could have a careful discussion with Li Wuyi, perhaps he would gain something unexpected.

Fortunately, both Dragon Isle and Spirit Beast Island lay above the great sea of the Eastern Territory. If Soul Palace was included as well, then in terms of foundation and strength, the Eastern Territory was absolutely the foremost of the Star Realm's four territories. Of course, these three great forces were hardly united. If Beast Great Emperor and the Dragon Clan ran into each other, it would already be fortunate if they did not come to blows. How could they possibly join hands?

In Desolate City, a man stared in the direction Yang Kai had left, surprise flashing across his face. A moment later, he took out a jade slip and immersed his mind in it, carefully examining its contents. His expression brightened. "It really is him."

He hurriedly put away the jade slip, took out a Transmission Compass, and poured his Divine Sense into it, communicating with someone for a while. A moment later, as though he had received orders, he gritted his teeth and followed the direction Yang Kai had gone.

But he was only in the Dao Origin Realm. How could he possibly keep up with Yang Kai? Once he left Desolate City, he had no idea where Yang Kai had gone. He wandered around blindly for a while, looking vexed.

A short while later, a group of people came speeding over. The young man leading them fixed a gloomy stare on him and asked, "Where is he?"

The man answered uneasily, "Reporting to Stronghold Master, this subordinate doesn't know where he went either. That person's cultivation is too high. He vanished in the blink of an eye."

The gloomy young man called Stronghold Master gave him a cold glance, and sweat immediately poured down the man's forehead. In panic, he said, "This subordinate is incompetent. Stronghold Master, please forgive me."

An old man beside them said, "Stronghold Master, that man was already a First Stage Emperor Realm Master back then. Xie San is only a Second Stage Dao Origin Realm cultivator. It may be just as well that he could not keep up. If that man had discovered he was being followed, we might have alerted him."

The gloomy young man's expression eased slightly at that. He nodded and said, "Clan Elder is right." Turning to the man who had reported the news, he asked, "Did you see his face clearly? Can you be certain it was him?"

The man said firmly, "I saw him clearly. It was definitely him. Back then, Stronghold Master gave all of us image jade slips of that person. This subordinate has always carried mine. I didn't notice at first, but when I suddenly felt he looked familiar, I remembered. If Stronghold Master does not believe me, you can ask the others in the city. I doubt I was the only one who saw him."

The Stronghold Master said lightly, "I suppose you wouldn't dare lie. From what you observed, how strong is his cultivation?"

The man hesitated for a moment, then answered cautiously, "He should be stronger than Stronghold Master!"

The Stronghold Master narrowed his eyes. "Stronger than me? Then he must be at least at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm! Damn it!" Venom twisted his face, as though he shared some deep blood feud with Yang Kai.

The old man who had spoken earlier looked slightly startled. "If this old man remembers correctly, that person advanced to the Emperor Realm in the Shattered Star Sea back then, did he not? How many years has it been? Yet he is already a Second Stage Emperor Realm Master. Such aptitude..." He paused, cupped his fists, and said, "Stronghold Master, this matter must be considered carefully. If that boy really is a Second Stage Emperor, my Qi Tian Stronghold may not be able to afford provoking him."

If Yang Kai had been present, those words would certainly have reminded him of the name Qi Tian Stronghold.

Qi Tian Stronghold was not considered a very powerful force in the Eastern Territory. It could barely be counted among the second- or third-rate forces, and its strongest member was only a First Stage Emperor Realm Master. It was roughly comparable to the former Luocha Sect, though a little better off.

Because its strength was limited, Qi Tian Stronghold occupied a border region of the Eastern Territory, where no one competed with it, giving it room to develop in peace.

The current Stronghold Master was none other than that gloomy young man, Qi Hai. Yang Kai had dealt with him several times before.

In the Shattered Star Sea, Yang Kai's possession of the Mountain and River Bell and the True Phoenix Fire had been exposed, and Qi Hai had learned of it. Afterward, Qi Hai had earnestly invited him to visit Qi Tian Stronghold in the Eastern Territory because Qi Hai's woman had once rashly entered the Wild Ancient Land and been afflicted by Heavenly Frost Earth Drizzle, one of the Ten Great Poisons. Her life was running out, and she urgently needed the True Phoenix Fire to drive out the poison and prolong her life. Qi Hai had risked entering the Shattered Star Sea precisely to search for the True Phoenix Fire. Unfortunately, Yang Kai had beaten him to it, so naturally he could only place his hopes on Yang Kai.

But to Yang Kai at the time, the Eastern Territory was impossibly far away. Why would he go all the way to Qi Tian Stronghold for no reason? He had merely brushed the matter off.

Later, when chance truly brought him to the Eastern Territory and he first entered Desolate City outside the Ancient Land passage, Qi Hai received the news immediately. After all, Qi Tian Stronghold was stationed in this borderland, and all of Desolate City lay under its watch and secret control. As the Young Stronghold Master at the time, Qi Hai could be said to have eyes and ears everywhere.

After that, Qi Hai placed hidden agents throughout the Ancient Land passage and quietly waited for Yang Kai to appear. His efforts paid off. One disciple of Qi Tian Stronghold really did wait until Yang Kai arrived and conveyed Qi Hai's intentions to him.

If the True Phoenix Fire had truly been on him at the time, Yang Kai might not have minded helping Qi Hai. It would have taken little effort and caused him no real trouble. But by then, the True Phoenix Fire had already been swallowed by Liu Yan and transformed into a phoenix egg, while Liu Yan herself had been taken by Jiu Feng to Spirit Beast Island to receive the Phoenix Race inheritance. How could Yang Kai have helped Qi Hai? On top of that, the True Phoenix Fire was of great importance, and Yang Kai was unhappy after sensing that his movements were being watched, so he refused on the spot.

As Yang Kai saw it, if someone was poisoned, they should properly seek a cure. Why keep coveting his True Phoenix Fire?

To Yang Kai, the events of that year were nothing more than passing smoke. He had long since forgotten them.

But to Qi Hai, they were something he could never let go of. He was certain the True Phoenix Fire had fallen into Yang Kai's hands, and that removing the poison of Heavenly Frost Earth Drizzle would have taken no great effort. Yet Yang Kai had heartlessly refused, causing his beloved to finally succumb to the deadly poison and pass from this world forever.

From then on, Qi Hai seemed to become a different person. The gentle, refined image of the former Young Stronghold Master changed completely, and he even paid an enormous price to join one of the Eastern Territory's top Sects, hoping only that one day, after achieving success in cultivation, he could personally kill Yang Kai and avenge his dead beloved.

In his eyes, the Ten Great Poisons had certainly killed his partner, but Yang Kai was no less to blame.

This time, after learning that Yang Kai had appeared in Desolate City again, Qi Hai rushed over without stopping. For one thing, he wanted revenge. For another, he wanted to ask Yang Kai face-to-face why he had been so cold-blooded and heartless back then!

The Young Stronghold Master of those days was now the Stronghold Master of Qi Tian Stronghold, and his cultivation had reached the First Stage of the Emperor Realm. He had thought he now possessed the qualifications to contend with Yang Kai, but only after hearing his subordinate's report did he realize that Yang Kai had once again left him behind.

Against a Second Stage Emperor Realm Master, he was probably still no match.

"Qi Tian Stronghold cannot provoke him, but that does not mean others cannot." Qi Hai stared coldly into the distance. "In the Eastern Territory, I, Qi Hai, am not the only one who wants his life. Since he dares to come here, he can forget about leaving alive!"

With a cold laugh, he took out a jade slip and crushed it in his hand. A stream of light flashed and shot off into the distance in the blink of an eye.

Seeing this, the old man beside him could not help sighing. The Young Stronghold Master of that year and the Stronghold Master before him were like two different people. Ever since that woman died, ever since the Young Stronghold Master joined that Sect, everything had changed. But the old man could not say much. Avenging his dead beloved was Qi Hai's final wish; if even that was taken from him, Qi Hai would probably be better off dead.
