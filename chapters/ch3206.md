## Chapter 3206 — How Could I Accept This

"Can't you smell it, Patriarch?" Yang Kai asked instead of answering.

Mu Na gave him a helpless look. Knowing he had his reservations, she said, "It is said that in ancient times, there was a wondrous tree called Firmament Tree, gold and silver in color, the two hues blending together to seal all things. Am I correct?"

Yang Kai stared at her, barely stopping himself from giving her a thumbs-up.

She could smell even that? It was simply miraculous. The Wood Spirit Race truly deserved its reputation as a race born able to commune with plants. Sealed in his Dantian was pure ancient Demonic Qi, held in place by the power of the Firmament Tree's gold and silver Two-Color Seal. It was only natural for a trace of its aura to leak out. Others lacked the Wood Spirit Race's innate gift, so naturally they could not sense it.

But to the Wood Spirit Race, especially to their Patriarch, that faint aura shone as brightly as a lamp in the dark.

From his expression alone, Mu Na knew she had guessed correctly. Shocked, she said, "You actually have the Firmament Tree?"

Yang Kai chuckled. "I came by it by chance, just by chance."

Mu Na said enviously, "Your luck really is excellent." Then her brow suddenly furrowed. "But there seems to be another aura on you. That aura..."

Yang Kai's heart skipped. It was one thing for Mu Na to smell the Firmament Tree's aura. Although the Firmament Tree was legendary, it only possessed the power to seal and isolate. Yang Kai was merely using it to seal the ancient Demonic Qi in his Dantian, so even if word got out, it probably would not matter much. There were many sealing methods in this world, and he was no weakling now. Few people would be blind enough to come after him for the Firmament Tree.

The other tree, however, was different. If news of that thing's existence spread, it would likely turn the entire Star Realm upside down.

That thing represented the mystery of immortality and indestructibility! How tempting was such a thing? Even Great Emperors could not guarantee that they would never die, for they too had limits to their lifespans. Yet according to legend, if one could refine that thing, one could comprehend the mystery of an undying, indestructible existence, share the lifespan of Heaven and Earth, and shine alongside the sun and moon.

Yang Kai's heart leapt into his throat. Mu Na was far too uncanny. He was truly afraid she would say the words Everlasting Tree aloud.

If that happened, he truly would not know how to respond. If it were anyone else, he could simply kill them to silence them, but Mu Na bore him no grudge. On the contrary, their relationship was fairly good, and the Wood Spirit Race had even fought on his behalf without hesitation. How could Yang Kai bring himself to be ruthless toward someone like that? If he really did such a thing, he would offend more than the Wood Spirit Race; the Stone Puppet Race would likely find it hard to tolerate him as well.

Fortunately, after hesitating for a long while, Mu Na finally shook her head and said, "I cannot tell what that aura is either."

Yang Kai immediately breathed a sigh of relief.

Mu Na clicked her tongue in wonder. "To think there is actually a plant in this world my Wood Spirit Race does not know. The world truly is full of marvels."

Yang Kai gave a dry laugh. "Perhaps Patriarch smelled it wrong."

"Impossible!" Mu Na shook her head decisively, completely unaware of Yang Kai's anxiety. Then her eyes lifted, and she said eagerly, "Give it to me!"

Her beautiful eyes shone with a bright, almost divine light. She looked extremely excited.

"What?" Yang Kai was stunned.

Mu Na said, "I said, give me the Firmament Tree and that other thing."

"What kind of joke is that!" Yang Kai flew into a rage. He had originally had a decent impression of Mu Na, but this sudden demand immediately soured it. He could not hand over the Firmament Tree. He was still counting on that gold and silver power to seal the Demonic Qi. Otherwise, if the seal in his Dantian ever came undone and he no longer had the Firmament Tree, he would likely fall straight into the Demonic path and lose himself.

As for the Everlasting Tree, that was even more impossible. Such a supreme treasure of Heaven and Earth, a sacred healing medicine that could revive the dead and regrow flesh on bare bones, an essential item for home and travel alike, how could he casually give it away?

Mu Na said hurriedly, "I can trade with you. You know, the things my Wood Spirit Race cultivates..."

Yang Kai raised a hand and said firmly, "There is no need to mention this again. Patriarch, if there is nothing else, this Junior will take his leave."

Hearing the chill in his voice, Mu Na abruptly came back to her senses. It seemed she had angered him. In that case, the thing even she could not identify by scent had to be precious beyond imagination.

But what in this world could be so precious, yet still beyond her ability to smell out? Names that existed only in legend flashed through her mind one after another, but none matched that peculiar scent.

Yang Kai had already walked to the tree hollow and was looking back at Mu Na.

Mu Na still wanted to persuade him, but the moment she met his eyes, she could only sigh. With a lift of her hand, the sealed tree hollow opened again, and Yang Kai rushed out. She had no choice but to open it. If she refused, Yang Kai could blast it open with one punch.

Hovering in midair, Mu Na pondered for a long time before her figure suddenly flickered and shot away.

Yang Kai returned to Xiaoxiao's tree house and checked everything over. Once he confirmed that the Space Array had been properly arranged, he walked toward the Elder's tree house.

After entering, Yang Kai said, "Elder, the matters here are settled, so I should return as well. I will have to trouble Elder to look after Xiaoxiao and keep an eye on the Blood Gate. If there is any sign of disturbance, please pay close attention."

The Elder nodded. "Rest assured. Shi Jiu is also one of my race, so naturally we will not be careless."

"Then until we meet again." Yang Kai cupped his fists and turned to leave, not even glancing at Mu Na, who sat on the Elder's shoulder.

Seeing Yang Kai about to leave, Mu Na suddenly reached out and tugged the Elder's ear. The Elder grimaced, raised a hand, and said, "Wait a moment!"

Yang Kai looked back. "Does Elder have some other matter?"

The Elder said awkwardly, "It is not me. It is Patriarch Mu Na..."

Yang Kai cut him off at once. "I already made myself very clear to Patriarch earlier. There is no need to mention this again."

Mu Na said angrily, "Boy, do you think I am trying to take advantage of you?"

Yang Kai frowned. "That is not what I mean. I simply will not trade those two things. They are of great use to me!"

"If they were useless, I would not even bother talking to you about them," Mu Na snorted, furious.

Seeing her expression, Yang Kai also felt that he might have overreacted. Cupping his fists, he said, "My apologies, Patriarch. In any case, no matter what, I cannot hand those two things over to you."

Mu Na gritted her teeth and glared at him. "Can you take proper care of them?"

Yang Kai shrugged. "At least I have not let them wither to death!"

Mu Na was so angry she turned her head away, clearly unwilling to speak with him.

The Elder said, "Both of you, calm down, calm down. Take a moment."

Yang Kai said, "I am perfectly calm. It is Patriarch who will not let this go. Elder should persuade her."

The Elder chuckled. "Do you know why moths fly into flames?"

"Because they are stupid?" Yang Kai answered offhandedly.

The corner of the Elder's eye twitched. "It is their nature. Nature is a wondrous thing. Take my Stone Puppet Race, for instance. When we encounter certain rare minerals, the urge to devour them is irresistible. As for you humans, when you see a beautiful woman, you find yourselves taking a few extra looks. When moths see an open flame, they throw themselves into it. This is all nature."

"Wolves roam the world eating meat, dogs travel ten thousand li eating filth!"

The corner of the Elder's eye twitched even harder. "That is the same principle. For the Wood Spirit Race, the rarer a plant is, the more strongly it stirs their instinct to care for it."

After all that circling around, they had ended up right back where they started.

Yang Kai was just about to speak when the Elder cut him off. "Although I do not know what Patriarch asked you for, I imagine it is something extremely valuable, something hard to part with and perhaps impossible to reveal publicly."

Yang Kai nodded. "Please forgive me, both of you."

The Elder smiled faintly. "Since it is hard to part with, then do not part with it. Patriarch has a proposal, and I hope you can hear it out patiently."

"What proposal?" Yang Kai looked curiously at Mu Na. As long as they did not bring up the previous matter again, he was willing to listen. The Wood Spirit Race's innate gifts gave them a thorough understanding of all kinds of plants. Perhaps he could even ask them for advice on how to care for the Firmament Tree and help its sealing power grow a little faster.

Mu Na kept her head turned, as if she did not want to look at him, but she still said, "I will send two clansmen over to care for those two things for you."

Yang Kai's eyes widened. "Send two clansmen?"

Yang Kai quickly calculated the gains in his head. His Small Xuan Realm contained a medicine garden, and within that garden were countless rare flowers and grasses, as well as the Firmament Tree and the Everlasting Tree. The problem was that he had always planted things there casually and had never managed the garden at all. Fortunately, it had Heavy Earth and an Earth Vein bead; otherwise, many of those spirit flowers and rare grasses would surely have withered already.

If two Wood Spirit Race clansmen managed the medicine garden for him, he would be spared the trouble, and the herbs there would likely be cared for extremely well. No one in the world was better suited to the task than Wood Spirits. In ancient times, they were natural-born medicine garden stewards. Whoever obtained a Wood Spirit no longer had to worry about the garden's affairs, and could even reap a harvest thirty percent greater than others.

His spirits rose. "Is that true?"

"Do I look like I am joking?" Mu Na said irritably.

Yang Kai rubbed his hands together, smiling so broadly his face seemed to bloom. “How could I possibly accept this? This is truly… aiya…” He had become somewhat incoherent. To be honest, he had also considered abducting a couple of Wood Spirits to take away. Such a rare race would be wasted here if they were not placed in a medicine garden to shine and make full use of their talents.

Lingxiao Palace would definitely have its own medicine garden in the future, and judging by its current scale, that garden certainly would not be small. Having Wood Spirits care for it would be ideal.

Mu Na turned to look at him with open contempt. This boy had been cold to her just moments ago, yet now he had suddenly changed his tune. He truly was hateful. How had she not noticed before?

Yang Kai raised a fist to his mouth and coughed lightly, then put on a solemn face. "May I ask what conditions Patriarch has? Let me say this first: do not set your sights on those two things."

"What conditions? What conditions could I possibly have!" Mu Na said angrily.

Yang Kai was stunned. "You mean you are giving them away for free?"

The Elder chuckled. "Did Patriarch not already state the condition just now? Let two clansmen go care for those two things."

"That counts as... a condition?" Yang Kai was so shocked his eyes nearly popped out.

This was clearly a benefit for him. How could it count as a condition?

The Elder said solemnly, "This is the nature of the Wood Spirit Race. Since Patriarch values those two things, letting them care for them is the best compensation."
