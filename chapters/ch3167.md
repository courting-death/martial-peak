## Chapter 3167 — Exterminate Them All

In the desolate Star Domain, a pitch-black Warship sailed slowly onward, silent as a massive ghost.

The skull emblem emblazoned on the Warship proclaimed its origin.

It was a Warship of the Netherworld Sect from the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

The enormous Warship drifted through the Starry Sky like a homeless wanderer, alone and adrift. Though it was only an object, it exuded an air of desolation, a tragic grandeur born of being driven to the end of the road.

It was heading toward a damaged void passage, one that had appeared only in the last two years and connected to the Great Wilderness Star Domain. As long as they could reach that damaged void passage, they would have a chance to return to the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

Ever since the Hengluo Star Domain began its counterattack five years ago, life had become unbearable for the cultivators of the Great Wilderness Star Domain. The invading enemies were cut down one after another, and panic spread through every Great Wilderness Star Domain cultivator's heart. They could not understand how the Hengluo Star Domain, which should have been powerless to resist, had suddenly risen up.

Every Sect force that had invaded this Star Domain had paid an extremely heavy price. Myriad Beasts Mountain Manor had been wiped out, the Heaven-Reliant Sword Sect had been wiped out, and the Wild Thunder Divine Cult had likewise been wiped out...

Which of these Sect forces had not stood at the peak of the Great Wilderness Star Domain? Yet before the Hengluo Star Domain, a ferocious beast that devoured men without spitting out the bones, they had not even the slightest strength to resist.

The surviving cultivators of the Great Wilderness Star Domain even suspected that this had been the Hengluo Star Domain's scheme all along.

Its show of weakness over the past dozen years had merely been bait to lure the enemy deep inside, drawing more cultivators here so they could all be exterminated.

Although the Netherworld Sect had not been wiped out, it was close. The entire Sect had only this single Warship left, and even it had been scorched by the flames of war. The Warship was pitted and scarred, stained with blood, and everywhere bore the marks of great battles.

It had escaped the encirclement only with great difficulty, then wandered through the Star Domain for a long time before finally shaking off its pursuers.

Home! They had to get home.

This Star Domain was absolutely not a place anyone could casually invade.

The damaged void passage was just a thousand li ahead. Inside the Warship, the Netherworld Sect cultivators who had barely survived could clearly see it through the crystal-polished windows. The scent of home seemed to linger at the tips of their noses, as if long-parted family were waving to them. Joy spread across every face, and their excitement was beyond words.

A white figure suddenly appeared ahead, blocking the Warship's path.

The cultivators inside the Warship quickly noticed her. One of them controlled the arrays inside the Warship, enlarging the white figure and projecting her image within. In an instant, a woman with hair like a waterfall, robes white as snow, and a peerlessly beautiful face appeared before everyone's eyes. Her expression was indifferent, but her bearing was noble and holy, enough to make anyone who looked at her feel ashamed of their own baseness.

A burst of sharp inhalations rang out. The entire Warship fell deathly silent, everyone holding their breath.

It was her!

The people of the Great Wilderness Star Domain did not know her name, but this woman was a nightmare to every last one of them.

Before her, even cultivators at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm were as fragile as children. Once that longsword began to dance, no one could stand against her.

Cold air spread unchecked through the Warship. Everyone's hands and feet turned icy, chills running through their bodies. The enormous Warship and that tiny figure made for a stark contrast, yet before her, the Warship creaked and groaned as if trembling in fear.

"You lowly slut, are you really going to wipe us out to the last?"

An old, furious voice rang out from inside the Warship.

Su Yan stood in the void, her beautiful eyes like autumn waters as she gazed at the oncoming Warship, with no intention of answering at all.

"Move aside at once, or this place will be your grave today!" the old voice threatened harshly, though the threat rang somewhat hollow.

How could Su Yan possibly move aside? She raised a fair hand and rested it on her sword hilt.

"Courting death! Ram her!" The old voice was plainly enraged. Although this woman had displayed cultivation and strength beyond ordinary people in recent years, no matter how strong an individual was, how could she contend with a Warship?

These people from the Great Wilderness Star Domain were eager to flee back home and did not want to stir up further trouble here, which was the only reason they had bothered threatening her.

Since she refused to know what was good for her, she could die here.

The Warship hummed, its speed suddenly surging as it rammed straight toward Su Yan!

The distance between them rapidly shrank. Su Yan still stood there quietly. She neither dodged nor drew her sword, and not even the slightest ripple crossed her expression, as if what was crashing toward her was not a colossal vessel, but a breeze brushing her face.

Only when it was thirty li away did a flash of sword light suddenly appear, striking the enormous Warship in an instant.

At the same moment, the thirty-li distance vanished. The Warship crashed through the place where Su Yan had stood and shot onward like wind and lightning.

"Hahaha, overestimating yourself!"

The old voice laughed without end, only for the laughter to cut off abruptly when he suddenly discovered that Su Yan, who should have been smashed to pieces, had appeared behind the Warship completely unharmed.

The longsword at her waist had been drawn at some unknown point. Now, with her back to the Warship, she sheathed it, transformed into a stream of light, and flew into the distance without looking back at the Warship even once.

"What the hell?" The owner of the old voice was utterly baffled.

But he soon realized something was wrong.

The Warship's speed suddenly slowed, as if an invisible force had bound it. At the same time, an astonishing chill spread from inside the Warship, and cracking sounds came from all around.

He turned his head to look and froze in horror.

Inside the Warship, snowflakes had appeared at some unknown point. Each one was about the size of a fist, blooming and spreading through every corner of the Warship. The arrays in operation were frozen in an instant, and the cultivators caught off guard stiffened where they stood, their bodies crawling with pure white snowflakes.

His hand suddenly went cold. The old man lowered his head, and his eyes shrank. A snowflake had appeared on the back of his hand as well, and wherever it covered, all feeling vanished in an instant. One flake, two flakes, three flakes...

It was the same inside the Warship, and the same outside.

Dense snowflakes wrapped around the Warship layer upon layer, as if a mischievous child had dressed it in an outer robe sewn from snow, turning it spotless white.

Crack, crack, crack...

The sound of shattering rang out. As it sailed on, the enormous Warship suddenly broke apart, turning into chunks of ice. None of the cultivators inside were spared. Carried onward by inertia, they sped forward and drifted forever through the endless void.

...

Several streams of light shot past like wind and lightning, chasing one another through the Star Domain. The three streams of light in front all carried deep, profound auras, each at least at the First Stage of the Void King Realm, while the leader was at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm.

The stream of light behind them had a restrained aura, with no sign of cultivation at all. What was more, the figure within it was only a little girl of seven or eight.

Yet the three fleeing ahead did not even dare look back, afraid they would see some terrifying sight. They put every thought into fleeing for their lives.

One day, two days, three days...

After a full ten days, the three fleeing Void King Realm cultivators were all somewhat exhausted. Their cultivations were not weak, but such a long flight had consumed an enormous amount of strength. If they had not been constantly taking Pills to replenish themselves, they would have died of exhaustion long ago.

They should have shaken her off, right? All three thought the same thing.

They looked back, and their souls nearly left their bodies.

That tiny figure was still trailing not far behind them like a maggot clinging to bone. Her delicate, pink-cheeked face was expressionless, her clear black-and-white eyes deep as they reflected their terrified faces.

Even more despairing was that after such a long chase, she had not shown any strain at all. Her face was not flushed, her heart was not racing, and she remained as indifferent and cold as when they had first seen her.

A monster! This little girl was simply a monster.

"Fight her!" The leader made a snap decision, spun around, and struck a palm toward Liu Yan.

The other two also knew fleeing was useless and immediately joined in. In an instant, an irresistible force like a rolling flood drowned that tiny figure.

Did they kill her? Joy rose in the three men's hearts, but once they saw clearly, that joy turned into deep despair.

"So boring." A childish voice rang out, as if she had grown tired of some game, and Liu Yan slightly raised one hand.

An immense sense of crisis enveloped the three of them, locking their bodies rigid so they could not move at all.

A cluster of flame ignited, leaving a crimson trail in the three men's eyes. It was the last sight they saw before death.

...

In the Starry Sky, Li Jiao's family lived in cheerful harmony. Although Yang Kai had assigned them an extremely dull task and they had been stationed here for seven or eight years, neither Li Jiao nor Lu Sanniang found it tedious.

Li Jiao had even found a huge meteorite from who knew where and set it in the Starry Sky, then built a simple stone house on it. The family of three lived there together.

A thousand li behind the meteorite lay a deep, chaotic Void Passage.

This was the passage the Great Wilderness Star Domain had first used to invade the Hengluo Star Domain. The people of the Great Wilderness Star Domain had relied on this very passage to reach this world.

Li Jiao had guarded this place for seven or eight years and killed countless cultivators coming over from the Great Wilderness Star Domain. These fellows had probably heard that the cultivators of the Hengluo Star Domain were easy to bully and had come here one after another from the far side of the Star Domain. Unfortunately for them, before they could gain any benefits, Li Jiao sent them off to meet the King of Hell.

He did not kill only the cultivators coming from the Great Wilderness Star Domain. Even more of his victims were cultivators trying to flee back to the Great Wilderness Star Domain.

In recent years, the Hengluo Star Domain had risen in counterattack, working with one heart, and it also had help from several extremely powerful allies. The Great Wilderness Star Domain cultivators were no match for them at all. The Cultivation Stars they had seized earlier had been recovered one by one, and they now fled for their lives like stray dogs.

Many wanted to flee back, but with Li Jiao guarding this place, who could escape?

If one came, he killed one; if two came, he killed the pair. Li Jiao was like a millstone blocking the Void Passage. Whether they were coming or going, all were ground into minced meat, and not one survived.

Since Yang Kai's return, the turbulent situation in the Star Domain had stabilized in a mere seven or eight years. The invading enemies had basically been exterminated, and even if a few fish slipped through the net, they could no longer stir up any waves.
