## Chapter 3178 — Is It Fun?

The devouring did more than lift Yang Kai's mood. The entire Hengluo Star Domain seemed to ripple with joy. A world had a will of its own, and it could naturally sense that what Yang Kai was doing would benefit it.

As time passed, the devouring grew faster and faster. Even when Wu Heng did everything in his power to stop it and contend with it, nothing worked. He could only watch his territory slip away before his eyes, his heart filled with grief.

At a certain moment, another idea suddenly arose in Yang Kai’s mind. While maintaining the Star Domain’s devouring, he considered whether this idea was feasible, and the more he thought about it, the more excellent it seemed, until he could hardly resist giving himself a hundred praises.

He opened his eyes and looked toward the Incarnation.

While he had been refining and devouring, the Incarnation had remained beside him as his Protector, never once leaving his side. The moment their eyes met, the Incarnation understood what wicked idea he was plotting. It nodded slightly, raised a hand, and opened its palm to Yang Kai.

Yang Kai urged the Star Domain Origin and forced it out of his Sea of Consciousness. A radiant Star Chart, shrunk countless times over, appeared in his palm and bloomed with dazzling light.

The Incarnation took it, swallowed it in one gulp, and sat cross-legged in the Starry Sky. Only after quite some time did it open its eyes and nod to Yang Kai, indicating that everything had gone smoothly.

At this very moment, the Incarnation had temporarily replaced Yang Kai as the Master of Hengluo Star Domain.

Only the Incarnation could be accepted by the Star Domain Origin so easily. No one else could have done it. Anyone else would likely have died from the backlash the instant they swallowed that Star Chart.

After all, strictly speaking, the Incarnation's consciousness had been transformed from Yang Kai's Soul Incarnation. The two came from the same source, and their auras matched, so the Star Domain Origin naturally would not reject it too strongly. On top of that, the devouring and refining during this period had let the Star Domain Origin taste the benefits, making the Incarnation's temporary role as Master of the Star Domain even more natural.

Moreover, the Incarnation had cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art for many years, and its understanding and use of this Secret Art far surpassed Yang Kai's. If it urged the Star Domain Origin to devour and refine, the efficiency would obviously be much higher than Yang Kai's.

With his hands now free, Yang Kai turned toward the darkness and bared his teeth in a ferocious grin. "Wu Heng, I'm coming!"

Although devouring and refining the other party's Star Domain let Yang Kai vent a great deal of anger, it had not truly caused Wu Heng himself much harm. Perhaps only after the entire Great Wilderness Star Domain had been thoroughly refined would it damage his foundation.

How could Yang Kai have so much time to waste on him? If he had said he would beat Wu Heng so badly even his mother would not recognize him, then that was exactly what he would do. Clashing across a World Barrier like this, even while holding the upper hand, offered no real satisfaction.

How could one catch tiger cubs without entering the tiger’s den?

Yang Kai would charge into that dragon's pool and tiger's den once more! This time, however, would be different from the last. Last time, he had gone to save Li Jiao and Lu Sanniang and withdrawn the moment he succeeded. This time, he was going to turn Great Wilderness Star Domain upside down!

Of course, there was another reason as well: Yang Kai had to find a way to repair the Small Xuan Realm.

As the saying went, the wool came from the sheep's back. Since the Small Xuan Realm had been damaged because of Wu Heng, Yang Kai naturally had to find a way to repair it from Wu Heng's side, and Great Wilderness Star Domain certainly did not lack materials capable of repairing the Small Xuan Realm.

As soon as he finished speaking, Yang Kai plunged headfirst into the darkness without the slightest hesitation.

In Great Wilderness Star Domain, within the Starry Sky, the black hole had already spread a million li wide. Even the nearby Dead Stars had been swallowed cleanly, leaving no trace behind.

Wu Heng kept retreating, using every trick he had to resist the black hole, yet all he could do was slow the devouring.

A moment ago, the devouring had suddenly stopped for an instant. Before he could rejoice, however, it started again, and this time it was even faster than before.

Naturally, he knew nothing about the change in status between Yang Kai and the Incarnation. Now that the Incarnation was the one urging the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, the terrifying power of this Secret Art had immediately risen to another level.

Just as shock and uncertainty gripped him, a figure suddenly flashed out from the enormous black hole.

Wu Heng focused his gaze, and his eyes instantly went red. Gritting his teeth, he roared, "Bastard, you still dare come over!"

Who else could this person be but Yang Kai? Wu Heng had never imagined this guy would actually dare come over again. Did he not know how to write the word death? Very soon, however, another thought struck him. If Yang Kai was here, why had the devouring on the other side become faster?

Logically speaking, if the Master of a Star Domain was not personally presiding over the Star Domain, there was absolutely no way to make it devour another. Yet Yang Kai had clearly crossed over and appeared right before his eyes.

Could it be that he was not the Master of that Star Domain? Had Wu Heng been wrong all along?

No! During their brief exchange last time, Yang Kai had indeed used the power of the Star Domain. Otherwise, he could never have sent Wu Heng flying with a single punch.

When enemies met, their eyes burned all the redder. With no time to ponder too much, Wu Heng roared ferociously, “Since you have come, then die here!”

After suffering two hidden losses at Yang Kai's hands, how could he dare be careless now? The instant he made a move, he used his full strength, reaching out with one large hand to seize Yang Kai through the air before fiercely clenching his fist.

Yang Kai's entire body was immediately imprisoned. Cracks rang out from all his bones, and he spat a mouthful of Golden Blood.

Having angered Wu Heng on his own territory, Yang Kai naturally had to suffer for it. Yet he remained calm, merely baring his teeth in a smile at Wu Heng.

That smile was full of ill intent, and Wu Heng felt a sudden jolt at the sight of it. He could not shake the feeling that Yang Kai was about to pull some trick again.

In the next moment, Wu Heng's expression changed drastically.

In that instant, as he split his attention to deal with Yang Kai, the strength he used to contend with the Star Domain on the other side weakened. As a result, the confrontation between the two Star Domains collapsed like a routed army. He had not been its match before either, but at the very least, while he presided over matters, he had been able to restrain the devouring speed somewhat.

The moment he split his attention here, the devouring over there instantly ran wild, expanding by a thousand li in a flash. Compared with this, its previous speed had been no better than a crawling turtle.

In order to restrain the devouring as much as possible, he had not been too far from the black hole. Boundless darkness spread toward him and nearly swallowed him as well.

Wu Heng was badly startled and hurriedly retreated, opening a gap of several thousand li at once. Not daring to delay, he hastily communicated with the Star Domain Origin again and pressed down on the darkness.

The devouring slowed, but Yang Kai seized the chance to break free.

He did not flee. He simply stood there with his arms folded, watching Wu Heng at his leisure, teasing written all over his face. "Is it fun?"

"You're courting death!" Veins throbbed wildly on Wu Heng's forehead, and killing intent surged like a tide.

Yang Kai chuckled, wiped the blood from the corner of his mouth, and said, "Then please hurry up and kill me!"

The corner of Wu Heng's eye twitched fiercely.

He had seen Yang Kai's methods and knew that Yang Kai seemed to carry the Dragon Clan bloodline. Leaving aside what consequences might follow from killing such a person, even he was not confident he could kill Yang Kai quickly.

The force of that grasp just now had been anything but ordinary, yet the other party had only suffered a slight injury. Clearly, Yang Kai's ability to withstand pressure far exceeded Wu Heng's imagination.

And if he could not kill Yang Kai quickly, then while he was distracted, the other Star Domain's devouring speed would certainly surge. Not only would that endanger his Star Domain, it might even leave an indelible wound on Wu Heng himself.

Frustration weighed on Wu Heng's chest like a mountain, almost making it impossible to breathe. He had never imagined he would suffer such a great loss in this Star Domain. What exactly was Yang Kai's background? Why had he never heard this name before? If Yang Kai was a rising talent, then his strength was far too astonishing.

"Looks like you're all talk after all."

Yang Kai seemed not to sense his murderous intent at all and kept adding fuel to the fire, as if he would not stop until Wu Heng was thoroughly enraged. It was like walking a tightrope along a sheer cliff. His courage was so great that even Wu Heng had to admire it.

“But I am not merely saying it.” Yang Kai slowly shook his head, gazing coldly at Wu Heng as he said unhurriedly, “Wait here. I will come back to kill you.” As he spoke, he held two fingers together like a blade and drew them across his own neck.

With that, he turned and left!

"Wait!" Wu Heng barked. With a thought, the space around Yang Kai transformed into a cage, imprisoning him.

But the instant Wu Heng split his attention, the black hole began expanding faster again. In the blink of an eye, it spread to his side, leaving him flustered and hard-pressed to cope.

Yang Kai turned his head and looked at him sidelong. "If you have something to say, say it. If you have a fart, let it out!"

Wu Heng's anger flared three zhang high. When had anyone ever dared show him such disrespect? Ever since he became Master of this Star Domain, he had felt as if he controlled Heaven and Earth. Everyone who dared disrespect him had died.

Of course, he wanted Yang Kai dead too. In this stretch of Heaven and Earth, perhaps he did have the ability, but the price would be so great that even he could not bear it.

A madman with nothing to lose! Those words sprang into Wu Heng's mind.

Taking a deep breath, Wu Heng suppressed the anger in his chest and said in a low voice, "As the saying goes, enmity is better resolved than deepened. Earlier, this Lord wanted to devour Hengluo Star Domain only because this Lord sensed that it was ownerless. Now that Your Excellency has become the Master of that Star Domain, this Lord naturally will not act against it again. Let us stop fighting and make peace. Otherwise, if this battle continues, only the billions of living beings in these two Star Domains will suffer."

Yang Kai widened his eyes and looked at him in surprise. "Are you begging for mercy?"

Wu Heng said solemnly, "This Lord is merely proposing a way to resolve the problem." Begging for mercy? What kind of joke was that? If they truly fought, it was still unclear who would live and who would die. He simply did not want to make a powerful enemy.

"Just proposing, huh..." Yang Kai looked disappointed and shook his head hard. "No good, no good."

"What is no good about it? Stopping the fight and making peace benefits both you and me." Wu Heng asked patiently.

Yang Kai said, "You drove the people of Great Wilderness Star Domain to run rampant through my territory. Countless people under this Young Master were killed or wounded, and living beings were plunged into misery. If we stop now, wouldn't I be taking a huge loss?"

Wu Heng disagreed inwardly, but on the surface he nodded and said, "That is also reasonable. If you find it improper, this Lord can offer some compensation."

"What compensation?"

Wu Heng said, "The Starry Sky that has been devoured will belong to you."

Yang Kai laughed. "That was mine to begin with. Can you take it back?"

The Starry Sky digested through the Heaven Devouring Battle Art had long since fused with Hengluo Star Domain. No matter how heaven-defying Wu Heng's methods were, he could forget about separating it again.

"Then what do you want?" Wu Heng asked in a deep voice.
