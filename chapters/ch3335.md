## Chapter 3335 - Old Witch

Anger erupted in Yang Kai's chest like a volcano. He stepped forward, pointed at Fu Zhun, and cursed, "Do not think you can do whatever you like just because you are the Dragon Clan's Second Elder. Qing'er may be of the Dragon Clan, but she has her own freedom, and it is not your place to meddle. If I am her husband for one day, I am her husband for life, and I will not let an old witch like you deny it. We came today to pay our respects to you, giving you face, but since you refuse that face, so be it. In any case, my wife and I do not need you to acknowledge anything."

The moment those words left his mouth, Zhu Qing knew things had gone wrong and sighed inwardly.

Sure enough, Fu Zhun's aura stirred, and the already frigid world grew colder still. She narrowed her eyes. "Boy, what did you call me?"

"Old witch, old witch, old witch, old witch, old witch, old witch!" Yang Kai rattled it off without hesitation, staring at her defiantly. "Enjoy that? If it was not enough, I can shout it a few more times."

Fu Zhun slowly rose and said in an icy voice, "If you still have your life after today, I imagine you will remember the lesson that disaster comes from the mouth."

Yang Kai gave a vicious laugh. "Want a fight? Come on, then. Let us see whether you die or I perish."

As soon as he finished speaking, a dragon roar exploded. The phantom of the Golden Sacred Dragon behind him lunged into Yang Kai's body, and Dragon Pressure swept out in an instant. At the same time, a resonant hum came from the distant Dragon Temple, and dragon roars rang out one after another between Heaven and Earth.

All of Dragon Isle seemed to tremble. Wherever they were, every giant dragon turned as one toward the Dragon Temple, astonishment written across their faces.

Yang Kai was surprised as well. He had not expected that activating the Golden Sacred Dragon Origin Power here would resonate with the Dragon Temple. Now he felt even more secure, and he laughed loudly. "Looks like the ancestors of the Dragon Clan are all standing on my side."

For all his bold words, he was like a leopard poised to strike, every thread of his aura drawn tight. At the slightest trigger, he could unleash a thunderous blow. The woman opposite him was the Dragon Clan's Second Elder, after all, an existence capable of trading blows with Great Emperors. How could he dare be careless?

To Yang Kai's surprise, Fu Zhun did not attack at once. She only stared at him coldly, killing intent swirling in her eyes, almost solid enough to take shape.

Zhu Qing flashed between them and said urgently, "Second Elder, please calm your anger. Husband did not mean it. Please do not take offense."

She turned and glared at Yang Kai, her eyes full of reproach. Her meaning could not have been clearer: before they came, they had plainly agreed that he had to endure, yet the instant they met, he had torn all face apart.

Yang Kai looked embarrassed. Seeing Zhu Qing put in such a difficult position, he could not help sniffing, and even his imposing manner shrank by a notch.

"If he did not mean it, did it just slip out by accident?" Fu Zhun looked coldly at Zhu Qing. "If I insist on killing him today, what will you do?"

Zhu Qing started and said in a strained voice, "Born a member of the Dragon Clan, I naturally ought to obey the Dragon Clan's rules. But if Husband is in danger, Qing'er cannot stand by and do nothing. If Second Elder insists on this, then Qing'er can only live and die with Husband!"

Moved, Yang Kai looked at her and tugged at her sleeve. "Qing'er, step aside. If this old witch wants to kill me, I'm afraid she is dreaming."

"Vile creature!" Fu Zhun's face darkened. She had never imagined that Zhu Qing would actually give such an answer. Drawing a deep breath, she said, "It seems Dragon Isle can no longer keep your heart. How can you still count as a member of the Dragon Clan?"

Zhu Qing said miserably, "Second Elder, please forgive me."

Yang Kai said, "Since you admit Dragon Isle cannot keep Qing'er's heart, why not set her free and let her leave with me, Second Elder? Wouldn't that be helping others fulfill a beautiful thing? In the future, we husband and wife will definitely set up a longevity tablet for you. One stick of incense morning and night, three kowtows at dawn and dusk. We will bless Second Elder with a lifespan equal to the Heavens and eternal youth."

Fu Zhun snorted. "Spare me your glib tongue. Even if I cannot keep her heart, I will keep her person. In life, she belongs to the Dragon Clan; in death, she will be a ghost of the Dragon Clan. It is not your turn to take advantage."

Yang Kai frowned. "A melon twisted off by force is not sweet."

Fu Zhun sneered again and again. "So what?"

Yang Kai looked at her calmly. "Is there really no room to turn this around?" Fu Zhun's hard stance had exhausted his patience for talking, while Zhu Qing's attempts to stop them kept the two from truly fighting. The anger in Yang Kai's chest had nowhere to go.

"You want room to turn things around?" Fu Zhun snorted. "There is."

Zhu Qing's eyes lit up. "Second Elder, please make it clear!"

Fu Zhun said, "As long as this man can make up for the losses he caused my Dragon Isle, I can naturally let bygones be bygones. I can even fulfill this good matter between you and let the two of you fly together!"

The last sentence sent Zhu Qing into delight. Second Elder was actually willing to fulfill their wish; that was something she had hardly dared to imagine. Yet the condition before it left her somewhat bewildered. "Dragon Isle's losses? May I ask what losses Second Elder is referring to... specifically?"

Fu Zhun smiled mockingly. "Could he really not know what losses he brought upon Dragon Isle? Why ask me?"

Yang Kai said angrily, "Even if there really were losses, you brought them on yourselves. Never mind that this old master lacks the ability to make up for them. Even if I did, I still would not!"

Fu Zhun's eyelid twitched. "In front of whom are you calling yourself old master?"

Yang Kai shouted, "This old master says what he likes. What are you going to do about it?"

"Boy, die!" Fu Zhun flew into a rage. The killing intent she had barely suppressed erupted again. This time, even with Zhu Qing blocking her, she truly meant to attack.

Zhu Qing hastily grabbed Yang Kai's clothes, flashed away, and flew outside, calling back, "Second Elder, please calm your anger. We have imposed on you today." She fled with remarkable speed, naturally knowing that if they did not leave now, a real fight was about to break out.

Fu Zhun remained where she was, her face full of anger. She had clearly not been angered lightly, but she showed no intention of chasing after them. She only glared hatefully in the direction where the two had disappeared.

A short while later, a figure flashed, and Great Elder Zhu Yan suddenly appeared beside Fu Zhun. "They left?"

Fu Zhun took a deep breath, calming the roiling Qi and blood in her chest, and slowly closed her eyes. "I truly want to kill him!"

Zhu Yan smiled. "What would killing him solve? Besides, that is the Ancestral Dragon Origin. You sensed the Dragon Temple's reaction just now as well. With the many Dragon Souls of the Dragon Temple protecting him, even if you and I joined hands, we might not be able to kill him on Dragon Isle. If you want to kill him, it can only be done outside Dragon Isle."

Fu Zhun snorted. "If not for that, how would he still be alive?"

Zhu Yan stared at her in astonishment. "You do not truly want to kill him, do you?"

Fu Zhun clenched her teeth. "So what if I do?"

Cold sweat poured down Zhu Yan's face. "It is fine to think about it, but you absolutely must not really act on it. The future of my Dragon Clan depends on him. You have always taken the revival of the Dragon Clan as your duty. Back then, you endured the pain of exiling Fu Xuan into the Dragon Tomb for this very reason, did you not? A little impatience will spoil great plans."

Fu Zhun nodded. "I understand the reasoning..." But when she faced that boy, she could not suppress her anger. With him calling her old witch again and again, it would be difficult for anyone not to develop killing intent. After a pause, she said, "I have already told him the conditions. Whether he can meet them will depend on his own ability."

Zhu Yan grinned. "As long as you told him. Given how much he values Qing'er, I imagine he will certainly work hard to accomplish it."

Fu Zhun suddenly turned to look at him. "If he fails, I will strip the Ancestral Dragon Origin from his body. A boy of such conduct is not worthy of possessing the Ancestral Dragon Origin!"

Zhu Yan's expression twitched. Noncommittally, he said, "We will see when the time comes." Then he raised his head, gazed up into the air, and sighed. "The Ancestral Dragon Origin has appeared, but where is the Ancestral Phoenix Origin?"

...

Yang Kai and Zhu Qing sped along.

Zhu Qing said nothing the entire way, her pretty face so gloomy it seemed almost covered in frost.

Yang Kai whistled as though nothing were wrong, though in truth he was carefully watching her expression the whole time. Seeing her like this, he knew things were bad. His behavior just now had certainly disappointed her, but if one really got down to it, he was at most only half responsible. The other half was entirely because of that wretch Fu Zhun.

"Qing'er, why don't I just take you away and elope? Why worry about so much? Can that old witch really keep an eye on the two of us forever? Once we are outside, with the sky high and the earth vast, where would she even find you?" Yang Kai suggested.

Zhu Qing sighed. "I can certainly leave with you, but if we truly did that, I'm afraid I would never be at peace for the rest of my life."

Eloping might be beautiful, but without the elders’ approval, it was destined to leave regrets.

Zhu Qing stopped, gazed tenderly at Yang Kai, and said, "Fortunately, neither Great Elder nor Second Elder has any intention of forbidding you from coming to Dragon Isle again. In the future, if you miss me, come to Dragon Isle more often to see me. I will wait for you here."

Yang Kai opened his mouth, but swallowed the persuasion on the tip of his tongue. Zhu Qing was not a child. Since she had such concerns, even if he truly took her away from Dragon Isle, she would be unable to eat or sleep in peace afterward. It would be better to let her remain on Dragon Isle. At the very least, she would not have to suffer that kind of torment.

He smiled freely. "Then this time, I will stay here on Dragon Isle and keep you company a while longer." He reached out and took Zhu Qing's soft hand, then sped side by side with her toward Crescent Island, neither of them speaking along the way.

When they returned to Crescent Island, the sky was already dark. Fu Xuan and Mo Xiaoqi had actually not left. The mother and daughter had been sitting together and talking all this time, and it was clear they were getting along very well. Fu Xuan's face shone with maternal love, and Mo Xiaoqi looked utterly happy.

When Mo Xiaoqi saw Yang Kai and Zhu Qing appear together, her expression dimmed for a moment, but she quickly beamed and called out, “Eldest Brother Yang, Elder Sister Qing.”

Fu Xuan smiled from the side. "Xiaoqi insisted on waiting for you to return so she could say farewell before she would come back to the island with me." As she spoke, she gently patted Mo Xiaoqi's head. "Now it is time to go back and rest, isn't it?"

Mo Xiaoqi nodded, looked at Yang Kai, and said, "Then I will go over there with Mother first. Big Brother Yang, when you leave, remember to tell me."

"Do not worry." Yang Kai smiled and nodded. "When the time comes, I will definitely let you know."

"Goodbye, Eldest Brother Yang, Elder Sister Qing." Mo Xiaoqi waved gently and followed Fu Xuan out of Crescent Island, looking back every few steps.

After the mother and daughter left, Yang Kai suddenly flashed to Zhu Qing's side and swept her up by the waist.

Zhu Qing cried out in surprise, her arms instinctively circling his neck. Their eyes met, and how could she not know what he wanted? Rosy color immediately flooded her cheeks, and she jerked her chin toward the bedroom.

Yang Kai laughed loudly, strode to the bedroom door with the gait of a dragon and the stride of a tiger, kicked it open, tossed Zhu Qing onto the fragrant bed, and threw himself after her.

It was a night of spring passion, the man stirring wind and waves while the tender beauty yielded in soft delight.
