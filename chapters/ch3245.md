## Chapter 3245 — Admit Defeat

Having escaped danger, Yu Zhuo did not pause for breath. She flicked the soft whip in her hand, and it snapped straight like a Spirit Snake leaving its hole, stabbing toward the black-clothed man's back with unstoppable momentum.

The black-clothed man's saber cut through empty air. The moment he lost sight of his target, he sensed something was wrong, spun around, and slashed out without even looking.

Clang...

A heavy crash rang out. The black-clothed man merely swayed once and was otherwise unharmed.

Yu Zhuo, on the other hand, was blasted back by a tremendous rebound. She coughed up a mouthful of blood in midair before stumbling to the ground.

Yang Kai secretly thought it was a pity. If Yu Zhuo had the same cultivation as her opponent, that strike would have been enough to decide the match. But because she was one Stage lower, and because she had struck in haste, she failed to seize such a fine opportunity and instead suffered minor injuries from the countershock.

In the span of a spark from flint, both of them had already brushed against defeat. The exchange was truly dazzling, leaving the spectators hard-pressed to follow it.

Yang Kai could not help paying closer attention. He had not expected to run into two people like this in the very first match on the first day. He could tell that both Yu Zhuo and the black-clothed man were outstanding among Dao Origin Realm cultivators. Even within the three top forces, they would be treated as disciples worthy of focused training. He truly wondered how they had cultivated such solid foundations.

He suddenly realized he might have underestimated the Sects of the world. Any Sect that could stand for millions of years and pass down its inheritance had to have a reason for existing. It was not only the top Sects that could produce outstanding talents.

In a daze, he thought again of Luo Yuan from Eight Directions Sect. Back then, the Four Seasons Land trial had made two people's reputations. One was Yang Kai himself; with Dao Origin Realm cultivation, he had refined a heaven-defying treasure like the Supreme Mystic Pill, so avoiding attention would have been impossible. The other was Luo Yuan of Eight Directions Sect.

In the Southern Territory, Eight Directions Sect counted as a small Sect, inferior even to the Biyu Sect of those days. Yet Luo Yuan, who came from Eight Directions Sect, had not feared even Wuchang. He had fought him head-on and had barely fallen behind, which showed just how formidable his own strength was.

Now that he thought of Luo Yuan, he had indeed seen someone yesterday who looked somewhat like him. The distance had simply been too great, and he had not paid close attention, so he did not know whether it was really Luo Yuan.

Perhaps it really was that fellow. After all, Eight Directions Sect was also from the Southern Territory. There was no reason for him not to come take part in the Martial Gathering.

As his thoughts drifted, Yu Zhuo and the black-clothed man clashed again. On the arena, their figures wove back and forth, saber and whip meeting again and again with a steady din of ringing impacts.

One fought with fierce momentum, the other with nimble movement. They traded blows evenly, the battle deadlocked rather than one-sided. The commotion here drew many cultivators over to watch, and before long, Arena Eight was surrounded by people. Every one of them looked gravely toward the stage, secretly wondering how they would win if they faced either of these opponents.

Yang Kai also watched with keen interest. If Lingxiao Palace had any need to open its doors and accept disciples right now, he would probably have Hua Qingsi organize a competition like this as well. Although such competitions were somewhat tedious, they were indeed the best way to select outstanding talents. Temperament and character, however, were another matter.

After an incense stick's worth of time, the black-clothed man's expression grew increasingly savage. In his view, his opponent's cultivation was lower, so he should have been able to deal with her easily. In reality, this woman darting all over the place was the strongest opponent he had met so far. That left him feeling rather humiliated, and his attacks grew fiercer and fiercer.

As for Yu Zhuo, although she relied on her quick reactions and agile movement skill to keep circling him, the gap in cultivation remained her greatest weakness. Lasting this long was already no easy feat; victory was nearly impossible. Her twin ponytails were in disarray, many strands cut short by the other party's saber qi, leaving her looking rather battered. She panted heavily, her chest rising and falling, fine beads of sweat on her face, yet she gritted her teeth and refused to admit defeat.

But Yang Kai could clearly see that the hand gripping her long whip was trembling nonstop. With each clash against the other party's great saber, a wild, fierce force traveled through the whip. How could she withstand that for long?

The black-clothed man clearly knew to use his strength against her weakness, and he gave Yu Zhuo no chance to catch her breath, forcing her to clash with him head-on.

At last, when the great saber met the soft whip once more, a faint snap rang out. The saber had cut the whip in two, and the front half shot away.

The black-clothed man was overjoyed. He had long since had enough of that whip. Now that he had broken this Secret Treasure, how could she still have any strength to fight back? He looked up, coldness filling his eyes, and saw panic flash across Yu Zhuo's face as she hurriedly tried to retreat.

But how could she retreat now?

"Die!" the black-clothed man roared, chopping down with his great saber, certain victory written across his face.

He had won. It truly had not been easy!

Before the thought had fully formed, however, he saw the panic vanish from Yu Zhuo's face. A sly glint flashed through her eyes, and she formed a seal with one hand. Behind him came the sound of something slicing through the air.

Not good! The black-clothed man's expression changed. Although he did not know exactly what trick Yu Zhuo had used, it was obvious he had carelessly fallen into her trap. It was too late to withdraw now. A vicious light flashed in his eyes, and he ignored everything else, bringing his great saber down even faster.

In her haste, Yu Zhuo used the half of the long whip in her hand to block, but the other party's all-out strike came too fiercely for her to stop.

The great saber cleaved down from her shoulder to her abdomen, nearly splitting her in two. An irresistible force slammed into her, and she sprayed a mouthful of blood as she flew backward like a torn sack.

While still in midair, the seal in her hand changed again.

The black-clothed man felt his body tighten and finally understood what Yu Zhuo had been up to.

The half of the soft whip he had sent flying had actually coiled around him while he was unprepared, binding him tight before he could react. A strange force surged from the whip, suppressing his cultivation and even making the Origin Qi in his body sluggish. When he looked up again, despair filled his face.

That was because Yu Zhuo, even as she flew backward, had flicked the remaining half of the soft whip in her hand and thrown it straight at his forehead.

If it struck him in his current state, he would undoubtedly die.

"I admit defeat!" he hurriedly shouted.

The long whip suddenly stopped one inch from his forehead. It had come within a hair's breadth of piercing straight through his head. The energy streaming from the whip tip stabbed at his forehead like an awl, hurting enough to make cold sweat break out all over his body.

Yu Zhuo had already thrown the half-length whip from her hand, so naturally it was not her control that stopped it in front of him. That was Yang Kai's doing.

The instant he shouted his surrender, Yang Kai reached out and clenched his hand, seizing the soft whip from afar and saving his life.

At the same time, Yang Kai lifted his other hand, and Yu Zhuo's flying body froze in midair.

Still badly shaken, the black-clothed man looked over, and his expression turned extremely grim. Yu Zhuo was hovering right at the edge of the arena, only the tiniest bit from leaving its bounds.

He had fallen short at the very last moment! If he had shouted just a little slower, Yu Zhuo would undoubtedly have lost.

But at the critical moment between life and death, how could he have dared hesitate? Regretting it now was useless.

When Yang Kai loosened his hand, the soft whip that had stopped before the black-clothed man's forehead fell to the ground. With a slight draw of his other hand, Yu Zhuo, supported by an invisible force, slowly floated over and landed in front of him.

Yang Kai lowered his head to look at the woman at his feet and frowned.

This first match could only be called brutal. Although the black-clothed man had not suffered any serious injury, he had taken a walk before the gates of hell. After this painful lesson, he likely would not dare look down on people with weaker cultivation again. Yu Zhuo was in far worse shape. She had already been at a disadvantage in a head-on clash, and now both her arms trembled uncontrollably. From her shoulder to the side of her waist ran a bloody wound, blood gurgling out of it, a sight too miserable to bear.

This already counted as a serious injury. Although her life was not in danger, without proper treatment, she would have no way to face the battles ahead.

With a soft sigh, Yang Kai crouched down. He pressed one hand to Yu Zhuo's shoulder and urged his Emperor Yuan to stabilize her injury, while his other hand discreetly flicked out a Healing Pill and slipped it into her mouth.

Yu Zhuo's eyes were somewhat dazed. Clearly, the blow just now had been heavy, and she had not quite recovered. When she saw that Yang Kai was treating her, she could not help feeling a little embarrassed and asked in a trembling voice, "Did I... win?"

Yang Kai looked at her expressionlessly and announced, "Number Three-Zero-One-Eight, victory!"

At those words, delighted surprise lit Yu Zhuo's eyes, and she smiled happily.

When the black-clothed man shouted his surrender, she had not yet fallen out of the arena, so naturally she was the winner.

Very quickly, however, Yu Zhuo could no longer smile. A sharp pain flared from the wound at her waist. Forcing herself to stay conscious, she raised her head for a look and immediately blushed.

Where the black-clothed man had cut her, her clothes hung in tatters, exposing the delicate dudou beneath. Others might not have seen it because of the angle, but the judge was crouching right in front of her. How could he not?

For an instant, Yu Zhuo felt the urge to faint on the spot.

Yang Kai truly had not noticed. He had only thought this woman had fought far too desperately. Only after he saw that her expression was wrong did he follow her gaze. His face twitched. He took a piece of clothing from his Spatial Ring, covered the wound for her, then looked down and called, "Who is from her Sect?"

Two rather attractive women immediately rushed up and cupped their fists. "Senior."

"Take her down to heal," Yang Kai said with a wave of his hand.

The two women thanked him, collected Yu Zhuo's Secret Treasure, then supported her from either side and helped her down.

Beneath the arena, there was absolute silence. When Yu Zhuo came down, everyone involuntarily parted to either side, opening a path for her, every face filled with respect.

On the arena, the defeated black-clothed man stood for a while, then shook his head dejectedly, cupped his fist to Yang Kai, and flew down as well. He had lost his first battle, but for him, it was not without gain. There would still be chances later, so he was not too worried.

Yang Kai stood on the arena, fully assuming the bearing of a judge, his face stern and meticulous as he held the jade slip and called out again, "Number Six-Zero-Five-One versus Number Eight-Zero-Three-Six."
