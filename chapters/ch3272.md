## Chapter 3272 — Sure Enough, There Was a Problem

The little black dog Guixu had followed as well, cradled in Liu Yan's arms. It bared its teeth and growled nonstop at the dozen or so people in the second group, who seemed deeply afraid of it. Terror lay hidden in every pair of eyes.

Yang Kai glanced over thoughtfully, then fixed his gaze on the seven or eight people in the first group and asked, "Have all of them been possessed by Demonic Intent?"

Wen Zishan nodded gravely. "That's right. See if you can save them."

“Difficult.” Yang Kai slowly shook his head. Although he had not tried, he knew that those demonized cultivators possessed by Demonic Intent were fundamentally different from those eroded by Demonic Qi. Even with his ancient Shamanic Art, there was probably little hope.

"Please try," Ma Qing pleaded. One of them was an Elder of Tianwu Sacred Land, and he truly could not bear to watch him fall here.

Yang Kai nodded. "Very well, but Saint Lord Ma shouldn't expect too much."

"It is enough that Elder Yang does his best." Ma Qing smiled bitterly.

Yang Kai rose and walked over to the demonized cultivators, studying them with great interest. To be honest, even now, he could not see the slightest trace of anything wrong with them. They looked no different from ordinary people. No wonder Wuhua Hall had fallen while the entire Southern Territory remained in the dark.

Among the seven or eight were men and women, elders and youths. Yang Kai circled them once, then casually stopped before a young man. Their eyes met, and clear nervousness showed in the young man's gaze.

Yang Kai gave him a faint smile. An incantation rose from his mouth as he pointed a finger at the young man's forehead. Milky-white light bloomed at his fingertip and struck the young man between the brows.

The halo washed over him. In an instant, the young man trembled like a sieve, his face twisting into a hideous snarl. Beast-like howls tore from his throat as he struggled with all his strength, but many restrictions had long since been placed on him. How could he possibly break free?

Under the Exorcism Spell, Demonic Qi soon coiled around the young man. As the spell took effect, the Demonic Qi on his body continually dispersed, but his vitality and spirit withered along with it. In an extremely short time, his face turned pale, and he looked as if he had just been fished out of water, his clothes soaked with sweat.

A moment later, his eyes rolled back, and he fainted.

Yang Kai withdrew his hand, stood still, and slowly shook his head.

At this, Ma Qing and the others let out a long sigh. They had prepared themselves before coming, but only after seeing with their own eyes that Yang Kai's method was useless against these demonized cultivators did they finally give up.

After all, they had been possessed by Demonic Intent. Their own wills had long since been devoured, and these demonized cultivators had changed at the root. Even if Yang Kai's Exorcism Spell drove out their Demonic Qi, it would also damage their foundations. Even if every trace of Demonic Qi could be purged, these people would become walking corpses, left without thoughts of their own.

Yang Kai understood this better than anyone. He still remembered vividly how Feng Ming had once taken out a Demon Seed to corrode his Sea of Consciousness and tried to possess him.

"There is no saving them." Yang Kai glanced at Ma Qing. "Did you get anything useful out of them?"

Wen Zishan sighed. "No. These bastards keep their mouths sealed and face death without flinching. We can't get anything out of them."

"Is that so?" Yang Kai stretched a hand toward Liu Yan, and Liu Yan immediately came forward and handed him Guixu.

Yang Kai held Guixu in one hand and stood before the demonized cultivators. Sweeping his gaze over them, he said, "If you want to live, then tell me exactly what you're plotting. If the information is useful, I may not be unable to spare your lives."

Apart from the young man who had fainted, all seven or eight of them stared quietly at him, but no one answered.

Yang Kai nodded. "None of you fear death. Very good." As he spoke, he gently patted Guixu's head. The little black dog let out a wild roar, suddenly thrust its head forward, opened its bloody maw, and swallowed one of the demonized cultivators standing before Yang Kai in a single bite.

Ma Qing and the others' eyelids jumped at the sight. Even though they had already seen this strange little black dog's methods before, they were still shocked. It was not strange for a Yao Beast to eat people, but Guixu had not eaten a person. It had eaten a demonized cultivator, and nothing had happened to it at all. Earlier, it had even swallowed the boundless Demonic Qi in the valley. It was truly astonishing.

Wen Zishan, however, looked thoughtful. He knew of Guixu's existence. Back then, Gong Yue had found Guixu in Qingyang Shrine, and at the time it had nearly swallowed Feng Ming in one bite. The combined attacks of a dozen or so Emperor Realm Masters had been swallowed into its belly and reflected back. According to Gong Yue, this little beast seemed to have the bloodline of a strange beast from the Demon Domain, so such bizarre abilities were only to be expected.

With one demonized cultivator swallowed in a single bite, the rest all grew restless. They looked at Guixu as though they were facing their natural predator, their faces full of terror and dread. But with all their strength sealed, they could not escape even if they wanted to.

Yang Kai watched them calmly and said in an indifferent tone, "Take your time thinking it over. Whoever figures things out can speak up. As long as you can provide some worthwhile information, I'll keep my word."

The moment he finished speaking, Guixu opened its huge mouth again and swallowed a second demonized cultivator.

Yang Kai stopped talking. He simply held Guixu and walked forward step by step. Each time he arrived before a demonized cultivator, Guixu swallowed that person in one bite. In the blink of an eye, five had been devoured, leaving only two standing there, their eyes venomous with hatred, yet still silent.

Seeing this, Yang Kai sighed helplessly. "Sure enough, all of you are stubborn heroes."

He turned to Ma Qing and the others. "I'm afraid we won't get anything out of them."

Ma Qing's expression turned cold. "Since that's the case, there is no need to leave them alive."

If they had not been demonized cultivators, Ma Qing might have thought of using some Secret Technique like Soul Searching to pry into the secrets in their minds. But with demons involved, who would dare? If he became tainted by Demonic Qi, the loss would far outweigh the gain.

Since keeping them served no purpose, they could only be eliminated at the root.

Guixu swallowed the remaining two demonized cultivators into its belly in two bites, looking as though it had not had nearly enough. Its two little eyes then turned toward the dozen or so people on the other side.

"What is going on with them?" Yang Kai asked curiously.

"You'll have to ask this beast pet of yours." Wen Zishan frowned. "It has been barking at these people the whole time. I don't know if there is something wrong with them." After the battle in the valley had ended, the great Sects had all been gathering survivors and collecting the remains of the dead and injured. Unexpectedly, Guixu had suddenly run up to one cultivator and started roaring at him. Given Guixu's strange behavior earlier, Wen Zishan and Ma Qing had grown suspicious and ordered that cultivator detained.

Who could have known Guixu would keep weaving through the crowd and pick out a dozen or so more people one after another, leading to the situation before them now?

"I've been wronged, Hall Master Wen! I am a disciple of Qingyun Sect. How could there be a problem with me?" a young woman protested bitterly. The others immediately chimed in as well.

Yang Kai said, "Whether there is a problem or not, we'll know once we try." With one step, he arrived directly before the young woman.

The young woman was startled and retreated several steps in panic. "You... what do you want?"

Yang Kai spread his large hand and clamped it directly over her head. The moment he used the Exorcism Spell, the young woman's expression changed. Threads of black Qi surged from within her body, and she wailed without stopping.

Everyone around them paled at the sight.

"Sure enough, there is a problem!" Ma Qing said, his expression solemn.

Wen Zishan also looked shaken. He clicked his tongue in wonder as he studied Guixu, knowing that if not for this little beast's abnormal behavior, he and the others truly might have been fooled.

When was a person's guard at its lowest? Right after winning a great battle.

Earlier, after the matter of Wuhua Hall had been settled, no one had expected that hidden demonized cultivators would still be lurking among the crowd, and no one had any way to identify them. If these people were allowed to leave alive, there might one day be a second or third Wuhua Hall.

Yang Kai checked them one by one and discovered that the dozen or so people Guixu had found had all, without exception, been secretly possessed by Demonic Intent. Whether they had been possessed beforehand or during the chaos, he did not know. Judging from their different backgrounds and uneven cultivations, he guessed the latter was more likely.

Since all of them had already been possessed, there was nothing more to say. In only a short while, they had all entered Guixu's belly.

Wen Zishan said, "Should we have this beast pet of yours search again? In case there is still some Demonic Intent remaining..."

Before, there had been no way to tell who had been possessed by Demonic Intent. Now that they had a sharp tool like Guixu, they naturally had to screen everyone carefully.

Yang Kai nodded. "Good idea." As he spoke, he tossed Guixu to Liu Yan. "Take it around."

Liu Yan caught Guixu, turned, and left.

Not far away, Wuchang watched the scene with a calm expression, though his heart was in turmoil.

More than a month ago, when everyone had met at the mountain gate of Wuhua Hall, he had still spoken rudely and mocked Yang Kai, brazenly thinking that Yang Kai was barely qualified to fight him. Yet who would have thought that in just a short month, this opponent he had not really taken seriously would be speaking to his own Saint Lord as an equal? Moreover, the matter of Wuhua Hall had practically been turned around and saved by Yang Kai alone. Without him, so many people from the Southern Territory would likely have been wiped out this time.

Thinking of the more than two hundred Emperor Realm helpers Yang Kai had casually brought over, then of those unruly Thirty-Two Yao Kings, and then of the three Divine Spirits who obeyed his every command... even someone as proud as Wuchang felt powerless.

At some point, this peer of his had already walked far ahead, becoming someone from an entirely different world.

Thinking back now on the bold words he had spoken more than a month ago, Wuchang realized how ridiculous and immature he had been. Back then, the other party probably had not taken him seriously either, had he? Deep down, he could not help feeling a profound sense of defeat, as if he were nothing more than a clown.

Chen Wenhao stood beside him. Seeming to notice the change in his mood, he raised a hand and patted his shoulder. "Everyone has their own fate and fortune. This man's fortune reaches the heavens, the sort that appears only once among hundreds of millions. Not everyone can compare with him."

Wuchang gently let out a breath and said, "I know." After a pause, he added, "After I return this time, I will enter retreat. Once my cultivation breaks through again, I will go out and temper myself."

Chen Wenhao turned to look at him, then nodded. "That is fine." Flowers raised in a greenhouse could never withstand wind and rain.
