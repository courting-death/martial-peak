## Chapter 3185 — It Can Even Do That?

Brilliant stars that had endured since time immemorial were collapsing and vanishing one after another, as if some invisible hand were wiping them from the vast Starry Sky.

Wu Heng could bear it no longer. Gritting his teeth, he growled, "Yang Kai!"

By this point, he was already at a complete disadvantage in his battle against Yang Kai. He had lost not only vast stretches of the Starry Sky, but even five Cultivation Stars within Great Wilderness Star Domain.

If this silent struggle continued, he would certainly lose everything he had. Deep regret surged through him. If he had withdrawn the moment he realized a Star Lord had been born in Hengluo Star Domain, perhaps he would not be facing so much trouble now.

But back then, he had assumed Yang Kai was easy to bully because he had only just become Master of a Star Domain and had not taken him seriously at all. He had even brazenly demanded that Yang Kai hand over Hengluo Star Domain. Their enmity was already set.

Regret was useless now.

No matter what, he had to deal with Yang Kai first. Even if it meant suffering greater losses, he could not tolerate Yang Kai running wild on his own territory.

With that thought, Wu Heng hesitated no longer and sent a portion of his mind to investigate Yang Kai's situation.

In that extremely distant stretch of Starry Sky, Wu Heng quickly found traces of Yang Kai. Once he saw what was happening there, he stared in shock, his face going blank. "What is that?"

The sixth Cultivation Star had already lost more than half its mass, and on that shattered star, a black monster as huge as a tadpole was drifting back and forth. Its enormous mouth opened and closed, swallowing the Cultivation Star chunk by chunk as if it were eating a delicious cake.

In the blink of an eye, another large piece of the Cultivation Star was gone.

Though Wu Heng was experienced and widely knowledgeable, he could not recognize what the hell this pitch-black strange beast was. The moment he saw it, however, instinctive horror and dread rose in him.

Yang Kai, seated cross-legged on Gungun's back, seemed to sense something. He raised his head, glanced toward the Starry Sky, and grinned.

The next moment, Wu Heng appeared before him. As Master of a Star Domain, crossing hundreds of millions of li in an instant was naturally no difficult feat for him.

He raised his hand, clenched it, and roared, "Die!"

The power of the Star Domain surged into motion at once, becoming invisible shackles that cinched fiercely around Gungun's enormous body. Before Yang Kai's eyes, Gungun's round form kept contracting. Its great mouth gaped open, its teeth clacking together loudly as if it were struggling against something.

Yang Kai also felt pressure descend like a mountain. His whole body went rigid, unable to move, and it felt as if Wu Heng were about to crush every bone in his body.

It really was never wise to act too recklessly on someone else's territory. But... Wu Heng was probably a little late to be making his move now.

If Wu Heng had been this decisive at the very beginning, when Gungun had not yet grown into anything formidable, he might still have had a chance at victory. But now... did he really think Yang Kai was a soft persimmon he could squeeze as he pleased? The corners of Yang Kai's mouth lifted slightly into an inscrutable smile.

Wu Heng's expression suddenly changed. Under the gnawing of the pitch-black beast's great maw, the shackles he had laid on them were being broken bit by bit.

No, they were not being broken. They were disappearing.

Just like the Cultivation Star being devoured before his eyes, the Star Domain power he had mobilized was being devoured as well.

With a crash that shook the depths of the soul, Gungun's compressed body abruptly returned to normal, and its movements became free once more.

“It can even do that?” Wu Heng was dazed for a moment. He had come to deal with Yang Kai with the resolve of a warrior cutting off his own wrist, because every breath he delayed here was another breath in which Hengluo Star Domain's devouring went unchecked. He simply could not afford the losses caused by that terrifying speed of devouring.

He had planned to kill Yang Kai outright, then return to continue his confrontation with Hengluo Star Domain. Who would have thought that plan would fail?

That pitch-black, ball-like monster was far harder to deal with than he had imagined. It could even devour the power of this Star Domain.

While he was still dazed, Gungun suddenly charged straight at him. Its seemingly fat, bloated body displayed unimaginable agility in that instant. By the time Wu Heng snapped back to himself, a great mouth packed with fangs had appeared right before his eyes.

Crack...

The great mouth snapped shut, and a large chunk of the Void vanished.

Wu Heng reappeared in another position, cold sweat covering his brow. A moment ago, if he had not been Master of a Star Domain and able to shuttle freely through this stretch of Void, he would likely already have been caught.

Standing on Gungun's back with one hand on his waist, Yang Kai threw his head back and laughed. "This is your home, so why are you running? Hurry up and come fight three hundred rounds with me. Let's hurt each other, you bastard."

"Impudent brat, don't be so arrogant!" A fire raged in Wu Heng's heart like a volcano ready to erupt at any moment, yet he still forced himself to remain calm and not be swayed by Yang Kai's words. But Yang Kai was right. This was his territory, his home. Yang Kai was the thief and robber who had snuck in, and he, the master of the house, had come to deal with him. Yet instead of showing the slightest guilt, Yang Kai was provoking and shouting at him. No one could tolerate such a thing.

Wu Heng focused his mind and reached into the Void with a grasp. Countless streams of light gathered, condensing into a long spear in his hand.

It was not an Imperial Treasure, but a weapon formed from the Star Force that filled this stretch of Starry Sky. Calling it the World Force of this Starry Sky would not be excessive. Of course, it was not the World Force of the entire Great Wilderness Star Domain. Although Wu Heng was Master of Great Wilderness Star Domain, mobilizing the power of the whole Star Domain was not so simple. Yang Kai could not do it either.

Even so, the power contained in this long spear was extraordinary. At the very least, it had gathered the power of the Starry Sky within a radius of ten million li. This was something only the Master of a Star Domain could accomplish with a casual gesture.

The spear formed, lightning flashed, and thunder roared.

The long spear became a ferocious striking viper, carrying Heaven-destroying, Earth-shattering might as it reached Gungun in an instant.

Yang Kai went rigid, every hair on his body standing on end.

If this shocking strike was a viper, then in that instant, he seemed to have become a frog facing its natural enemy. Even though he had already braced himself, facing it head-on still felt deeply troublesome.

"Gungun!" Yang Kai lifted his foot and stamped on Gungun's back.

Gungun split open its great mouth and swallowed that terrifying strike whole.

A cold smile hung on Wu Heng's face as he calmly watched the pitch-black giant monster. Very soon, however, that smile vanished.

The scene he had imagined, of killing his enemy in a single move, did not appear. The pitch-black giant monster's body only churned and wriggled violently a few times. Then a crack opened on its back, and nothing more happened.

Even that crack was closing and healing at incredible speed.

His eyes widened, and he almost wondered if his eyes had deceived him. His strike, condensed from the power of the Starry Sky within ten million li, had been neutralized so easily?

Just what the hell was this pitch-black giant monster? How could it possess such power?

What he did not know was that the moment Gungun swallowed that shocking strike, a stream of light tore across the sky inside the Small Xuan Realm, almost piercing through the entire world. It carried an aura capable of destroying Heaven and extinguishing Earth, and every living being in the Small Xuan Realm looked up, unable to suppress the urge to kneel and worship.

After the stream of light raced past, a deep scar remained branded across the sky of the Small Xuan Realm, as if a wound had been cut open there, lingering for a long time without fading.

Only World Force could oppose World Force.

Wu Heng's strike, condensed from the power of the Starry Sky within ten million li, was certainly terrifying, but the Small Xuan Realm was now a world of its own. The World Laws and Spiritual Qi nurtured within it were not much worse than those of a Star Domain, and were even somewhat superior. It was merely smaller in area.

For Wu Heng to think he could destroy the Small Xuan Realm with this strike was naturally wishful thinking. The Small Xuan Realm was enough to devour and digest his attack. Judging from the result, not only would it fail to cause much damage, it would even nourish the realm.

Yang Kai dug at his ear with his little finger and mocked loudly, "What a weak, limp attack. Are you trying to scratch my itch?"

"Shut up!" Wu Heng snapped, humiliated and furious.

How could Yang Kai do as he wished? His taunts only grew sharper. "We're both Masters of Star Domains, yet this Young Master is ashamed for you. If someone dared act this wildly in my home, I would have beaten him so badly his own mother wouldn't recognize him."

"You are insolent!"

Yang Kai sneered, "I am insolent. If you've got the guts... come hit me!"

Even knowing that Yang Kai was deliberately provoking him, Wu Heng could no longer endure it. His mind roiled violently, and both his eyes turned red.

Although Yang Kai's words were arrogant, one thing he said was right. To be unable to deal with a thief and robber on his own territory truly was a disgrace.

So Wu Heng said no more. He shook both arms, and specks of fluorescent light appeared over his hands. They gathered Star Force and transformed into thunderous attacks that blasted toward Gungun.

Although he did not know what the hell this pitch-black giant beast was, he instinctively felt that it was not as powerful as it appeared. Even if it could devour his attacks, it had to have a limit.

The wound slowly closing on its back was the best proof.

It was not that he could not injure it. He simply had not injured it enough! Since it was not enough, he would add more. Sooner or later, one strike would completely crush it.

Star Force came crashing in and was once again swallowed into Gungun's belly. Two more wounds suddenly split open across its back.

Yet Gungun suddenly grew excited. Without Yang Kai's instructions, it actually took the initiative to pounce at Wu Heng, as if it wanted to swallow him too.

How could Wu Heng sit still and wait for death? Relying on the convenience of being Master of a Star Domain, he shifted and darted through the Void, avoiding Gungun's attacks while continuously condensing Star Domain power between his hands and blasting it toward Gungun.

In an instant, the world inside the Small Xuan Realm changed violently.

Streams of light streaked across the sky one after another, as if a meteor shower had fallen. Inside the Small Xuan Realm, lightning flashed and thunder roared without end, as if doomsday had arrived and the world was about to collapse.

Its hundreds of millions of living beings, regardless of cultivation, all trembled, prostrating themselves and kneeling on the ground as they cried for the Lord God to spare their lives.

They did not even know how they had offended the World Master who had appeared before. They only thought the World Master had grown angry, and that was why these changes in Heaven and Earth had been stirred up.

Waves surged over the great sea. The earth split across the wilderness. Countless newly built structures collapsed. In the space of an instant, the peaceful world had become a place where danger lurked everywhere and killing intent filled the air.

It made them feel all the more deeply what it meant for divine grace to be as vast as the sea, and divine might as dreadful as a prison.
