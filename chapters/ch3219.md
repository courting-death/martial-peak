## Chapter 3219 — Junior Sister Again

"There is just one thing. Medicine Pill Valley..." Li Jiao looked at Yang Kai worriedly.

Although the Northern Territory had no Great Emperor Sect, and Medicine Pill Valley was only the place where Sublime Pill Great Emperor cultivated in seclusion rather than a Sect, if Yang Kai truly threw the Northern Territory into chaos, he might draw out the people of Medicine Pill Valley. Once Medicine Pill Valley intervened, the matter would probably be impossible to resolve.

Yang Kai sneered. "It is not as if I plan to exterminate the Northern Territory's billions of cultivators. I only want to take over some city industries and have those cities offer tribute every year. Why would Medicine Pill Valley bother with that?" He tilted his head and looked at Li Jiao. "Brother Li, don't tell me you thought I wanted to wipe out all the great Sects of the Northern Territory?"

Cold sweat broke out across Li Jiao's brow. "I misunderstood."

When he heard Yang Kai speak of unifying the Northern Territory, he had truly thought Yang Kai meant to begin a slaughter. Only now did he realize he had overthought it. If Yang Kai only meant to occupy some city industries and make the Northern Territory's great cities offer tribute every year, then it was not too excessive. After all, he was not driving anyone to extinction, and there was still plenty of room to ease the situation.

Yang Kai reached out and patted his shoulder. "Since I am going to do this, I need to establish my authority first. Mitian Sect is a good target, so I will have to trouble Brother Li to pass along a message for me. Tell Mi Qi to come to Lingxiao Palace to see me as soon as possible."

Li Jiao could only smile bitterly. "Brother Yang is asking me to play the villain."

Yang Kai blinked. "Who told Brother Li to be so familiar with Mi Qi?"

"Fine. I will notify him as soon as possible. I just do not know how he will take it."

"As long as the message reaches him." Yang Kai smiled faintly, turned, and stepped into the Space Array. Light flashed, and he vanished.

Mitian Sect was also one of the Northern Territory's top Sects. In terms of strength, it matched Li Dragon Palace and Ice Heart Valley. Each had a Third-Stage Emperor Realm expert holding the fort. Since Yang Kai wanted to establish his authority and make Lingxiao Palace's name resound, he naturally had to choose a suitable target.

Ice Heart Valley and Li Dragon Palace would not do, so Mitian Sect was the only one he could make an example of.

Speaking of which, Mitian Sect, like Li Dragon Palace, was another poor debtor who owed Yang Kai a large sum. It had repaid quite a few Source Crystals over the years, but as for clearing the debt completely, who knew how long that would take.

The days flew by, and Yang Kai stayed busy from morning to night, without even having time to exchange a few words with Shan Qingluo and Xue Yue. The food, shelter, daily needs, and cultivation of over a hundred thousand disciples were a major problem. Until that was properly settled, there was no way to do anything else.

Having received his instructions, Hua Qingsi went back to the Southern Territory again and bought a large batch of Origin Condensing Pills from Ziyuan Commerce Guild, distributing them to those cultivators whose cultivation had reached the peak of the Void King Realm and who were preparing to break through to the Dao Origin Realm.

A few days later, the one hundred instructors also arrived from Li Dragon Palace. After discussing it with Hua Qingsi, Yang Kai split them up and placed them on the various peaks. All one hundred had Dao Origin Realm cultivation, so teaching the disciples who had come from the Star Domain would certainly be no problem.

Once new Dao Origin Realm cultivators emerged on the various peaks, Lingxiao Palace's missing middle ranks could be filled. At that point, the old would lead the new in a continuing cycle, and there would be no need to worry too much.

The various peaks differed in size, and the number of residents varied as well. The smaller ones had two or three hundred people, while the larger ones had two or three thousand. The instructors assigned to the less crowded peaks were naturally delighted, while those assigned to peaks with two or three thousand people immediately suffered bitterly.

Just imagine one person having to teach two or three thousand people how to cultivate. Even if only one percent came each day to ask about cultivation matters, he would definitely have no time to rest.

One month later, inside Lingxiao Palace's Discussion Hall, Yang Kai sat upright on the seat above.

The three great Yao Kings and Chief Steward Hua Qingsi stood along the steps on either side, arranged in a spreading formation.

Below them, to the left and right, the crowd was packed shoulder to shoulder, more than a hundred people in all. Today was the first discussion meeting of Lingxiao Palace as a whole.

These hundred-plus people were the Peak Masters of Lingxiao Palace's various peaks. Their cultivations varied widely, and Yang Kai did not really recognize half of them.

With a sharp, imposing gaze, Yang Kai swept his eyes over those below. Everyone saluted together. "Greetings, Palace Master!"

No matter whether the people below had once been his elders or fellow disciples, now that they had come to the Star Realm and entered Lingxiao Palace, they were people of Lingxiao Palace. He was the Palace Master, so he naturally had to accept their salute.

Yang Kai raised a hand lightly, secretly amused.

Even he had never expected that founding Lingxiao Palace would lead to such a grand spectacle. He feared there was no other Sect in the entire Star Realm that gathered so many people for a discussion meeting. It made the whole thing look like a mortal emperor holding court.

Then again, no Sect truly had as many people as Lingxiao Palace. Such a situation was unavoidable.

Though it was called a discussion meeting, it was mainly to lay down some rules. A Sect could not function without order. With over a hundred thousand disciples and more than a hundred spirit peaks, even if Lingxiao Palace now had a vast foundation and no shortage of cultivation resources, those resources could not be handed out at random.

How many cultivation supplies each peak could receive each month, and how much would be allotted to each person, all required extremely meticulous calculation and work.

There were also many rules for rewards and punishments. Hua Qingsi and Bian Yuqing had discussed all of these long ago, and now they stood in the main hall and read them aloud. The main purpose was for the peak masters to remember them, then return and pass them on to the disciples, so no one would commit a mistake later, be punished, and then cry injustice. Since there were punishments, there were naturally rewards as well. Those whose cultivations advanced by leaps and bounds, and those who contributed to their peaks or to the Sect, would all be rewarded.

In all the bustle, half a day passed.

After that came the peak masters' reports on the conditions of their respective peaks. Fortunately, there were no troublesome matters. The more than one hundred thousand disciples had only just arrived, but they were all relatively settled. Most had received some cultivation supplies and gone into retreat to cultivate.

It was not until evening that everyone gradually dispersed.

Someone hurried in from outside the hall to report. "Greetings, Palace Master. Master asks that you come over."

Yang Kai looked closely and discovered that this person was none other than Ji Ying's eldest disciple. His spirits immediately rose. "Brother Ji is back?"

The disciple said, "Master only returned around noon. When he heard that Palace Master was discussing matters with the peak masters, he did not disturb you."

"I will go see him."

Yang Kai immediately hurried toward Spirit Pill Peak.

After reaching Spirit Pill Peak and entering the main hall, he looked around and saw only Ji Ying there. Joy lit Yang Kai's face, and he cupped his fists. "Brother Ji!"

Ji Ying returned the salute. "Brother Yang, fortunately, I did not fail my mission."

Although Yang Kai had already guessed as much when he did not see Xia Ningshang, hearing Ji Ying say this still filled him with joy. He grabbed Ji Ying's arm and pulled him down to sit. "Great Emperor agreed to accept Ningshang as a disciple?"

Ji Ying smiled. "No."

Yang Kai froze. "How could that be?"

Ji Ying had been gone for more than a month, and Yang Kai had not seen Xia Ningshang just now, so he had thought Sublime Pill Great Emperor had already accepted Little Senior Sister as a disciple. Who could have known Ji Ying would pour cold water on him like this? His excitement instantly cooled.

"Brother Yang, hear me out." Ji Ying smiled faintly. "Honored Master said back then that he would no longer accept disciples. Since he already said it, he naturally cannot go back on his word."

Yang Kai glared. "Ningshang has the Medicinal Spirit Saint Body. Even that did not move Great Emperor?"

Ji Ying said, "Precisely because sister-in-law has the Medicinal Spirit Saint Body, Honored Master felt that leaving her to develop on her own would waste such an excellent constitution, so he kept sister-in-law in Medicine Pill Valley."

"What does that mean?" Yang Kai was confused. Since Sublime Pill Great Emperor was not accepting Xia Ningshang as a disciple, why keep her in Medicine Pill Valley?

Ji Ying said, "Honored Master's intention is for Eldest Senior Brother, Second Senior Brother, Fourth Senior Sister, and the others to teach sister-in-law together. Honored Master even said that if there is anything she does not understand, sister-in-law may ask him for guidance herself. The only thing missing is the formal master-disciple title."

Yang Kai asked in astonishment, "How is that any different from accepting her as a disciple?"

Xia Ningshang had remained in Medicine Pill Valley, and several of Sublime Pill Great Emperor's disciples would teach her together. She could even consult Sublime Pill Great Emperor about pill refining.

No matter how one looked at it, this was no different from being his disciple.

Ji Ying smiled faintly. "Honored Master also seems somewhat vexed that he said he would no longer accept disciples back then, and even more vexed that word of it spread."

Yang Kai stood stunned for a long while before bursting into laughter. Only then did he realize that Sublime Pill Great Emperor was not unwilling to accept Xia Ningshang as a disciple; he simply could not bring himself to slap his own face. If that remark had never spread, Great Emperor definitely would not have refused.

"So Brother Yang can rest easy. With Junior Sister taught by several Senior Brothers, she will certainly be able to carry forward Honored Master's mantle."

Yang Kai looked at him teasingly. "Great Emperor has not accepted my humble wife, yet Brother Ji is already calling her Junior Sister. If Great Emperor heard that..."

“That is simply the truth,” Ji Ying said, utterly unconcerned. As the Great Emperor’s fifth disciple, how could he not see the Great Emperor’s thoughts? The Great Emperor was also someone who cherished talent. Although he could not take Xia Ningshang as his disciple because of a single sentence from back then, he would certainly teach her with great care. The title of Junior Sister merely lacked an official status; it was something Ji Ying and his Senior Brothers had all acknowledged together.

"Good, good, good!" Yang Kai clapped his hands, greatly encouraged.

Su Yan had gone to Ice Heart Valley and become a Junior Sister, and Xia Ningshang had gone to Medicine Pill Valley and also become a Junior Sister. They could be said to share the same fate. Still, this was unavoidable. Compared to the others, their age and cultivation simply could not match up. Junior Sister it was, then. It was easier to enjoy the shade beneath a great tree.

As for Shan Qingluo and Xue Yue, there was nowhere suitable to place them. Shan Qingluo was unwilling to go to the Wild Ancient Land, so the two women could only remain in Lingxiao Palace to cultivate. Shan Qingluo was a little better off, since she could ask the three great Yao Kings for cultivation experience. As for Xue Yue, Yang Kai could only teach her himself. Although he had stumbled and fought his way along this path, he at least had cultivation at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm now, so teaching her to cultivate should not be a problem.

In high spirits, he stayed on Spirit Pill Peak, drinking and chatting merrily with Ji Ying.

Mitian Sect was one of the Northern Territory's four top Sects. Its Sect Master, Mi Qi, also had cultivation at the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm and enjoyed illustrious renown throughout the Northern Territory. But ever since Lingxiao Palace appeared in the Northern Territory, its days had become rather difficult. Every year, more than half of its income had to be handed over to that Lingxiao Palace, leaving it strapped. Fortunately, Li Dragon Palace was in the same miserable boat; otherwise, it truly would not know what to do.

One day, Li Jiao came to visit.

Mi Qi was also an old acquaintance of his, so he naturally welcomed him warmly.

A banquet was laid out to entertain him. After three rounds of wine, Li Jiao smiled faintly and said, "This Li has come uninvited today because someone asked me to."

Mi Qi was startled. "At whose request? And for what?" Li Jiao was a cultivator at the Third Stage of the Emperor Realm just like him, and he also possessed a half-dragon bloodline. If they truly fought, Mi Qi admitted he was no match. Who had such great face that Li Jiao would come to pass on a message for them?
