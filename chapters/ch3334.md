## Chapter 3334 - Get Back

Before coming to Green Wood Island, Yang Kai and Zhu Qing had only just talked about this. Zhu Qing had even warned him specifically not to leave her sight, lest he give those Dragon Clan sisters an opening.

Yang Kai had also patted his chest and sworn that he would never be seduced by beauty.

Who would have thought Great Elder would suddenly bring it up now too? Without that earlier conversation, Yang Kai might not have made the connection, but after what Zhu Qing had said to him, the meaning behind Great Elder's words at this moment... was rather intriguing.

Yang Kai was dumbfounded. "Great Elder, you are not thinking of..."

Zhu Yan chuckled. His eyes looked cloudy, but a sharp light flashed within them as he sized Yang Kai up and down, as though he were looking at a flawless piece of jade. He stared until goosebumps rose all over Yang Kai, then nodded lightly. "This can be considered a bargain for you, boy."

So it really was...

Yang Kai was speechless. At that moment, he truly wanted to pry open this old fellow's skull and see what in the world he was thinking. It was one thing for Fu Ling to pose and flirt in front of him to tempt him, but why did even the Dragon Clan's Great Elder want to arrange this kind of good fortune for him?

Zhu Yan turned to Zhu Qing, his expression solemn. "Qing'er, I fear this matter will wrong you somewhat, but for the sake of my Dragon Clan's great plan, it must be done. I trust you understand."

Zhu Qing lowered her gaze and replied obediently, "Qing'er understands, but Great Elder need not say this to me. Everything still depends on his own wishes."

Zhu Yan said, "Good child. As long as you have no objections, what objections could he possibly have? Men... hmph!" That cold snort made it sound as though he were not a man himself.

Yang Kai immediately said, "I have objections."

Zhu Yan looked at him with great interest. "You have objections? What objections could you have?"

Yang Kai wiped his face. "Huge objections!"

Zhu Yan smiled. "If you have objections, then say them."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "I am a man, yes, and I do covet beauty, that is true, but I am not without a bottom line. Never mind that I am not familiar with those Dragon Clan women. Even if I were, this sort of thing must be mutual and happen naturally when the time is right. Qing'er and I shared hardships and faced danger together before reaching this point. How could it be the same with other Dragon women? Senior, what exactly do you take me for? A breeding boar?"

"So you are unwilling?" Zhu Yan stared at him in astonishment.

Yang Kai sneered. "Would Senior be willing in my place?"

Zhu Yan thought about it seriously for a moment. "It would be very hard to refuse..." Most likely, any man would find such a temptation very hard to refuse. After all, those were several Dragon women.

"Besides, the Dragon Clan already cannot tolerate the matter between Qing'er and me. If something were to happen with the other Dragon women as well, how could the Dragon Clan possibly tolerate that?"

Zhu Yan immediately said, "If you can make their bloodlines advance quickly, then never mind Qing'er alone, what harm is there in marrying all of them to you?"

Yang Kai was stunned speechless. This old fellow truly had nerve, and just imagining that scene... was enough to make one's blood boil. But if things were really done that way, the male Dragon Clan members on Dragon Isle would probably all treat Yang Kai as a thorn in their side. Even his own brother-in-law likely would not be an exception.

The Dragon Clan's ratio of men to women was already out of balance. If Yang Kai were to sweep up every Dragon woman on all of Dragon Isle, how were the remaining male Dragon Clan members supposed to endure it?

"Senior, please do not mention this matter again. I absolutely will not agree." Yang Kai waved his hand and refused with righteous severity, while also sneaking a glance at Zhu Qing's expression. Seeing that she looked somewhat overjoyed, his heart at once settled.

"Do not be so quick to refuse. Go back and think it over carefully. This matter has no real downside for you either," Zhu Yan said with a wave of his hand.

Yang Kai did not dwell on the matter any further. He cupped his fists and said, "I have come to Dragon Isle this time because there are still several matters for which I ask Great Elder's help."

Zhu Yan smiled. "The grievances between you and my Dragon Isle have yet to be settled, yet you actually dare ask this old man to help you? Is there something wrong with your head, or something wrong with mine?"

Yang Kai said, "Whether Great Elder acknowledges it or not, it is a fact that this Junior bears a Dragon Clan Origin."

Zhu Yan thought to himself that if not for that, Yang Kai would not even have the right to stand here talking. He would have slapped him to death long ago. Moreover, what this boy bore was the Ancestral Dragon Origin, a matter of truly grave importance.

"Since that is the case, this Junior can barely count as half a Dragon Clan member," Yang Kai continued. "Since I am half a Dragon Clan member, asking Great Elder to help with a few matters should not be excessive, right?"

"That depends on what matters you mean," Zhu Yan snorted lightly.

Yang Kai said solemnly, "To the Dragon Clan, these matters should not be anything serious. The first is that I hope Qing'er can stay by my side, leave Dragon Isle with me, and no longer suffer under house arrest."

Zhu Yan said, "And?"

"This Junior wants to enter the Dragon Temple for a look!" Yang Kai pondered for a moment, then said, "Last time, I came and went from the Dragon Temple in a hurry. Although I stirred many Dragon Souls, I had no time to comprehend anything. This time, I want to enter the Dragon Temple and learn the Dragon Clan's Secret Techniques!"

Zhu Yan was silent for a moment before asking, "Just these two matters?"

"Correct."

"This old man has no objections."

Yang Kai froze, then was overjoyed. "Many thanks, Great Elder, for granting this."

Zhu Yan chuckled. "This old man has no objections, but I do not know what Second Elder thinks. If you can persuade Second Elder, then naturally everything will be easy to discuss."

The corner of Yang Kai's mouth twitched. "You are the Great Elder. Should you not be the highest authority on Dragon Isle?"

Zhu Yan blinked. "Externally, I am naturally the highest authority, but internally, Second Elder manages affairs. Good, go find Second Elder, if you want your wishes fulfilled."

With that, he turned and walked toward the tree house.

Yang Kai stared at his back and muttered something through clenched teeth.

Zhu Yan stopped short and turned to look back, his expression twitching. "Boy, what did you just say? If you have the guts, say it again!"

Zhu Qing hurriedly said, "He did not say anything. Great Elder must have misheard. We will take our leave first." As she spoke, she tugged Yang Kai and flew away.

Yang Kai turned back and shouted, “The dignified Great Elder of the Dragon Clan is actually afraid of his wife!” His voice was like a dragon’s roar, shaking the leaves until they rustled down.

"Boy, get back here!" Zhu Yan flew into a flustered rage and rolled up his sleeves to give chase, but Yang Kai and Zhu Qing were already gone without a trace. After a bout of endless cursing, he clasped his hands behind his back, stuck out his neck, and said, "What a joke. Would this old man be afraid of her? Your eyes must be growing on your backside. This old man simply does not stoop to arguing with her."

Once he finished, he quickly turned and looked around. Only after making sure there was no one nearby did he strut back into his tree house, chest puffed and head high, like a proud rooster fresh from victory.

High in the sky, Zhu Qing twisted Yang Kai's ear and said angrily, "Why did you provoke Great Elder? Great Elder is a very good person."

Yang Kai grinned in apology. "Stop twisting, my ear is going to fall off. I only said it offhand." He reached out, caught her soft hand, and pulled it down from his ear.

Zhu Qing said angrily, "Besides, is being afraid of one's wife something shameful?"

Yang Kai said with a playful smile, "Men are not really afraid of their wives. How could a grown man be afraid of his wife?"

"Is that so?" Zhu Qing looked at him sidelong.

"This so-called fear is merely another way of showing tolerance and affection." At a time like this, Yang Kai naturally knew what to say to make her happy.

Zhu Qing's expression shifted. She looked at the base of his ear, still somewhat red, and the coldness on her face melted away at once, sweetness filling her heart. Before long, however, her mood turned heavy again, and she warned him, "Great Elder's trial can barely be considered passed. When you see Second Elder later, you absolutely must not lose your temper. As long as you pass Second Elder's trial too, it will be much easier for you to come to Dragon Isle in the future, and perhaps I will also be able to go out and see you."

"I know, I know. No matter what nasty things she says later, I will treat them all as farting."

Zhu Qing was torn between laughter and tears. "You absolutely cannot let her hear that."

Ice Snow Island was Fu Zhun's Spirit Island. Fu Zhun was an Ice Dragon, so the Spirit Island where she lived was naturally bitterly cold, with wind and snow filling the sky. From afar, the entire Spirit Island looked like one enormous iceberg, a place where no birds crossed a thousand li and no human tracks marked ten thousand paths.

Before they even drew near, an overwhelming chill rushed at them head-on. Even the surrounding sea had frozen solid, and within a radius of several thousand li there seemed to be not the slightest trace of life.

Biting though this cold was, it could not affect Yang Kai or Zhu Qing much.

The two descended straight toward the center of the island. From afar, they saw a huge ice platform forged from ten-thousand-year-old profound ice, with Fu Zhun seated upright upon it. Dressed all in white, pure and flawless, she almost blended perfectly into the surrounding scenery.

They dropped from the sky and landed thirty zhang from Fu Zhun.

Zhu Qing saluted and said, "Qing'er brings her husband Yang Kai to pay respects to Second Elder."

That sudden word "husband" left Yang Kai a little dazed. Only when Zhu Qing lightly bumped him with her arm did he cup his fists and say, "Yang Kai greets Second Elder."

Fu Zhun opened her eyes. Her beautiful eyes were cold and indifferent, their piercing chill seeming capable of freezing a person's mind and soul. It was as if Yang Kai did not exist at all. She only looked at Zhu Qing and said, "Who allowed you to leave the island without permission? Get back!"

At those words, a vein jumped on Yang Kai's forehead, and anger surged up inside him. What was this supposed to mean? Qing'er had kindly come to pay respects to her, yet Fu Zhun treated her with contempt and even told her to go back?

Seeing his displeasure, Zhu Qing hurriedly said, "Qing'er and Husband have come here because we have a matter to request of Second Elder."

"Husband?" Fu Zhun sneered. "You have never married, so where did this husband come from? Even if you had one, it should be the deceased Fu Chi, not some disreputable nobody."

Zhu Qing said, "Qing'er's husband is right here beside me. Why must Second Elder pretend not to see him?"

Fu Zhun closed her eyes and said, "I see no husband of yours. Even if there were one, I would not acknowledge him."

Zhu Qing sighed. "Whether Second Elder acknowledges it or not, it is a fact that Husband and I are married. Qing'er will never marry another in this life."

"Since that is so, there is nothing more to say. Get back. Come see me again when you have thought it through."

"Hey, you old witch!" Yang Kai shouted furiously. Although before coming here he had warned himself again and again that he absolutely had to endure, and although he had already prepared himself for Fu Zhun's attitude, now that he heard her telling Zhu Qing to get back with every sentence, he still could not quite hold himself back.
