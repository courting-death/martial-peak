## Chapter 3223 — Bitter Beyond Endurance

The next morning, Yang Kai slowly woke and let out a yawn. In each arm lay a soft, delicate body, and a strange scent lingered throughout the room.

Last night had been another wild night!

He gently slipped free of the two delicate bodies and waved open the window. A fresh breeze swept in, dispersing the odd smell.

The movement also woke Shan Qingluo and Xue Yue. Shan Qingluo was thick-skinned; she sat up and stretched languidly, her exquisite figure in full view. Glaring at Yang Kai, she gave two little snorts. "Satisfied now?"

Yang Kai chuckled, walked over, and gave her chest a squeeze. "You make it sound like you lost out. I wonder who was so wild last night?"

Shan Qingluo snorted. "Do not come looking for me for a while. I am going into retreat to break through to the Dao Origin Realm. Big Sister is already at the Second Stage of the Dao Origin Realm. If I do not work a little harder, I am afraid I will fall behind all of you." As she spoke, she reached out and pinched Xue Yue's cheek. "Still pretending to sleep? Hurry up and get up."

Exposed by Shan Qingluo, Xue Yue blushed and could only sit up as well. She was not as bold as Shan Qingluo, though, and grabbed a blanket to cover her chest.

Shan Qingluo reached over to tickle her. "What are you doing? It is not as if I have never seen it before. Getting shy now?"

"Luo'er, stop messing around." Xue Yue's face went crimson. She was not as easily embarrassed as Little Senior Sister, but she was nowhere near as uninhibited as Shan Qingluo. She stole a glance at Yang Kai and saw him standing there naked, smiling as he watched, with no intention of stopping them. Ashamed and annoyed, she snapped, "Big Sister is not here, and the two of you are trying to turn the heavens upside down. I will tell Big Sister later."

"Oh? You dare tattle!" Shan Qingluo gritted her pearly teeth, pounced, and tore the blanket off Xue Yue. Her hands scratched all over until Xue Yue kept crying for mercy. Shan Qingluo even called to Yang Kai, "Husband, how can one pacify the world if the rear palace is not at peace? Little Sister Yue needs to be dealt with. What are you waiting for?"

Yang Kai considered this seriously, nodded, and said, "That makes sense." He closed in with a wicked laugh, and with Shan Qingluo's help, thoroughly tormented Xue Yue.

Of course, Shan Qingluo did not escape his clutches in the end either. By the time everything settled down, it was already noon.

The two women helped Yang Kai dress, then had him sit before the mirror. One straightened his clothes while the other stood behind him and combed his hair.

As Xue Yue worked, she said, "Husband, Luo'er is going into retreat, and so am I. For the next while, do not come torment us again. It will be troublesome if our cultivations really cannot keep up with you and Big Sister in the future."

"Mm." Yang Kai nodded. The affection between men and women was hard to leave behind, but the path of a cultivator still required constant upward striving. Shan Qingluo and Xue Yue had only just arrived from the Star Domain, and this was the perfect time for them to build on their accumulated foundation and advance in one burst. Missing this chance could affect their entire lives, so he urged them, "Cultivate properly. If there is anything you do not understand, go ask someone. There are plenty of Emperor Realm Masters in the palace, so teaching you will be no problem."

"We know," Xue Yue nodded.

Shan Qingluo suddenly pursed her lips in a smile. "Big Sister has gone to Ice Heart Valley, Ningshang has gone to Medicine Pill Valley, and Little Sister Yue and I are about to enter retreat too. Husband, you will probably be alone for a while. Did you not say we had another sister, one from the Dragon Clan? Why not bring her over? When we are not here, at least someone will be by your side to take care of you."

"Her..." Yang Kai murmured. "We will talk about it later."

Zhu Qing's situation was not easy to handle. It concerned Dragon Isle's prestige, and the Dragon Clan would likely not let her go so easily. Moreover, he had caused a huge disturbance on Dragon Isle last time, and an Eighth-Tier giant dragon had even died after being indirectly implicated by him. The best outcome was already for the Dragon Clan not to view him with hostility, so how could they possibly allow Zhu Qing to come to Lingxiao Palace?

If he wanted to bring Zhu Qing out, he would need strength great enough to stand above all of Dragon Isle.

Once he was properly dressed, Yang Kai left Lingxiao Peak and found Hua Qingsi waiting outside. She came forward with a complaining look. "It was one thing when you were not in the palace before, but now that you have returned, it is still hard to see you even once."

"What is it?" Yang Kai asked.

"Take a look." Hua Qingsi handed him a jade slip.

Yang Kai took it and swept his Divine Sense inside. He could not help grimacing. "This many people are complaining?"

Hua Qingsi said, "Too many monks, too little gruel. Those instructors from Li Dragon Palace have had a rough time lately. They have hardly had any time to rest, day or night, let alone cultivate."

"How could they have no time to rest?" Yang Kai asked, stunned.

Hua Qingsi rolled her eyes. "Come with me and you will see."

As she spoke, she led Yang Kai in flight to a nearby Spirit Peak.

The cultivators living on this Spirit Peak seemed to be those who had come from Dark Star, about a thousand in all. In front of a palace atop the peak, a long line stretched out, with at least two or three hundred people waiting respectfully. From time to time, a cultivator would walk out of the palace looking suddenly enlightened. Whenever someone came out, the person at the front of the line went in, the cycle repeating endlessly.

Yang Kai's eye twitched. Pointing at the palace below, he said, "That is where the instructor lives?"

"Yes." Even Hua Qingsi felt a little unnerved by the sight. "The cultivators you brought here have only just arrived and are still not used to the Star Realm. They run into all kinds of problems in their cultivation, and once they do, they seek out their peak's instructor for answers. But each peak has only one instructor. Look at this line. How are the instructors supposed to rest? This is only a Spirit Peak with a thousand people. The peaks with two or three thousand are even worse. The peaks with only a few hundred are a little better, but they are still busy without pause. Several instructors have already come to me, saying they cannot endure life here and will return to Li Dragon Palace at once even if they have to suffer punishment. They told me to contact Palace Master Li."

"Ignore them."

Hua Qingsi said, "I have held them off for now, but with the instructors in this state, how can they properly guide the disciples in cultivation? If they do not teach well, the ones affected are the disciples, and it could affect their entire lives. Palace Master, we really must bring in more instructors."

"Mm, I have that plan."

Hua Qingsi said, "Ice Heart Valley has a good relationship with us. Could we borrow some people from them?"

"Forget Ice Heart Valley. I considered it before, but Ice Heart Valley's disciples all cultivate Ice Attribute Secret Arts. They are too specialized and not very suitable as instructors here."

"Then what should we do?"

"I have my own plan," Yang Kai replied. "But before more instructors arrive, you need to think of a way to appease these people. How about this: give them a monthly salary so they are not working for nothing."

"That was what I thought too. I was just about to report it to you."

"Decide this yourself. If you are not sure, discuss it with Steward Bian and settle on an appropriate amount. There is no need to ask me."

"Fine." Hua Qingsi was helpless. She truly had never seen a Palace Master like this. He could not wait to hand off every bit of authority in his hands and would only be happy as a hands-off Shopkeeper.

After parting with Hua Qingsi, Yang Kai made another trip to Spirit Pill Peak, found Ji Ying, and brought out many things.

Very soon, he appeared before the Space Array.

"Palace Master!" Bian Yuqing, who was meditating nearby, flew over to greet him.

Yang Kai lightly waved a hand, letting her return to her own business. Then he walked to the Space Array on his own, circulated his Power of Space, and vanished.

In the Southern Territory, at Qingyang Shrine, within a secluded valley behind Spirit Sword Peak, Yang Kai appeared at ease.

His Divine Sense swept out. After confirming that no one nearby had seen him, he flew straight toward Spirit Sword Peak.

He was a Guest Elder of Qingyang Shrine, and Spirit Sword Peak was the Spirit Peak where he lived. Yang Kai had chosen it deliberately. It was rather far from the other main peaks, making it convenient for him to hide that Space Array.

Once he reached Spirit Sword Peak, he took out his Elder Jade Token, opened the great array, and walked straight in.

Two young and beautiful maidservants were feeding fish beside a pond. Large red koi swam through the water, fighting over the food and making the two women giggle.

Yang Kai was immediately dumbfounded. He looked more carefully. He had not come to the wrong place. This was indeed his Spirit Sword Peak. Since when had two maidservants appeared here?

The two women did not have high cultivations. Only when Yang Kai came very close did they suddenly notice someone had arrived. They were startled at once, and the woman in red on the left called out, "Who are you!"

Yang Kai was amused. Looking at them, he asked, "Who are you?"

"We are from Qingyang Shrine. Who are you?" the woman in red asked, watching Yang Kai warily.

The other woman, dressed in green, suddenly seemed to realize something. She tugged at the woman in red's sleeve, then bowed gracefully. "This maidservant greets Elder Yang!"

Elder Yang?

The young woman in red froze. In all of Qingyang Shrine, there was only one Elder surnamed Yang. She immediately came back to her senses and bowed along with the other woman.

"No need for such courtesy." Yang Kai raised his hand slightly and asked curiously, "How did the two of you come to be here?"

The young woman in green said, "Elder Gao instructed us to come here. She said Elder Yang does not live here for most of the year and wanted us to stay and look after this place."

"Senior Sister Gao..." Yang Kai nodded. "So that is how it is."

The young woman in green said, "We have not touched anything on Spirit Sword Peak without permission. Everything remains as it was. On ordinary days, we only clean the palace and feed the fish. Please understand, Elder Yang."

Yang Kai smiled faintly. "Thank you both for your trouble."

Both young women felt flattered and overwhelmed. After all, the man before them was an Elder-level figure, and an Emperor Realm Master besides. Their statuses were humble and their cultivations low; the difference between them was like heaven and earth. Yet this Elder Yang was actually so amiable toward them that their hearts surged with excitement.

By the time they came back to their senses, Yang Kai had already walked toward the palace.

"So that is Elder Yang." The young woman in red watched Yang Kai's disappearing back with worship in her eyes. "He seems so young."

The young woman in green said, "I heard people say years ago that Elder Yang may be the youngest Elder here. Supposedly, he has cultivated for only a little over a hundred years."

"Really?" The young woman in red jumped in fright. Although their cultivations were not high, they still knew how difficult it was to become an Emperor Realm Master. Reaching such a realm in just over a hundred years was simply unimaginable.

"They say Elder Yang came from a Lower Plane Star Domain." The young woman in green clearly knew a little more. After saying this, she suddenly cried out in surprise.

"What is it?"

"Elder Gao said that when Elder Yang returned, we had to inform her. I almost forgot." Saying so, she hurriedly took out a Transmission Compass.
