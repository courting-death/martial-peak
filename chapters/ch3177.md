## Chapter 3177 — Repaying Him in Kind

Wu Heng had no intention of stopping him, but the darkness was not devouring nearly as fast as he had expected. Seeing this, Wu Heng sneered.

Although they were both Masters of Star Domains, one had held that position far longer than the other, so naturally their abilities were not equal. Yang Kai had only recently become a Star Domain Master, and he probably could not yet make full use of that identity. That was why his devouring was so slow.

Wu Heng retreated bit by bit, watching coldly from the sidelines as he waited for the moment to close the door and beat the dog.

In Hengluo Star Domain, Yang Kai's eyes were tightly shut. His state was rather strange at the moment. His Divine Sense drifted vaguely, as if he had transformed into the entire Star Domain and was opening a great mouth to gnaw at something.

Near the Void Passage, a stretch of Starry Sky that clearly did not belong to Hengluo Star Domain had appeared. Within that Starry Sky, meteorites of all sizes drifted about, and slightly different World Laws roamed and took root, creating subtle conflicts with the original Starry Sky.

Great Wilderness Star Domain and Hengluo Star Domain were both Star Domains, and their World Laws were broadly similar, but there were still differences between them. It was just like the differences in Laws between Cultivation Stars nurtured within the same Star Domain. Those differences were not obvious, but the deeper one's strength, the more clearly one could perceive them.

The Heaven Devouring Battle Art circulated, using Hengluo Star Domain as its foundation and a stretch of swallowed Starry Sky as its stomach, digesting the space it had swallowed.

The feeling in Yang Kai's heart grew even more wondrous. Although he was using the entire Star Domain as his foundation to display the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, his will still guided everything. So as the Star Domain digested the space that had been swallowed, he could clearly sense every detail within it.

In the Starry Sky that had originally belonged to Great Wilderness Star Domain, the World Laws collapsed and dissolved, gradually assimilated and steeped in Hengluo Star Domain's Laws. The two sides fused together, slowly becoming indistinguishable from one another.

There was actually no obstruction.

Where was that bastard Wu Heng? Logically, the devouring should not have gone this smoothly. Wu Heng must have made preparations, yet Yang Kai had not sensed the slightest obstruction, which puzzled him.

Puzzled or not, his movements did not slow in the slightest. While urging the Star Domain's power to transform into darkness and devour the Starry Sky of the other Star Domain, he circulated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art to digest it.

After some unknown length of time, tremendous resistance suddenly came from the other end of the darkness. The space he had devoured was rapidly dragged back. Not only that, but an extremely terrifying force passed through the dense darkness, trying to strike back at his mind.

Finally making your move? Yang Kai had been waiting for this moment all along, so he did not panic at all. He did not even defend, simply allowing that force to shuttle through the darkness and rush toward him.

At the other end of the darkness, Wu Heng sneered endlessly, his eyes full of ridicule.

Truly, a newborn calf did not fear the tiger. If Yang Kai had been willing to let the matter rest, so be it, but he had insisted on trying to devour Wu Heng's Star Domain in return. If Wu Heng did not teach him a lesson, how could he swallow this anger?

The farther and wider the darkness spread, the larger the range it devoured, and the more of Yang Kai's mind it consumed.

Wu Heng had waited until he was certain Yang Kai could not immediately withdraw before striking fiercely, preparing to use the power of his Star Domain to deal Yang Kai a heavy blow.

Just like the previous situation on Hengluo Star Domain's side, the darkness in Great Wilderness Star Domain now receded like a tide, revealing the Starry Sky that had been devoured. Barring any accidents, everything would soon recover as before.

Suddenly, the smile on Wu Heng's face stiffened. He seemed to notice something, and his eyes flew wide.

The power he had sent along the darkness had struck an invisible barrier and could no longer advance an inch, much less take the chance to harm the other party. And this barrier actually stood within his own Starry Sky.

How was that possible?

How could Yang Kai set up such a sturdy barrier in Wu Heng's own Starry Sky?

Wu Heng's heart sank, and unease began to grow within him.

At the same time, the darkness had already receded to its limit. No matter how Wu Heng tried, he could not make it advance even the slightest bit farther.

He looked up, and his mouth immediately fell open, his eyes filled with disbelief.

There in that Starry Sky, a black hole several hundred li in diameter came into view. Inside it was a mass of chaotic nothingness, as if Heaven and Earth had not yet opened, giving off an overwhelming sense of dread.

The very center of that black hole was where the Void Passage had originally been, but how could the original Void Passage have been this large? It had clearly expanded by more than a hundred times!

What was going on? Had Yang Kai actually refined part of his Star Domain?

That was the only explanation for why the Void Passage had expanded into a black hole a hundred li in diameter. The Starry Sky that had originally occupied the black hole no longer belonged to Great Wilderness Star Domain. Since that refined Starry Sky was missing, such a huge black hole had formed.

How exactly had he done it?

Wu Heng even suspected there was something wrong with his eyes.

After all, during his previous three years of effort, although he had devoured a vast stretch of Hengluo Star Domain's Starry Sky, he had only swallowed it temporarily. He had never managed to refine it. Otherwise, Yang Kai could not have taken it back so easily.

Devouring and refining were two different things.

It was like eating. Eating a bowl of rice and digesting a bowl of rice were not the same. What was eaten could still be vomited back out. Only what had been digested truly belonged to oneself.

His three years of effort had only been eating, while Yang Kai had truly digested this Starry Sky within a hundred-li radius.

His original plan had included a step for digestion as well, but he had needed to wait until he completely devoured Hengluo Star Domain and refined Hengluo Star Domain's Origin Power before he could begin. That process might have lasted a hundred years, a thousand years, or even longer.

But for the sake of increasing his strength, that cost in time was worth paying.

Before this, he had firmly believed that digesting a Star Domain was absolutely not something that could be done in a short time.

But after seeing the scene before him, he felt his common sense suffer a tremendous blow.

From the time Yang Kai began devouring his Star Domain to the time Wu Heng acted to stop him, only about a single day had passed. In that one day, he had digested a region of Wu Heng's space a hundred li in radius!

Wu Heng did not believe Yang Kai had that ability. Could Hengluo Star Domain be an existence one level higher than Great Wilderness Star Domain? Otherwise, how could such a bizarre thing have happened?

"Not good!"

As this thought flashed through his mind, Wu Heng's expression changed. He immediately connected with Great Wilderness Star Domain's Origin Power and pressed it toward that hundred-li black hole.

He had to eliminate this black hole as quickly as possible. Otherwise, once too much time passed, he would lose far more than a hundred-li stretch of territory. It might become a thousand li, ten thousand li, even a hundred million li.

He now regretted beyond measure that he had not repaired the Void Passage earlier, giving Yang Kai a chance to counterattack. If he had not tried to vent a moment's anger back then, if he had repaired the Void Passage and severed the connection between the two Star Domains, none of what followed would have happened.

Regret was useless now. He could only try to salvage the situation and pray it was not too late.

In his Sea of Consciousness, Great Wilderness Star Domain's Origin Power gave off a dazzling light, clearly having been pushed to its limit by Wu Heng. A squeezing force came from all directions, wrapping toward the black hole.

But at the edge of the black hole, it could no longer advance even an inch. The instant it touched the black hole, even that omnipresent Origin showed signs of being devoured and digested.

After struggling for a while, Wu Heng's face shifted between blue and red. He stared fixedly at the black hole, resentment brewing in his chest, making him seem like a volcano on the verge of eruption, his aura turning dangerous.

"Yang Kai!" he roared up at the sky, full of unwillingness.

Though he did not want to admit it, the truth was that by this point in his exchange with Yang Kai, he was at a complete disadvantage.

The reason was simple: that black hole no longer belonged to him. The Starry Sky that should have existed within it had all become Yang Kai's territory. No matter how he communicated with and urged the power of the Star Domain Origin, he could not repair that black hole. As long as the black hole existed, there was no way to sever the connection between the two Star Domains.

Unless Yang Kai voluntarily withdrew his troops!

Yang Kai clearly had no intention of withdrawing. At this moment, a great joy was spreading through his heart, and he wished he could devour all of Great Wilderness Star Domain until nothing remained. How could he spare any thought for Wu Heng's feelings?

The Heaven Devouring Battle Art truly deserved to be called the strongest Secret Art since time immemorial. If one had to summarize its essence in a single word, that word would be plunder!

Whether you were willing or not, anything useful would be plundered.

Cultivating the Heaven Devouring Battle Art with one's own body was already terrifying to the extreme, but at this moment Yang Kai was using the entire Star Domain as the vessel for this Secret Art. A heroic feeling of swallowing Heaven and devouring Earth immediately rose within him. He was completely convinced that as long as he had enough time, devouring and digesting the entire Great Wilderness Star Domain would not be a problem.

At first, his progress was not especially fast.

But as time passed and Yang Kai grew increasingly skilled in his comprehension and use of the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, the speed of devouring and digestion suddenly increased by quite a bit.

In less than a month, the black hole in Great Wilderness Star Domain that had originally covered only a hundred-li radius had expanded to a radius of several tens of thousands of li. Its pitch-black mouth stretched across Great Wilderness Star Domain's Starry Sky, appearing deep and terrifying at first glance. The black hole was still expanding, without the slightest sign of stopping, and its speed was imperceptibly growing faster.

Wu Heng's face looked terrible, as though his parents had just died. Great Wilderness Star Domain's damage fed back to him, and the most direct change was that a black spot had appeared in one part of the Star Domain in his Sea of Consciousness.

Compared to the entire Star Chart, this black spot was not very large, but it was like a drop of ink falling onto a beautiful painting, ruining the whole work's harmony and beauty. What made it even more unbearable was that this drop of ink was still constantly spreading in every direction. Sooner or later, it would dye the entire painting black.

If it truly reached that point, Great Wilderness Star Domain would have to change its surname to Yang.

It was not as if he had not resisted. Throughout this month, he had been constantly maneuvering and contending with Yang Kai, yet he had been unable to halt the other party's advance.

How could a mere Star Domain Master who had only just refined a Star Domain Origin possess such astonishing methods? If Wu Heng had such methods, he would have turned Hengluo Star Domain into his own rear garden long ago.

Only now did Wu Heng realize that the situation had turned dire. If he mishandled this matter, never mind raising his cultivation to another level, he might very well suffer a double loss instead.
