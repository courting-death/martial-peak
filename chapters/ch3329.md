## Chapter 3329 — Brother-in-law, You Really Made Me Suffer

Just as Qiong Qi was growing tense, Yang Kai's guiding voice reached his ear. Only then did he remember that this newly acknowledged Young Master of his was proficient in Space Force. Anyone else might get lost here, but Yang Kai never would. Without the slightest hesitation, he sped off in the direction Yang Kai indicated.

They wound left and right all the way, as if traveling through an enormous maze.

Only after a long while did a ray of light suddenly appear ahead.

This time, Qiong Qi needed no guidance. He knew at once that it had to be the entrance, so with a shake of his wings, he shot toward it and rushed out in the blink of an eye.

His vision blurred, and the world changed. Dense Spiritual Qi rushed toward them, while World Laws slightly different from those of the Star Realm lingered all around.

They had reached Dragon Isle.

Yet at that moment, a high dragon roar shook the heavens. Purple Qi From the East appeared, and in the blink of an eye, a giant dragon more than a dozen zhang long stretched across their path. Purple Qi coiled around its body, twin horns crowned its head, and every dragon scale shone with a faint glow. In an instant, it blocked Qiong Qi's way, raising its head high as Dragon Pressure spread out. Looking down from above, it thundered, "Who dares trespass on Dragon Isle!"

The voice was not deep and powerful, but rather somewhat sharp. This giant dragon was clearly female.

As soon as she spoke, the female dragon's eyes narrowed. "Qiong Qi? So it's you, old thing."

Clearly, she recognized Qiong Qi as well.

Qiong Qi tossed his head, visible heat spraying from his nostrils as he stood proudly in the air, utterly unafraid. A giant dragon just over a dozen zhang long was, at most, at the level of a Sixth-Tier Dragon Bloodline. Such a Dragon Clan member truly could not do much to him. Besides, he had come here under orders this time, and Yang Kai could handle anything that came up, so Qiong Qi simply kept his mouth shut.

On Qiong Qi's back, Mo Xiaoqi lifted her head and stared. This was her first time seeing a true Dragon Clan member, so she was naturally very curious. She wondered if her mother looked like this too when she revealed her true form. But if so, why did Mo Xiaoqi not have even the slightest trace of Dragon Clan bloodline?

"Oh?" The purple dragon suddenly looked toward Qiong Qi's back again, her gaze fixing on Yang Kai. Surprise and delight flashed in her eyes as she cried, "Brother-in-law?"

Brother-in-law? Qiong Qi was completely baffled by that address. Who was her brother-in-law? Who here could possibly be her brother-in-law? He himself certainly had nothing to do with the Dragon Clan, and Mo Xiaoqi was a girl, so it could not be her. Could it be...

The moment that thought struck him, Qiong Qi's expression changed dramatically.

Yang Kai's voice came from his back, impossible to read as pleased or angry. "You can eat whatever you like, but you can't say whatever you like. Who is your brother-in-law?"

The purple dragon paid him no mind at all. With a turn, her dozen-zhang-long body twisted and transformed, directly becoming a young girl dressed in purple. She was none other than Fu Ling, who had previously led Yang Kai to the place where the Dragon Blood Flowers were cultivated.

Across all of Dragon Isle, even counting every last member, the Dragon Clan had fewer than twenty clansmen. Their bloodlines were all incomparably pure, but some were still higher than others. At the top were the Great Elder and Second Elder, both with Tenth-Tier bloodlines. Below them, Fu Xuan, Zhu Kong, and Zhu Qing each had Ninth-Tier bloodlines. After that came Eighth-Tier, Seventh-Tier, Sixth-Tier, and Fifth-Tier bloodlines.

A Dragon Clan member's bloodline would gradually purify with time. In other words, the longer one lived, the higher one's bloodline rank became. Of course, natural advantages and the benefits of taking Dragon Blood Flowers also played a part.

Fu Ling was a purple dragon with a Sixth-Tier bloodline.

Last time, Yang Kai had forced her to take him to the place where the Dragon Blood Flowers were cultivated, and in the end, he had nearly swept clean every mature Dragon Blood Flower there. After the matter came to light, Fu Ling had naturally been punished and sent here to guard Dragon Isle's entrance.

If the Elders had not known that Yang Kai's bloodline was strange and that Fu Ling had been powerless to resist him, this offense alone would have been enough to have her exiled into the Dragon Tomb, never to see the light of day again.

After Fu Ling took human form, she flickered and appeared on Qiong Qi's back, standing face-to-face with Yang Kai. Less than a chi separated them, and a delicate fragrance immediately wafted toward him. With both hands clasped behind her back, she leaned in like an innocent young girl and giggled, "Of course it's you. In this world, who else would be worthy of having me, Fu Ling, call him brother-in-law?"

Yang Kai unconsciously leaned back, mainly because he was afraid her ample chest would touch him.

Seeing this, Fu Ling instead pressed her advantage and deliberately pushed her chest out, making Yang Kai's face darken. He said in a low voice, "You and I are neither kin nor close. What virtue or ability do I have to be your brother-in-law? Miss Fu Ling, please stop joking."

Fu Ling giggled. "I'm not joking. If I say you're my brother-in-law, then you're my brother-in-law." As she spoke, she actually took another step forward.

This time, before Yang Kai could retreat, Mo Xiaoqi suddenly rushed out from behind him and squeezed herself between Yang Kai and Fu Ling. Spreading her arms as if to protect Yang Kai, she flushed red and said, "What are you doing? If you want to talk, talk. Why are you standing so close?" She had clearly noticed Fu Ling's other intentions too, and a surge of anger rose in her, as if someone were coveting something precious to her.

Fu Ling looked Mo Xiaoqi up and down, then sneered, "Where did this ugly little girl come from? I'm talking to brother-in-law. What business is it of yours? Move aside. Move aside, or this Young Lady will beat you."

When had Mo Xiaoqi ever faced such a situation? On Spirit Beast Island, she was the little princess beneath one person and above ten thousand, cherished and pampered by everyone. She had truly never seen anyone as fierce and vicious as Fu Ling, and she could not help trembling in fright. Even so, she stubbornly kept her arms spread and blocked the way, her cheeks puffed with anger.

Yang Kai looked coldly at Fu Ling. "Who are you going to beat?"

Fu Ling heard the chill in his voice, and her thoughts shifted. Smiling, she said, "Aiya, brother-in-law, don't be angry. I was only teasing her. But brother-in-law, it's one thing for you to come see Elder Sister Qing. Why did you bring a little girl with you too? If Elder Sister Qing finds out..." She winked at Yang Kai, revealing an expression that said, You understand.

"That has nothing to do with you!" Yang Kai snorted, then turned to look around and asked curiously, “Why are you here instead of staying on Dragon Isle?”

This was only the entrance, still some distance from the true Dragon Isle. The people of Half-Dragon City called the true Dragon Isle the Dragon Palace, and the Dragon Clan lived over on the Dragon Palace side. Under normal circumstances, Dragon Clan members would not leave the Dragon Palace.

If he had not asked, it would have been fine, but the moment he did, Fu Ling's face fell. Looking utterly aggrieved and on the verge of tears, she choked out, "Brother-in-law, you really made me suffer terribly."

Yang Kai snorted. "What does that have to do with me?"

Fu Ling's eyes reddened, and she actually squeezed out two tears. Gazing at Yang Kai with watery eyes, she said, "Brother-in-law, have you forgotten those Dragon Blood Flowers?"

“Dragon Blood Flowers?” Yang Kai was startled when he heard this, then clenched his fist and coughed lightly. “How did this get dragged back to the Dragon Blood Flowers again?”

Fu Ling said, "Last time, I took you to steal so many Dragon Blood Flowers. After you left, the Elders discovered it and wanted to exile me into the Dragon Tomb. Fortunately, the Third Elder and Elder Sister Qing were merciful and spoke up for me, so I escaped that calamity. But as punishment, I have to guard this entrance for a hundred years." As she spoke, tears poured down without stopping, and she looked truly heartbroken.

After all, she had not been the one who stole the Dragon Blood Flowers. She had been forced into it and had no choice, yet in the end, she was punished with a hundred-year guard post and could not even casually return to the Dragon Palace. When all was said and done, Yang Kai had truly ruined her.

Those were more than a thousand Dragon Blood Flowers, including Lower-Level, Middle-Level, Upper-Level, and even two Supreme-Level Dragon Blood Flowers that the Great Elder and Second Elder had carefully cultivated for many years. Yang Kai had swept them all clean.

The Dragon Clan's accumulation over several thousand years had almost all gone straight into Yang Kai's pocket. If they wanted to restore it to its former state, it would be impossible without another several thousand years of accumulation. During those several thousand years, the Dragon Clan members on Dragon Isle would no longer have any chance to take Dragon Blood Flowers to improve their bloodlines, greatly delaying the time it would take for the Dragon Clan to grow stronger.

For the past dozen or so years, Fu Ling had hated Yang Kai to death. More than once, she had imagined killing him the next time she saw him, but that was only fantasy. She knew she could not possibly be Yang Kai's opponent. The suppression between their bloodlines was too severe, not to mention that she had offered Yang Kai her Soul Brand back then, placing her life and death entirely in his hands.

Now that she had finally seen the cause of her suffering, she naturally had to pour out her grievances.

Of course, there was another, even more important reason that could not be shared with outsiders. This brother-in-law seemed to have the ability to make a Dragon Clan member's bloodline rise rapidly. Hadn't Elder Sister Qing originally had only an Eighth-Tier bloodline? Now she was already Ninth-Tier and had been gloriously promoted to Elder.

As long as Fu Ling's scheme succeeded, her own Sixth-Tier bloodline could easily be raised to Seventh-Tier or even Eighth-Tier, which would be more effective than taking any Dragon Blood Flower.

"It's only a hundred years. To you, it's not that long." Yang Kai sniffed. Although he rather looked down on Fu Ling's character, this matter really had happened because of him, and he felt somewhat guilty about it. Still, he had no intention of dwelling on it, lest Fu Ling seize on some excuse and chatter endlessly. Waving his hand, he said, "The past is past. Why bring it up? I'm asking you, where is Zhu Qing now?"

Fu Ling looked resentful, but she did not dare refuse to answer. "Elder Sister Qing is on her own Spirit Island."

"And the Third Elder?"

"Also on her own Spirit Island." Her eyes suddenly rolled as she said, "Brother-in-law, do you not know the way? Do you want me to take you there?"

Yang Kai looked her over. "Weren't you punished to guard this place for a hundred years? If you leave without permission, won't you get in trouble?"

"No, no." Fu Ling pursed her lips in a sweet smile. "I've guarded this place for more than ten years and no one has come in, only you and your group, brother-in-law. What's the big deal if I leave for a little while?"

"If something happens, don't put the blame on me."

Fu Ling protested, "Am I such an unreasonable person? Brother-in-law, you look down on me too much."

"Enough nonsense. Hurry up and lead the way."

"Mm, mm!" Fu Ling answered repeatedly, then lifted her foot and stamped on Qiong Qi. "You old fellow, why are you still standing there in a daze? Hurry up and go. Go forward. Don't tell me you don't know where the Dragon Palace is."

Qiong Qi could not help rolling his eyes. Truly, once one became a mount for a day, all integrity was lost. Even a little girl of the Dragon Clan now dared stand on his back and order him about.

But... what exactly was going on? Why would this dragon girl call Yang Kai brother-in-law? Could this Young Master of his truly have a thing with some dragon girl?
