## Chapter 3191 — Let's Stop Here

The void shattered. Several nearby Dead Stars exploded with a thunderous crash, scattering into endless rubble in every direction.

When the Master of a Star Domain flew into a rage, the entire Star Domain would be swept into the fallout.

A dragon roar rang out, its long cry carrying above the nine heavens. In the next moment, golden light blazed, and an enormous golden dragon emerged serenely, its majestic dragon eyes looking down on Heaven and Earth as if all living beings were ants. With a shake of its head and a sweep of its tail, it charged toward the azure giant palm.

"Dragon Language!" The feather-crowned man's expression shifted, his face filled with astonishment and shock. He had heard that obscure, complex tongue once before. It was Dragon Language, without question. And this dazzling golden dragon was clearly a Dragon Clan Secret Technique.

What was going on?

"What is your relationship with the Dragon Clan?" he roared, and the descending azure giant palm faltered.

Even if Yang Kai was the Master of a Star Domain, the feather-crowned man still had the standing to kill him for offending him. At worst, he would be punished after returning to the Star Court. But if the boy before him had ties to the Dragon Clan, that was a matter he had to treat with care.

The Dragon Clan were proud beyond measure, and pride meant protecting their own and bearing grudges.

To provoke a single Dragon Clan member was to provoke all of Dragon Isle. Even he had no confidence in facing the wrath of the entire Dragon Isle. As far as he knew, whether it was Dragon Language or Dragon Clan Secret Techniques, only the purest Dragon Clan members had ever been qualified to learn them, or capable of learning them. Those with half-dragon bloodlines could not possibly grasp the mysteries of that tongue-twisting Dragon Language.

Could this boy not have cultivated some Secret Technique that let him transform into a half-dragon after all? Could he actually be a Dragon Clan member?

The moment Yang Kai displayed that Dragon Language Secret Technique, the feather-crowned man grew wary, and the power of his Divine Ability weakened despite himself.

Yang Kai was overjoyed when he saw this. In that split second, he understood everything, inwardly marveling that the words Dragon Clan really did make fine protective armor. Even in this Lower Plane Star Domain, they could fill others with dread. Their reputation was every bit as fierce as rumored.

Why would he explain anything? In a life-or-death battle, the slightest lapse could prove fatal, especially when his strength was already inferior to his opponent's.

Amid the dragon roar, the golden dragon slammed into the azure giant palm. Energy churned, and both palm and dragon suddenly locked into a strange stalemate. Azure and gold halos flashed against each other as invisible shockwaves spread outward with a rumble.

Yang Kai secretly broke out in a cold sweat. Luckily, the man had held back a little just now; otherwise, Yang Kai might truly have been no match for him.

"Boy, answer my question!" the feather-crowned man barked with stern authority.

Yang Kai's eyes went wide, nearly splitting at the corners, as he urged the Dragon Blood in his body even more frantically and roared, "What’s so great about the Dragon Clan? Your grandfather here is a man who will become a Dragon Emperor!"

At those words, the feather-crowned man froze.

Dragon Emperor! That was a supreme, unrivaled title.

Although Dragon Isle now had several Great Elders who jointly governed Dragon Isle and the entire Dragon Clan, even Great Elder Zhu Yan was not qualified to call himself a Dragon Emperor. Dragon Emperor meant the sovereign of the Dragon Clan, the one who ruled all Dragon Clan members under the heavens, the highest existence among them.

What absurd nonsense. The feather-crowned man immediately found the thought ridiculous and secretly mocked himself for being too cautious. Even if this boy had somehow learned a Dragon Clan Secret Technique by lucky chance, that did not mean he truly had anything to do with the Dragon Clan. How could a Dragon Clan member look like him? And yet, those words did not sound like simple bluster. They sounded like a matter-of-fact goal, one he meant to achieve sooner or later.

Kacha cha...

The sound of bones shifting reached his ears. The feather-crowned man looked closely, and his eyes narrowed.

Yang Kai, who had already stood twenty zhang tall, had suddenly shot up again, reaching twenty-five zhang in the blink of an eye! Not only had he grown taller, the Dragon Clan traits across his body had become even more pronounced. The dragon horns on his head had lengthened, the scales covering him had taken on a smoother luster, and his facial features writhed and changed until they had fully transformed into a massive dragon head. His golden dragon eyes brimmed with lofty indifference, as if nothing under the heavens was worth his notice.

Under this tremendous pressure, Yang Kai's Dragon Transformation Art had broken through again, advancing by leaps and bounds.

The Dragon Blood inside him flowed faster and more joyfully. In some unseen way, Yang Kai felt subtle changes in both his vision and his state of mind, and an aloof, superior feeling rose in him against his will.

The Dragon Clan called themselves the leaders, the foremost of the Divine Spirits. Though it was a self-proclaimed title, no living being disputed it, because no one in this world was qualified to do so. Every Dragon Clan member was born proud, born to look down upon all things.

Meeting those majestic dragon eyes, the feather-crowned man's heart gave an involuntary jump, and his mind rippled.

After Yang Kai's Dragon Transformation Art advanced, the power of the Dragon Clan Secret Technique seemed to surge as well. It had been locked in stalemate with the azure giant palm, but now it was beginning to gain the upper hand.

The feather-crowned man gritted his teeth and poured in more strength, only barely managing to suppress Yang Kai's resistance.

"Divine Dragon Tail Swing!" Yang Kai roared. His body did not move, but the dragon tail behind him curled and lashed out, sweeping toward the feather-crowned man at waist height.

For an instant, the feather-crowned man only felt a terrifying force rushing at him. He was just about to dodge when the dragon tail struck him squarely.

He had been caught completely off guard. The sweeping dragon tail seemed to ignore the barrier of space, leaving even someone as powerful as him no time to react.

It was as if a star had slammed into him. He could not help giving a muffled groan as bones cracked inside his body. Fortunately, he had been on guard, so the blow did not send him flying in a sorry state.

Even so, the strike utterly enraged him. Killing intent surged like a tide, and he gritted his teeth and roared, "Do you really think this Lord dares not kill you?"

This boy had humiliated him again and again. He would slaughter even a true Dragon Clan member for that, much less one whose exact relationship with the Dragon Clan remained unclear.

This was a Lower Plane Star Domain. Even if he killed Yang Kai, the Dragon Clan likely would never discover it. He did have reservations, but that did not mean he would endure without end.

His full strength erupted with a rumble, and the azure giant palm that had been deadlocked with the golden dragon suddenly pressed down.

Another dragon roar rang out, but the golden dragon could not withstand the crushing pressure and fell apart. The giant palm bore straight down toward the top of Yang Kai's head, sealing off the surrounding space and cutting off every path of escape.

At that life-or-death moment, all the madness vanished from Yang Kai's face, replaced by perfect calm.

Their eyes met, and the feather-crowned man's heart lurched. An intense unease rose in him, as if something terrifying would surely happen if his palm truly fell. A sense of danger actually took root in his heart.

But the arrow was already on the string. How could he not release it? Gritting his teeth and steeling himself, he thought, Boy, die!

A delicate jade hand suddenly appeared from thin air and lightly blocked the feather-crowned man's great hand as it descended toward Yang Kai.

"Let's stop here!" A faint voice rang out, crisp and pleasant as spring water over stone, so sweet that it was a delight to hear. Though the voice was soft, it carried a quiet force that made others want to believe and obey.

A graceful figure slowly emerged, standing right beside the feather-crowned man.

Yang Kai's eyes widened, and he stared blankly at the person who had suddenly appeared.

She wore golden robes and a red-gold phoenix crown, its phoenix wings spread as if ready to take flight. Her waterfall-like black hair was coiled into a bun behind her head, and a deep red plum blossom mark adorned the space between her brows. Her willow-leaf brows arched like new moons, her starry eyes were bright and moving, her jade nose was exquisite, and her delicate, flawless skin was white as snow and soft as cream. Her figure was alluring. Golden attire was normally gaudy, yet on her it carried an inexhaustible air of grace and nobility.

"Yang..." Yang Kai opened his mouth, then stopped, not daring to recognize her, and froze where he stood.

The azure giant palm descended over his head, only to collapse and scatter all at once. Its remaining power dispersed, turning into azure light that faded between Heaven and Earth.

The feather-crowned man turned to look at her. Once he saw her face clearly, he frowned.

Even though he had seen her many times, she still struck him as stunning. Along the Martial Dao, the higher the cultivation, the fewer women there were. Among the ten Great Emperors, only Flower Shadow Great Emperor was a woman; all the others were men. It was not that women's aptitudes were inferior, but that on the path of cultivation, women were far more easily disturbed than men, especially by matters of love.

How many outstanding women had been trapped by love, their cultivation brought to a standstill? That was why, at higher levels of cultivation, women became fewer and fewer.

Yet the woman before him was very likely to claim a place among those ten, and she had the qualifications to stand on equal footing with him.

The feather-crowned man thought highly of himself. He could look down on Yang Kai, and he even had the courage to secretly slaughter a dragon, but he still had to take her seriously.

"Why are you here?" the feather-crowned man asked.

The woman pursed her lips in a smile. "Why shouldn't I be?"

The feather-crowned man thought for a moment, then nodded. "True. This is the Star Domain under your jurisdiction, after all."

The woman said, "Indeed. A Master of a Star Domain has appeared in my Star Domain, so of course I had to come take a look. Otherwise, wouldn't you have cut him down?"

The feather-crowned man snorted. "He was seeking death."

Yang Kai immediately took offense at that. He pulled his gaze back from the woman at once, lifted his chin toward the feather-crowned man, and struck a wildly arrogant pose. "If you're not convinced, come fight again and we'll see who dies!"

The feather-crowned man's face sank, and he sneered, "Since you managed to keep your life, you should be grateful. If you dare run your mouth again..."

"What will you do?"

The feather-crowned man flicked his sleeve. "Forget it. This Lord will not lower himself to your level."

Since this woman had appeared, it was no longer appropriate for him to bully the weak. Earlier, he had decided to kill the boy here because no one would know, but now that there was a witness, he could only abandon the idea of eliminating him. Besides, if he really attacked Yang Kai, this woman would certainly not stand by and watch. This Star Domain was under her jurisdiction, after all.

In short, today's matter would probably have to end without resolution.

"Hand over this Lord's Star-Cutting Sword, and today's matter ends here!" The feather-crowned man clasped his hands behind his back and looked coldly at Yang Kai.

So it was called the Star-Cutting Sword! Yang Kai's heart stirred, but he merely looked left and right, as if he had not heard a word.

A vein jumped on the feather-crowned man's forehead. "Boy, this Lord is talking to you. Are you deaf?"

"What did you say? I can't hear you!" Yang Kai tilted his dragon head and raised one hand beside his ear, his voice shaking the universe.
