## Chapter 3193 — I Want to Cripple Your Cultivation

He had accepted his fate so readily mostly because his immediate superior was Yang Yan. If it had been the feather-crowned man from just now instead, Yang Kai definitely would not have been nearly so agreeable.

Yang Yan gave him a strange look. "What exactly are you planning to do with this Star Domain? Why are you so resistant to the Star Court's jurisdiction?"

"What could I possibly do?"

Yang Yan smiled. "You really don't need to be like this. We call it jurisdiction, but in truth, no one cares about these things. You are still the Master of this Star Domain, and you can do whatever you want. Of course, the one thing you cannot do is... destroy the Star Domain!" By the end, her voice had suddenly lowered, and her beautiful eyes turned toward that boundless darkness, grave and solemn.

Yang Kai hurriedly changed the subject. "I understand. Speaking of which, you showed up so quickly. Have you been watching this Star Domain all along?"

"Not really. It was just a coincidence." Although she governed Hengluo Star Domain, how could she pay attention to this place at every moment? Moreover, even from within the Star Court, keeping watch over this place took effort. Where would she find that much time and leisure? It was only because the feather-crowned man had just received a summons and come to Great Wilderness Star Domain that she sensed it. She had casually checked the situation in Hengluo Star Domain, discovered Yang Kai's presence, and in the end had no choice but to descend here and block a disaster for him.

Still... she kept feeling that Yang Kai had another trump card. Even if she had not acted, he might not have died beneath that attack, and might even have had the strength to strike back. It had only been a few dozen years, yet he had already grown to this point? Back then, she had deliberately left without bringing him along precisely because she wanted him to temper himself more. Only strength earned through one's own struggles truly belonged to oneself; what others bestowed would always remain an external thing.

She had long known Yang Kai was no fish meant for a pond, but she had not expected him to ride the wind and transform into a dragon in such a short span of time.

She had underestimated him!

"Coincidence or not, thank you." Yang Kai casually rubbed his belly. The seal there had nearly been released just now. If Yang Yan had not descended, he would certainly have demonified in his Dragon Transformation body, and when that happened, he would definitely have given the feather-crowned man a tremendous surprise. "You still haven't told me what terms you reached with that guy. He took away the Star-Cutting Sword. Does he really not have to pay any price at all? Isn't that letting him off far too cheaply?"

"What do you mean, that guy? His name is..."

"Stop!" Yang Kai raised a hand. "I have no interest in knowing his name."

Yang Yan smiled. "You really do hold grudges." She had seen almost the entire battle between Yang Kai and the feather-crowned man from beginning to end, so naturally she knew about the verbal clashes between them.

Yang Kai said solemnly, "One day, he will take the initiative to tell me his name when he is about to die."

"Then that will depend on whether you have the ability." Yang Yan did not try to advise him otherwise. It was not necessarily a bad thing for Yang Kai to carry such hatred. At the very least, shame could spur courage and drive him to improve. Hatred and women had always been the two great forces that pushed men to cultivate and grow stronger.

She waved a hand and pointed toward the boundless darkness. "The devoured Star Domain will belong to you from now on."

Yang Kai looked at her expectantly and waited for a long while, but nothing more came. Speechless, he said, "That's it?"

Yang Yan snapped, "What else do you want?"

Yang Kai gnashed his teeth. "You really aren't Yang Yan. Speak, who exactly are you!" In the early years, she would haggle over a few Crystal Stones, so how could she be this generous now? Yang Kai had still been hoping to devour the entire Great Wilderness Star Domain. Looking at it this way, what was even the point? Besides, the devoured Star Domain already belonged to him and could no longer be separated. Even if Yang Kai did not return it, the feather-crowned man had no way to do anything to him.

This benefit... could hardly be called a benefit at all.

"You think you lost out?" Yang Yan sneered.

Yang Kai felt as if his heart were bleeding. "How is this not losing out?"

"Wake up, boy. If he returns to the Star Court and reports what happened here, I guarantee you won't live three days. Iron Blood will immediately capture you and chop you into pieces!"

"It shouldn't go that far." Yang Kai felt inexplicably guilty. "Lord Iron Blood is one of the ten Great Emperors, after all. How could he personally act against me?"

"If you were merely an ordinary Master of a Star Domain, Iron Blood naturally wouldn't bother with you. But are you an ordinary Master of a Star Domain? What cultivation art have you been cultivating?"

Yang Kai mumbled, "The one you taught me, the Star Refining Art!"

"I am not blind!" Yang Yan poked her own eyes, then pointed toward the darkness ahead. "Do you think I can't recognize this familiar feeling? Boy, you really are something. It has only been a few years since we last met, yet you have actually inherited Heaven Devourer's mantle and even started cultivating the Heaven Devouring Battle Art."

"What Heaven Devouring Battle Art? Never heard of it."

"Still trying to lie to me?"

"Fine, fine, it's the Heaven Devouring Battle Art!" Since she had seen through him, Yang Kai simply admitted it.

Yang Yan's expression changed. "It really is the Heaven Devouring Battle Art!"

Yang Kai stared at her in shock, then gritted his teeth. "You damned woman, you tricked me?"

But Yang Yan was trembling with rage, her face ashen, her jade hands clenched into fists. In an instant, wisps of killing intent actually rose from her aura, startling Yang Kai so badly that every hair on his body stood on end. Although he had never fought Yang Yan, he had a feeling that she was stronger than the feather-crowned man from just now. Even if he truly demonified in his Dragon Transformation body, he still might not be her opponent.

The killing intent came quickly and vanished just as quickly. Yang Yan lowered her eyes, her long lashes casting shadows that hid her expression. In a pained voice, she asked, "How did you get involved with Heaven Devourer?"

She was different from the feather-crowned man. He had only sensed that something was a little off and felt that the cultivation art was extremely profound, something someone like Yang Kai should not have been able to grasp or come into contact with. Yang Yan, however, had participated in the War of Emperors back then. She had personally witnessed Heaven Devourer Great Emperor's might and knew the terror of the Heaven Devouring Battle Art.

Origin Cauldron, Flame Martial, Vast Sea, Green Lotus: all those Great Emperors had possessed illustrious reputations and were peerless talents rarely seen throughout the ages, yet in the end, all of them died at Wu Kuang's hands.

She had not dared be too certain that it was the Heaven Devouring Battle Art. Acting on the principle that it was better to be wrong than to let it slip by, she had bluffed Yang Kai once. She had not expected to receive an answer that made even her feel despair.

Wu Kuang was a sinner condemned through the ages, and the root of it was this Heaven Devouring Battle Art. Anyone who cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art could not be tolerated by the world.

When she said that if Iron Blood Great Emperor learned of this, Iron Blood would certainly act and tear him into ten thousand pieces, she was not trying to scare him. Iron Blood was bold by nature, loved conflict and battle, and could not tolerate the slightest flaw. Moreover, Vast Sea Great Emperor, who had died at Wu Kuang's hands during the War of Emperors, had been his close friend.

If Iron Blood learned that someone in this world had obtained Wu Kuang's mantle and inheritance, how could he possibly let the matter rest? When the time came, even if Yang Yan and Lin Yun'er pleaded together, it would probably be useless.

The agreement she had reached with the feather-crowned man was that Yang Kai would return the Star-Cutting Sword, while the devoured Starry Sky would belong to Yang Kai. It was an easy condition to accept. Yang Yan had not pressed the feather-crowned man too hard, because only in this way would he regard today's events as a humiliation and probably never mention them to anyone else.

Naturally, no one would notice the changes in Hengluo Star Domain. Great Wilderness Star Domain was under the feather-crowned man's jurisdiction, while Hengluo Star Domain was under Yang Yan's. As long as the two Star Envoys did not report the matter upward, it could be quietly dropped.

That was why she said Yang Kai had not lost out. Concealing the matter here alone was enough to make the feather-crowned man shoulder an enormous risk.

Yang Yan's voice was sorrowful and bitter, full of disappointment at his failure to live up to her hopes. It made Yang Kai feel as if he really had done something wrong. He grew guilty all at once, pursed his lips, and said, "This isn't that big a deal, is it?"

He also knew that the Heaven Devouring Battle Art could not be brought into the light, so he had always been extremely cautious over the years. Even so, he had always felt that cultivation arts themselves were not evil; people were evil. The Heaven Devouring Battle Art's characteristics were a little strange, but no matter how strange a cultivation art was, it was still only a cultivation art.

He had never expected Yang Yan to react so strongly. Warmth rose in his heart. Although Yang Yan had changed quite a bit, in the end, she still cared about him. Only care could bring such grief. If she did not care, she could simply capture Yang Kai and take him back to the Star Court to report in, clearing herself of any involvement.

"What do you know!" Yang Yan sighed. No wonder, then. His cultivation had become so impressive in a mere few dozen years. If he had cultivated the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, how could his cultivation not improve quickly? That was the cultivation art best suited to increasing cultivation and strength. She raised her head, suddenly arrived beside Yang Kai, grabbed his arm, and stared at him with shining eyes.

"What are you doing!" Yang Kai was startled, sensing something ominous in those bright eyes.

Yang Yan said, "Your aptitude is excellent, and your temperament is firm. Even if you start over from the beginning, I imagine it won't be a big deal. Don't worry. This time, I will bring you back to the Star Court and personally guide your cultivation. I guarantee you will recover your strength within a hundred years."

"What do you mean!" Yang Kai immediately felt that something was wrong.

Yang Yan said resolutely, "I want to cripple your cultivation!"

Yang Kai flew into a rage and raised his fist at her. "You dare!"

His cultivation had been won through countless brushes with death. How could it be crippled just because she said so?

"You have to agree whether you want to or not!" Yang Yan insisted, her face full of self-reproach. "This is my fault too. Back then, I left you here with good intentions, but I never imagined you would stray onto a crooked path."

"You're the one on a crooked path! Your whole family is on a crooked path! Let go already, or I'll beat you. Don't think I won't hit you just because you're a woman."

"Hit me, then! Hit me!" Yang Yan turned her face and deliberately leaned her head toward him.

Yang Kai swung his fist down, sending a fierce wind rushing at her face, but Yang Yan did not dodge at all.

His fist stopped three inches from her face, and Yang Yan smiled smugly.

"Crazy woman!" Yang Kai cursed through gritted teeth.

Yang Yan said solemnly, "Don't be afraid. It will be over quickly. Just endure it for a moment."

"Calm down!" Yang Kai grabbed her other hand and held it tight. "Calm down."

"You are the one who needs to calm down."

"Fine, I'm calm too. You really made me so angry I lost my head." Yang Kai looked utterly speechless. "Mm, listen to me finish speaking first. If you still want to cripple my cultivation after that, I won't resist."

"Truly?"

"Yes!" Yang Kai nodded heavily.

"Then speak!"

Yang Kai took a moment to organize his thoughts, then said, "When you say the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, you aren't wrong. I do indeed know the cultivation method for the Heaven Devouring Battle Art, but I have never cultivated it."
