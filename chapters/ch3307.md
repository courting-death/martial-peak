## Chapter 3307 — Ghost King Body

Whooshing sounds rang out as the remaining Emperor Realm Masters who were still alive fled from Yang Kai’s side as though avoiding a ghost or god. Every face was filled with lingering fear, each of them feeling as though they had somehow snatched their life from the jaws of death.

It was not that Yang Kai had been unwilling to wipe them all out; he simply had not found where they were hiding. Though the Mountain and River Bell had shattered most of the sea of blood, a few gaps still remained, and these survivors had happened to be concealed in those very places.

He ignored them. Instead, he turned his head, locked onto Fu Bo, and grinned savagely.

Their eyes met. Fu Bo's heart was still bleeding, and his mind jolted as he sensed Yang Kai's malice. A clear thought rose within him: He wants to kill me!

He actually wants to kill me? Fu Bo could hardly believe it.

He was the Sect Master of Netherworld Sect, a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master. Yet a junior brat at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm actually wanted to kill him? At that moment, Fu Bo was almost angry enough to laugh.

His vision blurred, and Yang Kai vanished from sight. Fu Bo could only make out a faint streak bursting from the sea of blood and pouncing straight at him.

Fu Bo's face went cold. He raised his hand and summoned a great banner wreathed in dense black Qi. As the Sect Master of Netherworld Sect, he naturally possessed his own Ten Thousand Souls Banner. This banner was bound to his life and cultivation, had been refined and nurtured for a full two thousand years, had reached the very limit of an Imperial Treasure, and was the method he relied on most.

Without the slightest hesitation, the Black Banner wrapped around him and instantly merged into his body, making his already sinister aura even colder and more terrifying.

At the same time, Fu Bo's body began to undergo a tremendous change. He had been only a short old man, half a head shorter than most people, but now his body suddenly swelled, transforming in an instant into a hideous monster five zhang tall. His face turned blue-green, fangs jutted from his mouth, two terrifying short horns grew from his forehead, and both hands became withered and long, their sharp nails pitch-black. One look was enough to tell they carried deadly poison.

"Ghost King Body!" Tang Sheng cried softly, his eyes trembling. He had not expected Fu Bo to be so cautious that he would use his most deeply hidden trump card right from the start.

On second thought, though, it was not strange. The strength Yang Kai had displayed earlier was far too terrifying. It was simply not power a Second Stage Emperor Realm Master should possess. If Tang Sheng were in his place, facing such an enemy, he would not dare be careless in the slightest; only using his strongest power would give him any sense of security.

That Ghost King's aura was terrifying, as though it truly had come from the Yellow Springs. Even the breath from its mouth was thick with Ghost Qi. An ordinary cultivator would die the moment he touched it, and the temperature between heaven and earth plunged.

The instant Fu Bo revealed his Ghost King Body, Yang Kai had already pounced in front of him. The broad Myriad Sword in his hand rose high, then came crashing down.

Fu Bo looked over coldly. He neither dodged nor retreated, merely raising one hand to block.

With a clang, sparks flew everywhere. Myriad Sword shuddered with a mournful cry, and the web between Yang Kai's thumb and forefinger went a little numb. Surprise flickered across his face. He had not used his full strength in that strike because Myriad Sword could not bear it. This was, after all, the Qin Family's inherited Imperial Treasure, and he had made an agreement with Qin Chaoyang: once Qin Yu advanced to the Emperor Realm, this sword would be returned. If he really damaged this Imperial Treasure, he would have no way to explain himself.

Even so, although he had not used his full strength, that sword strike was still not something an ordinary person could withstand. Yet Fu Bo, that old bastard, had actually taken it head-on and even seemed quite relaxed, forcing Yang Kai to reassess the monster the old fellow had become.

Although Yang Kai had dealt with people from Netherworld Sect many times, this was his first time seeing the Ghost King Body. He did not know that the Ghost King Body had copper tendons and iron bones, a powerful flesh body, and boundless strength.

After blocking the sword, Fu Bo did not pause at all. His other hand curved into an eagle claw and reached straight for Yang Kai's chest. The ghost claw was razor-sharp and flickered with the aura of death, clearly aiming to tear out Yang Kai's heart in one move.

Even with his Half-Dragon body, Yang Kai did not dare underestimate the power of this attack. He tapped his foot and drifted backward.

More than a dozen li away, Lan He's pretty face suddenly paled as she cried out, "Watch your back!"

In her field of vision, a figure had suddenly appeared behind Yang Kai just as he dodged the ghost claw. The man moved so quickly that even the few spectators had failed to notice him in time, only realizing he was there when he abruptly showed himself.

It was none other than Xu Changfeng, the Saint Lord of Brahma Heaven Sacred Land.

In Xu Changfeng’s hand was a pitch-black sword, its blade dull and lightless. He thrust it straight forward, silent and soundless. Though it lacked the imposing might of Fu Bo’s attack, it was a truly fatal killing move.

The combined strike of two Third-Stage Emperor Realm Masters, one from the front and one from behind, looked as if it had been planned in advance. Under all heaven, there were definitely not many people who could avoid it.

At the critical moment, Yang Kai suddenly flicked his hand. A figure appeared behind him in a bizarre flash, as tall as a person and shaped like a man. Its entire body seemed cast from earth and stone, yet it was covered in bone spikes, making it look like a stone hedgehog, ferocious beyond measure. Strange flames also rose from its body.

Clang... With a light ring, Xu Changfeng's pitch-black sword struck the stone hedgehog. That peerlessly sharp blade, keen enough to cut metal and sever jade, had no effect at all and was instead firmly blocked.

Xu Changfeng froze, his eyes widening as he stared at the stone hedgehog in front of him. For an instant, he almost thought he was dreaming.

What was this? Where had this thing come from? A sword strike that should have been a certain kill had actually been stopped by this bizarre creature.

The Incarnation lowered its head to look at the long sword pressed against its chest, then raised its head and looked at Xu Changfeng. A savage, unfathomable grin appeared on its hard, angular face.

Xu Changfeng was horrified. This thing was alive? The thought had barely crossed his mind when a heaven-overflowing fiendish aura rushed toward him. The Incarnation stepped forward, pushing against Xu Changfeng's pitch-black sword and forcing him back again and again.

"Divine Spirit!" Xu Changfeng cried out in alarm. He had actually sensed the aura of a Divine Spirit from this stone hedgehog. Looking at its form and appearance, then comparing it against what he knew, an irrepressible thought surfaced: Shi Huo!

The Divine Spirit Shi Huo, which had once destroyed Black Tortoise Sect, one of the Eastern Territory's top Sects!

How could it be here? How had it appeared? Was it not said to be in the Wild Ancient Land? Question after question rose in Xu Changfeng's mind, but he could not find an answer no matter how he tried. He only knew that he was in deep trouble now.

Although he also had a Third-Stage Emperor Realm cultivation, he was still far inferior to a Divine Spirit. In the Ancient Land, several Divine Spirits styled themselves Saint Venerables, and beneath them served the Thirty-Two Yao Kings. Xu Changfeng judged that he was, at most, equivalent to one of those Yao Kings.

Even the Yao Kings were subordinates of these Divine Spirits, so how could he possibly be their opponent?

Could today's events be a trap? If not, how could the Divine Spirit Shi Huo have suddenly appeared, and why would it be targeting him? But... that made no sense at all. The Divine Spirits of the Ancient Land had lain dormant for many years and had never had any disputes with the great forces of the Eastern Territory, because the Eastern Territory had several Great Emperor-level experts. It was one thing for them to hide within the Ancient Land and keep to themselves, but if they truly dared come out and stir up the world, it would be no different from courting death.

As all these thoughts flashed through his mind, Xu Changfeng was scared out of his wits. He shot backward, instantly widening the distance between himself and the Incarnation. The Incarnation did not pursue him. It merely shook its head and body, every bone spike burning and flashing with a frightening light as it stared coldly at Xu Changfeng. Its expression was like that of someone looking at a dead man, giving Xu Changfeng the urge to flee at once.

More than a dozen li away, Tang Sheng and the others were also dumbstruck by this turn of events.

When Lan He shouted her warning, Tang Sheng had been startled. After all, doing so could very well offend Xu Changfeng. Once Xu Changfeng and Fu Bo joined forces to kill Yang Kai, Heavenly Wolf Valley would be in danger. Earlier, his refusal to let Xu Changfeng use Heavenly Wolf Valley as a battlefield could still be justified, but allowing his own disciple to warn their enemy was unforgivable.

But Lan He had already shouted the words, so what could he do? While he was still vexed, he saw the Incarnation make its dazzling entrance, and then Xu Changfeng avoided it like a snake or scorpion. This whole chain of changes truly left everyone struggling to keep up.

"Valley Lord Tang, did you see how that fellow appeared?" Red Ghost asked subconsciously, staring fixedly toward the battlefield, his eyes almost bulging from their sockets.

Tang Sheng said, "I did not see clearly." He only knew that Yang Kai had waved his hand and the Divine Spirit Shi Huo had appeared. He faintly guessed that it had something to do with the Space Divine Ability Yang Kai controlled; perhaps Yang Kai had used some Secret Technique to teleport Shi Huo over.

But had this stretch of heaven and earth not already been sealed off? How had he managed to teleport?

Tang Sheng was not the only one with that suspicion. Xu Changfeng suspected the same thing. If that was truly the case, then what was the point of fighting? Since Yang Kai could teleport, it meant he stood in an invincible position; even if he could not defeat them, he could still escape.

But could he really not defeat them? Xu Changfeng's gaze swept past the Incarnation blocking his way and turned toward Fu Bo, and cold sweat broke out across his body. In that brief instant, the Ghost King Fu Bo had transformed into was actually being suppressed by Yang Kai, leaving him hard-pressed and roaring again and again.

The Ghost King Body was indeed incomparably tough and possessed boundless strength, but in the end, it had its limits. Yang Kai's Half-Dragon body was every bit its match, if not more so. Suppressed in his greatest advantage, what waves could Fu Bo possibly stir up?

Myriad Sword stabbed, chopped, swept, and hacked, ringing clang after clang against Fu Bo's body. Although it caused no real damage, Fu Bo looked utterly wretched in such a state.

Yang Kai kept attacking, giving him no chance to catch his breath, and sneered again and again, "I don't know why, but people surnamed Fu seem naturally at odds with me, and they never come to a good end." Naturally, he was referring to those Fu-lineage Dragon Clan members on Dragon Isle.

If it came down to strength alone, Dragon Isle had plenty of people stronger than him. Unfortunately, the Dragon Clan was far too restricted by bloodline. Yang Kai possessed the Golden Sacred Dragon Origin Power, which gave him an enormous advantage when fighting the Dragon Clan. Even if the Dragon Clan's Great Elder and Second Elder fought with all their strength, they might not be able to do anything to him. This was also the reliance that gave him the courage to return to Dragon Isle. Without it, how could he possibly go there just to seek death?

"Today, you die, and Netherworld Sect will be destroyed with you!" Yang Kai's attacks were vicious, and his tongue was just as sharp. After only a few words, Fu Bo's eyes had already gone scarlet, and he could not help roaring again and again, "You destroyed my Netherworld Sect's foundation in the Great Wilderness Star Domain! Today, either you die or I perish!"
