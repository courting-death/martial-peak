## Chapter 3257 — What a Vast Game

The gathered Yao Kings all shook their heads. No one knew why they had been summoned here. Each of them had come at the call of their own Saint Venerable. They did not understand the reason, but one thing was certain: it had to be connected to Yang Kai. Otherwise, he would not be here.

Yet Yang Kai sat there calm and unhurried, enjoying Du Mi'er's service without moving from his seat, and they could hardly step forward to disturb him. The Yao Kings all recognized him and knew what kind of power stood behind him, so naturally none of them went looking for trouble.

Time drifted by, and one Yao King after another arrived.

In less than two hours, the twenty-nine Yao Kings had all gathered together.

Luan Feng, Cang Gou, and Fan Wu appeared soon after, and the Yao Kings saluted as soon as they saw them.

Yang Kai finally opened his eyes, a brilliant light flashing in them. It had to be said that although Du Mi'er behaved a little frivolously, she truly knew how to serve someone. She was a Flower Yao in human form, and seemed to possess an innate divine ability of her own, one that could soothe the mind.

"Everyone's here?" Yang Kai turned and looked around.

Fan Wu's face did not look very good. Although he had already heard the gist of the matter from Luan Feng, Yang Kai's way of handling things still left him rather uncomfortable. The Wild Ancient Land was the yao race's territory, after all. Since when was it a human's place to call the shots here? After a moment of silence, he nodded. "They are all here."

"Then let's go." Yang Kai rose and sped off first toward the Stone Puppet clan's residence.

The Yao Kings from all twenty-nine territories looked eagerly to the three Saint Venerables. Things had already come to this, so what else could Fan Wu and the others say? They could only lift their hands. In an instant, figure after figure shot into the sky, following close behind Yang Kai.

Fan Wu and the other two set off later but caught up at once, flying side by side with Yang Kai. They looked at one another in silence.

Yang Kai spoke first. "The three of you understand the greater good and have given me your full support this time. This Yang will remember it. In the future, I will certainly repay you handsomely."

Luan Feng snorted. "That wasn't your attitude earlier." She was still holding a grudge over the way Yang Kai had ordered them about, as if no one else mattered. If he had taken this tone from the start, how much trouble would that have saved?

Yang Kai turned and glared at her. "You have the nerve to say that?"

"What? I can't even mention it?" Luan Feng's temper flared as well, and she glared right back at Yang Kai without yielding an inch.

Yang Kai said, "If you hadn't destroyed my Space Array back then, would we need to rush there like this now? We could have reached the destination directly through your Fengluo Palace. But now? The trip will take at least two hours. If this delays something important, just see how I deal with you."

Hearing him bring up what had happened back then, Luan Feng blinked, suddenly a little guilty. After all, it was true that she had destroyed that Space Array, but who could have known he would be in such a hurry today? She turned her head aside. "Why are you shouting at me? How was I supposed to know this would happen?"

"Enough. Things have already come to this, and arguing won't help." Fan Wu stepped in to smooth things over. No matter what, the Ancient Land did not want to fall out with Yang Kai completely, so appearances still had to be maintained. "Young Brother Yang, you should tell us what exactly is going on."

Earlier, Luan Feng had only told them that Yang Kai wanted to mobilize all of the Ancient Land's high-level combat strength, and that it seemed to concern the Demon Race. She had been vague and unclear. Now that they had already decided to act, they naturally had to learn more.

Hearing this, Yang Kai no longer bothered arguing with Luan Feng. After pondering for a moment, he said, "A dozen or so years ago, someone invited me to the Southern Territory's Southern Marsh to explore an ancient cave residence..."

Since they had some time on the road anyway, Yang Kai briefly recounted the trip to that ancient cave residence more than a dozen years ago and what happened afterward, then told them about this Southern Territory Martial Gathering as well.

When they heard that Demonic Intent had revived and that the Southern Territory had suffered a demonic calamity, all three were startled. When they then heard that the Hall Master of Wuhua Hall in the Southern Territory had actually been possessed by Demonic Intent and had laid a trap for the Southern Territory's several hundred Emperor Realm Masters and tens of thousands of elite disciples, their faces changed. They exchanged glances, each sensing the severity of the problem.

"So those several hundred Emperor Realm Masters from the Southern Territory are still trapped inside Wuhua Hall?"

"That was the situation when I left. Unless the array is broken, they cannot get out at all. I don't know exactly what is happening inside either."

Cang Gou rubbed his chin. "Several hundred Emperor Realm Masters... Who knows how many have yet to be corrupted by Demonic Qi."

Since Yang Kai had been attacked by more than a dozen Emperor Realm Masters who had Entered the Demonic State, it was certain that the ones who had fallen numbered far more than that. There might be many more. After all, the Martial Gathering had already been going on for a month. If Wuhua Hall had planned this in advance, it could certainly have let Demonic Qi corrupt even more people. In ordinary times, they could hide it well enough that nothing seemed wrong, but once they rose up without warning, they could cause tremendous destruction and infect even more people with Demonic Qi. If that cycle continued, perhaps everyone would fall and become demonkin.

"Let's hope the situation isn't that grim," Cang Gou sighed.

Luan Feng frowned. "Since Wuhua Hall had been plotting this for so long, why lure you out to assassinate you? Weren't they just making trouble for themselves?"

Yang Kai said coldly, "Perhaps they never expected to fail." In truth, that was likely the case. Even now, when Yang Kai thought back to that assassination attempt, a chill lingered in his heart. If the assassin had not been ignorant of his background and unaware that he possessed a Half-Dragon Body, Yang Kai would have died long ago.

Luan Feng shook her head. "I mean, why not keep you inside Wuhua Hall and trap you there, unable to escape? There was no need for an extra move."

Cang Gou said, "Young Brother Yang's Space Divine Ability should be no secret. I imagine they were afraid he would sense something was wrong and flee, so killing him first was the safer choice."

Luan Feng considered it and found the reasoning sound, then nodded slightly.

Fan Wu suddenly said in alarm, "Those demonkin truly laid out an enormous scheme."

"What do you mean?" Yang Kai looked at him suspiciously, not knowing what had prompted the sudden remark.

Fan Wu said, "Think about it. If you hadn't been lucky enough to survive the assassination, what would Wuhua Hall look like now?"

Yang Kai frowned and thought for a moment. "In the worst case, everyone still alive there would have been demonized."

"Exactly!" Fan Wu clapped his hands. "And if those who had Entered the Demonic State then returned to their own Sects and acted in secret..."

Yang Kai followed his meaning, and a chill ran through him. "The Southern Territory would be in grave danger!"

Fan Wu said, "By then, the entire Southern Territory would belong to those demonkin. Even if a Great Emperor acted, I fear it would be difficult to reverse the situation."

Yang Kai listened with growing alarm, realizing that Fan Wu's guess might truly be Wuhua Hall's plan. If he had not escaped the assassination by luck and spread the news, who would have known about the chaos inside Wuhua Hall?

Cang Gou said, "Since Wuhua Hall dared to move against the entire Southern Territory, they must have gathered quite a few demonkin. With only our manpower..."

Yang Kai said solemnly, "Rest assured. It won't just be you. I have invited others as well. This time, I absolutely will not let a single demonkin escape Wuhua Hall."

"Others!" Cang Gou glanced at Yang Kai. Seeing that he was unwilling to say more, he did not press him. He had only feared that Yang Kai had lost his head to anger and come without a plan. Since there was a plan, he did not need to worry too much.

Yang Kai said, "In any case, when all of you fight those demonkin later, be extremely careful not to be corrupted by Demonic Qi. More importantly, guard against possession by those Demonic Intents. I once saw someone possessed by Demonic Intent with my own eyes. There was no warning beforehand, and after the possession, there was not the slightest sign anything was wrong. It was truly bizarre."

"We understand," Fan Wu said with a nod.

None of them deliberately lowered their voices, so the Yao Kings behind them naturally heard every word. This was exactly what Yang Kai intended. When they heard they would be fighting people corrupted by Demonic Qi, the Yao Kings were utterly astonished. They had never experienced anything like this before, and none of them knew whether Demonic Qi was stronger or Yao Qi held the advantage. In any case, this was a perfect chance to distinguish themselves. Once they caught Yang Kai's eye, would they still need to worry about failing to become Divine Spirits in the future?

Luan Feng had never told them about Yang Kai's earlier promise. It was not that she feared Yang Kai would go back on his word afterward. The main issue was that once those words were spoken, how could the Yao Kings not become deeply grateful to him? It would shake the three great Saint Venerables' rule over the Ancient Land at its foundation.

They raced along, and in less than two hours, everyone arrived at the Stone Puppet clan's residence. The Elder had not left and was waiting there with Mu Na, the Wood Spirit Clan Chief.

Seeing the three great Saint Venerables and the Yao Kings from all twenty-nine territories descend from the sky, the two could not help exchanging a glance and sighing inwardly. They had long known that Yang Kai carried considerable weight with the Ancient Land's yao race, but they had not expected it to reach this extent. He had swept up the entire Ancient Land's upper echelon in one net.

When the two sides met, the Elder and the three great Saint Venerables exchanged a few brief pleasantries, then followed Yang Kai into the Space Array. Light flashed, and they arrived at that mountain peak.

The eight Stone Puppets had arrived long ago and were guarding the array. When movement stirred on it, they all turned to look.

The three great Saint Venerables and the Yao Kings from all twenty-nine territories appeared one after another. Everyone stared at one another, and soon the two sides stood apart in clear lines. The relationship between the Stone Puppets and the Ancient Land's yao race had never been very harmonious; it had only eased somewhat over the past dozen or so years, and that was because of Yang Kai.

After bringing the Ancient Land's yao here, Yang Kai told them to wait a moment, then turned and stepped into the Space Array again.

In Lingxiao Palace, the great hall housing the Space Array was packed with people. Nearly two hundred Emperor Realm Masters had gathered there. Some stood in groups of three or five, whispering among themselves, while others watched coldly from the side. As time passed, impatience gradually began to spread.

If not for the experts from the four top forces watching closely nearby, these Emperor Realm Masters from the various Sects might already have fallen into chaos. They had come after receiving Lingxiao Palace's transfer order, yet they had no idea what this was about. Even Chief Steward Hua Qingsi gave them no answer when asked, so unease was inevitable. They could not help suspecting that Lingxiao Palace had secretly raised the butcher's knife and was about to strike them down.

But what would Lingxiao Palace gain from doing that?

Yingfei, Xie Wuwei, and Xi Lei stood in a triangle, their Yao Qi soaring to the heavens as they coldly surveyed their surroundings. Anyone who met their eyes, even briefly, looked away at once, not daring to act out.

Hua Qingsi, Bian Yuqing, Ye Hen, Nanmen Dajun, and the people of Ice Heart Valley were gathered together. Five Emperor Realm Masters had come from Ice Heart Valley, led by Ji Yao. Her expression was cold and aloof, warning strangers away.

The people from Li Dragon Palace and Mitian Sect were gathered there as well. Aside from leaving two or three Emperor Realm Masters behind to guard their Sects, the four great Sects had basically sent everyone. Li Jiao and Mi Qi were huddled together, muttering between themselves, just as bewildered and unsure what Yang Kai was up to.
