## Chapter 3211 — Absolutely Not

In recent years, Ji Ying had been researching a new Alchemy technique, mainly a method for collecting pills. According to him, this pill-collecting method was relatively simple, far less complicated than other pill-condensing techniques. Even an ordinary Alchemist could master it after some study, and once mastered, it could increase the efficiency of collecting pills by at least ten to twenty percent.

That ten to twenty percent could not be underestimated. It might not look like much over a few furnaces of Spirit Pills, but an Alchemist would refine an enormous number of Spirit Pills over a lifetime. In terms of actual quantity, that extra ten or twenty percent represented an astonishing amount of wealth.

Since this was only the final pill-collecting step, Ji Ying did not choose to refine any especially high-level Spirit Pills. Instead, he picked a Dao Origin-Grade Pill. The early process was nothing remarkable; exquisite though it was, it was still only his usual standard.

The medicine room fell silent. Ji Ying focused on refining pills while Yang Kai and Xia Ningshang watched. Xia Ningshang in particular kept her beautiful eyes fixed on Ji Ying's movements, never looking away, a thoughtful gleam flashing through them from time to time.

Condensing the liquid, carving Spirit Arrays, nurturing the Spirit Pills; before long, it was time to collect the pills. Only then did Ji Ying's expression turn serious. "Brother Yang, watch closely."

As soon as he finished speaking, his hands shifted through seal after seal, sending streams of profound Emperor Yuan into the pill furnace. A strange fragrance immediately wafted from the furnace, clearly indicating that the pills had begun to condense.

A moment later, Ji Ying gave a low shout, "Rise!"

The furnace lid lifted, and several plump, round Spirit Pills flew out. Under his guidance, they entered the jade bottle he had prepared in advance, spinning merrily inside.

No more and no less, there were exactly nine. Nine was the limit of numbers. For an Alchemist, no matter how exceptional their technique or how high their skill, a single furnace of Spirit Pills could produce at most nine pills. Even Sublime Pill Great Emperor could not avoid this rule.

With Ji Ying's status as an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, he could have refined nine Spirit Pills from a furnace of Dao Origin-Grade Pills even without using his newly researched pill-collecting technique. After using it at the end, the result was still nine.

Even so, Yang Kai could tell that the technique truly had its own merits. He immediately calmed his mind, reflected on it carefully for a while, then began discussing it with Ji Ying.

Discussions between Alchemists were never limited to theory. Naturally, they also had to test things by hand.

Yang Kai therefore took out his own pill furnace and some herbs and began refining Spirit Pills. When it came time to condense the pills, he used the technique Ji Ying had researched, comparing its strengths and weaknesses.

Furnace after furnace of experimental Spirit Pills was refined, and time passed little by little. Ji Ying, however, showed no sign of fatigue. On the contrary, his eyes grew brighter and brighter.

Discussing this with Yang Kai had indeed been the right decision. Over the next several days, he discovered that the pill-collecting technique still had some flaws, yet through their mutual testing and comparison, those flaws were slowly corrected. Naturally, this delighted him.

Only after a full five days did the two put away their pill furnaces and walk out of the Alchemy Room together.

Ji Ying had gained much from the exchange and was in excellent spirits, so he ordered several disciples to prepare a banquet and properly entertain Yang Kai.

This suited Yang Kai perfectly. He gave Xia Ningshang a look, and Xia Ningshang immediately understood. Picking up the wine pot, she poured wine and water for the two of them.

Cups passed back and forth, and after three rounds of wine, Ji Ying finally slapped his forehead. "My fault, my fault. I have yet to ask this Young Lady's honored name!"

As he spoke, he stood and looked at Xia Ningshang apologetically.

At first, he had noticed that Yang Kai and Xia Ningshang seemed to share an unusual relationship, but he had soon immersed himself entirely in Alchemy. Aside from Alchemy itself, only Yang Kai, the person discussing it with him, had remained in his eyes, and he had simply stopped paying attention to Xia Ningshang.

He had not even noticed her waiting on them and pouring water at the side.

Only now did realization strike him.

"My wife, Xia Ningshang," Yang Kai introduced with a faint smile.

Xia Ningshang gave a graceful salute. "Greetings, Master Ji Ying!" Her cheeks reddened slightly. This was the first time Yang Kai had introduced her this way in front of outsiders.

"Madam Yang?" Ji Ying was startled. So the person who had been pouring wine for him all this time was actually Yang Kai's wife. Flustered, he said, "This... what am I to do? Please, sit, quickly!"

If he had known Xia Ningshang was Yang Kai's wife, he would never have let her do a servant's work. Was that not the same as slapping Yang Kai in the face? Fortunately, Yang Kai did not seem to mind.

"I have truly been negligent. This Ji has sinned. I offer this cup to Madam Yang and ask Madam Yang not to blame me!" Ji Ying raised his wine cup, looking deeply ashamed.

"Brother Ji is taking it too seriously. It is only a small matter," Yang Kai said with a smile, waving his hand.

Ji Ying looked at him with a wry smile. "Brother Yang, you are much too unkind. You brought Younger Sister-in-law here without saying a word, making this Ji embarrass himself. Later, you must punish yourself with three cups."

"As Brother Ji says," Yang Kai replied with a faint smile. He then continued, "But there is no hurry to drink. I still have a matter I would like to ask for Brother Ji's help with, and it happens to concern my wife."

Ji Ying said, "If there is anything you need my help with, Brother Yang, simply say so. As long as this Ji can do it, I will not refuse."

"This is certainly something Brother Ji can do." Yang Kai smiled confidently, gestured toward Xia Ningshang, and said, "It is like this. Since Ningshang began cultivating, she has also entered the Dao through Alchemy, and she loves the Alchemy Dao more than cultivation and battle."

"Oh? Younger Sister-in-law is also an Alchemist?" Ji Ying studied Xia Ningshang seriously and frowned slightly.

He was, after all, an Imperial-Grade Pill Master. If another Alchemist stood before him, he should have been able to tell, because Alchemists all carried a distinctive air. Those who dealt with strange flames and herbs year-round in particular would inevitably bear certain indelible traces.

Yet when he looked at Xia Ningshang, he could not find any such traces at all. At the same time, he could clearly smell all kinds of pill fragrances on her, leaving him somewhat puzzled.

"Exactly!" Yang Kai nodded. "It is just that conditions in the Lower Plane Star Domain were limited, and no one there could teach her Alchemy. Now that I have finally brought her here, I was wondering whether she might learn the Alchemy Dao from Brother Ji."

Ji Ying said, "Brother Yang's attainments in Alchemy far surpass mine, and the two of you are husband and wife, so it would be most convenient for you to guide her yourself. Why seek help elsewhere when you have it close at hand?"

Yang Kai smiled bitterly. "Brother Ji overpraises me. You also know that I do not have much time to teach her."

Hearing this, Ji Ying knew Yang Kai was telling the truth. He had spent quite some time in Lingxiao Palace, yet he had barely seen Yang Kai at all. This fellow seemed to appear and vanish unpredictably all year round, so how could he have time to teach others?

Ji Ying nodded slightly. "If that is the case, this Ji would be happy to help, only..."

There would be no "only." Yang Kai immediately cut him off, gave Xia Ningshang a look, and said, "Ningshang, come pay respects to your Honored Master!"

Ji Ying had Emperor Realm cultivation and was an Imperial-Grade Pill Master, so he was absolutely qualified to be Xia Ningshang's Master. Although he and Ji Ying addressed each other as brothers, that did not interfere with Xia Ningshang's matter. At worst, each of them could mind their own seniority.

Ji Ying was Sublime Pill Great Emperor's personal disciple, and what he had inherited was Sublime Pill Great Emperor's Alchemy Dao. If Xia Ningshang only learned from him casually, she would certainly still gain something, but formally becoming his disciple would be entirely different. Ji Ying would surely teach her everything he knew without holding anything back. Comparing the two, Yang Kai naturally wanted Xia Ningshang to enter Ji Ying's Sect. Losing a little in terms of status was of no concern.

Yang Kai had already spoken with Xia Ningshang before they came, so she naturally knew what to do. She quickly lowered herself in a graceful bow, but before she could say anything, Ji Ying waved his hand, and an invisible force held her up, preventing her from bowing no matter what.

"Brother Yang..." Ji Ying looked at Yang Kai helplessly and waved his hand. "This cannot be done. Absolutely not."

If he really accepted Xia Ningshang as a disciple, how would he and Yang Kai sort out their seniority in the future? More importantly, he was not an ordinary Imperial-Grade Pill Master. The whole of Medicine Pill Valley stood behind him, and accepting disciples was not something he could do on a whim. Medicine Pill Valley had to acknowledge it first.

Besides, he had no intention of accepting a disciple. Teaching Xia Ningshang was one thing, but taking her as a disciple was a major matter. How could it be handled so carelessly? Even though Xia Ningshang was Yang Kai's wife, Ji Ying did not dare casually bring her into his Sect before he knew her aptitude. Otherwise, it would be Medicine Pill Valley that lost face.

The few disciples under him were all dragons and phoenixes among men. Given time, each of them had a great chance of becoming an Imperial-Grade Pill Master.

"Why can it not be done? Could Brother Ji be unwilling?" Yang Kai thought that since things had already reached this point, there was no way he would let Ji Ying slip away so easily. Today, he had to accept her whether he wanted to or not.

"It is not that I am unwilling, only..." Ji Ying was thoroughly conflicted. "I am bound by sect rules, so this Ji cannot accept disciples at will. This matter must first be reported to Eldest Senior Brother and left for him to decide."

Although Medicine Pill Valley was Sublime Pill Great Emperor's place of quiet cultivation, the Great Emperor himself did not concern himself with every matter. The one currently managing Medicine Pill Valley was Sublime Pill Great Emperor's eldest disciple, the very Senior Brother Ji Ying had mentioned.

"There is such a sect rule?" Yang Kai was stunned, but he also knew Ji Ying would not lie to him. He understood that his plan would likely run into some complications.

"Exactly. Otherwise, Ji Ying would not refuse. How about this, Brother Yang? I can teach Younger Sister-in-law Alchemy, but let us dispense with the Master-disciple status."

Yang Kai was extremely unwilling. If it was only casual instruction, how many truly good things could she learn? He asked tentatively, "Does Medicine Pill Valley still accept disciples these days?"

How could Ji Ying not know what he was planning? With a bitter smile, he said, "We have not accepted any newcomers for five hundred years, but if someone had outstanding aptitude, I imagine Eldest Senior Brother would not refuse."

Yang Kai was delighted to hear this. "Ningshang, show Brother Ji a little of what you can do."

Ji Ying looked at him in astonishment, finding this rather hard to believe. After all, as far as he could tell, Xia Ningshang did not have the slightest trace of an Alchemist about her, yet Yang Kai seemed very confident in her. Since matters had already reached this point, however, it would be improper to stop them, so he could only look toward Xia Ningshang. Secretly, he decided that if her aptitude was passable, he would return and plead with Eldest Senior Brother to accept Xia Ningshang into Medicine Pill Valley. In any case, he could not very well deny Yang Kai this face.

"Please forgive my poor display." Xia Ningshang spoke softly, then searched through her Spatial Ring and took out some medicinal materials.

They were all Void King-Grade medicinal materials. There was nothing higher in the Star Domain.

This gesture alone made Ji Ying's eyes light up.
