## Chapter 3344 — Bad Egg

The latter possibility was all but impossible. Yang Kai was now at the Second Stage of the Emperor Realm; unless a Great Emperor came in person, there was no way he would fail to notice them. Could his guess have been wrong? Was there no one inside the cave?

But if no one was inside, what had Fu Zhun meant by those words before she left?

The whole affair was growing rather strange. Yang Kai did not let down his guard, keeping constant watch on his surroundings as he approached the cave.

It was pitch-black inside. A passage led deep into the cave, though there was no telling how far it extended, and not the faintest glimmer of light shone from within.

Yang Kai slipped inside without a sound and cautiously explored deeper.

The passage twisted and turned, but continued downward all the while.

After walking for who knew how long, Yang Kai estimated that he was at least several hundred zhang underground. At last, he spotted a glimmer of light ahead. His spirits lifted, and he concealed his aura with even greater care.

He would soon find out whether Fu Zhun was really keeping a lover on the side.

Before long, Yang Kai reached the source of the light: an enormous underground cavern covering one or two mu. It appeared to be a natural formation rather than something made by human hands.

Standing at the entrance, Yang Kai quickly peered inside but saw no sign of anyone. Instead, numerous luminous pearls were embedded in the cavern walls. Each was the size of a fist and cast a gentle glow, illuminating the entire cavern down to the smallest detail.

The passage ended at this cavern, with no other path leading onward.

Yang Kai stood at the cavern entrance, blinked, and called out, “Is anyone there?”

His voice echoed through the cavern until his eardrums buzzed, but no one answered.

“If no one’s here, I’m coming in!” Yang Kai called as he stepped into the cavern. He glanced around, his expression turning strange, then stroked his chin and muttered, “No one? What the hell is going on?”

He could take in the whole cavern at a glance. Apart from the luminous pearls casting their soft glow from the walls, there was nothing of note. Forget a living person—there was not even a corpse.

But if no one was here, who had Fu Zhun been speaking to earlier? Could that old witch have something wrong with her head? It seemed unlikely, but he could not rule it out. After all, he did not know Fu Zhun very well. All he knew was that the cheap slut possessed extraordinary cultivation and that, under normal circumstances, he was no match for her unless he could suppress her with his Dragon Bloodline and draw on the power of the Dragon Temple.

Since the cave was empty, Yang Kai no longer needed to skulk about. He clasped his hands behind his back and began strolling around as bold as could be.

A faint fragrance lingered in the cavern—Fu Zhun’s natural scent. It proved she had indeed come here and remained for three full days before leaving; otherwise, her fragrance could not have lasted until now.

As he walked, something off to one side caught Yang Kai’s eye. Several crystal-clear objects lay scattered on the ground, resembling droplets of water frozen solid.

Dragon Tear Beads! These were formed from the tears of Great Dragons and could be used to refine a special kind of Spirit Pill. Recalling Fu Zhun’s red, swollen eyes before she left, Yang Kai immediately realized that she had likely left these Dragon Tear Beads behind.

Judging by her condition, she had cried for a long time and shed a great many tears. She had probably collected most of the Dragon Tear Beads herself, leaving behind only those she had missed.

There were not many—only a dozen or so scattered around a pitch-black stone.

Yang Kai stroked his chin as a scene formed in his mind: Fu Zhun standing beside this pitch-black stone, lost in grief, shedding silent tears until at last she began to wail...

Why had she been crying over a rock? Yang Kai scoffed, but just as he was about to dismiss it, something occurred to him. He began to study the stone in earnest.

He had paid it little attention before, merely glancing around after discovering there was no one alive in the cave. But now that he examined it closely, the stone looked rather strange. It stood as tall as a person and was oval in shape. Whichever way he looked at it, it resembled an... egg!

A stone that looked like an egg.

Was this thing a dragon egg?

The thought sprang unbidden into his mind, giving Yang Kai a start. His eyes widened as he scrutinized the strange stone.

Come to think of it, he had no idea how members of the Dragon Clan were born. Did female Great Dragons give birth to them directly, or did they hatch from dragon eggs? No one had ever told him, but he had at least heard of dragon eggs.

Could this really be a dragon egg? The more he thought about it, the more plausible it seemed. Snakes laid eggs, so dragons probably did too...

Fu Zhun had stayed here for three days, then wept her heart out over what appeared to be a dragon egg. Th-this... Could it be the dragon egg of Fu Zhun and the Great Elder?

“Good heavens...” Yang Kai gaped, stunned by his own conjecture. If he was right, he had stumbled upon something incredible.

This was the... dragon egg of two Tenth-Tier Great Dragons! Having inherited both their bloodlines, it was destined to become a future pillar and hope of Dragon Isle!

But if this really was their dragon egg, why had they placed it here instead of on Green Wood Island or Ice Snow Island? Not placing it on Ice Snow Island was understandable. It was bitterly cold and rife with Ice Principles, so perhaps it was not a suitable place for a dragon egg to hatch. Green Wood Island, however, should have been an excellent choice.

So why had they left it here instead of placing it on Green Wood Island?

Frowning deeply, Yang Kai reached out and rapped his knuckles against it. Thump, thump, thump. The sound echoed through the cavern as though he were knocking on solid rock. He scratched his head. Was this a dragon egg or a stone? He could no longer tell. More importantly, if it really was a dragon egg, why could he sense no vitality within it whatsoever?

Could it be... a bad egg!

Yang Kai pressed his palm to its surface, closed his eyes, and probed it with his senses.

In the next instant, he knew it was not a stone. Though its surface looked no different from rock, its interior was not stone at all, but something with an extremely peculiar structure.

This thing really was a dragon egg!

And a bad egg at that!

Yang Kai finally understood why Fu Zhun had come here so distraught, why she had cried so bitterly, and why she had spoken those words before leaving.

This must be the dragon egg she had laid. Unfortunately, for some unknown reason, it had failed to hatch, and all life within it had apparently died. Such a thing would be difficult for any mother to accept, especially a member of the Dragon Clan. It was extraordinarily difficult for Dragons to produce offspring. Fu Zhun had finally glimpsed a ray of hope, only to see it turn to despair.

She had placed her dragon egg here and would visit every few days to look in on it and weep. Yang Kai had simply happened to catch her this time.

Yang Kai sighed. However unapproachable Fu Zhun might seem to others, however cold and unyielding, beneath it all she was still a woman. The Dragon Tear Beads scattered here and her red, swollen eyes betrayed both her deep regret and her longing for a child.

Yang Kai suddenly realized that he had glimpsed another side of Fu Zhun.

As that thought crossed his mind, Yang Kai suddenly stiffened and stared at the dragon egg. For a brief instant, he seemed to have felt a faint tremor from within.

But his emotions had been in turmoil, and the sensation had been indistinct. Perhaps it was only his imagination.

It probably was. After all, the fact that this bad egg had been left here meant Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun had already given up hope. Otherwise, they would surely have tended it with the utmost care.

Just as he was about to withdraw his hand, another tremor—faint beyond measure—pulsed against his palm.

Yang Kai’s eyes flew wide. Was this dragon egg still alive? When it trembled, he had clearly sensed an extremely faint ripple of vitality. But if it still held life, why had Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun abandoned it?

He probed it carefully for a long while and finally confirmed that a faint, elusive trace of vitality did remain—but nothing more.

Yang Kai withdrew his hand, gazed at the dragon egg before him, and sighed. Zhu Yan and Fu Zhun presumably knew this as well, but were powerless to save it. Otherwise, they would never have abandoned it.

He heaved a long sigh. “Even your mother and father can’t help you. There’s nothing I can do either.” He patted the dragon egg, then turned and headed outside.

Since there was no secret lover here, there was no reason to linger. By sheer chance, he had discovered one of Fu Zhun’s secrets, but it was hardly something he could use to threaten her.

What leverage could he possibly find in a mother’s love and care for her child, or in her guilt and regret?

Yang Kai left the cave, soared into the air, and sped toward the Dragon Temple.

A moment later, he abruptly stopped. He stroked his chin, eyes shifting as an idea seemed to occur to him.

He promptly turned around and returned to the cave, where he took the dragon egg and stuffed it into the Small Xuan Realm.

When Yang Kai emerged again, he flew toward the entrance instead of the Dragon Temple.

Fu Ling was not at the entrance. Yang Kai made a wide circuit before finally finding her in a clear lake no more than thirty li away.

Fu Ling was bathing!

Her purple robes lay on the shore while she swam through the lake like a fish.

Yang Kai’s sudden arrival gave her a fright. She looked up at him reproachfully. “Brother-in-law, what are you doing?”

Yang Kai could not help rolling his eyes. The lake water was crystal clear, and Fu Ling was completely naked. Though he had not meant to look, a single glance revealed everything: ample breasts, a slender waist, and round, pert buttocks. The rippling water distorted her figure somewhat, but could not conceal her peerless beauty.

“I have something to ask you.” Showing no concern for propriety, Yang Kai remained where he was and looked down at her.

Fu Ling kicked and paddled her way to a rock in the middle of the lake. Clinging to it with both hands, she exposed most of her smooth back and the breathtaking curves of her body. She looked up with a smile. “What do you want to ask, Brother-in-law?”

Yang Kai landed on the rock and crouched down. Stroking his chin, he asked, “Did the Second Elder once bear a child?”

Fu Ling blinked and asked in surprise, “How do you know about that?”

So she really had!
