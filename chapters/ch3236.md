## Chapter 3236 — Submission

The Emperor Realm cultivators of Mitian Sect lay sprawled on the ground, stunned and vacant-eyed, disbelief written all over them. Their own Sect Master had failed to withstand even a single move from the other side?

The people from Li Dragon Palace were just as stunned. Deep down, they found it utterly absurd. Mi Qi was a Third-Stage Emperor Realm Master, so how could he be this pathetic?

The three Yao Kings exchanged glances, shaken as well. They could all see that Yang Kai seemed far stronger than before.

Old Tao swallowed hard, grabbed his wine gourd, and poured wine into his mouth. He needed a drink to steady his nerves.

After being smashed down hard enough to flatten a mountaintop, Mi Qi was dizzy and seeing stars. His Qi and blood churned violently, and he only managed to suppress it with great difficulty. When his vision steadied, he saw Yang Kai drifting down from the sky, landing before him and looking down on him. The long spear in Yang Kai's hand spun, flicked, and blossomed into a spear flourish before stabbing toward him.

"Spare me!" Mi Qi shouted. "Palace Master Yang, spare me!"

Right now, staying alive came first. He had no room to care about face anymore. If he lost his life, what use would face be?

The spear tip grazed past his cheek, slicing off several strands of black hair. Mi Qi's eyes went wide, and he nearly pissed himself in fright.

"Tch..." Yang Kai curled his lip, planted the long spear in the ground at his feet, and turned around. "Who else refuses to submit?"

Who would dare refuse? The Emperor Realm cultivators of Mitian Sect had already been dealt with by Yingfei and Xie Wuwei, and now they all lay on the ground, groaning without end. The few Emperor Realm Masters who had fought Yingfei were covered from head to toe in claw marks, fresh blood streaming from them. Unfortunately, Yingfei had sealed their cultivation, so they could not even cast techniques to heal themselves. As for the ones who had fought Xie Wuwei, green light shone all over them, and huge blisters had risen across their exposed skin. Their faces shifted between blue and green; clearly, they had been poisoned badly.

It could be said that Mitian Sect's Emperor Realm leadership had been wiped out in one sweep.

From beginning to end, the entire battle had lasted less than the time it took to drink a cup of tea.

"Palace Master Yang, spare us!" The Dao Origin Realm disciples and everyone below the Dao Origin Realm dropped to their knees one after another, each of them looking as though their parents had just died.

Mi Qi's eyes dimmed as he stared at the figure with his back to him, then glanced at the long spear Secret Treasure planted before him. Even so, he could not muster the courage for a sneak attack. Yang Kai had exposed his back without the slightest care. It looked full of openings, yet that only made Mi Qi even warier. How could he dare make a reckless move?

Yang Kai swept a cold gaze around and snorted softly. "Whether you live depends on what your Sect Master chooses."

Mi Qi staggered to his feet, clutching his chest, and turned to look. Familiar faces were all watching him, their eyes filled with the desire to live. Things had already come to this, so what was left to say? He was weaker than the other party. He might as well surrender.

Although this had been a miserable affair, at least no one had died. It was clear that Yang Kai's people had still shown a fair amount of restraint.

With a heavy sigh, he turned, cupped his fists, and said, "Mitian Sect is willing to follow Palace Master Yang's lead in all things. Please be magnanimous, Palace Master Yang, and leave us a path to live."

Yang Kai glanced sideways at him, expressionless. "This is no small matter. Sect Master Mi, do you need to think it over before you answer? This Lord is in no hurry. I can give you some time. If you submit sincerely, that is fine. If your heart is not sincere and you are unwilling, there is no need to force yourself. Otherwise, people might say this Lord used his strength to bully others."

My life is already in your hands, so what the hell is there to think about? Mi Qi cursed inwardly. At a time like this, how could his attitude be anything but firm? If he showed any other thoughts, he feared he really would come to a bad end. Besides, several elders on his side had been poisoned, and one glance showed they would not last much longer. If they were not detoxified soon, their lives would likely be in danger.

Wasn't this bastard bullying them and still trying to look righteous?

Mi Qi cursed in his heart, but on the surface he did not dare show the slightest trace of it. In a solemn voice, he said, "Palace Master Yang's divine abilities are unrivaled. Mitian Sect, from top to bottom, had eyes but failed to see. Please forgive our offense. From this day forth, all of Mitian Sect sincerely submits, with absolutely no second thoughts."

The corner of Yang Kai's mouth lifted slightly. "Sect Master Mi is the master of a sect, and a man whose word carries weight. Naturally, I believe you. Since Sect Master Mi is so sincere, this Lord will accept your kindness."

The corners of Mi Qi's mouth twitched nonstop. Now he had truly seen how despicable and shameless the man before him was. Hadn't Yang Kai brought so many experts here because he wanted to subdue Mitian Sect? He had even sent Li Jiao over beforehand to give notice, then fought them outright just now. Yet now he actually had the face to say such things, making it sound as though Mi Qi had offered allegiance on his own initiative and Yang Kai was reluctantly accepting. He was shameless beyond belief.

Mi Qi simply could not understand it. Mitian Sect was not bad, but why was Yang Kai so fixated on it? Mi Qi already owed him a massive debt, and every year he made the required payment in full. Why did Yang Kai have to subdue his Mitian Sect no matter what? Was it only to display Lingxiao Palace's powerful strength and foundation?

He forced out a strained smile and said, "Thank you, Palace Master Yang."

What sort of situation was this? His own Emperor Realm Masters had been beaten savagely, all dignity lost; even the grand protective array had been broken, and they had been forced to offer allegiance, yet he still had to thank the other party. Mi Qi's heart was full of grief and rage. He only hated that his strength was inferior. Otherwise, he would definitely make Yang Kai pay.

Yang Kai said, "Words alone prove nothing. Sect Master Mi, please show a little sincerity."

Mi Qi froze, then asked cautiously, "What sincerity does Palace Master Yang want?" He had already humbled himself this much, yet the other party still pressed him step by step. Mi Qi secretly hardened his heart. If Yang Kai really made some outrageous demand, then he would... weigh his options.

Yang Kai said in a deep voice, "Sect Master Mi, please write an order informing all the major cities, clans, and subordinate sects under Mitian Sect's jurisdiction that, from today onward, Mitian Sect is a subordinate sect of my Lingxiao Palace."

"Ah!" Mi Qi abruptly raised his head when he heard this, his face shifting between blue and red.

What had happened today was indeed shameful, but it was still confined to Mitian Sect. Even if some news spread afterward, as long as Mitian Sect did not admit it, the world could do no more than speculate. But if he personally issued an order and announced the matter himself, then its nature would be completely different.

There were many cities, clans, and subordinate sects under Mitian Sect's jurisdiction. After all, it was one of the Northern Territory's top forces. As the saying went, good news stays home while bad news travels a thousand li. Once this order went out, it would probably not be long before the entire Northern Territory knew.

This was to make Mitian Sect lose face before the entire Northern Territory! A dignified top sect of the Northern Territory had actually fallen so far as to become someone else's subordinate sect. How was he supposed to endure that?

"Hm?" Yang Kai's face chilled. "It seems Sect Master Mi is somewhat unwilling. Or were those words just now merely something you said in passing?"

"No, no." Mi Qi hurriedly waved his hands. "It's just that this matter..."

Before he could finish, Yang Kai suddenly lifted his head and looked at the sky. In an indifferent tone, he said, "Sect Master Mi is so indecisive. It seems you are not suited to managing a sect. Sect Master Mi should step down and make way for someone worthy."

What was that supposed to mean? Was Yang Kai going to force him to give up the position of Sect Master? If anyone else had said such a thing, Mi Qi definitely would not have taken it seriously, but from Yang Kai, it was different. The other party was the butcher, and he was the fish on the chopping block. Yang Kai could kill him outright, then pick someone suitable from among the elders to manage Mitian Sect. Mi Qi believed there would certainly be someone willing to cooperate.

A chill ran through him. No longer daring to bargain, he gritted his teeth and said, "I'll write it! I'll write it!"

Yang Kai said, "Don't force yourself. If it is forced, there is no point."

Mi Qi said solemnly, "This Mi is entirely sincere. Palace Master Yang, please see clearly."

"Since that is so, then... write it and let me see." Yang Kai rubbed his chin.

Mi Qi immediately took out a jade slip and poured his Divine Sense into it to write the order.

Yang Kai lifted his chin. "Heal them."

Yingfei's figure flickered, and he flashed past several Mitian Sect elders, striking each of them with a palm to undo their seals. Xie Wuwei, meanwhile, opened his mouth wide. Green vapor immediately rose from the bodies of the poisoned Emperor Realm cultivators lying on the ground and was swallowed into his mouth.

With their seals lifted and the deadly poison cleared, the Emperor Realm Masters took out healing Spirit Pills one after another and stuffed them into their mouths, each of them wearing a complicated expression.

Before long, Mi Qi had finished writing. He handed the jade slip to Yang Kai and said, "Palace Master Yang, please see if this is acceptable."

Yang Kai took it and swept through it, then frowned and tossed the jade slip back. "Your Mitian Sect is at least one of the Northern Territory's top sects. Don't write it so servilely that people look down on you for no reason."

Mi Qi did not know whether to laugh or cry. Their lives were in Yang Kai's hands, so how could he not sound servile? But since Yang Kai was dissatisfied, there was nothing he could do. He could only write it again.

He was the dignified Sect Master of Mitian Sect, yet not only had he been forced to write such a humiliating order, he even had to endure the other party nitpicking every fault in it. Where could he possibly go to seek justice?

Before long, another order was written. After Yang Kai checked it, he nodded slightly. "That's more like it. Write this same order..." He raised his head and glanced at the Mitian Sect elders and Protectors healing their injuries nearby, then counted them. "Eight copies."

Mi Qi did not dare disobey. Honestly and obediently, he wrote another eight orders.

Yang Kai said, "Have the Emperor Realm cultivators of your Mitian Sect take these orders and set out in eight directions. When they pass a city, they enter the city; when they pass a sect, they enter the sect. In crowded places, they are to read the order aloud and announce Mitian Sect's submission to Lingxiao Palace."

At those words, the Mitian Sect elders' eyes went wide, and they stared blankly toward him.

Mi Qi's expression also shifted again and again. Grief, rage, and humiliation flashed across his face, mixed with a trace of viciousness.

He had thought that even if he wrote this order, it would still take some time for the news to spread. Who could have known Yang Kai would be so ruthless, actually making Mitian Sect's elders personally carry the orders to the major cities and sects of the Northern Territory and read them aloud?

This was simply meant to make Mitian Sect lose every last shred of face. How was he, the Sect Master of Mitian Sect, supposed to stand in the Northern Territory from now on? How were those Emperor Realm elders who read the orders aloud supposed to live with themselves?

And how would the entire Northern Territory look at his Mitian Sect?

As the saying went, a gentleman may be killed, but not humiliated. In that instant, Mi Qi even felt an urge to stake everything and fight Yang Kai. He felt that Yang Kai was deliberately humiliating him.

But the moment he met Yang Kai's icy eyes, he shuddered again. Those eyes looked at him as if he were already dead. Mi Qi had no doubt that if he dared attack again, Yang Kai absolutely would not spare him a second time.

His fists clenched tight, but in the end, he still did not dare make a move. After a bout of self-consolation, he told himself that a miserable life was still better than a good death. Wasn't it just losing a little face? What was so serious about that?
