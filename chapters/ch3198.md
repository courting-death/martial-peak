## Chapter 3198 — Ice Spirit

Ice Soul Star lived up to its reputation. When Yang Kai and Su Yan arrived, they found themselves in a world of ice and snow, wrapped in silver and breathtakingly beautiful. The plants, the earth, even the mountains and rivers all seemed carved from ice, crystalline and translucent. Ice Attribute spirit flowers and exotic grasses covered the mountains and wilderness; Ice Attribute Spiritual Herbs rarely seen elsewhere were common here.

These things were extremely valuable in the Star Domain, but they were no longer of much use to Yang Kai or Su Yan, so after a moment of surprise, neither of them had any intention of gathering them.

The cold here was intense enough that no ordinary person could remain for long, but Yang Kai had a powerful physique and extraordinary cultivation, so naturally he did not fear such a minor chill. As for Su Yan, not only was she free of discomfort, but she seemed delighted, as if she had returned to her homeland. The moment she entered this world of ice and snow, she clearly felt the power inside her circulate faster. The frigid environment did not suppress her strength at all; instead, it nourished her aura.

She cultivated an Ice Attribute Secret Art to begin with, and had also inherited the Ice Phoenix Source. To her, an environment like this was like a dragon returning to the sea or a tiger entering the mountains.

If she had spent her earlier years cultivating in a place like this, the speed of her improvement would likely have been beyond anyone's reach.

Yang Kai summoned his Incarnation and had it clear the way ahead, while he led Su Yan downward, deep into the earth's crust, in search of Ice Soul Star's Origin Power.

The Stone Puppet Race was skilled in earth and stone techniques, a gift of their innate talent, so carving a path through the ground was effortless.

A quarter hour later, the space ahead suddenly opened up, and they arrived in an underground cavern.

The cold around them grew even more severe, making Yang Kai click his tongue in wonder. Although he was not greatly affected by this place, he estimated that anyone who had not cultivated an Ice Attribute Secret Art, even a Third-Stage Void King Realm Master, would not last long here.

In the Star Domain, Ice Soul Star was practically a dead land. Only someone who had surpassed the limits of the Void King Realm could come here and see what lay within.

Yet the strongest existences in the Star Domain were only at the Third Stage of the Void King Realm.

Ice-blue flames flowed inside the cavern, giving off an extremely frigid aura. A single glance seemed enough to freeze a person's Divine Soul. This was clearly a kind of strange flame, and one of extreme cold at that. One could only sigh at the uncanny workmanship of Heaven and Earth; the natural world truly was unfathomable.

At the center of the cavern was a particularly conspicuous ball of light, twisting, writhing, and shifting without end, as if it possessed spirituality.

"The Origin?" Su Yan asked, turning to Yang Kai.

Yang Kai studied it for a moment, then grinned. "Fake."

"Fake?" Su Yan was startled.

"Fake!" Yang Kai said with certainty. He had come into contact with no small number of Stellar Origins, and now he was the Master of the Star Domain as well, so he could naturally see through the reality of that so-called "Origin" at a glance. It was clearly a condensation of icy flames, not Ice Soul Star's Origin at all, but a trap.

Yang Kai snapped his fingers, and light blazed throughout the cavern.

One lifelike ice sculpture after another suddenly came into view. Each ice sculpture sat cross-legged, scattered throughout every corner of the cavern.

The light here had been too dim, and they had not released the slightest aura. With all of Su Yan's attention drawn by the "Origin," she had actually failed to notice them before.

"These..." Su Yan frowned.

"Probably people who tried to refine Ice Soul Star's Origin."

Su Yan looked closer and discovered that there were far more than a dozen ice sculptures around them; there were over a hundred. If Yang Kai was right, then over a hundred people had fallen here because they wanted to refine Ice Soul Star's Origin. And how weak could anyone qualified to enter this place and attempt to refine the Origin be? Without a doubt, they had all been Third-Stage Void King Realm Masters.

They had died in perfect peace, without the slightest trace of struggle or pain on their faces, as if their lives had ended in sleep.

In other words, even at the moment of death, they had not realized death was coming!

No other Cultivation Star's Origin Power had ever been so dangerous. In the entire Star Domain, Ice Soul Star was probably unique, able to make so many Third-Stage Void King Realm Masters fall here without even knowing it.

Looking at the clothes they wore, Su Yan immediately discovered clear differences in era. The people who had died here were obviously not from the same age. These Third-Stage Void King Realm Masters might have spanned several thousand, even tens of thousands of years.

A chill ran through her heart. Although she now had cultivation at the Second Stage of the Dao Origin Realm, if she had truly come here alone and tried to refine that so-called "Origin," the consequences probably would not have been pleasant.

"The Origin isn't here!" Yang Kai shook his head.

"Then where is it?" Su Yan asked.

Yang Kai's gaze shifted, then fixed on one direction. He smiled. "This way!"

In that instant, he had clearly sensed a will peering at them. Although it was very well hidden, how could it escape his perception? Following that thread, he instantly locked onto a direction.

Under ordinary circumstances, even though a Stellar Origin represented the will of Heaven and Earth, it was still nothing more than an inanimate object and would not actively think or move. Ice Soul Star's Origin was clearly different.

It seemed to have developed spirituality!

Interesting, truly interesting. He suddenly remembered the Origin Power of Tongxuan Continent he had seen back then. At that time, Tongxuan Continent's Spiritual Qi had dried up and the world was on the verge of collapse. Unwilling to sit and wait for death, the Origin Power had transformed into a rabbit and wandered about. Perhaps by design, perhaps by chance, it encountered Xia Ningshang and was ultimately refined by her, allowing it to revive like a withered tree meeting spring.

Ice Soul Star's Origin Power bore some resemblance to Tongxuan Continent's.

Tongxuan Continent's world had been on the verge of collapse, and only after the Origin sensed the crisis did it take action and seek out an opportunity.

Ice Soul Star was clearly not the same.

Could Origin Power truly give birth to sentience? Yang Kai suddenly grew deeply curious. Somewhere in the unseen workings of fate, he felt that Su Yan would probably gain a great deal from this trip.

Following the passage the Incarnation had opened earlier, Yang Kai held Su Yan close and traveled upward, soon returning to the surface.

Unlike the calm from before, at this moment all of Ice Soul Star was swept up in wind and snow. The cold wind pierced to the bone, ice and snow sliced through all things like blades, and thick clouds pressed down across the sky. Lightning flashed and thunder roared, as if the world itself were enraged.

Yang Kai grinned, raised his hand toward the sky, and swept it down, roaring, "Disperse!"

The thunder died, the lightning vanished, the ice and snow ceased, and the sky cleared for ten thousand li without a single cloud.

Although he was not Ice Soul Star's Star Master, his status as Master of the Star Domain was already a level above that of an ordinary Star Lord. Every movement he made secretly accorded with the Great Dao Laws, so controlling the weather of a single star was naturally effortless.

The will seemed to realize something as well. It no longer overestimated itself by opposing Yang Kai. Instead, an ice bridge that connected heaven and earth suddenly appeared above the snowy land, stretching all the way to Yang Kai and Su Yan's feet and leading off into the unknown distance.

The ice bridge was clear and crystalline, pure and flawless. Its sudden appearance left even Su Yan stunned.

Yang Kai laughed. "The host seems very hospitable. Let's go."

If he had not displayed such extraordinary methods just now, there was no way he would have enjoyed this warm welcome.

Holding Su Yan's hand, he stepped onto the ice bridge. Without expending any effort, they sped toward the end of the bridge.

Atop an ice mountain that towered into the clouds, a cool, clear voice suddenly rang out, "Guests have come to visit. Prepare to welcome them."

The ice mountain shook, and figures emerged one after another from the ice caves throughout it. At first glance, they looked no different from ordinary humans, but a closer look revealed that they were not human.

Every one of them seemed carved from ice and jade, pure and flawless. The men were handsome, the women ethereal, and all were dressed in white robes. Their pointed ears resembled beast ears, yet rather than detracting from their beauty, they added a noble air.

They were spirits of ice and snow, the dustless Ice Spirit Clan.

There were tens of thousands of Ice Spirits on the ice mountain. If outsiders learned of this scene, they would likely go mad.

In the Star Domain, even a single Ice Spirit could sell for a sky-high price, let alone tens of thousands of them. Their very existence was an enormous fortune.

The many Ice Spirits were all shocked, looking at one another as if they could not believe what they had just heard.

The owner of that cool, clear voice was the supreme existence of the Ice Spirit Clan, the root from which the Ice Spirit Clan had been born. It was the Ice God.

Ice Spirits were xenophobic and had no friends, yet the Ice God had actually told them that guests had come to visit. Naturally, they were all astonished.

Since when did the Ice Spirit Clan need to welcome guests? Still, since the Ice God had spoken, they hurried to prepare despite the doubts in their hearts. They were pure, not only in body, but also in heart, so naturally they did not dare show the slightest disrespect toward the Ice God's words.

An ice bridge suddenly stretched in from the distant horizon and connected to the waist of the ice mountain.

From atop that ice bridge, a man and a woman came side by side. Their figures did not seem to move, yet they soon arrived at the end of the bridge.

Once they landed, Yang Kai immediately looked around, clicking his tongue in wonder. This was his first time seeing the so-called Ice Spirits. Under his Divine Sense, their bodies seemed to be formed from ice crystals, their entire forms exuding cold air and appearing extremely sturdy. Cultivation realms seemed almost meaningless when applied to them; divisions like Void King Realm and Origin Returning Realm could not encompass their abilities.

Even so, Yang Kai could sense that many of them possessed strength no weaker than the Void King Realm.

What a powerful force! Yang Kai was secretly shocked. That such a force existed within the Star Domain was truly hard to believe. Back when the Netherworld Sect invaded the Star Domain, if they had attacked Ice Soul Star, their entire army probably would have been wiped out before Yang Kai ever needed to act.

Fortunately, the Ice Spirit Clan had long remained apart from the world and only moved about on Ice Soul Star. Otherwise, they had the ability to rule the entire Star Domain.

An incomparably handsome male Ice Spirit walked forward and asked curiously, "Are you the Ice God's guests?"

Yang Kai smiled. "If no one else has come here, then we are." Ice God... he did not know whether this was a self-proclaimed title or the respectful name the Ice Spirit Clan used for it, but it was rather fitting. After all, it was this star's Origin. On this Cultivation Star, it truly was a god.
